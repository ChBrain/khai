# Piece: Preset

## Taxonomy

[Choice architecture](piece_choice_architecture.md), the facet this is a part of. Preset models the option that takes effect when the user does not act -- the box that arrives ticked or unticked, the plan you are on until you change it, the setting as shipped, the enrolment that happens unless you decline it. Madrian and Shea on automatic enrolment, Johnson and Goldstein on presumed consent: the facet with the strongest field evidence of the four, reproduced wherever an employer or a state has flipped one. It is distinct from the bias engine's default effect, which is the persona's own pull toward what is standing -- the reason a preset works, read on a person rather than on the object -- and from guile's sneaking, where the pre-set state was chosen to slip a charge past someone and the intent is the trick. A preset is not a trick. It is the one setting nobody can decline to make, since a state of no state does not exist.

## Place

On every field, toggle, checkbox, and enrolment that has a state before the user touches it -- and on the whole object at the moment it is handed over, since everything it ships with is set to something.

## Load Bearing

What a preset holds up is the share of users who ended on the standing option: the participation in the retirement plan, the names on the donor register, the telemetry that is on, the renewal that happened. Change the preset and that share moves with it, further and faster than any argument moves it, and without a single mind having been changed. It bears an outcome produced by inaction, and it holds because doing nothing is the most common thing a user does -- not from any conviction, but because the standing option is the only one that requires nothing of them.

## Apparent

Nothing shows. A preset presents as the state of the world: the form as it arrives, the setting as it ships, the plan you appear to have. Nothing announces that someone chose it, and where it is announced it stops being a preset and becomes a question. The strongest one is the one nobody notices, which is also why the honest use of it and the dishonest use of it look identical on the surface.

## Yearbook

The record of a preset is the record of everyone who never touched it -- the enrolments nobody made, the consents nobody gave in words, the settings nobody visited. Its dates are the flips: the day the box shipped the other way and the numbers moved overnight, with no campaign, no persuasion, and no user reporting that anything had happened to them.
