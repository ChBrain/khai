# Process: Conjunction

## Taxonomy

[Representativeness](process_representativeness.md), the added-detail form.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: representativeness

## Initiated by

The persona judges a specific, prototype-fitting story more probable than the broader class that contains it, because the added detail makes it resemble the type more closely.
This is the conjunction error: a conjunction read as likelier than one of its own constituents, representativeness rising with each fitting detail while probability can only fall.
The fuller, more vivid picture feels more likely than the plainer one it is a part of.

## Direction

Toward the more detailed story, judged more probable the more it fits.
The conjunction error reads coherence as likelihood: a description of a person plus a fitting extra trait -- the outspoken graduate who is a bank teller _and_ a feminist -- is judged more probable than the barer claim that she is a bank teller, because the pair paints a more representative portrait.
The direction is exactly backward to probability: every added condition narrows the class and lowers the odds, but each fitting detail raises the resemblance, so the judgment climbs as the probability descends.

## Lever

The coherence of the fuller story, mistaken for its probability.
The conjunction error runs on representativeness tracking the fit of the whole picture: adding a detail that belongs to the stereotype makes the story hang together and look more like the type, and the persona reads that improved likeness as improved odds.
The lever is the extra condition read as support rather than constraint: a specific scenario that fits is felt as more believable than the general case, though logically it must be rarer, because the mind scores the story's coherence, not its extension.

## Echo

The specific beats the general, and a probability is judged below one of its parts.
It is the sharpest representativeness error -- the Linda problem, the detailed forecast trusted over the vague one, the rich scenario that sells because it convinces -- and it survives training, because the felt plausibility of the coherent story overrides the logic that a conjunction cannot exceed its conjunct.
It is [priors](process_priors.md) and [randomness](process_randomness.md)'s sibling: statistics overridden by likeness, here the conjunction rule specifically. The extensional logic of probability the error violates is a matter for cold reasoning; this owns the reading of a fitting detail as raising the odds.
