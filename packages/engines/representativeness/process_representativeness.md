# Process: Representativeness

## Taxonomy

Cognition / the social psychology of judgment under uncertainty.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: representativeness

## Initiated by

A persona judging whether a thing belongs to a category, or came from a process, answers by how much it resembles the prototype of that category or process.
This is representativeness: probability read off likeness, the degree to which a case looks like the type taken for the chance that it is one.
Where a cold judgment would weigh base rates, sample sizes, and reliability, representativeness asks _how much does this look like the type?_ and reads the answer as _how likely is it to be the type?_

## Direction

Toward the prototype, and away from the statistics that should govern.
Representativeness maps resemblance onto probability: the case that fits the stereotype is judged a likely member and the case that fits it poorly a rare one, regardless of how common the category actually is or how reliable the evidence is.
The direction is set by similarity, not by frequency or diagnosticity: the judgment tracks how well the instance matches the picture, and slides off true wherever likeness and probability part -- which is wherever the base rate, the sample size, or the regression to the mean would have pulled the estimate the other way.

## Lever

Similarity to the type, mistaken for probability of the type.
What representativeness spends is a real cue -- members of a category often do resemble its prototype -- pushed past its warrant: the persona substitutes the easy question of resemblance for the hard question of probability, and reports the match as the odds.
The lever is the substitution: because resemblance is insensitive to base rate, to sample size, and to the reliability of the evidence, the judgment inherits none of them -- a vivid, well-fitting description overrides the prior, the fuller story feels likelier than the sparer, and chance is expected to look like chance.

## Echo

The estimate tracks the stereotype, and the statistics are overridden.
Representativeness is why the base rate is ignored, the specific story outsells the general one, and the gambler waits for the coin to correct -- because in each the persona has read a likeness where a probability was asked for.
It runs as [priors](process_priors.md), [conjunction](process_conjunction.md), or [randomness](process_randomness.md) -- the base rate ignored, the fitting detail trusted, chance expected to look representative. The sister heuristic that judges by ease of recall is the availability engine's; the one that draws an estimate toward a reference figure is the anchoring engine's; the mode by which a choice among options is reached is the decision engine's; the fixed social stereotypes this look hardens into, and the standing catalogued tilt it leaves, are the bias and stigma engines'. This engine owns the reading of resemblance as probability itself.
