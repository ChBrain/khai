# Plot: Triangulation

## Taxonomy

A three-party relational bind (Bowen).

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: triangulation

## Cue

Two people bound closely enough that the tension between them cannot simply be walked off -- a couple, a parent and child, two colleagues yoked to one task, any pair whose tie holds them in the same field. The strain between them rises past what the pair can settle head-on; the room holds the ordinary weight of two people who cannot resolve each other and cannot leave.

## Action

The rising tension finds a third. Rather than hold the strain between them, the pair routes it through someone outside the dyad -- pulling the third in, or displacing the conflict onto them -- and the two-person system becomes a three-person one that can bear what the two could not. Bowen's triangle is exactly this move: the smallest stable unit of a relationship system is not the pair but the trio, because a third gives the anxiety a place to go. The pair steadies; the third now carries what the pair set down.

## Stage

The triangle takes one of its forms as the third is brought in. [Detour](plot_detour.md) routes the couple's conflict onto the third as a problem to manage together; [Coalition](plot_coalition.md) binds two of the three against the one across a boundary; [Crossfire](plot_crossfire.md) catches the third between the two, pressed by each to side against the other; and [Stabilizer](plot_stabilizer.md) reads the whole the other way -- the third's binding-in as the brace holding the pair together, the symptom the system needs.

## Tension

The pair is stabilized, and the stabilizing is the trap. The third's position is not a passing role but a fixed one the system now depends on: the strain that should sit between the two is lodged in the trio, so the pair no longer has to face what divides them, and the third can no more step out than the pair can take the strain back. Resolving the pair's real conflict would collapse the triangle and return the tension it was built to hold -- so the triangle resists its own undoing, and the third stays bound because the pair cannot survive the pair without them.
