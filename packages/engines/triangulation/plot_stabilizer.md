# Plot: Stabilizer

## Taxonomy

[Triangulation](plot_triangulation.md), the form where the triangle is read as the system's own brace.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: triangulation

## Cue

A pair that looks, from outside, as though a third is the trouble -- the affair wrecking the marriage, the problem child straining the parents, the meddling in-law dividing the couple. The obvious reading is that the third is what is wrong. But underneath, the pair is one the strain would have broken long ago on its own, and the third is the only reason it still stands.

## Action

The triangle is read the other way: the third is not the dysfunction but the brace holding the pair up. Bowen's insight turned the frame -- the symptom, the scapegoat, the third party is doing the load-bearing work, absorbing the tension the pair cannot metabolize so the pair can go on. The child's illness gives the parents a shared project that keeps them married; the affair vents a pressure that would otherwise crack the couple; the feud with the in-law unites the two against a common outside. What presents as the wound is the suture.

## Stage

The third carries what the pair set down, and the pair endures on that carrying -- functional, even placid, so long as the third holds the strain. The cost is hidden because it is paid entirely by the one bound in: the family looks stable, and the stability is quietly built on the one member spending themselves to hold it.

## Tension

Pull the third out untimed and the pair, not the third, is what breaks. Cure the symptom, end the affair, banish the in-law, and the tension the triangle was holding floods back into the dyad that could never bear it -- so the well-meant rescue of the third can detonate the couple, and the system fights to keep its brace in place. This is the turn the whole engine rests on: the triangled-in third is not simply a victim of the pair's failure but the structural solution to it, and freeing them means facing what their binding-in was built to spare everyone -- the pair's own unsurvived conflict, handed back.
