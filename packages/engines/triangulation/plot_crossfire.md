# Plot: Crossfire

## Taxonomy

[Triangulation](plot_triangulation.md), the form where the third is caught between and pulled both ways.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: triangulation

## Cue

Two parties in open conflict, each with a claim on the same third -- two parents each wanting the child's loyalty, two friends each wanting the mutual friend to take their side, a divided house where the one in the middle is wanted by both. The third is close enough to each that neither will release their claim, and the two are far enough apart that the claims cannot both be met.

## Action

Each of the two presses the third to side with them against the other. This is triangulation in Minuchin's narrow sense: the third is not detoured onto, nor allied with one, but demanded by both at once -- each party requiring that the third's move toward them be a move against the other. Every gesture the third makes is scored by two scorekeepers with opposite books, so a kindness to one is read as a wound by the other, and there is no act available that does not betray somebody.

## Stage

The third stands in the open field between the two, each pull answered by the opposite pull, and learns the only safe move is not to move: the child who will not choose a parent, who goes silent, splits the difference, or sickens rather than side; the friend who ghosts both. The paralysis reads from outside as evasion or coldness, and is in truth the single position that does not detonate one of the two claims.

## Tension

The third is punished for every choice and for refusing to choose, so the bind has no exit from inside. To move toward either is to betray the other; to stay still is to fail both; to leave the field is forbidden by the closeness that made them wanted in the first place. The two, meanwhile, keep their own conflict alive by fighting it through the third -- the child's loyalty is the ground the divorce is still being waged on -- so the crossfire persists exactly because the third's paralysis lets neither party win and neither party lose.
