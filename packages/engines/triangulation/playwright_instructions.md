# Instructions: Triangulation

How a Playwright wires the triangulation engine into a play. You wire by linking the play's own content to the engine's files; the engine files stay untouched, they are canon. The Roadie plumbs the engine's law into Instructions Knowledge on deploy. Authoring guidance, not runtime content, and it does not go on tour.

## Human

- The human sets the three parties and the bind: which pair's tension cannot be settled head-on, which third it recruits or displaces onto, and whether the triangle is the play's slow structure or a live movement -- and never as a diagnosis of a real family, always as authored dramatic structure.

## Agent

- From the play's own content, a plot whose three-party bind is an actor links under Tension the form its triangle takes: detour, the couple's conflict displaced onto a third made the problem or the patient; coalition, two bound against one across a boundary and denied when named; crossfire, the third caught between two who each demand it side against the other; or stabilizer, the third's binding-in read as the brace holding the pair together. Cast the triangle as structure -- the routing of a pair's strain through a third, present in what the three do, never narrated -- and keep it the bind, not a persona's own trait, and never a claim about a real family. You link to the engine, you never edit it.

## Collaboration

- A persona's own disposition to triangulate or be drawn in is that persona's engines'; the rival-threatens-a-bond emotion is jealousy's; the violated trust is betrayal's; the two-party communicational trap is the double-bind engine's. Triangulation is the family-systems sibling of double-bind -- a named relational structure cast as plot, the three-party bind to double-bind's two-party one. Whether the triangle rings true to the three parties, and stays authored structure rather than diagnosis, is the team's review.

## Knowledge

- Triangulation is a three-party plot: a two-person tension, too great for the pair to settle head-on, recruits or displaces onto a third that binds it -- the triangle Bowen named the smallest stable unit of a relationship system, the trio that holds what the pair could not. It wires on a plot at Tension, and it is the structure of the bind, not a trait of any party. Four forms carry it -- detour is the couple's conflict submerged by making a third the problem (detour-attack) or the patient (detour-support), so the pair unites by facing the third instead of each other (Minuchin); coalition is a covert alliance of two against one across a boundary, denied when named, the perverse cross-generational triangle (Haley); crossfire is the third caught between two who each demand it side against the other, so any move betrays someone and paralysis is the only safety (Minuchin's triangulation proper); stabilizer is the systemic turn, the triangled-in third read as the brace holding the pair together, the symptom the system needs (Bowen). Read a plot by the form its triangle takes, and read stabilizer two ways: the third bound in is not only the casualty of the pair's failure but the structural solution to it, so freeing them hands the pair back its own unsurvived conflict.

## System

- Do cast the triangle before its forms: the pair whose tension cannot be held, the third it recruits, so the bind reads as a structure and not a simple conflict between two.
- Do route through the form that fits: detour for conflict displaced onto a scapegoat or patient, coalition for two covertly bound against one, crossfire for a third pulled both ways, stabilizer for the third whose binding-in holds the pair up.
- Do read stabilizer as the engine's turn: pulling the third out untimed breaks the pair, not the third -- the symptom is load-bearing, and the system fights to keep its brace.
- Do not model a persona's own trait (that is the persona's engines), the emotion inside the bind (jealousy, betrayal), or the two-party communicational trap (double-bind). Do not cast the triangle as a diagnosis of a real person or family; it is authored dramatic structure. Do not edit the engine's files; wire only from the play's side.
