# Process: Conjuring

## Taxonomy

[Imagination](process_imagination.md), the sensory mode -- a quasi-perceptual image summoned before the mind's eye.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: imagination

## Initiated by

A persona calls up a sensory image of what is not before it.
Conjuring starts wherever a persona forms a quasi-perceptual representation on purpose -- picturing the absent face, hearing the tune in the head, feeling the imagined touch, seeing the room rearranged before a thing is moved -- the mind's eye recreating a percept without the thing perceived.
It is the imagistic mode: imagination as it feels most like perceiving, the sensory recreation Kosslyn's studies caught in the mind rotating and scanning its own images as if they were seen.

## Direction

Into a sensory representation, held and inspected like a perception.
Conjuring builds an image with perceptual character and works it as if looking -- the persona rotating the pictured object, zooming in on a remembered detail, scanning across the imagined scene, reading off properties the image was not explicitly told (the taller of two conjured animals, the shape a folded paper would take).
It moves the mind from the given to the pictured: not receiving a sensory world but generating one, the quasi-percept standing in for the percept, available to be examined, altered, and combined the way a seen thing is -- but summoned, and answerable to the persona rather than to the light.

## Lever

The vividness of the summoned image against the faintness of the merely thought.
The carrying force is the persona's power to generate an image with enough perceptual body to be inspected -- the strong visualizer who sees the scene almost as if present; the resistance is the image's tendency to thin and slip, the effort of holding it against a competing real percept, and the wide variation in the power itself, down to the aphantasic mind that forms no image at all and must imagine without pictures.
Hold the image vivid and it can be scanned and turned like a seen thing; let it fade and the sensory mode collapses, and the imagining must run propositionally instead.

## Echo

The imagining read as its summoned image.
Conjuring leaves the persona holding a quasi-perceptual representation -- the pictured, the heard-in-the-head, the felt-in-imagination -- and hands that image to be inspected, altered, and built with.
It is the sensory face of imagination: the mode nearest to perception, the mind's eye that sees what is not there, and the raw material a fuller scenario is often rendered in.
Conjuring is read from the picturing, the persona known by the sensory images it summons before the mind's eye.
