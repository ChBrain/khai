# Imagination

Imagination as process: the deliberate forming and manipulation of mental representations of what is not present -- summoning a sensory image to the mind's eye (conjuring), entertaining a proposition as if it held without believing it (supposition), and building and running a novel scenario from parts (simulation) -- the voluntary construction of the non-present, apart from the spontaneous drift, the future self, and the counterfactual past that neighbor it.
