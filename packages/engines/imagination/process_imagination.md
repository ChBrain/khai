# Process: Imagination

## Taxonomy

Cognitive faculty, the making-present of the not-present.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: imagination

## Initiated by

A persona turns from what is present to hold before the mind something that is not.
The trigger is a call to represent the absent -- to picture a face not here, to suppose a state of affairs that does not hold, to build the scene of an event that never happened -- and the persona's turn inward to construct it deliberately, not to have it drift up unbidden.
What enters the process is the not-present made an object of thought on purpose: the mind's power to recreate perception without its object and to entertain what is not the case, set going by a persona choosing to imagine rather than merely to perceive or recall.

## Direction

Toward a representation held and worked, not received.
Imagination constructs where perception takes in and memory recovers: it recreates the sensory or propositional state (Currie and Ravenscroft's recreation -- imagining as the offline running of the same capacities perception and belief use online), and it holds the result to be turned, extended, and combined. The motion is voluntary construction: the persona summons the image, supposes the premise, assembles the scenario, and then manipulates what it has made -- rotating the pictured object, drawing out the supposition's consequences, running the scenario forward.
The direction is deliberate and offline: a made thing, quarantined from belief and action, entertained rather than taken as real.

## Lever

The power to recreate the absent against the tether to the actual.
Whether the imagining runs turns on the persona's capacity to decouple representation from the present world -- to hold a state as merely entertained, marked off from what it believes and perceives, so the image does not become a hallucination and the supposition does not become a belief (the offline quarantine that lets imagining inform without misleading). The resistance is the pull of the real: the difficulty of picturing against a crowded percept, of supposing against a fixed conviction, of building a scenario the known facts keep dragging back.
The lever is not perceiving harder but constructing freely inside the quarantine; hold the made state apart from the actual and it can be worked, let it collapse into belief or percept and it is no longer imagined.

## Echo

The construction runs in one of three modes, the standard carving of the imaginative act: [conjuring](process_conjuring.md), the sensory recreation of a quasi-perceptual image before the mind's eye; [supposition](process_supposition.md), the propositional entertaining of an as-if premise without belief; and [simulation](process_simulation.md), the constructive assembly of a novel scenario from remembered and invented parts, run forward to see how it would go (Schacter and Addis).
The three are not stages and not exclusive; one act of imagining may conjure an image, suppose a premise, and simulate a scenario at once.
What this engine owns is the deliberate construction of the non-present. It is not the spontaneous, task-unrelated drift of attention inward, which is the mind-wandering engine's; not the future self a persona hopes or dreads to become, which is possible-selves'; not the counterfactual undoing of the actual past, which is regret's; not the reconstruction of what did happen, which is memory's; and not the pretend stance a persona takes toward a real activity, which is play-mode's. Imagination supplies the made representation; those engines own the drift, the future self, the counterfactual, the recollection, and the stance.
