# Process: Supposition

## Taxonomy

[Imagination](process_imagination.md), the propositional mode -- an as-if premise entertained without belief.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: imagination

## Initiated by

A persona holds a proposition as if it were the case, without taking it to be.
Supposition starts wherever a persona entertains that something holds and reasons from it while withholding belief -- suppose the bridge were out, suppose she had said yes, suppose I were the one deciding -- the as-if premise taken up, worked, and kept quarantined from what the persona actually believes.
It is the propositional mode: imagination not as a picture but as a supposal, the mind running inferences off a premise it has adopted only for the imagining, in what Nichols and Stich modelled as a workspace held apart from the belief store.

## Direction

Out from a premise held as-if, along the inferences it licenses.
Supposition takes a supposed proposition and elaborates it -- feeding it to the same inferential machinery belief uses, so the persona draws out what would follow, updates the supposed world as real knowledge bears on it, and reaches conclusions that hold within the supposition without ever entering belief.
It moves the mind from a premise to its consequences under quarantine: the persona thinks as if P, follows where P leads, and returns -- the supposed world elaborated richly and then set down, its conclusions marked as belonging to the supposition, not to the actual.

## Lever

The discipline of the as-if against the collapse into belief or fancy.
The carrying force is the persona's capacity to hold a premise adopted-not-believed and still reason rigorously from it -- to let the supposition inherit real-world logic so its consequences are informative, not arbitrary; the resistance is contamination in both directions: the supposition leaking into belief (coming half to credit the as-if), or belief leaking into the supposition (refusing to follow the premise where it truly leads because the persona knows it is false).
Keep the premise quarantined and rigorously worked and the supposition yields real inference about the possible; let the wall fail and it decays into either a new conviction or an idle daydream.

## Echo

The imagining read as its entertained premise.
Supposition leaves the persona holding a proposition as-if and the conclusions it licenses -- the worked-out consequences of a premise adopted but not believed -- and hands that supposed structure to reasoning, planning, and the appraisal of what is merely possible.
It is the propositional face of imagination: the mode that lets a persona think past the actual into the hypothetical, test a premise without committing to it, and inhabit another's view or another world's rules for the length of the supposing.
Supposition is read from the as-if, the persona known by the premises it entertains without believing and the inferences it draws from them.
