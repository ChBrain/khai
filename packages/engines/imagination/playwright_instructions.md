# Instructions: Imagination

How a Playwright wires the imagination engine into a play. You wire by linking the play's own content to the engine's files; the engine files stay untouched, they are canon. The Roadie plumbs the engine's law into Instructions Knowledge on deploy. Authoring guidance, not runtime content, and it does not go on tour.

## Human

- The human sets what the persona imagines and why: the absent thing held before the mind -- a pictured scene, a supposed premise, a rehearsed scenario -- and whether that imagining is a quiet background capacity or a live act in the play.

## Agent

- From the play's own content, a persona whose imagining is an actor links the mode it runs in under Projection: conjuring, summoning a sensory image before the mind's eye; supposition, entertaining an as-if premise without believing it; or simulation, building and running a novel scenario. Play the imagination as the persona deliberately constructing something not present -- shown in what it pictures, supposes, or rehearses, present, not narrated -- and keep it quarantined: the imagined is held as made, apart from what the persona believes and perceives. Keep it the deliberate construction, not the spontaneous drift inward (that is mind-wandering), the future self (possible-selves), the counterfactual undoing of the actual past (regret), the recollection of what happened (memory), or the pretend stance toward an activity (play-mode). You link to the engine, you never edit it.

## Collaboration

- The spontaneous task-unrelated drift is the mind-wandering engine's; the future self a persona hopes or dreads to become is possible-selves'; the counterfactual re-running of the actual past is regret's; the reconstruction of what did happen is memory's (which shares machinery with imaginative simulation but aims at the real past); the pretend stance toward an activity is play-mode's. Imagination is an adjacent engine of the creativity cluster -- the faculty that constructs the non-present, the raw representing the generative engines draw on. Whether the imagining rings true to the persona is the team's review.

## Knowledge

- Imagination is the deliberate forming and manipulation of mental representations of what is not present: the voluntary making-present of the absent, the mind recreating perception or belief offline and quarantined from action (Currie & Ravenscroft's recreation). It wires on a persona at Projection, and it is the constructing the persona does, not the drift that happens to it. Three modes carry it -- conjuring is the sensory, imagistic mode, a quasi-perceptual image summoned before the mind's eye and inspected as if seen (Kosslyn); supposition is the propositional mode, an as-if premise entertained without belief and reasoned from by belief's own inference (Nichols & Stich); simulation is the constructive, combinatorial mode, a novel scenario assembled from remembered and invented parts and run forward to see how it would go (Schacter & Addis). One act of imagining may run in more than one mode at once, and the whole of it is held offline -- the image is not a hallucination, the supposition is not a belief, the scenario is not a forecast unless honestly run. Read a persona by the mode its imagining runs in, and read simulation two ways: run on real dynamics it pre-experiences a possibility, run on wishes or fears it rehearses a fantasy.

## System

- Do give a persona whose imagining is an actor its mode under Projection; do play the imagination as the deliberate construction of the non-present, shown not narrated; do keep the imagined quarantined -- held as made, apart from belief and percept.
- Do route through the mode that fits: conjuring for a summoned sensory image, supposition for an entertained as-if premise, simulation for a built and run scenario; one act may use more than one.
- Do read simulation two ways: built coherent and run honestly it pre-experiences a real possibility and informs a choice; built to flatter or to dread it rehearses a fantasy, and the persona may mistake its own construction for a forecast.
- Do not play the spontaneous drift inward as imagination; that is mind-wandering. Do not play the future self, the counterfactual past, or the recollection as imagination; those are possible-selves, regret, and memory. Do not play the pretend stance toward an activity as imagination; that is play-mode. Do not edit the engine's files; wire only from the play's side.
