# Process: Chance

## Taxonomy

[Attribution](process_attribution.md), the reading this is one form of.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: attribution

## Initiated by

An outcome asks its why, and the persona reaches for the luck of it.
The result is read as an accident of timing or fortune: it fell this way this once, and could as easily have fallen the other -- no standing cause behind it, in the actor or the world.

## Direction

Toward luck as the cause, at the external and unstable corner.
The persona locates the outcome in something outside them and passing -- the coin that came up, the day it happened to be, the break that did or did not come -- and reads it as unrepeatable, telling nothing certain about the next time.
The account points outward and leaves the future undetermined.

## Lever

The reading of the cause as random and unrepeatable.
When the outcome seems detached from both the actor's doing and any stable condition -- when the same act might well have produced the opposite result -- the cause is read as chance. Because it is unstable, it makes no prediction: a win here is no promise, a loss here no sentence.
It is the placement that neither credits nor condemns the self, which is exactly what makes it available as the flattering read of a loss and the modest read of a win.

## Echo

The cause is fixed in the luck, and the future is left open.
A failure read as bad luck keeps hope -- the wind turns, and the next attempt is not doomed by this one; a success read as luck brings relief or a surprised gratitude more than pride, and little confidence it repeats.
Set against [circumstance](process_circumstance.md), both readings clear the actor, but only chance leaves the odds resettable -- and where the persona lands between the wall that stays and the wind that turns decides whether they walk away resigned or willing to roll again.
