# Process: Effort

## Taxonomy

[Attribution](process_attribution.md), the reading this is one form of.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: attribution

## Initiated by

An outcome asks its why, and the persona reaches for how hard the actor tried.
The result is read as coming from application rather than nature: the thing was done or missed because of the exertion put in this time, not because of a standing trait.

## Direction

Toward the actor's trying as the cause, at the internal but unstable corner.
The persona locates the outcome in something that was inside them yet is not fixed -- the push given, the hours spent, the attention held -- and reads it as this occasion's doing, changeable by the next.
The account points inward and leaves the future open.

## Lever

The reading of the cause as within the actor's control.
Effort is the one corner the actor is held to command, so the outcome placed here carries responsibility and, with it, the room to do otherwise: a failure was not fated but under-worked, a success was earned rather than given.
Because the cause is unstable and controllable, it is the placement most tied to what the persona will try next -- and the one most open to the flattering read that a win was all effort and a loss all circumstance.

## Echo

The cause is fixed in the trying, and the future stays open around it.
A success read as effort earns a clean pride; a failure read as too-little-effort turns to guilt -- the discomfort of the undone that could have been done -- but keeps hope, because next time the effort can change.
Set against [disposition](process_disposition.md), this is the difference between "I cannot" and "I did not": the same inward locus, but the unstable corner is where persistence lives and the stable corner is where it dies.
