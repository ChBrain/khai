# Process: Circumstance

## Taxonomy

[Attribution](process_attribution.md), the reading this is one form of.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: attribution

## Initiated by

An outcome asks its why, and the persona reaches outside the actor into the situation.
The result is read as produced by the conditions the act happened in: the difficulty of the task, the rules of the place, the role the actor was cast in, the pressure they were under.

## Direction

Toward the situation as the cause, at the external and stable corner.
The persona locates the outcome in a standing feature of the world rather than in the person -- the system as it is built, the bar set where it is set -- and reads it as a condition that will still be there next time.
The account points outward and expects the same conditions to hold.

## Lever

The reading of the cause as a fixed property of the setting.
When most people would have fared the same in this situation, the cause is read into the situation, not the actor: a hard task defeats the able, an easy one flatters the weak, and a role scripts what the person in it does. Because the condition is stable, the reading carries forward -- the wall was there before and will be there again.
It is the placement that most nearly clears the actor without crediting them: the outcome was neither their doing nor their fault.

## Echo

The cause is fixed in the setting, and the future is read as holding that setting.
A failure laid on circumstance spares the persona shame -- it was not them -- but, because the condition is stable, breeds resignation rather than hope: the same wall stands next time. A success laid on an easy circumstance takes little pride, for the credit went to the conditions.
Its unstable twin is [chance](process_chance.md): both place the cause outside the actor, but where circumstance is the wall that stays, chance is the wind that turns -- and only one of them leaves the next attempt looking different.
