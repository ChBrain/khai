# Process: Attribution

## Taxonomy

Social cognition / causal explanation.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: attribution

## Initiated by

An outcome has landed -- a success, a failure, a slight, a kindness -- and the persona asks, out loud or beneath it, why.
The question fires whenever the result matters and its cause is not obvious: the organism does not rest on the outcome but reaches behind it for the cause that produced it.
What it settles on is rarely the whole truth; it is the account the persona can build from what they saw and what they already believe.

## Direction

Toward a cause the persona can name and place.
The reach is to locate the outcome's source -- in the actor or in the world, in something lasting or something passing -- so the result stops being a bare fact and becomes a thing explained.
The account, once fixed, is what the persona carries forward, not the outcome itself.

## Lever

Where the cause is placed, along two axes the persona reads it on.
The first is locus: is the cause inside the actor -- their nature, their trying -- or outside, in the situation or the luck? The second is stability: will the cause still hold next time, or was it particular to this once? A third, control, cuts across them: could the actor have done otherwise?
The persona weighs the evidence -- how others fared, how this one usually fares, how they fare elsewhere -- but weighs it quickly and under the pull of what flatters or protects, so the placement is a reading, not a proof.

## Echo

The cause is fixed, and the fixing sets what follows.
The emotion tracks the placement: a failure laid on a stable inner lack sinks toward shame and hopelessness, the same failure laid on a night's slack turns to guilt with room to try again; a good turn read as the giver's nature warms differently than one read as luck.
The expectancy tracks the stability: a cause that will hold predicts the same result next time, a cause that was passing leaves the future open.
The process continues as [disposition](process_disposition.md), [effort](process_effort.md), [circumstance](process_circumstance.md), or [chance](process_chance.md) -- the four corners of locus by stability. The systematic slant on the reading -- over-blaming the person, sparing the self -- is the bias engine's, not asserted here.
