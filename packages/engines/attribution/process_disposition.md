# Process: Disposition

## Taxonomy

[Attribution](process_attribution.md), the reading this is one form of.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: attribution

## Initiated by

An outcome asks its why, and the persona reaches into the actor's nature for the answer.
The result is read as corresponding to who the actor is: their ability, their character, their intent -- a standing property of the person, not the moment.

## Direction

Toward the actor as the cause, at the internal and stable corner.
The persona infers from the act to a disposition behind it and treats that disposition as the explanation: they did it because that is the kind of person they are, or the persona failed because they simply lack the thing the task required.
The account points inward and expects the same to hold next time.

## Lever

The leap from an act to an enduring trait.
A single behaviour is taken as a sign of a standing character, especially when the act is unusual or freely chosen; the more the outcome seems to fit a type, the faster the leap. Because the cause is read as stable, it carries a verdict about the future, not only the past.
The pull to make this leap about others, past what the evidence warrants, is the correspondence bias -- owned by the bias engine; this reading is the placement itself, not its overreach.

## Echo

The cause is fixed in the person, and the future is read as closed around it.
A success credited to ability lifts toward confident pride; a failure hung on a fixed inner lack sinks toward shame and the expectancy that trying will not change it -- the bleakest of the four failure readings.
Its near neighbour is [effort](process_effort.md): the same inward locus, but unstable and controllable -- and whether a failure is read as fixed ability or as a night's slack decides whether the persona gives up or goes again.
