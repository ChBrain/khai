# Instructions: Attribution

How a Playwright wires the attribution engine into a play. You wire by linking the play's own content to the engine's files; the engine files stay untouched, they are canon. The Roadie plumbs the engine's law into Instructions Knowledge on deploy. Authoring guidance, not runtime content, and it does not go on tour.

## Human

- The human defines the outcome and its terrain: what result the persona is explaining, whose behaviour it was, and what history and stake shape the account they reach for.

## Agent

- From the play's own content, a persona links the attribution reading the moment runs (disposition, effort, circumstance, or chance) under its Projection. Let the reading set what the persona now feels about the outcome and what they expect to follow; let the failure hung on a stable cause close the future and the one hung on an unstable cause leave it open. You link to the engine, you never edit it.

## Collaboration

- The systematic slant on the reading -- over-blaming the person, sparing the self -- is the bias engine's; this engine supplies the placement, bias supplies the tilt. The generalized sense of where control lies is the locus-of-control engine's. The feelings the reading triggers -- pride, shame, guilt, gratitude -- are their own engines'. The held theory of ability behind it is the mindset engine's, and the task-specific confidence is self-efficacy's.

## Knowledge

- Attribution is how a persona assigns a cause to an outcome, along its locus (in the actor or the world) and its stability (lasting or passing), and how that placement sets the emotion and the expectancy that follow. Disposition places the cause in the actor's enduring nature (internal, stable). Effort places it in how hard they tried (internal, unstable, controllable). Circumstance places it in the standing situation (external, stable). Chance places it in passing luck (external, unstable). A failure read as stable closes the future; one read as unstable leaves it open.

## System

- Do let the placement drive the sequel: the same failure is shame or guilt depending on whether it lands on a fixed lack or a night's slack.
- Do use the stability axis for the arc: a persona who moves a failure from disposition to effort is a persona who finds the will to try again.
- Do leave the systematic error to the bias engine; this engine reads the cause, it does not slant it.
- Do not edit the engine's files; wire only from the play's side.
