# Process: The Completing

## Taxonomy

[Collection](piece_collection.md), and where the engine is seen for one thing.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: collection

## Initiated by

The series approaches whole, and the collector does something about it.
The Completing starts when the gap narrows to a few items and the end of the thing becomes visible from where they are standing.

## Direction

Toward a boundary that moves as it is approached.
As the last items come in, the criterion begins to change: a variant is discovered that must be had, a condition threshold is revised, a related series suggests itself, a date range extends by a decade.
None of it is experienced as evasion. Each refinement is genuine, argued on the merits, and the collector could defend every one of them to another collector and be right.

## Lever

The sincerity of each refinement against what the refinements accomplish together.
The carrying force is that a criterion should evolve: a collector who learns their field will see distinctions they could not see at the start, and refusing to act on that knowledge would be the worse kind of collecting -- the refinements are knowledge, not excuses; the resistance is that they arrive precisely when they are needed and at no other time, and the collector has no way to tell an insight from a reprieve.
The Completing rides the engine's keystone knife-edge: a collection is made of what is missing from it.

## Echo

A series with one item outstanding, permanently.
It shows why a collection cannot be finished by anybody who wants it finished, and why the ones that are finished are finished by accident or by somebody else.
The Completing is dated every time the end comes into view.
