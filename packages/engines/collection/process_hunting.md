# Process: The Hunting

## Taxonomy

[Collection](piece_collection.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: collection

## Initiated by

The collector goes looking, and most of the time comes back with nothing.
The hunting is what the collection actually consists of: the searching, the watching, the near misses, the item seen and not bought, the long ordinary stretches in which nothing is added.

## Direction

Toward an acquisition that is over almost immediately.
The disproportion is the point. The search takes months and the having takes an afternoon: the object arrives, is examined, is placed, and joins the part of the collection nobody is thinking about any more.
So the collector's real occupation is the interval, and the acquisitions punctuate it.

## Lever

The pleasure of the finding against how briefly it lasts.
The carrying force is that the hunting is genuinely the good part and the collector would say so first: it is absorbing, it is skilled, it fills otherwise empty hours with a purpose, and a persona with a subject they know deeply is better off than one without; the resistance is that the satisfaction is delivered by the search and charged to the object, so the collector keeps buying things in order to go on having something to look for.
The Hunting is the product and the objects are the receipts.

## Echo

An interval with a purpose in it.
It shows that a collection is an activity rather than a holding.
The Hunting is dated continuously and is interrupted by acquisitions.
