# Position: Collector

## Taxonomy

[Collection](piece_collection.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: collection

## Has

A jurisdiction and a want list. The collector holds two things, and the objects are the smaller: the other is the criterion -- what belongs to the series, what is a variant worth having, what is merely similar -- which is theirs and is not shared.
They also hold an inventory of absences more detailed than their inventory of possessions, and they can recite it without checking.

## Orders

The position demands upkeep of the search and of the criterion. A collector who has stopped looking has a cupboard rather than a collection, so the position is maintained by activity: catalogues read, sales watched, other collectors known, the field's small news followed.
It also demands that the boundary be defensible. A criterion nobody can state is one the persona will eventually be unable to justify to themselves, which is where a collection quietly becomes an accumulation.

## Loses

The position forecloses ordinary use of the objects. What the collector owns they may not spend, wear out, or enjoy in the way the thing was built for, and the better the example the less it may be used.
It forecloses being finished, because a completed criterion returns the persona to being somebody who used to collect.
And it forecloses an honest answer to what the collection is worth, since the figure that would be produced by selling it is not the figure the collector is holding.

## Drives

When an item comes available -- or when the series is threatened by completion, by a rival holding the piece, or by somebody asking what it is all for -- the collector either acquires and closes the gap, which obliges them to find another, or widens the criterion so the field reopens, or starts a second series alongside the first.
