# Collection

Collection as piece: a series of objects withdrawn from the use they were made for and held to a criterion that exists only in its keeper -- not the possession experienced as part of the self, which is extended-self's, and not the object withheld from exchange to carry a lineage, which is heirloom's. The object is the piece; the members are what happens around it. One position names who holds it: a collector holding a jurisdiction and a want list, who cannot use the objects, cannot be finished, and cannot price it honestly. Three processes name what it does: the hunting, which is the activity the collection actually consists of and which charges the search's satisfaction to the object; the completing, where the criterion widens exactly when the end comes into view and the collector cannot tell an insight from a reprieve; and the dispersal, which leaves every object intact and destroys the only thing that made them a set. The twist: a collection is made of what is missing from it, so completing it would end it, and the item that would finish the series is the one most wanted and least wanted at once.
