# Process: Rage

## Taxonomy

[Anger](process_anger.md), the flooded form.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: anger

## Initiated by

Anger crosses into flooding -- arousal overtops control, the appraisal narrows to the offense and the impulse to strike, and the persona is carried past deliberation.
This is rage: the dyscontrolled extreme, the fury, the seeing-red in which the emotion overruns the one who has it.
It is anger past the point where it can still be aimed.

## Direction

Toward the offender, to overwhelm and obliterate the threat to the self -- the correction abandoned for the discharge.
Rage does not measure a response to a wrong; it floods at it, seeking not to right the account but to release the pressure through the nearest channel the moment offers.

## Lever

The loss of regulation -- the arousal that was mobilizing becomes disorganizing, foreclosing the pause in which anger could be checked, proportioned, or redirected.
What rage spends is judgment and proportion, both flooded out; the very intensity that makes it feel powerful is what strips it of aim.

## Echo

The offense is answered with force out of all scale -- the burnt bridge, the word that cannot be unsaid, the act regretted the moment the flood recedes.
Rage is where anger is most dangerous and least strategic, the emotion at its least governed and its most costly.
The violence or attack it can drive is the aggression engine's; the return to control after it is regulation's; rage is the emotion flooded past its banks.
