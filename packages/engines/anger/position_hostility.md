# Position: Hostility

## Taxonomy

Affect / the trait of hostility -- the standing readiness beneath [Anger](process_anger.md) (Spielberger's trait anger, the STAXI; the hostility component of the Aggression Questionnaire).

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: anger

## Has

A resting disposition to read the world as hostile and to anger readily, often, and long.
Trait anger, as against the state: the persona angered by more provocations, faster, and for longer than most -- a low threshold and a slow return.
Its cognitive core is cynical hostility -- the standing belief that others are selfish, untrustworthy, and out to provoke -- which supplies the anger its ready reasons.

## Orders

Run from here, ahead of the provocation.
The hostile persona reads ambiguous acts as deliberate slights (the hostile attribution bias), finds more offenses because it expects them, and carries the anger between occasions rather than discharging it clean; the tilt reaches even into the body, trait hostility tracking cardiovascular risk in the Type-A and cynical-hostility literature.
It colors the read before the event, the way a mood does, but on the single axis of the provoking.

## Loses

The benefit of the doubt.
The highly hostile persona meets a world already half-offending -- fewer acts read as neutral, more as aimed against it -- and loses the ease of the ungrudged encounter, the plain surface on which another's move is not yet an affront.
What is lost is latitude: the margin before offense narrows.

## Drives

On the loss of that latitude:
rides the hostility -- trusts the suspicion, answers the expected slight, lets the world be as adversarial as it appears -- or works against it, reappraising the ambiguous act and widening the benefit of the doubt, at a cost in vigilance.
The threshold holds underneath either way, a set point the persona manages rather than moves.
