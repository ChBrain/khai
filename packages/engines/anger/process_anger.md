# Process: Anger

## Taxonomy

Emotion / the psychology of the approach-response to a wrong.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: anger

## Initiated by

A persona appraises an offense against itself or what it cares about -- a demeaning slight, an injustice, a goal deliberately blocked, someone to blame who could have done otherwise -- and flares toward the source to set it right.
This is anger: what Lazarus called the response to _a demeaning offense against me and mine_, keyed on other-blame and the obstruction of a goal.
Where fear says _get me away_ and disgust says _get it away_, anger says _this is wrong and I will push against it_ -- and it moves toward, not back.

## Direction

Toward the offender and the wrong, to remove the obstacle, answer the transgressor, and restore what was violated.
Anger is an approach emotion, the exception among the negative feelings: it does not withdraw from the aversive but advances on it, mobilized to confront, correct, and set the account right.
The direction is at the source of the offense and at the state of affairs that offended -- to change them by force of will or force itself.

## Lever

The appraisal of blame -- that someone, or something, is responsible for an illegitimate harm and could have acted otherwise.
Anger spends the ease of the relationship and courts the risk of escalation, staking them on forcing a correction; what it mobilizes is arousal and readiness -- the flush, the raised voice, the felt urge to act now against the wrong.
The sharper the sense of injustice and of avoidable fault, the hotter the flare.

## Echo

The wrong is confronted -- and the tie is strained, the conflict escalates, or the account is squared.
Anger is the emotion most bound up with aggression yet distinct from it: it can drive an attack, but as readily an assertion, a protest, a hard conversation, or a repair -- the feeling is not the act.
It runs as [frustration](process_frustration.md), [indignation](process_indignation.md), [grievance](process_grievance.md), or [rage](process_rage.md), and it is tuned by a standing [hostility](position_hostility.md). The appraisal taxonomy of the basic emotions is the emotion engine's; the behavior of aggression is the aggression engine's; the cold downward look at an inferior is contempt's, its sibling in the CAD triad; the recoil from a contaminant is disgust's; the acute fight-or-flight routing of overwhelming demand is stress's. This engine owns the hot approach-emotion at a wrong.
