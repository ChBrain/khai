# Process: Indignation

## Taxonomy

[Anger](process_anger.md), the moral form.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: anger

## Initiated by

A persona witnesses a standard or moral norm violated -- a right breached, a fairness broken, the weak wronged -- often against a third party rather than itself, and flares on behalf of the wronged or the rule.
This is indignation: righteous anger, moral outrage -- the justice emotion, anger in the service of a norm.
In Rozin's CAD triad it is the response to violations of autonomy -- the rights and fairness a person is owed.

## Direction

Toward the transgressor and toward redress -- punishment, censure, the restoring of the violated standard -- and toward an audience, to recruit shared condemnation.
Indignation aims past the personal injury to the principle: it wants the wrong named as wrong and answered, and it reaches for others to make the answer collective.

## Lever

The appraisal of injustice or illegitimacy -- a wrong done, a duty shirked, a fairness broken -- felt as warranting correction whether or not the persona has a personal stake.
What indignation spends is the comfort of neutrality, traded for the standing of the one who is in the right; and it is the anger that scales, moving from the single voice to the moralized crowd.

## Echo

The norm is defended and the violator called to account -- and, overreaching, the self-righteousness and moralized aggression that punish past the wrong and brook no reply.
Indignation is the socially licensed anger, the one a persona will voice openly because it claims the high ground -- which is its force and its danger both.
The reasoned judgment of right and wrong that lights it is the moral-judgment engine's; indignation is the anger that judgment kindles.
