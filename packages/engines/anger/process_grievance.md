# Process: Grievance

## Taxonomy

[Anger](process_anger.md), the stored form.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: anger

## Initiated by

A wrong is not discharged but kept -- nursed, rehearsed, returned to -- so the anger banks rather than burns off, and a standing sense of having been unjustly treated accrues.
This is grievance: the slow, cold anger of the remembered injury, the account left open long after the occasion has passed.
Where the flare answers a wrong now, the grievance holds a wrong still -- carried, tended, and counted.

## Direction

Toward the past wrong held open and the offender held accountable across time -- the account kept, the debt unforgiven, the correction still felt to be owed.
Grievance points backward and forward at once: back to the injury it will not release, forward to the reckoning it still awaits.

## Lever

The rehearsal of the injury -- each return deepens the groove, so the wrong grows more certain, more detailed, and more central to how the persona stands.
What grievance spends is the possibility of letting go, traded for the grip of being demonstrably in the right; the longer it is held, the more it costs to set down.

## Echo

The wrong is not released -- which preserves the claim but corrodes the one who holds it: grievance sours toward bitterness, feeds the return-and-return of rumination, and forecloses the repair or forgiveness that would end it.
It is the anger that outlasts its occasion, and the seed of the enduring feud.
The release of a stored wrong is the forgiveness engine's; the circling rehearsal of it is the rumination engine's; grievance is the holding itself -- the wrong kept warm.
