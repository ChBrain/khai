# Process: Courage

## Taxonomy

Character / the psychology of courage.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: courage

## Initiated by

A persona faces a thing they fear -- a danger, a cost, a dread -- and a good that facing it would serve, and moves toward the fear rather than away from it.
Courage is not the absence of fear but action taken with the fear present: the fearless persona shows no courage, having nothing to overcome, and the persona who feels the fear fully and acts anyway shows all of it.
The occasion is always a fork -- toward the feared thing or away from it -- and courage is the step toward.

## Direction

Toward the feared thing, for the sake of the good beyond it.
Not toward danger for its own sake -- that is recklessness, fear felt too little -- nor away from it -- that is cowardice, fear felt too much -- but through it: the fear rightly sized, and the act taken because the end is judged worth the risk.
Courage is the mean between the two failures, and the mean is not a fixed point but a reading of how much this danger deserves to be feared and whether this good is worth that much.

## Lever

The good that outweighs the fear, and the will to act on it while afraid.
What makes an act courageous is precisely that it costs: fear is the price, and courage is the paying of it for something held to be worth more. Remove the fear and the act is merely easy; remove the worth and it is merely rash.
The lever is that weighing -- fear on one side, the good on the other -- and the moving of the body across it despite the pull to stay.

## Echo

The feared thing is faced, and the persona is changed by having moved through fear rather than under it.
Courage compounds -- each act taken makes the next more possible, the fear a little more known -- and its absence compounds too, each flinch making the next flinch easier.
It runs as [physical](process_physical.md), [conviction](process_conviction.md), or [vital](process_vital.md) courage -- the facing of bodily danger, of social cost, of an inner threat. The felt fear itself is the emotion engine's; the whole state of being under a threat that has not yet fallen is the jeopardy composite's; the long stamina toward a distant goal, distinct from the acute facing of a fear, is the grit engine's.
