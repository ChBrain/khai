# Process: Vital

## Taxonomy

[Courage](process_courage.md), the courage that faces an inner or existential threat -- psychological courage.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: courage

## Initiated by

The threat comes from within, or from one's own condition -- illness, despair, the prospect of one's own dissolution, a truth about oneself one would rather not meet -- and the persona faces it rather than fleeing into denial or distraction.
This is psychological courage: the going-on despite depression, the facing of a diagnosis, the looking-at what one is.
The fear here has no external enemy to charge.

## Direction

Toward the inner fear, for the sake of going on or being whole.
The persona turns toward the thing inside they most want to look away from -- the addiction admitted, the grief entered rather than numbed, the frailty accepted -- because facing it is the only way through, and fleeing it only defers the reckoning at compounding cost.
The direction is inward, and there is nowhere else to aim.

## Lever

The fear that comes from inside, met without an object to fight.
The hardest feature of vital courage is that there is no hill to take and no room to speak in -- only the self and its condition -- so it is endured more than enacted, sustained rather than spent in a moment, and its victories are quiet and often unwitnessed.
The lever is the refusal to look away, held against a pull toward the comfort of denial that no one else would even see the persona yield to.

## Echo

The inner threat is faced, and the persona goes on, or is made more whole, by the facing.
It is the least visible of the three and often the most demanding -- there is no audience, no medal, no single act, only the daily turning-toward of what would be easier to deny.
The mortality it may face is the mortality engine's; the despair it goes on despite is the province of grief and mood; this owns the facing itself -- the turning toward the inner threat rather than away.
