# Process: Physical

## Taxonomy

[Courage](process_courage.md), the courage that faces bodily danger.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: courage

## Initiated by

The threat is to the body -- injury, pain, death -- and the persona moves toward it: the soldier who advances, the firefighter who enters the burning house, the one who steps between a harm and its target.
This is the oldest and most recognized courage, Aristotle's, shown on the battlefield and wherever the flesh is at stake.
The fear here is the most immediate a body knows.

## Direction

Toward the bodily risk, for a good worth the body.
The persona carries their flesh into the place it fears -- the fire, the fight, the fall -- because something on the other side, a life, a duty, a person, is judged worth the risk of injury or death.
The direction is outward and physical: the body moved into danger it is wired to flee.

## Lever

The body's fear, overridden for the end.
Fear of injury is the most primitive and physical of fears -- the flinch is wired deep, below decision -- so to override it is a visible mastery, the reason physical courage is the most celebrated and the most legible: the act is external, sudden, and plainly costly.
The lever is the will bearing down on a reflex, holding the body to a course the body itself resists.

## Echo

The danger is met in the flesh, and the persona is marked by having carried their body through it -- injured or whole, but changed.
It is the most acute and briefest of the courages -- lived in the moment of the act, over when the moment is -- where [conviction](process_conviction.md) courage is slower and lonelier and [vital](process_vital.md) courage is quieter and longer.
Its excess is the recklessness that courts danger for its own sake; its deficiency the cowardice that flees a risk worth taking.
