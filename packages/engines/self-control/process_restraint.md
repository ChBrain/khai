# Process: Restraint

## Taxonomy

[Self-Control](process_self_control.md), the override itself -- holding back the prepotent impulse.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: self-control

## Initiated by

The persona meets an impulse ready to fire, and stops it.
This is restraint, self-control's core act: the effortful inhibition of a prepotent, wanted response -- the retort swallowed, the hand kept from the plate, the purchase not made -- in the moment it presses to be acted.
It is the felt no said to oneself, the impulse held at the edge of action.

## Direction

Against the impulse, at the point of action.
Restraint intervenes late, where the want has become a readiness to act: it does not prevent the desire but blocks its expression, the persona bearing the pull while denying it the deed.
Its direction is the counter-force to the impulse, applied where the two meet.

## Lever

What restraint spends -- the effort of holding, and the relief of letting go.
Blocking a ready impulse is active work, felt as strain, and the strain mounts with the strength of the pull and the length of the holding; restraint spends that effort to keep the standard, buying conduct at the cost of exertion the yielding persona never pays.
A production holds this lever by making the impulse genuinely strong and the holding genuinely effortful -- the visible cost of the swallowed word -- so restraint reads as work, not indifference.

## Echo

The impulse is denied its deed -- the line held, at the price of the effort it took to hold it.
Sustained over many such moments, restraint is the disciplined life; failing in one, it is the [lapse](process_lapse.md).
Where the impulse can be forestalled rather than fought in the moment -- the temptation avoided, the situation left before it presses -- the work shifts from restraint to the strategic [delay](process_delay.md); restraint is the harder case, the impulse met head-on and held.
