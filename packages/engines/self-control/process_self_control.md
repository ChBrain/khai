# Process: Self-Control

## Taxonomy

Self-regulation / the in-the-moment override of impulse.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: self-control

## Initiated by

A persona feels the pull of something it wants -- and holds it back for a standard it holds.
This is self-control: the effortful override of a prepotent impulse or temptation in favor of a goal the persona endorses (Baumeister; Mischel; Tangney).
Metcalfe and Mischel name the conflict a hot system against a cool one -- the impulsive, reflexive want to go, checked by the reflective, strategic knowledge of what one means to do -- and self-control is the cool mastering the hot, in the moment the two collide.

## Direction

Toward the endorsed standard, and against the wanted impulse.
Self-control does not remove the desire; it holds the line against it -- the persona still wants the treat, the outburst, the easier road, and acts otherwise, its conduct steered by the goal it means to keep rather than the pull it feels.
Its direction is the effortful holding of the ought over the want, the impulse felt in full and denied.

## Lever

What self-control spends -- effort, and the ease of just yielding.
Overriding an impulse is costly and unpleasant: it takes deliberate exertion to not do the wanted thing, and that exertion is finite in the moment, which is why control is hardest when the temptation is strong, the standard abstract, or the persona already taxed.
It fires precisely where the persona wants what it should not, or should do what it does not want -- the conflict, not the mere presence of a desire, being what calls control into play.

## Echo

The impulse is mastered or it wins -- and either way the exertion is felt, the line held at a cost or lost with one.
Reliable self-control predicts much -- health, achievement, relationships hold better for it -- yet it is effortful and it fails, and its failures are ordinary, not rare.
It runs as [restraint](process_restraint.md), [delay](process_delay.md), and [lapse](process_lapse.md), tuned by a standing [disciplined](position_disciplined.md) disposition. The cold cognitive inhibition of a prepotent response for accuracy is the executive-function engine's; the valuation and discount that make the temptation pull are the reward engine's; the long-horizon passion and perseverance toward a distant goal is the grit engine's; the if-then plans that pre-empt the conflict are the implementation-intention engine's; the compulsive craving of dependence is the addiction engine's. This engine owns the in-the-moment override of impulse.
