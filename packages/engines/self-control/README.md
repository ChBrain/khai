# Self-control

Self-control as process: the effortful, in-the-moment override of a prepotent impulse or temptation in favor of a standard the persona endorses -- the hot want against the cool ought, resisted, delayed, or lost -- across the restraint, delay, and lapse forms, tuned by a standing disciplined disposition (Baumeister; Mischel; Tangney).
