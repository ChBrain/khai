# Process: Delay

## Taxonomy

[Self-Control](process_self_control.md), delay of gratification -- forgoing the smaller-sooner for the larger-later.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: self-control

## Initiated by

The persona forgoes a reward now to hold out for a better one later.
This is delay of gratification (Mischel's marshmallow paradigm): choosing the larger-later over the smaller-sooner, and then bearing the wait -- the child leaving the one treat untouched for the promise of two.
It is self-control spread over time, the impulse not merely blocked once but held off across the interval until the better reward arrives.

## Direction

Toward the later, larger reward -- across the wait that guards it.
Delay points the persona past the immediate pull to the deferred gain, and its work is in the interval: not the single no, but the sustained not-yet, holding the impulse off while the temptation sits present and the reward stays future.
Its direction is forward, the smaller-sooner refused so the larger-later can be reached.

## Lever

What delay spends -- the felt pull of the sooner reward, and the strategies that blunt it.
The wait is hard because the immediate reward looms larger the closer it is; delay spends the cool-system tactics that shrink it -- looking away, thinking of the treat abstractly rather than deliciously, distracting oneself, distancing the temptation in mind -- so the persona survives the interval the raw wanting would not (Metcalfe & Mischel).
A production holds this lever by staging the wait and its tactics: the persona managing its own attention and construal to keep the sooner reward from swamping the later one.

## Echo

The wait is borne and the larger reward reached -- or the tactics fail and the sooner one is taken.
How a persona delays -- whether it can cool the temptation and hold the interval -- is a signature of its self-control, and predicts much across a life (the marshmallow's long shadow).
The valuation that sets how steeply the sooner reward outweighs the later -- the discount curve itself -- is the reward engine's; delay is the persona's work against that curve, the strategic holding-off that [restraint](process_restraint.md) performs stretched across time, and that a failed interval turns into a [lapse](process_lapse.md).
