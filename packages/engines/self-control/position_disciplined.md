# Position: Disciplined

## Taxonomy

Self-regulation / the trait of self-control -- the standing capacity beneath [Self-Control](process_self_control.md) (Tangney, Baumeister & Boone's trait self-control).

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: self-control

## Has

A standing capacity to override impulse reliably.
Trait self-control, as against the state of any one struggle: at the high end, the disciplined persona who holds the line as a matter of course, whose defaults keep the standard without a fight; at the low end, the impulsive one who yields to each pull, its conduct led by whatever it wants now.
It is one of the more consequential traits there is -- reliably self-controlled personas fare better in health, achievement, and relationships -- and much of its advantage comes not from winning hard fights but from arranging a life with fewer of them.

## Orders

Run from here, into every temptation.
The disciplined persona meets an impulse with the standard already weighted heavier -- it structures its situations to avoid the pull, builds habits that carry the goal without exertion, and treats the occasional head-on fight as the exception; the impulsive persona meets the same impulse with the want on top and the standard a distant second.
It sets the default balance of want against ought, before any single moment of control.

## Loses

At the high end, spontaneity and the ease of following the want; at the low end, the goods that reliable control secures.
The highly disciplined persona forgoes some pleasures of the impulse -- can be rigid, joyless, or over-controlled, holding lines that did not need holding; the impulsive one keeps its spontaneity but pays in the health, work, and ties that consistent control protects.
What each trades is the other's good -- freedom against reliability, across the same standing threshold.

## Drives

On that standing capacity:
rides it -- the disciplined persona keeps the line and, better, keeps arranging a life that needs little fighting; the impulsive keeps yielding -- or works against it, the impulsive building habits and structures that do the controlling for it, the over-controlled learning where to let the impulse have its way.
The threshold holds underneath either way, a resting level the persona builds and manages more than a state that merely comes and goes.
