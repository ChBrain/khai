# @chbrain/khai-engine-self-control

## 0.1.1

### Patch Changes

- 17371cb: Add the self-control engine (multi-type: process, position) -- the effortful, in-the-moment override of a prepotent impulse in favor of an endorsed standard. The gap analysis found this homeless in the seam between `executive-function`'s cold cognitive inhibition and `reward`'s discount curve: neither models the felt motivational struggle between an impulse the persona genuinely wants and a goal it genuinely holds. Metcalfe & Mischel's hot/cool framing names it -- the cool, reflective ought mastering the hot, impulsive want.

  - **Root (process):** `self-control` -- the hot want held back for the cool ought, the impulse felt in full and denied.
  - **Forms (process):** `restraint` (the override itself -- inhibiting the wanted-but-unwise response at the edge of action), `delay` (delay of gratification -- forgoing the smaller-sooner for the larger-later, and the cooling strategies that hold the interval; Mischel's marshmallow), `lapse` (the failure -- the impulse winning, the standard broken, an ordinary and recoverable defeat, not a rare collapse).
  - **Trait (position):** `disciplined` -- a standing capacity to override impulse reliably, working largely through habits and situations that spare the fight (Tangney/Baumeister/Boone; Galla & Duckworth).

  Warranted (LORE) on Mischel and Metcalfe & Mischel (delay of gratification; hot/cool), Baumeister/Vohs/Tice (the strength model -- with the ego-depletion state treated cautiously per the 2016 multi-lab replication), Tangney/Baumeister/Boone (trait self-control), Hofmann (everyday temptations), and Galla & Duckworth (habits mediate the trait). Bounded against `executive-function` (cold inhibition for accuracy vs the hot want-versus-ought), `reward` (the valuation/discount that makes the pull vs the resisting of it), `grit` (long-horizon perseverance vs the in-the-moment override), `implementation-intention` (the plan that forestalls the conflict vs the fight when it comes), and `addiction` (the compulsive craving of dependence vs the ordinary recoverable lapse). Set at patch as the free level. No whitelist required -- the `self_control` root, `restraint`/`delay`/`lapse` form stems, and `disciplined` trait stem are unique (`inhibition` remains executive-function's).
