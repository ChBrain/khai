# Instructions: Rumor

How a Playwright wires the rumor engine into a play. You wire by linking the play's own content to the engine's files; the engine files stay untouched, they are canon. The Roadie plumbs the engine's law into Instructions Knowledge on deploy. Authoring guidance, not runtime content, and it does not go on tour.

## Human

- The human sets the rumor terrain: the important, ambiguous situation no trusted channel explains, the community that must make sense of it, and whether the unverified account is a quiet background of the world or a live actor -- the vacuum that spawns it, the account it builds, the reshaping it undergoes, the intelligence it turns out to carry.

## Agent

- From the play's own content, a persona whose building and passing of unverified news is an actor links under Projection the facet its rumor serves: the vacuum it fills, the improvisation it assembles, the mutation it undergoes, or the hearsay it is dismissed as and runs on. Play the rumor as unverified news about a situation present in what the persona does -- the story pooled from scraps when no one will confirm, the account bent toward the tellers' fears as it passes, the "only a rumor" acted on all the same -- carried, never narrated, and never standing in for evaluative talk about a person or the spread of a feeling. Keep it the unverified account of an ambiguous situation, not gossip about persons (that is gossip), the spread of affect (contagion), the truth-by-repetition bias (bias), collective panic (fear), one persona's confusion (confusion), or a deliberately planted falsehood (a distinct force). You link to the engine, you never edit it.

## Collaboration

- Evaluative talk about absent persons is the gossip engine's; the spread of affect or behavior itself is the contagion engine's; the cognitive inflation of truth by repeated exposure is the bias engine's; the emotion of collective panic is the fear engine's; a persona's own felt ambiguity is the confusion engine's; a deliberately planted falsehood is a distinct force. Rumor is the improvised collective sensemaking of an ambiguous situation -- the sibling of gossip: gossip weighs absent persons, rumor makes sense of uncertain events. Whether the reading rings true to rumor is the team's review.

## Knowledge

- Rumor is unverified information about an ambiguous situation, circulated as a community's improvised collective sensemaking when the demand for news outruns the official supply (Allport and Postman's importance times ambiguity, and their leveling, sharpening, and assimilation; Shibutani's improvised news; DiFonzo and Bordia on rumor as sensemaking and threat-management; Kapferer on rumor as the oldest medium). It wires on a persona at Projection, and it is the unverified account a community builds to make sense of what it cannot yet know. Four facets carry it -- vacuum is the generative gap, where importance meets ambiguity and anxiety and no trusted channel speaks, so the demand for news outruns its supply; improvisation is the collective account-building, many tellers pooling scraps into a plausible story no one authored and all have shaped; mutation is the reshaping in transmission, the leveling and sharpening and assimilation that bend the account toward the tellers' fears as it passes; hearsay is the twist, the dismissed unverified word read not only as misinformation but as the community's fastest and often truest improvised intelligence. Read a persona by the unverified news they build and carry, and read hearsay two ways: the word condemned as mere rumor is the same word the group depends on to act.

## System

- Do give a persona whose building and passing of unverified news is an actor its facet under Projection; do play the rumor as the unverified account of a situation shown in what the persona does, present not narrated; do keep the reading the unverified news about an ambiguous situation -- the vacuum, the improvisation, the mutation, the hearsay -- not talk about a person and not a feeling.
- Do open the vacuum first: rumor rushes a gap where a situation is important and ambiguous and no trusted account arrives, so let the silence and the stakes create the pressure, and supply a credible account to drain it.
- Do read hearsay as the twist: the dismissed unverified word is not only misinformation but the group's improvised intelligence -- fast, often true, and load-bearing where nothing is yet confirmed, disowned in principle and depended on in fact.
- Do not model evaluative talk about persons (gossip), the spread of affect or behavior (contagion), the truth-by-repetition bias (bias), collective panic (fear), one persona's confusion (confusion), or a deliberately planted falsehood (a distinct force). Do not make rumor stand in for gossip about a person or the feeling it may spread. Do not edit the engine's files; wire only from the play's side.
