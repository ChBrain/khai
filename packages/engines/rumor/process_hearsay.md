# Process: Hearsay

## Taxonomy

[Rumor](process_rumor.md), the facet where the dismissed unverified word is read as real intelligence.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: rumor

## Initiated by

The account is waved away as mere hearsay -- unconfirmed, unreliable, not to be trusted -- and it is, at the same time, the fastest and often the truest word the community has.
Hearsay starts wherever a rumor is dismissed for being unverified and relied upon anyway -- the story everyone denies believing and everyone acts on, the "only a rumor" that reaches the room before the confirmation, the unofficial word that turns out to have been right.
It is rumor read the other way: not the misinformation it is condemned as but the improvised intelligence a community runs on when nothing confirmed is yet available.

## Direction

Toward an unverified account that is dismissed in principle and depended on in practice.
Hearsay works by the double standing of the unconfirmed: because it cannot be verified, the account is disowned, called mere rumor, held at arm's length as unreliable -- and because it is all there is, and often quicker and truer than the official word, it is quietly trusted, passed on, and acted upon.
The direction is the split between what is professed and what is used: the persona denies believing the rumor and orders their conduct by it, so the dismissed word governs the very actions its dismissal disclaims.

## Lever

The value of the fastest available account against the unreliability that condemns it.
The carrying force is that rumor is often right and always early -- the improvised intelligence that outruns verification, the people's word that reaches the truth before the authorities admit it, the account an anxious community must have to act at all; the resistance, and the reason it is condemned, is that the same word is unconfirmed and frequently wrong, so relying on it is both the community's rational recourse and its standing danger.
Read one way hearsay is misinformation, the unverified falsehood that panics and misleads; read the other it is improvised intelligence, the fast, often-true, load-bearing news a group builds when it is denied the confirmed -- and rumor is most itself exactly where the two cannot be told apart in advance.

## Echo

The community running on the very word it denies trusting.
It shows a persona who calls the account mere rumor and acts on it all the same -- and carries beneath it the rumor family's twist, the sibling of the ambivalence: that the dismissed unverified word is not only misinformation but the group's improvised intelligence, the fastest and often truest news it has where nothing is yet confirmed, disowned in principle and depended on in fact.
Hearsay is dated from the dismissal that changed nothing, the moment the account was called mere rumor and believed anyway.
