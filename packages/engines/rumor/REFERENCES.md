# Rumor: Reference

## Line of Work

Rumor as **process**: unverified information about an ambiguous situation, circulated
through a community as its improvised collective sensemaking when the demand for news
outruns the official supply. It wires on a persona at Projection, how a persona builds,
carries, and passes the unverified account, because rumor is legible in what its carriers
do -- the story pooled from scraps when no one will confirm, the account bent toward the
tellers' fears as it passes, the "only a rumor" acted on all the same. The domain does not
model evaluative talk about absent persons, which is gossip's; nor the spread of affect or
behavior itself, which is contagion's; nor the cognitive inflation of truth by repeated
exposure, which is bias's; nor the emotion of collective panic, which is fear's; nor a
persona's own felt state of ambiguity, which is confusion's; nor a deliberately planted
falsehood, which is a distinct force. It models the unverified account a community
improvises to make sense of an ambiguous situation.

The spine is the social psychology and sociology of rumor -- the recovery of rumor from
mere false report to collective sensemaking under uncertainty. Allport and Postman gave
rumor its first law: its force rises with the importance of the subject times its
ambiguity, and its content is reshaped in transmission by three processes -- leveling, as
detail falls away; sharpening, as a few points are heightened; and assimilation, as the
rest is bent to fit the tellers' habits and prejudices. Shibutani reframed rumor as
improvised news: not pathology but a community's collective problem-solving, arising
wherever the demand for news exceeds the supply from official channels, a group reasoning
together toward the account it was denied. DiFonzo and Bordia synthesized the empirical
study of rumor as sensemaking and threat-management under uncertainty, and drew the line
between rumor and gossip -- rumor about situations, gossip about persons. And Kapferer read
rumor as the oldest medium, the uncontrollable word of the people that runs beside and
often ahead of the official one. So the engine gives rumor three moments of its life and a
twist -- vacuum, improvisation, mutation -- and keeps a fourth, hearsay, the dismissed
unverified word read not only as misinformation but as the improvised intelligence a
community runs on.

## Restrictions

What the engine refuses to model, and to whom it delegates.

- **Evaluative talk about absent persons (gossip)**: Talk that weighs an absent third party --
  their conduct, their trustworthiness, their standing -- is the gossip engine's. Rumor is
  about situations and events, not the evaluation of persons; the two often travel together,
  but gossip judges people and rumor makes sense of happenings, and this engine models the
  latter.
- **The spread of affect or behavior itself (contagion)**: The transmission of a feeling or an
  action through a crowd -- the panic that leaps person to person, the mood that catches -- is
  the contagion engine's. Rumor is the spread of an unverified account, an informational
  content; the affect that rides along with it, catching as it goes, is contagion's, a distinct
  mechanism the rumor may carry but is not.
- **The truth-by-repetition bias (bias)**: The cognitive inflation by which a claim feels truer
  the more often it is encountered is the bias engine's. Rumor is the social circulation of the
  account; the illusory-truth effect that repeated exposure works inside a single mind is bias's,
  a cognitive mechanism distinct from the collective process of rumoring.
- **The emotion of collective panic (fear)**: The felt terror of a frightened crowd is the fear
  engine's. Rumor is the account that may trigger or ride such fear; the emotion itself, and its
  bodily grip, is fear's, not the unverified news that occasions it.
- **A persona's own felt ambiguity (confusion)**: The internal, felt state of not-knowing in a
  single persona is the confusion engine's. Rumor is the collective response to shared ambiguity
  -- the community's improvised account -- not one persona's private disorientation, though the
  vacuum it fills is felt as such by each.
- **A deliberately planted falsehood (a distinct force)**: Disinformation manufactured and
  seeded on purpose to deceive is a distinct phenomenon from rumor's spontaneous, emergent
  collective sensemaking. Rumor is improvised by a community trying to understand; the engineered
  lie is authored to mislead, and this engine does not model it.
- **The facet itself is not engine content**: The engine declares the four facets exist; which
  one a given persona shows lives on that persona, linked under its Projection chapter. The
  engine refuses to fix a persona's rumor for them.

## Encoding

Source to constraint, per file.

- **[rumor](process_rumor.md)** (the root): unverified news about an ambiguous situation,
  circulated as a community's improvised collective sensemaking. Anchored by Allport and Postman
  (importance times ambiguity) and Shibutani (improvised news).
- **[vacuum](process_vacuum.md)**: the generative gap -- where importance meets ambiguity and
  anxiety and no trusted channel speaks, the demand for news outrunning its supply. Anchored by
  Allport and Postman (the basic law) and Shibutani (demand exceeding supply).
- **[improvisation](process_improvisation.md)**: the collective account-building -- many tellers
  pooling scraps into a plausible story no one authored and all have shaped, the group
  problem-solving aloud. Anchored by Shibutani (improvised news as collective problem-solving).
- **[mutation](process_mutation.md)**: the reshaping in transmission -- leveling, sharpening,
  and assimilation bending the account toward the tellers' fears and frames as it passes.
  Anchored by Allport and Postman (the transmission distortions).
- **[hearsay](process_hearsay.md)**: the dismissed unverified word read not only as
  misinformation but as the community's fastest and often truest improvised intelligence. The
  engine's twist; anchored by DiFonzo and Bordia (rumor as sensemaking) and Kapferer (rumor as
  the oldest medium).
