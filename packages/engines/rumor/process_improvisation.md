# Process: Improvisation

## Taxonomy

[Rumor](process_rumor.md), the facet of the collective account-building.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: rumor

## Initiated by

Lacking an official account, people build one together out of whatever they have.
Improvisation starts wherever a community pools its fragments to make sense of the unexplained -- the half-heard fact, the plausible guess, the scrap from a cousin, the pattern someone thinks they see -- assembled aloud, in exchange, into a story that will do.
It is rumor's constructive work, Shibutani's improvised news: not one person's lie but a group's collaborative problem-solving, a shared account manufactured on the spot from the materials at hand.

## Direction

Toward a plausible shared story, assembled collectively from scraps too thin to stand alone.
Improvisation works by pooling and debate: one persona offers a fragment, another adds or doubts or amends, a third fits it to what they heard elsewhere, and between them a coherent-enough account takes shape that no single teller authored and all have shaped -- the group thinking out loud toward an explanation.
The direction is collective construction: the persona does not merely receive the rumor but helps make it, contributing, correcting, and endorsing, so the account that circulates is the community's joint product, built to be plausible enough to relieve the not-knowing.

## Lever

The pooling of scraps against the thinness of any one of them.
Improvisation runs on collective assembly: no single fragment could explain the situation, but pooled, debated, and fitted together they compose an account the group can use -- the resistance is the thinness of the materials, so the story leans on plausibility rather than evidence, filling its gaps with what would make sense given what the tellers already believe.
Pool the fragments and a usable account emerges from pieces none of which would convince alone; withhold them, or supply a verified whole, and the improvising stops for lack of both need and material.

## Echo

The community holding a story it built together from what little it had.
Improvisation leaves the group with a jointly-constructed account -- assembled, debated, and endorsed by many hands -- and hands that account to the transmission that will carry and reshape it.
It is the heart of Shibutani's reading: rumor as a form of collective problem-solving, a community pooling its intelligence to improvise the news it was denied, reasoning together toward the best account it can build.
Improvisation is dated from the assembly, the moment scattered fragments were pooled into a story the group could hold.
