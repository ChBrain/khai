# Process: Rumor

## Taxonomy

The social psychology and sociology of rumor / unverified news as collective sensemaking.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: rumor

## Initiated by

Something important and unclear happens, the official word is silent or distrusted, and people begin to tell each other what they think is going on.
This is rumor: unverified information about an ambiguous situation, passed from person to person as a community's improvised attempt to make sense of what it cannot yet know.
The force is collective and epistemic: not evaluative talk about a person, not the spread of a feeling, but a group building and circulating an account of an event when the demand for news outruns its supply.

## Direction

Toward a shared, plausible account of an uncertain situation, assembled and passed until the group can act on it.
Rumor fills a vacuum: where importance meets ambiguity and anxiety, and no trusted channel explains, a community pools its scraps and guesses into a story, reshapes that story as it travels, and circulates it as the best account available -- Allport and Postman's importance times ambiguity, Shibutani's improvised news.
The direction is sensemaking under uncertainty: the persona, needing to understand and unable to confirm, takes up the circulating account, adds to it, and passes it on, so the group arrives at a workable picture ahead of, or instead of, the verified one.

## Lever

The community's need to understand against its inability to confirm.
Rumor runs on the gap between demand and supply of news: because people must make sense of an important, ambiguous, threatening situation and cannot get an authoritative account, they improvise one collectively and trust it enough to act -- the resistance is that the same account is unverified and mutable, bent toward the tellers' fears as it passes, so what circulates is at once the group's real intelligence and its unconfirmed guess.
Open the vacuum -- importance, ambiguity, no trusted word -- and rumor rushes to fill it; supply a credible account and the pressure that drove it drains away.

## Echo

The community holds a shared, unverified account it built to make sense of what it could not yet know.
Rumor is why a story can sweep a frightened crowd faster than any confirmation, why the account bends toward what its carriers feared, why "just a rumor" is both a dismissal and a description of the fastest news a group has -- the improvised collective sensemaking that runs wherever the important and the unknown meet and no trusted channel speaks.
It runs as [vacuum](process_vacuum.md), [improvisation](process_improvisation.md), [mutation](process_mutation.md), or [hearsay](process_hearsay.md) -- the gap that spawns it, the account collectively built, the reshaping it undergoes in transmission, and the dismissed word that carries the group's real intelligence. Evaluative talk about absent persons is the gossip engine's; the spread of affect or behavior itself is the contagion engine's; the cognitive inflation of truth by repeated exposure is the bias engine's; the emotion of collective panic is the fear engine's; a persona's own felt ambiguity is the confusion engine's; a deliberately planted falsehood is a distinct force. This engine owns the unverified account a community improvises to make sense of an ambiguous situation.
