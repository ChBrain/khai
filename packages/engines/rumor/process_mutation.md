# Process: Mutation

## Taxonomy

[Rumor](process_rumor.md), the facet of the reshaping in transmission.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: rumor

## Initiated by

The story changes as it travels, and what arrives is not what set out.
Mutation starts wherever a rumor passes from teller to teller and is reshaped in the passing -- details dropped, a few points sharpened, the rest bent to fit what each carrier already fears or expects -- so the account drifts with every retelling toward the frame of those who carry it.
It is rumor's transmission distortion, Allport and Postman's leveling, sharpening, and assimilation: the systematic reshaping by which a story is worn smooth, pointed, and colored as it moves.

## Direction

Toward an account bent, at each retelling, toward the tellers' expectations and fears.
Mutation works in three motions: leveling, as most details fall away and the story shortens to a graspable few; sharpening, as those few are heightened, made vivid and central; and assimilation, as the remainder is reshaped to fit the tellers' habits, interests, and prejudices -- the ambiguous figure made to match the feared one, the detail altered to fit the expected plot.
The direction is convergence on the carriers' frame: the rumor becomes more tellable and more expected with each pass, so the version that arrives says less what happened than what its transmitters were prepared to believe.

## Lever

The tellability of the reshaped story against the detail that made it true.
Mutation runs on the pressures of retelling: a story that is shorter, sharper, and expected is easier to carry and more readily believed, so transmission selects for tellability over accuracy -- the resistance is the truth of the original detail, worn away in favor of the version that fits, so the rumor grows more compelling and less faithful the further it travels.
Let it pass hand to hand and it converges on the tellers' fears, vivid and plausible and increasingly wrong in the particulars; check it against the source and the accumulated reshaping stands exposed.

## Echo

The account arriving bent toward the fears of those who carried it.
Mutation leaves the rumor reshaped -- leveled, sharpened, assimilated -- so that its final form reveals as much about its carriers as about the event, and hands that bent account onward to be dismissed or believed.
It is Allport and Postman's core finding: rumor does not merely spread but transforms, and the direction of its drift is toward the stereotype and the fear already in the tellers, the story becoming the shape of the minds it passed through.
Mutation is dated from the retelling, the moment the account changed in a teller's mouth and traveled on altered.
