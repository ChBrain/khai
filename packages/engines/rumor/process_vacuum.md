# Process: Vacuum

## Taxonomy

[Rumor](process_rumor.md), the facet of the generative information gap.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: rumor

## Initiated by

Something matters, no one can say what it means, and the silence itself becomes unbearable.
Vacuum starts wherever a situation is at once important and ambiguous and anxious, and the channels that should explain it are silent, slow, or distrusted -- the layoffs no one will confirm, the sirens with no announcement, the crisis the officials will not name, the gap where the trusted account should be.
It is rumor's generative condition: the space that opens when the demand for news outruns its supply, the pressure of an unanswered important question that a community cannot leave alone.

## Direction

Toward a gap that must be filled, and will be, by whatever account can be improvised.
Vacuum works by intolerable uncertainty: the more a situation matters and the less it is understood, the harder the pressure to explain it, so that where no authoritative word arrives, the space does not stay empty -- it draws in guesses, fragments, and fears until some account, any plausible account, fills it.
The direction is the conversion of anxiety into a demand for story: the persona, unable to bear not knowing about something that matters to them, reaches for whatever explanation is circulating, and their reaching is the suction that pulls the rumor in.

## Lever

The importance of the question against the absence of a trusted answer.
Vacuum runs on Allport and Postman's law -- rumor's force rises with importance times ambiguity -- so the gap is widest exactly where the stakes are high and the facts are unclear and the official voice is missing or mistrusted; the resistance, the thing that closes the vacuum, is a credible account: supply trusted news and the pressure drains, leave the silence and the pressure builds until improvisation fills it.
Open the gap -- high stakes, no clear facts, no trusted word -- and rumor becomes not likely but nearly inevitable; close it with a believed account and the engine that fed on the silence stalls.

## Echo

The community facing an important question with no trusted answer, and about to make one.
Vacuum leaves the group under the pressure of an unexplained situation that matters -- the demand for news exceeding its supply -- and hands that pressure to the improvisation that will relieve it.
It is the condition Shibutani found beneath all rumor: not idle fancy but a community deprived of the news it needs, driven to produce its own; rumor is thickest where trusted information is thinnest.
Vacuum is dated from the silence, the moment an important question met no trusted answer and the space for rumor opened.
