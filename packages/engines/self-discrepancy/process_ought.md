# Process: Ought

## Taxonomy

[Self-Discrepancy](process_self_discrepancy.md), the gap from the ought self -- failing to be who one is bound to be.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: self-discrepancy

## Initiated by

The persona falls short of the self it feels bound to be.
This is the actual:ought discrepancy (Higgins): the gap between the actual self and the ought self -- the persona's duties, obligations, and responsibilities, who it believes it must be.
The ought self is the standard of what the persona is required to be, and to fall short of it is not to miss a hoped-for good but to breach a duty, to be exposed to a sanction, a wrong done or a responsibility failed.

## Direction

Toward the present negative -- the duty breached, the threat incurred.
The ought discrepancy reads the gap as a presence, not an absence: the obligation unmet, the standard violated, the censure or punishment such a failure invites; it measures the actual self against the required one and finds a transgression.
Its direction is the shortfall as a breach -- a negative outcome present, a threat the persona has brought on itself.

## Lever

What the ought discrepancy spends -- the persona's exposure to its own obligations.
Because the ought is what the persona must be, missing it is the presence of a negative outcome -- a threat of sanction, real or felt -- and that presence reads as agitation: anxiety, guilt, unease, self-reproach, a restless, on-edge sense of having failed a duty.
A production holds this lever by making the ought binding and the shortfall felt as a breach -- the persona should have been this and was not -- so the gap lands as the tense, apprehensive affect of a duty failed, not as sadness.

## Echo

The persona feels its ought unmet -- and the feeling is agitation, the emotional signature of a negative outcome present.
Where the actual self falls short of the ought, the persona is anxious, guilty, on edge, braced against the censure its failure invites -- a restless, threatened affect distinct from the deflated affect of the [ideal](process_ideal.md) gap.
The agitation emotions themselves -- the anxiety, the guilt, the self-reproach -- are the anxiety and guilt engines'; this form owns the specific discrepancy that produces them, the gap between the actual self and the ought, read as a duty breached and a threat incurred.
