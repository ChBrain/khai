# Process: Ideal

## Taxonomy

[Self-Discrepancy](process_self_discrepancy.md), the gap from the ideal self -- failing to be who one hoped to be.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: self-discrepancy

## Initiated by

The persona falls short of the self it hoped to be.
This is the actual:ideal discrepancy (Higgins): the gap between the actual self and the ideal self -- the persona's hopes, wishes, and aspirations for who it wants to become.
The ideal self is the standard of what the persona positively wants to be, and to fall short of it is to lack a hoped-for good, an accomplishment or quality it aspired to and has not reached.

## Direction

Toward the missing positive -- what the persona hoped to be and is not.
The ideal discrepancy reads the gap as an absence: the successes not achieved, the qualities not attained, the self not yet grown into; it measures the actual self against the wished-for one and finds the wished-for one missing.
Its direction is the shortfall as a lack -- the good hoped for and not present.

## Lever

What the ideal discrepancy spends -- the persona's stake in its own aspirations.
Because the ideal is what the persona positively wants, missing it is the absence of a positive outcome, and that absence reads as dejection: sadness, disappointment, discouragement, dissatisfaction, a deflated sense of falling short of one's hopes.
A production holds this lever by making the ideal real and the shortfall felt as a lack -- the persona wanted to be this and is not -- so the gap lands as the low, deflated affect of a hope unmet, not as fear.

## Echo

The persona feels its ideal unmet -- and the feeling is dejection, the emotional signature of a positive outcome absent.
Where the actual self falls short of the ideal, the persona is discouraged, disappointed in itself, sad at the distance between who it is and who it hoped to be -- a downcast, deflated affect distinct from the on-edge affect of the [ought](process_ought.md) gap.
The dejection emotions themselves -- the sadness, the disappointment, the discouragement -- are the sadness engine's; this form owns the specific discrepancy that produces them, the gap between the actual self and the ideal, read as a hoped-for good gone missing.
