# Instructions: Self-Discrepancy

How a Playwright wires the self-discrepancy engine into a play. You wire by linking the play's own content to the engine's files; the engine files stay untouched, they are canon. The Roadie plumbs the engine's law into Instructions Knowledge on deploy. Authoring guidance, not runtime content, and it does not go on tour.

## Human

- The human sets the self-discrepancy terrain: what self-guides the persona holds -- the ideals it hopes to reach, the oughts it feels bound to meet -- and where its actual self falls short of them, so a moment can run on a genuine gap between the self one is and the self one is measured against.

## Agent

- From the play's own content, a persona links the self-discrepancy a moment runs under its Projection: which guide its actual self is measured against, how far it falls short, and the affect the gap produces -- dejection where it fails its ideal, agitation where it fails its ought. Let the emotional colour follow the guide: the deflated, discouraged fall from an ideal, the tense, guilty fall from an ought. You link to the engine, you never edit it.

## Collaboration

- The eager-versus-vigilant manner in which a goal is pursued is the regulatory-focus engine's; the dejection emotions themselves -- sadness, disappointment -- are the sadness engine's; the agitation emotions -- anxiety, guilt -- are the anxiety and guilt engines'; the global sense of self-worth is the self-esteem engine's; the present self-story is the identity engine's; the future selves one could become are the possible-selves engine's. This engine supplies the gap between the actual self and its guides -- the ideal discrepancy and the ought discrepancy -- and the discrepant profile beneath them.

## Knowledge

- Self-discrepancy is the comparison of a persona's actual self against a self-guide, and the specific emotion a gap between them produces (Higgins). A persona holds guides for who it should be -- the ideal self (its hopes, wishes, aspirations) and the ought self (its duties, obligations, responsibilities) -- and measures its actual self against them. The ideal discrepancy is the gap from the ideal self: a hoped-for good absent, which brings dejection -- sadness, disappointment, discouragement. The ought discrepancy is the gap from the ought self: a duty breached and a threat incurred, which brings agitation -- anxiety, guilt, unease. Which guide the actual self falls short of sets the emotional signature of the shortfall. The discrepant profile is the standing version -- how large and accessible a persona's discrepancies chronically are, and whether ideal or ought dominates. Read a persona by which guide it is measured against, how far it falls short, and whether the shortfall reads as dejection or as agitation.

## System

- Do keep self-discrepancy the self-guide gap, not the pursuit style: it is the felt distance between the actual self and an ideal or ought guide and the emotion that distance carries -- let the eager-versus-vigilant manner of pursuing a goal be the regulatory-focus engine's, even though both use Higgins's ideal/ought vocabulary.
- Do hold the two emotional signatures apart: an ideal shortfall reads as dejection (deflated, discouraged, sad -- a positive outcome absent), an ought shortfall as agitation (tense, anxious, guilty -- a negative outcome present). Let the guide the persona fails set which colour falls, and do not blur the two into a generic bad feeling.
- Do make the guides real and the shortfall specific: a hoped-for self the persona is not, or a duty it should have met and did not -- so the gap is a genuine falling-short of a standard the persona holds, not free-floating low mood. Delegate the dejection emotions to the sadness engine and the agitation emotions to the anxiety and guilt engines.
- Do let the discrepant profile set a default: the chronically ideal-discrepant persona tilting toward dejection, the ought-discrepant toward agitation, the congruent at ease -- and let a persona work on the gap by lowering an impossible guide, raising the actual self, or shifting which guide governs. Do not conflate the guide-relative gap with the global self-worth of the self-esteem engine. Do not edit the engine's files; wire only from the play's side.
