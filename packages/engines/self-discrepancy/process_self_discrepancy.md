# Process: Self-Discrepancy

## Taxonomy

Self / the comparison of the actual self against a self-guide, and the affect of the gap.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: self-discrepancy

## Initiated by

A persona measures who it actually is against who it holds it should be.
This is self-discrepancy (Higgins): the actual self compared to a self-guide, and the specific emotion a gap between them produces.
A persona carries guides -- an ideal self of its hopes and aspirations, an ought self of its duties and obligations -- and its actual self either matches a guide or falls short of it, and the shortfall is never neutral: it carries a distinct affective signature set by which guide it fails.

## Direction

Toward the gap between the self one is and the self one is measured against.
Self-discrepancy points the persona's own state at its standard and reads the distance: how far the actual self falls short of the ideal it hopes to reach, or of the ought it feels bound to meet, and what that distance costs in feeling.
Its direction is the comparison itself -- the actual self held up to a guide, and the emotion the gap releases.

## Lever

What self-discrepancy spends -- the persona's exposure to its own standards.
To hold a self-guide is to be answerable to it, so a persona that measures itself against an ideal or an ought can fall short of it and feel the fall; the discrepancy spends the safety of a self accountable to no standard, making shortfall against one's own guides a live source of dejection or agitation.
A production holds this lever by giving the persona real guides it can miss -- a hoped-for self it is not, an obligation it is failing -- so the gap and its specific affect are present, not assumed away.

## Echo

The persona feels the gap between its actual self and its guide -- and which guide sets the colour of the feeling.
A shortfall against the ideal self reads as the absence of what was hoped and brings dejection; a shortfall against the ought self reads as the presence of a threat and brings agitation; the two are distinct emotional consequences of two distinct gaps.
It runs as the [ideal](process_ideal.md) discrepancy and the [ought](process_ought.md) discrepancy, tuned by a standing [discrepant](position_discrepant.md) profile. The eager-versus-vigilant manner in which a persona pursues a goal is the regulatory-focus engine's; the dejection emotions themselves -- sadness, disappointment -- are the sadness engine's; the agitation emotions -- anxiety, guilt -- are the anxiety and guilt engines'; the global sense of self-worth is the self-esteem engine's; the present self-story is identity's; the future selves one could become are the possible-selves engine's. This engine owns the gap between the actual self and its guides, and the affect it carries.
