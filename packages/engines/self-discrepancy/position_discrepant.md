# Position: Discrepant

## Taxonomy

Self / the standing profile of self-discrepancy -- the chronic gap and its dominant guide beneath [Self-Discrepancy](process_self_discrepancy.md) (Higgins's chronic accessibility).

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: self-discrepancy

## Has

A standing level of self-discrepancy and a dominant guide.
Chronic self-discrepancy, as against the gap felt in any one moment: how large and how accessible a persona's discrepancies are as a settled matter, and whether the ideal or the ought guide dominates its self-evaluation -- the persona forever short of its ideals and prone to dejection, or forever short of its oughts and prone to agitation, or largely congruent with its guides and at rest (Higgins's chronic accessibility).
It is the persona's resting relation to its own standards -- the gap it carries and the guide that most often measures it.

## Orders

Run from here, into every self-appraisal.
The chronically ideal-discrepant persona meets its days already short of its hopes, so its default affect tilts toward the dejected -- discouraged, dissatisfied, flat; the chronically ought-discrepant persona meets its days already short of its duties, so its default tilts toward the agitated -- anxious, guilt-prone, on edge; the largely congruent persona meets them with its actual self near its guides, and neither shadow falls by default.
It sets which self-guide is chronically accessible and how wide the standing gap, before any single shortfall.

## Loses

At the high end, ease with the self; at the low end, the pull of a standard.
The highly discrepant persona pays in a chronic vulnerability -- a resting dejection or agitation that colours its life, a self forever measured and found wanting; the largely congruent persona keeps its ease but may hold its guides loosely, less driven by an ideal to reach or an ought to meet.
What each trades is the other's good -- the goad of a demanding standard against the peace of a self at rest with itself, across the same standing gap.

## Drives

On that standing profile:
rides it -- the ideal-discrepant defaulting to dejection, the ought-discrepant to agitation, the congruent to ease -- or works against it, the persona lowering an impossible guide toward its actual self, raising its actual self toward the guide, or shifting which guide governs, so the chronic gap narrows or its dominant colour changes.
The profile holds underneath either way, a resting relation to one's own standards that a persona builds and manages more than a gap that merely comes and goes. It expresses in the [ideal](process_ideal.md) and [ought](process_ought.md) discrepancies a moment runs.
