# Process: Coping

## Taxonomy

Stress and adaptation / the effortful, appraisal-driven handling of a stressor.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: coping

## Initiated by

A persona meets a demand that taxes or exceeds its resources, and sets about handling it.
This is coping (Lazarus & Folkman): the effortful management of a stressor, launched and steered by how the persona appraises it -- what is at stake, and what can be done.
It is transactional, a back-and-forth between the persona and the situation: the demand is sized up, a response is mounted, the situation shifts, the appraisal updates, and the coping runs on -- deliberate effort, not the body's reflex.

## Direction

Toward the stressor, by way of the appraisal of it.
Coping points the persona's effort at a demand it has judged threatening, harmful, or challenging: acting to change the stressor where it appraises it as changeable, turning away from it where it does not, all routed by the reading of what is at stake and what lies within its power.
Its direction is the handling of the demand -- the effortful response the appraisal calls for, revised as the encounter turns.

## Lever

What coping spends -- the persona's effort and its limited resources against the demand.
Coping is work: it draws on attention, energy, time, and support, and spends them on the stressor by whichever route the appraisal favors -- so a persona copes only as far as its resources reach, and a demand that outruns them is where coping strains and stress bites.
A production holds this lever by letting the persona meet a real demand with a real, costly effort -- sizing it up, acting or escaping, drawing on what it has -- so the coping reads as effortful management, not as an untroubled reflex or a feeling that merely passes.

## Echo

The persona has handled the stressor -- well or badly, and the demand is met, deferred, or left standing.
How it copes bears on what the stressor costs it: problem-focused effort can remove the demand where it is changeable, escape can relieve the moment while leaving the demand to return, and the fit between the coping and the appraisal is much of what makes coping adaptive or not.
It runs as [appraisal](process_appraisal.md), the sizing-up that launches it; [problem](process_problem.md), the acting on the stressor; and [escape](process_escape.md), the turning away from it. The involuntary autonomic routing a threat triggers is the stress engine's; the management of the emotional response along its timeline -- emotion-focused coping -- is the regulation engine's; the drawing of meaning from values and beliefs under enduring stress is the meaning engine's; the long perseverance toward a distant goal is the grit engine's. This engine owns the effortful, appraisal-driven repertoire deployed against a stressor.
