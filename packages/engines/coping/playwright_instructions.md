# Instructions: Coping

How a Playwright wires the coping engine into a play. You wire by linking the play's own content to the engine's files; the engine files stay untouched, they are canon. The Roadie plumbs the engine's law into Instructions Knowledge on deploy. Authoring guidance, not runtime content, and it does not go on tour.

## Human

- The human sets the coping terrain: what stressor taxes the persona, how controllable it is, and what resources the persona has -- so a moment can run on a genuine, effortful handling of a demand rather than a reflex.

## Agent

- From the play's own content, a persona links the coping a moment runs under its Projection: appraisal, sizing up what is at stake and what it can do; problem-focused, acting on the stressor to change or remove it; or escape, turning away from the demand. Let the route follow the appraisal -- problem-focus where the stressor is judged changeable, escape where it is judged fixed or too costly to face -- and let the effort cost the persona something. You link to the engine, you never edit it.

## Collaboration

- The involuntary autonomic routing a threat triggers is the stress engine's; the management of the emotional response along its timeline -- emotion-focused coping -- is the regulation engine's; the drawing of meaning from values and beliefs under enduring stress is the meaning engine's; the long perseverance toward a distant goal is the grit engine's. This engine supplies the effortful, appraisal-driven handling of the stressor -- the appraisal, the problem-focus, and the escape.

## Knowledge

- Coping is the effortful, appraisal-driven management of a stressor -- a demand appraised as taxing or exceeding the persona's resources (Lazarus & Folkman). It is transactional: the persona sizes up the stressor, mounts a response, the situation shifts, and the appraisal updates. It runs in forms. Appraisal is the sizing-up -- primary, of what is at stake (harm, threat, or challenge), and secondary, of what can be done -- that launches and steers the response. Problem-focused is acting on the stressor -- planning, active steps, seeking instrumental help, altering or removing the source -- deployed most where the stressor is appraised as changeable. Escape is avoidant coping -- distraction, denial, disengagement -- a turning-away that relieves now and, sustained, leaves the demand to grow. Read a persona by how it appraises the stressor and whether it acts on it or turns away.

## System

- Do keep coping the effortful, chosen handling of a stressor, not the body's reflex: let the involuntary autonomic mobilization be the stress engine's, and coping the deliberate response the persona mounts on top of it.
- Do let the appraisal route the coping: a stressor judged changeable pulling problem-focused effort, one judged fixed or too costly pulling escape -- and let the appraisal update as the encounter turns, so the route can change with it.
- Do match adaptiveness to controllability: problem-focus paying off on a controllable stressor and exhausting the persona against an uncontrollable one; escape relieving now and, sustained against a controllable demand, leaving it to compound -- so neither route is simply good or bad.
- Do hand emotion-focused coping to the regulation engine: the soothing, reframing, expressing, or suppressing of the feeling is regulation's, not coping's, and coping's cognitive appraisal of the stressor is distinct from regulation's reappraisal of the emotion. Delegate meaning-making under chronic stress to the meaning engine. Do not edit the engine's files; wire only from the play's side.
