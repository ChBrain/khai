# Process: Escape

## Taxonomy

[Coping](process_coping.md), turning away from the stressor.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: coping

## Initiated by

The persona turns away from the stressor rather than toward it.
This is avoidant coping (Carver's denial, distancing, and disengagement): distraction, denial, wishful thinking, and behavioral or mental withdrawal from the demand -- a turning-away that puts the stressor out of view.
It opens most readily where the persona appraises the stressor as beyond its power to change, too costly to face head-on, or where acting on it has already failed.

## Direction

Away from the stressor, out of contact with it.
Avoidant coping moves the persona's attention and effort off the demand: not-looking, not-thinking, denying it is happening, busying itself elsewhere, or giving up the effort -- so that contact with the stressor is broken and its pressure, for now, is not felt.
Its direction is the retreat -- away from the demand rather than at it, the stressor left where it is while the persona steps back.

## Lever

What avoidant coping spends -- the future cost of a demand left unmet, traded for relief now.
It buys a real short-run relief: the stressor put out of view stops pressing for a while, which is why avoidance is reached for and why it can help when a demand is genuinely uncontrollable or a respite is needed. But sustained against a demand that will not go away on its own, it spends tomorrow for today -- the unmet stressor compounding while attention is turned from it.
A production holds this lever by letting the persona turn away -- distract, deny, disengage -- and get real relief for it, and by letting that relief come at the price of a demand that returns larger, so avoidance reads as a genuine but costly route, not mere weakness.

## Echo

The persona is, for now, out of contact with the stressor -- relieved, and the demand left standing.
Avoidant coping can serve where the stressor is truly uncontrollable or where a pause restores the resources to face it; sustained against a controllable demand, it tends to leave the problem to grow and the persona worse-placed for the delay.
Which route the persona takes is set by the [appraisal](process_appraisal.md); the alternative of acting on the stressor is [problem](process_problem.md)-focused. The behavioral withdrawal of loneliness and the expressive suppression of a feeling that the regulation engine owns are their own; this form owns the avoidant handling of a stressor -- the turning-away from the demand itself.
