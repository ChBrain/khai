# Process: Problem-Focused

## Taxonomy

[Coping](process_coping.md), acting on the stressor itself.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: coping

## Initiated by

The persona acts on the stressor to change or remove it.
This is problem-focused coping (Lazarus & Folkman; Carver): effort aimed at the source of the demand rather than at the feeling it causes -- making a plan, taking active steps, gathering information, seeking instrumental help, and altering or removing the stressor.
It opens most readily where the persona has appraised the stressor as changeable, something its effort can actually move.

## Direction

Toward the source of the demand, to alter it.
Problem-focused coping turns the persona's effort outward onto the stressor: defining the problem, weighing options, acting on the most promising, and recruiting the help and information the action needs -- so that the demand itself is reduced, not merely the discomfort it brings.
Its direction is at the stressor -- the effort spent to change the situation that is taxing the persona.

## Lever

What problem-focused coping spends -- effort and resources laid against a demand judged movable.
It is the costliest route when it works and the most wasted when it does not: acting on a stressor that will not yield burns effort for nothing, which is why it tracks the secondary appraisal of controllability -- a persona spends here in proportion to how far it judges the problem within its power.
A production holds this lever by letting the persona work the problem -- plan, act, enlist help, alter the source -- and by letting that work pay off where the stressor is changeable and strain where it is not, so problem-focus reads as effort aimed at the demand, matched or mismatched to what can be moved.

## Echo

The stressor is changed, reduced, or removed -- where the effort could reach it -- and the demand eases at its source.
Problem-focused coping is adaptive largely where the stressor is controllable; turned on an uncontrollable one, it exhausts the persona against an immovable demand, and there the [escape](process_escape.md) route, or the emotion-management the regulation engine owns, may serve better.
Which route the persona takes is set by the [appraisal](process_appraisal.md) of what can be done. This form owns the acting-on-the-stressor -- the effort spent to change the situation rather than the feeling.
