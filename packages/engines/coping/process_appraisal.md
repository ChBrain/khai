# Process: Appraisal

## Taxonomy

[Coping](process_coping.md), the sizing-up that launches and steers the response.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: coping

## Initiated by

The persona sizes up the stressor before and while it acts.
This is cognitive appraisal (Lazarus & Folkman): the reading that launches coping and keeps steering it -- primary appraisal of what is at stake, and secondary appraisal of what can be done about it.
Primary asks whether the encounter is a harm already done, a threat still ahead, or a challenge that might be met and gained from; secondary asks what options and resources the persona has, and whether the stressor is within its power to change.

## Direction

Toward a verdict on the stakes and the options, held open to revision.
Appraisal points the persona's attention at the demand and at itself: what this could cost or win, and what it can bring to bear -- and it does not settle once, but updates as the situation shifts and the persona's efforts land or fail, the reappraisal that keeps the coping matched to the encounter.
Its direction is the sizing-up that routes everything downstream -- the reading by which a demand becomes threat or challenge and a response becomes worth mounting.

## Lever

What appraisal spends -- the persona's read on the stressor and on its own capacity.
The same event is a threat to one persona and a challenge to another by the appraisal alone, and coping follows the reading rather than the event: a stressor judged changeable pulls effort toward the problem, one judged fixed pulls it toward escape, so appraisal spends the assumption that the demand and one's power over it are known.
A production holds this lever by letting the persona's reading set its response -- a harm mourned, a threat braced against, a challenge risen to -- and by letting the reading be revised as the encounter turns, so appraisal reads as a live sizing-up, not a fixed label.

## Echo

The persona has a verdict on the stressor -- what is at stake and what it can do -- and the coping runs from it.
A stressor appraised as changeable routes toward [problem](process_problem.md)-focused effort; one appraised as beyond change, or too costly to face, routes toward [escape](process_escape.md); and as the situation shifts, the appraisal updates and the route can change with it.
This cognitive appraisal of the stressor and one's options is coping's own; the reappraisal that changes the emotional meaning of a situation to manage a feeling is the regulation engine's -- a different operation on a different object, the feeling rather than the stressor. Appraisal here is the sizing-up that launches and steers the effortful response.
