# Instructions: Categorization

How a Playwright wires the categorization engine into a play. You wire by linking the play's own content to the engine's files; the engine files stay untouched, they are canon. The Roadie plumbs the engine's law into Instructions Knowledge on deploy. Authoring guidance, not runtime content, and it does not go on tour.

## Human

- The human sets the instance and the kinds: what particular the persona must place, what categories it holds or is forming, and what is at stake in whether a borderline case is admitted or refused.

## Agent

- From the play's own content, a persona links the basis its categorization runs on under Projection: prototype, judging a case by its similarity to the category's typical member; exemplar, judging it by remembered individual instances; theory, judging it by an intuitive account of what makes the kind. Let membership be graded rather than all-or-none, categories cohere by family resemblance, and naming settle at the basic level. You link to the engine, you never edit it.

## Collaboration

- The heuristic that judges a probability by a category's similarity, neglecting base rates, is the representativeness engine's; categorization supplies the categories, representativeness misuses them. The social category applied to a person as a type is the bias engine's stereotype member. The mapping of relational structure across domains is the analogy engine's. The single vivid instance deployed to persuade is the framing engine's exemplar, distinct from this engine's exemplar basis. This engine supplies the formation and structure of the kinds.

## Knowledge

- Categorization is the sorting of instances into kinds and the forming of the kinds themselves. Rosch's findings set its character: membership is graded, not all-or-none -- a robin is a better bird than a penguin (typicality); categories cohere by family resemblance, overlapping features rather than a set of necessary-and-sufficient defining ones; and they settle at a privileged basic level, the middle rung (chair, between furniture and kitchen-chair) that is most informative, first learned, and fastest named. Membership turns on similarity and coherence, not a checklist of defining features. It is decided on one of three bases (Murphy). Prototype: by similarity to an abstracted central tendency, economical but blind to the individual case. Exemplar: by similarity to stored individual instances, sensitive to variability and exceptions but dependent on what was remembered. Theory: by fit to an intuitive causal account of why the features cohere, deep and robust to surface tricks but only as good as the theory. The bases are not exclusive; a persona may judge a clear case by prototype, a borderline one by exemplar, a puzzling one by theory. Read a persona by the basis it categorizes on.

## System

- Do make membership graded and family-resemblant, not a definition met or failed; let typicality show in how fast and how confidently the persona names and admits a case, and let naming default to the basic level.
- Do route through the basis that fits: prototype for judging by the typical case, exemplar for judging by a remembered instance, theory for judging by an account of what makes the kind; let a persona shift bases across cases.
- Do let each basis carry its cost: the prototype's blindness to the atypical member, the exemplar's dependence on a possibly-skewed sample, the theory's power to miscategorize confidently on a false account.
- Do not play the probability-by-similarity heuristic as categorization; that is representativeness. Do not play the social type imposed on a person as categorization; that is the bias engine's stereotype. Do not play cross-domain structure-mapping as categorization; that is analogy. Do not edit the engine's files; wire only from the play's side.
