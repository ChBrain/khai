# Process: Prototype

## Taxonomy

[Categorization](process_categorization.md), the basis of similarity to an abstracted central tendency.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: categorization

## Initiated by

The persona meets an instance and measures it against the category's typical case.
It does not retrieve every bird it has seen; it holds a summary -- the central tendency of the kind, a prototype abstracted from many members -- and asks how near the new instance falls to it. What enters is a case to be judged against an average that no single member need exactly be.
This is categorization by the ideal-typical: the category represented by its middle, and membership by distance from that middle.

## Direction

Toward a graded judgment of similarity to the prototype.
The persona reads the instance's features against the prototype's and returns a degree of membership: the closer the fit, the more typical and more surely a member; the farther, the more borderline. This is what makes a robin a better bird than a penguin and a chair a better piece of furniture than a rug -- typicality is distance from the prototype, and it predicts how fast the persona names the thing, how readily it comes to mind, and how confidently it is admitted (Rosch; Posner & Keele).
The direction is toward a position on the gradient of typicality, not a yes-or-no verdict.

## Lever

The economy of a summary, against its blindness to the individual case.
Whether the prototype basis serves turns on the category being well-summarized by a central tendency -- which buys great economy, one stored ideal standing for a whole class -- at the cost of losing the individual members and their variability, so a legitimate but atypical instance can be wrongly rejected and the spread within the category is flattened. The prototype knows the middle and forgets the range.
Where the kind has a clean central tendency, the prototype judges fast and well; where its members are diverse or its exceptions matter, the summary misleads.

## Echo

An instance placed by its distance from the category's typical case, graded and fast.
Because it stores only a central tendency, the prototype basis is economical and captures typicality directly, but it discards the specific instances the [exemplar](process_exemplars.md) basis keeps and the causal account the [theory](process_theory.md) basis supplies.
That membership is graded and settles at the basic level is the [root's](process_categorization.md); this form owns the judgment by the abstracted middle. The heuristic that turns such similarity into a probability estimate is the representativeness engine's.
