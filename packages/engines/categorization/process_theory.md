# Process: Theory

## Taxonomy

[Categorization](process_categorization.md), the basis of fit to an intuitive explanatory account.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: categorization

## Initiated by

The persona meets an instance and asks not what it resembles but what makes it the kind it is.
Beyond surface similarity, the persona holds intuitive theories -- of what a thing is for, how its features come to go together, what causes make it a member -- and judges the case by its fit to that account. What enters is an instance to be understood, not merely matched: a category held as a bit of knowledge about the world.
This is categorization by explanation: the kind defined by the story of why its features cohere.

## Direction

Toward membership decided by causal and explanatory fit.
The persona admits the instance to the category its intuitive theory says it belongs to, even against surface similarity: a thing that looks like a member but fails the theory is rejected, and a thing that looks unlike one but fits the causal account is admitted. Coherence comes from knowledge -- why birds fly, why the counterfeit is not money though it looks it -- not from feature overlap alone (Murphy & Medin). It is the basis that lets a persona learn a category from a single explained case, and hold essential features apart from incidental ones.
The direction is toward the placement the persona's theory of the domain licenses.

## Lever

The explanatory knowledge the persona brings, against its absence.
Whether the theory basis can run turns on the persona actually having an intuitive theory of the domain -- a grasp of the causes and functions that make the kind cohere; where it does, category learning is fast, principled, and robust to surface tricks, and where it does not, the persona falls back on bare similarity. The theory basis is as good as the theory, and can be as wrong as a mistaken one.
Supply real explanatory knowledge and the categorizing is deep and correct; supply a folk theory that is false, and it confidently miscategorizes on principle.

## Echo

An instance placed by the account of why its features cohere, above mere resemblance.
Because it draws on explanatory knowledge, the theory basis catches essential-versus-incidental distinctions and coherent categories that neither the [prototype](process_prototype.md) nor the [exemplar](process_exemplars.md) basis, working from similarity alone, can secure.
That membership is graded and settles at the basic level is the [root's](process_categorization.md); this form owns the judgment by intuitive theory. The reasoning that maps one domain's relational structure onto another is the analogy engine's; a category held about persons and applied as a social type is the bias engine's stereotype member.
