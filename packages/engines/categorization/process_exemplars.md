# Process: Exemplar

## Taxonomy

[Categorization](process_categorization.md), the basis of similarity to stored individual instances.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: categorization

## Initiated by

The persona meets an instance and matches it against the particular members it remembers.
It does not consult an abstracted average; it holds the individual cases it has encountered -- specific birds, specific betrayals -- and asks which of them, and how many, the new instance resembles. What enters is a case to be compared against a store of remembered exemplars.
This is categorization by the remembered particular: the category represented not by its middle but by its members, kept.

## Direction

Toward a judgment summed over similarity to individual instances.
The persona compares the new case to its stored exemplars and admits it to the category whose members it most resembles, the pull of each remembered instance weighted by how close it is (Medin & Schaffer; Nosofsky's generalized context model). Because the actual members are retained, the basis handles diverse and exception-ridden categories the prototype flattens, and it stays sensitive to the correlations and the odd cases a summary would lose.
The direction is toward the category whose kept instances the new case is closest to, in sum.

## Lever

The fidelity of kept instances, against the cost of keeping them.
Whether the exemplar basis serves turns on the value of preserving the individual members: it captures within-category variability, correlated features, and legitimate atypical cases that a central tendency erases -- at the cost of storing and searching many instances, and of leaning on whichever happen to have been encountered and remembered. The exemplar store knows the range and pays for it in memory.
Where a category is diverse or its exceptions carry weight, the remembered instances judge it truer; where they are few or unrepresentative, the sample skews the call.

## Echo

An instance placed by its summed similarity to remembered members, sensitive to the range.
Because it keeps the individuals, the exemplar basis captures variability and exceptions the [prototype](process_prototype.md) basis averages away, without needing the causal account the [theory](process_theory.md) basis supplies.
That membership is graded and settles at the basic level is the [root's](process_categorization.md); this form owns the judgment by stored instances. A single such stored instance wielded as a vivid stand-in to persuade is the framing engine's exemplar, a rhetorical move rather than this categorizing basis.
