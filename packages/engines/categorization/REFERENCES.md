# Categorization: Reference

## Line of Work

Categorization as a **process** engine: the sorting of instances into kinds and the forming of the kinds themselves -- graded membership, family resemblance, and the privileged basic level -- taken across the three bases on which category membership is represented and decided. The domain does not model the heuristic that judges a probability by a category's similarity, which is the representativeness engine's; nor the social category applied to persons, which is the bias engine's stereotype member; nor the mapping of relational structure across domains, which is the analogy engine's. It models the formation and structure of categories -- how concepts are built and how an instance is placed in a kind.

The spine is Rosch's principles of categorization and Murphy's synthesis of the concepts literature. Rosch overturned the classical view that a category is a definition with necessary-and-sufficient features and all-or-none membership. On the horizontal dimension -- the internal structure of a category -- she showed membership is graded: some members are more typical than others, a robin a better bird than a penguin, and categories cohere by family resemblance, overlapping features in the manner Wittgenstein described rather than a shared essence. On the vertical dimension -- the levels of abstraction -- she found a privileged basic level, the middle rung (chair, between the superordinate furniture and the subordinate kitchen-chair) that maximizes the information a category carries, is learned first, named fastest, and used by default. Murphy's synthesis frames how membership is decided across three views: the prototype view, membership by similarity to an abstracted central tendency; the exemplar view, membership by similarity to stored individual instances; and the knowledge or theory view, membership by fit to an intuitive causal and explanatory account, where coherence comes from knowing why features go together rather than from similarity alone. The engine takes the Roschian structure as its root and the three views as its forms. The altitudes are those: the categorizing itself, and its prototype, exemplar, and theory bases.

## Restrictions

What the engine refuses to model, and to whom it delegates.

- **The representativeness heuristic (representativeness)**: The engine models the formation and structure of categories, not the heuristic that misuses them to judge likelihood. Estimating a probability by how similar a case is to a category's stereotype -- and neglecting base rates in doing so -- is owned by the representativeness engine; categorization supplies the categories and their graded structure, and representativeness is one thing a persona then does with them, wrongly. This engine builds the kinds; that one judges probability by resemblance to them.
- **The social stereotype (bias)**: The engine models categorization in general, not the social category applied to persons. A category held about a social group and imposed on an individual as a type -- with its overgeneralization and its resistance to disconfirmation -- is owned by the bias engine's stereotype member; categorization is the general mechanism of which social categories are one, affectively and morally loaded, case. The general sorting is this engine's; the person-stereotype and its harms are the bias member's.
- **Analogical structure-mapping (analogy)**: The engine models sorting into kinds, not mapping across domains. Aligning the relational structure of one domain with another and transferring inferences is owned by the analogy engine; categorization places an instance among its kind by similarity or theory, a different operation. A category can supply a source for an analogy and an analogy can seed a category, but assignment-to-kind is this engine's and cross-domain mapping is analogy's.
- **The rhetorical exemplar (framing)**: The engine models the exemplar basis of categorizing -- judging a case by stored instances -- not the single vivid instance deployed to persuade. A striking member wielded to stand in for and colour a whole class is owned by the framing engine's exemplar; this engine's exemplar basis is a mechanism of category membership, not a rhetorical move. Same word, two jobs: the cognitive basis here, the persuasive device there.
