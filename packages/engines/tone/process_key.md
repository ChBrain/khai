# Process: Key

## Taxonomy

[Tone](process_tone.md), the sustained-register form.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: tone

## Initiated by

Beneath the events, the telling holds one pervasive feeling the audience is steeped in -- a dread, a warmth, a melancholy -- that does not spike and pass but stays.
This is the key: the sustained affective register itself, the dominant mood a telling maintains as its atmosphere, the tenor it is played in.
It is the ground the events happen on, not an event among them.

## Direction

Toward a pervasive atmosphere held beneath the beats, coloring all before any one lands.
The key runs low and long: where a discrete emotion spikes at a moment and subsides, the sustained register is diffuse and durable, a mood the audience enters on arriving and carries throughout, so that the whole is bathed in one feeling before its parts are met.
The direction is atmosphere over incident: the key is what a telling is like to be inside of, the pervasive Stimmung that makes a scene ominous while nothing ominous occurs, and against which each event is read.

## Lever

The dominant affective register, sustained, spent as the atmosphere the telling is steeped in.
The key runs on pervasiveness and duration: it is not the intensity of any moment but the constancy of one feeling across the telling that sets the tone, a low-level mood held steadily rather than a high-level emotion struck once.
The lever is the steeping: because the register saturates the whole and persists beneath the events, the audience is coloured by it continuously, met by each moment already tinted -- the atmosphere doing its work by being everywhere and lasting, not by peaking.

## Echo

The telling holds one feeling beneath all it shows, and the audience lives in it while they watch.
It is why a work has an atmosphere one can name after the plot is forgotten, why the pervasive mood of a place-on-the-page outlasts its incidents, why "what it felt like" is the register and not the events -- the sustained key the thing a telling most is.
It is [cue](process_cue.md) and [attitude](process_attitude.md)'s middle, the register itself where they are its mechanism and its stance. The persona's own internal affective background is the mood engine's, and the felt ambiance of a physical place is the space engine's atmosphere; key owns the sustained affective register a telling maintains as its tone.
