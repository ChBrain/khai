# @chbrain/khai-engine-tone

## 0.1.1

### Patch Changes

- 0981447: Add the tone engine: how a telling -- a scene, a passage, a production -- is tuned to a dominant affective key, the mood it is built to induce and the stance it takes. The root (`process_tone.md`) declares tone as a property of the telling, not a state of a persona: the key the work is played in, which colours everything it contains before any single event lands, and which is built to induce a matching mood in the audience. Three forms carry it: `cue` (the mechanism -- redundant, convergent affect markers across every channel that establish the key and, in a loop with the mood they create, sustain it -- Smith's mood-cue theory), `key` (the sustained register itself -- the pervasive atmosphere the telling is steeped in, held beneath the beats rather than spiking with them -- Gumbrecht's Stimmung), and `attitude` (the teller's stance toward subject and audience, ironic or earnest or tender, that inflects how the content is meant to be received -- Richards' tone-as-attitude).

  Fills the aesthetics-of-affect gap and grounds the (currently uninstantiated) `pitch` architecture element scientifically without being it. Bounded against `mood` (the persona's internal affective background -- the state a telling's tone aims to induce, vs the inducing key itself), `emotion` (discrete, episodic, appraisal-driven feeling that spikes and subsides, vs tone's diffuse sustained register), `framing` (a presentation's tilt on judgment, vs tone's tuning of feeling), and the `space` engine's `atmosphere` (the felt ambiance of a physical place, vs the affective key of a telling). No homonym gate: `tone` and the form stems `cue`/`key`/`attitude` are all free. Warranted (LORE) on Greg M. Smith (_Film Structure and the Emotion System_, the mood-cue approach, 2003), Hans Ulrich Gumbrecht (_Atmosphere, Mood, Stimmung_, 2012), I. A. Richards (_Practical Criticism_, tone as the speaker's attitude, 1929), Carl Plantinga (_Moving Viewers_, 2009), and Ed S. Tan (_Emotion and the Structure of Narrative Film_, 1996). Set at patch as the free level; a new engine may warrant a minor at the maintainer's `bump:minor` label.
