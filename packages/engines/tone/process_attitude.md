# Process: Attitude

## Taxonomy

[Tone](process_tone.md), the stance form.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: tone

## Initiated by

The telling takes a posture toward what it tells and toward the one it tells it to -- wry, tender, reverent, mocking -- and that posture inflects how its content is meant to be taken.
This is attitude: tone as the teller's stance toward subject and audience, the classic sense of tone as a speaker's relation to their matter and their listener.
The same content, told with a different attitude, asks to be received differently.

## Direction

Toward content inflected by the teller's stance toward it and toward the audience.
Attitude works as posture: the telling is not neutral about its material -- it holds it at a wry distance, or close and warm, or up for reverence -- and it positions the audience accordingly, as fellow ironists, as intimates, as supplicants.
The direction is inflection over information: the attitude does not change what is told but frames how it is meant, so that a description delivered with contempt, affection, or detachment carries the teller's judgment of it folded into the telling.

## Lever

The teller's stance toward matter and listener, spent as the inflection it lends the content.
Attitude runs on posture made audible: word choice, distance, what is dwelt on and what is passed over, the irony or earnestness of the framing -- all encode how the teller regards the subject and casts the audience, and the audience reads that regard as part of the meaning.
The lever is the encoded stance: because the telling betrays its own attitude, the content arrives pre-judged by its teller, and the audience is invited into that judgment -- to share the wryness, the tenderness, the scorn.

## Echo

The telling shows how it feels about what it tells, and asks the audience to feel with it.
It is why the same event can be told with reverence or with mockery to opposite effect, why an ironic tone holds its subject at arm's length, why "it depends on the tone" is a judgment about stance -- the teller's posture toward matter and listener carried in the telling.
It is [cue](process_cue.md) and [key](process_key.md)'s counterpart, the stance where they are the mechanism and the sustained register. Where the surplus meaning is inferred from what a cooperative speaker must intend, that is the implicature engine's; attitude owns the teller's stance toward subject and audience that inflects how a telling is meant to be received.
