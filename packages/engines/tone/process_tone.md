# Process: Tone

## Taxonomy

Media aesthetics / the affective address of a telling -- tone, mood, and atmosphere.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: tone

## Initiated by

A telling -- a scene, a passage, a whole production -- carries a dominant feeling before any single event in it lands: ominous, tender, sardonic, elated.
This is tone: the affective key a telling is tuned to, the mood it is built to induce and the stance it takes, colouring everything it contains.
Tone is not a persona's own mood but a property of the telling -- the key the work is played in, which the audience enters and is coloured by.

## Direction

Toward a telling steeped in one dominant affective register, and an audience primed by it.
Tone works by tuning: cues across every channel -- music, light, pace, diction, staging -- converge on a single feeling-register, and once that register holds it primes the audience to read each further moment through it, the mood making them more susceptible to the very cues that sustain it.
The direction is pervasive and prior: the key is laid over the whole before the parts arrive, so that the same event -- a door opening, a line spoken -- lands warm or dread-laden depending on the tone the telling has already set.

## Lever

The convergence of a telling's cues on one affective key, spent as the mood it induces and the stance it takes.
Tone runs on redundancy pointed one way: no single cue makes a mood, but many cues agreeing do, and the agreement is what the audience catches and holds -- a sustained register rather than a discrete jolt, laid beneath the beats and inflected by the teller's own posture toward the material.
The lever is the tuned key: because the telling is pitched to a dominant feeling, everything in it is coloured before it is judged, and the audience is moved less by any one moment than by the atmosphere the whole is steeped in.

## Echo

The telling is played in a key, and the audience feels along it before it thinks.
Tone is why the same plot turns comic or tragic on how it is told, why a horror scene dreads before anything happens, why an ironic telling holds its subject at a wry distance -- the affective register of the whole doing the work beneath the events.
It runs as [cue](process_cue.md), [key](process_key.md), or [attitude](process_attitude.md) -- the markers that build the register, the sustained atmosphere it holds, the stance the teller takes. The persona's own internal affective background is the mood engine's; the discrete episodic emotion is the emotion engine's; the presentation-frame's tilt on judgment is the framing engine's; the felt ambiance of a physical place is the space engine's atmosphere. This engine owns the affective key a telling is tuned to.
