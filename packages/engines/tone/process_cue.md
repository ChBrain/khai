# Process: Cue

## Taxonomy

[Tone](process_tone.md), the mechanism form.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: tone

## Initiated by

The telling lays down affect markers across its channels -- a minor chord, low light, a slow cut, a dwelling on a bleak detail -- and where they agree, a mood begins to hold.
This is the cue: the mechanism of tone, the redundant, convergent markers that establish the affective key and then sustain it.
No one marker sets the tone; their agreement does, and the agreement is what the audience catches.

## Direction

Toward a mood built by convergence and kept by a loop between mood and cue.
The cue works cumulatively: a single expressive element is weak and ambiguous, but many pointing the same way establish a mood, and once the mood is established it primes the audience to read further cues in its light -- so the mood makes the audience more susceptible to the cues, and the cues sustain the mood.
The direction is cyclical and self-reinforcing: cues create the mood, the mood readies the audience for more cues, and brief jolts of intense emotion, landing on that readied ground, refresh the mood that made them land.

## Lever

The redundancy of convergent affect markers, spent as a mood established and sustained.
The cue runs on agreement across channels: because the markers are many and mutually reinforcing, the audience need not decode any one -- the convergence itself carries the key, robust to a single element being missed or ambiguous, and pervasive because it is everywhere at once.
The lever is the loop: the mood, once cued, lowers the threshold for reading the next cue as confirming it, so a telling that has set its key spends little to keep it -- the register holds on redundancy and self-priming rather than on any decisive stroke.

## Echo

The markers agree, the mood holds, and each further cue lands on ground it has already prepared.
It is why tone is built and not stated -- assembled from music and light and pace and word until the key emerges -- why a mood set early carries scenes that contain nothing overtly expressive, why the horror hums before the fright.
It is [key](process_key.md) and [attitude](process_attitude.md)'s ground, the mechanism where they are the sustained register and the stance. This owns the convergent, redundant cueing by which a telling's affective key is established and kept.
