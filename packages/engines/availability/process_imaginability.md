# Process: Imaginability

## Taxonomy

[Availability](process_availability.md), the construction form.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: availability

## Initiated by

For events not held in memory, the persona judges likelihood by how easily an instance can be constructed or pictured -- the outcome readily imagined judged likely, the one that resists imagining judged remote.
This is imaginability: availability where the instances are built rather than recalled, the ease of the construction taken for the probability of the event.
What can be vividly pictured is felt as probable.

## Direction

Toward the easily pictured, judged likely; the hard to picture, judged unlikely.
Imaginability tracks the fluency of generating a scenario -- how readily the mind assembles a concrete instance -- and reads that fluency as the event's chance, so a risk one can vividly imagine looms and a danger one cannot conjure recedes.
The direction is set by the ease of construction, not the base rate: to picture an outcome in detail is to raise its felt likelihood, and to imagine a plan succeeding is to believe it more probable.

## Lever

The ease of construction, mistaken for the probability of the event.
Imaginability spends the effort of building a scenario: when instances come to mind by rule-guided construction, the ease of that construction stands in for frequency, so vivid, concrete, or well-rehearsed possibilities are overweighted and abstract or hard-to-detail ones dismissed.
The lever is the act of imagining itself as evidence -- merely constructing an outcome makes it feel more likely to occur -- which is why picturing a success, or a catastrophe, bends the estimate toward it.

## Echo

The estimate tracks what is easy to picture, and vividly imaginable outcomes are overrated.
It is the forward-looking face of availability -- the mechanism behind inflated dread of the dramatic disaster and inflated confidence in the vividly pictured plan -- and it can be driven on purpose, since asking someone to imagine an outcome raises their sense of its likelihood.
It is [retrievability](process_retrievability.md) with the instance built rather than recalled and [searchability](process_searchability.md) where the scan is a construction. The mental simulation and scenario-building the ease rides on shade toward the memory and decision engines; this owns the reading of construction-ease as probability.
