# Process: Availability

## Taxonomy

Cognition / the social psychology of judgment under uncertainty.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: availability

## Initiated by

A persona asked how frequent or how likely some class of events is answers not by counting but by how readily instances of it come to mind.
This is availability: the estimate read off the ease of recall, the felt fluency of bringing examples up taken for the true rate at which they occur.
Where a cold judgment would tally or reason from base rates, availability asks _how easily do cases come to me?_ and reads the answer as _how often do they happen?_

## Direction

Toward whatever comes easily, judged frequent; whatever comes hard, judged rare.
Availability maps ease onto rate: the class whose instances surface quickly -- because they are recent, vivid, or easily pictured -- is judged common, and the class whose instances resist recall is judged uncommon, whether or not the ease tracks the frequency.
The direction is set by the fluency of retrieval, not the count retrieved: the estimate follows how the bringing-to-mind felt, and slides off true wherever ease and frequency come apart.

## Lever

The ease of recall, mistaken for evidence of frequency.
What availability spends is a real and usually useful signal -- common things are often easier to recall -- pushed past its warrant: the persona treats the felt ease as if it were a count, so anything that makes instances more retrievable, searchable, or imaginable inflates the judged rate independent of the world.
The lever is the substitution: a hard question about frequency answered with an easy one about fluency, and the answer to the easy one reported as though it were the answer to the hard.

## Echo

The estimate tracks memorability, and the vivid crowds out the common.
Availability is why the dramatic risk is overrated and the quiet one underrated -- the plane crash over the car, the shark over the ladder -- because coverage and vividness make instances leap to mind out of all proportion to their rate.
It runs as [retrievability](process_retrievability.md), [searchability](process_searchability.md), or [imaginability](process_imaginability.md) -- what comes readily, how the search runs, how easily an instance is pictured. The storage and retrieval machinery the ease rides on is the memory engine's; the pull of a first figure on a numeric estimate is the anchoring engine's; the mode by which a choice among options is reached is the decision engine's; the standing catalogued tilt this look leaves is the bias engine's. This engine owns the reading of ease as frequency itself.
