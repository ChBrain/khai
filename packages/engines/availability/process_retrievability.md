# Process: Retrievability

## Taxonomy

[Availability](process_availability.md), the recall form.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: availability

## Initiated by

The persona judges a class more frequent because its instances come to mind more readily -- the recent, the vivid, the emotionally charged surfacing first and fastest.
This is retrievability: availability driven by how easily stored instances are recalled, the memorable taken for the common.
What leaps to mind is read as what happens often.

## Direction

Toward the salient, judged frequent in proportion to how it surfaces.
Retrievability tracks the accidents of storage and recall -- recency, vividness, emotional charge, personal involvement, media coverage -- and lets them stand in for rate, so a class made memorable is judged common and a class that leaves faint traces is judged rare.
The direction follows the felt ease of the bringing-up, not the number brought up: even recalling many instances, if the recalling is effortful, can lower the judged rate, because it is the ease, not the count, that is read.

## Lever

The fluency of retrieval, taken as a frequency cue.
Retrievability spends the metacognitive feel of recall: the persona monitors how easily examples arrive and reads smooth arrival as commonness, so anything that eases or impedes recall -- salience, order, mood, how many instances are demanded -- moves the estimate without touching the world.
The lever is the ease itself as information: when six examples come easily but twelve come hard, the twelve can make the class feel less frequent, because the effort of the twelfth is read as scarcity.

## Echo

The estimate tracks what is memorable, and vivid rare events are overrated.
It is the core of availability -- the mechanism behind the overrated dramatic risk and the underrated quiet one -- and it holds even against knowledge of the true rate, because the felt ease arrives before the correction can.
It is [searchability](process_searchability.md) once the search has run and [imaginability](process_imaginability.md) for instances already stored rather than constructed. The storage and recall architecture the ease rides on is the memory engine's; this owns the reading of recall-ease as frequency.
