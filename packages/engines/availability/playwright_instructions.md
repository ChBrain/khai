# Instructions: Availability

How a Playwright wires the availability engine into a play. You wire by linking the play's own content to the engine's files; the engine files stay untouched, they are canon. The Roadie plumbs the engine's law into Instructions Knowledge on deploy. Authoring guidance, not runtime content, and it does not go on tour.

## Human

- The human defines the availability terrain: what frequency or likelihood a persona must judge, and what makes some instances leap to mind and others hide -- a recent event, a vivid image, a heavy coverage, a search cued one way rather than another -- so the ease has something to inflate and a rate to distort.

## Agent

- From the play's own content, a persona links the form the moment runs (retrievability, searchability, or imaginability) under its Projection. Let the form show how the estimate is read from ease -- the memorable taken for the common, the class the search reaches first judged larger, the outcome easy to picture judged likely -- and let it read as a rate judged by fluency, not a rate counted or reasoned. You link to the engine, you never edit it.

## Collaboration

- The storage, indexing, and retrieval machinery the ease rides on is the memory engine's; availability owns the inference, not the recall. The pull of a first figure on a numeric estimate is the anchoring engine's, its sister heuristic; the mode by which a choice among options is reached is the decision engine's; the standing catalogued tilt the look leaves on the read of a rate is the bias engine's. This engine supplies the reading of ease as frequency itself -- how readily instances come to mind, taken for how often they occur.

## Knowledge

- Availability is the estimate read from ease of recall: a persona judging how frequent or likely a class of events is answers by how readily instances of it come to mind, taking the felt ease for the true rate. Retrievability is the recall route -- the recent, vivid, and charged surface first, and their easy retrieval inflates the judged rate, so what is memorable is taken for what is common; it is the felt ease, not the count, that is read. Searchability is the search-set route -- the estimate depends on how memory is searched, a class easy to search along one dimension seeming more frequent than one hard to search. Imaginability is the construction route -- for events not stored, the ease of picturing an instance stands in for its probability, so what is easy to imagine is judged likely.

## System

- Do make availability read ease as frequency, not count as frequency: the persona judges by how readily instances arrive, not by tallying them -- so let the vivid, recent, and easily pictured be judged common out of proportion to their true rate.
- Do let the felt ease, not the number, drive retrievability: recalling many instances effortfully can lower the judged rate below recalling a few easily -- so let difficulty of recall read as scarcity even when the count says otherwise.
- Do let imaginability be driven on purpose: asking a persona to picture an outcome in detail raises their sense of its likelihood -- so let a vividly constructed success or catastrophe bend the estimate toward it.
- Do delegate the storage and retrieval machinery to the memory engine and the reference-pull of a first figure to the anchoring engine. Do not edit the engine's files; wire only from the play's side.
