# Position: Verticality

## Taxonomy

[Embodied Metaphor](position_embodied_metaphor.md), up and down scaffolding good, powerful, and happy.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: embodied-metaphor

## Has

A specific [embodied scaffolding](position_embodied_metaphor.md): the primary metaphors GOOD IS UP, POWER IS UP, HAPPY IS UP, MORE IS UP, physical verticality carrying value, status, and mood.
The persona locates the good, the strong, the glad, and the plentiful above and their opposites below, and the spatial and the abstract run as one: it looks up to the admired and down on the despised, rises to an occasion and sinks in defeat, feels up when glad and down when low (Meier & Robinson's good-is-up; Schubert's power-is-up).
It is the metaphor of the high and mighty and the downtrodden, where standing is not like height but felt as it.

## Orders

Read up as good, high, and glad, and down as their opposites.
The verticality metaphor orders the persona to let vertical position and abstract worth move together -- to place the virtuous, the powerful, and the happy high and the base, the weak, and the sad low, and to feel its own mood and status as elevation or descent -- so that looking or moving up lifts its reading and down lowers it.
It grounds the persona's judgments of worth and rank in the body's fight against gravity.

## Loses

A worth unmoved by up and down.
Because value rides on verticality, the persona forgoes an appraisal insulated from mere position -- a thing placed high or a glance directed up can lift its estimate, a low position sink it, independent of merit -- and it will not know the height, not the worth, moved it. What it trades is a position-proof judgment for a worth it feels in the spine, and pays in the sway of an irrelevant high or low.

## Drives

On that verticality scaffolding:
runs good, power, and mood through up and down, the spatial and the abstract fused -- or, held to the abstract alone, tries to weigh worth apart from position, against the pull of the metaphor.
The verticality source holds beneath the persona's judgments of value and rank, one of five bodily domains its abstractions ride on, set beside [temperature](position_temperature.md), [weight](position_weight.md), [cleanliness](position_cleanliness.md), and [brightness](position_brightness.md). That the abstract is scaffolded on the bodily at all is the [anchor's](position_embodied_metaphor.md); status and rank themselves are the hierarchy engine's.
