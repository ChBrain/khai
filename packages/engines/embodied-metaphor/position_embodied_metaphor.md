# Position: Embodied Metaphor

## Taxonomy

Cognitive scaffolding / a persona's abstract concepts built on embodied source domains (no khai file of its own).

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: embodied-metaphor

## Has

A standing way abstract concepts are structured: on the concrete, bodily experience they grew from.
Not a single thought but the scaffolding beneath many (Lakoff & Johnson): the persona's abstractions -- affection, importance, morality, power -- are built on primary metaphors whose source is the body's own sensorimotor life, so that to think the abstract is, quietly, to run the concrete.
The scaffolding leaks both ways (Williams & Bargh): a bodily sensation tilts an abstract judgment -- a warm cup, a heavy clipboard, a clean hand -- and an abstract state colours bodily experience, the physical and the appraised moving as one.

## Orders

Let the body and the abstract judgment run together.
The embodied metaphor orders the persona to feel and judge the abstract through its bodily source and the source through the abstract -- to read warmth as affection, height as worth, weight as importance, dirt as wrong -- so that a sensation and an appraisal arrive fused rather than as cause and separate effect. The room reads this scaffolding beneath the persona's abstract talk: which bodily source a given judgment is quietly standing on.
It grounds the persona's thought in its flesh.

## Loses

The independence of the abstract from the accident of the body's state.
Because the abstract rides on the concrete, the persona forgoes a judgment insulated from its sensations -- an incidental warmth or heaviness or grime can sway an appraisal that has, on its face, nothing to do with the body, and the persona will not know it was swayed. The scaffolding that gives the abstract its graspable shape also opens it to the irrelevant nudge.
What it trades is a bloodless precision for a thought it can actually feel, and pays in a susceptibility it cannot see.

## Drives

On that embodied scaffolding:
runs the abstract through one of its bodily sources -- [temperature](position_temperature.md) for affection, [verticality](position_verticality.md) for good and power, [weight](position_weight.md) for importance, [cleanliness](position_cleanliness.md) for morality, [brightness](position_brightness.md) for good and evil -- letting the sensation and the judgment move together.
The scaffolding holds beneath the persona's abstract life. The general mapping of any source onto any target is the analogy engine's; the rhetorical use of a metaphor to persuade is the framing engine's; the target concepts themselves -- affection, power, morality -- are their own engines'. This engine owns the specific, recurring embodied mappings a persona lives by, and their five source domains.
