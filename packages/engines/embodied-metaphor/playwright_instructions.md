# Instructions: Embodied Metaphor

How a Playwright wires the embodied-metaphor engine into a play. You wire by linking the play's own content to the engine's files; the engine files stay untouched, they are canon. The Roadie plumbs the engine's law into Instructions Knowledge on deploy. Authoring guidance, not runtime content, and it does not go on tour.

## Human

- The human sets the bodily scene and the abstract judgment it colours: the warmth or cold, the height, the weight, the dirt, the light in the persona's situation, and the affection, worth, importance, morality, or valence that quietly runs on it.

## Agent

- From the play's own content, a persona links the primary metaphor its body and its abstract judgment are running through under its Projection: temperature, warmth and affection moving together; verticality, up and good together; weight, heavy and important together; cleanliness, dirty and immoral together; brightness, dark and bad together. Let the sensation and the appraisal arrive fused -- a warm room warming a judgment, a washed hand easing a guilt. You link to the engine, you never edit it.

## Collaboration

- The general capacity to map any source onto any target is the analogy engine's; embodied metaphor is the entrenched, near-universal bodily subset of that. The rhetorical use of a metaphor to persuade is the framing engine's. The target concepts themselves are their own engines': affection the love and attachment engines', power the hierarchy engine's, moral judgment the moral-judgment engine's, disgust the disgust engine's, valence the emotion engine's. The setting of representational level by psychological distance is the construal engine's. This engine supplies the specific bodily-to-abstract scaffolding.

## Knowledge

- Embodied metaphor is the scaffolding of abstract concepts on the body's own sensorimotor experience (Lakoff & Johnson): a persona thinks affection, importance, morality, power, and valence through warmth, weight, verticality, cleanliness, and brightness, because those correlations were learned in the body from infancy, and the scaffolding leaks both ways -- a bodily sensation tilts an abstract judgment, and an abstract state colours bodily experience (Williams & Bargh). Five primary metaphors. Temperature: physical warmth and cold carry affection and hostility -- a warm cup reads a person warmer. Verticality: up and down carry good, power, and happiness -- the persona looks up to the admired, feels down when low. Weight: heavy and light carry importance -- a heavy clipboard makes a matter feel graver. Cleanliness: clean and dirty carry moral purity and pollution -- guilt seeks washing, and washing eases it. Brightness: light and dark carry good and evil -- the bright prospect, the dark mood. Read a persona by which bodily source its abstract judgment is standing on, and let the two move together -- including when an incidental sensation sways a judgment it should not.

## System

- Do let the bodily source and the abstract judgment run together, fused rather than as cause and separate effect: warmth as affection, height as worth, weight as importance, dirt as wrong, dark as bad.
- Do let the scaffolding leak both ways, and let an incidental sensation sway an abstract judgment the persona does not know it swayed -- that susceptibility is the phenomenon, not a bug.
- Do route through the primary metaphor that fits the scene, and keep to the bodily source domains the engine owns.
- Do not play the general mapping mechanism as embodied metaphor; that is analogy. Do not play a metaphor wielded to persuade as embodied metaphor; that is framing. Do not play the target concept -- the affection, the power, the morality -- as this engine's; those are their own. Do not edit the engine's files; wire only from the play's side.
