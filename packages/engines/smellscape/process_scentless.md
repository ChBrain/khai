# Process: Scentless

## Taxonomy

[Smellscape](process_smellscape.md), the facet where the absence of smell is read as a presence.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: smellscape

## Initiated by

The smell falls away, and the scentlessness is read not as nothing but as something the place is.
Scentless starts wherever a place's lack of smell becomes a quality of it -- the antiseptic blank of the hospital, the scrubbed neutrality of the new hotel, the odorless hush of the clean-room, the deodorized air of the mall, the pointed nothing of a place that has spent effort to smell of nothing at all.
It is smellscape read the other way: not the absence of the field but one of its states, the scentlessness a place can be as much as it can be fragrant -- and, in a deodorizing world, the state more and more places are worked toward.

## Direction

Down to the odorless, and toward the scentlessness felt as a statement.
Scentless strips the place -- the ground scrubbed out, the plumes filtered off, the redolence erased -- until the nose finds nothing, and then reads the nothing itself: clean, controlled, safe, expensive, or sterile and cold.
It moves the place from smelling to scentless, and the scentlessness is not a void but a felt quality -- the reassurance of the clean, the luxury of the unscented, or the unease of a place too blank to be alive, the deodorized nowhere that could be anywhere.

## Lever

The value of the odorless against the fragrant world it replaces.
The carrying force is that scentlessness reads as cleanliness, safety, and control -- the deodorized modern ideal, the sanitized hospital and the unscented luxury, the achievement of a place that smells of nothing; the resistance, and the loss, is that the same scrubbing strips away the ground and plume and redolence that made a place itself and returnable, trading the fragrant and the foul alike for a clean blank that holds no memory and belongs to nowhere.
Read one way the scentless place is the triumph of hygiene over the reeking old world; read the other it is the erasure of the very smells a place is known and remembered by -- the deodorized nowhere bought at the price of somewhere.

## Echo

The place offering, or imposing, its scentlessness as a thing in itself.
It shows a place gone odorless -- the antiseptic, the scrubbed, the deodorized -- and carries beneath it the smellscape's twist, the sibling of the family's ambivalence: that the absence of smell is not the failure of the smellscape but one of its states, the scentlessness a place is worked toward read as a presence and not a void, cleanliness and erasure in the same clean air.
Scentless is dated from the falling-away, the moment the smell was scrubbed out and the nothing became something the place meant to be.
