# Process: Redolence

## Taxonomy

[Smellscape](process_smellscape.md), the facet of the signature smell that binds to memory.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: smellscape

## Initiated by

A place has one smell that is unmistakably its own, and it holds a memory folded inside it.
Redolence starts wherever a place's smell identifies it and no other -- the exact incense of this church, the particular damp of this cellar, the mix of spice and dust that is this market, the smell of this house that no other house has -- the signature the place is known and remembered by.
It is the smell-analog of the soundscape's soundmark, deepened by smell's straight wire to memory: the aroma that is the place itself, and that, smelled again anywhere, returns the whole place unbidden and complete.

## Direction

Toward identity and back into memory, the place and its past in one breath.
Redolence marks the place as itself -- the one smell a returning native would know blindfold, the aroma that says this place and only this -- and carries beneath it the involuntary return, the smell that reaches memory before thought and brings back not a fact but the felt whole of a time and place.
It moves the place from somewhere smelled to somewhere remembered: the madeleine's lesson, that a smell can restore a vanished world entire, so the place's signature is also its hold on everyone who has ever breathed it, waiting to return them the instant they smell it again.

## Lever

The particularity of the signature smell against the deodorizing that makes it rare.
The carrying force is that a redolence belongs to one place alone and binds it to memory as nothing else can -- the aroma worth keeping, the smell of a place that its people would grieve to lose; the resistance is the standardizing, deodorizing world that scrubs the particular away, replacing the old signatures with the same clean nothing everywhere, so a place's own smell, like a species, can go extinct and take its memories with it.
Keep the redolence and the place stays itself and stays returnable; lose it and the place is not only blander but unmoored from every memory its smell once held the key to.

## Echo

The place named and remembered by its own smell.
Redolence leaves the place with its signature -- the aroma that is this place and no other, worth keeping like a monument -- and hands it forward as the surest thread back, the smell that returns the whole place to anyone who breathes it again.
It is the most particular of the facets and the deepest: the smell that is identity and memory at once, the place folded into an aroma that can restore it entire, long after sight and sound of it have faded.
Redolence is dated from the recognition, the moment the smell was known for this place alone and the memory it holds came back with it.
