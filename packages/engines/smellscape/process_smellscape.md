# Process: Smellscape

## Taxonomy

Olfaction.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: smellscape

## Initiated by

A place is entered, and it is smelled as much as seen.
Smellscape starts the moment a place gives off smell at all -- the bread, the brine, the diesel, the damp, the nothing -- and the nose takes it before the eye has named a single thing.
Every place has an olfactory field, a way it smells that is as particular as its look and reaches deeper than either sound or sight; smellscape is that field as an actor, not the odorless scenery a setting is usually taken for.

## Direction

Into the nose, in drifts and gusts rather than a steady wall.
Four facets compose the field: [Ground](process_ground.md) is the base smell the place sits in, [Plume](process_plume.md) the drifting foreground smell caught for its meaning, [Redolence](process_redolence.md) the signature smell that marks the place and returns you to it, and [Scentless](process_scentless.md) the twist -- the absence of smell read as a presence.
A place is not smelled as one continuous field but as episodes: a base you sit in until you forget it, the smells that arrive on the air and are gone, the one that is the place itself, and the scrubbed scentlessness beneath -- for smell, unlike sound, comes in fragments, non-continuous in space and time, here at the doorway and gone three steps on.

## Lever

The place's smell against the nose's fast fatigue and the deodorizing of the world.
The carrying force is everything at the place that gives off odor -- the natural, the human, the cooked, the rotting, the made; the resistance is olfactory habituation, the nose ceasing to register a smell within minutes so the ground goes unsmelled, and the modern deodorizing that scrubs places toward scentlessness, stripping the fragrant world down to the clean.
A place still rich in smell is known in a register nothing else reaches, straight to memory and feeling; a place deodorized to nothing is smelled as a blank, its ground and plume and redolence erased into the antiseptic.

## Echo

The place known by its smell, and returned to by it.
Smellscape does not close; it is the standing olfactory character of the place, gathering its smells into a field as much the place as its look, and binding it to memory more surely than any other sense.
It hands off what it is not: the weather that supplies some of the smell is weather's, the layout and standing mood are space's, a persona's own nose and smell-memory are theirs, the moral revulsion a smell provokes is disgust's, and an authored sensory key laid over the play is the pitch's.
The place is dated from its smell, and read by it -- a setting is not only somewhere seen but somewhere smelled, and the nose returns to a place the eye and ear have both let go.
