# Process: Ground

## Taxonomy

[Smellscape](process_smellscape.md), the facet of the base smell.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: smellscape

## Initiated by

A place holds a constant base odor, and everything else is smelled against it.
Ground starts wherever the place has a smell that is simply always there -- the salt and weed of the harbor, the yeast of the bakery street, the resin of the pine wood, the coal-smoke of the winter town, the hot dust of the summer road, the reek of the tannery.
It is the floor of the field, the smell-analog of the soundscape's keynote: the base note a place sits in, smelled not by attention but by sheer constancy, and noticed most sharply by the one just arrived or the one coming home.

## Direction

Under everything, constant and quickly unsmelled.
Ground fills the place beneath the drifting figures -- the base layer the nose stops registering within minutes precisely because it never changes, the smell a resident forgets entirely and a newcomer cannot get past.
It moves nothing and asks nothing; it sets the place's olfactory base, the note against which every plume and redolence is smelled, the smell that says where you are before any single smell is named -- and that a stranger will call the whole character of the place while its own people no longer smell it at all.

## Lever

The constancy of the base smell against the nose's fast fatigue.
The carrying force is that the ground is always giving off -- the source that does not stop, the sea, the ovens, the works; the resistance is olfactory habituation, the nose adapting to a steady smell in minutes so the ground shapes the place's feel while going wholly unsmelled by those who live in it.
Change the ground and the whole place changes character; return to it after long absence and it floods back at full strength -- the smell of the childhood house or the home city, unsmelled for years by those still in it, undeniable to the one who has been away.

## Echo

The place set to its base note.
Ground leaves the place with its constant background smell -- the odor it is always giving off, the olfactory floor the rest drifts over -- and hands that base to every plume smelled against it.
It is the least noticed and most defining of the facets: the smell a place is, under the smells it makes, the base the nose settles into and stops smelling, and that names the place unmistakably to anyone who has been away from it.
Ground is dated from the constancy, the standing base smell the place is smelled against.
