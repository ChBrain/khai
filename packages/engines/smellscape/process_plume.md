# Process: Plume

## Taxonomy

[Smellscape](process_smellscape.md), the facet of the drifting foreground smell.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: smellscape

## Initiated by

A smell arrives on the air, out of the base and gone again, and it carries something.
Plume starts wherever a place's smell moves in episodes -- the gust of frying that reaches the street, the smoke that means fire before the eye finds it, the perfume crossing a room, the sudden rot from the drain, the cut grass on the wind -- the foreground smell smelled for its meaning, not its constancy.
It is the smell-analog of the soundscape's signal, but truer to how smell actually behaves: non-continuous, fragmentary, borne on the air, here at one step and gone at the next -- the place smelled not as a field but as a drift of episodes.

## Direction

Across the place and toward the nose, in a gust and then gone.
Plume rises out of the ground and demands to be read -- the nose turning toward the cooking, flinching from the gas, chasing the scent, placing the smoke -- each arrival a fragment that says something and then disperses before it can be fixed.
It moves the place from a base smelled to a message caught: the smell that means food, danger, another person, decay, arrival -- information delivered straight and fast, ahead of sight and sound, and then carried off on the same air that brought it, leaving the nose reaching after a thing it can no longer smell.

## Lever

The meaning the drifting smell carries against the air that will not hold it still.
The carrying force is that a plume is smelled as news -- the cooking, the smoke, the body, the bloom, each a smell attended to for what it tells; the resistance is the air itself, which disperses the plume as fast as it delivers it, so a smell cannot be returned to and inspected the way a sight can be looked at again, only caught in the instant it drifts through.
Catch the plume and the place hands over its message in a breath -- the danger, the welcome, the warning; miss it, or have it blow the other way, and the message is simply never delivered, the fire unsmelled until it is seen.

## Echo

The place read by the smells that cross it.
Plume leaves the place marked by its episodes -- the drifts of cooking and smoke and bloom and rot that arrive and disperse -- and hands each caught fragment up as a thing the place said and then took back.
It is the most active of the facets and the most fleeting: the smell that carries meaning and will not stay to be examined, the place speaking to the nose in gusts, fast and gone, ahead of every slower sense.
Plume is dated from the arrival, the moment the smell drifted through and delivered, before the air carried it off.
