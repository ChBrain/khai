# Process: Expansive Appraisal

## Taxonomy

[Mania](process_mania.md), the reckoning -- risk dropping out of the estimate rather than being overruled by it.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: mania

## Initiated by

The rise reaches the machinery that weighs a decision, and the downside stops being computed.
Depue and Iacono's behavioral facilitation system does not merely raise approach; it changes what the persona is estimating. This is the movement's precise claim, and it is not bravado: the persona is not weighing a risk and accepting it. The risk is not in the reckoning at all. Asked afterwards what they thought might go wrong, the honest answer is that the question did not arise -- which is why the conversation in the moment never lands, because the room is arguing about a downside the persona is not holding.

## Direction

From a widened estimate of capacity, through decisions taken without a downside term, toward commitments whose failure modes were never modelled.
The movement runs through the persona's judgement rather than their mood. Money is spent on a plan whose collapse case does not exist; a reputation is staked on a claim that has no fallback; a relationship is spoken to with a candour that assumes no cost. Each is delivered with the fluency of someone who has thought it through, because they have -- the thinking is intact and fast. It is the input that is missing.

## Lever

Whether anyone can make the missing downside concrete enough to re-enter the reckoning.
General caution does not work and should not be staged as working: "be careful" has nothing to attach to. What sometimes lands is specific and external -- a number, a signature refused, a person naming the exact consequence to the exact person. Stage the failure of the general and the occasional purchase of the specific.

**The twist is that the persona is more articulate about the plan than their critics are about the objection.** They can answer every question, and the answers are good, because the plan really is coherent once you accept the estimate it rests on. The argument is lost by the room not because the persona out-reasons it but because the room is disputing a conclusion when the disagreement is upstream, in a premise nobody has said out loud.

## Echo

Expansive appraisal makes the escalation of [goal activation](process_goal_activation.md) survivable to the persona, and it is held open by [rest forfeit](process_rest_forfeit.md), which prevents the return to baseline where the downside would reappear. Grandiose self-inflation as a standing trait belongs to `dark-triad`; the general weighing of a threat against a resource belongs to `coping`; systematic error in judgement under uncertainty belongs to `bias`. This movement owns only the episodic loss of the downside term.
