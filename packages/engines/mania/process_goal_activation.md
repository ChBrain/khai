# Process: Goal Activation

## Taxonomy

[Mania](process_mania.md), the ascent -- pursuit escalating on the evidence of its own early wins.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: mania

## Initiated by

A goal is attained, and the attainment is read as information about capacity.
S. Johnson's finding is specific and it is the entry condition here: what precedes the ascent is not stress and not loss but _success_. A goal-attainment event -- an acceptance, a win, a first sale -- arrives, and in this persona the reward signal does not close the loop. Instead of the pursuit ending with the goal, the goal becomes a data point about how much is available, and the next target is set from that revised estimate rather than from the resources actually in hand.

## Direction

From one attained goal, through an escalating series set from each success, toward a portfolio of commitments no schedule can hold.
The movement compounds. Each completed step is genuine, which is why it is persuasive: the persona really did ship the thing, really did make the call, really did win the room. The escalation is in the setting of the next target, not in the doing of the last one. Ambition widens faster than delivery, projects are started rather than finished, and the persona experiences the growing pile not as overreach but as momentum -- because at the moment each commitment is made, the evidence for it is a real prior success.

## Lever

Whether the next target is set from what was attained or from what remains available to spend.
The lever sits at the moment of setting, not the moment of doing. Stage it concretely: the persona finishing something well and, in the same breath, committing to three things that only make sense if the next month contains more hours than a month contains. Nobody in the scene can point to the bad decision, because the bad decision is distributed across a sequence of good ones.

**The twist is that the early wins are real, so the case for continuing is real too.** This movement cannot be played as delusion. The persona is not imagining the successes; they are extrapolating from them, and extrapolation from genuine evidence is what reasonable people do. What has failed is not the reasoning but the rate at which the reasoning is allowed to run before anything checks it.

## Echo

Goal activation supplies the ascent that [expansive appraisal](process_expansive_appraisal.md) then makes survivable by dropping the downside out of the reckoning, and that [rest forfeit](process_rest_forfeit.md) keeps running by removing the nightly stop. The pleasure of a good outcome belongs to `joy`; the disciplined setting and holding of an objective belongs to `goal`; the effortful pursuit of a standard belongs to `achievement-motive`. This movement owns only the escalation of the target on the evidence of the last one.
