# Process: Rest Forfeit

## Taxonomy

[Mania](process_mania.md), the brake removed -- a need for rest that is absent rather than overridden.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: mania

## Initiated by

The night arrives and does not present a demand.
The distinction this movement rests on is the whole of it: the persona is not tired and pushing through. They are not caffeinated, not disciplined, not sacrificing. Sleep has stopped registering as something owed. They wake after three hours restored rather than depleted, and the extra hours are not endured but _available_ -- which is why the persona experiences the change as a windfall and describes it that way to anyone who asks.

## Direction

From a night that makes no claim, through hours nobody else is awake to witness, toward an ascent with nothing left to interrupt it.
The movement's work is subtraction. Sleep is the one thing that reliably ends a day's reckoning and forces a fresh estimate in the morning; with it gone, the ascent runs continuously and unobserved. Emails are sent at four; plans are committed to in hours when no colleague can push back; the persona arrives at breakfast having already acted on a decision the household is only now hearing about. The absence of rest does not merely add time -- it removes the audit.

## Lever

Whether the forfeit is read as a gift or as the symptom that keeps the others alive.
To the persona it is the best part, and it should be staged as genuinely enviable before it is staged as cost. The lever is whether anyone -- or the body -- reimposes the interruption. Stage the collapse when it comes as arriving all at once rather than gradually, because nothing has been metering the debt.

**The twist is that the one symptom the persona would keep is the one holding the episode open.** Offered a cure that returned their sleep, they would decline, and the refusal is coherent: they are more productive, more present, more alive on three hours. Remove any other movement and the ascent slows; remove this one and it stops, which is exactly why it is the last thing they will give up.

## Echo

Rest forfeit keeps [goal activation](process_goal_activation.md) escalating and [expansive appraisal](process_expansive_appraisal.md) from returning to a baseline where the downside would reappear. Rest, its architecture, its stages and the debt of losing it belong to `sleep`; sustained wakeful effort against fatigue belongs to `self-control`; the flat depletion that follows an episode belongs to `depression`. This movement owns only the episodic absence of the need itself.
