# Instructions: Mania

How a Playwright wires the mania engine into a play. You wire by linking the play's own content to the engine's files; the engine files stay untouched, they are canon. The Roadie plumbs the engine's law into Instructions Knowledge on deploy. Authoring guidance, not runtime content, and it does not go on tour.

## Human

- The human sets the ascent's terrain: which movement the persona runs, what attainment starts it, who is placed near enough to interrupt it and whether they will be believed -- and decides, as author, where the episode is allowed to be genuinely good before the story counts its cost.

## Agent

- From the play's own content, a persona links the mania movement it runs (goal activation, expansive appraisal, or rest forfeit) under its Projection. Let the movement set the rate at which the persona commits, not merely their volume. Route what the episode leaves behind -- the depletion, the debt, the repair -- through the depression, sleep and regulation engines, which own it. You link to the engine, you never edit it.

## Collaboration

- The joy engine owns the appraisal node that answers a good outcome; this engine owns activation that does not settle when the outcome is consumed. The flow engine owns absorption in a task matched to skill -- narrow and ending with the task; this engine's activation widens and outlives its occasion. The sleep engine owns rest and its debt; this engine owns only the episodic absence of the need. Trait grandiosity belongs to dark-triad; this expansion is episodic and returns.

## Knowledge

- Mania is an activation episode that rises rather than arrives, and the order between its movements is the mechanism. Goal activation converts an attainment into a larger pursuit, because the success is read as information about capacity rather than about the task. Expansive appraisal drops the downside out of the reckoning -- not overruled, not accepted, absent -- so the larger pursuit reads as reasonable from inside. Rest forfeit removes the nightly interruption that would force a fresh estimate, which is what keeps the other two running. Each movement is what makes the next survivable.

## System

- Do stage the ascent as persuasive before it is legible as damage: the early wins are real, the persona is genuinely faster and better company, and the people who love them are relieved to see it.
- Do make the interruption external. Insight is the faculty the appraisal has stopped producing, so an episode that ends because the persona reasons their way out of it contradicts the engine.
- Do let the room lose the argument. The persona can answer every objection, because the plan is coherent once its estimate is granted; the disagreement is upstream, in a premise nobody says out loud.
- Do not write the episode as a prescriptive account of a disorder, and do not carry criteria, thresholds or duration rules; it is a character's activation in a scene, never a diagnosis of a living person. Do not edit the engine's files; wire only from the play's side.
