# Process: Mania

## Taxonomy

Affect and motivation / episodic activation (no khai file of its own yet).

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: mania

## Initiated by

An attainment the persona reads as evidence about themselves rather than about the task.
The episode does not begin with elation; it begins with a result. A deal lands, a piece of work is praised, a plan works on the first attempt -- and the persona updates not their estimate of that project but their estimate of their own reach. Depue and Iacono's behavioral facilitation system is the machinery: an incentive appears, approach engages, and in this persona the engagement does not settle back to baseline once the incentive is consumed. Akiskal's hyperthymic temperament is the ground it rises from -- a persona already running warm, whose ordinary is other people's animated, so the first stretch of the ascent looks to everyone like them at their best.

## Direction

From a single attainment, through an ascent that feeds on its own early evidence, toward a commitment the persona cannot service.
The rise is not a mood inflating. It is three movements handing each other material: [goal activation](process_goal_activation.md) converts a success into a larger pursuit, [expansive appraisal](process_expansive_appraisal.md) removes the downside from the reckoning so the larger pursuit reads as reasonable, and [rest forfeit](process_rest_forfeit.md) withdraws the nightly interruption that would otherwise break the cycle. Each movement is what makes the next one survivable. The persona is not making one bad decision; they are making a sequence of individually defensible decisions at a rate nobody around them can audit.

## Lever

Whether anything external arrives with enough weight to interrupt the ascent before the commitments outrun the capacity.
The lever is not insight -- insight is what the appraisal has stopped producing. It is friction: a partner who will not sign, a body that stops, money that runs out, a person who says the sentence plainly and is believed for a moment. Stage the interruption as something that happens _to_ the persona rather than something they conclude, because the engine's whole claim is that the ascent removes the faculty that would end it.

**The twist is that the episode is at its most persuasive precisely where it is most dangerous.** Early on the persona is genuinely more capable -- faster, braver, more fluent, better company -- and the people who love them are relieved to see it. The evidence that the ascent is working is real, and it is the same evidence the persona uses to justify the next commitment. There is no moment at which the plans stop making sense from inside; there is only a moment at which the world stops absorbing them.

## Echo

The maintaining rise runs across three movements: [goal activation](process_goal_activation.md), which escalates pursuit on the evidence of its own wins; [expansive appraisal](process_expansive_appraisal.md), which drops risk out of the reckoning; and [rest forfeit](process_rest_forfeit.md), which removes the interruption that would end the cycle. The appraisal node that answers a good outcome belongs to `joy`; the task-locked absorption of a matched challenge belongs to `flow`; and rest, its architecture and its debt belong to `sleep`. This engine owns only the sustained, self-feeding activation and what it costs -- authored as character architecture for dramatic production, never diagnosed onto a living human.
