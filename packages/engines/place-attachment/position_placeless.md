# Position: Placeless

## Taxonomy

[Place Attachment](position_place_attachment.md), the absent bond -- at home nowhere.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: place-attachment

## Has

No particular place woven into the self -- at home nowhere and everywhere.
A specific [place attachment](position_place_attachment.md): the absent bond, where the persona holds no enduring tie to a particular place, moving through settings without belonging to any (Relph's placelessness).
It is being from nowhere in particular -- the ground interchangeable, no place carrying a piece of the self.

## Orders

Run from here, unanchored.
The placeless persona is oriented by no ground: it treats places as interchangeable, settles nowhere for long, feels the same mild belonging or none in each, and reads its own story without a place in it.
The freedom is real -- it can go anywhere because nowhere holds it -- and so is the lack.

## Loses

The depth and steadiness that being from somewhere supplies.
The placeless persona forgoes the belonging, continuity, and secure base a rooted place provides -- it pays for its mobility in a certain groundlessness, a self not anchored to any where, and can feel adrift even while free.
What it trades is roots for reach: it can leave anything because it is held by nothing.

## Drives

On that placelessness:
rides it -- stays mobile, keeps every place interchangeable, draws freedom from belonging nowhere -- or, meeting a place that begins to hold it, starts to root, moving toward the [rooted](position_rooted.md) or the [dependence](position_dependence.md) bond as a ground at last becomes irreplaceable. The bond holds underneath either way, absent, the persona unanchored to any where.
