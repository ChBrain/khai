# Position: Rooted

## Taxonomy

[Place Attachment](position_place_attachment.md), the identity bond -- the place woven into the self.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: place-attachment

## Has

A place woven into the self, home in the deep sense.
A specific [place attachment](position_place_attachment.md): the identity bond, where a particular place is part of how the persona defines itself -- a source of belonging, continuity, and who-I-am (place-identity, Proshansky; Relph's rootedness).
It is being from somewhere -- the place carried in the self, so that to name it is to say something about the persona.

## Orders

Run from here, toward the ground of the self.
The rooted persona is oriented by its place: it belongs there, is continuous with it, reads its own story through it, and feels most itself within it.
The place is not merely useful or familiar; it is constitutive -- a part of the self that happens to be made of ground.

## Loses

The lightness of belonging nowhere in particular.
The deeply rooted persona is bound to its place -- it cannot leave without leaving part of itself, cannot see the place changed or lost without a wound to identity, and may narrow its life to stay near it.
What it trades is the freedom of the mobile for the depth of being from somewhere; the root that holds it also ties it.

## Drives

On that rootedness:
rides it -- stays, tends the place, lets it hold and continue the self, draws strength and identity from belonging -- or, torn from it, becomes [homesick](position_homesick.md), the rootedness now an ache for the place that held the self. Uprooted often enough, or never rooted at all, the persona tends toward the [placeless](position_placeless.md). The bond holds underneath either way, the place a part of who the persona is.
