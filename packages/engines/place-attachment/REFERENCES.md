# Place Attachment: Reference

## Line of Work

Place attachment as a **position** engine: the enduring affective bond between a persona and a specific meaningful place, taken across the standings that bond can hold. The domain does not model the setting read as a mover in the moment -- what a place affords, holds, offers, or withholds -- which is the space engine's; nor the bond to other people, which is the attachment engine's; nor the sentimental longing for the past as such, which is the nostalgia engine's; nor the acute grief of a loss, which is the grief engine's. It models the person-side bond carried to a particular place over time: which ground a persona belongs to, what a place is worth to it, and what leaving or losing one costs.

The spine is environmental psychology's account of the person-place bond. Low and Altman framed place attachment as an affective bond to place, and Scannell and Gifford's tripartite model organizes it along three axes: the person (individual or collective), the psychological process (affect, cognition, behavior), and the place (its social and physical aspects). Two strands of the bond are classically distinguished: place-identity (Proshansky, Fabian and Kaminoff), where a place becomes part of the self-concept, a source of who-I-am; and place-dependence (Stokols and Shumaker), the functional bond where a place is valued because it serves goals better than alternatives. Relph's phenomenology adds the poles of rootedness -- deep, unselfconscious belonging, being at home -- and placelessness, the loss of distinctive, bonded places and the condition of being at home nowhere. The pain of the bond severed appears as homesickness and, in Albrecht's term, solastalgia: the distress of a home environment changed or degraded while one still inhabits it. The standings this engine offers -- rooted, dependence, homesick, placeless -- are the resting relations to place a persona holds. The altitude is the position: the bond to place as a standing part of who and where the persona is.

## Restrictions

What the engine refuses to model, and to whom it delegates.

- **Space**: The engine models the person's standing bond to a particular place, not the setting read as a mover in the moment. The space engine reads what a place affords, holds, offers, or withholds as it acts on whoever is in it; place attachment is the enduring tie a persona carries to a specific place across time, whether present in it or not. The two compose: space is the place acting on the persona now, place attachment is the persona's standing bond to it.
- **Attachment**: The engine models the bond to place, not the bond to people. The interpersonal attachment system -- proximity to a caregiver or partner as a secure base under threat -- is owned by the attachment engine; place attachment borrows that frame and applies it to place, and the two can diverge, a persona rooted in a place while insecurely attached to people, or the reverse.
- **Nostalgia**: The engine models the spatial bond and its severance, not the sentimental longing for the past. Nostalgia -- the bittersweet reach for a bygone time -- is owned by the nostalgia engine; the homesick standing is the place-bond held across separation, which may be colored by nostalgia but is a tie to a specific place, not a longing for a time.
- **Grief**: The engine models the standing condition of a place-bond severed, not the acute mourning of a loss. The acute grief response -- the shock, yearning, and reorganization after a loss -- is owned by the grief engine; place attachment owns the enduring bond and its displaced condition, and hands the acute mourning of a lost place across.
- **Third place; identity**: The engine models the bond to a specific place, not the sociability of a setting type, which is the third-place engine's (Oldenburg's cafes and pubs as a category), nor the whole self-concept, which is the identity engine's -- the rooted standing is the place-portion of self, one region of identity, not the whole.

- **[rooted](position_rooted.md)** (position, standing): The identity bond -- a place woven into the self, home in the deep sense, belonging and continuity. Anchored by Proshansky (place-identity) and Relph (rootedness).

- **[placeless](position_placeless.md)** (position, standing): The absent bond -- at home nowhere, no place woven into the self, mobile and unrooted. Anchored by Relph (placelessness).
