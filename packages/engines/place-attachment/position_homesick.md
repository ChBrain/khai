# Position: Homesick

## Taxonomy

[Place Attachment](position_place_attachment.md), the severed bond carried -- displaced from a place still held dear.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: place-attachment

## Has

A bond to a place the persona is no longer in, carried as an ache.
A specific [place attachment](position_place_attachment.md): the severed bond, where the persona is displaced from a place it still holds dear -- moved away, exiled, or left where its home was changed or destroyed beneath it (homesickness; the solastalgia of a home altered while one still lives in it).
It is missing a place -- the bond intact, the place out of reach or no longer as it was.

## Orders

Run from here, back toward the lost ground.
The homesick persona is oriented by an absence: it measures the present place against the missing one, longs to return, keeps the lost place alive in memory and habit, and feels not-quite-home wherever it now is.
The pull is backward and elsewhere -- toward a ground the persona cannot fully reach.

## Loses

The ease of being where one is.
The homesick persona cannot settle into its present place -- the bond to the absent one keeps it half-elsewhere, unable to fully belong where it stands -- and it carries a grief that a place, not a person, has been lost to it.
What it trades is presence for fidelity: it keeps faith with the lost place at the cost of being at home in the new one.

## Drives

On that carried loss:
rides it -- keeps the lost place alive, returns if it can, holds the bond across the distance -- or slowly transfers the bond, becoming [rooted](position_rooted.md) in a new ground, or, unable to bond anywhere again, drifts toward the [placeless](position_placeless.md). The specific sentimental longing for the past as such is the nostalgia engine's, and the acute grief of the loss is the grief engine's; this standing is the place-bond held across separation, homesickness as a condition. The bond holds underneath either way, turned toward the ground that is gone.
