# Position: Place Attachment

## Taxonomy

Environment / the enduring bond a persona holds to a specific place (no khai file of its own).

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: place-attachment

## Has

A standing affective bond to a particular meaningful place -- a home, a town, a landscape, a room.
How the persona is tied to its places over time: which ground it belongs to, what a place is worth to it, where it is at home (Low & Altman; Scannell & Gifford).
It is not how a place moves the persona in the moment, but the bond it carries to a place across time, into and out of it.

## Orders

Run from here, beneath every coming and going.
Place attachment does not respond to a setting; it sets the standing pull toward particular places -- the ground the persona returns to, defends, misses, and orients by, the place as a secure base and safe haven in the attachment sense, applied to place rather than to people.
Under no particular scene, this is how the persona is anchored, or not, to where it is.

## Loses

The freedom of the unrooted, and the interchangeability of ground.
The persona bonded to a place cannot treat all places as equivalent -- leaving one costs it, a change to it wounds, and no substitute is quite the same -- and so it forgoes the mobility of the placeless, for whom any ground will do.
What it trades is portability for belonging: the deeper the bond, the more a place is irreplaceable and its loss a real loss.

## Drives

On that standing bond to place:
rides it -- returns, roots deeper, defends and tends the place, lets it hold a piece of the self -- or is severed from it and carries the ache, or holds no such bond at all and moves unrooted.
The bond holds underneath either way, in one of four standings: [rooted](position_rooted.md) in a place woven into the self, bound by [dependence](position_dependence.md) on what it affords, [homesick](position_homesick.md) for a place lost or left, or [placeless](position_placeless.md) and at home nowhere. The setting read as a mover in the moment is the space engine's; the bond to other people is the attachment engine's; the sentimental longing for the past is the nostalgia engine's; the acute grief of a loss is the grief engine's. This engine owns the enduring bond a persona holds to a place.
