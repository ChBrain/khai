# Position: Dependence

## Taxonomy

[Place Attachment](position_place_attachment.md), the functional bond -- the place needed for what it affords.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: place-attachment

## Has

A place valued for what it affords, hard to replace with another.
A specific [place attachment](position_place_attachment.md): the functional bond, place-dependence (Stokols & Shumaker), where a particular place serves the persona's goals and needs -- the surf break for the surfer, the workshop for the maker, the city for the career -- better than an alternative would.
It is needing this place, not for who one is in it, but for what one can do there.

## Orders

Run from here, toward what the place makes possible.
The dependent persona is oriented by the place's affordances: it stays because the place works for its purposes, evaluates other places against it, and is bound as long as the place serves the goal better than the alternatives.
The tie is instrumental -- strong, but pinned to function rather than to identity.

## Loses

The interchangeability of settings, and the ease of relocation.
The persona dependent on a place cannot move freely -- to leave is to lose the affordances the goal requires, and no substitute quite matches -- and yet the bond is conditional: if the place stops serving, or a better one appears, the tie can break where an identity bond would hold.
What it trades is mobility for capability, and depth for function.

## Drives

On that functional bond:
rides it -- stays where the place serves the purpose, invests in it, defends its affordances -- or, as the place ceases to serve or a better ground appears, the dependence loosens and the persona moves on, where a [rooted](position_rooted.md) bond would have held past mere function. Dependence can deepen into rootedness as the place becomes part of the self, or stay purely instrumental to the end. The bond holds underneath either way, pinned to what the place affords.
