# @chbrain/khai-engine-place-attachment

## 0.1.1

### Patch Changes

- 87eae49: Add the place-attachment engine (position) -- the enduring affective bond between a persona and a specific meaningful place. Fills a gap the `space` engine leaves: space reads the setting as a mover in the moment (what a place affords, holds, offers, or withholds), while the person-side bond a persona carries to a particular place over time was unowned. Place attachment borrows the attachment frame -- the place as a secure base and safe haven -- and applies it to place rather than to people.

  - **Anchor (position):** `place attachment` -- the standing bond a persona holds to a particular place: which ground it belongs to, what a place is worth to it, what leaving or losing one costs.
  - **Standings (positions):** `rooted` (the identity bond -- a place woven into the self, home in the deep sense; place-identity, Relph's rootedness), `dependence` (the functional bond -- a place valued for what it affords, serving goals better than alternatives; Stokols & Shumaker's place-dependence), `homesick` (the severed bond carried -- displaced from a place still held dear; homesickness and Albrecht's solastalgia), `placeless` (the absent bond -- at home nowhere, mobile and unrooted; Relph's placelessness).

  Warranted (LORE) on Low & Altman (the affective bond to place), Scannell & Gifford (the person-process-place tripartite frame), Proshansky (place-identity), Stokols & Shumaker (place-dependence), Relph (rootedness and placelessness), and Albrecht (solastalgia). Bounded against `space` (the setting-as-mover in the moment vs the person's standing bond), `attachment` (the bond to people vs to place), `nostalgia` (the longing for a past time vs the bond to a specific place), `grief` (the acute mourning of a loss vs the standing displaced condition), and `third-place`/`identity` (the sociability of a setting type; the whole self-concept). Set at patch as the free level. No whitelist required -- the `place_attachment` anchor and `rooted`/`dependence`/`homesick`/`placeless` standing stems are unique (`displaced` was avoided as an existing aggression form).
