# Process: The Demand

## Taxonomy

[Confession](process_confession.md), how the telling is required.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: confession

## Initiated by

Somebody wants an account and is in a position to want it.
The Demand starts at the requirement: [an obligation](process_the_obligation.md) to tell that stands before any particular thing to tell, [a question](process_the_question.md) that cannot be declined without cost, and [a silence](process_the_silence_read.md) that will be read whatever it means.

## Direction

Toward a persona for whom not telling has become the expensive option.
The requirement is rarely enforced and rarely needs to be. What does the work is that the alternatives are all worse: refusing looks like guilt, deflecting looks like evasion, and asking why the question is being put looks like both.
So the persona speaks, and will describe having spoken as a choice.

## Lever

The reasonableness of asking against the absence of any free way to decline.
The carrying force is that the asking is usually legitimate and often kind: the doctor needs to know, the therapist cannot help without it, the interviewer has a duty, and the friend is asking because they care -- treating every question as an imposition would make care impossible and most questioners are not imposing; the resistance is that the persona has no move that reads as neutral, since silence carries meaning here whether or not they put any in it, and a question from somebody with standing has already changed what saying nothing costs.
The Demand does not need to compel, because it has made the alternative legible.

## Echo

A telling that will be remembered as freely given.
It is the movement that sets the whole operation running, and the one the persona will least remember.
The Demand is dated from the question, which is usually gentle.
