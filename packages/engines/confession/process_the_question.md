# Process: The Question

## Taxonomy

[The Demand](process_demand_confession.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: confession

## Initiated by

Somebody has asked.
The Question starts at the asking: put by a person with standing, in a setting arranged for it, about something the persona would not have raised.

## Direction

Toward an answer, because the alternatives cost more.
Declining is available and it is not free. A persona who says they would rather not has said something, and everybody in the room knows what.
So the question works by being answerable rather than by being enforceable, and the persona answers and later describes the answering as their own idea.

## Lever

The kindness of most asking against the pressure carried by any asking.
The carrying force is that the question is generally asked in good faith by somebody who needs the answer or wants to help, and a world in which nobody could ask anybody anything would be colder and less useful -- most questions are exactly what they look like; the resistance is that standing travels with the asker, so the same words from a friend and from an assessor are not the same act, and the persona cannot decline the second the way they could decline the first.
The Question is an ordinary act whose weight is set by who is asking.

## Echo

An answer given, and remembered as volunteered.
It is the most stageable moment in the engine and the one that looks like nothing.
The Question is dated precisely and forgotten immediately.
