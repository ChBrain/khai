# Process: The Record

## Taxonomy

[The Listener](process_listener_confession.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: confession

## Initiated by

What was said is written down.
The Record starts wherever the telling is retained: the note, the file, the transcript, the assessment, the summary that goes on somebody's system.

## Direction

Toward an account that outlives the conversation and travels without it.
The persona spoke into a moment and the moment has been fixed. The record can be read later by people who were not there, quoted in contexts they did not anticipate, and consulted long after they have changed.
And it is the listener's version, in the listener's categories, of what the persona said.

## Lever

The necessity of keeping a record against what a kept record becomes.
The carrying force is that recording is required and protective: continuity of care depends on it, an unrecorded account cannot be reviewed or appealed, and a persona whose history is written down is spared having to tell it again to every new person; the resistance is that the record is durable in a way the conversation was not, so a persona is answered for years afterwards by a paragraph they never saw, written in a vocabulary they did not choose, by somebody exercising judgement they had no way to check.
The Record is the confession's only permanent form and the persona does not hold a copy.

## Echo

A file that knows more about the persona than they can now recall telling.
It is where this engine meets the measure engine, since what was said has become what was recorded.
The Record is dated from the session and consulted indefinitely.
