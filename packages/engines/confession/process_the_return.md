# Process: The Return

## Taxonomy

[The Relief](process_relief_confession.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: confession

## Initiated by

The persona comes back.
The Return starts when the relief fades: next week's session, the following confession, the next appraisal, the friend asked again how it is going.

## Direction

Toward an arrangement with no completion in it.
Nothing about confession finishes. There is always more to say, the account can always be more complete, and the relief is temporary by construction -- so the persona returns, and returns having internalised that returning is what one does.
Which is when the requirement stops needing anybody to state it at all.

## Lever

The genuine benefit of continuing against the fact that it cannot conclude.
The carrying force is that continuation is usually right and not a trap: one telling rarely settles anything, the practices that work are the ones sustained over time, and a persona who came once and never again would have got the least out of it -- the recurrence is the treatment rather than a symptom of it; the resistance is that an arrangement with no terminus is one the persona will now maintain themselves, unprompted, indefinitely -- and at that point nobody is requiring anything, which is the most complete version of the demand there is.
The Return is where the obligation becomes the persona's own practice.

## Echo

Somebody who books the next one.
It shows the operation self-sustaining, which is what the twist predicts and what makes the whole arrangement so durable.
The Return is dated from the fading, and the interval shortens with practice.
