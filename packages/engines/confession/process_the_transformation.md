# Process: The Transformation

## Taxonomy

[The Relief](process_relief_confession.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: confession

## Initiated by

The persona is not who they were before they said it.
The Transformation starts after the telling: the account they produced is now the account they hold, and they have become the person that account describes.

## Direction

Toward a self reorganised around what was said about it.
Having named it, the persona now has it. The pattern they described becomes the pattern they notice, the category they were given becomes the category they belong to, and the version composed under questioning becomes their history.
This is the operation's actual product. Not information transferred but a persona altered.

## Lever

The genuine value of an organised self-understanding against its having been organised in company.
The carrying force is that being changed by telling is frequently exactly what the persona wanted and is the reason the practices work: somebody who understands themselves as having a condition rather than a failing, or as having been wronged rather than at fault, is better off in a way that is not merely a feeling -- the transformation is the therapy; the resistance is that the transformation was performed with somebody else's questions, terms, and standard of detail, so the persona now is what a particular conversation made them, and cannot recover the person who walked in.
The Transformation is what confession is for and what it costs.

## Echo

A persona who now matches their own account.
It is Foucault's central claim rendered as something that happens to somebody in a room.
The Transformation is dated from the telling and is not reversible by retracting it.
