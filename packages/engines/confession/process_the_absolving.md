# Process: The Absolving

## Taxonomy

[The Listener](process_listener_confession.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: confession

## Initiated by

The listener does something with what they heard.
The Absolving starts at the response: forgiveness, reassurance, a diagnosis, a plan, a penance, or simply the statement that this is not as bad as the persona thought.

## Direction

Toward a settlement only the listener can issue.
The persona cannot absolve themselves, which is the reason they told somebody. What they wanted was not to be heard but to be released, and release requires an authority.
So the response is the point of the whole operation, and the persona has been waiting for it since the first sentence.

## Lever

The genuine power of being released against having to be released by somebody.
The carrying force is that this works and is not a superstition: a persona told by somebody who counts that they are forgiven, or that what they have is a known condition, is measurably better afterwards, and the arrangement exists because nothing a persona does alone achieves the same thing; the resistance is that the release is issued rather than earned, so it can be withheld, qualified, or made conditional -- and a persona who needs it is in a poor position to disagree with anything else the listener says.
The Absolving gives the persona the thing they came for, from a hand they cannot reach past.

## Echo

A settlement received, and a standing confirmed by the giving of it.
It is the movement's payoff and the point at which the listener's authority becomes concrete.
The Absolving is dated from the response and remembered word for word.
