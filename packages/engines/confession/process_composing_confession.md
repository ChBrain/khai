# Process: The Composing

## Taxonomy

[Confession](process_confession.md), what happens in the saying.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: confession

## Initiated by

The persona begins to speak, and starts making something.
The Composing starts at the ordinary and misdescribed fact that an account has to be assembled: [made](process_the_account_made.md) rather than retrieved, filled in to the [detail](process_the_detail.md) the listener requires, and shaped to [the categories](process_the_category_fitted.md) the listener has available.

## Direction

Toward a version that will become the memory.
Nobody has an account of themselves lying ready. What a persona has is fragments, impressions, and a sense of things -- and the telling turns those into a sequence with causes, motives, and a shape, because that is what a telling is.
The version produced is then the one they will have from now on. The earlier fragments are not preserved alongside it.

## Lever

The necessity of composing against what composing costs the original.
The carrying force is that this is not falsification and the persona is not lying: assembling scattered material into an account is the ordinary work of understanding oneself, it is genuinely clarifying, and a persona who has never had to say it out loud understands themselves less well rather than more; the resistance is that the assembly happens under someone else's questions, to their standard of detail, in their vocabulary -- so what gets clarified is shaped by who was asking, and the persona will afterwards be unable to separate what they knew from what they said.
The Composing produces a self-knowledge that is real and was co-written.

## Echo

An account that is now the persona's memory of the thing.
It shows why confession is productive rather than merely revealing, which is the engine's central claim.
The Composing is dated from the telling and overwrites what came before it.
