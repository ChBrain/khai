# Process: The Relief

## Taxonomy

[Confession](process_confession.md), the unburdening, and where the engine is seen for one thing.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: confession

## Initiated by

The persona feels better, and they are not imagining it.
The Relief starts at the discharge: [the unburdening](process_the_unburdening.md) that follows having said it, [the transformation](process_the_transformation.md) it works on the speaker, and [the return](process_the_return.md), because it is never finished.

## Direction

Toward a persona who experiences a requirement as a liberation.
The relief is genuine, well documented, and frequently substantial. It is also the mechanism: a compulsion that produces suffering is visible as a compulsion, and one that produces relief is not.
So the persona describes the obligation as a need of their own -- the truth was in there, and it wanted out -- which is exactly what an internalised requirement feels like from inside.

## Lever

The reality of the release against what the release conceals.
The carrying force is that this is not a deception and there is no deceiver: the weight lifts, the persona is better afterwards, the practices built on it help people, and an account that treats the relief as false has got the phenomenon exactly wrong; the resistance is that the relief is what makes the demand invisible, so the more thoroughly it works the less anybody -- speaker or listener -- can see that a requirement was met, and the persona will volunteer next time.
The Relief rides the engine's keystone knife-edge: confession makes a requirement feel like a release.

## Echo

Somebody grateful, unburdened, and expected back.
It shows the three earlier movements complete -- a demand nobody had to enforce, an account produced under somebody's questions, a listener who now holds it -- and adds the thing that makes the arrangement self-sustaining, which is that it worked.
This is why the Relief is the hardest of the four to stage and the only one worth closing on: the listener must be kind, the relief must be real, nothing must have been extracted, and the audience must be able to see that a requirement was met by somebody who experienced it as a choice.
A play that stages the listener as sinister has replaced the mechanism with an interrogation, and one that stages the relief as hollow has replaced it with a lesson.
The Relief is dated from the moment the persona stopped talking, and it does not last, which is the point.
