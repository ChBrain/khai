# Process: The Silence Read

## Taxonomy

[The Demand](process_demand_confession.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: confession

## Initiated by

The persona has not answered, and that is an answer.
The Silence Read starts wherever declining is interpreted: the pause taken as evasion, the refusal taken as guilt, the change of subject noted.

## Direction

Toward a persona who cannot abstain.
Silence in this relation is not a null. It is data, and the listener will read it -- usually as concealment, sometimes as shame, occasionally as defiance, and almost never as nothing.
Which means the persona has no neutral option: they can supply an account or they can supply an inference, and they do not control the inference.

## Lever

The informativeness of silence against the fact that it informs regardless of intent.
The carrying force is that reading a silence is not paranoia and is frequently correct: people do go quiet about the things that matter, the pause before an answer is genuine evidence, and a listener who ignored it would be a poor one; the resistance is that the reading happens whether or not there is anything to read, so a persona who is silent because the question is intrusive, or because they do not know, produces the same signal as one with something to hide.
The Silence Read removes the possibility of not participating.

## Echo

A persona whose reticence has been entered into the account.
It is what closes the exit and completes the demand.
The Silence Read is dated from the pause.
