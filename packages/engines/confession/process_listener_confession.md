# Process: The Listener

## Taxonomy

[Confession](process_confession.md), who receives it, and what receiving is.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: confession

## Initiated by

Somebody is on the other side, and they are not just hearing.
The Listener starts at the [asymmetry](process_the_asymmetry.md): the one who speaks is the subject of the statement and the one who listens is not, does not reciprocate, and holds standing the speaker does not.
And they act on what they hear -- by [absolving](process_the_absolving.md), or by [recording](process_the_record.md).

## Direction

Toward an authority who now knows something and is not known in return.
The listener's role is intervention rather than reception: they judge, forgive, interpret, prescribe, file, or simply know -- and every one of those is an act performed on the speaker.
The relation does not reverse. A confessor who confessed back would have ended the arrangement, which is why the asymmetry is a feature rather than an accident.

## Lever

The value of a listener who can do something against what having one costs.
The carrying force is that the asymmetry is what makes it useful: a persona needs somebody who can absolve, treat, advise, or decide, and an equal cannot do any of those -- the whole point of telling this to this person is that they have standing, and a symmetrical exchange would be a conversation instead of a remedy; the resistance is that standing is the same thing whichever direction it is used in, so the persona has handed an account of themselves to somebody who is by construction not accountable to them for it.
The Listener has to be above the speaker to be worth telling, and is therefore above them afterwards.

## Echo

A relation with one person's interior in it.
It shows why the arrangement produces so much and why it cannot be made symmetrical without ceasing to work.
The Listener is dated from the first telling and holds what was told indefinitely.
