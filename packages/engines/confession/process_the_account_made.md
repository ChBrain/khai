# Process: The Account Made

## Taxonomy

[The Composing](process_composing_confession.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: confession

## Initiated by

The persona starts assembling.
The Account Made starts at the fact that nobody has a version ready: what is there beforehand is fragments, impressions, and a general sense, and a telling requires a sequence with causes in it.

## Direction

Toward a story where there was material.
The assembly happens fast and mostly below notice. Gaps are bridged, order is imposed, motives are supplied for actions that had none at the time, and the whole is made to hang together, because an account that does not hang together is not receivable.
The persona is not aware of having built anything. They experience it as remembering.

## Lever

The clarifying power of putting it into words against what the words settle.
The carrying force is that this is genuinely how understanding works and it is not a distortion to be avoided: a persona who has articulated something knows it better, sees the shape of it, and can act on it -- being made to say it out loud is one of the more useful things that can happen to somebody; the resistance is that the version produced then replaces the material it was made from, so the fragments are gone and the account stands in for them, and the persona has no way back to what they had before they explained it.
The Account Made improves the persona's understanding and destroys the evidence.

## Echo

A memory that is now a story.
It is the movement's core claim and the reason confession produces rather than reports.
The Account Made is dated from the telling, and everything earlier is dated from it afterwards.
