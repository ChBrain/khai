# Process: Confession

## Taxonomy

Speech (no khai file of its own yet).

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: confession

## Initiated by

A persona is telling somebody about themselves because they have to.
Confession starts wherever an account of a self is required rather than offered: the box, the couch, the interview room, the appraisal, the form that asks, the friend who will not let it go.
It is not the voluntary opening that deepens a relationship, which is self-disclosure's, and not the withheld fact and the work of keeping it, which is secret's. It is telling under a relation of authority, where the one who listens has standing the one who speaks does not.

## Direction

Toward a truth about the persona that did not exist in that form before they said it.
The engine reads four movements: [the demand](process_demand_confession.md), how the telling is required; [the composing](process_composing_confession.md), what happens in the saying, which is production rather than retrieval; [the listener](process_listener_confession.md), who is never merely a hearer; and [the relief](process_relief_confession.md), the twist -- the unburdening that is real and that hides the requirement.
The four are one operation, and it is among the most productive any society runs.

## Lever

The genuine release of having told against the fact that telling was not optional.
The carrying force is that confession works and mostly does good: a persona carrying something unspoken is carrying it alone, saying it out loud to somebody who will not use it against them relieves a real weight, and the arrangements built around this -- the confessor, the therapist, the friend who asks -- are among the better things people have made; the resistance is that the same act runs inside a relation the persona did not set, in a form the listener's categories decide, and produces an account they will afterwards take to be what they always were.
Confession is read on who required it, never on whether it felt good, because it nearly always feels good.

## Echo

Somebody lighter, and more thoroughly known by somebody else than by themselves.
Confession does not close; it is a truth being produced, gathered into one reading -- a demand, a composing, a listener, a relief.
The engine's twist is here. **Confession makes a requirement feel like a release.** The persona is obliged to speak and experiences the speaking as liberation, so the obligation disappears from view -- and the more relieved they feel, the less visible it becomes. This is what makes it so much more effective than any instrument that constrains openly: nobody has to coerce, the priest and the therapist and the interviewer are frequently kind, and the persona walks out grateful. What they have handed over is an account of themselves that somebody else now holds.
It hands off what it is not: the voluntary layer-by-layer opening is self-disclosure's; the withheld fact and its keeping are secret's; the account offered after a wrong is repair's, and the felt debt is guilt's; the surveillance, the norm, and the examination are discipline's; the standing a procedure confers is heeding's and recognition's; and a telling made to **stand for** a theme is authored key, the play's pitch, not the force.
The persona is dated from the first time somebody with standing asked.
