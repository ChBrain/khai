# Process: The Unburdening

## Taxonomy

[The Relief](process_relief_confession.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: confession

## Initiated by

The persona has said it and something lifts.
The Unburdening starts immediately: the physical loosening, the relief of no longer being the only one who knows, the sense of having put something down.

## Direction

Toward a state that is genuinely better than before.
This is not a story people tell about confession, it is what happens. Carrying something unspoken costs, and stopping carrying it alone is a real change with real effects.
The relief arrives whether or not anything has been resolved, which is worth noticing: nothing about the situation has altered, and the persona is better.

## Lever

The reality of the relief against its independence from any change in the facts.
The carrying force is that the effect is well attested and largely benign, the practices built around it help people, and treating it as illusory is both wrong and unkind -- a persona who has told somebody is in a better position than one who has not, and the gain is not merely felt; the resistance is that a relief which arrives regardless of whether anything was fixed is a relief that can be produced by any listener, including one whose interest in hearing it is not the persona's -- so the same discharge follows a confession to a friend and a confession to an interrogator.
The Unburdening is real and is not evidence that the telling was good for the persona.

## Echo

Somebody lighter, in circumstances that have not changed.
It is the engine's engine, and the reason nobody has to be coerced.
The Unburdening is dated from the last word and begins to fade shortly after.
