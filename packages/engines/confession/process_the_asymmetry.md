# Process: The Asymmetry

## Taxonomy

[The Listener](process_listener_confession.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: confession

## Initiated by

One of them is telling and the other is not.
The Asymmetry starts at the structure: the speaker is the subject of the statement, the listener is not, and nothing about the arrangement suggests the listener will reciprocate.

## Direction

Toward a relation with one interior in it.
The listener asks and does not answer, knows and is not known, and holds standing the speaker has surrendered by speaking.
This is not a failure of intimacy. It is the arrangement's design, and a listener who began confessing back would have destroyed the thing the speaker came for.

## Lever

The usefulness of an unreciprocating listener against what not reciprocating establishes.
The carrying force is that the asymmetry is exactly what the speaker needs: they came for somebody who can absolve, treat, or decide, and an equal cannot do any of it -- the professional who keeps their own life out of the room is being competent rather than cold, and mutual disclosure here would be a burden dressed as warmth; the resistance is that standing does not partition, so the person who is above the speaker in order to help them is above them afterwards too, holding an account they gave freely under conditions they did not set.
The Asymmetry has to be there for the arrangement to work, and it is the arrangement's whole cost.

## Echo

Two people, one of whom is now much better known.
It is the movement's premise and the reason the relation cannot be repaired by good manners.
The Asymmetry is dated from the first telling and is not reduced by familiarity.
