# Process: The Obligation

## Taxonomy

[The Demand](process_demand_confession.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: confession

## Initiated by

There is a standing requirement to account for oneself.
The Obligation starts before any particular question: the settled expectation that a persona in this relation -- penitent, patient, applicant, employee, partner -- will give an account when asked, and that having one ready is part of being in it properly.

## Direction

Toward a persona who prepares.
The requirement does not wait to be invoked. Somebody who knows they will be asked begins assembling in advance, rehearsing the version, deciding what to lead with -- so the operation has already started before any question is put.
And the preparation is experienced as conscientiousness rather than as compliance.

## Lever

The legitimacy of the expectation against the fact that it never has to be enforced.
The carrying force is that the expectation is usually earned and usually reasonable: a therapist cannot work without it, an employer has a case, a partner is entitled to know what is going on, and a persona who resents all of it is difficult rather than free; the resistance is that a requirement met in advance is a requirement nobody has to make, so it leaves no trace of having operated -- there is no demand to point at, only somebody who came prepared.
The Obligation is the part of the engine that does its work before anybody speaks.

## Echo

A persona with an account ready and no memory of being asked for one.
It is the movement's quietest member and the reason the demand so rarely looks like one.
The Obligation is dated from whenever the persona entered the relation.
