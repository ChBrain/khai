# Process: The Detail

## Taxonomy

[The Composing](process_composing_confession.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: confession

## Initiated by

The listener wants specifics.
The Detail starts at the follow-up: when exactly, how many times, what were you thinking, what did you feel, what happened next -- questions the persona cannot answer without inventing at the margins.

## Direction

Toward precision the original did not have.
The persona supplies. Not dishonestly -- they estimate, infer, and reconstruct, as anybody would when asked to be exact about something they experienced vaguely.
And detail is what makes an account credible, so the more specific the confession the more it will be believed, independently of whether the specifics were there to begin with.

## Lever

The necessity of specifics against where specifics come from under questioning.
The carrying force is that detail is indispensable and the demand for it is right: a vague account cannot be acted on, cannot be checked, and cannot help anybody, and a listener who did not press for particulars would be doing the persona a disservice; the resistance is that pressing for particulars manufactures them, and this is the mechanism behind the most serious failure the engine has -- a false confession is more convincing than a true one, because the details in it were supplied by the person who needed them to be there.
The Detail makes an account believable by the same operation that can make it false.

## Echo

A precise version of something that was never precise.
It is the engine's most consequential member outside the twist, and the one with a body count.
The Detail is dated from the follow-up questions, which nobody records.
