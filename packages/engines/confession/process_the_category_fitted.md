# Process: The Category Fitted

## Taxonomy

[The Composing](process_composing_confession.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: confession

## Initiated by

The account is being said in somebody else's vocabulary.
The Category Fitted starts wherever the listener's framework supplies the terms: sin, symptom, trauma, pattern, misconduct, risk factor -- the words available for what happened.

## Direction

Toward an experience that has become an instance of something.
The persona reaches for the words that will be understood, and the words that will be understood are the listener's. So the account arrives already sorted into a kind, and the sorting is what makes it legible and actionable.
Afterwards the persona thinks in those terms too, because those are now the terms they have.

## Lever

The usefulness of a shared vocabulary against what it does to the particular.
The carrying force is that categories are how help gets delivered: nothing can be treated, forgiven, or addressed until it is recognised as a kind of thing, and a persona given a name for what has been happening to them is frequently enormously relieved and better off -- the framework is a gift as often as an imposition; the resistance is that the framework was not built for this persona, so whatever in their experience has no term goes unsaid and then goes missing, and the persona ends up holding a version of their life written in a professional dialect.
The Category Fitted makes the experience usable by making it somebody else's kind of thing.

## Echo

A life described in the language of whoever was listening.
It shows the composing at its most consequential and its least noticed.
The Category Fitted is dated from the first session and hardens with each one.
