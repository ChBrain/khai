# Confession

Confession as process: an account of a self required rather than offered -- not the voluntary opening that deepens a relationship, which is self-disclosure's, and not the withheld fact and the work of keeping it, which is secret's, but telling under a relation of authority where the listener has standing the speaker does not -- across four movements: the demand (a standing obligation that never has to be enforced, a question that cannot be declined at no cost, and a silence that will be read whatever it means), the composing (an account assembled rather than retrieved, filled to the detail the listener requires and fitted to the categories they have, then replacing the material it was made from), the listener (an asymmetry that must be there for the arrangement to work and is its whole cost, an absolution only they can issue, and a record the persona does not hold a copy of), and the relief (an unburdening that is genuine, a transformation that is the operation's actual product, and a return, since nothing about it concludes) -- with the twist that confession makes a requirement feel like a release, so the more relieved the persona feels the less visible the requirement becomes.
