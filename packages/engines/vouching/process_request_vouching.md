# Process: The Request

## Taxonomy

[Vouching](process_vouching.md), the asking and who can be asked.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: vouching

## Initiated by

The persona needs three names and has to think about who.
The Request runs through what is available: [a short list](process_the_short_list.md) fixed long ago, [an unrefusable ask](process_the_unrefusable_ask.md), and [a second choice](process_the_second_choice.md) that says something the persona cannot prevent.

## Direction

Toward a set of names that was determined before the application existed.
Who can be asked is a fact about a life rather than a decision about a job: it is whoever was senior enough, close enough, and well-disposed enough at some point in the past, and it is heavily shaped by where the persona sat, who noticed them, and whether the person who noticed stayed in post.
By the time a form asks for referees, the answer has been settled for years.

## Lever

The fairness of asking for evidence from people who know the work against what determines who those people are.
The carrying force is that referees are the right people to ask and there is no better source: somebody who has watched a persona work for two years knows things no test discovers, the request is ordinary and expected, and a process that consulted nobody who had actually seen the candidate would be worse in every way; the resistance is that the availability of such people is distributed by proximity rather than by merit, so a persona who did excellent work in an unlucky place has fewer names than a mediocre one who sat near a generous professor.
The Request draws on a list nobody assembled deliberately.

## Echo

Three names, chosen from a set of about four.
It is the movement that decides most of the outcome and is treated as a formality.
The Request is dated to the application and was settled years before it.
