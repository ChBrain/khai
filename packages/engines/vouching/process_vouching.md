# Process: Vouching

## Taxonomy

Standing lent to somebody else's claim (no khai file of its own yet).

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: vouching

## Initiated by

One persona puts their own standing behind another's, to a third party who will decide something.
Vouching starts wherever that transfer is made: the reference for a job, the letter for a place, the word to a landlord, the introduction that says this one is fine, the name given as a guarantor.
It is not the request for discretionary help, which is asking's, and not the second judgement offered on a decision, which is advice's. It is a claim about a person, made by somebody who will be worse off if it is wrong.

## Direction

Toward a document with no information in it and a decision that turns on it anyway.
The engine reads four movements: [the request](process_request_vouching.md), the asking and who can be asked; [the writing](process_writing_vouching.md), a text that cannot be negative; [the stake](process_stake_vouching.md), what the voucher has actually put down; and [the reading](process_reading_vouching.md), the twist -- what the receiver takes from it.
The four run once per application and set who can be asked the next time.

## Lever

The protection a floor of positivity gives against what the floor does to the signal.
The carrying force is that the norm is humane and worth keeping: a single bad relationship should not be able to end a career, most people are better than their worst manager would say, a system in which referees spoke freely would be a system in which nobody could ever leave a difficult job, and the convention that a reference is either good or absent protects the weakest applicants most; the resistance is that a channel which cannot carry a negative cannot carry a positive either -- if every letter is warm, warmth is not evidence, and the reader is left grading the handwriting.
Vouching is read on who agreed to give it, never on what it said.

## Echo

A folder of praise, and a decision made on something not in it.
Vouching does not close; it is standing being lent, gathered into one reading -- a request, a writing, a stake, a reading.
The engine's twist is here. **Nothing in a reference is informative except the fact that somebody agreed to give one.** The text is constrained to the positive by law, by custom, and by decency, so it carries almost nothing; what carries is the prior question of who could be asked at all, and that is settled years earlier by proximity, luck, and whose office the persona happened to work near. So the instrument that appears to assess a candidate is mostly reporting the shape of their acquaintance, and the reader who discounts the words and weighs the source is behaving correctly.
It hands off what it is not: the request for discretionary help is asking's; the second judgement on a decision is advice's; the hour in a room is interview's; the credit given to a first-person account is credibility's; the obligation a transfer creates is debt's and the owing composite's; the talk that circulates about a person is gossip's; and a letter staged to **stand for** a theme is authored key, the play's pitch.
The persona is dated from the last person who agreed to speak for them.
