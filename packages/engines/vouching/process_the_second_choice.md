# Process: The Second Choice

## Taxonomy

[The Request](process_request_vouching.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: vouching

## Initiated by

The obvious person is not on the list.
Somebody else is, and the reader will notice.

## Direction

Toward a gap that speaks louder than any of the names present.
A reader who knows a field knows who should be named: the supervisor, the head of the unit, the person whose project it was. When that name is missing and a colleague appears in its place, an inference is available and will be drawn.
The reasons are frequently innocent -- the person is dead, on leave, estranged for unrelated causes, or simply bad at answering email -- and none of that reaches the reader.

## Lever

The reader's entitlement to notice against what the noticing usually gets wrong.
The carrying force is that the inference is reasonable and hard to fault: the pattern of who is named really does carry information, ignoring an obvious absence would be careless, and readers who noticed nothing would be easier to deceive; the resistance is that absence has many causes and only one interpretation, so a persona whose supervisor retired to Portugal is read as a persona whose supervisor would not vouch for them, and there is no way to say otherwise without making it worse.
The Second Choice is read as a substitution whatever it was.

## Echo

A name that is not the expected name.
It shows the engine's information arriving from outside the documents.
The Second Choice is dated to the form and cannot be annotated.
