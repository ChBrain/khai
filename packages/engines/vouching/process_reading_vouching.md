# Process: The Reading

## Taxonomy

[Vouching](process_vouching.md), what the receiver takes from it, and where the engine is seen for one thing.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: vouching

## Initiated by

The letters arrive and somebody has to use them.
The Reading is what they actually do: [discount the text](process_the_discounted_text.md), [weigh the source](process_the_weighed_source.md), and look for [an absent letter](process_the_absent_letter.md).

## Direction

Toward a reader who has correctly concluded that the documents say nothing.
Experienced readers do not grade references. They check that they exist, note who wrote them, scan for the two or three words that carry the code, register the length, and look for the name that should be there and is not.
This is not cynicism. It is the appropriate response to a channel with its bottom removed, and it is what anybody would do after reading two hundred of them.

## Lever

The correctness of discounting the text against what the discounting leaves the decision resting on.
The carrying force is that the reader is right and their method is defensible: the letters genuinely are uninformative, weighing the source is a rational use of the only variance available, and a reader who took the praise at face value would be a worse reader by every measure; the resistance is that once the text is discounted the decision rests on who the candidate could get to write, which is a fact about their acquaintance -- so a procedure designed to bring evidence of the work into the room ends up importing the shape of a network instead.
The Reading rides the engine's keystone knife-edge: nothing in a reference is informative except the fact that somebody agreed to give one.

## Echo

A folder read in ninety seconds, for three facts none of which are sentences.
It shows the three earlier movements complete -- a list fixed years ago, a text that could not be negative, a stake nobody names -- and adds what the receiving end does about it.
This is why the Reading is the hardest of the four to stage and the only one worth closing on: the referees must be sincere, the letters must be warm and true, the reader must be experienced and fair, and the audience must be able to watch a careful process arrive at a decision that none of the documents supported.
A play that stages a referee as sabotaging somebody has replaced the mechanism with a betrayal, and one that stages the reader as lazy has replaced it with a shrug.
The Reading is dated to the shortlisting meeting and takes very little time.
