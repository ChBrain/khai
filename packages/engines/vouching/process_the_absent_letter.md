# Process: The Absent Letter

## Taxonomy

[The Reading](process_reading_vouching.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: vouching

## Initiated by

Somebody who should be here is not here.
The reader notices in about two seconds.

## Direction

Toward the only unambiguous signal the whole instrument produces.
Everything present is positive and therefore uninformative. The single element with any discriminating power is the gap: the supervisor unnamed, the most recent employer missing, the years covered by nobody.
It is the one thing a candidate cannot manufacture, which is exactly why it carries, and it is also the one thing they cannot explain, since annotating an absence draws attention to it.

## Lever

The genuine information in an absence against how many innocent things produce one.
The carrying force is that the inference is sound in aggregate and readers would be negligent to ignore it: people who cannot get their last manager to write for them are, on average, different from people who can, the signal is uncompellable, and it is the closest thing to a negative the system permits; the resistance is that the base rate of innocent explanations is high -- illness, death, estrangement, a company dissolved, a manager who does not answer email -- and the reader cannot tell those from the other kind, so the one informative element of the process is also its least reliable.
The Absent Letter is the only signal available and cannot be interpreted.

## Echo

A gap doing the work of a paragraph.
It shows the loop closing, since who is missing from the list is decided by the same years that made the list.
The Absent Letter is dated to whatever happened three jobs ago.
