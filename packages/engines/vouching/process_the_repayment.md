# Process: The Repayment

## Taxonomy

[The Stake](process_stake_vouching.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: vouching

## Initiated by

Something has been given and nothing was asked for.
Both parties feel the difference afterwards.

## Direction

Toward an obligation that is real, unstated, and unenforceable.
The persona who was vouched for owes something. Not money and not a favour named in advance: an availability, a willingness to be asked, a reluctance to be the person who embarrasses the referee later.
The referee will never mention it and would be offended by the suggestion that they were owed anything, which is exactly the form the obligation takes.

## Lever

The genuine freeness of the gift against the debt it nonetheless creates.
The carrying force is that the referee means it as a gift and is not calculating: they wrote it because it was the right thing, they expect nothing, most of them will never ask for anything, and treating the exchange as a transaction would insult somebody who was being decent; the resistance is that the persona holds it as a debt regardless, so a career accumulates a set of quiet obligations to people who would deny holding any claim -- and the obligation shapes what the persona will say about them and to whom.
The Repayment is owed to somebody who is not collecting.
The obligation a transfer creates is debt's and the owing composite's; this reads only its arrival.

## Echo

A debt on one side of a ledger only.
It shows what the persona is carrying out of the transaction.
The Repayment is dated to the letter and does not expire.
