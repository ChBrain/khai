# Vouching: Reference

## Line of Work

Vouching as **process**: standing lent to somebody else's claim, and what a channel that cannot carry a
negative does to everybody using it.

The engine's subject is an instrument that every institution requires and no experienced reader trusts.
The measured starting point is blunt: unstructured references are among the weakest predictors in
personnel selection, and the reason is not that referees lie but that the range has had its bottom
removed. Almost every reference is positive, by law in some jurisdictions and by decency everywhere, so
a document that appears to grade a candidate cannot distinguish between the candidate everybody wanted
and the candidate the referee could not bring themselves to refuse. Once that compression is taken
seriously, the interesting questions move outward from the text: to who could be asked at all, to what
the referee has staked, and to what a reader does with a folder in which everything is warm.

The boundary with what khai already carries is clean. The request for discretionary help put to somebody
free to refuse is **asking**'s; this engine reads a request for a claim rather than for an act. The
second judgement offered on a decision is **advice**'s, and this is a judgement offered about a person
to somebody else. The hour in a room is **interview**'s, which shares this engine's readers and not its
mechanism. The credit given to a first-person account is **credibility**'s. The obligation a transfer
creates is **debt**'s and the **owing** composite's, and the talk that circulates about a person is
**gossip**'s.

**The request** draws on a list nobody assembled deliberately. Being vouchable requires that somebody
senior watched closely, formed a view, and stayed in post, which is distributed by seating plans and
staff turnover. **The unrefusable ask** converts a reservation into a letter, since declining is a
verdict nobody wants to deliver. **The second choice** is read as a substitution whatever produced it.

**The writing** produces warmth that cannot be graded. **The positive floor** protects the candidate by
disabling the instrument, which is a genuine trade rather than a failure. **The code** restores the
signal -- diligent and conscientious doing the work of a reservation, brilliant and original doing
something else -- and studies of large sets of letters find those registers distributed unevenly across
groups of candidates rather than across records. **The length** is the only uncompellable component and
is confounded by everything about the writer.

**The stake** is a loan of reputation. **The borrowed standing** values the candidate at the referee's
rate, which is the mechanism that makes the practice work at all. **The collateral** is real,
uncollected, and never itemised. **The repayment** is owed to somebody who would deny being owed
anything.

Hence the **twist**, at the root. **Nothing in a reference is informative except the fact that somebody
agreed to give one.** The text is constrained to the positive and carries almost nothing; what carries
is the prior question of who could be asked, and that was settled years earlier. So the instrument that
appears to assess a candidate is mostly reporting the shape of their acquaintance, and the reader who
discounts the words and weighs the source is behaving correctly.

The engine is built so that nobody has to be sabotaging anybody for it to run, and a play should keep it
that way. The referees were sincere. The letters were warm and true. The reader was experienced and
fair.

**On the evidence, stated plainly.** This engine has one firm measured leg, one striking narrow one, and
a good deal of structural reasoning, and they should not be read at the same weight. **The low validity
of unstructured references is well established.** Decades of selection research put them near the bottom
of the predictive table, and the uniform positivity that explains it is not in dispute; the writing
movement's central claim is the engine's solid ground. **The language findings are measured and narrow.**
The best-known study of differential wording examined a few hundred letters for medical faculty at one
American institution, and later work in other fields has found the pattern sometimes and not always. It
is a real and important finding, it is not a universal law of letters, and this engine states it as a
tendency rather than as a rate. **The stake movement is structural reasoning, not measurement.** That a
referee's reputation is genuine collateral, that this is why written letters are uniformly warm while
telephone calls are candid, and that the exposure disciplines the strongest vouches are all plausible
accounts of a practice everybody recognises, and nobody has quantified any of them. **The absent-letter
claim is the engine's own inference.** That the gap is the only discriminating element, and that readers
use it, follows from everything above and from what selectors say informally; it has not been studied,
and the same member concedes that the base rate of innocent absences is high enough to make it
unreliable in any individual case. **The engine is written from a professional-appointment setting.**
Academic, medical, and managerial hiring is where the literature is; tenancy references, character
references in court, immigration sponsorship, and the informal word to a friend all work differently in
ways this engine does not model. What should not be taken from this engine is that referees are
dishonest, that references should be abolished, or that a warm letter meant nothing to the person who
wrote it. The claim is narrow: the floor removes the range, the range removes the signal, and what is
left is a fact about whose acquaintance the candidate happens to be.

## Restrictions

What the engine refuses to model, and to whom it delegates.

- **The request for help (asking, gift, debt, owing)**: A request for discretionary help, the transfer
  that binds by naming no price, and the obligation it creates are those packages'. The repayment
  member names the obligation's arrival and hands the mechanism back.
- **The second judgement on a decision (advice, expertise)**: A judgement offered on somebody's own
  decision, and whether the person offering it knows anything, are those engines'. Vouching is a claim
  made about a person to a third party who will act on it.
- **The hour in a room (interview, presentation, face)**: The selection encounter, the front a candidate
  manages, and what a face costs are those engines'. This engine shares interview's readers and none of
  its mechanism.
- **The credit given to an account (credibility, heeding, recognition)**: What makes a first-person
  report believed, what a fair hearing buys, and what institutional acknowledgement does are those
  packages'. A vouch is a third party's claim, which is a different instrument.
- **The talk that circulates (gossip, grapevine, reputation, stigma)**: How information about a person
  moves informally, and what a mark does once attached, are those engines'. A reference is a document
  requested by an institution, and its informality is exactly what it lacks.
- **The law of it (law, document, the world)**: Defamation, qualified privilege, data protection, the
  right to see one's own file, and the statutory limits on what an employer may say are matters of law
  and practice. They set the format; this engine reads what the format does to the signal.
- **A letter as authored symbol (the play's pitch)**: A reference staged in order to **stand for** a
  theme -- the testimonial that mirrors a motif of loyalty -- is refused: that is authored key, the
  play's pitch. Vouching sets what was lent and what was read; the meaning is the play's.

## Encoding

Source to constraint. A process tree: one root, four movements, twelve forms.

- **[vouching](process_vouching.md)** (the root): standing lent to somebody else's claim, read on who
  agreed to give it, carrying the twist -- nothing in a reference is informative except the fact that
  somebody agreed to give one. Anchored by Aamodt (1993).
- **[the request](process_request_vouching.md)**: the asking and who can be asked. Anchored by Coleman
  (1988). [the short list](process_the_short_list.md), [the unrefusable ask](process_the_unrefusable_ask.md),
  [the second choice](process_the_second_choice.md).
- **[the writing](process_writing_vouching.md)**: a text that cannot be negative. Anchored by Aamodt
  (1993) and Trix & Psenka (2003). [the positive floor](process_the_positive_floor.md),
  [the code](process_the_code.md), [the length](process_the_length.md).
- **[the stake](process_stake_vouching.md)**: what the voucher has actually put down. Anchored by
  Coleman (1988). [the borrowed standing](process_the_borrowed_standing.md),
  [the collateral](process_the_collateral.md), [the repayment](process_the_repayment.md).
- **[the reading](process_reading_vouching.md)**: what the receiver takes from it, and where the engine
  is seen for one thing. Anchored by Nicklin & Roch (2009) and Highhouse (2008).
  [the discounted text](process_the_discounted_text.md), [the weighed source](process_the_weighed_source.md),
  [the absent letter](process_the_absent_letter.md).
