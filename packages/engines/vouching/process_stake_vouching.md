# Process: The Stake

## Taxonomy

[Vouching](process_vouching.md), what the voucher has actually put down.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: vouching

## Initiated by

The referee has attached their name to somebody else's future conduct.
The Stake is what that costs: [borrowed standing](process_the_borrowed_standing.md) transferred to the candidate, [collateral](process_the_collateral.md) that can be lost, and [a repayment](process_the_repayment.md) nobody names.

## Direction

Toward a transaction in reputation that neither party calls one.
What crosses is not information but credit. The reader who does not know the candidate does know, or can look up, the referee, and the vouch works by lending the candidate a portion of that standing for the duration of the decision.
If the appointment goes badly, the loan is called: the referee is the person who said this would be fine, and it was not.

## Lever

The generosity of lending standing against the fact that it is genuinely at risk.
The carrying force is that referees do this willingly and for good reasons: it costs nothing in the ordinary case, it is how anybody gets a first chance at anything, the profession runs on it, and refusing to lend would make a persona useless to everybody they have ever supervised; the resistance is that the loan is real and occasionally called, so the referee is quietly rationing -- writing warmly for everybody and pressing hard only for the ones they would defend afterwards.
The Stake is what makes the strongest vouches rare and the warm ones universal.

## Echo

A reputation on loan, at a small and non-zero risk.
It is the movement that explains why the letters are all the same and the phone calls are not.
The Stake is dated to the signature and is settled if anything goes wrong.
