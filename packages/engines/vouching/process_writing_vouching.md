# Process: The Writing

## Taxonomy

[Vouching](process_vouching.md), a text that cannot be negative.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: vouching

## Initiated by

The referee sits down to write and has one register available.
The Writing is what that produces: [a positive floor](process_the_positive_floor.md) below which nothing may be said, [a code](process_the_code.md) that has to carry everything else, and [a length](process_the_length.md) that is read before the words are.

## Direction

Toward a document whose content is fixed before the writer knows the candidate.
Almost every reference is warm. It is warm when the referee is enthusiastic, warm when they are indifferent, and warm when they have reservations they will never state, because the alternatives are a lawsuit, a scene, or the destruction of somebody's year.
So the variance the reader is looking for has to live somewhere other than the sentiment, and both parties know it.

## Lever

The necessity of the convention against the emptiness it produces.
The carrying force is that the floor is load-bearing and removing it would be worse: candidly negative references would silence referees entirely, expose them to legal risk, and let one bad relationship follow a persona for a decade, and every jurisdiction that has thought about it has landed on some version of this protection; the resistance is that a channel with a floor and no ceiling compresses every candidate into the same band, so the letters are indistinguishable and the reader is reduced to counting adjectives.
The Writing produces warmth that cannot be graded.

## Echo

Four letters that say the same thing about four different people.
It is the movement everybody believes carries the assessment.
The Writing is dated to a fortnight before the deadline and takes twenty minutes.
