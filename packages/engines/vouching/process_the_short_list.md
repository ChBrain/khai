# Process: The Short List

## Taxonomy

[The Request](process_request_vouching.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: vouching

## Initiated by

There are not many people the persona could plausibly ask.
Three or four, and one of them has retired.

## Direction

Toward a pool set by seating plans and staff turnover.
Being vouchable requires somebody senior to have observed the work closely and to have formed a view worth reporting, which requires proximity, time, and their continued presence. None of those is distributed by how good anybody is.
A persona who worked remotely, whose manager left, whose department reorganised twice, or who was excellent in a team of two has a list that looks thin for reasons that have nothing to do with them.

## Lever

The genuine value of close observation against how it happens to be allocated.
The carrying force is that closeness is what makes a voucher worth anything: a referee who barely knew the persona has nothing to say, the whole instrument depends on somebody having actually watched, and there is no way to manufacture that after the fact; the resistance is that closeness accumulates by circumstance -- who sat where, who stayed, who took an interest -- so the list is a record of a persona's luck in institutions as much as of their work in them.
The Short List is a network property presented as a personal one.

## Echo

A set of available names, thin or thick by accident.
It shows what the instrument is actually sampling.
The Short List is dated across a career and is discovered when a form asks.
