# Process: The Unrefusable Ask

## Taxonomy

[The Request](process_request_vouching.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: vouching

## Initiated by

Somebody is asked to be a referee and does not want to be.
And says yes.

## Direction

Toward agreement given to avoid the meaning of refusal.
Declining is not neutral. A former manager who says they would rather not has told the persona something devastating, will be asked why, and will have to either say it or lie about it -- so the ordinary course is to agree and then write carefully.
The persona, meanwhile, cannot distinguish an enthusiastic yes from an evasive one, because both are the word yes.

## Lever

The kindness of not refusing against what the kindness puts into the channel.
The carrying force is that saying yes is the decent thing and refusing would be a punishment out of all proportion: nobody should have their livelihood damaged by a colleague's private reservation, the referee genuinely does not want to harm them, and a norm permitting easy refusal would make asking impossible for everybody; the resistance is that the reluctant yes and the willing yes are identical from outside, so the one moment at which real information could enter the process is the moment everybody has good reasons to suppress.
The Unrefusable Ask converts a reservation into a letter.

## Echo

A referee who agreed and did not want to.
It shows where the negative goes when it cannot be said.
The Unrefusable Ask is dated to the email and is never described to anybody.
