# Process: The Weighed Source

## Taxonomy

[The Reading](process_reading_vouching.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: vouching

## Initiated by

The reader looks at who signed it.
And that is most of what they take.

## Direction

Toward an assessment of the referee conducted in the name of assessing the candidate.
Is this somebody whose judgement the reader trusts, has heard of, shares a field with, or has been right before? A warm letter from a known and discriminating referee is worth a great deal; the same words from a stranger are worth almost nothing.
The reader is not confusing the two people. They are using the only variance the channel permits, which happens to sit on the writer.

## Lever

The rationality of weighing the source against what it does to a candidate who has no famous friends.
The carrying force is that source-weighing is correct Bayesian practice and is not snobbery: a referee's track record is real evidence, knowing what a particular person means by excellent is genuinely informative, and pretending all sources are equal would throw away the only usable signal present; the resistance is that the weight attaches to the candidate, so the same work is credited differently according to who observed it -- and the persona cannot fix this by being better at their job.
The Weighed Source assesses the referee and scores the candidate.

## Echo

A judgement about one person, recorded against another.
It shows why the request movement decided most of the outcome.
The Weighed Source is dated to the letterhead.
