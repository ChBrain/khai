# Process: The Discounted Text

## Taxonomy

[The Reading](process_reading_vouching.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: vouching

## Initiated by

The reader does not really read it.
They scan it, which is different, and which is the right response.

## Direction

Toward praise treated as a formality by everybody except the person it is about.
The candidate believes the letter matters, sometimes agonises about who to ask, and often never sees it. The reader assumes before opening it that it will be positive and is looking for the exceptions to that assumption rather than for its content.
So the two parties hold completely different theories of the document, and neither is corrected.

## Lever

The reader's accurate calibration against the candidate's reasonable belief.
The carrying force is that the reader's calibration is earned and correct: after enough letters the base rate is obvious, treating warm praise as informative would be an error, and a selection process run by somebody who believed every reference would be trivially gamed; the resistance is that the candidate is operating on the official description of the instrument, in which the letter is evidence, and nobody has any incentive to tell them otherwise.
The Discounted Text is taken seriously by exactly one of the three parties.

## Echo

A document that matters intensely at one end and not at the other.
It shows the asymmetry that keeps the practice running.
The Discounted Text is dated to the scan and is never discussed with the candidate.
