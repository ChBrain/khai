# Process: The Borrowed Standing

## Taxonomy

[The Stake](process_stake_vouching.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: vouching

## Initiated by

The candidate arrives carrying somebody else's credit.
The reader has never met them and has heard of the referee.

## Direction

Toward an assessment of a stranger conducted through a known quantity.
This is the mechanism that makes vouching work at all. A reader cannot evaluate an unknown person and can evaluate a claim by a known one, so the reference converts an impossible judgement into a possible one -- which is a genuine service and the reason the practice exists.
It also means the candidate's apparent quality varies with the standing of whoever agreed to speak for them, holding the candidate constant.

## Lever

The usefulness of the conversion against what it makes the candidate depend on.
The carrying force is that the conversion is the only route available and it is not corrupt: judging strangers is the hardest problem in any selection, borrowing the judgement of somebody with a track record is what a sensible reader should do, and the referee's own accumulated standing is real evidence about their judgement; the resistance is that the borrowing scales with the lender, so two identical candidates are differently credible according to whose name is on the letter, and the difference is invisible to both of them.
The Borrowed Standing values the candidate at the referee's rate.

## Echo

A stranger assessed at somebody else's price.
It shows the mechanism that makes the whole practice function.
The Borrowed Standing is dated to the reading and is spent immediately.
