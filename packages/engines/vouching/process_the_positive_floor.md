# Process: The Positive Floor

## Taxonomy

[The Writing](process_writing_vouching.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: vouching

## Initiated by

Nothing below good may be written.
Not by law in every case, and by practice everywhere.

## Direction

Toward a scale with its bottom removed.
The referee has the range from good to extraordinary and nothing else. A reservation cannot be stated, a serious concern cannot be stated, and even a mild qualification reads, against the surrounding warmth, as a denunciation.
This is why the practice of ringing the referee persists in fields where it is possible: a voice can carry a pause where a letter cannot carry a sentence.

## Lever

The protection the floor gives the candidate against what it removes from the reader.
The carrying force is that the protection is the point and is doing real work: careers survive difficult managers because of it, personas recovering from a bad period are not permanently marked, and a referee who cannot damage anybody is a referee who can be asked by anybody; the resistance is that the same floor makes the instrument non-discriminating, so the decision it was meant to inform gets made on the interview, the network, or a phone call -- all of which are less accountable than the letter.
The Positive Floor protects the candidate by disabling the instrument.

## Echo

A range with one end missing.
It shows why the document cannot carry what everybody expects of it.
The Positive Floor is dated to the convention and is enforced by everybody at once.
