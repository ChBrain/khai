# Process: The Length

## Taxonomy

[The Writing](process_writing_vouching.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: vouching

## Initiated by

The letter is three paragraphs, or it is one.
The reader can see which before reading a word.

## Direction

Toward a signal that is read at a glance and is mostly about the writer.
A short letter is a bad letter regardless of what it says, because effort is the one thing the convention cannot compel: a referee obliged to be positive is not obliged to be lengthy, so the time spent is the only unfaked component.
It is also the component most affected by everything that has nothing to do with the candidate -- how busy the referee was that week, how many of these they write, whether they are the sort of person who writes at length about everybody.

## Lever

The honesty of effort as a cue against how noisy that particular cue is.
The carrying force is that reading length is a sensible response to a compressed channel: it is genuinely uncompellable, it correlates with enthusiasm often enough to be worth something, and a reader with nothing else to go on is right to use it; the resistance is that it is confounded by seniority, workload, discipline, and personal style, so a persona with a generous but terse referee is disadvantaged against one with a verbose indifferent one.
The Length is the only uncompellable part and is mostly noise.

## Echo

A page count doing the work of an assessment.
It shows what a reader falls back on when the text has been flattened.
The Length is dated to whatever else the referee had on that day.
