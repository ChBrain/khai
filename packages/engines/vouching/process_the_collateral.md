# Process: The Collateral

## Taxonomy

[The Stake](process_stake_vouching.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: vouching

## Initiated by

If it goes wrong, the referee is implicated.
Not formally, and unmistakably.

## Direction

Toward a risk carried quietly by the person who wrote the letter.
Nobody sends a bill, and the accounting happens anyway: a referee whose recommendations keep failing is asked less, believed less, and privately reclassified as somebody whose word does not mean much. A referee whose recommendations succeed becomes a person whose call is worth taking.
This is the closest thing to a validity check the system has, and it operates on the writer rather than the writing.

## Lever

The discipline the risk imposes against how weakly it is transmitted.
The carrying force is that the exposure keeps the practice honest and does real work: a referee with something to lose is a referee who thinks before pressing hard, the strongest vouches come from people who have accepted that exposure, and the accumulated record of a good referee is genuinely informative; the resistance is that the feedback is slow, private, and almost never reaches the referee explicitly, so the discipline operates as a vague caution rather than as any kind of correction.
The Collateral is real, uncollected, and never itemised.

## Echo

A quiet ledger nobody maintains and everybody keeps.
It shows why referees hedge in writing and speak plainly on the phone.
The Collateral is dated to the appointment and is assessed years later.
