# Process: Deliberate Practice

## Taxonomy

[The Making](process_making_expertise.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: expertise

## Initiated by

The persona is working at something they cannot yet do.
Deliberate Practice starts at the edge of the current capacity: a task chosen because it is currently beyond reach, attempted with full attention, repeated with correction.

## Direction

Toward improvement that ordinary doing does not produce.
It is effortful, unpleasant, and cannot be sustained for long stretches -- which is itself a signature, since anything that feels comfortable is almost certainly not it.
And it is specific: the improvement stays close to what was practised and travels much less far than anybody hopes.

## Lever

The effectiveness of practising what one cannot do against the discomfort of doing so.
The carrying force is that this is the best-supported prescription in the whole engine: work at the edge with correction improves performance where accumulated experience does not, across every domain it has been examined in, and the finding is not in dispute; the resistance is that it is aversive by construction and competes directly with doing the job -- so the persona who most needs it is generally the one with least room for it, and an organisation that wanted it would have to pay for hours that produce nothing today.
Deliberate Practice is what works and is not what anybody's schedule contains.

## Echo

Hours that improved something, at a rate nobody enjoys.
It is the movement's engine and the thing most often confused with experience.
Deliberate Practice is dated from the last session that was genuinely hard.
