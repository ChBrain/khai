# Process: The Plateau

## Taxonomy

[The Making](process_making_expertise.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: expertise

## Initiated by

The persona has got good enough that it no longer takes effort.
The Plateau starts at automaticity: the skill runs without attention, the performance is reliable, and the struggle is over.

## Direction

Toward stability, and the end of improvement.
Once a capacity has been compiled below the level of attention, further doing exercises it and does not develop it -- so performance holds steady for years while experience accumulates behind it.
The persona feels themselves getting better, because they feel themselves getting easier, and those are different things.

## Lever

The value of an effortless competence against what effortlessness costs.
The carrying force is that automaticity is the goal and not a trap: it frees attention for everything the task sits inside, makes performance robust under load and interruption, and a persona permanently at the edge of their ability could not hold a job; the resistance is that the same compilation ends the learning, so the ordinary reward for becoming good is arriving at a level one will occupy indefinitely.
The Plateau is where twenty years of experience becomes two years repeated ten times.

## Echo

A skill that works, held still.
It is the movement's unwelcome finding and the reason seniority and skill come apart so often.
The Plateau is dated from whenever it stopped being hard.
