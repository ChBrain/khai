# Process: The Apprenticeship

## Taxonomy

[The Tacit](process_tacit.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: expertise

## Initiated by

The persona is near somebody who can do it.
The Apprenticeship starts at proximity rather than instruction: standing in the workshop, on the ward, in the studio, doing the easy real parts of the real work while watching the rest.

## Direction

Toward competence acquired by participation.
The newcomer starts at the edge of the practice on tasks that are peripheral but genuine, and moves inward as they become able -- learning the judgement, the standards, and the vocabulary as a by-product of taking part rather than as a curriculum.
What is transmitted is not a syllabus. It is a way of seeing, and membership.

## Lever

The completeness of what proximity transmits against how badly it scales.
The carrying force is that this genuinely works and works for the tacit part specifically, which nothing else does: the apprentice acquires what the master cannot state, by watching them do it in real conditions, and every field that requires unspeakable judgement reinvents this arrangement independently; the resistance is that it is slow, expensive, dependent on the particular master's quality, capped by their availability, and invisible to any audit -- so it is the first thing cut and the last thing that can be replaced.
The Apprenticeship transmits exactly what no course can and nothing else about it is efficient.

## Echo

Knowledge that moved because two people were in the same room.
It is the movement's practical answer, and it is an answer that cannot be scaled or written down.
The Apprenticeship is dated from the first real task the newcomer was allowed to do.
