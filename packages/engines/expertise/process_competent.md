# Process: The Competent

## Taxonomy

[The Stages](process_stages.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: expertise

## Initiated by

There is too much to attend to, and the persona has to choose what matters.
The Competent starts where accumulated aspects and rules exceed what can be held at once, so the persona must adopt a plan -- select a perspective, decide what this case is about, and let the plan determine what to notice.

## Direction

Toward performance organised by a chosen goal.
This is the stage at which the persona becomes responsible in a way they were not before: the plan is theirs, it was not given, and outcomes now attach to their choice of it rather than to whether they followed instructions.
Performance improves markedly and so does the stress.

## Lever

The necessity of choosing a frame against having to own the choice.
The carrying force is that planning is the only way through the volume: a persona who attends to everything attends to nothing, and adopting a perspective is what makes a complex situation tractable at all -- competence is a genuine achievement and most working professionals are here; the resistance is that the perspective is now the persona's, so a bad outcome is theirs in a way it never was at the earlier stages, and this is where the emotional involvement that later fuels expertise first becomes painful.
The Competent is the stage where success and failure both start to belong to somebody.

## Echo

A performance with an author.
It is the stage most training programmes are designed to reach, and the one most careers stop at.
The Competent is dated from the first case the persona planned rather than processed.
