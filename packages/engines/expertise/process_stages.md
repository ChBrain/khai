# Process: The Stages

## Taxonomy

[Expertise](process_expertise.md), the shape of the climb.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: expertise

## Initiated by

The persona is somewhere on a road with a known shape.
The Stages starts wherever the Dreyfus model applies: skill acquisition as a progression through qualitatively different ways of engaging a task, not as an accumulation of more of the same.
[Novice](process_novice.md), [advanced beginner](process_advanced_beginner.md), [competent](process_competent.md), [proficient](process_proficient.md), [expert](process_expert_stage.md) -- and what changes across them is not speed but what the persona is looking at.

## Direction

Toward perception replacing calculation.
Early on the persona applies rules to features they have been taught to notice, and the rules are context-free because they have no context yet.
Later the situation presents itself as a whole with a salient feature already foregrounded, and the response comes with it. The climb is from following to seeing.

## Lever

The reliability of rules against the reach of perception.
The carrying force is that rules are exactly right for a beginner and should not be sneered at: they are transmissible, checkable, and they get somebody safely through work they cannot yet read, which is why every field teaches them; the resistance is that rules cannot cover the cases that matter, so a persona who never leaves them stays permanently at the level where the exception defeats them -- and the transition out is not more rules but exposure to enough real situations to start seeing.
The Stages is a ladder whose rungs are made of a different material at the top than at the bottom.

## Echo

A persona who is somewhere, and mostly cannot tell where.
It is the movement that gives a play its ranks, and the one that explains why two people doing the same job are not doing the same job.
What it hands upward is the shape the other two movements act on: how the climb is made, and what is lost in making it.
The Stages is dated from the first case the persona met that the rules did not cover.
