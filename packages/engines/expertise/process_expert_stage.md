# Process: The Expert

## Taxonomy

[The Stages](process_stages.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: expertise

## Initiated by

The persona does the right thing without having decided to.
The Expert starts where the split closes: situation and response arrive together, so there is no perceptible interval in which anything was chosen.

## Direction

Toward performance that is neither rule-following nor problem-solving.
The expert does not consider options, because options do not present themselves; what presents is what to do. Asked afterwards, they will supply reasons, and the reasons will be reconstructions -- frequently good ones, and not the process.
Under time pressure this is the only kind of performance that works.

## Lever

The correctness of unconsidered action against the fact that nothing was considered.
The carrying force is that this is genuine and superior, not mystical: the expert's response is the compressed residue of thousands of real cases, it outperforms deliberation in the domain it was built in, and slowing them down to explain reliably makes them worse; the resistance is that a performance with no deliberation in it also has no examination in it, so an expert operating outside the conditions their intuition was built for will be exactly as fast, exactly as confident, and wrong.
The Expert cannot tell from inside which of those two they are doing.

## Echo

The right move, arriving with no history attached.
It is the engine's destination and the source of its twist, since the account went where the deliberation went.
The Expert is dated from whenever the pause disappeared, which nobody notices at the time.
