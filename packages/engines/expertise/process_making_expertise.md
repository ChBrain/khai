# Process: The Making

## Taxonomy

[Expertise](process_expertise.md), what builds it and what does not.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: expertise

## Initiated by

The persona is putting in time, and the time is or is not doing anything.
The Making starts at the difference between practice and repetition: [deliberate practice](process_deliberate_practice.md) at the edge of the current capacity, with [feedback](process_feedback_loop.md) close enough to correct on, against the [plateau](process_plateau.md) where a skill becomes automatic and stops improving.
And it starts at a prior question: whether the [environment](process_low_validity.md) is one that can teach at all.

## Direction

Toward improvement that experience alone does not deliver.
The Making's central and unwelcome finding is that time on task is a poor predictor of skill. What predicts it is effortful work just beyond the current level, with error made visible fast enough to act on -- and most of what people do in their working lives is not that.
So twenty years of experience is frequently two years of learning followed by eighteen of doing.

## Lever

The comfort of competence against the discomfort that improvement requires.
The carrying force is that automaticity is the point and not a failure: a persona whose skill has become effortless can attend to something else, handle load, and act under pressure, and a life spent permanently at the edge of one's ability would be unbearable and unproductive; the resistance is that the same automaticity ends the improvement, so getting good enough to stop struggling is exactly the condition under which further experience teaches nothing.
The Making is where the reward for having learned is the end of learning.

## Echo

Skill that grew, or hours that accumulated.
It shows why the stages are not walked by everybody who stays long enough, and why some fields produce expertise and others only produce confidence.
The Making is dated from the last time the persona was working at something they could not yet do.
