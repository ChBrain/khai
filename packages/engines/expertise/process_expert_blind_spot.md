# Process: The Blind Spot

## Taxonomy

[The Tacit](process_tacit.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: expertise

## Initiated by

The expert is explaining, and leaving out most of it.
The Blind Spot starts wherever somebody who is genuinely good tries to teach: they name the goal, skip the steps that have become invisible to them, and misjudge how hard the task is for somebody who cannot yet see what they see.

## Direction

Toward instruction that is accurate and unusable.
The expert omits a large share of the steps a novice needs, underestimates the time required, and remembers their own learning as more direct than it was -- because the compiled version is the only one they still have access to.
They are not simplifying. They are reporting what is there.

## Lever

The expert's genuine willingness to help against their genuine inability to see what is missing.
The carrying force is that nothing here is bad faith and the diagnosis matters: experts overwhelmingly want to teach, believe they are being thorough, and are giving an honest account of their own experience -- treating this as gatekeeping is both unkind and wrong; the resistance is that the account is unavailable to introspection, so the expert cannot correct it by trying harder, being more patient, or being asked better questions, and the only reliable repairs come from outside them -- watching the novice fail, being contradicted by a record, or teaching alongside somebody nearer the beginning.
The Blind Spot is the engine's twist arriving as a scene.

## Echo

A lesson that was true and did not work.
It shows what the tacit movement costs at the moment it costs it, which is the moment somebody tries to pass it on.
The Blind Spot is dated from the same day the pause disappeared, and it is discovered years later by somebody else.
