# Instructions: Expertise

How a Playwright wires the expertise engine into a play. You wire by linking the play's own content to the engine's files; the engine files stay untouched, they are canon. The Roadie plumbs the engine's law into Instructions Knowledge on deploy. Authoring guidance, not runtime content, and it does not go on tour.

## Human

- The human places each persona on the stages, and remembers that two people doing the same job may not be doing the same job. Before making anybody an authority, decide whether their domain is one that could have taught them -- regular enough to be learnable, with feedback prompt enough to learn from -- because in a domain that fails those conditions, experience produces confidence and nothing else. And read expertise on what a persona can do, never on what they say they do.

## Agent

- From the play's own content, a persona links under Projection the stage they are at, and where the play turns on whether they are any good, whether their environment is high or low validity. Expect the stages to be ordered and the other two movements not to be: a persona can be plateaued while merely competent, or blind-spotted while only proficient. Keep it the acquisition: the response grooved by repetition is habit's and ingraining's, the theory of where ability comes from is mindset's, and influence held because one is believed to know is power's. You link to the engine, you never edit it.

## Collaboration

- The response grooved by repetition into a stable context is the habit engine's and the ingraining composite's; the held theory of where ability comes from is mindset's; belief in one's own capability is self-efficacy's; absorption in a matched challenge is flow's; persistence toward a long goal is grit's and achievement-motive's; expert power over another is power's; the expert's count of a hazard is risk's; the internalisation of a social world is socialization's. Whether a production's persona is genuinely proficient rather than competent, and whether their environment is high or low validity, are the team's review.

## Knowledge

- Expertise is a capacity moving out of deliberation and into the hands: the persona stops working through the rules and starts seeing what the situation requires, and the seeing arrives ahead of any account of it. It is a transformation rather than an accumulation -- the expert is not doing the same thing faster, they are doing a different thing. It wires on a persona at Projection and runs in three movements. The stages are Dreyfus's five, and what changes across them is what the persona attends to: the novice applies context-free rules to taught features, correctly and blindly, and is protected by the rule from everything except the case it does not fit; the advanced beginner starts using situational aspects they can recognise and cannot state, so being asked to justify a judgement is a demotion; the competent must adopt a plan because the accumulated aspects exceed what can be held at once, and in choosing it acquires ownership of the outcome; the proficient reads the case whole and immediately and still has to reason out the response, which is slower than either neighbouring stage; and the expert has closed the split, so situation and response arrive together and the reasons offered afterwards are reconstructions. The making carries the unwelcome findings: deliberate practice at the edge of the current capacity improves performance where accumulated experience does not; feedback must be close and specific enough to correct on, or practice consolidates whatever was being done; the plateau arrives when a capacity is compiled below attention and stops developing, so twenty years of experience becomes two repeated ten times and the persona feels themselves improving because they feel themselves getting easier; and the low-validity environment is the bound -- where the domain lacks regularity and prompt feedback, practitioners grow more confident and no more accurate, and cannot tell from inside which kind of domain they are in. The tacit is what cannot be stated: apprenticeship transmits it by proximity on real work and scales terribly, and the blind spot opens where a competence has gone under. The engine's twist is at the root: becoming an expert destroys the ability to teach it, because the mechanism producing the competence is the mechanism removing it from view -- so the better a persona is, the worse their account, and nobody is withholding.

## System

- Do place personas on the stages and let the difference show in what they notice rather than in how fast they work.
- Do stage the proficient as the awkward one: they see what is happening immediately and still have to argue themselves to a decision, which is slower and less comfortable than either neighbouring stage.
- Do let the expert's reasons be reconstructions. They will be offered confidently, will often be good, and will not be the process -- and slowing an expert down to explain reliably makes them worse.
- Do stage the blind spot as structural. The expert wants to teach, believes they are being thorough, and is giving an honest account of what they can still see; a play that stages them as guarding their knowledge has replaced the mechanism with a motive.
- Do ask what environment the persona practised in before granting them authority, and remember the sense of expertise feels identical whether or not the domain could teach.
- Do let automaticity be a good thing before it is a cost: the plateau is the reward for having learned, and a persona permanently at the edge of their ability could not hold a job.
- Do not model the grooved response (habit, ingraining), the theory of ability (mindset), belief in capability (self-efficacy), absorption (flow), persistence (grit), influence held for being believed to know (power), or the internalisation of a social world (socialization). Do not make the mastery stand for a theme; that is authored key, the play's pitch. Do not edit the engine's files; wire only from the play's side.
