# Process: The Feedback Loop

## Taxonomy

[The Making](process_making_expertise.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: expertise

## Initiated by

The persona finds out whether they were right.
The Feedback Loop starts wherever error becomes visible soon enough and specifically enough to act on -- the shot that misses, the note that is flat, the diagnosis confirmed the same afternoon.

## Direction

Toward correction, which is the only mechanism by which practice teaches.
Practice without feedback does not improve performance; it consolidates whatever was being done, correct or not.
And the loop's properties matter more than its presence: fast beats slow, specific beats general, and outcome feedback with no information about cause teaches remarkably little.

## Lever

The indispensability of the loop against how rarely working conditions supply one.
The carrying force is that where a good loop exists, expertise develops almost automatically and does not require anybody to be exceptional -- chess, surgery, firefighting, and instrument-playing all have this in common; the resistance is that most consequential work supplies feedback that is slow, noisy, confounded, or absent, so the persona is repeating without correcting and has no way to notice that they are.
The Feedback Loop is the difference between practice and rehearsal of an error.

## Echo

A performance that got told about itself, or one that did not.
It is what makes the environment part of the engine, and it points directly at the movement's last member.
The Feedback Loop is dated from how long it takes to find out, which is a property of the work and not of the persona.
