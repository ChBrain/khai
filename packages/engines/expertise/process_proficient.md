# Process: The Proficient

## Taxonomy

[The Stages](process_stages.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: expertise

## Initiated by

The situation presents itself already organised.
The Proficient starts where the persona stops choosing a perspective and starts arriving with one: enough cases have accumulated that the situation is seen whole, with what matters already salient, before any deliberation occurs.

## Direction

Toward seeing what is going on, and then deciding what to do.
Perception has gone over to the intuitive and action has not. The persona reads the case immediately and correctly, and then works out the response -- so the split is diagnostic-fast, decision-slow.
This is the stage at which somebody becomes visibly good.

## Lever

The speed of the whole-situation reading against the deliberation still required to act.
The carrying force is that intuitive situation-reading is enormously powerful and is what separates the proficient from the merely competent -- they are no longer assembling the case, they are recognising it, and recognising is fast and hard to fool; the resistance is that the persona is now in the awkward position of knowing what is happening and still having to reason out what to do about it, which is slower than either the stage before or the stage after and feels worse than both.
The Proficient sees clearly and still has to think.

## Echo

A correct reading, followed by an argument with oneself.
It is the last stage at which the persona can still give an account of the decision, which is why it is the best stage to learn from.
The Proficient is dated from the first case they understood before they had examined it.
