# Process: The Advanced Beginner

## Taxonomy

[The Stages](process_stages.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: expertise

## Initiated by

The persona has met enough real cases to start noticing things nobody taught them.
The Advanced Beginner starts where experience begins supplying what instruction could not: aspects of a situation that are recognisable but not definable -- the tone of a room, the sound of the machine, the way this one is going.

## Direction

Toward rules supplemented by things that cannot be written as rules.
The persona now works from a mixture: the taught features, which are stateable, and the situational aspects, which they can recognise and cannot specify.
The mixture is unstable and they know it, since they cannot say why they are doing half of what they are doing.

## Lever

The usefulness of the new perceptions against the persona's inability to justify them.
The carrying force is that the aspects are genuine information and the persona is right to use them -- this is the first appearance of real skill, and refusing to act on what one cannot articulate would throw away the only thing separating them from the manual; the resistance is that they cannot defend it, so under challenge they retreat to the rules, and a supervisor who demands justification for every judgement will hold them at the previous stage indefinitely.
The Advanced Beginner is the stage where being asked to explain is a demotion.

## Echo

Two kinds of knowledge in one performance, and only one of them sayable.
It is the first place the engine's twist appears in miniature, long before the persona is any good.
The Advanced Beginner is dated from the first thing they noticed that was not on the list.
