# Process: Expertise

## Taxonomy

Skill (no khai file of its own yet).

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: expertise

## Initiated by

A persona has got good at something, and can no longer say how.
Expertise starts wherever a capacity moves out of deliberation and into the hands: the persona stops working through the rules and starts seeing what the situation requires, and the seeing arrives faster than any account of it could.
It is not the habit installed by repetition, which is habit's and ingraining's, and not the theory of where ability comes from, which is mindset's. It is the transformation of a performance from something assembled to something perceived.

## Direction

Toward a competence that runs without consultation, and cannot be handed over.
The engine reads three movements: [the stages](process_stages.md), the shape of the climb from rule-following to perception; [the making](process_making_expertise.md), what actually builds it and what does not; and [the tacit](process_tacit.md), the part that was never in words and the part that has left them.
The three are not phases of a career. They are one process seen at different distances.

## Lever

The power of a competence that needs no deliberation against what removing the deliberation removes.
The carrying force is that this is one of the better things a person can do with a life: the expert genuinely perceives what the novice must work out, acts correctly under time pressure that would defeat reasoning, and is not merely faster but seeing a different situation -- the transformation is real and is the whole point of practice; the resistance is that what has been automated has also been removed from view, so the persona's own account of their competence degrades exactly as the competence improves.
Expertise is read on what the persona can do and never on what they say they do, because those two come apart by construction.

## Echo

Somebody who knows and cannot tell.
Expertise does not close; it is a capacity migrating out of words, gathered into one reading -- the stages, the making, the tacit.
The engine's twist is here: **becoming an expert destroys the ability to teach it**. The very mechanism that produces the competence -- the chunking and automating of what was once effortful and explicit -- is the mechanism that takes it out of the persona's own sight, so they omit the steps a novice needs, remember their own learning as faster and more obvious than it was, and give instruction that is accurate about the goal and useless about the route. The better they are, the worse their account. Nobody is being withholding.
It hands off what it is not: the response grooved by repetition into a context is habit's and ingraining's; the held theory of where ability comes from is mindset's; the belief in one's own capability is self-efficacy's; the absorption in a matched challenge is flow's; the persistence toward a long goal is grit's; expert power over another is power's; and a mastery made to **stand for** a theme is authored key, the play's pitch, not the force.
The persona is dated from the last time they could still explain it.
