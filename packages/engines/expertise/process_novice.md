# Process: The Novice

## Taxonomy

[The Stages](process_stages.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: expertise

## Initiated by

The persona has been given rules and has no situations to apply them to.
The Novice starts with instruction: recognise these features, and when you see them do this -- stated without context, because the persona has no context in which to state it.

## Direction

Toward correct performance by lookup.
The novice attends to what they have been told to attend to, ignores everything else because they cannot yet tell what else there is, and follows the rule regardless of whether this case is one the rule was built for.
Performance is slow, effortful, brittle at the edges, and frequently correct -- which is the point of teaching it this way.

## Lever

The safety of the rule against its blindness to the case.
The carrying force is that context-free rules are the only thing a beginner can actually use, are transmissible in an afternoon, and get somebody through work they could not otherwise be trusted with -- there is no better way to start and no field has found one; the resistance is that the rule cannot see when it does not apply, and the novice cannot either, so the failure mode is confident correctness in exactly the situation that needed something else.
The Novice is protected by the rule and cannot be protected from it.

## Echo

Correct work, done without understanding.
It is where every expert began and the stage most reliably misremembered afterward.
The Novice is dated from the training, and it ends the first time a rule is followed and the result is wrong.
