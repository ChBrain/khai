# Instructions: Elevation

How a Playwright wires the elevation engine into a play. You wire by linking the play's own content to the engine's files; the engine files stay untouched, they are canon. The Roadie plumbs the engine's law into Instructions Knowledge on deploy. Authoring guidance, not runtime content, and it does not go on tour.

## Human

- The human defines the elevation terrain: what act of moral excellence the persona witnesses -- whose kindness, courage, or generosity, done to whom -- so that a virtue in another stands before the persona to lift them, and not a benefit to the persona themselves.

## Agent

- From the play's own content, a persona links the form the moment runs (witness, warmth, or emulation) under its Projection. Let it show moral beauty lifting the persona -- the excellence perceived, the warmth it opens in the chest, the pull to be and do better -- and let it read as being moved by goodness done to anyone, not as thanks for a favor received, nor as awe at vastness, nor as the automatic catching of a mood. You link to the engine, you never edit it.

## Collaboration

- A benefit received by the self and the warm turn toward its giver is the gratitude engine's; the accommodation of a vastness that exceeds one's frame is the awe engine's; the automatic, unconscious catching of another's affect through mimicry is the contagion engine's. Admiration for non-moral excellence -- skill, talent -- is elevation's sibling but a cooler, distinct response. This engine supplies the uplift of witnessing moral excellence in another.

## Knowledge

- Elevation is the other-praising emotion of moral beauty: witnessing another's act of unexpected kindness, courage, or generosity lifts the persona toward the good, felt for virtue done to anyone and not for a benefit to oneself. Witness is the elicitor -- the perception of another's moral excellence, a deed beyond the ordinary that reveals a higher standard, which is what elevation answers, and which is always a virtue in another rather than a favor to the self. Warmth is the embodiment -- the open, dilating feeling in the chest, the tears without sorrow and the lump in the throat, the physical signature that marks being morally moved apart from a cool approval, and that softens the persona toward others. Emulation is the motive -- the pull to become a better person, to help, to affiliate with and imitate the exemplar, so that one good deed witnessed begets the wish, and often the doing, of good of one's own.

## System

- Do make the elicitor a virtue in another, not a benefit to the self: elevation is moved by goodness witnessed, done to anyone -- so let a stranger's kindness or an unrewarded courage lift the persona, and keep thanks for a favor received in the gratitude register instead.
- Do let it land in the body: elevation is warmth and opening in the chest, tears that are not grief -- so let the persona be physically moved and softened toward others, not merely approving in the abstract.
- Do let it raise, not shame: the witnessed good lifts the persona toward it, motivating them to be and do better and to pass the good along -- so let one good deed seen beget the wish for good of one's own.
- Do delegate the benefit-to-self and its warm turn to the source to the gratitude engine, the accommodation of vastness to the awe engine, and the automatic mimicry-based spread of affect to the contagion engine. Do not edit the engine's files; wire only from the play's side.
