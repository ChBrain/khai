# Process: Elevation

## Taxonomy

Behavioral science / the positive psychology of moral emotion.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: elevation

## Initiated by

A persona witnesses another do something of unexpected moral beauty -- an act of kindness, courage, or generosity beyond the ordinary -- and is lifted by it: warmed, moved, raised toward the good they saw.
This is elevation: the other-praising emotion evoked by moral excellence in another, a warm and expansive uplift felt not for a benefit to oneself but for virtue done to anyone.
The persona is not thanking a benefactor nor marveling at vastness; they are moved by goodness itself, seen in another, and drawn upward by it.

## Direction

Toward a self lifted by another's virtue and moved to rise to it.
Elevation runs from a perception to a feeling to a motive: moral excellence is witnessed, a warm openness answers it in the body, and the persona is drawn to become better, to help, to be like the one they saw.
The direction is upward and outward: where disgust at moral ugliness pushes down and away, elevation lifts and opens -- the witness raised on the spot toward the standard the act revealed, and turned, at least for the moment, toward the good.

## Lever

The moral excellence of another, witnessed, spent as an uplift that moves the persona toward the good.
Elevation runs on virtue perceived in a third party: an act that exceeds the moral ordinary reveals a higher possibility of conduct, and the seeing of it -- not any gain to oneself -- is what warms and raises the witness.
The lever is the goodness of the deed as such: because the act is admirable in the moral register and needs no personal benefit to move the persona, elevation is felt for kindness shown to strangers, for courage spent on others, for generosity one only observes.

## Echo

The witness is lifted by goodness seen in another, and turned toward becoming good themselves.
Elevation is why an act of grace observed can bring tears and a swelling in the chest, why reading of great virtue makes one want to be better, why one good deed witnessed moves the witness toward good deeds of their own -- the moral beauty of another raising the one who sees it.
It runs as [witness](process_witness.md), [warmth](process_warmth.md), or [emulation](process_emulation.md) -- the excellence perceived, the warmth it opens, the pull to emulate the good. A benefit received by the self and the warm turn toward its giver is the gratitude engine's; the accommodation of a vastness that exceeds one's frame is the awe engine's; the automatic, unconscious catching of another's affect is the contagion engine's. This engine owns the uplift of witnessing moral excellence in another.
