# Process: Warmth

## Taxonomy

[Elevation](process_elevation.md), the embodied form.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: elevation

## Initiated by

The persona, seeing the good deed, feels it in the body: a warmth spreading in the chest, an openness, a tightening of the throat, the prickle of tears, the sense of being physically moved.
This is the warmth: the embodied signature of elevation, the felt dilation that marks the emotion as something more than a cool approval.
The uplift is not only a thought about the deed; it is a heat and an opening the persona actually feels.

## Direction

Toward an open, dilated bodily state -- warm, expansive, softened toward others.
The warmth works in the body's own register: elevation is felt as a pleasant opening in the chest, often with tears and a lump in the throat, an embodied expansiveness distinct from the sharper thrill of other positive emotions.
The direction is opening, not arousal: where fear closes and braces, elevation warms and softens -- the persona physically loosened toward connection, the felt heat in the chest the sign that the moral beauty has landed in the flesh and not only the mind.

## Lever

The moved body, spent as the felt heat and openness that carry the uplift.
The warmth runs on elevation's embodiment: the emotion recruits a calm, open bodily state -- a warmth and swelling in the chest, tears without sorrow -- so that being morally moved has a physical form the persona can feel and be changed by.
The lever is the felt opening: because the uplift is embodied and not merely judged, it softens the persona toward others in the moment, the warm dilation itself inclining them to kindness before any decision to be kind.

## Echo

The good deed is felt as warmth in the chest, and the warmed body is a body opened toward others.
It is why elevation brings tears that are not grief, why the chest swells at an act of grace, why being moved by goodness is a bodily event and not a bloodless verdict -- the moral beauty landing as heat and opening the persona feels.
It is [witness](process_witness.md) and [emulation](process_emulation.md)'s middle, the feeling where they are the perceiving and the motive. The automatic, unconscious catching of another's bodily affect through mimicry is the contagion engine's; warmth owns the embodied uplift proper to witnessing moral excellence -- the felt heat and opening of being morally moved.
