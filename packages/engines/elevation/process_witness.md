# Process: Witness

## Taxonomy

[Elevation](process_elevation.md), the elicitor form.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: elevation

## Initiated by

The persona sees another act with unexpected moral excellence -- a stranger's kindness, a plain person's courage, an unrewarded generosity -- and the very seeing of it is what sets elevation going.
This is the witness: the perception of virtue in another that elicits the uplift, the moral beauty of a deed registered as beyond the ordinary.
What starts the emotion is not a thing done for the persona but a good thing done, observed.

## Direction

Toward a higher standard of conduct, glimpsed in another's deed.
The witnessing works by contrast and revelation: an act of moral excellence stands out against the ordinary run of behavior and, in standing out, shows the persona a better way of being than they had been holding in view.
The direction is disclosure: the deed lifts a corner on what people are capable of at their best, and the persona, having seen it, cannot quite unsee the standard it revealed -- the perception itself doing the lifting.

## Lever

The moral excellence of the act, perceived, spent as the trigger of the uplift.
The witness runs on virtue as a thing the persona can recognize in another: kindness, courage, loyalty, generosity, shown in a concrete deed, and recognized as admirable in the moral register rather than the merely skillful one.
The lever is the recognition of goodness: because it is the moral quality of the act -- not its cleverness, not its benefit to oneself -- that is seen, elevation is elicited by a saint's patience or a stranger's mercy where a display of sheer talent would elicit a cooler, different response.

## Echo

The good deed seen is what opens the door, and the standard it reveals is what the persona is lifted toward.
It is why elevation begins in the eyes -- in the catching sight of someone being better than they had to be -- why the trigger is always a virtue in another and never a favor to oneself, why the ordinary made witness to the extraordinary is moved.
It is [warmth](process_warmth.md) and [emulation](process_emulation.md)'s beginning, the perceiving where they are the feeling and the motive. The excellence of a non-moral skill witnessed is a cooler, sibling response; witness owns the perception of another's moral excellence that sets elevation in motion.
