# Process: Emulation

## Taxonomy

[Elevation](process_elevation.md), the motivational form.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: elevation

## Initiated by

Lifted by the good deed, the persona wants to be like the one who did it -- to help, to be kinder, to become a better person themselves.
This is emulation: the motivational upshot of elevation, the pull the witnessed virtue exerts to rise toward it and to do good in turn.
The uplift does not stay a feeling; it becomes a wish to act and to be better.

## Direction

Toward becoming and doing good, the witness raised toward the standard they saw.
Emulation turns the warm uplift into motive: the persona is moved to affiliate with the exemplar, to imitate the admired act, and more broadly to be a better version of themselves -- the goodness witnessed setting a pull the persona leans into.
The direction is prosocial and aspirational: elevation makes the witness measurably more likely to help, to give, to act well -- one good deed seen begetting the wish, and often the doing, of good deeds of one's own.

## Lever

The witnessed virtue as a standard to rise to, spent as the motive to be and to do good.
Emulation runs on the exemplar's pull: the moral excellence, having lifted the persona, draws them to close the gap between themselves and the standard it revealed -- to become more like the good person they saw, and to pass the good along.
The lever is aspiration toward the good: because the deed lifted rather than shamed, the persona is drawn upward willingly, motivated to emulate the virtue and to affiliate with the virtuous, the uplift converting into a reach toward better conduct.

## Echo

The good witnessed raises the witness toward good of their own, and the deed passes on.
It is why an act of kindness seen makes the seer want to be kind, why elevation moves people to help and to give, why goodness observed spreads to goodness done -- the moral beauty of another lifting the persona into the wish to rise to it.
It is [witness](process_witness.md) and [warmth](process_warmth.md)'s end, the motive where they are the perceiving and the feeling. The automatic, unchosen spread of affect from one persona to another is the contagion engine's; emulation owns the willed, aspirational pull to become and to do good that witnessing moral excellence sets going.
