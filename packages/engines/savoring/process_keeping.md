# Process: Keeping

## Taxonomy

[Savoring](process_savoring.md), the way of savoring that extends across time.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: savoring

## Initiated by

The persona banks the moment -- taking a mental snapshot, fixing the scene to return to -- and marks, even as it happens, that it is fleeting.
This is savoring across time: the deliberate storing of a good moment for later, and the acute awareness that it will not last.
It reaches both ways from the present -- forward, to the recollection it is building, and into the now, which its own transience sharpens.

## Direction

Toward a pleasure stretched beyond its moment.
By building a memory to reminisce on later, the persona extends the good forward past its ending; and by feeling how briefly it will last, they deepen it now -- the knowing that this will end, held rightly, makes the present sweeter rather than sadder.
The direction is temporal: the good stretched along the line of time, before and after its instant.

## Lever

Memory-building and temporal awareness.
The snapshot taken on purpose -- this, I want to keep -- fixes a good moment as a resource for later; and the bittersweet awareness of transience, that this is rare and passing, paradoxically intensifies the savoring of it in the moment, the way a last day is felt more than a first.
The lever is the persona's relation to time: refusing to let the good simply pass, they bank it and let its brevity concentrate it.

## Echo

The good is carried past its moment -- laid down as a memory and made keener now by its own passing.
The reminiscence itself, later, drawing the stored good back up to be felt again, is the memory engine's, and its sentimental ache the nostalgia engine's; this owns the banking of the moment for them, and the transience-awareness that sharpens it.
It turns toward time where [sharpening](process_sharpening.md) stays in the instant and [sharing](process_sharing.md) turns toward others.
