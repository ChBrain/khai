# @chbrain/khai-engine-savoring

## 0.1.1

### Patch Changes

- 013e120: Add the savoring engine: how a persona attends to and prolongs a positive experience -- turning toward a good moment to make it fuller and last, rather than letting it pass unmarked. The root (`process_savoring.md`) declares savoring as the up-regulation and prolonging of positive experience, the mirror of coping: where coping softens what hurts, savoring heightens and holds what pleases. It does not generate the good -- the event and the emotion do that -- but tends it, by attention and effort that make a pleasure more intense while it lasts and stretch it before and after. Four ways divide the moves: `sharpening` (intensify by attention -- narrow onto the good and let absorption take over, so a pleasure fully met is a larger one), `sharing` (amplify by expression -- tell someone or let it show, so the good grows in the telling and the witness's answer), `keeping` (extend across time -- bank the moment as a memory and feel its transience, stretching the pleasure beyond its instant), and `dampening` (the failure -- kill-joy thinking that turns attention against the good and cuts it short, savoring run in reverse).

  Found via a gap audit; the borderline case flagged in advance, resolved by boundary check. Carved against `regulation` (Gross's neutral machinery of intervening on any feeling, up or down) -- savoring is the positive-specific counterpart to coping, a distinct construct (Bryant), not a special case of general regulation; the modes are built from Bryant's Ways of Savoring (the amplification mechanisms) rather than savoring's emotion-contents, so they do not restate `pride`/`gratitude`/`awe`, which it delegates to along with `memory`/`nostalgia` (the later recollection of a banked good). Warranted (LORE) on Bryant & Veroff (the savoring model and Ways of Savoring), Bryant (savoring as distinct from coping), Jose, Lim & Bryant (savoring raises happiness, dampening lowers it), Langston and Gable & Reis (capitalization -- sharing amplifies), and Quoidbach et al. (savoring vs dampening strategies). Set at patch as the free level; a new engine may warrant a minor at the maintainer's `bump:minor` label.
