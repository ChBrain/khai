# Savoring: Reference

## Line of Work

Savoring as **process**: how a persona attends to and prolongs a positive experience -- turning toward a good moment to make it fuller and last, rather than letting it pass unmarked. The domain does not model the neutral machinery of intervening on any feeling, up or down, at any point on the emotion-generative timeline -- situation, attention, appraisal, response -- which is the regulation engine's; nor the specific positive emotions savoring heightens -- the pride of an achievement, the gratitude for a gift, the awe at a vastness -- which are those engines'; nor the later recollection of a banked good, which is the memory and nostalgia engines'. It models the appreciative turn toward the positive and the moves that enlarge it: the mirror of coping.

The spine is the psychology of savoring. As coping is the set of processes by which a person softens and manages negative experience, savoring is the parallel set by which they attend to, appreciate, and enhance positive experience -- a distinct capacity, not a special case of general emotion regulation. A good moment left unattended fades at its own rate; savored, it is intensified by focus, amplified by expression, and extended by memory and transience-awareness -- and the same attention, turned against the good, dampens it. The four ways are those moves: sharpening, sharing, keeping, dampening.

## Restrictions

What the engine refuses to model, and to whom it delegates.

- **Regulation**: The engine models the appreciative up-regulation and prolonging of a positive experience, not the neutral machinery of regulating any feeling. The general strategies of intervening on the emotion-generative timeline -- situation selection, reappraisal, suppression, applied to any emotion, up or down -- are owned by the regulation engine; savoring is the positive-specific counterpart to coping, and while it may recruit regulation's strategies, its aim and its Ways are its own.
- **Emotion, Pride, Gratitude, Awe**: The engine models the heightening of a positive experience, not the experience itself. The specific goods savoring dwells in -- joy, the pride of an achievement, the gratitude for a benefit, the awe at a vastness -- are owned by the emotion, pride, gratitude, and awe engines; savoring is the turning-toward and the prolonging, not the emotion it amplifies.
- **Memory and Nostalgia**: The engine models the banking of a good moment and the awareness of its transience, not the later recollection. The drawing-back-up of a stored good to be felt again is owned by the memory engine, and its sentimental ache by the nostalgia engine; savoring lays the moment down and sharpens it by its passing, where those engines own the returning to it.

## Encoding

Source to constraint, per file.

- **[savoring](process_savoring.md)** (the root): The up-regulation and prolonging of a positive experience -- the appreciative turn toward a good moment to make it fuller and last, coping's mirror. Anchored by Bryant & Veroff (the savoring model) and Bryant (savoring as distinct from coping).
- **[sharpening](process_sharpening.md)** (way): Intensify by attention -- narrow onto the good and let absorption take over, so a pleasure fully met is a larger pleasure. Anchored by Bryant & Veroff (sensory-perceptual sharpening and absorption).
- **[sharing](process_sharing.md)** (way): Amplify by expression -- tell someone or let it show, so the good grows in the telling and the witness's answer. Anchored by Langston and Gable & Reis (capitalization) and Bryant & Veroff.
- **[keeping](process_keeping.md)** (way): Extend across time -- bank the moment as a memory and feel its transience, stretching the pleasure beyond its instant. Anchored by Bryant & Veroff (memory-building and temporal awareness).
- **[dampening](process_dampening.md)** (way): The failure -- kill-joy thinking that turns attention against the good and cuts it short, savoring run in reverse. Anchored by Quoidbach et al. (dampening) and Jose, Lim & Bryant.
