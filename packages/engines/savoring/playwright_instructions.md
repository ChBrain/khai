# Instructions: Savoring

How a Playwright wires the savoring engine into a play. You wire by linking the play's own content to the engine's files; the engine files stay untouched, they are canon. The Roadie plumbs the engine's law into Instructions Knowledge on deploy. Authoring guidance, not runtime content, and it does not go on tour.

## Human

- The human defines the savoring terrain: the good thing -- present, past, or coming -- and what the persona does with it, so the pleasure has something to be enlarged from and a way of being met or missed.

## Agent

- From the play's own content, a persona links the way of savoring the moment runs (sharpening, sharing, keeping, or dampening) under its Projection. Let the way show what the persona does with a good moment -- the full attention paid, the telling, the snapshot taken, or the reflexive undercutting -- and let the good read as larger or thinner for it. You link to the engine, you never edit it.

## Collaboration

- The neutral machinery of regulating any feeling, up or down, is the regulation engine's; the specific goods savoring heightens -- joy, pride, gratitude, awe -- are those engines'; the later recollection of a banked moment is memory's and nostalgia's. This engine supplies the appreciative turn toward the positive and the moves that enlarge it -- savoring as coping's mirror.

## Knowledge

- Savoring is how a persona attends to and prolongs a positive experience: not generating the good but tending it, to make it fuller and last. Sharpening intensifies by attention -- narrowing onto the good and getting absorbed, so a pleasure fully met is larger. Sharing amplifies by expression -- telling someone or letting it show, so the good grows in the telling. Keeping extends across time -- banking the moment as a memory and feeling its transience, which deepens it now. Dampening is the failure -- kill-joy thinking that turns attention against the good and cuts it short.

## System

- Do make savoring a thing done: let the good be enlarged by the dwelling or lost by the rushing-past, so the persona's relation to the pleasure, not just the pleasure, is on the page.
- Do let sharing be the strongest amplifier -- a good told to someone glad grows -- and let the wrong witness (indifferent, envious) deflate it instead.
- Do let dampening be a real character note: the persona who cannot receive a good moment, deflecting praise and hunting the flaw, lives goods half-had -- and let the reason (guilt, fear of the fall) sit beneath it.
- Do delegate the specific emotions savoring heightens to their engines and the general regulation of feeling to the regulation engine. Do not edit the engine's files; wire only from the play's side.
