# Process: Sharing

## Taxonomy

[Savoring](process_savoring.md), the way of savoring that amplifies by expression.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: savoring

## Initiated by

The persona tells someone, or lets it show -- the good news spoken, the delight plain on the face and in the body.
This is savoring by expression: the good carried outward, to another or into the open, rather than kept inside.
It is the most social of the ways, and the most reliable amplifier of the ones there are.

## Direction

Toward a pleasure amplified by being expressed and witnessed.
Communicating a good experience to another, or simply letting it show, heightens and extends it -- the telling makes the persona re-live it, and the witness's answer, their gladness returned, adds to it.
The direction is outward and shared: the good made larger by leaving the single mind.

## Lever

Sharing with others and behavioural expression.
To voice a good event to someone who cares, or to let the smile and the gesture out rather than damp them down, is among the strongest single amplifiers of how much a positive event lifts a person -- the pleasure grows in the telling and in the answer it draws.
The lever is the outward turn: the good spent on another rather than hoarded, which pays it back enlarged.

## Echo

The good is larger for being shared, and the tie warmer for the sharing.
Its risk is the witness: a good met with indifference or envy rather than gladness can deflate rather than amplify, so the choice of whom to tell is part of the savoring.
It turns outward where [sharpening](process_sharpening.md) keeps the good inward; the answering gladness a good disclosure draws from another is the rapport composite's and self-disclosure's, where this owns the amplifying telling itself.
