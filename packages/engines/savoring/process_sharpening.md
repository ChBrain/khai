# Process: Sharpening

## Taxonomy

[Savoring](process_savoring.md), the way of savoring that intensifies by attention.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: savoring

## Initiated by

The persona narrows onto the good thing -- the taste, the warmth, the moment -- and shuts out the rest, letting themselves get lost in it.
This is savoring by attention: the deliberate turning of the whole field of notice onto a single pleasure.
It is the most immediate of the ways, present-tense and inward, done in the moment the good is happening.

## Direction

Toward a pleasure intensified by focus.
By sharpening perception and letting absorption take over, the persona makes the experience fill more of the mind -- and a pleasure fully attended to is a larger pleasure than the same one half-noticed.
The direction is inward and concentrating: not more good, but more of the good taken in.

## Lever

Sensory-perceptual sharpening and absorption.
Attention is the amplifier: the same good, half-registered while the mind is elsewhere, is half-felt, so the persona slows down, blocks the distraction, and lets the experience take the whole field -- the wine really tasted, the warmth really felt, the moment really entered.
The lever is the density of attention paid, which savoring raises on purpose where ordinary life lets it drift.

## Echo

The pleasure is fuller for having been fully met.
It is the ground the other ways build on -- a good not attended to cannot be shared, kept, or even properly had -- and its whole risk is fragility: absorption breaks the instant the mind is pulled away.
It turns inward where [sharing](process_sharing.md) turns outward and [keeping](process_keeping.md) turns toward time; its exact reversal, attention turned against the good, is [dampening](process_dampening.md).
