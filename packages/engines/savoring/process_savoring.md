# Process: Savoring

## Taxonomy

Emotion / the positive psychology of appreciating experience.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: savoring

## Initiated by

A good thing is happening, or has, or is coming -- and the persona turns toward it, attending to the pleasure rather than letting it pass unmarked.
This is savoring: the up-regulation and prolonging of a positive experience, the mirror of coping. Where coping works to soften what hurts, savoring works to heighten and hold what pleases.
It is a thing done, or not done: a good moment can be dwelt in and enlarged, or rushed past and lost.

## Direction

Toward a positive feeling made fuller and longer.
Not the generating of the good -- the event and the emotion do that -- but the tending of it: the attention and effort that make a pleasure more intense while it lasts, and stretch it before it comes and after it has gone.
The direction is appreciative: a leaning-into the good rather than a moving-on from it.

## Lever

Attention turned toward the positive, and the moves that amplify it.
A good moment left unattended fades at its own rate; savored, it is intensified by focus, amplified by expression, and extended by memory and anticipation. The lever is the choice to dwell rather than rush past -- and it is the same lever that can fail: the attention that sharpens a pleasure can, turned against it, kill it.
What savoring cannot do is manufacture the good; it can only make more of a good that is there.

## Echo

The positive experience is larger and lasts longer -- or, unsavored or actively dampened, it passes thin and quick.
It runs as [sharpening](process_sharpening.md), [sharing](process_sharing.md), [keeping](process_keeping.md), or [dampening](process_dampening.md) -- the ways a good is intensified, amplified, extended, or cut short.
The specific goods it heightens -- the pride of an achievement, the gratitude for a gift, the awe at a vastness -- are the pride, gratitude, and awe engines'; the neutral machinery of intervening on any feeling, up or down, is the regulation engine's. This engine owns the appreciative turn toward the positive, and the prolonging of it -- savoring as coping's mirror, not regulation's general case.
