# Process: Dampening

## Taxonomy

[Savoring](process_savoring.md), the way savoring fails -- the pleasure cut short.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: savoring

## Initiated by

The persona turns attention against the good -- finding the flaw, distracting themselves, jumping ahead to the next task, or reminding themselves that it will not last in a way that spoils rather than sharpens.
This is kill-joy thinking: the mind that will not let itself dwell in a pleasure, undercutting it as it arrives.
It is savoring run in reverse, the same lever thrown the other way.

## Direction

Away from the pleasure, cutting it short.
Instead of leaning into the good, the persona deflects the praise, hunts the imperfection, recalls what is undone, or braces for the letdown -- so the positive passes thin and quick, half-felt and quickly gone.
The direction is a turning-away: attention withdrawn from a good that was there to be had.

## Lever

Kill-joy thinking, the anti-savoring.
The same attention that sharpens a pleasure can, aimed against it, kill it -- and some personas do this reflexively, unable to receive a good moment without undercutting it: the compliment argued down, the achievement minimized, the holiday spent dreading its end.
The lever is attention turned to the negative within or beside the good, so that the good cannot fill the field.

## Echo

The positive experience is thinned or lost, unsavored on purpose.
Over time a persona who reflexively dampens lives a life of goods half-received -- not from want of good things but from an inability to let them land.
It is the shadow of [sharpening](process_sharpening.md): attention is the lever of both, toward the good or against it. Why a persona cannot let themselves enjoy -- the guilt that will not permit it, the fear of the fall after -- is the guilt engine's or the emotion engine's; this owns the dampening move itself, the reflex that cuts the good short.
