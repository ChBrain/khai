# Process: Calibration

## Taxonomy

[Metacognition](process_metacognition.md), the accuracy of the monitoring -- whether the read matches reality.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: metacognition

## Initiated by

The persona's confidence is measured against its performance -- and they match, or they do not.
This is calibration: the accuracy of the metacognitive monitoring, whether the persona's sense of what it knows tracks what it actually knows, and how far it runs over- or under-confident.
It is the quality of the read -- not what the persona thinks it knows, but whether that thinking is right.

## Direction

Toward the fit between the read and the reality.
Calibration compares the monitoring's report to the object-level truth: high confidence on correct answers and low on wrong ones is well-calibrated; high confidence on wrong answers is overconfidence, low confidence on right ones underconfidence, and the resolution is how sharply the persona's confidence separates what it knows from what it does not.
Its direction is the alignment, or the gap, between felt knowing and real knowing.

## Lever

What calibration spends -- the assumption that a persona's confidence can be trusted.
Confidence and accuracy come apart: fluency, familiarity, and ease inflate the sense of knowing past the fact, so a persona can feel it understands what it cannot do and feel sure of what is wrong; calibration spends the equation of feeling-sure with being-right, and its failures are the ground of a whole family of biases.
A production holds this lever by letting a persona's confidence and competence diverge where the scene calls for it -- the sure-and-wrong, the able-but-doubting -- so calibration is a live quality of the character, not assumed perfect.

## Echo

The persona's read is accurate or it is not -- and the accuracy governs whether its steering helps or harms, since effort routed by a miscalibrated monitor lands in the wrong place.
Well-calibrated, the persona studies what it truly does not know and trusts what it truly does; miscalibrated, it neglects real gaps and wastes effort on the mastered.
The systematic, named failures of calibration -- overconfidence, the Dunning-Kruger effect where the least able most overrate themselves, the curse of knowledge, the illusion of explanatory depth -- are catalogued as members of the bias engine; calibration here is the metacognitive accuracy itself, the fit of the [monitoring](process_monitoring.md) to the truth that those biases measure the failure of.
