# Instructions: Metacognition

How a Playwright wires the metacognition engine into a play. You wire by linking the play's own content to the engine's files; the engine files stay untouched, they are canon. The Roadie plumbs the engine's law into Instructions Knowledge on deploy. Authoring guidance, not runtime content, and it does not go on tour.

## Human

- The human sets the metacognitive terrain: what the persona is trying to know, learn, or recall, and where the edges of its knowledge sit -- so a moment holds a genuine read on its own cognition, a sense of knowing or not-knowing that it can act on.

## Agent

- From the play's own content, a persona links the metacognition a moment runs under its Projection: what it is monitoring about its own knowing (does it know this, has it learned it, will it come back, is it on the tip of its tongue), how it steers its cognition on that read (study longer, keep searching or give up, switch strategy), and how well-calibrated its standing skill is. Let it read as the persona managing its own mind, and its calibration in whether its confidence tracks its performance. You link to the engine, you never edit it.

## Collaboration

- The object-level cognition itself -- the remembering, reasoning, and knowing that is watched -- is the wider cognition's; the watching of one's own self-presentation to others is the self-monitoring engine's; the systematic, named errors of miscalibration (overconfidence, the Dunning-Kruger effect, the curse of knowledge) are the bias engine's members; the knowledge emotions a gap provokes (curiosity's pull, confusion's impasse) are those engines'; the general machinery of cognitive control is the executive-function engine's. This engine supplies the knowing about knowing -- the monitoring, the steering, and the calibration, and the metacognitive skill beneath them.

## Knowledge

- Metacognition is a persona's knowing about its own knowing -- a meta-level that watches the object-level of cognition and steers it, cognition turned upon itself (Nelson & Narens; Flavell). It runs on two flows. Monitoring is the upward flow -- the meta-level reading the object-level, yielding the judgment of learning (have I learned this), the feeling of knowing (do I know it even if I cannot retrieve it now), the tip-of-the-tongue, and the confidence a persona holds; these reads are inferential, drawn from cues like fluency and familiarity that can mislead (Koriat). Steering is the downward flow -- the meta-level acting on the object-level, allocating study time to the not-yet-learned, deciding whether to keep searching memory or give up, choosing which strategy to bring. Calibration is the accuracy of the monitoring -- whether confidence matches performance, and how over- or under-confident the persona runs, since a monitor that misreads steers its effort wrong. Metacognitive is the trait -- a standing skill at knowing one's own mind, from the finely calibrated to the systematically blind. Read a persona by what it senses about its own knowing, how it manages its cognition on that sense, and whether its confidence tracks the truth.

## System

- Do keep metacognition the knowing about knowing, not the knowing itself: it is the second-order read on the persona's own cognitive states and the control it exerts back on them -- let the object-level cognition be the wider cognition's, and metacognition strictly the layer over it. Do not confuse it with self-monitoring, which watches one's self-presentation to others, not one's own mind.
- Do stage the two flows: monitoring as the read taken (the sureness, the almost-have-it, the blank) and steering as the action on the read (restudy the weak spot, keep searching or give up, switch tack) -- and let the steering follow visibly from the monitoring, effort placed where the read directs.
- Do let confidence and accuracy come apart: make the read fallible, so a persona can feel it understands what it cannot do and feel sure of what is wrong (fluency mistaken for knowing) -- and let calibration be a live quality of the character, well-calibrated or over- or under-confident, not assumed perfect. Delegate the named error-patterns (overconfidence, Dunning-Kruger, the curse of knowledge) to the bias engine.
- Do let the metacognitive trait set a baseline: the finely calibrated persona reading itself true and steering well as a matter of course, the blind one systematically off -- and let it vary by domain, sharp about one kind of knowing and dull about another. Do delegate the knowledge emotions a gap triggers (curiosity, interest, confusion) to those engines. Do not edit the engine's files; wire only from the play's side.
