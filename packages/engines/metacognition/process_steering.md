# Process: Steering

## Taxonomy

[Metacognition](process_metacognition.md), the downward flow -- the meta-level acting on the object-level.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: metacognition

## Initiated by

The persona acts on what its monitoring told it, steering its own cognition.
This is control, the meta-to-object flow (Nelson & Narens): the meta-level regulating the object-level -- allocating study time to what is not yet learned, deciding whether to keep searching memory or abandon the effort, choosing which strategy to bring, setting how much to check before committing.
It is the persona managing its own thinking, spending effort where the monitoring says it is needed.

## Direction

Downward, from the read to the regulation.
Steering turns the metacognitive report into action on the cognition: study this longer because the judgment of learning was low, stop searching because the feeling of knowing says the answer will not come, switch approach because the current one is not working.
Its direction is the meta-level's hand on the object-level, effort routed by the monitoring's report.

## Lever

What steering spends -- effort, allocated by the monitoring's guidance.
Cognitive effort is limited and must be placed; steering spends it where the read directs -- and only as well as the read is accurate, since effort routed by a wrong monitoring signal is wasted on the already-known or withheld from the not-yet-learned.
A production holds this lever by letting the persona manage its own effort visibly -- restudying the weak spot, giving up the unretrievable, changing tack -- so the control reads as a decision driven by the metacognitive report, not as undirected trying.

## Echo

The cognition is regulated -- effort placed, searches begun and ended, strategies chosen -- all on the strength of the monitoring that preceded it.
This closes the loop: the persona reads its knowing and then governs its knowing by the read, which is why accurate monitoring matters so much, and miscalibration costs.
The general placement of cognitive effort and the inhibition, updating, and shifting that carry a task are the executive-function engine's; steering is specifically the control that metacognitive [monitoring](process_monitoring.md) directs -- effort governed by a read on one's own knowledge, its wisdom only as good as the read's [calibration](process_calibration.md).
