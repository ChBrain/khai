# Process: Monitoring

## Taxonomy

[Metacognition](process_metacognition.md), the upward flow -- the meta-level reading the object-level.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: metacognition

## Initiated by

The persona takes a reading of its own knowing.
This is monitoring, the object-to-meta flow (Nelson & Narens): the meta-level informed by the object-level, yielding the family of metacognitive judgments -- the judgment of learning (have I learned this well enough), the feeling of knowing (do I know this even though I cannot retrieve it now), the tip-of-the-tongue state, and the confidence a persona attaches to an answer or a memory.
It is the persona sensing the state of its own cognition, without yet doing anything about it.

## Direction

Upward, from the cognition to the read on it.
Monitoring turns the persona's attention from the content of thought to the state of thought: not what is the answer but how sure am I, not the memory itself but will it come, is it there, how well do I hold it.
Its direction is the meta-level gathering its report on the object-level below.

## Lever

What monitoring spends -- the persona's unawareness of its own knowledge states.
These readings are often inferential, not direct: a persona judges it knows by the fluency of retrieval, the familiarity of a cue, the ease of processing -- cues that usually track knowledge but can be fooled, which is why fluency can feel like knowing when it is not (Koriat's cue-based account).
A production holds this lever by letting the persona sense its own knowing -- the sureness, the almost-have-it, the blank -- so the metacognitive read is present and can be acted on, or misread.

## Echo

The persona has a reading of what it knows -- and everything downstream rides on it.
The [steering](process_steering.md) acts on this report, allocating effort by it, and the [calibration](process_calibration.md) measures whether the report was true; monitoring is the sensing that both depend on.
The knowledge emotions that a monitored gap can trigger -- the pull of curiosity, the impasse of confusion -- are those engines'; monitoring is the metacognitive read itself, the judgment of one's own knowing that precedes and provokes them.
