# Process: Metacognition

## Taxonomy

Cognition / the monitoring and control of one's own cognition.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: metacognition

## Initiated by

A persona does not merely think and remember -- it knows something about its own thinking and remembering.
This is metacognition (Nelson & Narens; Flavell): a meta-level that watches the object-level of cognition and steers it, cognition turned upon itself.
It runs on two flows between the levels: monitoring, where the meta-level reads the object-level -- does it know this, has it learned it, will it remember, is the word on the tip of its tongue -- and control, where the meta-level acts on the object-level, allocating effort by what the monitoring reports.

## Direction

Up from the object-level to the meta-level, and back down again.
Metacognition points inward at the persona's own cognitive states, reading them, and then outward onto those same states, steering them -- the loop by which a persona knows it does not yet know something and, on that knowing, studies it more.
Its direction is the two-way traffic between doing the cognition and overseeing it.

## Lever

What metacognition spends -- the persona's blindness to its own cognitive states.
Cognition can run unmonitored, the persona simply knowing or not knowing without any read on which; metacognition spends that opacity, giving the persona access to its own knowledge states so it can act on them -- and the value of the access depends entirely on its accuracy, since a persona that misreads what it knows will steer its effort wrong.
It is called into play wherever a persona must judge and manage its own knowing: studying, searching memory, deciding whether to trust a recollection or an answer.

## Echo

The persona reads and steers its own cognition -- well, if its monitoring is accurate; badly, if it is not.
Good metacognition is much of what makes a learner effective: knowing what one has not learned, and spending effort there.
It runs as [monitoring](process_monitoring.md), [steering](process_steering.md), and [calibration](process_calibration.md), tuned by a standing [metacognitive](position_metacognitive.md) skill. The object-level cognition itself -- the remembering, reasoning, and knowing -- is the wider cognition's; the watching of one's own self-presentation to others is the self-monitoring engine's; the systematic errors of miscalibration, from overconfidence to the Dunning-Kruger effect to the curse of knowledge, are the bias engine's members; the knowledge emotions that flag a gap -- curiosity's pull, confusion's impasse -- are those engines'. This engine owns the monitoring and control of one's own cognition.
