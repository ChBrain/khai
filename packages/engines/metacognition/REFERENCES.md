# Metacognition: Reference

## Line of Work

Metacognition as a **multi-type** engine: a persona's knowing about its own knowing -- the meta-level that watches the object-level of cognition and steers it -- taken across the two flows between the levels and the accuracy that governs them, plus the trait that tunes them. The domain does not model the object-level cognition itself, the remembering and reasoning and knowing that are watched, which is the wider cognition's; nor the watching of one's own self-presentation to others, which is the self-monitoring engine's; nor the named, systematic errors of miscalibration -- overconfidence, the Dunning-Kruger effect, the curse of knowledge, the illusion of explanatory depth -- which are the bias engine's members; nor the knowledge emotions that a monitored gap can trigger, curiosity's pull and confusion's impasse, which are those engines'. It models the monitoring-and-control loop itself: the persona reading the state of its own cognition and acting on that read.

The spine is the metamemory and metacognition literature. Flavell's founding work named metacognition -- cognition about cognition -- and its role in learning. Nelson and Narens's two-level framework is the structural core: an object-level and a meta-level, joined by two flows -- monitoring, where information passes up from the object-level to the meta-level (the meta-level is informed by the object-level), and control, where the meta-level acts down on the object-level (initiating, maintaining, or changing object-level processes). Koriat's cue-based account explains how the monitoring judgments are actually made: they are inferential, not direct read-outs, computed from cues like retrieval fluency and cue familiarity that usually track knowledge but can be fooled, which is why fluency can feel like knowing. Dunlosky and Metcalfe map the family of metacognitive judgments -- judgments of learning, feelings of knowing, confidence -- and the control processes they govern, study-time allocation chief among them. Fleming and Lau formalize metacognitive sensitivity and its measurement, the trait side: how well a persona's confidence discriminates its own correct from incorrect performance. The altitudes are those: the loop, its monitoring, steering, and calibration, and its trait.

## Restrictions

What the engine refuses to model, and to whom it delegates.

- **Cognition (the object-level)**: The engine models the watching of cognition, not the cognition watched. The object-level itself -- the remembering, reasoning, perceiving, and knowing that the meta-level reads and steers -- is the wider cognition's; metacognition is strictly the second-order layer over it, the read on the state of a cognition and the control exerted back on it, not the first-order processing that read is about. The two compose: cognition does the knowing, metacognition knows about the knowing.
- **Self-monitoring**: The engine models the watching of one's own mind, not the watching of one's own self-presentation. Self-monitoring -- the regulation of expressive behavior and self-presentation to fit a social situation (Snyder) -- is owned by the self-monitoring engine; metacognition is the monitoring of one's own knowledge and learning states, inward at cognition rather than outward at the impression one makes. The surface word is shared; the objects are different -- one's knowing versus one's image.
- **Bias**: The engine models the accuracy of the monitoring, not the catalogue of its named failures. The systematic, labeled errors of miscalibration -- overconfidence, the Dunning-Kruger effect where the least able most overrate themselves, the curse of knowledge, the illusion of explanatory depth -- are owned as members of the bias engine; metacognition owns calibration as the live quality of the read, the fit of confidence to performance, and hands the named error-patterns that measure its failure across to that engine.
- **Knowledge emotions**: The engine models the read on a gap, not the feelings the gap provokes. The knowledge emotions that a monitored gap can trigger -- the pull of curiosity toward what one senses one does not know, the impasse of confusion, the flush of interest -- are owned by the curiosity, interest, and confusion engines; metacognition is the metacognitive judgment itself, the sensing of one's own knowing that precedes and provokes those feelings, not the feelings.
- **Executive function**: The engine models the control that a read on one's own knowledge directs, not the general machinery of cognitive control. Executive function -- the inhibition, updating, and shifting that carry any task, the general placement of cognitive effort -- is owned by the executive-function engine; metacognition's steering is specifically the effort routed by a metacognitive report on one's own knowledge, and it recruits that machinery but is defined by being governed by the self-read, not by the control mechanism as such.

## Encoding

Source to constraint, per file.

- **[metacognition](process_metacognition.md)** (the root, process): A persona's knowing about its own knowing -- a meta-level watching the object-level of cognition and steering it, on two flows. Anchored by Nelson & Narens (the two-level model) and Flavell (the founding).
- **[monitoring](process_monitoring.md)** (process, form): The upward flow -- the meta-level reading the object-level, yielding the metacognitive judgments (judgment of learning, feeling of knowing, tip-of-the-tongue, confidence). Anchored by Nelson & Narens (monitoring) and Koriat (the cue-based account).
- **[steering](process_steering.md)** (process, form): The downward flow -- the meta-level acting on the object-level, allocating effort by what the monitoring reports (study time, search termination, strategy choice). Anchored by Nelson & Narens (control) and Dunlosky & Metcalfe (study allocation).
- **[calibration](process_calibration.md)** (process, form): The accuracy of the monitoring -- whether the persona's confidence matches its performance, and how over- or under-confident it runs. Anchored by Koriat (fluency and confidence coming apart) and the metacognitive-accuracy literature.
- **[metacognitive](position_metacognitive.md)** (position, trait): A standing skill at knowing one's own mind -- metacognitive sensitivity as a settled, trainable disposition. Anchored by Fleming & Lau (sensitivity) and Schraw & Dennison (metacognitive awareness).
