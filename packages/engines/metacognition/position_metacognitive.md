# Position: Metacognitive

## Taxonomy

Cognition / the trait of metacognition -- the standing skill beneath [Metacognition](process_metacognition.md) (Fleming & Lau's metacognitive sensitivity; Schraw & Dennison's metacognitive awareness).

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: metacognition

## Has

A standing skill at knowing its own mind.
Trait metacognition, as against the state of any one reading: at the high end, the finely calibrated persona whose sense of what it knows tracks what it actually knows, that feels the edge of its knowledge and studies the right side of it; at the low end, the persona systematically blind to that edge, sure where it should doubt and blank to its own gaps.
It is the persona's metacognitive sensitivity as a settled disposition -- how well, in general, its confidence discriminates what it has from what it lacks -- and it varies across domains, a persona sharp about one kind of knowing and dull about another.

## Orders

Run from here, into every act of monitoring.
The metacognitively skilled persona meets a question about its own knowing with a reading already close to true -- it doubts where it is weak and trusts where it is strong, so its steering lands its effort well; the metacognitively blind persona meets the same question with a reading systematically off, confident across the board or anxious across the board, so its effort scatters.
It sets the baseline accuracy of every self-read, before any single judgment of learning or feeling of knowing.

## Loses

At the high end, the ease of unexamined confidence; at the low end, the effort that a true read would have placed well.
The finely calibrated persona pays in the friction of self-doubt -- it must hold its own knowing in question, and can under-trust what it in fact commands; the blind persona keeps the comfort of certainty but pays in effort spent on the mastered and withheld from the unlearned, and in the failures its misplaced confidence walks it into.
What each trades is the other's good -- untroubled certainty against accurate self-knowledge, across the same standing sensitivity.

## Drives

On that standing skill:
rides it -- the skilled persona reads itself true and steers well as a matter of course, the blind one misreads and missteers; or works against it, the blind persona learning to distrust its flat confidence and check against the world, the skilled one learning where its sensitivity fails and leaning on external markers there.
The sensitivity holds underneath either way, a resting level of self-knowledge the persona can train and lean on more than a state that merely comes and goes.
