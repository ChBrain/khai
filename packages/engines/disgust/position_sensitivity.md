# Position: Sensitivity

## Taxonomy

Affect / the trait of disgust sensitivity -- the standing readiness beneath [Disgust](process_disgust.md) (the Disgust Scale; the Three-Domain Disgust Scale).

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: disgust

## Has

A standing threshold and reach for disgust -- how easily the persona is revolted, and how widely.
A trait, stable across time and partly heritable, that a persona carries before any object arrives: the low threshold that finds more of the world offensive, or the strong stomach that finds less.
It runs at the domain level too, so pathogen, sexual, and sociomoral sensitivities can dissociate -- squeamish at gore yet unmoved by taboo, or the reverse.

## Orders

Run from here, ahead of the object.
The sensitivity sets how quick the recoil, how far the contamination is felt to spread, and how much the persona's judgment borrows disgust's absolutism -- and the tilt reaches past the moment: higher disgust sensitivity tracks harsher moral condemnation, more disease-avoidant behavior, more contamination-based anxiety (the phobias, the washing of OCD), and more purity-valuing, tradition-guarding social attitudes.
It colours the read before the event, the way a mood does, but on the single axis of the offensive.

## Loses

The option of meeting the world with an even stomach.
The highly disgust-sensitive persona discovers that more of the world arrives already tainted -- fewer things it can eat, touch, or forgive, more contacts that spoil, more people it must hold at a distance -- and there is no neutral surface on which to meet the offensive plainly.
What is lost is latitude: the margin of the tolerable narrows.

## Drives

On the loss of that latitude:
rides the sensitivity -- lets the low threshold guard and narrow, trusting the recoil -- or works against it, by habituation, exposure, and reappraisal, widening the tolerable at a cost in vigilance.
The threshold holds underneath either way, a set point the persona manages rather than moves.
