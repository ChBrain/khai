# Process: Disgust

## Taxonomy

Emotion / the psychology of rejection and contamination-avoidance.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: disgust

## Initiated by

A persona meets something it appraises as offensive to take in, touch, or be near -- a potential contaminant -- and recoils.
This is disgust: oral in origin, the guardian of the mouth (Darwin, Rozin) -- revulsion, the wrinkled nose and gape, the turn of the stomach, and the impulse to expel what is in and withdraw from what is out.
Where anger says _you have wronged me_ and contempt says _you are beneath me_, disgust says _you contaminate me_ -- and it answers not with attack or dismissal but with rejection and distance.

## Direction

Away from the offending object, and toward a boundary restored.
Disgust does not seek to defeat the thing or to lower it in rank; it seeks to be rid of it and unmarked by it -- to spit out, to wash off, to step back, to bar the way in.
The aim is separation: the pure kept from the impure, the self kept clean of a taint felt to spread.

## Lever

The sense of contamination -- that the object carries a polluting essence which passes by contact and lingers.
What disgust spends is proximity: the tolerance to be near, to touch, to ingest is withdrawn, and the object is treated as though its mere contact could transfer its nature.
This is what sets disgust apart from every other rejection -- its object is not merely unwanted but felt to _spread_ and to _cling_, so a brief touch taints and no small dose is safe.

## Echo

The object is cast out and avoided -- and, because the contamination logic overreaches, more is cast out than the threat requires.
Disgust generalizes past its warrant: it clings to the once-touched, dominates the mixed (a drop of sewage spoils the wine, never the reverse), and recruits readily into the judgment of persons and acts, where it dehumanizes and forecloses repair more coldly than anger.
It runs across three domains -- [pathogen](process_pathogen.md), [sexual](process_sexual.md), and [sociomoral](process_sociomoral.md) -- by the [contamination](process_contamination.md) mechanism, and it is tuned by a standing [sensitivity](position_sensitivity.md). The appraisal taxonomy of the basic emotions is the emotion engine's; the downward look at an inferior is contempt's, its sibling in the CAD triad; the exclusion of the tainted person is stigma's; the moral judgment proper is moral-judgment's. This engine owns the rejection response itself.
