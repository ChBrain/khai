# Disgust: Reference

## Line of Work

Disgust as a **multi-type** engine: the rejection-and-contamination response through which a persona recoils from an offending object -- revulsion, the impulse to expel or withdraw, and the sense of a taint that spreads and clings -- taken across its functional domains, its distinctive mechanism, and the trait that tunes it. The domain does not model the appraisal taxonomy of the basic emotions, which is the emotion engine's; nor the cold downward look at an inferior, which is contempt's, disgust's sibling in the CAD triad; nor the social exclusion of the tainted person, which is stigma's; nor the reasoned appraisal of right and wrong, which is moral-judgment's. It models the recoil itself: the appraised contaminant, the revulsion and distancing it triggers, and the contamination logic that makes disgust unlike any other rejection.

The spine is the psychology of disgust, from its oral origin to its functional domains. Disgust began as the guardian of the mouth (Darwin; Rozin, Haidt & McCauley) -- a food-rejection reflex, revulsion at the potential contaminant -- and broadened into a general system for rejecting offending objects. Tybur, Lieberman and Griskevicius resolve that system into three functional domains keyed to three adaptive problems: pathogen disgust (disease avoidance), sexual disgust (mate choice), and moral disgust (social cooperation). What makes disgust singular among the emotions is its contamination logic -- the laws of sympathetic magic (Frazer; Rozin & Nemeroff), contagion and similarity, by which a taint transfers on brief contact and lingers indelibly, dose-insensitive and resistant to reason. Individual differences in disgust sensitivity (the Disgust Scale; the Three-Domain Disgust Scale) are stable, partly heritable, and predict moral judgment, disease behavior, contamination anxiety, and purity-valuing social attitudes. The altitudes are those: the response, its three domains, its mechanism, and its trait.

## Restrictions

What the engine refuses to model, and to whom it delegates.

- **Emotion**: The engine models disgust in its own depth -- the rejection response, its domains, its mechanism, its trait -- not its place in the taxonomy of the basic emotions. The appraisal structure by which an event becomes joy, sadness, anger, fear, or disgust is owned by the emotion engine; this engine unfolds the one node the emotion taxonomy names disgust. The two compose: emotion keeps disgust as one basic affect, this engine unfolds it.
- **Contempt**: The engine models the contamination-rejection of what revolts, not the cold downward look at an inferior. Contempt -- the placing of another beneath the level at which they merit consideration, regard withdrawn -- is owned by the contempt engine; disgust is its sibling in the CAD triad (Divinity to contempt's Community), and the two are distinct affects the moral emotions recruit together, but contempt owns the look-down and disgust owns the recoil-from-taint.
- **Stigma**: The engine models the disgust response, including where pathogen and moral disgust turn onto persons, but not the social mark and exclusion that result. The spoiled identity, the discrediting, and the structural exclusion of the tainted person are owned by the stigma engine; disgust supplies the visceral rejection that feeds stigma, but the marking of persons is stigma's to model.
- **Moral judgment**: The engine models the moral recoil -- the felt taint and its expulsion -- not the reasoned appraisal of right and wrong. Which foundation a persona reads a transgression through, and the judgment itself, are owned by the moral-judgment engine; sociomoral disgust lends that judgment its absolutism and contamination logic, but the judging is moral-judgment's.
- **Mortality**: The engine models pathogen and animal-reminder revulsion as disease-and-contamination avoidance, not the management of death-awareness. The terror of mortality and the defenses raised against it are owned by the mortality engine; disgust at gore and decay may brush death, but this engine keeps the contamination response, not the existential one.

## Encoding

Source to constraint, per file.

- **[disgust](process_disgust.md)** (the root, process): The rejection response -- revulsion, expulsion, withdrawal, and the sense of a spreading, clinging taint. Anchored by Rozin, Haidt & McCauley (core disgust, the oral origin) and Rozin & Fallon.
- **[pathogen](process_pathogen.md)** (process, domain): Disease avoidance -- the behavioral immune system reading infection cues and pulling the body clear, biased toward false alarms. Anchored by Schaller & Park and Curtis (disgust as disease-avoidance).
- **[sexual](process_sexual.md)** (process, domain): Mate choice -- recoil from reproductively costly partners and acts, calibrated and sex-differentiated, kin-tuned by the Westermarck effect. Anchored by Tybur et al. and Lieberman (incest avoidance).
- **[sociomoral](process_sociomoral.md)** (process, domain): The moral order -- violations of purity and sanctity met as contamination, condemnation that dehumanizes and forecloses repair. Anchored by Rozin et al. (CAD) and Tybur et al. (moral disgust).
- **[contamination](process_contamination.md)** (process, mechanism): The laws of sympathetic magic -- contagion and similarity, negativity dominance, indelibility -- that make disgust spread, cling, and resist reason. Anchored by Rozin & Nemeroff and Frazer.
- **[sensitivity](position_sensitivity.md)** (position, trait): Disgust sensitivity -- the standing, partly heritable threshold and reach that tilts moral judgment, disease behavior, contamination anxiety, and purity attitudes. Anchored by Haidt, McCauley & Rozin (the Disgust Scale) and Tybur et al. (the Three-Domain Disgust Scale).
