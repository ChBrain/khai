# Process: Pathogen

## Taxonomy

[Disgust](process_disgust.md), the disease-avoidance domain.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: disgust

## Initiated by

The persona meets a cue of infection -- spoiled food, feces, vomit, pus, blood, rotting flesh, vermin, the visibly ill -- and recoils before any harm is done.
This is pathogen disgust, the oldest and most cross-culturally universal face of the emotion (Curtis, Rozin): the behavioral immune system (Schaller) reading the world for contagion and pulling the body clear of it.
It is disease-avoidance run forward of the disease -- the reflex that guarded the mouth and the skin long before germs were named.

## Direction

Away from the source of infection, and the boundary of the body kept.
Pathogen disgust rejects the tainted food, avoids the sick, refuses the contact -- it works to keep what carries disease outside the perimeter of ingestion and touch.
The direction is prophylactic: not to cure but to never catch, to err on the side of the clean.

## Lever

Perceived infection risk, read cheaply and answered strongly.
The disease-avoidance system runs on the smoke-detector principle: because the cost of one missed pathogen dwarfs the cost of a hundred needless avoidances, it is tuned to false alarms -- a faint whiff, an odd texture, a foreign look is enough.
What it spends is the openness to eat, touch, and approach, withdrawn on thin evidence in exchange for a wide margin of safety.

## Echo

Disease is avoided -- at the price of over-avoidance, and of harm done by the false alarms.
The same cheap, greedy detector that shuns the rotten also shuns the healthy-but-abnormal, the disfigured, the elderly, the foreign, coding difference itself as contagion; the spill from disease-avoidance into the exclusion of persons is where pathogen disgust feeds prejudice, which is the stigma engine's to own.
This engine keeps the disease-avoidance response; where it turns onto people and hardens into their exclusion, it hands off.
