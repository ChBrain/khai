# Process: Sociomoral

## Taxonomy

[Disgust](process_disgust.md), the moral domain.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: disgust

## Initiated by

The persona meets a violation of the social and moral order -- betrayal, cruelty, hypocrisy, degradation, the act felt as base or impure -- and recoils as from a contaminant.
This is sociomoral disgust: the emotion lent to morality, guarding what Rozin's CAD model calls the ethics of divinity -- purity, sanctity, the ordered against the debased.
Where anger answers a wrong to autonomy and contempt a lapse of community, disgust answers a defilement -- the violator not merely blamed but felt to be tainted.

## Direction

Away from and against the violator, cast out as unclean.
Sociomoral disgust does not seek redress or a squared account; it seeks separation and expulsion -- the offender shunned, the act treated as staining, the tainted person put outside the moral community.
The direction is toward a purity restored by removal, not a wrong repaired by restitution.

## Lever

The appraisal of moral impurity, run through the contamination logic.
What sociomoral disgust lends the judgment is disgust's own signature -- absolutism, dose-insensitivity, indelibility: the violator is wholly tainted, not partly at fault; one act contaminates the whole person; the stain does not wash off with apology.
Whether this is core disgust co-opted or a metaphor hardened into intuition is debated (Rozin; Kelly; Pizarro), but its effect is settled -- it makes moral judgment harsher, faster, and less open to reason than anger alone.

## Echo

The violator is expelled, and the judgment is foreclosed.
Sociomoral disgust dehumanizes where anger does not: to be found disgusting is to be cast out of the circle of moral consideration, and it drives the politics of purity, contempt for whole categories, and the refusal of repair.
This engine keeps the moral recoil -- the felt taint and its expulsion; the reasoned appraisal of right and wrong is the moral-judgment engine's, the downward look at the lesser is contempt's, and the social exclusion that results is stigma's. Disgust supplies the contamination the judgment rides.
