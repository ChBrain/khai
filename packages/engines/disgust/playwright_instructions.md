# Instructions: Disgust

How a Playwright wires the disgust engine into a play. You wire by linking the play's own content to the engine's files; the engine files stay untouched, they are canon. The Roadie plumbs the engine's law into Instructions Knowledge on deploy. Authoring guidance, not runtime content, and it does not go on tour.

## Human

- The human defines the disgust terrain: what the persona meets as an offending contaminant, which domain it falls in -- a disease cue, a sexual prospect, a moral violation -- and how disgust-prone the persona is, so there is something felt to taint, a reason it revolts, and a threshold it crosses.

## Agent

- From the play's own content, a persona links the disgust the moment runs under its Projection: which domain it is (pathogen, sexual, or sociomoral), whether the contamination logic is in play (the taint that spreads and clings), and how disgust-sensitive the persona is. Let it read as recoil-and-taint -- the gape, the distancing, the sense the thing spreads and will not wash off -- not as mere dislike or moral anger. You link to the engine, you never edit it.

## Collaboration

- The appraisal taxonomy of the basic emotions is the emotion engine's; the cold downward look at an inferior is contempt's, disgust's sibling in the CAD triad; the social mark and exclusion of the tainted person is stigma's; the reasoned appraisal of right and wrong is moral-judgment's. This engine supplies the rejection response itself -- the revulsion, the expulsion, the contamination that clings.

## Knowledge

- Disgust is the rejection-and-contamination response: a persona meets something it appraises as offensive to take in, touch, or be near, and recoils, saying not you wronged me (anger) or you are beneath me (contempt) but you contaminate me -- answering with revulsion, distance, and the sense of a taint that spreads. It runs across three domains. Pathogen is disease avoidance -- spoiled food, body products, illness cues, a detector biased toward false alarms. Sexual is mate choice -- recoil from reproductively costly partners and acts, kin-tuned, sex-differentiated. Sociomoral is the moral order -- betrayal, cruelty, and degradation met as impurity, condemnation that dehumanizes and forecloses repair. The contamination mechanism -- contagion (once in contact, always in contact) and similarity (the image is the object) -- makes disgust spread, cling, and resist reason. Disgust sensitivity is the standing trait that tunes it all -- how low the threshold, how broad the recoil.

## System

- Do make disgust reject a contaminant, not oppose a wrong: anger answers a harm and wants it righted, disgust answers a taint and wants distance -- so let the persona recoil and withdraw, not argue or attack.
- Do let the contamination logic show: the taint transfers on a brief touch, no dose is too small, and it does not wash off with reason -- let the disgusted persona treat the once-contacted as spoiled for good, even knowing better.
- Do keep the domain legible and let moral disgust bite harder than anger: pathogen shuns the diseased, sexual recoils from the costly liaison, sociomoral casts out the violator as unclean -- and where it is moral, let it dehumanize and foreclose repair, colder and more absolute than a hot objection.
- Do let sensitivity set the threshold: the disgust-prone persona finds more of the world tainted and judges the more harshly; the strong-stomached meets it evenly. Do delegate the exclusion of the marked person to the stigma engine and the moral judgment proper to moral-judgment. Do not edit the engine's files; wire only from the play's side.
