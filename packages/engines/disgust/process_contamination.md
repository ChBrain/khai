# Process: Contamination

## Taxonomy

[Disgust](process_disgust.md), the mechanism.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: disgust

## Initiated by

An object the persona holds pure comes into contact -- real, remembered, or merely resembled -- with something it holds foul, and the purity is felt to be lost.
This is contamination: the sympathetic-magic logic (Frazer; Rozin & Nemeroff) that governs how disgust spreads, running on two laws -- contagion (once in contact, always in contact) and similarity (the image is the object).
A sterilized cockroach dropped in juice still spoils it; fudge shaped like feces still repels; a laundered sweater once worn by a killer still will not go on.

## Direction

To keep the pure apart from the impure, and to treat what they have touched as spoiled for good.
Contamination gives disgust its reach through space and time: the direction is not just away from the foul object but away from everything it has brushed, and the spoiling is one-way and near-permanent -- the contaminant taints the pure, never the pure purifies the contaminant.
It works to seal the clean off entirely, because any contact is total.

## Lever

The intuition of a transferable, dose-insensitive, indelible essence.
What contamination spends is proportion and reversibility: no dose is too small to taint (a trace is enough), no dilution undoes it, and no reasoning dispels it -- the persona knows the roach was sterile and recoils anyway, because the logic runs beneath belief.
This is negativity dominance -- the foul overpowers the pure on contact -- and it is what makes disgust uniquely resistant to correction.

## Echo

Disgust reaches where no other emotion does -- across a touch, a resemblance, a history -- and overreaches accordingly.
The mechanism is the engine of every domain's excess: the pathogen shunned in the healthy who merely resemble the sick, the partner tainted by association, the moral violator whose stain spreads to all they touch and never lifts.
It is what makes disgust both a keen guard and a blunt one: the same law that keeps real contagion out keeps out the harmless, the once-fallen, the forever-marked -- and hands the marking of persons on to the stigma engine.
