# Process: The Sugar

## Taxonomy

[The Substrate](process_substrate_fermentation.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: fermentation

## Initiated by

There is energy in the material that nothing has yet spent.
Simple sugars, starches that can be broken into them, or lactose in milk.

## Direction

Toward a store that everything present can use and that will be used by something.
Fermentation in the strict sense is the extraction of energy from that store without oxygen, which is a poor way to do it: an organism working anaerobically gets a fraction of what it would get by respiring, and it discards most of the energy in the waste it leaves behind.
That waste is the point. Alcohol, lactic acid, acetic acid, carbon dioxide -- the products people ferment for are all things an organism could not be bothered to finish digesting.

## Lever

The inefficiency of the metabolism against the usefulness of what it leaves.
The carrying force is that the inefficiency is what makes the process valuable and an efficient one would be worthless: complete oxidation would leave water and carbon dioxide and nothing to eat, and every product a person wants out of a ferment is an intermediate that was abandoned; the resistance is that the same inefficiency means the store lasts a long time and stays attractive, so a ferment is a resource that is still worth invading long after it has started.
The Sugar is spent badly on purpose, and the leavings are the food.

## Echo

An energy store being half-used.
It shows why fermentation produces something rather than merely reducing something.
The Sugar is dated to the ripeness of the material.
