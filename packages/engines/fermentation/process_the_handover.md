# Process: The Handover

## Taxonomy

[The Succession](process_succession_fermentation.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: fermentation

## Initiated by

The population changes without anything being done.
The bubbling slows, the smell shifts, and a different organism is now dominant.

## Direction

Toward a replacement caused by the waste of whoever is being replaced.
The acid that the first group produced is tolerated better by the second, which multiplies in conditions the first can no longer manage, and produces more of the same acid until it too becomes marginal. In an alcoholic ferment the same logic runs on ethanol, and in vinegar it runs a third time on acetic acid.
The practitioner does not schedule any of this and cannot speed it up except by temperature.

## Lever

The autonomy of the sequence against the practitioner's exclusion from it.
The carrying force is that the autonomy is the practice's greatest asset: nothing has to be timed, no organism has to be introduced at the right moment, and the vessel proceeds correctly while the household is away; the resistance is that the same autonomy means the only controls available are the ones set at the beginning and the temperature of the room, so a ferment going somewhere unwanted cannot be steered, only stopped.
The Handover is a change of tenant arranged by the outgoing one.

## Echo

A vessel under new management, on schedule and unattended.
It shows where the practitioner's authority ends, which is at the lid.
The Handover is dated to a change in the smell.
