# Process: The Line Drawn

## Taxonomy

[The Turn](process_turn_fermentation.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: fermentation

## Initiated by

Somebody says it is ready.
Or that it has gone too far, which is the same sentence at a different hour.

## Direction

Toward a boundary that lives in a cuisine rather than in the vessel.
Cheese is mould that a tradition learned to want, and the mould does not know which side of that it is on. The same is true of the film on a soy sauce, the funk of a long-aged ham, the sourness of a bread that a different country would have thrown out, and the smell of a fish sauce that half the world regards as spoilage.
None of these are cases of somebody being wrong. They are cases of the material offering no opinion.

## Lever

The competence of a trained palate against what the material actually contains.
The carrying force is that the judgement is skilled and reliable within its tradition: an experienced maker knows the point exactly, the agreement inside a cuisine is close, and there is nothing relativistic about a person who can tell a good batch from a bad one at ten paces; the resistance is that the skill is calibration to a convention, and the convention is what supplies the endpoint the chemistry does not -- so two competent judges from different traditions will disagree about a jar and neither will be making an error.
The Line Drawn is a real skill applied to a boundary the process does not contain.

## Echo

A verdict with nothing in the jar to check it against.
It shows what the engine means by directed spoilage, which is that the direction is the only thing added.
The Line Drawn is dated to a taste and varies by kitchen.
