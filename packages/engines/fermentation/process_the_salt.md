# Process: The Salt

## Taxonomy

[The Setting](process_setting_fermentation.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: fermentation

## Initiated by

Salt goes in, by weight, at a low percentage.
Two per cent, three, five for a long keep.

## Direction

Toward water that is more expensive to use.
Salt does not poison anything at these levels. It lowers water activity: it binds water so that an organism has to work harder to draw it in, and the cost of that work is paid differently by different species.
Lactic acid bacteria are unusually salt-tolerant. Many of the organisms that cause soft rot are not. So the salt does not remove the rot; it puts a tax on the whole vessel that the wanted population can afford and the unwanted one struggles with.

## Lever

The tolerance the process depends on against how narrow the useful band is.
The carrying force is that the mechanism is robust and forgiving in practice: the band that works is wide enough for a household to hit by eye, salt is cheap and stable, the effect is immediate, and the same handful also draws water out of the vegetable and makes the brine the process needs; the resistance is that the band has a bottom and a top -- too little and the tax is not high enough to matter, too much and the wanted population is taxed out along with everything else, leaving a jar that does nothing at all.
The Salt is a price rather than a poison.

## Echo

A vessel in which living is dearer for everybody and dearest for the wrong ones.
It shows the engine's method in its simplest form.
The Salt is dated to the weighing and does not change afterwards.
