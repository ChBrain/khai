# Process: The Acid Made

## Taxonomy

[The Succession](process_succession_fermentation.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: fermentation

## Initiated by

The pH falls.
Slowly at first, then quickly, then it stops falling.

## Direction

Toward a hurdle that the vessel produced itself.
The acid is the only condition in the whole arrangement that nobody added. It is a waste product, and it is the most decisive factor present: below a certain pH the great majority of spoilage organisms cannot function, which is why an established ferment is far safer than a new one.
So the vessel begins in its most vulnerable state and manufactures its own protection over the following days, using the resource it was trying to protect.

## Lever

The strength of the protection against the fact that it arrives late.
The carrying force is that self-acidification is the most elegant part of the process and needs no monitoring: the ferment builds a barrier that is stronger than anything the practitioner applied, it does so from the material itself, and once it is up the vessel will keep for a very long time with no further attention; the resistance is that the barrier does not exist at the start, so the days when the vessel is most exposed are the days when the least is defending it -- the salt and the closed lid are holding the ground alone until the acid arrives.
The Acid Made is the vessel producing the condition that will protect it.

## Echo

A hurdle built out of the food.
It shows why the first three days of a ferment are the ones that go wrong.
The Acid Made is dated from day two and levels off within weeks.
