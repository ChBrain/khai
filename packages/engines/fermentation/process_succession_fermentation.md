# Process: The Succession

## Taxonomy

[Fermentation](process_fermentation.md), the sequence of populations that follow each other.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: fermentation

## Initiated by

One organism gets going, and then a different one does.
The Succession is that sequence: [a first bloom](process_the_first_bloom.md) that starts the acid, [acid made](process_the_acid_made.md) that changes the vessel, and [a handover](process_the_handover.md) to whatever the change now suits.

## Direction

Toward a series in which each population makes the vessel unfit for itself.
The species that begins a vegetable ferment is not the species that finishes it. The first organisms are relatively salt-tolerant and relatively poor acid producers; they drop the pH a little, and the drop favours a second group that is more acid-tolerant and more productive, which drops it further and displaces the first.
Nobody manages the handovers. Each one is caused by the waste of the population being handed over from.

## Lever

The self-driving quality of the sequence against the fact that it consumes its own conditions.
The carrying force is that the succession needs no intervention and this is what makes the practice possible for anybody: the vessel is not tended, corrected, or dosed, the changeovers happen on their own schedule, and a household that does nothing for three weeks gets the same result as a laboratory; the resistance is that every stage is terminated by its own product, so nothing in the vessel is stable and there is no point at which the ferment has arrived and will stay.
The Succession runs itself and cannot hold still.

## Echo

A sequence of populations, each ended by what it made.
It is the movement that does all the work and receives none of the attention.
The Succession is dated from the first bubbles and takes days to months.
