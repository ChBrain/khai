# Process: The Substrate

## Taxonomy

[Fermentation](process_fermentation.md), what is present before anybody does anything.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: fermentation

## Initiated by

There is material, and there is already life on it.
The Substrate is three facts arriving together: [sugar](process_the_sugar.md) available as energy, [a wild load](process_the_wild_load.md) of organisms already there, and [a wound](process_the_wound.md) that puts the two in contact.

## Direction

Toward a race that has started whether or not anybody intends one.
Nothing is added at this stage and nothing needs to be. The cabbage carries its own bacteria, the grape carries its own yeast, the flour carries both, and the moment the material is cut, crushed, or wetted the sugars inside become available to all of them at once.
From that moment the only question is which population gets the resource, and every hour of delay is an hour somebody else is using.

## Lever

The abundance of the starting material against the fact that it is already contested.
The carrying force is that the substrate is generous and the process is not fragile: there is far more sugar than any single population can use quickly, the organisms that do the wanted work are almost always present in useful numbers, and nothing has to be introduced for the thing to begin; the resistance is that the same abundance is available to the rot, so the material cannot be left alone -- it will be fermented or it will be spoiled, and doing nothing selects the second.
The Substrate is a resource with several claimants and no default winner.

## Echo

A cut vegetable with a clock running.
It is the movement that makes clear the intervention is never sterilisation, because sterilisation was never on the table.
The Substrate is dated to the knife.
