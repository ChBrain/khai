# Process: The First Bloom

## Taxonomy

[The Succession](process_succession_fermentation.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: fermentation

## Initiated by

Something starts multiplying fast.
Bubbles at the surface, cloudiness in the brine, a smell that is not the smell of the vegetable.

## Direction

Toward an early population that is not the one anybody wants at the end.
The organisms that get going first are the ones best suited to the conditions as they are at hour zero: still near neutral, still with traces of oxygen, still comparatively hospitable. They are usually modest acid producers and they are usually gone by the end.
Their contribution is to make the vessel slightly worse for themselves, which is the only thing that needs to happen.

## Lever

The importance of a fast start against the unsuitability of who provides it.
The carrying force is that speed at the beginning is the whole safety margin: the sooner something is multiplying, the sooner the resource is claimed and the acid begins, and a sluggish start is the condition under which vessels fail; the resistance is that the fast starters are not the organisms whose products anybody is after, so the ferment's most urgent phase is conducted by a population whose only job is to be replaced.
The First Bloom is the wrong organism doing the necessary work.

## Echo

An early population that will not be in the final jar.
It shows that the useful sequence begins with something nobody would have chosen.
The First Bloom is dated to the first day or two.
