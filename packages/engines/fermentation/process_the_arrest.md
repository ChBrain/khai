# Process: The Arrest

## Taxonomy

[The Turn](process_turn_fermentation.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: fermentation

## Initiated by

The ferment is stopped.
Chilled, heated, dried, bottled, or eaten.

## Direction

Toward a pause rather than a conclusion.
Refrigeration slows the succession to a crawl and does not end it; pasteurisation ends it and takes the live culture with it; drying suspends it until water returns. Eating it is the most complete method available and the only one that resolves nothing about the process.
Whatever is chosen, the material is still fermentable and the population is still present or replaceable, which is why an opened jar in a warm room resumes exactly where it left off.

## Lever

The effectiveness of the available stops against what each one costs.
The carrying force is that stopping works well enough for every practical purpose: cold holds a ferment at a chosen point for months, heat makes it stable on a shelf for years, and both are cheap and reliable; the resistance is that the two useful properties are traded against each other -- the version that keeps longest is the one with nothing living in it, and the version that is most alive is the one that will not hold still.
The Arrest chooses between a ferment that lasts and a ferment that is working.

## Echo

A process paused at somebody's convenience.
It shows that the endpoint is imposed from outside in the physical sense as well as the judgemental one.
The Arrest is dated to the refrigerator and is undone by a warm afternoon.
