# Process: The Wound

## Taxonomy

[The Substrate](process_substrate_fermentation.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: fermentation

## Initiated by

The material is cut, crushed, bruised, or wetted.
And what was inside it is now outside it.

## Direction

Toward contact between a resource and the organisms that were only ever on the surface.
An intact cabbage keeps for months, not because nothing is on it but because the sugars are inside the cells and the bacteria are outside them. Shredding, salting, crushing, or milling breaks that separation, and the same organisms that sat harmlessly on the skin for weeks are suddenly in a nutrient solution.
Every ferment begins with an injury to the material, and the practitioner performs it deliberately.

## Lever

The necessity of the damage against what the damage releases.
The carrying force is that the contact must be made and there is no gentler route: the process needs the sugars available, whole fruit ferments only where the skin has been broken, and the crushing is the same act in a wine press, a mash tun, and a bowl of salted cabbage; the resistance is that the injury is indiscriminate, so the wanted and the unwanted organisms reach the resource at the same instant, and every hour after the cut is contested.
The Wound starts the process and starts the spoilage together.

## Echo

A separation that has been undone.
It shows why timing matters from the first minute and not from some later stage.
The Wound is dated to the moment of cutting and cannot be reversed.
