# Process: The Wild Load

## Taxonomy

[The Substrate](process_substrate_fermentation.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: fermentation

## Initiated by

The organisms are already on it.
On the leaf surface, in the flour, on the skin of the fruit, on the hands, in the air of the room.

## Direction

Toward a starting population nobody assembled and nobody can inspect.
A head of cabbage carries lactic acid bacteria, yeasts, moulds, and a full complement of things that cause soft rot, in proportions that vary by field, season, and weather. None of it is visible and none of it is chosen.
A starter culture changes the proportions rather than the cast: it puts a large number of one organism in at the beginning so that the race is decided early, which is a bias applied to a contest that was going to happen anyway.

## Lever

The sufficiency of what is already present against the impossibility of knowing what it is.
The carrying force is that the wild load is usually enough and the tradition depends on it: households fermented for millennia with no cultures, the organisms needed are ubiquitous on the material they belong to, and a practice that required a laboratory would never have spread; the resistance is that the same load contains everything that would ruin the batch, in unknown proportions, so the practitioner is not adding life to a clean substrate -- they are tilting a competition whose competitors they cannot see.
The Wild Load is the whole cast, present from the beginning.

## Echo

A population that arrived with the ingredients.
It shows that the intervention is never introduction, only advantage.
The Wild Load is dated to the field and varies with the season.
