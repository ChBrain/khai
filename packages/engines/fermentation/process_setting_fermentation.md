# Process: The Setting

## Taxonomy

[Fermentation](process_fermentation.md), the conditions imposed and what they actually do.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: fermentation

## Initiated by

Somebody adds salt, closes a lid, and puts the vessel somewhere.
The Setting is the whole intervention: [salt](process_the_salt.md) that prices the water, [shut air](process_the_shut_air.md) that removes an advantage, and [hurdles](process_the_hurdles.md) that are lethal only in combination.

## Direction

Toward an environment that is survivable by a minority of what is present.
Not one of these conditions kills anything outright at the levels used. Two per cent salt is tolerated by a great many organisms; an anaerobic vessel is comfortable for plenty of them; a cool room slows everything down without stopping it.
What the conditions do is change the ranking. The organisms that will make the ferment are ordinary performers in open conditions and unusually good ones here, and that relative advantage is the entire technology.

## Lever

The mildness of each condition against the decisiveness of the whole.
The carrying force is that mild conditions are what make the practice possible and severe ones would defeat it: enough salt to sterilise would be inedible, enough heat would cook the material, and the organisms doing the work are living things that have to survive the same environment they are being favoured in; the resistance is that mildness means nothing is excluded, so the vessel always contains its own failure and the margin is competitive rather than absolute.
The Setting does not defend the jar; it rigs the contest inside it.

## Echo

A set of conditions that would look like negligence at any other stage of food handling.
It is the movement where the whole intervention happens, and it takes about four minutes.
The Setting is dated to the closing of the vessel.
