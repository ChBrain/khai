# Process: The Hurdles

## Taxonomy

[The Setting](process_setting_fermentation.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: fermentation

## Initiated by

Several mild conditions are applied at once.
Salt and no air and a cool room and, shortly, acid.

## Direction

Toward a combination that no single factor could achieve.
This is the principle the preservation literature calls hurdle technology: an organism can usually adapt to one adverse condition by spending energy on it, and cannot spend that energy twice. Low water activity alone is survivable; low water activity with no oxygen and a falling pH is not, for most of what is present.
Each hurdle is set well below the level that would work on its own, which is why the food remains edible.

## Lever

The gentleness each factor is allowed against what their stacking does.
The carrying force is that stacking is what makes preservation compatible with palatability: any single hurdle raised to a lethal level would ruin the food, the combination lets each stay mild, and this is the reason a traditional method arrived at by trial matches what the modelling recommends; the resistance is that the hurdles are only jointly sufficient, so removing any one of them silently returns the vessel to a contest -- and the removal is usually accidental, a warm week or a lid left off.
The Hurdles work together or not at all.

## Echo

Four conditions, none of them enough.
It shows why the process cannot be reduced to its most visible ingredient.
The Hurdles are dated to the setting and one of them keeps rising by itself.
