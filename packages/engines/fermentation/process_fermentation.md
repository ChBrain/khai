# Process: Fermentation

## Taxonomy

Directed spoilage (no khai file of its own yet).

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: fermentation

## Initiated by

Something edible is left in conditions that favour one kind of rot over the others.
Fermentation starts wherever a substrate with sugars in it is held under a set of chosen conditions: cabbage under salt, milk under a culture, dough in a warm kitchen, grape juice in a sealed vessel, beans under a mould, tea under a mat of bacteria and yeast.
It is not matter losing the order imposed on it, which is decay's, and not degradation through use, which is wear's. It is decay pointed somewhere, and everything that follows from the pointing being the only intervention.

## Direction

Toward an outcome that is wanted, produced by the same machinery that would have ruined it.
The engine reads four movements: [the substrate](process_substrate_fermentation.md), what is present before anybody does anything; [the setting](process_setting_fermentation.md), the conditions imposed and what they actually do; [the succession](process_succession_fermentation.md), the sequence of populations that follow each other; and [the turn](process_turn_fermentation.md), the twist -- where it stops being wanted, and who decides.
The four run once per vessel and hand the next vessel its beginning.

## Lever

The reliability of the result against the fact that nothing was kept out.
The carrying force is that fermentation is one of the most dependable processes in the material world and has fed people for ten thousand years without any understanding of what was happening: the same cabbage under the same salt at the same temperature gives the same sauerkraut, the method survived every civilisation that used it, and it needs no equipment, no sterility, and no expertise; the resistance is that none of that reliability comes from exclusion -- the organisms that would have spoiled it are present the whole time, on the leaves, in the air, on the hands, and they are outrun rather than removed.
Fermentation is read on what was favoured, never on what was prevented.

## Echo

A jar of something that would be thrown away if one condition had been different.
Fermentation does not close; it is decomposition under direction, gathered into one reading -- a substrate, a setting, a succession, a turn.
The engine's twist is here. **A ferment is not defended from rot; it is rotted first by something you chose.** Preservation here is occupation rather than exclusion: salt, acid, and the absence of air do not kill what would spoil the food, they make the vessel expensive for it and cheap for something else, and the something else takes the ground and holds it. The organisms that would have ruined it are still present, still viable, and still waiting, and the only reason the jar is food is that a population got there first and made the place unfit for them.
It hands off what it is not: matter losing its imposed order is decay's; degradation through use is wear's; the transformation of a substance by heat is combustion's and fire's; the gathering that eats the result is meal's; and a jar staged to **stand for** a theme is authored key, the play's pitch.
The vessel is dated from the hour it was closed.
