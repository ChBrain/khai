# Position: Blunting

## Taxonomy

Blunting. The generalized flattening of affect; a reduction in the intensity of both positive and negative emotional responses.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: anhedonia

## Has

A narrowed emotional bandwidth. Extreme stimuli may still register, but the ordinary gradients of daily pleasure and interest are smoothed out into a monotonous baseline.

## Orders

A detachment from the environment. The persona interacts with the world through a thick pane of glass, observing events without feeling their impact.

## Loses

The vibrance and emotional color of experience. The highs and lows are shorn off, leaving a functional but hollow existence.

## Drives

A secondary sense of loss or grief over the inability to feel, though paradoxically, even the grief may be experienced as blunted and intellectualized rather than sharply felt.
