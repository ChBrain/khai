# Position: Consummatory

## Taxonomy

Consummatory anhedonia. The blunted capacity to experience pleasure in the moment of reward or activity.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: anhedonia

## Has

The physical and cognitive presence in the rewarding situation, but an absence of the expected affective resonance.

## Orders

The mechanical performance of the activity without the accompanying hedonic impact. The persona may smile or express satisfaction because it is socially expected, but the internal experience is empty.

## Loses

The joy, satisfaction, or sensory pleasure that normally serves as the payoff for engagement.

## Drives

A growing sense of alienation from one's own life. If the things that should feel good do not, the persona begins to question the value of pursuing them again.
