# Position: Consummatory Anhedonia

## Taxonomy

[Anhedonia](process_anhedonia.md), the failure at the fact -- present at the thing, and the resonance does not arrive.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: anhedonia

## Has

The second form, and the one the persona notices first because it happens in front of them. Everything works: they get there, they are attentive, they perceive the thing accurately and in detail. What does not happen is the return. Ribot's original description is still the best short account -- the persona is not in pain, they are not sad about the meal, there is simply no hedonic weight where weight should be, and the experience arrives flat rather than negative. The distinction matters for staging, because a flat experience does not produce the signs a bad one would: there is nothing to flinch from, nothing to complain about, and no expression that fits. Rizvi et al.'s work on the subjective experience is the corrective to reading it off behaviour, since the behaviour continues and only the report changes.

## Orders

Be there completely, and receive nothing.
The consummatory form orders the persona to complete the activity properly -- to eat the meal, watch the film, hold the child, take the holiday -- and to arrive at the end of it with the same interior as at the start. Because the ordinary social signals are produced on cue, the room sees an evening that went well, and the persona's later account of it contradicts a scene everybody else witnessed. It stages a peculiar kind of solitude: the persona is not absent, they are present and unreached, and the discrepancy is invisible to everyone but them.

## Loses

The return on everything already paid for. Carrying this form, the persona forgoes the payoff that ordinarily justifies effort retrospectively, and pays in a slow reappraisal of their own life -- if the things that are supposed to feel good do not, then either the things are wrong or they are, and neither conclusion is survivable for long. It loses the repair function of ordinary pleasure, so a hard week is no longer recoverable by the means that used to work. And it loses the persona's confidence in its own testimony, because they were visibly enjoying themselves and remember not enjoying themselves, and only one of those is admissible as evidence.

## Drives

On that flat arrival:
the persona is present, the perception is accurate, and nothing is returned. Consummatory anhedonia is one of three forms, the failure at arrival, set beside the [anticipatory](position_anticipatory.md) failure before it and the [social](position_social_anhedonia.md) case where what fails to return is other people. That the performance stays intact while the return is absent -- and that this is what makes the condition deniable -- is the [root's](process_anhedonia.md); the appraisal of an event as good belongs to the `joy` engine and the pull of a novel object to `interest`, where this form owns only the case in which the appraisal is correct and the feeling it should produce does not follow.
