# Process: Anhedonia

## Taxonomy

Anhedonia. The blunted capacity for pleasure or interest. A capacity failure, not a valuation, dissociating the drive to pursue from the enjoyment of the outcome.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: anhedonia

## Initiated by

The presentation of a normally rewarding stimulus, the prospect of a future reward, or the demand for effortful pursuit.

## Direction

Inward. The failure occurs in the persona's affective and motivational machinery, leaving the world unchanged but stripping it of its salience.

## Lever

The twist that makes this an engine rather than a definition is the dissociation itself: the persona keeps doing the things, so nobody can see it, and the report of feeling nothing is heard as ingratitude or as a mood rather than as a capacity that is gone. The persona goes through the motions of pursuit (wanting) without the payoff (liking), or they feel fine in the moment (liking) but cannot muster the effort to initiate (wanting). The trap is that the failure is invisible, and the world reads it as a choice.

## Echo

The persona is left isolated by the misunderstanding, carrying the burden of an invisible deficit while being judged for a moral failing they do not possess.
