# Process: Anhedonia

## Taxonomy

Anhedonia as process: the blunted capacity to be moved by what would ordinarily move the persona -- a failure of the machinery that converts an outcome into a feeling, rather than a judgement that the outcome is not worth having.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: anhedonia

## Initiated by

Something the persona used to want, or used to enjoy, arrives -- and nothing happens.
The engine begins at a non-event, which is why it is so hard to stage and so easy to misread. There is no onset the persona can point to and usually no moment they noticed; what they notice, weeks later, is the absence of something they never had to attend to before. The entry condition is not loss, grief, or disappointment, all of which are feelings and all of which this persona would welcome. It is that the apparatus which turns a good thing into the experience of a good thing has stopped returning a value, while everything upstream of it -- the perception, the knowledge, the memory that this was once pleasurable -- is entirely intact. The persona knows the meal is good. They can taste it. They can tell you it is good. There is simply nothing on the other side.

## Direction

From an intact appraisal, through an apparatus that no longer converts it, toward a persona going through motions they can no longer feel the point of.
The engine breaks the capacity into three, following the split affective neuroscience has settled on rather than treating it as one dulled feeling. [Anticipatory](position_anticipatory.md) anhedonia is the failure before the fact: the persona can say the thing is desirable and cannot generate the pull toward it, so the effort required to begin is priced against a payoff that is not felt in advance. [Consummatory](position_consummatory.md) anhedonia is the failure at the fact: the persona arrives, is present, and the resonance does not come. And [social](position_social_anhedonia.md) anhedonia is the specific case that costs the most, because the reward that is missing is other people, and the deficit therefore removes the thing that would ordinarily repair the rest. The three are not degrees of one severity. A persona may hold any one of them with the others working, which is what makes the condition possible to hide and possible to doubt.

## Lever

Whether the persona still performs the behaviour. Everything the engine stages turns on this, and it is the reason anhedonia is not read as an illness by the room. The apparatus that generates the feeling and the apparatus that produces the action are separable, so a persona can keep arriving, keep cooking, keep answering, keep turning up for the people they love -- entirely correctly, from the outside -- while receiving nothing back for any of it. Stage the performance intact and the return absent, because the gap between them is the whole engine, and a persona written as visibly withdrawn has skipped it.

**The twist is that the evidence of the deficit is the persona's own testimony, and the deficit removes their standing to give it.** They report feeling nothing about a birthday, a promotion, a child's news -- and the report arrives as ingratitude, or as sulking, or as a mood that could be shifted with effort, because from outside those are the ordinary causes of someone saying that. There is no visible sign to point to instead: the food was eaten, the party attended, the thanks given. So the persona learns that saying it costs them and changes nothing, and stops saying it, at which point the last external trace of the condition is gone and what remains is a person who seems merely difficult to please. What makes it a trap rather than a misunderstanding is that the concealment is rational: the room's reading is not stupid, and telling them again is genuinely worse than not.

## Echo

The capacity fails in three forms: [anticipatory](position_anticipatory.md), where the pull toward a known good cannot be generated and the effort of starting is never funded; [consummatory](position_consummatory.md), where the persona is present at the thing and the resonance does not arrive; and [social](position_social_anhedonia.md), where the missing reward is other people, and withdrawal follows from the absence of return rather than from fear of them. The discounting of a reward over time, and the calibration of effort against payoff, are the `reward` engine's; the appraisal of an event as good is `joy`'s; the pull of a novel or complex object is `interest`'s; and the general mobilisation to act is `motivation`'s. This engine owns none of those and only the case where they run correctly and produce nothing -- read as a capacity that has gone rather than a preference that has changed, and authored as a character's architecture, never diagnosed onto a real person.
