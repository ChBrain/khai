# Position: Social Anhedonia

## Taxonomy

[Anhedonia](process_anhedonia.md), the case where the missing reward is other people -- and so removes what would have repaired the rest.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: anhedonia

## Has

The third form, and the costly one, because of what it takes with it. The capacity that fails here is the return on company: the persona can read the room, hold the conversation, and perform every part of the exchange, and receives none of the warmth that ordinarily makes company restorative rather than expensive. Two things follow that the other forms do not produce. First, the interaction becomes net-negative -- it takes effort and returns nothing, so withdrawal is arithmetic rather than avoidance. Second, and this is what makes it worth its own form, the ordinary remedy for the other two failures is other people, so a persona who has lost the return on company has lost the route back. The RDoC positive-valence framing keeps the social case as its own axis rather than a severity of the general one, and the trait literature finds it can run in a persona whose other rewards are intact.

## Orders

Attend, perform, and leave depleted.
The social form orders the persona to keep the exchanges brief, to decline the second invitation, to let the friendship thin without any rupture -- and to do it while liking the people entirely, which is the part that will not be believed. The room reads withdrawal and reaches for its usual causes: they are angry with me, they are anxious, they have found better company. It stages a retreat with no grievance behind it and no fear in it, which is why it is so consistently misfiled as one or the other.

## Loses

The route back, along with the company. Carrying this form, the persona forgoes the return that makes other people restorative, and pays first in a social world that contracts by attrition rather than by any decision. But the larger loss is structural: company is what ordinarily interrupts the other forms, so losing its return removes the mechanism that would have carried the persona through them. It loses, too, the ordinary defence of affection -- _I do love you_ is true, is offered, and is heard as a formula, because a persona who loves someone is expected to want to see them, and this persona cannot produce the wanting from the loving.

## Drives

On that unreturned company:
the exchange is performed correctly, costs effort, and yields nothing, so withdrawal follows as arithmetic. Social anhedonia is one of three forms, the one that removes the remedy for the others, set beside the [anticipatory](position_anticipatory.md) failure before arrival and the [consummatory](position_consummatory.md) failure at it. That it can run while the other two are intact is the [root's](process_anhedonia.md); the fear of negative evaluation that also produces withdrawal belongs elsewhere and is not this -- there is no dread here, only absence -- and the general drive to act belongs to `motivation`, where this form owns only the case in which people are approached competently and return nothing.
