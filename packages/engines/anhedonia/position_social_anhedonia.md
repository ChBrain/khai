# Position: Social Anhedonia

## Taxonomy

Social anhedonia. A specific deficit in the capacity to experience pleasure from interpersonal interaction.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: anhedonia

## Has

The cognitive ability to interact, parse social cues, and perform the expected scripts of a relationship, but without the underlying affective reward that makes those scripts meaningful.

## Orders

Withdrawal from social contact, not out of anxiety or fear of judgment (as in social anxiety), but out of a profound lack of interest and the exhaustion of performing without a payoff.

## Loses

The warmth, connection, and restorative quality of being with others. People become indistinguishable from objects in their capacity to generate pleasure.

## Drives

Isolation. The persona retreats because maintaining the mask of engagement is effortful, and the interaction itself provides no energy to sustain it.
