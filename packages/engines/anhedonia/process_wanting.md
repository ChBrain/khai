# Process: Wanting

## Taxonomy

Wanting. The anticipatory drive to pursue a reward and the allocation of effort toward it.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: anhedonia

## Initiated by

The presentation of an opportunity, a cue that predicts reward, or a decision point requiring effort.

## Direction

Forward and outward. The engine projects the persona toward a future goal, calculating the effort required and generating the motivational salience to bridge the gap.

## Lever

The drive can be broken (anticipatory anhedonia) while the capacity to enjoy the outcome (liking) remains intact. The persona knows they would enjoy the reward if it were handed to them, but the motivational machinery fails to generate the effort required to get there.

## Echo

A profound inertia, often misread by the persona themselves as laziness or by others as a lack of care, masking the underlying failure of the anticipatory mechanism.
