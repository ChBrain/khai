# Process: Liking

## Taxonomy

Liking. The consummatory response and the hedonic impact experienced upon acquiring a reward or engaging in an activity.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: anhedonia

## Initiated by

The acquisition of the object, the arrival at the goal, or the sensory immersion in the experience.

## Direction

Inward. The engine measures the resonance of the experience against the persona's affective baseline, registering pleasure, satisfaction, or relief.

## Lever

The response can be blunted (consummatory anhedonia) while the drive to pursue (wanting) remains intact. The persona goes through all the necessary steps, pays the cost, and arrives at the destination, only to find the expected affective payoff missing.

## Echo

A hollow realization that the machinery of enjoyment is broken, often leaving the persona feeling alienated from their own experiences and disconnected from the rewards they worked for.
