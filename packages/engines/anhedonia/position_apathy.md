# Position: Apathy

## Taxonomy

Apathy. The behavioral manifestation of anticipatory anhedonia; a lack of initiation and a flattened allocation of effort.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: anhedonia

## Has

A profound inertia. The effort-based decision making machinery (Treadway & Zald) fails to calculate the payoff as worth the physical or cognitive cost of initiation.

## Orders

Inaction. The persona defaults to the path of least resistance, not out of a conscious choice to avoid, but out of a failure of the mechanism that initiates.

## Loses

The capacity to self-start, the appearance of engagement, and often the goodwill of observers who misread the deficit as laziness or willful neglect.

## Drives

A cycle of stagnation where the failure to initiate prevents the acquisition of rewards, further starving the affective system of input.
