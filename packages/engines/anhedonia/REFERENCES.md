# References

## Line of Work

Anhedonia as **process**: the blunted capacity to be moved by what would ordinarily move the persona. The engine's whole claim is in the distinction between a **capacity** and a **valuation** -- the machinery that converts an outcome into a feeling stops returning a value, while everything upstream of it stays intact. The persona knows the meal is good, tastes it accurately, will tell you it is good, and there is nothing on the other side. That is why this is not `joy` failing to fire, not `reward` discounting steeply, and not `motivation` running low: those are all working, and producing nothing.

**Three forms, and they are separable rather than degrees of one severity**, which is the finding that decides the engine's shape. Anticipatory anhedonia is the failure before the fact -- the persona reports the thing as desirable and cannot fund the effort of setting out. **Treadway & Zald's** effort-based decision work is what makes that precise rather than impressionistic: the deficit appears as a shifted willingness to expend effort for a reward whose value the persona correctly reports. Consummatory anhedonia is the failure at the fact: present, attentive, accurate, and unreached, with the experience arriving _flat_ rather than negative -- **Ribot's** original description, and still the best short account. Social anhedonia is the case where the missing return is other people, kept as its own axis by the **RDoC** positive-valence framing rather than as a severity of the general condition, and it costs the most because company is what ordinarily interrupts the other two.

**Why the forms can dissociate at all is Berridge's**, and it is worth stating even though the engine does not model the intact mechanisms it names. The wanting and liking systems are separable, so a persona can be unable to set out toward something they would in fact enjoy on arrival, or enjoy what they could not make themselves go to. The engine takes that as its licence for three distinct forms; it does **not** carry wanting and liking as members, because those are the working machinery and the working machinery is the `reward` engine's.

**The twist is that the only evidence is the persona's testimony, and the deficit removes their standing to give it.** They report feeling nothing about a birthday, a promotion, a child's news, and the report arrives as ingratitude or as a mood that effort could shift -- because from outside those are the ordinary causes of someone saying that. There is no sign to point to instead: the food was eaten, the party attended, the thanks given. So the persona learns that saying it costs them and changes nothing, stops saying it, and the last external trace of the condition disappears. What makes it a trap rather than a misunderstanding is that the concealment is rational: the room's reading is not stupid, and telling them again is genuinely worse than not.

**On the evidence, stated plainly.** The anticipatory/consummatory dissociation is well supported behaviourally and is cleaner in the laboratory than in a life; the boundary between a capacity failure and a changed valuation is phenomenologically fragile from outside, which is the engine's subject rather than a defect in it, but it does mean a persona written badly here is indistinguishable from one who has simply stopped caring. **Rizvi et al.** are the corrective on measurement: the behaviour continues, so anhedonia read off behaviour alone is read wrong, and the subjective report is the instrument. The social form has the thinnest operational base of the three -- it is well described as a trait and less settled as a state. And the engine takes **no position on cause**: it is agnostic between anhedonia as a feature of depression, a trait, a medication effect, or a consequence of something else, because the persona's experience is the same and the aetiology is the author's to set. What should not be taken from it: that this is a diagnosis, that it implies depression, or that a persona holding it has stopped caring about anyone.

## Restrictions

- **Reward**: The discounting of delayed rewards, the calibration of effort-to-payoff, and the preference for immediate stimulation belong to `reward`. Anhedonia is a capacity deficit, not a discount rate.
- **Joy**: The appraisal of an event as inherently positive and the expression of that affect belong to `joy`. Anhedonia is the failure of the mechanism that makes joy possible.
- **Interest**: The focused attention on a novel or complex object belongs to `interest`. Anhedonia is the blunting of that capacity.
- **Motivation**: The general drive to act and the mobilization of resources belong to `motivation`. Anticipatory anhedonia specifically blunts the reward-seeking aspect of motivation, but non-reward-based motivation (e.g., duty, fear) belongs elsewhere.
- **Diagnosis**: This is authored persona-architecture for a fictional play. It is never a clinical assessment or diagnosis of a real person.

## Encoding

- `process_anhedonia.md` is the root, declaring the generalized capacity failure.
- `process_wanting.md` models the anticipatory drive, carrying `position_anticipatory.md` (the inability to look forward) and `position_apathy.md` (the lack of initiation/effort).
- `process_liking.md` models the consummatory response, carrying `position_consummatory.md` (the inability to enjoy in the moment) and `position_blunting.md` (the flattening of the response).
- `position_social_anhedonia.md` specifically models the deficit as isolated to or most pronounced in interpersonal connection.
