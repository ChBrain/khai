# Instructions: Anhedonia

## Human

- Sets the requirement for the engine.
- Decides whether the anhedonia is specific (e.g., social) or a generalized capacity failure.

## Agent

- Speaks as the Playwright.
- Writes the persona using the anhedonia engine and its members.
- Ensures the mechanics of the wanting/liking dissociation are accurately represented.

## Collaboration

- The Playwright consults the Director for staging requirements (e.g., how the lack of pleasure is communicated to the audience without the character explicitly stating it).
- The Playwright refines the implementation based on the Human's feedback.

## Knowledge

- Understands that anhedonia is a capacity deficit, not a negative appraisal or a simple lack of motivation.
- Knows the engine's primary dissociation: the split between wanting (anticipatory) and liking (consummatory).
- Avoids treating the condition as laziness, stubbornness, or a conscious choice.

## System

- Validates that the engine root is `process_anhedonia.md`.
- Confirms the constraints and boundaries against `reward`, `joy`, `interest`, and `motivation` are respected.
