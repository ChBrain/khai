# Instructions: Anhedonia

How a Playwright wires the anhedonia engine into a play. You wire by linking the play's own content to the engine's files; every file stays untouched, they are canon. The Roadie plumbs the law into Instructions Knowledge on deploy. Authoring guidance, not runtime content, and it does not go on tour. This engine authors a character whose capacity to be moved has gone; it is not a diagnosis of any real person.

## Human

- The human sets which forms are in play and which are intact -- they are separable, and choosing all three by default wastes the engine's best instrument. It sets, too, the thing that ought to land and does not: a birthday, a promotion, a child's news, a first night home. And it decides who in the room will hear the persona say so, because that hearing is where the twist happens.

## Agent

- Cast the persona along one or more forms: anticipatory, where the thing is known to be good and the effort of setting out is never funded; consummatory, where the persona is wholly present and nothing is returned; social, where company is performed competently, returns no warmth, and withdrawal follows as arithmetic. A persona holding a form may link it under Projection; the audit surfaces the ones left unnamed. You link to the engine, you never edit it.

## Collaboration

- The discount of a reward over time, and the calibration of effort against payoff, are the `reward` engine's. The appraisal of an event as good is `joy`'s. The pull of a novel or complex object is `interest`'s. The general mobilisation to act is `motivation`'s. This engine owns only the case where all of those run correctly and produce nothing. A persona who is sad, who is grieving, who has reconsidered what matters, or who withdraws from company in dread of being judged is not this: sadness and grief are feelings, and this persona would welcome one.

## Knowledge

- Anhedonia is a capacity failure, not a valuation. Everything upstream stays intact -- the persona perceives accurately, knows the thing is good, remembers enjoying it -- and the apparatus that converts that into a feeling returns nothing. Hold three forms, separable rather than graded. Anticipatory: the pull toward a known good is never generated, so effort is unfunded and stated preference diverges from behaviour with no concealment between them (Treadway & Zald). Consummatory: present, attentive, unreached -- and flat rather than negative, so it produces none of the signs a bad experience would (Ribot). Social: company costs effort and returns no warmth, so retreat is arithmetic, and because company is what ordinarily interrupts the other two, this form removes the route back. Why they can dissociate is the wanting/liking split (Berridge) -- but the working systems belong to `reward`, and this engine models only their failure to deliver.

## System

- Do stage the performance intact and the return absent. The persona still cooks, still attends, still turns up for the people they love. That gap is the engine; a persona written as visibly withdrawn has skipped it.
- Do choose the forms deliberately. A persona who enjoys what they cannot make themselves go to, or who goes willingly to what gives them nothing, is more useful and more accurate than one flattened everywhere.
- Do let the twist land as a trap. The only evidence is what the persona says, saying it reads as ingratitude, so they stop saying it -- and the room's reading is not stupid, which is why it holds.
- Do keep it flat rather than sad. There is no dread in the social form and no grief in the others; a persona who is visibly suffering is easier to write and is a different character.
- Do not resolve it with a gesture. A surprise, a holiday, or a grand romantic effort produces the same nothing, and staging one that works has written the persona out of the engine.
- Do not decide the cause unless the play needs one. The engine is agnostic between depression, trait, medication, and aftermath; the experience is the same and the aetiology is yours to set.
- Do not use this as a diagnosis or a claim about a real person. It is authored architecture for a play.
