# Anhedonia

Anhedonia as process: the blunted capacity to be moved by what would ordinarily move the persona -- a failure of the machinery that converts an outcome into a feeling, not a judgement that the outcome is not worth having -- across its anticipatory, consummatory, and social forms (Ribot; Berridge; Treadway & Zald; Rizvi; RDoC).
