# Position: Anticipatory

## Taxonomy

Anticipatory anhedonia. The blunted capacity to look forward to a reward or to generate the motivational salience necessary to pursue it.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: anhedonia

## Has

A cognitive understanding that the goal is positive or desirable, decoupled from the affective drive to attain it.

## Orders

The abandonment of the pursuit before it begins, or the scaling back of ambition to match the flattened motivational state.

## Loses

The energy, excitement, and forward-looking momentum that ordinarily bridges the gap between the present state and a future reward.

## Drives

A withdrawal from goal-oriented behavior, leaving the persona marooned in the present, unable to generate the "wanting" required to act.
