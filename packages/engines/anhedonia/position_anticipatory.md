# Position: Anticipatory Anhedonia

## Taxonomy

[Anhedonia](process_anhedonia.md), the failure before the fact -- the pull toward a known good that cannot be generated.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: anhedonia

## Has

The first form, and the one most often mistaken for a decision. The persona's knowledge is intact and specific: they can say the concert will be good, that they enjoyed the last one, that they will probably enjoy this one. What is missing is the forward pull that ordinarily attaches to that knowledge and pays for the effort of getting there. Treadway & Zald's effort-based work is what makes this precise rather than impressionistic: the deficit shows up as a shifted willingness to expend effort for a reward whose value the persona correctly reports, so it is not that the reward is judged small but that the exertion is not funded. Berridge's dissociation supplies the reason it can happen alone -- the wanting system and the liking system are separable, so a persona can be unable to set out toward something they would in fact enjoy on arrival, and be unable to explain why they did not go.

## Orders

Know that it is good, and do not move toward it.
The anticipatory movement orders the persona to decline -- to not book the ticket, to let the invitation lapse, to shrink the plan to whatever needs no momentum -- while agreeing at every point that the thing is worth doing. The room hears the agreement and sees the non-attendance and concludes there is a hidden objection, so it looks for the real reason: they did not want to see me, they are being difficult, something is wrong they will not say. It stages a persona whose stated preferences and actual behaviour diverge without any concealment between them, which is the shape of a lie and is not one.

## Loses

The future's ordinary purchase on the present. Carrying this form, the persona forgoes the mechanism by which a good thing later makes an effort now feel worth it, and pays in a life that contracts to whatever requires no starting. It loses plans, then the reputation for keeping them, then the invitations themselves -- and each contraction is reasonable at the moment it is made and indefensible when the room adds them up. It loses, too, the ability to argue its own case, since the honest account is _I wanted to and could not begin_, which sounds like an excuse precisely because it is the form an excuse takes.

## Drives

On that unfunded pull:
the good is recognised, and the movement toward it is never financed. Anticipatory anhedonia is one of three forms, the one that fails before arrival, set beside the [consummatory](position_consummatory.md) failure at arrival and the [social](position_social_anhedonia.md) case where the missing reward is other people. That the persona may hold this form with the others working -- and so may enjoy what they cannot make themselves go to -- is the [root's](process_anhedonia.md); the discount over time and the calibration of effort against payoff belong to the `reward` engine, and the general mobilisation to act to `motivation`, where this form owns only the case in which both run correctly and the pull does not arrive.
