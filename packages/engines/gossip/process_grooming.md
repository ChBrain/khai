# Process: Grooming

## Taxonomy

[Gossip](process_gossip.md), the facet of the bond built by shared talk.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: gossip

## Initiated by

To share talk about a third is to draw closer to the one you share it with, and the closeness is the point.
Grooming starts wherever gossip binds its tellers -- the confidence passed low between two, the shared judgment of an absent other, the pleasure of being the one told, the trust implied in being let in on it -- the talk that makes a we of those who keep it.
It is gossip's second function, social grooming: the alliance and intimacy built not by what the talk is about but by the sharing of it, the human version of the mutual grooming that binds other primates.

## Direction

Toward two personas bound closer by the confidence they share about a third.
Grooming works by the gift of inclusion: to tell someone gossip is to trust them with it and to mark them as inside, and to be told is to be chosen, so the exchange itself -- more than its content -- knits the tellers into an alliance and sets them, together, apart from the one discussed.
The direction is bonding through shared evaluation: the personas find, in agreeing about an absent third, a confirmation of their own likeness and trust, and the running exchange of such confidences becomes the substance of the tie between them -- friendship carried, in large part, on talk about others.

## Lever

The intimacy of the shared confidence against the exclusion it works by.
Grooming runs on inclusion and its shadow: letting someone in on the talk binds them by trust and by complicity, but the same act draws the line that leaves the discussed one out, so the bond between the tellers is built on the very ground of the third's absence; the resistance is the betrayal always latent in it -- the confidence that can be passed on, the ally who gossips with you today and about you tomorrow, the closeness that was only ever the near side of an exclusion.
Share the talk and the tie is grown and confirmed; break the confidence, carry it on, and the same grooming that bound reveals how easily the teller becomes the told-about.

## Echo

The tie grown on the talk two personas keep about a third.
Grooming leaves the tellers bound -- allied, intimate, mutually trusted -- by the confidences they have shared, and hands that bond to the friendship it largely constitutes.
It is Dunbar's core claim made social: that gossip does at conversational scale what grooming does by hand, binding the group by the exchange of attention, so that much of human closeness is carried on talk about who is not in the room.
Grooming is dated from the confidence, the moment the shared talk about a third made the two who shared it a we.
