# Process: Ledger

## Taxonomy

[Gossip](process_gossip.md), the facet of the reputational record.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: gossip

## Initiated by

The talk keeps score, and each telling adds to a running account of who a person is.
Ledger starts wherever gossip carries information about conduct -- who repaid the debt and who dodged it, who kept the secret and who spread it, who is generous, who is careless, who cheated whom -- and lodges it in the shared memory of the group.
It is gossip's first function, the reputational record: the invisible, distributed database of who has done what, built and read by everyone and held on no authority's books.

## Direction

Toward a persona who can know whom to trust without having been burned by them.
Ledger works by pooling experience: what one member learned at their own cost about a third, the talk passes to all the rest, so that a persona arrives at a judgment of someone they have never dealt with, forewarned by the accumulated account.
The direction is reputation as knowledge: the persona reads the ledger to place a stranger, extends or withholds trust on the strength of what is said, and adds their own dealings back into it -- so that cooperation can reach past the people one has personally tested, riding on the record the talk maintains.

## Lever

The pooled record against the cost of learning each person firsthand.
Ledger runs on transmitted experience: because the talk carries what others have paid to learn, a persona need not be cheated themselves to know who cheats -- the record does the costly learning once and lends the lesson to everyone; the resistance is the record's fallibility, the false entry and the stale one, the reputation built on a story that was wrong or old, which the ledger carries as faithfully as the true.
Keep the ledger and a group can trust and cooperate at a scale no one's direct experience could support; corrupt it -- plant the lie, ruin the name unjustly -- and the same mechanism that enabled trust misplaces it.

## Echo

The persona placed by a record made in talk before they enter the room.
Ledger leaves the group holding a distributed account of its members -- who to trust, who to watch, who has done what -- and hands that record to every dealing it informs.
It is the mechanism of indirect reciprocity: cooperation extended to strangers on the strength of their reputation, the ledger the reason a good name is worth keeping and a ruined one so hard to rebuild.
Ledger is dated from the entry, the moment a person's conduct passed into the talk and became part of the record that precedes them.
