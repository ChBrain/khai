# Process: Policing

## Taxonomy

[Gossip](process_gossip.md), the facet of norm enforcement.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: gossip

## Initiated by

Everyone knows they will be talked about, and the knowledge holds them to the line without a soul to enforce it.
Policing starts wherever gossip carries a verdict -- the neighbor's meanness noted, the shirker's shirking passed around, the cheat's cheating made known, the arrogant one cut down to size in the telling -- so that a norm is upheld by the circulating talk of those it binds.
It is gossip's third function, decentralized social control: the threat of being talked about that disciplines conduct, and the reputational damage that answers a violation, in a group with no office to punish.

## Direction

Toward a persona who keeps the norm because they know their breaking of it would be discussed.
Policing works by the certainty of exposure: because a persona cannot be sure who is watching or who will hear, and knows the group's talk will carry any lapse, they govern themselves against the standing prospect of the talk -- and when someone does violate, the gossip that spreads it exacts the cost, warning others and cutting the offender's standing down.
The direction is enforcement without authority: the norm is held by everyone's readiness to talk and no one's appointment to punish, and the same talk levels the one who climbs too high -- the ridicule and the passed word that pull an upstart back into line, the group's check on anyone who would lord it over them.

## Lever

The threat of being talked about against the cost of the talk to the one it targets.
Policing runs on reputation as sanction: the talk's power to raise or ruin a name gives the group a penalty it can impose on anyone and a deterrent every member carries within, so conduct is held to the norm by the mere prospect of the murmur; the resistance is the injustice the same mechanism can do -- the true violator and the merely disliked punished by the same means, the leveling that keeps a group equal turned, at its worst, on the different rather than the arrogant.
Let the talk enforce and a group polices itself for free, upstarts and cheats alike held in check by everyone's tongue; question its verdicts and the line between just enforcement and mere persecution, both carried the same way, comes into view.

## Echo

The group holding its own to the norm by the talk it is ready to keep about them.
Policing leaves the persona governed by the prospect of the talk and the violator marked by its verdict -- norms upheld and upstarts leveled with no authority to do it -- and hands that enforcement to the order it maintains.
It is Boehm's reverse dominance made ordinary: subordinates using gossip and ridicule to hold the powerful and the deviant alike in check, the egalitarian group's police force carried entirely on its talk.
Policing is dated from the verdict, the moment the talk turned from describing a person to holding them to account.
