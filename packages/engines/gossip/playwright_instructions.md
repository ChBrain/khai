# Instructions: Gossip

How a Playwright wires the gossip engine into a play. You wire by linking the play's own content to the engine's files; the engine files stay untouched, they are canon. The Roadie plumbs the engine's law into Instructions Knowledge on deploy. Authoring guidance, not runtime content, and it does not go on tour.

## Human

- The human sets the gossip terrain: the community whose talk carries its members' reputations, who speaks and who is spoken of when absent, and whether that talk is a quiet background of the world or a live actor -- the record it keeps, the bonds it builds, the norms it enforces, the malice it relishes.

## Agent

- From the play's own content, a persona whose speaking and being-spoken-of is an actor links under Projection the facet its gossip serves: the ledger it keeps, the grooming bond it builds, the policing it does, or the venom it relishes. Play the gossip as evaluative talk about the absent present in what the persona does -- the reputation carried ahead of a newcomer, the confidence shared to bind a friend, the shirker's name passed around, the cruel tale relished -- talked, never narrated, and never standing in for the exclusion the talk may bring or the shame it induces. Keep it the evaluative talk about absent third parties, not the exclusion (that is ostracism), the felt exposure (shame), the vertical command or training (obedience, discipline), the present peer majority (conformity), or unverified news about events rather than persons (a distinct force). You link to the engine, you never edit it.

## Collaboration

- The exclusion the talk may bring is the ostracism engine's; the felt exposure of being talked about is the shame engine's; the vertical command of an authority is obedience's and its impersonal training discipline's; the direct pull of a present peer majority is conformity's; the spread of unverified news about events rather than persons is a distinct force. Gossip is the decentralized, horizontal social force carried on a group's talk -- the informal-social-control sibling of ostracism, the talk where ostracism is the exclusion. Whether the reading rings true to gossip is the team's review.

## Knowledge

- Gossip is evaluative talk about absent third parties, circulated through a community as its reputational memory, its social bond, and its decentralized enforcement (Dunbar's gossip as social grooming and the evolution of language; Gluckman's gossip as the maintenance of group unity and morality; Boehm's reverse-dominance leveling; Spacks on gossip's moral ambivalence). It wires on a persona at Projection, and it is the talk about the absent and what it does. Four facets carry it -- ledger is the reputational record, the running account of who did what and whom to trust, the distributed database read by all and held on no authority's books; grooming is the bond, the alliance and intimacy built by sharing talk about a third, the human form of social grooming; policing is the enforcement, the threat of being talked about that keeps a persona in line and the ridicule that levels an upstart, norms held with no office to hold them; venom is the twist, the condemned malice and relish in the wounding word read not only as a vice but as the sting that gives the record and bond and policing their teeth. Read a persona by the talk they keep and are subject to, and read venom two ways: the malice gossip is condemned for is inseparable from the social work the talk actually does.

## System

- Do give a persona whose speaking and being-spoken-of is an actor its facet under Projection; do play the gossip as evaluative talk about the absent shown in what the persona does, present not narrated; do keep the reading the talk about absent third parties -- the ledger, the grooming, the policing, the venom -- not the exclusion and not the emotion.
- Do run gossip on the third party's absence: because the one discussed is not there to answer, the talk weighs and judges and travels ahead of them freely -- and its verdict, right or wrong, reaches the room before they do.
- Do read venom as the twist: the malice gossip is condemned for is not only a vice but the essential sting -- the relish that recruits the tellers, the shared cruelty that binds them, the sharpest tooth the group's enforcement has, so the sin of the tongue is also the group's rough justice.
- Do not model the exclusion the talk may bring (ostracism), the felt exposure of being talked about (shame), the vertical command or impersonal training (obedience, discipline), the present peer majority (conformity), or unverified news about events (a distinct force). Do not make gossip stand in for the exclusion or the shame it neighbors. Do not edit the engine's files; wire only from the play's side.
