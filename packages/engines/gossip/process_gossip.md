# Process: Gossip

## Taxonomy

The anthropology and evolution of gossip / evaluative talk about the absent.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: gossip

## Initiated by

Two personas talk about a third who is not there, and the talk weighs them -- what they did, whether they can be trusted, how they measure against what is expected.
This is gossip: informal, evaluative talk about absent members of a shared social world, circulated through a community as its running account of who its people are.
The force is not the exclusion the talk may bring nor the shame it may induce, but the talk itself -- the low, constant, evaluative murmur by which a group knows and governs its own.

## Direction

Toward a group that knows and holds its members through the talk it keeps about them.
Gossip carries reputation: each telling adds to a shared, invisible ledger of who kept faith and who cheated, binds the tellers by the confidence they share, and warns every member that they too are talked about when their back is turned -- so the group comes to know its people, bond over them, and hold them to its norms, all in the same evaluative talk.
The direction is social knowledge made and enforced in speech: a persona learns whom to trust without being burned, feels the pull of belonging in the shared confidence, and governs their own conduct against the certainty that it will be discussed.

## Lever

The evaluative talk about the absent against the target's inability to answer it.
Gossip runs on the third party's absence: because the one discussed is not there to correct the account, the talk can weigh, judge, and circulate freely, and its verdict travels ahead of the person to everyone they have not yet met; the resistance is the talk's own unreliability and its condemnation -- gossip is distrusted as idle and malicious even as it is universally practiced, and the target, when they learn of it, can rarely catch or unmake what was said behind them.
Let the talk circulate and the group's memory, bond, and enforcement run for free, on no authority but everyone's tongue; expose the talker or discredit the talk and the reputation it was building comes into doubt.

## Echo

The group knows, bonds, and governs itself through the talk it keeps about its absent members.
Gossip is why reputation precedes a person into every room, why a group can trust and police itself with no office to do it, why the same talk that is condemned as a vice is the one nobody can do without -- the evaluative murmur that Dunbar found language may have evolved to carry, doing the social work that grooming does in other primates at a scale no grooming could reach.
It runs as [ledger](process_ledger.md), [grooming](process_grooming.md), [policing](process_policing.md), or [venom](process_venom.md) -- the reputational record, the bond of shared confidence, the norm enforced by the threat of being talked about, and the condemned malice that gives the enforcement its teeth. The exclusion the talk may bring is the ostracism engine's; the felt exposure of being talked about is the shame engine's; the vertical command of an authority is obedience's and its impersonal training discipline's; the direct pull of a present peer majority is conformity's; the spread of unverified news about events rather than persons is a distinct force. This engine owns the evaluative talk about absent third parties and what that talk does.
