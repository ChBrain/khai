# Process: Venom

## Taxonomy

[Gossip](process_gossip.md), the facet where the condemned malice is read as the essential sting.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: gossip

## Initiated by

The talk turns cruel, and the cruelty is relished -- and the very malice gossip is condemned for is what gives it its bite.
Venom starts wherever gossip carries not just news but appetite -- the pleasure in the wounding word, the delight in another's fall, the relish of the shared contempt, the sharpened tale told to diminish -- the malice that makes gossip a sin of the tongue and makes it, at the same time, irresistible.
It is gossip read the other way: not the benign functions of record and bond and enforcement but the venom beneath them, the malice condemned as vice that is inseparable from the work the talk actually does.

## Direction

Toward talk whose cruelty is exactly what makes it bite, and bind, and enforce.
Venom deepens gossip by owning its malice: the relish in the wounding word is what makes the telling worth the teller's while, so the appetite that condemns gossip is also what recruits the unpaid labor of policing; the shared cruelty toward a target is a fierce bond between those who share it; and the sting of the malicious tale is the sharpest tooth the group's enforcement has -- the norm feared most where the talk is cruelest.
The direction is the double face: the persona's spite is a real vice, mean and often unjust, and it is at the same time the fuel and the teeth of the whole social machinery of talk -- the group governed, in part, by the pleasure its members take in tearing each other down.

## Lever

The vice of the malice against the function it turns out to serve.
The carrying force is that gossip's cruelty works -- the relish recruits the tellers, the shared contempt binds them, the sting deters and levels harder than any neutral report could; the resistance, and the reason it is condemned, is that the same malice wounds unjustly, ruins the merely disliked, and corrodes the trust it also enforces -- so the vice is real and the function is real and they are the same act.
Read one way venom is simple cruelty, the sin of the tongue, the pleasure in a fellow's ruin; read the other it is the sting that gives gossip its power to bind and to govern -- and gossip is most potent exactly where the two cannot be told apart.

## Echo

The group bound and held in line by the very malice its talk is condemned for.
It shows a persona whose spite in the telling is a genuine vice -- and carries beneath it the gossip family's twist, the sibling of the ambivalence: that the condemned malice is not only a failing but the essential sting, the cruelty that gives the record and the bond and the policing their teeth, the sin of the tongue that is also the group's rough justice.
Venom is dated from the relish, the moment the talk took pleasure in the wound and became the sharpest thing the group can do to its own.
