# Position: Ambiguity Tolerance

## Taxonomy

Affect / the trait of ambiguity tolerance -- the standing threshold beneath [Confusion](process_confusion.md) (Frenkel-Brunswik; Budner; Kruglanski's need for cognitive closure).

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: confusion

## Has

A resting threshold for how much of the unresolved the persona can hold before it must be closed.
Trait ambiguity tolerance, as against the state of any one confusion: at the low end, a need for closure -- ambiguity felt as aversive, the unresolved seized shut fast, the single answer preferred to the open question; at the high end, an ease with the not-yet-understood, able to sit in an impasse without rushing it to an answer.
It tracks how the persona meets uncertainty, contradiction, and the open-ended before any particular block appears.

## Orders

Run from here, into every confusion.
The low-tolerance persona hits an impasse and reaches for closure -- picks an answer, drops the problem, escapes the disequilibrium quickly; the high-tolerance persona can bear the block, working it where the other would have shut it, and so is the more likely to reach the productive resolve.
It sets the standing pull toward or away from the unresolved that colours which fork a confusion tends to take.

## Loses

At the low end, the depth that only bearing an impasse can reach; at the high end, the speed and decisiveness that fast closure buys.
The persona who needs closure loses the learning that lives past the block, closing before the frame is truly rebuilt; the persona who tolerates ambiguity loses the efficiency of settling, and can sit in an openness that a decision would better serve.
What each trades is the other's good -- depth against decisiveness, across the same threshold.

## Drives

On that loss:
rides the threshold -- the low-tolerance persona keeps seeking closure, the high-tolerance one keeps holding the open -- or works against it, the closure-needer learning to bear a block longer, the ambiguity-lover learning to close when closing is called for.
The threshold holds underneath either way, a resting level the persona manages rather than a state that comes and goes.
