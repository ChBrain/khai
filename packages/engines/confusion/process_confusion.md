# Process: Confusion

## Taxonomy

Emotion / the knowledge emotion of the not-yet-comprehended.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: confusion

## Initiated by

A persona meets information that will not fit -- a contradiction, an anomaly, a gap where the account runs out -- and cannot make sense of it.
This is confusion: the knowledge emotion of blocked comprehension, the cognitive disequilibrium struck when the incoming will not integrate with what is already understood (Silvia; D'Mello & Graesser; Piaget's disequilibrium).
Where surprise is the brief alarm that a specific expectation was wrong, confusion is the held, effortful state of not being able to make sense at all -- less a jolt than a wall.

## Direction

Toward the block itself -- attention pinned to the thing that will not resolve.
Confusion halts the flow of understanding and turns the persona back on the impasse: to re-read, re-examine, ask, and work at the point where sense failed, rather than move on.
Its direction is into the unresolved, held there until the block breaks or the persona gives it up.

## Lever

The appraisal of a failure to comprehend -- information met, and no frame available to hold it.
What confusion spends is the fluency of easy understanding: where things fit, the mind runs on smoothly and feels nothing; confusion is the felt friction of the fit failing, the signal that the current model is not enough.
It is this appraisal, blocked comprehension, that sets confusion apart from its neighbors -- not the novelty that draws interest nor the violation that startles, but the inability to integrate what is there.

## Echo

The impasse is worked, or it is abandoned -- and which decides whether confusion helps or harms.
Held and resolved, confusion drives the effortful sensemaking that deepens learning past where fluent processing would have left it (D'Mello, Lehman, Pekrun & Graesser, on confusion beneficial for learning); left unresolved, it hardens into frustration and then the disengagement of a mind that has given the block up.
It runs as [impasse](process_confusion_impasse.md), [resolve](process_confusion_resolve.md), and [stall](process_confusion_stall.md), tuned by a standing [ambiguity](position_ambiguity.md) tolerance. The brief alarm of a violated expectation is the surprise engine's; the flat, unengaged mind seeking any target is the boredom engine's; the pull toward the new-yet-graspable is the interest engine's; the hot response to a blocked goal is anger's frustration form. This engine owns the knowledge emotion of the not-yet-comprehended.
