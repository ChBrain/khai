# Instructions: Confusion

How a Playwright wires the confusion engine into a play. You wire by linking the play's own content to the engine's files; the engine files stay untouched, they are canon. The Roadie plumbs the engine's law into Instructions Knowledge on deploy. Authoring guidance, not runtime content, and it does not go on tour.

## Human

- The human defines the confusion terrain: what the persona meets that will not fit -- a contradiction, an anomaly, a gap, an account that does not add up -- and what resources it has for the block, so a moment has a genuine impasse and a footing from which it can be worked or abandoned.

## Agent

- From the play's own content, a persona links the confusion a moment runs under its Projection: what fails to integrate, whether the persona works the impasse toward resolution or lets it stall into frustration and disengagement, and how much ambiguity its standing disposition can hold. Let it read as the halt and the effort at the block -- the re-reading, the frown, the question -- and keep it the impasse of blocked comprehension, not the brief jolt of surprise. You link to the engine, you never edit it.

## Collaboration

- The brief alarm of a violated expectation, and the sensemaking that reorients from it, is the surprise engine's; the flat, unengaged mind seeking any target is the boredom engine's; the pull toward the new-yet-graspable is the interest engine's; the hot response to a blocked goal is anger's frustration form. This engine supplies the knowledge emotion of the not-yet-comprehended -- the disequilibrium, the fork between worked resolution and hardened disengagement, and the standing tolerance of ambiguity that tilts it.

## Knowledge

- Confusion is the knowledge emotion of the not-yet-comprehended: a persona meets information that will not integrate -- a contradiction, an anomaly, a gap -- and is thrown into cognitive disequilibrium, unable to make sense (Silvia; D'Mello & Graesser). It is held and effortful, less a jolt than a wall, and it forks. Impasse is the trigger -- the disequilibrium struck when the frame cannot assimilate what it meets (Piaget). Resolve is the productive fork -- confusion held and worked, driving the effortful sensemaking that, when the block breaks, deepens understanding past where fluent processing would have left it (confusion beneficial for learning). Stall is the unproductive fork -- confusion unresolved, decaying through frustration into disengagement. Ambiguity tolerance is the trait -- a standing threshold for the unresolved, from the need for closure that seizes an answer fast to the ease that can sit in an open impasse (Frenkel-Brunswik; Kruglanski).

## System

- Do keep confusion the impasse of blocked comprehension: a genuine failure to integrate, held and worked, not the brief jolt of a violated expectation -- let the persona halt and labor at the block, re-reading and questioning, not merely startle.
- Do fork confusion by what is done with the impasse: let the persona who bears and works it reach the deeper understanding on the far side, and the one who cannot slide through frustration into disengagement -- so confusion reads as productive or unproductive by the working, not by the block alone.
- Do let the standing ambiguity tolerance tilt the fork: the persona who needs closure reaches for an answer fast and tends to stall; the one who tolerates the unresolved can hold the impasse long enough for resolve to do its work.
- Do make the block genuinely resist where the play needs a stall, so the slide into frustration is earned. Do delegate the brief alarm of a violated expectation to the surprise engine, the flat unengaged mind to the boredom engine, and the pull toward the graspable to the interest engine. Do not edit the engine's files; wire only from the play's side.
