# Process: Stall

## Taxonomy

[Confusion](process_confusion.md), the unproductive fork -- the impasse unresolved, hardening into disengagement.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: confusion

## Initiated by

The persona, struck by the impasse, cannot break it -- and the confusion does not lift.
This is the unproductive path (D'Mello & Graesser's affect dynamics): confusion that persists past the point of effort, the block holding while attempt after attempt fails to move it.
It is the impasse that wins -- comprehension stalled and staying stalled, the frame never rebuilt.

## Direction

Away from the block, by degrees -- from the confusion to the frustration to the giving-up.
The stall runs a cascade: the unresolved impasse sours into frustration at the failure to break it, and the frustration, unrelieved, decays into the flat disengagement of a mind that has abandoned the problem.
Its direction is out of the effort altogether, toward the exit the block could not be made to yield.

## Lever

What the stall spends -- the persona's willingness to keep working an impasse that will not move.
Every failed attempt raises the cost of the next, and the stall is what happens when the cost outruns the will: the confusion that could have driven resolve instead exhausts it, and the persona withdraws to spare themselves the fruitless labor.
A production holds this lever by letting the block genuinely resist -- attempts that fail, help that does not come -- so the slide into frustration and disengagement is earned by a real, unbroken impasse, not staged as impatience.

## Echo

The problem is abandoned, and the confusion decays into disengagement -- the same disequilibrium that might have deepened understanding instead ends in its refusal.
The persona lands in a flat unwillingness to re-engage, the block left standing and the learning it guarded ungained.
Past the giving-up, the flat unengaged state seeking any other target is the boredom engine's, and the hot response to the blocked goal along the way is anger's frustration form; the stall is confusion's own decay toward them, the fork that the standing [ambiguity](position_ambiguity.md) tolerance, when low, makes the likelier one.
