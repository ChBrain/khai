# @chbrain/khai-engine-confusion

## 0.1.1

### Patch Changes

- bd8a337: Add the confusion engine (multi-type: process, position) -- the knowledge emotion of the not-yet-comprehended. Completes Silvia's knowledge-emotions quartet (interest, surprise, awe already stand; confusion was the gap). Confusion is the held, effortful state of blocked comprehension -- the cognitive disequilibrium struck when information will not integrate with what is already understood -- distinct from the brief alarm of surprise and the flat disengagement of boredom.

  - **Root (process):** `confusion` -- cognitive disequilibrium when the incoming will not fit, held until worked or abandoned; the felt friction of the frame coming up short.
  - **Forms (process):** `impasse` (the trigger -- a contradiction, anomaly, or gap the frame cannot assimilate, on D'Mello & Graesser's cognitive disequilibrium and Piaget's lineage), `resolve` (the productive fork -- the impasse held and worked, driving effortful sensemaking that deepens learning when the block breaks, on D'Mello, Lehman, Pekrun & Graesser's "confusion can be beneficial for learning"), `stall` (the unproductive fork -- the impasse unresolved, decaying through frustration into disengagement, on the confusion→frustration→boredom dynamics).
  - **Trait (position):** `ambiguity tolerance` -- a standing threshold for the unresolved, from the need for cognitive closure that seizes an answer fast (tending the stall) to the ease that can sit in an open impasse (Frenkel-Brunswik; Budner; Kruglanski).

  Warranted (LORE) on Silvia (confusion as a knowledge emotion), D'Mello & Graesser (cognitive disequilibrium and the affect dynamics of learning), D'Mello et al. 2014 (confusion beneficial for learning), Piaget (disequilibrium), and the ambiguity-tolerance / need-for-closure literature (Frenkel-Brunswik, Budner, Kruglanski). Bounded against `surprise` (the brief alarm of a violated expectation vs the held impasse), `boredom` (the flat disengaged mind vs the effortful wrestling with a block), `interest` (the new-yet-graspable -- the other side of the same coping-potential appraisal), and anger's `frustration` form (the hot response to a blocked goal that the stall passes through). Set at patch as the free level. No whitelist required -- the `confusion` root, `confusion_*` form stems, and `ambiguity` trait stem are unique.
