# Process: Resolve

## Taxonomy

[Confusion](process_confusion.md), the productive fork -- the impasse held and worked toward sense.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: confusion

## Initiated by

The persona, struck by the impasse, does not turn away but bears down on it.
This is the productive path of confusion (D'Mello, Lehman, Pekrun & Graesser): the disequilibrium held long enough to drive effortful sensemaking -- the re-reading, the questioning, the deliberate work of building a frame that can hold what the old one could not.
It is confusion put to use, the block met as a problem to solve rather than a wall to leave.

## Direction

Through the block, toward a frame that fits.
Resolve pushes into the impasse: it revises the model, tests a new reading, seeks the missing piece, and keeps at the disequilibrium until the pieces settle into an understanding that holds.
Its direction is forward through the confusion to the comprehension on its far side, not around it.

## Lever

What resolve spends -- the ease the persona could have kept by giving up.
Bearing a confusion is effortful and unpleasant, and the productive fork pays that cost: it trades the relief of disengaging for the deeper understanding that only comes from working the impasse through, the learning that fluent, unconfused processing never reaches.
A production holds this lever by making the resolution earned -- the persona actually laboring at the block and the understanding arriving as its reward -- rather than the confusion simply lifting on its own.

## Echo

The frame is rebuilt, and the confusion resolves into a comprehension deeper than before -- this is why confusion, worked through, is beneficial for learning.
The persona ends not where they began but past it, holding an understanding the impasse forced them to build; the disequilibrium was the price of the depth.
Whether the persona can sustain the effort to reach here, rather than dropping into the [stall](process_confusion_stall.md), turns partly on the standing [ambiguity](position_ambiguity.md) tolerance they bring -- the disposition to sit in the unresolved long enough for resolve to do its work.
