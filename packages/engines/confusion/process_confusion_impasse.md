# Process: Impasse

## Taxonomy

[Confusion](process_confusion.md), the trigger -- the cognitive disequilibrium that strikes it.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: confusion

## Initiated by

The persona meets something the current frame cannot hold: a contradiction, an anomaly, a missing step, an account that does not add up.
This is the elicitor of confusion (D'Mello & Graesser): cognitive disequilibrium, the impasse struck when incoming information cannot be assimilated into existing schemas and the machinery of understanding stalls.
It is the moment comprehension hits a wall -- not the wrong answer, but no answer, the frame come up short against what it must explain.

## Direction

Toward the point of failure -- the exact place the account breaks.
The impasse localizes: it pins attention to the contradiction or the gap, the sentence re-read, the step that will not follow, the two facts that cannot both be true.
Its direction is at the fault line in understanding, where the persona's model meets what it cannot absorb.

## Lever

What the impasse spends -- the sufficiency of the current frame.
As long as incoming information fits, understanding runs unfelt; the impasse is the frame failing, the disequilibrium that announces the model on hand is inadequate to what it has met.
A production holds this lever by making the block real from where the persona stands -- a genuine contradiction or gap in their frame, not merely difficult material -- so the disequilibrium is earned, the understanding actually stalled and not just slowed.

## Echo

The disequilibrium is struck -- and now it must go somewhere: the impasse is the fork's stem, not its outcome.
Held and worked, it drives the effortful [resolve](process_confusion_resolve.md) that can deepen understanding; left to sit, it hardens into the [stall](process_confusion_stall.md) of frustration and disengagement.
The impasse itself is neutral between them -- the same block that becomes a breakthrough for one persona becomes an abandonment for another, and what decides is not the disequilibrium but what is done with it.
