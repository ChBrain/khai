# Process: Justification

## Taxonomy

[Moral Disengagement](process_moral_disengagement.md), the conduct-reconstruing form.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: moral-disengagement

## Initiated by

The persona reconstrues the harmful act itself so that it no longer reads as wrong -- cast as serving a worthy end, renamed into something harmless-sounding, or measured against a worse alternative until it looks minor.
This is justification: disengagement worked on the deed, the act made to look right, or trivial, before it is done.
What would be condemned as itself is permitted as something else.

## Direction

Toward an act relabeled into acceptability at its source.
Justification works at the first locus, the conduct: by moral justification the harm is recast as serving a higher cause; by euphemistic labeling it is renamed -- "collateral damage," "letting go," "enhanced" this or that -- so the words no longer carry the weight of the thing; by advantageous comparison it is set beside a greater wrong until it seems, by contrast, benign.
The direction is a reframing of the deed so that the standard, still intact, simply does not apply to it as described.

## Lever

The construction of the act, spent to make it pass the persona's own standard.
Justification runs on the fact that a standard is applied to an act as construed, not as it is: change what the act is taken to be -- a service, a lesser evil, a sanitized procedure -- and the same standard that would have condemned it now clears it, without the standard itself being touched.
The lever is the description: the persona does not lower its morals, it re-narrates the deed until its morals no longer catch it.

## Echo

The act is done under a name that acquits it, the persona's standard honored on paper.
It is the most language-borne disengagement -- the euphemism, the noble cause, the "at least it's not as bad as" -- the reframing that lets a persona commit what it would denounce if it were called what it is.
It is the sibling of [displacement](process_displacement.md), [minimizing](process_minimizing.md), and [blaming](process_blaming.md), each disengaging at a different locus; this one works on the conduct. The moral standard being reframed around is the moral-judgment engine's; this owns the reconstruing of the act to exempt it.
