# Process: Blaming

## Taxonomy

[Moral Disengagement](process_moral_disengagement.md), the victim-devaluing form.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: moral-disengagement

## Initiated by

The persona devalues the one it harms -- stripping them of full humanity, or casting them as the author of their own suffering -- so that they are not a person wronged but a thing, or a cause of their own hurt.
This is blaming: disengagement worked on the recipient, the victim made unworthy of the sanction's protection.
If the harmed one does not count, or brought it on themselves, the harm is not a wrong.

## Direction

Toward a victim who is either less than a person or the real culprit.
Blaming works at the fourth locus, the recipient: by dehumanization the persona divests the target of human qualities -- reducing them to vermin, numbers, enemies, less-than -- so the inhibitions that guard persons no longer apply; by attribution of blame it casts the victim as having provoked or deserved the harm -- "they started it," "they should have known," "they made me" -- so the persona becomes the wronged party and the victim the offender.
The direction is a recasting of who the victim is, until harming them is no violation.

## Lever

The moral standing of the victim, spent to place them outside the sanction's reach.
Blaming runs on the fact that moral self-sanctions guard persons who count: lower the target's standing -- out of the human category, or into the role of aggressor -- and the guard lifts, because one owes nothing to a non-person and a striking-back is owed to one who started it.
The lever is the victim's worth: dehumanization denies it, and blame inverts it, and either way the harmed one is put where the persona's morals do not have to reach them.

## Echo

The victim is made to not count, and the harm becomes permissible or deserved.
It is the darkest disengagement and the engine of atrocity -- the enemy called vermin, the sufferer told they asked for it, the whole category of persons placed beyond the moral circle -- the mechanism by which the worst harms are not only permitted but felt as justified.
It is [justification](process_justification.md), [displacement](process_displacement.md), and [minimizing](process_minimizing.md)'s counterpart at the last locus, the victim. The standing cold devaluation of another as beneath one is the contempt engine's, and the between-group version of the dehumanizing is the intergroup composite's; this owns the devaluing of the victim in service of exempting one's own harm.
