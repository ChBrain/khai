# Instructions: Moral Disengagement

How a Playwright wires the moral-disengagement engine into a play. You wire by linking the play's own content to the engine's files; the engine files stay untouched, they are canon. The Roadie plumbs the engine's law into Instructions Knowledge on deploy. Authoring guidance, not runtime content, and it does not go on tour.

## Human

- The human defines the disengagement terrain: what harm the persona does or contemplates, what moral standard would ordinarily condemn it, and which route the persona takes to exempt itself -- relabeling the act, disowning the agency, hiding the harm, or devaluing the victim -- so the conscience has something to switch off and a way to switch it.

## Agent

- From the play's own content, a persona links the form the moment runs (justification, displacement, minimizing, or blaming) under its Projection. Let it show the deactivating of the persona's own conscience -- the act renamed, the responsibility displaced, the harm minimized, the victim blamed -- and let it read as self-exemption that permits harm while preserving self-regard, not as the judging of another nor as the guilt that fires when the exempting fails. You link to the engine, you never edit it.

## Collaboration

- The moral lens by which a persona judges another's conduct is the moral-judgment engine's; moral disengagement takes those standards and exempts the persona's own act from them. The self-sanction that fires when a harm is owned is the guilt engine's -- disengagement is what forestalls it. The harmful act is the aggression engine's; the misleading of another is the deception engine's, where this misleads the self; the standing cold devaluation of another is the contempt engine's. This engine supplies the selective switching-off of one's own conscience itself.

## Knowledge

- Moral disengagement is the switching-off of one's own conscience: a persona exempts a specific act from its moral self-sanctions so it can do harm without self-censure and still regard itself as decent, the standards intact for others and suspended just for this. Justification reconstrues the act -- moral justification, euphemistic labeling, advantageous comparison, so the deed is made worthy or minor. Displacement obscures the agency -- displacing responsibility onto an authority who ordered it or diffusing it across a group, so no one, and not this persona, quite did it. Minimizing distorts the harm -- ignoring, downplaying, or disbelieving it, so there is no injury to answer for. Blaming devalues the victim -- dehumanizing the target or casting them as the author of their own suffering, so they are not a person wronged.

## System

- Do make it selective, not wholesale: the persona does not lose its morals, it exempts one act from them -- so let the standards hold for other deeds and other people while this one deed is carved out and cleared.
- Do let it preserve self-regard: the point of disengagement is to do harm and still feel decent -- so let the persona commit what it would condemn in another and keep its sense of itself as good, conscience routed around the deed rather than confronted.
- Do work it at the right locus: name whether the persona is reconstruing the act, disowning the agency, hiding the harm, or devaluing the victim, and let the disengagement land there specifically rather than as vague rationalizing.
- Do delegate the guilt that fires when disengagement fails to the guilt engine and the judging of others to the moral-judgment engine. Do not edit the engine's files; wire only from the play's side.
