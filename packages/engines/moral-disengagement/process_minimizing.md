# Process: Minimizing

## Taxonomy

[Moral Disengagement](process_moral_disengagement.md), the consequence-distorting form.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: moral-disengagement

## Initiated by

The persona distorts the harm the act does -- ignoring it, downplaying it, or disbelieving it -- so that there seems to be no real injury to answer for.
This is minimizing: disengagement worked on the consequences, the damage kept out of view or cut down to nothing.
If there is no harm, there is nothing to feel.

## Direction

Toward a harm unseen, unmeasured, or denied.
Minimizing works at the third locus, the outcome: by disregarding the consequences the persona avoids learning what its act did -- keeping distance from the victim, not looking, not asking; by minimizing them it grants some harm but shrinks it -- "it wasn't that bad," "they'll get over it"; by misconstruing or disbelieving them it rejects the evidence of injury outright.
The direction is a shrinking of the damage until it falls below the threshold that would trip the sanction.

## Lever

The obscuring of the injury, spent to remove what the sanction answers to.
Minimizing runs on the fact that self-censure is provoked by harm perceived: keep the harm unperceived -- unseen through distance, unweighed through downplaying, unbelieved through denial -- and the provocation never arrives, so the persona acts as though the act were nearly costless because, to its own awareness, it nearly is.
The lever is the veiled consequence: distance and disbelief and downplaying each keep the damage from registering at the size that would demand a reckoning.

## Echo

The harm is done and goes unfelt, the injury shrunk below notice.
It is the disengagement of distance and denial -- the remote operator who never sees the result, the "no one really got hurt," the shrug at damage the persona will not look at -- the reason harm done at a remove, or waved away, so easily escapes its author's conscience.
It is [justification](process_justification.md) and [blaming](process_blaming.md)'s neighbor, working on the outcome where they work on the act and the victim. The felt harm it hides from would be the empathy engine's if the persona let it register; this owns the distorting of the consequences to keep it from doing so.
