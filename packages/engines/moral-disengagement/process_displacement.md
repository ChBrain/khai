# Process: Displacement

## Taxonomy

[Moral Disengagement](process_moral_disengagement.md), the agency-obscuring form.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: moral-disengagement

## Initiated by

The persona obscures its own agency in the harm -- placing the responsibility on an authority who ordered it, or spreading it so thin across a group that no single hand, and not this one, quite did the thing.
This is displacement: disengagement worked on the agency, the deed done but not, in the persona's account, by it.
The act happens; the author goes missing.

## Direction

Toward a harm whose responsibility rests anywhere but on the persona.
Displacement works at the second locus, the agency: by displacement of responsibility the persona attributes the act to a legitimate authority who sanctioned or commanded it -- "I was only following orders" -- so the deed is the commander's; by diffusion of responsibility the act is done by a group, divided among many, decided by committee, or lost in a division of labor, so that each contributor did only a part and no one did the whole.
The direction is a vanishing of the agent: the harm is real, but the persona's authorship of it is routed away.

## Lever

The relocation of authorship, spent to sever the persona from its own act.
Displacement runs on the gap between doing and owning: a self-sanction fires on the deeds a persona takes as its own, so relocating the ownership -- upward to an order, outward to a group -- lets the persona perform the act while disowning the authorship the sanction would attach to.
The lever is the missing author: obedience makes the hand an instrument, and diffusion makes it one of many, and in both the persona acts without being, to itself, the one who acted.

## Echo

The harm is done and no one owns it, the persona's part dissolved into an order or a crowd.
It is the disengagement of the functionary and the committee -- the obedient subordinate, the anonymous contributor, the "just doing my job" -- the reason systems of many hands can produce cruelties no single member would author alone.
It is [justification](process_justification.md) worked on the agent rather than the act, and it leans on the obedience the conformity engine studies and the crowd's diffusion the crowd engine names; here they are turned to exempting the self. This owns the obscuring of one's own agency to escape the sanction.
