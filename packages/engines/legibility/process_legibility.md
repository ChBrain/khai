# Process: Legibility

## Taxonomy

Urbanism.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: legibility

## Initiated by

A place is entered, and the eye tries to organize it.
Legibility starts the moment a place has to be read to be moved through -- the newcomer at the station, the stranger in the quarter, anyone working out where they are and how the place hangs together -- and the place either gives the eye what it needs to build a map or leaves it a blur.
Every place has an image, a way it can be held in the mind that is as particular as its look; legibility is that image as an actor, not the inert layout a setting is usually taken for, but the readability the place offers or withholds.

## Direction

Into the mind, as a map the place is built into.
Five elements compose the image: [Path](process_path.md) is the channel one moves along, [Edge](process_edge.md) the seam that bounds one section from another, [District](process_district.md) the section of common character one is inside of, [Node](process_node.md) the focal point one enters, and [Landmark](process_landmark.md) the reference point one steers by without entering.
A place is not held as one flat picture but as a structure of these -- the paths that thread it, the edges that seam it, the districts it falls into, the nodes it turns on, the landmarks it is fixed by -- or, where the elements are weak or confused, as a place one cannot hold, gets lost in, and never learns.

## Lever

The place's power to be imaged against the confusion of a place that will not resolve.
The carrying force is the place's own form offering the eye its handles -- the clear path, the sharp edge, the distinct district, the strong node, the singular landmark that together let a stranger build the map; the resistance is sameness and confusion, the gridless sprawl, the district without character, the missing landmark, the place so uniform or so tangled that no image forms and the newcomer stays lost.
A legible place is learned fast and held with ease, moved through with the quiet confidence of knowing where one is; an illegible one is a daily disorientation, and the same walk never becomes familiar.

## Echo

The place known as an image, and carried as a map.
Legibility does not close; it is the standing readability of the place, the structure the mind builds from what the place shows and keeps as the way it knows the place at all.
It hands off what it is not: the layout and standing mood are space's, the sound the place makes is soundscape's, the social life it hosts is street-life's, and a persona's own sense of direction is theirs.
The place is read as an image -- a setting is not only somewhere seen but somewhere held, and a place is a clear map or a blur by what it gives the eye to organize it by. A too-perfect legibility can even bore, and a measure of mystery invite; but the image the place offers is what a mind has to hold it by.
