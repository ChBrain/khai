# Process: Node

## Taxonomy

[Legibility](process_legibility.md), the element of the strategic focal point one enters.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: legibility

## Initiated by

The place gathers at points, and one enters them.
Node starts wherever a place has a strategic focus one can go into and pause at -- the junction where paths meet, the square, the transit interchange, the plaza, the busy corner, the concentration a place turns on and travels to and from.
It is the image's hinge: the intensive focus one is within for a moment, where paths join and decisions are made, the place-within-the-place a journey converges on and radiates from.

## Direction

Inward to a focus, where the ways meet and the traveller pauses.
Node draws the place to its points of concentration -- the crossing that gathers its streets, the square that collects its quarter, the station that funnels its lines, the market that draws its district -- the spot a traveller enters, orients at, and sets off from again.
It moves the journey through its joints: a strong node is a distinct, entered focus, a place one clearly arrives at and leaves, the strategic point where paths converge and a person stops to choose a direction, and it anchors the surrounding image around itself.

## Lever

The node's focus against the crossing that fails to concentrate.
The carrying force is the node that reads as a place -- a junction or square with enough identity and enclosure to be entered, arrived at, and remembered, the focus that gathers its paths and holds a moment of the journey; the resistance is the weak node, the crossing or space too diffuse to register as a somewhere, so the paths meet at nothing the mind can hold and the journey passes through without a hinge.
Give the convergences identity and the place turns on strong points one navigates by; leave them formless and the map has no joints, and the traveller crosses without ever arriving anywhere.

## Echo

The place hinged at its focal points.
Node leaves the place turning on its concentrations -- the strategic foci one enters and pauses at, the joints the journey converges on -- and hands the traveller the points the map pivots around.
It is the image's junction element: the intensive foci a place gathers at, the squares and crossings and interchanges one arrives at, orients in, and departs from, closely tied to the paths that meet there.
Node is read from the entering, the place known by the focal points one goes into and turns at.
