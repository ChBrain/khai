# Process: District

## Taxonomy

[Legibility](process_legibility.md), the element of the section of common character one is inside of.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: legibility

## Initiated by

The place falls into regions, and one is inside of them.
District starts wherever a place has a medium-to-large section with a character shared across it -- the quarter, the neighborhood, the market district, the old town, the region a stranger can name and know they have entered and left.
It is the image's mass: not a line but an area, the two-dimensional region the mind enters and is within, the part of the place that has a common texture, use, or look one recognizes from the inside.

## Direction

Across an area, as a region entered and inhabited.
District spreads the place into named parts -- the financial quarter, the university area, the warehouse district, the leafy suburb -- each with a thematic unity of building, use, activity, or texture that marks it off and makes it a place within the place.
It moves nothing; it organizes by region: a strong district has a clear character and a sensed extent, so one knows on entering that the texture has changed, knows roughly how far it runs, and holds it as a distinct area of the map, a somewhere one is in.

## Lever

The district's character against the sameness that leaves no region.
The carrying force is the district that reads as itself -- a thematic unity strong enough to be felt on entry and told from its neighbors, an identity the region wears across its whole extent; the resistance is the uniformity or the incoherence that leaves no districts at all, the endless sameness where no part differs from the next, so the mind has no regions to divide the place into and cannot tell where it is within the whole.
Give the parts distinct character and the place organizes into knowable regions; wash that out and the place is one undifferentiated spread the mind cannot section.

## Echo

The place massed into its regions.
District leaves the place divided into areas -- the sections of common character one enters and is inside of, the regions the map is composed of -- and hands the mind the parts it holds the place in.
It is the image's areal element: the two-dimensional regions a place falls into, the quarters and neighborhoods a stranger learns to name and a resident knows themselves to be within.
District is read from the being-inside, the place known by the regions one enters and their common character.
