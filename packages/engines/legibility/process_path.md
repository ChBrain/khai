# Process: Path

## Taxonomy

[Legibility](process_legibility.md), the element of the channel one moves along.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: legibility

## Initiated by

The place is known first by the ways one moves through it.
Path starts wherever a place offers a channel of movement that the eye follows and the feet take -- the street, the walkway, the transit line, the canal, the corridor, the route along which a place is customarily travelled.
It is the backbone of the image: most people know a place by moving through it, and the paths are the threads the rest of the map hangs on, the element the other four are arranged along and seen from.

## Direction

Along, in the line of travel, gathering the place to either side.
Path draws the place out as a route -- the way with a beginning and a direction and a destination, the street that gathers its shops and doors and crossings as one moves down it, the line the eye runs ahead along to see where the place goes.
It moves the traveller through and organizes what they pass: a strong path has continuity and direction, a clear where-from and where-to, and it strings the districts, nodes, and landmarks into an order the mind can follow and remember.

## Lever

The path's clarity against the tangle that loses the traveller.
The carrying force is the path that reads as a path -- continuous, directed, distinct enough to be followed and told from its neighbors, its destination sensed from along it; the resistance is the confusion of paths without character, the tangle where every way looks alike and none declares its direction, so the traveller cannot tell one from another or hold the route.
Give the paths clear identity and direction and the place threads together and is learned along them; blur them and the whole image loses its backbone, and the place will not resolve into a route.

## Echo

The place strung along its ways.
Path leaves the place organized as routes -- the channels one moves along, the backbone the rest of the image is arranged on -- and hands the traveller the order in which the place unfolds as they go.
It is the image's dominant element: the way most people hold most places, the line the districts and nodes and landmarks are met along and remembered by.
Path is read from the moving, the place known as the route one takes through it.
