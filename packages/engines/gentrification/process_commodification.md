# Process: Commodification

## Taxonomy

[Gentrification](process_gentrification.md), the facet this force turns on.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: gentrification

## Initiated by

The place's own character becomes the thing being sold.
Commodification starts when the neighborhood's difference -- its grit, its history, its working life, the very marks the decline left -- is packaged as authenticity and priced: the old sign kept as decor, the factory's name on the loft, the immigrant kitchen turned to a destination.
It is gentrification discovering that what it displaces is also what it can sell.

## Direction

Onto the Withheld, at the level of meaning.
Not only the rent is now withheld but the place's identity: its character is curated for the incomer and closed to the ones who lived it, the culture kept as a surface once its bearers are gone.
Commodification moves the place from a lived character to a consumed one -- the authenticity marketed precisely as the authentic community is dispersed.

## Lever

The place's character against the price that selling it sets.
The carrying force is the market value of authenticity -- difference is scarce, and scarcity sells; the resistance is whatever keeps the character in the hands that made it, a culture that resists being staged for sale.
The cruelty of the facet is its circularity: the more the place's real life is displaced, the more valuable its remembered life becomes, so the selling of the character accelerates the loss of it.

## Echo

The place's soul kept as a brand, its bearers gone.
It shows the heritage plaques, the artisanal everything, the murals of a community no longer housed there, and carries beneath it the deepest withholding -- a place that sells belonging to those who just arrived while withholding it from those who made it.
Commodification is dated from the moment the character became a selling point, and it is the facet that reveals gentrification's inverted ambivalence in full: the loss is not only borne but marketed, sold back as the very value it destroyed.
