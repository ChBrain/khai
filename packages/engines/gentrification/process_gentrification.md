# Process: Gentrification

## Taxonomy

Reinvestment.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: gentrification

## Initiated by

Capital returns to a place it once fled, because the gap it left has grown wide enough to profit.
Gentrification starts not with taste but with the rent-gap: the distance between what the disinvested place earns now and what it could earn reinvested, dug deep by the decline, until closing it pays.
When the gap is wide enough, capital re-enters -- and the place that was let go begins to be taken back, at a price its holders never set.

## Direction

Onto the place's Withheld, converting what it offered.
Four facets carry the re-entry:
[Rent-gap](process_rent_gap.md) is the mechanism that draws the capital, [Upgrading](process_upgrading.md) the fabric improved to realize it, [Eviction](process_eviction.md) the prior occupants cleared, and [Commodification](process_commodification.md) the place's own character sold back as authenticity.
What the place once freely offered -- a rent one could meet, a belonging one could keep -- is released now only to those who can pay, and withheld from those who cannot.

## Lever

What the place once offered against the price re-entry now sets on it.
The carrying force is the capital returning to close the gap; the resistance is whatever holds the place for its occupants -- rent control, tenure, a public claim, an organized refusal.
Remove the protections and the offer converts to a withholding; keep them and the gap can stay open, the capital held at the door.
Gentrification is the family's ambivalence inverted: where decay and dereliction were loss that might be read as value, gentrification is apparent revival that carries a borne loss.

## Echo

The place reinvested, and its prior occupants gone.
Gentrification closes the rent-gap dereliction's disinvestment opened -- the down and the up of one curve -- and hands the place onward changed: improved in fabric, raised in price, emptied of the ones who held it through the decline.
It shares its edge with restoration, which also re-enters and repairs; the two part on what they serve -- restoration keeps the fabric for its age, gentrification withholds the place for its yield.
The place is dated from the wave that took it, and from the belonging it withheld to those who could no longer meet its price.
