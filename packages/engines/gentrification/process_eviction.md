# Process: Eviction

## Taxonomy

[Gentrification](process_gentrification.md), the facet this force turns on.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: gentrification

## Initiated by

The rising price reaches the sitting occupants, and pushes them out.
Eviction here is the whole of displacement, not only the formal notice: the lease not renewed, the rent raised past reach, the building sold from under, the harassment that hurries a holdout -- and the exclusion of those who can no longer move in, priced out of a place that would once have taken them.
It starts wherever the new price meets an occupant who cannot pay it.

## Direction

Onto the Withheld, made human.
What the place offered its holders -- shelter they could afford, a footing they could keep -- is now withheld, released only to the ones who can meet the raised price and closed to the ones who held it through the decline.
Eviction moves the occupants out in order, the least able first, until the population that survived the disinvestment is replaced by the one that follows the capital.

## Lever

The occupant's claim to stay against the price that would remove them.
The carrying force is the raised rent and the value of the emptied unit; the resistance is tenure, rent regulation, right-of-return, an organized tenancy -- whatever lets the occupant keep the place against its rising worth.
Strip the protection and the withholding completes; hold it and the occupant stays while the gap around them widens, an island in the wave.

## Echo

The prior occupants gone, and the place kept from them.
It shows the moving trucks, the shuttered old shops, the names on the buzzer all changed within a few years, and carries beneath it the cost the upgrading's polish hides -- the belonging withheld, the community dispersed to wherever the next disinvested place will hold them.
Eviction is dated from the departures, and it is the harm gentrification's revival is built on: the place bettered for its next occupants by the removal of its last.
