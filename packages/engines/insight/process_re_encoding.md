# Process: Re-encoding

## Taxonomy

[Insight](process_insight.md), the mechanism where a part is read anew.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: insight

## Initiated by

The persona has read some element of the problem one way, and that reading is what holds the impasse.
A word taken in its common sense, an object seen only in its usual role, a fact registered but mis-weighted -- the frame is wrong not because a rule was added but because a part was encoded misleadingly. The problem cannot be solved while that element means what the persona has taken it to mean.
What enters is a representation with a mis-read component, waiting to be read again.

## Direction

Toward the element reinterpreted, and the solution that reinterpretation reveals.
The restructuring is a change of meaning: the persona suddenly reads the part differently -- the pliers become a pendulum bob, the "operation" a different operation, the overlooked detail the crucial one -- and with the new reading the problem reorganizes around it and the answer appears.
The direction runs through the reinterpreted part: change what it means, and the whole falls into place.

## Lever

The mis-encoded element meets a cue or a pause that lets it be re-read.
Whether the re-encoding fires turns on the misleading reading being loosely enough held to give -- a hint that recolours the element, a fresh look after setting the problem down, an association that supplies the overlooked sense. The more fixed the persona's first reading, the harder the part is to see anew.
Loosen the grip of the first encoding and the element re-reads; hold it and the impasse stays.

## Echo

A part re-read, and the problem reorganized around its new meaning.
Because the change is to what an element means rather than to the rules or the groupings, re-encoding is the mechanism of the problem whose crux was a single misleading reading -- undo it, and the rest was already in place.
It is one of three ways the stuck frame gives: the [constraint relaxation](process_constraint_relaxation.md) form drops a needless rule, the [chunk decomposition](process_chunk_decomposition.md) form breaks a fused whole; this form re-reads a part. That the whole arrives suddenly and with no warmth ramp is the [root's](process_insight.md); the effortful sensemaking it may follow is the confusion engine's.
