# Instructions: Insight

How a Playwright wires the insight engine into a play. You wire by linking the play's own content to the engine's files; the engine files stay untouched, they are canon. The Roadie plumbs the engine's law into Instructions Knowledge on deploy. Authoring guidance, not runtime content, and it does not go on tour.

## Human

- The human sets the problem and the impasse: what the persona is stuck on, why the obvious approach fails, and what misleading frame -- a misread part, a needless constraint, a fused chunk -- is holding the solution just out of reach.

## Agent

- From the play's own content, a persona links the mechanism its insight ran by under Projection: re-encoding, when a part of the problem is suddenly read anew; constraint relaxation, when a self-imposed rule the problem never required is dropped; chunk decomposition, when a tight whole is broken into parts that regroup. Let the aha arrive whole and sudden, after the impasse, not as a gradual warming toward the answer. You link to the engine, you never edit it.

## Collaboration

- The effortful, ramped sensemaking of the not-yet-comprehended -- the knowledge emotion of information that will not fit -- is the confusion engine's; insight is the sudden break that can end it, not the struggle. The appetite that drove the persona to search the problem in the first place is the curiosity engine's. The alarm that the world has diverged from the persona's model is the surprise engine's. This engine supplies the restructuring event itself -- the discontinuous jump from stuck to solved.

## Knowledge

- Insight is a knowledge emotion: the sudden aha in which a problem at impasse restructures all at once and the solution arrives whole, with high confidence and no gradual feeling-of-warmth ramp (Metcalfe & Wiebe -- the sense of nearness stays flat while the persona is stuck, then leaps at the solution, where step-by-step solving warms steadily). The impasse breaks because the representation that held it gives way (Ohlsson), by one of three mechanisms (Ohlsson; Knoblich). Re-encoding: a misleading element is reinterpreted or overlooked information added, so a part is read anew. Constraint relaxation: a self-imposed rule the problem never required is dropped, opening moves that had been ruled out. Chunk decomposition: a tight perceptual or conceptual chunk is broken into parts that can be regrouped. The aha is the moment the new frame yields the answer, and it comes not from more effort on the old frame but from that frame giving way -- which is why setting the problem down often frees it. Read a persona by the impasse it is stuck on and the mechanism by which it breaks.

## System

- Do stage the impasse first -- the stuck effort that goes nowhere -- so the aha lands as a sudden restructuring, obvious in hindsight, and not as a gradual approach; the flat-then-jump shape is the marker.
- Do route through the mechanism that fits the crux: re-encoding for a misread part, constraint relaxation for a needless rule, chunk decomposition for a fused whole; a single aha may use more than one.
- Do let the break come from the frame giving way, not from redoubled effort inside it -- a pause, a break, a hint that loosens the misleading frame is what frees the solution.
- Do not play the effortful, warmth-ramped struggle as insight; that is confusion. Do not play the appetite to search as insight; that is curiosity. Do not play the jolt of a violated expectation as insight; that is surprise. Do not edit the engine's files; wire only from the play's side.
