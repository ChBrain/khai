# Process: Chunk Decomposition

## Taxonomy

[Insight](process_insight.md), the mechanism where a fused whole is broken up.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: insight

## Initiated by

The persona is holding part of the problem as a single tight unit that its habit will not break.
A shape seen only as a whole, a familiar grouping perceived as one thing, a chunk so well-learned the parts inside it have gone invisible -- the frame is stuck because a unit that needs splitting is being treated as indivisible. The persona has the components it needs, fused where it cannot use them.
What enters is a representation with a chunk too tight to work with.

## Direction

Toward the chunk broken, and its components regrouped.
The restructuring is a decomposition: the persona suddenly sees the tight whole as separable -- the letter or numeral as strokes that can be moved, the familiar figure as parts that can be recombined -- and the freed components regroup into the configuration the solution needs.
The direction runs through the broken chunk: split the unit habit had fused, and the parts fall into a new and solving arrangement.

## Lever

The tight chunk meets whatever loosens its grip.
Whether the decomposition fires turns on the chunk's tightness -- the more meaningful and well-practised the unit, the harder its parts are to see as parts, and the deepest impasses sit on the chunks the persona knows best. A cue that draws attention to a component, or a pause that loosens the perceptual habit, lets the whole come apart.
Hold the chunk as one thing and the impasse stays; break it into parts and the regrouping becomes possible.

## Echo

A fused whole broken, and its parts regrouped into the answer.
Because the change is to the grain of a perceptual or conceptual unit rather than to a part's meaning or a search's boundary, chunk decomposition is the mechanism of the problem whose crux was a unit held too tight -- break it, and the components were always enough.
It is one of three ways the stuck frame gives: the [re-encoding](process_re_encoding.md) form re-reads a part, the [constraint relaxation](process_constraint_relaxation.md) form drops a needless rule; this form splits a chunk. That the whole arrives suddenly and with no warmth ramp is the [root's](process_insight.md); the alarm at a violated expectation is the surprise engine's.
