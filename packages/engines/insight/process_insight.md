# Process: Insight

## Taxonomy

Knowledge emotion of the sudden restructuring.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: insight

## Initiated by

A persona is stuck at an impasse on a problem, its analytic effort spent and going nowhere, when the problem suddenly gives.
The trigger is not the steady closing-in of step-by-step work but its failure: the persona has run the obvious moves, exhausted the approach it started with, and reached the wall where more of the same yields nothing. What enters the process is a problem whose current framing cannot solve it -- and the readiness of that framing to break.
This is the ground the aha needs: not a problem being worked toward solution, but one whose solution requires the persona to stop seeing it the way it has been.

## Direction

Toward the sudden arrival of the whole solution.
Insight does not deliver its answer in pieces; the restructuring happens all at once, and the solution appears complete, obvious in hindsight, and carried by a jump in confidence rather than a gradual growing-warm. Metcalfe and Wiebe's signature marks it: on a problem solved by insight, a persona's rated feeling-of-warmth -- its sense of nearness to the answer -- stays flat while it is stuck, then leaps at the moment of solution, where a problem solved by incremental analysis warms steadily as the answer is approached.
The direction is a discontinuity: from no visible progress to the finished answer, with little in between.

## Lever

An impasse meets a representation ready to restructure.
Whether the aha fires turns on the problem's current framing being both wrong for the solution and loose enough to give: the persona must be genuinely stuck (the analytic route foreclosed) and the misleading frame -- the wrong reading of a part, the needless constraint, the fused chunk -- must be one that can break. Setting the problem down, sleeping, or turning away often does the freeing, the frame relaxing off the conscious effort that was holding it.
The lever is not more effort on the old frame but the collapse of that frame; push harder inside it and the impasse holds, let it give and the solution falls out.

## Echo

The restructuring runs by one of three mechanisms, the ways a stuck frame gives way (Ohlsson; Knoblich): [re-encoding](process_re_encoding.md), where a part of the problem is reinterpreted or overlooked information added; [constraint relaxation](process_constraint_relaxation.md), where a self-imposed rule the problem never required is dropped; or [chunk decomposition](process_chunk_decomposition.md), where a tight chunk is broken into parts that can be regrouped.
The three are not stages and not exclusive; a single aha may re-encode a part and relax a constraint at once.
What ends the process is the solution arriving whole, and the confidence that comes with it -- but the effortful, ramped sensemaking of the not-yet-comprehended is the confusion engine's, the appetite that drove the search is the curiosity engine's, and the alarm at a violated expectation is the surprise engine's. This engine owns the sudden restructuring itself.
