# Process: Constraint Relaxation

## Taxonomy

[Insight](process_insight.md), the mechanism where a needless rule is dropped.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: insight

## Initiated by

The persona is working the problem under a constraint the problem never actually imposed.
An assumption smuggled in with the setup -- the lines must stay inside the square, the arithmetic must move a digit not an operator, the parts must be used as given -- rules out the very moves the solution needs. The persona is not short of options; it has fenced them off with a rule it never checked.
What enters is a search space wrongly narrowed by a self-imposed limit.

## Direction

Toward the constraint lifted, and the moves it had forbidden.
The restructuring is a widening: the persona suddenly sees that the assumed rule does not bind -- the lines may leave the square, the equation may be reread across the equals sign -- and the moves that were unthinkable a moment before become available, and one of them solves it.
The direction runs through the dropped constraint: release it, and the space that held the answer opens.

## Lever

The unnecessary constraint meets the recognition that it is unnecessary.
Whether the relaxation fires turns on the assumed rule being both false to the problem and available to be doubted -- a constraint held tacitly, never stated, is the hardest to drop, and much of the impasse is not knowing there is a rule there to question. Prompts that license the forbidden move, or a break that loosens the tacit assumption, let it go.
Keep the constraint unexamined and the impasse holds; expose it as optional and the space opens.

## Echo

A needless rule dropped, and the forbidden move made available.
Because the change is to the boundary of the search rather than to a part's meaning or a chunk's grouping, constraint relaxation is the mechanism of the problem whose crux was an assumption never required -- see that it does not bind, and the solution was always reachable.
It is one of three ways the stuck frame gives: the [re-encoding](process_re_encoding.md) form re-reads a part, the [chunk decomposition](process_chunk_decomposition.md) form breaks a fused whole; this form lifts a rule. That the whole arrives suddenly and with no warmth ramp is the [root's](process_insight.md); the appetite that drove the search is the curiosity engine's.
