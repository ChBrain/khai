# Insight: Reference

## Line of Work

Insight as a **process** engine and a knowledge emotion: the sudden aha in which a problem at impasse restructures all at once and the solution arrives whole, with high confidence and no gradual feeling-of-warmth ramp -- taken across the three mechanisms by which the stuck representation gives way. The domain does not model the effortful, ramped sensemaking of the not-yet-comprehended, which is the confusion engine's; nor the appetite that drives the search toward the unknown, which is the curiosity engine's; nor the alarm at a violated expectation, which is the surprise engine's. It models the restructuring event itself -- the discontinuous break from stuck to solved, and how the frame that held the impasse changes.

The spine is Metcalfe and Wiebe's phenomenological signature and Ohlsson's mechanistic account. Metcalfe and Wiebe distinguished insight from non-insight problem solving by the shape of the feeling-of-warmth curve: asked to rate how near they felt to the answer, people solving incremental problems warmed steadily as they approached it, but people solving insight problems stayed cold -- no sense of nearness -- until the solution appeared suddenly, the warmth leaping only at the end. The absence of the ramp is the marker: insight arrives whole, not by accumulation. Ohlsson's representational change theory says why: an insight problem stalls at an impasse because the solver's initial representation makes the solution unreachable, and the aha is the moment that representation restructures. Ohlsson and, with matchstick-arithmetic experiments, Knoblich and colleagues named the mechanisms of that change -- re-encoding a misleading element, relaxing a self-imposed constraint the problem never required, and decomposing a perceptual chunk into recombinable parts. The engine takes the signature as its root and the three mechanisms as its forms. The altitudes are those: the sudden restructuring, and its re-encoding, constraint-relaxation, and chunk-decomposition mechanisms.

## Restrictions

What the engine refuses to model, and to whom it delegates.

- **The effortful not-yet-comprehended (confusion)**: The engine models the sudden break, not the gradual struggle. The knowledge emotion of cognitive disequilibrium -- information that will not fit, worked through effortful sensemaking with a felt ramp of approach -- is owned by the confusion engine; insight is what can end that struggle discontinuously, the impasse breaking all at once rather than being resolved step by step. Confusion is the effortful climb with a warmth ramp; insight is the leap with none. Insight often follows confusion, but the two are different events, and the marker that tells them apart is the shape of the approach.
- **The appetite for the unknown (curiosity)**: The engine models the resolution event, not the drive toward it. The itch to close an information gap, the appetite that sends a persona searching, is owned by the curiosity engine; insight is what may arrive at the end of that search, not the wanting that began it. Curiosity opens the question; insight closes it, suddenly.
- **The violated expectation (surprise)**: The engine models the restructuring toward a solution, not the alarm at a mismatch. The pre-valence jolt that the world has diverged from the persona's model is owned by the surprise engine; an aha can carry surprise, but insight is specifically the reorganization that yields the answer, not the registering that something did not fit. Surprise says the model was wrong; insight supplies the new model.
- **Incremental, analytic problem solving**: The engine models insight specifically, not step-by-step search. The deliberate working-through of a problem by successive moves, warming steadily toward the answer, is the non-insight route Metcalfe and Wiebe contrast insight against; this engine owns only the sudden-restructuring case, where the analytic route has foreclosed and the solution arrives whole. Analysis accumulates; insight restructures.

## Encoding

Source to constraint, per file.

- **[insight](process_insight.md)** (the root, process): The sudden aha -- an impasse whose representation restructures all at once, the solution arriving whole with no warmth ramp. Anchored by Metcalfe & Wiebe (the flat-then-jump signature) and Ohlsson (impasse as representation, aha as restructuring).
- **[re-encoding](process_re_encoding.md)** (process, form): A misleading element reinterpreted or overlooked information added, so a part is read anew. Anchored by Ohlsson (re-encoding) and Mednick (the associative leap).
- **[constraint-relaxation](process_constraint_relaxation.md)** (process, form): A self-imposed constraint the problem never required, dropped, opening forbidden moves. Anchored by Ohlsson and Knoblich et al. (constraint relaxation).
- **[chunk-decomposition](process_chunk_decomposition.md)** (process, form): A tight perceptual chunk broken into parts that can be regrouped. Anchored by Knoblich et al. (chunk decomposition).
