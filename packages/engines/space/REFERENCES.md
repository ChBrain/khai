# Space: Reference

## Line of Work

Space as **place**: the physical setting treated as a party to what happens in it -- the built and natural environment that acts on the persona who enters and that the persona acts back on, so that bare space becomes lived place. The domain does not model the mass of other people as an environment, which is crowd's; nor the matching of one's conduct to the others present, which is conformity's; nor the behaving-for-an-audience of others, which is face's; nor the specific attention-refilling setting, which is restorative-environment's; nor the all-encompassing institution, which is total-institution's; nor the governance of a shared resource, which is commons's; nor the persona's own bodily state, which is body's. It models the setting itself as a mover: what it affords, what the persona claims in it, and how it feels -- and it holds that apart from the people who happen to be inside it.

The spine is environmental psychology and the geography of place. A physical setting is not a neutral container: it offers and refuses conduct, it carries a standing behavioral program that presses on whoever enters, it is claimed and marked by its dwellers, and it induces mood. The distinction the engine turns on is Tuan's -- space is abstract extent, place is space endowed with meaning through human dwelling -- and the transaction is reciprocal: the setting shapes the dweller and the dweller charges the setting. The three expressions are the setting's faces: affordance (what it offers and scripts), territory (what the persona claims back), and atmosphere (how it feels).

The engine's boundary is sharpest where a setting's effect looks social. The hush of a church is a worked example: part of it is the **place** -- the sanctuary carries a behavior setting, a standing program of reverence that hushes even a single visitor in a near-empty nave, because the program lives in the space, not in the people -- and that part is this engine's. The rest is delegated: the hush that comes from the press of other silent bodies is the crowd engine's, and the hush from matching what the others present are doing is the conformity engine's. Space owns the setting's own action, not the influence of the others in it.

## Restrictions

What the engine refuses to model, and to whom it delegates.

- **Crowd**: The engine models the physical setting as a mover, not the mass of other people as an environment. The press of bodies that acts on a persona before anyone speaks -- density, growth, the crowd prior to its members -- is owned by the crowd engine; space owns the room, not the throng in it.
- **Conformity**: The engine models the setting's own program, not the matching of one's conduct to the others present. Falling silent because everyone else is silent is the conformity engine's; falling silent because the sanctuary itself hushes even an empty nave is space's -- the behavior setting lives in the place, the social matching in the people.
- **Face and Restorative-environment**: The engine models the setting broadly, not the audience-management or the attention cure specifically. Behaving for the others who can see one is the face engine's; the particular setting that refills depleted directed attention -- being-away, extent, fascination, compatibility -- is the restorative-environment engine's. Space owns the general transaction; these own their slices.
- **Total-institution, Commons, and Body**: The engine models the ordinary setting, not the total institution, the shared resource, or the body. The all-encompassing place that governs every sphere of life is total-institution's; the governance of a common-pool resource is commons's; the persona's own physical state, hunger and fatigue and pain, is body's. Space owns the place, not the institution, the resource-regime, or the flesh.

## Encoding

Source to constraint, per file.

- **[space](place_space.md)** (the anchor): The physical setting as a party to the scene -- acting on the persona who enters and claimed by them in return, bare space become lived place. Anchored by Tuan (space and place) and the environmental-psychology tradition.
- **[affordance](place_affordance.md)** (expression): The setting acting -- what it offers for action and the standing behavioral program it pulls from whoever enters, the church that hushes even near-empty. Anchored by Gibson (affordances) and Barker (behavior settings).
- **[territory](place_territory.md)** (expression): The persona claiming -- the region bounded, marked, personalized, and defended, privacy regulated by the borders drawn in space. Anchored by Altman (territoriality and privacy regulation).
- **[atmosphere](place_atmosphere.md)** (expression): The setting felt -- the mood a space induces and the approach or avoidance that follows, space charged into place. Anchored by Mehrabian & Russell (environmental mood, pleasure and arousal) and Appleton (prospect-refuge).
