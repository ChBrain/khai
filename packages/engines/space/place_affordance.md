# Place: Affordance

## Taxonomy

Affordance is an expression of the space engine. It models the setting acting on the persona: what a space offers for action and the standing behavioral program it carries -- the conduct its layout, features, and character make possible, invite, and pull from whoever enters. It is the setting scripting behavior, the church that hushes even near-empty, distinct from the other people present who might hush a persona too.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: space

## Shown

The features that announce what can be done here before anyone does it: the handle that says pull, the path that says walk, the pew that says sit and face forward, the threshold that says lower your voice. The space presents its possibilities and its program in its very form -- the persona reads what the place is for, and how one behaves in it, from the arrangement itself.

## Holds

The standing pattern of behavior the setting pulls from whoever enters -- the behavior setting, prior to and larger than its current occupants. A church holds a program of hush and reverence, a stadium a program of roar, a library a program of quiet industry, and each presses that program on the newcomer whether the room is full or nearly empty: the setting hushes the single visitor as surely as the crowd, because the program lives in the place, not in the people. What the space affords, it holds as a claim on all who cross into it.

## Offers

The actions the space makes possible and invites: the affordances its surfaces and layout hold out -- ground to walk, a wall to lean on, a nook to confer in, a rostrum to command from -- and the conduct its program calls for, the reverence the sanctuary asks, the ease the parlor grants, the vigilance the alley demands. The space offers the persona a script for how to be in it, and most take the script the place holds out.

## Withheld

The conduct the setting will not afford or forbids: the door with no handle, the wall with no opening, the vast hall that withholds any private word, the plan that offers no way to do the thing the persona came to do. And it withholds its program from the one who defies it -- but at a cost, for to shout in the silent sanctuary or kneel in the roaring stand is to fight the place itself. What a space does not afford, the persona cannot easily do in it, however they try.
