# Instructions: Space

How a Playwright wires the space engine into a play. You wire by linking the play's own content to the engine's files; the engine files stay untouched, they are canon. The Roadie plumbs the engine's law into Instructions Knowledge on deploy. Authoring guidance, not runtime content, and it does not go on tour.

## Human

- The human defines the space terrain: what setting the scene is in and what kind of place it is -- its scale, bounds, light, and layout -- what conduct it affords and refuses, what ground the personas claim in it, and what mood it lays on them, so the setting has something to act with and something to be acted on.

## Agent

- From the play's own content, a persona links the face of the setting the moment turns (affordance, territory, or atmosphere) under its Projection. Let it show the setting moving the scene -- the conduct the space invites or forbids, the ground the persona bounds and marks as their own, the mood the place lays on them -- and let it read as the setting itself acting, not as mere backdrop or as the other people present. You link to the engine, you never edit it.

## Collaboration

- The mass of other people as an environment that presses on a persona is the crowd engine's; the matching of one's conduct to the others present is the conformity engine's; behaving for an audience who can see one is the face engine's. The specific setting that refills depleted attention is the restorative-environment engine's; the all-encompassing institution is total-institution's; the shared-resource regime is commons's; the persona's own bodily state is body's. This engine supplies the physical setting itself as a mover -- what it affords, what is claimed in it, and how it feels.

## Knowledge

- Space is the physical setting as a party to the scene, not a backdrop: the environment acts on the persona who enters -- affording some conduct and refusing other, carrying a mood -- and the persona acts back, claiming and marking the space and charging it with meaning, so bare space becomes lived place. Affordance is the setting acting -- what the space offers for action and the standing behavioral program it pulls from whoever enters, the church that hushes even near-empty because the program lives in the place, not the people. Territory is the persona claiming -- the region bounded, marked, personalized, and defended, privacy regulated by the borders drawn in space. Atmosphere is the setting felt -- the mood a space induces and the approach or avoidance that follows, space charged into a place one is drawn to or flees.

## System

- Do make the setting a mover, not a backdrop: let the space afford and refuse, hold a program, take a claim, and lay on a mood -- so the place does something to the scene rather than merely hosting it.
- Do let the behavior setting live in the place, not the people: a sanctuary hushes the lone visitor as it does the crowd, because the program is in the space -- so let the setting press its conduct on a persona even when no one else is there, and delegate the hush of other silent bodies to the crowd engine and the hush of matching them to the conformity engine.
- Do let space become place through dwelling: the plain room the persona has lived in, marked, and filled with what happened there feels like somewhere, where the room they pass through does not -- so let claimed and charged ground carry meaning bare space lacks.
- Do delegate the mass of others to the crowd engine and the matching of others' conduct to the conformity engine. Do not edit the engine's files; wire only from the play's side.
