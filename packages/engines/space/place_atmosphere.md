# Place: Atmosphere

## Taxonomy

Atmosphere is an expression of the space engine. It models the setting acting on the persona through feeling: the mood a space induces and the approach or avoidance that follows -- the affective charge of light, sound, scale, and order that draws a persona in or drives them out, and by which bare space becomes a place one is bound to or flees. It is how a place feels, distinct from what it affords and from what the persona claims in it.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: space

## Shown

The felt tone a space gives off before it is examined: the warmth or chill of a room read in a glance, the calm of a still garden, the menace of a dim underpass, the grandeur of a vaulted hall. Atmosphere shows itself as an immediate impression -- the persona feels the place before they parse it, the mood arriving with the sight of it and coloring everything that follows.

## Holds

The affective charge the setting's qualities carry: the pleasure or displeasure and the arousal or calm that its light, sound, spaciousness, order, and nature lay on whoever is in it -- the two axes along which a place's mood is felt. It holds the pull of prospect and the safety of refuge, the open view and the sheltered nook that together make a place feel right; and it holds the accreted meaning that turns a space one has dwelt in into a place that feels like itself, charged with what has happened there. What the space holds in its atmosphere is felt as belonging to the place, not to the persona.

## Offers

What the mood does to the persona: a place that feels good draws them in, invites them to stay, to explore, to return -- approach; a place that feels wrong urges them out -- avoidance. Atmosphere offers the settledness of a soothing room, the lift of a bright open one, the awe of a great space, the felt safety of a corner with a view of the door. It offers, above all, the turn of space into place: the plain ground that, through the mood it lays on the dweller and the meaning that gathers there, becomes somewhere loved, or feared, or missed.

## Withheld

What the atmosphere denies: the place whose mood repels withholds ease and welcome, the sterile room that gives no comfort, the oppressive one that allows no rest, the exposed expanse that offers no refuge and so no peace. And a mood cannot be argued away -- the persona who knows a place is safe may still be unable to feel it so, the atmosphere withholding the calm the facts would grant. What a place feels like, it lays on the persona whether they consent or not.
