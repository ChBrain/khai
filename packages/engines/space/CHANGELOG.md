# @chbrain/khai-engine-space

## 0.1.1

### Patch Changes

- 85629e1: Add the space engine: the physical setting treated as a party to what happens in it -- the built and natural environment that acts on the persona who enters and that the persona acts back on, so bare space becomes lived place. A `place`-type engine (like `crowd` and `total-institution`), its anchor (`place_space.md`) turns on Tuan's distinction -- space is abstract extent, place is space endowed with meaning through dwelling -- and models the reciprocal transaction across three expressions: `affordance` (the setting acting -- what it offers for action and the standing behavioral program it pulls from whoever enters, the church that hushes even near-empty), `territory` (the persona claiming -- the region bounded, marked, personalized, and defended, privacy regulated by the borders drawn in space), and `atmosphere` (the setting felt -- the mood it induces and the approach or avoidance that follows).

  Gives `time-perception` its long-missing spatial partner, and stands ready for a later psychological-distance (`construal`) engine that would unify temporal and spatial distance. The boundary is drawn sharpest where a setting's effect looks social: the hush of a church is the place's behavior setting (this engine), while the hush from the press of other silent bodies is `crowd`'s and the hush from matching the others present is `conformity`'s -- space owns the setting itself, not the people in it. Further bounded against `face` (behaving for an audience), `restorative-environment` (the attention-refilling setting specifically), `total-institution` (the all-encompassing place), `commons` (the shared-resource regime), and `body` (the persona's physical state). No homonym gate: `space` and the expression stems `affordance`/`territory`/`atmosphere` are all free. Warranted (LORE) on Tuan (_Space and Place_, 1977), Barker (behavior settings, 1968), Gibson (affordances, 1979), Altman (territoriality and privacy regulation, 1975), and Mehrabian & Russell with Appleton (environmental mood and prospect-refuge, 1974-75). Set at patch as the free level; a new engine may warrant a minor at the maintainer's `bump:minor` label.
