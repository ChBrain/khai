# Process: Present-Meaning

## Taxonomy

[Dramatic Irony](process_dramatic_irony.md), the gap that sits in the present -- the room knows the true sense of the now.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: dramatic-irony

## Initiated by

The play lets the audience read the character's present words and situation more truly than the character can.
The character speaks a line that carries a second meaning only the room hears -- Oedipus swearing to drive out the murderer, not knowing he curses himself -- or acts with confidence on a belief the room knows to be false: the wrong twin embraced, the feigned death mistaken for real, the trusted friend already turned.
What enters is a concurrent gap: not the future but the meaning of the present is doubled, one sense for the character and a fuller one for the room.

## Direction

Toward the doubling of the character's own words and acts.
The room takes each line the character speaks and hears it twice -- as the character means it, and as it truly lands -- so the character's confidence, its earnestness, its very fluency become the material of the irony; the more sincerely it speaks inside its false picture, the sharper the second meaning the room receives.
The gap sits in the now, in the gap between what the character intends and what the room understands.

## Lever

The distance between the character's reading of the moment and the true one the room holds, weighted by how much the character stakes on its reading.
The press is strongest when the character acts with most conviction on the picture the room knows to be wrong -- a tentative guess misjudged barely registers, a whole course built on the false belief charges every word. What sustains it is the character keeping its mistaken reading while the room keeps the true one.
Let the character's reading turn out true and the doubling vanishes; let the character catch the second meaning and the gap begins to close.

## Echo

The room hears the character's present doubled -- its words truer or more damning than it knows, its confidence built on ground the room can see is not there.
Because the audience holds the real meaning, the character's every sincere line arrives with an edge the character did not put there, and the scene runs on the distance between the said and the meant.
What it runs toward is the character catching up -- but that discovery of the true meaning is the recognition engine's, and where the character is deceived by another figure who also knows, the concealment between them is the secret engine's. This form owns the doubled present; the [foreknowledge](process_foreknowledge.md) form owns the gap that points at what is coming.
