# Process: Panic

## Taxonomy

[Fear](process_fear.md), the flooding form.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: fear

## Initiated by

Fear surges past control -- an alarm fires at full force, the body flooded with the autonomic rush of mortal threat, whether or not a danger warrants it.
This is panic: the acute overwhelming fear, the false alarm at its most total, the fear of fear that feeds itself.
It is fear's flooding, as rage is anger's -- the emotion overrunning the one who has it.

## Direction

Toward escape at any cost -- blind flight, the frantic need to get out, away, safe.
Panic does not aim its response; it discharges it, the urge to flee overrunning the read of where safety actually lies.
Where it cannot flee, it thrashes -- the trapped animal against the walls of the room.

## Lever

The autonomic surge itself -- racing heart, breathlessness, the sense of impending death or collapse -- read as proof of danger, so the alarm confirms itself and spirals.
What panic spends is judgment and the read of the actual threat, both lost in the flood; the very intensity that feels like certain doom is the body's alarm mistaking itself for its cause.

## Echo

The surge crests and recedes -- but panic teaches the fear of its own return, so the persona comes to dread the places and states that triggered it.
This is panic disorder's engine: the agoraphobic retreat, the shrinking map of where one dares to go, built from the fear of the next attack more than the first.
It is fear at its most disorganizing and least discriminating, the alarm firing loudest with the least to fire at.
