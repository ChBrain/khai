# Process: Phobia

## Taxonomy

[Fear](process_fear.md), the learned form.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: fear

## Initiated by

A specific object or situation -- a snake, a height, a crowd, a needle -- comes to command a fear out of all proportion to its actual danger, fixed and persistent.
This is phobia: conditioned fear (Watson's Little Albert), sharpened by preparedness -- the readiness to learn fear of ancestrally dangerous things faster and more durably than of modern ones (Seligman; Öhman).
It is fear learned, and learned too well: a single pairing can set it, and reason will not lift it.

## Direction

Away from the feared object, and away from all that portends it.
Phobia drives avoidance -- the detour, the excuse, the exit -- and the avoidance is its own trap: each escape spares the fear and, by sparing it, preserves it.
The direction narrows the world by degrees, each avoided thing pulling its neighbors into the perimeter.

## Lever

The learned association of the object with danger, and the avoidance that prevents its correction.
What phobia spends is the freedom of the avoided ground: because the persona never stays to see the feared thing pass harmlessly, the disconfirming encounter never comes, and the fear stands unrefuted.
The lever is exactly this foreclosure -- the fear kept alive by never being tested.

## Echo

The feared thing is shunned -- and the fear, never met, persists indefinitely, disproportionate and known to be so yet unmoved by the knowing.
It is the fear that exposure cures and avoidance sustains, which is why its treatment is to approach what it says to flee.
The conditioning that laid it down is the conditioning engine's mechanism; phobia is fear held in that learned grip.
