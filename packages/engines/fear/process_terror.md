# Process: Terror

## Taxonomy

[Fear](process_fear.md), the overwhelming form.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: fear

## Initiated by

A present threat so overwhelming or so mortal that the persona is gripped past flight -- frozen, breathless, the world narrowed to the danger and the nearness of harm.
This is terror: fear at its extreme, the horror before the overwhelming, where escape seems foreclosed and the body locks.
It is the fear of the cornered and the doomed, past the point where mobilization can help.

## Direction

Toward the threat held in frozen regard, and away only if a gap appears.
Terror often immobilizes before it flees -- the tonic stillness of the prey that cannot outrun, the freezing that is itself a defense when flight is hopeless.
Its direction is arrested: the impulse to escape is there but dammed, waiting for an opening that may not come.

## Lever

The appraisal of a threat beyond one's capacity to meet -- inescapable, lethal, or uncanny -- which overwhelms the mobilization fear usually brings and replaces it with paralysis.
What terror spends is agency itself: the sense of being able to act drains away, leaving the frozen witness to its own danger.
It is the point where fear stops readying and starts overwhelming.

## Echo

The moment sears -- terror is the fear that traumatizes, laid down deep and returned to involuntarily, the seed of the flashback and the nightmare.
It is fear at the edge of what the self can bear, and what it leaves behind outlasts the danger by years.
The freezing read as one of stress's acute routes is the stress engine's; the wound terror carves and the involuntary return to it are the memory and stress engines'; terror is the emotion at that extreme, fear past the limit of flight.
