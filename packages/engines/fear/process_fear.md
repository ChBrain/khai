# Process: Fear

## Taxonomy

Emotion / the psychology of the defensive response to danger.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: fear

## Initiated by

A persona appraises a present or imminent threat to its safety -- a danger it can locate, near enough to demand response now -- and alarms, the body readied to escape or avoid.
This is fear: the defensive emotion, keyed on threat and the imminence of harm.
Where anger says _this is wrong and I will push against it_ and disgust says _get it away_, fear says _get me away from this danger_; and it is distinguished from anxiety, which anticipates an uncertain, absent threat, where fear meets a present one.

## Direction

Away from the danger and toward safety -- escape, avoidance, or the freezing that precedes flight.
Fear's motivation is defensive withdrawal: to put distance between the self and the threat, or to drop from its notice.
It is the mirror of anger's approach -- both mobilize hard, but anger advances on the offense and fear retreats from the danger.

## Lever

The appraisal of a threat one cannot simply ignore -- real, close, and capable of harm -- answered by the body before deliberation with the surge that readies flight.
Fear runs on a fast defensive circuit (LeDoux): the amygdala's alarm fires ahead of conscious appraisal, flooding the system with the readiness to act and narrowing attention to the danger and the exits.
What fear spends is composure and the capacity for anything but the threat: the world contracts to what menaces and what might escape it.

## Echo

The threat is escaped or endured -- and, overlearned, the fear outlasts the danger and generalizes past it.
Fear is adaptive at its origin and maladaptive in excess: the same alarm that saves a life becomes the panic that floods without cause, the phobia fixed on the harmless, the terror that overwhelms.
It runs as [panic](process_panic.md), [phobia](process_phobia.md), or [terror](process_terror.md), and is tuned by a standing [fearfulness](position_fearfulness.md). The acute routing of an overwhelming demand into fight, flight, freeze, or fawn is the stress engine's; the anticipation of an absent, uncertain threat is the hope engine's dread and the apprehension engine's baseline; the startle reflex is the surprise engine's; the appraisal taxonomy of the basic emotions is the emotion engine's. This engine owns the defensive emotion at a present danger.
