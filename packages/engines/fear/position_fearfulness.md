# Position: Fearfulness

## Taxonomy

Affect / the trait of fearfulness -- the standing readiness beneath [Fear](process_fear.md) (behavioral inhibition, Kagan; the Behavioral Inhibition System, Gray; harm avoidance, Cloninger).

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: fear

## Has

A low threshold for threat and a strong, slow-settling defensive response to it.
Trait fear, as against the state: a resting disposition to detect danger readily, react to it strongly, and recover from it slowly -- the persona alarmed by more, sooner, and longer, and inclined to avoid the novel and the uncertain.
Its temperamental core is behavioral inhibition -- the wariness to the unfamiliar visible from infancy, one of the most stable of early traits.

## Orders

Run from here, ahead of the threat.
The fearful persona scans for danger, reads the ambiguous as threatening, avoids the novel and the risky, and holds back where the bold advance; and the tilt sets a lasting course, early behavioral inhibition among the strongest predictors of later anxiety disorder.
It colors the read before the event, the way a mood does, but on the single axis of the dangerous.

## Loses

The ease of the untried.
The highly fearful persona meets a world thick with possible threats -- more situations read as risky, more novelty as danger -- and loses the latitude to approach the uncertain freely; what narrows is the range it will willingly enter.
The plain, unmenacing surface of a new thing is what it cannot easily find.

## Drives

On the loss of that latitude:
rides the fearfulness -- heeds the alarm, keeps to the safe and known -- or works against it, approaching the feared, tolerating the novel, letting the alarm sound without obeying it, at a cost in vigilance.
The threshold holds underneath either way, a set point the persona manages rather than moves.
