# Process: Resonance

## Taxonomy

[Transportation](process_transportation.md), the emotional component -- feeling the fortunes of the tale.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: transportation

## Initiated by

The persona is moved by the story -- feeling for its people and events, though it knows them invented.
This is the emotional component of transportation (Green & Brock): genuine affective response to the fortunes of the narrative, the reader who weeps, dreads, and hopes for characters who do not exist.
It is the paradox of fiction felt from the inside -- real emotion for what one knows is not real.

## Direction

Toward the tale's people and their fortunes -- caring, from inside the story, what becomes of them.
Resonance turns the persona's feeling onto the narrative: it hopes the lovers meet, dreads the danger, grieves the loss, its emotions tracking the story's events as if they were its own concerns.
Its direction is the affective binding of the persona to the tale, the fortunes of the invented felt as stakes.

## Lever

What resonance spends -- the persona's emotional distance from the invented.
The uninvolved reader notes that a character suffers; the transported one suffers with them, and resonance is what spends the difference, letting the fictional exact real feeling -- which is both the pleasure of stories and the depth of their hold.
A production holds this lever by giving the persona a stake in the story's people, so their fortunes land as feeling rather than as information, the emotion carrying the transportation deeper.

## Echo

The story is felt, not merely followed -- and the feeling is much of what carries the belief, since an emotionally-held tale is far harder to counterargue than a coldly-noted one.
It completes the convergence: the [entry](process_entry.md) gives the attention, the [imagery](process_imagery.md) gives the world, the resonance gives the caring, and together they carry the persona in.
The capacity to feel into another's inner state as a channel between persons is the empathy engine's, and the audience's moral evaluation of particular characters is the allegiance engine's; resonance here is the emotional engagement with the narrative as a whole, the feeling that binds a persona to the world of a tale.
