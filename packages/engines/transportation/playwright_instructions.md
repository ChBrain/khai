# Instructions: Transportation

How a Playwright wires the transportation engine into a play. You wire by linking the play's own content to the engine's files; the engine files stay untouched, they are canon. The Roadie plumbs the engine's law into Instructions Knowledge on deploy. Authoring guidance, not runtime content, and it does not go on tour.

## Human

- The human sets the narrative terrain: what story a persona reads, watches, or hears -- and how engaging it is -- so a persona has a tale that can carry it, with the sensory and emotional purchase transportation needs.

## Agent

- From the play's own content, a persona links the transportation a moment runs under its Projection: what narrative carries it, which components are present -- the attentional entry, the vivid imagery, the emotional resonance -- and how transportable its standing disposition is. Let it read as the real world receding and the persona returning changed, its guard against the story's claims lowered. You link to the engine, you never edit it.

## Collaboration

- The total absorption in a matched challenge one is acting in is the flow engine's; the general, content-agnostic absorption of attention is the absorption composite's; the audience's moral bond to particular characters is the allegiance engine's; the general mechanism of influence is the persuasion engine's, of which narrative transportation is one route; the capacity to feel into a present other is the empathy engine's. This engine supplies being carried into a narrative world -- the attentional entry, the imagery, the emotional resonance, and the belief-softening they work.

## Knowledge

- Transportation is a persona's absorption into a narrative world: reading, watching, or hearing a story, the persona is carried out of the here-and-now and into the tale, and returns, as Gerrig put it, a traveler come back changed (Green & Brock). It converges from three components. Entry is the attentional crossing -- all mental resources on the story, the surround gone, the reader who does not hear their name. Imagery is the sensory picturing -- the story world seen and felt vividly in the mind's eye. Resonance is the emotional engagement -- genuine feeling for the fortunes of invented people. Its signature and its difference from mere absorption is a persuasive one: the transported are less able to counterargue and more apt to take on the story's beliefs, which is why narrative changes minds where argument cannot. Transportability is the trait -- a standing readiness to be carried into stories. Read a persona by how far a tale takes it, and let what it is transported by leave its mark.

## System

- Do keep transportation narrative-specific and receptive: it is being carried into a story one takes in, not absorbed in a challenge one performs -- let the persona be the traveler, not the agent, moved and pictured and feeling rather than acting.
- Do stage the three components: the attention crossing over, the story world pictured vividly, the feeling for invented people -- and let all three converge, so the persona is genuinely inside the tale, not half-watching from outside.
- Do let the belief-softening show: the transported persona counterargues less and takes on the story's values and claims more -- so a tale can move and persuade it where an argument would meet its guard; let what it is transported by mark what it comes to believe.
- Do let transportability set the reach: the readily-transported persona lost in any decent tale and the more open to what stories do, the resistant one holding every narrative at a distance. Do delegate the performer's absorption to the flow engine and the general mechanism of influence to the persuasion engine. Do not edit the engine's files; wire only from the play's side.
