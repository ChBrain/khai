# Process: Entry

## Taxonomy

[Transportation](process_transportation.md), the attentional component -- the crossing into the story world.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: transportation

## Initiated by

The persona's attention crosses over -- the real world receding as the story world becomes its present.
This is the attentional component of transportation (Green & Brock): all of the persona's mental resources gathered onto the narrative, so completely that the surround falls away.
It is the reader who does not hear their name called, the viewer who forgets the room -- attention wholly given to the tale.

## Direction

Across the threshold, from the here into the there of the story.
Entry moves the persona's regard off its actual surroundings and onto the narrative present: the page or screen stops being an object looked at and becomes a window looked through, the story's place and time occupying the attention the room no longer holds.
Its direction is out of the world of the reader and into the world of the read.

## Lever

What entry spends -- the persona's awareness of its actual situation.
The crossing costs the surround: while transported, the persona loses track of time, of its body, of the room, of the fact that it is reading at all -- the resources those would claim are all spent on the story.
A production holds this lever by letting the narrative take the whole of the attention, so the persona genuinely leaves its situation for the tale's, rather than half-watching from outside.

## Echo

The persona is inside the story, its surround gone dim -- and only from inside can the imagery and the feeling do their work.
This is transportation's doorway: without the attentional crossing there is no world to be pictured or felt, only a text held at arm's length.
The attention itself, as a spotlight on any field, is the attention engine's, and its content-agnostic total absorption is the absorption composite's; entry is that absorption turned specifically into a narrative world, the crossing that opens the story to [imagery](process_imagery.md) and [resonance](process_resonance.md).
