# Process: Imagery

## Taxonomy

[Transportation](process_transportation.md), the sensory component -- the story world pictured.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: transportation

## Initiated by

The persona sees the story -- its places, faces, and events rising vivid in the mind's eye.
This is the imagery component of transportation (Green & Brock): the narrative rendered as sensory experience, pictured, heard, and felt internally, vivid enough to stand in for perception.
It is the reader who can describe a character's face the author never drew, who walks rooms that exist only in the telling.

## Direction

Toward the sensory realization of the tale -- words become world.
Imagery turns the narrative from proposition into picture: the persona builds the story's world in perception-like detail, filling what the telling leaves implicit, so the tale is not merely followed but seen.
Its direction is from the described to the imagined-as-real, the story made vivid and present to the inner senses.

## Lever

What imagery spends -- the thinness of the merely-told.
A story held only as information stays flat and arguable; imagery spends that distance, rendering the narrative in sensory fullness so that it is experienced rather than noted, and the experienced carries a conviction the noted does not.
A production holds this lever by giving the narrative the concrete, sensory detail that invites picturing -- the imaginable scene, not the abstract summary -- so the persona builds the world rather than skims the report.

## Echo

The story stands vivid in the mind -- and the vividness is part of why the transported believe: what has been seen, even in imagination, is harder to hold at arm's length than what has only been asserted.
It joins the [entry](process_entry.md) that clears the attention and the [resonance](process_resonance.md) that supplies the feeling, the three converging into the transported state.
The general capacity to hold and manipulate mental images is the wider cognition's; imagery here is that capacity given over to a narrative, the story realized in the mind's senses.
