# Position: Transportability

## Taxonomy

Narrative reception / the trait of transportability -- the standing readiness beneath [Transportation](process_transportation.md) (Dal Cin, Zanna & Fong).

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: transportation

## Has

A standing readiness to be carried into stories.
Trait transportability, as against the state of any one transportation: at the high end, a persona lost in any half-decent book, film, or tale, quick to cross into the story world and slow to leave it; at the low end, one who stays outside every narrative, watching the pages from without.
It tracks how easily the persona's attention, imagery, and feeling converge on a narrative -- the disposition the state of transportation rides on.

## Orders

Run from here, into every story met.
The highly transportable persona gives itself readily to narratives -- crosses over fast, pictures vividly, feels for invented people, and is the more moved and the more persuaded by them; the low-transportable persona holds the story at a distance, following the plot without being carried, its guard never quite lowered.
It sets how far any given tale will take the persona before the story even begins.

## Loses

At the high end, the guard that distance keeps; at the low end, the pleasure and the yield of being carried.
The readily-transported persona is more open to what stories do -- moved, changed, and persuaded by them, for good or ill, its critical distance the price of its immersion; the untransportable one keeps its distance and its guard, and pays in the flatness of narratives that never quite reach it.
What each trades is the other's good -- openness against defense, across the same standing threshold.

## Drives

On that standing readiness:
rides it -- the transportable persona keeps giving itself to stories, the resistant keeps its distance -- or works against it, the guarded learning to let a tale carry it, the readily-carried learning to hold a persuasive story at arm's length when its claims should be weighed rather than felt.
The threshold holds underneath either way, a resting level the persona manages rather than a state that comes and goes.
