# @chbrain/khai-engine-transportation

## 0.1.1

### Patch Changes

- 440ac37: Add the transportation engine (multi-type: process, position) -- a persona's absorption into a narrative world. One of the reception-side gaps the gap analysis found: khai's structural/authorial narrative axis is saturated, but how an audience engages a fiction was nearly empty. Transportation is being carried out of the here-and-now and into a story, and its signature is a persuasive one -- the transported counterargue less and take on story-consistent beliefs, the route by which narrative changes minds where argument cannot (Green & Brock; Gerrig).

  - **Root (process):** `transportation` -- carried into the story world by converging attention, imagery, and feeling, returning changed with belief softened.
  - **Components (process), Green & Brock's three:** `entry` (the attentional crossing -- the real world receding, the reader who does not hear their name), `imagery` (the sensory picturing -- the story world seen vividly in the mind's eye), `resonance` (the emotional engagement -- feeling the fortunes of invented people).
  - **Trait (position):** `transportability` -- a standing readiness to be carried into stories, from the reader lost in any book to the one who stays outside every one (Dal Cin, Zanna & Fong).

  Warranted (LORE) on Gerrig (_Experiencing Narrative Worlds_), Green & Brock (the transportation scale and narrative persuasion), Busselle & Bilandzic (narrative engagement), Dal Cin/Zanna/Fong (transportability), and van Laer et al. (the transportation-imagery meta-analysis). Bounded against `flow` (absorption in a matched challenge one performs vs a narrative one receives -- no challenge-skill, no agency), the `absorption` composite (content-agnostic absorption vs the narrative-specific carrying), `allegiance` (the moral bond to particular characters vs entry into the world), `persuasion` (the general mechanism of influence, of which transportation is the narrative route), and `empathy` (feeling into a present other vs emotional engagement with a tale). Set at patch as the free level. No whitelist required -- the `transportation` root, `entry`/`imagery`/`resonance` component stems, and `transportability` trait stem are unique (`immersion` was avoided as an existing flow form).
