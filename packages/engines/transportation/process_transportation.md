# Process: Transportation

## Taxonomy

Narrative reception / a persona's absorption into a story world.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: transportation

## Initiated by

A persona reads, watches, or hears a story, and is carried into it.
This is transportation (Green & Brock; Gerrig): the convergence of attention, imagery, and feeling upon a narrative until the persona is drawn out of the here-and-now and into the world of the tale.
Gerrig's metaphor is exact -- the reader is a traveler who journeys into the story and returns changed, having been somewhere else while the pages turned.

## Direction

Into the narrative world, and out of the real one -- attention, mind's eye, and heart all turned to the tale.
Transportation carries the persona across a threshold: the room, the time, the self's own concerns recede, and the story's events become, for a while, the persona's present -- felt, pictured, and attended as if lived.
Its direction is inward to the story and away from the surround, the world of the reader dimming as the world of the read brightens.

## Lever

What transportation spends -- the persona's guarded distance from what it is told.
This is its signature and its difference from mere absorption: the transported persona is less able to counterargue, its critical defenses lowered by the very engagement, so the story's claims and values slip past the guard a bald assertion would meet, and belief bends toward the tale (Green & Brock's account of narrative persuasion).
It spends the reader's skepticism, buying the felt reality of the invented -- which is why a story can move and persuade where an argument cannot.

## Echo

The persona returns from the story, and is not quite the same -- carried somewhere, it comes back with the tale's images, feelings, and beliefs still on it.
This is why narrative persuades: transportation, not argument, is the route by which stories change what their audience thinks and feels, and the more transported the persona, the more the story's world colors its own.
It runs as [entry](process_entry.md), [imagery](process_imagery.md), and [resonance](process_resonance.md), tuned by a standing [transportability](position_transportability.md). The total absorption in a matched challenge one is acting in is the flow engine's; the general, content-agnostic absorption of attention is the absorption composite's; the audience's moral bond to particular characters is the allegiance engine's; the general mechanism of influence is the persuasion engine's, of which narrative transportation is one route. This engine owns being carried into a narrative world.
