# Process: Misgrounding

## Taxonomy

[Grounding](process_grounding.md), the move this is one form of.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: grounding

## Initiated by

The criterion is treated as met when it is not -- a nod taken for comprehension, a term the two read differently, a fact presupposed as shared that only one of them holds -- and the talk moves on over ground it does not actually stand on.
This is grounding's failure: not a contribution that stalls and is repaired, but one that is falsely counted landed and never checked.
It usually passes unnoticed in the moment; that is what makes it dangerous.

## Direction

Away from real understanding, while both believe they have it.
Each proceeds on their own version of what was settled, and each new contribution is built on the false base, so the two accounts drift apart even as the talk runs smoothly on the surface.
The divergence propagates: every later turn grounded against the unshared ground inherits and compounds the error.

## Lever

Evidence skipped or faked, and common ground presumed.
The acknowledgment given without uptake, the demonstration never asked for where it was needed, the clarification never opened, the "yes" that meant "go away" -- grounding's checks omitted, so nothing catches the miss and it hardens into assumed fact.
The least-effort that keeps ordinary talk cheap is exactly what, misapplied, lets this through: the same reluctance to stop and check.

## Echo

The two diverge while sure they agree, and the gap surfaces later -- often as a rupture -- when the unshared ground finally shows and each finds the other was never where they thought.
It is the failure the other three moves exist to prevent: [acknowledgment](process_acknowledgment.md) and [demonstration](process_demonstration.md) give the evidence it skips, [clarification](process_clarification.md) the repair it never opens.
It is the seed of two people talking past each other -- the point where a grounding failure, left to compound against a mismatch of frames, becomes a loop neither can see out of.
