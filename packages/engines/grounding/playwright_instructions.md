# Instructions: Grounding

How a Playwright wires the grounding engine into a play. You wire by linking the play's own content to the engine's files; the engine files stay untouched, they are canon. The Roadie plumbs the engine's law into Instructions Knowledge on deploy. Authoring guidance, not runtime content, and it does not go on tour.

## Human

- The human defines the grounding terrain: what the two personas are trying to reach a shared understanding of, what it would cost to get it wrong, and how much common ground they already stand on -- so the bar the grounding must clear, and the damage if it is missed, are set.

## Agent

- From the play's own content, a persona links the grounding move the moment runs (acknowledgment, demonstration, clarification, or misgrounding) under its Projection. Let the move show whether the ground is being laid lightly, shown and confirmed, mended, or silently lost -- and let the evidence between the two read: a nod that lands, a readback that catches an error, a "which one?" that saves it, a "yes" that only seems to. You link to the engine, you never edit it.

## Collaboration

- The one-pass gap between what is said and what is understood is the language engine's; the mending of a damaged relationship, as against a misunderstanding, is the repair engine's. The utterance-act being grounded is the speech-act engine's; the reading of another's inner state is empathy's; the standing store of shared knowledge is memory's. This engine supplies the joint construction of understanding itself -- the evidence given and read, the criterion reached or missed.

## Knowledge

- Grounding is how two personas build mutual understanding turn by turn: a contribution is landed not when said but when the addressee returns evidence it was understood well enough to go on, and only then does it enter their common ground. Acknowledgment is light evidence -- a nod, a continuer -- that keeps the talk moving. Demonstration is strong evidence -- a readback or paraphrase -- that shows understanding for confirmation. Clarification is repair, trouble flagged and mended before the talk resumes. Misgrounding is the failure: evidence skipped or faked, the criterion falsely assumed met, so the error compounds unseen.

## System

- Do make understanding a thing built between two, not delivered by one: let the reader see the evidence pass -- or fail to pass -- so a contribution visibly lands or hangs, rather than being understood by fiat.
- Do match the move to the stakes: a nod is right for a bus stop and wrong for a contract; let a scene that grounds high-stakes content on mere acknowledgment carry the risk of that thinness.
- Do let misgrounding run smooth on the surface and surface later -- the two agreeing warmly over ground they do not share, the gap breaking open a scene or an act afterward.
- Do delegate the one-pass gap to the language engine and the mending of the relationship to repair. Do not edit the engine's files; wire only from the play's side.
