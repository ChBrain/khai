# Process: Clarification

## Taxonomy

[Grounding](process_grounding.md), the move this is one form of.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: grounding

## Initiated by

Evidence of understanding is missing, weak, or wrong, and one of the two flags the trouble -- "wait, which one?", "you mean the second?", or the speaker's own "no, sorry, I meant --."
This is repair: the grounding stalls on purpose, because to go on would be to build over a fault.
Either party can start it -- the addressee who did not follow, or the speaker who sees they were not followed -- and the talk turns aside to fix the understanding before resuming.

## Direction

Toward the trouble located and mended before the talk moves on.
A side-sequence opens: the trouble-source is named, a correction is offered, the correction is accepted, and only then does the main line pick up where it stalled.
The direction is briefly backward -- back to the thing not understood -- in service of going forward on ground that will hold.

## Lever

The initiation of repair, self or other.
The trouble is flagged -- other-initiated by the one who did not follow ("huh?", "who?", "the second what?"), or self-initiated by the one who catches their own unclarity -- and a fix is put forward and ratified, restoring the criterion the contribution had failed to meet.
The repair is preferred quick and local: caught in the next beat, mended in a turn or two, the main talk barely broken.

## Echo

The ground is mended and the contribution grounded after all -- or the repair itself misfires and the trouble survives its own fixing.
It is the corrective the other moves lean on: [acknowledgment](process_acknowledgment.md) and [demonstration](process_demonstration.md) build the ground, this one saves it when the building goes wrong, and it is the move whose absence lets [misgrounding](process_misgrounding.md) run.
The mending here is of a misunderstanding in the moment; the mending of a damaged relationship -- a rupture taken to resolution -- is the repair engine's, a different repair at a different altitude.
