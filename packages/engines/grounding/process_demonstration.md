# Process: Demonstration

## Taxonomy

[Grounding](process_grounding.md), the move this is one form of.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: grounding

## Initiated by

The stakes or the doubt are high enough that a nod will not do, and the addressee shows their understanding rather than merely signalling it -- repeating the number back, paraphrasing the instruction, enacting the request.
This is the strong end of the evidence ladder: not "I have it" but "here is what I have, check it."
It is called for exactly when a mistaken landing would be expensive, and cheap acknowledgment cannot be trusted to catch the mistake.

## Direction

Toward understanding made visible and confirmable.
By displaying what they took, the addressee hands the speaker something to measure against what they meant -- so the speaker can ratify it or catch the error before the talk builds on it.
Grounding is raised here from assumed to demonstrated: the contribution is not counted landed until the speaker has seen the landing and agreed it is right.

## Lever

Display and demonstration -- the top rungs of the evidence ladder.
The readback ("so, the three o'clock, platform two"), the paraphrase in the addressee's own words, the worked example, the thing done: evidence that cannot be produced without having actually understood, and so grounds strongly.
Its cost is the pause and the effort; its worth is that it makes comprehension, not just receipt, the thing on the table.

## Echo

The contribution is grounded on evidence the speaker has seen and ratified -- costly but secure, the grounding used when a mistake would be dear.
What the display reveals decides what happens next: a match confirms the ground and the talk goes on more surely than a nod could have left it; a mismatch, now visible, hands off to [clarification](process_clarification.md) to be repaired.
It is [acknowledgment](process_acknowledgment.md) made strong and checkable -- the same grounding raised to a bar that hollow uptake cannot clear.
