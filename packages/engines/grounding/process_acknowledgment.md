# Process: Acknowledgment

## Taxonomy

[Grounding](process_grounding.md), the move this is one form of.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: grounding

## Initiated by

The addressee has taken in the speaker's contribution and signals as much, lightly -- a "mm-hmm," a nod, a "right," or simply the next turn that fits, presupposing the last one landed.
This is the cheapest positive evidence: enough to say "I have it, go on," and no more.
It is the ordinary texture of talk, the constant low hum by which most of what is said stays grounded without anyone stopping to check.

## Direction

Toward the contribution counting as landed, at least provisionally, so the talk keeps moving.
The signal releases the speaker to continue; the ground is laid lightly and the two go on, neither pausing to confirm because the stakes do not seem to ask for it.
It keeps the pace up -- grounding that costs almost nothing and mostly suffices.

## Lever

The continuer and the acknowledgment token -- the low rungs of the evidence ladder.
Continued attention, a fitting next turn, a "yeah," an "okay": each tells the speaker the contribution was received, without showing what, exactly, was understood. The bar it clears is low, which is right for low-stakes content and wrong for high.
It is evidence of receipt more than of comprehension, and the two are not the same.

## Echo

The contribution enters the common ground on light evidence, and the pace holds.
Cheap and usually right, but thin: an acknowledgment can be given without real understanding -- the nod that means "keep going," not "I follow" -- so the ground it lays can be hollow.
When the stakes rise past what a token can carry, it gives way to [demonstration](process_demonstration.md); when the receipt was hollow and no one checked, it is where [misgrounding](process_misgrounding.md) begins.
