# Process: Grounding

## Taxonomy

Communication / the psychology of language use and conversation.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: grounding

## Initiated by

Two personas in talk need more than one to speak and the other to hear: they need to establish that the message landed, that they now share enough understanding to go on.
This is grounding -- the joint work of building mutual understanding, turn by turn, on top of the common ground they already share.
A thing said is not yet a thing understood; the speaker offers a contribution and waits, and the two of them together decide, by what comes back, whether it has been taken.

## Direction

Toward the grounding criterion: the mutual belief that the addressee has understood well enough for the current purpose.
Not perfect understanding -- understanding sufficient for what the talk is trying to do, reached by the least collaborative effort that will serve. A direction to a bus stop is grounded to a lower bar than the wording of a contract.
The two work toward that bar together: the speaker shaping the contribution to be takeable, the addressee returning the evidence that it was taken.

## Lever

Evidence of understanding, given and read.
The speaker presents; the addressee returns evidence -- a continuer, an acknowledgment, a paraphrase, a fitting next turn -- and only when the evidence clears the bar does the contribution count as grounded and pass into the common ground the two now share. Weak or missing or contrary evidence stalls it: the contribution hangs, un-landed, until it is repaired.
The whole process is this ledger of evidence, run in both directions at once, each party both offering contributions and grounding the other's.

## Echo

The common ground grows by one contribution -- or the two proceed on ground they only believe they share.
Grounded, the contribution becomes a base the next turn can build on without re-checking; ungrounded but assumed, it becomes a crack that widens with every turn laid over it.
The process runs as [acknowledgment](process_acknowledgment.md), [demonstration](process_demonstration.md), [clarification](process_clarification.md), or [misgrounding](process_misgrounding.md) -- the moves by which understanding is reached, shown, mended, or missed. The one-pass gap between what is said and what is understood is the language engine's; the utterance-act being grounded is the speech-act engine's; the mending of a damaged relationship, as against a misunderstanding, is the repair engine's.
