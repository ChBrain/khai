# Position: Fear of Failure

## Taxonomy

[Achievement Motive](position_achievement_motive.md), the motive to avoid failure -- pulled away from the test.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: achievement-motive

## Has

A specific [achievement motive](position_achievement_motive.md): the motive to avoid failure -- an anxiety about failing and what failing would mean (Atkinson's motive to avoid failure).
The avoidance face of the disposition: not an appetite for success but a dread of its opposite, and of the consequences failure threatens -- the shame, the lowered estimate of the self, the uncertain future, the disapproval and lost regard of those who matter (Conroy's multidimensional fear of failure).
It is the motive that meets the achievement situation as a danger to be managed, and would rather not be tested than be tested and found wanting.

## Orders

Protect yourself from failing, and from what failing would cost.
The fear-of-failure motive orders the persona to guard against the possibility of failure -- to avoid the test where it can, and where it cannot, to hedge: to pick the safely easy task that cannot embarrass or the impossibly hard one that carries no shame in losing, to hold back effort so failure can be blamed on not trying, to procrastinate, to over-prepare. It keeps the persona's attention on the prospect of doing badly and what that would expose.
It moves the persona to keep itself, above all, from being caught short.

## Loses

The challenges the fear makes it forgo, and the growth in them.
Pulled away from the test, the persona forgoes the moderate, meaningful challenges the hope-driven motive seeks -- it settles for the safe or flees to the impossible, withholds the full effort that risks a real verdict, and buys short-term protection at the cost of the accomplishment and the development that engaging the test would bring. The self-handicapping that shields it also caps it.
What it trades is achievement for safety: the persona protected from failure is also protected from the success it will not risk, and pays in a life narrowed to the tests it is sure of.

## Drives

On that avoidance motive:
rides it -- avoids or hedges the test, protects itself from failure and its costs, and withholds the effort that would expose it -- or, where hope of success also runs high, meets the achievement situation torn, wanting to succeed and dreading to fail at once, the classic conflict of the anxious achiever.
The avoidance baseline holds underneath, the motive away from failing rather than, like [hope of success](position_hope_of_success.md), toward succeeding. That this motive energizes but is not the achievement goal a persona adopts is the [anchor's](position_achievement_motive.md); the felt anxiety and shame it runs on are the anxiety and shame engines'. This face owns the disposition to avoid failure.
