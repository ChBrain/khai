# Position: Achievement Motive

## Taxonomy

Dispositional motive / a persona's resting orientation in achievement situations (no khai file of its own).

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: achievement-motive

## Has

A standing dispositional motive that colours how a persona meets a challenge, prior to any particular goal.
Not the goal a persona adopts but the pull beneath it (Atkinson; McClelland): the resting orientation it brings to any situation where its competence can be tested -- toward the satisfaction of doing well, or away from the cost of doing badly.
It runs on two separable motives at once: a hope of success that draws the persona toward achievement, and a fear of failure that pushes it to protect itself from failing -- and the persona's resultant tendency is the difference between them.

## Orders

Meet the achievement situation as the motive dictates, before framing a goal.
The motive orders the persona how to approach a test of its competence -- to seek it out and engage it, or to hedge and avoid it -- and how to weight the prospect of success against the prospect of failure. The room reads this dispositional pull beneath the persona's pursuits: whether it is drawn to the challenge or wary of it, energized or anxious, before any specific goal is named.
It sets the persona's resting stance toward the possibility of being measured.

## Loses

The other motive's stance toward the test, and its risks.
Weighted toward one motive, the persona forgoes the other's way of meeting achievement: the hope-driven persona loses the fear-driven one's protective caution and its sensitivity to what failure would cost; the fear-driven persona loses the hope-driven one's appetite for challenge and the growth that engaging it brings. What it trades is the reach of one motive for the blind spot of the other, and it will misjudge a persona moved by the motive it lacks.

## Drives

On that dispositional motive:
runs on one of two, or a mix -- [hope of success](position_hope_of_success.md), the motive to approach success, drawn to challenge and the satisfaction of doing well; or [fear of failure](position_fear_of_failure.md), the motive to avoid failure, pulled to protect itself from failing and its costs -- and meets every achievement situation through the balance it holds.
The motive holds underneath the particular goal a persona adopts, energizing it. Which achievement goal it then frames -- mastery or performance, approached or avoided -- is the goal engine's; the promotion-or-prevention orientation of a pursuit is the regulatory-focus engine's; the source of the drive itself, intrinsic to external, is the motivation engine's. This engine owns the upstream achievement motive, and its two forms.
