# Position: Hope of Success

## Taxonomy

[Achievement Motive](position_achievement_motive.md), the motive to approach success -- drawn toward the test.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: achievement-motive

## Has

A specific [achievement motive](position_achievement_motive.md): the motive to approach success -- the need for achievement, a persona drawn toward challenge and the satisfaction of doing well (McClelland's nAch; Atkinson's motive to approach success).
The approach face of the disposition: not a fear to be managed but an appetite to be fed, an anticipation of the pride and accomplishment that succeeding brings.
It is the motive that seeks the achievement situation out, and prefers a challenge pitched where success is real but not certain -- the moderate risk where doing well means most.

## Orders

Seek the test, and reach for the success in it.
The hope-of-success motive orders the persona to engage achievement situations rather than avoid them, to take on challenges of genuine difficulty, and to hold its attention on the prospect of doing well and the satisfaction it promises. It draws the persona toward the moderately difficult task, where the chance of success is live and its meaning greatest, and away from the trivially easy and the impossibly hard alike.
It moves the persona to put itself forward to be measured, expecting to measure up.

## Loses

The protection that wariness of failure would give.
Drawn toward the test, the persona forgoes the fear-driven caution that would keep it from overreaching -- it can take on more than it should, expose itself to failures a warier motive would sidestep, and underweight what a public failure might cost. Its appetite for challenge is a strength that can run past prudence.
What it trades is safety for growth: the persona that reaches is the persona that can be caught reaching, and it pays in the risks its hope makes it willing to run.

## Drives

On that approach motive:
rides it -- seeks challenge, engages the test, and reaches for the satisfaction of doing well -- or, where fear of failure also runs high, meets the same situation in conflict, drawn to the challenge and dreading the fall at once.
The approach baseline holds underneath, the motive toward success rather than, like [fear of failure](position_fear_of_failure.md), away from failing. That this motive energizes but is not the achievement goal a persona adopts is the [anchor's](position_achievement_motive.md); this face owns the disposition to approach success.
