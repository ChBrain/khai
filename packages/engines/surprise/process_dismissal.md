# Process: Dismissal

## Taxonomy

[Surprise](process_surprise.md), the failure to update.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: surprise

## Initiated by

The anomaly arrives, and the persona will not let it stand -- explaining it away, minimizing it, forcing it back into the model that failed rather than repairing the model.
This is dismissal: the surprise damped and the schema preserved, the jolt absorbed and discounted.
It was a fluke, a one-off, not what it looked like -- and the persona goes on with the model that just failed them, unrevised.

## Direction

Away from the update, back to the intact expectation.
Instead of redrawing the map where the territory contradicted it, the persona defends the map -- discounting the anomaly as noise, coincidence, or misperception -- so that nothing has to change.
The direction is conservative in the literal sense: the model conserved at the cost of accuracy.

## Lever

The refusal of the prediction error.
The same jolt that should trigger repair is instead filed as noise, often because the update would cost too much -- a belief too dear to revise, a person too trusted to re-see, a self-image too fragile to correct -- so the error is absorbed rather than acted on.
The lever is the price of the update, weighed against the comfort of the model, and found too high.

## Echo

The model stands, and the persona is set up to be surprised the same way again -- blindsided twice by the same thing, because the first blindsiding taught them nothing.
It is [sensemaking](process_sensemaking.md) refused: the interrupt fired correctly and its correction was declined.
Why the update is refused -- the belief too dear to revise, the dissonance too sharp to bear -- is the belief engine's; this owns the surprise turned away from its own function, the anomaly met and sent back unlearned.
