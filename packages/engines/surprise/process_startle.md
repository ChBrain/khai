# Process: Startle

## Taxonomy

[Surprise](process_surprise.md), the reflex form.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: surprise

## Initiated by

A sudden, intense stimulus -- a bang, a lunge, a touch in the dark -- fires a response before any appraisal can run: the flinch, the jump, the freeze, wired below thought.
This is startle: the fastest and oldest form, sub-cortical, over almost before it began.
It does not wait to know what happened; it reacts to the abruptness itself.

## Direction

Toward protection, ahead of understanding.
The startle reflex braces the body against a threat it has not yet identified -- eyes shut, shoulders up, a hitch in the breath -- in the fraction of a second before the mind knows what the stimulus even was.
The direction is defensive and bodily: safety first, meaning later.

## Lever

Suddenness and intensity, not expectation-violation as such.
Startle is triggered by the abruptness of a stimulus more than by any model being wrong -- it fires even when the persona knows the bang is coming and braces for it, and cannot fully suppress it. It is a reflex, not an appraisal, which is why it is so hard to fake and so hard to stop.
The lever is the raw shock of the sudden, below the level where expectation lives.

## Echo

The body has already reacted before the mind arrives.
It is the reflex beneath surprise, not quite an emotion -- set apart for exactly this reason: it runs on a faster, lower circuit than the appraised feelings.
Once appraisal catches up, it hands off to the [jolt](process_jolt.md) and what follows -- relief if the bang was nothing, fear if it was not, the valenced settling that is the emotion engine's.
