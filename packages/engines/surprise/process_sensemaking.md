# Process: Sensemaking

## Taxonomy

[Surprise](process_surprise.md), the resolution that updates the model.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: surprise

## Initiated by

The jolt has stopped the persona at an anomaly, and they turn to it: what just happened, how could it be, what did I have wrong.
This is sensemaking: the work the interrupt exists to trigger, the turning of attention onto the violation to understand it.
It is surprise doing its job -- the flag raised, now answered.

## Direction

Toward a model repaired to fit the anomaly.
The persona analyzes the unexpected thing, hunts for the expectation that failed, and revises it -- so the same event, met again, would no longer surprise them.
The direction is reconstructive: not back to the old model but forward to a better one, the map redrawn where the territory proved it wrong.

## Lever

The analysis of the violation, and the update it drives.
This is the evolutionary point of the whole mechanism: the interrupt exists so that a faulty schema is found and rebuilt, and sensemaking is that rebuilding -- the search for what assumption was mistaken and the correction of it, so the model tracks the world a little better than before.
The lever is the willingness to let the anomaly teach: to treat the surprise as information rather than noise.

## Echo

The model is updated, and the surprise is spent well -- the persona has learned something.
The belief revised in the process is the belief engine's; this owns the surprise-driven turn toward the revision, the moment the anomaly is taken as a lesson.
A sensemaking that opens into a standing hunger to know more hands off to the curiosity engine; one that meets a vastness too large to fit the model at all overflows into the awe engine's accommodation. Its opposite -- the anomaly refused rather than learned from -- is [dismissal](process_dismissal.md).
