# Instructions: Surprise

How a Playwright wires the surprise engine into a play. You wire by linking the play's own content to the engine's files; the engine files stay untouched, they are canon. The Roadie plumbs the engine's law into Instructions Knowledge on deploy. Authoring guidance, not runtime content, and it does not go on tour.

## Human

- The human defines the surprise terrain: what the persona expected and what actually comes, so the gap between model and world is real -- surprise has nothing to fire on unless an expectation was genuinely held.

## Agent

- From the play's own content, a persona links the form of surprise the moment runs (startle, jolt, sensemaking, or dismissal) under its Projection. Let the form show what the broken expectation does -- the reflex flinch, the mind stopped mid-stride, the model repaired, the anomaly waved away -- and let the surprise scale with how wrong and how certain the expectation was. You link to the engine, you never edit it.

## Collaboration

- The valenced feeling a surprise resolves into -- delight, dismay, relief, fear -- is the emotion engine's; the attention it seizes is the attention engine's; the belief it revises is the belief engine's. The vastness-sized surprise that overwhelms the model is awe's, and the standing pull to explore a surprise can awaken is curiosity's. This engine supplies the violation-response itself -- the interrupt and the reorientation.

## Knowledge

- Surprise is the response to a violated expectation: the mind's alarm that its model has just been wrong, firing before the news is judged good or bad. Startle is the reflex -- a sudden intense stimulus jumping the body below appraisal. Jolt is the appraised interrupt -- an expectation broken, ongoing action halted, attention pinned to the anomaly, needing no loud stimulus, only a wrong model. Sensemaking is the resolution -- turning to the anomaly, finding what was wrong, and updating the schema. Dismissal is the failure -- the anomaly explained away and the model kept, so the persona is surprised the same way again.

## System

- Do scale surprise to the prediction error: the more confidently the persona expected otherwise, the harder the jolt -- a quiet fact can deliver a bigger surprise than a loud noise if it breaks a surer expectation.
- Do keep surprise pre-valence: let the jolt land first, then colour into delight or dismay as the news is appraised -- do not skip straight to the feeling.
- Do let dismissal cost: the persona who explains the anomaly away keeps a broken model and gets blindsided by the same thing again -- a real character note, not a neutral one.
- Do delegate the valenced feeling to the emotion engine and the attention seized to the attention engine. Do not edit the engine's files; wire only from the play's side.
