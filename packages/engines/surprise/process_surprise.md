# Process: Surprise

## Taxonomy

Emotion / the cognitive psychology of the unexpected.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: surprise

## Initiated by

The world diverges from what the persona expected -- a thing happens that their model said would not, or fails to happen that it said would -- and the mismatch registers before anything else can.
This is surprise: the response to a violated expectation, the mind's alarm that its model of the world has just been wrong.
It is not a feeling about what the persona cares about but about what they did not see coming -- pre-valence, prior to whether the news turns out good or bad.

## Direction

Toward the anomaly, and toward a model made to fit it.
Surprise interrupts whatever was underway, seizes attention, and turns the whole apparatus toward the unexpected thing -- to work out what it means and what the model got wrong.
The direction is corrective: the jolt is the mind flagging a schema for repair, and everything else is dropped until the flag is dealt with.

## Lever

The size of the gap between expected and actual.
The more a thing violates the model -- the more confidently the persona expected otherwise -- the harder the surprise; the fully anticipated does not surprise at all, and the mildly-off barely registers.
The lever is the prediction error: surprise scales with how wrong the model was, not with how loud or large the event is.

## Echo

The interrupt passes, and the model is updated -- or it is not, and the persona is left to be surprised the same way again.
Surprise resolves fast, usually within a second or two, and hands off to a valenced feeling once the news is appraised: the same jolt becomes delight if the news is good and dismay if bad, and both are the emotion engine's.
It runs as [startle](process_startle.md), [jolt](process_jolt.md), [sensemaking](process_sensemaking.md), or [dismissal](process_dismissal.md). The attention it seizes is the attention engine's; the belief it revises is the belief engine's; the vastness-sized surprise that overflows into accommodation is the awe engine's. This engine owns the violation-response itself -- the interrupt and the reorientation.
