# Process: Jolt

## Taxonomy

[Surprise](process_surprise.md), the appraised interrupt.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: surprise

## Initiated by

An expectation breaks -- a fact learned that the model said was false, a face where none should be, a turn no one saw coming -- and the mind lurches, whatever it was doing halted mid-stride.
This is the jolt: surprise proper, the appraised recognition that the world has just contradicted the model.
Unlike the reflex, it needs no loud stimulus -- a single quiet sentence can deliver it.

## Direction

Toward the anomaly, everything else dropped.
The jolt seizes attention and pins it to the unexpected thing, freezing ongoing action -- the sentence unfinished, the step un-taken -- because nothing else matters until the mismatch is understood.
The direction is a hard turn: the whole field of notice swung onto the one thing that should not be.

## Lever

The prediction error, appraised.
The jolt fires on a violated expectation, not on intensity -- it can be perfectly silent, a piece of news delivered flatly that upends everything -- and it scales with how wrong the model was and how confident the persona had been: the more certain the expectation, the harder the lurch when it breaks.
The lever is the collision between a held model and a fact that will not fit it.

## Echo

The persona is stopped, turned, and gripped by the anomaly, poised at the edge of making sense of it.
The attention it pins to the surprising thing is the attention engine's; the jolt itself is the halt and the seizing.
From here it hands on to [sensemaking](process_sensemaking.md) -- the work of repairing the model to fit the anomaly -- or to [dismissal](process_dismissal.md), the refusal of that work; and it colours, once appraised, into the delight or dismay that is the emotion engine's.
