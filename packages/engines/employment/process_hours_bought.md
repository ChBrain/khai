# Process: The Hours Bought

## Taxonomy

[The Indeterminacy](process_indeterminacy.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: employment

## Initiated by

A period of the persona's life has been purchased.
What the employer has acquired is availability: the persona will be here, directed by somebody else, for this many hours, and will not be doing anything of their own during them.

## Direction

Toward a block of time that belongs to somebody else.
The transaction is unusually clean at this level and unusually strange: it is not the sale of a thing or of a result but of a stretch of a life, during which the persona's capacities are at another's disposal.
Everything the job later requires is fitted inside a container agreed at the start.

## Lever

The clarity of the hours against everything the hours do not settle.
The carrying force is that the container is real and protective: a bounded day is one of the oldest things workers fought for and won, and a persona who knows when the claim on them ends holds something substantial; the resistance is that the boundary is around the time and not around the work, so intensification costs the employer nothing -- more can be required inside the same hours indefinitely, without any term being altered and without anybody having broken an agreement.
The Hours are a fence around the wrong quantity.

## Echo

A day whose edges are settled and whose contents are not.
It is the movement's premise: the thing that was bought.
The Hours are dated from the offer letter, and they are the only part of the arrangement anybody can point to.
