# Process: Work to Rule

## Taxonomy

[The Gift](process_gift_work.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: employment

## Initiated by

The persona does exactly what is required, and nothing else.
Work to Rule starts wherever the gift is withdrawn while the contract is honoured: every procedure followed, every break taken, every ambiguity referred upward, nothing anticipated, nothing volunteered.

## Direction

Toward a demonstration that the enforceable terms are not enough.
The work slows dramatically and no rule has been broken, which is the entire point: the persona cannot be disciplined for it, and the employer cannot name what has stopped, because what has stopped was never specified.
It is the engine's proof, run in public.

## Lever

The unanswerable safety of compliance against what it reveals about the ordinary day.
The carrying force is that this is the persona's most defensible move and it works: they are doing what they were told, exactly, and the resulting collapse is an argument nobody has to make out loud; the resistance is that it exposes something both sides preferred not to state -- that the organisation has been running on unpaid goodwill all along -- and once that is in the open, the goodwill is harder to give back innocently, since it has been shown to be leverage.
Work to Rule wins the argument by naming the thing that was working because it was unnamed.

## Echo

An organisation running on its terms, and failing.
It shows the gift by its absence, which is the only way it can be shown.
Work to Rule is dated from the day it starts and changes the relationship whether or not it succeeds.
