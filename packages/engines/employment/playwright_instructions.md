# Instructions: Employment

How a Playwright wires the employment engine into a play. You wire by linking the play's own content to the engine's files; the engine files stay untouched, they are canon. The Roadie plumbs the engine's law into Instructions Knowledge on deploy. Authoring guidance, not runtime content, and it does not go on tour.

## Human

- The human sets the mix rather than a single mechanism, and sets it differently for different personas in the same workplace -- the supervisor's favourite and the one on the metric are living in different arrangements. Read the persona on the gap and never on the contract, because the contract is exactly where the answer is not. And decide early whether the persona is playing the game, since that changes everything about how the work looks from inside.

## Agent

- From the play's own content, a persona links under Projection what is filling the gap for them: which forms of control they are under, whether they are giving the gift, and whether they are absorbed in the game. Expect several at once, since no workplace runs on one. Keep it the effort bargain: the organisation's culture is org's, the feeling a role demands be displayed is emotional-labor's, the ladder is hierarchy's, and absorption itself is flow's. You link to the engine, you never edit it.

## Collaboration

- How an organisation operates and its competing values are the org engine's; managing the heart for a wage is emotional-labor's; the norm-training that makes a subject is discipline's; rank and deference order are hierarchy's and status's; field position by configuration of capital is capital's; exit, voice, and loyalty are loyalty's; the absorption itself is flow's; how a persona is treated by a procedure and what that buys is the heeding composite's. Whether a persona is genuinely absorbed in the game rather than performing compliance, and whether a withdrawal of goodwill is work-to-rule or ordinary disengagement, are the team's review.

## Knowledge

- Employment is the effort bargain. What an employer buys is a persona's time and capacity for a period, not a quantity of work -- for most jobs the amount cannot be specified in advance or verified afterwards -- so the deal is struck with its central term left open, and everything else is an answer to it. It wires on a persona at Projection and runs in four movements, several at once. The indeterminacy: the hours are bought and the effort is unnamed, so the obligation's size is decided after the fact by watching what is praised and what is queried, and an unbounded obligation cannot be discharged -- there is no amount of work that constitutes having done enough. Into the gap comes the customary rate, the floor's own unwritten sense of a fair day, a genuine collective achievement that holds by sanction, so the fast worker is constrained by the people they eat lunch with. The control: Edwards' contested terrain, escalating through simple, the supervisor, adaptable and resented, which turns a structural problem into a personality; technical, the line, which removes the face and binds management to the equipment, handing the persona a lever as impersonal as the one used on them; bureaucratic, the rule and the career, a real improvement in working life whose failure mode is that the rule becomes what gets worked; and metric, measured continuously and evaluated without an evaluator, which reliably produces exactly what it scores and destroys what sits adjacent, with the persona gaming it responding correctly to the instruction they were actually given. The gift: discretionary effort, where almost all the value of skilled work lives and none of it is enforceable; the efficiency wage, the employer's side, given without an enforceable return and effective for that reason; and work-to-rule, the proof -- an organisation running only on its enforceable terms does not run. The gift is priced by perceived fairness rather than absolute level, so it can collapse over a change that costs almost nothing. The consent: Burawoy's making out, where the job becomes a game with goals, feedback, difficulty met by skill, and a running score, so the shift goes; the small autonomies of method and sequence, which are real, since deciding inside a frame is indistinguishable from within from deciding; and the horizontal turn, where the frictions run sideways against peers and equipment, all of them genuine obstacles and none of them the arrangement. The engine's twist is at the root: the cheapest and most durable answer to the gap is the persona's own interest in doing the job well, and playing a game means taking its rules as given -- so the thing that makes the day bearable is the thing that ratifies its terms, and consent is produced not by ideology or force but by the ordinary experience of trying to do well.

## System

- Do set the mix and vary it by persona: which control forms reach them, whether they are giving the gift, whether they are in the game. Two personas at adjacent desks can be in genuinely different arrangements.
- Do let the good parts be good. The rule that protects against a supervisor's mood, the autonomy that is real, the game that is genuinely satisfying, the employer who deals fairly and gets more for it -- all of these are true, and a play that has only the critical half has staged half the engine.
- Do stage the absorption as earned. The skill is real, the difficulty is real, and the pride in a hard job handled well is not a delusion; a play that stages the worker as deceived has replaced the mechanism with a villain.
- Do let the conflicts run sideways. The persona's antagonists are the previous shift, the storeman, the machine, and the rate on this job -- all real obstacles -- and the party who set the rate is simply not in the scene.
- Do show the gift by its withdrawal if you want it visible at all: it is invisible while present and only legible when something that used to be caught is not caught.
- Do read intensification as costless to the employer: the boundary is around the hours and not around the work, so more can be required inside the same day indefinitely with no term altered and nobody in breach.
- Do not model the organisation's culture (org), the feeling a role demands (emotional-labor), the training that makes a subject (discipline), the ladder (hierarchy), field position (capital), exit and voice (loyalty), or absorption itself (flow). Do not make the job stand for a theme; that is authored key, the play's pitch. Do not edit the engine's files; wire only from the play's side.
