# Process: Technical Control

## Taxonomy

[The Control](process_control_work.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: employment

## Initiated by

The equipment sets the pace.
Technical control is embedded in the plant: the line moves, the queue fills, the system assigns the next task, and the persona's tempo is determined by a machine rather than by an instruction.

## Direction

Toward effort with no one to argue with.
The great achievement is depersonalisation -- nobody is telling the persona to go faster, the work simply arrives -- so the resentment that simple control attracted has nothing to attach to.
The great vulnerability is that the pace is now a physical fact, and physical facts can be stopped.

## Lever

The impersonality of the machine against what the machine concentrates.
The carrying force is that this genuinely removes the arbitrary supervisor and gives the persona a predictable day, and the predictability is worth something real; the resistance is that management has bound itself to the equipment, so a stoppage anywhere stops everything -- technical control invented the modern strike by making the whole plant interdependent, and it hands the persona a lever exactly as impersonal as the one used on them.
Technical control removes the argument and supplies the weapon.

## Echo

A pace nobody set and everybody can halt.
It is the form that made collective action structurally powerful, which was not the intention.
Technical control is dated from the installation and lasts until the equipment changes.
