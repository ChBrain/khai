# Process: Metric Control

## Taxonomy

[The Control](process_control_work.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: employment

## Initiated by

Everything is being counted.
Metric control is continuous and impersonal measurement: handling time, keystrokes, route adherence, response latency, utilisation, a score -- gathered automatically, compared across persons, and acted on without anybody having watched.

## Direction

Toward supervision that never rests and never appears.
It combines the reach of technical control with the criteria of bureaucratic control and adds a property neither had: the persona is aware of being measured at every moment and has no counterpart to address, since the assessment is produced by a system and delivered as a fact.
The evaluation arrives without an evaluator.

## Lever

The precision of measurement against what measurement selects for.
The carrying force is that measurement is not inherently oppressive and much of it is welcome: a persona can see how they are doing, can demonstrate their contribution, and is spared a manager's impressionistic verdict -- being counted is often fairer than being remembered; the resistance is that any measure that becomes a target stops measuring what it measured, so metric control reliably produces exactly the behaviour it scores and quietly destroys everything adjacent -- and the persona doing the gaming is not cheating, they are responding correctly to the instruction they were actually given.
Metric control is the most precise of the four and the most thoroughly gamed.

## Echo

A workplace optimised toward its instruments.
It is the newest form and it repeats the pattern: an answer to the previous form's weakness that becomes the next terrain of contest.
Metric control is dated from the last dashboard, and the workarounds begin the same week.
