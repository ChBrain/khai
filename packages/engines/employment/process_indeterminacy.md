# Process: The Indeterminacy

## Taxonomy

[Employment](process_employment.md), the open term.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: employment

## Initiated by

The agreement is complete and the important number is missing from it.
The Indeterminacy starts at the structure of the deal: [the hours are bought](process_hours_bought.md) and [the effort is unnamed](process_effort_unnamed.md), so what has been purchased is a capacity to work rather than an amount of work done.
It is the face of the engine that is present in every job and visible in almost none.

## Direction

Toward a rate that settles without ever being agreed.
Since the term cannot be left genuinely open -- nobody can work at an unspecified intensity for thirty years -- something fills it, and the first filler is always [the customary rate](process_customary_rate.md): the shared, unwritten sense on the floor of what a fair day looks like, enforced by the people doing the work rather than by the people buying it.
The rate is a settlement. It is also a permanent invitation to renegotiate.

## Lever

The necessity of the open term against the impossibility of leaving it open.
The carrying force is that the indeterminacy is productive and not a defect to be engineered away: work specified to the movement is work that cannot adapt, cannot improve, and cannot handle the case nobody anticipated, so every attempt to close the term completely produces a worse job and usually a worse output; the resistance is that an unspecified obligation is an unbounded one -- a persona who has agreed to work has agreed to something whose size is set afterwards, by somebody else, or by a norm they will be punished for breaking.
The Indeterminacy is where a contract turns out to be a beginning.

## Echo

A settled rate nobody signed.
It is the movement that generates the other three, since control, gift, and consent are all answers to the same question.
What it hands upward is a persona who is not working to a specification but to an understanding -- and understandings move, which is why the number can rise for years without anybody ever having asked for more.
The Indeterminacy is dated from the first day and never resolves.
