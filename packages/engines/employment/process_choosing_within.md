# Process: Choosing Within

## Taxonomy

[The Consent](process_consent_work.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: employment

## Initiated by

The persona decides how to do it.
Choosing Within starts at the small autonomies that survive every control system: which order, which method, when to take the difficult one, how to set up, when to push and when to coast.

## Direction

Toward a day that feels chosen.
These choices are genuinely the persona's and genuinely consequential to how the shift goes, which is what makes them effective: the experience of deciding is real, and the experience of deciding inside a frame is indistinguishable, from within, from the experience of deciding.
The frame is not hidden. It is simply not what the persona is attending to while they work.

## Lever

The reality of the small choices against the fixity of the large ones.
The carrying force is that the autonomy is not fake and the persona is not being tricked: a job with room to decide how is a far better job than one without, the room is what expertise lives in, and any account that dismisses it has misunderstood what people value about work; the resistance is that the choices available are all of the same order -- how, not whether, and never how much -- so a persona can spend a career exercising real judgement without ever having been asked a question about the terms.
Choosing Within gives the persona authorship of the method and none of the frame.

## Echo

A day full of decisions, none of them about the arrangement.
It is what makes the game playable and the consent durable.
Choosing Within is dated moment to moment, which is why it never surfaces as a question.
