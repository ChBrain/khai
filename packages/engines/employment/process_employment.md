# Process: Employment

## Taxonomy

Work (no khai file of its own yet).

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: employment

## Initiated by

A persona has agreed to work for somebody, and nobody has said how hard.
Employment starts at the gap in the agreement itself. What an employer buys is a persona's time and capacity for a period -- not a quantity of work, which cannot be specified in advance for most jobs and cannot be verified afterwards for many -- so the deal is struck with its central term left open.
It is not the culture of the organisation, which is org's, and not the feeling a role demands be displayed, which is emotional-labor's. It is the permanently unsettled question of how much work is in a day's work.

## Direction

Toward an answer, arrived at by one of three routes and usually all three at once.
The engine reads the gap and then the ways it gets filled: [the indeterminacy](process_indeterminacy.md), the open term and the customary rate that quietly settles it; [the control](process_control_work.md), effort got by direction, in Edwards' escalating forms; [the gift](process_gift_work.md), effort given freely because the persona feels fairly dealt with; and [the consent](process_consent_work.md), effort produced by the persona's own absorption in doing the job well.
No workplace runs on one. What varies is the mix, and the mix is what a persona is actually describing when they say what it is like to work there.

## Lever

What was bought against what has to be got.
The carrying force is that the arrangement is not a swindle and mostly works: an employer genuinely cannot specify in advance what a day should contain, a persona genuinely benefits from not having every movement prescribed, and the open term is what makes skilled work possible at all -- a job with no discretion in it is a job nobody wants; the resistance is that an open term has to be closed by something, and everything available for closing it is either surveillance, reciprocity, or absorption, each of which costs the persona something they did not price when they took the job.
Employment is read on how the gap gets filled, never on the contract, because the contract is exactly where the answer is not.

## Echo

A persona working at a rate nobody wrote down, for reasons they did not choose between.
Employment does not close; it is a bargain being settled daily, gathered into one reading -- an indeterminacy, a control, a gift, a consent.
The engine's twist is here. The cheapest and most durable answer to the gap is the persona's own interest in doing the job well: absorbed in the small problems of the shift, trying to make the number, taking pride in beating a difficulty, the persona produces effort nobody had to extract. And playing that game requires taking its rules as given, so the very thing that makes the day bearable is the thing that ratifies the terms. Consent is not manufactured by argument or by force. It is manufactured by the ordinary experience of trying to do well.
It hands off what it is not: the organisation's culture and its competing values are org's; the feeling a role demands be displayed is emotional-labor's; the norm-training that makes a subject is discipline's; the ladder and its rungs are hierarchy's; the field position by configuration of capital is capital's; exit, voice, and loyalty are loyalty's; and a job made to **stand for** a theme is authored key, the play's pitch, not the force.
The persona is dated from the day the rate settled, which nobody announced.
