# Process: Making Out

## Taxonomy

[The Consent](process_consent_work.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: employment

## Initiated by

The persona is trying to hit the number, and it has become interesting.
Making Out starts wherever the job turns into a game: the rate to beat, the difficult set-up to be handled, the awkward job to be got through, the record to hold -- played for the satisfaction of playing it and discussed constantly with the people playing alongside.

## Direction

Toward a shift that passes quickly and a day that is the persona's own.
The game supplies what the work itself does not: goals, immediate feedback, a difficulty that can be met with skill, and a running score.
Personas play hard, get good at it, and take real pride in it -- and the day, which is otherwise long, goes.

## Lever

The genuine pleasure of playing well against the terms the playing accepts.
The carrying force is that this is not delusion and should never be staged as one: the skill is real, the difficulty is real, the satisfaction of handling a bad job well is one of the better things available in a working life, and a persona absorbed in it is having a better day than one who is not; the resistance is that a game is defined by its rules, and the rules here are the rate, the piece price, the allocation, and the standard -- none of which the persona set, all of which they must accept as given in order to play at all.
Making Out is the good part of work doing the employer's work.

## Echo

Effort at full stretch, freely given, inside somebody else's frame.
It is the mechanism the engine's twist runs on, and it explains what neither control nor gift could: why willing hard work appears where nobody is being watched and nobody is being especially well treated.
Making Out is dated from whenever the job got interesting.
