# Process: The Horizontal Turn

## Taxonomy

[The Consent](process_consent_work.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: employment

## Initiated by

The persona's antagonist is at their own level.
The Horizontal Turn starts wherever the game's frictions arrive: the previous shift that left it badly, the storeman who is slow with parts, the operator who took the easy jobs, the machine that will not hold tolerance, the rate that is wrong on this one.

## Direction

Toward a conflict that runs sideways rather than upward.
The obstacles to making out are overwhelmingly local, immediate, and personal -- and they are real obstacles, so the irritation is earned.
What follows is that the persona's working experience of struggle is against peers and equipment, and the party who set the rate is not in the story at all.

## Lever

The reality of the frictions against where the frictions point.
The carrying force is that these conflicts are not manufactured and the persona is not misdirected by anybody: the storeman really is slow, the previous shift really did leave a mess, and attending to what is actually in the way is exactly what a competent worker does; the resistance is that the arrangement that made these the salient problems is itself never salient, so the more energetically the persona contests what is in front of them, the less any of it accumulates into a question about the frame.
The Horizontal Turn does not conceal the vertical relation. It simply leaves nobody with the attention to look at it.

## Echo

A workplace full of live disputes, none of them about the terms.
It shows the consent movement complete: absorption supplies the effort, the small choices supply the ownership, and the sideways conflict absorbs whatever is left over.
The Horizontal Turn is dated from the shift handover, and it renews itself daily.
