# Process: Simple Control

## Taxonomy

[The Control](process_control_work.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: employment

## Initiated by

Somebody is standing there.
Simple control is direct and personal: an owner, a foreman, a supervisor watching, instructing, praising, and dismissing, with the authority resting in a person rather than in a system.

## Direction

Toward effort produced by a specific individual's attention.
It is immediate, flexible, and adapts to the case in front of it -- a good supervisor is a considerable asset and can get work done that no rulebook could specify.
It is also arbitrary at the point of use, since the same discretion that lets a supervisor be sensible lets them be capricious.

## Lever

The responsiveness of a person against the resentment a person attracts.
The carrying force is that personal direction is the most adaptable form there is and remains dominant in small workplaces because it works: somebody who knows the job and the people can allocate, adjust, and encourage in ways no system replicates; the resistance is that it does not scale and it personalises everything, so the persona's grievance is against a named human being who eats in the same room -- and conflict that has a face is conflict that escalates.
Simple control converts a structural problem into a personality.

## Echo

Effort secured, and an enemy created.
It is the form the others were invented to escape, and the one that returns whenever a workplace is small enough.
Simple control is dated from whoever is on shift.
