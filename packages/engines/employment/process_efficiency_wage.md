# Process: The Efficiency Wage

## Taxonomy

[The Gift](process_gift_work.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: employment

## Initiated by

The employer is paying more than they have to.
The Efficiency Wage starts wherever the employer's side of the exchange is deliberately generous: pay above the market rate, security, latitude, forbearance, a training investment -- given without an enforceable return.

## Direction

Toward effort bought with something other than instruction.
The logic is sound and well evidenced: personas who are paid and treated above the minimum work harder, quit less, and are more costly to lose -- so the generosity is not charity but a purchase, made in a currency that reciprocity accepts and contracts cannot.
The employer is not being kind. They are being effective, which is a different thing and not a worse one.

## Lever

The return on generosity against the fact that it must be felt to be generous.
The carrying force is that the exchange is real and mutual: both sides get more than the enforceable arrangement would give them, and a workplace running on it is a genuinely better place to be; the resistance is that the currency is perceived fairness rather than absolute level, so the exchange is priced by comparison -- to what others get, to what was promised, to what used to be -- and a change that costs the employer very little can end it, since what was withdrawn is not the money but the reading.
The Efficiency Wage buys a great deal and can be forfeited by a memo.

## Echo

A relationship maintained by a payment neither side calls a payment.
It is the answer to the gap that costs the most and produces the most, and its accounting is entirely in the persona's head.
The Efficiency Wage is dated from the last comparison the persona made.
