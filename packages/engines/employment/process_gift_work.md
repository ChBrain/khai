# Process: The Gift

## Taxonomy

[Employment](process_employment.md), effort given rather than got.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: employment

## Initiated by

The persona is doing more than they have to, and nobody asked.
The Gift starts wherever Akerlof's partial gift exchange runs: an employer gives more than the market requires -- pay, security, latitude, decency -- and personas give more effort than the minimum enforceable, and neither transfer is contractual.
It is the face of the engine that runs on reciprocity rather than on extraction.

## Direction

Toward a norm of fair dealing that both sides are inside.
The Gift reads three things: [discretionary effort](process_discretionary_effort.md), the part of the job that cannot be required; [the efficiency wage](process_efficiency_wage.md), the employer's side of the exchange, paid above what the market would clear at; and [work to rule](process_work_to_rule.md), which is the proof -- when the gift is withdrawn and personas do exactly what is required, the work stops.
That last is the demonstration the engine rests on: an organisation running only on its enforceable terms does not run.

## Lever

The generosity of the exchange against its dependence on being seen as fair.
The carrying force is that this is the best answer to the gap and the least coercive: personas genuinely do give more when they are dealt with well, the giving is not naive, and an employer who invests in fair dealing gets effort that no control system could have bought; the resistance is that a gift is not a term, so it can be withdrawn without breach, and it is priced by perceived fairness rather than by absolute level -- which means it can collapse over a change that costs almost nothing, and no contract records what was lost.
The Gift is the most valuable thing an employer holds and the only one that appears nowhere in the accounts.

## Echo

An arrangement running on something neither side can name in writing.
It shows the gap filled by a relationship rather than by a system, which is why it is stronger and more brittle at once.
What it hands upward is the discovery that the persona's willingness is the asset -- and the next movement is about the cheapest way to secure it, which does not require anybody to be treated well at all.
The Gift is dated from the last thing that was felt to be fair, and it is repriced every time something changes.
