# Process: Bureaucratic Control

## Taxonomy

[The Control](process_control_work.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: employment

## Initiated by

The rules say.
Bureaucratic control locates authority in the structure of the organisation: written procedure, job grades, evaluation criteria, seniority, and a career that rewards the persona for staying and conforming.

## Direction

Toward effort secured by a future.
It is the most sophisticated of the four because it does not merely constrain, it recruits: the persona is given a stake -- a grade to reach, a review to pass, a pension that vests -- so that their own interest points the same way as the requirement.
Rules also protect, which is why they are frequently defended by the people they bind.

## Lever

The fairness of rule against the invitation rule extends.
The carrying force is that this was a real improvement in working life and should be read as one: treated by criterion rather than by favour, with a grievance procedure and a visible ladder, a persona is far better placed than under a supervisor's mood -- much of what is now taken for granted about decent employment is bureaucratic control's; the resistance is that once the rule is the authority, the rule is what gets worked -- the persona does what is measured, meets the criterion rather than the purpose, and can bring an organisation to a halt by following its own procedures exactly.
Bureaucratic control is the form that makes work-to-rule possible and devastating.

## Echo

Effort bought with a career, and a manual that can be turned around.
It is the form most workplaces still run on, and the one whose failure mode is compliance.
Bureaucratic control is dated from the last policy revision.
