# Process: Discretionary Effort

## Taxonomy

[The Gift](process_gift_work.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: employment

## Initiated by

The persona does the part nobody could have required.
Discretionary effort is what is given rather than owed: noticing the thing that was about to go wrong, staying to finish, helping the new person, saying the awkward thing, caring how it turns out.

## Direction

Toward an organisation that works better than its rules can account for.
Almost all of the value in skilled work lives here, and none of it is enforceable -- it is the difference between a job done and a job done properly, and no specification has ever captured it.
It is also the first thing to go, silently, when something else changes.

## Lever

The value of what cannot be required against the ease of losing it.
The carrying force is that personas want to do this: the impulse to do the job properly is genuine, widespread, and largely independent of pay, and treating it as bought is the surest way to misunderstand it; the resistance is that it is a gift and gifts are conditional on the relationship -- so it is withdrawn without notice, without breach, and usually without any statement, and the withdrawal is invisible until something that used to be caught is not caught.
Discretionary effort is the most valuable thing in the workplace and it is not owned by anybody.

## Echo

A margin of goodwill that everything depends on and nothing records.
It is the substance of the gift movement, and the thing the other three movements are all trying to secure by other means.
Discretionary effort is dated from the last time the persona felt fairly dealt with.
