# Process: The Customary Rate

## Taxonomy

[The Indeterminacy](process_indeterminacy.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: employment

## Initiated by

The floor already knows what a fair day is.
The Customary Rate starts wherever the people doing the work have arrived at a shared, unwritten sense of the proper pace -- and enforce it on each other, which is the part that surprises people who have not worked there.

## Direction

Toward a norm defended from below.
The rate protects: it stops the pace from ratcheting, it makes the job survivable over years rather than months, and it distributes the standard so no individual has to negotiate it alone.
And it disciplines: the persona who works far above it is not admired but marked, because their output becomes the evidence that the rate could be higher.

## Lever

The protection of a collective standard against what it costs those it constrains.
The carrying force is that the rate is a genuine achievement and mostly benign: without it the indeterminate term is settled by whoever is most desperate, and the pace climbs until it breaks people -- the persona who honours the rate is not lazy, they are maintaining a fence; the resistance is that the fence holds by sanction, so the fast worker, the eager newcomer, and the one who needs the overtime are all held down by their colleagues rather than by their employer, and the pressure they feel comes from the people they eat lunch with.
The Customary Rate is solidarity and constraint in the same act.

## Echo

A number nobody set, kept by everybody.
It is the first and quietest answer to the open term, and it operates before any manager arrives.
The Customary Rate is dated from before the persona joined, and it will outlast them.
