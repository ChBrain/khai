# Process: The Control

## Taxonomy

[Employment](process_employment.md), effort got by direction.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: employment

## Initiated by

Somebody has to make sure the work is being done.
The Control starts wherever the gap is filled from above: Edwards' contested terrain, where each answer to the problem of extraction becomes a terrain on which it is contested again.
It escalates through forms rather than replacing them -- [simple](process_simple_control.md), the boss watching; [technical](process_technical_control.md), the line setting the pace; [bureaucratic](process_bureaucratic_control.md), the rules and grades and career; and [metric](process_metric_control.md), the measurement that supervises continuously and impersonally.

## Direction

Toward effort that does not depend on the persona's willingness.
Each form is a solution to the previous form's failure. Personal supervision does not scale and breeds resentment at a named individual; the machine paces without a face but binds management to the machine; the rulebook depersonalises and buys loyalty with a career, but makes rules the thing to be worked; the metric measures everything and turns out to be the most gameable of the four.

## Lever

The reliability of directed effort against what direction costs to run.
The carrying force is that control works and is frequently welcome: a persona who knows what is expected, is treated by rule rather than by favour, and can see a ladder is in a far better position than one at the mercy of a supervisor's mood -- bureaucratic control was, among other things, a genuine improvement in a worker's life; the resistance is that every form is expensive, every form is gameable, and each one moves the contest rather than ending it, since a persona who is measured optimises the measurement and a persona who is ruled works the rule.
The Control never wins, and it is not supposed to; it holds a position.

## Echo

An arrangement that gets the work done and is being worked back.
It is the movement most visible to everybody and least sufficient on its own, because directed effort has a ceiling: it can compel presence and compliance, and it cannot compel the part of the job that requires somebody to care.
What it hands upward is the residue -- everything that matters and cannot be specified -- which the next two movements are about.
The Control is dated from the last reorganisation and is already being adapted to.
