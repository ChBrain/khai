# Employment

Employment as process: the effort bargain, read on the gap in the agreement rather than on the agreement -- what an employer buys is a persona's time and capacity for a period, not a quantity of work, which for most jobs cannot be specified in advance or verified afterwards, so the deal is struck with its central term left open and something has to close it -- across four movements: the indeterminacy (hours bought, effort unnamed, and the customary rate the floor defends from below), the control (Edwards' escalating forms -- simple, technical, bureaucratic, metric -- each an answer to the last one's failure and each a new terrain of contest), the gift (discretionary effort, the efficiency wage, and work-to-rule as the proof that an organisation running only on its enforceable terms does not run), and the consent (Burawoy's making out, the small real autonomies, and the conflict that runs sideways) -- with the twist that the cheapest and most durable answer to the gap is the persona's own interest in doing the job well, so the thing that makes the day bearable is the thing that ratifies its terms.
