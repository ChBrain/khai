# Process: The Effort Unnamed

## Taxonomy

[The Indeterminacy](process_indeterminacy.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: employment

## Initiated by

Nobody said how hard.
The Effort Unnamed starts at the absence: no clause specifies intensity, pace, care, attention, or how much of themselves the persona is expected to bring, and for most work no clause could.

## Direction

Toward an obligation whose size is decided after the fact.
The persona finds out what is expected by doing the job and watching what happens -- what is praised, what is queried, who is thought sound, who is thought to be coasting -- and the standard assembles itself out of a hundred small reactions rather than being stated once.
It is learned the way an accent is learned.

## Lever

The freedom of an unspecified job against the exposure of an unbounded one.
The carrying force is that the absence is what makes skilled work possible: a persona whose effort was fully specified could not exercise judgement, could not improve on the method, and would be doing something closer to operating than to working -- most of what personas value in a job lives in the unnamed part; the resistance is that an unbounded obligation cannot be discharged, so there is no amount of work that constitutes having done enough, and the persona who wants to be sure has only one strategy available, which is more.
The Effort Unnamed is the reason nobody can tell whether they are working hard enough.

## Echo

A standard held by everybody and written by nobody.
It is where the whole engine's problem lives, and every later movement is a way of answering it.
The Effort Unnamed is dated continuously, since the standard is being revised by every day's ordinary reactions.
