# Process: The Arranging

## Taxonomy

[Pet](piece_pet.md), the removals that make the keeping possible.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: pet

## Initiated by

The animal is fitted to a life indoors.
Fed on a schedule, confined to a territory, taught what is permitted, and in most cases surgically prevented from reproducing.

## Direction

Toward a creature that cannot meet a single one of its own needs.
Each step is defensible on its own and most are recommended by every veterinary body there is: neutering prevents disease and unwanted litters, confinement prevents traffic deaths and predation, a schedule prevents obesity, training prevents the bites that end with an animal destroyed.
Taken together they produce something the animal was not before, which is an organism whose survival is entirely contingent on somebody continuing to bother.

## Lever

The welfare each removal buys against the dependence they sum to.
The carrying force is that the removals are genuinely protective and the numbers are not close: an indoor cat outlives an outdoor one by years, a neutered animal avoids a list of cancers, and a keeper who declined all of it in the name of the animal's freedom would be producing a shorter and more frightening life, not a freer one; the resistance is that the sum of them is a creature that would die without its keeper in a matter of weeks, and that dependence is not a side effect of the care but the thing the care is organised around.
The Arranging protects the animal by removing its capacity to do without protection.

## Echo

A safe animal that cannot be released.
It shows why the twist is not an accusation: every step in it is the right one.
The Arranging is dated to the alterations and never reverses.
