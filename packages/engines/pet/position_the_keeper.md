# Position: The Keeper

## Taxonomy

[Pet](piece_pet.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: pet

## Has

The food, the door, the lead, the vet, and the decision.
Every route the animal has to anything it needs runs through one person, and none of those routes existed before that person opened them.
They also hold the interpretation: what the animal wants, whether it is happy, whether it is in pain, and whether it has had enough. The animal cannot correct any of it, and the keeper is usually the only person who would notice if they got it wrong.

## Orders

The whole of another creature's day, without ever issuing an order it could refuse.
When it eats, where it sleeps, whether it goes out, who it meets, whether it reproduces, and how far it may walk before the lead stops. None of this is imposed by force in an ordinary household; it is simply the shape of the only life available.
The powers are odd because they are total and almost never felt as power: a keeper experiences the arrangement as looking after something, and the description is accurate.

## Loses

Any way of finding out whether the animal would have chosen this.
There is no version of the question the animal can answer. It can show distress, and a good keeper reads it; it cannot decline the arrangement in general, and no amount of attentiveness converts its preferences into consent.
They also lose the option of neutrality. A keeper who stops deciding has not freed the animal, they have neglected it, so there is no position available in which the keeper is not directing another creature's life.

## Drives

To be the one it comes to.
The motive is rarely mastery and almost never cruelty. It is the ordinary and considerable pleasure of being singled out by something that owes nobody a performance, met at the door by a creature with no view on how the meeting went.
The Keeper has complete authority and experiences it as being chosen.
