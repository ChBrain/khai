# Process: The Acquiring

## Taxonomy

[Pet](piece_pet.md), how the animal arrives.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: pet

## Initiated by

Somebody decides to get one.
Bought, bred to order, taken from a litter, or rescued -- and in every version the choosing runs one way.

## Direction

Toward a household that selected an animal from among others it did not select.
The criteria are the keeper's: size, temperament, coat, cost, whether it is good with children, whether it will fit the flat. Breed animals are shaped over generations toward exactly these criteria, which is why so many of them carry the illnesses that come with the shape.
Rescue inverts the moral story and not the structure. The animal is still chosen, still by somebody, still from a room of others who were not, and it still has no say in where it goes.

## Lever

The reality of the choice against the absence of a second one.
The carrying force is that the choosing is often careful and generous, and that the alternative for the animal is frequently much worse: a rehomed dog gets a decade it was not going to have, and the person who took it on is not a villain in any reading of the situation; the resistance is that no part of the transaction involves the animal agreeing to it, and no amount of care afterwards converts a good decision made about a creature into a decision made with it.
The Acquiring is a decision about a life, taken entirely on one side.

## Echo

A household with an animal in it, and an animal with nowhere else.
It shows that the asymmetry is present from the first day rather than developing later.
The Acquiring is dated to the day it came home.
