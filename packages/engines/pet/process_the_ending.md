# Process: The Ending

## Taxonomy

[Pet](piece_pet.md), the decision nobody else gets to make.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: pet

## Initiated by

The keeper decides when the animal dies.
A diagnosis, a stiffness on the stairs, a bad week, a cost, a vet saying it is time.

## Direction

Toward a death chosen by somebody else, at a time they pick, and usually held while it happens.
This is the one part of the arrangement with no equivalent anywhere in the keeper's other relationships. Nobody schedules a parent's death, and the impossibility of doing so is treated as a hard moral boundary. Here it is the responsible act, and declining it is the failure.
The keeper is also the last thing the animal sees, and knows this while deciding.

## Lever

The mercy of the decision against the fact that it is a decision.
The carrying force is that it is a genuine mercy and the alternative is worse in a way anybody who has seen it will confirm: an animal cannot be told what is happening, cannot consent to treatment, and cannot understand why the pain continues, so an ending chosen early and held calmly is the kindest thing available and is very often exactly that; the resistance is that the timing turns on the keeper's judgement, the keeper's finances, and the keeper's tolerance for watching, and no method exists for telling a merciful decision from a convenient one, including from inside the person making it.
The Ending is the arrangement's last act and the only one with no version the animal survives.

## Echo

A household that decided, and a grief with a date on it.
It shows what total dependence finally means, which is that it includes the end.
The Ending is dated to the appointment.
