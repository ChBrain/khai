# Process: Attunement

## Taxonomy

[Accommodation](process_accommodation.md), the strategy this is one form of.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: accommodation

## Initiated by

The persona wants approval, closeness, or an easier exchange, and moves their manner of speaking toward the other's.
This is convergence: the accent drifts toward theirs, the pace matches, the vocabulary and formality settle to a shared level, until the two sound more like each other than they did.
The pull is mostly unspoken -- to be liked, to belong, to be understood without friction -- but the persona is doing it, shaping their own style to close the gap.

## Direction

Toward the other, the distance narrowed.
By sounding more like the interlocutor the persona signals alignment -- I am with you, I am one of us -- and invites the same in return, so a matched pair of styles reads as a warmer, closer tie.
The nearer the styles move, the more the exchange feels like collaboration rather than negotiation.

## Lever

Convergence: the persona's own features slide toward the interlocutor's.
Speech rate slows or quickens to meet theirs, the accent softens toward the shared one, jargon is dropped or picked up to match, the register rises or falls to their level -- each a step toward being more similar, and each a small bid for approval and smoothness.
It works best unforced; convergence a shade too eager, or aimed at a caricature of the other rather than the other, tips over into [overaccommodation](process_overaccommodation.md).

## Echo

Met in kind, convergence builds liking and eases the channel -- the two settle into a shared way of speaking and the tie warms.
Unreturned, it exposes: the persona has reached toward a closeness the other did not offer back, and the unmatched bid leaves them a step over-extended.
It is the move [holding](process_holding.md) declines to make and [divergence](process_divergence.md) reverses; the belief the persona may be trying to shift by winning approval is the persuasion engine's, not this.
