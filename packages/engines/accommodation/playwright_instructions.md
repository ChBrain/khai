# Instructions: Accommodation

How a Playwright wires the accommodation engine into a play. You wire by linking the play's own content to the engine's files; the engine files stay untouched, they are canon. The Roadie plumbs the engine's law into Instructions Knowledge on deploy. Authoring guidance, not runtime content, and it does not go on tour.

## Human

- The human defines the accommodation terrain: whom the persona is speaking with, what social distance stands between them, and what the persona wants from the exchange -- approval, distinctiveness, deference, a smooth channel -- so the direction of any style shift has a reason.

## Agent

- From the play's own content, a persona links the accommodation strategy the moment runs (attunement, divergence, holding, or overaccommodation) under its Projection. Let the strategy shape how the persona's manner of speaking moves against the other's -- toward it, away from it, held steady, or overshot -- and let the reader hear the distance it sets. You link to the engine, you never edit it.

## Collaboration

- The automatic, unchosen mimicry beneath the adjustment is the contagion engine's; the variety of language being drawn from is the register engine's. The group belonging that a divergence invokes is social-identity's; the move on standing it may carry is the status-move engine's; the change of mind a convergence may serve is persuasion's; the reading of the other that a misjudged adjustment got wrong is empathy's. This engine supplies the motivated tuning of style itself -- toward, away, held, or overshot.

## Knowledge

- Accommodation is how a persona adjusts the style of their speech -- accent, rate, vocabulary, formality -- toward or away from another to manage the distance between them. Attunement converges the style toward the other's to win approval or ease. Divergence accentuates the differences to mark identity or hold distance. Holding keeps one's own style unchanged, a non-move that where convergence was due reads as quiet divergence. Overaccommodation adjusts past what the other wants or needs -- the patronizing over-shift, or too little where more was owed -- so the bid to close distance opens it.

## System

- Do let the strategy set the distance: convergence reads as warmth and alignment, divergence as a line drawn, and the reader should feel the tie warm or cool with the movement of the style, not just the words.
- Do let an unmet convergence expose the persona -- a bid toward closeness the other did not return leaves them a step over-extended.
- Do let over-accommodation backfire -- the patronizing simplification lands as insult, so the attempt to close distance opens it; and let under-accommodation strand the one who needed the adjustment.
- Do delegate the automatic mimicry to the contagion engine and the variety of language to register. Do not edit the engine's files; wire only from the play's side.
