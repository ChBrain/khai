# Process: Accommodation

## Taxonomy

Interpersonal communication / sociolinguistics.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: accommodation

## Initiated by

A persona, in talk with another, adjusts how they speak -- not what they say, but the style of the saying: accent, speed, word choice, formality, the length of the pauses.
The other's manner is there as a reference, and turn by turn the persona can move their own toward it, away from it, or leave it where it is.
The move is motivated -- by a want for approval, for distance, for identity, for a channel that runs smoothly -- and mostly runs below notice; but it is a choice a persona makes, aimed at the other, not the reflex that catches beneath it.

## Direction

Toward a social distance set by style.
Moving the manner toward the other's narrows the distance, moving it away widens it, holding it steady keeps it -- and the persona uses the shape of their speech to say where they stand relative to the other: closer or apart, one of them or not one of them.
The other reads the movement and answers it, in kind or against it, so the distance between two people is negotiated in how they sound as much as in what they mean.

## Lever

The features of the persona's own speech, tuned toward or away from the other's.
Rate, accent, vocabulary, formality, gesture, the length of a turn -- any of them can slide. Toward the other is convergence, approval and ease bought with the matching; away is divergence, difference asserted; unmoved is maintenance, which where a shift was expected becomes its own message.
The adjustment can be right-sized to what the other wants, or overshoot into the patronizing, or fall short of what they needed -- and the size is read as clearly as the direction.

## Echo

The relationship is placed by how the two styles moved.
Convergence, met, builds liking and smooths the exchange; unreturned, it leaves the persona over-extended, reaching for a closeness not offered back. Divergence marks a line -- of identity, of standing, of disapproval -- and usually hardens it. And a misjudged adjustment costs more than none: over-accommodation talks down to the competent, under-accommodation strands the one who needed the help.
The process continues as [attunement](process_attunement.md), [divergence](process_divergence.md), [holding](process_holding.md), or [overaccommodation](process_overaccommodation.md) -- the strategies by which a persona's style is tuned toward or away from another. The automatic, unchosen mimicry beneath the adjustment is the contagion engine's; the variety of language the persona is drawing from is the register engine's.
