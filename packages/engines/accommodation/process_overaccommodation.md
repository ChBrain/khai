# Process: Overaccommodation

## Taxonomy

[Accommodation](process_accommodation.md), the strategy this is one form of.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: accommodation

## Initiated by

The persona, trying to converge, misjudges the other and adjusts too far or in the wrong direction.
This is over-accommodation: the manner is bent past what the other wants or needs -- the slow, loud, simplified speech aimed at the elderly, the baby-talk, the over-explaining to someone perfectly competent, the exaggerated foreigner-talk to a fluent speaker.
It comes from a stereotype of the other rather than the other themselves; the persona means to close the distance and instead speaks to a caricature.

## Direction

Past the mark -- toward the other as the persona imagines them, not as they are.
The adjustment aims at a supposed need or limit, and in aiming there it misses the person, treating a competent adult as fragile or slow, a peer as a novice.
Its twin, under-accommodation, misses the other way: too little adjustment where the other genuinely needed it, the specialist who will not drop the jargon for the patient who cannot follow.

## Lever

Over-adjustment, or its twin under-adjustment.
Over: the register dropped too far, the pace slowed past dignity, the vocabulary thinned to the point of condescension, the help no one asked for pressed on the capable. Under: the register never lowered, the terms never explained, the pace never eased for the one who could not keep up.
Both are convergence gone wrong -- the first a bid that overshoots, the second one that never arrives -- and both are read as clearly as the miss deserves.

## Echo

It backfires: the attempt to close distance opens it.
Over-accommodated, the recipient feels talked down to, stereotyped, diminished -- the patronizing help lands as insult and the tie sours where warmth was intended. Under-accommodated, they are left stranded, the exchange failing for a want of the adjustment that was owed.
It is [attunement](process_attunement.md) that missed its target; the misjudged reading of the other that caused the miss is the empathy engine's failure, not this engine's -- this owns the mis-sized adjustment itself and the distance it opens.
