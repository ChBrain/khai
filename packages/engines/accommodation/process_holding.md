# Process: Holding

## Taxonomy

[Accommodation](process_accommodation.md), the strategy this is one form of.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: accommodation

## Initiated by

The other's style shifts, or a setting invites the persona to converge, and the persona does not -- keeping their own manner of speaking exactly as it was.
This is maintenance: no move toward the other and no move away, the persona's accent, pace, and register held constant whatever the interlocutor does.
It can be simple constancy -- being oneself, unbothered by the other's manner -- or a pointed refusal to meet a style that expected meeting.

## Direction

Neither toward nor away by any motion, but placed by standing still where movement was expected.
In a neutral exchange the non-move sets no particular distance; it just reads as the persona being who they are.
But where the moment called for convergence -- a warmth offered, a register lowered to include -- holding one's own ground reads as declining the invitation, and the distance the persona did not close becomes the distance they chose to keep.

## Lever

Maintenance: the persona holds their features constant regardless of the other's.
The rate does not slow to meet a slower one, the jargon is not dropped for the outsider, the formality does not bend toward the other's ease -- nothing moves, and the not-moving is the whole of it.
Its meaning is entirely contextual: constant among equals it is unremarkable; constant against a clear bid to converge it functions as a muted [divergence](process_divergence.md), distance kept without the effort of asserting it.

## Echo

Read as mere constancy, holding costs nothing -- the persona is simply themselves and the exchange runs on.
Read as refusal, it cools the tie without a declared break: the other extended a manner and the persona did not take it up, and the unmet bid leaves a quiet distance neither named.
It is the move [attunement](process_attunement.md) makes and this declines; where a shift was plainly due, the difference between holding and [divergence](process_divergence.md) is only that one moves away and the other simply will not come.
