# @chbrain/khai-engine-accommodation

## 0.1.1

### Patch Changes

- b6b3fd4: Add the accommodation engine: how a persona adjusts the style of their speech -- accent, rate, vocabulary, formality -- toward or away from an interlocutor to manage the social distance between them and to signal who they are. The root (`process_accommodation.md`) declares Communication Accommodation Theory -- style tuned toward the other, away, or held, motivated by needs for approval, identity, and an efficient channel, and read as a signal of distance, group, and standing. Four strategies are the ways style is tuned: `attunement` (converge -- shift the style toward the other's to win approval, closeness, or a smoother exchange; unreturned, it over-extends), `divergence` (accentuate the differences to mark distinctiveness, assert identity, or hold distance and power), `holding` (maintenance -- keep one's own style unchanged whatever the other's, a non-move that where convergence was due reads as quiet divergence), and `overaccommodation` (adjust past what is wanted or needed -- the patronizing over-shift, or its twin under-adjustment -- so the bid to close distance opens it).

  Bounded against `contagion` (the automatic, unchosen mimicry beneath the adjustment, which accommodation is the strategic, motivated counterpart to -- and which has no form of divergence or maintenance), `register` (the variety of language being tuned between vs the act of tuning), `social-identity` (the group belonging a divergence invokes), `status-move` (the move on standing a shift may carry), `persuasion` (the change of mind a convergence may serve), and `empathy` (the reading of the other that a misjudged adjustment got wrong). Warranted (LORE) on Giles (accent mobility -- Speech Accommodation Theory), Giles & Ogay (the CAT synthesis), Giles, Coupland & Coupland (Contexts of Accommodation), Coupland, Coupland, Giles & Henwood (over- and under-accommodation), and Gallois, Ogay & Giles (the CAT propositions and maintenance). Set at patch as the free level; a new engine may warrant a minor at the maintainer's `bump:minor` label.
