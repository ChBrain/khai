# Accommodation: Reference

## Line of Work

Accommodation as **process**: how a persona adjusts the style of their speech -- accent, rate, vocabulary, formality, gesture -- toward or away from an interlocutor, and how the social distance between them is set by that adjustment. The domain does not model the automatic, unconscious mimicry that catches beneath the adjustment -- the reflex by which one face or voice pulls another's toward it -- which is the contagion engine's; nor the variety of language the persona is drawing from, the code tied to a domain and a community, which is the register engine's. It models the motivated, strategic tuning of style itself: the move toward the other to win approval, the move away to mark difference, the deliberate non-move, and the adjustment that overshoots.

The spine is Communication Accommodation Theory: in interaction people continually adjust their communicative behaviour relative to one another, and those adjustments -- converging toward the other, diverging away, or maintaining one's own style -- are motivated by needs for approval, for identity and distinctiveness, and for efficient, comfortable communication, and are read by the other as signals of social distance, group membership, and standing. Convergence tends to win liking and cooperation; divergence asserts a boundary; miscalibrated adjustment -- over- or under-accommodation -- backfires. The four strategies are the ways style is tuned toward or away from another: attunement, divergence, holding, overaccommodation.

## Origin

The sociolinguistics and social psychology of language adjustment in interaction, applied directly as process constraints.

## Restrictions

What the engine refuses to model, and to whom it delegates.

- **Contagion**: The engine models the strategic, motivated adjustment of style, not the automatic mimicry beneath it. The unchosen pull by which one persona's expression, posture, or voice imitates another's -- the reflex that converges without deciding to -- is owned by the contagion engine; this engine governs the adjustment a persona makes, aimed and motivated, and running the other way too as divergence and maintenance, which the reflex has no form of.
- **Register**: The engine models the act of shifting style toward or away from a person, not the variety of language being shifted between. The specialized code a persona commands -- tied to a domain and a community, binding insiders -- is owned by the register engine; this engine governs the movement across styles, drawing on register as the inventory it tunes from.
- **Social identity**: The engine models the stylistic pulling-toward or pulling-away, not the group belonging it signals. The part of the self drawn from group membership, and how a persona holds a group whose standing is in question, is owned by the social-identity engine; divergence invokes that belonging, but the belonging itself is social-identity's.
- **Status move**: The engine models the adjustment of style, not the move on standing it may carry. The tactical raising or lowering of one's own or another's rank is owned by the status-move engine; accommodation can signal deference by converging up or assert power by diverging, but the move on standing itself is status-move's.
- **Persuasion**: The engine models the tuning of style, not the change of mind it may serve. The attempt to change what another believes, values, or does is owned by the persuasion engine; a persona may converge to win the approval that makes persuasion easier, but the belief-change goal is persuasion's, not this.
- **Empathy**: The engine models the adjustment and its mis-sizing, not the reading of the other that guides it. The capacity to take in another's inner state is owned by the empathy engine; over- and under-accommodation are adjustments built on a misread of the other, but the reading itself -- accurate or stereotyped -- is empathy's, and this engine owns only the adjustment that results.

## Encoding

Source to constraint, per file.

- **[accommodation](process_accommodation.md)** (the root): The adjustment of communicative style toward or away from an interlocutor, setting the social distance between them and signaling who the persona is. Anchored by Giles (accent mobility) and Giles & Ogay (the CAT synthesis).
- **[attunement](process_attunement.md)** (strategy): Convergence -- shift the style toward the other's, matching accent, rate, and register to win approval, closeness, or a smoother exchange; unreturned, it over-extends. Anchored by Giles (convergence) and Giles & Ogay.
- **[divergence](process_divergence.md)** (strategy): Accentuate the differences in style, moving away from the other's, to mark distinctiveness, assert identity, or hold distance and power. Anchored by Giles (divergence) and Giles, Coupland & Coupland (accommodation as intergroup and identity work).
- **[holding](process_holding.md)** (strategy): Maintenance -- keep one's own style unchanged whatever the other's; where convergence was due, the non-move reads as a quiet divergence. Anchored by Gallois, Ogay & Giles (maintenance and non-accommodation) and Giles & Ogay.
- **[overaccommodation](process_overaccommodation.md)** (strategy): Adjust past what the other wants or needs -- the patronizing over-shift (elderspeak, baby-talk) or its twin under-adjustment -- so the attempt to close distance opens it. Anchored by Coupland, Coupland, Giles & Henwood (over- and under-accommodation) and Giles & Ogay.
