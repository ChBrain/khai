# Process: Divergence

## Taxonomy

[Accommodation](process_accommodation.md), the strategy this is one form of.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: accommodation

## Initiated by

The persona wants to mark difference -- of identity, of group, of standing -- or to resist the other, and moves their manner of speaking away from theirs.
This is divergence: the accent thickens rather than softens, the in-group vocabulary comes out rather than the shared one, the register is held apart on purpose, so the persona sounds less like the interlocutor, not more.
The pull is to be distinct, to belong to one's own side, to keep or claim ground -- and the style is where the persona plants it.

## Direction

Away from the other, the distance widened, a line drawn.
By sounding less like the interlocutor the persona signals separation -- I am not one of you, I stand elsewhere -- whether to assert a group identity, to keep a rank, or to answer a slight.
The move is legible: the other hears the manner pull away and reads the distance being set, and often diverges back, so the line hardens on both sides.

## Lever

Divergence: the persona accentuates the features that set them apart.
The native accent is leaned into, the specialist or dialect vocabulary is chosen where a plainer one would serve, the formality is pushed up or down against the other's -- each a step toward being less similar, and each a statement of a boundary.
Where convergence courts approval, divergence spends it, trading warmth for distinctiveness or for the assertion of who and what the persona is.

## Echo

The difference is asserted and, once read, usually hardened -- the tie cools to the distance the style set, and a mutual divergence can lock two sides into their separate manners.
It can be a claim of identity, a mark of disapproval, or a move on standing; the group whose belonging it invokes is the social-identity engine's, and the rank it may assert or defend is the status-move engine's -- this engine owns only the stylistic pulling-away that signals them.
It is the reverse of [attunement](process_attunement.md); done by refusing to shift at all, it becomes [holding](process_holding.md).
