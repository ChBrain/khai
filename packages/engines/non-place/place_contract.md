# Place: The Contract

## Taxonomy

The Contract is an expression of the non-place engine. It models the relation between the persona and the space, which is not membership, residence, or acquaintance but a transaction: a ticket, a boarding pass, a trolley coin, a card at a reader.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: non-place

## Shown

The instruments of the relation, everywhere and unmissable: barriers, readers, screens showing a name and a seat number, a receipt, a wristband, a printed slip with a time on it.
What is shown is the terms. The persona can read exactly what they are entitled to and for how long, which is more than most places tell anybody.

## Holds

The verification, held once and then filed. The boundary is where all the checking happens -- the gate, the till, the desk -- and behind it the persona moves freely because the question has already been answered.
The place holds a record of the transaction and nothing whatever about the person who made it.

## Offers

Entitlement without acquaintance. The persona is owed a seat, a room, a lane, a checkout, and is owed it by a system rather than by anybody's goodwill; no favour is being done and none needs returning.
This is a genuinely good arrangement and is the reason strangers can use these spaces at all. A place that admitted people on the basis of who they knew would be closed to almost everybody who needs it.

## Withheld

Standing, discretion, and any margin. The contract is exact: it covers what it covers, and nothing outside it is available at any price of charm, history, or explanation.
A persona whose circumstances fall outside the terms discovers that there is nobody to appeal to, because the relation was never with a person -- and the same completeness that made the place open to them is what leaves them with no purchase on it.
