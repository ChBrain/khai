# Place: Transit

## Taxonomy

Transit is an expression of the non-place engine. It models the space defined entirely by being passed through: a route rather than a destination, occupied by people whose whole business there is leaving.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: non-place

## Shown

Direction, and the means of following it. Corridors that only go one way, travelators, lane markings, gates numbered in sequence, the sign that says how many minutes to the next thing.
The place shows its own throughput. Whatever else is there -- shops, seats, a chapel -- is arranged along the line of movement rather than at the end of it.

## Holds

Everybody in motion at once, briefly, at a density no ordinary street sustains. It holds them for a duration set by a timetable rather than by anything they want, and it holds the arrangements that make waiting survivable: the seat, the screen, the coffee, the plug socket.
It also holds a great deal of unaccounted time, which nobody planned to spend and everybody spends.

## Offers

A route that works, and a warrant for doing nothing. The persona in transit has a licence available almost nowhere else: they are legitimately unoccupied, visibly waiting for something not in their control, and nobody can ask them to be doing anything else.
Some of the most genuinely restful hours in a working life happen here, and they are not usually described that way afterwards.

## Withheld

Any reason to be here other than not being here. The place cannot be arrived at; every version of a good outcome involves leaving it, which means nothing that happens inside can be a purpose.
So a persona who is delayed is not merely inconvenienced -- they are held in a space that offers no way to make the time count, since the space is constituted by its own being-passed-through.
