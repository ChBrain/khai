# Place: Solitude

## Taxonomy

Solitude is an expression of the non-place engine. It models what the space does to the people inside it: a crowd in which nobody is related to anybody, in a room that is nearly identical to the equivalent room in forty other cities.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: non-place

## Shown

Similarity. The concourse resembles every other concourse, the service station is the same one the persona stopped at three hundred miles ago, the hotel corridor gives no clue what country it is in.
And a crowd behaving as a crowd of individuals: everybody facing forward, nobody speaking, each attending to a device or a screen.

## Holds

Proximity without contact. The persona is closer to more strangers here than anywhere except a stadium, and the whole apparatus of ordinary co-presence -- the nod, the excuse me, the recognition -- is present only in its most minimal form.
The place holds this arrangement stably, for hours, without anybody finding it strange.

## Offers

Relief from being known. No history to maintain, no reputation in play, nobody who will draw a conclusion about the persona from what they eat at three in the afternoon or how long they sit doing nothing.
For a persona who is exhausted by being somebody, this is not emptiness but rest, and it is the reason these spaces are so often described with a kind of guilty affection.

## Withheld

Recognition, and the possibility of it. The place is arranged so that no encounter accumulates: the staff rotate, the crowd is replaced hourly, and nothing that happens leaves a mark on the room.
A persona who wanted to be seen here would have no route to it, and the same feature that rests them is the one that makes an emergency in this space so peculiarly frightening -- there is nobody present for whom they are anybody.
