# Place: Non-Place

## Taxonomy

Non-Place is a place engine (TO SHOW). It models the space built for passing through -- the airport concourse, the motorway service station, the interchange, the supermarket at eleven at night, the chain hotel corridor -- which is entered by contract, occupied in common with strangers who have no relation to each other, and which asks nothing of anybody once they are inside.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: non-place

## Shown

Everything is addressed to a person who has never been here before. Signage in several languages, pictograms, arrows on the floor, a counter with a queue barrier, seating fixed in rows facing nothing. Nothing is worn in a way that records use, and nothing indicates which decade it is.
What is shown above all is legibility to a stranger, which is the design brief and is achieved completely.

## Holds

The apparatus of passage: the barrier that reads a ticket, the screen that lists departures, the trolley that needs a coin, the till, the card reader, the camera. It also holds a very large number of people who are not connected to each other and will not be.
None of it is held for anybody in particular. The place keeps nothing on behalf of a returning visitor, because it has no concept of one.

## Offers

Passage, provisioning, and the total absence of obligation. Nobody here needs anything from the persona: no greeting is owed, no account of themselves is required, no history is known, nothing they do will be remembered by the place or by anybody in it.
For a great many people this is the offer that matters. It is the only public space in ordinary life where they are not somebody's neighbour, colleague, parent, or patient.

## Withheld

Any further claim on the persona, and any claim the persona might make on it.
The engine's twist is here. **A non-place checks who you are in order to have no further use for it.** The identity is verified precisely once, at the boundary -- ticket, passport, card, membership number -- and having been established it is discarded: inside, the persona is a passenger, a customer, a driver, and nothing else. That is exactly what makes the space restful, since being nobody in particular is a holiday from every determination a persona carries everywhere else; and it is exactly what makes it empty, since a space that cannot know anybody cannot hold anything for them, cannot recognise them on return, and will not have registered that they were ever in it.
It is not the informal gathering place with regulars and standing, which is third-place's; not the setting that refills depleted attention, which is restorative-environment's; not the threshold state between identities, which is liminality's; and not the enclosing institution that runs a whole life under one authority, which is total-institution's.
Nobody built it to be alienating. Every feature of it solves a real problem -- throughput, safety, readability to a stranger, staff who change every season -- and the indifference is a consequence rather than an intention.
