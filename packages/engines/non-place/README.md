# Non-place

The space built for passing through, which verifies a persona at the boundary and has no further use for them -- the concourse, the service station, the interchange, the supermarket at eleven at night. It is not the informal gathering place with regulars and standing, which is third-place's, and not the threshold state between identities, which is liminality's. Three expressions: transit, the route that cannot be arrived at, since every good outcome involves leaving it; the contract, an entitlement owed by a system rather than by anybody's goodwill, exact in what it covers and offering no margin outside it; and solitude, proximity without contact, which rests a persona exhausted by being somebody and leaves nobody present for whom they are anybody. The twist: a non-place checks who you are in order to have no further use for it.
