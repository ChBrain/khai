# Instructions: Non-Place

How a Playwright wires the non-place engine into a play. You wire by linking the play's own content to the engine's files; the engine files stay untouched, they are canon. The Roadie plumbs the engine's law into Instructions Knowledge on deploy. Authoring guidance, not runtime content, and it does not go on tour.

## Human

- The human keeps the place indifferent rather than hostile: nobody designed it to alienate anybody, every feature of it solves a real problem, and the engine stops working the moment the space is staged as an antagonist. Decide before the scene whether the persona is resting in it or stranded in it, because the engine supplies both from the same feature. And let the verification happen once, at the boundary, and then let nothing ask anything of them again.

## Agent

- From the play's own content, a persona links under Projection which facet they are standing in: the route that cannot be arrived at, the contract that covers exactly what it covers, or the crowd in which nobody is anybody. Read the engine on what the place stops requiring, never on how it looks. Keep it the passing-through case: the gathering place with regulars and standing is third-place's, the threshold between identities is liminality's, and the institution that runs a whole life is total-institution's. You link to the engine, you never edit it.

## Collaboration

- Neutral ground, the leveller, and the home away from home are third-place's, the dwelling composite's, and hospitality's; the suspension between identities is liminality's and role-exit's; the place that runs sleep, work, and play under one authority is total-institution's and the subjection composite's; what refills depleted attention is restorative-environment's and restoration's; the bond to a location and what it carries are place-attachment's, nostalgia's, and memory's; delay as a technique and the daily crossing are waiting's and commute's. Wayfinding standards, security regulation, retail layout, and the economics of throughput are the trade and set the format. Whether a production's persona is resting or stranded, and whether the staff are part of the place or exceptions to it, are the team's review.

## Knowledge

- A non-place is the space built for passing through: the airport concourse, the motorway service station, the interchange, the supermarket at eleven at night, the chain hotel corridor. It is entered by contract rather than by membership, occupied in common with strangers who have no relation to each other, and its good outcome for everybody present is leaving it. It is not third-place's informal gathering place with regulars and standing, not liminality's threshold state, and not total-institution's enclosing authority. It wires on a persona at Projection, in three facets. Transit is the route that cannot be arrived at, so nothing happening inside can be a purpose -- and it grants a licence available almost nowhere else, to be legitimately unoccupied and visibly waiting for something outside one's control, which produces some of the most genuinely restful hours in a working life. The contract offers entitlement without acquaintance: a seat owed by a system rather than by anybody's goodwill, which is exactly what makes the space usable by strangers, and which leaves a persona whose circumstances fall outside the terms with nobody to appeal to, since the relation was never with a person. Solitude is proximity without contact, held stably for hours, which rests a persona exhausted by being somebody. The engine's twist is in the anchor: a non-place checks who you are in order to have no further use for it -- the identity is verified exactly once, at the boundary, and then discarded, which is what makes the space restful and what makes it unable to hold anything for anybody.

## System

- Do check the persona at the boundary and then stop. Ticket, card, passport, membership number, and after that nobody asks them anything at all.
- Do make the design good. The signage works, the seats are adequate, the coffee is fine, and the space does its job for a stranger who has never been there.
- Do stage the rest before the emptiness. Somebody is enjoying being nobody, and they are not wrong to.
- Do let the same feature turn. The persona who was resting becomes the persona who is stranded, and nothing about the room has changed.
- Do keep the crowd close and unrelated. More strangers within a metre than anywhere except a stadium, and the whole apparatus of acknowledgement reduced to its minimum.
- Do put somebody outside the terms. Their circumstances are real, the contract does not cover them, and there is nobody present with the discretion to help.
- Do let it be somewhere the persona has been forty times and does not count as having been.
- Do not model the gathering place with regulars (third-place, dwelling, hospitality), the threshold state (liminality, role-exit), the enclosing institution (total-institution, subjection), attention restored (restorative-environment), the bond to a location (place-attachment, nostalgia), or the wait itself (waiting, commute). Do not make the concourse stand for a theme; that is authored key, the play's pitch. Do not edit the engine's files; wire only from the play's side.
