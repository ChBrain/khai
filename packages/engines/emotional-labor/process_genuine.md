# Process: Genuine

## Taxonomy

[Emotional Labor](process_emotional_labor.md), the spontaneous form.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: emotional-labor

## Initiated by

The required emotion is simply felt -- the nurse's compassion, the host's delight, the mourner's grief arise on their own, and the display the role demands needs no acting because the feeling is already there.
This is the genuine form: the part and the heart aligned without effort, the labor dissolving into sincerity.
The persona shows what they feel and feels what the role asks, and the two are one.

## Direction

Toward a display that is owed and also true, with no gap to close.
The genuine form meets the display rule by coincidence of feeling: the emotion the role prescribes is the emotion the persona actually has, so the expression flows from the state rather than being laid over it or worked up toward it.
The direction is a collapse of the distance between felt and shown -- not bridged, as in masking, nor built, as in method, but absent, because the required feeling was present from the start.

## Lever

The alignment of role and feeling, spending no management at all.
The genuine form runs on fit: when the persona's own affect already matches what the part demands -- the caregiver who cares, the greeter who is glad -- there is no dissonance to hold and no feeling to induce, and the display costs only what any sincere expression costs.
The lever is the match itself: the more a role is filled by someone whose real feeling suits it, the more the emotional labor disappears into ordinary emotion.

## Echo

The display is sincere, and the persona pays little for it.
It is the cheapest form to the self and the most convincing to the other -- no leak to guard, no self to divide -- and the reason the fit between a person and an emotionally demanding role matters so much: the labor is lightest where the heart already holds what the part requires.
It is what [masking](process_masking.md) counterfeits at the surface and [method](process_method.md) works to approximate from within. The spontaneous emotion it rests on is the emotion engine's; this owns the case where that emotion happens to meet the role's display rule, and the labor vanishes into it.
