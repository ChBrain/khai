# Process: Masking

## Taxonomy

[Emotional Labor](process_emotional_labor.md), the surface form.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: emotional-labor

## Initiated by

The persona shows the required emotion without feeling it -- the smile painted on over boredom or irritation, the display managed at the surface while the inner state is left untouched.
This is masking: surface acting, the expression put on like a costume over a feeling it does not match.
What is shown and what is felt are pulled apart, and held apart by effort.

## Direction

Toward a convincing display laid over an unchanged, contrary feeling.
Masking works only on the outside: the face, the voice, the posture are shaped to the display rule while the emotion beneath stays as it was, so the persona manages the gap between a false front and a true state rather than closing it.
The direction is a holding of the surface against the depth -- the pleasant mask kept steady over the annoyance or fatigue that continues, unfelt by the customer and unrelieved in the worker.

## Lever

The separation of expression from feeling, held by vigilance.
Masking spends attention and control: because the shown and the felt diverge, the persona must monitor the leak -- the flat tone, the tired eyes, the smile that slips -- and keep suppressing what is real while performing what is not, a effortful modulation of the response alone.
The lever is the maintained divergence itself: the mask holds only as long as the persona spends the effort to hold it, and the strain is the price of the gap left open.

## Echo

The display convinces, and the persona is worn and estranged by the holding.
It is the costliest form to the self -- the emotional dissonance between a face and a feeling breeds exhaustion and burnout, and a self long divided from its own expression grows unsure which is real -- the mask serves the role while taxing the wearer.
It is [method](process_method.md)'s shallower cousin, working the surface where method works the depth, and the estrangement [genuine](process_genuine.md) is spared. The exhaustion the dissonance drives toward is the emotional-labor cost the burnout of the caregiving engine also names; here it is the specific strain of the held mask. This owns surface acting: the display managed over an unchanged feeling.
