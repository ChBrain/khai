# Process: Method

## Taxonomy

[Emotional Labor](process_emotional_labor.md), the deep form.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: emotional-labor

## Initiated by

The persona works themselves into actually feeling the required emotion -- summoning a memory, adopting a frame, reappraising the moment -- so that the display, when it comes, comes from a feeling made real.
This is method: deep acting, the emotion induced on purpose the way a method actor conjures grief from their own remembered loss.
The persona changes not the face but the heart, so the two need not be forced apart.

## Direction

Toward a genuinely felt emotion, produced to meet the role's demand.
Method reaches inward before it shows outward: the persona works up the required feeling -- imagining the rude customer as harried rather than hostile, recalling what makes the care sincere -- and lets the true display follow the manufactured feeling, closing the gap by moving the depth rather than holding the surface.
The direction is a self-induction: the feeling is not present at the start and is not faked at the end, but built in between by the persona's own effort on themselves.

## Lever

The techniques of self-induction, spent to feel to order.
Method spends the persona's imaginative and reappraising work: it recruits the antecedent moves of regulation -- reframing the situation, attending differently, calling up an apt memory -- and turns them to the role's end, so the required emotion is generated rather than mimicked.
The lever is the malleability of feeling itself: because an emotion can be worked up by how a situation is construed, the persona construes it into being, and the display rides out on a real, if deliberately made, feeling.

## Echo

The emotion is felt and shown as one, and the persona is spared the dissonance masking pays.
It is the kinder form to the self -- deep acting costs the effort of induction but not the strain of division, and can even yield a sense of authentic accomplishment where surface acting yields only depletion -- though the practice of feeling to order can, over time, blur what the persona feels of their own accord.
It is [masking](process_masking.md)'s deeper counterpart, moving the feeling where masking holds the face, and the route by which labor approaches [genuine](process_genuine.md) without quite being it. The reappraisal and situation-construal it borrows are the regulation engine's, turned here to a role's display rule; this owns deep acting: the feeling induced to meet the part.
