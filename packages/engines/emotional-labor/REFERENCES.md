# Emotional Labor: Reference

## Line of Work

Emotional labor as **process**: the effort a persona spends to feel or feign the emotion a role demands they display -- managing the heart to meet a display rule, for a wage or a part. The domain does not model the persona's own raw emotions, which are the emotion engine's; nor the general self-directed regulation of feeling for one's own ends, which is regulation's; nor the hidden region where the performance is dropped, which is backstage's; nor the self-presentation of competence and image, which is face's; nor the manipulation of another for gain, which is deception's. It models the managing of feeling to meet a role's prescribed display: the gap between the felt and the required, and the work of closing it.

The spine is Hochschild's sociology of managed feeling. Some roles require the worker to produce a particular emotional display as part of the job -- the flight attendant's warmth, the debt collector's edge -- and to do so the worker performs emotional labor, managing their feeling to fit the display rule. The labor is done in more than one way: by surface acting, painting the display on over an unfelt or contrary emotion; by deep acting, working oneself into actually feeling the required emotion; and, where the required feeling is spontaneously present, by expressing it genuinely with no acting at all. The strategies differ in their cost to the self, surface acting the most estranging. The three forms are those: masking, method, genuine.

## Restrictions

What the engine refuses to model, and to whom it delegates.

- **Emotion**: The engine models the management of feeling for a role's display, not the feeling itself. The persona's own raw joy, anger, fear, and the rest are owned by the emotion engine; emotional labor takes those emotions as the material it masks, induces, or -- in the genuine form -- happens to be handed, and owns the managing, not the emotion managed.
- **Regulation**: The engine models the role-and-display-driven management of feeling, not the general self-directed regulation of it. Gross's regulation -- selecting the situation, reframing its meaning, holding back its expression for one's own ends -- is owned by the regulation engine; emotional labor recruits those very moves (deep acting borrows reappraisal, surface acting borrows suppression) but bends them to a role's prescribed display for an audience, and owns that occupational, other-directed performance of feeling.
- **Backstage and Face**: The engine models the emotional performance itself, not the region where it is dropped nor the image it protects. The hidden back region where the front is prepared and released is owned by the backstage engine; the self-presentation of competence, honor, and standing is owned by the face engine; emotional labor is the managed feeling shown in the front region, distinct from where it rests and from the image it serves.
- **Deception**: The engine models a sanctioned role-performance, not manipulation for gain. The strategic misleading of another to exploit them is owned by the deception engine; the masking of surface acting is an openly institutional display -- the expected smile, not a con -- and owns the role-required feigning of feeling, not the deceit that works another for advantage.

## Encoding

Source to constraint, per file.

- **[emotional labor](process_emotional_labor.md)** (the root): The managing of feeling to meet a role's display rule -- the required emotion produced by effort, the gap between the felt and the shown closed for a wage or a part. Anchored by Hochschild (the managed heart) and Ekman & Friesen (display rules).
- **[masking](process_masking.md)** (form): Surface acting -- the display painted on over an unfelt or contrary emotion, felt and shown held apart by effort, the costliest form to the self. Anchored by Hochschild (surface acting) and Grandey (surface acting as response modulation).
- **[method](process_method.md)** (form): Deep acting -- the persona working themselves into actually feeling the required emotion, the display riding out on a feeling induced to order. Anchored by Hochschild (deep acting) and Grandey (deep acting as antecedent reappraisal).
- **[genuine](process_genuine.md)** (form): The spontaneous case -- the required emotion already felt, so the display is sincere and the labor vanishes into ordinary emotion. Anchored by Ashforth & Humphrey (genuine emotion) and Diefendorff et al. (expression of naturally felt emotion).
