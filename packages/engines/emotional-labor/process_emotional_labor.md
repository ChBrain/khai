# Process: Emotional Labor

## Taxonomy

Emotion / the social psychology of managed feeling.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: emotional-labor

## Initiated by

A persona's role requires a particular emotion be displayed -- the served greeted with a smile, the patient met with calm, the bereaved attended with gravity -- and the persona spends effort to show it, and sometimes to feel it, whether or not it arises on its own.
This is emotional labor: the managing of feeling to meet a display rule, the heart put to work for a wage or a part.
The emotion is not merely had; it is produced to specification, because the role has made it a duty.

## Direction

Toward the display the role prescribes, met by whatever inner work that takes.
Emotional labor aims at the required expression and works backward to supply it: where the feeling is present, it need only be shown; where it is absent or wrong, it must be painted on over the truth or summoned into being.
The direction is set by the display rule, not by the persona's own state -- the smile is owed whether or not there is warmth behind it, so the labor bridges whatever gap lies between what is felt and what must be shown.

## Lever

The gap between the felt and the required, closed by effort.
What emotional labor spends is the persona's own affect and energy: the wider the distance between the emotion that is present and the one the role demands, the more work it takes to close it, whether by holding a false face steady or by manufacturing a true feeling to order.
The lever is the display rule backed by the role's authority -- the job, the part, the office -- which makes the prescribed emotion not a choice but an obligation, and its production labor rather than expression.

## Echo

The role's emotion is delivered, and the persona pays for the delivery.
Emotional labor is why the smile can be exhausting, why the gap between a feeling faked and a self held estranges the worker from their own heart, why the induced feeling is kinder to the persona than the merely masked one -- the management of emotion for a role has a cost, and the cost differs by how it is done.
It runs as [masking](process_masking.md), [method](process_method.md), or [genuine](process_genuine.md) -- the surface held over the unfelt, the feeling induced to order, the emotion that needs no inducing. The persona's own raw emotions are the emotion engine's; the general self-directed regulation of feeling for one's own ends is the regulation engine's; the hidden region where the performance is dropped is the backstage engine's. This engine owns the managing of feeling to meet a role's display rule itself.
