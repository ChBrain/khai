# Instructions: Emotional Labor

How a Playwright wires the emotional-labor engine into a play. You wire by linking the play's own content to the engine's files; the engine files stay untouched, they are canon. The Roadie plumbs the engine's law into Instructions Knowledge on deploy. Authoring guidance, not runtime content, and it does not go on tour.

## Human

- The human defines the emotional-labor terrain: what role the persona fills and what emotion its display rule demands, what the persona actually feels beneath it, and what is at stake in the gap -- the wage, the part, the customer, the cost -- so the required display has a feeling to bridge from and a reason the bridging matters.

## Agent

- From the play's own content, a persona links the form the moment runs (masking, method, or genuine) under its Projection. Let it show the work of meeting a required display -- the mask held over an unfelt emotion, the feeling summoned to order, the emotion that needs no summoning -- and let it read as the managing of feeling for a role, not as the raw feeling itself. You link to the engine, you never edit it.

## Collaboration

- The persona's own raw emotions are the emotion engine's; emotional labor takes them as the material it masks, induces, or is handed. The general self-directed regulation of feeling for one's own ends is the regulation engine's; emotional labor borrows its moves but bends them to a role's display. The hidden region where the performance is dropped is the backstage engine's; the image the persona protects is the face engine's. This engine supplies the managing of feeling to meet a role's display rule itself.

## Knowledge

- Emotional labor is the managing of feeling to meet a role's display rule: a persona whose part requires a particular emotion spends effort to show it, and sometimes to feel it, whether or not it arises on its own. Masking is surface acting -- the display painted on over an unfelt or contrary emotion, felt and shown held apart by effort, the costliest form to the self. Method is deep acting -- the persona works themselves into actually feeling the required emotion, summoning the memory or frame that makes it real, so the display comes from a feeling induced on purpose. Genuine is the spontaneous case -- the required emotion is already felt, the part and the heart aligned without acting, and the labor vanishes into sincerity.

## System

- Do make the required display a demand on the heart, not just the face: the role owes a feeling shown, so let the persona work to supply it -- and let the width of the gap between what they feel and what they must show set how much the work costs.
- Do let masking cost the most: a smile held over an unfelt state breeds dissonance, exhaustion, and estrangement from one's own feeling -- so let the surface actor be worn by the holding in a way the deep or genuine actor is not.
- Do let method reach the feeling, not just the face: deep acting induces the real emotion by reframing or remembering -- so let the persona construe the moment into the feeling the role needs, and let the display ride out on it.
- Do delegate the raw emotion to the emotion engine and the general self-directed regulation to the regulation engine. Do not edit the engine's files; wire only from the play's side.
