# Process: Authenticity

## Taxonomy

Self / living and acting from one's true self.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: authenticity

## Initiated by

A persona acts, chooses, and shows from its own values, feelings, and convictions -- not from external pressure, nor from behind a false front.
This is authenticity (Kernis & Goldman; Wood et al.): the alignment of the outward life with the inward truth, the self one lives matching the self one is.
Its lineage is Rogers' congruence -- the fully functioning person whose expression matches their experience -- and Deci and Ryan's true self, acting from one's own reasons rather than another's.

## Direction

Outward from the true self -- what is felt and valued within, lived and shown without.
Authenticity closes the distance between the inner self and the presented one: the persona speaks its own mind, acts on its own values, and lets its close others see the real thing, so the shown self is the felt self and not a performance of it.
Its direction is the true self made visible, expression matched to experience.

## Lever

The gap between the felt and the shown -- and the pull to close it with a false self rather than a true one.
What authenticity spends is the safety of the mask: the persona could conform, please, or perform the self expected of it, and authenticity forgoes that cover, acting from its own truth at the risk of friction, rejection, or exposure.
It fires on the same occasions that tempt a false front -- pressure to fit, to impress, to avoid conflict -- and answers them by living from the self rather than around it.

## Echo

The life is lived truly, and the self is whole across its inside and its outside -- what the persona shows costs no split from what it is.
Its lack is self-alienation: the false self worn until the true one is lost track of, the life run on accepting external influence until the persona no longer knows its own wanting (Winnicott's false self; Harter's false-self behavior).
It runs as [awareness](process_awareness.md), [congruence](process_congruence.md), and [transparency](process_transparency.md), tuned by a standing [authentic](position_authentic.md) disposition. The situational tuning of self-presentation to fit the room is the self-monitoring engine's; the revealing of personal information is the self-disclosure engine's; the content of who one is, the self-concept, is the identity engine's; the managed emotional display of a work role is the emotional-labor engine's. This engine owns living and acting from the true self.
