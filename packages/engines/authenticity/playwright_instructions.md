# Instructions: Authenticity

How a Playwright wires the authenticity engine into a play. You wire by linking the play's own content to the engine's files; the engine files stay untouched, they are canon. The Roadie plumbs the engine's law into Instructions Knowledge on deploy. Authoring guidance, not runtime content, and it does not go on tour.

## Human

- The human defines the authenticity terrain: what the persona actually values and feels, and what pressures push against living from it -- to conform, please, perform, or advance -- so a moment holds a true self and a temptation to a false front.

## Agent

- From the play's own content, a persona links the authenticity a moment runs under its Projection: which components are present or absent -- the self-knowledge, the congruent action, the relational openness -- and how authentic its standing disposition is. Let it read as the match between the felt self and the shown one, and its lack as the gap between them, the false front worn under pressure. You link to the engine, you never edit it.

## Collaboration

- The situational tuning of how one comes across, without betraying the self beneath, is the self-monitoring engine's; the revealing of personal information is the self-disclosure engine's; the content of who one is, the self-concept, is the identity engine's; the managed emotional display of a work role is the emotional-labor engine's; the judgment of the self's worth is the self-esteem engine's; the compassionate stance toward the suffering self is the self-compassion engine's. This engine supplies the alignment of the shown self with the felt one -- the self-knowledge, the congruent action, and the relational genuineness of living from the true self.

## Knowledge

- Authenticity is living and acting from one's true self: the persona's outward life -- what it says, chooses, and shows -- aligned with its inward truth, its own values, feelings, and convictions, rather than driven by external pressure or held behind a false front (Kernis & Goldman; Rogers' congruence). It runs in three components. Awareness is self-knowledge -- knowing one's own motives, feelings, and values clearly and without distortion, the ground the rest stands on. Congruence is authentic action -- behaving in accord with the true self rather than to please, perform, or gain reward, the match of expression to experience. Transparency is relational authenticity -- being genuine and open in close relationships, the real self shown to intimates without a false front. Its trait runs from the reliably authentic to the self-alienated, whose life is shaped more by others' expectations than its own truth.

## System

- Do keep authenticity the deep alignment, not the surface style: it is the shown self matching the felt one, whether or not the persona is watched -- let it read in the match between what the persona holds and what it does, not in how polished its presentation is.
- Do distinguish it from self-monitoring: a persona can tune how it comes across to fit the room and still act from its own values, or hold one manner everywhere while alienated from itself -- let tuning the surface and aligning the depth come apart.
- Do stage the three components and their failures: the self-knowledge or its self-serving distortion, the congruent act or the compliant betrayal, the relational openness or the front kept up even with intimates -- and let genuine authenticity cost something, the safety of the mask forgone.
- Do let self-alienation be a real state, not a label: the false self worn until the persona loses track of its own wanting. Do delegate the content of the self to the identity engine and the situational presentation to the self-monitoring engine. Do not edit the engine's files; wire only from the play's side.
