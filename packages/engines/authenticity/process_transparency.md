# Process: Transparency

## Taxonomy

[Authenticity](process_authenticity.md), the third component -- relational authenticity, the real self shown to close others.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: authenticity

## Initiated by

The persona lets those close to it see the real thing -- open and genuine in its close ties, without a false front held up even to intimates.
This is authenticity's relational component (Kernis & Goldman's relational orientation): valuing and achieving openness and truthfulness in one's close relationships, so that the self others meet there is the true self, not a managed version of it.
It is authenticity turned toward others -- being genuine with them, not only within oneself.

## Direction

Toward the close other -- the true self made visible where it can be seen and known.
Transparency lowers the front with those who matter: the persona lets its real feelings, doubts, and convictions show to intimates rather than performing a curated self even there, so the relationship runs on the genuine person and not a presented one.
Its direction is the real self offered into the close tie.

## Lever

What transparency spends -- the safety of the front, even with those closest.
A managed self-presentation is a shield, and it is tempting to keep it up even in intimacy, where being truly seen risks rejection of the real thing rather than the performed one; transparency spends that shield, letting the close other see the genuine self and bear the exposure that comes with it.
A production holds this lever by letting the persona be genuinely seen by an intimate -- the real feeling shown, the front lowered -- at some risk, rather than performing even in private.

## Echo

The close other meets the real self, and the relationship runs on the genuine person -- the deepest ties held by who the persona actually is, not by a front it must maintain.
Its failure is the false front kept up even with intimates, the self performed until no one, sometimes not even the persona, meets the real thing.
The mere disclosure of personal facts is the self-disclosure engine's -- transparency is the wider genuineness of showing the real self, not only revealing information about it; joined to [awareness](process_awareness.md) that knows the self and [congruence](process_congruence.md) that acts it, it completes the alignment across the persona's inner life, its actions, and its close relationships.
