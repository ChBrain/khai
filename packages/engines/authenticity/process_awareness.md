# Process: Awareness

## Taxonomy

[Authenticity](process_authenticity.md), the first component -- self-knowledge, clear and undistorted.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: authenticity

## Initiated by

The persona knows its own motives, feelings, values, and desires -- and knows them without denying, distorting, or exaggerating what it finds.
This is authenticity's first component (Kernis & Goldman's awareness and unbiased processing, taken together): the clear self-knowledge that is the ground authentic living stands on -- one cannot act from a true self one has not seen.
It is the honest inward look, the persona's access to what it actually wants and feels.

## Direction

Inward, toward the true self -- to know it as it is.
Awareness turns the persona's attention onto its own motives and feelings and holds them in view without flinching or flattering: the unwelcome wish acknowledged, the real reason admitted, the feeling named rather than managed away.
Its direction is the undistorted self-view, the precondition for everything downstream.

## Lever

What awareness spends -- the comfort of not looking, and the flattering distortions that guard it.
Self-knowledge is effortful and often unwelcome; the persona could leave its motives unexamined or read them through a self-serving filter, and awareness spends that comfort, seeing the true self clearly even where the view is unflattering.
A production holds this lever by letting the persona actually know something true and difficult about itself -- a real motive, a real feeling -- rather than a convenient self-image, so the awareness reads as honest access, not self-flattery.

## Echo

The self is seen, and the raw material of authenticity is in hand -- there is now a true self to act from and to show.
Without it the other components have nothing to align to: [congruence](process_congruence.md) cannot match action to a self unknown, and [transparency](process_transparency.md) cannot reveal one.
The distorted, self-serving self-view that awareness works against is the ordinary default the bias engine catalogs; awareness is the clear seeing deliberately held against it, the honest knowledge the rest of authenticity requires.
