# Process: Congruence

## Taxonomy

[Authenticity](process_authenticity.md), the second component -- authentic action, expression matched to the true self.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: authenticity

## Initiated by

The persona acts in accord with the self it knows -- behaving from its own values and needs rather than to please, perform, or gain a reward.
This is authenticity's behavioral component (Kernis & Goldman), and Rogers' congruence: the match between what the persona experiences within and what it does and expresses without.
It is authenticity made act -- the true self not merely known but lived.

## Direction

From the true self into action -- the value enacted, the conviction followed, the felt thing done.
Congruence carries self-knowledge across into behavior: the persona does what accords with its values even when another course would be easier or better received, so its actions read out its real self rather than the expected one.
Its direction is the felt self expressed in the done thing.

## Lever

What congruence spends -- the rewards of acting against the self.
Conforming, pleasing, and performing all pay -- in approval, in ease, in advantage -- and congruence forgoes those payments, acting from the true self at the cost of the rewards a false compliance would earn.
A production holds this lever by putting the persona's values against an incentive to betray them, and letting the congruent persona act on the value and pay the cost -- so the authenticity is tested by what it gives up, not merely asserted.

## Echo

The action reads out the true self, and the persona's outside matches its inside -- integrity in the literal sense, a self not split between what it holds and what it does.
Its failure is incongruence: acting against one's values under pressure, the compliance that, repeated, is the self-alienation the root warns of.
The situational adjusting of how one comes across, without betraying the self beneath, is the self-monitoring engine's -- one can tune presentation and still act congruently; congruence is the deeper match of deed to value, joined to [awareness](process_awareness.md) that supplies the value and [transparency](process_transparency.md) that shows it to others.
