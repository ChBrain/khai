# Position: Authentic

## Taxonomy

Self / the trait of dispositional authenticity -- the standing readiness beneath [Authenticity](process_authenticity.md) (Wood et al.'s Authenticity Scale; Kernis & Goldman).

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: authenticity

## Has

A standing disposition to live from the true self rather than a false one.
Trait authenticity, as against the state of any one moment: at the high end, a persona reliably self-knowing, congruent, and open, whose life runs on its own values; at the low end, one given to self-alienation and accepting external influence, its life shaped more by others' expectations than its own truth.
It tracks the standing balance of the three components against their failures -- self-knowledge over self-ignorance, congruent action over compliance, relational openness over the maintained front.

## Orders

Run from here, into every choice and tie.
The highly authentic persona meets a pressure to conform already tilted toward its own values -- it consults what it wants, acts on it, shows it; the self-alienated persona meets the same pressure tilted toward the expected, reading others' wants before its own.
It sets the default weighting of the true self against external influence, before any deliberate stand.

## Loses

At the high end, the cover and ease the false self buys; at the low end, the self itself.
The authentic persona forgoes the safety of the mask, meeting friction, rejection, and exposure it could have avoided by performing the expected self; the self-alienated persona buys that safety at the price of a life not its own -- fitting in, pleasing, and advancing while losing track of what it actually wants.
What each trades is the other's good -- cover against wholeness, across the same standing tilt.

## Drives

On that tilt:
rides the disposition -- the authentic persona keeps living from its own truth, the alienated from others' expectations -- or works against it, the alienated persona learning to consult and act on its own values, the authentic learning where a front is the kinder or wiser course than raw genuineness.
The threshold holds underneath either way, a resting level the persona cultivates or neglects rather than a state that merely comes and goes.
