# Position: Ambivalence

## Taxonomy

Evaluative structure / opposed evaluations co-held toward one object (no khai file of its own).

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: ambivalence

## Has

A single object held with a strong positive and a strong negative evaluation at once.
Not a lukewarm midpoint on one scale but two full charges together: Bleuler, who coined the word, and Freud, who made it central, saw it plainest in love and hate borne toward the same person; the modern evaluative-space model shows how it is possible -- positivity and negativity are separable dimensions, so a persona can be high on both, not merely middling on one.
It is distinct at both ends from a weak attitude: the indifferent persona is low on both, the ambivalent one high on both, and the two feel nothing alike though a single bipolar scale would score them the same.

## Orders

Hold both charges toward the one object, without cancelling either.
Ambivalence orders the persona to keep the positive and the negative both alive toward the same thing -- to love and resent, want and dread, admire and despise the one object -- rather than resolving to a net verdict. The room reads two full evaluations at once beneath the persona's stance, not an average; the object draws approach and avoidance together.
It sets a divided valuation the persona carries whole.

## Loses

The ease of a settled, single-signed attitude.
Holding both charges, the persona forgoes the clarity of simply liking or disliking -- it cannot act on the object without acting against half of its own evaluation, is slower and more effortful in deciding, and is destabilized when the conflict is forced into the open. The very fullness of both sides is what costs it: a weak attitude would not tear.
What it trades is the comfort of a made-up mind for a truer, richer, but divided relation to the object, and pays in the strain that division can become.

## Drives

On that co-activated evaluation:
holds in one of two forms -- [potential](position_potential.md), the structural conflict genuinely present whether or not it is felt; or [felt](position_felt.md), the subjective tension of being torn, flaring when both sides are brought to mind or a choice forces them.
The divided valuation holds beneath either way. The discomfort of inconsistent cognitions that pushes toward change is the dissonance engine's; the specific affective ambivalences toward a particular object -- love and hate together, joy and sorrow together -- are their own composites, the love-hate and bittersweet composites being instances of the structure this engine names. This engine owns the co-activation of opposed evaluations itself, and its two forms.
