# Position: Felt

## Taxonomy

[Ambivalence](position_ambivalence.md), the conflict as experienced -- the tension of being torn.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: ambivalence

## Has

A specific form of [ambivalence](position_ambivalence.md): the subjective, experienced kind -- the felt discomfort of being pulled two ways at once toward the one object, the sense of wanting and not wanting, of being torn.
This is ambivalence as it is lived rather than merely held: the strain, the unease, the mixed feeling the persona actually undergoes. It does not simply track the [potential](position_potential.md) conflict beneath it -- a persona can carry a large structural ambivalence and feel little of it until something calls it up. Felt ambivalence flares when both sides are brought to mind at once, when the object is made salient, or when a choice forces the two charges together (Newby-Clark, McGregor & Zanna).
It is the conflict arrived in awareness, the divided attitude the persona is presently paying for.

## Orders

Feel the pull of both, at once, as strain.
The felt form orders the persona to undergo the conflict -- to experience the tension of the two opposed charges rather than merely hold them -- so that the room reads not a settled stance but a persona torn, uneasy, wanting and recoiling in the same breath. It surfaces when the two sides are drawn together: made salient, brought to mind, or forced by a decision.
It sets the lived discomfort of the divided attitude into the persona's present experience of the object.

## Loses

The composure of a conflict that stays quiet.
Feeling both charges at once, the persona forgoes ease: it pays the discomfort the [potential](position_potential.md) form defers, is slowed and unsettled by the pull, and can be driven to resolve the strain -- to reappraise, to avoid the object, to force a verdict -- simply to be rid of it. The felt tension is costly in the moment in a way the latent conflict is not.
What it trades is present comfort for the honesty of the experienced division -- it feels the whole of its own opposed stance rather than holding it numb.

## Drives

On that experienced conflict:
flares into felt tension -- the discomfort of being torn -- when both sides are brought to mind at once, the object made salient, or a choice forces the two charges together; and subsides back toward the latent [potential](position_potential.md) conflict when they are apart.
The felt form is one of two, the experienced tension, set against the present-but-latent [potential](position_potential.md) conflict it does not simply track. That the two opposed charges are co-activated toward a single object is the [anchor's](position_ambivalence.md); the discomfort of inconsistent cognitions that pushes toward change -- a different thing, though it too is felt -- is the dissonance engine's.
