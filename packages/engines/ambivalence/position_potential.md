# Position: Potential

## Taxonomy

[Ambivalence](position_ambivalence.md), the structural conflict present whether or not it is felt.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: ambivalence

## Has

A specific form of [ambivalence](position_ambivalence.md): the objective, structural kind -- both a strong positive and a strong negative evaluation genuinely held toward the object, present in the persona whether or not it is, at this moment, feeling the strain.
This is ambivalence as a fact about the persona's evaluations rather than an experience: the two opposed charges are there, latent, ready. Its magnitude is a matter of how intense the two sides are and how close in strength -- greatest when both are strong and evenly matched (Thompson, Zanna & Griffin).
It can sit quiet for long stretches, a divided attitude the persona is not currently torn by but genuinely holds.

## Orders

Hold both evaluations, present and ready, felt or not.
The potential form orders the persona to carry the two opposed charges as a standing fact of its stance toward the object -- to genuinely value and genuinely disvalue it -- without requiring that the conflict be experienced at any given moment. The ambivalence is real and measurable in the persona's evaluations even while it lies dormant.
It sets the latent divided attitude beneath the persona's dealings with the object.

## Loses

Nothing yet felt -- and that is its character.
Because the conflict is structural rather than experienced, the persona forgoes, for now, the discomfort the [felt](position_felt.md) form brings: it holds both charges without paying the tension, so long as the two are not brought together in mind. But it carries the vulnerability -- the strain is latent, ready to be triggered, and the stronger and more balanced the two sides, the more it will cost when it is.
What it trades is present ease for a stored conflict that a salient reminder or a forced choice can ignite.

## Drives

On that structural conflict:
holds quiet -- both strong evaluations present and ready, an objectively divided attitude the persona is not currently torn by -- or is brought to life as [felt](position_felt.md) ambivalence when both sides are drawn to mind at once or a decision forces them together.
The potential form is one of two, the present-but-latent conflict, set against the [felt](position_felt.md) experience of it. That the two opposed charges are co-activated toward a single object is the [anchor's](position_ambivalence.md); the discomfort of inconsistent cognitions, a different thing, is the dissonance engine's.
