# Process: The Manners

## Taxonomy

[The Order](process_order_meal.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: meal

## Initiated by

The body is doing something animal in front of witnesses.
Manners start where chewing, swallowing, reaching, and appetite become visible to other people, which at a table is continuously.

## Direction

Toward a persona managing their own creatureliness in public.
Table manners are the densest set of behavioural rules a culture applies to any activity, they are taught earlier than almost anything else, and their content is largely the suppression of evidence that eating is what it is.
A persona who has internalised them experiences their violation as revulsion rather than as a breach of a rule, which is how they were installed.

## Lever

The kindness of the rules against what the rules also do.
The carrying force is that manners are consideration made routine -- they exist so that eating near other people is pleasant, they protect the person sitting next to you, and somebody who dismisses them is usually just imposing; the resistance is that they are also the most efficient class marker in existence, acquired only in childhood, unteachable to an adult without humiliation, and read instantly by anybody who already has them.
The Manners protect the table and sort the room in one motion.

## Echo

A persona placed by how they hold a fork.
It shows the meal's ordinary politeness doing work nobody at the table intends.
The Manners are dated from the age of three and are never entirely lost.
