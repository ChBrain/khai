# Process: The Belonging

## Taxonomy

[The Bond](process_bond_meal.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: meal

## Initiated by

A persona counts as one of the people who were there.
Not as a member of anything named, and not by anybody's decision -- simply as somebody the others now class with themselves.

## Direction

Toward a category with no name and real effects.
Having eaten with somebody changes how they are treated afterwards: more benefit of the doubt, faster agreement, a lower threshold for a favour. It shows up in strangers made to eat together and in negotiators sharing a plate, and the people it happens to attribute it to the other party having turned out to be reasonable.
The category survives long absences and is maintained by nothing.

## Lever

The mildness of the effect against how much it decides.
The carrying force is that it is small, benign, and one of the few social effects that is straightforwardly good news: people who have eaten together are kinder to each other, which is an improvement on the alternative and costs a sandwich; the resistance is that the same mildness makes it invisible, so the persona reads a shift in their own judgement as a discovery about the other person, and the meal that produced it is nowhere among the reasons they would give.
The Belonging is felt as an accurate assessment.

## Echo

Somebody who now gets the benefit of the doubt.
It shows the meal's product arriving disguised as an opinion.
The Belonging is dated from the meal and is credited to the person.
