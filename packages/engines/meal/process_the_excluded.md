# Process: The Excluded

## Taxonomy

[The Table](process_the_table.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: meal

## Initiated by

Somebody is not asked, and finds out.
The exclusion does not have to be enacted, announced, or even intended. It is constituted by the meal having happened.

## Direction

Toward a statement nobody made and nobody can retract.
There is no way to hold a meal that does not exclude, because a table has edges, and there is no form of words that undoes it afterwards: an explanation offered later confirms that the absence was noticed and needed accounting for.
The sharpest boundaries any society draws are commensal ones -- who may not eat with whom -- and they are not experienced as rules but as something closer to revulsion, which is why they outlast the beliefs that produced them.

## Lever

The necessity of an edge against the totality of what the edge says.
The carrying force is that exclusion here is mostly arithmetic rather than judgement: rooms have sizes, evenings have limits, and a host who invited everybody they had not decided against would be feeding a hundred people; the resistance is that a meal is the medium in which a society states its separations most completely, so the arithmetic and the verdict arrive in the same envelope and the recipient cannot tell them apart.
The Excluded were told something exact by an event they did not attend.

## Echo

A boundary in force.
It shows why commensal rules are the last ones to fall and why they are felt as taste rather than as law.
The Excluded is dated from the evening itself and is learned about afterwards.
