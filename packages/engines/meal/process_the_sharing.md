# Process: The Sharing

## Taxonomy

[Meal](process_meal.md), the co-ingestion, and how it does the work.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: meal

## Initiated by

The same food goes into different people.
The Sharing starts at the physical fact the engine turns on: [the same substance](process_the_same_substance.md) entering everybody, [the dividing](process_the_dividing.md) of one quantity among several, and [the passing](process_the_passing.md) of it hand to hand.

## Direction

Toward people who have been made, briefly and literally, of the same material.
Eating is the most complete thing a person can do with an object: it crosses the boundary of the body and becomes the body. Two people who eat from the same dish have done that with the same object.
No agreement, contract, oath, or declaration reaches as far, which is why the mechanism does not require anybody to believe in it.

## Lever

The plainness of the act against the size of what it accomplishes.
The carrying force is that nothing is happening except lunch, the participants would be right to say so, and an account that makes co-ingestion mystical has mistaken the engine for a religion; the resistance is that the effect does not depend on the account, since people who have eaten together treat each other differently afterwards -- in negotiations, in courts, in kitchens -- and it shows up in people who would deny that the food had anything to do with it.
The Sharing works on nobody's belief.

## Echo

A bond made out of a physical fact.
It is the movement that explains why a meal is not merely an occasion for talk.
The Sharing is dated from the first mouthful and does not depend on the second.
