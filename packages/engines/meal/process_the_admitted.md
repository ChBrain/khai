# Process: The Admitted

## Taxonomy

[The Table](process_the_table.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: meal

## Initiated by

Somebody is asked, and they come.
Being at a table is not a reward for belonging; it is the operation that produces belonging, and it works on the first occasion as well as on the hundredth.

## Direction

Toward a persona who is now in the set.
The admission is not conditional on anything happening at the meal. A guest who says little, eats less, and leaves early has still been at the table, and every later account of the evening will include them.
This is why a difficult person keeps being asked, and why taking somebody off the list is so much harder than putting them on it.

## Lever

The generosity of asking against what asking commits.
The carrying force is that inviting somebody is a kindness, costs the host real money and effort, and is meant exactly as it appears; the resistance is that the invitation enters the persona into a set with terms attached -- future tables, an expectation of return, a standing nobody described -- and what the persona accepted was a dinner.
The Admitted joined more than they were offered.

## Echo

One more name on the list.
It shows the mechanism at its plainest: presence at the eating is the whole of the qualification.
The Admitted is dated from the first time they came and does not require a second.
