# Process: The Bond

## Taxonomy

[Meal](process_meal.md), what the meal leaves, and where the engine is seen for one thing.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: meal

## Initiated by

The plates are cleared, and something is different.
The Bond starts after the eating: [the belonging](process_the_belonging.md) produced by having been there, [the owed return](process_the_owed_return.md) that nobody stated, and [the next one](process_the_next_one.md), because a meal makes another meal.

## Direction

Toward a group that forms without anybody having convened one.
The bond is not a memory of the conversation and does not depend on the conversation having gone well. What was produced is a set of people who have eaten together, which is a category the mind treats as real, and it was produced by attendance rather than by anything anybody did.
It is also cheap, fast, and reliable, which is why every institution that needs people to cooperate feeds them.

## Lever

The reality of what the meal produces against the fact that producing it was not the point.
The carrying force is that this is the good news of the engine and should be read that way: a shared meal leaves people more generous, more trusting, and more willing to settle, at a cost of one lunch, and it is among the better-supported small findings anybody has; the resistance is that it works by not being the stated purpose, so the moment a meal is convened in order to produce it -- the mandated team dinner, the enforced family sit-down, the reconciliation lunch -- everybody present attends to the purpose instead of to the food, and the operation does not run.
The Bond rides the engine's keystone knife-edge: a meal binds in proportion to how little it is about binding.

## Echo

People who will pick up where they left off.
It shows the three earlier movements complete -- a table with an edge on it, a sharing that made everybody briefly the same material, an order that placed everyone -- and adds what they were for, which nobody was pursuing.
This is why the Bond is the hardest of the four to stage and the only one worth closing on: the meal must be about something else, the pleasure must be real, nobody must be trying, and the audience must be able to watch a group form that nobody convened.
A play that stages the host as working the table has replaced the mechanism with a scheme, and one that stages the meal as hollow has replaced it with a satire.
The Bond is dated from the end of the meal and is claimed at the next one.
