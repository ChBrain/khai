# Instructions: Meal

How a Playwright wires the meal engine into a play. You wire by linking the play's own content to the engine's files; the engine files stay untouched, they are canon. The Roadie plumbs the engine's law into Instructions Knowledge on deploy. Authoring guidance, not runtime content, and it does not go on tour.

## Human

- The human decides what the meal is ostensibly about before writing it, and it is never the bond, because the engine runs on the bond being a by-product. Decide the guest list separately and deliberately, since the list is the movement that classifies and it will be read by everybody including the people not on it. And keep nobody working the table: the arrangement is at its most interesting when the pleasure is real and the group forms anyway.

## Agent

- From the play's own content, a persona links under Projection where they stand at the table: whether they were asked, what they are eating and whether it is what everybody else is eating, how they are placed by the form, and what the last meal left outstanding. Read the engine on who ate, never on what was said. Keep it the co-ingestion: the conversion of a stranger into a guest is hospitality's, the transfer that binds by naming no price is gift's, the rite that marks a passage is ritual's. You link to the engine, you never edit it.

## Collaboration

- The conversion of a stranger into a guest is the hospitality engine's; the transfer that binds by naming no price is gift's and the owing composite's; the rite marking a passage is ritual's and liminality's; the kept flame is hearth's; neutral ground where nobody is host is third-place's; the mouth's rejection of a contaminant is disgust's; hunger and the eating body are needs's and body's; taste as a marker of position is capital's and status's. Restaurants and the food trade supply the setting and are not the operation. Whether a production's dinner bound anybody, and whether a refusal was heard as dietary or social, are the team's review.

## Knowledge

- The meal is people eating the same food at the same time, and what the co-ingestion does to the people doing it. Eating is the most exclusive act a person performs -- what one person eats, no other can -- and society has built on exactly that its most inclusive repeated form, converted by form alone: regularity, a fixed shape, manners, a table. It is not hospitality's conversion of a stranger into a guest, not gift's transfer that binds by naming no price, and not ritual's rite marking a passage. It wires on a persona at Projection and runs in four movements. The table classifies: the admitted are not being rewarded for belonging, since presence at the eating is the whole qualification; the excluded are constituted by the meal having happened, with no act required and no words available afterwards, and commensal boundaries are the sharpest any society draws and are held as revulsion rather than as rule; the place set includes somebody absent, because a table counts in settings. The sharing is the mechanism: eating crosses the boundary of the body and becomes the body, so people eating one dish are briefly made of the same material, which is why a rule about what may be eaten is always a rule about who may be eaten with; the dividing runs a public allocation at stakes so trivial that measuring them would be the strange act; the passing accumulates goodwill through a run of small granted requests none of which was meant to. The order is about signs rather than nutrition: a sequence in which each element takes its sense from its position, manners that protect the person beside you and sort the room in the same motion, and a declining that is heard as being about the eater. The bond is what it was for and nobody was pursuing: a belonging felt as an accurate assessment of the other party, a return that cannot be settled without insult, and a next one whose interval reports the relation more accurately than either party could. The engine's twist is at the root: a meal binds in proportion to how little it is about binding, so the declared subject is always something else and the mandated dinner fails at what the accidental one does without effort.

## System

- Do give the meal a stated subject that is not the bond. A birthday, a deal, a Tuesday. The engine will not run if the table knows what it is for.
- Do keep the pleasure real. Nobody is working anybody, the food is good, the talk is the point as far as anybody present can tell, and the group forms regardless.
- Do not stage the host as managing the table. That replaces the mechanism with a scheme, and it collapses the movement the engine exists for.
- Do write the guest list on purpose and never explain it. The list is the most precise statement of the social order in the play, and an explanation offered later only confirms the absence was noticed.
- Do put one dish in the middle where you can. Separate portions are several simultaneous meals; one pot is a single event, and the engine works harder on it.
- Do let the dividing be watched. A spoonful allocated in front of everybody is still an allocation, and a table remembers who took the good piece long after it has forgotten the food.
- Do give somebody a reason not to eat. The declining is the one position from which the engine's costs are visible, and the refusal is heard as being about the eater however graciously it is offered.
- Do close on the interval rather than the dinner. What the meal leaves is a gap that is either shortening or lengthening, and both parties can read it without either ever raising it.
- Do not model hospitality's conversion, the gift, the rite of passage, the hearth, neutral ground, disgust, hunger, or taste as distinction. Do not make the dinner stand for a theme; that is authored key, the play's pitch. Do not edit the engine's files; wire only from the play's side.
