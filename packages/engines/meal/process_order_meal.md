# Process: The Order

## Taxonomy

[Meal](process_meal.md), the form the eating is required to take.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: meal

## Initiated by

The eating has a shape, and the shape is not about nutrition.
The Order starts at the fact that a meal is not simply food consumed: [a sequence](process_the_sequence.md) that has to be run, [manners](process_the_manners.md) governing the body while it does something animal, and [the declining](process_the_declining.md), which is never only about the food.

## Direction

Toward a form everybody knows and nobody was taught in so many words.
A meal is structured -- elements in an order, a beginning and an end, parts that may not be swapped -- and the structure repeats at every scale, so the humblest version and the grandest are recognisably the same object.
The structure carries the meaning. It is what makes a meal a meal rather than eating.

## Lever

The arbitrariness of the form against how much it decides.
The carrying force is that none of it is important, the rules are local and would be different a border away, and holding them lightly is the mark of a good host; the resistance is that a form everybody knows is a form that sorts people the instant somebody does not know it, and the sorting is immediate, silent, and read as breeding rather than as unfamiliarity.
The Order teaches without instructing and tests without examining.

## Echo

An occasion with a grammar.
It is the movement that makes a meal legible to everybody at once, and readable about everybody at once.
The Order is dated from childhood and is never revised.
