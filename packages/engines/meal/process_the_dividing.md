# Process: The Dividing

## Taxonomy

[The Sharing](process_the_sharing.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: meal

## Initiated by

One quantity has to become several.
Somebody carves, ladles, cuts, or hands round, and in doing so decides how much each person gets.

## Direction

Toward a small, public, repeated act of distribution.
Every meal that shares a dish runs an allocation in front of witnesses, and everybody can see the outcome. Whoever is doing it stands, briefly, in the position of distributing a scarce good to a group that will remember.
The rules that appear here are unwritten, universally known, and enforced by nothing: take less than you want, notice who has not had any, leave the last one.

## Lever

The triviality of the amounts against the visibility of the act.
The carrying force is that it does not matter and everybody knows it does not matter: it is a spoonful, the stakes are nil, and the person auditing the portions would be the one behaving strangely; the resistance is that an allocation watched by everybody is an allocation regardless of size, and a table learns very quickly who takes the good piece, which is information about a person that no interview produces.
The Dividing is the smallest test of character anybody runs and one of the most trusted.

## Echo

A group that has watched somebody share.
It shows the meal generating evidence nobody set out to gather.
The Dividing is dated from the serving and is remembered longer than the food.
