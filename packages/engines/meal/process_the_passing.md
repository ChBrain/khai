# Process: The Passing

## Taxonomy

[The Sharing](process_the_sharing.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: meal

## Initiated by

The dish moves hand to hand.
Salt, bread, the bottle, the bowl -- each transfer a small request granted without discussion.

## Direction

Toward a rhythm of tiny obligations discharged immediately.
A table runs on continuous minor cooperation: somebody wants something, somebody else moves it, nobody thanks anybody very much, and it happens twenty times an hour without anybody keeping count.
This is the engine's low-grade continuous work, and it is why a meal is a better setting for a difficult conversation than a room with two chairs in it.

## Lever

The insignificance of each transfer against the effect of the sequence.
The carrying force is that the passing is genuinely nothing, involves no calculation at all, and would be spoiled by attention; the resistance is that a sustained run of granted requests is exactly the material out of which people build a sense that somebody is on their side, so a meal accumulates goodwill through actions none of which were meant to.
The Passing works because nobody is counting.

## Echo

A table where things have been going one way for an hour.
It shows the mechanism running underneath the conversation the whole time.
The Passing is dated continuously and leaves no record.
