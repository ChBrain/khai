# Process: The Same Substance

## Taxonomy

[The Sharing](process_the_sharing.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: meal

## Initiated by

Everybody eats the same thing.
Not similar things, and not each their own -- the one pot, the one loaf, the dish that goes round.

## Direction

Toward an identity claim made by the stomach.
What a person eats becomes them, so people who eat the same thing become, in the only sense the body recognises, more alike. Every society that has thought about food has arrived at this independently, and the ones that repudiate it in doctrine keep it in practice.
It is also why a rule about what may be eaten is always at the same time a rule about who may be eaten with.

## Lever

The literalness of the mechanism against its indifference to intention.
The carrying force is that it is a good and generous arrangement: one dish is warmer than separate portions, it is how most of humanity has eaten for most of history, and it makes a table into a single event rather than several simultaneous ones; the resistance is that the same logic turns dietary difference into social distance automatically, so the person who cannot eat this food is not being excluded by anybody and is outside the operation nonetheless.
The Same Substance does not care why anybody is eating it.

## Echo

People with something in common that is not an opinion.
It shows why the engine is not a theory of conversation.
The Same Substance is dated from the dish rather than from the meal.
