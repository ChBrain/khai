# Process: The Table

## Taxonomy

[Meal](process_meal.md), who eats with whom.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: meal

## Initiated by

A list exists, and everybody can read it.
The Table starts before the food: [who is admitted](process_the_admitted.md), [who is not](process_the_excluded.md), and [the place set](process_the_place_set.md) for somebody expected to fill it.

## Direction

Toward a boundary that has been drawn without being stated.
Nobody announces the criterion. The invitations go out, the seats are taken, and the resulting list is the most precise account of a social order available anywhere, because it was produced by a hundred small decisions not one of which was about the order.
Whoever can read the room can read the list, and the list is not deniable.

## Lever

The innocence of an invitation against the completeness of what it says.
The carrying force is that a table has a size and somebody has to decide, the decisions are usually practical -- who was free, who lives near, who gets on with whom -- and treating every guest list as a verdict would make it impossible to have anybody over; the resistance is that practical reasons produce the same list as principled ones and the list is read the same way either way, so the person not asked receives the full statement whether or not anybody sent it.
The Table says who we are by saying who ate.

## Echo

A group with an edge on it.
It is the movement that does the classifying, and the only one that works before anybody arrives.
The Table is dated from the invitation and is remembered by whoever did not get one.
