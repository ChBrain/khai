# Process: The Owed Return

## Taxonomy

[The Bond](process_bond_meal.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: meal

## Initiated by

Somebody has been fed and now owes a meal.
Nobody said so, no amount was named, and both parties know.

## Direction

Toward an obligation with no terms and no expiry.
The return is not a repayment and must not resemble one: giving back the same meal at the next opportunity is worse than not returning it at all, because it settles an account that was not supposed to be an account.
So the debt is discharged late, differently, and approximately, and the approximation is what keeps the relation running.

## Lever

The lightness of the obligation against the fact that it does not clear.
The carrying force is that it is pleasant to owe somebody a dinner, the obligation is a form of connection rather than a burden, and the person who says you must come to us means it; the resistance is that an unstated debt cannot be paid off, only maintained, and a persona who wants no relation with the host has no way to decline the meal retroactively.
The Owed Return is a tie in the form of a pleasure.

## Echo

A relation with something outstanding in it.
It shows why the transfer that binds by naming no price is the gift engine's, and why this engine borrows that logic rather than owning it.
The Owed Return is dated from the invitation and is never fully settled.
