# Meal: Reference

## Line of Work

The meal as **process**: people eating the same food at the same time, and what the co-ingestion does
to the people doing it.

The engine's subject is the cheapest instrument anybody has for making a group. Simmel's essay is the
spine, and its observation is the one everything else here hangs from: eating is the most exclusive
and most individual act a person performs -- what one person eats, no other person can eat -- and
society has built on precisely that act its most inclusive and most frequently repeated form. The
meal is the physiologically lowest thing people do together and the socially most reliable, and the
conversion is done by form: regularity, a fixed shape, manners, a table.

The boundary with what khai already carries is clean. The conversion of a stranger into a guest is
**hospitality**'s, and the food at that door is an offering that seals a status change -- this engine
reads the repeated eating among people already inside. The transfer that binds precisely by naming no
price is **gift**'s; the meal uses gift logic in one member and does not own it. The rite performed to
mark a passage is **ritual**'s and **liminality**'s. The kept flame of a household is **hearth**'s, and
the informal public ground where nobody is host is **third-place**'s. And the mouth's rejection of a
contaminant -- the food-rejection reflex that broadens into a general system -- is **disgust**'s: that
engine reads what may not be swallowed, and this one reads what happens when it is.

**The table** is the movement that classifies. The **admitted** are not being rewarded for belonging;
presence at the eating is the whole of the qualification, which is why a difficult person keeps being
asked and why taking somebody off a list is so much harder than adding them. The **excluded** are
constituted by the meal having happened, with no act required and no form of words available
afterwards -- and commensal boundaries, who may not eat with whom, are the sharpest separations any
society draws and the ones held as revulsion rather than as rule, which is why they outlast the
beliefs that produced them. The **place set** is the engine's one instrument that operates on somebody
absent: a table counts in settings, so laying one includes a person who is not there, and the day it
stops being laid is a decision announced to everybody without a word.

**The sharing** is the mechanism. Eating is the most complete thing a person can do with an object,
since it crosses the boundary of the body and becomes the body, so two people eating from one dish
have done that with the same object -- Fischler's incorporation principle, and the reason a rule about
what may be eaten is always simultaneously a rule about who may be eaten with. The **dividing** runs a
public allocation of a scarce good in front of witnesses, at stakes so trivial that measuring them
would be the strange act, and a table learns from it who takes the good piece. The **passing** is the
engine's low-grade continuous work: a sustained run of small granted requests, none of them meant to
do anything, which is exactly the material people build a sense of an ally out of.

**The order** is Douglas's contribution and is about signs rather than nutrition. The **sequence** is a
small ordered set in which each element takes its sense from its position, so violating it produces
not inconvenience but a sense that something is wrong, and the same pattern repeats upward through the
day, the week, and the year. The **manners** are the densest behavioural code a culture applies to any
activity, taught earlier than almost anything else, and largely devoted to suppressing the evidence
that eating is what it is -- consideration made routine, and simultaneously the most efficient class
marker in existence, acquired only in childhood and unteachable to an adult without humiliation. The
**declining** is the one position from which the engine's costs are visible: food offered is a person
offering, so a refusal registers below the level anybody would defend, and registers again next time.

**The bond** is what the operation was for, and nobody was pursuing it. Having eaten with somebody
raises the benefit of the doubt afterwards, and the persona reads that shift as a discovery about the
other party rather than as an effect of lunch. The **owed return** cannot be settled without insult --
repaying the exact meal at the next opportunity closes an account that was not supposed to be one --
so it is discharged late and approximately, and the approximation keeps the relation running. The
**next one** is where the relation is actually kept, since a bond made this way is stored nowhere and
has no other form: the interval reports the relation more accurately than either party could.

Hence the **twist**, at the root. **A meal binds in proportion to how little it is about binding.** The
declared subject is always something else -- the food, the birthday, the deal, the fact that it was one
o'clock -- and the group is a by-product, which is exactly why it forms. A meal convened openly to make
people into a unit is the one that does not work, because everybody at it is attending to the purpose
instead of to the food; the mandated team dinner fails at what the accidental lunch does without
effort. Elster's argument about states that are essentially by-products is the sharpening: some
outcomes cannot be produced by aiming at them, and willing them is self-defeating.

The engine is built so that nobody has to be working anybody for any of it to run, and a play should
keep it that way. The host is not managing the table. The pleasure is real. The bond forms anyway.

**On the evidence, stated plainly.** This engine mixes classical social theory with a small
experimental literature, and the two are not the same kind of thing. **The twist is an inference and
should be read as one.** Simmel's essay is an essay, Elster's by-product argument is philosophical and
general, and nobody has run a study contrasting meals convened for bonding against meals convened for
anything else -- the failure of the mandated dinner is widely observed, easily recognised, and not
measured. That is the engine's central claim and its least evidenced one, and it is stated here rather
than dressed up. **Douglas's grammar of the meal is structuralist analysis of one culture** -- largely
her own household's British meals -- and the further claim that every culture runs some meal grammar is
a convergence across ethnography rather than a survey. **Elias' account of table manners is
historically contested**: the documentary evidence from etiquette manuals is real and the direction of
travel is not seriously disputed, but the psychogenetic story built on it drew a sustained critique
from Duerr, and the engine takes the manners and leaves the theory of civilisation. **The experimental
leg is small and should not be leaned on.** Woolley and Fishbach's shared-plate findings, Balachandra's
negotiation-over-a-meal result, and Kniffin's firehouse study are lab studies and single-organisation
correlations of exactly the size and kind that the last fifteen years have taught everybody to hold
loosely; they are consistent with the engine and they do not carry it. **Dunbar's social-eating work is
survey and correlational**, and the endorphin mechanism is extrapolated from other bonding research
rather than measured at a table. What should not be taken from this engine is that meals are
manipulation, that shared eating is a technique to be deployed, or that any of these effects is large
enough to justify a budget. The claim is narrow: a table has an edge and the edge speaks, co-ingestion
does something no agreement does, the form sorts everybody present, and the bond that results was
nobody's object.

## Restrictions

What the engine refuses to model, and to whom it delegates.

- **The conversion of a stranger into a guest (hospitality)**: The stranger resolved as guest or
  threat, the crossing, the offering that seals it, and the term nobody can name are the hospitality
  engine's. Food appears there as the instrument of a status change; this engine reads the repeated
  eating among people who are already inside.
- **The transfer that binds by naming no price (gift, owing)**: How a transfer creates obligation and
  requires a return is the gift engine's and the owing composite's. The owed return member uses that
  logic and hands the mechanism back rather than restating it.
- **The rite that marks a passage (ritual, liminality, passage)**: A performance that moves somebody
  from one status to another is those packages'. A feast can be one and mostly is not, and the engine
  reads the eating rather than the transition.
- **The kept flame and the neutral ground (hearth, third-place)**: The fire tended daily by a keeper is
  hearth's; the informal public gathering place where nobody is host or guest is third-place's. A meal
  happens in both and is neither.
- **The mouth's rejection of a contaminant (disgust)**: The food-rejection reflex, contamination logic,
  and the three functional domains are the disgust engine's. That engine reads what may not be
  swallowed; this one reads what happens when it is.
- **Hunger, nutrition, and the eating body (needs, body, self-control)**: Appetite as a drive, the body
  as a felt thing, and the regulation of intake are those engines'. The engine models no eater's
  relationship with food, only people's relationship through it.
- **Taste as a marker of position (capital, status, differentiation)**: Cultivated preference as a form
  of capital, and distinction generally, are those engines'. The manners member reads one narrow case
  -- a code acquired in childhood and read instantly -- and delegates the rest.
- **The commercial trade in eating (the world)**: Restaurants, catering, and the food industry are a
  priced service with stated terms. They supply the setting for many meals and are not the operation.
- **The dinner as authored symbol (the play's pitch)**: A meal made to **stand for** a theme -- the
  shared loaf that mirrors a motif of communion -- is refused: that is authored key, the play's pitch.
  The meal sets what the eating cost and produced; the meaning read into it is the play's.
