# Process: The Sequence

## Taxonomy

[The Order](process_order_meal.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: meal

## Initiated by

The courses come in an order that cannot be varied.
Savoury before sweet, this before that, the cup at the end -- and the order holds even where nobody could say why.

## Direction

Toward a structure that carries meaning by pattern rather than by content.
A meal is a small ordered set, and each element takes its sense from its position exactly as a word does in a sentence. Change the order and the meal is not badly executed, it is unreadable.
The same pattern repeats upward: the day's meals, the week's, the year's feasts, each an expansion of the same small structure.

## Lever

The rigidity of the sequence against how little it costs anybody.
The carrying force is that the order is convenient, partly digestive in origin, and nobody suffers from it; the resistance is that a structure this stable is doing semantic work rather than practical work, and the proof is that violating it produces not inconvenience but a sense that something is wrong.
The Sequence is a grammar, and it is felt as a preference.

## Echo

A meal that could be read from outside.
It shows the meal as a system of signs that the people running it experience as habit.
The Sequence is dated from the culture and is held individually as taste.
