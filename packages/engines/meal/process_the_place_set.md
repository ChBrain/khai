# Process: The Place Set

## Taxonomy

[The Table](process_the_table.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: meal

## Initiated by

A place is laid for somebody who is not there.
The chair kept at the family table, the setting laid for the one who has died, the cup poured for the expected, the seat nobody takes.

## Direction

Toward an absence that is a form of membership.
A table counts its people in settings, so laying one is an act of inclusion that does not require the person to be present, and failing to lay one is a removal performed in front of everybody.
It is the only instrument the engine has that operates on somebody who is not there, and it is unusually strong for that reason.

## Lever

The tenderness of keeping a place against what keeping it enforces.
The carrying force is that it is nearly always love: the place is kept because the person is wanted, missed, or expected, it costs almost nothing, and it comforts the whole table; the resistance is that a kept place holds the absent person's membership open on the household's terms rather than on theirs, and the day it stops being laid is a decision announced to everyone present without a word being said.
The Place Set makes an absence into an item on the table.

## Echo

Somebody present by arrangement.
It shows that the table's list is not a list of bodies.
The Place Set is dated from the first meal without them, and its ending is never announced.
