# Process: Meal

## Taxonomy

Eating (no khai file of its own yet).

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: meal

## Initiated by

People sit down and eat the same food at the same time.
The meal starts wherever eating stops being solitary and becomes co-ordinated: the family dinner, the shift lunch, the wedding breakfast, the deal done over a table, the two friends who meet over food because it is easier than meeting for no reason.
It is not the conversion of a stranger into a guest, which is hospitality's, and not the transfer that binds by naming no price, which is gift's. It is the co-ingestion itself, and what co-ingestion does to the people doing it.

## Direction

Toward a group that did not exist before the food went round.
The engine reads four movements: [the table](process_the_table.md), who eats with whom and who does not; [the sharing](process_the_sharing.md), the co-ingestion that is the mechanism; [the order](process_order_meal.md), the form the eating is required to take; and [the bond](process_bond_meal.md), the twist -- what the meal leaves behind, and why it works best when nobody is trying.
The four are one operation, and it is the cheapest instrument for making people into a unit that anybody has.

## Lever

What the people at the table think they are doing against what the table is doing.
The carrying force is that a meal is a good time and the pleasure is not a cover for anything: people eat together because it is warm and easy and food is better shared, the talk is the point as far as anybody present can tell, and a reading that treats every dinner as a covert operation has got the phenomenon exactly wrong; the resistance is that the group forms anyway, on terms nobody negotiated, and the list of who was at the table is a statement about the social order that everybody can read and nobody made.
A meal is read on who ate, never on what was said.

## Echo

A set of people who now count as a set.
The meal does not close; it is a bond being made, gathered into one reading -- a table, a sharing, an order, a bond.
The engine's twist is here. **A meal binds in proportion to how little it is about binding.** The declared subject is always something else -- the food, the birthday, the deal, the fact that it was one o'clock -- and the group is a by-product, which is exactly why it forms: a meal convened openly for the purpose of making people into a unit is the one that does not work, because everybody at it is attending to the purpose instead of to the food. The mandated team dinner fails at the thing the accidental one does without effort.
It hands off what it is not: the conversion of a stranger into a guest is hospitality's; the transfer that binds by naming no price is gift's; the rite performed to mark a passage is ritual's and liminality's; the kept flame of a household is hearth's; the informal public ground where nobody is host is third-place's; the mouth's rejection of a contaminant is disgust's; and a dinner made to **stand for** a theme is authored key, the play's pitch.
The persona is dated from the last table they were asked to.
