# Process: The Next One

## Taxonomy

[The Bond](process_bond_meal.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: meal

## Initiated by

The meal ends by proposing another.
We should do this again is not a pleasantry; it is the mechanism's only means of continuing.

## Direction

Toward a relation that exists as a series of tables.
A bond made this way is not stored anywhere and has no other form: it is refreshed by repetition or it fades, which is why people who have stopped eating together have generally stopped, whatever either of them would say about it.
The interval is the measure. Households eat together daily, friendships monthly, obligations annually, and the frequency reports the relation more accurately than anybody inside it could.

## Lever

The warmth of the proposal against what its absence discloses.
The carrying force is that the offer is sincere and usually meant: people do want to do it again, the intention is real at the moment it is spoken, and lives are busy in ways that have nothing to do with anybody's feelings; the resistance is that the engine keeps a record anyway, in the form of an interval that is either shortening or lengthening, and both parties can read it without either ever raising it.
The Next One is where the relation is actually kept.

## Echo

A gap that is either closing or opening.
It shows why the operation never concludes and why nothing else substitutes for it.
The Next One is dated from the last meal and counted from there.
