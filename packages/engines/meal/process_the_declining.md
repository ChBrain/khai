# Process: The Declining

## Taxonomy

[The Order](process_order_meal.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: meal

## Initiated by

Somebody does not eat what is offered.
They are not hungry, they do not eat that, they are unwell, they are fasting, they have a reason -- and the reason will be asked for.

## Direction

Toward a refusal that is read as social rather than dietary.
Food offered is a person offering, so food refused is a person refused, and no explanation entirely converts the second reading back into the first.
This is why dietary rules make such effective boundaries and why they are so hard to carry across a table: the persona holding one is refusing repeatedly, in public, at every meal, and is required to be gracious about it every time.

## Lever

The obviousness of the reason against the persistence of the reading.
The carrying force is that nobody at the table thinks a refusal is an insult, the reasons are usually given and accepted, and hosts adapt without resentment; the resistance is that the offer was of the host and the substance at once, so the refusal registers somewhere below the level anybody would defend, and it registers again at the next meal.
The Declining is heard as being about the eater.

## Echo

A person outside the operation for the length of the dish.
It shows the mechanism from the one position where its costs are visible.
The Declining is dated at each offer and accumulates no credit.
