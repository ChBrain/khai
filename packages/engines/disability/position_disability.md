# Position: Disability

## Taxonomy

A standing assigned by arrangements, held by a persona whose body the arrangements did not anticipate (no khai file of its own yet).

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: disability

## Has

A standing the persona did not apply for and cannot resign, produced by decisions taken before they arrived.
It carries a category somebody else administers, entitlements that exist but must be claimed, and a set of expectations about capacity that arrive in the room ahead of the persona and are rarely stated aloud.
What it does not carry is the body. The impairment is the persona's own and travels with them everywhere; this standing is issued by a particular arrangement and can be handed back by changing the arrangement, which is the whole of the distinction the engine is built on.

## Orders

It orders the persona to do in advance what everyone else does without noticing.
Route planned before leaving, entrance confirmed, seat asked for, timing padded, proof carried in case it is wanted, a fallback held for the lift that is out. The order is not to endure difficulty; it is to **prepare**, continuously, for a world that assumed somebody else would arrive.
And it orders explanation. The persona is required to account for themselves to people with no standing to ask, in terms the asker will accept, at moments not of their choosing -- and to do it pleasantly, because a refusal is read as the difficulty everyone suspected.

## Loses

It strips the unremarkable.
What goes first is anonymity: the ability to enter a room and be nothing in particular. Then spontaneity, which is the freedom to arrive somewhere without having checked it. Then the assumption of access -- the belief, which costs the rest of the room nothing at all, that the building will simply work.
What is not stripped, and what the arrangement keeps insisting is stripped, is competence. The standing is routinely read as a statement about what the persona can do, when it is a statement about what the room was built to permit.

## Drives

It drives the persona at the arrangement rather than at the body.
Toward the ask, the workaround, the route that works, the informal map of which entrances are real; toward other people holding the same standing, because the map is passed between them and almost never published; and toward the political claim underneath all of it -- that the thing which should change is the building, the form, the schedule, the assumption.
The engine's twist is here. **Nothing about the body changes when it becomes a disability; the arrangements were settled before the persona arrived, and the standing is issued at the moment the two meet.** The impairment is real and can hurt, and the engine does not pretend otherwise. But the disadvantage is manufactured elsewhere, in a specification nobody wrote down and everybody built to. And the remedy completes the trap: every fix is delivered as a **personal exception**, granted to this persona on request, which quietly puts the problem back inside the body the moment it appears to have been solved.
It is not the discrediting mark and its management, which is stigma's; not the sick role, the diagnosis-as-currency and the biography it disrupts, which is illness's; not the fit of an object to a body, which is ergonomics'; and not the setting read as a mover in the moment, which is space's.
It fails when the persona is asked to be grateful for access, when the accommodation becomes the story, and when the room concludes from the standing what the persona is capable of.
