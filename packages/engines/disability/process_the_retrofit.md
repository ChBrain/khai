# Process: The Retrofit

## Taxonomy

[Disability](position_disability.md), access added afterwards.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: disability

## Initiated by

A place, already built, being made to admit the bodies it was not drawn for.
The ramp beside the steps, the lift in a shaft cut through the middle of the plan, the accessible entrance at the side of the building, the alternative arrangement reached by a different corridor. It is initiated late -- after the design, usually after a law, occasionally after somebody sued.
Lateness is the whole character of it. Everything a retrofit does could have been done at the drawing stage for a fraction of the money and none of the signposting.

## Direction

Into the fabric, and simultaneously into the room's account of who belongs.
Physically it moves toward inclusion and genuinely arrives: the persona gets in, which is not nothing and is the point. Socially it moves the other way, because the addition is legible as an addition. The main entrance is where people enter; this is where **you** enter.
The two directions are both real and do not cancel. An engine that only says the first is advertising; one that only says the second is arguing against the ramp.

## Lever

Compliance, and its minimum.
The lever that actually moves buildings is a standard with a date and a penalty, and what a standard produces is the least that satisfies it: the ramp at the steepest permitted gradient, the lift that is smallest, the accessible toilet used for storage, the button that has not worked since spring.
Which is why maintenance is the true test and almost never the reported one. A place is not accessible because it has a lift; it is accessible because the lift works today, and nobody counts that.

## Echo

Access that announces itself keeps the standard intact.
The persona is admitted, on a route that says the route was made for them, into a building that still describes the other entrance as the entrance. Being let in by an addition is a different fact from having been expected.
And there is a settled quality to it that is hard to argue with, because it looks like the problem was solved. **A retrofit is the arrangement conceding the individual case while keeping the specification** -- the persona gets through the door, and the next building goes up to the same drawing.
