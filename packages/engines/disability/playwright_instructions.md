# Instructions: Disability

How a Playwright wires the disability engine into a play. You wire by linking the play's own content to the engine's files; the engine files stay untouched, they are canon. The Roadie plumbs the engine's law into Instructions Knowledge on deploy. Authoring guidance, not runtime content, and it does not go on tour.

## Human

- The human decides the room before the persona. What was the place drawn for -- what reach, what stature, what stamina, what length of meeting, what shape of name in what box? Nobody wrote it down, so the human has to, because the standing cannot be issued until the specification exists. Then stage the order: the persona arrives and the mismatch appears at the seam. If they arrive already disabled, the play has put the disadvantage in the body and the read is gone before the scene starts. Keep the impairment real -- it can hurt, tire and limit on days when every door works -- because the engine's own sources insist on it and the claim is only ever that the _disadvantage_ is manufactured.

## Agent

- From the play's own content, a persona links under Projection where they stand in the arrangement: what the room withholds from them, what they must ask for, and what the asking has cost so far. Read the engine on the arrangement, never as a fact about the persona. Keep it the standing: the discrediting mark and its management are stigma's, the sick role and the diagnosis are illness's, and the fit of an object to a body is ergonomics'. You link to the engine, you never edit it.

## Collaboration

- The discrediting attribute, passing and disclosure are stigma's; the sick role, the diagnosis as currency and biographical disruption are illness's; anthropometric fit, how a form communicates its use and the setting as mover are ergonomics's, usability's and space's; pain, fatigue and the physical course of a condition are body's and illness's; equality legislation, the duty to adjust, the tribunal and the grievance are law's, org's and complaint's; what happens inside somebody meeting another's need is caregiving's, the tending composite's and compassion's. Whether a particular arrangement is reasonable is a judgement the engine declines, so the team does not adjudicate it either -- it holds that the specification is a decision and that the disadvantage is issued, and leaves where the line falls open on purpose.

## Knowledge

- Disability is a standing assigned by arrangements rather than carried in the body: issued where a persona meets a world built to a specification they do not meet, held without application, and dissolvable by changing the room. Impairment is the bodily fact and travels with the persona everywhere; disability is what a particular arrangement makes of it. It is not stigma's discrediting mark, not illness's sick role, not ergonomics' fit of an object to a body, and not space's setting-as-mover. It wires on a persona at Projection. One place and three processes hang beneath the standing. The standard is a world that presents as neutral and is a specification: percentile tables that did real work and cannot contain the range of actual bodies, encoded in step heights, counter depths, form fields and the length of a meeting, offering everything to the body they were drawn for at no cost and withholding from every other without issuing a decision anybody can appeal -- a withholding with no author is read as a fact about the one it excludes. The misfit is the encounter itself, relational and material, in neither the body nor the room alone, dissolvable from either end and in practice adjustable at only one, since the world is enormously adjustable and the mismatch costs the people who could adjust it nothing. The ask is the remedy and its price: the adjustment usually exists and is often trivial, and it happens only when the persona requests it from somebody who can refuse, produces proof, and pitches it neither too small nor too often -- which turns a structural claim back into a permission held by a named individual, arriving as a favour, and a favour is the one form of access that can be withdrawn. The retrofit admits the persona by a route that announces itself and leaves the next building going up to the same drawing. The engine's twist is in the anchor: nothing about the body changes when it becomes a disability, the arrangements were settled before the persona arrived, and every remedy is delivered as a personal exception that puts the problem back inside the body the moment it appears to have been solved.

## System

- Do build the room first. Decide what it was drawn for, in dimensions, before anybody walks into it.
- Do let the mismatch appear on arrival, in front of somebody, so the standing is seen being issued rather than assumed.
- Do keep the impairment real. Pain, fatigue and limits that no ramp reaches, on a day when every door works.
- Do make the person who grants the accommodation decent. The trap is in the shape of the remedy and closes just as tightly when everybody means well.
- Do show the map. Which entrance is real, which lift is out, which form can be done by phone -- expensive to learn, useless to anybody who fits, and passed sideways.
- Do let the fix arrive as a permission. Applied for, assessed, granted to a named person, and not travelling to the next building.
- Do leave the specification standing at the end. The persona gets through the door; the drawing is unchanged.
- Do not resolve whether an arrangement is reasonable; the engine keeps that open on purpose.
- Do not write gratitude as the resolution. Access accepted as a favour is the failure state the anchor names, not the ending.
- Do not model the discrediting mark (stigma), the sick role and diagnosis (illness), the object's fit to a body (ergonomics, usability, space), the body's own demands (body), rights and statute (law, org, complaint), or the interior of care (caregiving, tending, compassion). Do not make the persona stand for a theme; that is authored key, the play's pitch. Do not edit the engine's files; wire only from the play's side.
