# Process: The Misfit

## Taxonomy

[Disability](position_disability.md), the encounter the standing is issued at.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: disability

## Initiated by

A body arriving somewhere built to a specification it does not meet.
Nothing has changed about the body and nothing has changed about the room; they have simply been put together, and at the seam a mismatch appears that was in neither of them separately.
It is initiated by arrival, which is why it is not a property. The same persona fits this doorway and not the next one, works in this meeting and not that one, is unremarkable on Tuesday and conspicuous on Wednesday, and none of it required them to change.

## Direction

Outward into the encounter, never inward into the flesh.
This is the direction the whole engine turns on. A misfit is relational and material: it is located between a body and a world, and it can be dissolved from either end -- by a different body, or by a different world. The temptation, which everyone in the room including the persona is under, is to relocate it into the body, where it looks like a fact rather than an arrangement.
The engine refuses that move as a matter of grammar. Where the mismatch is put decides what there is to do about it.

## Lever

The fit, and it is adjustable at only one end in practice.
The body is largely not available for adjustment -- it can be trained, medicated, braced and endured, and it remains the body. The world is enormously adjustable and mostly is not adjusted, because adjusting it costs somebody money and the misfit costs them nothing.
So the lever exists, is known, is affordable at the margin, and sits with people who do not feel the mismatch. That asymmetry is the engine's engine.

## Echo

The persona learns the map, and the map does not get published.
What accumulates is knowledge: which entrance is real, which lift is out, which form can be done by phone, which colleague will just do it without a conversation. It is expensive to acquire, useless to anyone who meets the standard, and passed sideways between people who do not.
And there is an echo in the room, too. A misfit that happens in front of others is read by them as an event about the persona -- the trouble, the delay, the exception -- rather than as evidence about the place, which is the reading it actually supports.
