# Place: The Standard

## Taxonomy

[Disability](position_disability.md), the world the standing is issued by.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: disability

## Shown

A place that presents itself as neutral, and is a specification.
Step heights, door widths, counter depths, the reach to a handle, the size of type, the length of a meeting, the assumption of a voice, a form with one box for a name and none for anything unusual. None of it announces a body, and all of it encodes one.
What shows is ordinary. That is the point: a place built to a standard looks like a place built to nothing at all, and only ceases to look that way to whoever does not meet it.

## Holds

It holds an average that was never a person.
Somebody sized the doorway, and they sized it from a table -- reach, stature, grip, stamina, the fiftieth percentile of a population measured decades ago and often somewhere else. The table is a real engineering artefact and it did real work; what it cannot do is contain the range of actual bodies, and the ones it excludes were not decided against so much as never counted.
So the place holds a decision that nobody in it made and nobody in it can point to. There is no author to appeal to. There is a floor plan, signed off, long paid for.

## Offers

To the body it was drawn for, it offers everything and charges nothing.
The entrance works, the seat fits, the form has the right box, the hour is long enough. The offer is invisible precisely because it is complete -- nobody who meets the specification ever discovers they were being accommodated all along, which is why they experience the place as merely a place.
That invisibility is what makes the standard hard to argue with. It reads as the natural size of things.

## Withheld

From every other body it withholds the same things, and calls it their difficulty.
Entry, use, the stair without a lift, the counter above eye level, the queue that must be stood in, the meeting with no break in it -- each withheld not by refusal but by dimension, which is harder to contest than a refusal because nobody said no.
And it withholds the account. A place that excludes by measurement gives no reason, issues no decision, names no one responsible; the persona who cannot get in is left holding a problem that presents as personal, in a building that presents as neutral. **A withholding with no author is read as a fact about the one it excludes.**
