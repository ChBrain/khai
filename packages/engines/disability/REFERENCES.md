# Disability: Reference

## Line of Work

Disability as **position**: the standing a persona is put in by arrangements built to a specification
their body does not meet. The engine begins where a bodily fact meets a made world and a disadvantage
appears that was in neither of them alone -- at a doorway, a form, a schedule, a meeting -- and it
reads that disadvantage as issued by the arrangement rather than carried in the flesh.

That boundary is the reason the engine exists, and khai already carries the neighbours it is not. The
discrediting mark and its management are `stigma`'s. The sick role, the diagnosis as currency, and the
biography it disrupts are `illness`'s. The fit of an object to a body is `ergonomics`'. The setting
read as a mover in the moment is `space`'s. What none of them carries is the standing itself: assigned
by measurement, held without application, and dissolvable by changing the room.

Oliver's _The Politics of Disablement_ (1990) is the founding statement of the
account this engine builds on, and it is deliberately not an Origin row here:
`illness` cites it as its own declared bound, and the science-overlap wall is
right that one work restaged by two engines is a warning rather than a
coincidence. The warrant is taken from Oliver's own systematic restatement
instead, which costs the engine nothing -- the model is the same and the
argument is his.

**The distinction is the whole engine.** Impairment is the bodily fact and travels with the persona
everywhere. Disability is what a particular arrangement makes of it, and can be handed back by
changing that arrangement. The engine keeps the two apart in its grammar, because where the mismatch
is put decides what there is to do about it -- and everyone in the room, including the persona, is
under continuous pressure to relocate it into the body, where it stops looking like a decision.

**The twist is that the remedy completes the trap.** The account moves the problem off the body and
onto the building; the fix then arrives as a personal exception, applied for and granted to a named
individual, leaving the building untouched and the persona holding a permission. The specification
survives every accommodation made to it. A retrofit concedes the case and keeps the drawing.

**Where the engine does not overreach.** The strong social model is contested from inside disability
studies, not only from outside it, and the objection is that impairment causes difficulty on its own
-- pain, fatigue, and limits no ramp reaches. The engine takes that objection as true and builds it in
rather than around: the anchor says the impairment is real and can hurt, and the claim is only ever
that the _disadvantage_ is manufactured elsewhere. An engine that denied the body would be arguing
with its own sources.

## Origin

Disability studies and its critics, wired as a position on a persona with the place-side reading the
model requires. The twist follows from the accommodation literature rather than from the founding
texts: the social model diagnoses structurally, and the remedy it produced is administered
individually.

## Restrictions

What the engine refuses to model, and to whom it delegates.

- **The discrediting mark (stigma)**: An attribute that discredits its bearer, and the management of it
  -- passing, disclosure, the discreditable and the discredited -- is `stigma`'s. The seam is that a
  mark is carried by the persona and travels with them; this engine's standing is issued by a room.
- **The sick role and the diagnosis (illness)**: What a condition costs a persona in standing and story,
  the diagnosis as currency, and biographical disruption are `illness`'s. That engine names this one as
  its own declared bound and does not settle the argument; this engine takes the other side of it.
- **The object's fit to a body (ergonomics, usability, space)**: Anthropometric fit, how a form
  communicates its use, and the setting read as a mover are those engines'. They model the artefact and
  the room; this one models the standing that a mismatch issues.
- **The body's own demands (body, illness)**: Pain, fatigue, hunger and the physical course of a
  condition are `body`'s and `illness`'s. The engine takes them as real -- it says so in the anchor --
  and does not model them, because its subject is what the arrangement does with them.
- **Rights, statute and entitlement (law, org, complaint)**: Equality legislation, the duty to adjust,
  the tribunal and the grievance procedure are those engines'. They set the format the ask is made in;
  this engine reads what making it costs.
- **Care and its interior (caregiving, tending, compassion)**: What happens inside somebody meeting
  another's need is those packages'. This engine reads the arrangement, never the helper's feelings
  about it.
- **A disabled figure as authored meaning (the play's pitch)**: A persona staged in order to **stand
  for** courage, or for what a society is like, is refused -- that is authored key, the play's pitch.
  This engine sets the standing and what it costs; the meaning is the play's.

## Encoding

Source to constraint. A position engine: one anchor, one place, three processes, all flat beneath the
standing.

- **[disability](position_disability.md)** (the anchor): the standing arrangements issue, carrying the
  twist -- nothing about the body changes when it becomes a disability, and every remedy is delivered
  as a personal exception. Anchored by UPIAS (1976) and Oliver (1996).
- **[the standard](place_the_standard.md)** (the place): the world built to a specification nobody
  wrote down, offering everything to the body it was drawn for and withholding without an author.
  Anchored by Dreyfuss (1960) and Hamraie (2017).
- **[the misfit](process_the_misfit.md)** (the encounter): body meets world and a mismatch appears that
  was in neither, adjustable at one end and mostly not adjusted. Anchored by Garland-Thomson (2011).
- **[the ask](process_the_ask.md)** (the remedy): the adjustment that exists, must be requested, and
  turns a structural claim back into a permission held by a named person. Anchored by the accommodation
  literature and Shakespeare (2006).
- **[the retrofit](process_the_retrofit.md)** (the addition): access added after the drawing, which
  admits the persona and keeps the specification. Anchored by Hamraie (2017).
