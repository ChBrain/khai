# Process: The Ask

## Taxonomy

[Disability](position_disability.md), the remedy and its price.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: disability

## Initiated by

The persona having to request, from a person with the power to refuse, a change that would cost almost nothing.
The adjustment usually exists. It is a chair, a deadline, a door held open by policy rather than by a colleague, a meeting that breaks every hour, a file in another format. It is rarely expensive and frequently trivial, and it does not happen on its own.
So it is initiated by asking -- which means it is initiated by the persona, every time, in a room that has already been arranged for everyone else without anybody asking for anything.

## Direction

Toward the individual, which is the direction the whole arrangement was supposed to stop travelling in.
Here is the sharpest turn in the engine. The social account moves the problem off the body and onto the building. The remedy then arrives as an **exception granted to a named person**: applied for, justified, assessed, approved, reviewed. The building is untouched and remains the standard; what has changed is that this persona now has a permission.
The fix and the diagnosis therefore point opposite ways, and the fix wins the argument in practice, because a permission is administered and a specification is not.

## Lever

Proof, and the willingness to keep producing it.
The persona must be legible to whoever decides: documented, categorised, plausible, and consistent across the several people who will ask independently. A condition that fluctuates is the hardest of all, because the proof must cover the bad days while the asker is looking at a good one.
And the ask must be pitched. Too small and it is not granted; too large and the persona is difficult; too often and they are a problem; not at all and they are managing fine, which becomes the record.

## Echo

The asking is itself the cost, and it does not appear on any ledger.
What it takes is time, nerve, self-explanation, and the acceptance of being the only person in the room whose participation was conditional on a conversation. It is spent before the work starts, and it is spent again at each new employer, building, term and system, because permissions do not travel.
Then the granted adjustment is read as generosity, and generosity expects gratitude. **The remedy arrives as a favour, and a favour is the one form of access that can be withdrawn.**
