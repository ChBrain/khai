# Process: Commute

## Taxonomy

The daily journey between home and work (no khai file of its own yet).

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: commute

## Initiated by

A persona lives in one place and works in another, and the gap has to be crossed twice a day.
Commute starts wherever that gap was accepted as part of an arrangement: the house bought for its garden, the job taken across a city, the school that fixed the postcode, the office that moved after the mortgage did.
It is not the return of feeling to a baseline after a change, which is hedonic-adaptation's, and not the wait imposed by an institution, which is waiting's. It is a cost agreed once and incurred every morning, and what happens to each side of that bargain over time.

## Direction

Toward a trade whose two halves move in opposite directions after it is struck.
The engine reads four movements: [the trade](process_trade_commute.md), the calculation made once; [the passage](process_passage_commute.md), the thing itself; [the settling](process_settling_commute.md), what fades and what does not; and [the ledger](process_ledger_commute.md), the twist -- why the account is never settled.
The four run once per move and are repeated, largely unchanged, at the next one.

## Lever

The reasonableness of the original decision against what it turns out to cost.
The carrying force is that the trade is rational on its face and most people would make it again: space, quiet, a school, a mortgage that clears, and a job that pays are all real and large, the journey is a known quantity going in, and refusing to travel for work would be an extraordinary constraint to accept on a life; the resistance is that the two sides are not experienced on the same schedule -- the house is lived in once and then becomes the floor, and the journey is not lived in once, it is done four hundred and sixty times a year.
Commute is read on what the trade did afterwards, never on whether it was sensible.

## Echo

Somebody who got what they wanted and is tired in a way they cannot account for.
Commute does not close; it is one decision being paid for in instalments, gathered into one reading -- a trade, a passage, a settling, a ledger.
The engine's twist is here. **Everything the commute was traded for stops being felt, and the commute does not.** The extra room becomes the ordinary size of a room within a season, the salary becomes the ordinary amount of money, the garden becomes the view from the kitchen -- all of it absorbed, exactly as any gain in circumstance is absorbed. The journey is the one part of the arrangement that adaptation handles badly, because what makes it hard is unpredictability and the absence of any control, and those are the conditions nothing gets used to. So the gain quietly stops registering and the cost does not, and neither movement is visible to the person making them.
It hands off what it is not: the return of feeling to a baseline is hedonic-adaptation's and the lasting composite's; the wait imposed by an institution is waiting's; the load on the body is stress's; the bond to a place is place-attachment's; the effort bargain at the other end is employment's; and a journey staged to **stand for** a theme is authored key, the play's pitch.
The persona is dated from the last time they moved.
