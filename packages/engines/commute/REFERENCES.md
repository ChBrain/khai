# Commute: Reference

## Line of Work

Commute as **process**: a cost agreed once and incurred every morning, and what happens to each side of
that bargain over time.

The engine's subject is a trade that ought to balance and does not. Stutzer and Frey's account is the
spine, and it names the puzzle precisely: people who travel further to work report systematically lower
well-being, and they are not compensated for it -- not by the wages, not by the housing, not by anything
the market is supposed to equalise. On the standard account this should not persist, because anybody
worse off would move. The engine takes their proposed explanation seriously and builds on it: the two
halves of the trade are not experienced on the same schedule, so the side that justified the
arrangement stops registering while the side that costs does not.

The boundary with what khai already carries is clean. The return of feeling toward a baseline after any
change in circumstance is **hedonic-adaptation**'s, gathered with savoring and capitalization by the
**lasting** composite; this engine borrows that mechanism for one documented asymmetry and hands it
back. The wait imposed on a persona by an institution is **waiting**'s. The load on the body is
**stress**'s, the bond to a place is **place-attachment**'s, the effort bargain at the other end is
**employment**'s, and how a stretch of time feels while it passes is **time-perception**'s.

**The trade** compares something inspected with something estimated: months go into the house, which is
walked through and imagined in, and the journey is a number on a map produced for its best day. **The
one calculation** is made in advance and never checked, because nothing in the arrangement supplies an
occasion to check it.

**The passage** costs most on the days it goes as expected. The measurements are consistent that
impedance -- the unpredictable blockage -- predicts commuting strain better than distance or duration
do, and the reason is arithmetic: an unpredictable journey has to be budgeted for its worst plausible
version, so **the variance** charges every morning for a delay that arrives occasionally. **The no
control** keeps the effort and removes the option, which is the combination that produces strain
wherever it has been measured. **The hollow hour** is worth having and not worth what it costs, which is
why the people inside the arrangement defend it.

**The settling** erases one side of the bargain. **The house absorbed** is entirely intact and no longer
counts for anything; **the drive unabsorbed** keeps its edge because its edge is made of exactly the
conditions habituation does not reach; and **the widening gap** is a deterioration made entirely of
adaptation, with nothing in the world having moved.

Hence the **twist**, at the root. **Everything the commute was traded for stops being felt, and the
commute does not.** The extra room becomes the ordinary size of a room, the salary becomes the ordinary
amount of money, and all of it is absorbed exactly as any gain in circumstance is absorbed. The journey
is the one part of the arrangement adaptation handles badly. So the gain quietly stops registering, the
cost does not, and neither movement is visible to the person making them.

The engine is built so that nobody has to have made a mistake for any of it to run, and a play should
keep it that way. The trade was sensible. The house is good. The journey is mostly fine.

**On the evidence, stated plainly.** This engine rests on a well-known result that is weaker and more
contested than its fame suggests, and that should be the first thing a reader has. **The commuting
paradox is panel evidence, not experimental.** Stutzer and Frey identify it in a large longitudinal
survey by looking at what happens to the same people as their commutes change, which is a good design
and is not a randomised one; the possibility remains that people who take on long commutes differ in
ways that also bear on how they report their lives. **There is real contrary evidence and it is not
dropped here.** Several studies find more adaptation than the paradox predicts, and mode is an enormous
moderator: people who walk or cycle to work report better well-being than people with much shorter
journeys by car, and some rail commuters with usable time rate their journeys positively. An engine
built around a single shape flattens that, and the flattening is the largest thing wrong with it.
**The asymmetric-adaptation claim is the engine's load-bearing inference.** That unpredictable and
uncontrollable conditions habituate poorly is well supported in its own right, mostly from noise and
crowding research; that this is what explains the commuting paradox specifically is an argument, not a
demonstration, and it is the argument the whole engine is built on. **The impedance finding is old and
small.** The work establishing that unpredictable blockage rather than distance predicts strain comes
from a handful of studies in the 1980s and 1990s with modest samples, and it has been more often cited
than repeated. **The day-reconstruction ranking is one method on one sample.** Commuting appearing near
the bottom of a list of daily activities is a widely quoted result from a single study of working women
in one American city, and it should be read as an illustration rather than as a measurement of anything
general. **The unattributed-cost member is the engine's own claim.** That the shortfall gets charged to
work, the weather, or the household rather than to the journey follows from the members above it and
has not been measured. What should not be taken from this engine is that commuting is bad for
everybody, that people who commute have erred, or that a shorter journey is always better -- some
personas value the buffer sincerely, many commutes are unremarkable, and abolishing the transition
between two roles turns out to have costs of its own. The claim is narrow: the two halves of the trade
adapt at different rates, the cost is paid in the buffer rather than the delay, and nothing in the
arrangement produces a figure anybody could act on.

## Origin

twist is Stutzer and Frey's; the account of what makes a journey costly is Novaco's and Evans's.

## Restrictions

What the engine refuses to model, and to whom it delegates.

- **The return to a baseline (hedonic-adaptation, lasting, savoring)**: How feeling returns toward a
  set point after a change, what attending to a pleasure does, and what survives of a good thing are
  those packages'. The settling movement borrows the mechanism for one asymmetry and names it as
  borrowed.
- **The imposed wait (waiting, queue norms)**: Delay as a technique of institutions, and what standing
  in line does to the person standing, are the waiting engine's. The commute's delays are structural
  rather than administered, and nobody is being made to wait by anybody.
- **The load on the body (stress, coping, restoration, restorative-environment)**: What sustained
  demand does physiologically, what a persona does about it, and what restores them are those engines'.
  This engine reads the arrangement, not the cortisol.
- **The bond to a place (place-attachment, dwelling, third-place)**: What a home comes to mean, and what
  a place does for a persona, are those packages'. The extra-room member reads the asset and its
  signal, not the attachment.
- **The job at the other end (employment, org, hierarchy)**: The effort bargain, the reporting line, and
  the day's demands are those engines'. The commute ends at the door and so does this engine.
- **How the time feels while it passes (time-perception, boredom, mind-wandering)**: The stretch and
  compression of a duration, what an unoccupied mind does, and what boredom is are those engines'. The
  hollow-hour member reads whose time it is, not what it is like.
- **The infrastructure (the world)**: Timetables, road capacity, fares, planning policy, housing
  markets, and the geography that produced the gap are matters of trade and statute. They set the
  format; this engine reads the persona crossing it.
- **A journey as authored symbol (the play's pitch)**: A commute staged in order to **stand for** a
  theme -- the road that mirrors a motif of futility -- is refused: that is authored key, the play's
  pitch. Commute sets what was traded and what became of it; the meaning is the play's.
