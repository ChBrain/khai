# Process: The One Calculation

## Taxonomy

[The Trade](process_trade_commute.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: commute

## Initiated by

The sum is done once.
And then the arrangement runs for six years without it being done again.

## Direction

Toward a decision that is never audited against what it produced.
There is no moment built into the arrangement at which anybody asks whether the trade is still worth it. The mortgage does not prompt it, the employer does not, and the journey itself supplies no occasion, since every individual instance of it is small.
The question comes back only when something else forces it -- a new job, a school ending, a move -- and it is then answered afresh, from the same position as the first time.

## Lever

The stability a settled decision provides against the absence of any review.
The carrying force is that not reopening it is sensible and healthy: a household that re-litigated where it lives every quarter would be unbearable, moving is enormously expensive, and the commitment is what lets everybody get on with their lives; the resistance is that the one variable the decision most needs updating on is the one the persona has been accumulating evidence about daily, and there is no route by which that evidence reaches the decision.
The One Calculation is made in advance and never checked against the outcome.

## Echo

A settled arrangement with no feedback loop.
It shows why experience of commuting does not improve anybody's commuting decisions.
The One Calculation is dated to the move and expires only when something else does.
