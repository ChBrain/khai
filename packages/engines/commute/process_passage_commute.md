# Process: The Passage

## Taxonomy

[Commute](process_commute.md), the thing itself.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: commute

## Initiated by

The persona leaves the house and gets to work, or does not.
The Passage is made of three things: [variance](process_the_variance.md) rather than length, [no control](process_the_no_control.md) over any of it, and [a hollow hour](process_the_hollow_hour.md) that belongs to nobody.

## Direction

Toward a burden whose weight is carried by its unpredictability.
The measurements are consistent about which part is hard. Distance and duration predict how people rate a commute less well than impedance does -- the blockage, the stop, the interruption that could not be planned for.
So two journeys of the same length are not the same journey, and the one that varies is worse in a way that does not average out.

## Lever

The ordinariness of the trip against the specific features that make it expensive.
The carrying force is that most of it is genuinely unremarkable and most days are fine: the majority of commutes go roughly to plan, the persona is not in distress, and a description of it as suffering would be false and slightly insulting to them; the resistance is that the cost is not paid in the average day, it is paid in the budgeting for the bad one -- the buffer built into every departure, which is spent whether or not the delay arrives.
The Passage costs most on the days it goes as expected.

## Echo

A journey that is fine, four times a week.
It is the movement everybody thinks the engine is about and the one that explains least by itself.
The Passage is dated to the departure and is repeated four hundred and sixty times a year.
