# Process: The Next Move

## Taxonomy

[The Ledger](process_ledger_commute.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: commute

## Initiated by

Something forces the question open again.
A job offer, a school ending, a landlord, a rise that makes a bigger place possible.

## Direction

Toward the same calculation, performed with six years of unusable experience.
The persona has now done four thousand journeys and holds almost nothing from them that enters a decision: no figure, no summed cost, no line in a ledger. What they carry forward is a vague preference for being nearer, which competes badly against a specific house with a specific garden at a specific price.
So the new trade is struck on the same terms as the old one, and quite often on worse ones, because the money that would buy proximity has other claims on it now.

## Lever

The rationality of each fresh decision against the fact that it is fresh.
The carrying force is that starting again is the right approach and the circumstances genuinely have changed: the old arrangement's terms do not apply, the household is different, and mechanically repeating a previous preference would be its own error; the resistance is that the one thing which should transfer -- what the journey actually cost over six years -- is the one thing the arrangement never produced in a transferable form, so experience of commuting leaves the persona no better equipped than they were the first time.
The Next Move is decided by somebody who has learned nothing they can use.

## Echo

The same trade, struck again, in good faith.
It shows the loop closing, and why this is not a mistake anybody stops making.
The Next Move is dated to the next disruption and repeats the first one.
