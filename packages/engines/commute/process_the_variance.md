# Process: The Variance

## Taxonomy

[The Passage](process_passage_commute.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: commute

## Initiated by

The journey takes thirty-five minutes, or fifty-five, and there is no telling which.
The same road, the same train, the same departure time.

## Direction

Toward a cost paid daily for an event that happens occasionally.
Because the bad day cannot be predicted, it has to be prepared for on every day. The persona leaves early enough to survive the worst plausible version, and on the ordinary morning arrives with twenty minutes of nothing, which is time removed from the house rather than given to anything.
The delay itself is the smaller part of what unpredictability costs. The buffer is the larger part, and it is spent in full every single morning.

## Lever

The prudence of leaving early against what the prudence consumes.
The carrying force is that the buffer is correct and abandoning it would be worse: arriving late repeatedly has consequences, the persona is managing a real risk sensibly, and anybody advising them to cut it fine would be giving bad advice; the resistance is that the buffer converts an occasional event into a daily charge, so a journey with a long tail extracts its cost from every morning including the ones on which nothing goes wrong.
The Variance charges every day for the worst one.

## Echo

Twenty minutes spent this morning on a delay that did not happen.
It shows why duration is the wrong measure of a commute.
The Variance is dated to the timetable and is not visible in any average.
