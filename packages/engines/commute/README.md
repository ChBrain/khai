# Commute

Commute as process: a cost agreed once and incurred every morning, read on what the trade did afterwards rather than on whether it was sensible -- not the return of feeling to a baseline after a change, which is hedonic-adaptation's, and not the wait imposed by an institution, which is waiting's -- across four movements: the trade (an extra room that keeps its value and loses its voice, minutes priced at the journey's best day, and one calculation made in advance and never checked against the outcome), the passage (variance that charges every day for the worst one, no control that keeps the effort and removes the option, and a hollow hour that is worth having and not worth what it costs), the settling (a house absorbed entirely intact and no longer counting for anything, a drive unabsorbed because of what its edge is made of, and a widening gap that is a deterioration made entirely of adaptation), and the ledger (an invisible gain that is a good argument with nothing behind it, an unattributed cost paid in a currency it is not recorded in, and a next move decided by somebody who has learned nothing they can use) -- with the twist that everything the commute was traded for stops being felt, and the commute does not.
