# Process: The No Control

## Taxonomy

[The Passage](process_passage_commute.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: commute

## Initiated by

Something stops, and there is nothing to do.
No route around it, no way to hurry it, no information about how long, and no way to leave.

## Direction

Toward effort with no available action attached to it.
The persona is alert, invested in an outcome, and unable to affect it -- which is the combination that reliably produces strain in every setting anybody has measured, from open-plan noise to hospital wards to trains.
It is also the reason the same delay is quite different from the driver's seat and the passenger's, and different again for somebody who can work on the train and somebody who cannot get a seat.

## Lever

The reality of the constraint against the effort it does not switch off.
The carrying force is that the persona is behaving perfectly sensibly and there is genuinely nothing to be done: the road is closed, the signal has failed, the correct response is to wait, and somebody who tried to force it would only be worse off; the resistance is that the body does not stand down merely because action is unavailable, so the persona is spending the arousal of a problem with none of the discharge of doing anything about it.
The No Control keeps the effort and removes the option.

## Echo

Somebody working hard at nothing.
It shows which feature of a journey the persona will not get used to.
The No Control is dated to the stoppage and is over when the train moves.
