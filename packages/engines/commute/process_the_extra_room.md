# Process: The Extra Room

## Taxonomy

[The Trade](process_trade_commute.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: commute

## Initiated by

The distance bought something specific.
A third bedroom, a garden, a catchment area, thirty thousand off the price, or a job that would not have come otherwise.

## Direction

Toward a gain that is real, sizeable, and durable in every sense except one.
The room does not shrink. The school does not get worse. The salary arrives every month. Nothing about the benefit is illusory, and a persona who claimed afterwards to have been cheated would be wrong about the facts.
What happens to it is not loss. It is that a room somebody has lived in for eight months is simply the size their house is.

## Lever

The permanence of the asset against the impermanence of noticing it.
The carrying force is that the gain is substantial and keeps working whether or not anybody feels it: the family really does have the space, the child really is at that school, the household really is not overstretched, and those facts continue to be true on the days nobody thinks about them; the resistance is that being pleased about it has a half-life measured in months, so the side of the trade that justified everything becomes the least available thing in the arrangement.
The Extra Room keeps its value and loses its voice.

## Echo

A benefit still fully present and no longer heard from.
It shows what the whole bargain rests on and why nobody can find it later.
The Extra Room is dated to the move and is unremarkable by the following spring.
