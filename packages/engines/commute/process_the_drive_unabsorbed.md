# Process: The Drive Unabsorbed

## Taxonomy

[The Settling](process_settling_commute.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: commute

## Initiated by

The journey does not become normal in the way the house did.
Six years in, the bad morning is still a bad morning.

## Direction

Toward the one element of the arrangement that adaptation does not take away.
Some of the commute does settle: the route becomes automatic, the platform is known, the persona no longer thinks about how to do it. What does not settle is the variance and the powerlessness, and those are the parts that were doing the damage.
This is not a claim about weakness. It is the ordinary finding that what people habituate to is the predictable and the controllable, and a delayed train is neither.

## Lever

The genuine familiarity of the route against the parts familiarity cannot reach.
The carrying force is that the persona really has got used to most of it and is not suffering daily: the journey is routine, they have a system, they would describe it as fine if asked, and any account of them as ground down by it would be wrong on most days; the resistance is that the specific features that make it costly are the specific features nothing habituates to, so the cost persists at close to full strength for as long as the arrangement lasts.
The Drive Unabsorbed keeps its edge because of what its edge is made of.

## Echo

A journey done a thousand times and still not neutral.
It shows why length of exposure does not solve this and sometimes worsens it.
The Drive Unabsorbed is dated to every morning and does not trend.
