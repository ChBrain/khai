# Process: The Widening Gap

## Taxonomy

[The Settling](process_settling_commute.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: commute

## Initiated by

The two sides of the trade separate.
Not because either changed, but because one is still being felt.

## Direction

Toward an arrangement that gets worse over time without anything getting worse.
At the moment of the decision the two halves were roughly balanced, and the persona weighed them honestly. A year later the gain has gone quiet and the cost has not, so the same arrangement, unchanged in every particular, is now experienced as a worse deal than the one that was agreed.
Nothing has deteriorated. The house is the same house and the road is the same road.

## Lever

The stability of the facts against the drift of the experience.
The carrying force is that the persona is not being irrational and their sense of it is accurate: they really are getting less out of this than they were, that really is a change worth registering, and telling them the deal is the same as it was would be true and useless; the resistance is that nothing in the world has moved, so there is no event to point at, no party to renegotiate with, and no obvious moment at which anybody was entitled to feel differently.
The Widening Gap is a deterioration made entirely of adaptation.

## Echo

The same bargain, worth less every year, by nobody's action.
It shows what the engine is actually tracking, which is a rate rather than a state.
The Widening Gap is dated from the move and does not stop opening.
