# Instructions: Commute

How a Playwright wires the commute engine into a play. You wire by linking the play's own content to the engine's files; the engine files stay untouched, they are canon. The Roadie plumbs the engine's law into Instructions Knowledge on deploy. Authoring guidance, not runtime content, and it does not go on tour.

## Human

- The human makes the trade sensible and the house genuinely good, because the engine needs no bad decision and stops working the moment it gets one. Keep the journey ordinary and mostly fine, since the cost is paid in the buffer rather than in the disaster. And decide what the household blames a difficult evening on, because the journey will not be among the candidates and that absence is the engine.

## Agent

- From the play's own content, a persona links under Projection where they stand to it: what the distance bought, how long the journey takes on a bad day, whether they can do anything while making it, what they say about it when asked, and what they blame the evening on. Read the engine on what the trade did afterwards, never on whether it was sensible. Keep it the arrangement: the return of feeling to a baseline is hedonic-adaptation's, the imposed wait is waiting's, and the load on the body is stress's. You link to the engine, you never edit it.

## Collaboration

- The return of feeling toward a baseline, what attending to a pleasure does, and what survives of a good thing are hedonic-adaptation's, savoring's, and the lasting composite's; delay as a technique of institutions is waiting's; the physiological load and what restores it are stress's, coping's, and restorative-environment's; what a home comes to mean is place-attachment's and the dwelling composite's; the effort bargain at the other end is employment's and org's; the stretch of a duration and what an unoccupied mind does are time-perception's, boredom's, and mind-wandering's. Timetables, fares, road capacity, planning policy, and housing markets are the trade and set the format. Whether a production's persona is defending the journey or enduring it, and whether a difficult evening came from the office or the road, are the team's review.

## Knowledge

- A commute is a cost agreed once and incurred every morning: a gap between home and work accepted as part of an arrangement, and what happens to each side of that bargain over time. It is not hedonic-adaptation's return of feeling to a baseline, not waiting's imposed delay, and not stress's load on the body. It wires on a persona at Projection and runs in four movements. The trade compares something inspected with something estimated: months go into the house, which is walked through and imagined in, while the journey is a number on a map produced for its best day, and the calculation is made once by people who have never done it and is never checked afterwards, because nothing in the arrangement supplies an occasion to check. The passage costs most on the days it goes as expected, since an unpredictable journey has to be budgeted for its worst plausible version and the buffer is spent every morning whether or not the delay arrives; what makes it expensive is impedance and the absence of any available action rather than distance, and the hollow hour between two roles is genuinely worth something and is being bought at ninety minutes a day. The settling erases one side of the bargain: the extra room is absorbed into the baseline within a season and remains entirely intact while no longer counting for anything, and the variance and the powerlessness are exactly the conditions habituation does not reach, so the same arrangement is worth less every year with nothing in the world having moved. The engine's twist is at the root: everything the commute was traded for stops being felt, and the commute does not.

## System

- Do make the trade defensible. The house is good, the school is right, the money works, and any outsider examining the decision would call it sound.
- Do give the journey a number and let the number be optimistic. Thirty-five minutes on the map, and the household planning its mornings against it.
- Do spend the buffer on an ordinary day. They leave early for a delay that does not come and arrive with twenty minutes of nothing.
- Do stage one stoppage with nothing to do. No information, no alternative, no way to leave, and a persona being perfectly reasonable inside it.
- Do let somebody defend the journey sincerely. It is the only time nobody wants anything from them, and they mean it.
- Do let the house stop being mentioned. Nobody says the room is lovely any more, and nothing is wrong with the room.
- Do charge the evening to something else. Work was hard, the weather is bad, they slept badly -- all plausible, all sometimes true, and the one daily input goes unnamed.
- Do close on the next move rather than this one. The same calculation, made again in good faith, by somebody carrying four thousand journeys they cannot put a figure on.
- Do not model the baseline return (hedonic-adaptation, lasting, savoring), the imposed wait (waiting), the bodily load (stress, coping, restorative-environment), the bond to the home (place-attachment, dwelling), the job (employment, org), or how the time feels (time-perception, boredom, mind-wandering). Do not make the journey stand for a theme; that is authored key, the play's pitch. Do not edit the engine's files; wire only from the play's side.
