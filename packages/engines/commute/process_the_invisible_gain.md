# Process: The Invisible Gain

## Taxonomy

[The Ledger](process_ledger_commute.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: commute

## Initiated by

The benefit is banked rather than received.
It sits in an asset, a postcode, a monthly figure -- all of them permanent and none of them events.

## Direction

Toward a return that cannot be drawn on to justify anything.
A gain that has been absorbed into the baseline still exists, and it cannot be produced on demand. Asked whether it was worth it, the persona can recite the reasons and cannot feel them, which is not the same as having them available.
What they can feel, immediately and without effort, is this morning.

## Lever

The soundness of the reasons against their unavailability at the moment of asking.
The carrying force is that the reasons are good and remain good: the house did what it was bought to do, the choice would look correct to any outsider examining it, and a persona who abandoned the arrangement on a feeling would probably be making a worse decision than the one they made; the resistance is that every occasion on which the trade comes up for judgement is an occasion on which one side is vivid and the other is a list, and lists lose.
The Invisible Gain is a good argument with nothing behind it.

## Echo

A justification that has to be recited rather than felt.
It shows why the arrangement cannot be defended and cannot be faulted.
The Invisible Gain is dated to the move and is unavailable ever after.
