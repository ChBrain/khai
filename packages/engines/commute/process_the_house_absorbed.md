# Process: The House Absorbed

## Taxonomy

[The Settling](process_settling_commute.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: commute

## Initiated by

The extra room becomes the size of the house.
The garden becomes the view. The salary becomes the number in the account.

## Direction

Toward a gain that is fully present and no longer produces anything.
Nothing has been taken. The persona could point at every element of what the distance bought, and would defend all of it. What has stopped is the arriving -- the small daily registration that this is better than what came before, which is what a gain feels like and which does not survive familiarity.
The house is now simply where they live, which is the whole point of a house and is also the end of it as evidence.

## Lever

The completeness of the benefit against the disappearance of its signal.
The carrying force is that this is not a loss and describing it as one would be self-indulgent: the space is used every day, the school is attended, the mortgage is affordable, and the family is materially better off in ways they would fight to keep; the resistance is that the argument for the arrangement was made in the currency of how it would feel, and that currency has stopped being issued while the bill in the other one keeps arriving.
The House Absorbed is entirely intact and no longer counts for anything.

## Echo

A benefit that can be pointed at and not felt.
It shows the side of the trade the persona will be unable to produce when they need it.
The House Absorbed is dated to about eight months in.
