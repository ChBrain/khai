# Process: The Unattributed Cost

## Taxonomy

[The Ledger](process_ledger_commute.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: commute

## Initiated by

The bad evening is charged to something else.
To work, to the weather, to how badly the persona sleeps, to the person they live with.

## Direction

Toward a cost that is real, daily, and never posted to the right account.
The journey does not present a bill. It produces a persona who arrives home with less patience than they would otherwise have had, and the shortfall shows up in whatever happens next -- which is a conversation, a child, an evening, a Sunday.
Every one of those has its own plausible explanation available, and the journey has no advocate in the room.

## Lever

The plausibility of the other explanations against where the shortfall came from.
The carrying force is that the other explanations are frequently correct and the persona is not being obtuse: work is demanding, the weather is bad, sleep is poor, and attributing every difficult evening to a train would be a strange and self-serving habit; the resistance is that the one input present on every single occasion is the one nobody names, so a cost incurred four hundred and sixty times a year is distributed across explanations that vary daily and never accumulates anywhere.
The Unattributed Cost is paid in a currency it is not recorded in.

## Echo

A shortfall that always turns out to be about something else.
It shows why the arrangement produces no evidence against itself.
The Unattributed Cost is dated to every arrival and is filed under other headings.
