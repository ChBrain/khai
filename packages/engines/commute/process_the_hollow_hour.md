# Process: The Hollow Hour

## Taxonomy

[The Passage](process_passage_commute.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: commute

## Initiated by

The time is not work and is not home.
And it is not rest, because it has to be steered.

## Direction

Toward a portion of the day that belongs to no part of the persona's life.
It is not paid, so the employer does not own it. It is not chosen, so it is not leisure. It cannot be spent with anybody, and on most modes it cannot be spent on anything requiring both hands or continuous attention.
Some of it is genuinely useful -- a podcast, a book, a decompression between two roles that would otherwise run into each other -- and everybody in this arrangement has learned to say so.

## Lever

The real value of a buffer between two lives against what the buffer is charged at.
The carrying force is that the transition is worth something and is often defended sincerely: personas describe the journey as the only time nobody wants anything from them, the shift from one role to another needs somewhere to happen, and a commute abolished overnight is not straightforwardly a gain, which anybody who has worked from a bedroom can confirm; the resistance is that the buffer is being bought at ninety minutes a day and nobody chose that price for it, so a genuine benefit is being used to justify a quantity it never justified.
The Hollow Hour is worth having and not worth what it costs.

## Echo

Time that is nobody's, defended by the person spending it.
It shows why the arrangement is so rarely described as a burden by the people inside it.
The Hollow Hour is dated to the journey and is counted as neither work nor rest.
