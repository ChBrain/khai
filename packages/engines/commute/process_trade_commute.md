# Process: The Trade

## Taxonomy

[Commute](process_commute.md), the calculation made once.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: commute

## Initiated by

Somebody chooses where to live, or where to work, and a distance falls out of it.
The Trade is three things arriving together: [an extra room](process_the_extra_room.md) that is what the distance bought, [minutes priced](process_the_minutes_priced.md) at their best case, and [one calculation](process_the_one_calculation.md) performed by people who have never done the journey.

## Direction

Toward a decision that is genuinely careful and structurally unable to be accurate.
The comparison is between two things of different kinds: a place, which is inspected, walked through, imagined in, and discussed for weeks; and a journey, which is a number on a map.
Everybody knows the number. Almost nobody has experienced what the number is like on a wet Tuesday in February with a delay at the junction.

## Lever

The seriousness of the deliberation against the difference between the two things being compared.
The carrying force is that the deliberation is real and usually thorough: households spend months on this, they view the house repeatedly, they check the timetable, they drive the route once at a weekend, and the resulting choice is defensible on every ground they had available; the resistance is that one side of the comparison is a place they have stood in and the other is an abstraction, so the care goes almost entirely into the half that will stop mattering.
The Trade compares something inspected with something estimated.

## Echo

A decision taken well, on unequal information.
It is the movement everybody remembers making and nobody revisits.
The Trade is dated to the offer and is not reopened for years.
