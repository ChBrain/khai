# Process: The Minutes Priced

## Taxonomy

[The Trade](process_trade_commute.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: commute

## Initiated by

The journey is given a number.
Thirty-five minutes, says the map, on a Sunday, with no roadworks.

## Direction

Toward a figure that describes the journey's best day and is used as its ordinary one.
The number is an average at best and a free-flow estimate at worst, and it is the only quantity in the whole decision. So it does the work of a description: forty minutes sounds like forty minutes, and the household budgets its mornings against it.
What the number cannot carry is the shape of the distribution, which is where the difficulty lives.

## Lever

The necessity of a single figure against everything a single figure omits.
The carrying force is that a number is what a comparison needs and there is no better one available: the household has to weigh the journey against the price somehow, the estimate is honestly produced, and a distribution of travel times would be unusable even if anybody published it; the resistance is that the summary quietly drops the variance, so the household is planning around a mean and will be living with a tail.
The Minutes Priced describe the journey on its best day.

## Echo

A figure that will be wrong in one direction only.
It shows where the estimate goes astray, which is not in the length.
The Minutes Priced are dated to the search and are quoted for years afterwards.
