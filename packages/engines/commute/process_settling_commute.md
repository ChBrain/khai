# Process: The Settling

## Taxonomy

[Commute](process_commute.md), what fades and what does not.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: commute

## Initiated by

Months pass and the arrangement stops being new.
The Settling is what that does to each side: [a house absorbed](process_the_house_absorbed.md) into the baseline, [a drive unabsorbed](process_the_drive_unabsorbed.md), and [a widening gap](process_the_widening_gap.md) between them.

## Direction

Toward two halves of one bargain adapting at completely different rates.
Feeling returns toward a baseline after almost any change in circumstance, and it does so reliably: a bigger house, a better school, a higher salary are all absorbed within a year or so, which is a well-measured and generally benign fact about people.
What the same literature also finds is that the absorption is uneven. Conditions that are noisy, unpredictable, and outside the persona's control are the ones it handles worst, and a commute is made of exactly those.

## Lever

The mercy of adaptation against its refusal to apply evenly.
The carrying force is that adaptation is the reason life is bearable and nobody would trade it away: it takes the edge off losses as well as gains, it is what lets a persona absorb bad news and keep working, and a mind that felt every improvement permanently would feel every deterioration permanently too; the resistance is that it does not act on both halves of this particular trade, so the arrangement the persona agreed to is not the arrangement they end up living in, and neither half of the change is announced.
The Settling erases one side of the bargain and leaves the other standing.
The general return to a baseline is hedonic-adaptation's; this reads only the case where it works on one side of a trade.

## Echo

A deal that has quietly become a different deal.
It is the movement that does the engine's work, and it happens over about eighteen months with nothing to mark it.
The Settling is dated from the move and is complete before anybody notices it starting.
