# Process: Maturity

## Taxonomy

[Succession](process_succession.md), the mode of the mature community the sequence tends to.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: succession

## Initiated by

The sequence reaches a community that renews itself in place rather than giving way to another, and the march settles.
Maturity starts where succession stops advancing -- the old-growth forest, the climax prairie, the community whose own seedlings grow up beneath it and replace it in kind -- the stage that is not a rung to the next but the top of the sequence for this ground under this climate.
It is the settled mode: the mature, self-perpetuating community the whole reclaiming was tending toward.

## Direction

Toward the self-renewing community that holds the ground indefinitely.
Maturity holds the place in a standing community that reproduces itself -- the shade-tolerant trees whose young grow up in their own shade, the deep-rooted stability, the closed canopy, the accumulated soil and dead wood, the richness that only long undisturbed time builds.
It moves the place from changing to holding: where progression turned over stage by stage, maturity persists, renewing in place, until a disturbance resets the whole sequence to the start.

## Lever

The community's power to renew itself against the disturbance that would reset it and the doubt that it is fixed at all.
The carrying force is self-replacement -- the mature community's own regeneration beneath itself, the stability that outlasts the stages before it; the resistance is disturbance, which no maturity is proof against, and the truth that the end-state is a tendency, not a destiny: the same ground under the same climate may settle differently by the chance of what arrived and when.
Left long undisturbed, the place holds its mature community; but no climax is permanent, and the next fire or flood or fall returns it to bare ground and the sequence begins again.

## Echo

The place holding its mature community, until the next disturbance.
Maturity leaves the place at the top of its sequence -- the old-growth, the climax, the deep stable community that says the reclaiming has run its course for now -- and holds it there, renewing in place, the richest and most enduring state the ground reaches.
It is the reclaiming's destination and not its end: the mature community stands until the wheel turns again, the disturbance returns, and the whole sequence -- pioneer, progression, maturity -- runs once more.
Maturity is dated from the settling, the long undisturbed time it took the ground to reach its fullest community.
