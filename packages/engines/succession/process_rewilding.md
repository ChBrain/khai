# Process: Rewilding

## Taxonomy

[Succession](process_succession.md), the mode where the wild takes back what was human.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: succession

## Initiated by

The human hand withdraws from a place it held, and the living begins to take it back.
Rewilding starts wherever people leave -- the field left fallow, the lot abandoned, the factory shut, the town emptied, the whole city given up -- and the succession that was held off by use resumes on ground that was, until now, kept human.
It is succession read on the human's own works: not bare rock reclaimed but the road, the wall, the roof taken by the same sequence, the world going on without us.

## Direction

Over the human place, dismantling and greening it stage by stage.
Rewilding runs the reclaiming through what people built -- the weed in the pavement crack, the sapling in the gutter, the root that lifts the road, the vine that pulls the wall, the forest closing over the street -- each stage taking the built place further back toward ground.
It moves the place from human to wild: the maintained held open by the hand, and the moment the hand is gone, the living pressing in from the edges and up through the seams until the human work is a ruin in a wood.

## Lever

The living's patience against the human maintenance that held it off.
The carrying force is that every human place is kept human only by constant work -- the weeding, the paving, the repair -- and the living is always waiting at the edge; the resistance was the maintenance, and once it stops there is nothing left to hold the wild back, so the reclaiming that takes a wild place in decades takes a human one in the same time, or faster for the cracks the building gives it.
Keep up the work and the place stays human; drop it, and the succession that never left resumes, and the built place is on its way back to forest.

## Echo

The human place taken back by the living, and read two ways at once.
It shows a place returning to the wild -- the green through the ruin, the forest in the street, the world without us -- and carries beneath it the land's-forces family's keystone twist, the sibling of the whole family's ambivalence: that the wild retaking the human place is not only the human's loss but the land's return, the ruin of the one and the reclaiming of the other in a single green advance.
Rewilding is dated from the withdrawal, the moment the hand fell and the living began to take the place home.
