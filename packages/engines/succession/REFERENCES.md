# Succession: Reference

## Line of Work

Succession as **process**: the biological reclaiming of a place -- life returning in an
ordered sequence once the ground is bared or the human hand withdraws, from the first
pioneers on the raw ground, through the progression of communities, to the mature
community the sequence tends toward, and the wild taking back what was human. It wires on
a place at Shown, the place's physical self-presentation, because the green coming back --
the weed in the crack, the scrub on the burn, the forest closing over the street -- is
what a reclaimed place puts in front of whoever enters. The domain does not model the
disturbance that cleared the ground -- the quake, the burn, the flood, the wearing --
which is geomorphology's, fire's, hydrology's, and erosion's; nor the material rot that
precedes it, which is decay's; nor the owner's withdrawal that opens the built place,
which is dereliction's; nor the climate that sets what can grow, which is climate's. It
models the life that returns, in the order it returns, and it is the land's-forces
family's answer to all the rest: the reclaiming that follows every ruin.

The spine is the study of how a cleared place fills back in -- and the long argument over
whether that filling is an ordered march to a fixed end or a looser, chancier affair.
Cowles read the sequence first on the Lake Michigan dunes, the vegetation changing in a
readable order from the raw sand inland. Clements made it a law: succession as a
directional, deterministic climb to a single climax community set by the climate, the
whole community a kind of superorganism developing toward maturity. Gleason answered that
the reclaiming is individualistic and chancy -- each species for itself, the end-state a
tendency, not a destiny. And Weisman carried the reading to the human's own works: the
world without us, the built place reclaimed by the living the moment the hand withdraws.
So the engine gives succession three stages of the sequence -- pioneer, progression,
maturity -- and keeps a fourth, rewilding, the same reclaiming read on the human place, the
wild's return that is the human's loss.

## Restrictions

What the engine refuses to model, and to whom it delegates.

- **The disturbance that cleared the ground (geomorphology, fire, hydrology, erosion)**:
  Succession is the life that returns _after_ the ground is bared; the baring itself is the
  other forces'. The quake and the eruption that stripped it are geomorphology's, the burn
  is fire's, the flood is hydrology's, the wearing is erosion's. Those clear the ground;
  succession is the reclaiming that follows. Fire's renewal reads the burn _as_ the trigger;
  succession is the returning life the trigger releases.
- **The material rot, and the owner's withdrawal (decay, dereliction)**: When a built place
  is abandoned, three forces run in a chain: dereliction is the owner ceasing to maintain,
  decay is the fabric rotting, and succession is the living reclaiming. Decay is the matter
  breaking down; succession is the life moving in. The roof rots (decay) and the sapling
  grows through it (succession); the two meet in the ruin but are not the same force.
- **The climate that sets what can grow (climate)**: Climate sets the destination -- which
  mature community a place's regime allows, whether the sequence ends in forest, prairie, or
  tundra; succession is the actual sequence of getting there. Climate is why the climax is
  what it is; succession is the march toward it. Climate sets the endpoint; succession is
  the journey, and the journey's chances (Gleason) can still divert it.
- **A persona's feeling, and the reclaiming as authored symbol**: How the returning green
  makes a persona feel -- the peace of the overgrown ruin, the unease of the retaken city --
  is the persona's own engines'; succession delivers the reclaiming, not the feeling. And
  the wild's return made to **stand for** a persona's own recovery or a civilization's fall,
  the forest reclaiming the ruin that mirrors a healing or a doom, is refused: that is
  authored key, the play's pitch, not the force on the place.
- **The stage itself is not engine content**: The engine declares the four stages exist;
  which one a given place shows lives on that place, linked under its Shown chapter. The
  engine refuses to fix a place's reclaiming for it.
