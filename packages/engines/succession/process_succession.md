# Process: Succession

## Taxonomy

Regrowth.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: succession

## Initiated by

The ground is bared, or the hand that held it back withdraws, and life begins to return.
Succession starts wherever there is ground for the taking -- the burn cooled, the flood's silt settled, the field left fallow, the lot abandoned, the roof fallen in -- and the first life reaches it and takes hold.
Every cleared place is already being reclaimed; succession is that reclaiming, the living coming back in order, the ground that will not stay bare.

## Direction

Onto the cleared ground, and forward through the sequence toward maturity.
Four modes work the reclaiming: [Pioneer](process_pioneer.md) is the first life on the raw ground, [Progression](process_progression.md) the ordered march of communities each readying the next, [Maturity](process_maturity.md) the mature community the sequence tends to, and [Rewilding](process_rewilding.md) the twist -- the wild taking back what was human.
A place does not green evenly or at one pace: the wound heals fast where the soil is left, slow where the ground is raw, and the sequence stalls, restarts, or diverts with every fresh disturbance.

## Lever

The living's drive to occupy the ground against everything that keeps it bare.
The carrying force is life's reach for open ground -- the seed on the wind, the runner, the spore, the fast colonizer that takes any opening; the resistance is whatever holds the ground open -- the repeated fire, the mowing, the poison, the paving, the human hand that keeps a place from returning.
Withdraw the disturbance and the green comes; keep disturbing, and the sequence is held at an early stage, the meadow kept from the wood, the lot from the forest -- but let the hand fall and the reclaiming resumes where it left off.

## Echo

The place greened, healed, or wholly retaken.
Succession does not close like a disturbance passing; it runs on as long as the ground is left, handing each stage to the next until the mature community holds -- and, on the human's ground, until the wild has taken it back.
This is the land's-forces family's answer to all the rest: the ground the earth broke, the fire cleared, the water drowned, and the wearing lowered is the ground life returns to -- the reclaiming that follows every ruin.
The place is dated from the disturbance it is recovering from, and read by how far the green has come back over it.
