# Process: Pioneer

## Taxonomy

[Succession](process_succession.md), the mode of the first life on the raw ground.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: succession

## Initiated by

Bare ground opens, and the first life reaches it and takes hold where nothing else yet can.
Pioneer starts on the rawest ground -- the fresh lava, the burned earth, the scraped soil, the crack in the pavement, the flood's new silt -- taken by the colonizers built for it: the lichen on the rock, the moss, the fireweed, the windborne weed, the fast and the tough.
It is the vanguard mode: the life that asks least of the ground and gives most to it, arriving first where the sequence has nothing to build on yet.

## Direction

Onto the open ground, spreading fast and readying it for what follows.
Pioneer takes the disturbed ground quickly -- the r-strategists that grow fast, seed heavily, and die young -- and in living and dying begins to make the soil that was not there: the lichen breaking the rock, the weed's litter feeding the first thin earth, the nitrogen-fixer enriching the raw ground.
It moves the place from bare to barely living, and in doing so changes the ground enough that the next community, which the raw ground could not have held, can come.

## Lever

The colonizer's toughness against the hostility of the raw ground.
The carrying force is the pioneer's fitness for the opening -- the tolerance of sun, drought, and poor ground, the fast growth, the reach of the windborne seed; the resistance is the rawness itself -- the bare rock, the sterile ash, the ground with no soil, no shade, no shelter, that only the toughest life can take.
Where the ground is hardest the pioneers are fewest and slowest; where the disturbance left some soil, they flood in and the reclaiming leaps ahead.

## Echo

The raw ground first taken, and readied for the rest.
Pioneer leaves the place with its first thin skin of life -- the lichen crust, the weed field, the fireweed flush -- and hands the ground on changed: soil begun, shade started, the way opened for the community that comes next.
It is the reclaiming's foothold: the least life on the worst ground, doing the work that lets the sequence begin, spent and given way to as it makes the place fit for its successors.
Pioneer is dated from the first taking, the moment the bare ground became living ground again.
