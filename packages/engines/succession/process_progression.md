# Process: Progression

## Taxonomy

[Succession](process_succession.md), the mode of the ordered march of communities.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: succession

## Initiated by

The pioneers have readied the ground, and one community begins to give way to the next.
Progression starts once the raw ground is no longer raw -- the soil begun, the shade started -- and the sequence of communities gets underway: the weeds giving to the grasses, the grasses to the shrubs, the shrubs to the first trees, each stage growing on what the last one made.
It is the sequence mode: not the first taking nor the final holding but the ordered turnover between, the seres the place runs through on its way to maturity.

## Direction

Forward through the stages, each community changing the ground and yielding to its successor.
Progression moves the place up the sequence -- each stage shading, enriching, and sheltering the ground until it favours the next community over itself, and is replaced by what it made possible: the sun-loving pioneers shaded out by the shrubs they let grow, the shrubs overtopped by the trees they nursed.
It moves the place toward the mature community not in a leap but in a march, each stage a rung, the whole a directional change that runs as long as the ground is left undisturbed.

## Lever

Each community's changing of the ground against its own tenure on it.
The carrying force is that each stage alters the ground -- more soil, more shade, more shelter -- in ways that favour the next community and disfavour itself, so the sequence advances by each stage preparing its own replacement; the resistance is disturbance, which knocks the sequence back a stage or to the start, and the pace of growth, which sets how fast the march can run.
Leave the ground and the progression climbs; disturb it and it restarts, so a place burned or ploughed every few years is held forever in an early stage, never reaching the wood.

## Echo

The place partway through its sequence, on its way to the mature community.
Progression leaves the place at whatever stage its history has brought it to -- the young scrub, the pole-wood, the thickening forest -- and hands the ground on toward maturity, each stage the ground the next is built on.
It is the reclaiming's middle: the visible march of a place healing, the field going to forest read in the stages standing on it, the sequence that says how far along the return has come.
Progression is dated from the sequence's run, the ordered turnover between the raw ground and the mature.
