# Process: Detonation

## Taxonomy

Detonics.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: detonation

## Initiated by

An explosive converts in a fraction of a millisecond, and the place receives the work not as heat over time but as a single driven shock.
A detonation begins where confinement meets explosive energy released in an instant, and the shock wave races outward faster than the speed of sound, compressing and shattering whatever it strikes on its way -- not burning but blasting, the pressure doing the work that fire takes time to do.
The detonation is the phenomenon the siting either permits or forbids, the force that writes itself into the place as craters, overpressure rings, exclusion lines, and the doctrine of standoff that holds it from inhabited ground.

## Direction

Outward in all directions from the point or zone of initiation, as expanding overpressure rings.
Five modes work the blast: [Shot](process_shot.md) is the controlled shot, [Sympathetic](process_sympathetic.md) the neighbour that goes because the first did, [Misfire](process_misfire.md) the armed shot that did not, [Uxo](process_uxo.md) the blast that waits decades in the ground, and [Breach](process_breach.md) the wall opened on purpose -- and in every mode the wave expands spherically unless terrain breaks it, rebounding and reinforcing where it meets obstacles.
The direction is the whole place, written at once, from the crater in at the point of release to the shock-damaged trees and shattered windows at the perimeter where the pressure wave's strength falls below the damage threshold.

## Lever

The confinement and proximity against the expansion and overpressure the shock delivers.
The carrying force is the explosive released instantaneously; the resistance is the distance and the exclusion that the siting imposes -- every metre of standoff carries the overpressure down by its own law, and the exclusion radius is the distance at which the shock is too weak to destroy or kill.
Bring the charge closer or the place closer and the damage rises without warning: the difference between a standing building and a ruined one is metres and doctrine, and both are decided before anyone approaches with the match.

## Echo

The place divided into shock-damaged, destroyed, and cleared zones, and the crater that marks the work done.
A detonation is read by its signature -- the shock wave's sound, a single bang louder than any fire, felt as overpressure, the sudden rupture that breaks windows at vast distances, the blast of displaced air that carries fragments and dust; the debris field maps the shock's strength and direction; the damage tiers -- standing but shattered, collapsed, or completely cleared to the crater -- show the intensity at each radius.
The detonation is dated from the instant of firing, the moment the explosive was detonated and the shock began its expansion, the moment everything changed all at once.
