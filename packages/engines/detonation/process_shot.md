# Process: Shot

## Taxonomy

[Detonation](process_detonation.md), the mode of the controlled shot, planned whole before the button is pressed.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: detonation

## Initiated by

The shot is a countdown to a prearranged detonation -- the shot planned, spaced, delayed, exclusion zones marked, personnel cleared, and the entire script written before the first charge is armed.
The detonation cannot be controlled once the shot is fired: the blast expands faster than sound, and at that speed nothing can be adjusted, nothing can be unshot, nothing can be redirected or stopped.
So the whole of the shot's craft is spent in the hours before the shot -- the site inspection, the charge placement, the spacing and timing that will break the rock or drop the building the right way, the exclusion radius where nothing living will stand.

## Direction

Inward through the shot sequence, then outward through the exclusion zone as the blast propagates.
The shot runs its own direction before the firing: the electronic detonators time their sequences in millisecond delay, the primer counts down from the shot timer, the devices are checked and rechecked for continuity -- this is the direction of command and control, the moment-by-moment state of readiness.
At the instant the firing circuit closes, the direction reverses: the blast expands outward and the only thing left is to watch the outcome and protect those outside the exclusion radius from its force.

## Lever

The precision of the charge placement and timing against the force released all at once.
The carrying force is the explosive load and its detonation sequence -- designed to break a specific rock face, to drop a building into its own footprint, to create a specific crater -- and the leverage is in the spacing and the delays that direct the force.
The resistance is silence: once the exclusion zone is declared and cleared, the wait begins, and at the moment of the shot all the planning is done, all the guesses are placed, and everything that will happen is already determined by the charges and the devices that lie beneath the surface or within the structure.

## Echo

The moment of the shot -- the siren's final wail, the silence, then the ground shaking and the debris cloud rising.
The shot is read by its signature -- the countdown and the siren that warns the exclusion zone is now absolute, the audio alarm that stops all work and turns all attention to the shot site, then at the moment scheduled the blast and the shockwave and the debris field rising into the air.
The shot is dated from the firing moment, the instant the detonator sequence was triggered and the blast began, the point past which nothing in the exclusion zone can move or be saved.
