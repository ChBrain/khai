# Process: Breach

## Taxonomy

[Detonation](process_detonation.md), the mode of the wall opened on purpose, the fall designed as carefully as the standing.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: detonation

## Initiated by

The breach is a detonation designed to create a specific outcome: a hole in a wall, the controlled fall of a building into a designated footprint, the opening of a mountain pass in mining or tunnelling, the rubble where there was once a standing structure.
The breach craft is the inverse of all other construction: while building works upward and outward with additions, the breach works downward and inward with removals, but both require equal precision in where the force is applied and how the structure responds.
The building that will stand for a century and the building that will fall in a specific direction both depend on understanding the load path, the structure, and the moment of failure -- one to prevent it and the other to cause it exactly where and when intended.

## Direction

Downward and inward into the structure, then outward as the rubble settles.
The breach is directed at the failure points of the building or barrier: the charge is placed at the base of a column to drop the floor above it, or through the roof to cascade the collapse downward, or at the perimeter of a mountain to open a passage through, and the direction of collapse or breach is dictated by where the charges are placed and their timing.
The rubble falls into the void left behind or stays within the demolished footprint, and the direction of the debris and dust cloud follows the structure's own geometry and the sequence of the detonations that brought it down.

## Lever

The precision of the charge placement and timing against the building's weight and geometry.
The carrying force is the explosive load placed at the critical points where the structure's own weight becomes the instrument of its fall; the resistance is in the design: every structural element must fail at the right moment so that the whole building falls as one unified event, not partially or unevenly.
The paradox is that the skill is all in the planning and placement -- once the detonation begins, nothing can be adjusted; the building either falls as designed or it fails catastrophically, and the precision that prevents a misfire is the same precision that ensures the breach goes as intended.

## Echo

The moment the charges fire, the structure shudders, the supports fail, and the building falls into itself.
The breach is read by its signature -- the final count and the blast as the charges fire in sequence, the building's sound as the structure fails -- the crack and groan as load redistributes, the roar as stories collapse and the dust cloud rises from the rubble, all over in seconds.
The breach is dated from the moment of detonation, the instant when the building transforms from standing to fallen, from a structure that took months or years to build into rubble that took seconds to create -- the moment that all the planning and precision converge into a single event that cannot be reversed.
