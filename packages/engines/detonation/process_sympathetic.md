# Process: Sympathetic Detonation

## Taxonomy

[Detonation](process_detonation.md), the mode of the unplanned chain, the neighbour that fires because the first did.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: detonation

## Initiated by

Sympathetic detonation begins where two explosive charges or stores lie close enough that the shock from one can trigger the other -- not by direct contact but by the pressure wave reaching the second item and initiating it without the control or foreknowledge that allows a planned sequence.
The sympathetic detonation is the catastrophe of proximity and confinement: a magazine or depot sited too close to another, or the shock wave reaching across open ground and reflecting off terrain to strike the next store, or fragments from the first blast flying far enough and fast enough to reach a sensitive item.
It is the reason every explosive store follows the quantity-distance rules, the spacing that says how far apart things must be held so that if one goes, it will not force the next to follow.

## Direction

Outward from the first blast to the second item, then outward from the second as its own detonation expands.
Sympathetic detonation multiplies the blast -- the first explosion goes, its shock reaches the second store, the second detonates, and now the place sees two explosions in rapid succession, their shock waves compounding and their debris fields overlapping to cover far more ground than either alone would.
The direction is a cascade of failure: one loss that should have stayed one instead becomes two or more, each one awakening the next, until the entire site is consumed in a chain of unplanned detonations.

## Lever

The siting and the spacing of the stores against the shock wave's reach.
The carrying force is the propagating shock from the first detonation; the resistance is the distance and the barriers that the quantity-distance doctrine provides -- every metre between stores reduces the overpressure the second item sees, and the quantity limits say how much explosive can be held at any single location.
The entire defence is built before the detonation: a magazine designed so that one loss stays one is a magazine sited and spaced so the shock wave never reaches the neighbour, so sympathetic detonation is impossible before it happens.

## Echo

The chain of blasts, each awakening the last, the debris field growing with each detonation.
Sympathetic detonation is read by its signature -- the first blast and then, in immediate succession, a second explosion, often louder and larger than the first as it adds its force; multiple shock waves and their overlapping debris fields; fragmentation patterns that show the blast wave's reflection and reinforcement.
Sympathetic detonation is dated from the first firing, but understood by the second -- the moment a siting error or a spacing violation transformed one loss into many, the instant the store design failed to isolate and keep the neighbour safe.
