# Position: Appearance-Contingency

## Taxonomy

[Body Image](position_body_image.md), the movement this is one form of.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: body-image

## Has

Self-worth staked on whether the body meets the appearance standard.
The persona arrives having organized a significant portion of their self-evaluation around the question of whether their body looks as it should: when it does, they are worth something; when it does not, their value as a person is in question.
This is Cash's contingency-of-self-worth applied to the appearance domain -- not merely caring about appearance but needing the body to meet the standard to feel adequate as a self.

## Orders

Protect the self-worth by managing the body's relationship to the standard.
The position turns every appearance-relevant event -- a weigh-in, a photograph, a comment, a mirror -- into a potential verdict on the self, not merely on the body.
The persona cannot receive appearance-related feedback as information about their looks; they receive it as information about their worth.

## Loses

The insulation that separates a bad appearance day from a bad self-day.
Without contingency, the perceived-vs-ideal gap is an aesthetic shortfall: uncomfortable, perhaps, but not a self-threat.
With contingency, every moment the gap is visible -- to the persona or to others -- is a moment the self is at risk, and the coping resources that would otherwise be free are consumed by managing that risk.

## Drives

Toward securing the self-worth by closing the gap or by preventing the gap from being seen.
When the body meets the standard, contingency produces a fragile self-esteem elevation that is quickly undermined by the next evaluation; when it does not, contingency produces a self-threat that other domains cannot easily compensate.
The self-worth stake is what transforms the body-image position from a preference gap into a clinical liability: it is the variable that the distress, restriction, and shame the other engines carry are amplified by, not caused by -- this engine supplies only the stake.
