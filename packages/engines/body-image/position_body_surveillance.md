# Position: Body Surveillance

## Taxonomy

[Body Image](position_body_image.md), the movement this is one form of.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: body-image

## Has

A habitual posture of monitoring the body's exterior from an imagined observer's viewpoint.
The persona arrives treating their own body as an object in a visual field -- not felt from inside but watched from outside, the way another person's body might be watched.
This is the attentional stance Fredrickson and Roberts identify as the proximate mechanism between cultural objectification and its psychological consequences: the observer's gaze has been taken up and directed inward.

## Orders

Keep a running audit of how the body appears.
The position turns the persona's attention outward onto themselves -- toward how the body looks at this moment, from this angle, under these conditions -- and runs the audit continuously rather than on occasion.
The monitoring is not motivated by pleasure in the body but by the need to know whether it is presenting acceptably to the imagined outside eye.

## Loses

Immersion in what the body is doing rather than how it appears.
A persona high on body surveillance cannot enter a scene in which the body is visible without some portion of attention redirected to its exterior; the flow states, physical concentration, and unselfconscious engagement that require full attentional absorption are interrupted by the audit.
The body is never simply lived; it is also managed.

## Drives

Toward maintaining control of the body's appearance in the imagined observer's field.
The habitual audit generates body shame when the appearance falls short of the standard, and drives the persona toward managing exposure -- what is worn, how the body is positioned, which angles are allowed -- before the imagined observer arrives at a verdict.
The shame the audit produces and the regulation it motivates are carried by the shame and regulation engines; this engine supplies only the watching.
