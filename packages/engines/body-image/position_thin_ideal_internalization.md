# Position: Thin-Ideal Internalization

## Taxonomy

[Body Image](position_body_image.md), the movement this is one form of.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: body-image

## Has

A socioculturally transmitted size-and-shape standard taken in as personally binding.
The persona arrives having absorbed the thin ideal -- the culturally circulated template of what a body should look like -- not merely as an observed norm but as their own standard: the one their body is measured against, the one that makes the gap real.
The internalization is the step from "this is what culture values" to "this is what I need my body to be."

## Orders

Hold the perceived body against the internalized template and read the distance as a deficiency to be resolved.
The position turns the persona toward cultural images, body comparisons, and media that reiterate the ideal -- not because they are sought but because the internalized standard makes them legible as evidence.
The more fully the ideal is owned, the more ordinary exposure to it functions as a measure rather than an observation.

## Loses

The position of receiving cultural beauty images as external facts about what others value.
The persona cannot encounter the thin ideal -- in advertising, social media, peers, or their own reflection -- without their own body being measured against it, because the standard is no longer out there but inside.
What a non-internalizing persona sees as a depiction of one cultural ideal, this persona experiences as a verdict on their own body.

## Drives

Toward meeting the internalized standard -- and, when the gap is felt as failure, toward the behaviors that promise to close it.
The internalized ideal is what makes the perceived-vs-ideal gap motivationally active: without a standard owned as one's own, the gap is an observation; with it, the gap is a demand.
The pressure it creates is the upstream condition for the restriction and comparison behaviors that the self-control and shame engines carry.
