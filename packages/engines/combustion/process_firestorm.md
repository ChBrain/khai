# Process: Firestorm

## Taxonomy

[Combustion](process_combustion.md), the phenomenon of coalesced fires drawing their own inflowing wind.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: combustion

## Initiated by

Many fires over a large area merge into one, and their combined column sucks in air from every side.
Firestorm starts when numerous simultaneous fires -- urban or wildland-urban -- coalesce, their enormous combined heat release generating a single giant convective column whose in-draft pulls air inward from all directions; ground-level temperatures in a developed firestorm reach 800-1000 C and above (Hamburg 1943, Dresden 1945, Peshtigo 1871).
It is the fire become self-organising: a stationary, self-sustaining system that makes its own hurricane.

## Direction

Inward and up, the in-draft feeding a fire that stays where it is until the fuel is gone.
Firestorm draws hurricane-force inflow winds -- often over 50 km/h, reported higher -- toward its centre, which makes it largely stationary and self-sustaining until the fuel is consumed; the violent indraft and oxygen consumption are lethal well beyond the flames.
The direction is a fixed inferno: not a front that passes but a system that holds, pulling the air of the whole district into itself.

## Lever

The self-made inflow field against any hope of escape "upwind."
The carrying force is a column strong enough to organise its own inflow; the resistance is total -- it is unsuppressable at scale and dictates its own weather. The in-draft prevents escape upwind (there is no upwind -- wind blows in from all sides), it asphyxiates and bakes shelters and cellars, and only fuel exhaustion or peripheral firebreaks end it.
You cannot outrun a wind that blows in from every direction; only the fuel running out brings it down.

## Echo

The sky-filling glow, the roar, the wind blowing toward the fire from all around.
Firestorm is read by its signature -- a sky-filling glow, a sustained deafening roar, a violent inward-blowing ground-level wind (counter-intuitively toward the fire), superheated air, widespread ember fallout, darkness under the smoke pall. It hands death beyond the flames, by heat and by the air pulled from the shelters.
Firestorm is dated from the coalescence, the moment the separate fires became one system that made its own wind.
