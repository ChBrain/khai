# Process: Cladding Fire

## Taxonomy

[Combustion](process_combustion.md), the compartment phenomenon of the building's envelope alight.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: combustion

## Initiated by

A room fire breaks out of its window and finds the face of the building itself combustible.
Cladding fire ignites a facade system -- polyethylene-cored aluminium composite panels, combustible insulation, the ventilated cavity of a rainscreen -- and the cavity's chimney draft drives flame up the face storeys per minute, re-entering through windows floor after floor; Grenfell Tower burned this way in 2017, seventy-two dead, its refurbished envelope carrying the fire past every internal compartment line.
It is the compartment fire inverted: the building's protection layer become its fuel, the fire on the outside of every wall at once.

## Direction

Up the face, around the corners, and back in at every floor.
Cladding fire runs vertically in the concealed cavity faster than it shows on the surface, sheds burning droplets of molten polymer that start new fires below, and turns a single-flat fire into a whole-building event in under an hour -- outrunning the compartmentation, the stairs, and the assumptions every internal system was designed on.
The direction is an outflanking: the fire bypassing the building's fire strategy by travelling on its skin.

## Lever

The envelope built to shed weather against the compartment doctrine it silently bypasses.
The carrying force is a continuous vertical fuel path -- the polymer core and the cavity draft -- wrapped around the whole structure; the resistance fails because the doctrine is inside-facing: stay-put advice, correct for a compartmented building, turns lethal when the fire is climbing the outside of every compartment, and hose streams reach neither the cavity nor the upper floors.
Trust the compartment lines and the facade goes around them; the fire was decided at specification, in the choice of panel, long before ignition.

## Echo

The blackened skeleton of a facade, the frame striped with burned-out floors.
Cladding fire is read by its signature -- flame racing the face in sheets, burning droplets raining, the cavity roaring, windows failing inward floor by floor -- and afterwards by the stripped elevation, the panels gone and every storey behind them torched from the outside in.
Cladding fire is dated from the breakout, the moment the room fire left its compartment for the skin of the building.
