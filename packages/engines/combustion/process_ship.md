# Process: Ship Fire

## Taxonomy

[Combustion](process_combustion.md), the conveyance phenomenon of the burning vessel at sea.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: combustion

## Initiated by

A fire starts inside a hull that is days from any help, and the crew are the only brigade there is.
Ship fire ignites in the enclosed decks of a vessel under way -- the ro-ro car deck packed bumper to bumper, the container stack whose manifest no one can fully verify, the engine room's fuel and heat -- and burns in steel compartments that hold the heat in and the firefighters out; the Felicity Ace burned and sank in 2022 with nearly four thousand vehicles, its EV batteries feeding a fire that resisted everything, the Fremantle Highway burned in 2023 with a crew death, the X-Press Pearl burned and sank off Sri Lanka in 2021 with its chemical cargo.
It is the compartment fire with no outside: the building is the lifeboat, and the fire is aboard it.

## Direction

Deck by deck through the hull, against a crew that cannot call anyone.
Ship fire spreads through open vehicle decks and ventilation runs, radiates through steel bulkheads to ignite the next compartment, and burns for days on cargo no one can reach -- while the response runs the ship's own sequence: crew attack, then fixed CO2 flooding of the sealed space, then boundary cooling of the surrounding steel, and, when that fails, abandonment and a salvage tug watching it burn.
The direction is inward and long: a fire fought from outside its own walls, in the middle of the sea, on the clock of the cargo's fuel.

## Lever

The sealed space the doctrine depends on against every reason to open it.
The carrying force is a dense fuel load in steel confinement far from help -- and, in the modern hold, traction batteries whose runaway carries its own oxygen and laughs at CO2; the resistance fails in the seal paradox: the fixed CO2 flooding that is the ship's main weapon works only while the space stays shut, so opening a deck to fight, ventilate, or verify re-arms the fire with air and spends the one extinguishing shot the ship carried.
Keep it sealed and cool the boundaries and the fire starves on schedule; open it once too early and the blanket is gone; and the battery cargo may burn through the blanket regardless -- then the sea, not the crew, decides.

## Echo

The gutted hull under tow, or the ship gone entirely.
Ship fire is read by its signature -- smoke streaming from a hull at sea, decks too hot to walk days later, the list as firefighting water accumulates, the tug standing off a ship abandoned to burn -- and afterwards by the salvage record: the cargo written off whole, the hull scrapped or on the bottom.
Ship fire is dated from the alarm at sea, the moment the crew learned the fire was aboard and no one else was coming.
