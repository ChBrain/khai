# Process: Flash Fire

## Taxonomy

[Combustion](process_combustion.md), the industrial phenomenon of a vapour cloud deflagrating without blast.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: combustion

## Initiated by

A drifting cloud of flammable vapour finds an ignition and a flame sweeps back through it in an instant.
Flash fire starts when a dispersed flammable vapour, gas, or mist cloud within its flammable limits ignites; a flame front sweeps back through the cloud to the source at a few to tens of metres per second, with a brief high radiant flux and low or negligible overpressure.
It is the cloud burning off: over in a fraction of a second to a few seconds, hazardous by its searing radiant heat, not by blast.

## Direction

Back through the cloud to the leak, and gone.
Flash fire's flame travels from the ignition point back to the source, consuming the cloud; its hazard is skin and respiratory radiant burns and the ignition of secondary fuels, not a pressure wave -- unless congestion or confinement accelerates it into a vapour cloud explosion.
The direction is a sweep and self-consumption: the cloud lights, burns through, and leaves little behind.

## Lever

The dispersed cloud against the speed that is over before anything can be done.
The carrying force is a flammable cloud waiting on a spark; the resistance fails because the event is too fast to fight -- over before suppression can act -- so the defence is prevention: gas detection, ventilation, ignition control, flame-resistant clothing. Geometry, not chemistry, decides whether the same cloud gives a flash fire or a vapour cloud explosion.
There is nothing to extinguish in the moment; only keeping the cloud from forming or from finding a spark prevents it.

## Echo

The searing orange sheet with a whoomp, gone as fast as it came.
Flash fire is read by its signature -- a fast-moving orange-yellow flame sheet with a whoomp, often a visible cloud edge before ignition, a brief searing heat, and little smoke because it self-consumes. It hands its danger to the burns it inflicts in that instant and to the secondary fires it lights.
Flash fire is dated from the ignition, the moment the drifting cloud met a spark and swept itself out.
