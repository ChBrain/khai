# Process: Dust Explosion

## Taxonomy

[Combustion](process_combustion.md), the industrial phenomenon of a suspended dust cloud deflagrating.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: combustion

## Initiated by

A cloud of combustible dust hangs within its explosible concentration, finds an ignition, and deflagrates through itself.
Dust explosion burns a suspended combustible dust -- grain, flour, sugar, coal, wood, metal, plastics, pharma -- as a cloud within its explosible range; the dust pentagon is fuel plus oxidiser plus ignition plus dispersion plus confinement, and the burn is a rapid deflagration rated by its Kst and Pmax into St classes.
It is a solid made explosive by division: a material that would only smoulder in a heap, exploding once it is fine enough and airborne enough to burn all at once.

## Direction

A small primary blast that lofts settled dust into a far larger secondary one.
Dust explosion runs in two stages: a primary deflagration, often small and inside equipment, sends a pressure wave that lofts settled dust layers into a fresh cloud, giving a far larger secondary explosion that is usually the deadly one; confinement builds the destructive overpressure. The escalation is self-amplifying -- the first blast makes the fuel for the second.
The direction is a cascade off the floor: the layer no one cleaned becoming the cloud that kills.

## Lever

The settled dust layer against the instinct to hose it down.
The carrying force is a deflagrable cloud in a confined space; the resistance fails because you cannot extinguish a millisecond deflagration, and hosing down dust can loft it into the explosible cloud that becomes the secondary event -- and water is barred on metal dusts. Control is engineering: explosion venting, rapid suppression bottles, isolation, inerting, and the housekeeping that denies the layer.
There is nothing to put out in the instant; only denying the cloud, the confinement, and the spark keeps the layer on the floor.

## Echo

The muffled thump, then the larger blast and the rolling flame through the cloud.
Dust explosion is read by its signature -- a muffled thump then a larger blast, a rolling flame front through the cloud, often little pre-event smoke, with metal dusts adding bright sparking and characteristic colours. It hands its damage to the secondary blast the first one lofts.
Dust explosion is dated from the ignition, the moment the suspended cloud lit and the layer joined it.
