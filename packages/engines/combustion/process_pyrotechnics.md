# Process: Pyrotechnic Fire

## Taxonomy

[Combustion](process_combustion.md), the made-fire phenomenon of the display fire, and its mass-explosion turn.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: combustion

## Initiated by

Compositions built to burn beautifully on command catch outside their command.
Pyrotechnic fire burns oxidizer-fuel compositions -- black powder lifts and bursts, metal salts for the colours -- engineered for precise, self-oxidised burning; lit as designed it is the display, but a fire in storage or transport drives sympathetic ignition from item to item, and stock that tested as mild burning goods can transition to mass explosion, as at Enschede in 2000, where a fireworks depot fire ended in blasts that levelled the district and killed twenty-three.
It is the celebration fire's double face: the same chemistry that makes the show, unpacked from its choreography, makes the crater.

## Direction

From one item alight, through sympathetic ignition, toward the mass event.
Pyrotechnic fire escalates by radiant heat and flying burning debris lighting neighbouring items, packaging burning away the separations the hazard classification assumed, confinement of a container or store turning burning into bulk explosion -- the behaviour class is a property of packing, spacing, and containment, not of the composition alone.
The direction is a category jump: goods classified to burn like a bonfire behaving, in bulk and confinement, like a bomb.

## Lever

The composition that carries its own oxygen against every agent, and the classification that was never a property of the chemical.
The carrying force is self-oxidised burning at its own pace -- water and smothering reach nothing the mixture does not already carry; the resistance fails on the paperwork paradox: the safe hazard class was earned by the tested packaging and separations, so repacked, crowded, or confined stock silently re-enters a class no one around it is defending against, and the only real controls -- quantity-distance separation, licensing, evacuation on first fire -- stand or fall before ignition.
Fight it and it burns on its own oxygen; trust its label and the label changes in storage; once a store is alight the radius, not the hose, is the tool.

## Echo

The crater in the town where the depot stood.
Pyrotechnic fire is read by its signature -- coloured flame and screaming projectiles off the first burning stock, accelerating crackle into deep detonations, then the blast damage mapped in rings -- and by the inquiry that follows, finding the classification correct for the boxes and wrong for the store.
Pyrotechnic fire is dated from the transition, the moment the display stock stopped burning as items and went as one.
