# Instructions: Combustion

How a Playwright wires the combustion engine into a play. You wire by linking the play's own content to the engine's files; the engine files stay untouched, they are canon. The Roadie plumbs the engine's law into Instructions Knowledge on deploy. Authoring guidance, not runtime content, and it does not go on tour.

## Human

- The human sets which places show a specific fire that acts -- the crown fire in the canopy, the grass fire across the range, the peat smouldering below, the firebrands spotting ahead -- and whether that fire is the place's standing condition or a live event, naming the phenomenon rather than treating all fire as one thing.

## Agent

- From the play's own content, a place whose fire is an actor links under Shown the combustion phenomenon it shows: the crown, grass, chaparral, peat, holdover, spotting, slash, or serotiny it presents. Play the fire as the specific named phenomenon shown -- the canopy running, the fine-fuel front outrunning a person, the ground smouldering unseen for a season, the embers leaping the line -- present in what the place puts before whoever enters, never narrated, and never as a mirror of a persona's own burning. Keep it the specific phenomenon and its behaviour, not the place's abstract burn regime (that is fire), the fire-weather that primes it (weather), the drought that cures the fuel (hydrology), the regrowth that follows (succession), or the fire from below (geomorphology). You link to the engine, you never edit it.

## Collaboration

- The place's burn regime and its modes are the fire engine's; the fire-weather is weather's; the drought that cures the fuel is hydrology's; the regrowth after is succession's; the volcanic fire from below is geomorphology's. Combustion is the typology beneath fire's regime -- the named phenomenon a place shows, read by its fuel, its dynamics, its sensory signature, and its paradox. Fire reads the regime; combustion reads the type. Whether the phenomenon rings true to the place and its fuel is the team's review.

## Knowledge

- Combustion is the catalog of the named phenomena of fire: each a distinct way something burns, fixed by its fuel and chemistry, its physical dynamics, its sensory signature, and the paradox by which the obvious way to fight it fails or feeds it (the wildland fire-behaviour and fire-ecology tradition -- Rothermel's spread, Van Wagner's crown-fire thresholds, Rein's smouldering, Christensen's fire-adapted regeneration). It wires on a place at Shown, and it is the specific fire a place shows, not the abstract regime (fire's). The catalog is organised by domain and grows domain by domain; the wildland domain opens it. Crown is the canopy fire, passive torching to active running to independent, wind-driven and spotting; grass is the fast fine-fuel front, seconds of residence and lethal by rate; chaparral is the volatile-oil brush fire that ignites ahead of the front; peat is the flameless organic-soil smoulder that burns for months and is fed by the air a lance lets in; holdover is the fire that overwinters below the snow and re-emerges; spotting is the firebrands that leap every line; slash is the heavy-residue burn that glows for days; serotiny is the fire-cued regeneration a fire-adapted seedbank requires. Read a place by the specific fire it shows, and read each phenomenon by its own paradox -- the way the standard method fails or feeds it.

## System

- Do give a place whose fire is an actor the specific phenomenon it shows under Shown; do play the fire as the named phenomenon shown, present not narrated; do keep the reading the specific type -- the crown, the peat, the spotting -- not fire-in-general and not a symbol.
- Do read each phenomenon by its paradox: water spreading the pool fire, the lance aerating the peat, ventilation feeding the backdraft, the fire kept out arming a worse one -- the way the obvious method meets the one fire it makes worse.
- Do keep the phenomenon distinct from the regime: fire reads that a place burns and how its modes turn; combustion reads the specific fire it shows -- crowning, smouldering, spotting -- with its own chemistry, dynamics, and sensory read.
- Do not model the place's abstract burn regime (that is fire), the fire-weather (weather), the drought that cures the fuel (hydrology), the regrowth that follows (succession), or the fire from below (geomorphology). Do not make the burn mirror a persona's own consuming or ruin (that is the play's pitch). Do not edit the engine's files; wire only from the play's side.
