# Instructions: Combustion

How a Playwright wires the combustion engine into a play. You wire by linking the play's own content to the engine's files; the engine files stay untouched, they are canon. The Roadie plumbs the engine's law into Instructions Knowledge on deploy. Authoring guidance, not runtime content, and it does not go on tour.

## Human

- The human sets which places show a specific fire that acts -- the crown fire in the canopy, the grass fire across the range, the peat smouldering below, the firebrands spotting ahead -- and whether that fire is the place's standing condition or a live event, naming the phenomenon rather than treating all fire as one thing.

## Agent

- From the play's own content, a place whose fire is an actor links under Shown the combustion phenomenon it shows -- from the wildland domain (crown, grass, chaparral, peat, duff, holdover, spotting, slash, serotiny, the coal-seam fire, the WUI fire), the compartment and extreme-dynamics domain (flashover, backdraft, rollover, thermal layering, the chimney effect, pyrolysis, the cladding fire, the tunnel fire, the convection column, the fire whirl, pyrocumulonimbus, firestorm, the eruptive blow-up), the industrial and chemical domain (the pool fire, the pressurised jet, the flash fire, the vapour cloud explosion, the BLEVE, the boilover, the combustible-metal fire, lithium runaway, the dust explosion, self-heating, pyrophoric ignition, the oxidizer-fed fire, hypergolic ignition, the arc flash, the polymer fire, the hydrogen fire, the runaway reaction), or the made-fire domain (the backfire, the prescribed burn, swidden, fire-stick farming, the charcoal clamp, the forge, the blast furnace, the kiln, the incinerator, the flare, the cutting torch, the premixed burner, the pyrotechnics, the barbecue, the candle, the cremation). Play the fire as the specific named phenomenon shown -- the canopy running, the fine-fuel front outrunning a person, the ground smouldering unseen for a season, the embers leaping the line -- present in what the place puts before whoever enters, never narrated, and never as a mirror of a persona's own burning. Keep it the specific phenomenon and its behaviour, not the place's abstract burn regime (that is fire), the fire-weather that primes it (weather), the drought that cures the fuel (hydrology), the regrowth that follows (succession), or the fire from below (geomorphology). You link to the engine, you never edit it.

## Collaboration

- The place's burn regime and its modes are the fire engine's; the fire-weather is weather's; the drought that cures the fuel is hydrology's; the regrowth after is succession's; the volcanic fire from below is geomorphology's. Combustion is the typology beneath fire's regime -- the named phenomenon a place shows, read by its fuel, its dynamics, its sensory signature, and its paradox. Fire reads the regime; combustion reads the type. Whether the phenomenon rings true to the place and its fuel is the team's review.

## Knowledge

- Combustion is the catalog of the named phenomena of fire: each a distinct way something burns, fixed by its fuel and chemistry, its physical dynamics, its sensory signature, and the paradox by which the obvious way to fight it fails or feeds it (the wildland fire-behaviour and fire-ecology tradition -- Rothermel's spread, Van Wagner's crown-fire thresholds, Rein's smouldering, Christensen's fire-adapted regeneration). It wires on a place at Shown, and it is the specific fire a place shows, not the abstract regime (fire's). The catalog is organised by domain and grows domain by domain; the wildland domain opens it. Crown is the canopy fire, passive torching to active running to independent, wind-driven and spotting; grass is the fast fine-fuel front, seconds of residence and lethal by rate; chaparral is the volatile-oil brush fire that ignites ahead of the front; peat is the flameless organic-soil smoulder that burns for months and is fed by the air a lance lets in; duff is the ground fire at the combustion limit, a mineral-ballasted smoulder creeping the forest floor along organic seams and root channels, invisible to infrared and shedding water; holdover is the fire that overwinters below the snow and re-emerges; spotting is the firebrands that leap every line; slash is the heavy-residue burn that glows for days; serotiny is the fire-cued regeneration a fire-adapted seedbank requires; coal-seam is geological fuel alight underground, creeping metres a year for decades to millennia, its extinguishment priced past the land above it; wui is the interface fire in which ember attack makes the houses the fuel and the loss maps to the home ignition zone, not the front. The compartment and extreme-dynamics domain adds the enclosure fire and its rapid progression -- flashover (the whole room igniting at the radiant threshold, which ventilation can trigger), backdraft (the air-starved room exploding on inrush, the opening the trigger), rollover (the ceiling layer igniting ahead of the fire), thermal layering (the two-layer structure a straight stream collapses), the chimney effect (fire drawn up a shaft or slope), pyrolysis (solids gasifying into the fuel that burns), the cladding fire (the combustible envelope carrying flame up the face and past every compartment line, where stay-put turns lethal), and the tunnel fire (fire in a confined bore whose ventilation allocates the smoke, backlayering when too weak) -- and the coupled fire-atmosphere extremes: the convection column, the fire whirl, pyrocumulonimbus, the firestorm that makes its own inflowing wind, and the eruptive blow-up. The industrial and chemical domain adds the process-plant hazards: the pool fire (a liquid burning from its face, spread by water and cut by foam), the jet fire (a pressurised release burning as a torch, put out by shutting the valve), the flash fire and the vapour cloud explosion (the same cloud giving a searing sweep or, given congestion, a blast), the BLEVE (a fire-heated vessel bursting into a fireball, answered by evacuation), the boilover (a burning tank erupting when its heat wave reaches the water bottom), the combustible-metal fire and lithium runaway (fires that feed on water and reignite from their own oxygen), the dust explosion (a suspended cloud whose secondary blast is lofted from the settled layer), self-heating (a bulk pile lit from within, and by the air that digging admits), pyrophoric ignition (a substance lit by contact with air), the oxidizer-fed fire (immune to smothering, carrying its own oxygen), hypergolic ignition (fuel and oxidiser lit on contact, where the agent may itself be fuel), the arc flash (ionised-air plasma defused by de-energising, not water), the polymer fire (an easily quenched flame whose smoke is the real fire), the hydrogen fire (flammable from 4 to 75 percent, ignited by almost nothing, nearly invisible in daylight -- isolate before extinguishing, or the flame becomes the cloud), and the runaway reaction (heat generation doubling every ten degrees against linear cooling, lost while the gauges still read calm). The made-fire domain closes the catalog with the fire lit on purpose and held to a task, each read by the moment its control slips: the backfire (the counter-fire drawn into a wildfire's indraft, slopping over on a wind shift), the prescribed burn (held inside a written prescription, a wildfire the moment it escapes), swidden (the slash-and-burn farmed for its ash, impoverishing when the fallow is shortened), fire-stick farming (the cool mosaic burn whose withholding builds the megafire), the charcoal clamp (air-starved to char wood, ruined by one open vent), the forge (the reducing fire that works steel, the oxidising one that burns it), the blast furnace (run for years, wrecked by the chill that freezes iron in the hearth), the kiln (a firing curve destroyed by thermal shock), the incinerator (held above its legal heat, its dioxins re-forming as the flue gas cools), the flare (kept always alight, its emergency the pilot loss that vents raw gas), the cutting torch (the oxy-fuel flame whose flashback runs back to the cylinders), the premixed burner (clean when near-invisible, failing by flashback or lift-off), the pyrotechnics (self-oxidised display compositions whose tested hazard class belongs to the packaging, transitioning in bulk and confinement to mass explosion), the barbecue (the radiant bed whose grease fire water turns to a fireball), the candle (the self-feeding wick relightable down its own wax-smoke), and the cremation (overwhelmed by a high-fat body or a pacemaker that explodes). Read a place by the specific fire it shows, and read each phenomenon by its own paradox -- the way the standard method fails or feeds it.

## System

- Do give a place whose fire is an actor the specific phenomenon it shows under Shown; do play the fire as the named phenomenon shown, present not narrated; do keep the reading the specific type -- the crown, the peat, the spotting -- not fire-in-general and not a symbol.
- Do read each phenomenon by its paradox: water spreading the pool fire, the lance aerating the peat, ventilation feeding the backdraft, the fire kept out arming a worse one -- the way the obvious method meets the one fire it makes worse.
- Do keep the phenomenon distinct from the regime: fire reads that a place burns and how its modes turn; combustion reads the specific fire it shows -- crowning, smouldering, spotting -- with its own chemistry, dynamics, and sensory read.
- Do not model the place's abstract burn regime (that is fire), the fire-weather (weather), the drought that cures the fuel (hydrology), the regrowth that follows (succession), or the fire from below (geomorphology). Do not make the burn mirror a persona's own consuming or ruin (that is the play's pitch). Do not edit the engine's files; wire only from the play's side.
