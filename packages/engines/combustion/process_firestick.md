# Process: Fire-Stick Farming

## Taxonomy

[Combustion](process_combustion.md), the made fire kept small and frequent so a great one never builds.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: combustion

## Initiated by

Small cool fires are lit patch by patch, early in the dry season, keeping the fuel low so a hot fire has nothing to run on.
Fire-stick farming -- cultural burning, patch or mosaic burning, cool or right-way fire, Indigenous fire stewardship -- keeps fine grass and litter low with a low-intensity backing or creeping fire -- flames under a metre, brief residence, cool in intensity though not in flame temperature, sparing canopies and fauna; frequent small burns prevent the fuel build-up that would feed a hot crown fire. It is lit early in the dry season in small patches, read from seasonal indicators -- flowering, wind, humidity -- rather than instruments.
It is fire used as a tending hand: many small burns that keep the country from ever holding a big one. The practice is Aboriginal Australian peoples' own, tens of millennia of cultural burning; the name is Rhys Jones's coinage -- packaged here with credit, never claimed.

## Direction

Patch by patch into a mosaic, each new fire self-limiting against an earlier scar.
Fire-stick farming makes a mosaic of burnt and unburnt patches that self-limits spread, each new fire running into an earlier scar and stopping. The escalation it prevents is the megafire; its own spread is held by the patchwork it has already laid.
The direction is a spreading restraint: a fire whose whole purpose is to keep the next one small.

## Lever

The frequent small fire against the suppression that lets the load build.
The carrying force is a cool, self-containing mosaic burn; the resistance is inverted -- withholding this fire is what makes catastrophic fire, decades of exclusion building the load a single ignition then releases as a megafire -- and the knowledge is calendar- and place-specific, so applied at the wrong season or without the mosaic it loses its cool, self-contained character. The danger is not the fire but its absence.
Keep lighting the small ones and no great one can build; withhold them and the fuel you spared becomes the fuel that burns everything.

## Echo

The low flickering flame, the thin pale smoke, the walking-pace crackle.
Fire-stick farming is read by its signature -- a low flickering flame, thin pale smoke, a grassy woodsmoke smell, a walking-pace crackle rather than a roar. It hands its outcome not to what it burns but to the megafire it keeps from forming.
Fire-stick farming is dated from the lighting, the moment the country was tended with a small fire so it would not hold a large one.
