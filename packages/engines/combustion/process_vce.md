# Process: Vapour Cloud Explosion

## Taxonomy

[Combustion](process_combustion.md), the industrial phenomenon of a vapour cloud accelerating to blast.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: combustion

## Initiated by

A large flammable cloud ignites in congested plant and the flame accelerates until it builds a destructive pressure wave.
Vapour cloud explosion -- VCE, unconfined VCE -- starts when a large flammable vapour cloud (LPG, ethylene, hydrocarbons) mixes with air to within its limits and ignites; unlike a flash fire, its destructive feature is flame acceleration generating blast overpressure.
It is the same fuel as a flash fire turned lethal by geometry: a cloud that, given congestion, does not merely burn but explodes.

## Direction

Through the congestion, faster and faster, into overpressure and sometimes detonation.
Vapour cloud explosion runs on turbulence: in congested or confined geometry -- pipe racks, process modules (Flixborough 1974) -- the flame accelerates as a deflagration, building overpressure, and in extreme cases undergoes deflagration-to-detonation transition (Buncefield 2005, where dense roadside vegetation accelerated the flame to devastating overpressure despite an otherwise open, low-congestion site); truly open, unobstructed clouds tend to give only a flash fire.
The direction is amplification by structure: the more the cloud must burn through, the harder it pushes.

## Lever

The congestion that accelerates the flame against the impossibility of fighting a blast.
The carrying force is a large cloud in a turbulence-generating layout; the resistance fails because there is nothing to extinguish -- the event is a deflagration or detonation over in milliseconds, and the damage is done by blast. Only prevention applies: leak detection, ignition control, minimising congestion, gas dispersion. A smaller open cloud that flash-fires is far less lethal than the same fuel trapped in plant congestion.
Fight it and there is nothing there; the only defence is to keep the cloud, the congestion, and the spark from ever meeting.

## Echo

The blinding flame front and the concussive blast that flattens the plant.
Vapour cloud explosion is read by its signature -- a brief blinding flame front then a concussive blast, broken windows and collapsed structures, a ground-hugging heavier-than-air cloud sometimes visible or odour-detectable beforehand, a deep boom rather than a roar. It hands its damage not to fire but to overpressure across the site.
Vapour cloud explosion is dated from the ignition, the moment the congested cloud lit and the flame outran itself into blast.
