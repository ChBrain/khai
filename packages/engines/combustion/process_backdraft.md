# Process: Backdraft

## Taxonomy

[Combustion](process_combustion.md), the compartment phenomenon of the air-starved fire exploding on inrush.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: combustion

## Initiated by

A sealed, oxygen-starved room full of hot unburnt gas waits, above its ignition temperature, for air.
Backdraft starts in an underventilated compartment (oxygen often below 10-15%) charged with accumulated unburnt pyrolysate and high carbon monoxide (flammable from 12.5 to 74% in air) at high temperature -- a mixture that is too rich and too oxygen-starved to burn, held only by the lack of air.
It is the fire that is not out but suspended, its heat already present, needing only the one thing withheld.

## Direction

Back toward the new opening, as a deflagration and a vented fireball.
When a door or window admits fresh air as a floor-level inrush, it mixes the over-rich hot gases into the flammable band; ignition propagates as a deflagration back toward the opening, venting a fireball and a pressure wave outward. The trigger is oxygen, not heat.
The direction is a delayed explosion: the room does nothing until air enters, then answers all at once.

## Lever

The stored chemical energy against the intuition that opening the room helps.
The carrying force is a compartment full of hot fuel gas needing only oxygen; the resistance fails in the signature paradox -- the correct-seeming action, opening the door or breaking the window to vent, is exactly the trigger. The counter is anti-backdraft: read the signs, do not ventilate low, cool the gases through high openings before entry, control the inflow.
Open it at the wrong height or time and the room explodes; recognise it and vent high first and it is defused.

## Echo

The fireball out the opening, disproportionate to the small fire that seemed to be dying.
Backdraft is read by its pre-signs -- smoke pulsing and "breathing" in and out of gaps, blackened oily condensate on the windows, whistling and sucking at cracks, little or no visible flame, hot pressurised smoke under doors -- then, on air admission, a low whoomp, an inrush whistle, and the vented fireball. It is why a decayed but sealed compartment is read as backdraft-primed.
Backdraft is dated from the inrush, the moment air reached the waiting gas and it detonated back at the door.
