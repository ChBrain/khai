# Process: Fire Whirl

## Taxonomy

[Combustion](process_combustion.md), the phenomenon of fire spun into a rotating vertical column.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: combustion

## Initiated by

Vorticity stretched by a fire's updraft spins the flame into a rotating column that burns hotter than the fire around it.
Fire whirl -- fire devil, fire tornado -- starts when ambient or fire-generated vorticity (wind shear, terrain, plume interaction) is stretched and spun up by the buoyant updraft into a rotating vortex; the axial in-draft boosts the burning so the core can exceed 1000 C, hotter than the surrounding fire.
It is fire given rotation: a vortex that concentrates and intensifies combustion into a spinning tube of flame.

## Direction

Up and around, intensifying itself and flinging burning debris.
Fire whirl scales from small "fire devils" (under a metre, seconds long) to rare true fire tornadoes coupled to a pyrocumulonimbus mesocyclone (Carr 2018, Canberra 2003) with tornado-strength winds; axial entrainment feeds it more air and lofts firebrands.
The direction is self-intensification and unpredictability: the more it spins, the harder it burns, and the harder to say where it will go.

## Lever

The rotation that concentrates the fire against every attempt to fight or predict it.
The carrying force is a vortex drawing air axially and boosting heat release; the resistance is total -- erratic, fast-moving, and self-intensifying, it is impossible to attack directly, flings burning debris to start new fires, and can lift and hurl material. The only tactic is distance.
There is nothing to fight and no path to predict; you can only get away from it.

## Echo

The spinning column of flame and glowing debris, gone as suddenly as it formed.
Fire whirl is read by its signature -- a spinning column of flame and lofted debris, a loud roar or whine as it intensifies, a localised violent in-draft wind; it can be nearly invisible if lean. It hands new ignitions to wherever its debris lands and, at the extreme, tornado-strength destruction.
Fire whirl is dated from the spin-up, the moment the fire's updraft caught a rotation and stood it up as a tube of flame.
