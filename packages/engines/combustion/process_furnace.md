# Process: Blast Furnace

## Taxonomy

[Combustion](process_combustion.md), the made fire run for years to smelt iron from ore.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: combustion

## Initiated by

Coke, ore, and flux are fed into a tall shaft and blasted with preheated air so the coke burns and reduces the ore to molten iron.
Blast furnace -- the cupola, the hot blast -- burns coke, near-pure baked-coal carbon, as fuel and reductant with iron ore and limestone flux, under a forced draft of preheated air at 900-1300 C through tuyeres; the raceway coke burns at 1900-2200 C, carbon monoxide reduces the ore, and molten iron runs at 1450-1500 C with slag from the flux and gangue. It is a counter-current shaft: the burden descends as the hot reducing gas rises.
It is fire made permanent: a burn run continuously for years to keep iron flowing from the ore.

## Direction

Down the shaft as burden, up as reducing gas, out at the taphole as molten iron.
Blast furnace runs continuously for a campaign of years, its output tuned by blast volume and temperature, oxygen enrichment, and burden ratios, the blast preheated in regenerative stoves and the carbon-monoxide-rich top gas captured and burned. The escalation is any drift in temperature or gas chemistry that the operators do not correct.
The direction is a sustained descent and rise: a fire held at a steady state that must never be allowed to wander.

## Lever

The unbroken heat and gas chemistry against the chill that freezes iron in the hearth.
The carrying force is a furnace held at temperature and reducing chemistry; the resistance fails not by going out but by drifting -- a chill, a loss of blast or heat, freezes iron in the hearth as a salamander that can wreck the furnace, the top gas is a lethal invisible carbon-monoxide hazard, and a hanging burden that slips causes a pressure surge and blowback. It is a controlled bomb kept stable only by never letting the temperature or the gas drift.
Hold the blast and chemistry steady and it pours iron for years; let them drift and the fire freezes solid or surges back at you.

## Echo

The blinding white taphole, the roar of the blast, the periodic gush of tapping.
Blast furnace is read by its signature -- a blinding white-yellow incandescence at the taphole, iron glowing white and slag orange, an odourless but deadly carbon-monoxide top gas, the roar of the blast, and the periodic gush of tapping. It hands its product to the molten iron and its danger to the chill, the gas, and the slip.
Blast furnace is dated from the blow-in, the moment the shaft was lit to run its campaign.
