# Process: Barbecue

## Taxonomy

[Combustion](process_combustion.md), the made fire banked to a radiant bed to cook by browning.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: combustion

## Initiated by

A bed of charcoal or a gas burner is brought to a steady radiant heat that browns food at its surface without burning the fire out of control.
Barbecue -- grilling, char-grilling, searing -- burns charcoal, briquettes, or gas under natural or lightly forced draft, a glowing bed radiating at 500-700 C; the chemistry is at the food surface, the Maillard reaction of amino acids and sugars from 140-165 C and caramelisation from 160-180 C, while dripping fat pyrolyses to smoke. The cook manages the bed with two zones, direct and indirect, and vent and lid control.
It is fire banked for cooking: a burn held at a radiant heat whose real work happens on the surface of the food.

## Direction

Up off the bed as radiant heat, and into flare-ups where the fat drips and catches.
Barbecue sets its temperature by two-zone fires and vent and lid control, the cook moving food off direct heat to manage flare-ups as fat ignites, searing the surface past the Maillard threshold while the interior stays below denaturation. The escalation is the grease fire, dripping fat igniting and pooling into a self-feeding blaze.
The direction is a tended radiant burn with a fat-fed hazard: heat up to the food, flame up where the fat falls.

## Lever

The browning heat against the grease fire that water turns into a fireball.
The carrying force is a radiant bed browning the food; the resistance fails in the grease fire -- dripping fat ignites and, pooling in a drip tray or firebox, becomes self-feeding, and water makes it worse, flashing to steam and ejecting burning oil as a fireball, so it must be smothered by closing the lid and cutting the air or hit with a Class-K extinguisher, never doused -- while the same browning chemistry at excess produces char and carcinogenic compounds. Flavour and fire hazard lie on the same rising curve.
Bank it and vent it and it browns the food; let the fat pool and light, then throw water, and it answers with a fireball.

## Echo

The orange coal glow, the flare-up's yellow flame and sooty smoke, the sizzle and fat-crackle.
Barbecue is read by its signature -- an orange coal glow, fat flare-ups giving bright yellow flame and black sooty smoke, prized blue-grey wood smoke, a Maillard roast and guaiacol woodsmoke odour, and the sizzle and whoosh of a flare-up. It hands its danger to the grease fire and its reward and its carcinogen to the same browning heat.
Barbecue is dated from the lighting, the moment the bed was brought to its cooking heat.
