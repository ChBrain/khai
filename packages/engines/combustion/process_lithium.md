# Process: Lithium Runaway

## Taxonomy

[Combustion](process_combustion.md), the industrial phenomenon of a battery cell venting and burning from its own oxygen.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: combustion

## Initiated by

A lithium cell shorts internally, self-heats past control, and vents jetting flammable gas while its cathode releases its own oxygen.
Lithium runaway -- thermal runaway of a Li-ion or Li-metal battery -- burns the flammable carbonate electrolyte, separator, and electrode materials while the cathode (especially NMC) decomposes and releases oxygen, making the cell partly self-oxidising; runaway runs roughly 400-1000 C, venting hydrogen, carbon monoxide, methane and ethylene, and toxic corrosive hydrogen fluoride. The trigger is an internal short, overcharge, over-temperature, or damage.
It is a fire sealed inside its fuel: a cell carrying its own oxidiser, off-gassing before it flames and re-lighting long after.

## Direction

Cell to cell, jetting gas then flame, propagating through the pack.
Lithium runaway is an exothermic decomposition cascade that self-heats one cell, vents a jet of flammable gas, then propagates cell-to-cell; off-gassing often precedes flame, and vented gas can re-accumulate and explode. The escalation is a chain: one cell's heat driving the next into runaway.
The direction is a spreading internal reaction: a fire that moves through a pack faster than it can be reached.

## Lever

The oxygen inside the cell against the smothering that cannot reach it.
The carrying force is a self-heating cell with its own oxygen supply; the resistance fails because smothering with CO2 or foam does not stop an internal reaction, cells reignite hours to days later, and the internal short cannot be reached -- so the response is massive water flooding for cooling to halt propagation, accepting the hydrogen and hydrogen-fluoride trade-offs, or immersion and containment with thermal monitoring; small metallic-lithium cells still need Class D powder. You do not extinguish the reaction, you cool its neighbours out of the chain.
Smother it and it waits and relights; flood it with water to cool the pack and the propagation stalls.

## Echo

The hissing vent-gas jet before flame, the bright jetting fire, the sharp acid smell.
Lithium runaway is read by its signature -- hissing and popping and a white-grey vent-gas jet before flame, bright jetting flames sometimes green or blue, a sweet-then-acrid electrolyte odour, a sharp acidic hydrogen-fluoride smell, firework-like sparking. It hands its danger to the next cell and to the toxic corrosive gas it vents the whole time.
Lithium runaway is dated from the vent, the moment the cell first off-gassed and began to run away.
