# Process: Runaway Reaction

## Taxonomy

[Combustion](process_combustion.md), the industrial phenomenon of the reaction that outruns its own cooling.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: combustion

## Initiated by

An exothermic batch crosses the temperature where it makes heat faster than the plant can take it away.
Runaway reaction starts in a reactor or storage vessel when cooling fails, agitation stops, a charge is mis-measured, or a contaminant catalyses -- and the Arrhenius arithmetic takes over: reaction rate roughly doubling with every ten degrees, heat output exponential against cooling that is at best linear; T2 Laboratories' reactor in 2007 killed four this way, and Seveso's released its dioxin cloud from the same curve.
It is combustion's logic inside a closed vessel: a self-accelerating oxidation-or-decomposition whose fuel, confinement, and ignition are all the process itself.

## Direction

Up the exponential, to boiling, decomposition, and the vessel's limits.
Runaway reaction accelerates through venting solvent, gas-generating decomposition, and rising pressure toward vessel failure -- ending as a burst, a fireball of its own vented reagents, or a toxic release, on a timetable that compresses from hours to minutes to seconds as the temperature climbs.
The direction is a race already decided: past the point of no return, no cooling the plant owns can catch the curve.

## Lever

The exponential heat rate against the linear cooling, and the calm gauge faces at the moment it is lost.
The carrying force is the chemistry's own acceleration -- every degree gained speeding the gain of the next; the resistance fails quietly: the reactor looks normal at the crossing point, operators read a slow drift while the margin is already gone, and the defences that count -- relief sized for two-phase runaway flow, quench and dump systems, inhibitor injection -- only exist if they were designed in before the batch was ever charged.
Catch the drift early and a dump or quench ends it; trust the gauges an hour too long and the only remaining question is what the relief valve was sized for.

## Echo

The burst vessel, the scorched plant, the batch record ending mid-line.
Runaway reaction is read by its signature -- a temperature trace bending away from setpoint, venting that will not stop, drumming and hammering from the reactor, then the burst and a fire fed by the batch itself -- and afterwards by the investigation's curve, reconstructed from the data logger, showing exactly when it was still stoppable.
Runaway reaction is dated from the crossing, the degree at which heat made outran heat removed and the batch stopped being a process.
