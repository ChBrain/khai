# Process: Incinerator

## Taxonomy

[Combustion](process_combustion.md), the made fire held hot enough to destroy waste and its toxins.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: combustion

## Initiated by

Mixed waste is burned on a grate under forced air, held above a legal minimum temperature long enough to destroy the toxins it would otherwise release.
Incinerator -- waste-to-energy, mass-burn -- burns heterogeneous municipal or hazardous waste of variable calorific value under a forced draft, primary air under the grate and secondary above for burnout, and must hold at least 850 C for two seconds (1100 C for heavily halogenated waste) to destroy dioxins, yielding carbon dioxide, water, ash, and scrubbed pollutants. Waste moves through drying, ignition, and burnout zones, tuned by the three Ts -- temperature, time, and turbulence -- plus excess air.
It is fire run as a destroyer: a burn held hot and complete enough that what it emits is safe.

## Direction

Along the grate through drying, ignition, and burnout, with the flue gas quenched through the danger band.
Incinerator holds a minimum temperature with auxiliary burners and controls combustion by flue-gas oxygen and carbon-monoxide feedback; the flue gas must be quenched rapidly through the 200-450 C window where dioxins re-form as it cools. The escalation is any drop below the legal temperature, or a slow cool through the re-formation band.
The direction is a complete consumption followed by a fast quench: burn hot, then cool through the toxic window before it can re-form.

## Lever

The high complete burn against the dioxins that re-form as the flue gas cools.
The carrying force is a fire held hot enough to destroy the toxins; the resistance fails in the dioxin paradox -- dioxins re-form in the 200-450 C window as the flue gas cools, so rapid quenching through that band is required -- while wet low-value waste drops the furnace below the legal 850 C and forces auxiliary fuel, high-calorific slugs overheat it, and bunker self-heating and wet-ash hydrogen recur. Destroying the toxin in the flame is undone if the cooling is slow.
Burn it hot and the toxins are destroyed; let the flue gas linger as it cools and they build themselves back.

## Echo

The yellow-orange bed flame, the secondary flame above, the near-invisible clean plume.
Incinerator is read by its signature -- a yellow-orange bed flame and a secondary flame above, a near-invisible steam plume in clean operation with dark smoke signalling incomplete combustion, a sweet-acrid mixed-waste odour at the tipping hall. It hands its outcome to the destroyed waste, or to the dioxins a slow cool lets re-form.
Incinerator is dated from the ignition, the moment the waste on the grate was brought to the destroying heat.
