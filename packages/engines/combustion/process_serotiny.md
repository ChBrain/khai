# Process: Serotiny

## Taxonomy

[Combustion](process_combustion.md), the wildland phenomenon of fire-cued regeneration.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: combustion

## Initiated by

The fire's heat, not its flame, opens the sealed cone and releases the seed onto the cleared ground.
Serotiny -- pyriscence -- is not itself combustion but the phenomenon fire triggers: serotinous cones and fruits (jack and lodgepole and Aleppo pine, many eucalypts, banksias, proteas) sealed by resin or wax that melts open only when heated by fire, at roughly 45-60 C, reached briefly by a passing flame or radiant heat.
It is the catalog's counter-entry: the fire read not as ruin but as the trigger a fire-adapted plant is built to require.

## Direction

Toward release and germination on the burn's own seedbed.
Serotiny turns fire's heat into regeneration -- the melted seal drops seed onto a nutrient-rich, competitor-cleared, mineral-soil bed; other cues run alongside, smoke-borne karrikins and heat-cracked seed coats stimulating post-fire germination. It ties a species' renewal to the fire regime's frequency and intensity.
The direction is timed dependence: the plant withholds its seed until fire comes and then spends it all at once on the ground fire has prepared.

## Lever

The fire the seedbank needs against the suppression that withholds it, and the severity that destroys it.
The carrying force is a regeneration strategy that only fire can trigger and that nothing else provides; the resistance runs two ways -- suppressing all fire removes the very trigger these systems require, degrading regeneration, while too-frequent fire (before the seedbank matures) or an extreme high-severity crown fire can destroy even a serotinous seedbank. Managing for these systems means restoring an appropriate fire regime, not eliminating fire.
Keep fire out and the seed is never released; burn too often or too hot and the seedbank is spent or destroyed -- only fire on the right interval renews it.

## Echo

The burn followed by a flush of even-aged regeneration the fire itself sowed.
Serotiny is read by its signature -- no fire of its own, but the post-fire evidence: opened cones, mass seedfall, dense even-aged seedling flushes across a burn, the fireweed and morel in the ash. It hands the ground fire cleared to a regeneration that reads the burn as the beginning, not the end.
Serotiny is dated from the heat that opened the cone, the moment the fire that destroyed the stand became the fire that reseeded it.
