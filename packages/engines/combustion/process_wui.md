# Process: WUI Fire

## Taxonomy

[Combustion](process_combustion.md), the wildland phenomenon of the interface, where the houses become the fuel.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: combustion

## Initiated by

A wildfire reaches the built edge and the structures themselves start to carry it.
WUI fire -- the wildland-urban interface fire -- ignites homes mostly not by the flame front but by ember attack and small surface flames finding the receptive spots of the structure itself: the dry gutter, the vent, the deck, the mulch against the wall; whether a home ignites is decided by the home and its immediate surroundings, the home ignition zone, more than by the fire's intensity.
It is the wildfire changing fuel class: once houses are lighting houses, the event is an urban conflagration wearing a wildfire's name.

## Direction

From the vegetation into the structures, then structure to structure down the street.
WUI fire runs as a shower of embers ahead of the front, igniting buildings that then radiate and cast embers at their neighbours, so whole blocks burn house-by-house while the front is elsewhere or already passed; it overwhelms both doctrines at once -- too many structure fires for structural crews, too much built fuel for wildland tactics.
The direction is a conversion: a fire that arrived through the fuels of the land continuing through the fuels of the town.

## Lever

The home ignition zone against the instinct to fight the front.
The carrying force is the receptivity of the structures -- the ember-catching details and the fuel within metres of the wall, multiplied by every house already burning; the resistance fails because the front cannot be stopped in extreme weather and defending it saves nothing, while what actually decides survival -- the hardened vent, the clean gutter, the cleared zone -- had to be in place before the smoke was ever visible.
Fight the flame front and the embers go around you; harden the house and clear its zone and the same ember shower finds nothing to take.

## Echo

Streets of foundations and chimneys beside standing green trees.
WUI fire is read by its signature -- houses burned to the slab while the forest around them holds unburned or lightly scorched, the proof that the structures, not the trees, carried the fire; the loss maps to the ignition zones, not to the front.
WUI fire is dated from the conversion, the moment the first house became fuel and the wildfire became a fire of the town.
