# Process: Prescribed Burn

## Taxonomy

[Combustion](process_combustion.md), the made fire set within a written prescription to reduce the fuel.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: combustion

## Initiated by

A crew lights the surface fuel on purpose, but only inside a written set of limits, to burn off the load before a wildfire can.
Prescribed burn -- controlled, broadcast, hazard-reduction, Rx fire -- ignites surface litter, grass, and understory only within a prescription of fuel moisture, humidity, wind, mixing height, and transport winds, via drip or terra torch in strips and rings, contained by firelines and wetlines; the flaming front runs 300-800 C, the smouldering duff cooler and longer. The firing sequence -- backing, then flanking, then heading -- throttles the intensity.
It is fire administered like a dose: the same burn as a wildfire, held inside the numbers that keep it a tool.

## Direction

Across the unit at the intensity the firing pattern sets, held to the lines.
Prescribed burn is contained by firelines, wetlines, and natural barriers, its intensity governed by the firing sequence from backing to flanking to heading; mop-up, gridding for residual heat, is where the control is finally won. The escalation is any heat left behind when the crews leave.
The direction is a bounded consumption: a fire kept walking at the pace the prescription and the lines allow.

## Lever

The prescription that holds it against the escape that makes it a wildfire.
The carrying force is a fire lit within its limits; the resistance fails in the escape -- a burn that jumps containment becomes a wildfire the agency itself set, from a forecast bust, from residual heat in snags and duff reigniting after the crews leave, or from a poor mixing height trapping smoke over a highway. The line between tool and disaster is the prescription and the mop-up.
Stay inside the numbers and it clears the load; drift outside them and the fire you set is the one that gets away.

## Echo

The low orange flame, the steam-laden grey smoke, the steady crackle.
Prescribed burn is read by its signature -- a low-to-moderate orange flame, grey-to-white steam-laden smoke, resinous woodsmoke of guaiacol, syringol, and terpenes, a steady crackle rather than a roar. It hands its result to the reduced fuel it leaves, or to the escape it becomes.
Prescribed burn is dated from the ignition, the moment the crew lit the unit inside its window.
