# Process: Combustion

## Taxonomy

The typology of fire -- a catalog of the named phenomena of combustion.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: combustion

## Initiated by

Something burns, and it burns in a particular way that has a name.
Combustion starts wherever a place shows a fire that is not fire-in-general but a specific phenomenon -- the crown fire in the canopy, the peat smouldering below the surface, the pool fire on the spill, the backdraft in the sealed room -- each with its own fuel, its own chemistry, its own way of moving, and its own way of resisting the hand that fights it.
It is the catalog of how things burn: not the burn regime a place lives within, which is fire's, but the named type of combustion a place puts before whoever enters.

## Direction

Toward the specific -- each member a distinct fire with its fuel, its dynamics, its sensory signature, and its paradox.
Combustion sorts the world's fires into named phenomena across four domains -- the wildland (the crown fire, the grass fire, the peat smoulder, the spotting front), the compartment (flashover, backdraft, the rolling gas layer), the industrial plant (the pool fire, the BLEVE, the metal fire, the dust explosion), and the made fire (the backburn, the kiln, the flare, the flame on tissue) -- and fixes each by how it eats, how it moves, how it looks and smells and sounds, and how the obvious way to fight it fails.
The direction is precision: a place does not simply burn; it shows crowning or smouldering or a boilover, and the phenomenon named is the phenomenon that can be read, anticipated, and answered.

## Lever

The specificity of the phenomenon against the reflex to treat all fire as one thing.
The carrying force is that each combustion type behaves by its own law -- the grass fire measured in the seconds a front takes to pass, the peat fire in the months it burns unseen, the jet fire in the pressure that feeds it, the metal fire in the water that arms it; the resistance is the intuition that one method fits all fire, which is exactly the intuition each phenomenon's paradox punishes -- water spreading the pool fire, ventilation triggering the backdraft, the lance aerating the peat.
Name the phenomenon and its fuel, dynamics, and paradox follow; treat it as fire-in-general and the standard method meets the one fire it makes worse.

## Echo

The place read by the specific fire it shows, and the phenomenon answered on its own terms.
Combustion does not close; it is the standing catalog a place is read against -- the named fire it shows, and what that name predicts. It hands off what it is not: the place's burn regime and its modes are the fire engine's; the fire-weather that primes the burn is weather's; the drought that cures the fuel is hydrology's; the regrowth that follows is succession's; the fire from below, the eruption, is geomorphology's. This engine owns the typology: the named phenomena of combustion, each with its own chemistry, dynamics, sensory signature, and the paradox by which the obvious way to fight it fails or feeds it.
