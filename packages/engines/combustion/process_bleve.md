# Process: BLEVE

## Taxonomy

[Combustion](process_combustion.md), the industrial phenomenon of a pressure vessel bursting into a fireball.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: combustion

## Initiated by

A fire-heated pressure vessel of liquefied gas weakens and bursts, and the superheated liquid flashes to a rising fireball.
BLEVE -- boiling liquid expanding vapour explosion -- starts when a vessel of pressure-liquefied gas (LPG, propane) held above its atmospheric boiling point is heated by an external fire until the shell fails; the depressurised superheated liquid flash-boils explosively, and if flammable, ignites into a rising fireball with a surface around 1000-1200 C.
It is the vessel's own violence: not the fire on it but the fire it becomes when it tears open.

## Direction

Out as blast and fragments, up as a fireball tens of metres across.
BLEVE builds as fire heats the vessel, internal pressure rising while the vapour-space wall above the liquid line overheats and weakens; the shell ruptures, the flash boiling drives a blast wave and vessel fragmentation, and the flammable release rises as a fireball -- and time-to-BLEVE can be minutes.
The direction is a delayed catastrophic burst: the vessel absorbing fire until, in an instant, it releases all of it.

## Lever

The overheating vapour-space wall against the water that cannot reach it in time.
The carrying force is a weakening shell over a superheated liquid; the resistance fails because you do not fight a BLEVE-threatened vessel -- you evacuate: water helps only if applied early and copiously to the vapour-space shell, and once the relief valve is screaming and the wall is dry-hot, unmanned monitors and withdrawal are doctrine. The relief valve doing its job does not prevent the wall from failing.
Approach to cool it as it fails and it kills you; the correct answer is a large exclusion zone.

## Echo

The rising fireball, the tearing bang, the fragments rocketing beyond the fence.
BLEVE is read by its signature -- a rising-pitch relief-valve scream, a bulging discoloured tank, a changing flame sound, then a tearing bang, a large rising fireball with intense radiant heat, rocketing fragments, and a strong pressure wave. Its lethal radius far exceeds the fire that caused it.
BLEVE is dated from the rupture, the moment the weakened shell gave way and the superheated liquid became a fireball.
