# Process: Cremation

## Taxonomy

[Combustion](process_combustion.md), the made fire that reduces a body to calcined bone.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: combustion

## Initiated by

Auxiliary burners bring a chamber to temperature and the body, once alight, becomes much of its own fuel, reducing to calcined bone.
Cremation -- the cremator, the retort -- burns the body, mostly water with fat, protein, and bone mineral, with gas or oil burners and forced air, the body fat becoming significant fuel once established; the primary chamber runs 760-1000 C and above, the secondary afterburner 850-1000 C to combust the smoke and odour, through water evaporation, soft-tissue combustion, and calcination to bone, in about one and a half to three hours. The ashes are pulverised bone, ground in a cremulator, not soft-tissue ash.
It is fire administered to a body: a burn brought up by burners and then sustained by the body itself.

## Direction

Through evaporation, soft-tissue combustion, and calcination, held complete by the afterburner.
Cremation brings the retort up on auxiliary burners, then body fat sustains much of the burn, air injection and burner modulation keeping the primary chamber complete and the secondary above the smoke-and-odour threshold. The escalation is a high-fat body that overwhelms the chamber, or a metal implant that survives or explodes.
The direction is a governed reduction: a body walked through the stages to bone, held complete so nothing escapes the stack.

## Lever

The controlled chamber against the high-fat body and the implant that overwhelm it.
The carrying force is a chamber held at temperature and completeness; the resistance fails when a high-fat body overwhelms the chamber, exceeding its temperature limits with dark smoke and a fire risk that historically set crematory chimneys alight, and medical implants add danger -- cardiac pacemakers and batteries explode at temperature and must be removed first, while joint metals survive and are separated from the ashes -- and an under-temperature or too-fast cycle leaves incompletely combusted remains and odour breakthrough. The body's own fat is both the fuel and the thing that can overrun the burn.
Modulate the burners and the afterburner and it reduces the body clean; let the fat overwhelm it or leave an implant in, and the same fire smokes, damages, or bursts.

## Echo

The intense white-orange chamber glow, the clean stack of a good secondary, the afterburner's target odour.
Cremation is read by its signature -- an intense white-orange chamber glow, a near-invisible clean stack when the secondary chamber runs well or dark smoke and odour when it does not, the burnt-protein odour the afterburner is there to destroy. It hands its outcome to the calcined bone and its danger to the overwhelmed chamber and the unremoved implant.
Cremation is dated from the charge, the moment the body entered the retort and the burners were brought up.
