# Process: Vehicle Fire

## Taxonomy

[Combustion](process_combustion.md), the conveyance phenomenon of the burning road vehicle and its stranded energy.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: combustion

## Initiated by

A vehicle catches at the roadside, and what kind of fire it is depends on what drives it.
Vehicle fire burns the machine as its own fuel package -- the fuel-fed fire of a leaking line or crash spill, the electrical fire behind the dash, the tyre and plastics fire that makes the black acrid plume -- and, in the electric vehicle, the traction battery's thermal runaway: cell venting into cell under the floor, jetting flame, and a store of energy that no nozzle reaches.
It is the everyday fire with a new heart: the familiar car fire, and beneath it a battery that carries its own oxygen and its own schedule.

## Direction

Through the cabin in minutes, and, in the pack, cell to cell on its own clock.
Vehicle fire takes a cabin fast -- plastics, foam, and trim flashing into a total loss at the roadside -- while a burning traction pack propagates module by module under the floor, reigniting through and after suppression; the fire that looked finished can stand back up on the recovery truck, in the tow yard, days later, from the stranded energy still in the undamaged cells.
The direction is double: outward through the vehicle on the fire's usual pace, and inward through the pack on the battery's -- and the second clock does not stop when the flames do.

## Lever

The stranded energy against the word "extinguished".
The carrying force is an energy store that suppression cannot enter -- the pack casing that keeps water from the cells that need cooling, the charged modules that survive the fire as ignition sources in waiting; the resistance fails in the closure paradox: crews can knock down every visible flame and have removed almost none of the hazard, so the doctrine is not extinguish-and-leave but cool in vast quantities, then quarantine -- the isolation distance, the monitoring, sometimes the immersion tank -- treating the burned vehicle as a fire that has paused.
Call it out and drive away and the tow yard gets the second fire; treat the wreck as live until the pack is discharged or dead, and the fire finally ends when the energy does.

## Echo

The burned-out shell on the shoulder, still under quarantine.
Vehicle fire is read by its signature -- the fast acrid plume of burning plastics and tyres, the pops of struts, and, from a pack in runaway, the hiss and jet of venting cells, the milky electrolyte smoke, the popcorn crackle -- and afterwards by the placard on the wreck: distances kept, temperature watched, a fire officially over that everyone treats as merely asleep.
Vehicle fire is dated twice: from the ignition at the roadside, and from the clearance -- the later moment the pack was finally declared dead.
