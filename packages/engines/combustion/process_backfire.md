# Process: Backfire

## Taxonomy

[Combustion](process_combustion.md), the made fire lit to rob an oncoming fire of its fuel.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: combustion

## Initiated by

A crew lights a fire ahead of the main front so the two burn toward each other and the ground between them is gone before the wildfire arrives.
Backfire -- backburn, counter-fire, burning out -- is set from an anchor (a road, a stream, a dozer line) with drip torch, fusee, or aerial spheres; a backing fire burns cool at 200-500 C with long residence, while a counter-fire drawn into the main front's indraft flares to 800-1100 C. It fights fire with fire, deliberately enlarging the burn to deny the wildfire its fuel.
It is fire turned against fire: a burn made on purpose so the black widens to meet the front.

## Direction

Toward the main fire, pulled into its own indraft, widening the black.
Backfire exploits the main fire's convective indraft to draw the counter-fire toward it, widening the burned ground, steered by lighting pattern -- strip-head, ring, flanking. The escalation is the merger, where the two fires meet and the fuel between them is consumed.
The direction is a controlled collision: a fire steered into the one coming, so they annihilate the fuel between them.

## Lever

The indraft that pulls it the right way against the wind shift that turns it wrong.
The carrying force is the main fire's indraft drawing the counter-fire inward; the resistance fails when a wind shift or the front's own draft reverses and the backfire slops over the line, or the merger throws spotting embers across the anchor -- lit too early it fails to link with the front's draft, too late and the black cannot widen in time. The tool and the threat are the same fire.
Time it to the front's draft and it saves the line; misjudge the wind and the fire you lit is the one that jumps it.

## Echo

The low backing flame or the tall counter-run, the columns merging into a roar.
Backfire is read by its signature -- a backing fire's low flame under half a metre and pale grey-white smoke, or a counter-fire run's tall orange flame and dark column, woodsmoke of guaiacol and syringol, a low crackle rising to a roar as the fires merge. It hands its outcome to the black it makes, or to the line it crosses.
Backfire is dated from the lighting, the moment the crew set fire to stop a fire.
