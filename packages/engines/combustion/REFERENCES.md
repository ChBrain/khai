# Combustion: Reference

## Line of Work

Combustion as **process**: a catalog of the named phenomena of fire -- each a distinct way
something burns, fixed by its fuel and chemistry, its physical dynamics, its sensory
signature, and the paradox by which the obvious way to fight it fails or feeds it. It wires
on a place at Shown, the specific fire a place puts before whoever enters, because a place
does not simply burn: it shows crowning, or smouldering, or spotting, and the phenomenon
named is the phenomenon that can be read, anticipated, and answered. Where the fire engine
reads a place's **burn regime** -- its abstract modes, ignition to conflagration to renewal
-- combustion reads the specific **type**, the named phenomenon and its behaviour. The two
compose: fire gives the regime, combustion gives the fire.

The catalog is organised by four domains -- the wildland, the compartment, the industrial
plant, and the made fire -- and grows domain by domain. The wildland domain opens it: the
crown fire that runs the canopy, the grass fire that outruns a person, the chaparral fire
whose volatile oils ignite ahead of the front, the peat fire that smoulders unseen for
months, the holdover that overwinters and re-emerges, the spotting that leaps every line,
the slash fire that glows for days, and serotiny, the fire-cued regeneration a fire-adapted
seedbank requires. The compartment and extreme-dynamics domain follows: flashover and
backdraft, the rolling gas layer and the thermal layering it forms, the chimney effect and
the pyrolysis that feeds every fire, and the coupled fire-atmosphere extremes -- the
convection column, the fire whirl, pyrocumulonimbus, the firestorm that makes its own wind,
and the eruptive blow-up. Each is fixed as a process -- how it starts and on what fuel, how
it moves, the carrying force against the resistance, and the outcome and sensory read -- so
that a phenomenon named is a phenomenon whose chemistry, dynamics, and paradox follow.

The spine is fire science and fire ecology. In the wildland, Rothermel's surface-fire
spread model and the crown-fire work that followed (Van Wagner's crown-fire thresholds,
Finney's landscape fire modelling) fix how surface, crown, grass, and brush fires ignite,
spread, and transition; Rein carried the account below the flame to smouldering combustion,
the peat and organic-soil fires that are Earth's largest and most persistent; Pyne set the
regime frame the whole catalog sits within; and Christensen made plain the fire-adapted
ecology that serotiny serves. In the compartment, Drysdale's fire dynamics fix the enclosure
fire and its rapid-progression phenomena -- flashover, backdraft, rollover, the plume, and
the pyrolysis beneath them all -- and the coupled fire-atmosphere research reads the extreme
tier, the columns, whirls, pyrocumulonimbus, and firestorms a large fire generates as its
own weather.

## Restrictions

What the engine refuses to model, and to whom it delegates.

- **The place's burn regime and its modes (fire)**: The abstract regime a place lives within
  -- its combustibility and the modes ignition, creep, conflagration, smoulder, exclusion, and
  renewal -- is the fire engine's. Combustion is the specific named phenomenon, not the regime:
  fire reads that a place burns and how its regime turns; combustion reads that the place shows
  a crown fire, a peat smoulder, a boilover. Fire is the mode; combustion is the type.
- **The fire-weather that primes the burn (weather)**: The heat, wind, and low humidity that
  make a place ready to burn are the weather engine's; combustion is the phenomenon that
  readiness allows. The Santa Ana is weather's; the chaparral fire it drives is combustion's.
- **The drought that cures the fuel (hydrology)**: The long dry that turns living fuel to tinder
  is hydrology's; combustion is what the cured fuel then does when lit.
- **The regrowth that follows (succession)**: The sequence of life returning after a burn is
  succession's; serotiny reads the fire as the trigger and the cleared seedbed, but the returning
  life itself is succession's.
- **The fire from below (geomorphology)**: The volcanic fire -- magma, the eruption -- is
  geomorphology's, the ground's violence from within; combustion is the burning of fuel.
- **The facet itself is not engine content**: The engine declares the phenomena exist; which one
  a given place shows lives on that place, linked under its Shown chapter. The engine refuses to
  fix a place's fire for it.

## Encoding

Source to constraint, per file. (The wildland and compartment/dynamics domains are built;
the industrial plant and the made fire follow.)

- **[combustion](process_combustion.md)** (the root): the typology of fire -- the named phenomena
  of combustion, each with its own fuel, dynamics, sensory signature, and paradox. Anchored by the
  wildland fire-behaviour tradition.
- **[crown](process_crown.md)**: the canopy fire -- passive torching to active running to
  independent, wind- and slope-driven, spotting and plume-dominated. Anchored by Van Wagner
  (crown-fire thresholds) and Rothermel (spread).
- **[grass](process_grass.md)**: the fast fine-fuel front -- seconds of residence, spread
  outrunning a person, lethal by rate and by the flank-to-head wind shift. Anchored by Rothermel.
- **[chaparral](process_chaparral.md)**: the volatile-oil brush fire -- high intensity, radiant
  ignition ahead of the front, wind-driven under offshore events. Anchored by Rothermel.
- **[peat](process_peat.md)**: the flameless organic-soil smoulder -- months-long, water-shedding,
  fed by the air a lance lets in. Anchored by Rein (smouldering fires).
- **[holdover](process_holdover.md)**: the fire that overwinters below the snow and re-emerges --
  the subsurface smoulder read across time. Anchored by Rein (smouldering persistence).
- **[spotting](process_spotting.md)**: the firebrands lofted ahead of the front, starting fires
  across every break -- the fire reproducing at a distance. Anchored by Van Wagner and crown-fire
  firebrand behaviour.
- **[slash](process_slash.md)**: the heavy-residue burn -- intense front then days of deep-seated
  glowing that resists mop-up. Anchored by the wildland fuels literature.
- **[serotiny](process_serotiny.md)**: fire-cued regeneration -- the sealed cone opened by fire's
  heat onto the cleared seedbed. Anchored by Christensen (fire-adapted regeneration).
- **[flashover](process_flashover.md)**: the whole room igniting at once at the radiant-flux
  threshold -- and the ventilation that can trigger it. Anchored by Drysdale (compartment fire).
- **[backdraft](process_backdraft.md)**: the air-starved compartment exploding on inrush -- the
  opening the trigger, not the relief. Anchored by Drysdale.
- **[rollover](process_rollover.md)**: the ceiling gas layer igniting ahead of the fire, the
  countdown to flashover. Anchored by Drysdale.
- **[layering](process_layering.md)**: the two-layer hot-and-cool structure and the neutral plane,
  the gauge a straight stream collapses. Anchored by Drysdale.
- **[chimney](process_chimney.md)**: fire drawn up a vertical or inclined shaft, the trench effect
  and the stack effect. Anchored by Drysdale.
- **[pyrolysis](process_pyrolysis.md)**: solids gasifying into the fuel that burns, the feedback
  beneath fire growth and reflash. Anchored by Drysdale.
- **[column](process_column.md)**: the buoyant convection column a large fire drives, its in-draft
  and its lofted brands. Anchored by Drysdale and the coupled fire-atmosphere research.
- **[whirl](process_whirl.md)**: fire spun into a rotating, self-intensifying column of flame.
  Anchored by the coupled fire-atmosphere research.
- **[pyrocb](process_pyrocb.md)**: the fire that grows its own thunderstorm, with lightning and
  downdrafts of its own making. Anchored by the coupled fire-atmosphere research.
- **[firestorm](process_firestorm.md)**: coalesced fires drawing a self-made inflowing wind, a
  stationary self-sustaining inferno. Anchored by the mass-fire literature.
- **[blowup](process_blowup.md)**: the eruptive fire that surges up confining terrain far beyond
  its expected behaviour. Anchored by the wildland fire-behaviour and terrain-coupling research.
