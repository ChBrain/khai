# Combustion: Reference

## Line of Work

Combustion as **process**: a catalog of the named phenomena of fire -- each a distinct way
something burns, fixed by its fuel and chemistry, its physical dynamics, its sensory
signature, and the paradox by which the obvious way to fight it fails or feeds it. It wires
on a place at Shown, the specific fire a place puts before whoever enters, because a place
does not simply burn: it shows crowning, or smouldering, or spotting, and the phenomenon
named is the phenomenon that can be read, anticipated, and answered. Where the fire engine
reads a place's **burn regime** -- its abstract modes, ignition to conflagration to renewal
-- combustion reads the specific **type**, the named phenomenon and its behaviour. The two
compose: fire gives the regime, combustion gives the fire.

The catalog is organised by four domains -- the wildland, the compartment, the industrial
plant, and the made fire -- built domain by domain and now complete. The wildland domain opens it: the
crown fire that runs the canopy, the grass fire that outruns a person, the chaparral fire
whose volatile oils ignite ahead of the front, the peat fire that smoulders unseen for
months, the duff fire that creeps at the very limit of combustion through mineral-laced
forest floor, the holdover that overwinters and re-emerges, the spotting that leaps every line,
the slash fire that glows for days, and serotiny, the fire-cued regeneration a fire-adapted
seedbank requires. The compartment and extreme-dynamics domain follows: flashover and
backdraft, the rolling gas layer and the thermal layering it forms, the chimney effect and
the pyrolysis that feeds every fire, and the coupled fire-atmosphere extremes -- the
convection column, the fire whirl, pyrocumulonimbus, the firestorm that makes its own wind,
and the eruptive blow-up. The industrial and chemical domain adds the process-plant hazards:
the pool fire and the pressurised jet fire, the flash fire and the vapour cloud explosion,
the BLEVE that turns a vessel into a fireball, the boilover that erupts a burning tank, the
combustible-metal fire and lithium runaway, the dust explosion, the self-heating that lights
a pile from within, pyrophoric ignition on contact with air, the oxidizer-fed fire that
carries its own oxygen, hypergolic ignition on mixing, the arc flash of ionised-air plasma,
and the polymer fire whose smoke kills before its flame. The made-fire domain closes the
catalog with the fire humans light on purpose and hold to a task: the backfire and the
prescribed burn that fight or forestall a wildfire, swidden and fire-stick farming that
work the land with fire, the charcoal clamp starved of air, the forge and the blast furnace
and the kiln, the incinerator and the flare that destroy waste, the cutting torch and the
premixed burner, and the domestic fires -- the barbecue, the candle, and the cremation --
each read by the moment its control slips. Each is fixed as a process -- how it starts and on
what fuel, how it moves, the carrying force against the resistance, and the outcome and
sensory read -- so that a phenomenon named is a phenomenon whose chemistry, dynamics, and
paradox follow.

The spine is fire science and fire ecology. In the wildland, Rothermel's surface-fire
spread model and the crown-fire work that followed (Van Wagner's crown-fire thresholds,
Finney's landscape fire modelling) fix how surface, crown, grass, and brush fires ignite,
spread, and transition; Rein carried the account below the flame to smouldering combustion,
the peat and organic-soil fires that are Earth's largest and most persistent, and Frandsen
fixed the combustion limit of ground smouldering -- the moisture, mineral, and bulk-density
edge at which forest duff will or will not carry a fire; Cheney and Sullivan fixed the
grassfire's speed-over-residence danger, Keeley the Mediterranean-shrubland fire, Albini
the spot-fire distance, and Scholten and colleagues quantified the overwintering
holdover; Pyne set the
regime frame the whole catalog sits within; and Christensen made plain the fire-adapted
ecology that serotiny serves. In the compartment, Drysdale's fire dynamics fix the enclosure
fire and its rapid-progression phenomena -- flashover, backdraft (Fleischmann's defining
experiments), rollover, the plume, and the pyrolysis beneath them all -- and the coupled
fire-atmosphere research reads the extreme tier -- Byram's convection column, Forthofer and
Goodrick's fire whirls, Fromm's pyrocumulonimbus, Countryman's mass fires, Viegas's eruptive
blow-up -- the weather a large fire generates as its own. In the industrial plant, fire protection and process-safety engineering fixes
the hazard set -- the SFPE handbook tradition for pool, jet, and compartment fire behaviour,
and the process-safety literature (CCPS) for the pressure-and-cloud events, the BLEVE, the
vapour cloud explosion, the dust deflagration, and the reactive-chemical fires that carry
their own oxidiser or ignite on contact -- with the named standards and treatises behind
each hazard: Eckhoff on dust explosions, NFPA 484 on combustible metals, Bowes on
self-heating, Babrauskas on ignition, Clark on hypergolics, IEEE 1584 and NFPA 70E on the
arc flash, Purser on the smoke that kills. In the made fire, the account is Pyne's again --
the human history of fire as a tool, from the fire-stick to the furnace -- with the applied
crafts and combustion engineering behind each: wildland firing practice for the backfire and
the prescribed burn (Byram's fireline intensity and Wotton's flame-temperature work fixing
what a cool burn is), the collier's and smith's and potter's craft for the charcoal clamp,
forge, and kiln, metallurgical and process practice for the furnace, incinerator, and flare,
and the everyday combustion of hearth and cooking fire for the burner, barbecue, and candle.
The cultural fires are carried with credit, never claimed: fire-stick farming is Aboriginal
Australian peoples' practice, tens of millennia of cultural burning -- the term Rhys Jones's
coinage, the estate it made documented by Gammage, the practice's own voice Steffensen's --
and swidden is the practice of Indigenous and smallholder farming peoples across the
tropics, the Maya milpa and the Bemba chitemene among them, with Conklin's founding
ethnography and Richards's account of chitemene as the scholarship.

## Restrictions

What the engine refuses to model, and to whom it delegates.

- **The place's burn regime and its modes (fire)**: The abstract regime a place lives within
  -- its combustibility and the modes ignition, creep, conflagration, smoulder, exclusion, and
  renewal -- is the fire engine's. Combustion is the specific named phenomenon, not the regime:
  fire reads that a place burns and how its regime turns; combustion reads that the place shows
  a crown fire, a peat smoulder, a boilover. Fire is the mode; combustion is the type.
- **The fire-weather that primes the burn (weather)**: The heat, wind, and low humidity that
  make a place ready to burn are the weather engine's; combustion is the phenomenon that
  readiness allows. The Santa Ana is weather's; the chaparral fire it drives is combustion's.
- **The drought that cures the fuel (hydrology)**: The long dry that turns living fuel to tinder
  is hydrology's; combustion is what the cured fuel then does when lit.
- **The regrowth that follows (succession)**: The sequence of life returning after a burn is
  succession's; serotiny reads the fire as the trigger and the cleared seedbed, but the returning
  life itself is succession's.
- **The fire from below (geomorphology)**: The volcanic fire -- magma, the eruption -- is
  geomorphology's, the ground's violence from within; combustion is the burning of fuel.
- **The facet itself is not engine content**: The engine declares the phenomena exist; which one
  a given place shows lives on that place, linked under its Shown chapter. The engine refuses to
  fix a place's fire for it.

## Encoding

Source to constraint, per file. (All four domains -- the wildland, the compartment and
extreme dynamics, the industrial plant, and the made fire -- are built.)

- **[combustion](process_combustion.md)** (the root): the typology of fire -- the named phenomena
  of combustion, each with its own fuel, dynamics, sensory signature, and paradox. Anchored by the
  wildland fire-behaviour tradition.
- **[crown](process_crown.md)**: the canopy fire -- passive torching to active running to
  independent, wind- and slope-driven, spotting and plume-dominated. Anchored by Van Wagner
  (crown-fire thresholds), Rothermel (spread), and Butler et al. (measured crown-fire temperatures).
- **[grass](process_grass.md)**: the fast fine-fuel front -- seconds of residence, spread
  outrunning a person, lethal by rate and by the flank-to-head wind shift. Anchored by Cheney &
  Sullivan (Grassfires) and Rothermel.
- **[chaparral](process_chaparral.md)**: the volatile-oil brush fire -- high intensity, spotting
  and driven flame ahead of the front, wind-driven under offshore events. Anchored by Keeley et
  al. (Fire in Mediterranean Ecosystems) and Rothermel.
- **[peat](process_peat.md)**: the flameless organic-soil smoulder -- months-long, water-shedding,
  fed by the air a lance lets in. Anchored by Rein (smouldering fires).
- **[holdover](process_holdover.md)**: the fire that overwinters below the snow and re-emerges --
  the subsurface smoulder read across time. Anchored by Rein (smouldering persistence) and
  Scholten et al. (overwintering fires quantified).
- **[spotting](process_spotting.md)**: the firebrands lofted ahead of the front, starting fires
  across every break -- the fire reproducing at a distance. Anchored by Albini (spot-fire
  distance) and Van Wagner.
- **[slash](process_slash.md)**: the heavy-residue burn -- intense front then days of deep-seated
  glowing that resists mop-up. Anchored by Fahnestock (Logging Slash Flammability) and Albini
  (fuel modelling).
- **[serotiny](process_serotiny.md)**: fire-cued regeneration -- the sealed cone opened by fire's
  heat onto the cleared seedbed. Anchored by Christensen (fire-adapted regeneration), Lamont et
  al. (canopy seed storage), and Flematti et al. (the karrikin smoke cue).
- **[duff](process_duff.md)**: the ground fire at the combustion limit -- a marginal, mineral-ballasted
  smoulder creeping through the forest floor along organic seams and burned-out root channels,
  invisible to infrared, shedding water, and too airless to starve. Anchored by Frandsen (combustion
  limits of duff), with the decoupled surface signature from Hartford & Frandsen and the fire-induced
  water repellency from DeBano.
- **[flashover](process_flashover.md)**: the whole room igniting at once at the radiant-flux
  threshold -- and the ventilation that can trigger it. Anchored by Drysdale (compartment fire)
  and Thomas (the flashover criteria).
- **[backdraft](process_backdraft.md)**: the air-starved compartment exploding on inrush -- the
  opening the trigger, not the relief. Anchored by Drysdale and Fleischmann (the defining
  backdraft experiments).
- **[rollover](process_rollover.md)**: the ceiling gas layer igniting ahead of the fire, the
  countdown to flashover. Anchored by Drysdale.
- **[layering](process_layering.md)**: the two-layer hot-and-cool structure and the neutral plane,
  the gauge a straight stream collapses. Anchored by Drysdale.
- **[chimney](process_chimney.md)**: fire drawn up a vertical or inclined shaft, the trench effect
  and the stack effect. Anchored by Drysdale.
- **[pyrolysis](process_pyrolysis.md)**: solids gasifying into the fuel that burns, the feedback
  beneath fire growth and reflash. Anchored by Drysdale.
- **[column](process_column.md)**: the buoyant convection column a large fire drives, its in-draft
  and its lofted brands. Anchored by Byram (the convection column) and the coupled
  fire-atmosphere research.
- **[whirl](process_whirl.md)**: fire spun into a rotating, self-intensifying column of flame.
  Anchored by Forthofer & Goodrick (vortices in wildland fire).
- **[pyrocb](process_pyrocb.md)**: the fire that grows its own thunderstorm, with lightning and
  downdrafts of its own making. Anchored by Fromm et al. (pyrocumulonimbus).
- **[firestorm](process_firestorm.md)**: coalesced fires drawing a self-made inflowing wind, a
  stationary self-sustaining inferno. Anchored by Countryman (mass fires) and the Hamburg record.
- **[blowup](process_blowup.md)**: the eruptive fire that surges up confining terrain far beyond
  its expected behaviour. Anchored by Viegas & Simeoni (eruptive fire).
- **[pool-fire](process_pool-fire.md)**: the Class B liquid burning from its face, its rate set by
  radiative feedback, spread by water and cut by foam. Anchored by the SFPE handbook and
  Babrauskas (pool burning rates).
- **[jet](process_jet.md)**: the pressurised release burning as a directional torch, put out by
  shutting the valve, not the flame. Anchored by the SFPE handbook and Lowesmith et al.
  (jet-fire hazards).
- **[flash-fire](process_flash-fire.md)**: the drifting cloud deflagrating without blast, over
  before it can be fought. Anchored by the CCPS flash-fire guidelines.
- **[vce](process_vce.md)**: the vapour cloud accelerated to blast by congestion, defended only by
  prevention. Anchored by the CCPS vapour-cloud-explosion guidelines and the Buncefield
  investigation.
- **[bleve](process_bleve.md)**: the fire-heated vessel bursting into a fireball, answered by
  evacuation, not attack. Anchored by the CCPS pressure-vessel-burst and BLEVE guidelines.
- **[boilover](process_boilover.md)**: the burning tank erupting when its heat wave reaches the
  water bottom, fought from behind the bund. Anchored by Broeckmann & Schecker (the heat-wave
  mechanism) and the tank-fire literature.
- **[metal](process_metal.md)**: the combustible metal burning as its own fuel, feeding on water
  and CO2, answered only by Class D powder. Anchored by NFPA 484 and Glassman (metal combustion).
- **[lithium](process_lithium.md)**: the battery cell venting and reigniting from its own oxygen,
  cooled by flooding rather than smothered. Anchored by Feng et al. (the thermal-runaway
  mechanism).
- **[dust](process_dust.md)**: the suspended dust cloud deflagrating, its secondary blast lofted
  from the settled layer. Anchored by Eckhoff (dust explosions).
- **[self-heating](process_self-heating.md)**: the bulk organic pile oxidising to its own
  ignition, lit by the air that digging admits. Anchored by Bowes (self-heating and spontaneous
  combustion).
- **[pyrophoric](process_pyrophoric.md)**: the substance igniting on contact with air, answered by
  exclusion, not extinguishment. Anchored by Babrauskas (the Ignition Handbook).
- **[oxidizer](process_oxidizer.md)**: the fire carrying its own oxygen, immune to smothering and
  cooled at best. Anchored by NFPA 400 and 53, and Babrauskas (ammonium nitrate explosions).
- **[hypergolic](process_hypergolic.md)**: the fuel and oxidiser igniting on contact, where the
  agent itself may be fuel. Anchored by Clark (Ignition!) and the reactive-chemical literature.
- **[arc-flash](process_arc-flash.md)**: the ionised-air plasma conducting a fault, defused by
  de-energising, not by water. Anchored by IEEE 1584 and NFPA 70E, on Lee's arc-blast work.
- **[polymer](process_polymer.md)**: the plastic burning with easily quenched flame and lethal
  smoke, the smoke the real fire. Anchored by Purser (combustion toxicology, SFPE).
- **[backfire](process_backfire.md)**: the counter-fire lit into a wildfire's indraft to widen the
  black, undone by a wind shift that slops it over the line. Anchored by wildland firing practice
  and Byram (fireline intensity).
- **[prescribed](process_prescribed.md)**: the controlled burn held inside a written prescription,
  a wildfire the moment it escapes. Anchored by prescribed-fire practice, Wotton et al. (flame
  temperature and residence), and Pyne.
- **[swidden](process_swidden.md)**: the slash-and-burn clearing farmed for its ash, impoverishing
  when the fallow is shortened. Anchored by Conklin (Hanunoo Agriculture, the founding
  ethnography), Richards (the Bemba chitemene), and Pyne -- the practice of Indigenous and
  smallholder farming peoples across the tropics, credited, never claimed.
- **[firestick](process_firestick.md)**: the frequent cool mosaic burn, whose withholding is what
  builds the megafire. Anchored by Jones (the coinage), Gammage (The Biggest Estate on Earth),
  and Steffensen (Fire Country) -- the practice of Aboriginal Australian peoples, tens of
  millennia of cultural burning, credited, never claimed.
- **[charcoal](process_charcoal.md)**: the air-starved clamp that chars wood rather than burning it,
  ruined by one open vent. Anchored by the collier's craft and FAO's carbonisation chemistry.
- **[forge](process_forge.md)**: the forced-draft bed that works steel, its oxidising fire burning
  the very metal. Anchored by the smith's craft (Weygers; Biringuccio's Pirotechnia).
- **[furnace](process_furnace.md)**: the blast furnace run for years, wrecked not by fire but by the
  chill that freezes iron in the hearth. Anchored by ironmaking practice (Peacey & Davenport's
  The Iron Blast Furnace).
- **[kiln](process_kiln.md)**: the firing curve that transforms ware, destroyed by the thermal shock
  of too-fast heating or cooling. Anchored by the ceramic, lime, and cement literature (Hamer's
  Potter's Dictionary).
- **[incinerator](process_incinerator.md)**: the waste burn held above its legal temperature, its
  dioxins re-forming as the flue gas cools. Anchored by the EU incineration directive and Vogg &
  Stieglitz (de-novo dioxin synthesis).
- **[flare](process_flare.md)**: the stack kept always alight, its emergency the pilot loss that
  vents raw gas. Anchored by API 521 and the EPA flare-efficiency studies.
- **[torch](process_torch.md)**: the oxy-fuel flame that cuts steel, its killer the flashback back to
  the cylinders. Anchored by welding and cutting practice (OSHA 1910.253; CGA G-1).
- **[burner](process_burner.md)**: the premixed flame clean and safe when near-invisible, failing by
  flashback or lift-off. Anchored by Lewis & von Elbe (flashback and blow-off) and the Bunsen
  tradition.
- **[barbecue](process_barbecue.md)**: the radiant bed that cooks by browning, its grease fire turned
  to a fireball by water. Anchored by McGee (the chemistry of browning) and Class-K fire practice.
- **[candle](process_candle.md)**: the self-feeding wick flame, relightable down its own wax-smoke
  trail without contact. Anchored by Hamins & Bundy (the NIST candle-flame characterisation) and
  the Faraday tradition.
- **[cremation](process_cremation.md)**: the cremator that reduces a body to bone, overwhelmed by a
  high-fat body or a pacemaker that explodes. Anchored by cremation-practice engineering (the
  DEFRA process guidance).
