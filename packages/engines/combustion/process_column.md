# Process: Convection Column

## Taxonomy

[Combustion](process_combustion.md), the phenomenon of the buoyant plume a large fire drives.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: combustion

## Initiated by

A large fire's hot products rise as a buoyant column, drawing air in at the base and lofting embers from the top.
Convection column starts wherever combustion products (500-1000 C at the source) rise through ambient air, entraining and diluting as they climb; the rising column pulls a strong in-draft toward the fire at ground level and lofts firebrands aloft.
It is the fire's own weather-engine: the vertical current that couples a fire to the atmosphere above it.

## Direction

Upward, kilometres high, entraining air and driving the fire's own wind.
Convection column rises on the density deficit; entrainment (increasing with height) sets the dilution, and in the open the column can rise kilometres and feed pyroconvection. Its ground-level in-draft is the fire's self-made wind, and its top is where spotting brands are launched.
The direction is a self-organising circulation: the fire making the very wind that feeds it.

## Lever

The buoyant updraft against the aerial attack it disperses and the collapse it can suffer.
The carrying force is buoyancy strong enough to drive a kilometres-high current; the resistance fails because aerial attack into a strong plume is turbulent and ineffective (the updraft disperses drops and hazards aircraft), and a plume-dominated fire runs on its own convection rather than ambient wind -- so it can shift suddenly if the column collapses, washing embers and hot gas back to the surface.
Fly water into the column and it scatters; the column governs the fire until it collapses on its own.

## Echo

The towering smoke column, the in-draft at its foot, the ember rain from its top.
Convection column is read by its signature -- a visible column whose colour keys to the fuel and completeness, a roar of entrainment near the base, a strong ground-level in-draft toward the fire. It hands the fire its spotting brands and, when it grows deep, a pyrocumulonimbus above.
Convection column is dated from the lift, the moment the fire's heat organised the air above it into a rising current of its own.
