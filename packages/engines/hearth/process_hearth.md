# Process: Hearth

## Taxonomy

The kept flame.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: hearth

## Initiated by

Fire is kept because a place needs its warmth, its light, and the continuity it pledges to those gathered around it.
A hearth begins wherever a fire is not made and left to run but made and entrusted to a keeper -- the vestal, the youngest child, the stoker sworn to the duty, the household's heart beating as the flame's steady rise and fall.
It is the fire that must not die, and the keeping itself that makes the place a home.

## Direction

Through the keeper's watching, and toward the identity made from the fire's state.
The fire is not only combustion but an institution -- a place organized around the flame, the tending a duty, the keeper an office held by the family, the household's standing read in the flame's height and color and steadiness.
Five modes work the kept flame: [Stoking](process_stoking.md) is the daily feeding and watching, [Banking](process_banking.md) the fire put to sleep through the night in its own ash, [Rekindling](process_rekindling.md) the relight from coal or borrowed ember, [Going Cold](process_going-cold.md) the hearth extinguished as loss or sanction, and [Perpetual](process_perpetual.md) the flame that cannot die, burning for centuries in the temple and the sanctuary.

## Lever

The keeper's hand against the fire's nature to consume and die away.
The carrying force is the keeper's vigilance and the daily duty -- the feeding, the watching, the stirring, the ash removed and laid back to hold the coal alive -- and the resistance is the fire's own hunger and its will to burn to nothing, the slope toward cold that stopping the watch lets run.
Let the keeper tend and the fire holds; let the watch fail a moment and the fire teaches loss.

## Echo

The place made by the kept flame and the keeper it makes.
A kept fire is read in its steadiness, its rise and fall matched to the keeper's rhythm -- and the hearth speaks of what the place values, whom it trusts with its warmth, and what happens when that trust fails.
The place is dated from the lighting, and from the day the flame is let go cold or a keeper dies, and the fire with them.
