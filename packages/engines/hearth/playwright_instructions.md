# Instructions: Hearth

How a Playwright wires the hearth engine into a play. You wire by linking the play's own
content to the engine's files; the engine files stay untouched, they are canon. The
Roadie plumbs the engine's law into Instructions Knowledge on deploy. Authoring
guidance, not runtime content, and it does not go on tour.

## Human

- The human sets which places carry a fire that is kept -- tended daily by a keeper, banked to survive the night, rekindled when lost, extinguished as loss or sanction, perpetuated across centuries in sanctuary and temple -- and whether that keeping is the place's standing condition or a live event in the play.

## Agent

- From the play's own content, a place whose fire is an actor links under Shown the mode of its kept flame: the stoking that feeds and watches it daily, the banking that puts it to sleep through the night in its own ash, the rekindling that revives it from coal or borrowed ember, the going cold that marks the household's end or the institution's loss, or the perpetual flame that has burned for centuries and binds keeper to duty across time. Play the fire as the keeping itself shown -- the keeper's hand laying fuel, the ash drawn around the coals, the cold hearth in the morning, the relay of Vestals, the torch carried from one house to another -- present in what the place puts before whoever enters, never narrated, and never as a mirror of a keeper's own devotion or loss. Keep it the institution of the kept flame itself, not the flame's mechanics (that is combustion), the burn regime and the fire that escapes the hearth (fire), or the rite performed around the flame (ritual). You link to the engine, you never edit it.

## Collaboration

- The combustion itself is combustion's; the ritual structure of fire-keeping is ritual's; the burn regime and the fire that escapes the keeping is fire's; the keeper's feeling and what the fire means to them is the persona's own engines' -- hearth reads the keeping as an institution shown, answerable by ritual and combustion but distinct from them. Whether the mode rings true to the place and its keeper, and whether the dependency and obligation of borrowing fire are read honestly, is the team's review.

## Knowledge

- Hearth is the kept flame as an institution of a place -- a fire that is tended daily by a keeper, banked to survive the night, rekindled when lost, extinguished as loss or sanction, and perpetuated across centuries in sanctuary and temple, shown in the place's warmth and the keeper's life (Fustel de Coulanges' hearth as a sacred deposit grounding the family; Goudsblom's fire-keeping as humanity's first regime, preceding fire-making by over a million years; Boyce's Zoroastrian Atash Bahram fed daily by generations of keepers; Bachelard's reverie of the tended flame; Beard's Vestals and Rome's eternal fire). It wires on a place at Shown, and it is the kept flame itself -- the fire not made but maintained, tended by a keeper whose hands read the flame's character and answer with fuel and ash -- not the flame's mechanics (combustion), the burn regime and the escaped fire (fire), the rite performed around it (ritual), or the keeper's feeling and the fire as their mirror (the persona's engines, and the play's pitch). Five modes carry it -- stoking is the daily keeping, the feeding and watching that holds the flame steady and makes it a clock of care; banking is the fire put to sleep in ash through the night, held alive by knowledge of how deep to bury the coals; rekindling is the relight from one's own coal or from a borrowed ember or from a neighbour's fire, each relight carrying its own obligation; going cold is the cold hearth as the social end of the household or institution, public notice of dissolution; perpetual is the eternal flame of Vesta or the Atash Bahram, kept by a relay of keepers across centuries, binding the duty to time itself. Read a place by the mode of its kept flame.

## System

- Do give a place whose fire is kept its mode under Shown; do play the keeping as the institution itself shown, present not narrated; do keep the reading the kept flame's institution -- the stoking, the banking, the rekindling, the going cold, the perpetual -- not a feeling and not a symbol.
- Do read the obligation honestly: borrowing fire from a neighbour carries a debt written into the hearth itself; rekindling as a blessing from the sanctuary or festival carries dependence; perpetual flames are written in the relay of keepers, each one bound to the next across time.
- Do read going cold honestly as the end of the household or the institution, not as a temporary affair to be recovered from; the cold hearth is the social statement of dissolution.
- Do not model the flame's mechanics (that is combustion), the burn regime and the fire that escapes the hearth (fire), the rite performed around the flame (ritual), or the keeper's feeling (the persona's own engines). Do not make the keeping mirror a keeper's own devotion or loss, or stand in for an abstract duty (that is the play's pitch). Do not edit the engine's files; wire only from the play's side.
