# Process: Regime

## Taxonomy

[Climate](process_climate.md), the mode of the place's standing type.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: climate

## Initiated by

The long-run drivers set a place its baseline of temperature and moisture, and that baseline is the kind of place it is.
Regime starts wherever a place's climate settles into a type -- the arid, the humid, the tropical, the temperate, the continental, the polar -- the standing average of warmth and water that the climate classifications name and every place is one of.
It is the ground fact of the climate: not a season and not an event but the type the place belongs to, the terms it is set in before any weather falls.

## Direction

Toward the place lived and built as its type.
Regime sets the place's whole character to its baseline -- the rainforest lush because it is wet and warm as a rule, the desert bare because it is dry as a rule, the tundra low because it is cold as a rule -- the vegetation, the soil, the very look of the place following from the type.
It moves nothing in the hour; it is the standing frame the hours fall within, the reason a place looks and lives the way its kind does anywhere on earth.

## Lever

The long-run balance of sun and moisture against the sheer constancy of the type.
The carrying force is the place's position in the world -- its latitude, altitude, distance from the sea, place in the great circulations -- fixing how much warmth and water it gets across the years; the resistance is inertia: the type holds, and no storm or dry spell changes what kind of place it is.
Move a place's latitude or raise its mountains and the type changes over ages; short of that, the regime is the fixed baseline everything else is read against.

## Echo

The place set in its kind, and built to it.
Regime leaves the place its standing type -- the wet, the dry, the hot, the cold -- and hands that type to everything downstream: the water it tends to, the fire season it keeps, the life adapted to its terms.
It is the baseline the other modes vary and the other forces play out within: seasonality is the type's rhythm, extremity its edge, shift its slow change, and the flood and the burn are the type's weather made extreme.
Regime is dated from the long run, the average of decades that says what kind of place this is.
