# Process: Seasonality

## Taxonomy

[Climate](process_climate.md), the mode of the place's reliable seasonal structure.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: climate

## Initiated by

The place's regime runs a pattern within the year that repeats, and the place lives by it.
Seasonality starts wherever the climate has a shape across the year the place can count on -- the monsoon that comes each summer, the dry season and the wet, the Mediterranean summer drought, the deep freeze of the continental winter, or the equatorial sameness that has almost none.
It is the regime's rhythm: not this season's weather but whether and how the place has seasons at all, the reliable cycle the year is built around.

## Direction

Around the year, in the pattern the place keeps.
Seasonality moves the place through its reliable turns -- the flood that comes with the monsoon, the fire season that comes with the dry, the growing season between the frosts, the fallow the winter enforces -- and the whole life of the place, its planting and harvest and shelter, is timed to the cycle.
It moves the place not toward one state but around a loop it can predict: the place knows, from its climate, what part of the year it is by what its climate is doing.

## Lever

The reliability of the cycle against the harm when the place mistimes it or the cycle fails.
The carrying force is the regularity -- the monsoon that comes near enough the same date each year that a civilization can be built on it, the frost-free window a farmer can plant into; the resistance, and the danger, is the year the pattern breaks -- the monsoon that fails, the late frost, the season that does not come, wrecking everything timed to it.
Depend on the cycle and it carries the place; lean too hard on it and the failed season is a famine.

## Echo

The place timed to its year, and thrown when the year misfires.
Seasonality leaves the place with a calendar written by its climate -- the wet and the dry, the frost and the thaw, the season to plant and the season to wait -- and hands that calendar to everything the place does in time.
It is the regime's structure the other forces ride: the fire keeps the dry season, the flood keeps the wet, the wearing keeps the freeze-thaw, all on the schedule the seasonality sets.
Seasonality is dated from the cycle, the reliable turn of the year the place has learned to live by.
