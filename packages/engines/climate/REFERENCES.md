# Climate: Reference

## Line of Work

Climate as **process**: the standing, long-run atmospheric regime a place is set in --
what a place expects, against weather's what it gets on the day -- its baseline type, its
reliable seasonal structure, the extreme it is built for, and the slow shift of the
regime itself, shown in the whole adapted landscape before anyone names it. It wires on a
place at Shown, the place's physical self-presentation, because a place's climate is
written across everything it shows -- the vegetation tuned to it, the architecture built
for it, the bare or lush face the regime gives it. The domain does not model the day's
weather, the storm or the heat in the hour, which is the weather engine's; nor the actual
water and its extremes, which is hydrology's; nor the actual burn, which is fire's; nor
the actual wearing, which is erosion's. It models the standing regime that governs which
of those forces dominate, and reads it as the odds every other place-force plays out
within.

The spine is the distinction between weather and climate -- the day against the decades --
and the recognition that the regime, long taken as the fixed backdrop, is itself a thing
that moves. Koppen gave the standing regimes their names, sorting the world's climates
into the types a place is one of. Lamb founded the historical reading -- that climate has
a history, the little ice age and the medieval warm period, the regime shifting within
human time. And Hulme carried the reading into the present, the changing climate as a
fact a place and a society must now reckon with rather than assume. So the engine gives
climate four modes -- regime, the standing type; seasonality, its reliable rhythm;
extremity, its hard edge; and shift, the regime itself in motion -- the frame every other
force falls within, and the frame that can itself move.

## Restrictions

What the engine refuses to model, and to whom it delegates.

- **The day's weather (weather)**: This is the engine's defining boundary. Weather is what
  a place gets on the day -- the storm, the heat, the front in the hour; climate is what a
  place expects over the decades -- the standing regime the weather falls within. Weather
  is the roll; climate is the odds. A cold morning is weather; a place that is cold as a
  rule is climate. The two are the same medium at two timescales, and the engine owns only
  the long run.
- **The actual water and its extremes (hydrology)**: The flood this year, the drought this
  season, the river's flow, the aquifer are hydrology's; climate is the standing tendency
  that sets whether a place runs wet or dry over the decades. Climate is why a place is
  arid; hydrology is the drought it has now. Climate sets the water regime's baseline;
  hydrology is the water that comes.
- **The actual burn (fire)**: The fire that burns, its ignition and its front, are fire's;
  climate is the regime that sets the length and severity of the fire season, the
  fuel-drying, the odds of the burn. Climate primes the fire season; fire is the burn
  within it.
- **The actual wearing (erosion)**: The surface worn, the frost-shatter, the wind-strip are
  erosion's; climate is the regime that sets how often the ground freezes and thaws, how
  dry and windy it is, the rate the wearing runs at. Climate sets the pace; erosion is the
  wearing at that pace.
- **The current season (weather's season)**: The weather engine reads the season the place
  is in now, the standing tilt running under the day's weather. Climate's seasonality is
  the reliable seasonal structure the regime has -- whether and how the place has seasons
  at all, over the long run. Weather's season is which part of the cycle it is; climate's
  seasonality is that the cycle exists and what shape it keeps.
- **A persona's feeling, and the climate as authored symbol**: How the climate makes a
  persona feel -- the gloom of the grey regime, the lift of the warm -- is the persona's
  own engines'; climate delivers the regime, not the feeling. And the climate made to
  **stand for** a persona's standing mood or temperament, the cold place that mirrors a
  cold heart, is refused: that is authored key, the play's pitch, not the force on the
  place.
- **The mode itself is not engine content**: The engine declares the four modes exist;
  which one a given place shows lives on that place, linked under its Shown chapter. The
  engine refuses to fix a place's climate for it.
