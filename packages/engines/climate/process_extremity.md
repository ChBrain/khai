# Process: Extremity

## Taxonomy

[Climate](process_climate.md), the mode of the extreme the regime imposes.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: climate

## Initiated by

The place's regime runs to an extreme that presses on everything living there, and the place is defined by it.
Extremity starts wherever the climate's edge is hard enough to set the terms of survival -- the killing cold of the poles and the high mountains, the lethal heat of the desert, the deep aridity that allows almost no water, the drowning humidity, the relentless wind.
It is the regime at its most demanding: not a bad day but the standing severity the place's whole life must be built to withstand.

## Direction

Toward the place shaped and narrowed by what its climate will not relent on.
Extremity presses everything at the place toward what can bear it -- the life that survives the cold small, slow, and few; the desert life spare and water-hoarding; the settlement dug in, shuttered, or huddled against the standing hardship.
It moves the place toward the specialist and the scarce: an extreme climate holds only what is built for it, and the harsher the edge, the fewer the things that can live within the terms it sets.

## Lever

The severity of the regime against the adaptation and shelter life brings to meet it.
The carrying force is the standing harshness -- the cold that never truly breaks, the heat that returns each day, the dry that gives no water; the resistance is everything the place and its life do to endure it -- the fur and the fat, the deep root, the thick wall, the stored water, the whole apparatus of surviving the edge.
Meet the extreme with adaptation and the place holds a hard, spare life; fail to, and the extreme is simply lethal, the climate that kills what is not built for it.

## Echo

The place defined and narrowed by its climatic edge.
Extremity leaves the place marked by its hardship -- the polar, the desert, the alpine, the place known for the one thing its climate does without mercy -- and hands that edge to everything that must live there, on the climate's terms and no gentler.
It is the regime's severity the other forces sharpen against: the drought is deeper here, the cold-wearing harder, the fire-drying more total, all because the climate's edge sets them at their worst.
Extremity is dated from the standing hardship, the edge of the regime the place is built, if it can be, to survive.
