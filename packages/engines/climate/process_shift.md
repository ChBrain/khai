# Process: Shift

## Taxonomy

[Climate](process_climate.md), the mode where the regime itself moves.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: climate

## Initiated by

The standing regime, assumed to hold, begins to move, and the baseline the place was built to changes under it.
Shift starts wherever the climate is no longer the same as it was -- the slow warming or cooling, the drying or the wetting, the seasons creeping earlier, the belt of a climate migrating across the map -- the little ice age, the medieval warm spell, the warming of this century.
It is the regime in motion: not the weather changing day to day, which is its nature, but the climate itself changing, the terms of every day drifting from what they were.

## Direction

Away from the old normal, toward a regime the place was not built for.
Shift moves the baseline -- the frosts coming later and the growing season longer, the rains failing where they were reliable, the range of the heat and the drought creeping poleward and uphill, the coast losing to a rising sea.
It moves the place out from under its own adaptation: the vegetation tuned to the old climate, the farming timed to the old seasons, the city built for the old sea level, all left standing in a climate that no longer matches them.

## Lever

The drift of the regime against the fixity of everything built to the old one.
The carrying force is whatever moves the long-run balance -- the slow wobble of the orbit, the sun, the volcanoes, and now the changed air itself; the resistance is the sheer inertia of adaptation -- the forests, the farms, the cities, the whole settled life set to a climate assumed permanent and slow to move when it does not hold.
When the regime shifts faster than the adaptation can follow, the gap is the harm: the stranded forest, the failed crop, the drowned coast, the place caught living by a climate that has left.

## Echo

The place stranded between the climate it was built for and the one it has.
Shift leaves the place mismatched to its own regime -- the adaptation set to a normal that has moved, the reliable season gone unreliable, the map of the climate redrawn under the standing place.
It is the mode that undoes the others' assumption: regime, seasonality, and extremity all read as fixed, and shift is the fact that they are not -- that the frame the place is set in can itself move, and when it does, everything set to it must move or be stranded.
Shift is dated from the drift, the slow departure of the climate from the one the place learned to expect.
