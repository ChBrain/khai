# Process: The Report Back

## Taxonomy

[The Return](process_return_advice.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: advice

## Initiated by

The persona tells the adviser what happened.
Selectively, and without meaning to.

## Direction

Toward an account shaped by what the adviser will want to hear.
The parts of the decision that ran with the advice are recounted; the parts that ran against it are compressed, or presented as forced by circumstances. The persona is not covering anything up. They are telling a story to a specific listener, which is what everybody does.
The adviser takes the account as feedback, and it is the only feedback they will ever get.

## Lever

The ordinary tact of telling somebody what they contributed against the record it creates.
The carrying force is that shaping an account for its listener is normal and mostly kind: nobody reports their own decisions in full to anybody, the adviser does not need the parts that were not about them, and the edit is small and made without calculation; the resistance is that this account is the adviser's entire evidence about their own judgement, so an adviser accumulates decades of confirmations and never learns whether they were right about anything.
The Report Back is the only feedback in the system and it is not feedback.

## Echo

A tidy version, filed as evidence.
It shows why advisers get more confident with practice and not more accurate.
The Report Back is dated to the next conversation.
