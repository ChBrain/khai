# Process: The Return

## Taxonomy

[Advice](process_advice.md), what goes back to the adviser.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: advice

## Initiated by

The decision is taken and the adviser is still there.
The Return is what reaches them: [a report back](process_the_report_back.md) that is edited, [thanks](process_the_unfollowed_thanks.md) for advice that was not used, and [an adviser now invested](process_the_advisor_invested.md) in how it turns out.

## Direction

Toward an adviser who believes their advice was taken.
Almost nobody reports back accurately. The persona does not say that they kept seven parts of their own view; they say that it was really helpful, that it clarified things, and then describe a decision the adviser can read as the one they recommended.
This is not a lie and would be strange to correct. It is also the reason the adviser's estimate of their own influence is much larger than their influence.

## Lever

The kindness of the report against what it leaves the adviser believing.
The carrying force is that the persona is doing the right thing and the alternative is worse: somebody who gave their time deserves to hear it mattered, an itemised account of how little was used would be gratuitous, and the warmth is genuine even when the uptake was not; the resistance is that the adviser now holds a false reading of their own effect, which they will carry into the next episode and into their sense of what they are worth to this person.
The Return leaves the adviser better informed about the friendship and worse about the advice.

## Echo

Gratitude that overstates its own cause.
It is the movement nobody thinks is part of the mechanism, and it is what makes the next one run.
The Return is dated to the follow-up and is not experienced as a report.
