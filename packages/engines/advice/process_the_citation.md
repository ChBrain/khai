# Process: The Citation

## Taxonomy

[The Keeping](process_keeping_advice.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: advice

## Initiated by

It has gone badly, and the persona says who they asked.
Not as an excuse. As context.

## Direction

Toward the consultation being produced as evidence, and only in one direction.
When the decision goes wrong the fact of having asked appears: it was looked at properly, somebody experienced was consulted, the thing was not done rashly. When it goes right the adviser is not mentioned, because a good outcome needs no account of how it was reached.
So the record of the consultation surfaces asymmetrically, and it surfaces most in exactly the cases where least of the advice was used.

## Lever

The fairness of showing that a decision was taken carefully against what the showing does with somebody else's name.
The carrying force is that the citation is true and relevant: the persona did consult, that is a fact about how the decision was made, and a listener judging them is entitled to know it -- concealing it would be the stranger choice; the resistance is that the adviser is being placed next to an outcome they did not shape, and they will hear about it from a third party, in a version where their contribution was decisive.
The Citation is accurate about the asking and misleading about the influence.

## Echo

A name produced at the worst moment for the person holding it.
It shows where the second name goes when it is needed.
The Citation is dated to the bad news and is never issued after good news.
