# Process: The Confident Voice

## Taxonomy

[The Weighing](process_weighing_advice.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: advice

## Initiated by

One adviser sounds sure and the other hedges.
The sure one is followed.

## Direction

Toward manner treated as a signal about accuracy.
Between two advisers, the more confident is taken more, is trusted more, and is asked again. It is a reasonable heuristic in the abstract, since somebody who knows more often does feel surer -- and the relation between how confident an adviser sounds and how right they are is weak enough that it cannot carry the weight put on it.
The hedging adviser is being more informative, and is punished for it.

## Lever

The reasonableness of reading confidence against how little confidence tracks.
The carrying force is that confidence is not nothing and using it is not foolish: it is often the only cue available, an adviser with no view at all is no use, and the person who says "I do not know" has genuinely given the persona less to work with; the resistance is that confidence is cheap, unevenly distributed by temperament and by training, and easy to produce without any of the knowledge it is standing in for -- so the persona is selecting on a trait of the adviser rather than a property of the advice.
The Confident Voice is followed for a signal it cannot support.

## Echo

The surest person in the room, taken most seriously.
It shows how an adviser is chosen when nobody can check the advice.
The Confident Voice is dated to the delivery and outlives the outcome.
