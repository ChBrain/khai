# Process: The Next Adviser

## Taxonomy

[The Keeping](process_keeping_advice.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: advice

## Initiated by

There is another decision, and the persona picks somebody to ask.
Usually without deliberating about it.

## Direction

Toward a selection made on the feel of the last consultation.
What the persona remembers is not whether the adviser was right, which they mostly cannot establish, but what the conversation was like: whether they were taken seriously, whether they left steadier, whether the adviser was easy to talk to and did not make them feel foolish.
The adviser who agreed is remembered as insightful. The one who disagreed is remembered as not having understood the situation. Both readings are honestly held, and the second one selects that adviser out.

## Lever

The sensibleness of choosing an adviser you can talk to against what the criterion excludes.
The carrying force is that comfort is a real requirement and not a weakness: a persona who cannot speak frankly to an adviser will withhold the facts that matter, rapport is what makes the whole exchange possible, and an adviser who leaves somebody worse than they found them has failed at part of the job; the resistance is that comfort correlates with agreement, so the persona is building an advisory circle filtered on concord -- and each round of it feels like learning who is worth asking.
The Next Adviser is chosen on the conversation rather than on the outcome.

## Echo

A circle of advisers assembled by how they felt to consult.
It shows the loop closing, and why a persona's counsel narrows as their experience of seeking it grows.
The Next Adviser is dated from the last episode and decides the next one.
