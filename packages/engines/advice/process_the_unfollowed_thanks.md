# Process: The Unfollowed Thanks

## Taxonomy

[The Return](process_return_advice.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: advice

## Initiated by

The persona thanks the adviser warmly for advice they did not take.
And means it.

## Direction

Toward gratitude that is sincere and is not about the content.
What the persona is thanking the adviser for is real: the time, the attention, the willingness to say something difficult, the fact that somebody was there while they thought about it. None of that is the advice.
The adviser hears it as being thanked for the advice, because that is what was on the table, and there is no version of the sentence that separates the two.

## Lever

The genuineness of the thanks against the absence of the thing being thanked for.
The carrying force is that the gratitude is not false and should not be withheld: the help was real even where the content was not used, and a persona who thanked people only for advice they followed would thank almost nobody and would deserve what followed; the resistance is that language gives them one word for both, so the adviser is told their judgement was valued when what was valued was their presence -- and both parties leave the conversation with different accounts of what just happened.
The Unfollowed Thanks are true about the person and false about the advice.

## Echo

A sincere sentence read as a different sincere sentence.
It shows where the adviser's misreading actually comes from, which is not from anybody's dishonesty.
The Unfollowed Thanks are dated to the end of the conversation.
