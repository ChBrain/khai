# Process: The Prepared Answer

## Taxonomy

[The Approach](process_approach_advice.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: advice

## Initiated by

The asker already knows what they are going to do.
They have known for a while, and they are asking anyway.

## Direction

Toward a consultation held after the decision it is supposed to inform.
Most advice is sought late. The options have been narrowed, one of them is quietly preferred, and what is wanted is a second voice next to it -- which is why the question is so often posed as a choice between the favourite and something obviously worse.
The asker is not being dishonest and does not experience this as rigged. They genuinely want to hear what the other person thinks, and they have already decided.

## Lever

The reality of wanting a second view against the fact that the first one is settled.
The carrying force is that the asker is not wasting anybody's time and the exercise has value: a preference held privately has not been said out loud to a person who might object, saying it is itself a test, and an adviser who does object will be heard even at this stage; the resistance is that the decision has already absorbed most of the work, so the advice arrives as a check on a conclusion rather than as an input to one, and an adviser who does not realise this will make a case that has nowhere to go.
The Prepared Answer sets the advice to arrive after the decision.

## Echo

A question about an option that is not open.
It shows why so much advice is described afterwards as unhelpful by people who took none of it.
The Prepared Answer is dated earlier than the asking.
