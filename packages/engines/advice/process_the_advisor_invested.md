# Process: The Adviser Invested

## Taxonomy

[The Return](process_return_advice.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: advice

## Initiated by

The adviser starts following the outcome.
Asking how it went, remembering the dates, wanting to know.

## Direction

Toward an adviser with a stake in a decision that is not theirs.
Having given a judgement, the adviser is now attached to whether it was right, and the attachment does the ordinary things: they notice the evidence for it, they are relieved by good news out of proportion to their involvement, and the next round of advice comes out harder than the first.
None of this is meddling. It is what happens to anybody who has said what they think in front of somebody else.

## Lever

The care behind the interest against what having a stake does to the next judgement.
The carrying force is that the investment is a form of loyalty and the persona benefits from it: an adviser who forgot the whole thing the moment it was over would be no use, and continued interest is most of what makes somebody worth asking; the resistance is that an adviser with a position now has something to be right about, so their second opinion is no longer independent of their first -- and the persona, who asked for an outside view, is now being advised by somebody inside.
The Adviser Invested loses the outsideness that made them worth asking.

## Echo

A second judgement that is no longer second.
It shows the cost of consulting the same person twice about the same thing.
The Adviser Invested is dated from the giving and hardens with each update.
