# Process: The Keeping

## Taxonomy

[Advice](process_advice.md), what the advice turns out to have been for, and where the engine is seen for one thing.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: advice

## Initiated by

The decision is made and the adviser is part of it.
The Keeping is what survives the episode: [a second name](process_the_second_name.md) on the decision, [a citation](process_the_citation.md) available if it goes wrong, and [a next adviser](process_the_next_advisor.md) chosen by how the last one felt.

## Direction

Toward the durable product of a consultation, which is not the content.
The advice was discounted, most of it in the first few minutes. What was not discounted at all is that a second person now knows about the decision, was asked, and said something -- which changes what the decision is, who owns the outcome, and what can be said about it afterwards.
The engine reads that this is produced in full every time, at the same strength whether the advice was good, bad, taken, or ignored.

## Lever

The genuine relief of not deciding alone against what the relief is made of.
The carrying force is that the sharing is real and mostly healthy: a persona carrying a hard decision by themselves is worse at it and suffers more, consulting somebody is how people bear things, and a decision that two people looked at is usually a better decision even after the discounting; the resistance is that the sharing is doing the work the content was supposed to do, so the episode succeeds by the persona's own measure while the advice fails by its -- and nobody has any way of noticing, because the two are reported in the same sentence.
The Keeping rides the engine's keystone knife-edge: a persona takes almost none of the advice and keeps all of the adviser.

## Echo

A decision with two names on it and one person's reasoning in it.
It shows the three earlier movements complete -- an approach that set the terms, a weighing that moved almost nothing, a return that told the adviser otherwise -- and adds the thing the episode was actually producing.
This is why the Keeping is the hardest of the four to stage and the only one worth closing on: the adviser must be competent, the asker must be sincere, the advice must be sound, and the audience must be able to watch a consultation work perfectly while none of the advice is used.
A play that stages the asker as manipulative has replaced the mechanism with a con, and one that stages the adviser as a fool has replaced it with a fable.
The Keeping is dated from the decision and is what gets remembered.
