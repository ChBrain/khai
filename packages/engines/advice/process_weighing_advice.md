# Process: The Weighing

## Taxonomy

[Advice](process_advice.md), what happens to its content.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: advice

## Initiated by

The advice has been given and now has to be worth something or not.
The Weighing is what the persona does with it: [their own view kept](process_the_own_view_kept.md) at most of its original strength, [a confident voice](process_the_confident_voice.md) followed for its manner, and [an agreeing word](process_the_agreeing_word.md) adopted whole.

## Direction

Toward a revision much smaller than the persona believes they made.
Given somebody else's estimate, people move about three parts in ten toward it and keep seven of their own -- a ratio that holds across tasks, holds when the adviser is better informed, and holds when the persona has been shown that it holds.
And the revision they report making is much larger than the revision they made, because taking some advice is experienced as taking the advice.

## Lever

The correctness of trusting your own information against the size of the discount.
The carrying force is that some discounting is right and a persona who split the difference with everybody would be worse off: they know their own reasons and cannot see the adviser's, their situation has particulars no outsider holds, and advice from somebody who has not thought about it for long should not weigh the same as a decision they have lived with; the resistance is that the discount does not track any of that -- it is applied at roughly the same rate to the informed and the ignorant adviser, so the persona is not weighing the source, they are keeping their own view and admitting a fixed fraction of somebody else's.
The Weighing moves less than it feels like and by less than it should.

## Echo

A judgement adjusted at the edges and reported as reconsidered.
It is the movement the whole literature measures, and the one participants are least able to observe in themselves.
The Weighing is dated to the minutes after the advice and is complete almost immediately.
