# Process: The Approach

## Taxonomy

[Advice](process_advice.md), how the advice gets into the room.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: advice

## Initiated by

Somebody is about to be told what they should do.
The Approach starts at one of three openings: [an invitation](process_the_invitation.md) that makes the advice sayable, [a prepared answer](process_the_prepared_answer.md) the asker already holds, or [the unbidden](process_the_unbidden.md), which arrives without either.

## Direction

Toward an exchange whose meaning is set before any content is in it.
Advice is a claim to know better, so how it enters decides how it lands. Invited, it is a service and the asker has protected themselves by asking. Uninvited, the same sentence is a statement that the speaker considers the other's handling of their own life to be improvable.
The words can be identical. The two events are not related.

## Lever

The usefulness of what somebody has to say against the standing they claim by saying it.
The carrying force is that advice is generous and is usually offered from concern: the person speaking has seen the mistake before, would be negligent to watch it happen in silence, and is spending their own credit to say something awkward; the resistance is that the offer names a competence gap in the direction of the speaker, so a good point badly placed is heard as the point that the listener could not have got there alone -- and once it has been heard that way the content is unreachable.
The Approach settles what the advice will mean before anybody has evaluated it.

## Echo

A judgement in the room, already read as a position.
It is the movement most people think is only a preliminary, and it decides more than the weighing does.
The Approach is dated from the opening sentence.
