# Process: The Second Name

## Taxonomy

[The Keeping](process_keeping_advice.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: advice

## Initiated by

The decision now has two people attached to it.
One who made it, and one who was asked.

## Direction

Toward an outcome that is no longer solely owned.
This is one of the reasons people seek advice at all, and it operates whether or not anybody has it in mind: a decision taken after consultation is a different object from the same decision taken alone. It is defensible, it is witnessed, and its consequences fall on a slightly wider surface.
The adviser has consented to none of this and is usually unaware of having taken any of it on.

## Lever

The legitimacy of not deciding alone against what the second name quietly transfers.
The carrying force is that spreading the weight is reasonable and often necessary: nobody is required to carry a serious decision unaided, consultation is what any careful person does, and the outcome really is partly the adviser's if their view was used; the resistance is that the transfer runs at full strength even when the view was not used at all -- the adviser is attached by having been asked rather than by having been heeded, so the persona gets the relief without the input having done anything.
The Second Name is acquired by the asking rather than by the advice.

## Echo

A shared outcome from an unshared decision.
It shows what the episode was producing all along.
The Second Name is dated to the consultation and is claimed later.
