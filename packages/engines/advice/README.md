# Advice

Advice as process: a second judgement placed next to a first, read on what was kept rather than on what was said -- not the request for discretionary help, which is asking's, and not the attempt to move a mind that was not offered for moving, which is persuasion's -- across four movements: the approach (an invitation that buys candour with standing, a prepared answer that sets the advice to arrive after the decision, and the unbidden, which pays for its accuracy with its reception), the weighing (an own view kept at seven parts in ten and discounted for being outside the persona's head, a confident voice followed for a signal it cannot support, and an agreeing word taken whole because it costs nothing to take), the return (a report back that is the only feedback in the system and is not feedback, thanks that are true about the person and false about the advice, and an adviser who loses the outsideness that made them worth asking), and the keeping (a second name acquired by the asking rather than by the advice, a citation issued only after bad news, and a next adviser chosen on the conversation rather than on the outcome) -- with the twist that a persona takes almost none of the advice and keeps all of the adviser.

This engine is defined by its [manifest](package.json), which the canon renders as the WIRES card. The manifest is the single source of truth; this README is generated, not edited by hand.
