# Process: The Agreeing Word

## Taxonomy

[The Weighing](process_weighing_advice.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: advice

## Initiated by

The adviser says what the persona was already thinking.
And it lands like a finding.

## Direction

Toward advice adopted in full precisely when it changes nothing.
Advice that matches the persona's existing view is taken almost whole; advice that departs from it is discounted steeply, and the further it departs the more steeply. So the uptake curve runs backwards to the usefulness curve: the advice with the most information in it, because it is the most different, is the advice that gets least of it through.
Afterwards the agreeing adviser is remembered as having been decisive, and the disagreeing one as having missed something.

## Lever

The sense of a second opinion confirming against what confirmation is evidence of.
The carrying force is that convergence is real evidence and should count: two people reasoning independently to the same answer is worth more than one, and dismissing agreement as flattery would throw away the main thing a second opinion can supply; the resistance is that the persona cannot tell independent convergence from an adviser reading the room, and the adviser has usually heard which way the persona is leaning before saying anything -- so the confirmation is scored at full value and was partly supplied by the person receiving it.
The Agreeing Word is taken whole because it costs nothing to take.

## Echo

Full uptake of the one message that carried nothing.
It shows why more consultation does not reliably produce more revision.
The Agreeing Word is dated to the agreement and is cited for years.
