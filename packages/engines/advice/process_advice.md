# Process: Advice

## Taxonomy

Being told what to do (no khai file of its own yet).

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: advice

## Initiated by

One persona tells another what they should do.
Advice starts wherever a judgement is offered on somebody else's decision: the friend consulted about the job, the parent with a view about the house, the colleague asked to look at a draft, the specialist paid for an opinion, the acquaintance who volunteers one.
It is not the request for discretionary help, which is asking's, and not the attempt to change a mind that was not offered for changing, which is persuasion's. It is a second judgement placed next to a first, and what the first one does with it.

## Direction

Toward a decision that is barely moved by the advice and is permanently attached to the adviser.
The engine reads four movements: [the approach](process_approach_advice.md), how the advice gets into the room; [the weighing](process_weighing_advice.md), what happens to its content; [the return](process_return_advice.md), what goes back to the adviser; and [the keeping](process_keeping_advice.md), the twist -- what the advice turns out to have been for.
The four run once per episode and select who gets asked the next time.

## Lever

The obvious sense of consulting somebody else against how little of what they say is used.
The carrying force is that seeking a second judgement is correct and usually helps: another person has information the persona lacks, is not inside the persona's own preference, and can see a bad plan from outside it -- and on measurable tasks the plain average of two judgements beats almost everybody's unaided one; the resistance is that a persona given an outside estimate keeps around seven parts of their own for every three of the adviser's, holds that ratio even when the adviser is demonstrably better informed, and describes the outcome as having taken the advice.
Advice is read on what was kept, never on what was said.

## Echo

Somebody who decided what they were going to decide, with a name attached.
Advice does not close; it is a second judgement placed next to a first, gathered into one reading -- an approach, a weighing, a return, a keeping.
The engine's twist is here. **A persona takes almost none of the advice and keeps all of the adviser.** The content is discounted, sometimes correctly and usually more than it should be, and none of that touches the other thing the episode produced, which is a second person now attached to the decision. That attachment is the durable part: it survives the discounting, it is what makes the consultation feel worth having, and it is produced in full whether or not a single word was used.
It hands off what it is not: the request for discretionary help is asking's; the attempt to move a mind that was not offered is persuasion's; whether the adviser actually knows anything is expertise's; the disagreement between a rule and a practitioner is overriding's; the credit given to an account is credibility's; the sense of having been properly heard is heeding's and recognition's; and counsel staged to **stand for** a theme is authored key, the play's pitch.
The persona is dated from the last person they asked.
