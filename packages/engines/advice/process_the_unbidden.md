# Process: The Unbidden

## Taxonomy

[The Approach](process_approach_advice.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: advice

## Initiated by

Nobody asked.
The advice arrives about a decision the speaker has heard about, from somebody with a view.

## Direction

Toward a judgement whose content is irrelevant to how it lands.
Uninvited advice is received as a claim about the relationship: that the speaker holds a position from which the listener's handling of their own affairs can be assessed. Accuracy does not soften this, and being right about it usually makes it worse.
Which is why the standard reply is not a counter-argument but a closing sentence -- it is fine, it is under control, thank you -- and why the adviser so often concludes that the listener is being defensive about a good point.

## Lever

The value of the thing being said against the position taken by saying it.
The carrying force is that a great deal of unbidden advice is correct and some of it is important: the person who says something before a disaster has done a real service, waiting to be asked would mean never speaking, and there are relationships in which this is exactly what is owed; the resistance is that the listener has to answer the claim about standing before they can reach the content, and answering it usually means declining the content -- so being told costs the point and the speaker keeps the point and loses the hearing.
The Unbidden pays for its accuracy with its reception.

## Echo

A good point that cannot be taken from where it came from.
It shows the standing question running underneath every episode, visible here because nothing else is.
The Unbidden is dated to the moment it is offered and is not forgotten by either party.
