# Process: The Own View Kept

## Taxonomy

[The Weighing](process_weighing_advice.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: advice

## Initiated by

The persona holds their position and shaves it.
A little toward the adviser, and not much.

## Direction

Toward a ratio that is remarkably stable and almost never chosen.
Set somebody an estimate, give them another person's, and the average revision is small: most of the original survives. Simple equal-weight averaging of the two would beat what nearly everybody does, and this is true of the confident, the expert, and the people who have just been told about the effect.
The persona has one thing the adviser does not, which is their own reasoning, and they can see all of it and none of the adviser's. So the asymmetry is not arrogance. It is a difference in what is visible.

## Lever

The privileged access a persona has to their own reasons against what that access is worth here.
The carrying force is that the asymmetry is real and the persona is not being vain: they can inspect their own grounds and cannot inspect anybody else's, an opaque estimate genuinely is harder to trust than a transparent one, and in the cases where they hold information the adviser lacks the discount is the right response; the resistance is that visibility and validity are not the same thing, so the persona is systematically weighting a judgement by how much of its working they can see -- which is a property of the channel and not of the judgement.
The Own View Kept discounts the adviser for being outside the persona's head.

## Echo

Seven parts of one view and three of another, called a consultation.
It shows the engine's firmest measured fact, and the one everybody assumes does not apply to them.
The Own View Kept is dated to the weighing and is invisible from inside.
