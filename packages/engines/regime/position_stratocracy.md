# Position: Stratocracy

## Taxonomy

[By the Substitute](position_substitute_regime.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: regime

## Has

Rule that is the army. Distinct from a junta seizing a civil state, the military here is the state: the chain of command is the administration, citizenship and service are the same qualification, and there is no separate civil apparatus to hand power back to.

## Orders

Keep the whole society legible as a formation. The regime's competence is command, so it succeeds where the task can be given as an order and organised in ranks -- mobilisation, emergency, defence -- and it converts other tasks into that shape rather than acquiring a second method.

## Loses

Any settled way of disagreeing. A hierarchy of command has one legitimate direction for a decision to travel, so a policy dispute has no procedural home; it becomes indiscipline, and factional politics moves into the officer corps, where it is settled by seniority or by coup.

## Drives

On the loss of disagreement:
keeps a council of senior officers as a standing forum, which restores an arena at the price of admitting the chain of command does not decide everything,
or forbids the dispute, and finds that the argument continues where it cannot be seen.
