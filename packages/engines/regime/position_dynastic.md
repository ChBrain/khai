# Position: Dynastic

## Taxonomy

[By the Holder](position_holder_regime.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: regime

## Has

An autocracy held by a family, with succession settled inside it. The claim is traditional, the pool of eligible rulers is small and known, and the family itself is the institution.

## Orders

Manage the relatives. The threats are internal and specific -- brothers, uncles, sons -- so the regime spends its attention on the household, and its succession rules are its constitution.

## Loses

Any way to recruit talent that does not dilute the family's hold, and any way to remove a bad heir without unsettling the rule that produced them.

## Drives

On the loss of recruitment:
brings in outsiders on terms that keep them dependent, and governs competently at the cost of a permanent tension,
or keeps it in the family, and is governed by whoever the family happened to produce.
