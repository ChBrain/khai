# Position: Tyranny

## Taxonomy

[By the Count](position_count_regime.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: regime

## Has

Rule by one, for the ruler. The deviant twin of monarchy, identical in structure and opposite in purpose, and describing itself throughout in monarchy's language.

## Orders

Hold the position. Where the polity's good is not the point, the ruler's whole problem becomes staying in place, and every institution is bent toward that -- which is why tyrannies are so much more concerned with loyalty than with competence.

## Loses

Information. A ruler who punishes bad news receives none, so a tyranny is the arrangement least able to learn what is true about itself, and the loss compounds because the flatterers are promoted.

## Drives

On the loss of information:
rules on a picture that everybody around knows is false and nobody will correct,
or is told the truth once, by somebody with nothing to lose, and does not believe it.
