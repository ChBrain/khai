# Position: By the Claim

## Taxonomy

[Regime](position_regime.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: regime

## Has

Weber's question: not who rules but why these rulers are obeyed rather than merely feared. Three grounds -- inherited custom, personal grace, and impersonal rule -- and any real polity mixes them.

## Orders

Find where the obedience is anchored, since that is what will fail first. A regime resting on one ground and losing it must find another quickly or become naked coercion, which is expensive and short.

## Loses

The comfort of a single answer. Legitimacy is plural and unevenly distributed: the same regime is traditional to one part of the polity, legal-rational to another, and a personal following to a third, and the parts do not fail together.

## Drives

On the loss of a single ground:
rests on several and survives the loss of any one,
or rests on one and is one funeral, one election, or one broken procedure from having none.
