# Position: Aristocracy

## Taxonomy

[By the Count](position_count_regime.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: regime

## Has

Rule by the few, for the common good: government by those held to be best qualified for it, with the qualification -- birth, learning, virtue, service -- specified by the regime itself.

## Orders

Select, and keep the selection honest. Everything depends on the standard by which the few are chosen, so the regime's real work is the maintenance of that standard against the pressure to admit the well-connected.

## Loses

The ability to justify the standard to those it excludes. Any criterion for the best is contestable, and the excluded are not obliged to accept a definition of merit written by the included.

## Drives

On the loss of justification:
keeps the standard demanding and narrow, and is respected and resented in the same measure,
or lets the standard drift toward whoever is already inside, and becomes the deviant form without anybody deciding to.
