# Position: Polity

## Taxonomy

[By the Count](position_count_regime.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: regime

## Has

Rule by the many, for the common good. Aristotle's true form of popular rule and the one he thought most durable: a broad middle governing in its own interest, which is broad enough to be nearly everyone's.

## Orders

Keep the middle large. The arrangement works because the ruling many are not desperate and not idle, so the regime's real business is whatever sustains that condition rather than any particular policy.

## Loses

Stability against inequality. The form depends on a distribution it cannot itself guarantee, so a polity that lets its middle hollow out has lost the thing that made it the true form and will slide toward one of the deviants.

## Drives

On the loss of the middle:
defends the distribution that holds the regime up, which looks like self-interest and is structural,
or lets it go, and finds the same institutions now serving the few or the mob.
