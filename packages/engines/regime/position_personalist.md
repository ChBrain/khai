# Position: Personalist

## Taxonomy

[By the Holder](position_holder_regime.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: regime

## Has

An autocracy that is a person. Access, appointment, and policy run through one individual; the party and the army exist but neither can act without them, and there is no body with a will of its own.

## Orders

Prevent anybody else becoming necessary. The ruler must rotate subordinates, keep rivals weak, and avoid institutionalising anything that could function in their absence -- which is also the definition of the regime's principal weakness.

## Loses

A successor, and an exit. Having destroyed every body that could have chosen a heir, the ruler cannot hand over safely and cannot retire safely, so they usually stay until they die or are removed by force.

## Drives

On the loss of an exit:
rules to the end, since leaving is more dangerous than staying,
or is removed abruptly, and takes the regime with them because there was never anything else holding it up.
