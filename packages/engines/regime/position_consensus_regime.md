# Position: Consensus

## Taxonomy

[By the Architecture](position_architecture_regime.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: regime

## Has

Rules that disperse. Proportional representation, coalition cabinets, a strong second chamber, entrenched rights, sometimes an explicit veto for a named community -- Lijphart's other pattern, built for polities too plural for a bare majority to govern.

## Orders

Include, and keep including. The arrangement is designed so that no substantial group can be governed against, which means every decision is a negotiation among most of the polity.

## Loses

The ability to decide against anybody. What protects each group also arms it, so a determined minority can stop what a large majority wants, and immobility is the characteristic failure.

## Drives

On the loss of decisiveness:
negotiates, slowly, and holds together a polity a majoritarian arrangement would have split,
or deadlocks, and the groups it was built to protect start looking for a way around the arrangement itself.
