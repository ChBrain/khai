# Position: Semi-Presidential

## Taxonomy

[By the Architecture](position_architecture_regime.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: regime

## Has

Both at once: a directly elected president with real powers, and a prime minister answerable to the assembly. Two executives, whose relative weight depends on whether they come from the same majority.

## Orders

Read the arithmetic first. The arrangement is a different regime depending on whether the president's party holds the assembly, and it changes character without any rule changing.

## Loses

A settled answer to who is in charge. Where the two do not share a majority, the constitution's division of powers must be improvised under pressure by the two people it divides them between.

## Drives

On the loss of a settled answer:
cohabits, with the powers divided by negotiation and precedent,
or contests, and the ambiguity that was tolerable becomes the whole of the politics.
