# Position: Ochlocracy

## Taxonomy

[By the Count](position_count_regime.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: regime

## Has

Rule by the many, for the many at the expense of the rest: Aristotle's deviant form of popular rule and Polybius's name for it, the assembly acting as a crowd rather than as a body.

## Orders

Act now, on the case in front of it. The deviant form's mark is not the number ruling but the absence of the pause that a rule imposes -- decisions taken in the heat of a particular grievance, against a particular target.

## Loses

Any protection for whoever is outnumbered today. A majority that recognises no constraint on itself has made every minority's position a matter of luck, including the majority's own, since majorities change and the precedent does not.

## Drives

On the loss of protection:
binds itself with rules it cannot suspend for a good cause, which is what turns the deviant form into the true one,
or keeps the power to make exceptions and discovers who it belongs to when the composition shifts.
