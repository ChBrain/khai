# Position: By the Constraint

## Taxonomy

[Regime](position_regime.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: regime

## Has

The modern comparative question, and the one that decides which political levers exist: can the rulers be removed by anybody other than themselves? The axis runs from liberal democracy, where they can be removed and are also bound while in office, through electoral democracy and the competitive and hegemonic authoritarianisms, to the closed autocracy where the question does not arise -- with totalitarianism sitting off the line as a distinct kind rather than an extreme degree.

## Orders

Place a polity by two separate tests, not one. Contestation asks whether losing an election is a live possibility for the incumbent; participation asks who is allowed to take part. A polity can be high on one and low on the other, and Dahl's point is that they are different achievements with different histories.

## Loses

Sharp edges. The middle of this axis is genuinely contested in the literature, since a competitive authoritarian regime holds real elections it does not intend to lose, and telling one from a flawed electoral democracy requires judgements about a whole system that reasonable observers make differently.

## Drives

On the loss of sharp edges:
treats the middle as a range and says which way a polity is moving, which is usually the answerable question,
or demands a clean label and gets one by ignoring whichever half of the evidence is inconvenient.
