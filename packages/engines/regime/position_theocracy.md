# Position: Theocracy

## Taxonomy

[By the Substitute](position_substitute_regime.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: regime

## Has

Rule whose warrant is divine. The law is held to be revealed rather than made, and the body that interprets the revelation outranks any body that legislates -- so the clerical office is not an influence on the state but the state's court of last resort.

## Orders

Keep the interpretation single. A revealed law is only sovereign while there is one authorised reading of it, so the regime's central work is doctrinal control -- appointing the interpreters, licensing the schools, and treating a rival exegesis as sedition rather than as scholarship.

## Loses

The ability to be wrong cheaply. A policy justified as ordinary judgement can be reversed as ordinary judgement; a policy justified as revealed cannot be reversed without conceding that the revelation was misread, so error accumulates rather than being corrected, and each correction costs a little of the warrant.

## Drives

On the loss of cheap error:
splits the register, ruling on most things as administration and reserving the divine warrant for a few, which preserves reversibility at the cost of admitting the state is mostly secular,
or holds the warrant over everything, and pays for each reversal with a piece of the claim that made it sovereign.
