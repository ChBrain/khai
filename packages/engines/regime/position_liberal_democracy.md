# Position: Liberal Democracy

## Taxonomy

[By the Constraint](position_constraint_regime.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: regime

## Has

Rulers who can be removed by an election they might lose, and who are bound while in office by courts, rights, and a press they do not control. Both halves are required: contested removal, and constraint between removals.

## Orders

Lose gracefully and stay bound. The arrangement is defined by the incumbent's acceptance of a result they did not want and by their submission to constraints while they still hold power.

## Loses

Speed, and the ability to bind a successor. Everything that makes the regime correctable makes it slow, and every commitment it makes can be undone by the next holder of the same office -- which is the price of the same property.

## Drives

On the loss of speed:
accepts it, and handles emergencies badly but recovers from mistakes,
or suspends the constraints for the emergency, and finds that the suspension is the easiest thing in the arrangement to make permanent.
