# Position: By the Substitute

## Taxonomy

[Regime](position_regime.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: regime

## Has

What rules where none of the named forms does. Some of these are regimes with an unusual basis for the claim to rule -- a scripture, an expertise, a fortune, an army -- and some are the absence or suspension of a regime altogether.

## Orders

Name what is actually deciding. A polity described by what it lacks is described badly; there is almost always something settling matters, and the useful question is what.

## Loses

Precision. Most of these words are used as insults more often than as descriptions -- kleptocracy and ochlocracy are almost always somebody's verdict on a regime they oppose -- so the type is easy to apply and hard to apply honestly.

## Drives

On the loss of precision:
reserves the word for the case where the named basis really is what decides, and accepts that it will disappoint people who wanted an epithet,
or spends it as an insult, at which point it stops carrying information.
