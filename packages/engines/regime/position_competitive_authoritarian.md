# Position: Competitive Authoritarian

## Taxonomy

[By the Constraint](position_constraint_regime.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: regime

## Has

Real elections that the incumbent does not intend to lose and has arranged not to. The opposition is legal, funded, and on the ballot; the state's resources, the courts, and the media are the incumbent's, and the playing field is tilted rather than the count falsified.

## Orders

Keep the contest real enough to be worth having. The regime needs the election for its legitimacy at home and abroad, so it must not cancel it and must not obviously rig it -- the whole art is in winning a genuine contest that was never fair.

## Loses

The ability to be sure. Because the elections are real, the incumbent can lose one by miscalculation, and every tilt applied to prevent that makes the eventual loss more dangerous for them personally.

## Drives

On the loss of certainty:
keeps tilting and wins, at rising cost and with a narrowing circle it can trust,
or miscounts the tilt and loses, which is the most common way this regime type ends and the most dangerous moment in it.
