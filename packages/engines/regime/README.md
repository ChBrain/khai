# Regime

Regime as position: the standing arrangement under which a persona's polity decides, which they did not choose and cannot individually change -- a catalogue of political systems routed through six axes: the count (who rules, in Aristotle's true and deviant pairs), the claim (why them, in Weber's three grounds of legitimate domination), the constraint (whether the rulers can be removed, across the liberal-to-closed spectrum), the holder (who actually holds an autocracy), the architecture (how the offices are arranged), and the substitute (what rules where none of the named forms does) -- with the twist that a regime's power over what may be said is inversely related to its knowledge of what is true (Aristotle; Weber; Linz; Dahl; Levitsky & Way; Geddes; Lijphart; Kuran).

This engine is defined by its [manifest](package.json), which the canon renders as the WIRES card. The manifest is the single source of truth; this README is generated, not edited by hand.
