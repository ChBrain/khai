# Position: Oligarchy

## Taxonomy

[By the Count](position_count_regime.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: regime

## Has

Rule by the few, for the few. Aristotle's deviant form, and in his account the decisive fact is usually wealth: the ruling number is small and the interest served is its own.

## Orders

Keep the circle closed and keep it plausible. An oligarchy must exclude reliably while presenting the exclusion as something other than what it is, which is why it invests so heavily in the forms of wider consultation.

## Loses

Renewal. A closed circle cannot recruit the talent outside it without admitting the outside, so it ages, narrows, and grows less competent at exactly the rate it grows more secure.

## Drives

On the loss of renewal:
admits enough newcomers to stay capable and dilutes what it was defending,
or stays closed and is eventually overtaken by whatever it refused to let in.
