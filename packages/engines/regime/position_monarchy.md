# Position: Monarchy

## Taxonomy

[By the Count](position_count_regime.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: regime

## Has

Rule by one, for the common good. Aristotle's true form of single rule: a sole ruler whose interest is taken to be the polity's, and who is restrained by that identification rather than by anybody else.

## Orders

Decide, and decide quickly. Single rule is the fastest arrangement there is, needs no assembly and no coalition, and can act on a long view no elected body could sustain.

## Loses

Any mechanism for the case where the ruler is wrong. There is no route by which the polity corrects a monarch that is not also the route by which it removes one, so error and treason are the same procedure and both are catastrophic.

## Drives

On the loss of correction:
governs well and is remembered as a golden reign that could not be repeated,
or governs badly and the polity discovers it has only one instrument, which is the end of the regime rather than of the mistake.
