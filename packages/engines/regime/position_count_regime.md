# Position: By the Count

## Taxonomy

[Regime](position_regime.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: regime

## Has

Aristotle's cut, and still the sharpest: rule by one, by few, or by many, each in a true form that governs for the common good and a deviant form that governs for the rulers' own. Six types from two questions, and the second question is the one that matters.

## Orders

Ask how many hold power, then ask whom they hold it for. The count alone settles nothing -- a monarchy and a tyranny have the same number of rulers -- so a polity is placed only when both answers are in.

## Loses

Any way to tell the pairs apart from outside. The difference between a monarchy and a tyranny is whose good is served, which is a judgement about purposes rather than an observable feature of the arrangement, and each deviant form describes itself in the language of its true one.

## Drives

On the loss of the outside test:
reads the count as a description and the purpose as a claim, holding the two apart,
or collapses them, and takes every ruler's account of who they rule for at face value.
