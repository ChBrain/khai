# Position: By the Holder

## Taxonomy

[Regime](position_regime.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: regime

## Has

Geddes's question, asked only of autocracies: who actually holds this one. A person, an army, a party, or a family -- and the answer is not decoration, because it predicts how the regime behaves, how long it lasts, and above all how it ends.

## Orders

Look past the office to the thing that would survive its holder. A single-party regime has a machine that can replace a leader; a personalist regime is the leader, and has nothing underneath.

## Loses

The tidiness of the categories. Real autocracies are hybrids and they drift, most often toward personalism as a leader outlives the coalition that installed them, so the type is a reading of a moment rather than a permanent property.

## Drives

On the loss of tidiness:
reads the drift and asks which way, since a party regime becoming personal is the common direction and a dangerous one,
or fixes the label at the founding and is surprised by an ending the type no longer predicted.
