# Position: Majoritarian

## Taxonomy

[By the Architecture](position_architecture_regime.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: regime

## Has

Rules that concentrate. Single-member districts, two dominant parties, a single chamber that matters, a cabinet drawn from one party -- Lijphart's Westminster pattern, in which a bare plurality converts into a working majority.

## Orders

Deliver a government that can act, and let the voters remove it whole. The arrangement trades representativeness for clarity: somebody is in charge and can be blamed.

## Loses

Any voice for a substantial minority between elections, and any proportion between votes and seats.

## Drives

On the loss of the minority's voice:
governs decisively and alternates, so today's minority expects its own turn,
or the alternation stops for a permanently outnumbered group, and the arrangement offers them nothing at all.
