# Position: Totalitarian

## Taxonomy

[By the Constraint](position_constraint_regime.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: regime

## Has

A distinct kind rather than an extreme degree, and Linz's contribution is the distinction: an official ideology reaching into every sphere, a single mass party demanding active participation rather than mere quiet, and no autonomous social space left standing.

## Orders

Mobilise, permanently. Ordinary autocracy asks for compliance and leaves people alone; this asks for enthusiasm, which cannot be delegated to police and must be demanded again every day.

## Loses

The capacity to stop. A regime whose legitimacy is continuous transformation cannot announce that the transformation is complete, so it must keep finding enemies and campaigns, and the machinery outlasts any use for it.

## Drives

On the loss of the ability to stop:
keeps mobilising past the point of exhaustion, consuming its own cadres,
or slows into ordinary authoritarianism, which is the usual fate and looks from inside like a betrayal of the founding.
