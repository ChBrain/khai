# Position: Parliamentary

## Taxonomy

[By the Architecture](position_architecture_regime.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: regime

## Has

An executive drawn from the assembly and removable by it. The government holds office only while it commands a majority, and losing that majority ends it without waiting for a term to expire.

## Orders

Keep the confidence of the chamber. Every decision is taken with an eye to whether it survives the next vote, which fuses the executive and legislative questions into one.

## Loses

A fixed term, and any executive independent of the legislature. There is no separate mandate to appeal to and no arrangement in which the two branches check each other, because they are not two.

## Drives

On the loss of separation:
governs flexibly and falls quickly when it loses support, which is the arrangement working,
or is held hostage by a small pivotal party whose votes it cannot do without.
