# Position: Single-Party

## Taxonomy

[By the Holder](position_holder_regime.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: regime

## Has

An autocracy held by a party with a machine: a membership, a cadre system, an internal procedure for advancement, and a doctrine that outranks any individual leader.

## Orders

Feed the machine. The party must recruit, promote, discipline, and reward, and it must do so predictably enough that able people see a career in it, which is what gives the regime its depth.

## Loses

The ability to change direction quickly, and any way to remove the party itself. Doctrine and cadre both take years to turn, and the arrangement offers no procedure by which the party could lose.

## Drives

On the loss of speed:
survives its leaders, replaces them internally, and is the most durable autocracy there is,
or calcifies, and finds the machine that made it stable is the thing preventing it from adapting.
