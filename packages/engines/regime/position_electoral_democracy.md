# Position: Electoral Democracy

## Taxonomy

[By the Constraint](position_constraint_regime.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: regime

## Has

Elections that are genuinely contested and genuinely might be lost, without the full apparatus of constraint between them: weak courts, a captured or cowed press, rights that hold in principle and bend in practice.

## Orders

Win the vote and take the powers. The regime is real -- incumbents do lose here -- but nothing much restrains a winner until the next election, so each victory delivers more than a liberal democracy's does.

## Loses

Protection for whoever lost. With no constraint between elections, the only remedy for a defeated party is the next election, and the winner has the whole term to make that one harder.

## Drives

On the loss of interim protection:
holds the line by custom and forbearance, which is unwritten and depends on the winners' restraint,
or uses the term to tilt the field, which is the ordinary route into competitive authoritarianism and rarely looks like a coup.
