# Position: Unitary

## Taxonomy

[By the Architecture](position_architecture_regime.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: regime

## Has

One centre. Whatever regional or local bodies exist hold powers delegated from the centre, which can alter or withdraw them by ordinary legislation.

## Orders

Decide once, apply everywhere. The arrangement's virtue is uniformity: a decision taken at the centre is the decision, and there is nobody with standing to refuse it.

## Loses

Any local check, and any way to vary. Regional grievance has no institutional home, and a policy that suits the centre must be applied where it does not fit.

## Drives

On the loss of the local check:
devolves by statute, which relieves the pressure and can be reversed by the body that granted it,
or holds the line, and the grievance goes somewhere other than into the constitution.
