# Position: Technocracy

## Taxonomy

[By the Substitute](position_substitute_regime.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: regime

## Has

Rule whose warrant is expertise. Office is held on the strength of trained competence -- the economist at the ministry, the engineer at the utility, the physician at the health authority -- and the decision is presented as the answer the evidence gives rather than as a choice somebody made.

## Orders

Keep the question technical. The regime's authority holds only where the matter admits a correct answer, so it works to define disputes as problems of implementation -- which is also how it takes questions of who bears the cost out of contention.

## Loses

The standing to lose an argument. Having claimed that the answer is not a preference, it has no way to concede that somebody preferred otherwise; disagreement can only be read as ignorance or as bad faith, so the regime cannot absorb opposition, only diagnose it.

## Drives

On the loss of standing to lose:
keeps a political chamber above the expert one and lets it overrule, which restores the losing move at the cost of the claim that the answer was compelled,
or treats every objection as a failure of understanding, and discovers that a public which cannot argue with the regime stops believing it.
