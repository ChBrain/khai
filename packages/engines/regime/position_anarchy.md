# Position: Anarchy

## Taxonomy

[By the Substitute](position_substitute_regime.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: regime

## Has

No regime at all. There is no authority whose decision binds the territory -- either because none has been built, or because one has collapsed -- so what obtains is whatever the local, armed, kin, or contractual arrangements can enforce among themselves.

## Orders

Watch what fills it. The condition is rarely stable and almost never empty: militias, clans, gangs, churches, and firms supply protection and adjudication at a price, and the useful reading is which of them is charging what, not whether order exists.

## Loses

Portability. Nothing agreed in one valley is worth anything in the next, so undertakings cannot travel, credit cannot extend past the reach of the enforcer, and every transaction beyond the local pays for its own security.

## Drives

On the loss of portability:
federates the local orders into something wider, which is the beginning of a regime and is usually paid for in submission,
or stays local, and pays the cost in everything that requires strangers to trust each other.
