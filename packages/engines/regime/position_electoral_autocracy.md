# Position: Electoral Autocracy

## Taxonomy

[By the Constraint](position_constraint_regime.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: regime

## Has

Elections held and not contested in any meaningful sense: one party or leader wins by a margin arranged in advance, the opposition is licensed rather than real, and the exercise is a ritual of confirmation rather than a choice.

## Orders

Turn out the vote anyway. The margin is the point, so the regime spends heavily on participation it does not need arithmetically, because a large number consenting is the product being manufactured.

## Loses

Any reading of what the polity actually thinks. Having built an instrument that returns the required answer, the regime has destroyed the one device that would have told it the truth, and must guess by other means.

## Drives

On the loss of the reading:
builds a parallel apparatus -- police reports, private polling, informers -- to learn what the election was supposed to tell it,
or believes its own margins, which is how such regimes are surprised.
