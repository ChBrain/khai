# Position: Federal

## Taxonomy

[By the Architecture](position_architecture_regime.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: regime

## Has

Two orders of government, each with powers the other cannot simply withdraw, and a text and a court to police the line between them.

## Orders

Respect the division, including where it is inconvenient. The arrangement's whole content is that the centre cannot do certain things, so a centre that does them anyway has ended the federation without amending it.

## Loses

Uniformity, and speed in a crisis. The same question is answered differently in different places by design, and any national response requires assembling agreement the constitution does not compel.

## Drives

On the loss of uniformity:
accepts the variation as the price of holding a large or plural polity together,
or centralises in an emergency, and discovers afterwards that emergencies recur.
