# Position: Military Junta

## Taxonomy

[By the Holder](position_holder_regime.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: regime

## Has

An autocracy held by the armed forces as an institution, usually by a committee of senior officers rather than one general, and usually justified as a temporary correction.

## Orders

Return to barracks, eventually. The military has an institutional life outside government and a professional interest in preserving it, so the junta's own officers are the constituency most likely to want the arrangement ended.

## Loses

Cohesion under governing. Ruling forces the armed forces to take positions on questions that divide them, and the split runs along the lines of the institution itself, which is the one thing it cannot afford to damage.

## Drives

On the loss of cohesion:
hands power back on terms it negotiates while it is still united, which is why juntas are the shortest-lived autocracies and the likeliest to leave peacefully,
or fractures, and the fight is between armed factions rather than political ones.
