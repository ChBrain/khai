# Position: Presidential

## Taxonomy

[By the Architecture](position_architecture_regime.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: regime

## Has

An executive elected separately and serving a fixed term, removable before it only by an extraordinary procedure. Two branches, two mandates, and no requirement that they agree.

## Orders

Hold the term. The executive's independence is the point: it can pursue a course the assembly dislikes and cannot be dismissed for it.

## Loses

Any routine way to break a deadlock. When the two mandates conflict there is no procedure to resolve it and no early election to appeal to, so the constitution offers only waiting or rupture.

## Drives

On the loss of the tiebreak:
waits out the deadlock and governs by whatever the executive can do alone, which is where emergency powers get their exercise,
or one branch moves against the other, which is the crisis the architecture makes structurally available.
