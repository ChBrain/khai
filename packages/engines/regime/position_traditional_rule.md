# Position: Traditional

## Taxonomy

[By the Claim](position_claim_regime.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: regime

## Has

Authority resting on the sanctity of arrangements that have always been. The ruler is obeyed because this is how it has been done, and the person occupies an inherited position rather than holding a delegated office.

## Orders

Do not innovate visibly. Every change must be presented as a restoration or a clarification, since the ground of obedience is continuity itself and an admitted novelty is an admitted choice.

## Loses

Any answer to a genuinely new question. Where custom is silent the regime has no procedure, and inventing one exposes that somebody is deciding, which is the thing the claim denies.

## Drives

On the loss of the new case:
finds a precedent, sincerely or otherwise, and absorbs the novelty as something that was always allowed,
or rules openly by decision and discovers that the ground it was standing on was the absence of decision.
