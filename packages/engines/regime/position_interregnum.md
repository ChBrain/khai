# Position: Interregnum

## Taxonomy

[By the Substitute](position_substitute_regime.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: regime

## Has

A regime between regimes. The old authority no longer binds and the new one does not yet, so offices are held provisionally, decisions are made in somebody's name, and every actor is calculating against an arrangement that does not exist yet.

## Orders

Read every act as positioning. Nothing done in an interregnum is only what it is; appointments, pardons, elections, and inquiries are all bids on the successor arrangement, and their stated purpose is usually the smaller half of the reason.

## Loses

The power to bind the future. A provisional authority cannot make a commitment that survives it, so it cannot borrow, treat, or promise credibly, and every party it deals with discounts the undertaking to whatever the next regime is likely to honour.

## Drives

On the loss of binding:
hurries -- calls the election, writes the constitution, hands over -- and accepts a worse settlement for a real one,
or extends itself, and becomes a regime of the ordinary kind while continuing to call itself temporary.
