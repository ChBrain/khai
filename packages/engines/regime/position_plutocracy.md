# Position: Plutocracy

## Taxonomy

[By the Substitute](position_substitute_regime.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: regime

## Has

Rule whose warrant is wealth. Formal office may be elective and formally open, but the qualifications that matter -- funding a campaign, owning the press, holding the capital an administration needs to keep moving -- are held by a propertied few, so the decisive selection happens before any vote.

## Orders

Keep the two ladders separate in public and joined in fact. The regime works best where the constitutional route to office is genuinely open and the practical route runs through money, since the first supplies the legitimacy and the second supplies the outcome.

## Loses

The capacity to tax itself. A regime whose selectorate is its own wealthiest stratum can redistribute only against the interest of the people who staff it, so the one instrument that would answer its central grievance is the instrument it is least able to use.

## Drives

On the loss of self-taxation:
buys the grievance off in kind -- transfers, employment, cheap credit -- which relieves the pressure without touching the distribution that produced it,
or lets it accumulate, and is eventually taxed by somebody who did not come up the second ladder.
