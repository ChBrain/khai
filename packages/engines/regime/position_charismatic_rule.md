# Position: Charismatic

## Taxonomy

[By the Claim](position_claim_regime.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: regime

## Has

Authority resting on a particular person's extraordinary quality, taken as a gift or a calling. The claim is not to an office and not to a custom but to this individual, and it is confirmed by their followers rather than conferred by any procedure.

## Orders

Keep proving it. Charisma is validated by results and by proximity, so the regime is a continuous demonstration and the leader cannot be absent, ordinary, or defeated for long.

## Loses

Succession. The claim is by construction untransferable, so a charismatic regime cannot hand over what it rests on and must convert to something else -- an office, a bloodline, a doctrine -- or end with the person.

## Drives

On the loss of succession:
routinises in time, becoming traditional or legal-rational and much less than it was,
or does not, and leaves an institution shaped entirely around a person who is no longer there.
