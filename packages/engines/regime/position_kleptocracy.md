# Position: Kleptocracy

## Taxonomy

[By the Substitute](position_substitute_regime.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: regime

## Has

Rule turned to extraction. The apparatus of state -- licensing, procurement, customs, the courts -- is operated as a private revenue stream by those holding it, and the public purpose of each office is the cover under which the office is worked.

## Orders

Keep the theft distributed. Extraction concentrated in one hand makes enemies of everybody else in the apparatus, so the durable form is a shared one: every rank is given a level to steal at, which converts each official into a stakeholder in the regime's survival.

## Loses

The ability to build anything. A road that is worth more unbuilt than built will not be built, so the regime is structurally incapable of the public works that would justify it -- and its own officials are the first to place their savings, and their children, somewhere else.

## Drives

On the loss of building:
imports the capacity, hiring foreigners to deliver what its own apparatus cannot, and pays a premium for the reputation it destroyed,
or stops pretending to build, and rules on force alone once nothing else recommends it.
