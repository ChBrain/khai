# Position: Legal-Rational

## Taxonomy

[By the Claim](position_claim_regime.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: regime

## Has

Authority resting on impersonal rules. Obedience is owed to the office and not to its holder, the holder is bound by the same rules as everybody, and the rules can be changed by a procedure the rules themselves specify.

## Orders

Follow the procedure, including where it produces a result the officeholder dislikes. The claim is the procedure, so an exception made for a good reason costs more than the reason is worth.

## Loses

Warmth, and speed. Impersonal rule gives nobody anything to love and answers no case faster than its forms allow, so it is chronically vulnerable to somebody offering to cut through it on behalf of the people it has ground down.

## Drives

On the loss of warmth:
is defended by those who understand what the forms are for, which is never a majority and is rarely inspiring,
or is set aside for somebody who promises to get things done, at which point the ground of obedience has quietly changed.
