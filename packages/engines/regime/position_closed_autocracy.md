# Position: Closed Autocracy

## Taxonomy

[By the Constraint](position_constraint_regime.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: regime

## Has

No national election for the executive worth the name, and no pretence of one. Power is held by a person, a family, a party, or an army, and transferred among them by rules internal to that group.

## Orders

Manage the inner circle. Where the polity has no say, the only people who can remove the ruler are those close enough to try, so the regime's real politics is entirely a matter of who is in the room.

## Loses

Any legitimate route in for anybody outside. The arrangement offers no way to join, petition, or be represented, so every excluded grievance accumulates with nowhere to go and no early warning of its size.

## Drives

On the loss of the route in:
buys off or represses the grievances it can see, having no reliable way to see them,
or is surprised by a mobilisation that everybody outside the room knew about and nobody inside it did.
