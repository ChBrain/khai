# Position: By the Architecture

## Taxonomy

[Regime](position_regime.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: regime

## Has

How the offices are arranged, cutting across every axis above. Where the executive comes from and who can remove it (parliamentary, presidential, semi-presidential), how power is distributed by territory (unitary, federal), and whether the rules concentrate a decision in a bare majority or force it through a broad one (majoritarian, consensual).

## Orders

Read the architecture as a set of veto points and a clock. Every arrangement here is a choice about how many hands a decision must pass through, and therefore about how fast the polity can act and how hard it is to reverse what it has done.

## Loses

Any claim to be about legitimacy or freedom. The architecture is orthogonal to the constraint axis: autocracies have federal structures and democracies have unitary ones, and a persona who reads the arrangement as evidence of the regime's character will read it wrong.

## Drives

On the loss of the moral reading:
assesses an arrangement by what it makes possible and what it makes slow, which is the only question it answers,
or treats a familiar architecture as a sign of freedom and is startled by the same one holding up something else.
