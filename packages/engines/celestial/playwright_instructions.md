# Instructions: Celestial

How a Playwright wires the celestial engine into a play. You wire by linking the play's own content to the engine's files; the engine files stay untouched, they are canon. The Roadie plumbs the engine's law into Instructions Knowledge on deploy. Authoring guidance, not runtime content, and it does not go on tour.

## Human

- The human sets which places carry a sky that acts -- the sun as governor of day and season, the eclipse that breaks the noon, the comet or meteor visible on the place's night, the aurora alight at high latitudes, or the starfield that the place's darkness allows or hides -- and whether that sky-showing is the place's standing condition or a live event in the play.

## Agent

- From the play's own content, a place whose sky is an actor links under Shown the mode of its celestial display: the sun that tracks the place's day and seasons, the eclipse that moves through the place, the comet or meteor on the place's night, the aurora that dances above the poles, or the starfield that the place's darkness holds or hides. Play the sky-body as the apparition itself shown -- the sun's shadow cast at midday, the moon's shadow crossing the day into noon-darkness, the comet hanging for weeks, the meteor streaking for seconds, the aurora's curtains rippling, the Milky Way bright or dim depending on the place's light. Show these as what the place puts before whoever enters, present in what is seen, never narrated, and never as a mirror of a persona's own burning or wandering or fate. Keep it the sky-body itself and the place's own showing of it, not the weather overhead (that is weather), the climate that enables it (that is climate), the landed stone (that is geomorphology), or the meaning someone reads into it as omen or symbol (that is authored key). You link to the engine, you never edit it.

## Collaboration

- The weather that fills the sky -- wind, cloud, storm, rain, lightning -- is weather's; the live sky-body shown above the place is celestial's. The climate regime that places the sun's intensity and the aurora's latitude is climate's; the actual sun's arc on the day is celestial's. The stone the meteor becomes after it lands is geomorphology's; the apparition as it lights the place's sky is celestial's. The feeling and meaning a persona reads into the eclipse or comet is the persona's own engines'; celestial delivers the showing, not the feeling. Whether the sky-body rings true to the place and its latitude, season, and darkness is the team's review.

## Knowledge

- Celestial is the sky's bodies as the place's own Shown: the sun as the place's governor of day and season (the daily arc from latitude and date, the seasonal swing from solstice to solstice, the light aligned-to monuments and fields); the eclipse where totality breaks the day (the sudden cold, the 360-degree sunset, animals falling silent, the alignment moving through the place in minutes); the comet as the visitor on a lifetime's clock (the apparition hanging for weeks, the return on a schedule generations long, the path as mathematics); the meteor as fire from above (the streak of seconds, the shower on calendar nights like Perseids and Leonids, the bolide that lights the place, the only sky-body that touches it with force like Chelyabinsk 2013's airburst); the aurora as the sky alight (the solar wind meeting the magnetosphere, the green and red dance of the light at high latitudes, the great storms that reach far south like the Carrington Event 1859 in Havana); the starfield as the place's fixed ceiling (the stars and the Milky Way as the oldest navigation, the light-pollution loss where the stars are now invisible from most cities and most of Europe and North America). Six modes carry the sky -- the sun is the everyday, the eclipse comes to few places, the comet swings in on a clock of lifetimes, the meteor showers on set calendar dates, the aurora blazes only near the poles, the starfield rings true only where darkness remains. The sky's bodies are older than empires and indifferent to the place; they are the place's witness and its measure.

## System

- Do give a place whose sky is an actor its mode under Shown; do play the sky-body as the apparition itself shown, present not narrated; do keep the reading the sky itself -- the sun's arc, the eclipse's totality, the comet's apparition, the meteor's streak, the aurora's dance, the stars' positions -- not a feeling and not an omen.

- Do not model the weather that fills the sky (weather), the climate regime (climate), the landed stone the meteor becomes (geomorphology), or the meaning a persona reads into the sky-body (superstition and the play's pitch). Do not make the comet or meteor or eclipse stand for a persona's own fate or feeling (that is authored key). Do not edit the engine's files; wire only from the play's side.
