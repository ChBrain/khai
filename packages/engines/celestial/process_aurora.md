# Process: Aurora

## Taxonomy

[Celestial](process_celestial.md), the mode where the sky alight at high latitudes shows the solar wind meeting the magnetosphere, the place under a sky that moves.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: celestial

## Initiated by

Solar wind streams from the sun toward the Earth, and the magnetosphere responds by drawing the charged particles toward the polar regions where they collide with the atmosphere and excite the nitrogen and oxygen to light.
The Aurora starts where the place sits near enough to the poles for the sky to move and redden and glow with a light that rises from no source on the ground but flows from the collision of the solar wind and the place's own magnetic shield -- green and red and violet, the colors of excited gas, moving in curtains and arcs that ripple across the sky, sometimes silent and sometimes humming, the entire night transformed to a stage of moving light.
Most places on the Earth will never see the aurora in any human lifetime, but the places at high latitude know it as a resident visitor to the night sky, more regular than the comet and more rare than the stars.

## Direction

At high latitudes where the magnetosphere funnels the solar wind to the poles.
The aurora is a resident of the far north and south, the places where the Earth's magnetic field forms its gate and funnels the solar wind toward the poles, the geometry that makes the aurora possible only there -- Aurora Borealis at the north, Aurora Australis at the south, the two mirroring each other across the planet as the solar wind streams against the Earth's magnetic shield.
In the greatest storms, when the solar wind surge is strong and sudden, the aurora swings so far south that it reaches latitudes that have never seen it in recorded history; in 1859, the Carrington Event sent auroras across the sky in Havana and Honolulu, the magnetic storm so severe that newspapers could be read at midnight by a light whose sun had already set.

## Lever

The solar wind's fury against the place's own magnetic shelter and the sky's own fixed dark.
The carrying force is the solar wind itself, driven by the sun's outer atmosphere and arriving at the Earth with no warning and no control, storms arising from the sun's own magnetics that play out across the Earth's magnetic field in waves of particles and light; the resistance is the magnetosphere that shields the place and the sky it has always known, the darkness transformed to light by a force that has no local source or cause.
The aurora is the proof that the place stands beneath a sky that does not end at the horizon but connects to forces older and larger than the Earth itself.

## Echo

The place under a sky that moves and glows with an alien light.
The aurora moves in curtains and arcs, now still and now rippling as if alive, the colors shifting from green to red and back, the darkness transformed for hours at a time, the entire night sky animated by a force that rises from above and plays across the magnetosphere, visible only where the geometry of the poles makes the dance visible.
The aurora is dated from the solar storm that carried it and the latitude where it reached, and the place is marked as a witness to the collision of the solar wind and the magnetosphere above it.
