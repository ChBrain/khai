# Process: Eclipse

## Taxonomy

[Celestial](process_celestial.md), the mode where totality is the day broken and the sun's light withdrawn from the place's face entirely.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: celestial

## Initiated by

The moon slides between the sun and the place, and the noon light dims degree by degree until the ground casts no shadow and day becomes indistinguishable from dusk.
The Eclipse starts where the moon's shadow races across the place and daylight stops -- the sun dwindled to a sliver, then gone, the sky in the totality turning to the color of the horizon at twilight all around, the temperature dropping as the light withdraws, the animals falling silent as if night had come in the middle of the day.
When totality is complete, the place stands in a darkness that has not come from the Earth's own turn but from an alignment two bodies overhead have made.

## Direction

Through the place at the moment when the moon's shadow crosses it.
The eclipse comes in stages: the penumbra's dimming, subtle and difficult to see until the sun wanes noticeably; the partial eclipse, when the sun becomes clearly wounded; and totality, when the sun is covered entirely and the sky falls into day-darkness, the stars and planets emerging, the light all around the horizon glowing red where, outside the shadow, the sun still shines.
No place sees the same eclipse twice in the same way, and for most places on the Earth, no lifetime will show them totality -- only the partial eclipse, the sun wounded and dimmed but not gone.

## Lever

The withdrawal of daylight against the place's own rhythm and certainty.
The carrying force is the celestial mechanics that place the moon exactly between the sun and the place for those few minutes; the resistance is the place's own standing and the shock it receives when the noon light goes, when the animals understand the day is broken, when the horizon rings with color from a light that should not be possible at that hour.
Totality is the rarest alignment, a perfection of distance and position that feels like a breaking of the day's own law.

## Echo

The place rendered strange in its own light.
The 360-degree sunset rings the entire horizon during totality, the coronal light of the sun showing around the moon's black face, the shadow bands racing across the place in the final seconds, the sudden cold and the gathering dark all at once in a place the sun was overhead not an hour before.
The eclipse is dated from the path the shadow traced and the day's own turn that will not come again for years, and the place is marked by the alignment that moved through it.
