# Process: Celestial

## Taxonomy

The sky's bodies as a place's own show.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: celestial

## Initiated by

The sun rises and sets, the moon waxes and wanes, the stars wheel overhead in their unchanging arc, and the place stands beneath them all, indifferent to who watches or cares.
Celestial starts where the sky puts its bodies before the place -- the sun's arc traced by the place's own aligned monuments, the moon's shadow darkening day to night, the comet hanging for weeks as a visitor on a return it will not live to see, the meteor streaking for seconds and burning out, the aurora alight at high latitudes when the solar wind reaches the magnetosphere, the stars above as the place's unmoving ceiling all night long.
These are the Shown themselves: not the weather overhead (that is weather's), not the light's feeling (that is mood's), not the climate regime (that is climate's) -- but the sky's bodies as they are, read by a place's position and the clock that sees them, on the ground.

## Direction

Through the place's own facing and the clock that stands overhead.
Six modes work the sky: [Sun](process_sun.md) is the place's governor, the day's arc and the seasons' swing, the light that architecture bends itself toward and the calendar traced in solstice-aligned monuments; [Eclipse](process_eclipse.md) is the day broken, totality as the place cast sudden into dusk in midday and the animals falling silent; [Comet](process_comet.md) is the visitor on a clock of lifetimes, the apparition that hangs for weeks and returns on a schedule no human lifetime sees twice; [Meteor](process_meteor.md) is the streak and the fall, the seconds-long trail and the stone that arrives on the place itself; [Aurora](process_aurora.md) is the sky alight at high latitudes, the solar wind meeting the magnetosphere and painting the dark sky in green and red -- the place under a sky that moves; [Starfield](process_starfield.md) is the night sky as the place's fixed ceiling, the one Shown that does not change and the oldest navigation tool humanity has, now invisible to most the world over.
A place does not show all of these at once: the sun rises on every place, but the eclipse comes to few; the comet swings into view on a clock of decades, the aurora blazes only near the poles, the meteor shower strikes on its calendar night.

## Lever

The place's own position on the Earth against the clock that grinds above it, indifferent.
The carrying force is the celestial body's own course -- the sun's seasonal swing, the moon's shadow crossing on its schedule, the comet's return, the aurora's solar-wind trigger -- fixed and knowable and older than the place; the resistance is the place's own facing and whether anyone stands there to witness it, whether it makes a difference to the place's standing or shapes only its story.
The sky's bodies are not a place's doing and do not pause for it: they are its witness and its measure, not its choice or its moment.

## Echo

The place read by what hangs above it.
The sun's path has shaped the place's architecture and its fields; the eclipse comes through and stops the place's time at the horizon; the comet and meteor pass through the place's sky and are gone; the aurora and the stars are the place's unchanging dome.
What the sky shows is dated from the place's own latitude and the night the sky is read, and the place is known by what its sky puts before it.
