# Process: Maxims

## Taxonomy

[Implicature](process_implicature.md), the cooperative-baseline form.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: implicature

## Initiated by

The hearer takes the speaker to be playing fair -- saying enough but not too much, saying what they believe true, staying on point, and putting it clearly -- and reasons from that expectation.
This is the Cooperative Principle and its maxims: quantity, quality, relation, and manner, the shared standard of well-formed talk against which every utterance is read.
The maxims are the baseline the whole of implicature is computed from -- the assumption that makes the surplus recoverable.

## Direction

Toward a hearer who reads the utterance against a standard of cooperative talk, and fills its gaps by that standard.
The maxims set four expectations: be as informative as required and no more (quantity), say what you believe true and can support (quality), be relevant (relation), and be orderly and clear (manner) -- and the hearer, presuming them in force, treats any shortfall as meaningful rather than as mere failure.
The direction is normative-then-inferential: the standard is assumed, the utterance measured against it, and whatever the measuring leaves unexplained is taken to be implied -- so the maxims are less rules the speaker obeys than expectations the hearer reasons from.

## Lever

The four maxims held as a default expectation, spent as the frame the hearer computes meaning within.
The maxims run as the hearer's working assumption: because they expect quantity, quality, relation, and manner to be honored, the hearer can treat the utterance as fully cooperative and read its literal content as the intended contribution -- or, where it seems not to fit, look for the implicature that restores the fit.
The lever is the presumed standard: the maxims give the hearer a fixed expectation against which to interpret, so that meaning can be inferred at all -- without the assumed cooperation there is nothing to reason from and no surplus to recover.

## Echo

The talk is read against a standard of cooperation, and that standard is what makes the meaning inferable.
It is why we take a plain, on-point answer at face value, why an under-informative reply prompts "and?", why an irrelevant remark makes us hunt for the relevance it must have -- the maxims the yardstick every utterance is measured by.
It is [flouting](process_flouting.md) and [scalar](process_scalar.md)'s ground, the baseline they work from -- flouting breaches a maxim in the open, scalar reads the quantity default off it. This owns the Cooperative Principle and its maxims as the standing expectation from which implicature is computed.
