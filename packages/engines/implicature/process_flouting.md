# Process: Flouting

## Taxonomy

[Implicature](process_implicature.md), the maxim-breach form.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: implicature

## Initiated by

The speaker breaks a maxim on purpose and in plain view -- says the obviously false, the far too little, the plainly irrelevant -- yet the hearer still takes them to be cooperating, and asks what they must mean.
This is flouting: the overt, deliberate breach of a maxim that generates a particularized implicature, because the hearer, unwilling to give up the assumption of cooperation, reads the breach itself as the message.
The literal is set aside as untenable; what the speaker must have meant by breaching is taken up in its place.

## Direction

Toward a meaning carried by the breach, read off against the cooperation the hearer refuses to abandon.
Flouting works by tension: the utterance flagrantly violates a maxim, but the hearer holds the Cooperative Principle fixed, so the only way to square the two is to find the implicature the speaker intended by so obviously breaking the rule.
The direction is particular and context-bound: irony floods quality with a plain falsehood meant to convey its opposite, a curt "he has lovely handwriting" floods quantity to damn by faint praise, an abrupt change of subject floods relation to warn off a topic -- each breach a signal shaped by the exact occasion.

## Lever

The open, deliberate breach of a maxim, spent as an implicature the hearer must supply to keep the speaker cooperative.
Flouting runs on the clash between the breach and the retained assumption of cooperation: because the hearer will not conclude the speaker is simply uncooperative, they resolve the clash by reading the breach as intentional and meaningful -- the more blatant the violation, the clearer that an implicature is meant.
The lever is the preserved Cooperative Principle: the maxim is broken precisely so the hearer will look past the literal, and the meaning is whatever the speaker must have intended for the flagrant breach to count as cooperation after all.

## Echo

The rule is broken in the open so the hearer will read the breaking, and the breach becomes the message.
It is why irony means the reverse of its words, why saying far less than expected can say the most, why a pointed non-answer answers -- the flouted maxim turned into a vehicle for meaning by a hearer who keeps faith with cooperation.
It is [maxims](process_maxims.md) and [scalar](process_scalar.md)'s counterpart, the deliberate breach where maxims are the baseline and scalar the default read off it. Where the literal is not breached but merely does an act, that force is the speech-act engine's; flouting owns the meaning generated by an overt, deliberate violation of a maxim.
