# Process: Implicature

## Taxonomy

Linguistics / pragmatics, the theory of conversational implicature.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: implicature

## Initiated by

A persona says one thing and means more than they said, and the hearer, taking them to be talking sensibly, works out the rest.
This is conversational implicature: the meaning a speaker conveys beyond the literal content of their words, recovered by the hearer on the assumption that the speaker is cooperating -- being appropriately informative, truthful, relevant, and clear.
What is meant is not exhausted by what is said; the surplus is inferred, not decoded.

## Direction

Toward a meaning the hearer computes, not just receives -- the said plus what must be meant for the saying to make sense.
Implicature runs on the Cooperative Principle: the hearer assumes the speaker is playing the conversational game, and reads the utterance against that assumption, so that anything the literal words leave open is filled by asking what a cooperative speaker must have meant.
The direction is inferential: from what was said, plus the presumption of cooperation, plus the context, the hearer derives what was meant -- a meaning calculable by that reasoning and cancelable if the speaker adds a word that defeats it.

## Lever

The presumption of cooperative talk, spent as the inference of meaning beyond the literal.
Implicature runs on the maxims of cooperation held as a default: because the hearer expects the speaker to give the right amount of true, relevant, clear information, any gap or oddity in the literal utterance becomes a signal -- the hearer asks why a cooperative speaker would say just this, just so, and the answer is the implicature.
The lever is the reasoning from cooperation: the meaning is not in the words but in what the words, said by someone assumed cooperative, must be intended to convey -- which is why it can be worked out step by step, and taken back without contradiction.

## Echo

What is meant outruns what is said, and the hearer catches the rest by assuming good faith.
Implicature is why "it's cold in here" can close a window, why "some passed" tells you not all did, why a faint compliment can damn -- the meaning carried past the literal by a hearer who trusts the speaker to be cooperating and reasons out what that requires.
It runs as [maxims](process_maxims.md), [flouting](process_flouting.md), or [scalar](process_scalar.md) -- the cooperative expectations banked on, a maxim breached in the open to imply, or the default inference read off the words. The illocutionary force that makes an utterance an act is the speech-act engine's; the lived gap between what is said and what is understood is the language engine's; the tilt a presentation frame puts on judgment is the framing engine's. This engine owns the inference of speaker meaning beyond the literal by the assumption of cooperation.
