# Implicature: Reference

## Line of Work

Implicature as **process**: how a hearer works out what a speaker means beyond what they literally say, by assuming cooperative talk -- Grice's theory of conversational implicature. The domain does not model the illocutionary force that makes an utterance an act rather than a description, which is the speech-act engine's; nor the lived gap between what is said and what is understood as a persona moves meaning across it, which is the language engine's; nor the tilt a presentation frame puts on judgment, which is the framing engine's. It models the inference of speaker meaning beyond the literal: the surplus a hearer recovers by presuming cooperation, working through the routes by which that surplus is carried.

The spine is Grice's pragmatics and the tradition that formalized it. Grice held that what a speaker means divides into what is said and what is implicated, and that the implicated part is recovered by the hearer on the assumption that the speaker observes the Cooperative Principle and its maxims of quantity, quality, relation, and manner. An implicature arises either by observing the maxims -- generalized implicatures like the scalar inference from "some" to "not all" -- or by flouting one in the open, which forces a particularized implicature such as irony. Grice held implicatures to be calculable (derivable by reasoning) and cancelable (defeasible without contradiction). Horn and Levinson systematized the generalized and scalar cases, and Sperber and Wilson's Relevance Theory recast the whole account around a single principle of relevance. Three routes carry it: maxims, flouting, scalar.

## Restrictions

What the engine refuses to model, and to whom it delegates.

- **Speech act**: The engine models the inference of meaning beyond the literal, not the force that makes an utterance an act. The illocutionary force by which saying does something -- asserting, requesting, promising, declaring -- is owned by the speech-act engine; implicature is about what is meant past what is said, force about what is done in saying it, so an utterance can implicate without any special force and can carry force with nothing implicated. The engine delegates the doing-by-saying to speech-act.
- **Language**: The engine models the pragmatic inference of the surplus, not the lived movement of meaning across the gap. The persona's experience of speaking and hearing, of what is said not being what is understood, is owned by the language engine; implicature is the specific machinery -- the cooperative reasoning -- by which one part of that gap is closed, not the gap as inhabited. The engine delegates the lived said/understood gap to language.
- **Framing**: The engine models the meaning a hearer infers from what a cooperative speaker must intend, not the effect of presenting content one way rather than another. How the same fact framed as a gain or a loss, or through a metaphor, shifts a judgment is owned by the framing engine; implicature is about recovering intended meaning, framing about the persuasive tilt of presentation, so a framing effect can operate with no implicature and an implicature carry no framing. The engine delegates the presentation-frame's tilt to framing.
- **Register**: The engine models the inference of speaker meaning, not the specialized code a persona commands. The variety of language tied to a domain and community, binding insiders and gating outsiders, is owned by the register engine; implicature works within whatever register is in play and does not model the code itself. The engine delegates the specialized-variety layer to register.

## Encoding

Source to constraint, per file.

- **[implicature](process_implicature.md)** (the root): The inference of speaker meaning beyond the literal -- the surplus a hearer recovers by assuming the speaker observes the Cooperative Principle, calculable and cancelable. Anchored by Grice (logic and conversation) and Sperber & Wilson (relevance).
- **[maxims](process_maxims.md)** (form): The cooperative-baseline route -- the Cooperative Principle and its maxims of quantity, quality, relation, and manner, the standing expectation the hearer reasons from. Anchored by Grice (the Cooperative Principle and the maxims) and Horn (the Q- and R-based principles).
- **[flouting](process_flouting.md)** (form): The maxim-breach route -- the overt, deliberate violation of a maxim that generates a particularized implicature, the hearer keeping cooperation fixed and reading the breach as the message. Anchored by Grice (flouting and particularized implicature).
- **[scalar](process_scalar.md)** (form): The default-inference route -- the generalized, cancelable inference from a weaker scalar term to the negation of the stronger, keyed to the word rather than the occasion. Anchored by Horn (scales) and Levinson (generalized conversational implicature).
