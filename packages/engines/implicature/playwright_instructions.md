# Instructions: Implicature

How a Playwright wires the implicature engine into a play. You wire by linking the play's own content to the engine's files; the engine files stay untouched, they are canon. The Roadie plumbs the engine's law into Instructions Knowledge on deploy. Authoring guidance, not runtime content, and it does not go on tour.

## Human

- The human defines the implicature terrain: what a persona says, what they mean past it, and what the hearer knows and assumes -- so there is a surplus of meaning to recover, and a cooperative expectation to recover it from.

## Agent

- From the play's own content, a persona links the form the moment runs (maxims, flouting, or scalar) under its Projection. Let it show meaning inferred beyond the literal -- the cooperative maxims banked on, a maxim flouted in the open to imply, a scalar default read off the words -- and let it read as speaker meaning recovered by assuming cooperation, not as the literal force of the utterance nor as a framing effect. You link to the engine, you never edit it.

## Collaboration

- The illocutionary force that makes an utterance an act -- asserting, requesting, promising -- is the speech-act engine's; the lived gap between what is said and what is understood is the language engine's; the tilt a presentation frame puts on judgment is the framing engine's; the specialized code a persona commands is the register engine's. This engine supplies the inference of speaker meaning beyond the literal by the assumption of cooperation.

## Knowledge

- Implicature is the meaning a speaker conveys beyond the literal content of their words, recovered by the hearer on the assumption that the speaker is cooperating -- being appropriately informative, truthful, relevant, and clear. Maxims is the baseline -- the Cooperative Principle and its maxims of quantity, quality, relation, and manner, the standing expectation the hearer measures every utterance against and reasons from, so that a gap or oddity in the literal becomes a signal. Flouting is the overt, deliberate breach of a maxim -- the speaker says the plainly false, the far too little, the clearly irrelevant, and the hearer, holding cooperation fixed, reads the breach itself as the message: irony, damning faint praise, the pointed non-answer. Scalar is the generalized default -- a weaker term on a scale ("some," "warm," "or") implying the stronger does not hold ("not all," "not hot," "not both"), an inference keyed to the word rather than the occasion and cancelable when context supplies the stronger reading.

## System

- Do make the meaning inferred, not just stated: the hearer works out what the speaker must mean by assuming cooperation -- so let a persona convey more than their words say, and let the hearer recover it by trusting the speaker to be informative, truthful, relevant, and clear.
- Do route by mechanism: maxims is the baseline expectation reasoned from, flouting is a maxim broken in the open to imply, scalar is the default read off a weaker word -- so name which the moment is rather than a vague "reading between the lines."
- Do keep implicature calculable and cancelable: the meaning should be derivable step by step from the words plus cooperation plus context, and defeasible when the speaker adds a word that takes it back -- so let an implicature be cancelled without contradiction rather than treated as part of the literal.
- Do delegate the utterance's illocutionary force to the speech-act engine, the lived said/understood gap to the language engine, and the presentation-frame's tilt to the framing engine. Do not edit the engine's files; wire only from the play's side.
