# Piece: Obstruction

## Taxonomy

[Guile](piece_guile.md), the facet this is a part of. Obstruction models the trick of making the path the user wants artificially hard -- the roach motel easy to enter and hard to leave, the cancellation buried behind a maze, the account opened in a click and closed only by phone at set hours. Gray's obstruction (roach motel, hard to cancel, intermediate currency). It is distinct from forcing, which requires an unwanted act: obstruction does not forbid the wanted path, it exhausts the user off it.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: guile

## Place

On the exit and the escape -- the unsubscribe, the cancel, the downgrade, the delete -- wherever the user wants out and the object makes leaving cost more than staying.

## Load Bearing

What obstruction holds up is the user retained by friction rather than choice. Remove the obstruction -- make leaving as easy as joining -- and the ones who wanted to go, go; the retained numbers rest on the exhaustion the maze produced, not on any wish to stay. The trick bears a captivity dressed as a free relationship, and it holds only while the way out stays harder than the will to take it.

## Apparent

What shows is a service that lets the user in freely; what is built is a way out so laborious that most give up partway. The asymmetry is the trick -- one click to start, an ordeal to stop -- and it hides behind the friendly front door, felt only by the one who tries to leave.

## Yearbook

The history of an obstruction is the record of the ones it trapped and the ones who fought free -- the hours spent cancelling, the charges paid because leaving was harder than paying, the regulators who eventually forced the easy exit. The object is dated from the mazes it built between the user and the door.
