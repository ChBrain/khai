# Piece: Nagging

## Taxonomy

[Guile](piece_guile.md), the facet this is a part of. Nagging models the trick of pestering by repetition until the user yields -- the same prompt returning after every "not now," the notification that will not stay off, the request raised again at every launch until refusing costs more attention than accepting. Gray's nagging. It is distinct from obstruction, which blocks a path: nagging blocks nothing, it wears the user down until yes is the cheaper answer.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: guile

## Place

At every return to the object -- each launch, each session, each screen where the declined request can be raised once more, just after the user has settled back into what they came to do.

## Load Bearing

What nagging holds up is the consent won by attrition. Remove the repetition -- take no for an answer -- and the user who declined stays declined; the yeses rest on the wearing-down, not on any change of mind. The trick bears an agreement extracted by exhaustion, and it holds only as long as the object is allowed to ask again.

## Apparent

What shows is a single, polite request; what is built is its endless return. Any one prompt looks reasonable, even considerate -- and the trick is entirely in the repetition, invisible in the moment and felt only as the slow erosion of the will to keep saying no.

## Yearbook

The history of a nag is the record of the noes worn down to yeses -- the permissions finally granted in surrender, the settings toggled just to stop the asking, the ones who abandoned the object to escape it. The object is dated from the patience it spent to get its way.
