# Instructions: Guile

How a Playwright wires the guile engine into a play. You wire by linking the play's
own content to the engine's files; the engine files stay untouched, they are canon.
The Roadie plumbs the engine's law into Instructions Knowledge on deploy. Authoring
guidance, not runtime content, and it does not go on tour.

## Human

- The human sets which objects in the world are built to deceive their users, and for whose gain.

## Agent

- From the play's own content, a piece built to deceive links under Apparent the facet of its trick: what it hides (sneaking), the wanted path it makes hard (obstruction), the request it repeats (nagging), the unwanted act it forces (forcing), or the choice it rigs (misdirection). Play the trick as a trusted surface that works against the one who trusts it, discovered only when it has already cost them. Keep it deceptive by intent -- for the maker, against the user -- and hand an honest defect to usability and a social program to agency. You link to the engine, you never edit it.

## Collaboration

- An honest design defect is usability's; the object enforcing a social program is agency's; a persona deceiving another person is the deception engine's; the reward loop that forms a habit is captology's. Whether the trick rings true for the object is the team's review.

## Knowledge

- Guile is the object designed to mislead its user for the maker's benefit -- the dark pattern, where the lie is the intent and not a defect. Five tricks over a deceit root -- hiding what would be refused (sneaking), making the wanted path hard (obstruction), pestering by repetition (nagging), forcing an unwanted act for a wanted one (forcing), rigging the presentation of a choice (misdirection). The object shows one thing and works another; the deceit is in the gap, kept invisible until the user is caught. The tell is intent: the false pull on a push door is a defect; the pre-checked box is a trick.

## System

- Do give a deceiving piece its facet under Apparent; do play the trick as a trusted surface, not an announced trap; do keep the reading deceptive by intent (for the maker, against the user).
- Do not model an honest defect (that is usability), a social program (that is agency), a persona's deceiving of another (that is the deception engine), or a habit-forming loop (that is captology). Do not edit the engine's files; wire only from the play's side.
