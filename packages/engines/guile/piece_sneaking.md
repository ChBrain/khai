# Piece: Sneaking

## Taxonomy

[Guile](piece_guile.md), the facet this is a part of. Sneaking models the trick of hiding or delaying what the user would refuse if they saw it in time -- the cost slipped in at the last step, the item added to the basket unasked, the subscription buried in the fine print, the price revealed only after the commitment. Gray's sneaking (drip pricing, sneak-into-basket, hidden costs, hidden subscription). It is distinct from misdirection, which steers a choice the user can see: sneaking keeps the thing itself out of sight until it is too late to refuse.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: guile

## Place

At the late steps of a flow, after the user is committed -- the final checkout, the confirmation screen, the buried clause -- where the unwanted thing can be slipped in past a user who has stopped looking closely.

## Load Bearing

What sneaking holds up is the acceptance the user never gave. Remove the concealment -- show the cost, the add-on, the renewal up front -- and the user refuses it; the sale rests entirely on their not having seen it in time. The trick bears a consent manufactured by hiding what the consent was for, and it collapses the moment the hidden thing is brought into the light.

## Apparent

What shows is a clean, ordinary flow; what is hidden is the charge, the item, or the term folded quietly into it. The surface looks like the expected transaction, and the sneaked thing is invisible until the receipt, the next statement, the renewal that was never announced. The deceit is an absence made to pass for completeness.

## Yearbook

The history of a sneak is the record of the surprises it sprang -- the charges disputed, the baskets emptied at the last second by the ones who caught it, the subscriptions cancelled in anger. Over time the sneak is exposed and named, and the object is dated from what it kept out of sight.
