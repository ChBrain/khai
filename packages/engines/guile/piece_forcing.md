# Piece: Forcing

## Taxonomy

[Guile](piece_guile.md), the facet this is a part of. Forcing models the trick of requiring an unwanted act to get the wanted one -- the registration demanded before you can look, the free trial that forces a card and auto-charges, the bundle you cannot decline to get the one part you came for. Gray's forced action (forced registration, forced continuity, forced enrollment). It is distinct from obstruction, which makes the wanted path hard: forcing makes an unwanted act the price of the wanted one.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: guile

## Place

At the gate before the thing the user came for -- the sign-up before the content, the card before the trial, the consent before the service -- where the wanted thing is held just out of reach behind a toll.

## Load Bearing

What forcing holds up is the unwanted act taken as tribute. Remove the forcing -- let the user have the wanted thing without the tax -- and the accounts, the cards, the enrollments it collected fall away; they rest on the gate, not on any want. The trick bears a tribute exacted at the door, and it collects only while the door cannot be passed without paying.

## Apparent

What shows is access to the wanted thing; what is built is the mandatory toll standing in front of it. The user sees the goal first, and the demand only after -- when turning back has already cost the effort of getting there, so the toll is paid to salvage the effort.

## Yearbook

The history of a forcing is the record of the tolls it took -- the accounts made only to be abandoned, the trials that quietly became charges, the enrollments no one wanted. The object is dated from the door it barred against the thing the user actually came for.
