# Process: Abstraction

## Taxonomy

[Construal](process_construal.md), the far pole.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: construal

## Initiated by

Distance lifts the persona's representation to its high level -- the thing far off is drawn in gist and essence, stripped of the particulars that only nearness supplies.
This is abstraction: the far pole of construal, the schematic reading a thing takes on when it is remote in time, space, social distance, or likelihood.
What is distant is known by what it fundamentally is, not by how it happens to be.

## Direction

Toward the essence, the purpose, the central and defining features.
Abstraction reads the far thing for its core: the why over the how, the end over the means, the desirable over the feasible, the category over the instance, the forest over the trees.
The direction is a rising above detail -- a distant vacation is "relaxation," a distant career is "doing good," a distant act is a trait -- the incidental burned off and only the defining kept.

## Lever

The gap distance opens, filled with schema rather than specifics.
Abstraction runs on the poverty of the remote: a thing not directly experienced offers no concrete detail to the mind, which represents it instead by its stable, essential, superordinate features -- the ones that survive the distance.
The lever is the removal itself: because the particulars of the far are unavailable or irrelevant, the mind keeps what generalizes -- the goal, the meaning, the type -- and lets the context fall away.

## Echo

The far thing is a clean ideal, and the persona judges it by its essence.
It is why distant plans look simple and coherent, why the far future is weighed by whether it is worth doing rather than whether it can be done, why remote people and events are read by their defining traits -- the abstraction that distance grants makes the far legible but loses its texture.
It is [concreteness](process_concreteness.md) inverted, and it travels: whatever pushes a thing off on one dimension of distance lifts it here, by the interchange the [traversal](process_traversal.md) form holds. The felt length of the time that makes the future distant is the time-perception engine's; this owns the lift to high-level construal itself.
