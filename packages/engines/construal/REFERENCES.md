# Construal: Reference

## Line of Work

Construal as **process**: the process through which psychological distance sets the level at which a persona mentally represents a thing -- the far drawn in abstract, schematic, essential terms and the near in concrete, detailed, contextual ones. The domain does not model the felt passage and availability of duration, which is time-perception's; nor the physical setting itself, which is space's; nor the mode by which a choice among options is reached, which is decision's; nor the goal system whose representation construal shifts, which is goal's. It models distance as the dial on construal itself: how far a thing lies from the self's direct experience -- in time, in space, in social relation, or in likelihood -- setting how coarsely or finely it is drawn.

The spine is Construal Level Theory. A thing can be represented at different levels of abstraction, and the level is set by psychological distance -- the removal of the thing from the self's direct experience along four dimensions: temporal, spatial, social, and hypothetical. Greater distance raises the level of construal, to the schematic, superordinate, and essential; nearness lowers it, to the detailed, subordinate, and contextual. The dimensions are cognitively interrelated -- one underlying sense of distance, cognitively interchangeable -- and the relation is bidirectional: distance sets construal, and construal cues distance. The three forms are the far pole, the near pole, and the interchange: abstraction, concreteness, traversal.

## Restrictions

What the engine refuses to model, and to whom it delegates.

- **Time-perception**: The engine models temporal distance as a driver of construal, not the felt passage of duration. How real the future feels, how available the past remains, and how duration registers as it passes are owned by the time-perception engine; construal takes temporal remove -- how far from now -- as one dial on abstraction, and owns that, not the texture of time itself.
- **Space**: The engine models spatial distance as a driver of construal, not the physical setting. The setting that affords conduct, is claimed, and induces mood is owned by the space engine; construal takes spatial remove -- how far from here -- as one dial on abstraction, and owns that, not the place itself.
- **Decision**: The engine models the level at which options are represented, not the mode by which a choice is reached. How a persona decides, and the choosing itself, are owned by the decision engine; construal shifts the weighting of desirability against feasibility by distance, and owns that shift in representation, not the decision it feeds.
- **Goal and Regulatory-focus**: The engine models the abstraction of a representation, not the goal system or the promotion/prevention orientation. The structure of goals and the regulatory orientation toward them are owned by the goal and regulatory-focus engines; construal sets whether a goal is seen as its why or its how, and owns that level-setting, not the goal or the focus.

## Encoding

Source to constraint, per file.

- **[construal](process_construal.md)** (the root): Distance as the dial on mental altitude -- the far drawn in essence, the near in detail, across the four interchangeable dimensions of remove. Anchored by Trope & Liberman (construal-level theory of psychological distance).
- **[abstraction](process_abstraction.md)** (form): The far pole -- distance lifting the representation to high-level construal, the gist, the why, the central and desirable and essential. Anchored by Trope & Liberman (temporal construal) and Vallacher & Wegner (high-level action identification).
- **[concreteness](process_concreteness.md)** (form): The near pole -- nearness dropping the representation to low-level construal, the detail, the how, the feasible and contextual. Anchored by Liberman & Trope (near-future feasibility) and Vallacher & Wegner (low-level action identification).
- **[traversal](process_traversal.md)** (form): The interchange -- the four distances as one dimension, a shift on any moving construal and construal cuing distance, the mental timeline that lays time out as space. Anchored by Trope & Liberman (the interchangeable dimensions) and Fujita et al. (spatial distance and construal).
