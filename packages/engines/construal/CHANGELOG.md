# @chbrain/khai-engine-construal

## 0.1.1

### Patch Changes

- 9384a10: Add the construal engine: the process through which psychological distance sets the level at which a persona mentally represents a thing -- the far drawn in abstract, schematic, essential terms and the near in concrete, detailed, contextual ones. The root (`process_construal.md`) declares Trope & Liberman's Construal Level Theory: how far a thing lies from the self's direct experience -- in time, space, social relation, or likelihood -- dials its mental altitude, and the four distances are one cognitively interchangeable dimension. Three forms carry it: `abstraction` (the far pole -- distance lifts to high-level construal, the gist, the why, the desirable and essential), `concreteness` (the near pole -- nearness drops to low-level construal, the detail, the how, the feasible and contextual), and `traversal` (the interchange -- the four removes as one dial, a shift on any moving construal and construal cuing distance, the mental timeline that lays time out as space).

  This is the capstone the space/time thread was reaching for: the scientifically-sound version of a "time/space" link is not a physics continuum but psychological distance, and construal now sits over its two concrete substrates -- `time-perception` (temporal) and `space` (spatial) -- unifying them with social and hypothetical distance under one concrete-abstract mechanism. Bounded against `time-perception` (temporal remove as a construal dial vs the felt passage of duration), `space` (spatial remove vs the physical setting itself), `decision` (the level options are represented at vs the choice), and `goal`/`regulatory-focus` (the why/how level-setting vs the goal system). No homonym gate: `construal` and the form stems `abstraction`/`concreteness`/`traversal` are all free (`immersion` was avoided as `flow` owns it). Warranted (LORE) on Trope & Liberman (_Construal-Level Theory_, 2010; _Temporal Construal_, 2003), Liberman & Trope (near/distant future, 1998), Fujita et al. (spatial distance and construal, 2006), and Vallacher & Wegner (action identification, 1987). Set at patch as the free level; a new engine may warrant a minor at the maintainer's `bump:minor` label.
