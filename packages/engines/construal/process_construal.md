# Process: Construal

## Taxonomy

Cognition / the social psychology of psychological distance.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: construal

## Initiated by

A persona represents a thing at a level of abstraction set by how far it is from their direct experience -- how far off in time, how far away in space, how socially removed, how unlikely.
This is construal: psychological distance dialing mental altitude, the far seen in coarse, essential terms and the near in fine, contextual ones.
The same object is a different thing to the mind depending on its distance -- an abstract idea when remote, a concrete particular when close.

## Direction

Toward the abstract as distance grows, toward the concrete as it shrinks.
Construal moves the level of representation with the distance: a far thing is drawn in high-level terms -- its gist, its purpose, its central and defining features, why it matters -- while a near thing is drawn in low-level terms -- its details, its mechanics, its incidental features, how it is done.
The direction tracks remove on any of the dimensions: push a thing off in time, in space, in social distance, or in probability, and it lifts toward the schematic; draw it near on any, and it drops toward the particular.

## Lever

The distance from the here-and-now-and-self-and-real, spent as a setting of altitude.
What construal spends is the removal itself: to represent something not directly experienced, the mind must fill the gap with abstraction, so the further a thing lies from the point of the self's experience, the more it is known by its essence rather than its particulars.
The lever is the traversable gap -- the four distances are one psychological dimension, cognitively interchangeable -- so distance sets construal, and construal in turn cues distance, the abstractly-drawn thing feeling remote and the vividly-detailed one feeling near.

## Echo

The thing is known at the altitude its distance sets, and the level colors everything downstream.
Construal is why the far goal is a clean ideal and the near one a tangle of logistics, why the distant plan weighs desirability and the imminent one feasibility, why a stranger's act is read by character and a familiar's by circumstance -- the level of representation, set by distance, governs what is seen.
It runs as [abstraction](process_abstraction.md), [concreteness](process_concreteness.md), or [traversal](process_traversal.md) -- the far lifted to essence, the near dropped to detail, the dimensions carrying across as one. The felt passage and availability of duration is the time-perception engine's; the physical setting itself is the space engine's; the mode by which a choice among options is reached is the decision engine's. This engine owns distance as the dial on the level of construal.
