# Instructions: Construal

How a Playwright wires the construal engine into a play. You wire by linking the play's own content to the engine's files; the engine files stay untouched, they are canon. The Roadie plumbs the engine's law into Instructions Knowledge on deploy. Authoring guidance, not runtime content, and it does not go on tour.

## Human

- The human defines the construal terrain: what thing a persona is representing and how far it lies from them -- how far off in time, how far away in space, how socially removed, how likely -- so distance has something to draw abstractly or concretely, and a level to set.

## Agent

- From the play's own content, a persona links the form the moment runs (abstraction, concreteness, or traversal) under its Projection. Let it show distance setting mental altitude -- the far thing seen in gist and essence, the near thing in detail and context, or the remove on one dimension carrying to the others -- and let it read as psychological distance dialing the level of representation, not as the passage of time or the physical setting itself. You link to the engine, you never edit it.

## Collaboration

- The felt passage and availability of duration is the time-perception engine's; construal takes temporal remove -- how far from now -- as one dial on abstraction, not the texture of time. The physical setting itself is the space engine's; construal takes spatial remove -- how far from here -- as one dial, not the place. The mode by which a choice is reached is the decision engine's; the goal system whose representation construal shifts is the goal engine's. This engine supplies distance as the dial on the level of construal itself.

## Knowledge

- Construal is the setting of mental altitude by distance: how far a thing is from the persona's direct experience -- in time, space, social remove, or likelihood -- sets the level at which they represent it, the far seen in abstract, schematic, essential terms and the near in concrete, detailed, contextual ones. Abstraction is the far pole -- distance lifts the representation to its high level, the gist over the detail, the why over the how, the desirable over the feasible. Concreteness is the near pole -- nearness drops it to its low level, the detail over the gist, the how over the why, the feasible over the desirable. Traversal is the interchange -- the four distances are one dimension, so a shift on any moves construal alike and construal in turn cues distance, the mental timeline laying time out as space.

## System

- Do make distance set the level, not the content: the same thing is drawn in essence when far and in detail when near -- so let a persona weigh the distant plan by whether it is worth doing and the imminent one by whether it can be done, the level shifting with the remove.
- Do let the dimensions travel together: spatial distance makes a thing feel temporally and socially far too, and an abstract framing makes a near thing feel remote -- so let a shift on one remove carry construal across the others, the four distances read as one.
- Do let construal cue distance as well as distance cue construal: a thing rendered in vivid, concrete detail feels near, and one framed in abstract essence feels far -- so the representation and the remove move together in both directions.
- Do delegate the felt passage of duration to the time-perception engine and the physical setting to the space engine. Do not edit the engine's files; wire only from the play's side.
