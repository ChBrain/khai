# Process: Traversal

## Taxonomy

[Construal](process_construal.md), the interchange of the dimensions.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: construal

## Initiated by

The persona treats the four removes -- distance in time, in space, in social relation, and in likelihood -- as one thing, so that a shift on any of them moves construal the same way and stands in for the others.
This is traversal: the interchange that binds the dimensions of psychological distance into a single dial, the reason far-in-time, far-in-space, far-from-self, and unlikely all feel like versions of the same remove.
Distance is one dimension wearing four faces, and the mind crosses freely between them.

## Direction

Toward a single scale of remove, and back from construal to distance.
Traversal runs both ways: any distance lifts or drops construal alike -- the far-future, the far-away, the stranger's, and the improbable are all read in abstract terms -- and construal in turn cues distance, so a thing framed abstractly feels remote and a thing rendered in vivid detail feels near, on every dimension at once.
The direction is a crossing: activate one remove and the others come with it -- the spatially distant is also felt as temporally and socially far -- because they share the single underlying dimension of removal from the self's here-and-now-and-real.

## Lever

The common core of the four distances, spent as their interchangeability.
Traversal runs on what the dimensions share: each is a way a thing departs from direct experience -- from now, from here, from me, from the actual -- and because the departure is the same, the mind represents them with the same abstraction and lets them substitute for one another.
The lever is the shared origin point, the self's direct experience: measured from it, a mile and a year and a stranger and a maybe are all simply "far," and what makes one far can be borrowed to make another far.

## Echo

The dimensions move together, and distance on one carries construal across all.
It is the binding that makes psychological distance a single construct rather than four -- why spatial distance makes an event feel temporally remote, why an abstract frame makes a near thing feel far, why the mental timeline lays time out as space -- the interchange that lets the removes stand in for each other.
It is what unifies [abstraction](process_abstraction.md) and [concreteness](process_concreteness.md) across the dimensions rather than within one. The felt duration that is temporal distance's own texture is the time-perception engine's, and the physical extent that is spatial distance's is the space engine's; this owns the interchange by which all four removes become one dial on construal.
