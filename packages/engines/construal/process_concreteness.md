# Process: Concreteness

## Taxonomy

[Construal](process_construal.md), the near pole.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: construal

## Initiated by

Nearness drops the persona's representation to its low level -- the thing close at hand is drawn in detail and context, the particulars that distance would have burned off now fully present.
This is concreteness: the near pole of construal, the fine-grained reading a thing takes on when it is close in time, space, social distance, or likelihood.
What is near is known by how it actually is, in all its incidental specifics.

## Direction

Toward the detail, the mechanics, the peripheral and contextual features.
Concreteness reads the near thing for its particulars: the how over the why, the means over the end, the feasible over the desirable, the instance over the category, the trees over the forest.
The direction is a descent into specifics -- the trip tomorrow is packing and traffic and the alarm set early, the imminent task is its steps, the present act is its circumstances -- the essence dissolved into the concrete detail of doing.

## Lever

The richness proximity supplies, filled with specifics rather than schema.
Concreteness runs on the wealth of the near: a thing about to be experienced presents all its detail, its context, its contingencies, and the mind represents it by exactly those low-level, subordinate, incidental features that distance would strip.
The lever is the presence itself: because the particulars of the near are available and pressing, the mind holds the how and the context, and the abstract essence recedes behind the concrete work of the moment.

## Echo

The near thing is a tangle of logistics, and the persona judges it by feasibility.
It is why imminent plans dissolve into their difficulties, why the near future is weighed by whether it can be done more than whether it is worth doing, why familiar people and present events are read by their circumstances rather than their traits -- the concreteness that nearness forces makes the near workable but crowds out the point.
It is [abstraction](process_abstraction.md) inverted, and it travels: whatever draws a thing near on one dimension drops it here, by the interchange the [traversal](process_traversal.md) form holds. The physical setting whose closeness supplies the detail is the space engine's; this owns the drop to low-level construal itself.
