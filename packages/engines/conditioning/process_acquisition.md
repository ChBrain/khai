# Process: Acquisition

## Taxonomy

[Conditioning](process_conditioning.md), the learning form.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: conditioning

## Initiated by

The cue and the outcome arrive together, again and again, and the persona comes to expect the second when the first appears -- the cue evoking, at last, an anticipatory response of its own.
This is acquisition: the building of the predictive link, the neutral stimulus becoming a conditioned one as it earns its status as a reliable sign of what follows.
The response the outcome would command begins to appear at the cue, readied before the outcome is there.

## Direction

Toward a cue that predicts, built by surprise and won only where the cue adds news.
Acquisition is not the mere stamping-in of a pairing: the cue is learned in proportion to how much it improves the prediction, and each pairing teaches only to the degree the outcome was unexpected -- so learning is fast when the outcome is surprising and slows to nothing as the cue comes to predict it fully.
The direction is error-driven: a cue already announced by another is blocked, learning nothing though it is paired, because the outcome it would predict is no longer a surprise -- the persona learns the cue that reduces the error, not the cue that merely accompanies the outcome.

## Lever

The contingency between cue and outcome, spent as prediction weighted by surprise.
Acquisition runs on the improvement in prediction a cue affords: the persona revises what the cue foretells by the gap between the outcome expected and the outcome received, and where that gap is closed -- by this cue or another already present -- there is nothing left to learn.
The lever is the prediction error: because it is surprise that teaches, a cue that tells the persona what they already knew is not conditioned, and a redundant predictor is blocked -- learning tracks information, not togetherness.

## Echo

The cue earns its meaning, and the response comes forward to meet it.
It is why the reliably-signalled outcome comes to be met at its signal and not before, why a redundant warning teaches nothing, why the surprising pairing is learned fastest and the fully-predicted one not at all -- the cue conditioned exactly to the news it carries.
It is [extinction](process_extinction.md) and [generalization](process_generalization.md)'s counterpart, the building where they are the waning and the spreading. The value of the outcome the cue predicts is the reward engine's, and the response worn by repetition into a habit is the habit engine's; acquisition owns the building of the cue's prediction, error by error.
