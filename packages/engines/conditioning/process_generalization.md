# Process: Generalization

## Taxonomy

[Conditioning](process_conditioning.md), the spreading form.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: conditioning

## Initiated by

A cue is conditioned, and cues that merely resemble it draw the response too -- the persona answering not only the sign they learned but its likenesses, the more alike the stronger the pull.
This is generalization: the conditioned response spreading along a gradient of similarity to stimuli near the original, before discrimination sharpens it back to the true predictor.
The child bitten by one dog fears dogs; the tone learned as a warning is answered by tones close to it.

## Direction

Toward a response cast wide across resembling cues, then narrowed to the one that pays out.
Generalization runs in two motions. The spread: the response transfers to stimuli like the trained cue in proportion to their likeness, a gradient falling off with difference -- useful, because the world rarely repeats a cue exactly, and a predictor too literal would miss its own recurrence. The sharpening: when some of the resembling cues are followed by the outcome and others are not, discrimination teaches the persona to answer the payer and withhold from the rest.
The direction is a width set by experience: broad where any near cue might mean the outcome, narrow where only the exact one does.

## Lever

The similarity between a new cue and the trained one, spent as response transferred along the gradient.
Generalization runs on likeness as a proxy for prediction: absent evidence to the contrary, a cue resembling a known predictor is treated as predicting too, and the closer the resemblance the fuller the response -- so the gradient is the persona's bet that similar signs carry similar news. Discrimination is the correction of that bet: differential outcomes across similar cues teach which resemblances matter and which mislead.
The lever is the tuning of breadth: because similarity stands in for prediction, the response is as wide as the persona's uncertainty about which features of the cue did the predicting, and it narrows as those features are taught apart.

## Echo

The response reaches past the exact cue to its likenesses, and experience decides how far.
It is why a fear learned once can shadow a whole class of things, why a brand rides the coattails of the one it apes, why the near-miss cue still triggers until it is taught to be safe -- the response spread by resemblance and reined by discrimination.
It is [acquisition](process_acquisition.md) and [extinction](process_extinction.md)'s counterpart, the spreading where they are the building and the waning. The judging of a case's likelihood by its resemblance to a type is elaborated by the representativeness engine; generalization owns the transfer of a conditioned response along the similarity gradient and its sharpening by differential outcome.
