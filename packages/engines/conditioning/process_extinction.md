# Process: Extinction

## Taxonomy

[Conditioning](process_conditioning.md), the waning form.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: conditioning

## Initiated by

The cue keeps appearing but the outcome no longer follows, and the conditioned response fades -- the persona ceasing to answer a herald that has stopped bringing its news.
This is extinction: the weakening of the learned response when the cue is presented without the outcome, the prediction it once carried contradicted by its own repetition.
The mouth stops watering at the bell that no longer means food; the flinch stops coming at the sound that no longer means danger.

## Direction

Toward a response quieted -- but by new learning laid over the old, not by its erasure.
Extinction does not undo acquisition; it teaches a second, competing lesson -- that the cue means nothing _now_ -- and files it alongside the first rather than in its place.
The direction is superimposition, and its proof is return: the old response comes back with the passage of time (spontaneous recovery), in a context other than the one where extinction was learned (renewal), and after a lone re-encounter with the outcome (reinstatement) -- the original prediction never gone, only overlaid.

## Lever

The cue presented without the outcome, spent as a new inhibitory prediction that competes with the old.
Extinction runs on contradicted expectation: the cue predicts the outcome, the outcome fails to arrive, and the surprise of its absence teaches a new meaning for the cue -- but the old meaning, once learned, is retained beneath the new, so which prevails depends on the context and the time that tell the persona which lesson applies.
The lever is the layering of the two: because the original learning is not erased but merely out-competed, a change of place, a lapse of time, or a reminder of the outcome can tip the balance back and the response reappear.

## Echo

The response falls silent, but the learning it fell from is still there.
It is why a fear treated in the clinic can return in the world outside it, why an old craving quelled comes back after a single taste, why time alone can revive a response thought gone -- extinction a new lesson over the old, not a wiping of the slate.
It is [acquisition](process_acquisition.md) and [generalization](process_generalization.md)'s counterpart, the waning where they are the building and the spreading. The relapse of a needed reward is elaborated by the addiction engine, and the intentionless persistence of a worn response by the habit engine; extinction owns the contradicted prediction and the return that proves it was overlaid, not erased.
