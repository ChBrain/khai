# Process: Conditioning

## Taxonomy

Behavioral science / the psychology of Pavlovian (classical) conditioning, the learning of what predicts what.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: conditioning

## Initiated by

A cue that meant nothing begins to reliably precede something that matters -- a bell before food, a place before pain -- and the persona comes to treat the cue as a sign of what follows.
This is Pavlovian conditioning: a neutral stimulus that predicts a significant outcome coming, by that prediction, to evoke a response of its own before the outcome arrives.
The persona has not learned to act for a reward; they have learned what the world foretells, and their body answers the foretelling.

## Direction

Toward a cue that carries the outcome's meaning, so the response the outcome would command is called up by its herald.
Conditioning turns on prediction, not mere pairing: a cue is learned as a signal only insofar as it tells the persona something the rest of the scene did not already tell them -- a cue that adds no information, however often paired, is not learned.
The direction is anticipatory: the response migrates forward in time from the outcome to its predictor, the persona readied by the sign for what the sign announces -- the mouth watering at the bell, the chest tightening at the doorway, the flinch at the raised hand.

## Lever

The predictive relation between cue and outcome, spent as the response the cue comes to evoke.
What conditioning spends is contingency and surprise: the cue is learned in proportion to how much it improves the prediction of the outcome, and each pairing teaches only to the extent the outcome was unexpected -- a fully predicted outcome teaches nothing, so a cue already announced by another is blocked from being learned.
The lever is information, not contiguity: the persona is a predictor tuned by error, revising what each cue foretells by how far the outcome departs from what was expected, and the conditioned response is the standing shape of that expectation.

## Echo

The herald comes to carry the news, and the body answers the herald.
Conditioning is why the smell of a place can bring back a dread learned there, why a jingle can water the mouth, why a survivor flinches at a sound that once meant danger -- the cue evoking, on its own, the response its outcome once compelled, because the cue has become the outcome's reliable sign.
It runs as [acquisition](process_acquisition.md), [extinction](process_extinction.md), or [generalization](process_generalization.md) -- the prediction built, waned without erasure, or spread and sharpened across resembling cues. The response repeated into an intentionless habit is the habit engine's; the discount rate on the outcome's value is the reward engine's; the wanting that turns to needing is the addiction engine's. This engine owns the learning of the cue's prediction itself.
