# Instructions: Conditioning

How a Playwright wires the conditioning engine into a play. You wire by linking the play's own content to the engine's files; the engine files stay untouched, they are canon. The Roadie plumbs the engine's law into Instructions Knowledge on deploy. Authoring guidance, not runtime content, and it does not go on tour.

## Human

- The human defines the conditioning terrain: what cue the persona meets, what significant outcome it comes to predict, and how the pairing runs over the scene -- built, broken, or spread to like cues -- so the cue has an outcome to foretell and a response to evoke.

## Agent

- From the play's own content, a persona links the form the moment runs (acquisition, extinction, or generalization) under its Projection. Let it show the cue's prediction at work -- the response built as the cue earns its meaning, waning when the cue stops paying out yet ready to return, spreading to cues that resemble the first -- and let it read as learned prediction, not as a valued reward nor as a worn habit. You link to the engine, you never edit it.

## Collaboration

- The response repeated in a stable context until it runs without intention is the habit engine's (the instrumental side); the discount rate on the outcome's value is the reward engine's; the wanting that turns to needing is the addiction engine's; the judging of likelihood by resemblance to a type is the representativeness engine's. This engine supplies the predictive learning itself -- the cue coming to evoke the response its outcome once compelled.

## Knowledge

- Conditioning is Pavlovian predictive learning: a neutral cue that reliably precedes a significant outcome comes to predict it and to evoke an anticipatory response of its own. Acquisition is the building of the link -- and it is built by surprise, the cue learned only insofar as it improves the prediction, so a fully predicted outcome teaches nothing and a cue already announced by another is blocked. Extinction is the waning -- the cue presented without the outcome and the response fading, but as new learning laid over the old rather than an erasure, which is why the old response returns with time, in another place, or after a reminder. Generalization is the spread -- the response transferring to cues that resemble the first along a gradient of similarity, then sharpening back to the true predictor as discrimination teaches which cue pays out and which does not.

## System

- Do make the cue's prediction the engine, not the outcome's value: the persona answers the herald because it foretells the news -- so let the response come forward to the cue that reliably precedes the outcome, and readied before the outcome arrives.
- Do build by surprise: let a cue be learned when it tells the persona something new, and let a redundant or already-predicted cue teach little -- conditioning tracks information, not mere togetherness.
- Do let extinction return: the response quieted by a cue that stopped paying out is overlaid, not erased -- so let a change of place, a lapse of time, or a single reminder revive it, rather than treating the learning as wiped.
- Do delegate the worn-in habit to the habit engine, the outcome's value to the reward engine, and the wanting-turned-needing to the addiction engine. Do not edit the engine's files; wire only from the play's side.
