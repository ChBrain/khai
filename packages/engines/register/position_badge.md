# Position: Badge

## Taxonomy

[Register](position_register.md), the mode this is one form of.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: register

## Has

The register as a mark of membership.
Commanding the code shows the persona is of the community; fumbling it shows they are not -- the accent of the initiated, worn in the choice of word and the ease of its use.
It is the shibboleth: not what is said but the fluency of the saying, which cannot be faked for long.

## Orders

Speak to be placed.
The position tilts the persona to use the code as a sign that they belong -- and to read, from how others use it, whether the belonging is real.
Every deployment is also a display; the register is spoken partly at the room, to say what the persona is.

## Loses

The neutral read.
Once the register is a badge, every use of it is also a test -- the persona is placed by their command of it, and cannot opt out of placing others by theirs.
The novice over-uses it to prove the badge; the veteran under-uses it because they no longer need to; either way the code is read for membership, not only meaning.

## Drives

Toward marking and reading belonging through the code.
The persona wears the register as a credential and scans others' for the same -- the way a discourse community recognises its own, the way a practice shows in how one talks.
Where the display hardens into keeping others out it becomes [closure](position_closure.md); where it rests easy among equals it is the plain [shorthand](position_shorthand.md) of those with nothing to prove.
