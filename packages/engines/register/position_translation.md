# Position: Translation

## Taxonomy

[Register](position_register.md), the mode this is one form of.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: register

## Has

A command of both the register and the plain tongue, and a listener who holds only the plain.
The persona can move between the code and the common language, and faces someone -- a patient, a client, a layman, a child -- who shares the base tongue but not the specialized one.

## Orders

Drop the code.
The position tilts the persona to switch down -- to unpack the term, gloss the shorthand, replace the mark of the guild with the words the outsider already has, and carry the meaning across into common ground.
The move is to be understood by the one outside, at the cost of the register's economy.

## Loses

The speed, and the badge.
Translating spends the compression that made the register fast, and sets aside the mark of membership that made it a claim; the persona meets the outsider as an equal in plain language rather than as an initiate above them.
It is slower, plainer, and gives up the standing the code conferred -- which is why it is not always done.

## Drives

Toward reaching the non-member by leaving the register.
The persona carries the meaning across the line the code draws -- the doctor who explains, the expert who makes it plain -- and in doing so shows the wall of [closure](position_closure.md) was a choice, not a necessity.
It is the elaborated answer to [shorthand](position_shorthand.md)'s presupposition: what was left unsaid among insiders is made explicit for the one who does not share the ground.
