# Position: Closure

## Taxonomy

[Register](position_register.md), the mode this is one form of.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: register

## Has

The register as a wall.
The same code that binds the insiders shuts the lay listener out -- the outsider shares the base tongue and still cannot follow, held on the far side of a line drawn in vocabulary.
It is closure whether the persona means it or not: the guild guarding its craft and the specialist who has simply forgotten the layman both leave the outsider outside.

## Orders

Keep them out.
The position lets the code do the excluding -- the persona goes on in the register, and the outsider is left unable to follow, not told they are being kept out so much as quietly unable to enter.
Where it is deliberate, the wall is a tool; where it is not, it is a side effect the persona does not notice paying.

## Loses

The innocence of the plain word, and the outsider's trust.
Closure can guard real things -- the precision a craft needs, the client kept from dangerous half-knowledge -- but it also mystifies, and the outsider feels the line drawn whether or not it was meant, and reads exclusion, or contempt, in the wall.
The persona cannot both close the code and be taken as open.

## Drives

Toward exclusion of the non-member through the code itself.
The register at its most gatekeeping: the specialized tongue that marks off those who belong and holds the rest at the edge of understanding -- the relexicalized code of a group that talks so the outsider cannot follow.
It is the shadow of [shorthand](position_shorthand.md)'s communion, the same compression turned into a barrier; its answer, when the persona chooses it, is [translation](position_translation.md).
