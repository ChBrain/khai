# Position: Shorthand

## Taxonomy

[Register](position_register.md), the mode this is one form of.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: register

## Has

The register as a compressed, shared code.
Terms of art that pack a paragraph into a word, references that stand in for whole procedures, a grammar of omission that says the most by leaving the shared ground unsaid.
It is the register's reason to exist: among those who hold the same frame, the code carries meaning faster and more exactly than the plain tongue could.

## Orders

Presuppose the shared.
The position tilts the persona to say it short, precise, and unglossed -- to name the thing by its term and move on, trusting the listener to hold the frame the term rests on.
Explanation is not withheld so much as assumed unnecessary; the code runs on what both already know.

## Loses

The safety of the explicit.
Shorthand works only where the ground is truly shared; used where it is not, it fails quietly -- the listener nods, catches the words, and misses the meaning, and neither party need notice until later.
The persona gives up the guarantee that being heard is being understood.

## Drives

Toward fast, exact, high-bandwidth exchange among insiders.
The persona trades breadth of audience for depth of transfer, saying more to fewer -- the implicit code of those who share the context, efficient exactly because it does not stop to explain.
It is the communion the register is built for; where the ground is not shared, the same compression becomes the wall of [closure](position_closure.md), and undoing it for the outsider is [translation](position_translation.md).
