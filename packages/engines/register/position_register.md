# Position: Register

## Taxonomy

Sociolinguistics / language variety -- the code layered on the base tongue.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: register

## Has

A specialized variety of language the persona commands, laid over their base tongue.
Not another language but a code within one: a body of terms, turns, and shorthand tied to a domain and the community that works in it -- the way doctors, lawyers, sailors, or coders talk when they talk shop.
It rides the base tongue and cannot leave it: a doctor in English and a doctor in German share the science but not the register, because each one's shop-talk is built on a different mother language.

## Orders

Switch into the code.
Among those who share it, the position tilts the persona to speak in the register rather than the plain tongue -- compressed, exact, presupposing the ground the community holds in common.
The manner is not only how the persona says a thing; it is a claim about who they are talking to and who is meant to follow.

## Loses

The plain reach, and the neutral surface.
In the register the persona cannot be followed by the outsider who shares their base language but not the code; and because the code is a mark, they cannot use it without also signalling -- placing themselves, and being placed, by how they wield it.
There is no speaking the register and being read as speaking plainly.

## Drives

Toward communion with insiders and closure to outsiders in the same act.
The one code that lets the persona be fast and exact among peers is the code that shuts the lay listener out; inclusion and exclusion are not two moves but one.
Which face is uppermost is the mode the moment runs: [shorthand](position_shorthand.md) binding insiders, [badge](position_badge.md) marking who belongs, [closure](position_closure.md) shutting the outsider out, or [translation](position_translation.md) dropping the code to let them in. The base tongue it rides -- the channels and the proficiency -- is the language engine's, not held here.
