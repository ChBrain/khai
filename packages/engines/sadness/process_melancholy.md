# Process: Melancholy

## Taxonomy

[Sadness](process_sadness.md), the diffuse form.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: sadness

## Initiated by

A sadness settles with no clear object -- not this loss or that failure, but a pervasive, lingering low, wistful and reflective, that colours the world grey without naming its cause.
This is melancholy: the objectless sadness, the pensive gloom the old physicians named a humour.
It arrives unbidden and unattached, a sadness about nothing in particular and everything in general -- transience, absence, the mere passing of things.

## Direction

Inward and backward -- toward reflection, reverie, the bittersweet turning-over of what is absent or passing.
Melancholy does not push toward action or away from a threat; it lingers and muses, half-sad and half-savouring the sadness.
Its direction is contemplative: not through a loss toward its accommodation, but around a diffuse ache toward reflection on it.

## Lever

A low tone without a discrete trigger -- the diffuse sense of loss, transience, or lack that suffuses experience rather than answering an event.
What melancholy spends is brightness and drive, dimmed to a reflective quiet; and its peculiar texture is the ache that is almost pleasant, the sadness one does not entirely wish gone.
It runs on mood more than on event, a colour laid over the world rather than a response to a thing in it.

## Echo

The world is seen through a grey and pensive light -- and melancholy, at its best, is the sadness of art and reflection, the mood that deepens rather than disables.
Sustained as a resting tone it shades into the trait of [dysphoria](position_dysphoria.md) and, as a persisting background, into the mood engine's province; as a bittersweet reflection on what is gone it colours nostalgia's terrain.
It is sadness turned reflective and unanchored, the low that muses rather than mourns.
