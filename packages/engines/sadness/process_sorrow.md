# Process: Sorrow

## Taxonomy

[Sadness](process_sadness.md), the deep-loss form.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: sadness

## Initiated by

A real and weighty loss lands -- not a mere letdown but the going of something or someone genuinely valued -- and the sadness runs deep, aching, and slow.
This is sorrow: the heavy sadness of true loss, the depth a passing disappointment never reaches.
It is sadness with weight, the emotion that must be gone through rather than shrugged off.

## Direction

Inward and down, into the loss and the ache of it.
Sorrow dwells: it turns the loss over, feels its full weight, and does not deflect it -- the direction is not away from the pain but through it.
It slows the days and pulls the gaze toward the absence, insisting the loss be felt before it can be borne.

## Lever

The value of what was lost and the depth of the attachment to it -- sorrow's weight is proportionate to what the loss meant.
What sorrow spends is a great deal of engagement with the world, drawn down and turned toward the absence: the heaviness in the chest, the slowed and inward days, the tears that come unbidden.
The more the lost was loved, the deeper the sorrow cuts.

## Echo

The loss is felt in full and, over time, accommodated -- sorrow is the working-through, the sadness that honours what was lost by feeling its going.
It is the emotion at the border of grief: where the loss is a bereavement and the working-through a sustained remaking of a life around the absence, it becomes the grief engine's.
Sorrow is the deep sad emotion; grief is the long process. This engine keeps the feeling of the weighty loss.
