# Process: Despair

## Taxonomy

[Sadness](process_sadness.md), the hopeless form.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: sadness

## Initiated by

Sadness fuses with hopelessness -- the loss or failure is met with the conviction that nothing can be done, that no effort will reach relief -- and the persona sinks past sorrow into the dark.
This is despair: sadness stripped of hope, the giving-up, the bottom of the low emotions.
It is the sadness that has stopped expecting, where even the ache of sorrow gives way to a flat and lightless still.

## Direction

Down and still -- toward the cessation of effort altogether, the future collapsing into an unbroken present of the unbearable.
Despair does not disengage from one goal but from goals as such; it is sadness that has quit the field.
Its motion is the going-out of motion: the slowing that does not slow toward rest but toward stop.

## Lever

The appraisal of helplessness made total -- not _this is lost_ but _nothing can be regained_ -- learned helplessness hardened into a stance (Seligman).
What despair spends is the will to act itself: with hope withdrawn, the ground of motivation goes with it, and the persona is left without the sense that anything it does could matter.
The lever is exactly this: hope removed, and effort collapsing into its absence.

## Echo

The persona ceases to strive -- and despair, held, is the doorway to depression and its dangers, the sadness that most needs another to reach into it.
Where ordinary sadness calls for care and can be answered, despair can fall past the calling, its very hopelessness foreclosing the reach for help.
It is the emotion at its most dangerous and least self-correcting; the persisting clinical low it can settle into is the mood engine's and the depression literature's, while despair is the acute collapse of hope within sadness.
