# Position: Dysphoria

## Taxonomy

Affect / the trait of dysphoria -- the standing readiness beneath [Sadness](process_sadness.md) (the depressive-melancholic disposition; the sadness facet of neuroticism).

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: sadness

## Has

A low resting affective set-point and a ready tilt toward sadness.
Trait sadness, as against the state: a disposition to feel loss keenly, to sink readily, and to lift slowly -- the persona saddened by more, sooner, and longer, carrying a baseline nearer the low.
It is the melancholic temperament the old humours named, the dysphoric pole of dispositional affect.

## Orders

Run from here, ahead of the loss.
The dysphoric persona reads outcomes as losses, recalls the sad more readily (mood-congruent memory), expects the disappointing, and finds the grey in the mixed; and the tilt is a standing risk, dispositional dysphoria among the well-worn paths toward depression.
It colours the read before the event, the way a mood does, but on the axis of loss and lack.

## Loses

The easy access to lift.
The dysphoric persona meets a world already dimmed -- fewer things registering as gain, more as loss -- and loses the ready buoyancy with which a brighter disposition meets the same day.
What narrows is the reach of gladness: the ground on which good news lands lightly.

## Drives

On the loss of that lift:
rides the dysphoria -- sinks with it, sees by its grey light, and sometimes turns it toward reflection or art -- or works against it, by behavioral activation, reappraisal, and the deliberate turn toward the reachable and the glad, at a cost in effort.
The set-point holds underneath either way, a resting level the persona manages rather than moves.
