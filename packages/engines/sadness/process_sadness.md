# Process: Sadness

## Taxonomy

Emotion / the psychology of the low-approach response to loss.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: sadness

## Initiated by

A persona appraises a loss, a failure, or a goal gone unmet -- something of value gone or beyond reach, with no clear way to restore it -- and sinks: the energy drains, the gaze turns inward and down.
This is sadness: the low-arousal negative emotion, the response to loss where anger is the response to a wrong and fear to a danger.
Where anger and fear mobilize -- toward the offense, away from the threat -- sadness demobilizes, slowing and turning in.

## Direction

Inward and down -- toward withdrawal from the world and from effort, the disengagement from what cannot be reached.
Sadness does not move against or away; it slows, turns down, and pulls in.
Yet it points two ways at once: away from the lost, and toward those who might comfort -- for sadness also calls, its very posture a signal to others to draw near.

## Lever

The appraisal of an irrevocable or unmeetable loss, and of helplessness before it -- there is nothing to push against as in anger, nothing to flee as in fear, only something gone.
What sadness spends is drive and engagement: it withdraws effort from the world, conserving what remains and releasing the persona from a goal it cannot reach (Nesse).
Its texture is the heaviness, the slowing, the inward turn, the tears.

## Echo

The loss is registered and, slowly, accommodated -- and the sadness signals while it does.
Sadness has two functions: it disengages the persona from unattainable goals, freeing it to turn toward reachable ones, and it broadcasts need -- the sad face and slumped posture elicit care and support from others (Bowlby; Averill).
It runs as [disappointment](process_disappointment.md), [sorrow](process_sorrow.md), [despair](process_despair.md), or [melancholy](process_melancholy.md), and is tuned by a standing [dysphoria](position_dysphoria.md). The sustained adaptation to an irreversible bereavement is the grief engine's; the persisting affective background beneath the moment is the mood engine's; the ache of social isolation is the loneliness engine's; the counterfactual sting of a bad choice is the regret engine's; the appraisal taxonomy of the basic emotions is the emotion engine's. This engine owns the emotion of loss itself.
