# Process: Disappointment

## Taxonomy

[Sadness](process_sadness.md), the goal-failure form.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: sadness

## Initiated by

An outcome falls short of what the persona expected, hoped, or worked for -- the prize missed, the plan failed, the promise unkept -- and the sadness of the gap between the wanted and the got.
This is disappointment: the mildest and most common sadness, the everyday small grief of an unmet expectation.
It is sadness scaled to a letdown, not a loss -- the lift of anticipation collapsing into the flatness of a lesser real.

## Direction

Down from the expected to the actual, and toward the letting-go of the hope that was raised.
Disappointment deflates: it does not dwell or darken so much as sag, the air going out of an expectation that will not now be met.
Its motion is the quiet lowering of a raised hope back to the ground.

## Lever

The size of the gap between expectation and outcome, and the value staked on it -- the higher the hope, the harder the fall.
What disappointment spends is the investment in the hoped-for; the letdown is the withdrawal of that investment, felt as a small sinking.
The more the persona let itself expect, the more there is to take back.

## Echo

The hope is relinquished and the expectation recalibrated -- disappointment is the sadness that teaches, tuning future hopes toward the achievable.
Where the shortfall was one's own doing, it borders regret, which is the regret engine's; where another let one down, it can sour toward hurt or anger.
It is the recoverable sadness, the one most quickly metabolized -- felt, learned from, and set down.
