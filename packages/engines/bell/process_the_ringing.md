# Process: The Ringing

## Taxonomy

[Bell](piece_bell.md), what a stroke does to everybody in range.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: bell

## Initiated by

The rope is pulled and several hundred people receive a message.
None of whom agreed to.

## Direction

Toward an instruction, a fact, or a piece of news delivered without an addressee.
Everybody in range stops for a fraction of a second and interprets: the hour, a fire, a death, a wedding, an arrival, a warning. The code is learned in childhood, never formally taught, and read accurately by people who could not describe it.
What is remarkable is the absence of any channel back. Nobody can query the ring, ask it to repeat, or indicate that they got it.

## Lever

The efficiency of one message reaching everybody against the fact that it reaches everybody.
The carrying force is that the reach is the whole value and it is not achievable any other way: an alarm that finds every person in a parish within seconds, with no equipment, no literacy, and no subscription, is a genuinely remarkable public instrument, and the people who paid for it were paying for exactly this; the resistance is that the same property makes it unrefusable, so the person asleep after a night shift, the person mourning, and the person who has left the church all receive the message on the same terms as everybody else.
The Ringing is a broadcast with no way to switch it off.

## Echo

A parish that has all been told the same thing at the same time.
It shows the mechanism the whole engine turns on.
The Ringing is dated to the stroke and is over in seconds.
