# Process: The Earshot

## Taxonomy

[Bell](piece_bell.md), the territory the sound makes.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: bell

## Initiated by

The sound carries as far as it carries.
Further with the wind behind it, less in summer leaf, further at night, and further over water.

## Direction

Toward a community defined acoustically and by nothing else.
The people who are in the parish, in the sense that matters here, are the people who can hear it -- which is a boundary drawn by topography, wind, and the height of a tower, and which coincides with no administrative line anybody ever drew.
Where two ranges overlap, the households in between are in both and in neither, and can tell the two voices apart. Historically these were precisely the places where the arguments happened.

## Lever

The reality of the boundary against the arbitrariness of what drew it.
The carrying force is that the acoustic community is not a metaphor: the people in range share a clock, an alarm, and a running account of the local dead, which is more than most administrative units give anybody, and being inside it is a real condition with real consequences; the resistance is that the line was set by a hill, a prevailing wind, and how much metal the parish could afford in 1740, so a boundary that structures a life was drawn by an acoustic accident nobody would defend if asked to justify it.
The Earshot is a real community with an arbitrary edge.

## Echo

A map nobody drew, followed by everybody in it.
It shows what the object actually constitutes, which is not a building's congregation.
The Earshot is dated to the tower's height and varies with the weather.
