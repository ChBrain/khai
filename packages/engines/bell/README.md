# Bell

Bell as piece: the instrument that reaches everybody at once, read on what it constitutes rather than on how it sounds -- not the auditory field of a place with its keynote and its soundmarks, which is soundscape's, and not the record an institution keeps of a person, which is document's. The object is the piece; the members are what happens around it. One position names who holds it: a ringer with the rope and an authorisation somebody granted, who can compel every person within two miles to receive a message and none of them to do anything, who cannot ring privately or be wrong quietly, and who holds the smallest possible authority over the largest possible audience. Three processes name what it does: the casting, which fixes a voice in an afternoon and issues it for four hundred years; the ringing, a broadcast with no way to switch it off and no channel back; and the earshot, a real community with an arbitrary edge, drawn by a hill, a prevailing wind, and how much metal the parish could afford. The twist: nobody can decline a bell, which is why it is a service and why it is a claim.
