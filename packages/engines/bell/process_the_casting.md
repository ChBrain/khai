# Process: The Casting

## Taxonomy

[Bell](piece_bell.md), how the object is made and what that fixes.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: bell

## Initiated by

Metal is melted and poured into a mould in the ground.
Usually in the churchyard itself, because the finished object cannot be moved far.

## Direction

Toward a voice decided once, by a founder who guessed.
The pitch is set by the shape and the thickness of the metal, and it is fixed at the moment it cools. A bell that comes out wrong can be shaved a little and otherwise cannot be corrected: it is either the voice the parish now has for four hundred years, or it goes back into the fire and is paid for twice.
Which is why the casting was a public event, attended, blessed, and remembered, and why the bell is inscribed with a name and a date like a person.

## Lever

The permanence of the object against the irreversibility of the moment that made it.
The carrying force is that the permanence is the point and is extraordinary value for money: a well-made bell outlasts the building, the parish register, and every family in the village, needs almost no maintenance, and continues to work through wars and neglect; the resistance is that everything about its voice was settled in an afternoon by a craftsman working to a recipe, so a community lives for centuries with a specific pitch that nobody now alive chose and nobody can alter.
The Casting fixes a voice in an afternoon and issues it for four hundred years.

## Echo

An object with a name, a date, and a fixed opinion.
It shows why the bell is treated as a member of the community rather than as equipment.
The Casting is dated to the pour and is recorded on the object itself.
