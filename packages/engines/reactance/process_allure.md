# Process: Allure

## Taxonomy

[Reactance](process_reactance.md), the indirect form.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: reactance

## Initiated by

The persona finds the blocked option growing more desirable the moment it is blocked -- the pull rising not because the thing improved but because reaching for it is no longer allowed.
This is allure: reactance discharged into wanting, the forbidden fruit made sweet by the forbidding.
The restriction does not close the option; it gilds it.

## Direction

Toward the withheld option, its value swollen by the ban.
Allure does not yet act; it revalues -- the censored book more worth reading, the sold-out item more worth having, the off-limits person more worth wanting, each raised in the persona's regard by the very scarcity the restriction imposed.
The direction is inward and upward: the freedom defended by inflating the worth of what one is told one may not have.

## Lever

The scarcity of the freedom, read as the worth of the thing.
Allure spends the restriction as if it were information about value -- what is banned must be potent, what is limited must be precious -- so the persona's wanting tracks the barrier rather than the object behind it.
The lever is the false signal: a ban treated as a mark of quality, so the harder a thing is to reach, the more the persona takes it to be worth reaching for.

## Echo

The forbidden is wanted, and the persona loses the clean read on the thing itself.
It is the quietest of the three, working before any act -- the desire simply rises -- and the most exploitable, since a source can manufacture wanting by manufacturing scarcity, the limited edition and the deadline built to trip it.
It is [defiance](process_defiance.md) still held as wish rather than deed, and the seed derogation needs -- for the wanting, once frustrated, curdles into resentment of [the source](process_derogation.md) that placed the thing out of reach. The static valuation tilt this leaves, catalogued among the biases, is the bias engine's; this owns the live rise of wanting under threat.
