# @chbrain/khai-engine-reactance

## 0.1.1

### Patch Changes

- 04a4327: Add the reactance engine: the motivational pushback through which a persona whose behavioral freedom is threatened or removed is aroused to restore it -- and moves against the very force that pressed. The root (`process_reactance.md`) declares Brehm's psychological reactance: where `persuasion` says _come toward this_ and `conformity` says _come toward us_, reactance answers _do not tell me I cannot_, the boomerang that grows sharper the harder the freedom is pressed. It is the resistance third leg of the influence family. Three routes carry the restoration: `defiance` (direct -- do the threatened act, visibly, so the freedom is proven by its use), `allure` (indirect -- the blocked option's pull rises with the ban rather than its worth, the forbidden gilded by the forbidding), and `derogation` (source-directed -- turn on the agent that pressed, discrediting it so the threat loses its grip).

  Cleared the member-scope gate via the `reactance` homonym whitelist (governance PR #903), mirroring `framing`: `bias` carries `position_reactance.md` as one static valuation tilt in its codex, and a codex entry at the `position` altitude does not preempt the full `process` engine of the same phenomenon. Bounded against `persuasion` (the uptake of the message vs the pushback against it), `conformity`'s `independence` (standing unmoved vs pushed the opposite way by the push), `aggression` (the raw hostility derogation recruits), `bias` (the static tilt vs the live arousal), and `contempt` (the cold devaluation vs the freedom-restoring discredit). Warranted (LORE) on Brehm (_A Theory of Psychological Reactance_, 1966), Brehm & Brehm (_Psychological Reactance_, 1981), Dillard & Shen (the anger-cognition boomerang, 2005), Clee & Wicklund (commodity theory, 1980), and Miron & Brehm (the 40-years-later review, 2006). Set at patch as the free level; a new engine may warrant a minor at the maintainer's `bump:minor` label.
