# Process: Reactance

## Taxonomy

Motivation / the social psychology of threatened freedom.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: reactance

## Initiated by

A persona reads one of their behavioral freedoms as threatened or taken away -- an option forbidden, a choice pressured, a course mandated -- and is aroused to get it back.
This is reactance: the motivational pushback that a threat to freedom sets off, a state that turns the persona against the very force that pressed.
Where persuasion says _come toward this_ and conformity says _come toward us_, reactance answers _do not tell me I cannot_ -- and moves against the push rather than toward any position of its own.

## Direction

Toward the freedom reclaimed, against the source that threatened it.
Reactance does not weigh the option on its merits; it defends the ability to choose it, so the persona moves to restore the freedom whether or not they wanted the option before it was pressed.
The arousal scales with the threat, not the prize: the more important the freedom, the more of it a single restriction implies, and the more high-handed the pressure, the sharper the boomerang -- and it grows, not shrinks, the harder the freedom is pressed.

## Lever

The threat to freedom itself, felt as arousal to undo it.
What reactance spends is the pressure it was given: a heavy hand, a hard sell, a blanket ban, an order that leaves no room -- each is read as a freedom removed and converted into motive to take it back.
The lever is the restriction, turned against its own aim: the same force that meant to close the option is what makes reclaiming it feel necessary, so the push manufactures the resistance.

## Echo

The freedom is defended, and the pressure backfires.
Reactance is why influence rebounds: the ban that makes the thing wanted, the warning that invites the deed, the order obeyed a beat too slowly on purpose -- to be told one cannot is, for the persona, reason enough to show that one can.
It runs as [defiance](process_defiance.md), [allure](process_allure.md), or [derogation](process_derogation.md) -- the forbidden act done, the forbidden option wanted, the source that pressed resented. The uptake of a persuasive message is the persuasion engine's; standing unmoved by group pressure, rather than pushed against it, is the conformity engine's independence; the hostility reactance recruits toward the source is the aggression engine's; the static tilt this look leaves on the read of an option's worth is the bias engine's. This engine owns the pushback itself.
