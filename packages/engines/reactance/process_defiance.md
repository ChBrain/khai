# Process: Defiance

## Taxonomy

[Reactance](process_reactance.md), the direct form.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: reactance

## Initiated by

The persona reclaims the threatened freedom by doing the very thing they were told they could not -- directly, and in the open, so the freedom shows itself restored in the act.
This is defiance: reactance discharged straight through the blocked behavior, the option exercised precisely because it was forbidden.
It is the most literal restoration -- the freedom proven by its use.

## Direction

Toward the threatened act, performed and seen.
Defiance does not merely decline to comply; it does the opposite, and does it visibly -- the rule broken where it will be noticed, the warning label the reason to try, the curfew missed on principle.
The direction is a countermove: not away from the pressure but straight against it, into the behavior the pressure meant to close off.

## Lever

The exercise of the freedom as its own proof.
Defiance spends the act itself -- the persona chooses the blocked path, sometimes against their own actual preference, because performing it is what demonstrates the freedom still holds.
The lever is visibility: a defiance done in secret restores nothing, so the point is to be seen choosing what one was told one could not -- the freedom is not felt reclaimed until it is shown.

## Echo

The freedom is reasserted, and the restriction is answered in kind.
It is the sharpest of the three to meet, because it produces the exact behavior the pressure sought to prevent -- the harder the push, the more certain the countermove -- and it can cost the persona, who may take the worse road just to prove the road is theirs to take.
It is [allure](process_allure.md) turned from wanting into doing, and [derogation](process_derogation.md) turned from the source onto the act -- the restoration carried out rather than merely desired or aimed at the one who pressed.
