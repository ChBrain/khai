# Instructions: Reactance

How a Playwright wires the reactance engine into a play. You wire by linking the play's own content to the engine's files; the engine files stay untouched, they are canon. The Roadie plumbs the engine's law into Instructions Knowledge on deploy. Authoring guidance, not runtime content, and it does not go on tour.

## Human

- The human defines the reactance terrain: which freedom the persona holds and how much it matters to them, what threatens it -- a ban, a hard sell, an order, a scarcity -- and how high-handed the pressure is, so the pushback has a freedom to defend and a force to push back against.

## Agent

- From the play's own content, a persona links the route the moment runs (defiance, allure, or derogation) under its Projection. Let the route show how the freedom is reclaimed -- the rule broken in the open, the forbidden thing grown sweet, the pusher resented and discredited -- and let it read as a move against the push, not a considered move toward a position. You link to the engine, you never edit it.

## Collaboration

- The uptake of a persuasive message is the persuasion engine's; reactance is what its controlling excess produces, the boomerang against the hard sell. Standing unmoved by group pressure, rather than pushed against it, is the conformity engine's independence. The raw hostility the source-directed form recruits is the aggression engine's; the static tilt the look leaves on the read of an option's worth is the bias engine's. This engine supplies the pushback itself -- a freedom threatened and the arousal to take it back.

## Knowledge

- Reactance is the motivational pushback: a persona reads a freedom as threatened or removed and is aroused to restore it, moving against the very force that pressed. Where persuasion says come toward this and conformity says come toward us, reactance answers do not tell me I cannot -- and the boomerang grows sharper, not weaker, the harder the freedom is pressed. Defiance is the direct route -- do the threatened act, visibly, so the freedom is proven by its use. Allure is the indirect route -- the blocked option grows desirable in proportion to the ban rather than its worth, the forbidden gilded by the forbidding. Derogation is the source-directed route -- turn on the one who pressed, discrediting them so the threat loses its grip.

## System

- Do make reactance move against the push, not away from a position: the persona is not weighing the option and declining it, they are defending the freedom to choose it -- so let the pressure itself, not the prize, drive the response.
- Do let the boomerang scale with the threat: a light touch carries where a heavy hand rebounds -- so let a high-handed, absolute, or important-freedom threat produce a sharper pushback than a gentle one, and let an overbearing persuader lose the very point a softer one would have made.
- Do let allure work before any act: the ban gilds the thing, so the persona wants it more merely for being told they may not -- keep the wanting tracking the barrier, not the object behind it.
- Do delegate the uptake of the message to the persuasion engine and the raw hostility to the aggression engine. Do not edit the engine's files; wire only from the play's side.
