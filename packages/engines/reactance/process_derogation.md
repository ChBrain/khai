# Process: Derogation

## Taxonomy

[Reactance](process_reactance.md), the source-directed form.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: reactance

## Initiated by

The persona defends the threatened freedom by turning on the one who threatened it -- discrediting, resenting, resisting the source rather than the restriction alone.
This is derogation: reactance discharged onto the agent that pressed, the messenger devalued so the message loses its grip.
The freedom is protected by lowering the standing of whoever tried to close it.

## Direction

Toward the source of the pressure, met with hostility and distrust.
Derogation does not go to the act or the option; it goes to the pusher -- the persuader recast as manipulative, the authority as overbearing, the advice as an agenda -- so their power to constrain is broken by breaking their credibility.
The direction is outward at the agent: the freedom kept by making the one who threatened it not worth heeding.

## Lever

The credibility of the source, spent to disarm the threat.
Derogation runs on a simple exchange: a demand from someone discredited is no demand at all, so the persona attacks the trustworthiness, the motives, the right of the one who pressed, and the pressure dissolves with the standing behind it.
The lever is the recast: the source reframed from helper to imposer, from expert to zealot, so that resisting them reads as sense rather than mere stubbornness.

## Echo

The freedom is defended, and the relationship with the source is damaged.
It is the most corrosive of the three to a tie, because the pushback lands not on a rule but on a person -- the harder or more high-handed the pressure, the more the source is resented -- and a heavy-handed persuader can lose all standing to a message that a lighter touch would have carried.
It is [defiance](process_defiance.md) and [allure](process_allure.md) redirected from the freedom onto the one who threatened it. The freedom-restoring turn against the source is this engine's; the raw hostility and its expression are the aggression engine's, the cold devaluation of another as beneath one is the contempt engine's, and the uptake of the message this defends against is the persuasion engine's.
