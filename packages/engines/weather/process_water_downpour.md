# Process: Water, Downpour

## Taxonomy

[Water](process_water.md), the band this force is a level of.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: weather

## Initiated by

Water past what the place easily sheds.
The besieging band of [Water](process_water.md):
heavy rain or hail, the drains loading, the low ground beginning to fill.

## Direction

Down in weight, then hard along the low lines, faster than the place can clear.
Toward the hollow, the culvert, the basement step.

## Lever

The place's shedding is at its limit.
The drain is full, the ground is saturated, the runoff finds its own way.
Conduct is forced: the crossing abandoned, the low ground left, the way changed for it.

## Echo

What the place could not clear in time.
The line the water took is marked, and the low ground remembers it.
