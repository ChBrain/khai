# Process: Sky, Shut

## Taxonomy

[Sky](process_sky.md), the band this light is a level of.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: weather

## Initiated by

Light taken to where the place cannot be seen across.
The besieging band of [Sky](process_sky.md):
whiteout, dust-dark, the blackout of night without a moon, the far side of the place simply gone.

## Direction

Over everything at once, closing the place down to arm's length.
Toward the body's own edge, past which nothing is given to the eye.

## Lever

The place's need to be seen is at its limit.
There is no distance left; the eye fails and the place is crossed by memory and touch.
Conduct is forced: the crossing held, the hand kept on the wall, the known way trusted over the seen one.

## Echo

The place navigated blind, or not at all.
Who knew it well enough to move in it, and who was stranded where the light left them, is known.
