# Process: Sky

## Taxonomy

[Weather](process_weather.md), the root this channel is a movement of.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: weather

## Initiated by

The light the place is under, given or taken by the sky.
A channel of [Weather](process_weather.md):
the sun, the cloud, the dark, deciding how much of the place can be seen, and how far.

## Direction

Onto every surface at once, or off it, setting how the place reads to the eye.
Toward the far edge of the place, which the light either gives or takes away.

## Lever

The light the sky allows against the place's need to be seen across.
The load has three bands, and the band is how much sight the sky permits.
It runs from [Clear](process_sky_clear.md), the place shown plainly,
through [Veiled](process_sky_veiled.md), where the place dims and its distances go,
to [Shut](process_sky_shut.md), where the place cannot be seen across at all.
It has no fourth band: taken past shut, there is simply no seeing, and the place waits for the light to return.

## Echo

How far the place could be read, and by whom.
The light passes with the cloud, but what could be seen and what could not, while it held, is known.
