# Process: Cold, Biting

## Taxonomy

[Cold](process_cold.md), the band this force is a level of.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: weather

## Initiated by

Cold the place must now stand against.
The stirring band of [Cold](process_cold.md):
it finds the gaps in the layers, takes the hands and the face first, sharp wherever the wind runs.

## Direction

Into every opening, at the exposed edge of the body, from the windward side.
Toward warmth, sought now rather than braved.

## Lever

The place keeps warmth with effort.
The lee holds and the open does not; the fire matters and the wall is worth standing near.
Conduct bends to it: hands pocketed, the crossing hurried, the warm side kept.

## Echo

The place sorted into warm and exposed.
Who could stay by the warmth and who had to cross the open is known; the cold is felt long after.
