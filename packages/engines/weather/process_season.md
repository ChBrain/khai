# Process: Season

## Taxonomy

[Weather](process_weather.md), the standing channel the others move against.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: weather

## Initiated by

Nothing starts it.
The place is always already tilted somewhere on its year.
Season does not arrive the way a front does; it is the ground the fronts arrive onto,
the standing condition every transient load is measured from and returns to.

## Direction

Underneath the other five channels, never toward the moment.
It sets the floor each transient channel relaxes toward, and it moves only by the slow turn of the year.
Toward the resting state of the place, not any event in it.

## Lever

Not a band but a datum.
Season does not climb the ladder; it decides where the ladder's foot sits.
A place low on its year sits colder and darker at rest, so its [Cold](process_cold.md) and its [Sky](process_sky.md)
start from higher up and its [Heat](process_heat.md) from lower down; a place high on its year sits the other way.
It holds two standing states, [Dormant](process_season_dormant.md) and [Growing](process_season_growing.md),
the poles the place tilts between.
Remove the tilt and there is no floor, only whatever the front happened to bring.

## Echo

The slow turn, recorded across the whole year.
The place is never quite the same place in dormant as in growing.
The fronts pass and are forgotten; the tilt stays, and slides, and is the thing the place is known by.
