# Process: Heat

## Taxonomy

[Weather](process_weather.md), the root this channel is a movement of.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: weather

## Initiated by

The air and the sun loading the place toward too warm.
A channel of [Weather](process_weather.md):
the heat the place holds, the sun on its surfaces and the still air over them,
pressing the bodies in it toward the edge of what they can shed.

## Direction

Into everything the place exposes, and into everyone standing in it.
Toward the shade, which is suddenly the only cool the place has.

## Lever

The heat load against the place's ability to cool anyone in it.
The load has four bands, and the band is how far the place is pushed past comfort.
It runs from [Warm](process_heat_warm.md), still within comfort,
through [Sweltering](process_heat_sweltering.md) and [Scorching](process_heat_scorching.md),
to [Lethal](process_heat_lethal.md), where the place cannot cool anyone and the heat kills.
Under a deepening front the load climbs, and the place slides up the bands
toward the heat its shade and air cannot temper.
Remove the place's shade and still air and there is no band, only the sun.

## Echo

Who found cool and who did not.
The place is read by its shade and its water.
The heat passes with the sun, but who it emptied the open of, and who it kept indoors, is known.
