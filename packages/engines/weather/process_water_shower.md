# Process: Water, Shower

## Taxonomy

[Water](process_water.md), the band this force is a level of.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: weather

## Initiated by

Rain or snow the place must now take.
The stirring band of [Water](process_water.md):
it wets through, turns the ground, runs from the roof and gathers at the edge.

## Direction

Down and then along, into the ground and the first low channels.
Toward cover, which everything in the place now wants.

## Lever

The place sheds it with effort.
The roof runs, the drain carries, the ground turns soft but holds.
Conduct bends to it: cover sought, the crossing timed, the surface trusted less.

## Echo

The place sorted into dry and soaked.
Who reached cover and who did not is known; the ground keeps the wet a while.
