# Process: Weather

## Taxonomy

Atmosphere.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: weather

## Initiated by

A mass of different air moves over the place.
The sky turns, the pressure shifts, and the conditions the place stands in begin to move.
Weather starts when the air over the place changes, not when anyone in it looks up.
The front is the threshold: it delivers the loads together, and the place is already inside them.

## Direction

Toward the place, on every face at once.
Five channels move the place's conditions, each its own load.
[Wind](process_wind.md), [Water](process_water.md), [Heat](process_heat.md), and [Cold](process_cold.md)
press on the place from outside; [Sky](process_sky.md) is the light it is under.
A sixth, [Season](process_season.md), runs underneath the other five, standing, never arriving.
The place does not undergo the loads evenly.
It undergoes each at the band that channel has reached.

## Lever

The atmospheric force against the place's own capacity to temper it.
The carrying force is the energy in the air; the resistance is what the place puts between that energy and the bodies inside it,
its walls and lee and shade and drains.
The gap has four bands, from a load the place tempers without effort
to a load it cannot temper at all.
Under a deepening front the load climbs, and the place slides up the bands
toward the weather its tempering cannot hold.
Remove the energy and there is no weather; remove the place's tempering and there is no felt weather, only air.

## Echo

Whether the place held or was overrun, recorded per channel.
The live load passes and the place settles back toward its [Season](process_season.md) floor.
What the weather wore, soaked, or bleached does not vanish:
it enters time as the residue that decay and erosion later inherit.
The loop closes when the air over the place settles, and opens again when the next front arrives.
