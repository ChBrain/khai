# Process: Season, Dormant

## Taxonomy

[Season](process_season.md), the standing state this tilt is a pole of.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: weather

## Initiated by

Nothing starts it.
The withdrawn pole of [Season](process_season.md):
the place at the low of its year, held there through the cold half, not turned to by any event.

## Direction

Down and in, the sun low and the day short, the place turned toward its own keeping.
Toward rest, toward the indoors, toward waiting the year out.

## Lever

It lowers the floor.
[Cold](process_cold.md) and dark [Sky](process_sky.md) start from nearer to hand, [Heat](process_heat.md) and light from further;
a front that would only chill a summer place can numb this one, because it began the day already low.

## Echo

The place at rest and closer to the cold.
Everything in it is nearer the shelter and the fire; the place is known this half-year as a thing endured, not used.
