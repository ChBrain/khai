# Process: Wind

## Taxonomy

[Weather](process_weather.md), the root this channel is a movement of.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: weather

## Initiated by

A pressure difference the place stands in the path of.
A channel of [Weather](process_weather.md):
moving air arrives across the place, and there is nothing between it and the place
but what the place puts there.

## Direction

Across the place, along its open lines, pressing on everything not fixed down.
Toward whatever will give first: the loose, the light, the standing.

## Lever

The force of the moving air against the place's shelter.
The load has four bands, and the band is the force the place stands in.
It runs from [Light](process_wind_light.md), a movement barely felt,
through [Fresh](process_wind_fresh.md) and [Gale](process_wind_gale.md),
to [Storm](process_wind_storm.md), where the air takes what the place cannot hold down.
Under a deepening front the force climbs, and the place slides up the bands
toward the wind it cannot shelter from.
Remove the shelter and there is no band, only air moving.

## Echo

What the wind moved, and what held.
The place is read by what it kept down and what it lost.
The exposure it showed, which faces gave and which stood, is now known.
