# Process: Heat, Scorching

## Taxonomy

[Heat](process_heat.md), the band this force is a level of.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: weather

## Initiated by

Heat past what the place easily cools.
The besieging band of [Heat](process_heat.md):
the surfaces burn to the touch, the air shimmers off the ground, the shade itself is warm.

## Direction

Into everything exposed, and into the body faster than it can shed.
Toward whatever water and shade the place still has, and hard against everyone without them.

## Lever

The place's cooling is at its limit.
The shade is loaded, the water matters, the exposed hour is dangerous.
Conduct is forced: the open abandoned in the worst of it, the plan moved to the cool ends of the day.

## Echo

What the exposure cost, and who paid it.
The place is read by who had shade and water and who did not; the heat marks the ones it caught.
