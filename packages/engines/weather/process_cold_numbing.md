# Process: Cold, Numbing

## Taxonomy

[Cold](process_cold.md), the band this force is a level of.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: weather

## Initiated by

Cold past what the place easily holds off.
The besieging band of [Cold](process_cold.md):
the extremities go first and stop reporting, the wind takes the warmth as fast as the body makes it.

## Direction

Deep, past the skin into the hands and feet, then inward toward the core.
Toward the fire and the shut door, hard against everyone without them.

## Lever

The place's warmth is at its limit.
The fire is loaded, the shelter matters, the exposed hour is dangerous.
Conduct is forced: the open abandoned, the fire kept, the exposed skin covered or lost.

## Echo

What the cold took while it could not be felt.
The place is read by who reached the fire in time; the numbed hand remembers, and the frost marks the ones it caught.
