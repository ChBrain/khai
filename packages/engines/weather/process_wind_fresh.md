# Process: Wind, Fresh

## Taxonomy

[Wind](process_wind.md), the band this force is a level of.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: weather

## Initiated by

Wind the place must now stand in.
The stirring band of [Wind](process_wind.md):
it fills the loose cloth, bends the branch, turns the corner into a push.

## Direction

Against everything with a face to it, steady, from one quarter.
Toward the windward side of every thing the place holds.

## Lever

The place tempers it with effort.
The lee holds and the open does not.
Conduct bends to it: the hat held, the door pulled, the walk angled into it.

## Echo

The place sorted into windward and lee.
Who stood in which, and who crossed the open anyway, is now known.
