# Process: Sky, Clear

## Taxonomy

[Sky](process_sky.md), the band this light is a level of.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: weather

## Initiated by

Light the place is shown plainly by.
The still band of [Sky](process_sky.md):
open sun or open star, the place lit to its far edge, every distance readable.

## Direction

Onto every surface, even and full, giving the place whole to the eye.
Toward the horizon, which the place can see all the way to.

## Lever

The place is seen without effort.
The one edge is glare: at full sun the light can flatten the near and hide what it overexposes.
Short of that there is nothing to temper; the place simply stands revealed.

## Echo

The place known to its edges.
Nothing is hidden by the light, and what is done in it is done in the open, seen.
