# Process: Sky, Veiled

## Taxonomy

[Sky](process_sky.md), the band this light is a level of.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: weather

## Initiated by

Light the place must now be read through.
The stirring band of [Sky](process_sky.md):
overcast, haze, dusk, the place dimmed and its far edges softening into grey.

## Direction

Flat across the place, taking the distance and the shadow with it.
Toward the near, which is all the place still clearly gives.

## Lever

The place is seen with effort.
The middle distance goes first, then the far; the near holds.
Conduct bends to it: the eye kept close, the far judged less, the crossing made on what can still be seen.

## Echo

The place shrunk to what the eye could reach.
What lay in the lost distance went unread, and whoever used the dimming knew it did.
