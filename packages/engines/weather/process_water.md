# Process: Water

## Taxonomy

[Weather](process_weather.md), the root this channel is a movement of.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: weather

## Initiated by

Water falling out of the air onto the place.
A channel of [Weather](process_weather.md):
rain, snow, hail, or the mist that settles, the sky giving up its water onto everything the place holds.

## Direction

Down, onto every open surface, then along the place's low lines.
Toward the ground, the drain, the hollow, and whatever will not shed it.

## Lever

The water arriving against the place's ability to shed it.
The load has four bands, and the band is how much the place must take.
It runs from [Damp](process_water_damp.md), which only settles,
through [Shower](process_water_shower.md) and [Downpour](process_water_downpour.md),
to [Deluge](process_water_deluge.md), where the place cannot shed it and the low lines fill.
Under a deepening front the fall climbs, and the place slides up the bands
toward the water it cannot drain.
Remove the place's shedding and there is no band, only water falling.

## Echo

What soaked in, what ran off, and what pooled where it should not.
The place is read by its low ground and its cover.
The standing water it could not clear is handed on to hydrology; the wet it took is its own.
