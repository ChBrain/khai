# Weather: Reference

## Line of Work

Weather as **process**: the atmospheric force a place undergoes, moving what the
place shows before anyone in it speaks, taken channel by channel and band by
band. The domain does not model the setting itself, which is the space engine's;
nor a persona's feeling about the weather, which is the mood engines'; nor the
abstract multi-decadal climate regime, which is the climate engine's. It models
the live load a place stands in, and how far past its own capacity to temper it
that load has climbed.

The spine is the graded intensity scale. Every operational weather scale, from
Beaufort's wind force through the Saffir-Simpson and Fujita category scales to
the wet-bulb heat and wind-chill thresholds, collapses to the same four-band
shape: a load the place tempers without effort, a load it tempers with effort, a
load that presses past ordinary tempering, and a load it cannot temper at all,
where the question stops being conduct and becomes survival. The engine gives
each transient load its own channel and its own ladder to that shape, and keeps
one standing channel, season, that never climbs but sets the floor the others
rise from, the way the language engine keeps thinking underneath its outward
channels.

## Restrictions

What the engine refuses to model, and to whom it delegates.

- **The setting itself (space)**: The engine models the moving of a place, never
  the place as such. Its layout, scale, affordances, territory, and standing
  induced mood are the space engine's; weather borrows the place's Shown face and
  animates it, and re-owns none of the furniture. Space builds the room; weather
  is the front on it.
- **The abstract climate regime (climate)**: The multi-decadal statistical
  regime, the place read as a climate class, is the climate engine's. Weather
  keeps the live front and the lived season-tilt of this year and hands off the
  regime. Season is where the two meet: the near-ground standing tilt is weather's;
  where it hardens into the statistical abstraction it is climate's.
- **A persona's feeling about the weather (mood, emotion), and pathetic
  fallacy**: How a load makes a persona feel, the grey that flattens a mood or the
  storm that thrills, is the persona's own engines'; weather delivers the load,
  not the feeling. And weather made to **mirror** a feeling, the storm that stands
  for the hero's rage, is refused outright: that is authored key, the play's
  pitch, not the place's force. Weather is the force that hits the place whether
  or not anyone in it feels anything, real and not a mirror.
- **The downstream events (hydrology, fire, erosion, soundscape)**: Weather owns
  the atmospheric load only. The flood the water becomes on the ground is
  hydrology's; the burn the drought-heat-and-wind feeds is fire's; the surface
  worn over seasons is erosion's; thunder as sound is soundscape's, while the
  lightning as light is the sky channel's. Weather is the live atmosphere, not its
  consequences in water, fire, stone, or sound.
- **The band itself is not engine content**: The engine declares the channels and
  their bands exist; the specific channel and band a place presents lives on the
  place, linked under its Shown chapter. The engine refuses to fix a place's
  weather for it.

## Encoding

Source to constraint, per file.

- **[weather](process_weather.md)** (the root): the loop of the whole
  weather-world, the front's load felt on every face of the place at once and
  filed per channel. Anchored by Ingold (the weather-world, felt before named) and
  Beaufort (force read as a graded load).
- **the four transient channels** ([wind](process_wind.md),
  [water](process_water.md), [heat](process_heat.md), [cold](process_cold.md)):
  each a separable load the place undergoes, on its own scale, wind by Beaufort,
  water by precipitation-rate, heat and cold by the Fanger, wet-bulb, and
  wind-chill thresholds. What one channel loads, another does not relieve.
- **[sky](process_sky.md)** (the luminous channel, three bands): the light the
  place is under, from shown-plainly to seen-no-further, capped at shut because
  past it there is simply no seeing. Anchored by the daylighting and bioclimatic
  tradition (Heschong, Olgyay).
- **the bands** (Light, Damp, Warm, Cool up to Storm, Deluge, Lethal, Killing):
  the four-band intensity shape, benign to survival, that every real weather scale
  collapses to. Anchored by Beaufort and the severe-end scales (Saffir-Simpson,
  Fujita).
- **[season](process_season.md)** (the standing channel, two states): the place's
  near-ground tilt on its year, never climbing the ladder, setting the floor each
  transient channel rises from. Anchored by Geiger; delegates the abstract regime
  to the climate engine.
- **the Lever's resistance side**: the place's own tempering, its walls, lee,
  shade, and drains, without which the load produces no felt weather. Anchored by
  Olgyay, Givoni, Banham, and Heschong.
