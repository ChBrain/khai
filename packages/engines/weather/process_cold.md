# Process: Cold

## Taxonomy

[Weather](process_weather.md), the root this channel is a movement of.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: weather

## Initiated by

The air pulling the place's warmth out of it.
A channel of [Weather](process_weather.md):
the cold the place stands in, taking heat from every surface and every body faster than they can make it,
sharpened wherever the wind can reach.

## Direction

Out of everything warm, from the extremities first, toward the cold air waiting for it.
Toward the fire, the wall, the lee, whatever the place keeps the warmth behind.

## Lever

The heat loss against the place's ability to keep anyone warm.
The load has four bands, and the band is how fast the place is losing its warmth.
It runs from [Cool](process_cold_cool.md), a bracing edge,
through [Biting](process_cold_biting.md) and [Numbing](process_cold_numbing.md),
to [Killing](process_cold_killing.md), where the place cannot keep anyone warm and exposure kills.
Under a deepening front the loss climbs, and the place slides up the bands
toward the cold its walls and fire cannot temper.
Remove the place's warmth and shelter and there is no band, only the cold.

## Echo

Who stayed warm and who did not.
The place is read by its fire and its walls.
The cold lifts with the front, but who it drove indoors, and who it caught outside, is known.
