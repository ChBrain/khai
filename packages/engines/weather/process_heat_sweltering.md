# Process: Heat, Sweltering

## Taxonomy

[Heat](process_heat.md), the band this force is a level of.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: weather

## Initiated by

Heat the place must now work against.
The stirring band of [Heat](process_heat.md):
the air heavy and still, the surfaces hot to sit on, the sweat no longer drying.

## Direction

Down onto the open and into the slow, thick air that will not move.
Toward the shade, sought now rather than chosen.

## Lever

The place cools with effort.
The shade holds and the sun does not; the still air gives nothing back.
Conduct bends to it: the pace slowed, the exertion put off, the cool side kept.

## Echo

The place sorted into shade and glare.
Who could keep the shade and who had to cross the open is known; the day is measured out in it.
