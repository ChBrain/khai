# Process: Wind, Light

## Taxonomy

[Wind](process_wind.md), the band this force is a level of.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: weather

## Initiated by

Air barely on the move.
The still band of [Wind](process_wind.md):
a breath across the place, felt on the skin and in the leaf, nothing more asked of the place.

## Direction

Toward being noticed and no further.
It stirs the light things and leaves the rest where they stand.

## Lever

The place tempers it without trying.
There is no edge here. The wind is present, not pressing,
and the place carries it the way it carries the light.

## Echo

A freshness, or nothing at all.
The place is unchanged. The day is simply moving through it.
