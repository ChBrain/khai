# Process: Water, Damp

## Taxonomy

[Water](process_water.md), the band this force is a level of.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: weather

## Initiated by

Water that settles rather than falls.
The still band of [Water](process_water.md):
drizzle, mist, the wet that arrives without a sound and beads on everything.

## Direction

Onto every upward face at once, even and slow.
Toward a sheen, not a stream.

## Lever

The place takes it without shedding anything.
There is no edge here. Nothing runs; the place only darkens a shade and holds the damp.

## Echo

A dampness that outlasts the sky.
The place is unchanged in shape, only closer, greyer, and slower to dry than the day around it.
