# Process: Countertransference

## Taxonomy

[Transference](process_transference.md), the reciprocal -- the responder transfers back in turn.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: transference

## Initiated by

The one being transferred onto has significant others of their own, and the encounter stirs them.
Where a persona transfers a past relationship onto a responder, the responder is not a blank screen: the transferrer, or the role they cast the responder into, resembles someone in the responder's own past, and their stored representation is triggered in turn. Freud named this in the clinic -- the analyst's own relationships stirred by the patient -- and the relational-self account generalizes it to any two people.
What enters is a second transference, running the other way, so both parties bring old bonds to the meeting.

## Direction

Toward the responder applying their own past to the one before them.
The responder feels toward the transferrer what their own significant other drew from them, and acts from that old bond -- the analyst drawn to rescue, to punish, to be admired, according to their own history rather than the patient's need. The two transferences interlock: each is partly relating to a ghost the other cannot see, and the relationship runs on two overlaid pasts.
The direction is a return volley, the responder's history aimed back at the one aiming theirs.

## Lever

The responder's own unresolved bonds meeting the role the transference casts them in. Whether countertransference fires turns on the responder having a live, charged relationship the encounter evokes -- and on their not noticing it stir; the more the transferrer pulls them into a familiar role, the more their own past answers. What sustains it is the same blindness that sustains any transference: the responder takes their reaction as a response to the person, not a revival of their history.
Recognized, countertransference becomes information -- a clue to what the transferrer evokes; unrecognized, it entangles the responder in the transferrer's script and their own at once.

## Echo

Two people relate through overlaid pasts, each transferring onto the other, the responder answering an old bond of their own.
Because the transference is now mutual, the relationship is doubly distorted and doubly informative -- in the clinic, the analyst's countertransference, once seen, reveals what the patient stirs in others.
That transference is a whole past relationship carried onto a present figure is the [root's](process_transference.md); this form owns its reciprocal, the responder's answering transference. The transferrer's own outgoing warmth or injury is the [positive](process_transference_positive.md) and [negative](process_transference_negative.md) forms; the standing bonds both draw on are the attachment engine's.
