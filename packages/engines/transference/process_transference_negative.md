# Process: Negative

## Taxonomy

[Transference](process_transference.md), the injury of the old bond carried over.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: transference

## Initiated by

The new figure resembles a significant other who hurt, failed, or frightened the persona.
What the resemblance activates is an injured relationship: the resentment of a harsh parent, the mistrust of a betrayer, the fear of a tyrant. The stored bond that comes online is a wounded one, and it arrives braced for the old harm to come again.
What enters is a readiness to distrust, resent, or fear the new person on the old relationship's account.

## Direction

Toward meeting the new figure with an unprovoked suspicion.
The persona directs onto the present person the hostility, mistrust, and fear owed the old one -- it reads their neutral acts as slights, expects the betrayal or the coldness the remembered figure dealt, and casts them in the role of the one who once wounded it. The new figure is put on trial for a crime another committed.
The direction is wary or hostile, and aimed past the real person at the injuring figure they stand in for.

## Lever

The wound of the stored bond meeting the resemblance that revives it. The sharper and less-healed the old injury, and the more the new figure evokes it, the more readily the suspicion transfers -- and the more it wrongs the person actually there, who is met as guilty before they act. What sustains it is that the new figure keeps failing to be the old one, yet is read as if they were.
Where the wariness once protected the persona it now isolates it; where the hostility provokes, it can pull the new figure into actually becoming the antagonist the transference expected.

## Echo

The new figure is met with a suspicion, resentment, or fear they did not provoke, borrowed from a wounding past.
Because the injury is transferred, it can sabotage a bond before it starts -- and it can flip from [positive](process_transference_positive.md) transference when an idealized figure disappoints, or provoke the very rejection it feared, the transference making itself come true.
That the injury is a whole past relationship carried onto a present figure by resemblance is the [root's](process_transference.md); the standing wary or fearful bond it is borrowed from is the attachment engine's. When the responder's own injured representation is triggered back, that is [countertransference](process_countertransference.md).
