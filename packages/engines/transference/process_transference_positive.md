# Process: Positive

## Taxonomy

[Transference](process_transference.md), the warmth of the old bond carried over.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: transference

## Initiated by

The new figure resembles a significant other the persona loved, trusted, or looked up to.
What the resemblance activates is a warm relationship: the affection for a cherished parent, the ardour of an early love, the trust in a protector. The stored bond that comes online is a fond one, and it arrives wanting to be resumed.
What enters is a readiness to love, idealize, or trust the new person on the old relationship's account.

## Direction

Toward meeting the new figure with an unearned warmth.
The persona directs onto the present person the affection, admiration, and trust owed the old one -- it idealizes them, wishes to please and be approved of by them, credits them with the virtues of the remembered figure, and hopes from them what it once hoped. In analysis this is the positive transference that lets the work proceed, the patient's trust in the analyst borrowed from an earlier bond.
The direction is warm, and aimed past the real person at the loved figure they stand in for.

## Lever

The fondness of the stored bond meeting the resemblance that revives it. The stronger and warmer the old relationship, and the more the new figure evokes it, the more freely the affection transfers -- and the more it overshoots the person actually there. What sustains it is the gap between the idealized figure and the ordinary human now carrying their glow.
Where the warmth serves the bond it can open trust and cooperation; where it blinds the persona to who the new figure really is, it sets up a fall when they prove merely themselves.

## Echo

The new figure is met with a fondness, trust, or idealization they did not earn, borrowed from a loved past.
Because the warmth is transferred, it can build a bond fast -- and can curdle into [negative](process_transference_negative.md) transference when the idealized figure disappoints, the love turning to the grievance of a hope betrayed.
That the warmth is a whole past relationship carried onto a present figure by resemblance is the [root's](process_transference.md); the standing bond it is borrowed from is the attachment engine's. When the responder's own fond representation is triggered back, that is [countertransference](process_countertransference.md).
