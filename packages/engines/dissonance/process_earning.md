# Process: Earning

## Taxonomy

[Dissonance](process_dissonance.md), the effort-justification form.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: dissonance

## Initiated by

The persona has spent great effort or suffered to reach a goal, and the cost clashes with a return that, coolly judged, was not worth it.
This is effort justification: having paid dearly for something, the persona resolves the dissonance between the price and the payoff by valuing the payoff more.
The suffering is done and cannot be refunded, so the goal is made to seem worth the suffering.

## Direction

Toward a goal prized in proportion to what it cost to reach, the severe initiation raising the prize.
Effort justification turns on unrecoverable cost: a harsh initiation, a painful trial, a long labor sits dissonant with a modest reward, and because the cost cannot be undone, the persona lifts the reward's value until the ledger balances -- the more severe the effort, the dearer the goal it bought.
The direction is the ennobling of the hard-won: what is suffered for is loved the more, not despite the suffering but because of it, the cost converted into worth.

## Lever

The unrecoverable cost of the effort, spent as an inflated value placed on the goal.
Earning runs on the sunk price as fixed and the goal's worth as movable: since the effort cannot be reclaimed, consistency is restored by raising the estimate of what it purchased, so that the prize grows to justify the price already paid.
The lever is the conversion of cost into value: because to hold the goal cheap would be to admit the suffering was wasted, the persona instead holds it dear, and the severity of the initiation becomes the measure of the goal's worth.

## Echo

What is dearly bought is dearly held, and the harder the road the more the destination is prized.
It is why the group with the brutal initiation is loved most by those who endured it, why the hard-won prize outshines the easy one, why "after all I went through, it had better be worth it" becomes "it was worth it" -- the cost justified by the value it is made to confer.
It is [induced compliance](process_induced_compliance.md) and [free choice](process_free_choice.md)'s counterpart, the justified cost where they are the counter-attitudinal act and the post-decisional spread. The severity-of-initiation result is its paradigm; earning owns the effort justification by which the value of a goal rises to match what was paid to reach it.
