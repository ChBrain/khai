# Process: Free Choice

## Taxonomy

[Dissonance](process_dissonance.md), the post-decisional form.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: dissonance

## Initiated by

The persona has chosen one good thing over another nearly as good, and the merits of the road not taken -- now forgone -- clash with the choice just made.
This is post-decisional dissonance: after a hard decision between close alternatives, the attractive features of the rejected option and the flaws of the chosen one are dissonant with the commitment.
The choice is made and cannot be unmade, so the discomfort is resolved by re-rating the options themselves.

## Direction

Toward a chosen option upgraded and a rejected one downgraded, the two spreading apart after the decision.
Free-choice dissonance works only once the choice is committed: before deciding, the options sit close; after, the persona revalues them so the picked one looks better and the passed one worse than they did a moment ago -- the "spreading of alternatives" that widens the gap the decision closed.
The direction is retrospective justification: the harder the choice and the closer the rivals, the greater the dissonance and the wider the spread, the persona convincing themselves they chose not a near-tie but a clear winner.

## Lever

The forgone value of the rejected alternative, spent as a re-rating that justifies the choice.
Free choice runs on the commitment as fixed and the valuations as movable: the attractive features of what was given up cannot be recovered, so instead the persona lowers their worth and raises the chosen one's, moving the cheap cognitions -- the ratings -- to fit the expensive one, the act of choosing.
The lever is the post-choice revaluation: because the decision is done, consistency is restored not by reversing it but by adjusting how good each option now seems, so the choice comes to look obviously right.

## Echo

The chosen grows more attractive and the rejected less, until the near-tie looks like no contest.
It is why the car just bought seems better than its rival the day after, why we talk ourselves into the rightness of a hard decision, why "I definitely made the right call" firms up after the call and not before -- the alternatives spread apart to justify the commitment.
It is [induced compliance](process_induced_compliance.md) and [earning](process_earning.md)'s counterpart, the post-decisional spread where they are the counter-attitudinal act and the justified cost. The free-choice paradigm and its spreading of alternatives are its warrant; free choice owns the revaluation of options that reduces the dissonance left by a hard decision.
