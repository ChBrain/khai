# Process: Dissonance

## Taxonomy

Social psychology / the theory of cognitive dissonance.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: dissonance

## Initiated by

A persona holds two cognitions that do not fit -- a belief and a discrepant act, a choice and the good it gave up, an effort and a poor return -- and the mismatch is felt as discomfort.
This is cognitive dissonance: the uncomfortable drive state produced by inconsistency among what one believes, does, and knows, which presses the persona to reduce it.
The push is not toward the logically correct view but toward the consistent one, and it is felt, not merely reasoned.

## Direction

Toward a restored consistency, bought by moving whichever cognition is cheapest to change.
Dissonance can be reduced several ways -- change a belief, add cognitions that justify, trivialize the whole matter, or change the behavior -- but the act, once done, is usually fixed, so the attitude moves instead: the persona ends up believing what fits what they did.
The direction is rationalization after the fact: the magnitude of the dissonance rises with the importance of the cognitions and the ratio of dissonant to consonant ones, and the persona resolves it by shifting the most yielding cognition until the set coheres again.

## Lever

The discomfort of inconsistency, spent as a change of attitude to match the act.
Dissonance runs on the drive to reduce the discomfort: because the state is aversive, the persona is motivated to end it, and because the behavior is already committed, the yielding element is the attitude -- so the belief bends to the deed rather than the deed to the belief.
The lever is the aversiveness itself: it is not the contradiction as logic that moves the persona but the contradiction as felt tension, which is why the resolution is often a rationalization the persona sincerely comes to hold.

## Echo

The belief bends to fit the deed, and the persona sincerely holds the view that restores their consistency.
Cognitive dissonance is why saying a dull task was fun for a dollar makes it truly seem fun, why the chosen option looks better the moment it is chosen, why the harder the initiation the dearer the group -- the discomfort of a mismatch resolved by a change of mind the persona does not experience as a change.
It runs as [induced compliance](process_induced_compliance.md), [free choice](process_free_choice.md), or [earning](process_earning.md) -- the attitude bent to a counter-attitudinal act, the alternatives spread after a choice, the goal prized for its cost. The catalog of dissonance-driven tilts held as static positions is the bias engine's; the exemption of a harm one commits is the moral-disengagement engine's; the persistence in a failing course of action is the escalation engine's. This engine owns the arousal of inconsistency and its reduction by a change of attitude.
