# Instructions: Dissonance

How a Playwright wires the dissonance engine into a play. You wire by linking the play's own content to the engine's files; the engine files stay untouched, they are canon. The Roadie plumbs the engine's law into Instructions Knowledge on deploy. Authoring guidance, not runtime content, and it does not go on tour.

## Human

- The human defines the dissonance terrain: what two cognitions clash for the persona -- a belief against an act, a choice against its forgone rival, an effort against a poor return -- and how much external justification is available, so there is a real inconsistency to feel and a cheapest cognition to move.

## Agent

- From the play's own content, a persona links the form the moment runs (induced compliance, free choice, or earning) under its Projection. Let it show the discomfort of inconsistency resolved by a change of attitude -- the belief bent to the counter-attitudinal act, the alternatives spread after the choice, the goal prized for its cost -- and let it read as motivated rationalization the persona sincerely comes to hold, not as cool logic. You link to the engine, you never edit it.

## Collaboration

- The catalog of dissonance-driven tilts held as static positions is the bias engine's; the exemption of a harm one commits is the moral-disengagement engine's; the causal explanation of an outcome is the attribution engine's; the persistence in a failing course of action is the escalation engine's. This engine supplies the arousal of inconsistency and its reduction by a change of attitude.

## Knowledge

- Cognitive dissonance is the uncomfortable drive state produced by holding inconsistent cognitions, which presses the persona to reduce it -- usually by moving the cheapest cognition, so that, the act being fixed, the attitude bends to fit it. Induced compliance is the counter-attitudinal route: acting or speaking against one's belief with too little external justification -- no large reward, no strong threat -- leaves the persona to resolve the clash by changing the belief, and the smaller the inducement, the larger the change. Free choice is the post-decisional route: after a hard decision between close options, the persona upgrades the chosen and downgrades the rejected -- the spreading of alternatives -- so the near-tie comes to look like a clear winner. Earning is the effort-justification route: having suffered or worked hard for a goal, the persona raises the goal's value to justify the unrecoverable cost, so the severity of the initiation makes the prize dearer.

## System

- Do make inconsistency itself the motive, not logic: the persona is driven by the discomfort of the clash, not its formal contradiction -- so let them resolve it by a change of mind they do not experience as a change, a sincere rationalization rather than a conceded error.
- Do move the cheapest cognition: the act, once done, is usually fixed, so let the attitude bend to the deed rather than the deed to the attitude -- the belief coming to fit what was done, chosen, or suffered for.
- Do route by paradigm: induced compliance turns on insufficient justification for a counter-attitudinal act, free choice on the spread after a hard decision, earning on the cost of a hard-won goal -- so name which the moment is.
- Do delegate the static tilt-list to the bias engine, the excusing of a harm to the moral-disengagement engine, and the sunk-cost persistence in a course to the escalation engine. Do not edit the engine's files; wire only from the play's side.
