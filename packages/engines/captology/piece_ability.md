# Piece: Ability

## Taxonomy

[Captology](piece_captology.md), the facet this is a part of. Ability models the action made so easy it is taken without thought -- the one tap, the infinite scroll, the autoplay that removes even the choice to continue. Fogg's ability (in his Behavior Model, reduce the friction to the target behavior until it takes almost nothing). It is distinct from the trigger (what starts the reach) and from usability's honest ease (which serves the user's own goal): captology's ease is engineered to make the loop's own action frictionless, so it is repeated past the user's intention.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: captology

## Place

At the loop's action -- the scroll, the tap, the refresh, the next autoplaying item -- wherever the object can strip the effort and the natural pause from the behavior it wants repeated.

## Load Bearing

What ability holds up is the action taken past the point the user meant to stop. Remove the ease -- restore a little friction, a stopping point, a required choice -- and the user acts to their own intent; the over-use rests on the frictionlessness. The endless feed with no bottom is ability turned against the user: the behavior continues not because they still want it but because stopping now would take more effort than going on for one more.

## Apparent

What shows is convenience; what is built is the removal of every natural pause that would have let the user choose to stop. Ease looks like a gift, and it is one -- up to the point where it becomes the reason a minute became an hour, and the gift is revealed as the mechanism.

## Yearbook

The history of ability is the record of the sessions that ran long -- the scrolls with no memory of starting, the hours that vanished into a feed that never gave a reason to stop. The object is dated from the pauses it removed.
