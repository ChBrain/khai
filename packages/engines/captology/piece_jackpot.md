# Piece: Jackpot

## Taxonomy

[Captology](piece_captology.md), the facet this is a part of. Jackpot models the variable, unpredictable payoff that keeps the user pulling -- the refresh that might bring something good, the new like, the rare find among the ordinary, dealt on no fixed schedule so the next pull always might be the one. Eyal's variable reward, the intermittent reinforcement of Skinner's variable-ratio schedule (the slot machine). It is distinct from usability's feedback (a certain, informative response) and from a fixed reward (which sates): the jackpot's power is the uncertainty, which does not sate but sharpens. The payoff need not be large; it needs to be unsure.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: captology

## Place

At the moment of payoff -- the pull-to-refresh, the open of the app, the check of the feed, the spin -- wherever the object can make the reward uncertain in size and in timing.

## Load Bearing

What the jackpot holds up is the compulsion to check again. A certain reward is taken and set down; an uncertain one is pulled and pulled, because a pull that pays nothing reads not as "nothing here" but as "not yet." Remove the variability -- make the reward fixed and predictable -- and the checking collapses to need; the compulsion rests entirely on the maybe. This is the slot machine's whole engine, wired into a thing that is not a slot machine and does not look like one.

## Apparent

What shows is sometimes a delight and sometimes nothing; what is built is the schedule that makes the nothing feel like a near-miss rather than an empty result. The payoff looks like luck; the variability is the design, tuned to the ratio that most keeps the hand coming back.

## Yearbook

The history of a jackpot is the record of the pulls it drew -- the refreshes on nothing, the opens that found only the ordinary and reached again anyway, the checking that outlasted any reason for it. The object is dated from the uncertainty it dealt.
