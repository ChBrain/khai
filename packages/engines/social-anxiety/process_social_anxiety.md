# Process: Social Anxiety

## Taxonomy

Social anxiety as process: the chronic fear of negative evaluation by others -- the persistent appraisal of the social arena as an evaluative trap where scrutiny is inevitable, inadequacy is exposed, and rejection carries existential weight.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: social-anxiety

## Initiated by

The prospect, presence, or memory of social exposure where the persona believes they are being scrutinized or evaluated.
The engine begins not with an external threat or a physical hazard, but with an evaluative appraisal. The persona enters an interpersonal context carrying deeply held conditional assumptions: that one must appear composed, articulate, and impressive; that any sign of anxiety or imperfection will be noticed; and that failure to meet this assumed standard will result in devastating loss of standing, ridicule, or social abandonment. The threat is not the people themselves, but their perceived judgment. The persona views the audience as critical, exacting, and observant, while viewing the self as deficient, fragile, and bound to be exposed.

## Direction

From an anticipated evaluative hazard, through inward self-monitoring during exposure, toward a retrospective post-mortem that cements the conviction of failure.
The engine breaks the maintenance cycle into three temporal phases, following Clark & Wells' cognitive architecture. [Anticipatory processing](process_anticipatory_processing.md) occurs prior to exposure: the persona rehearses catastrophic scenarios, predicts humiliation, and prepares defensive strategies. [Self-focused attention](process_self_focused_attention.md) occurs during the encounter: attention shifts inward toward interoceptive signs of anxiety and an observer-perspective mental image of the self as a social object, while deploying safety behaviors that block genuine interaction. [Post-event processing](process_post_event_processing.md) occurs after leaving: the interaction is reviewed through an anxious filter that selectively highlights perceived flaws, ignores neutral or reassuring signals, and confirms the persona's inadequacy. Together, these movements form a self-perpetuating loop where each encounter feeds the fear of the next.

## Lever

Whether the persona relies on safety behaviors to survive the encounter.
Everything the engine stages hinges on the covert maneuvers the persona uses to conceal their anxiety and protect themselves from scrutiny. These behaviors -- rehearsing words before speaking, gripping a glass to hide shaking hands, avoiding direct eye contact, speaking in brief neutral sentences, or checking exits -- are adopted to prevent catastrophe. Yet they carry the engine's central paradox: they consume the persona's working memory, make natural conversation impossible, prevent the persona from discovering that the room was benign, and often make the persona appear aloof, unfriendly, or stiff to others.

**The twist is that the defenses deployed to avoid rejection actively produce social distance.** When the persona retreats behind careful silence, avoids gaze, or keeps physical distance to conceal trembling, the audience does not see fear: they see disinterest, arrogance, or coldness. The persona's protective armor invites the very coolness, exclusion, or polite withdrawal that they dreaded. The persona reads this cool reception not as a reaction to their defensive wall, but as definitive proof of their original premise: that others find them inadequate and unwelcome.

## Echo

The maintaining loop runs across three movements: [anticipatory processing](process_anticipatory_processing.md), which dreads and rehearses the exposure beforehand; [self-focused attention](process_self_focused_attention.md), which monitors the self as a social object and executes safety behaviors during the event; and [post-event processing](process_post_event_processing.md), which reconstructs the past encounter as a confirmation of failure. The acute fright of an immediate physical danger belongs to `fear`; the transient mortification of a real revealed social breach belongs to `embarrassment`; and the global self-condemnation of personal worthlessness belongs to `shame`. This engine owns only the chronic, perseverative surveillance of negative evaluation -- authored as character architecture for dramatic production, never diagnosed onto a living human.
