# Process: Post-Event Processing

## Taxonomy

[Social anxiety](process_social_anxiety.md), the distortion after the event -- the detailed retrospective post-mortem that reconstructs a finished encounter as a confirmed social failure.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: social-anxiety

## Initiated by

The social situation concludes and the persona is left alone.
The encounter has ended, the persona has stepped away, left the room, or returned home, yet the event refuses to close. In the safety of solitude, post-event processing initiates. Rather than feeling relief that the ordeal is over, the persona's mind replays the interaction in granular, agonizing detail. The raw material for this review is not an objective recording of what transpired, but the internal sensations of anxiety and self-consciousness experienced during the event. The persona uses how anxious they felt as the baseline measurement of how terribly they performed.

## Direction

From the end of social exposure, through selective negative reconstruction of the interaction, toward consolidated failure that primes the next anticipatory cycle.
The movement runs backward across the memory of the encounter, repeatedly revisiting key moments. Ambiguous glances from others are reinterpreted as suppressed contempt; conversational lulls become damning proof of boring the other person; minor verbal slips are inflated into unforgivable blunders. Any positive feedback, genuine laughter, or warmth from others is dismissed as pity, polite deception, or unmerited luck. Over successive hours or days of rumination, memory of the event becomes progressively more negative and distorted, settling into an enduring conviction of public humiliation.

## Lever

Whether the persona accepts the biased post-mortem as historical fact.
The lever governs whether the reconstructed memory crystallizes into core self-belief. Because memory is malleable, repeated post-event rumination alters the retrospective representation of the encounter: the persona eventually "remembers" the event as far worse than it actually was. Stage the quiet devastation of this review: lying awake at night replaying five seconds of conversation, cringing visibly at a perceived misstep that nobody else noticed, and deciding that future invitations must be turned down.

**The twist is that the solitary review is far more brutal than any real audience ever was.** In the actual room, people were distracted, forgiving, or occupied with themselves. In the solitary post-mortem, the imagined audience is omniscient, cruel, and unforgiving. The persona inflicts upon themselves in private the exact humiliation they labored so desperately to avoid in public, and the conclusion reached -- _I failed again_ -- directly feeds the [anticipatory processing](process_anticipatory_processing.md) that will dread the next encounter.

## Echo

Post-event processing is the concluding phase of [social anxiety](process_social_anxiety.md), following [anticipatory processing](process_anticipatory_processing.md) and [self-focused attention](process_self_focused_attention.md). The objective cognitive review of performance belongs to `evaluation`; ruminative brooding over depressed mood belongs to `rumination`; and moral remorse over wrongdoing belongs to `guilt`. This movement owns the selective, anxiety-driven post-mortem review of social interaction that converts an ordinary encounter into a confirmed catastrophe of negative evaluation.
