# Process: Anticipatory Processing

## Taxonomy

[Social anxiety](process_social_anxiety.md), the failure before the event -- the detailed mental simulation of catastrophic evaluation and social failure in advance of an encounter.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: social-anxiety

## Initiated by

An upcoming social situation appears on the horizon.
Days or hours before the event, the persona begins to focus on the prospective exposure. The entry condition is not an ongoing conversation, but the mere awareness of a future encounter: a presentation, a dinner, an introduction, or an informal gathering. Instead of preparing functionally, the persona's cognitive resources are commandeered by intrusive imagery and worst-case forecasting. Past social missteps are recalled with vivid embarrassment, and the upcoming audience is endowed with relentless critical standards. The persona approaches the event with their nervous system already primed for catastrophe.

## Direction

From the scheduling of a future social encounter, through obsessive mental simulation of failure, toward avoidance or arrived exhaustion.
The movement runs forward in time from distant prospect to immediate threshold. As the event approaches, anticipatory processing accelerates: the persona visualizes themselves stumbling over words, trembling, blushing, or running out of things to say. They construct rigid emergency scripts, prepare excuses for early departure, or contemplate outright cancellation. If the situation cannot be avoided, the persona enters the room in a state of high autonomic arousal and cognitive depletion, already convinced that failure is imminent.

## Lever

Whether the persona avoids the situation or attends in an armored state.
The lever controls whether the persona yields to the urge to flee before arrival. If they cancel or withdraw, immediate relief reinforces the avoidance, but personal freedom shrinks and shame deepens. If they force themselves to attend, they do so burdened by catastrophic expectations and elaborate defensive preparations. Stage the persona's extensive pre-event labor: ironed clothes inspected for sweat marks, rehearsed opening lines repeated in the mirror, and an agonizing internal calculus over whether to attend at all.

**The twist is that the rehearsal of catastrophe creates the exact state that makes normal engagement impossible.** By spending hours dwelling on worst-case scenarios, the persona arrives at the gathering exhausted, hypersensitive to threat, and deprived of spontaneous ease. The meticulous preparation does not produce confidence; it produces rigid self-consciousness that makes their worst fears feel like foregone conclusions.

## Echo

Anticipatory processing is the pre-event phase of [social anxiety](process_social_anxiety.md), operating ahead of [self-focused attention](process_self_focused_attention.md) during the encounter and [post-event processing](process_post_event_processing.md) afterward. The simple scheduling of tasks belongs to `planning`; the general dread of an unknown future belongs to `anxiety`; and the conditioned avoidance of a specific physical phobic stimulus belongs to `fear`. This movement owns specifically the pre-event mental simulation of negative evaluation and public humiliation before a perceived critical audience.
