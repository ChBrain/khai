# Process: Self-Focused Attention

## Taxonomy

[Social anxiety](process_social_anxiety.md), the distortion during the event -- the inward shift of attention toward monitoring the self as an evaluated social object.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: social-anxiety

## Initiated by

The persona steps into the social situation and perceives scrutiny.
Upon crossing the threshold into social interaction, an immediate attentional pivot occurs. Attention shifts away from the external world, the task, and the conversation partner, turning sharply inward toward the persona's own internal state. Somatic sensations of autonomic activation -- increased pulse, warmth in the face, tension in the throat, trembling hands -- become the primary focal objects of conscious awareness. The persona treats these private physiological experiences as public, observable facts: if they feel hot and tense, they assume they must look conspicuously flushed, awkward, and ridiculous to everyone in the room.

## Direction

From entry into social exposure, through internal somatic monitoring and construction of an observer self-image, toward protective safety behaviors.
The movement runs throughout the duration of the social encounter. The persona constructs an internalized mental representation of how they must appear to an outside observer. This self-image is distorted and grotesque, magnifications of internal tremor or hesitation projected outward as visible incompetence. To prevent this imagined image from being exposed, the persona deploys safety behaviors: checking their posture, monitoring their vocal tone, gripping objects tightly to suppress shaking, or withholding opinions to avoid saying something foolish.

## Lever

Whether the persona can disengage from self-monitoring and observe the room.
The lever governs the allocation of attention between internal surveillance and external reality. Because the persona's working memory is consumed by monitoring their own performance, they miss subtle external cues: smiles of encouragement, nods of agreement, or normal conversational pauses. They misinterpret neutral expressions as disapproval and take absence of effusive praise as evidence of disdain. Stage this lever as a divided mind: the persona is present in body, but wholly occupied in running an internal audit of their hands, voice, and facial expression.

**The twist is that the self-monitoring and safety behaviors actively disrupt the performance.** By attempting to manually control things that normally happen automatically -- smiling, breathing, speaking smoothly, gesturing naturally -- the persona makes their behavior stiff, unnatural, and halting. The safety behaviors adopted to hide awkwardness are the very things that produce it, validating the persona's terror that they are incapable of natural interaction.

## Echo

Self-focused attention is the in-situation core of [social anxiety](process_social_anxiety.md), preceded by [anticipatory processing](process_anticipatory_processing.md) and followed by [post-event processing](process_post_event_processing.md). Pure interoceptive awareness of visceral sensations belongs to `body`; focused attentional concentration on a task belongs to `attention`; and self-reflection on one's identity belongs to `self`. This movement owns the inward capture of attention during social exposure, where internal anxious sensations are taken as visible evidence of catastrophic failure before an audience.
