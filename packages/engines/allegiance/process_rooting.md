# Process: Rooting

## Taxonomy

[Allegiance](process_allegiance.md), the disposition itself -- sympathy for the liked, antipathy for the disliked.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: allegiance

## Initiated by

Having sorted the characters, the audience takes their side, or against it.
This is the affective disposition itself (Zillmann; Smith's allegiance): sympathy and the wish to see the liked character prosper, antipathy and the wish to see the disliked one fall -- the felt partisanship that follows the verdict.
It is the audience made a supporter and an opponent, no longer neutral toward anyone it has judged.

## Direction

Toward the liked character's good and the disliked character's ill.
Rooting points the audience's wishes: for the sympathetic character it wants safety, success, and reward, and dreads their peril; for the antipathetic one it wants exposure, defeat, and comeuppance, and resents their success.
Its direction is the two-signed pull -- drawn toward the fortunes of the loved, set against the fortunes of the loathed.

## Lever

What rooting spends -- the audience's indifference to who wins.
The disposition converts the moral verdict into stakes: because the spectator now wants particular characters to win and others to lose, the outcomes acquire a weight they would not have for a neutral viewer, and the story gains the power to satisfy or frustrate.
A production holds this lever by making the sympathy and antipathy real -- the audience genuinely for someone and against someone -- so the plot's turns land as hope and dread rather than as mere events, and a character worth rooting for anchors the whole.

## Echo

The audience is partisan -- for some, against others -- and every coming outcome now carries the charge of the side it falls on.
This is what makes a story grip: the plot threatens and rewards people the audience has come to want things for.
The capacity to feel into a character's inner state is the empathy engine's, and one can empathize with a disliked character or root for one without feeling into them at all; rooting is the evaluative taking-of-sides, sympathy and antipathy as partisanship, which the [payoff](process_payoff.md) then cashes when the fortunes fall.
