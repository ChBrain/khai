# Process: Verdict

## Taxonomy

[Allegiance](process_allegiance.md), the moral monitoring -- the sorting of characters into liked and disliked.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: allegiance

## Initiated by

The audience watches what the characters do, and judges them for it.
This is the moral-monitoring component of allegiance (Zillmann; Raney): the spectator as an untiring moral monitor, continuously appraising the characters' conduct and sorting them into the deserving and the undeserving, the worthy of good fortune and the worthy of bad.
It is the reading that must come before the rooting -- the audience deciding who is on the right side.

## Direction

Toward the character's conduct, weighed against the audience's morality.
The verdict runs the spectator's own moral sense over the people of the tale: kindness, courage, and fairness earn approval; cruelty, cowardice, and betrayal earn condemnation, and each character is placed on a scale from beloved to detested by the sum of what it is shown to do.
Its direction is the moral sorting, the audience's judgment settling on each character before their fortunes come due.

## Lever

What the verdict spends -- the possibility of watching characters without judging them.
An audience cannot help but morally monitor; the verdict spends the neutral view, so the characters arrive already sorted into those the spectator wants to prosper and those it wants to suffer, and this sorting -- not the plot -- is what makes the coming outcomes matter.
A production holds this lever by giving characters legible moral conduct -- deeds the audience can weigh -- so the sorting is earned, and by knowing that a character's morality, more than its charm, sets which way the audience will lean.

## Echo

The characters are sorted, and the sides are set -- the audience now has people it is for and people it is against.
This is allegiance's ground: the disposition and its payoff both rest on the verdict, since one can only root for a character one has judged worthy and enjoy a fall one has judged deserved.
The general mechanism of appraising an act as right or wrong, on which foundation, is the moral-judgment engine's; the verdict is that mechanism turned by an audience onto the characters of a story, the reception-side sorting that the [rooting](process_rooting.md) then follows.
