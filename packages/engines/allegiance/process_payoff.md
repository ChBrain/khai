# Process: Payoff

## Taxonomy

[Allegiance](process_allegiance.md), the outcome-contingent enjoyment -- the fortunes matching the disposition.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: allegiance

## Initiated by

The story delivers its outcomes, and the audience's enjoyment rises or falls by whom they befall.
This is the payoff of affective disposition theory (Zillmann): the spectator enjoys the tale most when the liked characters prosper and the disliked ones get their comeuppance, and is dissatisfied when the story lets the deserving lose and the undeserving win.
It is the cashing of the disposition -- the moral sorting paid out in the pleasure of poetic justice or the frustration of its absence.

## Direction

Toward the fit between the fortune and the disposition.
The payoff measures each outcome against the sides the audience has taken: the liked character's triumph and the disliked one's fall are enjoyed as just and satisfying; the liked one's ruin and the disliked one's reward land as wrong, unjust, and unpleasant, however well-made the plot.
Its direction is congruence -- enjoyment where outcome matches disposition, dissatisfaction where it violates it.

## Lever

What the payoff spends -- the idea that an outcome is enjoyed for itself.
The same event carries opposite value by whom it lands on: a death, a victory, a fall is savored or mourned entirely by the audience's disposition toward the one it befalls, so the payoff spends the notion of a neutral outcome, revealing that the audience's pleasure was in the justice, not the event.
A production holds this lever by choosing when to grant poetic justice and when to withhold it -- the satisfying comeuppance, or the tragic loss of the loved and triumph of the loathed that leaves the audience bereft, its disposition betrayed on purpose.

## Echo

The outcomes are enjoyed or resented by their fit to the sides taken -- and this is why happy endings for the deserving satisfy, and why a story that lets evil win must mean to unsettle.
It is the whole point of the taking of sides: the disposition exists to be paid out, in justice served or justice denied.
The specific pleasure at a disliked character's misfortune is the schadenfreude engine's, which the payoff recruits when the villain falls; the tragic dread and pity when the loved are lost is the tragic composite's; the payoff owns the contingency itself -- that enjoyment tracks the match between fortune and disposition, the reward of the [rooting](process_rooting.md) the [verdict](process_verdict.md) set.
