# Process: Allegiance

## Taxonomy

Narrative reception / the audience's moral bond to the characters of a story.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: allegiance

## Initiated by

An audience meets the characters of a story and takes sides.
This is allegiance (Zillmann's affective disposition theory; Raney; Murray Smith): the spectator morally monitors the people of the tale, sorts them into liked and disliked by how they conduct themselves, and thereafter cares which way their fortunes fall.
It is the engine of rooting interest -- why an audience wants the hero to win and the villain to lose, and why a story with no one to root for holds no one.

## Direction

Toward the characters, sorted -- and toward the outcomes their fortunes will bring.
Allegiance turns the audience from a neutral observer into a partisan: having judged the characters, the spectator hopes for the liked and against the disliked, and reads every turn of the plot for what it means for the people it has taken sides on.
Its direction is the taking of sides and the stake it creates -- the audience bound, from outside the story, to the fates of those inside it.

## Lever

What allegiance spends -- the audience's neutrality toward the story's people.
A tale is merely watched until the spectator sorts its characters; allegiance spends that detachment, making the audience partisan, so that the same event -- a character's triumph, a character's fall -- is enjoyed or dreaded entirely by which character it befalls and how the audience has judged them.
It is the moral reading, not the event alone, that sets the stake: the villain's victory and the hero's, formally identical, land as opposite for the audience that has taken sides.

## Echo

The characters are judged, sided-with, and their fortunes made to matter -- and the story is enjoyed as those fortunes match the disposition or dreaded as they threaten it.
This is why an audience cares: without allegiance the plot is only events; with it, the events carry the weight of the sides the audience has taken.
It runs as [verdict](process_verdict.md), [rooting](process_rooting.md), and [payoff](process_payoff.md). A persona's own exit-cost bond to a group inside the story is the loyalty engine's; the general mechanism of judging conduct against a moral standard is the moral-judgment engine's; the entry into the story world as a whole is the transportation engine's; the joy at a disliked character's misfortune is the schadenfreude engine's, which the payoff recruits. This engine owns the audience's moral bond to the people of a tale.
