# Process: Enhancement

## Taxonomy

[Social Facilitation](process_social_facilitation.md), the lifting form.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: social-facilitation

## Initiated by

The persona performs a dominant, well-learned, or simple task while others watch or work alongside, and the presence sharpens it -- the arousal driving the already-strong response harder and truer.
This is enhancement: the boost, the audience or co-actor lifting a performance the persona already commands.
Where the right answer is the ready one, being seen makes it readier.

## Direction

Toward better performance on the task the persona already has in hand.
Enhancement rides the arousal of presence onto a dominant response: because the strongest, most practised reaction is also the correct one for an easy or mastered task, driving that response harder improves the result -- faster, stronger, more fluent under the eyes of others than alone.
The direction is a lift proportioned to mastery: the more thoroughly the task is learned, the more surely the presence sharpens rather than spoils it.

## Lever

The arousal of presence, applied to a response already right.
Enhancement spends the drive that being observed or accompanied produces, and spends it well: arousal strengthens the dominant response, and when that response is the one the task needs, the strengthening is pure gain -- the sprinter driven faster in the pack, the expert crisper before the crowd, the simple sum done quicker when watched.
The lever is the fit between the aroused dominant response and the task's demand: presence helps exactly when the ready answer is the right one.

## Echo

The persona does better for being seen, the easy task lifted by the audience.
It is the bright face of social facilitation -- the reason performers and athletes and the well-rehearsed rise to a crowd, the reason competition and company quicken practised work -- the presence of others turning the already-capable sharper.
It is [choking](process_choking.md)'s mirror, the same arousal helping where choking has it hurting, split by whether the task is mastered or not; and the opposite of [loafing](process_loafing.md), where presence thins effort instead of sharpening it. The raw arousal itself is the body engine's; this owns the lift that arousal gives a dominant response before others.
