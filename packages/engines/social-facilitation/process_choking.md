# Process: Choking

## Taxonomy

[Social Facilitation](process_social_facilitation.md), the spoiling form.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: social-facilitation

## Initiated by

The persona performs a non-dominant, complex, or novel task while others watch, and the presence spoils it -- the arousal driving the wrong, ready response over the right, effortful one, and the felt evaluation tightening the failure.
This is choking: the impairment, the audience unmaking a performance the persona has not yet secured.
Where the correct answer is not the ready one, being seen makes the ready wrong one likelier.

## Direction

Toward worse performance on the task the persona has not yet mastered.
Choking rides the arousal of presence onto a dominant response that is, for a hard or novel task, the wrong one: driving the practised-but-inapt reaction crowds out the careful, deliberate one the task needs, so the learner fumbles, the complex calculation slips, the delicate skill seizes -- worse watched than alone.
The direction is a spoiling proportioned to difficulty and scrutiny: the less mastered the task and the more evaluative the audience, the more surely the presence undoes it.

## Lever

The arousal and evaluation of presence, applied to a response that is wrong.
Choking spends the same drive enhancement does, but ill: arousal strengthens the dominant response, and when that response is not what the task needs, the strengthening is loss -- and the sharpened sense of being judged adds self-conscious monitoring that further disrupts a skill better left automatic.
The lever is the mismatch between the aroused dominant response and the task's demand, deepened by evaluation: presence hurts exactly when the ready answer is the wrong one, and hurts more when the eyes are appraising.

## Echo

The persona does worse for being seen, the hard task spoiled by the audience.
It is the dark face of social facilitation -- the reason the novice falters at the recital, the athlete misses the pressured shot, the interviewee's mind blanks under the panel's gaze -- the presence of others turning the not-yet-capable worse, and the more so the more it counts.
It is [enhancement](process_enhancement.md)'s mirror, the same arousal hurting where enhancement has it helping, split by whether the task is mastered or not. The anxiety the evaluation stirs is the apprehension engine's, and the self-conscious monitoring that disrupts the skill shades toward the executive-function engine's; this owns the spoiling that arousal gives a non-dominant response before others.
