# Process: Loafing

## Taxonomy

[Social Facilitation](process_social_facilitation.md), the slackening form.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: social-facilitation

## Initiated by

The persona's contribution is pooled with others' into a group product where no single effort can be told apart, and their effort drops -- less pulled on the rope, less shouted in the chorus, because no one hand is seen.
This is loafing: the reversal, presence thinning effort where it cannot be traced to the person.
Submerged and unaccountable, the persona gives less than they would alone.

## Direction

Toward reduced individual effort as the contribution is diluted in the group.
Loafing tracks the loss of accountability: when output is collective and inputs are indistinguishable, the persona's exertion is neither credited nor blamed to them, and effort slackens -- each of many pulling less than each of few, the per-person force falling as the group grows.
The direction is a withdrawal proportioned to invisibility: the larger the group and the more submerged the individual, the more effort thins.

## Lever

The diffusion of accountability, spent as withdrawn effort.
Loafing runs on the reversal of the very accountability that drives enhancement and choking: where being individually seen sharpens effort, being individually unseen releases it, so pooling contributions removes the observation that would have driven them and effort settles toward the least that goes unnoticed.
The lever is the untraceable contribution: because the persona's share cannot be identified, the incentive that presence usually supplies is absent, and the slack is safe.

## Echo

The group's per-person effort falls, each member coasting on the unseen.
It is the reversal at the heart of social facilitation -- the reason committees underperform their members, the reason the eighth hand on the rope pulls softest, the reason the free rider rides -- presence subtracting effort where it dilutes accountability rather than adding it where it concentrates.
It is the opposite of [enhancement](process_enhancement.md) and [choking](process_choking.md): where those ride accountability up, loafing rides it down, effort released by the very submersion that presence elsewhere would drive. The group's development and shared pattern over time is the group engine's; this owns the individual's effort-drop when pooled unaccountably among others.
