# Instructions: Social Facilitation

How a Playwright wires the social-facilitation engine into a play. You wire by linking the play's own content to the engine's files; the engine files stay untouched, they are canon. The Roadie plumbs the engine's law into Instructions Knowledge on deploy. Authoring guidance, not runtime content, and it does not go on tour.

## Human

- The human defines the social-facilitation terrain: what the persona is performing and how well they have mastered it, who is present to watch or work alongside and whether they can evaluate, and whether the persona's effort stands alone or is pooled unseen in a group -- so the presence has a task to lift or spoil and an accountability to drive or diffuse.

## Agent

- From the play's own content, a persona links the form the moment runs (enhancement, choking, or loafing) under its Projection. Let it show others' presence moving the persona's own performance -- the easy task sharpened, the hard one spoiled, the pooled one slackened -- and let it read as an effect on effort and skill, not as the persona matching an opinion or obeying a demand. You link to the engine, you never edit it.

## Collaboration

- The mass of bodies as an environment pressing on everyone at once is the crowd engine's; social facilitation works from as little as one observer and lands on the individual. The matching of one's position to others' is the conformity engine's; the group's development over time is the group engine's. The raw arousal the presence produces is the body engine's, and the anxiety that evaluation recruits is the apprehension engine's. This engine supplies the effect of others' presence on the individual's performance itself.

## Knowledge

- Social facilitation is the effect of others' mere presence on a persona's own performance: being watched or worked alongside raises arousal, which strengthens the dominant response. Enhancement is the lift -- on a dominant, well-learned, or simple task, the arousal sharpens performance, the expert crisper before a crowd, the cyclist faster in a race. Choking is the spoiling -- on a non-dominant, complex, or novel task, that same arousal drives the wrong ready response and the felt evaluation disrupts the skill, the learner fumbling under watching eyes, the pressured shot missed. Loafing is the reversal -- when the persona's contribution is pooled and unaccountable in a group, effort drops rather than rises, each hand pulling less on the rope because no hand is seen.

## System

- Do split the presence effect by mastery: presence lifts what the persona has mastered and spoils what they have not -- so let the same audience sharpen the expert's simple task and rattle the novice's hard one, the arousal helping the right ready response and hurting the wrong one.
- Do let evaluation deepen the effect: being judged, not merely seen, drives it harder -- so let an appraising audience lift the sure performer more and choke the unsure one worse than an indifferent one would.
- Do reverse it when effort is pooled and unseen: submerged unaccountably in a group, the persona gives less, not more -- so let the per-person effort thin as the group grows and the single contribution disappears.
- Do delegate the mass of bodies to the crowd engine and the matching of positions to the conformity engine. Do not edit the engine's files; wire only from the play's side.
