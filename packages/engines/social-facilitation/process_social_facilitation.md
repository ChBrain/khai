# Process: Social Facilitation

## Taxonomy

Social influence / the effect of others' presence on individual performance.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: social-facilitation

## Initiated by

A persona performs while others are present -- watching them, or working the same task alongside them -- and their performance changes for it, before anyone has said a word.
This is social facilitation: the mere presence of others as a force on the individual's arousal, effort, and skill.
The same persona at the same task does it differently watched than alone, moved by an audience they need not even interact with.

## Direction

Toward a sharpened easy task, a spoiled hard one, and a slackened pooled one.
Social facilitation routes the presence three ways: on a dominant, well-learned response, the arousal others bring lifts performance; on a non-dominant, complex one, that same arousal spoils it; and where the persona's effort is pooled unseen in a group, it drops instead of rises.
The direction is set by the task and the accountability, not by the persona's wish: presence helps where the strong response is right, hurts where it is wrong, and thins where no single effort can be seen.

## Lever

The presence of others, spent as arousal and as accountability.
What social facilitation spends is the charge that being observed or accompanied produces: the presence raises drive, which strengthens whatever response is dominant -- a boon on the easy, a curse on the hard -- and the felt possibility of being evaluated sharpens the effect; while the diffusion of that same accountability across a group withdraws it, and effort falls.
The lever is others as audience and as cover: watched and answerable, the persona is driven; submerged and unaccountable, the persona is released.

## Echo

The performance is moved by who is there to see it, apart from what they do.
Social facilitation is why the runner is faster in a race and the novice worse at the recital, why the expert shines before a crowd that would rattle the beginner, why the rope is pulled with less force by each of eight than by one -- the presence of others reaches the individual's effort directly, through arousal and accountability, not through persuasion.
It runs as [enhancement](process_enhancement.md), [choking](process_choking.md), or [loafing](process_loafing.md) -- the easy lifted, the hard spoiled, the pooled slackened. The mass of bodies as an environment pressing on everyone at once is the crowd engine's; the matching of one's position to others' is the conformity engine's; the raw arousal the presence produces is the body engine's; the anxiety that evaluation recruits is the apprehension engine's. This engine owns the effect of others' presence on the individual's performance itself.
