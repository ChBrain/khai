# Instructions: Extended Self

How a Playwright wires the extended-self engine into a play. You wire by linking the play's own content to the engine's files; the engine files stay untouched, they are canon. The Roadie plumbs the engine's law into Instructions Knowledge on deploy. Authoring guidance, not runtime content, and it does not go on tour.

## Human

- The human sets what the object is, whose self it carries, and what part of that self it holds -- a tool, a garment, a keepsake, a room's contents that a persona has come to feel as itself rather than as mere property.

## Agent

- From the play's own content, place the extended self: link the possessor stance under the holding persona's Projection, and link the process it is in -- appropriation while the object is being taken into the self, dispossession where it is being torn out. Keep the object's specifics (what it is, whose, what it commemorates) in the play's content, not in the engine. Let the object be treated as self across plots: guarded past its price, its loss wounding beyond the cost of replacement. You link to the engine, you never edit it.

## Collaboration

- The persona's present self-story is the identity engine's; the bond to a place is place-attachment's; the possession made inalienable to a line is the heirloom engine's, made sacred to a collective the totem engine's, made an inscribed record the document engine's; the mere over-valuation of what one owns is the bias engine's endowment member; the grief or shame of a loss is the grief and shame engines'. This engine supplies the object experienced as a part of the self -- the possessor who holds it, and the appropriation and dispossession that carry self in and out.

## Knowledge

- The extended self is a possession experienced as a part of the self -- an ordinary object into which a persona's identity has extended, so that having it enlarges the self and losing it diminishes it (Belk; James's material self). It is owned like a thing but felt like a limb: non-fungible to its owner even where the market would swap it, storing memory, capability, status, or continuity. An object becomes self through appropriation -- controlling and mastering it, creating or shaping it, knowing it intimately, investing time and energy in it -- and stops being self through dispossession, when it is lost, stolen, broken, or given up, which shrinks the self it held. Read a persona by which objects it holds as itself, how they became self, and what their loss would take away.

## System

- Do link the possessor under Projection and name the process the object is in; do let the object read as self -- kept past its price, its loss felt as a diminishment of the person, not merely a gap in the inventory.
- Do let appropriation run through real routes -- use and mastery, making, knowing, the sediment of time -- so the object earns its charge of self; and let the same investment be what its later loss costs.
- Do let a chosen parting go easier when the persona first draws its self back out of the object (a divestment), and an involuntary loss cut sharp and unmourned-for; keep the self-diminishment the engine's own and hand the felt grief to the grief engine.
- Do keep it the ordinary possession-as-self: do not price the object as a plain good (that is the money or bias engines'), do not model the inalienable-to-a-line object (heirloom's), the sacred-to-a-collective object (totem's), or the inscribed record (document's). Do not edit the engine's files; wire only from the play's side.
