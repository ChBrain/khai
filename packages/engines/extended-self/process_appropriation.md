# Process: Appropriation

## Taxonomy

[Extended Self](piece_extended_self.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: extended-self

## Initiated by

The taking of an object into the self. The process opens whenever a persona begins to hold a thing as more than a thing -- on acquiring it, making it, mastering its use, or simply living with it long enough that it gathers the persona's history. It is the crossing by which a possession stops being property the persona has and becomes a part of the self the persona is (Belk; Csikszentmihalyi & Rochberg-Halton).

## Direction

From thing to self -- inward, the object drawn across the boundary of the person. What carries it are the routes by which possessions become self: controlling and mastering the object so that acting through it is acting as oneself; creating or shaping it so that it holds one's own labor; knowing it intimately, its quirks and history; and investing time and psychic energy in it until the investment is what the object is worth. The more of these run, and the more the persona puts in, the further the object crosses.

## Lever

Investment: the object becomes self in proportion to what the persona pours into it. A thing merely bought and untouched stays property; a thing used, worked, known, and lived-with takes on the self of the one who did the using -- so appropriation spends the persona's own energy and history, trading them for a self stored outside the body. The lever is that what one puts into an object one gets back as identity: the labor, attention, and time become the object's charge of self.

## Echo

The object is now a part of the [possessor](position_possessor.md), and the self is enlarged by it -- more capable, more expressed, more continuous for having it. What was an item in the inventory is now a support the identity leans on, and the persona will guard it as itself. But the same crossing that enlarges the self also exposes it: the more fully the object has become self, the more its later [dispossession](process_dispossession.md) will take away, so appropriation is also the quiet setting of a future wound.
