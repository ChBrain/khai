# Process: Uncoupling

## Taxonomy

[Drift](process_drift.md), the mechanism this is a form of.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: drift

## Initiated by

A local fix that works.
The uncoupling form of [Drift](process_drift.md):
practice adapts to the situation in front of it, the adaptation works, and practice quietly comes loose from the rule it was meant to follow.

## Direction

Away from the written procedure, toward whatever the local conditions reward.
Each unit drifts on its own, so the gap opens differently everywhere and matches nowhere; the rule and the practice stop meeting, and nothing yet needs them to.

## Lever

Local success against global coherence.
The adaptation is right for its own corner and wrong for the system, and nothing feels the mismatch while the corners run alone.
The resistance is any moment that forces the drifted parts to act as one; until that comes, the uncoupling pays everywhere it happens.

## Echo

A set of practices that each work alone and fail in combination.
When the parts are finally coupled -- the crisis, the handoff, the joint operation -- the accumulated gaps meet at once, and the failure looks sudden though every step of it was slow.
