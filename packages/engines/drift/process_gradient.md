# Process: Gradient

## Taxonomy

[Drift](process_drift.md), the mechanism this is a form of.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: drift

## Initiated by

A standing pressure with nothing pushing back.
The gradient form of [Drift](process_drift.md):
a constant pull -- cost, speed, effort, workload -- tilts the ground the process runs on, and with no counter-pressure the process migrates down the tilt.

## Direction

Down the gradient, toward the boundary of safe operation, one efficient step at a time.
Toward the edge, because every step toward it is rewarded and the edge itself is unmarked; the process is drawn, not driven.

## Lever

The pull toward efficiency against the margin to the boundary.
Each step trades a little margin for a real, immediate gain; the margin is invisible and the gain is counted, so the trade always looks good until the margin is spent.
The resistance is whatever still prices the margin; where only the gain is on the books, the slope runs to the edge.

## Echo

A process running at the edge it was never steered to, drawn there by a gradient no one set out to follow.
The boundary is found only by crossing it; until then the drift reads as ordinary good performance, and the last safe step is indistinguishable from the first unsafe one.
