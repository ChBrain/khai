# Process: Normalization

## Taxonomy

[Drift](process_drift.md), the mechanism this is a form of.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: drift

## Initiated by

An exception tolerated once.
The normalizing form of [Drift](process_drift.md):
a deviation is allowed to pass without correction, and in passing it becomes the new measure of normal.

## Direction

Toward a moving yardstick.
Each tolerated exception resets what counts as acceptable, so the next deviation is judged against the last one and not against the origin.
The standard follows the practice down, and no single step ever looks like a fall.

## Lever

The past that did not break against the record that would say it should have.
Each time the deviation caused no harm it is read as proof the deviation is safe, and the margin it ate is booked as room that was never needed.
The resistance is any measure that still holds the exception against the original line; where only the last step is the reference, there is none.

## Echo

A norm no one would have chosen at the start, reached by steps each of which looked reasonable against the step before.
The record shows only reasonable calls; the drift is in the sequence, not in any one of them, and cannot be found by reviewing them one at a time.
