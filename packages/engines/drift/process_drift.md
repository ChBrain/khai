# Process: Drift

## Taxonomy

Deviation.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: drift

## Initiated by

The first exception that goes uncorrected.
Drift begins wherever a running process has a baseline that can be let slide -- a standard, a practice, a tolerance -- and the first small deviation passes without being pulled back.
Nothing decides it and nothing announces it; the absence of a correction is the whole threshold.
Once one deviation is tolerated, the baseline that would have caught the next one has already moved.

## Direction

The baseline slides, one unremarked step at a time, and never turns.
Toward wherever the local pressure points -- easier, cheaper, faster, quieter -- and away from the standard no one is still watching.
It has no moment; it is all slope.
Three mechanisms carry the slide: the yardstick moves ([normalization](process_normalization.md)),
practice comes loose from the rule ([uncoupling](process_uncoupling.md)),
and a standing pressure tilts the floor ([gradient](process_gradient.md)).

## Lever

The small local gain of each step against whatever still checks the baseline against its origin.
Every exception saves something real and costs nothing anyone can see, so the step always pays;
the only resistance is a measure that still reaches back to the original standard.
Where nothing re-measures against the origin, the resistance is gone and the slide runs free.
Remove the local gain and there is no drift; remove the checking and there is nothing to drift from.

## Echo

What changed while no one decided.
The baseline is now somewhere no one chose, and because the yardstick moved with it, the distance is invisible from inside: it reads as normal.
The gap is inherited by whatever finally measures against the origin -- an audit, an accident, an outside eye.
Drift is only ever seen from outside, or after it breaks.
