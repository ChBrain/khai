# Position: The Identity Reading

## Taxonomy

[By the Divergence](position_divergence_risk.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: risk

## Has

A hazard that has become a marker of which side somebody is on.
Not all of them are: most hazards are argued about on their merits. But a handful get recruited into a standing division, and after that a persona's sizing of them predicts their group far better than it predicts anything about the hazard.
Kahan's finding is the sharp one -- on these hazards, greater science literacy and numeracy go with **wider** polarisation, not narrower, because a persona who is better at evidence is better at reaching the answer their side holds.

## Orders

Size it in a way that keeps you in the room.
This is not stupidity and it is not dishonesty. A persona's ability to affect a national hazard is nil and their stake in their own community is total, so holding the position their people hold is the individually sensible thing to do, and being persuaded is individually expensive.

## Loses

The usefulness of being right.
Since accepting a finding costs standing and rejecting it costs nothing, a persona's incentive runs against accuracy on exactly the hazards where accuracy matters most collectively -- and the better their reasoning, the more efficiently it serves the position rather than the question.
Nobody in this has to be acting in bad faith for the whole to behave as though everybody is.

## Drives

On the loss of useful rightness:
changes what accepting would cost -- messengers from inside the persona's own world, framings that do not demand a surrender, a version of the response their side could author,
or supplies more evidence, and hands the most capable people on both sides better materials.
