# Position: Uncontrollable

## Taxonomy

[By the Dread](position_dread_risk.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: risk

## Has

A hazard the persona cannot act to avoid.
No skill, care, or attention on their part changes their exposure -- the air, the water supply, the aircraft they are a passenger in, the decision taken elsewhere.
It is the single heaviest feature on the axis, and it is why the same activity is sized quite differently by the person at the controls and the person beside them.

## Orders

Size it large, and place the fear where the control is.
A persona with no lever reaches for the only one available, which is objection: they turn to whoever does hold the lever and press there, so an uncontrollable hazard reliably becomes a political question rather than a technical one.

## Loses

Everything the persona could have done instead.
Because the sizing runs on control rather than harm, effort flows to hazards where control is absent and away from ones where it is present -- so a persona will fight a distant installation while continuing to do the far more dangerous thing they do every day, and be entirely consistent by their own measure.

## Drives

On the loss of the available action:
takes back some control, however partial -- a monitor they can read, a switch they can throw, a say in the siting -- and finds the sizing drops further than the actual exposure did,
or is told the hazard is small and remains without a lever, which leaves the objection as the only move and hardens it.
