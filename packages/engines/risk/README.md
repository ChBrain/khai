# Risk

Risk as position: the size a hazard has for a persona, which is not its death rate -- ask how many a thing kills and the answer is roughly right, ask how risky it is and the answer moves, because risk means how dreadful and how unfamiliar the harm is rather than how often it comes -- read on three axes: the dread (uncontrollable, catastrophic, involuntary, inequitable, irreversible, inherited by the unborn), the unknown (unobservable, delayed, novel, unsettled in the science, unwitting in the exposed), and the divergence (the affect heuristic linking risk and benefit backwards, the signal value of a small event, the amplification that carries it, the identity reading that sorts hazards by side, and the expert's count, which is a different quantity rather than a corrected one) -- with the twist that a hazard's size cannot be corrected by its own numbers, and on a contested hazard the personas best equipped to weigh evidence polarise hardest (Slovic; Fischhoff; Starr; Finucane; Kasperson; Kahan).

This engine is defined by its [manifest](package.json), which the canon renders as the WIRES card. The manifest is the single source of truth; this README is generated, not edited by hand.
