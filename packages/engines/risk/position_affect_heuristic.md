# Position: The Affect Heuristic

## Taxonomy

[By the Divergence](position_divergence_risk.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: risk

## Has

Risk and benefit linked backwards in the persona's head.
In the world the two tend to run together -- activities worth doing are frequently worth some danger -- but in judgment they are **inversely** related: what feels good is judged safe, what feels bad is judged dangerous, and the two readings come from one underlying feeling rather than from two assessments.
The demonstration is that the link is causal in both directions. Tell a persona a technology's benefits are greater and their judgment of its risk falls, though they were told nothing about its risk.

## Orders

Consult the feeling, and read both numbers off it.
Under time pressure the inversion strengthens, which is the signature of a heuristic doing its ordinary job: one affective tag is cheaper to carry than two independent estimates, and it is right often enough.

## Loses

The ability to hold a thing as both good and dangerous.
The persona cannot easily maintain that something is genuinely valuable **and** genuinely risky, so the honest position on most real technologies is the one hardest for them to occupy -- and any argument for the benefits will silently lower the perceived risk, including when the arguer did not intend that.

## Drives

On the loss of the mixed reading:
splits the two questions apart deliberately -- asks for the benefit case and the risk case at separate times, from separate people -- and gets an assessment the heuristic cannot collapse,
or argues benefit and risk together, and moves both whichever way the feeling went.
