# Position: Unwitting

## Taxonomy

[By the Unknown](position_unknown_risk.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: risk

## Has

A hazard whose exposed do not know they are exposed.
The additive nobody was told about, the surveillance nobody was informed of, the leak upstream -- and, crucially, the persona learning about it afterwards, which is the form the feature usually takes.

## Orders

Size the concealment separately, and above the harm.
Finding out late is its own event: the persona is now sizing two things, the hazard and the fact that they were not told, and the second sets what they will believe about the first from then on.

## Loses

Every subsequent assurance from the same source.
The concealment is unfalsifiable evidence about the discloser rather than about the hazard, so it does not merely raise this estimate, it disqualifies the instrument the persona would use to lower any future one.
An institution that has withheld once has spent the ability to reassure, and cannot earn it back with the very statements that are now in question.

## Drives

On the loss of future assurance:
discloses early and against interest, repeatedly, until the disclosures are the record rather than the exception, which is slow and cannot be hurried,
or manages the disclosure, and finds that every later statement is read as the managed version of something worse.
