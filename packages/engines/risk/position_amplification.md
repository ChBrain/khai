# Position: Amplification

## Taxonomy

[By the Divergence](position_divergence_risk.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: risk

## Has

The signal carried, magnified, or muffled by everything it passes through.
Kasperson's ripple: an event moves through reporting, professional bodies, activist groups, agencies, and conversation, and each station amplifies or attenuates it -- so the risk that reaches a persona has been through a chain none of whose links is the hazard.

## Orders

Follow the stations, not the hazard.
The size a hazard has in a population is predicted better by how many stations carried it and how they framed it than by anything about the hazard, and the same is true in reverse: hazards nothing carries are attenuated into invisibility however much harm they do.

## Loses

The connection between where the harm falls and where the cost lands.
Amplified consequences travel: property values, insurance, tourism, and stigma settle on places and industries adjacent to the hazard rather than on the exposed, so the people who pay for an amplified risk are frequently not the people at risk from it.

## Drives

On the loss of that connection:
treats the ripple as part of the hazard and plans for it -- who will carry it, what they will say, who pays for the secondary damage,
or attends only to the physical harm, and is surprised by a bill from three stations away.
