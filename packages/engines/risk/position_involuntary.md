# Position: Involuntary

## Taxonomy

[By the Dread](position_dread_risk.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: risk

## Has

A hazard the persona did not agree to run.
Starr's asymmetry, and it is very large: a persona will tolerate a voluntarily accepted risk hundreds of times greater than one imposed on them for the same benefit.
Consent, not magnitude, is doing most of the work.

## Orders

Ask who agreed, and treat the answer as most of the question.
An imposed hazard is a wrong before it is a danger, so the persona's response runs on the grammar of permission -- who was asked, who decided, what were they given for it -- and no revision of the magnitude touches any of that.

## Loses

The distinction between being endangered and being disregarded.
Because consent carries the sizing, an imposition of trivial magnitude is felt as a serious hazard, and a persona objecting to it will describe the objection in the language of danger because that is the language available -- which makes them sound like they have the numbers wrong when what they have is a grievance.

## Drives

On the loss of that distinction:
answers the consent question directly -- asks, compensates, gives a veto, opens the siting -- and often finds the danger argument evaporates with it,
or argues magnitude against a persona who was never talking about magnitude, and confirms that nobody intended to ask them.
