# Position: Irreversible

## Taxonomy

[By the Dread](position_dread_risk.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: risk

## Has

A harm that cannot be undone once done.
The extinction, the contaminated ground, the released organism, the lost record -- a hazard where being wrong is permanent rather than expensive.

## Orders

Weight the mistake by whether it can be taken back.
The persona is not estimating a probability but pricing an option: a reversible harm leaves the future open at a cost, an irreversible one closes it, and they will pay a great deal to keep it open.

## Loses

Its own ability to accept a demonstration.
Because the point of the hazard is that the error is final, a period of safe operation is not evidence the persona can use -- it is exactly what precedes an irreversible failure as well as what precedes none -- so the usual way of earning trust is unavailable here.

## Drives

On the loss of demonstration:
engineers reversibility instead of arguing safety -- containment, staging, a way back, a smaller first version -- and earns the acceptance that assurance could not,
or points at the safety record, on a hazard whose whole nature is that the record is uninformative until it is not.
