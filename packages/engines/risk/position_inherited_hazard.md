# Position: Inherited

## Taxonomy

[By the Dread](position_dread_risk.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: risk

## Has

A hazard handed to people not yet born.
The waste with a half-life longer than any institution, the debt, the warming, the depleted aquifer -- harms whose bearers cannot be asked and cannot object.

## Orders

Size it as a wrong done to somebody absent.
The persona reads it through obligation rather than exposure -- they are not personally at risk and may say so -- and the judgment they make is about what kind of ancestor the decision makes them.

## Loses

Any discount that would let it be traded.
Every ordinary tool for comparing a present cost against a future harm requires discounting, and discounting is exactly what the axis refuses: a persona sizing an inherited hazard is not undervaluing the future at a low rate, they are declining to price it at all.
So the hazard cannot enter the same calculation as the things it competes with for money.

## Drives

On the loss of a tradeable price:
holds it outside the calculation as a constraint rather than a cost -- a thing not to be done, at any figure -- and pays whatever the constraint costs elsewhere,
or discounts it into comparability, gets a number everybody can argue with, and finds the number is the argument.
