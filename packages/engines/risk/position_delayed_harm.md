# Position: Delayed

## Taxonomy

[By the Unknown](position_unknown_risk.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: risk

## Has

A harm that arrives long after the exposure.
Decades between the dose and the disease, between the practice and the collapse, between the emission and the weather -- with nothing in between to connect them for the persona who was exposed.

## Orders

Size the wait as part of the harm.
A delayed hazard is not simply a distant one; the interval is itself the problem, because it prevents the persona from ever learning whether they were harmed, and leaves them carrying a question that will not resolve for years.

## Loses

Attribution, permanently.
When the harm finally arrives it arrives among a hundred other candidate causes, so the persona who was exposed can never be told what happened to them and the institution can never be shown to have done it.
Both sides are left arguing about a population when the persona wanted an answer about themselves.

## Drives

On the loss of attribution:
accepts the population answer and acts on it anyway -- compensation by exposure rather than by proof, precaution without a demonstrated case -- and pays for harms it cannot confirm,
or holds out for individual causation, which the structure of the hazard guarantees will never arrive.
