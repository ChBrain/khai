# Position: Unsettled

## Taxonomy

[By the Unknown](position_unknown_risk.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: risk

## Has

A hazard the people who study it disagree about.
Not merely uncertain -- genuinely contested, with credentialled experts on both sides, and no body a persona can consult that will simply tell them.

## Orders

Size it by the disagreement itself.
A persona who cannot evaluate the evidence evaluates the state of the argument instead, and reasonably: visible expert conflict is information, and what it says is that nobody knows, which on a hazard means the upper end is live.

## Loses

Any use for further study in the short run.
More research on a contested hazard predictably produces more findings on both sides before it produces convergence, so the honest process of settling the question looks, from outside, exactly like the question failing to settle -- and it is trivially easy for an interested party to keep it looking that way.

## Drives

On the loss of short-run resolution:
says plainly where the weight of evidence sits and how confident that is, accepting that a stated confidence can be wrong in public,
or waits for consensus before saying anything, and leaves the persona with a disagreement as their only reading.
