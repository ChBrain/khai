# Position: The Count

## Taxonomy

[By the Divergence](position_divergence_risk.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: risk

## Has

The expert's sizing, which is a different quantity.
Asked to rank hazards by risk, experts produce something close to expected annual fatalities; laypeople produce dread and unknown. The gap is not that one group knows the numbers and the other does not -- asked directly for fatality rates, laypeople answer roughly in line with the statistics.
They mean different things by the word.

## Orders

Count the deaths, and treat the count as the risk.
The count is the right instrument for allocating a budget across many hazards, is the only one that divides and compares, and is what any large protective decision must eventually be made in.

## Loses

Everything the other axes measure.
The count cannot see consent, control, equity, reversibility, or what the harm would be like -- and those are not decorations on a risk, they are most of what a persona means by one.
An expert who calls the persona wrong has made a substantive claim about what should count, without noticing that they made it, and will be heard as saying that none of the things the persona cares about are real.

## Drives

On the loss of the qualities:
puts the count and the qualities side by side as two readings of one hazard and argues the trade in the open, which is slower and forfeits the authority of a single number,
or presents the count as **the** risk, and finds that the argument continues in a language it declined to speak.
