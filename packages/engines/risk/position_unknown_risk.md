# Position: By the Unknown

## Taxonomy

[Risk](position_risk.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: risk

## Has

The second quality: how far the hazard lies outside what can be seen, felt, or settled.
Five features load together -- the harm is [unobservable](position_unobservable.md), it is [delayed](position_delayed_harm.md), the hazard is [novel](position_novel_hazard.md), the science on it is [unsettled](position_unsettled_science.md), and those exposed are [unwitting](position_unwitting.md).
Where dread asks what it would be like, the unknown asks whether the persona would even find out.

## Orders

Ask what the persona could learn on their own, and how long it would take.
The axis sizes by epistemic exposure: a hazard a persona can smell, see coming, and test for is small on this axis whatever it does to them, and one that acts silently over decades is large even where the eventual harm is modest.

## Loses

The ability to be reassured by an absence of evidence.
On an unknown hazard, nothing observed so far is informative -- the harm was always going to be invisible and late -- so the reassurance a persona is offered has exactly the form the danger predicted, and the more emphatic it is the more it resembles what a real hazard would look like early.
Which means a genuinely safe novel thing and a genuinely dangerous one produce the same evidence for years.

## Drives

On the loss of reassurance:
buys observability instead of assurance -- monitoring, testing, disclosure, an independent body with the standing to look -- and accepts the cost and the delay,
or asserts safety, and finds each assertion read as confirmation that nobody knows.
