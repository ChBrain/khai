# Position: By the Dread

## Taxonomy

[Risk](position_risk.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: risk

## Has

The first and larger of the two qualities: how terrible the harm would be to undergo, and how little the persona could do about it.
Six features load together and move as one -- the hazard is [uncontrollable](position_uncontrollable.md), it is [catastrophic](position_catastrophic.md) rather than spread thin, it is [involuntary](position_involuntary.md), it is [inequitable](position_inequitable.md) in who bears it against who gains, it is [irreversible](position_irreversible.md), and it is [inherited](position_inherited_hazard.md) by people not yet born.
They are not independent judgments a persona makes separately. They arrive together, which is why the axis behaves like one quantity.

## Orders

Ask what it would be like, and who gets to decide.
The dread axis is answered by imagining the harm from inside rather than counting it from outside, and the two questions that dominate are whether the persona can act to avoid it and whether they agreed to be exposed at all.

## Loses

Any sense of proportion between hazards.
Because dread is a quality rather than a quantity, it does not divide: a dreaded hazard that kills a few is sized above a mundane one that kills many, and no amount of arithmetic reconciles them, since the persona is not comparing amounts.
So a society allocating protection by dread will overspend where the harm is terrible and rare and underspend where it is ordinary and constant -- and will feel, correctly by its own measure, that it has got the priorities right.

## Drives

On the loss of proportion:
holds the count and the dread as two separate readings and argues about the trade openly, which is uncomfortable and honest,
or lets dread do the allocating, and protects most thoroughly against what frightens rather than against what kills.
