# Position: Signal Value

## Taxonomy

[By the Divergence](position_divergence_risk.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: risk

## Has

A small event that stands for a large one.
Slovic's observation: a mishap is not only what it did, it is what it says about what could happen -- and the saying is worth far more than the doing in a hazard that is poorly understood or badly controlled.
A minor failure at a nuclear plant and a fatal coach crash have wildly different signal values, and the difference has nothing to do with the body count.

## Orders

Read the event as news about the system.
A persona treats a small failure in an unfamiliar, poorly controlled domain as a first sample from a distribution they cannot see, so its meaning is the distribution rather than the sample -- which is a sound inference and enormously leveraged.

## Loses

The proportionality of its own response.
Because the signal, not the harm, drives the reaction, the costs that follow a high-signal event -- shutdowns, litigation, regulation, abandonment -- routinely exceed the event's direct damage by orders of magnitude, and are borne by people the event never touched.

## Drives

On the loss of proportionality:
manages the signal as seriously as the harm -- explains the mechanism, shows the control, makes the small failure legible as a small failure -- and treats that work as part of operating the hazard,
or answers with the casualty figure, which is the one thing that was never the point.
