# Position: By the Divergence

## Taxonomy

[Risk](position_risk.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: risk

## Has

What happens where the sizing and the count part company.
Five standing forms: the [affect heuristic](position_affect_heuristic.md), which links risk and benefit backwards; [signal value](position_signal_value.md), which makes a small event stand for a large one; [amplification](position_amplification.md), which carries the signal through institutions and lands harms nowhere near the hazard; the [identity reading](position_identity_reading.md), which sorts which hazards a persona sizes large by which side they are on; and [the count](position_expert_count.md), the expert's own sizing, which is a different quantity rather than a corrected one.
This is the axis that decides whether a disagreement about a hazard can be settled.

## Orders

Ask what the disagreement is actually about before supplying evidence for it.
Two personas who size a hazard differently are frequently not disagreeing about frequency at all -- they are disagreeing about who chose, who benefits, whether the source can be trusted, and what sizing it large would say about them -- and evidence aimed at the frequency lands on none of that.

## Loses

The possibility of a neutral fact.
Once a hazard has become a marker of allegiance, no finding about it arrives as merely a finding: it comes with an implication about who was right, so accepting it costs something socially and rejecting it costs nothing.
And there is no way to hand somebody an unmarked version -- the marking is not in the evidence, it is in the room.

## Drives

On the loss of a neutral fact:
changes who is carrying the evidence and what accepting it would cost -- messengers from inside the persona's own world, framings that do not require a surrender -- and treats the social price as the real obstacle it is,
or keeps sending the finding, and watches it get filed by which side it helps.
