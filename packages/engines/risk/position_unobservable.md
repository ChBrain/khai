# Position: Unobservable

## Taxonomy

[By the Unknown](position_unknown_risk.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: risk

## Has

A hazard with no sensory signature.
Radiation, most contaminants, a pathogen before symptoms, a field: nothing to see, smell, hear, or feel, and no way for a persona to know from inside their own body whether they have been exposed.

## Orders

Size it by what could be there rather than by what is.
With no signal to bound the estimate, the sizing floats: it is set by whatever the persona is told and by whom, which makes the hazard's size a function of the trustworthiness of the messenger rather than of the hazard.

## Loses

The persona's own senses as an instrument.
Ordinarily a persona calibrates against experience -- they have been near this and are fine -- and an unobservable hazard removes that, leaving them dependent on an institution for a fact about their own body.
That dependence is the actual grievance in most disputes about invisible hazards, and it is not a dispute about parts per million.

## Drives

On the loss of their own senses:
hands the instrument over -- a meter they hold, a test they can request, a reading they can check against somebody else's -- and converts a matter of trust back into a matter of fact,
or asks to be believed, and makes the hazard's size depend on a relationship that is usually already strained.
