# Position: Novel

## Taxonomy

[By the Unknown](position_unknown_risk.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: risk

## Has

A hazard nobody has lived alongside yet.
New in kind rather than new to this persona: a technology, a substance, a practice with no accumulated experience anywhere, so there is no body of ordinary familiarity for the estimate to rest on.

## Orders

Size the unfamiliar high, and let time bring it down.
The novelty premium is real and it decays: a hazard the same in every measurable respect is sized smaller after twenty years of living near it, which is neither irrational nor informative -- familiarity is genuine evidence, and it is also just familiarity.

## Loses

The distinction between having been safe and having been lucky.
Time reduces the sizing whether or not anything was learned, so a hazard that has quietly not yet failed becomes indistinguishable from one that has been shown to be safe -- and the sizing falls fastest in the personas who live closest to it.

## Drives

On the loss of that distinction:
keeps the estimate keyed to evidence rather than to elapsed time -- monitoring that continues after the novelty wears off, review that does not relax with familiarity -- and pays to stay alert to something nobody is frightened of any more,
or lets familiarity do the work, and arrives at acceptance without ever having found anything out.
