# Position: Inequitable

## Taxonomy

[By the Dread](position_dread_risk.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: risk

## Has

A hazard borne by one set of people and enjoyed by another.
The plant that employs a distant city and stands in this valley; the drug trialled in one population and sold in another; the noise under the flight path.
The feature is not that somebody profits, it is that the two sets do not overlap.

## Orders

Read the split, and size by which side of it the persona is on.
Where risk and benefit fall together, a persona tolerates a great deal; where they come apart, tolerance collapses -- and the same objective exposure produces acceptance in one valley and a campaign in the next.

## Loses

Any argument from the aggregate.
Net benefit is precisely the wrong case to make: it concedes the split and then asks the exposed side to be satisfied by somebody else's gain, so the strongest version of the proponent's argument is the strongest version of the objector's.

## Drives

On the loss of the aggregate argument:
moves benefit to where the harm sits -- payments, jobs, ownership, a share -- and buys acceptance with the only currency the axis reads,
or presses the aggregate, and teaches the exposed that being outweighed is the plan.
