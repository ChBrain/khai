# Position: Catastrophic

## Taxonomy

[By the Dread](position_dread_risk.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: risk

## Has

A harm that arrives all at once, to many, in one place.
The same annual toll delivered as one event of five hundred and as five hundred separate events is not the same hazard to a persona: the first is a catastrophe and the second is a background rate.

## Orders

Size the worst case, not the average.
The persona's estimate is anchored on the largest plausible single event, which is why a hazard's maximum matters far more to its sizing than its expectation, and why "it has never happened" is heard as "not yet".

## Loses

The distributed harm entirely.
A toll spread thin over years and places has no event to attach to, no image, and no moment -- so it is not sized small, it is not sized at all, and the largest killers in most societies are the ones nobody experiences as hazards.

## Drives

On the loss of the distributed harm:
gives the diffuse toll an event -- a figure, an anniversary, a name, a place -- and makes it visible at the cost of manufacturing an occasion that was never there,
or leaves it distributed, and lets the biggest thing in the room remain the one nothing is spent on.
