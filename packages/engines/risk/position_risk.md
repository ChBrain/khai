# Position: Risk

## Taxonomy

Judgment (no khai file of its own yet).

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: risk

## Has

The size a hazard has for a persona, which is not its death rate.
Ask somebody how many people a thing kills each year and they will often answer roughly right. Ask how risky it is and the answer moves somewhere else entirely -- because for a persona **risk** does not mean expected fatalities, it means how dreadful and how unfamiliar the harm is.
Two qualities set nearly all of it: [the dread](position_dread_risk.md), how terrible the harm would be to undergo and how little the persona could do about it; and [the unknown](position_unknown_risk.md), how far the hazard lies outside what can be seen, felt, or settled. A third axis, [the divergence](position_divergence_risk.md), reads what follows -- why the sizing parts company with the count, and what happens when somebody tries to close the distance.
The persona is not estimating badly. They are estimating something else.

## Orders

Size the hazard by what it would be like, not by how often it happens.
A death that arrives slowly, invisibly, without warning, to people who did not choose it, from a source they cannot inspect, counts for more than a death that arrives quickly to somebody who took their chances -- and the persona will spend, vote, move house, and refuse on that basis.
Read the sizing as a judgment with content rather than as an error with a cause, because everything else in the engine depends on that being right.

## Loses

The ability to be corrected by its own numbers.
Handing a persona the fatality rate does not adjust the size, because the size was never made of fatality rates; the number answers a question they were not asking, and arrives as an evasion -- which is exactly how it is usually received.
Worse, on a hazard that has become a marker of which group somebody belongs to, the correction runs backwards: greater science literacy and greater numeracy are associated with **more** polarisation rather than less, because a persona better equipped to work with evidence is better equipped to fit it to what their side already holds.
So the instrument that ought to close the gap is the instrument that widens it, and it widens it most in the personas best able to use it.

## Drives

On the loss of correction by numbers:
argues the qualities instead of the count -- who chose this, who can stop it, what it would be like, who bears it and who gains -- and gets a conversation that can actually move, at the cost of conceding that the statistic was never the argument,
or repeats the number louder, treats the persona as innumerate, and produces a better-informed opponent.
