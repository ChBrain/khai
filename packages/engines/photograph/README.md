# Photograph

Photograph as piece: an image that certifies a moment occurred and cannot say what it was -- not the possession into which an identity has extended, which is extended-self's, and not the object withheld from exchange to carry a lineage, which is heirloom's. The object is the piece; the members are what happens around it. One position names who is in it: a pictured persona holding a likeness in somebody else's keeping, who cannot recall the image, govern its caption, or stop a version they would not have chosen from becoming the standard one. Three processes name what it does: the taking, which hands the remembering to a device, so the cost is in the delegating rather than in the camera; the showing, which borrows the image's certainty for a statement the image did not make, since a photograph carries no account of itself and its holder writes the caption; and the outliving, which keeps the face and loses the name, because the distance that makes a picture precious is the distance that removes the people who could identify it. The twist: a photograph is taken to keep a moment and is the reason the moment is not kept.
