# Process: The Taking

## Taxonomy

[Photograph](piece_photograph.md), and where the engine is seen for one thing.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: photograph

## Initiated by

Somebody raises a camera during the thing they came for.
The Taking starts at a small substitution: for the length of the framing, the persona is arranging a picture rather than attending to what is in front of them.

## Direction

Toward a moment handed to a device.
The camera is not a passive addition to the occasion. It takes over the job of registering it, and the mind lets it: what has been photographed is treated as saved, and the effort that would have gone into holding it is not spent.
This is offloading rather than distraction, which is why it happens even when the taking is quick and the persona is entirely present in every other respect.

## Lever

The value of having the picture against the cost of having taken it.
The carrying force is that the photograph is genuinely worth having and the impulse is right: the occasion will not come again, the image will outlast everybody's recall of it, and a persona who never photographed anything would arrive at old age with less rather than more; the resistance is that the handing over is what does the damage -- the evidence is that photographing to capture impairs what is remembered and photographing with attention does not, so the cost is not in the camera but in the delegating.
The Taking exchanges an hour of attending for an object that will be looked at twice.

## Echo

An image where a memory would have been.
It rides the engine's keystone knife-edge: a photograph is taken to keep a moment and is the reason the moment is not kept.
The Taking is dated to a hundredth of a second and costs more than that.
