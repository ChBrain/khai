# Process: The Showing

## Taxonomy

[Photograph](piece_photograph.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: photograph

## Initiated by

The picture is put in front of somebody who was not there.
Passed across a table, framed on a wall, sent, posted, produced from a wallet at the moment it is needed.

## Direction

Toward an image doing work the moment never did.
A photograph shown is an argument: that this was a good day, that these people are close, that the persona was somewhere, that the thing occurred as claimed. None of that is in the image, which contains only a fragment with the edges removed.
The caption supplies the rest, and whoever holds the picture writes it -- so the same image serves a celebration and an accusation without being altered at all.

## Lever

The honesty of the image against the openness of what it means.
The carrying force is that showing is generous and usually straightforward: people share pictures to include somebody in something they missed, the picture really was taken there, and treating every display as a performance would make ordinary warmth impossible; the resistance is that a photograph cannot speak for itself, so its meaning is always supplied by its holder, and the viewer receives the caption as a property of the image rather than as a claim somebody made.
The Showing borrows the photograph's certainty for a statement the photograph did not make.

## Echo

A fragment that has been given a meaning.
It shows why the same picture appears in a family album and in a case file.
The Showing is dated at each viewing and is recaptioned every time.
