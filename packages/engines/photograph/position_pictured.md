# Position: Pictured

## Taxonomy

[Photograph](piece_photograph.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: photograph

## Has

A likeness in somebody else's keeping. The pictured persona is in the frame and does not hold the object: it belongs to whoever pressed the button, and it will be kept, shown, cropped, captioned, and circulated on their judgement.
What the persona has is a version of themselves at one instant, chosen by another person, which will be treated by everybody afterwards as what they looked like at that age.

## Orders

The position demands composure in advance of an instant nobody can time. A persona who knows a camera is present manages their face continuously and cannot stop, since the shutter takes a hundredth of a second out of an hour and any hundredth may be the one that is kept.
It also demands acquiescence. Objecting to a photograph is a larger act than taking one, so the burden of the exchange falls on whoever wants it not to happen.

## Loses

The position forecloses control of the likeness. The pictured persona cannot recall the image, cannot govern its caption, and cannot prevent a version of themselves they would not have chosen from becoming the standard one.
It forecloses the ordinary defence of a self-account, because the photograph is treated as evidence and any correction is treated as a claim -- so the persona's own testimony about the moment now argues against a document.
And it forecloses ageing privately, since the earlier version is available for comparison at any time, by anybody.

## Drives

When the image appears -- shown to a stranger, put on a wall, brought out at an occasion, or found decades later -- the persona either supplies the account the photograph cannot give, narrating what was happening outside the frame, or accepts the version and lets it stand as the record.
