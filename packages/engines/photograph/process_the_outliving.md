# Process: The Outliving

## Taxonomy

[Photograph](piece_photograph.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: photograph

## Initiated by

The picture is still here and the people in it are not.
The Outliving starts when the last person who could name everybody in the frame stops being able to.

## Direction

Toward an object that gains value as it loses its account.
A photograph becomes more precious every year, for the same reason it becomes less legible: the distance that makes it worth keeping is the distance that removes the caption. The generation that could say who the woman on the left was is the generation the picture is now standing in for.
So the household ends up holding images it cannot part with and cannot identify, and both facts strengthen together.

## Lever

The reach of the object against the reach of the knowledge that goes with it.
The carrying force is that this is the photograph doing exactly what it was kept for and doing it well: it carries a face to people who never met it, it is the only instrument that does, and the descendants who inherit an unidentified picture are still receiving something real; the resistance is that what survives is the certification and not the account -- the picture proves that somebody existed and cannot say who -- so the inheritance is a claim without its own evidence, and no amount of care preserves the part that was in people's heads.
The Outliving keeps the face and loses the name.

## Echo

A likeness with nobody left to place it.
It shows what the photograph actually preserves, at the point where everything else has gone.
The Outliving is dated from the last person who knew and is not noticed when it happens.
