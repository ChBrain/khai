# Piece: Photograph

## Taxonomy

The image that certifies a moment (no khai file of its own yet).

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: photograph

## Place

Wherever the moment is not. A photograph exists only after the thing it shows has stopped: on a shelf, in a wallet, in a folder nobody opens, on a wall in a corridor, in a file that is backed up automatically and never looked at again.
It occupies almost no space and is almost never thrown away, which is a combination no other object manages -- so households accumulate them at a rate they would not tolerate in anything else.
Its place is defined by afterwardness. Nothing in the frame is still happening.

## Load Bearing

What it holds up is a claim that this occurred. A photograph is not evidence of what a moment was like; it is evidence that it was, and that is a different and much stronger thing, which is why it carries in court, in a passport, and in an argument about whether somebody was there.
It also holds up the identity of whoever keeps it: a family that displays pictures is asserting that it is a family, and it takes them at exactly the occasions where that most needs asserting.
Remove it and the claim survives as testimony, which is a weaker instrument, and everybody in the room knows the difference.

## Apparent

Read as a window and made as a choice. What shows is a fragment somebody selected, framed, and kept, from which everything outside the edge has been removed -- and it is received as though the edge were not there.
The same image will support opposite arguments depending on what is written under it, because a photograph carries no account of itself: it says that this happened and cannot say what it was.
What actually shows is that somebody was present and decided to raise a camera, which is a fact about the keeper rather than the subject.

## Yearbook

The arc runs from the moment to the shelf to the box. It is taken at an occasion judged worth keeping, looked at often for a year or two, filed, and then not consulted for decades -- and it becomes more valuable exactly as it stops being viewed, since what makes it precious is that the people in it are gone.
The engine's twist is here. **A photograph is taken to keep a moment and is the reason the moment is not kept.** The persona raises the camera instead of attending, hands the remembering to the device, and afterwards has an image where the memory would have been -- and the image is what they now recall, in its frame, from its angle, with everything outside the edge gone. It is not that the photograph is false. It is that it is the only version left, and it was made by a machine pointed at part of the room.
It is not the possession into which an identity has extended, which is extended-self's, nor the object withheld from exchange to carry a lineage, which is heirloom's, nor the record an institution keeps of a person, which is document's; and a picture made to **stand for** a theme is authored key, the play's pitch.
It fails by being uncaptioned, by being unviewed, and by outliving everybody who could say who is in it.
