# Instructions: Photograph

How a Playwright wires the photograph engine into a play. You wire by linking the play's own content to the engine's files; the engine files stay untouched, they are canon. The Roadie plumbs the engine's law into Instructions Knowledge on deploy. Authoring guidance, not runtime content, and it does not go on tour.

## Human

- The human decides what is outside the frame before deciding what is in it, because the engine reads the edge and an inventory of the image is not the same thing. Decide who holds the picture separately from who is in it -- they are different positions and only one of them has any say. And keep the taker's motive ordinary: they wanted the photograph, which is a good reason and the only one most people have.

## Agent

- From the play's own content, a persona links under Projection how they stand to the image: whether they hold it or are in it, what the picture is being made to say, and which process is running -- the taking, the showing, or the outliving. Read the engine on what is outside the frame, never on what is in it. Keep it the image: the possession that is part of the self is extended-self's, the object withheld to carry a lineage is heirloom's, how memory works is memory's. You link to the engine, you never edit it.

## Collaboration

- The possession into which an identity has extended is the extended-self engine's; the object withheld from exchange for a lineage is heirloom's; encoding, alteration, and reconstruction are memory's; the institutional record is document's and legibility's; longing for the past is nostalgia's and the bittersweet composite's; the series bounded by a criterion is collection's; impression management and facework are presentation's, face's, and self-monitoring's. Composition, technique, and the print market are the trade and set what a photograph can look like rather than what it does. Whether a production's photograph replaced the memory it was taken to preserve, and whether a caption is a description or a claim, are the team's review.

## Knowledge

- A photograph is an image that certifies a moment occurred and cannot say what it was. It is not extended-self's possession-as-part-of-the-self, not heirloom's object withheld to carry a lineage, and not document's institutional record of a person. It wires at Piece, the persona links at Projection, and the members hang flat beneath the object. Its place is defined by afterwardness -- nothing in the frame is still happening -- and it takes almost no space and is almost never discarded, which is why households accumulate them at a rate they would tolerate in nothing else. What it holds up is the claim that this occurred, which is a stronger instrument than testimony and is why it carries in court and in an argument about whether somebody was there. What shows is read as a window and was made as a choice: everything outside the edge has been removed, and the image is received as though the edge were not there. The pictured persona holds a likeness in somebody else's keeping: they manage a face continuously because any hundredth of a second may be the one that is kept, and they cannot recall the image, write its caption, or stop an unchosen version becoming the record. The taking hands the job of registering the occasion to the device and the mind lets it, so what has been photographed is treated as saved and the effort of holding it is not spent -- and the impairment comes from the offloading rather than from the camera, which is why photographing with attention does not produce it. The showing puts a fragment in front of somebody who was not there, and since a photograph carries no account of itself the holder supplies the caption, which is why the same image serves a celebration and an accusation unaltered. The outliving keeps the face and loses the name: the distance that makes a picture precious is the distance that removes everybody who could identify it. The engine's twist is at the anchor: a photograph is taken to keep a moment and is the reason the moment is not kept, because the image is what the persona now recalls -- in its frame, from its angle, with everything outside the edge gone.

## System

- Do decide what is outside the frame first. That is the engine; the image is the scenery.
- Do keep the taker ordinary. They wanted the picture. Staging photography as vanity replaces the mechanism with a character note.
- Do stage the taking as a small substitution. For the length of the framing the persona is arranging an image rather than attending, and that is the whole of it.
- Do not claim the camera damages memory. The evidence says the delegating does, and photographing with attention does not, so write the offloading rather than the device.
- Do separate the holder from the pictured. Two positions, one object, and only one of them decides where it goes and what is written under it.
- Do let the caption do the work. The same picture, unaltered, supports a celebration and an accusation, and the viewer takes the meaning as a property of the image.
- Do put the twist in a recollection. The persona describes the day and describes the photograph, in its frame, from its angle, and does not notice they have done it.
- Do close on the outliving. A face nobody can name, in a house that cannot throw it away, and both facts getting stronger.
- Do not model the possession-as-self (extended-self), the lineage object (heirloom), memory itself (memory), the institutional file (document, legibility), longing (nostalgia), the series (collection), or impression management (presentation, face). Do not make the picture stand for a theme; that is authored key, the play's pitch. Do not edit the engine's files; wire only from the play's side.
