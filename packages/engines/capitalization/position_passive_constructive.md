# Position: Passive-Constructive

## Taxonomy

[Capitalization](position_capitalization.md), the reception that is for the news but withdrawn -- warm, understated, and no builder of the bond.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: capitalization

## Has

A specific [reception](position_capitalization.md): constructive but passive -- the persona is genuinely pleased at the partner's good news but shows it quietly and briefly.
A warm nod, a understated "that's great," a real but low-key gladness that does not open into engagement or draw the news out.
It does the news no harm; it simply does not make more of it -- the benefit of the sharing is neither punctured nor grown.

## Orders

Approve, but small.
The passive-constructive reply orders the persona to register the good news kindly and then let it rest: to be pleased without making an occasion of it, to acknowledge without asking, to hold its warmth understated rather than turning fully toward the moment.
It grants the news a friendly reception and stops there, moving on before the sharer has fully told it.

## Loses

The closeness the fuller reply would have built.
By keeping its gladness quiet and its attention brief, the persona forgoes the bond-building of the [active-constructive](position_active_constructive.md) reception -- it neither harms the sharer nor draws them nearer, and the good news, kindly received, still lands more softly than it might have.
What it trades is the cost of full engagement for a safe, low warmth -- and pays in the closeness left on the table, the moment received but not seized.

## Drives

On that warm but withdrawn reception:
rides it -- receives the news kindly, approves quietly, and lets it pass without harm or growth -- or, pressed, warms into the fuller engagement that would build, or cools into a reception the sharer feels as indifference.
The reception holds underneath, constructive in valence but passive in engagement, set apart from the bond-building [active-constructive](position_active_constructive.md) reply, the puncturing [active-destructive](position_active_destructive.md), and the absent [passive-destructive](position_passive_destructive.md). This face owns the kind, understated reply that holds the bond steady without growing it.
