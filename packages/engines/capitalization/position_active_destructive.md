# Position: Active-Destructive

## Taxonomy

[Capitalization](position_capitalization.md), the reception that is engaged but against the news -- attentive, and deflating.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: capitalization

## Has

A specific [reception](position_capitalization.md): active but destructive -- the persona engages fully with the partner's good news, and uses the engagement to puncture it.
It seizes on the downside, the risk, the hidden cost, the catch the sharer had not raised: "are you sure you can handle it?", "that means more travel, doesn't it?"
It looks like attention -- the persona is engaged, asking, responding -- but the attention works against the news, and the sharer comes away with the good thing shrunk.

## Orders

Engage, and find the problem.
The active-destructive reply orders the persona to take up the news and turn it -- to point out what could go wrong, what the sharer has overlooked, what the good thing will cost -- so that the disclosure ends smaller than it began.
It presents the deflation as care or realism, which is what lets it land: the persona is not ignoring the news but working on it, against it.

## Loses

The good news, and some of the trust in bringing the next.
By meeting the news with its downside, the persona takes from the sharer the joy of the telling and teaches them that good news brought to this persona comes back diminished -- it forgoes the bond entirely, and does worse than the withdrawn replies, because engaged deflation is felt more sharply than absence.
What it spends is the sharer's confidence that this is a safe person to be glad in front of, and it may not get the disclosure again.

## Drives

On that engaged, deflating reception:
rides it -- takes up the news and punctures it, in the guise of realism or concern, and sends the sharer away with the good thing shrunk -- or, caught, converts the engagement it already has into the [active-constructive](position_active_constructive.md) reply that would build instead.
The reception holds underneath, active in engagement but destructive in valence, set against the bond-building [active-constructive](position_active_constructive.md) reply, the merely warm [passive-constructive](position_passive_constructive.md), and the absent [passive-destructive](position_passive_destructive.md). This face owns the attentive reply that deflates; the raw intent to harm through the bond is the aggression engine's relational form, where the deflation is meant as harm rather than worn as care.
