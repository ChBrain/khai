# Instructions: Capitalization

How a Playwright wires the capitalization engine into a play. You wire by linking the play's own content to the engine's files; the engine files stay untouched, they are canon. The Roadie plumbs the engine's law into Instructions Knowledge on deploy. Authoring guidance, not runtime content, and it does not go on tour.

## Human

- The human sets the occasion: a partner brings the persona good news, and the stakes of how it is received -- what the bond stands to gain if the persona meets it well, or lose if it deflates or ignores it.

## Agent

- From the play's own content, a persona links the response style it gives a partner's good news under its Projection: active-constructive, engaged and enthusiastic, drawing the news out and building the bond; passive-constructive, warmly but quietly pleased, holding steady; active-destructive, attentive but deflating, seizing on the downside; passive-destructive, withdrawn or self-turning, letting the news fall. Let the style show the bond -- and remember only the active-constructive reply builds it. You link to the engine, you never edit it.

## Collaboration

- The sharer's own amplifying of its good feeling by expressing it -- telling someone, letting the delight show -- is the savoring engine's sharing form; this engine is the responder's reply, not the sharer's savoring. The general revealing of the self to another, deepening a relationship layer by layer, is the self-disclosure engine's. Where a deflating or withholding response is meant as harm aimed through the bond, that intent is the aggression engine's relational and passive forms. This engine supplies the responder's fork -- how a persona receives a partner's good news, in one of four styles.

## Knowledge

- Capitalization is the process by which sharing a positive event yields more than the event alone -- more joy, more closeness (Langston) -- and the benefit turns on the response the sharing meets (Gable). The persona is the responder. Its reception falls on two axes: engaged or withdrawn, and for the news or against it, giving four styles. Active-constructive is engaged and for the news -- enthusiastic, drawing it out, dwelling in it; this is the only one of the four that builds the relationship, raising the sharer's joy and felt understanding and the closeness between them. Passive-constructive is warm but understated, pleased quietly, neither harming the bond nor growing it. Active-destructive is engaged but against -- seizing on the downside, the risk, the catch, deflating the news while seeming to attend. Passive-destructive is withdrawn and against -- ignoring the news or turning it to oneself, letting the moment pass unmarked. How a persona responds to good news predicts the bond as strongly as how it responds to bad. Read a persona by the style it gives a partner's good news.

## System

- Do keep it the responder's fork: the sharer's amplifying of its own delight by expressing it is the savoring engine's, and the general revealing of the self is the self-disclosure engine's; this engine owns only how a persona receives a partner's good news.
- Do place the response on both axes -- engaged or withdrawn, for the news or against it -- and let only the active-constructive style build the bond; the other three hold it steady or erode it.
- Do let the response teach the sharer: an active-constructive reception invites the next disclosure, while a deflating or absent one teaches the sharer not to bring good news to this persona -- and a destructive reception can wound as much as a poor response to bad news.
- Do not play a destructive style as an intended weapon here; a deflation or withholding meant to harm through the bond is aggression's relational or passive form. Do not edit the engine's files; wire only from the play's side.
