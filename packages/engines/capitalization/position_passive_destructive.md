# Position: Passive-Destructive

## Taxonomy

[Capitalization](position_capitalization.md), the reception that is withdrawn and against the news -- absent, turning away.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: capitalization

## Has

A specific [reception](position_capitalization.md): passive and destructive -- the persona neither engages the partner's good news nor even meets it, letting it fall or turning it to itself.
It changes the subject, offers a flat acknowledgement and moves on, or answers the news with its own: "that's nice -- anyway, you won't believe my day."
The good news is not attacked but ignored, and the sharer is left holding it, met with a wall where they brought a gift.

## Orders

Let the news pass, or make it yours.
The passive-destructive reply orders the persona to withhold engagement -- to not ask, not mark, not dwell -- and often to redirect the moment onto itself, so the sharer's good news becomes a doorway to the persona's own concern.
It leaves the disclosure unreceived, the moment unclaimed, the sharer talking into quiet.

## Loses

The moment entirely, and the sharer's sense of being heard.
By turning away, the persona forgoes any bond the news could have built and inflicts the particular sting of the unreceived telling -- the sharer learns that its good news does not register here, which can wound as much as active deflation and teaches the same lesson: do not bring the next one.
What it spends is the sharer's feeling of mattering, traded for the small saving of an attention it did not give.
