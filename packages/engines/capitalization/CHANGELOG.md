# @chbrain/khai-engine-capitalization

## 0.1.1

### Patch Changes

- 234e9b6: Add the capitalization engine (position: anchor + expressions) -- the reception a persona gives when a partner shares good news, the responder's fork on which the relationship benefit of sharing turns. The gap analysis (Tier-3 #24) found `savoring` owns only the intrapersonal "sharing" (the sharer amplifying its own good feeling); nothing owned the _responder_ fork Gable's work isolates.

  - **Anchor (position):** `capitalization` -- capitalization is the process by which telling someone about a positive event yields more than the event alone (Langston), and the benefit turns on the response the disclosure meets (Gable); this engine is the responder's reception.
  - **Faces (position)** -- Gable's 2x2, active/passive engagement by constructive/destructive valence:
    - `active-constructive` -- engaged and for the news; enthusiastic, drawing it out, dwelling in it. **The only one of the four that builds the bond.**
    - `passive-constructive` -- warm but understated; pleased quietly, neither harming nor growing the bond.
    - `active-destructive` -- engaged but against; seizing on the downside, deflating the news while seeming to attend.
    - `passive-destructive` -- withdrawn and against; ignoring the news or turning it to the self.

  **Scope note -- the sharer's side stays with `savoring` and `self-disclosure`.** The sharer amplifying its own delight by expressing it is `savoring`'s sharing form; the general revealing of the self layer by layer is `self-disclosure`. Also bounded against `aggression` (its relational/passive forms own a deflating or withholding response _meant as harm_; this engine owns the response style as poor responsiveness, not a wielded weapon).

  Warranted (LORE) on Gable, Reis, Impett & Asher (2004, "What Do You Do When Things Go Right?" -- the responder typology and active-constructive responding as the bond-builder), Langston (1994, capitalization named), and Gable, Gonzaga & Strachman (2006, perceived partner responsiveness to good news as the mechanism). Set at patch as the free level. No whitelist required -- the `capitalization` and four `*_constructive`/`*_destructive` stems are unique.
