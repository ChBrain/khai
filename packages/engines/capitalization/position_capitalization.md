# Position: Capitalization

## Taxonomy

Interpersonal emotion / a responder's reception of a partner's shared good news (no khai file of its own).

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: capitalization

## Has

A way of receiving another's good news when they bring it.
Capitalization is the process by which telling someone about a positive event yields more than the event alone -- more joy, more closeness -- and Gable's finding is that the benefit turns not on the sharing but on the response it meets.
The persona here is the responder: a partner discloses a good thing, and the persona's reception decides whether the disclosure draws the two closer or leaves the sharer more alone with their good news than before they spoke.

## Orders

Meet the good news on two axes at once.
The reception the persona gives is read for its engagement -- whether it turns toward the news and draws it out, or lets it fall -- and for its valence -- whether it is for the news or against it. The room reads the persona's answer to a partner's good fortune before it reads the fortune: how it received the telling, not what was told.
Only one of the four receptions capitalizes; the rest let the moment pass or spoil it.

## Loses

The safety of a neutral, unmarked reply.
To receive good news is to be caught out taking a position -- there is no response that is not one, and the withheld or lukewarm reply is itself an answer that the sharer reads. The persona forgoes the illusion that it can hear good news and stay uncommitted; even turning away marks the bond.
What it stakes is the relationship in a small moment most personas underrate: the response to good news predicts the bond as strongly as the response to bad, and the persona that guards its enthusiasm spends the chance to build.

## Drives

On that reception:
gives it well -- turns toward the news, draws it out, and dwells in it with the sharer, the [active-constructive](position_active_constructive.md) reply that builds the bond -- or gives it thinly, the [passive-constructive](position_passive_constructive.md) nod that holds steady, the [active-destructive](position_active_destructive.md) deflation that punctures the news while seeming to attend, or the [passive-destructive](position_passive_destructive.md) turning-away that lets it pass unmarked.
The responder's fork holds underneath either way. The sharer's own amplifying of its delight by expressing it is the savoring engine's sharing form; the general revealing of the self to another is the self-disclosure engine's. This engine owns how the persona receives a partner's good news, and the four styles it can receive it in.
