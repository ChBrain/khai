# Capitalization: Reference

## Line of Work

Capitalization as a **position** engine: the reception a persona gives when a partner shares good news -- the responder's fork on which the relationship benefit of sharing turns -- taken across the four styles that reception can take. The domain does not model the sharer's side: the amplifying of one's own positive feeling by expressing it outward is owned by the savoring engine's sharing form, and the general revealing of the self to another, layer by layer, is the self-disclosure engine's. It models the responder's reply to a partner's good news, and the fork by which that reply builds the bond, holds it steady, or erodes it.

The spine is Gable's work on capitalization, building on Langston. Langston named capitalization: telling others about a positive event, and thereby gaining more from it than the event alone -- more positive affect, over and above the objective good. Gable, Reis, Impett, and Asher showed that the interpersonal benefit hinges on the response the disclosure meets, and mapped that response on two dimensions -- active versus passive engagement, and constructive versus destructive valence -- yielding four styles. Only the active-constructive response, enthusiastic and engaged, drawing the news out and dwelling in it, builds the relationship: it raises the sharer's positive emotion, their felt understanding and validation, and the closeness and trust between them. The passive-constructive response is genuinely warm but understated and does not build; the active-destructive response engages but deflates, seizing on the downside; the passive-destructive response withdraws, ignoring the news or turning it to the self. The finding that reframed the field is that how a partner responds to good news predicts relationship wellbeing at least as strongly as how they respond to bad -- perceived partner responsiveness at the moment of capitalization is the mechanism. The altitudes are those: the responder's reception, and its four styles.

## Restrictions

What the engine refuses to model, and to whom it delegates.

- **The sharer's savoring (savoring)**: The engine models the responder's reception, not the sharer's own amplifying of the good feeling. Savoring by expression -- telling someone or letting the delight show, to make one's own positive emotion larger -- is owned by the savoring engine's sharing form; capitalization is the other side of that exchange, what the persona does when it is the one receiving the news. The one grows the sharer's joy from within by expressing it; the other decides, from outside, whether the expression is met in a way that grows it further.
- **The disclosure of the self (self-disclosure)**: The engine models the reply to a partner's good news, not the act of disclosing. Revealing the self to another and deepening a relationship layer by layer -- the discloser's opening, paced by what is returned -- is owned by the self-disclosure engine; sharing good news is one special case of disclosure, but this engine owns only the responder's fork, not the sharer's opening. Disclosure is the telling; capitalization is how the telling of good news, specifically, is received.
- **Harm through the bond (aggression)**: The engine models a reception that fails to build, not a reception designed to wound. The active-destructive and passive-destructive styles erode the bond, but as poor responsiveness worn as care or as mere absence; where the deflation or the withholding is meant as harm -- a weapon aimed through the relationship -- that intent is the aggression engine's relational and passive forms. This engine owns the response style; aggression owns the response wielded to injure.

- **[active-constructive](position_active_constructive.md)** (position, face): Engaged and for the news -- enthusiastic, drawing it out, the one style that builds the bond. Anchored by Gable, Reis, Impett & Asher (active-constructive responding).
- **[passive-constructive](position_passive_constructive.md)** (position, face): For the news but withdrawn -- warm, understated, no builder of the bond. Anchored by Gable, Reis, Impett & Asher (passive-constructive responding).
- **[active-destructive](position_active_destructive.md)** (position, face): Engaged but against the news -- attentive and deflating. Anchored by Gable, Reis, Impett & Asher (active-destructive responding).
- **[passive-destructive](position_passive_destructive.md)** (position, face): Withdrawn and against the news -- ignoring it or turning it to the self. Anchored by Gable, Reis, Impett & Asher (passive-destructive responding).
