# Position: Discretion

## Taxonomy

[By the Gap](position_gap_law.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: law

## Has

The latitude of the official at the point of contact.
Lipsky's street-level bureaucrat: the caseworker, the inspector, the officer, the clerk -- who works with more demand than resources, must decide who gets what, and whose accumulated decisions are, in practice, the policy.
The discretion is not a leak in the system. It is how a system with finite resources and infinite cases manages to run at all.

## Orders

Decide the case, and let the deciding become the rule.
The official develops routines, categories, and shortcuts that make the workload survivable -- and those coping devices, not the statute, determine what the policy turns out to be.

## Loses

The authorship of its own policy.
What the legislature enacted and what the persona receives are separated by a layer nobody designed, so the body that made the rule cannot deliver it and the official who delivers it did not make it.
Neither can be held responsible in the way the other's existence implies.

## Drives

On the loss of authorship:
scripts the encounter -- checklists, targets, systems, review -- and gets consistency at the cost of the judgement that was absorbing the mismatch,
or leaves the latitude, keeps the judgement, and accepts that the policy is whatever the counter is doing this month.
