# Position: Before the Law

## Taxonomy

[By the Standing](position_standing_law.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: law

## Has

Law as a realm apart: impartial, majestic, operating by its own logic and on its own timetable, above the interests of anybody in particular.
The persona standing here speaks of the law with respect and at a distance, expects it to be objective, and does not expect to influence it.

## Orders

Defer, and stay outside.
Conduct from this standing is formal and self-effacing: the persona waits, submits properly, does not press, and treats a bad outcome as the price of a system that must be the same for everyone.

## Loses

Every remedy that requires pushing.
Reverence and passivity are the same posture from two sides, so the persona who holds law in the highest regard is also the least likely to appeal, complain, negotiate, or find the person who could actually fix it.
The standing that most respects the law gets the least out of it.

## Drives

On the loss of remedy:
keeps the distance, and accepts what the process produces as what the law is,
or steps closer and starts to work it, which means seeing the machinery and losing the majesty that made deference feel like virtue.
