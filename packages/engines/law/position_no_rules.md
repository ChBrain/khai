# Position: No Rules at All

## Taxonomy

[By the Failing](position_failing_law.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: law

## Has

Government by particular decision. Every case is settled ad hoc, on its facts, by whoever is deciding, and nothing generalises from one to the next.
It can look like justice -- each case given its own attention -- and it is Fuller's first failure because a persona facing it has nothing to know in advance.

## Orders

Decide the case in front of you, and let the next one be its own case.
Where the decisions are wise this is defensible and sometimes preferred; the failure is not that the outcomes are bad but that they are not rules.

## Loses

The ability to be obeyed.
A persona cannot comply with a rule that does not exist until after they act, so compliance is replaced by anticipation of the decider -- reading their mood, their history, their preferences.
The system's subjects become students of a person rather than of a rule, which is the condition every other item on this list eventually produces too.

## Drives

On the loss of obedience:
begins to publish its decisions and treat like cases alike, which is the birth of a rule and the surrender of the discretion that was the point,
or keeps every case particular, and rules over personas who study the decider instead of the law.
