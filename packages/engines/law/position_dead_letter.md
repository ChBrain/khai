# Position: The Dead Letter

## Taxonomy

[By the Gap](position_gap_law.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: law

## Has

A rule in force that is not enforced.
It was not repealed, it is still cited, and it is available -- and the ordinary reason it survives is that repealing it would require somebody to argue publicly for the conduct it forbids.

## Orders

Ignore it, and remember where it is.
A dead letter costs nothing to keep and occasionally does work its supporters never have to defend: it is there when a charge is needed, and it marks the conduct as officially disapproved without anybody bearing the cost of enforcement.

## Loses

The information in its own silence.
A persona cannot tell a dead letter from a live rule that has not reached them yet, so the unenforced rule teaches nothing reliable and, when it is revived, arrives as a change in the law that was not a change in the law.
The revival is the point at which it does its real damage, and it can happen at any time without any procedure.

## Drives

On the loss of that information:
repeals what it will not enforce and pays the political price of saying so out loud,
or keeps the letter dormant, and holds a lawful charge in reserve against conduct everybody had reasonably concluded was permitted.
