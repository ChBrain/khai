# Position: Against the Law

## Taxonomy

[By the Standing](position_standing_law.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: law

## Has

Law as an arbitrary power: capricious, indifferent, and not to be engaged on its own terms.
The persona standing here does not petition and does not play. They evade, delay, make do, tell stories about officials, and take small satisfactions in having got around something -- Ewick and Silbey's resistance, which is made of foot-dragging and jokes far more often than of defiance.

## Orders

Get by without it.
Conduct from this standing is tactical and concealed: avoid the notice, use the informal channel, hold the useful ambiguity, and never put anything in a form that can be used later.

## Loses

Everything law is for.
The persona who will not engage the system cannot use it either -- no enforcement of their contract, no protection of their claim, no record when they need one -- so the standing that costs the law least in compliance costs the persona most in cover.
It is also the standing most available to those with the least, which is why the resistance is real and the position is not a choice in the way the other two are.

## Drives

On the loss of law's protection:
stays outside and builds the substitutes -- reputation, kin, the informal arrangement -- that do the work law would have done,
or comes in when something is at stake that only law can hold, and discovers what the years of staying out cost them in standing.
