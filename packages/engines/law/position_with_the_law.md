# Position: With the Law

## Taxonomy

[By the Standing](position_standing_law.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: law

## Has

Law as a game: a field with rules, positions, and players, where the skilled do better than the deserving.
The persona standing here is not cynical about legality, they are fluent in it -- they know which form, which deadline, which argument, which office, and they expect that knowing to matter.

## Orders

Play, and play well.
Conduct from this standing is strategic and unembarrassed: press the claim, use the delay, take the appeal, hire the person who does this every day, and treat the rules as the terms of the contest rather than as commands.

## Loses

Any complaint about the outcome.
A persona who treats law as a game has accepted its terms, so a loss is a loss on the merits of play and there is nothing left to appeal to -- the unfairness of the game itself is not a move available inside it.
And the standing is not equally available: playing well costs money, time, and prior experience, so the position that gets the most out of law is the one fewest personas can take.

## Drives

On the loss of complaint:
plays on, wins what skill wins, and stops asking whether the contest was fair to enter,
or steps out and says the game itself is the problem, which is a different standing and forfeits the fluency that was working.
