# Position: Selective Enforcement

## Taxonomy

[By the Gap](position_gap_law.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: law

## Has

A rule enforced against some of the personas who breach it.
Universal enforcement of everything is impossible, so every legal order selects -- and the selection is made by complaint patterns, by visibility, by where the officials are, and by whom it is cheap to proceed against.

## Orders

Enforce where enforcement is available.
The selection is rarely a decision anybody makes; it is the sum of what gets reported, what gets noticed, and what will survive a challenge, which is why it tracks visibility and vulnerability so closely.

## Loses

The rule's claim to be about the conduct.
Once only some breaches are answered, being penalised stops meaning that a persona did the thing and starts meaning that they were reachable -- so the rule communicates about the enforcer's attention rather than about the act.
A persona who is caught knows others were not, which is the fact that does the damage.

## Drives

On the loss of the conduct claim:
narrows the rule to what it can actually enforce across the board, and gives up the reach,
or keeps the broad rule and enforces where it can, gaining coverage on paper and teaching everybody that the law is about who you are.
