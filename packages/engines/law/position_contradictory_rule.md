# Position: The Contradictory Rule

## Taxonomy

[By the Failing](position_failing_law.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: law

## Has

Two rules in force that cannot both be obeyed.
The persona is required to do and not do the same thing, or required by one authority to do what another forbids, and the system offers no ranking that settles it.

## Orders

Choose which rule to break.
The persona must decide, and having decided is in breach of something whatever they do -- so their real question stops being what the law requires and becomes which enforcer is likelier to arrive.

## Loses

The possibility of innocence.
Where the contradiction stands, no course of conduct is lawful, so the system can find any persona in breach at any time without changing a rule or catching them out.
That is the same structure as a licence to enforce selectively, arrived at without anybody deciding to grant one.

## Drives

On the loss of possible innocence:
ranks the rules -- a hierarchy of norms, a conflicts doctrine, a court to resolve -- and restores a course of conduct that is lawful,
or leaves the contradiction standing, and holds a permanent, deniable hold over everybody subject to both.
