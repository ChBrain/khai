# Position: The Unpublished Rule

## Taxonomy

[By the Failing](position_failing_law.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: law

## Has

A rule that binds without being available to those it binds.
Sometimes it is literally secret; more often it is technically published and practically unfindable -- buried in a schedule, held in an internal guidance note, discoverable only by somebody who already knew to look.

## Orders

Comply with what you could not have read.
The rule operates normally in every respect except the one that matters: it is applied, enforced, and defended as binding, and the persona's ignorance of it is treated as their problem.

## Loses

Any distinction between the compliant and the lucky.
A persona who did not breach an unpublished rule did not obey it -- they happened not to trip it -- so the system loses the ability to reward compliance, because it cannot tell compliance from coincidence.
It also loses the deterrent, since nothing is deterred by a threat nobody has heard.

## Drives

On the loss of the compliant/lucky distinction:
publishes, and accepts that publishing tells the persona exactly where the edges are and therefore how close they may safely stand,
or keeps the rule dark and enforces it anyway, which converts every enforcement into a piece of news and the whole order into a hazard.
