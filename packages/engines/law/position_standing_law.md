# Position: By the Standing

## Taxonomy

[Law](position_law.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: law

## Has

Ewick and Silbey's finding: ordinary personas do not hold one attitude to law, they occupy one of three standings toward it, and the same persona moves between them within a single week.
[Before the law](position_before_the_law.md), it is majestic, impartial, and remote -- a realm apart, to be respected and not approached. [With the law](position_with_the_law.md), it is a game with rules and players, to be used by whoever knows how. [Against the law](position_against_the_law.md), it is an arbitrary power to be evaded, made fun of, and worked around by whoever has no other move.
The three are not stages of sophistication and not types of person. They are positions available to everybody.

## Orders

Read which standing a persona is speaking from, and expect it to change.
The standings are contradictory and personas hold them simultaneously without discomfort -- revering the law in the abstract, gaming it in their own dispute, and slipping it entirely when it reaches into something they will not give up.

## Loses

The legitimacy that would come from any one of them being the truth.
Because the three coexist, a legal order cannot secure its standing by being genuinely majestic or genuinely fair: the persona who reveres it in principle is the same one working around it in particular, and neither cancels the other.
The contradiction is not a failure of public understanding to be corrected. It is how legality is actually inhabited, and it is what makes it durable -- a system nobody wholly believes in is also one nobody is wholly disappointed by.

## Drives

On the loss of a single true standing:
takes the three as the real terrain and reads each persona's position case by case,
or insists on one -- teaching reverence, or unmasking the game -- and misreads everybody who is holding two at once.
