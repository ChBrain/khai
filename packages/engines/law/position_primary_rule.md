# Position: The Primary Rule

## Taxonomy

[By the Making](position_making_law.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: law

## Has

A rule of obligation: do this, do not do that.
It is the part of a legal order that a persona actually meets -- the speed limit, the tax due, the contract enforced, the assault forbidden -- and the only part most personas ever read.
On its own it is indistinguishable from a strong custom, which is the point: what makes it law is not its content but the secondary rules standing behind it.

## Orders

State the conduct and attach the consequence.
A primary rule works by being specific enough that a persona can tell in advance whether they are inside it, which is why legal drafting reads as it does and why the reading is not a failure of style.

## Loses

Its own authority.
A primary rule cannot say why it counts. Presented alone it has no answer to "who says", and a persona confronted with one they do not accept has nothing to argue with except force.
It also cannot resolve a dispute about itself: two personas reading the same primary rule in incompatible ways have no way, within the rule, to find out who is right.

## Drives

On the loss of its own authority:
is backed by the secondary rules and becomes law, gaining a settled answer to "who says" and accepting everything that answer drags with it,
or stands alone and becomes a custom or a threat, holding only as long as agreement or force does.
