# Position: The Unstable Rule

## Taxonomy

[By the Failing](position_failing_law.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: law

## Has

A rule changed faster than a persona can act on it.
Each version may be clear, published, and prospective; the failure is in the frequency, which puts the current rule permanently out of date with the conduct it is meant to guide.

## Orders

Track the rule as a moving object.
Compliance becomes a monitoring task rather than a knowing one, and the cost of that monitoring falls on the persona -- unevenly, since watching a rule change is a job somebody has to be able to afford.

## Loses

Any commitment longer than the change interval.
A persona cannot build, borrow, invest, or plan past the next revision, so activity shortens to the horizon the instability sets -- and the system's own signals stop being informative, because nothing it announces survives long enough to be relied on.

## Drives

On the loss of long commitment:
slows the change, publishes a timetable, grandfathers what was begun under the old rule, and accepts being wrong for longer,
or keeps revising at pace, stays responsive, and finds nobody is doing anything that takes time.
