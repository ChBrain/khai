# Position: The Retroactive Rule

## Taxonomy

[By the Failing](position_failing_law.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: law

## Has

A rule that reaches backward: conduct lawful when done, made unlawful afterward, or an act's consequences altered after the act.
Its milder and far more common form is the reinterpretation -- the rule was always there, and the meaning it turns out to have had is not the one anybody acted on.

## Orders

Answer now for what you did then, under terms that did not exist then.
The persona is held to a standard they could not have consulted, and the system's account of this is usually that the standard was implicit all along.

## Loses

The point of having announced anything.
Prospective rules buy a persona the ability to plan, and a rule that can reach backward withdraws that purchase from every rule in the system -- not only from itself, since a persona who has seen one retroactive change must now discount all the others.
The damage is systemic and outlives the particular rule.

## Drives

On the loss of planning:
confines retroactivity to the cure -- validating what was done in good faith under a rule later found defective, which is the one use Fuller allowed -- and defends the confinement fiercely,
or reaches back when the outcome is worth it, and finds that no assurance it gives afterward is worth very much.
