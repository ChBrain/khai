# Position: Administered Otherwise

## Taxonomy

[By the Failing](position_failing_law.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: law

## Has

A divergence between the rule as declared and the rule as applied.
Fuller's eighth, and the one that swallows the other seven: everything can be clear, published, prospective, consistent, possible, and stable, and the administration can still do something else.

## Orders

Read the practice, not the text.
A persona governed by such a system learns the real rule from cases, from officials, from other subjects -- and the declared rule becomes a document about the system rather than an instruction from it.

## Loses

The usefulness of its own law.
Once declared and applied come apart, consulting the rule is no longer the way to find out what will happen, so the system's published law stops being a source of guidance and becomes at best a claim about itself.
This is the failure that makes the other seven survivable in the reverse direction too: a technically defective rule administered sensibly can still govern, and a perfect rule administered otherwise cannot.

## Drives

On the loss of its law's usefulness:
brings declaration and administration back together -- publishing guidance, disciplining officials, reviewing decisions -- and accepts that this exposes how much of the real rule was never written,
or lets them run apart, and governs through a practice its own law no longer describes.
