# Position: The Unintelligible Rule

## Taxonomy

[By the Failing](position_failing_law.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: law

## Has

A rule nobody can determine the meaning of.
Not merely technical or long: genuinely indeterminate -- contradictory in its terms, circular in its definitions, or so compounded by cross-reference that no reader converges on what it requires.

## Orders

Guess, or hire somebody to guess for you.
An unintelligible rule is not inert; it is enforced, and its meaning is settled in the only place available, which is at the moment of enforcement, by the enforcer.

## Loses

Its content, to whoever applies it.
An unreadable rule transfers the power to say what it means from the body that wrote it to the official at the counter, so the rule's real author is not its drafter -- and neither the drafter nor the persona can predict what they have made.
It is also the failure that most reliably prices access, since the persona who can afford an interpreter is governed by a different rule from the one who cannot.

## Drives

On the loss of its content:
drafts for the reader it actually has, accepting that plain rules are cruder and cover less well than precise ones,
or drafts for precision and lets the counter decide, which is discretion arrived at by accident rather than by design.
