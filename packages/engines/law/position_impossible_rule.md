# Position: The Impossible Rule

## Taxonomy

[By the Failing](position_failing_law.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: law

## Has

A rule requiring what cannot be done.
Sometimes physically impossible; more often impossible in practice -- a filing no one has the records for, a standard no available technology meets, a deadline shorter than the process it governs.

## Orders

Comply anyway, or be in breach.
The system's usual accommodation is not to amend the rule but to under-enforce it, which means the rule survives as a condition everybody is in breach of and nobody is usually punished for.

## Loses

The line between the enforced and the merely available.
An impossible rule cannot be generally enforced, so it is enforced occasionally -- and a rule enforced occasionally against people already in breach for structural reasons is a rule whose real content is the enforcer's choice of target.
Universal breach and selective enforcement are the same arrangement seen from two ends.

## Drives

On the loss of that line:
repeals or amends to what can actually be done, and gives up the standard it wished it could hold,
or keeps the impossible standard as an aspiration, and holds a charge in reserve against everybody it applies to.
