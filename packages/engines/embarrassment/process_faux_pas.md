# Process: Faux Pas

## Taxonomy

[Embarrassment](process_embarrassment.md), the blunder form.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: embarrassment

## Initiated by

The persona commits a breach -- trips, spills, forgets a name, says the wrong thing, is caught with a button undone -- a small violation of social convention exposed before others.
This is the faux pas: the classic elicitor of embarrassment (Sabini et al.), the pratfall, the gaffe, the slip that reveals the self as clumsy, careless, or out of step.
It is the blunder that cannot be denied because it was seen.

## Direction

Toward covering the breach and restoring the smooth surface.
The faux pas drives the apology, the laugh, the quick recovery that folds the blunder back into normal interaction -- the scramble to make the lapse a passing thing rather than a lasting mark.
Its direction is repair by recovery: get past it, and get the room past it.

## Lever

The visibility of the mistake and the gap it opens between the self one meant to present and the one just exposed.
The faux pas spends the persona's poise, catching them in a lapse they cannot deny; and the more public the setting and the more competent the self was meant to look, the sharper the sting.
What it stakes is the managed impression, punctured by the slip.

## Echo

The blunder is, usually, laughed off and forgiven -- a faux pas is minor by nature, and the embarrassment display is what earns the pardon.
Its residue is the cringe of memory: the old gaffe that returns unbidden years later, still able to flush the cheeks in private.
The reasoned social convention that was breached is the conformity and face engines'; the faux pas is the exposed slip and the fluster it brings.
