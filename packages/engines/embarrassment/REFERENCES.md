# Embarrassment: Reference

## Line of Work

Embarrassment as a **multi-type** engine: the acute, self-conscious emotion of unwanted social exposure -- a blunder, a spotlight, a breach witnessed -- and the appeasing fluster it triggers, taken across its forms and the trait that tunes it. The domain does not model the deep devaluation of the self against a moral standard, which is the shame engine's; nor the public self-image a persona claims and a breach costs, which is the face engine's; nor the capacity to feel into another that carries the secondhand cringe, which is the empathy engine's; nor the facilitation or inhibition of performance under others' eyes, which is the social-facilitation engine's. It models the emotion itself: the appraisal of an unwanted exposure before an audience, the flustered response, and the appeasing display that repairs the rupture.

The spine is the social psychology of embarrassment. Embarrassment is a self-conscious emotion elicited by unwanted social exposure -- a predicament, a breach of convention, or mere conspicuousness -- before an audience real or imagined. It is distinguished from shame (Tangney; Miller): shame is the deep, hidden devaluation of the whole self against a moral standard, while embarrassment is lighter, sharper, more transient, tied to social convention and to an audience, and answered not by hiding but by appeasement. Its signature is the blush (Darwin) -- an involuntary, uncontrollable display that, precisely because it cannot be faked, serves as a trustworthy appeasement signal restoring the standing a breach cost (Keltner & Buswell). Its elicitors sort into faux pas, conspicuousness, and empathic (vicarious) embarrassment (Sabini et al.; Miller), and individual differences in embarrassability tune the whole (Miller's Embarrassability Scale). The altitudes are those: the emotion, its four forms, and its trait.

## Restrictions

What the engine refuses to model, and to whom it delegates.

- **Shame**: The engine models the lighter, social sting of exposure, not the deep devaluation of the self. Shame -- the appraisal of the whole self as flawed against a moral standard, answered by hiding -- is owned by the shame engine (which keeps embarrassment as a node at its lighter end); this engine unfolds embarrassment in its own right, the emotion of a social predicament that appeases rather than hides and passes rather than lingers. The two compose: shame keeps the deep self-devaluation, this engine the social fluster.
- **Face**: The engine models the emotion of exposure, not the social image it threatens. The public self-image a persona claims in interaction -- the standing, the approval, the room to act -- is owned by the face engine; embarrassment is what a persona feels when that face is threatened or lost, and the face itself, and its repair, are the face engine's.
- **Empathy**: The engine models the vicarious cringe, not the capacity that carries it. The channel for feeling into another's inner state is owned by the empathy engine; empathic embarrassment runs through that channel, but the capacity itself is empathy's, and the secondhand emotion is this engine's.
- **Social facilitation**: The engine models the fluster of being watched, not the effect of watchers on performance. The facilitation of dominant responses and inhibition of difficult ones under an audience is owned by the social-facilitation engine; conspicuousness embarrassment is the felt discomfort of being observed, not the performance change it may accompany.
- **Self-conscious composite**: The engine models embarrassment as its own emotion, not the appraisal-of-self-against-a-standard family. The self-directed appraisal that yields guilt, shame, and pride is read whole by the self-conscious composite; embarrassment is a fourth self-conscious emotion, lighter and more social than those, which that composite may one day wire over but does not author here.

## Encoding

Source to constraint, per file.

- **[embarrassment](process_embarrassment.md)** (the root, process): The flustered response to unwanted social exposure, and the appeasing display that repairs the rupture. Anchored by Miller (the emotion) and Keltner & Buswell (the appeasement function).
- **[faux pas](process_faux_pas.md)** (process, form): The blunder -- a breach of convention exposed, the gaffe or pratfall that catches the self out of step. Anchored by Sabini et al. (the elicitors).
- **[conspicuous](process_conspicuous.md)** (process, form): The spotlight -- the fluster of being watched, singled out, or praised, embarrassment without wrongdoing. Anchored by Sabini et al. and Miller (conspicuousness).
- **[cringe](process_cringe.md)** (process, form): The vicarious form -- empathic embarrassment, the secondhand sting felt for another who exposed themselves. Anchored by Miller (empathic embarrassment, 1987).
- **[blush](process_blush.md)** (process, display): The appeasement display -- the involuntary reddening, averted gaze, and nervous smile that concede the breach and, by being uncontrollable, are believed. Anchored by Darwin (the blush) and Keltner & Buswell (the display).
- **[embarrassability](position_embarrassability.md)** (position, trait): A standing readiness to flush, keenly attuned to how one appears, tracking social-evaluative concern. Anchored by Miller (the Embarrassability Scale) and Leary (social-evaluative concern).
