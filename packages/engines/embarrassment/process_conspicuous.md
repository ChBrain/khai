# Process: Conspicuous

## Taxonomy

[Embarrassment](process_embarrassment.md), the spotlight form.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: embarrassment

## Initiated by

The persona is made the center of attention -- singled out, watched, put on the spot -- even with no blunder committed, and even when the attention is favorable.
This is conspicuousness embarrassment (Sabini et al.; Miller): the fluster of being looked at, the discomfort of the spotlight, the blush at being sung "Happy Birthday" to by a table of waiters.
It is embarrassment without wrongdoing -- exposure alone is enough.

## Direction

Toward escaping the gaze or deflecting it -- shrinking, looking down, passing the attention on, making oneself smaller before the many eyes.
The conspicuous form seeks to end the exposure, to return from the spotlight to the crowd where one is not singularly seen.
Its direction is out of the light.

## Lever

The sheer weight of being observed, and the self-consciousness it forces.
Conspicuousness spends the ease of the unwatched self, and it embarrasses because to be watched is to be evaluated -- the audience's regard felt as pressure even when it is warm; praise embarrasses for exactly this reason, turning the spotlight on.
What it stakes is the comfort of going unseen.

## Echo

The attention passes and composure returns -- but the conspicuous form shows that embarrassment needs no wrongdoing, only exposure: to be seen too much is enough.
It shades toward the social anxiety of the chronically self-conscious, for whom every entrance is a spotlight.
The facilitation or inhibition of performance under others' eyes is the social-facilitation engine's; conspicuousness is the fluster of simply being the one looked at.
