# Position: Embarrassability

## Taxonomy

Affect / the trait of embarrassability -- the standing readiness beneath [Embarrassment](process_embarrassment.md) (Miller's Embarrassability Scale).

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: embarrassment

## Has

A low threshold for social exposure -- a disposition to flush readily, to feel the eyes on one keenly, and to be caught out by small breaches that others shrug off.
Trait embarrassability, as against the state: the persona easily flustered, quick to blush, and highly attuned to how they appear, where another sails through the same exposure unbothered.
It tracks social-evaluative concern, self-consciousness, and a fear of negative appraisal.

## Orders

Run from here, ahead of the exposure.
The embarrassable persona scans for how they are being seen, anticipates the blunder, and flushes at the mildest conspicuousness; and the tilt shapes a cautious, self-monitoring social style, careful not to be caught out.
It colours the read before the event, on the axis of being seen.

## Loses

The ease of the unselfconscious.
The highly embarrassable persona meets a social world thick with potential exposure -- more moments read as risky, more attention felt as pressure -- and loses the poise of one who does not mind being seen.
What narrows is the range of situations it can enter unflustered.

## Drives

On the loss of that ease:
rides the embarrassability -- stays careful, avoids the spotlight, monitors the self -- or works against it, braving the exposure, letting the blush come, learning that the blunder is survivable, at a cost in vigilance.
The threshold holds underneath either way, a resting level the persona manages rather than moves.
