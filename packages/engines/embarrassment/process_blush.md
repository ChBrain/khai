# Process: Blush

## Taxonomy

[Embarrassment](process_embarrassment.md), the appeasement display.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: embarrassment

## Initiated by

The embarrassment reaches the face and the skin -- the involuntary reddening of cheeks, ears, and neck, the averted gaze, the sheepish smile, the hand brought to the face.
This is the blush and its display: the signature of embarrassment (Darwin -- the blush unique to human beings and to this emotion), the body's uncontrollable concession of the breach.
It is embarrassment made visible on the surface of the self.

## Direction

Outward as a signal, not inward as a feeling.
The blush is a display aimed at the audience -- an involuntary broadcast that the persona knows they have breached and cannot hide it -- and the averted gaze and nervous smile complete the appeasing gesture (Keltner & Buswell).
Its direction is toward the watchers, conceding to them.

## Lever

The very uncontrollability of it.
The blush cannot be faked or suppressed, which is exactly what makes it a trustworthy signal: it appeases because it proves the embarrassment genuine, the breach acknowledged from a self that cannot pretend otherwise.
What it spends is concealment -- the blusher cannot hide their state, and in not hiding it, is forgiven.

## Echo

The display appeases, and the audience, seeing the honest fluster, softens -- the blush restores the standing the breach cost, its function the repair of the social rupture.
Its curse is its involuntariness: the persona who most wishes not to blush blushes the harder, and the fear of blushing (erythrophobia) becomes its own predicament.
The public standing it repairs is the face engine's; the blush is embarrassment made visible and appeasing.
