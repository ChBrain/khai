# Process: Cringe

## Taxonomy

[Embarrassment](process_embarrassment.md), the vicarious form.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: embarrassment

## Initiated by

The persona witnesses another commit a blunder or expose themselves, and feels the embarrassment as if it were their own -- the secondhand sting, the wish to look away.
This is empathic, or vicarious, embarrassment (Miller): the cringe, felt for another who may not even feel it themselves.
It is embarrassment caught from someone else's predicament.

## Direction

Away -- the wish to escape the scene, to cover one's own eyes, to not witness the other's exposure that one is somehow feeling.
The cringe drives the flinch, the peek through fingers, the "I can't watch" -- a turning-from a discomfort that is not, by rights, one's own.
Its direction is out of the secondhand light.

## Lever

The empathic taking-on of another's social predicament -- the persona projects into the exposed other and flushes on their behalf.
It fires most where the persona identifies with the blunderer or cannot escape watching, and what it spends is the comfort of the mere observer: the cringe makes a witness suffer an exposure they did not commit.
The stronger the identification, the sharper the vicarious sting.

## Echo

The other's blunder is felt -- and often the witness suffers it more than the blunderer does, especially when the blunderer is oblivious.
Vicarious embarrassment is why some scenes are unwatchable, the whole comedy of cringe built on it.
It rests on the empathic channel: without the capacity to feel into another, there is no secondhand cringe -- and that capacity itself is the empathy engine's, while cringe is embarrassment run through it, felt for another.
