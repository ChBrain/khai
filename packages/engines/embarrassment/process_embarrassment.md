# Process: Embarrassment

## Taxonomy

Emotion / the psychology of the response to unwanted social exposure.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: embarrassment

## Initiated by

A persona is caught in an unwanted social exposure -- a blunder committed, a convention breached, a private thing made public, or simply the spotlight turned on -- before an audience real or imagined, and flushes.
This is embarrassment: the acute, self-conscious emotion of a social predicament witnessed, the flustered response to being improperly exposed.
Where shame is the deep devaluation of the self against a moral standard, embarrassment is the lighter, sharper sting of a social convention broken in view -- more about the eyes on you than the badness of the self.

## Direction

Toward the restoration of the disrupted social order -- and, in the body, toward appeasement.
Embarrassment does not hide the self as shame does; it signals -- the blush, the averted gaze, the nervous smile -- an involuntary display that concedes the breach and appeases the audience, restoring the standing the blunder cost (Keltner & Buswell).
Its direction is back toward smooth interaction, the rupture repaired.

## Lever

The appraisal of an unwanted exposure before an audience -- a self presented worse, odder, or more visible than wanted, with others watching.
What embarrassment spends is composure: the poise of a managed self-presentation, suddenly lost, the persona flustered and scrambling to recover.
It needs an audience -- even an imagined or remembered one -- as shame does not always; it is the emotion of the self caught out.

## Echo

The breach is conceded and, by the appeasing display, often forgiven -- embarrassment is the emotion that repairs small social ruptures, the blush that says _I know, I'm sorry, I'm one of you still_.
Its excess is the paralysis of the highly embarrassable, and its absence reads as shamelessness.
It runs as [faux pas](process_faux_pas.md), [conspicuous](process_conspicuous.md), [cringe](process_cringe.md), and [blush](process_blush.md), tuned by a standing [embarrassability](position_embarrassability.md). The deep moral devaluation of the self is the shame engine's; the public self-image the breach costs is the face engine's; the empathic capacity that carries the secondhand cringe is the empathy engine's; the performance under others' watching eyes is the social-facilitation engine's. This engine owns the flustered response to unwanted exposure.
