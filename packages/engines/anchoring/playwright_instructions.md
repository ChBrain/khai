# Instructions: Anchoring

How a Playwright wires the anchoring engine into a play. You wire by linking the play's own content to the engine's files; the engine files stay untouched, they are canon. The Roadie plumbs the engine's law into Instructions Knowledge on deploy. Authoring guidance, not runtime content, and it does not go on tour.

## Human

- The human defines the anchoring terrain: what uncertain quantity a persona must estimate, what value enters first and from where -- their own head, another's mouth, a price tag, a random figure -- and what is at stake in the estimate, so the anchor has something to bend and a reason the bend matters.

## Agent

- From the play's own content, a persona links the mechanism the moment runs (adjustment, accessibility, or planting) under its Projection. Let the mechanism show how the estimate is bent -- the guess that stops too soon, the anchor that colors what comes to mind, the figure named first to move a counterpart -- and let it read as an estimate pulled off true, not a cold weighing of the evidence. You link to the engine, you never edit it.

## Collaboration

- The mode by which a persona reaches a choice among options is the decision engine's; anchoring bends a magnitude judgment under uncertainty, not the choosing. The bargaining a planted anchor serves is the negotiation engine's; the shift worked by how an option is described rather than by a number is the framing engine's; the standing catalogued tilt the look leaves on the read of a quantity is the bias engine's. This engine supplies the pull toward the reference itself -- a first figure met and the estimate drawn to it.

## Knowledge

- Anchoring is the estimate drawn to a reference: a persona judging an uncertain quantity takes a value that entered first -- relevant or not, chosen or merely present -- as the point every later guess is read against, and adjusts away from it too little. Adjustment is the self-generated route -- step away from a starting value you hold, but stop as soon as the estimate feels plausible, short of the truth. Accessibility is the external route -- a supplied anchor primes anchor-consistent evidence, so what comes to mind is what would be true if the anchor were near, and the target is judged closer to it. Planting is the deliberate route -- set a number on purpose, a first offer or a list price, to drag another persona's estimate toward it.

## System

- Do make anchoring bend the estimate, not replace it: the persona corrects away from the anchor, only not far enough -- so let the final figure land between the evidence and the anchor, pulled off true rather than pinned to the number.
- Do let the anchor grip even when it is known to be arbitrary or irrelevant: a random figure, a price the persona denies using, an offer they mean to discount -- keep the pull working under warning and against incentive, because that is what makes anchoring anchoring.
- Do let the first number own the range in planting: whoever names the figure first claims the ground the rest is measured from -- so let an early, precise, extreme-but-defensible anchor shape the settlement more than a late or timid one.
- Do delegate the mode of choosing to the decision engine and the bargaining the plant serves to the negotiation engine. Do not edit the engine's files; wire only from the play's side.
