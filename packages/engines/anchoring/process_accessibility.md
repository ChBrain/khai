# Process: Accessibility

## Taxonomy

[Anchoring](process_anchoring.md), the externally-supplied form.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: anchoring

## Initiated by

A value arrives from outside -- a number in the question, a figure someone named -- and testing whether it might be the answer calls anchor-consistent facts to mind, which then color the estimate.
This is accessibility: the anchor priming its own evidence, so what comes readily to mind is what would be true if the anchor were near.
The persona does not adjust away from the figure; they see the target through the material the figure made available.

## Direction

Toward the anchor, along a path the anchor itself laid.
Accessibility works by selective retrieval: to consider the supplied value, the persona checks it against memory, and that check surfaces the features and instances consistent with it, leaving the inconsistent ones dim.
The direction is set by what is now easy to recall -- an evidence base tilted toward the anchor -- so the estimate is drawn to it not by a cut-short correction but by a loaded sample of what seems true.

## Lever

The confirmatory test the anchor invites.
Accessibility spends the way a hypothesis is checked: asked whether the value is high or low, the persona tests _is it this?_ and that positive test makes anchor-consistent knowledge accessible, which is then read as the state of the world.
The lever is the priming -- the anchor does not have to be believed or adjusted from; merely entertaining it reorganizes what comes to mind, and the estimate follows the newly available evidence.

## Echo

The estimate is drawn to the anchor, and the pull survives warning and incentive.
It is the mechanism behind provided anchors -- the ones handed to the persona from outside -- and it is stubborn where mere effort-failure is not: because the evidence base itself is tilted, being told to try harder or paid to be accurate does little, since the persona is reasoning faithfully from a sample the anchor skewed.
It is [adjustment](process_adjustment.md) with the anchor arriving from without rather than from within, and the grip a [planting](process_planting.md) counts on when a figure is set on purpose. The standing tilt this leaves catalogued among the biases is the bias engine's; this owns the live priming of anchor-consistent evidence.
