# Process: Planting

## Taxonomy

[Anchoring](process_anchoring.md), the deliberate form.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: anchoring

## Initiated by

The persona sets a number on purpose -- a first offer, a list price, a figure named early -- to drag another persona's estimate toward it.
This is planting: anchoring turned from an accident of judgment into a move, the anchor placed where it will do work.
What the others suffer as a pull, this persona wields as a lever.

## Direction

Toward a figure of the planter's choosing, imposed on another's estimate.
Planting aims the anchor: a high first offer pulls the settled price up, a low one pulls it down, and the mere act of naming the number first claims the ground every counter is measured from.
The direction is outward and strategic -- not the planter's own estimate bent, but the counterpart's, moved toward a value the planter picked before the reckoning began.

## Lever

The first number, spent to own the scale.
Planting runs on being early and being specific: the opening figure sets the frame, and even a counterpart who knows the move and resists it adjusts insufficiently from it, so the planted value shapes the outcome merely by having been said first.
The lever is priority -- whoever names the anchor names the range -- and a precise, extreme-but-defensible figure grips harder than a round or timid one.

## Echo

The counterpart's estimate is moved, and the planter gains the range.
It is the mechanism behind the first-offer advantage -- the reason opening with a number so often beats waiting to hear one -- and it holds even against expert and warned targets, though an informed counter-anchor can contest the ground.
It is [adjustment](process_adjustment.md) and [accessibility](process_accessibility.md) put to use: the planter supplies the anchor precisely because the other will adjust from it too little and read the world through it. The bargaining process the plant serves is the negotiation engine's; the persuasive framing of an offer by description rather than number is the framing engine's; this owns the setting of the reference to move another's estimate.
