# Process: Anchoring

## Taxonomy

Cognition / the social psychology of judgment under uncertainty.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: anchoring

## Initiated by

A persona forming a numeric or magnitude judgment under uncertainty meets a value that entered first -- a price glimpsed, a figure named, a number that need not even be relevant -- and takes it as the point from which to reason.
This is anchoring: the estimate drawn toward the reference that came before it, the first figure fixing the scale every later guess is read against.
Where a cold judgment would weigh the evidence on its own, anchoring starts from the anchor and works out from there -- and does not work far enough.

## Direction

Toward the anchor, and not far enough away from it.
Anchoring does not replace the estimate with the anchor; it bends the estimate in the anchor's direction, so the final figure lands between what the evidence warrants and what the anchor suggested, pulled off true toward whatever came first.
The higher the anchor, the higher the estimate; the lower, the lower -- the judgment tracks the reference it was given as much as the quantity it was meant to measure.

## Lever

The reference value, treated as information it is not.
What anchoring spends is the mere presence of a number: once a figure is in mind, the persona reasons from it rather than around it, and the adjustment away from it stops as soon as the estimate enters a plausible range -- short of where a full accounting would put it.
The lever is the insufficiency of the correction: the anchor need not be trusted, or even relevant, to hold; it is enough that it was there first and the moving-away ran out of effort too soon.

## Echo

The estimate is bent, and the persona cannot recover the figure they would have named cold.
Anchoring is why the first number matters out of all proportion -- the opening bid, the suggested donation, the list price -- and why it holds even against the knowledge that it is arbitrary: the anchor's shadow falls on every count made after it.
It runs as [adjustment](process_adjustment.md), [accessibility](process_accessibility.md), or [planting](process_planting.md) -- the correction that stops too soon, the anchor that primes its own evidence, the figure set on purpose to move another. The mode of choosing among options is the decision engine's; the bargaining the planted anchor serves is the negotiation engine's; the shift worked by how an option is described rather than by a number is the framing engine's; the standing catalogued tilt this look leaves is the bias engine's. This engine owns the pull toward the reference itself.
