# Process: Adjustment

## Taxonomy

[Anchoring](process_anchoring.md), the self-generated form.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: anchoring

## Initiated by

The persona starts from a value they hold -- a figure they generated themselves, a known nearby quantity -- and steps away from it toward the answer, but stops as soon as the estimate feels plausible.
This is adjustment: anchoring as a correction that runs out too soon, the move away from the starting value cut short.
The anchor is a place to begin, and the beginning drags the end toward it.

## Direction

Toward the truth, but only partway there.
Adjustment does move -- the persona knows the starting value is not the answer and corrects away from it -- but the correction halts at the near edge of the range that seems acceptable, rather than pressing on to the figure the evidence would demand.
The direction is right and the distance is short: the estimate lands on the first plausible value encountered on the way out, nearer the anchor than the truth.

## Lever

The satisfaction of the first plausible answer.
Adjustment spends effort, and effort is scarce: moving away from an anchor is work, so the persona stops the moment the estimate crosses into a believable range, taking the near boundary of plausibility for the center of it.
The lever is where the stepping stops -- terminated at good-enough rather than driven to accurate -- so the more effortful or uncertain the correction, the less of it gets done.

## Echo

The estimate settles short, biased toward wherever the persona began.
It is the mechanism behind self-generated anchors -- the ones the persona supplies from their own knowledge -- and it eases when they are slowed, warned, or made to consider that the true value lies further out, because the failure is one of insufficient effort, not of the anchor's grip.
It is [accessibility](process_accessibility.md) with the anchor coming from within rather than from outside, and the raw material a [planting](process_planting.md) exploits when the anchor is supplied on purpose. The catalogued tilt this leaves on the read of a quantity is the bias engine's; this owns the live correction that stops too soon.
