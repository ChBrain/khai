# Process: The Private Number

## Taxonomy

[The Estimating](process_estimating_bidding.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: bidding

## Initiated by

The party arrives at a figure and it feels like the figure.
Worked out, checked, defensible, and theirs.

## Direction

Toward a single point standing in for a range.
What the party actually holds is an uncertain estimate with a wide interval around it, and what they carry into the room is one number, because a bid has to be a number.
The interval is dropped at the moment of writing, and after that the party reasons about their figure as though it were the value rather than a draw from a distribution.

## Lever

The necessity of committing to a figure against what committing loses.
The carrying force is that a point estimate is required and hedging is not available: a bid cannot be a confidence interval, the party has to decide, and somebody who refused to name a number would simply not participate; the resistance is that the uncertainty is discarded at exactly the step where it was about to matter, so the party defends a point with the confidence appropriate to a range and cannot recover the doubt afterwards.
The Private Number is a range with the range removed.

## Echo

One figure carrying the weight of a distribution.
It shows the uncertainty being dropped by a step nobody could avoid.
The Private Number is dated at the commitment and hardens immediately.
