# Process: The Field

## Taxonomy

[The Contest](process_contest_bidding.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: bidding

## Initiated by

The party finds out how many others are in.
Two, nine, forty. A number that changes how the whole thing feels.

## Direction

Toward a signal everybody reads exactly backwards.
More rivals is taken as confirmation that the thing is worth having, and it is treated as a reason to bid higher. The arithmetic runs the other way: a larger field means the winner is drawn from further out in the tail of the estimates, so the more competition there is, the more the winner will have overpaid.
This is the single most counter-intuitive fact in the domain, and it holds in every experiment anybody has run on it.

## Lever

The reasonableness of reading a crowd as evidence against what a crowd does to the outcome.
The carrying force is that other people's interest genuinely is information and ignoring it would be foolish: forty people wanting something is a fact about the thing, the market is often right, and a party who dismissed the field as noise would misprice most items most of the time; the resistance is that the same crowd that supplies the evidence also supplies the selection, so the number of rivals raises both the party's estimate and the amount by which the winning estimate will exceed the truth.
The Field is read as a reason to bid more and is a reason to bid less.

## Echo

A count that ought to produce caution and produces the opposite.
It shows the mechanism at the point where good judgement inverts.
The Field is dated when the party learns the number and is rarely revised downward.
