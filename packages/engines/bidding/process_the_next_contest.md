# Process: The Next Contest

## Taxonomy

[The Reckoning](process_reckoning_bidding.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: bidding

## Initiated by

Another thing comes up, and the party bids again.
Same methods, same confidence, and a field of new rivals.

## Direction

Toward a correction that is local when it happens at all.
Parties who bid repeatedly in one market do learn to shade their numbers, and the shading is real -- it is the main way anybody ever escapes the curse. What does not travel is the lesson: moved to a different market, a different asset class, or a different kind of thing, the same party bids as though the correction had never been learned.
So the discipline is a property of a market rather than of a person.

## Lever

The genuineness of the learning against how little of it transfers.
The carrying force is that experience does work and the correction is real: seasoned bidders in a familiar market are measurably better calibrated, the adjustment is teachable within a domain, and a party who claimed nothing could be done would be wrong; the resistance is that the correction is stored as a rule of thumb about this market rather than as an understanding of the mechanism, so it does not follow the party out of the room, and the first contest in a new domain is bid by a novice wearing an expert's confidence.
The Next Contest is where the lesson turns out to have been about the market.

## Echo

A party who has learned and is about to demonstrate the limits of it.
It shows why the operation never concludes.
The Next Contest is dated from the next opportunity and starts most of the way back.
