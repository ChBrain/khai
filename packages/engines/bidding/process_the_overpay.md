# Process: The Overpay

## Taxonomy

[The Reckoning](process_reckoning_bidding.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: bidding

## Initiated by

The number turns out to have been high.
Not catastrophically, usually. Somewhat, and steadily, and in a way that shows up as everything being slightly harder than it should be.

## Direction

Toward a shortfall distributed across everything that follows.
An overpayment is not a discrete loss on a date. It is a permanently higher base against which every later result is measured, so it appears as thin margins, as a return that is fine rather than good, as pressure that never quite lifts.
Because it never appears as an event, it is never treated as one.

## Lever

The size of what was lost against the form the loss takes.
The carrying force is that a moderate overpayment is survivable and is often the right risk to have taken: the thing was worth having, a party who bid to their exact estimate would win almost nothing, and being somewhat above is the price of ever winning anything at all; the resistance is that the shortfall compounds against every later decision and is never named in any of them, so the party is running a permanent handicap that has no line in the account.
The Overpay is real, moderate, continuous, and never itemised.

## Echo

A base that everything afterwards is measured against.
It shows why the curse is chronic rather than acute.
The Overpay is dated from the award and is paid in every subsequent year.
