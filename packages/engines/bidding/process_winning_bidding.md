# Process: The Winning

## Taxonomy

[Bidding](process_bidding.md), a mechanism that reads only the top of the distribution.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: bidding

## Initiated by

The bids are compared and one of them is highest.
The Winning starts at the selection itself: [the top estimate](process_the_top_estimate.md) taken rather than the middle one, [the pushing](process_the_pushing.md) that carries it further in the open forms, and [the triumph](process_the_triumph.md), which arrives before any information does.

## Direction

Toward an outcome selected on error rather than on value.
The mechanism does not average the estimates, weight them, or discard the outliers. It takes the maximum -- which is the party furthest above the truth, in every case where the truth is a single number the parties were guessing at.
Nothing has gone wrong. The arrangement did exactly what it was built to do, and what it was built to do is find the highest number.

## Lever

The correctness of taking the best offer against what the best offer is.
The carrying force is that the seller is entitled to the highest bid and the rule is the fairest available: it is transparent, it cannot be gamed by favour, everybody knew it in advance, and any other rule would be worse for the seller and harder to defend; the resistance is that under a common value the highest bid is a measurement of the largest error, so the rule that makes the process fair is the rule that guarantees the winner is the party who was most mistaken.
The Winning selects the maximum, which is not the same as selecting the keenest.

## Echo

A result that is exactly correct and systematically wrong.
It is the movement where the arithmetic becomes visible to anybody standing outside it.
The Winning is dated at the award and reveals nothing to the winner.
