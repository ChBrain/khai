# Process: The Contest

## Taxonomy

[Bidding](process_bidding.md), one object and a field of people who want it.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: bidding

## Initiated by

There is one of the thing and more than one party who wants it.
The Contest starts at the arrangement itself: [the one thing](process_the_one_thing.md) that cannot be divided, [the field](process_the_field.md) of others in for it, and [the wanting seen](process_the_wanting_seen.md), which changes what the thing appears to be worth.

## Direction

Toward a situation where the others are part of the object.
A contested thing is not the same thing uncontested. The presence of rivals supplies information, urgency, and a deadline, and it converts a decision about whether the thing is worth having into a decision about whether to lose it.
Nobody arranges this. It is what happens when a single item meets more than one buyer.

## Lever

The fairness of an open contest against what an open contest adds to the item.
The carrying force is that competition is the honest way to allocate: everybody gets a look, the seller is not choosing favourites, the process is visible, and a party who did not want to compete was free to walk away at any point; the resistance is that the competition is not neutral with respect to what is being valued, since the rivals become a feature of the thing -- their interest is read as evidence, their persistence as urgency -- and the party ends up pricing an item that only exists in the contest.
The Contest supplies information that is not about the object.

## Echo

An item with a crowd attached.
It is the movement that sets the whole operation, and it is nobody's design.
The Contest is dated from the second interested party.
