# Process: The Scatter

## Taxonomy

[The Estimating](process_estimating_bidding.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: bidding

## Initiated by

The numbers, laid side by side, are far apart.
Where somebody can see all of them afterwards, the range is routinely wider than any single party would have guessed.

## Direction

Toward a spread that decides the outcome and is invisible during it.
The distance between the highest and lowest estimate is the engine's whole content: a narrow spread produces a winner who paid about right, and a wide one produces a winner who paid a great deal too much, with the same parties behaving identically in both cases.
And the spread is set by how hard the thing is to value, which is exactly what the parties are unable to judge, because judging it would require the estimates they cannot see.

## Lever

The inevitability of the spread against its complete unavailability to anybody in it.
The carrying force is that dispersion is the honest signature of a hard problem and its absence would be more worrying: identical estimates from independent parties would suggest they were copying each other rather than working, and the spread is what independence looks like; the resistance is that the one number which would tell a party whether to be cautious -- how far apart everybody is -- is the one number the arrangement never produces, and it is only assembled afterwards by whoever collects the bids.
The Scatter is the answer and is held by the seller.

## Echo

A width that everything turns on.
It shows why the engine is about the shape of a set rather than the size of a bid.
The Scatter is dated at the opening of the envelopes and belongs to nobody who bid.
