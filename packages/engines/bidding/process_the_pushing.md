# Process: The Pushing

## Taxonomy

[The Winning](process_winning_bidding.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: bidding

## Initiated by

In the open forms the number keeps moving.
Another increment, another round, another call, and the figure is now above where anybody started.

## Direction

Toward a contest that has stopped being about the thing.
Where bids are visible and sequential, the object recedes and the rivalry takes over: the question becomes whether to be the one who stops, and stopping is the only move that produces a definite loss.
The increments are small, each is defensible, and the total is not something any party would have written down at the beginning.

## Lever

The rationality of each increment against the shape of the sequence.
The carrying force is that every step is genuinely small and genuinely worth it: at each point the party is a little above their number and one increment from the thing, the marginal cost is minor against the total, and refusing to move would forfeit everything already committed; the resistance is that a sequence of individually correct increments has no natural stopping point, so what governs the end is which party's patience or budget runs out first, which is not information about the object at all.
The Pushing is decided by whoever tires last.

## Echo

A price arrived at by attrition.
It shows the open form making the curse worse, and it hands the mechanism of the commitment itself to the escalation engine, which owns it.
The Pushing is dated from the first counter and ends when somebody stops.
