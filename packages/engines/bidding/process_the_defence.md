# Process: The Defence

## Taxonomy

[The Reckoning](process_reckoning_bidding.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: bidding

## Initiated by

The winner explains why the price was right.
To a board, to a partner, to themselves, and with real arguments.

## Direction

Toward a number that has become a position.
Once the bid is won it stops being an estimate and becomes the winner's judgement, so questioning it questions them -- and they will defend it with the same competence they used to arrive at it, which is considerable.
The defence also recruits everything that goes well afterwards as evidence, and assigns everything that goes badly to other causes, which is not dishonesty and is very hard to avoid.

## Lever

The soundness of the defence against what it forecloses.
The carrying force is that the arguments are usually good and the winner is not spinning: the thing did have the qualities they identified, the alternatives really were worse, and demanding that people disown every decision that did not go perfectly would produce worse decision-makers rather than better; the resistance is that a defended number cannot be studied, so the one case the party has closest access to becomes unavailable as evidence -- and the party who most needs to learn the lesson is the one least able to.
The Defence protects the winner and destroys the data.

## Echo

An estimate converted into a commitment.
It shows why the curse is documented in aggregate and denied in every instance.
The Defence is dated from the first question about the price.
