# Process: The Top Estimate

## Taxonomy

[The Winning](process_winning_bidding.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: bidding

## Initiated by

The highest number takes it.
Not the best-reasoned, not the median, not the one with the most work behind it.

## Direction

Toward a selection rule that is blind to everything except magnitude.
Two parties may have done identical work and arrived at different numbers because they had different fragments; the mechanism cannot see the work, only the figure, so it treats the difference between them as a difference in valuation rather than as a difference in error.
Where the parties are each valuing the thing for themselves, that is right and the mechanism is doing well. Where they are all estimating the same underlying figure, it is picking the biggest mistake.

## Lever

The neutrality of the rule against the thing it happens to be neutral about.
The carrying force is that a rule blind to everything but the number is the point: it is what makes the process auditable, it removes discretion, and every alternative introduces judgement that somebody will contest; the resistance is that the same blindness prevents the rule from distinguishing a party who values the thing highly from a party who has miscounted, and only the second case produces a problem -- so the mechanism performs perfectly in one situation and pathologically in another, with no way to tell which it is in.
The Top Estimate cannot tell keenness from error and does not need to.

## Echo

A rule that works exactly as designed.
It shows that the difficulty is structural rather than a failure of anybody's conduct.
The Top Estimate is dated at the comparison and is not a judgement.
