# Process: The Unshared

## Taxonomy

[The Estimating](process_estimating_bidding.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: bidding

## Initiated by

Each party knows something the others do not, and none of it is pooled.
One has surveyed the ground, one has the previous owner's accounts, one has walked the site in February.

## Direction

Toward a fix that the arrangement forbids.
Pooled, the fragments would produce an estimate far better than any of the private ones, and every party would be better off for it. The contest makes pooling the one thing nobody can do: shared information is an advantage given away, and a party who volunteered theirs would be bidding against a better-informed field.
So the mechanism guarantees that the collective is worse informed than its members could be, and each party is right not to fix it.

## Lever

The value of pooling against the impossibility of pooling here.
The carrying force is that withholding is correct and is not bad faith: information is what the party spent money to obtain, giving it to rivals would be a straightforward loss, and nobody is under any obligation to improve the competition's estimates; the resistance is that every party reasons this way, so the arrangement systematically discards most of what is known about the object, and the winner is drawn from a field made worse at estimating by the same structure that made them compete.
The Unshared is what everybody keeps and nobody benefits from.

## Echo

A pile of knowledge with no way to reach the decision.
It shows the spread being manufactured by the contest rather than by anybody's limits.
The Unshared is dated from the start and is never assembled.
