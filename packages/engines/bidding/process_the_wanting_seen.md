# Process: The Wanting Seen

## Taxonomy

[The Contest](process_contest_bidding.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: bidding

## Initiated by

Other people's wanting is visible, and it does something.
The rival who came back for a second viewing, the competitor who sent their best person, the offer that arrived within the hour.

## Direction

Toward an appetite that has been supplied from outside.
A party arriving with a mild interest and finding others urgent does not stay mildly interested. The urgency transfers, and it transfers as a feeling about the object rather than as a belief about other people.
So the party's own wanting -- which is the input they trust most -- has been partly manufactured by the contest they are in.

## Lever

The honesty of the transfer against the fact that it is a transfer.
The carrying force is that catching wanting from other people is not a defect and is frequently accurate: enthusiasm is contagious because it is often warranted, other buyers have often seen something the party missed, and somebody immune to it would be a poor judge of what is good; the resistance is that the feeling arrives labelled as a judgement about the item, so the party cannot separate what they wanted on arrival from what the room gave them, and the number they write down includes both.
The Wanting Seen is other people's appetite, felt as one's own.

## Echo

A desire with a source outside the object.
It shows where the estimate has been contaminated before any estimating happens.
The Wanting Seen is dated at the first sight of a rival and does not fade before the bid.
