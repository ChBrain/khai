# Process: The One Thing

## Taxonomy

[The Contest](process_contest_bidding.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: bidding

## Initiated by

The object cannot be split.
One house, one contract, one post, one lease. Half of it is worth nothing to anybody.

## Direction

Toward an all-or-nothing outcome with no middle result.
Indivisibility is what makes the arrangement a contest rather than a market: there is no price at which everybody gets some, so the parties are not finding a level, they are producing a winner.
And a party who comes second gets exactly what a party who never bid gets, which is nothing -- so the whole spread of effort between them was spent for no return.

## Lever

The clarity of a single outcome against what having only one does to the bidding.
The carrying force is that indivisibility is a fact about the thing rather than a defect in the arrangement: houses do not divide, a post has one holder, and pretending otherwise would produce worse outcomes than an honest contest; the resistance is that all-or-nothing removes every intermediate result, so a party's cost of stopping rises continuously while their cost of continuing rises only by increments, which is the shape that produces bad decisions in everybody.
The One Thing makes second place identical to not having tried.

## Echo

A prize that cannot be shared out.
It shows why the mechanism cannot be softened without becoming a different mechanism.
The One Thing is dated from whatever makes it singular.
