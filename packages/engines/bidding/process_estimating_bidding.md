# Process: The Estimating

## Taxonomy

[Bidding](process_bidding.md), the private number each party arrives at.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: bidding

## Initiated by

Each party works out what the thing is worth, alone.
The Estimating starts where the pricing happens: [a private number](process_the_private_number.md) built from what one party can see, [a scatter](process_the_scatter.md) across all the numbers nobody can observe, and [the unshared](process_the_unshared.md) information that would fix it and is never pooled.

## Direction

Toward a set of estimates that are individually careful and collectively dispersed.
Every party is doing the work properly: they survey, they model, they discount, they consult. And each is working from a different fragment of the evidence, so the answers come out spread around whatever the truth is, in both directions.
The spread is not incompetence. It is what estimating under uncertainty looks like when done well by several people separately.

## Lever

The quality of each estimate against the width of the set they form.
The carrying force is that the estimates are good and getting better would not help much: the parties are professionals, the methods are sound, the surveys are real, and there is no version of this where somebody simply knows the answer; the resistance is that the whole outcome is decided by the width of the distribution rather than by its centre, and no individual party can observe the width -- each of them sees one number, which is their own, and has no way to learn where in the spread it sits.
The Estimating is done well by everybody and the spread is the only thing that matters.

## Echo

A distribution nobody can see.
It is the movement everything else runs on and the one no participant has access to.
The Estimating is dated before the bid and is never compared.
