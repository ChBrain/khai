# Process: The Triumph

## Taxonomy

[The Winning](process_winning_bidding.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: bidding

## Initiated by

The party is told they have it, and they are delighted.
The call, the letter, the handshake, the moment the rivals fall away.

## Direction

Toward a celebration held at the point of least information.
Nothing has been learned. The party knows only that their number was above everybody else's, which is the one fact that should give them pause, and it arrives feeling like vindication.
It is also the moment at which the price becomes theirs: from here on the figure is not a bid, it is their judgement, and defending the thing means defending the number.

## Lever

The genuineness of the pleasure against what the pleasure is a response to.
The carrying force is that winning is a real achievement and the party is right to be pleased: they wanted the thing, they got it, the work was theirs, and the pleasure of prevailing over rivals is one of the ordinary satisfactions of doing anything competitive; the resistance is that the event being celebrated -- having been the highest -- is the only piece of evidence in the whole affair that bears on whether they overpaid, and it points the wrong way.
The Triumph is the first news of the curse, received as its opposite.

## Echo

A party congratulating themselves on the one worrying fact available.
It shows why the reckoning arrives so much later than the information does.
The Triumph is dated at the award and closes off the question.
