# Process: The Two Performances

## Taxonomy

[The Room](process_room_interview.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: interview

## Initiated by

Both sides are selling and both know it.
The candidate presents a version of themselves; the interviewer presents a version of the place.

## Direction

Toward an exchange in which neither account is the ordinary one.
The candidate has prepared, rehearsed the difficult question, and chosen which projects to name. The interviewer describes a role that is more coherent than the role, a team more settled than the team, and a manager they may themselves find difficult.
Both are being truthful in the way that people advertising something are truthful, and each treats the other's version as evidence.

## Lever

The legitimacy of both presentations against what is inferred from them.
The carrying force is that preparing is correct and expected on both sides: a candidate who did not rehearse would be read as uninterested, an employer who described the job's worst month would be failing at recruitment, and presenting well is itself a skill many of these jobs require; the resistance is that the interviewer scores the candidate's preparation as candour and the candidate accepts the employer's description as a report, so the two most rehearsed statements in the process become the two most relied on.
The Two Performances are honest advertisements read as disclosures.

## Echo

Two curated accounts, each taken at face value by the other party.
It shows why the encounter is not a source of plain information even when nobody lies.
The Two Performances are dated to the room and are dropped within a fortnight of the start date.
