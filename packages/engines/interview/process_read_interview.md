# Process: The Read

## Taxonomy

[Interview](process_interview.md), what an interviewer takes from an hour.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: interview

## Initiated by

The candidate leaves and the interviewer has an impression.
The Read is what formed while they talked: [an early verdict](process_the_early_verdict.md) reached in minutes, [a fit test](process_the_fit_test.md) nobody wrote into the criteria, and [a told story](process_the_told_story.md) that carries more weight than the record it describes.

## Direction

Toward a judgement made of the few things an hour can actually deliver.
An hour delivers manner, warmth, fluency, references to things the interviewer recognises, and the shape of a narrative. It does not deliver reliability, judgement under pressure, or what the person is like in month nine.
So the interviewer reads what is present and reports having read what matters, and the substitution is invisible from inside.

## Lever

The realness of what is perceived against the distance between it and the question.
The carrying force is that the perceptions are accurate as perceptions and often useful: people really do differ in how they explain their work, an interviewer who liked nobody would be no use, and warmth and clarity are genuine job-relevant qualities in most roles that involve other people; the resistance is that the criteria the panel agreed to score are not these, and the hour supplies these ones in abundance and the agreed ones barely at all, so the scoring drifts toward whatever the room happened to make available.
The Read is a true account of the wrong hour's worth of evidence.

## Echo

A confident impression built from the material that was in the room.
It is the movement in which the decision is effectively taken, before the one in which it is explained.
The Read is dated from the first minutes and is closed before the candidate stands up.
