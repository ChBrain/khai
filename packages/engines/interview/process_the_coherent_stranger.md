# Process: The Coherent Stranger

## Taxonomy

[The Sense Made](process_sense_interview.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: interview

## Initiated by

A whole character is in hand at the end of the hour.
Motives, working style, how they will be under pressure, what they are like on a Thursday afternoon.

## Direction

Toward a portrait far larger than the fragments it was made from.
What was in the room was a handful of answers, a manner, and some biography. What leaves the room is a person the interviewer could describe to somebody else in a paragraph, with predictions attached.
The gap between the two is closed silently, by the ordinary machinery of understanding other people, which is not doing anything wrong.

## Lever

The necessity of a working model of a person against the thinness of this one's inputs.
The carrying force is that the model has to be built and no decision is possible without one: the panel is being asked to predict behaviour over years, prediction needs a model, and the alternative -- reporting only what was literally observed -- would leave the institution unable to choose at all; the resistance is that the confidence attaching to the model is set by its coherence and not by its inputs, so a portrait built from an hour feels exactly as solid as one built from a year of working alongside somebody.
The Coherent Stranger is a full account of an acquaintance of forty minutes.
The general appetite for a coherent picture is bias's and surprise's; this reads only that the interview satisfies it every time.

## Echo

A stranger described with the confidence of a colleague.
It shows the size of the inference nobody notices making.
The Coherent Stranger is dated to the end of the hour.
