# Process: The Reasons After

## Taxonomy

[The Sense Made](process_sense_interview.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: interview

## Initiated by

The verdict is written up for the file, the panel, and the person who has to approve it.
After the verdict.

## Direction

Toward a record of grounds that were assembled to support a conclusion already reached.
The write-up is not a lie and is usually careful. It cites moments from the hour, quotes an answer, notes a hesitation -- all of which happened. What it does not contain is the minute in which the decision was actually taken, because nobody has access to that.
The document then outlives everybody's memory of the room and becomes the institution's account of why this person was appointed.

## Lever

The discipline of having to justify against what the justification is made of.
The carrying force is that requiring written reasons is a real safeguard and removing it would be worse: it forces the panel to name something, it catches the decision that cannot be defended in any words at all, and it gives a rejected candidate something to be told; the resistance is that the reasons are composed downstream of the verdict from an hour that would have supported several verdicts, so the safeguard certifies the decision rather than testing it.
The Reasons After document a judgement they did not produce.
The general form of accounting for a decision belongs to the moral-account composite and to attribution; this reads only the selection file.

## Echo

A written justification that arrived second.
It shows how a room's impression becomes an institution's evidence.
The Reasons After are dated after the decision and are filed as though before it.
