# Process: The Room

## Taxonomy

[Interview](process_interview.md), the occasion and the instrument it improvises.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: interview

## Initiated by

Two people who have never met sit down with a decision between them.
The Room starts at the appointment: [a short hour](process_the_short_hour.md) standing in for years of work, [two performances](process_the_two_performances.md) running at once, and [a free hand](process_the_free_hand.md) for whoever is asking the questions.

## Direction

Toward a measuring device assembled on the spot by the person doing the measuring.
Almost nothing about the encounter is fixed. The questions are chosen in the moment, the order varies, the follow-ups depend on how the first answers land, and the scoring happens in the same head that set the questions.
So each candidate is examined by a slightly different instrument, and the results are compared as though they came from one.

## Lever

The value of a real conversation against what a conversation cannot be compared with.
The carrying force is that the looseness is doing something and interviewers know it: a scripted exchange misses the thing worth chasing, the good follow-up is the one nobody could have written down in advance, and a panel that reads from a card learns less about a difficult candidate than one allowed to push; the resistance is that comparison needs the same instrument twice, so the freedom that makes any single interview better makes the set of them incommensurable -- and the decision is a comparison, not a series of impressions.
The Room improvises the instrument and then trusts the readings.

## Echo

An examination with no two papers alike.
It is the movement everything else runs inside, and the one nobody experiences as a design choice.
The Room is dated from the appointment and lasts about an hour.
