# Process: The Missing Half

## Taxonomy

[The Keeper](process_keeper_interview.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: interview

## Initiated by

Only the appointed are ever observed.
The rejected go elsewhere and are never heard of again by anybody who decided.

## Direction

Toward a record that cannot show a mistake.
The candidate who was hired and did well is remembered as a good call. The candidate who was hired and did badly is explained by circumstances, or by the reference that was not chased, or by the manager. The forty who were turned away produce no data at all, and the one among them who would have been excellent is unimaginable to the panel by construction.
So an interviewer accumulates decades of practice and no feedback, and the feeling of having got better is the only measure available.

## Lever

The reasonableness of learning from outcomes against a record with one side missing.
The carrying force is that panels genuinely try to learn and often look back: the good appointments are discussed, the bad ones are reviewed, and an institution that ignored its hiring outcomes entirely would be worse than one that studies half of them; the resistance is that the half available is the half selected by the very judgement under examination, so every review confirms the method that produced the sample -- and the correction that would matter is the one no institution can run.
The Missing Half means experience at interviewing cannot accumulate into accuracy.
The general mechanism of a sample selected on the outcome is bias's; this reads only that hiring is where it is structural.

## Echo

Decades of practice with no scoreboard.
It shows why confidence rises with experience while accuracy does not.
The Missing Half is dated from every decision and is never assembled.
