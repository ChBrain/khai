# Process: The Told Story

## Taxonomy

[The Read](process_read_interview.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: interview

## Initiated by

The candidate accounts for their own past, and the account is what gets scored.
The gap year, the two short jobs, the project that failed, the reason for leaving.

## Direction

Toward the quality of a narrative standing in for the quality of the history.
The same record can be told as drift or as deliberate breadth, and the telling is what is present in the room while the record is a line on a page. A candidate who has rehearsed a clean account of a messy few years is read as somebody whose years were less messy; one who answers honestly and haltingly about the same shape of history is read as evasive.
What is being measured is the fluency of the retelling, which is a real skill and a different one.

## Lever

The fairness of letting somebody explain against what the explaining rewards.
The carrying force is that the chance to account for a record is a protection and removing it would be worse: a bare record is read at its most damning, the candidate with a caring gap or an illness or a bad manager deserves the sentence in which they say so, and giving that sentence is basic decency in a selection process; the resistance is that the protection is claimed unequally, since the person who has been coached, who is at ease with panels, and who has told the story before gets a return on the same facts that a nervous or unpractised candidate does not.
The Told Story converts the same history into different evidence.

## Echo

A record scored at the quality of its narration.
It shows where practice at being interviewed becomes indistinguishable from being suitable.
The Told Story is dated to the question about the gap.
