# Process: Interview

## Taxonomy

Selection by encounter (no khai file of its own yet).

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: interview

## Initiated by

A stranger is booked for an hour, and a place will be given or withheld on it.
Interview starts wherever a candidate is judged in a meeting arranged for the purpose: the hiring panel, the admissions board, the visa desk, the tenancy viewing, the grant committee, the conversation that decides a promotion.
It is not the disagreement between a rule and a judgement, which overriding reads, and not the self a candidate is performing, which is presentation's. It is the occasion itself, and what an hour with a stranger does to the person who has to decide.

## Direction

Toward a decision made in a room and credited to what was seen there.
The engine reads four movements: [the room](process_room_interview.md), the occasion and the instrument it improvises; [the read](process_read_interview.md), what an interviewer takes from an hour; [the sense made](process_sense_interview.md), how twenty minutes becomes a whole person; and [the keeper](process_keeper_interview.md), the twist -- why the practice survives every demonstration against it.
The four run once per candidate and run again unchanged for the next one.

## Lever

The obvious reasonableness of meeting somebody against what a meeting is able to carry.
The carrying force is that no serious institution appoints a stranger unseen and nobody seriously proposes it: a person is more than a file, an encounter catches things no record holds, candidates expect one and would read its absence as contempt, and the structured version -- same questions, same order, scored as they are answered -- is among the better predictors anybody has; the resistance is that the version almost always run is the loose one, and a loose interview is an instrument redesigned for every candidate, scored by the person who just built it, on a sample of one hour.
An interview is read on the certainty it produced, never on the information it collected.

## Echo

Somebody who has decided, and is sure, and could not tell you from what.
Interview does not close; it is a judgement being manufactured in an hour, gathered into one reading -- a room, a read, a sense made, a keeper.
The engine's twist is here. **The interview contributes least to the decision and supplies all of the confidence in it.** The file, the record, the work sample, and the test each carry more of what can be known about a candidate, and not one of them produces the sensation of having met somebody; the hour does, and the decision is then hung on the sensation. Which is why the loose interview cannot be argued out of an institution: what is defended is not its accuracy, it is the only part of the process that feels like judging rather than filing.
It hands off what it is not: the disagreement between an instrument and a practitioner is overriding's; the candidate's management of their own front is presentation's and face's; the halo and the overconfidence are bias's positions; what the job turns out to demand once won is employment's; the credit given to an account is credibility's; and a panel staged to **stand for** a theme is authored key, the play's pitch.
The persona is dated from the last room they were judged in.
