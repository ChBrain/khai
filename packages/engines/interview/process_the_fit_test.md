# Process: The Fit Test

## Taxonomy

[The Read](process_read_interview.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: interview

## Initiated by

The question under the questions is whether this person is one of us.
Asked in no criterion, answered in every interview.

## Direction

Toward similarity assessed as quality.
What evaluators reach for, when they explain a decision, is fit: whether the candidate is somebody they could spend a long delay in an airport with, whether they lit up at the same things, whether their leisure and bearing and way of talking about themselves matched the room's. The material of that judgement is largely class-marked -- the sports, the schools, the travel, the confident tone -- and the people who share it experience the match as brilliance rather than as resemblance.
Nobody in the room is trying to hire their own kind. Each of them is trying to hire somebody excellent, and excellence keeps looking like them.

## Lever

The reality of team fit against what the test is actually made of.
The carrying force is that fit is not a euphemism and matters: a person who cannot work with the team will fail regardless of their record, culture is a real constraint, and hiring somebody the panel could not talk to would be an expensive kindness; the resistance is that the version applied in an hour cannot separate compatible from familiar, so an intake selected on fit reproduces the room that selected it, and each round of it has looked to the people in the room like the best candidate winning.
The Fit Test finds resemblance and reports merit.

## Echo

An intake that looks like the panel and was chosen on the merits.
It shows the mechanism by which selection stays closed without anybody closing it.
The Fit Test is dated from the small talk and is defended in the summing up.
