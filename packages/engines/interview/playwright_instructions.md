# Instructions: Interview

How a Playwright wires the interview engine into a play. You wire by linking the play's own content to the engine's files; the engine files stay untouched, they are canon. The Roadie plumbs the engine's law into Instructions Knowledge on deploy. Authoring guidance, not runtime content, and it does not go on tour.

## Human

- The human keeps the panel conscientious: the engine needs no prejudice and stops working the moment it is handed a villain, so make the questions reasonable and the candidate well treated. Show the instrument being improvised rather than announced, because nobody in the room experiences the looseness as a design choice. And decide what each persona says afterwards about why, since that is the sentence the whole engine is aimed at.

## Agent

- From the play's own content, a persona links under Projection where they stand to the encounter: which side of the table they were on, what they took from the hour, what account they have given since, and how many of these rooms they have sat in. Read the engine on the certainty the hour produced, never on the information it collected. Keep it the encounter: the disagreement between an instrument and a practitioner is overriding's, the front the candidate manages is presentation's, the halo and the selected sample are bias's. You link to the engine, you never edit it.

## Collaboration

- The instrument-versus-judgement dispute is the overriding composite's and expertise's and measure's; the candidate's managed front is presentation's, face's, and deception's; the halo, the overconfidence, and the sample selected on its outcome are bias's; the appetite for a coherent picture is bias's and surprise's; accounting for a decision afterwards is the moral-account composite's and attribution's; the effort bargain once the job is won is employment's; what a fair hearing buys and the credit given to an account are the heeding composite's, recognition's, and credibility's; who was able to reach the room at all is capital's, heritage's, and gentrification's. Job specifications, scoring rubrics, and employment law are the trade and set the format. Whether a production's panel was reading capability or reading resemblance, and whether a written justification records the decision or certifies it, are the team's review.

## Knowledge

- An interview is a stranger judged in a meeting arranged for the purpose, and what an hour does to the person who has to decide. It is not overriding's disagreement between a standardised instrument and a practitioner, not presentation's management of a front, and not bias's halo. It wires on a persona at Projection and runs in four movements. The room improvises the instrument and then trusts the readings: an hour samples talking about the work rather than the work, and samples well only how somebody handles being questioned by a stranger who holds something they want; both sides give rehearsed accounts and each reads the other's as disclosure; and the free hand, which makes any single interview better, makes the set of them incommensurable, because comparison needs the same instrument twice. The read is a true account of the wrong hour's worth of evidence: a verdict formed in the first minutes turns the remaining questions into a defence rather than a test; fit is assessed as quality when its material is largely resemblance, so an intake selected on fit reproduces the room that selected it with nobody intending it; and a record is scored at the fluency of its retelling, which is a real skill and a different one. The sense made is why the room never feels wasted: interviewers questioning candidates instructed to answer at random still formed confident pictures and still preferred the interview to the file, so the instrument has no reading for absence, and the written reasons are composed after the verdict from an hour that would have supported several. The engine's twist is at the root: the interview contributes least to the decision and supplies all of the confidence in it -- which is why it survives every demonstration against it, since what is defended is not its accuracy but the only part of the process that feels like judging rather than filing.

## System

- Do keep the panel conscientious. The engine needs no prejudice, and staging a bigoted interviewer replaces the mechanism with a villain.
- Do improvise the instrument on stage. The questions get chosen in the moment, the follow-ups depend on how the first answers landed, and nobody notices that each candidate is being examined differently.
- Do let both sides perform. The candidate has rehearsed and the employer is describing a job more coherent than the job, and both are being truthful the way advertisements are truthful.
- Do form the verdict early and keep interviewing. The later questions are softer for the candidate already liked and harder for the one already doubted, and both sets get answered.
- Do put fit in somebody's mouth as praise. The airport-delay test, the shared enthusiasm, the ease of the conversation -- said as an observation about quality, and made of resemblance.
- Do let the picture form on nothing. A flat hour, an odd answer, a candidate who said very little, and a complete character walks out of the room with the panel.
- Do write the reasons afterwards. Careful, honest, citing real moments, and composed downstream of a decision nobody has access to the minute of.
- Do close on the next vacancy rather than the appointment. The scoring sheet is in the pack, the exception is reasonable, and the free hand comes back.
- Do not model the instrument dispute (overriding, expertise, measure), the candidate's front (presentation, face, deception), the halo or selected sample (bias), the appetite for coherence (bias, surprise), the account given afterwards (moral-account, attribution), the job once won (employment), or who could reach the room (capital, heritage). Do not make the panel stand for a theme; that is authored key, the play's pitch. Do not edit the engine's files; wire only from the play's side.
