# Process: The Short Hour

## Taxonomy

[The Room](process_room_interview.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: interview

## Initiated by

A working life is sampled for forty-five minutes.
Against which will be set several years of doing the thing, most of it unobserved.

## Direction

Toward a sample too small and too unlike the work to stand for it.
The hour contains talking about work rather than work: no deadline, no colleagues, no dull middle, no bad week. What it does sample well is how somebody handles being asked questions by a stranger who holds something they want.
For the small number of jobs that mostly consist of that, the sample is apt. For the rest it is a different activity performed under observation.

## Lever

The necessity of a bounded encounter against how little of the job fits inside it.
The carrying force is that the hour is what can be had and its brevity is nobody's fault: the institution cannot run a trial month for forty applicants, the candidate cannot take unpaid weeks to prove themselves, and a short standard slot is fairer to the candidate with a job already than any longer arrangement would be; the resistance is that everybody then reasons from the sample as though it were representative, and no amount of care inside the hour repairs the fact that the hour is not the work.
The Short Hour is the right length for the institution and the wrong length for the question.

## Echo

A sample of the wrong activity, taken at the right convenience.
It shows the constraint from which the rest of the engine follows.
The Short Hour is dated to the calendar slot.
