# Process: The Absorbed Answer

## Taxonomy

[The Sense Made](process_sense_interview.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: interview

## Initiated by

Whatever the candidate says is taken in and made to mean something.
Including, in the experiment that established this, answers generated at random.

## Direction

Toward material that fits the picture no matter what the material is.
An odd answer becomes evidence of an unusual mind; a flat one becomes evidence of steadiness; a contradiction becomes depth or nerves depending on which way the picture was already leaning. Interviewers told outright that the answers might be random still felt they had learned something, and still wanted the interview.
The absorption is not gullibility. It is the same interpretive work that makes a difficult colleague comprehensible, applied to an input with nothing in it.

## Lever

The generosity of interpretation against the fact that it cannot decline.
The carrying force is that reading charitably and looking for what somebody meant is a virtue in an interviewer and improves the process for anxious candidates: a panel that took every awkward sentence at its worst would select for polish alone, and the interpretive effort is how a nervous good candidate gets through at all; the resistance is that an interpreter who can accommodate anything has no way to register that this particular hour told them nothing, so the hour that should have come back empty comes back full.
The Absorbed Answer means the instrument has no reading for absence.

## Echo

Noise received as signal, by somebody doing their job well.
It shows why interviewer skill does not rescue the loose interview.
The Absorbed Answer is dated to each reply and is never flagged.
