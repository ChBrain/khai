# Process: The Next Panel

## Taxonomy

[The Keeper](process_keeper_interview.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: interview

## Initiated by

The vacancy reopens and the reform has quietly gone.
The scoring sheet is still in the pack, and nobody fills it in until afterwards.

## Direction

Toward a structure that is adopted and then relaxed without a decision.
Reform arrives after a bad appointment or a legal exposure: fixed questions, anchored scales, scores recorded as they are given. It holds for a round or two. Then somebody skips a question because the answer was obvious, a follow-up is allowed because the candidate raised something interesting, and the sheet gets completed at the end from memory.
Every one of those steps is defensible in the moment, made by a person acting sensibly, and their sum is the loose interview back again.

## Lever

The sensibleness of each relaxation against what the relaxations restore.
The carrying force is that a rigid procedure administered without judgement is genuinely bad and the panels know it: an interviewer who reads the next scripted question after a candidate has just disclosed something important is failing them, and the small departures are usually the humane ones; the resistance is that the departures are not evenly distributed, since they are made most for the candidate the panel already likes, which is precisely the case the structure existed to check.
The Next Panel restores the free hand one reasonable exception at a time.

## Echo

A method that reverts between vacancies.
It shows why the finding has been known for forty years and has changed almost nothing.
The Next Panel is dated from the last reform and is complete within a few rounds.
