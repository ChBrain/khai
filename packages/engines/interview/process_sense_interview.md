# Process: The Sense Made

## Taxonomy

[Interview](process_interview.md), how twenty minutes becomes a whole person.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: interview

## Initiated by

The interviewer has a picture, and the picture is complete.
The Sense Made is how it got that way: [a coherent stranger](process_the_coherent_stranger.md) assembled from fragments, [an absorbed answer](process_the_absorbed_answer.md) that fits whatever it was, and [reasons after](process_the_reasons_after.md) written up as the grounds.

## Direction

Toward an instrument that cannot return nothing.
The finding this movement rests on is a strange one and it is about the interview specifically: interviewers who questioned candidates giving deliberately random answers still came away with a picture, still rated it informative, and still preferred it to the record. The answers had no content and the picture formed anyway.
So the interview has no null result. Whatever happens in the hour, an account of the person comes out, and the account's completeness is not evidence that the hour contained anything.

## Lever

The competence of ordinary understanding against a setting where it cannot fail.
The carrying force is that building a picture of somebody from little is not a defect and is most of what social life consists of: the interviewer is doing what people are unusually good at, they are not credulous, and refusing to form any view of a person one has just spent an hour with is not available to anybody; the resistance is that the same capacity produces the same confident picture from a rich hour and an empty one, which means the picture cannot be used to tell them apart -- and the interview supplies no other way of telling.
The Sense Made is the reason the room never feels wasted.

## Echo

A complete person, assembled, with the assembly invisible.
It is the movement that explains why the practice is trusted from inside and fails from outside.
The Sense Made is dated to the hour and is experienced as observation.
