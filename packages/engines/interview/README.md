# Interview

Interview as process: a stranger judged in a meeting arranged for the purpose, read on the certainty it produced rather than on the information it collected -- not the disagreement between an instrument and a practitioner, which overriding reads, and not the front the candidate is managing, which is presentation's -- across four movements: the room (a short hour that samples the wrong activity at the right convenience, two rehearsed performances read by each side as disclosures, and a free hand that is defended as skill and behaves as noise), the read (an early verdict that makes the rest of the hour a defence, a fit test that finds resemblance and reports merit, and a told story that converts the same history into different evidence), the sense made (a coherent stranger described with the confidence of a colleague, an absorbed answer proving the instrument has no reading for absence, and reasons after that document a judgement they did not produce), and the keeper (a missing half that stops experience accumulating into accuracy, a shared ceremony owed to the candidate and then used against the file, and a next panel that restores the free hand one reasonable exception at a time) -- with the twist that the interview contributes least to the decision and supplies all of the confidence in it.

This engine is defined by its [manifest](package.json), which the canon renders as the WIRES card. The manifest is the single source of truth; this README is generated, not edited by hand.
