# Process: Tipping-point

## Taxonomy

Nonlinear dynamics -- the critical transition.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: tipping-point

## Initiated by

A load that has been building without a sign of strain.
Tipping-point begins wherever a running process absorbs an accumulating pressure while holding its shape -- a bond taking grievance, a slope taking snow, a market taking leverage, a wing taking angle -- and gives, up to the very edge, no proportional warning of how close it is to giving way.
Nothing about the surface says the threshold is near; the system is as steady in appearance the moment before as an hour before, because it genuinely is steady -- until the load has brought it to a point past which one more increment cannot be absorbed.

## Direction

Not a slide but a jump: flat, flat, flat, and then, at once, gone.
The course runs in phases the reverse of drift's slope -- [loading](process_loading.md), the strain taken on unseen; [criticality](process_criticality.md), the edge where a trivial trigger comes to decide everything; [snap](process_snap.md), the discontinuous fall to a new state out of all proportion to that trigger; and [ratchet](process_ratchet.md), the twist -- the new state locked, the old one no longer reachable by undoing the trigger that tipped it.
It moves the process from a stable basin, over a threshold, into a different stable basin, and the crossing is fast where everything before it was slow: the catastrophe Thom set on the cusp, the regime shift Scheffer found in lakes and societies alike, the collapse that reads from inside as sudden and from outside as long overdue.

## Lever

The accumulated load against the system's resilience, and the last increment against a threshold already reached.
The carrying force is that load builds where resilience quietly erodes, so the distance to the edge shrinks unseen while the surface holds; the resistance is the system's capacity to absorb -- real, and reassuring, and finite. What decides the moment is not the size of the final push but the state of the system when it lands: the same dropped mug, the same cross word, the same grain of sand is nothing at all far from the edge and everything at it.
This is why the trigger is always the wrong thing to blame: it is the last unit of a load long since gathered, the occasion of the collapse and never its cause, and the search for a proportionate reason -- "over this?" -- misreads a threshold crossing as an event.

## Echo

A different world, and no way back through the door it came in by.
Tipping-point closes on a new stable state, discontinuous from the old and inherited whole by whatever comes next: the bond broken, the slope bare, the market re-priced, the regime fallen -- and, by the [ratchet](process_ratchet.md), holding, so that reversing the trigger does not reverse the state.
It hands off what it is not: the slow uncorrected slide that loaded the system is drift's (drift is all slope and no moment; this is all moment); the loud reciprocal spiral is escalation's; the bare inversion of direction is reversal's; the adaptation to the loss once it has landed is grief's. This engine owns the crossing itself -- the accumulation that looks like safety, and the snap that was never proportional to its trigger.
