# Process: Criticality

## Taxonomy

[Tipping-point](process_tipping-point.md), the phase of the edge where a trivial trigger comes to decide everything.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: tipping-point

## Initiated by

The load has brought the system to the threshold, and it now hangs on the next small thing.
Criticality starts wherever a loaded process reaches the edge of its stable basin -- the point Bak's sandpile reaches when one more grain can start an avalanche, the point Scheffer's lake reaches when its resilience is nearly spent -- so that the system is now maximally sensitive, poised, its outcome no longer set by large forces but by the least of them.
It is the state of being on the brink: not the slow filling before it and not the fall after, but the taut, unmarked moment when everything the loading gathered has come due and waits on a trigger.

## Direction

Toward maximal sensitivity, where the response to a nudge becomes unbounded.
Criticality sharpens the system to a point -- what would have been absorbed without trace an hour ago now decides the whole, because the margin that would have absorbed it is gone; the same word, the same grain, the same degree of angle that meant nothing far from the edge means everything at it.
It moves the process to the place where cause and magnitude come apart: a trivial trigger and a total consequence, the dropped mug and the ended marriage -- and, near the edge, the quiet tells (the system slow to recover from small knocks, its wobbles widening) that resilience is nearly spent, if anyone is reading for them.

## Lever

The trivial trigger against a threshold already reached by everything before it.
The carrying force is not in the trigger at all -- it is in the state: a system at criticality will tip on the next increment whatever it is, so the actual trigger is merely whichever small thing arrives first, and could have been any of a hundred others. The resistance is whatever thin margin remains, and it is thin by definition; that is what criticality means.
This is the phase that fools every witness: the trigger is visible and small, the load is invisible and large, so the eye assigns the cause to what it can see -- "over this?" -- and misreads the last grain as the weight, the occasion as the reason.

## Echo

A system on a hair-trigger, its fall now a matter of when and not whether.
Criticality leaves the process poised at the threshold -- primed, sensitized, awaiting the increment that any moment will supply -- and hands that brink to the [snap](process_snap.md) that the next trigger will release.
It is the phase where prevention still just barely lives and blame is already being misassigned: a system at the edge can sometimes be drawn back if the load itself is eased, but it cannot be saved by bracing against the trigger, because the trigger was never the problem.
Criticality is read from the sensitivity, the process brought by its load to the point where the smallest thing decides it.
