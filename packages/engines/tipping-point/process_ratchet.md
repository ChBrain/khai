# Process: Ratchet

## Taxonomy

[Tipping-point](process_tipping-point.md), the phase where the new state locks and the old one no longer returns.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: tipping-point

## Initiated by

The system has fallen, and undoing the trigger does not undo the fall.
Ratchet starts wherever a snapped process settles into a new stable state that is its own basin, so that reversing the small thing that tipped it fails to reverse it -- the mug picked up, the apology made, the angle eased, and still the marriage ended, the wing stalled, the trust gone; the door it came through does not open backward.
It is the one-way catch of the transition: the recognition that a threshold crossed is not a threshold un-crossed by retracing the last step, because the system now rests where retracing does not reach.

## Direction

Held, against every attempt to return the way it came.
Ratchet keeps the new state -- Scheffer's alternative stable state, the hysteresis by which a lake pushed to turbid does not clear when the nutrients that tipped it are merely brought back to the level they tipped at; the wing that will not re-fly until dropped far below the angle where it stalled; the relationship that a resumed kindness does not restore.
It moves nothing forward and lets nothing back: to return, the system must be driven not to the threshold it crossed but far past it in the other direction, a cost out of all proportion to the trigger that was enough to tip it -- so that the ease of falling and the difficulty of returning are the same asymmetry, read twice.

## Lever

The new basin's own stability against the memory and the wish for the old one.
The carrying force is that the post-snap state is genuinely stable -- it holds itself, it is not a perch but a floor -- so the pull of the old state, however strong the longing, meets a system that has no gradient back; the resistance is the depth of the new basin, and it is deep by the same token that made the fall complete.
This is the phase that turns a collapse from an event into a fact: what snapped could perhaps have been prevented at criticality by easing the load, but once ratcheted it can only be left or laboriously overcome, never simply undone -- and the one who reaches back for what was, by the gesture that would have worked before the snap, finds the gesture no longer reaches.

## Echo

A changed world that will not change back, and a hard-won change that will not slip.
Ratchet leaves the process locked in its new state -- and here the twist turns, the sibling of the family's ambivalence: the one-way catch that dooms the broken thing is the very thing that makes a mended one durable. The same irreversibility by which a betrayal cannot be un-known and a fallen regime cannot be restored by a word is the irreversibility by which a system driven up to a better state does not backslide the moment conditions dip -- the recovered trust, the reformed institution, the habit finally broken, held by the ratchet that held the ruin. Irreversibility is the trap and the guarantee at once.
Ratchet is read from the not-returning, the process resting in a new basin that the undoing of its trigger cannot reach.
