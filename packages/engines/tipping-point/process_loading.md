# Process: Loading

## Taxonomy

[Tipping-point](process_tipping-point.md), the phase of the strain taken on unseen.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: tipping-point

## Initiated by

A pressure begins to accumulate on a system that can still absorb it.
Loading starts wherever a process takes on a strain it holds without visible cost -- the grievances a patient partner forgives, the snow a slope bears, the debt a market carries, the small concessions a norm survives -- each increment added to a store that does not yet show.
It is the filling of the reservoir: not the break and not the edge, but the long, quiet gathering of the load that will one day be enough, while the surface reports nothing wrong.

## Direction

Up, in the hidden variable, while the visible one holds flat.
Loading raises the accumulated strain -- Scheffer's slow variable creeping while the system's fast, visible state stays put -- so that the distance to the threshold shrinks steadily even as nothing on the surface changes; the wing's angle rises while its lift still climbs, the sand piles while the pile still stands.
It moves the system toward its edge without moving its appearance: every increment is absorbed, every day looks like the last, and the very steadiness is the deception -- the system is not failing to register the load, it is successfully bearing it, right up until bearing it is no longer possible.

## Lever

Each increment's absorption against the shrinking margin it silently spends.
The carrying force is the system's real capacity to take the strain -- it holds, and holding is not pretence; the resistance that is being spent is the margin to the threshold, drawn down invisibly with each unit absorbed, so that resilience erodes in exact measure as the surface reassures.
Add load to a system far from its edge and nothing happens and nothing is lost that shows; add the same load near the edge and the same nothing-that-shows is the last of the margin -- the store is finite, and loading is the spending of it under a surface that keeps its own counsel.

## Echo

A system at its capacity, wearing the face of a system at its ease.
Loading leaves the process fully strained and outwardly unchanged -- the reservoir near its brim, the margin nearly gone, the appearance identical to the appearance of safety -- and hands that primed state to the [criticality](process_criticality.md) where the smallest further increment will matter.
It is the phase that makes the tipping-point cruel: the loading is invisible from inside precisely because it is being borne, so the warning that should precede the break is the one thing the successful bearing of the load conceals.
Loading is read from the accumulation, the strain a process takes on while it still looks like it is taking nothing.
