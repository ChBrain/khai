# Process: Snap

## Taxonomy

[Tipping-point](process_tipping-point.md), the phase of the discontinuous fall to a new state.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: tipping-point

## Initiated by

The last increment lands, and the system does not bend -- it breaks.
Snap starts at the instant a process at criticality takes the trigger it can no longer absorb and jumps, all at once, to a wholly different state -- the smooth airflow detaching into stall, the poised slope releasing as avalanche, the forgiving partner rising with a stone-cold face and a packed bag.
It is the discontinuity itself: not a fast slide but a jump, the moment the curve that was flat drops sheer, and the system that was one thing is, between one instant and the next, another.

## Direction

Down and over, fast, and out of all proportion to the push that released it.
Snap carries the system across the threshold in a rush -- Thom's catastrophic jump, Zeeman's fold where the smooth control change yields a sudden leap in the state, the phase transition where a whisker of added order flips the whole -- so that the magnitude of the effect bears no relation to the magnitude of the trigger, only to the load that had gathered behind it.
It moves the process from basin to basin in the time it took the last grain to fall: the change that loading spent months preparing is spent in a moment, and what looked, an instant before, like the most stable of systems is now unrecognizably elsewhere.

## Lever

The gathered load discharging against nothing that can now hold it.
The carrying force is the whole accumulated store releasing at once -- the strain that loading banked and criticality primed, converted in an instant into change; the resistance is gone, because resistance was the margin, and the margin is what the threshold-crossing spent. Once the basin's wall is topped there is nothing between the system and the new state, and it falls the whole way.
The snap is why the collapse feels causeless to those inside it and inevitable to those outside: from within, a trivial trigger produced a total effect and the arithmetic will not balance; from without, a load long visible finally met the edge it was always heading for.

## Echo

A new state, arrived at in a moment, that the old one cannot be recovered from by the door it left through.
Snap leaves the process discontinuously elsewhere -- the bond severed, the slope run out, the market re-priced, the wing fallen -- and hands that new state to the [ratchet](process_ratchet.md) that will hold it, and to a world that must now take the changed thing as its ground.
It is the phase the whole dynamic is named for and the one no witness forgets: the flat, flat, flat, and then the gone -- the split-second in which years of loading become an accomplished fact.
Snap is read from the discontinuity, the process falling whole to a new state in the moment its threshold is crossed.
