# Process: Transcendence

## Taxonomy

[Gratitude](process_gratitude.md), the mode this is one form of.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: gratitude

## Initiated by

A good arrives so large, or a life feels so given, that the persona cannot address the thanks to any person.
The benefit exceeds any human giver -- a recovery, a deliverance, the sheer given-ness of existence -- and the impulse to answer it reaches past every face for its source.

## Direction

Beyond persons, toward whatever the persona holds as the source of the good -- God, nature, fate, life itself.
The turn is upward and outward, often carrying humility and awe: the persona is small before what they are thanking, and the thanks is directed at an order larger than the transaction any person could enter.

## Lever

The benefit read as given by more than a person.
Transpersonal gratitude is directed where no benefactor is adequate to the good: the persona thanks the sacred or the cosmos or their own fortune-beyond-desert, according to the order they already hold. It leans on that held order -- the faith engine's standing orientation to a transcendent source -- to have somewhere to send the thanks.
It borders the accommodation of vastness that the awe engine models, but where awe meets the vast by enlarging its frame, this meets it by giving thanks to it.

## Echo

The persona is oriented in humility and thanks toward a source beyond any giver.
Held over time this is often devotional -- a standing gratitude woven into how the persona meets their life and its scale.
Where the good has a human face the turn is toward them, and the mode is [benefaction](process_benefaction.md); where it has none but stays within the ordinary, it rests as [appreciation](process_appreciation.md) rather than rising past it.
