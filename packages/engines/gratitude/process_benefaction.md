# Process: Benefaction

## Taxonomy

[Gratitude](process_gratitude.md), the mode this is one form of.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: gratitude

## Initiated by

An identified other has given the persona a benefit -- something intentional, at some cost to the giver, and aimed at the persona's good.
The good comes with a face on it: the persona knows who provided it and reads that they meant to.

## Direction

Toward the benefactor.
The persona is turned to acknowledge the giver, to warm toward them, and to draw the tie closer -- the impulse is not only to repay but to keep and deepen the relationship the gift revealed.
What the persona returns may be to the giver or passed on to a third; either way the movement is outward and binding.

## Lever

The benefit reveals the giver's regard, and gratitude finds it.
A costly, responsive gift is read as a sign that this person is attentive to the persona's needs; the emotion finds a good partner, reminds the persona of them, and binds the two more tightly -- so the gift's real work is done on the relationship, not the ledger.
It scales with how responsive the benefit shows the giver to be, not with its price.

## Echo

The persona reciprocates or draws closer, and the tie strengthens.
The good, met with gratitude rather than mere repayment, becomes the seed of a stronger bond -- and often of onward generosity, the benefit passed to someone the giver will never meet.
Where the persona instead reads the same gift as a bill, the turn is not toward the giver but away, and the mode is [indebtedness](process_indebtedness.md) rather than this.
