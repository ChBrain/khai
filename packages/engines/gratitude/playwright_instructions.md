# Instructions: Gratitude

How a Playwright wires the gratitude engine into a play. You wire by linking the play's own content to the engine's files; the engine files stay untouched, they are canon. The Roadie plumbs the engine's law into Instructions Knowledge on deploy. Authoring guidance, not runtime content, and it does not go on tour.

## Human

- The human defines the received good: what benefit the persona has been given, by whom or by nothing, at what cost, and what history the persona brings to being on the receiving end.

## Agent

- From the play's own content, a persona links the gratitude mode the moment runs (benefaction, indebtedness, appreciation, or transcendence) under its Projection. Let the mode shape how the persona reads the good they received and where their impulse turns -- toward the giver, toward discharge, toward the good itself, or beyond persons. You link to the engine, you never edit it.

## Collaboration

- The structure of the exchange and the obligation to return is the gift engine's domain; the ledgered obligation is the debt engine's; the acknowledgment of who another is belongs to recognition. Gratitude as a settled character disposition is the virtue engine's pole; this engine is the emotion-state. Transcendent gratitude leans on the faith engine's orientation to a source and borders the awe engine's vastness.

## Knowledge

- Gratitude is the appraisal of a benefit received as freely and benevolently given, and the impulse to turn toward its source and answer it. Benefaction is gratitude to an identified benefactor, drawing the tie closer. Indebtedness is the same benefit read as an obligation, felt as weight and repaid to be free -- gratitude's cold twin. Appreciation is gratitude with no benefactor, for fortune or the ordinary good. Transcendence is gratitude directed beyond persons, to the sacred, nature, or life itself.

## System

- Do let the appraisal, not the benefit, decide: the same good can warm the persona toward its source or land as a debt to discharge.
- Do let indebtedness read as a genuine, distinct response -- the hurried repayment that keeps distance -- not as weak gratitude.
- Do not write gratitude as mere politeness or the reflex of receiving; it is the turn toward a good read as a gift.
- Do not edit the engine's files; wire only from the play's side.
