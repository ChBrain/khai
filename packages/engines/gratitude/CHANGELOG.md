# @chbrain/khai-engine-gratitude

## 0.1.1

### Patch Changes

- 4243a62: Add the gratitude engine: the appraisal of a benefit received as freely and benevolently given, and the prosocial impulse that follows -- the warm turn back toward the source of a good, to acknowledge it and answer it. The root (`process_gratitude.md`) declares gratitude as an appraisal, not a reflex of receiving: the same benefit warms the persona toward its source and binds them closer, or lands as a debt to be discharged, depending on whether it is read as freely, costlessly, and benevolently given. Four modes are the turns the received good can take: `benefaction` (gratitude to an identified benefactor -- find-remind-bind, drawing the tie closer), `indebtedness` (the same benefit read as an obligation, felt as weight and repaid to be free -- gratitude's cold twin), `appreciation` (gratitude with no benefactor, for fortune or the ordinary good), and `transcendence` (gratitude directed beyond persons, to the sacred, nature, or life itself).

  Bounded against gift (the exchange and the obligation to return), debt (the ledgered obligation), recognition (acknowledging who another is), forgiveness (the negative counterpart of the account), awe (the accommodation of vastness), and virtue -- which carries gratitude as a dispositional character pole (`position_gratitude.md`, the axis-partner of envy); this engine is the same phenomenon at the altitude of the emotion-state, exactly as `pride` and `envy` are each both a virtue pole and their own engine. Warranted (LORE) on Emmons & McCullough (gratitude as state/disposition; counting blessings; transpersonal gratitude), Algoe (find-remind-bind), Simmel (the moral memory of a benefit), Greenberg (the theory of indebtedness), and Fredrickson (broaden-and-build). Set at patch as the free level; a new engine may warrant a minor at the maintainer's `bump:minor` label.
