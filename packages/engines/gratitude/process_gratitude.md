# Process: Gratitude

## Taxonomy

Emotion / Prosocial appraisal.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: gratitude

## Initiated by

A benefit lands -- something good the persona receives that they did not have to be given.
The organism reads it against a question it asks of every received good: was this freely given, at some cost to the giver, and meant for me?
The reading is not deliberate; it fires the moment the good arrives, and it decides what the good becomes.

## Direction

Toward the source of the good, in acknowledgment and the impulse to answer it.
Gratitude turns the persona back toward whatever provided the benefit -- to mark it, to hold it, to return the good or pass it on.
It is an outward turn: the received good is not banked as luck but felt as owed to something, and the persona is moved to meet it.

## Lever

The appraisal of the benefit as a gift rather than a due.
Gratitude scales with how far the good is read as intended for the persona, costly to the giver, valuable, and unearned; it thins the moment the good is read as owed, purchased, accidental, or self-serving on the giver's part.
The same benefit, read one way, warms the persona toward its source and binds them closer; read another, it lands as a debt to be discharged -- so the appraisal, not the benefit, is the lever.

## Echo

The persona acknowledges the good and turns toward its source, warmed and moved to answer.
Gratitude is the moral memory of a benefit: the tie that outlasts the transaction, the reason a good received keeps working on the one who received it after the exchange itself is closed.
The process continues as [benefaction](process_benefaction.md), [indebtedness](process_indebtedness.md), [appreciation](process_appreciation.md), or [transcendence](process_transcendence.md), each a distinct turn the received good can take.
