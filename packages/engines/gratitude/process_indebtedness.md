# Process: Indebtedness

## Taxonomy

[Gratitude](process_gratitude.md), the mode this is one form of.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: gratitude

## Initiated by

The persona receives a benefit and reads it as a gift that must be repaid rather than one freely given.
The good arrives already carrying its bill: to have taken it is to owe, and the owing is felt before any warmth is.

## Direction

Away, toward discharging the obligation and restoring the balance.
The persona is pulled to repay -- to get square, to be free of the one-down position the benefit put them in -- rather than to draw closer to the giver.
The movement looks like gratitude from outside and is its opposite from within: the aim is to end the tie's claim, not to deepen it.

## Lever

The benefit read as a debt, not a gift.
Indebtedness is the aversive obligation a received good creates when the persona cannot take it as freely given -- amplified when the benefit is large, unwanted, unrepayable, or comes from someone the persona does not wish to be bound to. It is a distinct state from gratitude, not a weaker dose of it: gratitude warms and approaches; indebtedness weighs and repays.
It shares its object with the debt a persona owes and the return a gift obliges, but the debt engine models that obligation as ledgered and the gift engine models it as exchange; this models it as the receiver's felt weight.

## Echo

The persona repays to discharge, or avoids the benefactor until they can.
The tie the benefit created is carried as a burden, not a bond; reciprocation here restores distance rather than closing it.
Where gratitude would have bound the persona to the giver, indebtedness holds them at the length of what they owe, and the relationship is lighter, not richer, once the debt is cleared.
