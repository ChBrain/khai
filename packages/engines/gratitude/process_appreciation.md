# Process: Appreciation

## Taxonomy

[Gratitude](process_gratitude.md), the mode this is one form of.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: gratitude

## Initiated by

A good with no giver presents itself -- health held, a near-miss survived, a clear morning, the plain fact of being alive.
Nothing was handed over and no one is owed, yet the persona registers the good as a good, and as one they did not have to be granted.

## Direction

Toward the good itself, held and savored.
The persona's attention widens to what is present and unearned, dwelling on it rather than passing over it as ordinary.
There is no source to turn back to, so the turn is a turning-toward: the good is met and appreciated rather than acknowledged to anyone.

## Lever

The reframing of what is taken for granted as a gift.
Appreciation is the grateful outlook applied where there is no benefactor: the persona counts the good instead of the lack, and treats the given -- the body, the day, the near-thing that went right -- as fortune rather than as owed.
It scales with noticing: the same life yields more of it to the persona who attends to what is there than to the one for whom it has gone invisible.

## Echo

The persona savors the good, and the noticing lifts and widens them.
Held as a habit rather than a moment, this becomes a standing disposition -- a way of meeting the ordinary that finds the unearned in it and is steadied by the finding.
Where the good does have a giver, the turn is toward them and the mode is [benefaction](process_benefaction.md); where the good exceeds any giver, it rises to [transcendence](process_transcendence.md).
