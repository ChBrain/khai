# Instructions: Bystander Effect

How a Playwright wires the bystander-effect engine into a play. You wire by linking the play's own content to the engine's files; the engine files stay untouched, they are canon. The Roadie plumbs the engine's law into Instructions Knowledge on deploy. Authoring guidance, not runtime content, and it does not go on tour.

## Human

- The human defines the bystander terrain: what emergency the persona witnesses, how many other witnesses are present and how they appear, and how ambiguous or public the situation is -- so the presence of others has a help to inhibit and a route by which to inhibit it.

## Agent

- From the play's own content, a persona links the form the moment runs (diffusion, pluralism, or audience) under its Projection. Let it show the presence of other witnesses staying the persona's own help -- the responsibility divided, the emergency defined away by others' calm, the intervention feared before onlookers -- and let it read as inhibition by the crowd, not as callousness nor as the tending of a victim already being helped. You link to the engine, you never edit it.

## Collaboration

- The effort-drop when a persona's contribution is pooled in a shared task is the social-facilitation engine's loafing; bystander diffusion is of the responsibility to help in a crisis. The caregiving system that tends the victim once help begins is the caregiving engine's; the anxiety of being judged is the apprehension engine's; the image guarded from a watched error is the face engine's; the diffusion that exempts a harm one commits is the moral-disengagement engine's. This engine supplies the inhibition of helping by the presence of other witnesses itself.

## Knowledge

- The bystander effect is the inhibition of helping by the presence of others: a persona who witnesses an emergency is less likely to intervene, and slower to, the more other witnesses are present. Diffusion is the responsibility route -- the duty to act divided among the witnesses, so the more who share it the less any one feels it is theirs, and no one moves because everyone might. Pluralism is the interpretation route -- each witness, uncertain, reads the others' calm as evidence nothing is wrong, and all stay calm because all are reading each other, the emergency defined away by mutual reassurance. Audience is the evaluation route -- the fear of misjudging the intervention before onlookers, of overreacting or bungling in front of watchers, holding the persona back from acting.

## System

- Do make the presence of others the brake, not the persona's coldness: the witness is stayed by the crowd, not indifferent -- so let a persona who would help alone fail to help among many, and let the odds of help fall as the crowd grows.
- Do route the inhibition to the right step: diffusion works on the responsibility, pluralism on the reading of the emergency, audience on the risk of acting -- so name which step the others' presence stalls the persona at, rather than a vague reluctance.
- Do let pluralism circulate: each witness masks their own alarm and reads the others' masks as calm, so the composure is shared but the safety is false -- let the persona be reassured by a crowd as unsure as they are.
- Do delegate the pooled-task effort-drop to the social-facilitation engine and the fear of judgment to the apprehension engine. Do not edit the engine's files; wire only from the play's side.
