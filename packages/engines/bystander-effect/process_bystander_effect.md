# Process: Bystander Effect

## Taxonomy

Social influence / the social psychology of emergency (non)intervention.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: bystander-effect

## Initiated by

A persona witnesses an emergency with others present, and is less likely to help -- and slower to -- than if they had been alone.
This is the bystander effect: the presence of other potential helpers inhibiting the individual's own helping, the very number who could act making any one less likely to.
The persona is not callous; they are stayed, by the others, from a help they might have given alone.

## Direction

Toward inaction, more surely the more witnesses are present.
The bystander effect works against helping through the steps a witness must pass to intervene -- noticing the event, reading it as an emergency, taking the responsibility, knowing what to do, and acting -- and the presence of others can stall the persona at each: dividing the responsibility, clouding the reading, and threatening the act with judgment.
The direction scales with the crowd: alone, the persona usually helps; among many, the odds that any one helps fall as the group grows.

## Lever

The presence of other witnesses, spent as inhibition of the persona's own help.
What the bystander effect spends is the crowd itself: others who could also act divide the felt responsibility, others who stay calm supply a reading that nothing is wrong, and others who could judge raise the cost of acting and being wrong -- each converting the presence of fellow witnesses into a reason this witness does not move.
The lever is the others as co-witnesses: the same presence that could have multiplied the help instead inhibits it, so that a victim before many can be less likely to be helped than a victim before one.

## Echo

The emergency goes unmet, each witness stayed by the rest.
The bystander effect is why a person can collapse before a crowd and no one step forward, why the larger the audience the longer the delay, why "someone else will handle it" is thought by all and acted by none -- the presence of others, which feels like safety, working as a brake on rescue.
It runs as [diffusion](process_diffusion.md), [pluralism](process_pluralism.md), or [audience](process_audience.md) -- the responsibility divided, the emergency defined away, the act feared before onlookers. The effort-drop when a persona's contribution is pooled in a task is the social-facilitation engine's loafing; the caregiving system that would tend the victim once help begins is the caregiving engine's; the anxiety of being judged is the apprehension engine's; the diffusion that exempts a harm one commits rather than a help one omits is the moral-disengagement engine's. This engine owns the inhibition of helping by the presence of other witnesses itself.
