# Process: Audience

## Taxonomy

[Bystander Effect](process_bystander_effect.md), the evaluation form.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: bystander-effect

## Initiated by

The persona hangs back from acting because others are watching, and to act and be wrong -- to overreact, to bungle the help, to make a scene where none was needed -- would be to be judged for it before them.
This is audience inhibition: the fear of a misjudged intervention before onlookers holding the persona from intervening at all.
Better, the reluctance runs, to do nothing unremarked than to help and be found foolish.

## Direction

Toward inaction chosen to avoid the risk of public error.
Audience inhibition works at the acting step: the persona may have noticed, read the emergency, and felt the responsibility, yet stall at the last, because intervening is a public act with a public verdict -- and if the emergency proves less than it seemed, or the help clumsy, the watchers will have seen the persona misjudge.
The direction is a shrinking from exposure: the move to help is weighed against the cost of helping wrongly in front of others, and the cost of the watched mistake holds the hand.

## Lever

The onlookers as potential judges, spent as the risk of acting.
Audience inhibition runs on evaluation apprehension: to act in an emergency is to be visible and appraisable, and the persona, imagining the embarrassment of an overreaction or a fumbled rescue seen by all, treats the safer course as the unremarked one -- the presence of watchers turning helping into a performance that can fail.
The lever is the fear of the watched error: because others are there to see, the persona weighs not only the helping but the being-seen-to-help-wrongly, and the second can outweigh the first.

## Echo

The help is withheld to avoid a public misstep, and the emergency is left to no one.
It is the bystander effect at the level of nerve -- the reason the fear of "what if I'm overreacting?" before a crowd is heaviest exactly where a crowd is present, the reason a witness who would act unseen freezes when seen -- the audience that could have shared the help instead making it too risky to give.
It is [diffusion](process_diffusion.md) and [pluralism](process_pluralism.md)'s partner, working on the risk of the act where they work on the responsibility and the reading. The anxiety of being judged that it rides on is the apprehension engine's, and the image the persona guards from the watched error is the face engine's; this owns the inhibition of helping by the fear of misjudging it before an audience.
