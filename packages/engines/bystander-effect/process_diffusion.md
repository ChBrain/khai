# Process: Diffusion

## Taxonomy

[Bystander Effect](process_bystander_effect.md), the responsibility form.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: bystander-effect

## Initiated by

The persona feels the responsibility to act divided among all the witnesses, so that the more who share it, the less any of it is theirs.
This is diffusion of responsibility: the obligation to help spread across the bystanders until each carries only a fraction, and the fraction feels too small to compel.
No one moves, because everyone might.

## Direction

Toward a responsibility so divided it binds no one.
Diffusion splits the duty to intervene by the number present: alone, the whole of it falls on the persona and they act; among many, it is parceled out, and each witness, feeling only their share, waits for one of the others to carry it -- a waiting that, done by all, leaves it carried by none.
The direction is a thinning: the obligation that would have moved a lone witness dissolves across a crowd until it moves no one.

## Lever

The division of the duty by the number of possible helpers.
Diffusion runs on responsibility treated as shared rather than personal: when others could act too, the persona's own obligation is felt as one part of many, and the larger the group the smaller that part, so the felt duty to help falls as the crowd rises even though the victim's need does not.
The lever is the shared burden: because the responsibility is not the persona's alone, they can decline it without feeling they have declined it, each assuming another will.

## Echo

The duty is everyone's and so no one's, and the victim waits.
It is the core of the bystander effect -- the reason a large crowd helps less reliably than a single passerby, the reason "surely someone else will" leaves the someone unfound -- the responsibility diluted to nothing by the very abundance of those who hold it.
It is [pluralism](process_pluralism.md) and [audience](process_audience.md)'s partner, working on the responsibility where they work on the reading and the risk. Its mirror in the pooling of task effort rather than emergency duty is the social-facilitation engine's loafing, and its use to exempt a harm one commits is the moral-disengagement engine's displacement; this owns the division of the responsibility to help among witnesses.
