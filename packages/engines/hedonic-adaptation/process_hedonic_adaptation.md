# Process: Hedonic Adaptation

## Taxonomy

Emotion / the social psychology of well-being over time.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: hedonic-adaptation

## Initiated by

A persona's circumstances change for better or worse -- a windfall, a wound, a promotion, a loss -- and the feeling the change brings, sharp at first, fades with time as they drift back toward the level of well-being they held before.
This is hedonic adaptation: the return to baseline, the reason no lasting change lands a lasting blow or lift.
What thrilled or crushed at the start comes, in time, to register barely at all.

## Direction

Toward the prior baseline, the feeling subsiding whichever way the change first pushed it.
Hedonic adaptation pulls the persona's affect back toward its resting level: the gain that lifted them settles, the loss that sank them lifts, and both converge on a level not far from where they stood before the change arrived.
The direction is a reversion, not a memory -- the circumstance may persist unchanged, yet the feeling it produces erodes, so the persona ends near their starting point while their situation does not.

## Lever

The waning of feeling for the constant, spent against every lasting change.
What hedonic adaptation spends is the impermanence of affective response: a feeling is a reaction to change, and a change that becomes the new constant stops being a change, so the response to it decays -- by the weakening of the reaction, by the rising of the standard it is judged against, and by the reclaiming pull of a dispositional baseline.
The lever is time applied to constancy: the longer a circumstance holds, the more completely the feeling it once produced gives way to the level that was there before.

## Echo

The change is absorbed, and the persona is left about where they began.
Hedonic adaptation is why lottery winners and the newly disabled alike drift back toward their former happiness, why the raise thrills for a month and the new house for a season, why chasing better circumstances buys less lasting well-being than it promises -- the baseline reasserts itself against the pull of events.
It runs as [habituation](process_habituation.md), [aspiration](process_aspiration.md), or [setpoint](process_setpoint.md) -- the response weakening, the standard rising, the baseline reclaiming. The present affective state is the mood engine's; the deliberate turning-toward that prolongs a good moment against the fade is the savoring engine's; the reward-valuation system is the reward engine's; the tolerance that drives a dependency to need ever more is the addiction engine's. This engine owns the return of feeling to baseline itself.
