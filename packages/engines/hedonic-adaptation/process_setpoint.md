# Process: Setpoint

## Taxonomy

[Hedonic Adaptation](process_hedonic_adaptation.md), the baseline form.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: hedonic-adaptation

## Initiated by

The persona keeps returning to a stable, characteristic level of well-being -- a baseline set largely by temperament, to which events lift or lower them only for a time before they drift back.
This is the setpoint: the resting level of happiness a persona carries, the destination the fading and the rising bar deliver them toward.
Events move the persona around it, but the level itself holds.

## Direction

Toward a dispositional baseline, the persona's affect orbiting a level it keeps coming home to.
The setpoint anchors well-being: a windfall raises the persona above it and a blow drops them below, but in both cases the deviation decays and the level reasserts itself, so a person's happiness over years tracks their temperament more than their fortunes.
The direction is a homing: not a single event's fade but the long-run tendency of a persona's affect to settle at its own characteristic altitude, wherever circumstance has lately pushed it.

## Lever

The dispositional anchor of well-being, spent as a return.
The setpoint runs on the heritable, stable core of affect: a large part of a persona's happiness is set by disposition rather than situation, so the affective system behaves as though regulated toward a reference level, correcting deviations from it over time the way a thermostat returns a room to its setting.
The lever is the baseline's stability: because the resting level is anchored in temperament, circumstances can perturb it but struggle to relocate it, and the persona is carried back to it once a change's novelty and standard-shift have run their course.

## Echo

The persona comes home to their own level, the event's mark erased.
It is the destination of adaptation and the ground of set-point theory -- the reason lasting happiness is so stubborn to raise by circumstance and, mercifully, so resilient to lower, the level reclaiming the persona from both fortune and disaster -- though the baseline is not wholly fixed, and sustained change or deliberate practice can, slowly, move it.
It is where [habituation](process_habituation.md) and [aspiration](process_aspiration.md) deliver the persona. The resting temperament that sets the baseline's height is the temperament engine's, and the present feeling that orbits it is the mood engine's; this owns the return of well-being to its characteristic level.
