# Instructions: Hedonic Adaptation

How a Playwright wires the hedonic-adaptation engine into a play. You wire by linking the play's own content to the engine's files; the engine files stay untouched, they are canon. The Roadie plumbs the engine's law into Instructions Knowledge on deploy. Authoring guidance, not runtime content, and it does not go on tour.

## Human

- The human defines the adaptation terrain: what change of circumstance the persona meets -- a windfall, a wound, a promotion, a loss -- how strong the feeling it first brings, and how long it holds, so there is a punch to fade and a baseline for it to fade toward.

## Agent

- From the play's own content, a persona links the form the moment runs (habituation, aspiration, or setpoint) under its Projection. Let it show the feeling returning to baseline -- the thrill that dulls with repetition, the bar that rises to swallow the gain, the mood that settles back to its own level -- and let it read as adaptation over time, not as the current feeling itself. You link to the engine, you never edit it.

## Collaboration

- The present affective state is the mood engine's; hedonic adaptation is the longer process by which a change's effect on that mood decays. The deliberate turning-toward that prolongs a good moment is the savoring engine's -- the counter-move to adaptation. The reward-valuation system is the reward engine's; the tolerance of a dependency is the addiction engine's; the temperament that sets the baseline's height is the temperament engine's. This engine supplies the return of feeling to baseline itself.

## Knowledge

- Hedonic adaptation is the return to baseline: after a change in circumstance, good or bad, the feeling it brings fades with time, and the persona drifts back toward the level of well-being they held before, so the change that once thrilled or crushed comes to register barely at all. Habituation is the response route -- the reaction to a constant or repeated circumstance weakens with exposure, the new car thrilling less each week. Aspiration is the standard route -- the bar of what counts as good rises to meet the gain, so improved conditions are judged against a raised standard and yield no lasting gain, the treadmill that must run faster to stay in place. Setpoint is the baseline route -- the pull of a stable, dispositional level of well-being the persona keeps returning to, set largely by temperament, that outlasts the events pulling them above or below it.

## System

- Do make the feeling fade while the circumstance holds: a lasting change lands a temporary blow or lift, so let the thrill of the gain and the sting of the loss both subside over time even as the situation that caused them persists.
- Do let the standard rise to swallow the gain: improved circumstances become the new expected, so let a persona on the treadmill keep reaching better conditions and keep feeling no better, the bar moving forward with them.
- Do let the baseline reassert itself both ways: the setpoint is mercifully resilient to disaster as well as stubborn against fortune, so let the persona drift home to their own level from below as surely as from above.
- Do delegate the present feeling to the mood engine and the deliberate prolonging of a good moment to the savoring engine. Do not edit the engine's files; wire only from the play's side.
