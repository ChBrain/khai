# Process: Habituation

## Taxonomy

[Hedonic Adaptation](process_hedonic_adaptation.md), the response form.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: hedonic-adaptation

## Initiated by

The persona's affective response to a constant or repeated circumstance weakens with exposure -- the same good or bad draws less and less feeling each time it recurs or as it persists.
This is habituation: the desensitizing of the reaction itself, the thrill or the sting worn down by repetition.
The circumstance is unchanged; the response to it simply fades.

## Direction

Toward a flatter response, the same input met with less feeling over time.
Habituation dampens the reaction at its source: the first taste, the first day, the first shock is strongest, and each recurrence lands softer, until the once-remarkable becomes unremarked -- the luxury that stops feeling like luxury, the noise that stops being heard.
The direction is a decay of intensity, automatic and largely outside choice: the persona does not decide to feel less, the system simply responds less to what no longer surprises it.

## Lever

The waning sensitivity of the affective system to the repeated.
Habituation spends the response's own economy: a feeling is costly and a constant needs no continual signalling, so the system tunes down its reaction to what stays the same, reserving affect for what changes.
The lever is repetition and constancy themselves: the more a stimulus recurs or persists unchanged, the more completely the response to it desensitizes, the good and the bad alike ground down toward neutrality.

## Echo

The circumstance stops moving the persona, its feeling worn to nothing.
It is the most automatic engine of adaptation -- the reason the new possession delights briefly and then joins the unnoticed background, the reason chronic hardship can dull into numb endurance -- the response spent down by the very constancy of its cause.
It is [aspiration](process_aspiration.md) at the level of the reaction rather than the standard, and one of the two currents that carry the persona to their [setpoint](process_setpoint.md). The deliberate re-noticing that fights it, restoring a good's freshness, is the savoring engine's; this owns the automatic weakening of the response to the constant.
