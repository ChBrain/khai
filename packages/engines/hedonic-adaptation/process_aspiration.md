# Process: Aspiration

## Taxonomy

[Hedonic Adaptation](process_hedonic_adaptation.md), the standard form.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: hedonic-adaptation

## Initiated by

The persona's standard of what counts as good rises to meet an improved circumstance -- what was once a longed-for gain becomes the new baseline expected, and satisfaction is measured against the raised bar rather than the old one.
This is aspiration: the shifting adaptation level, the standard climbing so the gain is swallowed.
The circumstance improves and stays improved, yet it no longer feels like more, because more is now what is expected.

## Direction

Toward a raised standard that absorbs each gain, so satisfaction returns to where it was.
Aspiration lifts the reference point the persona judges by: the promotion becomes the ordinary, the once-coveted becomes the merely owed, and each achieved improvement resets what would now be required to feel improved -- the goalposts moving forward exactly as fast as the persona advances toward them.
The direction is a treadmill: the persona runs harder into better circumstances and stays in the same place, because the standard against which they feel their circumstances runs forward with them.

## Lever

The relativity of satisfaction, spent as a rising reference point.
Aspiration runs on judgment against a standard rather than an absolute: because a circumstance is felt as good or bad relative to what the persona has come to expect, raising what they have raises what they expect, and the surplus that a gain would represent is consumed by the higher baseline it establishes.
The lever is the adaptation level itself -- the moving zero-point against which pleasure and disappointment are read -- which drifts toward whatever the persona currently has, so that having more simply relocates the standard for wanting more.

## Echo

The gain is kept and the satisfaction is not, the bar risen to swallow it.
It is the cognitive engine of the treadmill -- the reason wealth and status and acquisition deliver so much less lasting contentment than they promise, the reason each rung reached reveals the next -- the standard climbing to convert every gain back into ordinary.
It is [habituation](process_habituation.md) at the level of the standard rather than the response, and the other current that carries the persona toward their [setpoint](process_setpoint.md). The comparison of one's lot against other people's, rather than one's own past, is the social-comparison territory of the bias engine; this owns the rising of one's own standard to swallow one's own gain.
