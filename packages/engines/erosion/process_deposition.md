# Process: Deposition

## Taxonomy

[Erosion](process_erosion.md), the mode where the worn material is laid down as new ground.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: erosion

## Initiated by

The agent carrying the worn material loses its power, and what it held settles out.
Deposition starts wherever the water slows, the wind drops, or the ice melts and can no longer carry its load -- the river reaching the flat, the flood spreading over the bank, the wind stilled in the lee, the glacier's front melting back.
It is erosion read the other way: not the ground taken from a place but the ground given to one, the theft become a gift somewhere downstream.

## Direction

Down out of the slowing agent, and up as the new ground builds.
Deposition sorts and settles what was carried -- the coarse dropped first where the water first slows, the fine carried on to the still water, the dust falling out of the calmed air -- and layer on layer the new surface rises.
It moves the receiving place toward the built ground: the delta growing into the sea, the floodplain laid rich over the valley floor, the loess piled deep downwind, the beach and the bar and the dune, the moraine dumped where the ice gave out.

## Lever

The loss of the agent's carrying power against the load it must let go.
The carrying force is, paradoxically, the failing of the force -- the slackening current, the dropping wind, the melting ice -- each releasing what it can no longer hold; the resistance is anything that keeps the agent moving and the load aloft.
Where the energy falls, the ground is laid down; dam the river, still the wind, melt the glacier, and the carried land comes to rest and becomes a place.

## Echo

New ground built of the old, gathered where the carrying stopped.
Deposition leaves the receiving place raised and enriched -- the delta, the floodplain, the loess soil, the beach -- and carries beneath it the erosion family's twist: that the wearing is not only loss but the making of new land, the same grains that left the mountain building the plain.
The richest soils on earth are worn from somewhere else and laid down here; the theft that lowers one place is the gift that raises another.
Deposition is dated from the settling, the moment the carried ground came to rest and began to be a place again.
