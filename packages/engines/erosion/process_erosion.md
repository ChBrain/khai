# Process: Erosion

## Taxonomy

Denudation.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: erosion

## Initiated by

An agent from outside meets the exposed surface and begins to take it away.
Erosion starts wherever the ground is bared to water, wind, or ice and has nothing holding it -- the slope stripped of its cover, the rock left to the frost, the soil loosed to the rain -- and the surface begins to go, grain by grain.
Every exposed surface is being lowered; erosion is that lowering, the slow theft the place cannot see happening until the shape has changed.

## Direction

Downward and away, off the high ground toward the low.
Three agents work the surface, each carrying it off its own way: [Scour](process_scour.md) is water's cut, [Deflation](process_deflation.md) is wind's strip, and [Frost](process_frost.md) is ice's shatter.
A fourth, [Deposition](process_deposition.md), is where the carried material comes to rest and builds anew.
A place does not wear evenly: the soft bed cuts while the hard cap holds, the windward face strips while the lee gathers, the shaded rock shatters while the sunned one stands.

## Lever

The agent's carrying power against the ground's own resistance to being moved.
The carrying force is the energy in the moving water, wind, and ice, and the load they already carry to grind with; the resistance is the ground's cohesion -- the root that binds the soil, the cement in the rock, the cover that shields the surface.
Strip the cover and the ground goes fast; bind it and the wearing slows to the geologic crawl that still, given time, levels mountains.
Unlike the ground's own violence, this comes from outside and takes its time: geomorphology raises the range in an age or an instant; erosion wears it to stumps over the ages after.

## Echo

The surface lowered, and the worn land carried off to build elsewhere.
Erosion does not close; it works as long as the surface is bared, handing the ground steadily downhill and, at the far end, laying it down as new.
What is lost from the high ground is not gone: the mountain worn becomes the plain built, the cliff cut becomes the beach, the field washed becomes the delta's soil -- the same grains, moved.
Erosion is dated from the wearing, the slow lowering that, given only time, is the strongest force on the face of the land.
