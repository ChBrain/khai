# Erosion: Reference

## Line of Work

Erosion as **process**: the slow wearing of a place's surface by agents from outside --
water, wind, and ice detaching and carrying the ground off grain by grain, lowering the
land over time, and laying the worn material down elsewhere as new ground. It wires on a
place at Shown, the place's physical self-presentation, because the worn surface -- the
gullied slope, the sandblasted rock, the scree, the delta -- is what the place puts in
front of whoever enters. The domain does not model the ground's own sudden violence from
within, which is geomorphology's; nor the water regime that supplies the scour and the
flood, which is hydrology's; nor the atmospheric event above, which is weather's; nor the
material entropy of made things, which is decay's. It models the gradual, exogenic
lowering of the surface, and reads it two ways: as the land lost from here, and as the
land built there.

The spine is the recognition, from Hutton onward, that the slow wearing of the surface,
given only time, is the strongest force shaping the face of the inhabited land -- and
that what it takes from one place it gives to another. Hutton read the deep cycle: the
land worn to the sea and raised again, "no vestige of a beginning, no prospect of an
end." Strahler made the wearing quantitative, the drainage basin a system with measurable
work. And the modern readers of landscape -- Macfarlane tracing what the worn ground
holds -- carry the wearing into the felt place. So the engine gives erosion three agents
-- scour in the water, deflation in the wind, frost in the ice -- and keeps a fourth,
deposition, the same worn material laid down as new ground, the loss become a gift
downstream.

## Restrictions

What the engine refuses to model, and to whom it delegates.

- **The ground's own sudden violence (geomorphology)**: This is the sharp boundary of
  the land's-forces family. Erosion is the slow, exogenic force -- water, wind, frost --
  that wears the surface away from outside, grain by grain over time. Geomorphology is
  the sudden, endogenic force -- plates, fault, magma, void -- that builds and breaks the
  landform from within. Geomorphology raises the range; erosion wears it to stumps. One
  makes and breaks the ground; the other lowers it from the surface down.
- **The water regime (hydrology)**: The rain, the river's flow, the flood, the drought,
  the water-table are hydrology's; erosion is what that water does to the surface it runs
  over -- the scour, the cut, the wash. The two meet in the eroding river: the flow is
  hydrology's, the ground it cuts is erosion's. Hydrology supplies the agent; erosion is
  the wearing the agent works.
- **The atmospheric event above (weather)**: The storm, the wind as it blows, the rain
  as it falls are the weather engine's; erosion is the slow surface work the weather's
  residue then does -- the wind that strips the field, the rain that washes the slope,
  read over years not hours. Weather delivers the load in the hour; erosion is what
  seasons of that load make of the surface.
- **The material entropy of made things (decay)**: Decay is the breakdown of the matter a
  maker imposed order on -- rust, rot, crumble -- from within the material. Erosion is the
  removal of surface by an outside agent. A statue's face lost to acid rain and windblown
  grit is erosion; the same statue's bronze greening from within is decay. The boundary is
  whether the surface is carried off from outside or the matter fails from within.
- **A persona's feeling, and the worn place as authored symbol**: How the worn place makes
  a persona feel -- the melancholy of the eroded ruin, the awe of the canyon -- is the
  persona's own engines'; erosion delivers the worn surface, not the feeling. And the
  wearing made to **stand for** a persona's own wearing down, the eroded coast that
  mirrors a diminished life, is refused: that is authored key, the play's pitch, not the
  force on the place.
- **The mode itself is not engine content**: The engine declares the four modes exist;
  which one a given place undergoes or receives lives on that place, linked under its
  Shown chapter. The engine refuses to fix a place's wearing for it.
