# Instructions: Erosion

How a Playwright wires the erosion engine into a play. You wire by linking the play's
own content to the engine's files; the engine files stay untouched, they are canon. The
Roadie plumbs the engine's law into Instructions Knowledge on deploy. Authoring
guidance, not runtime content, and it does not go on tour.

## Human

- The human sets which places wear -- worn down by water, wind, or ice, or built up by what those agents lay down -- and whether that wearing is the place's slow history or a live pressure in the play.

## Agent

- From the play's own content, a place whose surface is worked links under Shown the mode of erosion it undergoes or the deposition it receives: the scour of water, the deflation of wind, the shatter of frost, or the deposition that builds new ground. Play the erosion as the worn surface itself shown -- the gullied slope, the sandblasted rock, the scree, the growing delta -- present in what the place puts before whoever enters, never narrated, and never as a mirror of a persona's own wearing down. Keep it the slow surface wearing from outside, not the ground's own sudden violence (that is geomorphology), the water regime (hydrology), the air above (weather), or the decay of made things (decay). You link to the engine, you never edit it.

## Collaboration

- The ground's own sudden making and breaking is geomorphology's; the water regime that supplies the scour is hydrology's; the atmospheric event above is weather's; the decay of made things is decay's. Erosion is the sharp sibling of geomorphology -- the slow, exogenic wearing of the surface against geomorphology's sudden, endogenic making and breaking. Whether the mode rings true to the surface and its agents is the team's review.

## Knowledge

- Erosion is the slow wearing of a place's surface by agents from outside: water, wind, and ice detach and carry the ground off grain by grain, lowering the land over time, and lay the worn material down elsewhere as new ground (Hutton's deep cycle of wearing and renewal; the slow force that, given time, levels mountains). It wires on a place at Shown, and it is the exogenic, gradual counterpart to geomorphology's sudden endogenic violence -- geomorphology raises the range, erosion wears it to stumps. Four modes carry it -- scour is water's cut, the river incising and the rain washing the slope; deflation is wind's strip, the dry loose ground lifted and the dust carried off; frost is ice's shatter, water freezing in the cracks and prising the rock apart; deposition is the twist, the worn material laid down elsewhere as new ground -- the delta, the floodplain, the loess soil -- the loss become a gift downstream. Read a place by the mode of wearing its surface undergoes, or the deposition it receives.

## System

- Do give a place whose surface is worked its mode under Shown; do play the erosion as the worn surface itself shown, present not narrated; do keep the reading the slow surface wearing from outside -- the scoured, the deflated, the frost-shattered, the deposited -- not a feeling and not a symbol.
- Do read deposition as the twist: what is worn from one place is not gone but building another -- the mountain worn becomes the plain built, the field washed becomes the delta's soil, the loss and the gift one process.
- Do not model the ground's own sudden violence (that is geomorphology), the water regime (hydrology), the air above (weather), or the decay of made things (decay). Do not make the wearing mirror a persona's own diminishment (that is the play's pitch). Do not edit the engine's files; wire only from the play's side.
