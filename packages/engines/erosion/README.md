# Erosion

Erosion as process: the slow wearing of a place's surface from outside -- water scouring, wind deflating, ice and frost shattering, grain by grain over time -- and the twist of that worn material laid down elsewhere as new ground, the exogenic force that lowers the land geomorphology raised.
