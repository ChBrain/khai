# Instructions: Contempt

How a Playwright wires the contempt engine into a play. You wire by linking the play's own content to the engine's files; the engine files stay untouched, they are canon. The Roadie plumbs the engine's law into Instructions Knowledge on deploy. Authoring guidance, not runtime content, and it does not go on tour.

## Human

- The human defines the contempt terrain: who holds another beneath them, on what grounds -- inability, baseness, low standing -- and how close the tie is, so the downward look has a target, a reason, and something to corrode.

## Agent

- From the play's own content, a persona links the form of contempt the moment runs (disdain, scorn, or condescension) under its Projection. Let the form show how the other is lowered -- the cool snub, the withering sneer, the patronizing help -- and let it read as looking down on a lesser, not opposing an equal. You link to the engine, you never edit it.

## Collaboration

- The hot, provoked objection to a wrong is the emotion engine's anger, and the contamination-rejection is its disgust; contempt recruits both but is colder and more lasting. The upward reckoning with another's superiority is the envy engine's; the rank itself is status's and hierarchy's; the moral lens that lit the judgment is moral-judgment's. This engine supplies the downward look itself -- another judged beneath and their worth withdrawn.

## Knowledge

- Contempt is the downward look: a persona judges another as beneath them and withdraws regard, saying not you wronged me (anger) or you revolt me (disgust) but you are beneath me -- colder and more lasting, and the strongest sign a bond is failing. Disdain is the cold form -- regard quietly withheld, the other dropped beneath notice, dismissed by omission. Scorn is the hot form -- the sneer, the mockery, the open derision that lowers the other aloud and before an audience. Condescension is the veiled form -- the patronizing talk-down that treats the other as lesser under the cover of care, so the lowering is deniable.

## System

- Do make contempt look down, not across: anger opposes an equal, contempt dismisses a lesser -- so let the persona hold the other beneath, not merely against.
- Do let it be colder and more lasting than anger: an angry tie can be repaired, a contemptuous one erodes -- let contempt poison a relationship where a flare of anger would not.
- Do let condescension deny itself: the patronizing giver retreats to good intentions, which is what makes it so galling and so hard to protest -- keep the mask sincere-seeming and the contempt real beneath it.
- Do delegate the upward reckoning to the envy engine and the rank itself to status. Do not edit the engine's files; wire only from the play's side.
