# Contempt: Reference

## Line of Work

Contempt as **process**: the downward look through which a persona reckons with another judged beneath them -- regard withdrawn, worth lowered, another held below the level at which they merit full consideration. The domain does not model the hot, provoked objection to a wrong, which is anger's, nor the contamination-rejection of what revolts, which is disgust's -- contempt is analysed as recruiting both but functioning as a colder, more enduring thing; nor the upward reckoning with another's superiority, which is the envy engine's; nor the rank itself, which is the status engine's; nor the moral lens that lit the judgment, which is moral-judgment's. It models the downward look itself: the appraisal of another as lesser and the withdrawal of esteem that follows.

The spine is the social psychology of contempt. Contempt is a sentiment -- a structured, often lasting attitude toward a specific other -- that integrates negative appraisals of them into a summary devaluation: a judgment that the other is of low worth, which lowers their standing in the holder's mind and tunes down the regard, cooperation, and consideration extended to them. It is the mirror of envy, which looks up; and it is corrosive in a way anger is not, because to be held beneath is worse than to be opposed. The three forms are how the downward look is carried: disdain, scorn, condescension.

## Restrictions

What the engine refuses to model, and to whom it delegates.

- **Emotion (anger and disgust)**: The engine models the cold downward devaluation, not the hot objection or the contamination-rejection it recruits. Anger -- the provoked, approach-oriented response to a wrong done by an equal -- and disgust -- the rejection of what revolts -- are owned by the emotion engine; contempt draws on both but functions as a colder, more lasting placing-beneath, and owns that, not the hot or the visceral component.
- **Envy**: The engine models the downward look; envy models the upward one. The pained reckoning with another's superiority and the wish to close the gap are owned by the envy engine; contempt is its mirror -- the dismissive reckoning with another's inferiority and the wish to keep the gap -- and the two are distinct directions of the same comparative gaze.
- **Status and Hierarchy**: The engine models the affective devaluation, not the rank itself. The place a persona holds in a deference order or a formal structure is owned by the status and hierarchy engines; contempt places another below in regard, but the standing it presumes and confirms is theirs.
- **Moral judgment**: The engine models the contempt, not the moral lens that lit it. Which moral foundation a persona reads a transgression through is owned by the moral-judgment engine; contempt is the downward look that a moral judgment of baseness or failure can produce, not the judging itself.
- **Self-esteem**: The engine models contempt for another, and the mechanism of the downward look when it is turned on the self, but not the resting global self-worth. The standing attitude a persona holds toward themselves is owned by the self-esteem engine; self-contempt recruits this engine's look-down turned inward, and lowers that self-worth, but the resting level itself is self-esteem's.

## Encoding

Source to constraint, per file.

- **[contempt](process_contempt.md)** (the root): The downward look -- another judged beneath and their worth lowered, the cold mirror of envy's upward gaze, corrosive where anger is not. Anchored by Gervais & Fessler (contempt as devaluing sentiment) and Rozin et al. (the CAD triad).
- **[disdain](process_disdain.md)** (form): The cold contempt -- regard quietly withheld, the other dropped beneath notice and dismissed by omission rather than attack. Anchored by Rozin et al. (contempt for those who fail their place) and Fiske et al. (low-warmth, low-competence contempt).
- **[scorn](process_scorn.md)** (form): The hot contempt -- the sneer, mockery, and open derision that lower the other aloud and before an audience. Anchored by Ekman & Friesen (the contempt expression) and Gottman (contempt as relationship-corroding).
- **[condescension](process_condescension.md)** (form): The veiled contempt -- the patronizing talk-down that treats the other as lesser under the cover of benevolence, so the lowering is deniable. Anchored by Fiske et al. (paternalistic prejudice toward the warm-but-incompetent).
