# Process: Scorn

## Taxonomy

[Contempt](process_contempt.md), the hot form.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: contempt

## Initiated by

The persona's contempt comes out -- the sneer, the mockery, the open derision -- the other put down aloud and to their face.
This is scorn: contempt expressed as attack, not merely felt but performed.
Where disdain withholds, scorn spends -- and the spending is part of the point.

## Direction

Toward the other diminished in the open, and often before an audience.
Scorn does not just withhold regard; it actively lowers the other -- ridiculing, deriding, making them small where others can see -- so the contempt is not only felt by the holder but witnessed and, ideally, shared.
The direction is downward and outward: the other's lowering made public.

## Lever

Derision and the sneer.
Scorn wears the universal contempt expression -- the lip curled, the half-smile of superiority -- and arms it with the withering remark, the mocking imitation, the laugh at the other's expense; it recruits an audience where it can, because contempt confirmed by others lands harder.
The lever is the performance of the other's inferiority, staged so they and everyone present must see it.

## Echo

The other is humiliated, and the tie, if there was one, is corroded.
In a close relationship, scorn -- the eye-roll, the mockery, the sneer -- is the surest single sign the bond is failing, because contempt made hot and public is the hardest wound to take back: what is said in derision cannot be unsaid.
Its cold twin is [disdain](process_disdain.md), which lowers without heat; its masked form is [condescension](process_condescension.md), which lowers without seeming to.
