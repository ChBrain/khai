# Process: Contempt

## Taxonomy

Emotion / the social psychology of the downward look.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: contempt

## Initiated by

A persona judges another as beneath them -- lesser in worth, ability, or standing -- and withdraws their regard rather than raising a hot objection.
This is contempt: the downward look, the placing of another below the level at which they merit full consideration.
Where anger says _you have wronged me_ and disgust says _you revolt me_, contempt says _you are beneath me_ -- and it is colder and more lasting than either.

## Direction

Toward the other diminished, and the distance between them kept.
Contempt lowers the other in the persona's regard and holds them there; it does not seek to close a gap or right a wrong but to confirm a hierarchy in which the other ranks below.
It is the mirror of envy: envy looks up at a superior with pain and wants the gap gone; contempt looks down at an inferior with dismissal and wants the gap kept.

## Lever

The judgment of another's inferiority, and the withdrawal of esteem that follows.
What contempt spends is regard: the other is appraised as lesser -- inept, base, or common -- and their claims, their feelings, their very presence are discounted accordingly, treated as mattering less because they are held to be less.
The colder the contempt, the less the other is even engaged with; the hottest is still, at bottom, a refusal to take them seriously.

## Echo

The other is held beneath, and the relation is poisoned.
Contempt is corrosive where anger is not: an angry tie can be repaired, but a contemptuous one erodes -- to be held beneath is worse than to be opposed, and contempt is the single strongest sign a bond is failing.
It runs as [disdain](process_disdain.md), [scorn](process_scorn.md), or [condescension](process_condescension.md) -- the cold withdrawal, the hot derision, the veiled talking-down. The upward reckoning with another's superiority is the envy engine's; the rank itself is the status engine's; the moral lens that lit the judgment is moral-judgment's; contempt turned on the self, and the resting self-worth it lowers, is self-esteem's. This engine owns the downward look itself.
