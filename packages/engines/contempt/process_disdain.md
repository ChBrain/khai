# Process: Disdain

## Taxonomy

[Contempt](process_contempt.md), the cold form.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: contempt

## Initiated by

The persona finds another beneath their notice and simply withdraws regard -- no heat, no display, only the cool ceasing to take the other seriously.
This is disdain: contempt at its coldest, the other not worth the energy of an attack, only the economy of being ignored.
It is the aloof contempt of one who need not even perform their superiority.

## Direction

Toward the other unengaged, dropped below the threshold of attention.
Disdain does not lower the other in the open; it lowers them by omission -- the unanswered remark, the gaze that slides past, the request not dignified with a refusal.
The direction is a quiet withdrawal: the other placed outside the circle of those who merit a response.

## Lever

The withholding of regard, silently.
Disdain spends nothing and displays nothing -- the aristocrat's cool, the expert's flat impatience with the amateur, the snub -- and its power is exactly its economy: to be dismissed without even the tribute of anger is a sharper lowering than to be shouted at.
The lever is the refusal to engage, which says the other is not worth engaging.

## Echo

The other is placed beneath notice, and usually knows it by the cold.
It is the purest contempt -- regard simply withheld -- and often the most wounding for its restraint, because there is nothing to argue with: no charge to answer, only a door that did not open.
It is [scorn](process_scorn.md) with the heat removed and [condescension](process_condescension.md) with the mask removed -- the contempt that does not bother to dress itself at all.
