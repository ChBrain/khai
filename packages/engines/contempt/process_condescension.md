# Process: Condescension

## Taxonomy

[Contempt](process_contempt.md), the veiled form.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: contempt

## Initiated by

The persona looks down while appearing to reach down -- the patronizing help, the talking-down, the praise that carries a sting of surprise that the other managed at all.
This is condescension: contempt wearing benevolence, the lowering dressed as care.
It reaches toward the other with one hand and holds them beneath with the other.

## Direction

Toward the other kept below, under cover of kindness.
Condescension treats the other as lesser -- incapable, naive, in need of things simplified -- while presenting the treatment as generosity, so the lowering is deniable and the other is left unable to object without seeming ungrateful.
The direction is downward behind a forward-facing smile: the contempt real, the mask sincere-seeming.

## Lever

The mask of benevolence over the devaluation.
It is the paternalistic stance toward those seen as warm but not capable -- pitied, helped, and never taken as equals: the over-explanation, the exaggerated patience, the credit handed down as if to a child, the compliment that is really astonishment.
The lever is the cover story -- _I am only trying to help_ -- which lets the contempt operate while denying it is contempt at all.

## Echo

The other is diminished and cannot easily protest, because the contempt was dressed as care.
It is the most galling of the three to receive and the easiest to deny giving -- the giver can always retreat to their good intentions -- which is exactly what makes it corrode trust so quietly.
The patronizing speech that carries it -- the slowed, simplified, over-explained register -- is the accommodation engine's over-accommodation; this owns the contempt beneath the manner. Its unmasked forms are [disdain](process_disdain.md) and [scorn](process_scorn.md).
