# Process: Malice

## Taxonomy

[Hatred](process_hatred.md), the passion -- active hostility and the wish to see the target harmed.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: hatred

## Initiated by

The persona actively wishes the target harm.
This is the passion of hate (Sternberg's duplex theory): a hot hostility toward the target, the desire to see it thwarted, hurt, or destroyed, going beyond cold dismissal to an engaged will against the other.
It opens where the sentiment turns from mere aversion to a wanting of the target's undoing -- the persona not indifferent to the enemy's fate but invested in its ruin.

## Direction

Toward the target's harm, willed.
Malice moves the persona to want and, where it can, to work the enemy's damage: to relish its setbacks, hope for its defeat, and aim at its injury -- an active hostility that leans in rather than turning away.
Its direction is the target's undoing -- the persona set toward the enemy's harm as an end it desires.

## Lever

What malice spends -- the persona's own peace, bound up in the enemy's fate.
Hate that wishes harm ties the persona to the target: it spends its equanimity on the other's fortunes, gladdened by its losses and galled by its wins, so the enemy occupies the hater even in absence.
A production holds this lever by letting the persona want the target's harm and be moved by its fate -- pleased at its stumbles, bent on its defeat -- so the malice reads as an engaged hostility, hotter than contempt's cold turning-away, that costs the persona its own ease.

## Echo

The persona wills the target harm -- and hate becomes dangerous, not merely distant.
This passion is what separates hate from cold contempt: contempt looks down and disengages, but malice leans in and wants the enemy undone, and where it finds means it feeds the aggression that inflicts real harm.
The behavioral infliction of that harm is the aggression engine's; the schadenfreude at an enemy's deserved misfortune is that engine's; this component owns the wish itself -- the active hostility that wants the hated other's undoing, distanced by [repulsion](process_repulsion.md) and justified by [essentializing](process_essentializing.md).
