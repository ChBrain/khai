# Process: Essentializing

## Taxonomy

[Hatred](process_hatred.md), the commitment -- fixing the target's badness as its stable essence.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: hatred

## Initiated by

The persona fixes the target's badness in its very nature.
This is the commitment component of hate (Sternberg) joined to its appraisal core (Fischer et al.): attributing the target's badness not to a passing act or circumstance but to a stable, malevolent essence -- the enemy bad by disposition, and so beyond change or redemption.
It opens when the persona stops reading the target's wrongs as things it did and starts reading them as what it is.

## Direction

Toward a fixed, global verdict on the target's nature.
Essentializing moves the persona from act to essence: generalizing a deed into a character, a character into a kind, so the target is condemned wholesale and permanently, its every trait recruited to the verdict and no evidence admitted against it.
Its direction is the fixing of badness as essence -- the enemy defined by its worst and denied the capacity to be otherwise.

## Lever

What essentializing spends -- the target's standing as a changeable, particular person.
By making badness the enemy's nature, the persona spends the possibility that the other could act well, be understood, or change -- and buys, in return, a hatred licensed to persist past any single act and to generalize from the person to the category it is taken to represent.
A production holds this lever by letting the persona read the target's badness as essence -- "that is simply what they are," "they were always going to" -- so the essentializing reads as the fixing of a verdict that makes the hatred durable and its object interchangeable with its kind.

## Echo

The target is bad by nature, in the persona's eyes -- and the hatred is now durable and generalizable.
This is the commitment that lets hate last: because the badness is essence, no act redeems the target, the sentiment survives the enemy's individual conduct, and it can pass from the person to a whole group the person is taken to stand for -- the engine of hatred's most dangerous reach.
The moral licensing of harm against the essentialized other -- the dehumanizing that switches off self-sanction -- is the moral-disengagement engine's; this component owns the appraisal itself, the fixing of the target's badness as its stable essence that anchors the [repulsion](process_repulsion.md) and the [malice](process_malice.md).
