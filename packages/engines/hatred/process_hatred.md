# Process: Hatred

## Taxonomy

Emotion / a durable, eliminatory sentiment toward a devalued other.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: hatred

## Initiated by

A persona comes to hold a settled sentiment against another it has judged bad to the core.
This is hatred (Sternberg; Fischer et al.): not a flare at a single act but a standing orientation against a target, a durable wish to distance from, harm, or be rid of an other seen as malevolent in its very nature.
It is fed and sustained rather than triggered and spent: a history, a threat, an injury generalized past its occasion into a verdict on the person, so the target becomes an enemy the persona carries.

## Direction

Toward the target, sustained, and aimed at its undoing.
Hatred points the persona against a particular other across time: pushing it away, wishing it harm, and holding it as bad by essence -- an orientation that colours every reading of the target and does not pass when the provoking moment does.
Its direction is elimination rather than redress -- not to correct the other or restore a balance, but to distance from, diminish, or be rid of it.

## Lever

What hatred spends -- the persona's openness to the target as redeemable or as kin.
Hate closes the door: it spends the possibility that the other might be understood, forgiven, or seen as sharing common ground, converting a person into an enemy defined by its worst and fixed there.
A production holds this lever by letting the persona carry a real, sustained sentiment against a named other -- not a passing anger but a held verdict -- so the hatred reads as a standing orientation that shapes conduct across scenes, and costs the persona the tie it forecloses.

## Echo

The persona holds an enemy -- distanced, wished ill, and fixed as bad by nature -- and acts against that other accordingly.
Hatred is more dangerous than anger precisely because it lasts and generalizes: it licenses sustained hostility, survives the target's individual acts, and can pass from a person to a whole category the target is taken to stand for.
It runs as [repulsion](process_repulsion.md), the distancing; [malice](process_malice.md), the wish to harm; and [essentializing](process_essentializing.md), the fixing of the target's badness as essence. The incident-level reaction to a provocation is the anger engine's; the cold disengaged looking-down is the contempt engine's; the visceral revulsion reflex is the disgust engine's; the behavioral infliction of harm is the aggression engine's; the moral licensing of that harm is the moral-disengagement engine's. This engine owns the standing sentiment of hate against a devalued other.
