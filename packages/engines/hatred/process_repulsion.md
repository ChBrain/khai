# Process: Repulsion

## Taxonomy

[Hatred](process_hatred.md), the negation of intimacy -- distancing and revulsion that push the target away.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: hatred

## Initiated by

The persona pushes the target away, denying it any closeness.
This is the negation of intimacy in hate (Sternberg's duplex theory): a distancing and revulsion that refuses the target warmth, approach, or common ground, so it is held at arm's length and felt as alien.
It opens wherever the sentiment demands separation -- the enemy not merely opposed but repelling, something the persona recoils from and will not be near.

## Direction

Away from the target, into distance and disgust.
Repulsion moves the persona to open a gap: to avoid, exclude, and recoil, treating any nearness to the target as contaminating and any commonality as denied -- the opposite of the closeness intimacy would grant.
Its direction is the severing of connection -- the target placed outside the circle of the approachable.

## Lever

What repulsion spends -- the common ground the persona might have shared with the target.
It buys separation by denying kinship: the persona spends the sense that the other is like it, one of its own, or someone it could stand beside, converting shared humanity into alien difference so the distance feels warranted.
A production holds this lever by letting the persona recoil from the target -- avoiding its presence, refusing its touch or company, feeling the wrongness of nearness -- so the repulsion reads as an active pushing-away, not mere dislike.

## Echo

The target is held at a distance, refused closeness -- and the gap makes the rest of hate easier.
Once the other is alien and not-to-be-approached, the [malice](process_malice.md) toward it meets no restraining sense of kinship, and the [essentializing](process_essentializing.md) of its badness runs unchecked by any felt commonality.
The visceral disgust reflex that repulsion borrows -- the reflexive recoil from the contaminating -- is the disgust engine's; this component owns the interpersonal negation of intimacy, the deliberate refusal of closeness to a hated other.
