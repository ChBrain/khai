# Position: Expansive

## Taxonomy

[Time-horizon](position_time-horizon.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: time-horizon

## Has

A future felt as open-ended, and goals weighted toward what pays off later.
The persona takes itself to have time -- room ahead to build, learn, and expand -- and so invests in breadth and preparation over immediate return (Carstensen; the open time-horizon and its future-oriented goals).
The self oriented forward, treating the present as a step toward a later it expects to reach.

## Orders

Invest in what will pay off later.
The horizon orders the persona to gather -- knowledge, skills, contacts, options -- to meet new people and widen its world, to tolerate the unfamiliar and even the unpleasant for the sake of future gain, and to defer the merely pleasant now for the larger later.
It sources worth in growth and preparation: to be expanding, learning, building toward what is coming, so that the present is spent as an investment rather than savored as an end.

## Loses

The depth and presence of the foreshortened view.
The persona reaching always toward a later forgoes what is here -- it defers the moment, spreads itself across many ties rather than deepening the few, and can arrive at the future it prepared for having missed the present it passed through; the breadth it gains costs it the depth it did not stop for.
What it trades is now for later: the reach and readiness of an open horizon, bought with the presence it keeps postponing.

## Drives

On that open horizon:
rides it -- gathers, expands, meets and prepares, weighting its goals toward a future it expects to have -- or works against it, its horizon foreshortened by an ending it did not choose, turned abruptly from breadth toward depth, from later toward now.
The expansive horizon is the open-ended pole, twinned with [limited](position_time-horizon.md), the foreshortened view that invests in the present the expansive defers. It is not youth as such -- a young persona with a foreshortened future holds the limited horizon, an old one with an open project holds this -- nor the stance toward death, which is mortality's; it is the future felt as long, and the goals that length invites.
