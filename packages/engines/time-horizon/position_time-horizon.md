# Position: Time-horizon

## Taxonomy

Time / a persona's sense of how much time it has left, and what that does to what it wants (no khai file of its own).

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: time-horizon

## Has

A resting sense of how much time lies ahead -- not the clock's measure but the time the persona feels itself to have -- and a reweighting of its goals to match.
A read on a single axis: whether the persona meets its choices as one with an open-ended future, time enough to invest in what pays off later, or as one with a foreshortened future, time only for what pays off now (Carstensen's socioemotional selectivity).
The default weighting of later against now the persona brings to what it pursues, before any single decision.

## Orders

Spend your time where the horizon points.
The time-horizon tilts whether the persona invests in breadth and preparation -- gathering knowledge, meeting new people, expanding its world for a future it expects to have -- or in depth and the present -- its closest ties, its most meaningful moments, the felt now it will not have forever; and where, accordingly, it looks to source its worth.
The room reads the horizon beneath the conduct: who is building toward a later, who is savoring a now.

## Loses

The reach of the other horizon's goods.
The persona weighted toward the open-ended future forgoes the depth and presence a foreshortened view brings -- it defers, prepares, and spreads itself wide, and can miss what is here for what is coming; the one weighted toward the foreshortened present forgoes the expansion and preparation an open horizon affords -- it deepens and savors, and can narrow its world to the known and the dear too soon.
What no horizon escapes is the trade of later against now: to invest in the one is to spend the other.

## Drives

On that felt horizon:
rides it -- the open horizon investing in breadth and the future, the foreshortened one investing in depth and the present -- or works against it, its horizon shifted by circumstance: an ending that foreshortens a young persona's view and turns it toward what matters, or a reprieve that reopens an old one's and lets it plan again.
The baseline holds underneath either way, in one of two horizons: [expansive](position_expansive.md), the future felt as open-ended and invested toward, or [limited](position_limited.md), the future felt as foreshortened and invested in now. The horizon is not fixed to age -- any ending foreshortens it, any reopening expands it. The stance a persona takes toward its own death is the mortality engine's; the future self-images it pursues are possible-selves'; the relishing of a present moment is savoring's; the subjective sense of duration and time-perspective is time-perception's. This engine owns how much time a persona feels it has, and what that does to what it wants.
