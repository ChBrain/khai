# Position: Limited

## Taxonomy

[Time-horizon](position_time-horizon.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: time-horizon

## Has

A future felt as foreshortened, and goals weighted toward what pays off now.
The persona takes its time as running short -- an end in view, whether from age, an ending, or a turn of fortune -- and so invests in depth and the present over preparation for a later it may not reach (Carstensen; the limited time-horizon and its emotion-focused goals).
The self oriented to the now, treating the present not as a step toward a later but as the thing itself.

## Orders

Spend your time on what matters most, now.
The horizon orders the persona to deepen rather than widen -- to turn toward its closest and most meaningful ties, to savor the present, to seek emotional meaning over information, and to prune the shallow, the merely useful, and the future-only from its life; it lets the small and the peripheral fall away and holds what it most values close.
It sources worth in meaning and presence: to be with who and what matters, fully, now, so that the foreshortening becomes a clarifying rather than only a loss.

## Loses

The reach and preparation of the open view.
The persona invested wholly in the present forgoes what a long horizon builds -- it stops gathering, narrows its world to the known and the dear, and can foreclose futures it might still have had by living as though there were none; and where the foreshortening is misjudged, the pruning cuts what a longer life would have wanted kept.
What it trades is later for now: the depth and presence of a short horizon, bought with the breadth and reach it sets down.

## Drives

On that foreshortened horizon:
rides it -- deepens, savors, turns to what matters and lets the rest fall away, weighting its goals toward a present it will not have forever -- or works against it, its horizon reopened by a reprieve or a fresh start that restores the future and lets it plan and gather again.
The limited horizon is the foreshortened pole, twinned with [expansive](position_time-horizon.md), the open view that invests in the future this one sets down -- and it carries the family's twist: the foreshortening that reads as loss is also the clarity that turns a persona toward what matters most, so a short horizon is not only less time but a deeper way of spending it. It is not old age as such, nor the stance toward death, which is mortality's; it is the future felt as short, and the depth that shortness invites.
