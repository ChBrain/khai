# Process: Expressive

## Taxonomy

[Speech Act](process_speech_act.md), the act this is one form of.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: speech-act

## Initiated by

A persona performs, by saying so, a feeling about some state of affairs: a thanks, an apology, a congratulation, a condolence, a welcome.
The utterance does not report the feeling so much as enact it -- the saying is the doing, and the social move is made in the words.

## Direction

Toward the other, in acknowledgment of a shared state of affairs.
There is no direction of fit to argue: the state -- the gift received, the wrong done, the good that befell them -- is taken as given, and the words register the speaker's stance toward it.
The deed is the performing: to apologise is to have apologised, whether or not the sorrow beneath it is deep.

## Lever

The social act carried in the form, apart from the sincerity behind it.
An apology said does repair-work even when the contrition is thin, and thanks given discharges an acknowledgment even when felt lightly; the form performs the move, which is why a withheld apology wounds and a hollow one still counts for something.
The feeling the words perform is owned elsewhere -- the emotion, the repair, the forgiveness engines hold the states themselves; this act is the saying that puts them on the table.

## Echo

The social move is on the table, and the other must now meet it.
An apology opens a repair the wronged may take up or refuse; a thanks acknowledges a benefit and eases a debt; a congratulation confers a small recognition. The move having been made, its absence would have been felt, and its making changes what the other owes or is owed.
It is the act that most nearly is its own effect: the expressive does its work in the doing, and the gap between the words and the heart behind them is read separately, in what the persona shows and withholds.
