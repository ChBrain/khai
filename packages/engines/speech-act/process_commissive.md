# Process: Commissive

## Taxonomy

[Speech Act](process_speech_act.md), the act this is one form of.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: speech-act

## Initiated by

A persona binds themselves, by saying so, to some future course: a promise, a vow, an offer, a guarantee, a threat.
The utterance reaches forward and puts the speaker on the hook for a thing not yet done.

## Direction

Toward the speaker's own future, which the words now bind.
The direction of fit runs world-to-word -- the world, by the speaker's own hand, is to be made to match the commitment -- but the one held to the fit is the speaker, not the hearer.
The deed is the binding: to commit is to place an obligation on oneself and hand the other a claim on it.

## Lever

The stake the speaker puts on their own follow-through.
A commitment transfers something real: the hearer may now rely, plan, and hold the speaker to it, and the speaker's standing to be trusted next time rides on keeping it. The heavier the vow and the more it is witnessed, the more its breaking costs.
A threat is the same act turned dark -- a commitment to a harm -- and binds the speaker to it in the same way a promise binds to a good.

## Echo

The speaker is now answerable for a future act, and the hearer holds the claim.
Kept, the commitment builds the speaker's credit and the tie; broken, it spends both, and a broken promise wounds in a way a mere failure does not, because a bond was given and withdrawn.
Where the persona's binding is a private plan to themselves rather than a pledge given to another, that self-directed if-then is the implementation-intention engine's; the commissive is the pledge made across to someone who now holds it.
