# Process: Speech Act

## Taxonomy

Pragmatics / language use.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: speech-act

## Initiated by

A persona speaks or writes, and the utterance does not merely describe the world -- it acts in it.
To say "I promise," "you're fired," "I'm sorry," or "shut the window" is not to report a fact but to perform a deed: a bond is made, a status changed, a repair offered, a demand placed.
The question is not whether the words are true but what, in the saying, they do.

## Direction

Toward a deed performed by the saying, and toward a meaning the hearer must take past the literal.
The utterance carries a force that makes it the act it is; and it often means more than it states, the rest recovered by the hearer on the assumption that the speaker is cooperating -- "it's cold in here" doing the work of "close the window."
The persona aims the force, and trusts the hearer to catch what is meant but not said.

## Lever

The illocutionary force, and the conditions that let it land.
The same words carry a force -- asserting, directing, committing, expressing, declaring -- and it is the force, not the words, that fixes which act is done; a question can do the work of an order, a statement the work of a promise.
But an act comes off only when its felicity conditions hold: one can promise only what one can do, order only where one has standing, declare only from the office that empowers it. Where the conditions fail, the act misfires -- the words are said and the deed does not take.

## Echo

The deed is done, or it misfires, and the world is not the same for the saying.
A claim is now on the record and the speaker answerable for it; an obligation now sits on the hearer, or on the speaker's own future; a feeling has been performed; a status has changed. What the words did outlasts the moment they were said.
The process continues as [assertive](process_assertive.md), [directive](process_directive.md), [commissive](process_commissive.md), [expressive](process_expressive.md), or [declaration](process_declaration.md) -- the five points the force can take. The channel the utterance rides -- the tongue, the medium, the reach -- is the language engine's, not held here.
