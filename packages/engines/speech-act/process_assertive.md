# Process: Assertive

## Taxonomy

[Speech Act](process_speech_act.md), the act this is one form of.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: speech-act

## Initiated by

A persona puts a claim about how things are into words: a statement, a report, a description, an insistence.
The utterance commits the speaker to the truth of what it says -- it holds the world to be a certain way and stakes the speaker on it.

## Direction

Toward the world, which the words are held to fit.
The persona lays the claim on the record and stands behind it; the direction of fit runs word-to-world -- the words answer to how things are, and are wrong if things are otherwise.
The deed is the committing: to assert is to become answerable for the truth of the assertion.

## Lever

The stake the speaker takes on the claim's truth.
An assertion carries the speaker's warrant that it is so; its force scales with how flatly it is put and how much the speaker is known to vouch for. Once made, it can be checked, held to, and held against.
Whether the claim is sincere or a lie is not settled here -- the assertive is the act of putting it on the record; the deliberate placing of a falsehood to mislead is the deception engine's.

## Echo

The claim stands on the record, and the speaker is answerable for it.
Others may now rely on it, repeat it, or catch the speaker out by it; a retraction costs, because something was staked.
It is the plainest of the acts and the ground the others stand on -- a [directive](process_directive.md), a [commissive](process_commissive.md), even a [declaration](process_declaration.md) all rest on assertions about how things are and who may do what.
