# Process: Illness

## Taxonomy

Condition (no khai file of its own yet).

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: illness

## Initiated by

Something has gone wrong with a persona's body, and the trouble has stopped being only theirs.
Illness starts where a bodily fact becomes a social one: where the persona has to tell somebody, ask for something, be believed, be excused, or be treated -- and where what happens next depends on other people rather than on the condition.
It is not the physical demand itself, which is the body's, and not the mark it may carry, which is stigma's. It is what a condition makes of a life and of a persona's standing in it.

## Direction

Toward a persona who has become a case, a story, and a claim.
The engine reads four movements, and a play may run them in any order or run several at once: [the disruption](process_disruption.md), where the taken-for-granted breaks and the biography has to be rewritten; [the ticket](process_ticket.md), where the persona seeks a name without which nothing is available to them; [the role](process_sick_role.md), the bargain a society offers the sick and the terms attached to it; and [the telling](process_telling.md), the shape of story the persona is left able to tell.
It moves nothing in the body and rearranges everything around it.

## Lever

The relief a society offers the sick against the condition it attaches.
The carrying force is that the bargain is real and generous: a persona who is ill is genuinely released from work, obligation, and blame, and that release is one of the more humane things a society does -- it is not a trick, and most personas who need it get it; the resistance is that the release is granted **on condition that the persona wants to leave it**, since the exemption comes bundled with a duty to regard the condition as undesirable, to seek competent help, and to cooperate in getting well.
So the relief is a loan against a recovery, and everything else in the engine follows from what happens when the recovery does not come.

## Echo

A persona relieved of everything except the obligation to stop needing the relief.
Illness does not close; it is a condition becoming a position, gathered into one reading -- a disruption, a ticket, a role, a telling.
The engine's twist is here: because the exemption is advanced against a return to health, the personas who need it most permanently are the ones who cannot hold it. A condition that will not end offers two options and no third -- perform recovery-seeking indefinitely, which is exhausting and eventually implausible, or stop, and watch the relief and the absolution go together, since the same bargain supplied both.
It hands off what it is not: the body's demands are the body's; the discrediting mark is stigma's; the effortful handling of a stressor is coping's; the carer's side is caregiving's; the stance toward death is mortality's; and an illness made to **stand for** a theme is authored key, the play's pitch, not the force.
The persona is dated from the day somebody else agreed that something was wrong.
