# Process: The Wrong Name

## Taxonomy

[The Ticket](process_ticket.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: illness

## Initiated by

The persona has been given a category that does not fit them.
The Wrong Name starts wherever the diagnosis is accepted and misdescribes: a label that carries the wrong prognosis, the wrong treatment, the wrong assumptions about what the persona can do, or a set of associations they must now live inside.

## Direction

Toward a persona managed as somebody else.
The category, once applied, does the work: decisions are made from it, professionals read the persona through it, and the persona's own reports that do not fit are heard as noise around a known condition rather than as evidence against it.
Dislodging it is far harder than acquiring it was, because the name is now the frame every new fact is read inside.

## Lever

The usefulness of categories against the particularity of a body.
The carrying force is that categories are indispensable and mostly right -- medicine cannot proceed one body at a time, and the persona benefits from everything known about people like them; the resistance is that a category is a bet, and once the bet is placed the evidence that would overturn it is precisely the evidence the category teaches everybody to discount.
The Wrong Name is where the ticket becomes a cost rather than a currency.

## Echo

A persona correctly filed and wrongly treated.
It shows the gate's other failure mode: not exclusion, but admission to the wrong room.
The Wrong Name is dated from the diagnosis, and its cost is only legible in retrospect, if ever.
