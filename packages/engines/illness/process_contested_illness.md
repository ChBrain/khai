# Process: The Contested

## Taxonomy

[The Ticket](process_ticket.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: illness

## Initiated by

The persona is ill and the profession will not name it.
The Contested starts wherever a condition lacks accepted standing: the tests come back normal, the presentation does not match a category, or the category exists and is disputed -- and the persona is left with a body that is not working and no word for it.

## Direction

Toward a persona arguing for their own reality.
Without a name there is no ticket, and without a ticket there is no exemption, no absolution, and no treatment -- so the persona's effort moves from getting better to being believed, which is a different and far more punishing project.
They become expert, they gather documentation, they find others with the same account, they seek the practitioner who will say it -- and every one of those moves reads, from outside, as the behaviour of somebody who wants to be ill.

## Lever

The gate's necessity against what it does to the person outside it.
The carrying force is that no society can extend exemptions on assertion alone, that diagnosis is the least bad instrument available, and that the profession's caution is not malice but the same caution that protects everybody else; the resistance is that the instrument's failures are not neutral -- being unnameable is treated as evidence of not being ill, so the persona is asked to prove a negative with the one thing that cannot count, which is their own experience.
Nothing here says whether a condition is real. It says what a society is currently able to issue a name for, and those two have never been the same set.

## Echo

A persona doing the work of proof instead of the work of recovery.
It is the branch where the whole apparatus is present and none of it is available.
What it hands on is a persona who will be read as difficult by everybody who has not followed the reasoning, and who has spent on advocacy the energy the condition had already made scarce.
The Contested is dated from the first normal result, and it runs for years.
