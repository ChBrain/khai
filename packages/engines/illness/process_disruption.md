# Process: The Disruption

## Taxonomy

[Illness](process_illness.md), the life that stops working.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: illness

## Initiated by

The ordinary machinery of a persona's day has stopped running, and it will not simply restart.
The Disruption starts wherever illness does what Bury named biographical disruption: the structures of everyday life come apart, the explanations a persona held about how bodies and futures work stop applying to them, and both have to be rebuilt while they are unwell.
It is the face of the engine that has nothing to do with anybody else's opinion.

## Direction

Toward a biography rewritten in both directions.
The Disruption runs through four movements: the [broken frame](process_broken_frame.md), where the taken-for-granted stops being taken for granted; the [broken account](process_broken_account.md), where the persona has no story for why this happened to them; the [mobilising](process_mobilising.md), where money, kin, time, and knowledge are marshalled; and the [rethinking](process_rethinking.md), where the past is reread and the future re-drawn.
The rewriting is retrospective as much as prospective, which is what makes it a disruption of a biography rather than an interruption of a schedule.

## Lever

The demand to make sense of it against the absence of anything to make sense with.
The carrying force is that the persona has to rebuild -- they cannot function on a frame that no longer holds, and the reconstruction is genuine work that mostly succeeds; the resistance is that illness supplies no material for it: there is generally no reason, no lesson, and no fault, and the persona is left constructing an account out of a fact that has no meaning in it.
The Disruption is the part nobody can help with and the part everybody offers to.

## Echo

A persona still living the same life, in a world where none of its assumptions apply.
It is the movement that runs whether or not there is a name, whether or not anybody believes them, and whether or not a role is available -- which is why a play can stage it before anything else has happened.
What it hands upward is a persona already changed, arriving at the clinic as somebody who has been rearranged rather than as somebody with a complaint.
The Disruption is dated from the moment the ordinary stopped, which is frequently earlier than the diagnosis and rarely the same day.
