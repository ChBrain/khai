# Process: The Restitution Story

## Taxonomy

[The Telling](process_telling.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: illness

## Initiated by

The persona tells it as an episode that will end.
Restitution starts with Frank's basic shape: yesterday I was healthy, today I am sick, tomorrow I will be healthy again -- the story of an interruption, with the resolution built into the telling.

## Direction

Toward an account everybody in the room can receive.
It is the culturally preferred shape by a very large margin, the one medicine tells, the one visitors want, and the one the persona is rewarded for producing -- and where the condition genuinely is acute, it is also true.
It also does real work for the teller, holding a future open through a period with no evidence for one.

## Lever

The comfort of a resolvable story against what it costs when the resolution does not come.
The carrying force is that most illness is acute and this shape fits it -- the persona is not being naive, they are describing what will probably happen, and the optimism is load-bearing; the resistance is that the story has only one ending, so a condition that fails to resolve does not modify it, it falsifies it -- and the persona must then either keep retelling a story that is no longer true or fall out of narrative altogether.
Restitution is the shape that cannot bend.

## Echo

A story the room finds satisfying, resting on a premise nobody has checked.
It is the shape a play should give a persona before it takes it away from them.
Restitution is dated from the first telling and expires without notice.
