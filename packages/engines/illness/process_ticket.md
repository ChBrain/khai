# Process: The Ticket

## Taxonomy

[Illness](process_illness.md), the name without which nothing is available.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: illness

## Initiated by

The persona needs somebody with standing to say what is wrong with them.
The Ticket starts wherever a diagnosis becomes the gate: to time off, to treatment, to benefit, to being believed by an employer, a family, or a spouse -- none of which is issued against symptoms, all of which is issued against a name.
It is the face of the engine where the persona's own testimony is not enough.

## Direction

Toward a name, and toward whatever the name turns out to bring with it.
The Ticket runs through four movements: the [naming](process_naming.md), where the diagnosis arrives and reorganises everything backward; the [contested](process_contested_illness.md), where the profession does not accept the condition; the [unseen](process_unseen.md), where it is accepted and invisible; and the [wrong name](process_wrong_name.md), where the category granted does not fit the persona inside it.
The persona is not seeking an explanation. They are seeking a currency.

## Lever

The need for a name against what a name costs.
The carrying force is that the gate is not arbitrary and mostly works: a society cannot extend exemptions on assertion alone, diagnosis is the least bad instrument anybody has found, and the persona who gets one is generally helped enormously by it; the resistance is that the instrument decides who is real -- a condition without a name leaves its bearer indistinguishable from somebody malingering, and no amount of suffering substitutes, because suffering is exactly what the gate cannot read.
The Ticket is where a persona learns that being ill and being recognised as ill are two different achievements.
None of this is a claim about whether a condition is real. It is a claim about what a society can currently issue a ticket for, and the two have never matched.

## Echo

A persona holding a word that changes what everybody around them is willing to do.
It shows what the disruption could not settle: whether any of this will be treated as illness at all.
What it hands upward is either admission to the role or exclusion from it, and the exclusion is the more consequential outcome, because a persona outside the gate carries the whole condition and none of the relief.
The Ticket is dated from the day it was issued, and everything before that day is reread in its light.
