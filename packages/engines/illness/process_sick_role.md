# Process: The Role

## Taxonomy

[Illness](process_illness.md), the bargain and its terms.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: illness

## Initiated by

A society has decided that this persona is ill, and something is now owed to them.
The Role starts wherever Parsons' bargain is struck: illness treated not as a private misfortune but as a recognised social position, with entitlements attached and duties attached to the entitlements.
It is the face of the engine that is an arrangement rather than an experience.

## Direction

Toward a persona held to the terms of their own relief.
The Role runs through four movements, two given and two owed: the [exemption](process_exemption.md) from normal obligations; the [absolution](process_absolution.md) from blame for the condition; the [obligation](process_obligation.md) to regard being ill as undesirable and to want to leave it; and the [cooperation](process_cooperation.md) required with those competent to treat it.
The first two are a gift. The second two are the price, and the price is not usually stated out loud.

## Lever

The generosity of the arrangement against the shape of the recovery it assumes.
The carrying force is real and should not be undersold: being excused work, released from duty, and held blameless for one's own body is a considerable thing for a society to extend, it is extended routinely, and the persona who receives it is genuinely protected by it; the resistance is that the whole structure was built around an illness that ends -- an acute episode with an onset, a treatment, and a return -- and it has no terms at all for a condition that does not.
The Role rides the engine's keystone: the exemption is a loan against a recovery, so the personas who need it most permanently are the ones least able to hold it.

## Echo

A persona excused everything except the requirement to stop needing to be excused.
It shows the ticket cashed and what it bought, which is protection with a repayment schedule.
What it cannot do is remain open. A persona whose condition will not end must go on performing recovery-seeking past the point where anybody believes it, or stop and lose the absolution along with the exemption, since one bargain supplied both -- and a persona who has lost the absolution is no longer ill in the eyes of the room, they are difficult.
The Role is dated from the grant, and it starts running down immediately.
