# Process: The Broken Frame

## Taxonomy

[The Disruption](process_disruption.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: illness

## Initiated by

The assumptions a persona ran their day on have stopped holding.
That the body will do what it is asked, that tomorrow resembles today, that a plan made is a plan that can be kept -- none of these were beliefs the persona held, they were the floor they stood on, and the floor is what has gone.

## Direction

Toward a life conducted deliberately, in the places it used to run itself.
Everything that was automatic becomes a decision: how far, how long, whether to commit, what to say yes to, what it will cost tomorrow.
The exhaustion this produces is not a symptom and is frequently mistaken for one.

## Lever

The relief of no longer being deceived against the cost of having to attend to everything.
The carrying force is that the old floor was always an assumption and the persona is now seeing accurately -- bodies do fail, plans are contingent, and a persona who has learned this is not deluded; the resistance is that the assumption was doing enormous work, since a life in which nothing can be taken for granted has to be run consciously, and consciousness is the one resource illness reliably takes.
The Broken Frame charges the persona attention for facts everybody else gets free.

## Echo

A persona managing what used to manage itself.
It is the movement least visible from outside, because what it costs is invisible and what it produces is a persona who seems to be doing less.
The Broken Frame is dated from the first plan that could not be kept, and it does not repair when the condition improves -- the floor, once seen, cannot be stood on unconsciously again.
