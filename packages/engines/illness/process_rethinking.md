# Process: The Rethinking

## Taxonomy

[The Disruption](process_disruption.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: illness

## Initiated by

The persona's past and future have both stopped meaning what they meant.
The Rethinking starts wherever the biography itself is re-drawn: earlier years reread for signs that were not signs at the time, and the expected future -- the work, the plans, the version of themselves they were going to be -- withdrawn and something else put in its place.

## Direction

Toward a life story with a new middle.
The rereading runs backward first, since the persona now has an ending that the earlier chapters have to be made to lead to, and forward second, where the drawing is harder because the material is unknown.
What is being revised is not the plan but the identity the plan belonged to.

## Lever

The honesty of a revised story against what the revision takes.
The carrying force is that the revision is accurate and often generous: personas do arrive at a life they can affirm, priorities do genuinely reorder, and the reordering is not a consolation prize invented after the fact; the resistance is that a version of the persona has been lost and there is nobody to mourn it with, since the loss is of a future rather than a person and the room has no ritual for that.
The Rethinking is a bereavement with no funeral.

## Echo

The same life, told as a different story.
It is the movement that decides who the persona is going to be about this, and it happens largely without witnesses.
The Rethinking is dated from whenever the future was first said out loud by somebody with authority, and it continues for years after the condition has settled.
