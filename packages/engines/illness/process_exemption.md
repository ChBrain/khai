# Process: The Exemption

## Taxonomy

[The Role](process_sick_role.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: illness

## Initiated by

The persona has been released from what they would otherwise owe.
The Exemption starts wherever the ordinary obligations are lifted: the work not done, the shift covered, the duty passed on, the appearance not required -- and lifted as of right rather than as a favour.

## Direction

Toward a persona held outside the accounts for a period.
The release is calibrated to the condition as the room understands it, which means it is calibrated to the diagnosis rather than to the experience -- so it arrives in the size the category specifies and not in the size the persona needs.
It is also visible: an exemption is a thing other people notice being extended.

## Lever

The humanity of the release against the fact that somebody is carrying it.
The carrying force is that this is one of the better things a society does and it does it routinely: a persona who cannot work is not made to, and is not asked to justify it daily; the resistance is that every exemption is a load transferred, borne by colleagues, family, or the institution, and the transfer is felt -- so the persona's relief is somebody else's extra shift, and both of them know it.
The Exemption is generous and audited.

## Echo

A persona released, and aware of the arithmetic.
It is the half of the bargain the persona wanted, and the half that generates the guilt nobody expected.
The Exemption is dated from the grant, and the goodwill supporting it has a half-life measured in weeks.
