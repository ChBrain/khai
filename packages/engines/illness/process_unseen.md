# Process: The Unseen

## Taxonomy

[The Ticket](process_ticket.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: illness

## Initiated by

The condition is named, accepted, and invisible.
The Unseen starts wherever the persona looks well: pain, fatigue, cognitive difficulty, a fluctuating capacity -- real, diagnosed, and producing no external sign that anybody can read.

## Direction

Toward a persona demonstrating what should not need demonstrating.
Because nothing is visible, the persona is either disbelieved or continually re-explaining, and both are work: they find themselves performing the condition to be credited with it, and then feeling like a fraud for having performed it.
The fluctuation makes it worse, since a good day is taken as the true state and every bad one as a relapse into complaint.

## Lever

The ordinary reliance on visible evidence against a condition that supplies none.
The carrying force is that people are not being unreasonable: bodies mostly do show what is wrong with them, reading the visible is how anybody assesses anything, and a room that could not do this would be unmanageable; the resistance is that the heuristic misses exactly the conditions most dependent on accommodation, and it puts the persona in the position of having to look worse than they are in order to be treated as badly as they are.
The Unseen taxes the persona for the good days.

## Echo

A persona believed on paper and doubted in the room.
It shows the ticket issued and only half honoured, which is a different failure from having none.
The Unseen is dated from the first time somebody said how well they looked, and it is renewed at every reunion.
