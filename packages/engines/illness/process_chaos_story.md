# Process: The Chaos Story

## Taxonomy

[The Telling](process_telling.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: illness

## Initiated by

The persona cannot get the account into a sequence.
Chaos starts where Frank found the anti-narrative: no plot, no agency, no because -- and then, and then, and then -- the shape of a life in which nothing is being caused by anything and nobody is doing anything about it.

## Direction

Toward an account that empties the room.
It is unbearable to hear and is therefore interrupted, redirected, and answered with advice, which is the listener's discomfort dressed as help.
The persona learns quickly that this account is not receivable, and stops offering it -- so the condition's worst period is also its least documented.

## Lever

The truth of the account against the impossibility of telling it.
The carrying force is that chaos is frequently the accurate report, and Frank's insistence matters here: it is not a failure of the teller or a stage to be moved through, it is what some illness is actually like from inside; the resistance is that a story requires a sequence and this one has none, so the persona holding it has no way to make themselves legible and no way to ask for anything, since asking requires an account.
Chaos is the shape that cannot be told, and it is the one the persona most needs somebody to sit with.

## Echo

A persona present and unnarratable.
It is the hardest of the three to stage and the only one that shows the engine's floor: illness before it has been made into anything.
A play that resolves it into a lesson has replaced it with quest, and a play that has the persona pull themselves together has replaced it with restitution.
Chaos is dated from nothing, which is the point.
