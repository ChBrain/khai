# Process: The Mobilising

## Taxonomy

[The Disruption](process_disruption.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: illness

## Initiated by

The condition has to be paid for, in something.
The Mobilising starts wherever the persona begins marshalling what the illness will take: money, time, transport, information, somebody to come with them, somebody to take the children, a job that will hold.

## Direction

Toward a life reorganised around a logistics problem nobody trained for.
The work is substantial, unpaid, and mostly invisible: appointments, forms, arguing with institutions, becoming an amateur expert, holding a schedule that several bodies control and none of them is theirs.
It is the movement where illness stops being a private matter and starts consuming a household.

## Lever

What the persona can bring against what the condition requires.
The carrying force is that the resources are frequently there and the mobilising works -- families rearrange, employers accommodate, and a persona with money, information, and people does substantially better, which is not a scandal but the ordinary operation of having support; the resistance is that this makes outcome track resources rather than condition, so two personas with the same illness live different illnesses, and the one with less is also the one with less capacity to argue about it.
The Mobilising is where a medical fact becomes an economic one.

## Echo

A household rearranged around one body.
It is the movement whose costs land on people who are not ill, which is why illness is never confined to the person who has it.
The Mobilising is dated from the first thing that had to be cancelled, and it compounds quietly for as long as the condition lasts.
