# Process: The Absolution

## Taxonomy

[The Role](process_sick_role.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: illness

## Initiated by

The persona is not being held responsible for their own condition.
The Absolution starts wherever illness is treated as something that happened to a persona rather than something they did: not a failure of will, not a moral event, not something they could have prevented by being different.

## Direction

Toward a persona relieved of the charge of having caused themselves.
It is the quieter half of the bargain and frequently the more valuable, because the persona's own Broken Account has usually already convicted them, and the absolution is the room declining to agree.
It is also unevenly issued: conditions read as self-inflicted receive less of it, and the reading is moral rather than medical.

## Lever

The decency of not blaming against the conditions it is withheld from.
The carrying force is real -- most illness is granted this without argument, and a persona who is not blamed for their body is spared something enormous; the resistance is that the absolution is rationed by how the condition looks morally, so lung disease, addiction, obesity, liver failure, and much mental illness receive a fraction of it, and the persona in those categories carries the condition and the verdict together.
The Absolution is the part of the bargain that reveals what a society actually thinks illness is.

## Echo

A persona let off, or not, on grounds that have nothing to do with pathology.
It shows the bargain's moral machinery running in the open.
The Absolution is dated from the first telling, and it is withdrawn the moment the condition is read as chosen.
