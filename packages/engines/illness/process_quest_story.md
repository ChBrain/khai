# Process: The Quest Story

## Taxonomy

[The Telling](process_telling.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: illness

## Initiated by

The persona tells the illness as a journey they went on.
Quest starts where the condition is accepted as the occasion for something: a summons, a passage, a thing endured that yielded knowledge the persona now holds and offers.

## Direction

Toward a persona who has become an authority on their own experience.
It is the shape of the memoir, the advocate, the person who speaks to others newly diagnosed -- and it requires two things the other shapes do not: enough distance to be telling from somewhere, and an arrival of some kind to be telling from.
Where those exist it is genuinely the persona's own story, told in their own voice, which neither of the others is.

## Lever

The dignity of a story that yields something against the pressure to produce one.
The carrying force is that quest is real and hard-won: personas do arrive at knowledge they would not otherwise have, do speak from it usefully, and are not consoling themselves when they do; the resistance is that a room which cannot bear chaos and has lost restitution will start demanding quest -- and a persona pressed to find the meaning in a condition that has none is being asked to perform a recovery of a different kind.
Quest is the only shape available for an unresolving condition, which is exactly why it should not be required.

## Echo

An account the persona owns, and may have been made to produce.
It shows the engine's exit, and shows that the exit is narrow.
A play should let a persona reach quest and should never make it the reward for suffering correctly.
Quest is dated from whenever the persona first told it to somebody else newly ill, and it is the only one of the three that is told outward.
