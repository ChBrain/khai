# Process: The Cooperation

## Taxonomy

[The Role](process_sick_role.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: illness

## Initiated by

The persona is required to seek competent help and to do what it says.
The Cooperation starts wherever the second duty attaches: the exemption holds only while the persona is under treatment and complying with it.

## Direction

Toward a persona whose standing depends on their conduct as a patient.
Questioning a treatment, declining one, seeking a second opinion, or managing the condition their own way all put the exemption at risk, because the bargain assumed a competent authority and a compliant recipient.
The word non-compliant does the work: it is a clinical term that functions as a moral one, and once applied it travels with the persona.

## Lever

The sense of requiring treatment against what it does to a persona who knows their own condition.
The carrying force is that the requirement is sound in its own case: an exemption granted without any obligation to treat would be an exemption without an end, and most personas are helped by doing what they are told; the resistance is that a persona living with a long condition frequently knows more about it than the practitioner in front of them, and every use of that knowledge is legible as non-cooperation.
The Cooperation prices expertise in the patient as insubordination.

## Echo

A persona kept in the role by behaving well inside it.
It shows the bargain as an ongoing conditional rather than a grant, which is what makes it revocable at any point.
The Cooperation is dated from the first appointment, and it is re-assessed at every one.
