# Process: The Broken Account

## Taxonomy

[The Disruption](process_disruption.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: illness

## Initiated by

The persona cannot say why this happened to them.
The Broken Account starts wherever the explanatory system fails: the persona did what was asked, or did not, and neither answers it -- illness distributes itself with an indifference that no available account of a life accommodates.

## Direction

Toward an explanation, and usually toward a bad one.
The persona will build something rather than nothing: a cause, a fault, a lesson, a punishment, a thing they should have noticed -- and the constructed account is frequently harsher than any anybody else would offer, because a story in which they were at fault is one in which the world still makes sense.
Around them the same machinery runs: others will supply a reason too, and their reasons will also mostly land on the persona.

## Lever

The need for a reason against the absence of one.
The carrying force is that meaning-making is not weakness -- a persona who can place their condition in some order copes measurably better, and the reaching for it is the same faculty that gets them through everything else; the resistance is that most illness is simply an event, so the account is being built out of nothing, and the most available material is self-blame, which is why the persona ends up with an explanation that costs them.
The Broken Account is the place where an engine about bodies turns out to be about justice.

## Echo

A story that holds, at the persona's own expense.
It is the movement that sets what the persona will believe about themselves for the duration, and it runs before any professional has said anything.
The Broken Account is dated from the first time somebody asked what caused it, and it is rebuilt every time the answer changes.
