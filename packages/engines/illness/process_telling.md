# Process: The Telling

## Taxonomy

[Illness](process_illness.md), the shape of story left available.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: illness

## Initiated by

The persona has to say what is happening to them, over and over, to people who need it to make sense.
The Telling starts wherever Frank's illness narratives begin: the persona becomes the teller of their own condition, and only certain shapes of story are receivable by the people listening.
It is the face of the engine that the persona performs and the room grades.

## Direction

Toward whichever of three shapes the condition will permit.
The Telling runs through three: [restitution](process_restitution_story.md) -- I was healthy, I am sick, I will be healthy again -- which is the shape everybody wants and the only one the room finds comfortable; [chaos](process_chaos_story.md), which has no sequence and no agency and can barely be told at all; and [quest](process_quest_story.md), where the illness is made a journey that yielded something.
The persona rarely chooses. The condition mostly chooses for them.

## Lever

The room's need for a story against the story the condition allows.
The carrying force is that telling helps and is not a performance for others alone: a persona who can put a shape on what is happening recovers some authorship of it, and both restitution and quest genuinely do that; the resistance is that the shapes are not equally available -- restitution requires a recovery, quest requires enough distance to have arrived somewhere, and a persona in the middle of an unresolving condition has only the shape nobody can bear to hear.
The Telling is where the persona discovers that their honest account is the one that empties a room.

## Echo

An account that satisfies the listener, or one that cannot be finished.
It shows what the role could not: what the persona is left able to say about themselves once the arrangement has been made or withheld.
What it hands on is the persona's own reading of their life, which is not a report on the condition but a thing built under pressure from an audience -- so a play that takes an illness narrative as evidence about the illness has misread what it is looking at.
The Telling is dated from whenever somebody last asked how they were, and it is re-dated every time.
