# Process: The Obligation

## Taxonomy

[The Role](process_sick_role.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: illness

## Initiated by

The persona is required to want to get well.
The Obligation starts wherever the price of the exemption becomes visible: being ill must be treated as undesirable, and the persona must be seen to be trying to leave it.

## Direction

Toward a persona performing an intention.
It is a duty about an attitude, which makes it unlike every other obligation: the persona cannot discharge it by doing anything, only by displaying the right relationship to their own condition -- appropriately troubled, appropriately hopeful, appropriately motivated.
Adjustment reads as complacency, and equanimity reads as giving up.

## Lever

The reasonableness of the condition against what it demands of the incurable.
The carrying force is that the requirement is not unreasonable in the case it was built for: an exemption from ordinary life must end, and the expectation of wanting it to end is the mechanism that ends it, without which the arrangement could not be offered at all; the resistance is that a persona whose condition will not end must go on wanting something unavailable, in public, indefinitely -- and a persona who has genuinely made peace with a permanent condition is in breach of a duty they were never told about.
The Obligation is the term that makes acceptance look like a failure of will.

## Echo

A persona required to be dissatisfied.
It is the clause the engine's keystone runs through: the exemption is a loan against a recovery, and this is the interest.
The Obligation is dated from the grant and never falls due, which is precisely the difficulty.
