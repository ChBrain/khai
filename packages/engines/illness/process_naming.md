# Process: The Naming

## Taxonomy

[The Ticket](process_ticket.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: illness

## Initiated by

Somebody with standing has said what it is.
The Naming starts at the diagnosis: a word arrives, and with it a category, a literature, a prognosis, a set of other people who have it, and an entitlement to things the persona could not previously ask for.

## Direction

Toward a past reorganised and a future issued.
The name works backward: symptoms that were complaints become evidence, months that were confusing become a course, and the persona's own reports are retrospectively upgraded from opinion to data.
And it works forward: the persona is handed a trajectory somebody else wrote, and will be read against it from now on.

## Lever

The relief of being named against the loss of being particular.
The carrying force is enormous and frequently underrated -- the diagnosis ends the ambiguity, converts the persona from a complainer into a patient, unlocks the entire apparatus, and is very often experienced as good news even when the news is bad; the resistance is that the persona is now an instance, and the instance comes with expectations they did not agree to, some of which will fit and some of which will be applied to them anyway.
The Naming is the moment the persona stops arguing and starts belonging to something.

## Echo

A word that does more than the persona expected, in both directions.
It shows the gate opening, and the gate opening is the single most consequential event in the engine.
The Naming is dated precisely, remembered precisely, and told afterwards as a scene.
