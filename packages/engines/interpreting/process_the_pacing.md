# Process: The Pacing

## Taxonomy

[The Coordinating](process_the_coordinating.md), the move this is one form of.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: interpreting

## Initiated by

The interpreter sets the speed.
By asking for pauses, by breaking a script into pieces, by not starting until a sentence is finished.

## Direction

Toward a tempo that the rendering can survive.
Institutional speech is fast, rehearsed, and built for a listener who shares the language: the consent form, the caution, the standard explanation, the list of options. It cannot be held in one piece, so it has to be broken up, and the person who breaks it up is the interpreter.
The whole encounter therefore runs at a speed nobody chose, set by the memory of the participant who is not in it, and everything about how the exchange feels -- deliberate, hurried, stilted, patient -- follows from that.

## Lever

The tempo the rendering requires against the tempo the encounter would otherwise have had.
The carrying force is that the imposed slowness is a genuine improvement for the weaker party, who gets an explanation delivered in pieces, with gaps, at a speed that admits questions, which is more than the same explanation would have given them in their own language; the resistance is that the pacing is attributed to whoever is speaking, so a doctor is remembered as unusually patient and an official as unusually curt on the strength of decisions the interpreter made about how much they could hold.
The Pacing shapes how both parties come across and is credited to neither.

## Echo

An encounter with a rhythm, and two people who each think it was the other's.
It shows the interpreter altering the impression without altering a word.
The Pacing is dated to the first pause requested.
