# Process: The Booking

## Taxonomy

[The Summoning](process_the_summoning.md), the move this is one form of.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: interpreting

## Initiated by

A professional is arranged in advance: certified, paid, scheduled, and given a slot.
Increasingly the slot is a telephone line or a screen on a trolley.

## Direction

Toward a competent stranger who is rationed.
What booking buys is real: training in the terminology, a code of practice, no relationship with either party, and no stake in the outcome.
What booking also fixes is the shape of the encounter -- the appointment is the institution's length, the line is as good as the institution's contract, and the interpreter leaves when the slot ends whether the conversation has or not.

## Lever

The competence of a stranger against the arithmetic of the slot.
The carrying force is that this is the good version and everybody should want it: an interpreter with no interest in the answer, who will render an unwelcome one, is the only arrangement in which the weaker party's account can survive intact; the resistance is that the booking is made by one party, for one party's schedule, at one party's expense, so the arrangement designed to be neutral arrives inside the institution's constraints and is the first thing cut when the constraints tighten.
The Booking is neutrality supplied on the terms of one side.

## Echo

A qualified person, present for a fixed number of minutes.
It shows that the professional version is a purchase and is budgeted like one.
The Booking is dated to the appointment.
