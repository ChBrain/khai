# Process: The Retention

## Taxonomy

[The Invisibility](process_the_invisibility.md), the move this is one form of.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: interpreting

## Initiated by

The conversation ends and everybody leaves.
Two of them heard half of it.

## Direction

Toward one person carrying the whole thing out of the room.
The interpreter is the only participant who received every word in both directions, understood all of it, and has no standing to say anything about any of it. Confidentiality forbids discussing it; the conduit norm implies there is nothing to discuss; and the booking ended when the slot did.
What is carried out is not always neutral. Asylum accounts, terminal diagnoses, child protection interviews, and police statements are rendered by somebody who then goes to the next appointment.

## Lever

The completeness of what they hold against the absence of anywhere to put it.
The carrying force is that this completeness is exactly what the role requires and is not a defect: an interpreter who forgot, filtered, or unburdened themselves as they went could not do the work, and the discipline of holding it is a real professional skill honestly practised; the resistance is that the arrangement provides no debrief, no supervision in most settings, and no acknowledgement that anything was carried, because the role's own description says the carrier was not present.
The Retention leaves the whole conversation with the person the room agreed was not in it.

## Echo

Two partial memories and one complete one, held by somebody with no claim on it.
It shows what the invisibility costs when the room has emptied.
The Retention is dated to the end of the appointment and does not end there.
