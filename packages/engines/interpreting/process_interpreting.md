# Process: Interpreting

## Taxonomy

A conversation conducted through a third person (no khai file of its own yet).

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: interpreting

## Initiated by

Two parties need to transact and have no language in common, so somebody else is brought into the room.
The transaction is usually one neither party can walk away from -- a consultation, a hearing, an interview, an arrest, a purchase of some size -- and it is usually the weaker party who cannot proceed without help.
From that moment the conversation has three people in it and is described, by everybody present, as having two.

## Direction

Toward an exchange that both parties experience as direct and neither of them is having directly.
Each side speaks to the other, watches the other's face, judges the other's honesty, and forms a view -- and every word they are judging arrived through somebody else's mouth, at somebody else's pace, in somebody else's phrasing.
The exchange succeeds exactly to the extent that both parties forget the arrangement they are in.

## Lever

The necessity of the third person against the requirement that they not be there.
The carrying force is that the arrangement genuinely works: two people with no shared language conduct an accurate, detailed, consequential conversation, and the profession that makes this possible is skilled, trained, and largely invisible by design, which is what makes it usable -- an interpreter who is noticed is an interpreter who is interrupting; the resistance is that the invisibility is a description of how the role is received rather than of what it involves, so the actual work of the role has no name in the room, no place in the codes, and no defender when it goes wrong.
Interpreting is a conversation between two people that a third person is holding together.

## Echo

Two parties who each believe they spoke to the other, and one person who knows what was actually said.
Interpreting does not close; it is a conversation being conducted through somebody, gathered into one reading -- a summoning, a relaying, a coordinating, an invisibility.
The engine's twist is here. **The room requires the interpreter to add nothing, and what they add is the only reason the conversation runs.** Every code, every instruction, and every party's expectation says the same thing: render, do not intervene, be a conduit. But two people who cannot hear each other's turn-endings talk over one another, and somebody has to decide who goes next; a four-minute explanation cannot be held whole, and somebody has to decide what survives; a question that means nothing in the other language has to be asked another way, and somebody has to choose it. Those decisions are the conversation. They are made continuously, by the one participant who is not supposed to be making any, and they are the first thing denied when anybody asks what happened.
It hands off what it is not: the work two people do to establish that they understood each other is grounding's; the trouble and its mending is repair's; converging on the other's way of speaking is accommodation's; the specialized variety and the moving between varieties are register's; what a saying does and what it conveys without stating are speech-act's and implicature's; whether somebody is believed is credibility's; and an interpreted scene staged to **stand for** a theme is authored key, the play's pitch.
The conversation is dated from the moment the third person arrived.
