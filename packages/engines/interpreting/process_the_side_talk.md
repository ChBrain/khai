# Process: The Side Talk

## Taxonomy

[The Coordinating](process_the_coordinating.md), the move this is one form of.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: interpreting

## Initiated by

The interpreter says something to one party that the other does not receive.
A clarification, a request to repeat, a check on which of two things was meant.

## Direction

Toward an exchange the room contains three people in and two people know about.
Most side talk is unavoidable and dull: a question was ambiguous, a name was unclear, a number was missed. It has to be resolved before the turn can cross, and resolving it means talking to one party alone.
Some of it is not dull. A warning, an explanation of what the institution actually wants, a quiet steer -- these happen, and they happen most where the parties are most unequal.

## Lever

The accuracy the check buys against the party who is left out of it.
The carrying force is that a rendering built on a guess is worse than a pause: the interpreter who checks produces a version the parties can rely on, and refusing to check on the grounds of impartiality would be a fastidiousness paid for by whoever is misunderstood; the resistance is that every check is an alliance for a few seconds, visible to the excluded party as an unexplained conversation about them, and there is no way to hold the check in public because holding it in public would be the rendering.
The Side Talk buys accuracy with exclusion, every time, in both directions.

## Echo

A party watching two people talk in a language they do not have.
It shows why the neutral role cannot be performed neutrally.
The Side Talk is dated to the check.
