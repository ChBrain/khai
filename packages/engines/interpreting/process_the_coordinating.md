# Process: The Coordinating

## Taxonomy

[Interpreting](process_interpreting.md), the movement this is one of.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: interpreting

## Initiated by

Two people who cannot hear each other's turn-endings begin talking over one another.
Somebody has to sort it out, and there is only one person in the room in a position to.

## Direction

Toward a conversation with a workable rhythm, managed by the participant with no standing to manage it.
The interpreter decides who goes next, holds one party's turn while rendering the other's, cuts a speaker off who has run past what can be held, asks for a repetition, requests a pause, and signals when a rendering is finished. None of this is rendering.
Studied closely, it turns out to be roughly half of everything the interpreter does and says.

## Lever

The necessity of the management against the norm that forbids it.
The carrying force is that without it there is no conversation to render: two speakers, blind to each other's endings and each waiting for a signal the other cannot give, produce a collision every second turn, and the traffic control that prevents this is skilled, continuous, and largely unnoticed; the resistance is that it is the one thing the role is not supposed to include, so the more competently it is done the more completely it disappears -- an interpreter whose coordination is visible is an interpreter who is intervening, and an interpreter whose coordination is invisible is credited with doing nothing.
The Coordinating is the work, and the role is defined as its absence.

## Echo

An orderly conversation that nobody attributes to anybody.
It shows the movement that carries the engine's twist: what the interpreter adds is the reason the exchange runs, and adding nothing is the entire description of their job.
The Coordinating is dated continuously and recorded nowhere.
