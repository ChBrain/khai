# Process: The Summoning

## Taxonomy

[Interpreting](process_interpreting.md), the movement this is one of.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: interpreting

## Initiated by

Somebody decides the conversation cannot proceed as a pair.
Rarely both parties, and rarely at the same moment: usually an institution notices, or a clerk asks a question, or the conversation has already failed once.

## Direction

Toward a third body in the room, arriving on terms nobody negotiated with them.
Who they are is decided by whoever is arranging: a certified professional on a scheduled slot, a voice on a speakerphone, a relative who came along, the bilingual member of staff who was free, or the eleven-year-old who is already there.
The party who needs the interpreter has, in almost every case, no say in which of these they get.

## Lever

The need for the person against the reluctance to be seen needing one.
The carrying force is that once summoned the arrangement is enormously better than the alternative, which is a consequential conversation conducted through gesture and guesswork; the resistance is that accepting an interpreter is a public statement about oneself -- that one is the party who cannot follow -- so the summoning is refused, minimised, or quietly skipped by exactly the people it exists for.
The Summoning brings in the person the conversation needs and marks the party who needed them.

## Echo

A triad where a dyad was expected, assembled by whoever was arranging.
It shows that the interpreter's terms are set before they arrive.
The Summoning is dated to the arrangement, not to the conversation.
