# Process: The Relaying

## Taxonomy

[Interpreting](process_interpreting.md), the movement this is one of.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: interpreting

## Initiated by

One party stops talking and the interpreter starts.
This is the part everybody means by the word.

## Direction

Toward a rendering that both parties treat as the original.
Neither party can check it. The one who spoke does not know what was produced; the one who heard does not know what was said. Between them sits an utterance that has been reconstructed by a third person under time pressure, and the whole room proceeds as though it were a copy.
It is not a copy, and it cannot be one: the languages do not carry the same distinctions, the sentence was too long to hold, and the register that was polite in one is cold in the other.

## Lever

Fidelity against usability.
The carrying force is that skilled relaying is astonishingly good and gets the substance across with a reliability that ought to be impossible; the resistance is that every rendering involves choices that no rule determines and that neither party is in a position to audit, so the accuracy the room is trusting is real and unverifiable at the same time.
The Relaying produces an utterance nobody in the room can check.

## Echo

Two utterances treated as one.
It shows the visible part of the role and the limit of what it can be.
The Relaying is dated to the turn.
