# Process: The Turn Holding

## Taxonomy

[The Coordinating](process_the_coordinating.md), the move this is one form of.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: interpreting

## Initiated by

Somebody is about to speak at the wrong moment, and is stopped.
A raised hand, a look, a palm, a word.

## Direction

Toward turns that arrive one at a time.
The interpreter holds a party who has more to say until the previous turn has crossed, releases them when it has, and stops the other party who has begun replying to a rendering that is not finished.
Held turns are also lost turns: a person interrupted mid-thought does not always come back to it, and what they were going to add goes unsaid without anybody registering that it did.

## Lever

The order the holding creates against the authority the holding requires.
The carrying force is that somebody must do it and the interpreter is the only participant who can -- they are the only person who knows when a turn is complete, because they are the only one who heard it -- so the sequencing they impose is not a preference but a technical requirement of the arrangement; the resistance is that stopping people from speaking is an exercise of authority over a conversation, performed several times a minute by the participant who is formally not in it, and nobody in the room, including the interpreter, would call it that.
The Turn Holding is traffic control described as punctuation.

## Echo

A sequence, and the turns that did not survive being held.
It shows authority being used by somebody who does not have any.
The Turn Holding is dated to the interruption.
