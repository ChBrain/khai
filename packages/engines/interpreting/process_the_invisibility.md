# Process: The Invisibility

## Taxonomy

[Interpreting](process_interpreting.md), the movement this is one of.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: interpreting

## Initiated by

A code, a training, or a bare expectation states that the interpreter is a conduit.
Render accurately. Add nothing. Omit nothing. Take no side. Do not advise.

## Direction

Toward a role described as a machine and performed as a person.
Interpreters, asked, largely endorse the description: they say they are invisible, a channel, a voice. Watched, the same interpreters coordinate constantly, and do not report it, and often do not appear to notice it.
The description is not a lie. It is what the role feels like from inside, because the coordinating is skilled enough to be automatic, and because there is no vocabulary in the room for anything else.

## Lever

The trust the norm buys against the work the norm denies.
The carrying force is that the conduit norm is load bearing and should not be casually dismissed: it is the reason a party can say something damaging and expect it to cross intact, the reason the other side accepts the rendering without argument, and the protection standing between a vulnerable person and an interpreter who has decided what is best for them; the resistance is that a norm which describes half the job as not existing leaves that half unexamined, untrained, unsupervised, and undefended, so the decisions with the most consequence are the ones nobody prepared anybody to make.
The Invisibility protects the conversation by refusing to describe it.

## Echo

A person the room has agreed not to see, doing work the room has agreed not to name.
It shows why the twist survives everybody's good intentions.
The Invisibility is dated to the code and renewed every session.
