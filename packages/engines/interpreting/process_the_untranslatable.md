# Process: The Untranslatable

## Taxonomy

[The Relaying](process_the_relaying.md), the move this is one form of.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: interpreting

## Initiated by

A term arrives with no equivalent on the other side.
A legal category, a kinship term, a benefit, a diagnosis, an insult, a religious obligation, a joke.

## Direction

Toward a choice between rendering the word and rendering the effect.
The word can be carried across and left inert -- accurate, and meaningless to the person receiving it. Or it can be replaced by the nearest thing that would do the same work in the other language, which is what the speaker wanted and is not what they said.
There is no rule that decides this. Codes of practice say be accurate, which does not distinguish the two, and both readings of accuracy are defensible.

## Lever

The word against the work the word was doing.
The carrying force is that a competent interpreter usually finds a third way -- a short gloss, a near equivalent with a flag, a request for clarification -- and the parties get something they can use; the resistance is that the third way is an addition, made on the interpreter's judgement, unrecorded and unremarked, and if anybody later reconstructs the conversation it will not appear, because the transcript shows only what each party said.
The Untranslatable forces a decision that the role formally forbids and practically requires.

## Echo

A term that crossed, in a form somebody chose.
It shows the smallest unit of the engine's whole problem.
The Untranslatable is dated to the word.
