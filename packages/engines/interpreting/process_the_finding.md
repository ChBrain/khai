# Process: The Finding

## Taxonomy

[The Invisibility](process_the_invisibility.md), the move this is one form of.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: interpreting

## Initiated by

The conversation goes wrong and somebody asks where.
An answer that contradicts an earlier one, a treatment refused, an instruction not followed, a claim disbelieved.

## Direction

Toward the error landing on the interpreter regardless of where it started.
It may have started with a question that was ambiguous in any language, an official using terms of art on somebody who has never heard them, a speaker who genuinely did contradict themselves, or a form that asks for a date nobody records.
It lands on the interpreter because the interpreter is the only participant whose contribution can be held against a written standard. Everybody else was expressing themselves; the interpreter was supposed to be accurate.

## Lever

The auditability of the interpreter against the unauditability of everybody else.
The carrying force is that accountability has to sit somewhere and an interpreter who could never be wrong would be a serious hazard: mistranslations happen, they do damage, and a profession that could not be checked would deserve none of the trust it is given; the resistance is that the check is available only on the interpreter, so the whole encounter's ambiguity converges on the one participant with a standard, and the finding that "the interpretation was inadequate" closes a case that was never really about the interpretation.
The Finding collects the room's errors at the only address that has one.

## Echo

A finding, and a set of causes it did not reach.
It shows the price of being the only measurable participant.
The Finding is dated to the review.
