# Process: The Waiver

## Taxonomy

[The Summoning](process_the_summoning.md), the move this is one form of.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: interpreting

## Initiated by

The party who needs an interpreter declines one.
"It's fine, I understand." Sometimes true, often nearly true, and occasionally not true at all.

## Direction

Toward a conversation conducted in a language one participant is managing rather than using.
The waiver is rarely about language. Accepting an interpreter is accepting a description -- the one who cannot follow, the one who needs the extra arrangement, the one holding up the clinic -- and a person who has spent years assembling a competent adult self in a second language is being asked to set it down in front of a stranger.
So they nod at the right points, catch most of it, and lose the part that was conditional.

## Lever

The dignity of managing against the cost of the part that was missed.
The carrying force is that the waiver is a reasonable act by a competent person: they do understand most of it, the interpreter would double the length of the appointment, and being treated as a person who can hold their own conversation is not a small thing to want; the resistance is that the parts that fall out are the parts that were hardest to say, which are also the parts that mattered -- the qualification, the side effect, the deadline, the condition on which the whole thing turns.
The Waiver protects a self at the cost of the conditional clause.

## Echo

A conversation that both sides record as having gone well.
It shows why provision existing is not provision used.
The Waiver is dated to the offer that was declined.
