# Process: The Conduit Norm

## Taxonomy

[The Invisibility](process_the_invisibility.md), the move this is one form of.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: interpreting

## Initiated by

The standard is written down.
Accuracy, impartiality, confidentiality, no addition, no omission, no advice.

## Direction

Toward a profession with a defensible account of itself.
The codes did real work. They ended an era in which the interpreter was whoever was available and whatever they said was the record, and they gave a low-status occupation a standard to be measured against and a reason to be paid.
They also set the standard at conduit, which is the one thing the work is not, so the profession's account of itself and the profession's practice have been diverging quietly ever since.

## Lever

The protection the standard gives against the practice it fails to describe.
The carrying force is that this is the right standard to have written and the alternatives are worse: a code that licensed the interpreter to explain, soften, advise, or improve would license every abuse the code exists to prevent, and the people it would be practised on are the ones with least ability to object; the resistance is that a rule which forbids what the work requires is not followed -- it is worked around, silently, by every competent practitioner, and the workaround is exactly the part that never gets taught.
The Conduit Norm is a good rule that describes a job nobody has.

## Echo

A written standard and an unwritten practice, both necessary.
It shows the gap the whole engine sits in.
The Conduit Norm is dated to the code in force.
