# Instructions: Interpreting

How a Playwright wires the interpreting engine into a play. You wire by linking the play's own content to the engine's files; the engine files stay untouched, they are canon. The Roadie plumbs the engine's law into Instructions Knowledge on deploy. Authoring guidance, not runtime content, and it does not go on tour.

## Human

- The human keeps everybody competent, because the engine needs no incompetence and stops working the moment it gets some: the interpreter is skilled, the official is not obstructive, and the party who declined an interpreter was making a reasonable decision about themselves. Then stage the coordinating in full view and let nobody in the scene notice it -- that is the twist working, and it only works if the audience sees what the room does not. Decide before writing what each of the two parties thinks happened, since both of them will be sure they spoke to the other.

## Agent

- From the play's own content, a persona links under Projection which movement they stand in: how they came to be in the arrangement, what crossed, what they are managing, and what they carried out. Read the engine on what the third person is doing, never on what either party meant. Keep it the triad: the work two people do to establish they understood each other is grounding's, the trouble and its mending is repair's, and the specialized variety is register's. You link to the engine, you never edit it.

## Collaboration

- Establishing mutual understanding, locating and mending trouble in talk, and converging on the other's way of speaking are grounding's, repair's, and accommodation's; what a saying performs, what it conveys without stating, and the key it is said in are speech-act's, implicature's, tone's, and the utterance composite's; the specialized variety that gates who can follow is register's; whether a speaker is believed and the front kept up in front of others are credibility's, face's, and backstage's; the occupational cost of repeated exposure is compassion's, upkeep's, and vigil's. Clinic scheduling, language service procurement, court rules, and asylum procedure are the trade and set the format. Whether a production's side exchange was a clarification or a steer, and whether a finding of inadequate interpretation reached the actual cause, are the team's review.

## Knowledge

- Interpreting is a conversation conducted through a third person: two parties who must transact without a shared language, and the participant who carries every word between them while everybody present describes the exchange as having two people in it. It is not grounding's mutual understanding, not repair's trouble and mending, not accommodation's convergence, and not register's specialized variety. It wires on a persona at Projection. Four movements over one root. The summoning brings in the person the conversation needs and marks the party who needed them: a professional booking is competent and rationed on the institution's schedule, a pressing puts a relative, a member of staff, or a child inside the encounter with a stake and no protection, and a waiver is the party who declines to avoid being described as the one who needs help, which costs them the conditional clause. The relaying produces an utterance nobody in the room can check, since compression systematically drops the hedges and self-corrections a listener judges honesty by, first-person voicing is good craft that manufactures the interpreter's absence sentence by sentence, and an untranslatable term forces a choice between the word and the work it was doing. The coordinating is roughly half of everything the interpreter says and is the one thing the role formally excludes: turns are held and sometimes lost, every side check buys accuracy with a few seconds of exclusion, and the tempo of the encounter is set by the working memory of the participant who is not in it. The invisibility is a good rule describing a job nobody has, so the errors converge on the only participant with an auditable standard and the whole conversation leaves with the person the room agreed was not there. The engine's twist is in the root, carried by the coordinating: the room requires the interpreter to add nothing, and what they add is the only reason the conversation runs.

## System

- Do make the interpreter good. Fast, accurate, unshowy, and better at this than anybody watching realises.
- Do show the coordinating and let nobody name it. A raised hand, a held turn, a request to slow down, and not one person in the scene registering that a decision was made.
- Do let a side exchange be innocent. A number checked, a name confirmed, and the excluded party watching two people talk about them in a language they do not have.
- Do stage a waiver. Somebody says they will manage, they mostly do manage, and the part they miss is the condition the whole thing turned on.
- Do give the pressed interpreter no exit. They are competent, they are related to one party, and they have to go home afterwards.
- Do let a finding land on the interpreter. The ambiguity started somewhere else and there is nowhere else with a standard to measure it against.
- Do close after the room empties. Two people who each think they had a conversation, and one who has all of it and nowhere to put it.
- Do not model the dyad's machinery (grounding, repair, accommodation), what a saying carries (speech-act, implicature, tone, utterance), the specialized variety (register), whether somebody is believed (credibility, face, backstage), or the occupational cost afterwards (compassion, upkeep, vigil). Do not make a mistranslation stand for a theme; that is authored key, the play's pitch. Do not edit the engine's files; wire only from the play's side.
