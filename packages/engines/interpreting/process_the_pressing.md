# Process: The Pressing

## Taxonomy

[The Summoning](process_the_summoning.md), the move this is one form of.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: interpreting

## Initiated by

The nearest bilingual person is asked to help.
A relative, a neighbour, a cleaner pulled off the ward, another patient in the waiting room, or a child.

## Direction

Toward an interpreter who is inside the situation being interpreted.
Children do a great deal of this, routinely and unremarked, in shops, surgeries, benefit offices, and parent evenings, and they are often good at it -- fluent, quick, and unusually motivated to get the result right.
That last part is the difficulty. They are not rendering an account; they are conducting their family's business, in front of their family, in a language their family cannot check.

## Lever

The availability of the person against everything their presence brings into the room.
The carrying force is that the pressed interpreter is there, is free, is immediate, and often knows the context better than any professional would -- the history, the household, what the question is really asking; the resistance is that they have a relationship with one party, a stake in the outcome, no protection, and no way to decline, and what is said in the room cannot be unheard afterwards, so an adult receives a diagnosis, a refusal, or an accusation in a version shaped by somebody who has to go home with them.
The Pressing gets the conversation done by putting somebody inside it who cannot leave afterwards.

## Echo

A conversation completed at a cost that lands outside the room.
It shows the version that most interpreting in the world actually is.
The Pressing is dated to whoever was standing nearest.
