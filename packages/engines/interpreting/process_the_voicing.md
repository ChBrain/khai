# Process: The Voicing

## Taxonomy

[The Relaying](process_the_relaying.md), the move this is one form of.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: interpreting

## Initiated by

The interpreter renders in the first person.
"I have had this pain since March," said by somebody who has no pain.

## Direction

Toward a grammar in which the third person does not exist.
The convention is deliberate and it works: it keeps the exchange direct, saves a clause on every turn, and stops the conversation collapsing into a stream of reported speech in which nobody addresses anybody.
It also means that every sentence the interpreter produces claims, grammatically, to be somebody else's. The pronoun is the strongest available evidence that there is no third person present, and it is supplied by the third person, hundreds of times an hour.

## Lever

The directness the convention buys against the person it writes out.
The carrying force is that voicing is good craft and good for the weaker party: rendered in the first person, an account keeps its force and its speaker keeps their standing, where "he says that he has had" turns a person into a case being discussed; the resistance is that the convention is doing more than saving words -- it trains everybody in the room, including the interpreter, to describe the arrangement as one that is not happening.
The Voicing lends a pronoun and takes a presence.

## Echo

A room in which nobody has said "the interpreter" out loud.
It shows the invisibility being manufactured sentence by sentence.
The Voicing is dated to the first turn and never varies.
