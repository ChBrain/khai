# Process: The Compression

## Taxonomy

[The Relaying](process_the_relaying.md), the move this is one form of.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: interpreting

## Initiated by

A long turn arrives and has to come out shorter.
The four-minute explanation, the rehearsed script, the account that circles back on itself.

## Direction

Toward a version that keeps what is load bearing and loses the rest.
Somebody has to decide which is which, in real time, with no chance to consult, while the next sentence is arriving. That decision is made by the person the room regards as making none.
Compression is not laziness and mostly not error. Working memory has a ceiling, the speaker did not pause, and a rendering that reproduced every hedge would be unusable.

## Lever

The necessity of the cut against the invisibility of what was cut.
The carrying force is that the compression is what makes the conversation possible at all and is usually done well: the substance survives, the point lands, and the exchange proceeds at a human speed; the resistance is that the losses are systematic rather than random -- hedges, qualifications, repetitions, self-corrections, and emotional colouring go first, and those are precisely the features on which a listener judges whether somebody is being straight with them.
The Compression keeps the content and drops the evidence of how it was said.

## Echo

A shorter turn that carries the meaning and not the manner.
It shows how an accurate rendering can still change what the listener concludes.
The Compression is dated to the turn it shortened.
