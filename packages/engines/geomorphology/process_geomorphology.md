# Process: Geomorphology

## Taxonomy

Tectonics.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: geomorphology

## Initiated by

The forces within and below the ground move, and the place's own floor is made or broken.
Geomorphology starts not at the surface but beneath it -- the plates shoving, the strain releasing, the magma rising, the void collapsing -- and the place is standing on the result before anyone reads the ground for it.
Every place stands on a floor that was built and can be broken; geomorphology is that floor as an actor, not the backdrop it is taken for.

## Direction

Up from below, and out through the ground the place stands on.
Four modes work the land itself, the making and the breaking of it: [Uplift](process_uplift.md) raises the ground, [Quake](process_quake.md) ruptures it, [Eruption](process_eruption.md) pours it new and buries what was, and [Subsidence](process_subsidence.md) drops it away.
A place does not bear its ground evenly: one face is lifted while another cracks, a slope is built by fire from below while a hollow settles and gives.

## Lever

The energy within the earth against the ground's own strength to hold its form.
The carrying force is the deep engine -- the plates' shove, the pressure of magma, the pull of a drained void; the resistance is the rock's strength, the fault locked, the ground bearing its own weight until it cannot.
Where the strain exceeds the strength the ground moves, suddenly or by slow shove, and the place is remade under itself.
Unlike the surface worn grain by grain from outside, this comes from within: erosion wears the ground the earth here builds and breaks.

## Echo

The place remade under itself -- raised, cracked, poured new, or dropped.
Geomorphology does not close like a front passing; it leaves the land changed and hands the new ground onward, for the surface forces to work and life to reclaim.
The same violence makes and unmakes: the eruption that buries a town builds the most fertile plain, the uplift that raises the range makes the valley, the quake that levels a city reveals the fault that raised the hills.
The place is dated from the ground it stands on, and every later force -- the wearing, the water, the fire, the returning green -- works the floor this one built or broke.
