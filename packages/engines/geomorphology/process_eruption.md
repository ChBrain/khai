# Process: Eruption

## Taxonomy

[Geomorphology](process_geomorphology.md), the mode where new ground is poured from below.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: geomorphology

## Initiated by

The pressure of magma exceeds the crust that caps it, and the earth opens.
Eruption starts wherever molten rock has risen and found or forced a way out -- the vent, the fissure, the long-quiet cone waking -- and pours the inside of the earth onto its surface as lava, ash, and gas.
It is the mode that makes by destroying: new ground born of the same event that buries the old.

## Direction

Out and over, then down and settling into new land.
Eruption covers the place -- the lava sheeting the slope, the ash falling and burying, the flow filling the valley -- and in burying it lays down the ground that will be there when it cools.
It moves the place from what it was to what the eruption makes of it: the buried town, the new black field, the island risen from the sea where none was, the caldera where the mountain stood.

## Lever

The pressure of the magma against the crust that holds it down.
The carrying force is the rising melt and the gas dissolved in it, driving upward; the resistance is the weight and strength of the rock capping the chamber, holding until the pressure wins or a crack lets it go.
The place has no temper for what buries it, only the distance from the vent and the luck of the wind that carries the ash -- and the long grace that the buried ground becomes, in time, the richest.

## Echo

New ground made, and the old buried under it.
Eruption leaves the place transfigured -- the fresh rock, the ash mantle, the island, the crater -- and hands that new and violent ground to the forces that will weather it and the life that will, in time, find it the most fertile it has.
The eruption that kills is the one that builds: the ash that buries a city is the soil that feeds the next, the fire from below is how the land is made new.
Eruption is dated from the opening of the earth, the moment the inside became the surface.
