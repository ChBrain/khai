# Geomorphology: Reference

## Line of Work

Geomorphology as **process**: the ground's own violence, the landform made and broken
by forces from within and below -- the place's very floor raised, ruptured, poured
new, or dropped away, shown in the ground before anyone reads it. It wires on a place
at Shown, the place's physical self-presentation, because the ground a place stands on
is the first thing it puts in front of whoever enters. The domain does not model the
built fabric of the setting, which is the space engine's; nor the atmospheric load from
above, which is the weather engine's; nor the slow surface wearing from outside, which
is the erosion engine's; nor the water regime that floods and carves, which is
hydrology's; nor the material entropy of made things, which is decay's. It models the
sudden, endogenic reshaping of the land itself, and reads it as one force with two
faces: the making and the breaking of the ground.

The spine is the recognition, from Lyell onward, that the land is not a fixed stage but
a body in slow and sudden motion -- and that the same forces build and destroy. Lyell's
_Principles of Geology_ made the ground a process, its present form the sum of forces
still at work; the modern narrators of deep time -- McPhee tracing a continent's
assembly in a roadcut, Winchester reading a catastrophe as the earth's ordinary
violence -- carry that reading into the single place. So the engine gives the ground's
violence four modes -- uplift that raises the land, quake that ruptures it, eruption
that pours it new, and subsidence that drops it away -- the land as its own maker and
breaker, felt in the place as the floor it cannot take for granted.

## Restrictions

What the engine refuses to model, and to whom it delegates.

- **The built fabric of the setting (space)**: The engine models the ground the place
  stands on, never the setting built upon it. The layout, the walls, the affordances,
  the standing induced mood are the space engine's; geomorphology borrows the place's
  Shown face and works the floor beneath the fabric, and re-owns none of the furniture.
  Space builds on the ground; geomorphology is the ground's own motion under it.
- **The atmospheric load from above (weather)**: The wind, the rain, the heat that hit
  the place from the sky are the weather engine's; geomorphology is the violence from
  within and below. Weather works the surface from above in the hour; geomorphology
  raises, cracks, pours, and drops the ground itself. The two meet where a storm
  triggers a slide, but the air is weather's and the ground is geomorphology's.
- **The slow surface wearing from outside (erosion)**: This is the sharp boundary of the
  land's-forces family. Geomorphology is the sudden, endogenic force -- the plates, the
  fault, the magma, the void -- that builds and breaks the landform from within.
  Erosion is the slow, exogenic force -- wind, water, frost -- that wears the surface
  away from outside. Geomorphology raises the range; erosion wears it to stumps. One
  makes and breaks the ground; the other lowers it grain by grain.
- **The water regime (hydrology)**: The flood, the drought, the water-table, the river
  in spate are hydrology's; geomorphology is the ground the water runs on and carves.
  The two meet where water drives a landslide or a river cuts a canyon, but the moving
  water is hydrology's and the moving ground is geomorphology's; the canyon read as
  landform is here, the flow that cut it is there.
- **The material entropy of made things (decay)**: Decay is the breakdown of the matter
  a maker imposed order on -- rust, rot, crumble. Geomorphology is the motion of the
  land itself, made or unmade, not the slow failure of what stands on it. A building
  cracks because the ground quaked (geomorphology) or because its own fabric rots
  (decay); the boundary is whether the floor moved or the thing on it failed.
- **A persona's feeling, and the ground as authored symbol**: How the upheaval makes a
  persona feel -- the terror of the quake, the awe of the volcano -- is the persona's
  own engines'; geomorphology delivers the moving ground, not the feeling. And the
  ground's violence made to **stand for** a persona's own upheaval, the earthquake that
  mirrors a breaking life, is refused: that is authored key, the play's pitch, not the
  force on the place.
- **The mode itself is not engine content**: The engine declares the four modes exist;
  which one a given place undergoes or bears the mark of lives on that place, linked
  under its Shown chapter. The engine refuses to fix a place's ground for it.
