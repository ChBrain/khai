# Instructions: Geomorphology

How a Playwright wires the geomorphology engine into a play. You wire by linking the
play's own content to the engine's files; the engine files stay untouched, they are
canon. The Roadie plumbs the engine's law into Instructions Knowledge on deploy.
Authoring guidance, not runtime content, and it does not go on tour.

## Human

- The human sets which places stand on a ground that is an actor -- raised, cracked, poured new, or dropped -- and whether that violence is the place's deep history or a live event in the play.

## Agent

- From the play's own content, a place whose ground moves links under Shown the mode of geomorphology it undergoes or bears the mark of: the uplift that raised it, the quake that ruptured it, the eruption that made or buried it, or the subsidence that dropped it away. Play the geomorphology as the ground itself shown -- the raised coast, the fault scarp, the ash field, the sinkhole -- present in what the place puts before whoever enters, never narrated, and never as a mirror of a persona's own upheaval. Keep it the ground's own sudden violence from within and below, not the surface worn from outside (that is erosion), the water (hydrology), the air (weather), or the decay of made things (decay). You link to the engine, you never edit it.

## Collaboration

- The built fabric on the ground is the space engine's; the atmospheric load from above is weather's; the slow surface wearing is erosion's; the water regime is hydrology's; the decay of made things is decay's. Geomorphology is the sharp sibling of erosion -- the sudden, endogenic making and breaking of the land against erosion's slow, exogenic wearing of it. Whether the mode rings true to the ground and its geology is the team's review.

## Knowledge

- Geomorphology is the ground's own violence: the landform made and broken by forces from within and below, the place's very floor raised, ruptured, poured new, or dropped away, shown in the ground before anyone names it (Lyell's land as ongoing process; the same forces that build also destroy). It wires on a place at Shown, and it is the sudden endogenic force behind erosion's slow exogenic wearing. Four modes carry it -- uplift is the ground raised, the range built and the coast lifted; quake is the fault ruptured, the stored strain released and the ground shaken and offset; eruption is the volcanic, magma and ash and new rock poured from below and what it buries; subsidence is the ground given way, the void and the sinkhole and the settling, the land let down where its support withdrew. The same violence makes and unmakes -- the eruption that buries builds the most fertile plain, the fault that levels raised the hills. Read a place by the mode of ground-violence its floor undergoes or bears.

## System

- Do give a place whose ground is an actor its mode under Shown; do play the geomorphology as the ground itself shown, present not narrated; do keep the reading the ground's own sudden violence -- the raised, the cracked, the poured, the dropped -- not a feeling and not a symbol.
- Do read the making and the breaking as one force: the same eruption buries and builds, the same fault levels and raises -- geomorphology is the land as its own maker and breaker, not only its ruin.
- Do not model the built fabric on the ground (that is space), the air from above (weather), the slow surface wearing (erosion), the water regime (hydrology), or the decay of made things (decay). Do not make the ground's upheaval mirror a persona's own (that is the play's pitch). Do not edit the engine's files; wire only from the play's side.
