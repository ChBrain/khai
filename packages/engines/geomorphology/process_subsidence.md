# Process: Subsidence

## Taxonomy

[Geomorphology](process_geomorphology.md), the mode where the ground gives way.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: geomorphology

## Initiated by

The support beneath the ground is lost, and the land settles or drops into the space left.
Subsidence starts wherever the floor is undermined -- the limestone dissolved to a void, the water or oil drawn out from the pores, the peat drained and shrinking, the fill compacting under its own weight -- and the ground above comes down to meet the gap.
It is the quiet mode: not thrown up but let down, the land sinking where its support withdrew.

## Direction

Downward, into the space the lost support leaves.
Subsidence lowers the place -- the slow settling that cracks the walls and tilts the floors, the sudden sinkhole that swallows the road, the whole delta or basin sinking below the water it once stood above.
It moves the place toward the low ground and the water: the land that was dry going wet, the surface that was level going bowl, the ground that held going hollow.

## Lever

The pull of the weight above against the support that is failing beneath.
The carrying force is gravity on the unsupported ground; the resistance is whatever still holds it up -- the roof of the cavern, the pressure in the pores, the fibre of the peat -- failing slowly as it is dissolved, drained, or squeezed.
The place cannot see this coming as it sees a front: the void grows unseen below, and the ground is sound until the moment it is not.

## Echo

The place lowered, hollowed, or swallowed.
Subsidence leaves the ground dropped -- the settled foundation, the sinkhole, the sinking coast, the basin gone below its water -- and hands the low new ground to the water that gathers in it and the weight that goes on pressing.
Where uplift builds and eruption pours, subsidence takes away from below: the land quietly un-made, the support withdrawn, the ground come down to fill the space that opened under it.
Subsidence is dated from the giving way, the moment the unseen void reached the surface.
