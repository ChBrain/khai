# Process: Quake

## Taxonomy

[Geomorphology](process_geomorphology.md), the mode where the ground ruptures.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: geomorphology

## Initiated by

The strain stored in a locked fault exceeds the rock's strength, and it slips.
Quake starts wherever the ground has been loading in silence -- the fault held fast while the plates dragged past it, the strain building year on year -- and then releases all at once, the rock breaking and the shock running out through the land.
It is the sudden mode: no warning at the surface, the whole load delivered in seconds.

## Direction

Outward from the rupture, through the ground and everything on it.
The quake shakes the place and everything the place holds -- the ground heaving, cracking, and shifting, the fault scarp opening, the slope shaken loose, the soft ground turned briefly to liquid.
It moves the place from standing to shaken: the walls that held come down, the ground that was one is offset, the still land made, for a moment, a sea.

## Lever

The stored strain against the strength of the locked fault.
The carrying force is the years of drag loaded into the rock; the resistance is the fault's grip, holding until it cannot -- and the longer it holds, the more it has stored to release.
The place cannot temper this as it tempers weather: there is no lee from a quake, only ground that shakes more or less, built to sway or built to fall.

## Echo

The place shaken and offset, the ground no longer what it was.
Quake closes in seconds but leaves the land changed -- the scarp raised, the course of the river jumped, the coast dropped or lifted, the built things down -- and hands the broken ground to the slow forces and the rebuilding.
The same fault that levels the town is the one whose long slips raised the hills above it; the ground's worst violence and its building are one motion, felt here as catastrophe.
Quake is dated from the rupture, the instant the silent load became the shaken ground.
