# Process: Uplift

## Taxonomy

[Geomorphology](process_geomorphology.md), the mode where the ground is raised.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: geomorphology

## Initiated by

The plates shove, or the crust rebounds, and the ground begins to rise.
Uplift starts wherever the land is lifted -- the range built by collision, the coast raised by the fault, the terrace jacked up a notch in a single quake, the whole region rebounding as its ice load melts away.
It is the making mode: the ground gaining height, slowly over ages or in one lurch.

## Direction

Upward, and toward relief.
Uplift raises the land above what surrounds it -- the mountain from the plain, the cliff from the shore, the plateau from the basin -- and in raising it sets the stage for everything the height then invites: the steepened slope, the quickened river, the weather the peak makes.
It moves the place toward prominence: the high ground, the vantage, the watershed, the barrier -- the land that stands over the rest because the earth pushed it up.

## Lever

The deep shove against the ground's weight and the wearing that opposes it.
The carrying force is the plates' collision or the crust's rebound, lifting rock against gravity; the resistance is the ground's own mass and the erosion that begins to lower it the instant it rises.
A range is the balance of the two: built faster than it wears, it climbs; built slower, it is worn to stumps -- the height a place holds is uplift winning, for now, against the wearing.

## Echo

The high ground, raised and holding.
Uplift leaves the place standing above its surroundings -- the peak, the ridge, the raised coast, the plateau -- and hands that height to every force the height then drives: the water runs faster off it, the frost works its bared rock, the weather breaks on it.
It is dated from the raising, and it is the mode where the land is made tall: the ground built up, the relief the rest of geomorphology and the surface forces then work.
