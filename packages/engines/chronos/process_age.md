# Process: Age

## Taxonomy

[Chronos](process_chronos.md), the facet of sheer accrued quantity.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: chronos

## Initiated by

The clock starts at the origin, and age is the count it keeps.
Age begins with the thing's first moment and grows by every one after -- the plain quantity of elapsed time the thing carries, whether it is used, stored, or forgotten.
It needs no event to advance: a thing left untouched in a drawer is a year older for the year that passed over it, and that count is the first fact chronos reads.

## Direction

Upward, one way, never falling.
Age moves only toward more: the vintage-year fixed and the distance from it widening, the object placed ever further into the past of its own making.
It carries the thing into an era -- the piece of its decade, its century, its age -- so that to name a thing's age is to name where in the long sequence of made things it belongs.

## Lever

The accrued quantity against nothing that can lower it.
The carrying force is that each passing unit adds and none can be returned -- age is the one figure of the thing that only rises, the tally no upkeep, repair, or care can subtract from.
A thing can be mended to look new, but its age is untouched by the mending: the restored clock is as old as ever, its years spent and uncountable back.

## Echo

The thing placed by how old it is.
Age leaves the piece dated -- ancient or recent, antique or new-made, of this era or that -- and hands that datum to every other reading: the sequence orders from it, the endurance is measured against it, and the maturation is only fullness that this much age could bring.
Age is the ground figure of the object's time, the plain how-old on which the rest of chronos stands.
