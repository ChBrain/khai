# Process: Chronos

## Taxonomy

Time.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: chronos

## Initiated by

The thing comes into being, and measured time begins to run on it.
Chronos starts at the origin -- the making, the minting, the felling of the tree that became the beam -- and from that first moment every later one is dated against it; the clock does not wait to be noticed.
A thing without a beginning in time is not yet a piece but an abstraction: the instant it is made it is placed in the sequence and starts to age.

## Direction

Forward, one way, accruing.
The passage works four facets of the thing's own career in measured time, read under its Yearbook: [Age](process_age.md) is the quantity it has gathered from its origin, [Sequence](process_sequence.md) the ordered run of dated events it carries, [Endurance](process_endurance.md) its having lasted through the years, and [Maturation](process_maturation.md) the twist -- the fullness that only elapsed time can bring.
A thing does not sit in time evenly: it may be old yet barely endured, richly sequenced yet unripe, matured in one part and merely aged in another.

## Lever

The one-way passage against the thing's power to carry its own dates.
The carrying force is that time runs irreversibly and the thing gathers it whether or not anything happens to it -- unused, untouched, it is a year older by a year's passing; the resistance is the thing's endurance, that it persists legibly enough to still bear its origin and its order.
Chronos cannot be arrested the way decay can be maintained against: no upkeep returns a spent year. What can fail is the reading -- when the thing is lost, broken past identity, or its provenance cut, the clock still ran but the piece can no longer be dated by it.

## Echo

The thing dated, aged, its history a stratum the next force reads.
Chronos does not close; it accrues, carrying the years forward as the piece's datable age and its ordered past.
It hands onward what it is not: the material the years work in the matter is decay's and wear's, the career between commodity and singular status is biography's, the opportune moment cut from the flow is kairos's; chronos keeps the measure -- how old, in what order, how long endured, and whether the years made the thing more.
The piece is dated from its origin, and every later reading of it stands on the time it has already gathered.
