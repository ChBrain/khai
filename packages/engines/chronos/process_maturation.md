# Process: Maturation

## Taxonomy

[Chronos](process_chronos.md), the facet where the passage is read as gain.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: chronos

## Initiated by

The same accrual that ages one thing toward its end brings another to its fullness.
Maturation starts wherever elapsed time completes rather than spends -- the wine come round in the cellar, the timber seasoned true, the violin opened by a lifetime of playing-in, the whisky that only the years in the cask could make.
It is chronos read the other way: not the thing nearer its end but the thing arrived at its best, a state no new-made thing can be given, only an aged one can grow into.

## Direction

Toward fullness, readiness, the condition only years confer.
Where age merely counts and endurance merely holds, maturation completes: the matter or the make settles into a quality it could not be handed, only earned by real elapsed time.
It moves toward the vintage -- the year itself become the mark of worth, the thing valued for the time held in it, sought out precisely because it is not new.

## Lever

The worth the years confer against the pull to prize the new.
The carrying force is that maturation is unfakeable -- fullness comes only by true elapsed time, and the eye that knows it, the vintner's or the luthier's, values the earning; the resistance is the impulse to reach for the fresh, the unaged, the not-yet-come-into-itself.
This is chronos's own twist, and it is not decay's patina: patina is the surface time marks on the matter, read as beauty; maturation is the developing state elapsed time brings to fullness, read as readiness -- the pristine-labelled wine deeply matured, the distressed shelf richly patinated, and neither the other.

## Echo

The thing made more, not less, by its years.
It shows a thing come into its own -- the matured, the seasoned, the vintage -- and carries beneath it the object-time's twist, the sibling of the whole family's ambivalence: that elapsed time is not only the thing's wearing toward its end but, read the other way, the very making of its worth.
Maturation is dated from the years it took to come full, and it is the facet where a piece stops merely ageing and begins to be prized for the time it holds.
