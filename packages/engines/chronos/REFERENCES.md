# Chronos: Reference

## Line of Work

Chronos as **process**: the measured, sequential passage of time as it bears on a
made thing -- the quantitative, one-way flow that a piece gathers from its origin,
read under its Yearbook (the object's history told as a story in time). It is the
first force to wire on a piece's Yearbook chapter, and it reads the measure of time,
not its matter: how old the thing is, in what order its dated events fell, how long
it has endured, and whether the years brought it to a fullness worth more than its
newness. The domain does not model the material the years work in the fabric or the
substance, which is decay's and wear's; nor the object's career between commodity and
singular status, which is biography's; nor the object being kept and passed across
generations, which is the heirloom engine's; nor a persona's felt duration or resting
orientation toward time, which is the time-perception engine's; nor the opportune
moment cut from the flow, which is kairos's. It models the plain passage the clock
lays on an object, and reads it two ways -- as the years spent, and as the years that
made the thing more.

The spine is the classical distinction between _chronos_ and _kairos_: quantitative,
sequential, measurable time against the qualitative, opportune moment. Newton named
absolute time as that which "flows equably" without relation to anything external --
the pure measure this engine reads. McTaggart parted the two axes the facets stand on:
the tenseless earlier-than / later-than ordering (the B-series, which the sequence
facet carries) and the tensed past the age facet accrues from. And Kermode read the
difference in narrative terms -- _chronos_ as mere "passing time," the tick following
tock, against _kairos_ as the moment "charged with significance." So the engine gives
the object's passage three facets of accrual -- the age gathered, the sequence
ordered, the endurance held -- and keeps a fourth, maturation, the same elapsed time
read not as the thing spent toward its end but as the thing brought to its fullness,
the vintage the years alone can make.

## Restrictions

What the engine refuses to model, and to whom it delegates.

- **The material the years work (decay, wear)**: Chronos reads the passage; decay and
  wear read what the passage does to the matter. The years gather on the coin as age
  (chronos); the same years rust its metal (decay); the hand that spends it dulls its
  edge (wear). Chronos owns how old, in what order, how long endured, and never the
  oxide, the rot, or the abrasion -- the measure, not the matter it works in.
- **The value of the surface time marks (patina)**: Decay's patina and chronos's
  maturation are the family's two time-twists, and they are distinct. Patina is the
  surface record the years leave on the matter, read as beauty -- a mark on the fabric.
  Maturation is the developmental state elapsed time brings to fullness, read as
  readiness -- a condition grown into, not a mark. The pristine-labelled wine is deeply
  matured with no patina; the artificially distressed shelf is richly patinated with no
  maturation. They come apart, and each engine keeps its own.
- **The object's career of status (biography)**: The charted biography engine reads the
  object's movement between commodity and singular status -- an economic and social
  career. Chronos reads the plain measured time beneath it: the age, the order, the
  endurance. The two are parallel readings of the object's past, one of status and one
  of the clock; endurance is where they touch, the survivor become singular by having
  lasted, but chronos owns only the lasting, not the status it confers.
- **The object kept and passed on (heirloom)**: The heirloom engine reads the object
  deliberately withheld from exchange and carried across generations as a lineage's
  charge. Chronos reads the measured time the object gathers whether or not anyone keeps
  it; the drawer-forgotten thing ages exactly as the treasured one does. Heirloom is the
  keeping; chronos is the clock that runs under it, kept or not.
- **A persona's felt time (time-perception)**: How long a stretch feels, how real the
  future seems, the subjective texture of duration are the time-perception engine's, on
  the persona. Chronos is the objective measure on the object: the thing is a hundred
  years old whether it feels old to anyone or not. Time-perception is time experienced;
  chronos is time gathered.
- **The opportune moment (kairos)**: The qualitative, charged moment cut from the flow --
  the window that opens and shuts, the ripe instant that must be seized -- is kairos's,
  the family's second engine. Chronos is the measured passage the moment is located on,
  never the fittingness of the moment itself. Chronos runs the clock; kairos reads the
  one moment on it that counts.
- **A persona's feeling, and time as authored symbol**: What the object's age makes a
  persona feel -- the awe of the ancient, the pang of the outlived -- is the persona's
  own engines'; chronos delivers the measured fact, not the feeling. And the object's
  time made to **stand for** a persona's own ageing or mortality, the old clock that
  mirrors the dying line, is refused: that is authored key, the play's pitch, not the
  force on the thing.
- **The facet itself is not engine content**: The engine declares the four facets exist;
  which one a given object shows lives on that object, linked under its Yearbook chapter.
  The engine refuses to fix a thing's time for it.

## Encoding

Source to constraint, per file.

- **[chronos](process_chronos.md)** (the root): the measured, one-way passage of time a
  made thing gathers from its origin, read as the measure and not the matter. Anchored
  by Newton (absolute time that flows equably) and Kermode (chronos as passing time).
- **[age](process_age.md)**: the sheer quantity of time accrued from the origin, the
  one figure that only rises. Anchored by McTaggart's A-series past.
- **[sequence](process_sequence.md)**: the ordered, unreorderable run of the thing's
  dated events, its provenance. Anchored by McTaggart's B-series (earlier / later).
- **[endurance](process_endurance.md)**: the thing's having lasted, the years survived
  where its cohort is gone, the ground the rest stands on.
- **[maturation](process_maturation.md)**: the same elapsed time read as fullness rather
  than loss, the vintage the years alone confer -- distinct from decay's patina. The
  engine's twist, the sibling of the family's ambivalence.
