# Process: Endurance

## Taxonomy

[Chronos](process_chronos.md), the facet of persistence.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: chronos

## Initiated by

The thing outlasts the span things like it are given, and its having-lasted becomes the fact.
Endurance begins where a thing is still here after its cohort is gone -- the surviving copy, the last of its make, the tool still whole when its fellows are scrap.
It is measured not by how old the thing is but by how long it has held against the passage: the years survived, not merely the years counted.

## Direction

Onward, holding, toward rarity.
Endurance runs the thing forward through the time that ends most of its kind -- the manuscript that outlived the fire, the coin that outlived the empire, the chair still sound after the household that made it is dust.
It moves toward the singular: to endure is to become the one still standing, prized less for what it is than for the sheer fact that it, alone, remained.

## Lever

The thing's persistence against all that would end it.
The carrying force is the plain achievement of having lasted -- every year survived is one that cannot be taken back, banked in the thing's favour; the resistance is everything that ends a thing before its span, and this is where decay and wear press hardest, spending the matter endurance is the tally of.
Chronos reads only whether the thing lasted, never the matter they spent to end it: endurance is the temporal fact of survival, not the material state that decay and wear own.

## Echo

The thing prized, or merely still here, for having lasted.
Endurance leaves the piece a survivor -- the relic, the last one, the thing that made it through -- and it is what keeps a piece datable at all: only a thing that persists can go on gathering years.
It is the ground the other facets stand on, for a thing that does not endure gathers no more age, extends no more sequence, and never reaches the fullness maturation would bring.
