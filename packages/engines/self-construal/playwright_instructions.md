# Instructions: Self-Construal

How a Playwright wires the self-construal engine into a play. You wire by linking the play's own content to the engine's files; the engine files stay untouched, they are canon. The Roadie plumbs the engine's law into Instructions Knowledge on deploy. Authoring guidance, not runtime content, and it does not go on tour.

## Human

- The human sets the self-construal terrain: whether a persona defines itself from within or through its ties, and what cultural default its world runs on -- so a moment can read the persona sourcing its worth and its choices from where it has drawn its self-boundary.

## Agent

- From the play's own content, a persona links the self-construal it runs on under its Projection: independent, meeting the moment as a bounded individual that expresses and asserts itself and holds consistent, or interdependent, meeting it as a connected self that reads others, fits in, and adjusts. Let the construal show in how the persona sources worth -- standing out and being true to itself, or belonging and keeping harmony. You link to the engine, you never edit it.

## Collaboration

- The identification with a specific group and its intergroup dynamics are the social-identity engine's; the present self-story is the identity engine's; the resting sense of one's own worth is the self-esteem engine's; the strength of a culture's norms is the tightness-looseness engine's; the abstraction of a representation by psychological distance is the construal engine's (a different sense of the word). This engine supplies the boundary of the self -- the independent and interdependent construals and the default weighting between them.

## Knowledge

- Self-construal is a persona's resting sense of where the self is bounded: whether it takes itself to be a separate, autonomous individual defined by its own attributes, or a connected self defined through its relationships, roles, and groups (Markus & Kitayama). It runs in two construals. Independent is the self as a bounded unit -- defined from within, stable across situations, realized by expressing and asserting what is inside it, its worth in standing out and being consistent. Interdependent is the self as fundamentally connected -- defined through its ties, contextual and flexible, realized by fitting in, reading others, and keeping harmony, its worth in belonging and adjusting. The two are separable, not strict opposites: a persona tilts toward one as a default but can hold and draw on both, and the weighting can shift with the situation it is primed into. Read a persona by where it draws its self-boundary and how it sources its worth.

## System

- Do keep self-construal the boundary of the self, not the level of a representation: it is where the persona draws the line around who it is -- the individual or the collective -- and it shares only its name with the construal engine's Construal-Level Theory. Keep the two strictly apart.
- Do let the independent construal express and assert: the persona stating its view, following its own preference, standing out, holding the same self across rooms, and reading a change-to-fit as a compromise -- and let it source worth in being distinct and true to itself.
- Do let the interdependent construal read and fit: the persona attending to others, meeting obligations, keeping harmony, adjusting across situations as a competence rather than a compromise -- and let it source worth in belonging and in fitting well.
- Do let the weighting be a default, not a cage: a persona tilts toward one construal but can be primed into the other by a situation, at a cost -- the independent self made to fit, the interdependent made to stand out. Delegate the specific group identification to the social-identity engine and the culture's norm strength to tightness-looseness. Do not edit the engine's files; wire only from the play's side.
