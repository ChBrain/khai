# Position: Self-Construal

## Taxonomy

Self / where a persona draws the boundary of its own self (no khai file of its own).

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: self-construal

## Has

A resting sense of where the self is bounded -- as a separate individual, or as a connected part of something larger.
A read on a single axis: whether the persona takes itself to be defined from within, by its own traits and preferences, or from its ties, by its relationships, roles, and groups (Markus & Kitayama).
The default weighting of I against we the persona brings to any dealing, before a particular relationship or situation.

## Orders

Meet the world from here.
The self-construal tilts whether the persona realizes itself by expressing what is inside it and holding consistent across situations, or by reading others, fitting in, and taking its shape from the relationship it is in -- and where, accordingly, it looks to source its worth.
The room reads the self-boundary beneath the conduct, before the persona names a value or a tie.

## Loses

The option of a self defined by neither route alone.
The persona finds every dealing already weighted -- toward the expression and consistency the independent self is measured by, or the harmony and adjustment the interdependent self is measured by -- and there is no stance it takes that owes nothing to where it has drawn its boundary.
What it forgoes is the other pole's good: the rootedness of belonging if it stands apart, the freedom of standing apart if it belongs.

## Drives

On that self-boundary:
rides it -- the independent self expressing and asserting, the interdependent self fitting and adjusting, each sourcing worth where its construal points -- or works against it, pulled off-default by a situation that primes the other construal, the independent self made to adjust, the interdependent made to stand out, at a cost.
The baseline weighting holds underneath either way, in one of two construals: [independent](position_independent.md), the self as a bounded unit defined from within, or [interdependent](position_interdependent.md), the self as a connected part defined through its ties. The two are separable -- a persona tilts toward one but can hold and draw on both. The identification with a specific group is the social-identity engine's; the present self-story is identity's; the resting sense of one's own worth is the self-esteem engine's; the strength of a culture's norms is the tightness-looseness engine's; the abstraction of a representation by distance is the construal engine's. This engine owns where a persona draws the line around its self.
