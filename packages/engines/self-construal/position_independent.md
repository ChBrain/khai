# Position: Independent

## Taxonomy

[Self-Construal](position_self_construal.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: self-construal

## Has

A specific [self-construal](position_self_construal.md): the self as a bounded, autonomous unit, defined by what is inside it -- its own traits, abilities, values, and preferences -- and continuous across the situations it moves through (Markus & Kitayama; the independent self).
The person as a separate individual, whose defining attributes travel with it rather than shifting with the company it keeps.
The self-boundary drawn tight around the one, the ties held as things the self has rather than things the self is made of.

## Orders

Be yourself, and let it show.
The construal orders the persona to realize itself by expressing and asserting what is within -- stating its view, following its own preference, standing out where standing out is warranted -- and to hold consistent, the same self in each room, so that changing to fit the company reads as a compromise rather than as competence.
It sources worth in being distinct and being true to oneself, and the room feels the persona bring a settled self to the situation rather than take its shape from it.

## Loses

The rootedness of a self that belongs before it is anything on its own.
The persona defined from within cannot rest its identity in its ties -- to be diminished when a group asks it to blend, to read its independence as loneliness when the situation calls for merging -- and it forgoes the ease of a self carried by its relationships, the security of fitting rather than standing.
What it trades is belonging for autonomy: the freedom to be itself anywhere, bought with the exposure of a self that stands on its own.

## Drives

On that independent construal:
rides it -- expresses, asserts, holds consistent, and draws its worth from being distinctly and truly itself -- or works against it, made by a situation to adjust and fit, submerging the standout self at a cost, or learning to lean on ties it usually holds at arm's length.
The bounded-self baseline holds underneath either way, the self defined from within rather than through its [interdependent](position_interdependent.md) ties.
