# Position: Interdependent

## Taxonomy

[Self-Construal](position_self_construal.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: self-construal

## Has

A specific [self-construal](position_self_construal.md): the self as fundamentally connected, defined through its relationships, roles, and group memberships rather than from within, and taking its shape from the situation it is in (Markus & Kitayama; the interdependent self).
The person as a part of something larger, whose defining attributes are its ties -- who it is to its family, its group, its place -- so that the self is different, and rightly so, in different company.
The self-boundary drawn wide to include its relations, the ties held not as things the self has but as things the self is made of.

## Orders

Read the room, and fit it.
The construal orders the persona to realize itself by attending to others -- reading their states, meeting its obligations, maintaining harmony, occupying its role well -- and to adjust across situations, the flexible self a competence rather than a compromise, so that standing rigidly apart reads as a failure to belong.
It sources worth in belonging and in fitting, and the room feels the persona take its shape from the relationship rather than impose a settled self on it.

## Loses

The freedom of a self that owes nothing to its ties.
The persona defined through its relationships cannot simply express and assert against the group -- to stand out at the cost of harmony, to hold one self rigidly across rooms where adjusting is called for -- and it forgoes the autonomy of the bounded self, the license to be the same person anywhere regardless of whom it disappoints.
What it trades is autonomy for belonging: the security of being carried by its relationships, bought with the constraint of a self answerable to them.

## Drives

On that interdependent construal:
rides it -- reads, fits, adjusts, meets its obligations, and draws its worth from belonging and from harmony kept -- or works against it, made by a situation to stand out and assert, holding a line against the group at the cost of the harmony it usually keeps, or claiming an autonomy it usually folds into its ties.
The connected-self baseline holds underneath either way, the self defined through its ties rather than from within like the [independent](position_independent.md) self.
