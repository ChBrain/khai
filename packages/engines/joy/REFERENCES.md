# Joy: Reference

## Line of Work

Joy as a **multi-type** engine: the positive-valence emotion through which a persona lifts at a good -- the felt uplift that broadens and builds -- taken across its forms and the trait that tunes it. The domain does not model the appraisal taxonomy of the basic emotions, which is the emotion engine's; nor the appraisal-specific positive emotions, which are their own engines (the wonder of awe, the achievement of pride, the benefactor of gratitude, the incongruity of amusement/humor, the moral beauty of elevation, the anticipation of hope); nor the wanting that precedes the liking, which is the desire engine's; nor the valuation of outcomes over time, which is the reward engine's; nor the prolonging of a good moment, which is the savoring engine's. It models the core felt uplift itself: the hedonic yes and its qualities of arousal and source.

The spine is the psychology of positive affect. Joy is the positive-valence emotion, the response to a good -- a gain, an attainment, a need met, a threat lifted -- and its signature function is to broaden and build (Fredrickson): where the negative emotions narrow attention and ready a defense, positive emotions widen thought and action and, over time, lay down lasting physical, social, and intellectual resource. Affective neuroscience splits the core of it in two (Berridge & Kringelbach): "liking," the hedonic reaction painted onto a sensation in the brain's hedonic hotspots, and "wanting," the incentive salience that pulls toward it -- dissociable systems, so joy the liking is not the desire the wanting. The felt uplift comes in qualities: the sensory liking of pleasure, the high-arousal peak of elation (the positive prediction error, the gain that overshoots the forecast), the low-arousal serenity of contentment (Fredrickson's serenity), and the subtractive sweetness of relief, the good that is a bad removed. Individual differences in positive affectivity -- the sanguine temperament, trait Positive Affect (Watson & Clark), positive emotionality (Tellegen) -- are stable, form the affective core of extraversion, and predict sociability, resilience, health, and longevity. The altitudes are those: the emotion, its four forms, and its trait.

## Restrictions

What the engine refuses to model, and to whom it delegates.

- **Emotion**: The engine models joy in its own depth -- the felt uplift, its forms, its trait -- not its place in the taxonomy of the basic emotions. The appraisal structure by which an event becomes joy, sadness, anger, fear, or disgust is owned by the emotion engine; this engine unfolds the one node the emotion taxonomy names joy. The two compose: emotion keeps joy as one basic affect, this engine unfolds it.
- **The appraisal-specific positive emotions**: The engine models the core felt uplift, not the positive emotions that add a specific appraisal. The wonder at vastness is the awe engine's, the joy of achievement the pride engine's, the warmth toward a benefactor the gratitude engine's, the response to incongruity the humor engine's, the response to moral beauty the elevation engine's, and the anticipatory joy of a hoped-for good the hope engine's. Joy owns the hedonic yes itself; these own its appraisal-specific flavours.
- **Desire and Reward**: The engine models the liking, not the wanting or the ledger. The pull toward an object of appetite is owned by the desire engine (Berridge's "wanting"), and the valuation of outcomes across time -- the discounting that weighs near against distant -- by the reward engine; joy is the liking, the hedonic reaction, which the affective neuroscience dissociates from both the wanting that precedes it and the valuation that records it.
- **Savoring**: The engine models joy, not its regulation. The attending-to and prolonging of a positive experience -- the deliberate turning-toward that makes a good moment fuller and last -- is owned by the savoring engine, the mirror of coping; joy is the emotion, savoring is what a persona does to it.
- **Mood**: The engine models the joy episode, not the persisting affective background. The durable positive tone a persona carries beneath the moment is owned by the mood engine; contentment sustained as a resting background shades into mood's province, but the felt uplift at a good is this engine's.
- **Addiction**: The engine models relief the emotion, not the negative-reinforcement loop. The engineered, recurring pursuit of relief from a self-made withdrawal or craving -- relief-seeking as the engine of dependence -- is owned by the addiction engine; this engine owns relief the ordinary emotion, the sweet of a danger past.

## Encoding

Source to constraint, per file.

- **[joy](process_joy.md)** (the root, process): The positive-valence uplift at a good -- the brightening, the opening outward, the broaden-and-build that widens thought and lays down resource. Anchored by Fredrickson (broaden-and-build) and Ekman (enjoyment as a family).
- **[pleasure](process_pleasure.md)** (process, form): The sensory form -- core hedonic "liking," the immediate bodily yes generated in the hedonic hotspots, distinct from the wanting that sought it. Anchored by Berridge & Kringelbach.
- **[elation](process_elation.md)** (process, form): The high-arousal form -- the thrill and exhilaration of a large or unexpected good, the positive prediction error, joy's flooding peak. Anchored by the circumplex model and the reward-prediction-error account.
- **[contentment](process_contentment.md)** (process, form): The low-arousal form -- the calm, settled sufficiency of all being well, the serene pole of positive affect. Anchored by Fredrickson (serenity) and the circumplex model.
- **[relief](process_relief.md)** (process, form): The aversive-removed form -- the exhale of a threat passed or a pain stopped, positive affect by subtraction. Anchored by the relief literature; bounded against addiction's negative-reinforcement loop.
- **[sanguine](position_sanguine.md)** (position, trait): Trait positive affectivity -- a high resting positive tone and ready access to joy, the affective core of extraversion, predicting sociability, resilience, and well-being. Anchored by Watson & Clark (trait Positive Affect) and Tellegen (positive emotionality).
