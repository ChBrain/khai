# Process: Elation

## Taxonomy

[Joy](process_joy.md), the high-arousal form.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: joy

## Initiated by

A good arrives large and often beyond expectation -- the win, the triumph, the sudden turn for the better -- and joy surges upward into a high-energy peak: the thrill, the exhilaration, the leap and the shout.
This is elation: joy at high arousal, the crest of the positive emotions -- joy's flooding, as rage is anger's and panic is fear's, but welcome.
It is the summit of positive affect, the high that lifts the whole body with it.

## Direction

Up and outward at full energy -- toward expression, movement, celebration, the overflow that must be shared and shown.
Elation cannot sit still; it discharges in the jump, the cheer, the embrace, the need to tell.
Its direction is expansive and outward-thrown, the good too big to keep in.

## Lever

The size of the good and the suddenness of its arrival, especially where it exceeds what was expected -- the positive prediction error, the gain that overshoots the forecast, spikes the arousal.
What elation spends is composure, flooded out by the surge the way its dark mirrors flood judgment -- though here the flood is wanted, the loss of restraint the point.

## Echo

The peak crests and, by design, subsides -- elation is unsustainable, the high that must come down, and its comedown can leave the ordinary flat by contrast.
It is joy at its most intense and least durable, the summit the affect system is built to touch and then leave.
Where the elation is specifically the fierce joy of meeting a hard challenge -- the _fiero_ of the hard-won -- it borders pride, which is the pride engine's; elation is the high-arousal joy itself, whatever its source.
