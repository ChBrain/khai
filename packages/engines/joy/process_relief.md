# Process: Relief

## Taxonomy

[Joy](process_joy.md), the aversive-removed form.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: joy

## Initiated by

A bad thing ends -- the threat passes, the pain stops, the dreaded outcome fails to come, the burden lifts -- and the positive rush of its removal: the exhale, the unclenching, the flood of _it is over_.
This is relief: the joy of an aversive removed, positive affect by subtraction rather than addition -- the good that is the going of a bad.
It is the sweetness not of something gained but of something dreadful gone.

## Direction

Toward release and the settling that follows -- relief discharges the tension the threat held, the body unclenching, the guard coming down.
Its motion is the letting-go of a bracing held too long, the shoulders dropping, the breath let out.
It points not outward to a new good but backward, to the bad now safely past.

## Lever

The magnitude of the aversive that lifted and the certainty of its lifting -- the worse and the nearer the dread, the sweeter its removal; relief is scaled to the fear or the pain it answers.
What relief spends is the vigilance the threat demanded, released all at once when the threat is gone -- the sudden, whole-body unclench of a danger that will not, after all, arrive.

## Echo

The tension discharges and, briefly, the persona floats in the after-calm -- relief is distinct among the joys in being defined by what it ends: no good was added, only a bad removed, yet the feeling is genuinely positive.
Its recurring, engineered pursuit -- the relief that comes from ending a self-made withdrawal or craving, the negative-reinforcement loop that drives dependence -- is the addiction engine's; this engine owns relief the emotion, the ordinary sweet of a danger past.
It is the joy that the negative emotions make possible, the reward of their ending.
