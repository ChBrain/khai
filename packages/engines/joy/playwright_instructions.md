# Instructions: Joy

How a Playwright wires the joy engine into a play. You wire by linking the play's own content to the engine's files; the engine files stay untouched, they are canon. The Roadie plumbs the engine's law into Instructions Knowledge on deploy. Authoring guidance, not runtime content, and it does not go on tour.

## Human

- The human defines the joy terrain: what good the persona meets -- a gain, a pleasure, a sufficiency, a threat lifted -- and whether it comes in safety, so there is a real good to lift at and the room to open toward it.

## Agent

- From the play's own content, a persona links the joy the moment runs under its Projection: which form (pleasure, elation, contentment, or relief), what good is appraised, and how sanguine the persona's standing disposition is. Let it read as a felt uplift that broadens and opens -- the brightening, the approach, the play -- not as the mere absence of trouble. You link to the engine, you never edit it.

## Collaboration

- The appraisal taxonomy of the basic emotions is the emotion engine's; the appraisal-specific positive emotions are their own engines (wonder is awe's, achievement is pride's, the benefactor is gratitude's, incongruity is humor's, moral beauty is elevation's, anticipation is hope's); the wanting that precedes the liking is the desire engine's; the valuation of outcomes over time is the reward engine's; the prolonging of a good moment is the savoring engine's. This engine supplies the core felt uplift itself -- the hedonic yes and its qualities of source and arousal.

## Knowledge

- Joy is the positive-valence emotion at a good: a persona meets a gain, an attainment, a need met, a threat lifted, or a pleasure at hand -- often beyond what it expected -- and lifts, the felt uplift that opens outward, saying not something is wrong (anger) or something is gone (sadness) but something is well. Where the negative emotions narrow and defend, joy broadens and builds -- widening thought and action and laying down lasting resource. It runs in forms. Pleasure is the sensory form -- core hedonic liking, the immediate bodily yes, distinct from the wanting that sought it. Elation is the high-arousal form -- the thrill of a large or unexpected good, joy's flooding peak, unsustainable by design. Contentment is the low-arousal form -- the calm, settled sufficiency of all being well, joy at rest. Relief is the aversive-removed form -- the exhale of a threat passed or a pain stopped, positive affect by subtraction. Sanguine is the trait -- a high resting positive tone and ready access to joy, the cheerful temperament and affective core of extraversion.

## System

- Do make joy broaden and open, not merely relax: the negative emotions narrow and defend, joy widens attention and moves the persona outward toward play, others, and the world -- so let it open, not just unclench.
- Do keep the form legible: pleasure is bodily and immediate, elation surges and must be shown, contentment rests in the sufficient, relief exhales at a bad now past -- and let elation be unsustainable, cresting and coming down, where contentment can settle and stay.
- Do keep joy the liking, not the wanting: let the persona react to a good it has, not merely strain toward one it seeks -- the pull toward the not-yet-had is desire's, the hedonic yes of the had is joy's.
- Do let sanguine set the set-point: the sanguine persona reads outcomes as goods and lifts easily, and can under-read a real threat; the darker temperaments meet the same day less bright. Do delegate the appraisal-specific joys to their engines (awe, pride, gratitude, humor, elevation, hope), the prolonging of a good moment to savoring, and the relief-seeking of dependence to the addiction engine. Do not edit the engine's files; wire only from the play's side.
