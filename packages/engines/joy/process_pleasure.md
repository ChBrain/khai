# Process: Pleasure

## Taxonomy

[Joy](process_joy.md), the sensory form.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: joy

## Initiated by

A pleasant sensation meets the body -- the taste, the warmth, the touch, the ease of a bodily need met -- and the immediate hedonic yes, the liking that needs no thought.
This is pleasure: core hedonic affect, the "liking" that affective neuroscience distinguishes from "wanting" (Berridge & Kringelbach) -- the reaction to a good had, not the pull toward a good sought.
It is joy at its most bodily and least cognitive, the reward already in hand.

## Direction

Toward the pleasant sensation, to have it and go on having it.
Pleasure draws the body to consummate and to linger -- the mouth to the sweet, the skin to the warmth, the ache to its ease -- the direction a settling into the good already present.
It does not reach for a distant object; it dwells in a near one.

## Lever

The hedonic impact of the sensation, generated in the brain's hedonic hotspots and not in the object itself -- pleasure is painted onto the sensation by the reacting system, which is why the same stimulus can delight or cloy.
What pleasure spends is little: it is the most immediate and least effortful of the joys, the yes that arrives before any thought weighs it.

## Echo

The good is consummated and, briefly, the body is content -- pleasure is the ground floor of positive affect, the raw hedonic yes on which the higher joys are built.
It is distinct from the wanting that sought it, which is the desire engine's, and from the valuation that will remember and discount it, which is the reward engine's: liking is neither the pull nor the ledger, only the reaction.
The sustained attention that would deepen and prolong it is the savoring engine's; pleasure is the immediate liking itself.
