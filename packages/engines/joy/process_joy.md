# Process: Joy

## Taxonomy

Emotion / the psychology of the positive-valence response to a good.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: joy

## Initiated by

A persona meets a good -- a gain, an attainment, a need met, a threat lifted, a pleasure at hand -- often beyond what it expected, and lifts: the felt uplift, the brightening, the opening outward.
This is joy: the positive emotion, the response to a good where sadness is the response to a loss.
Where the negative emotions narrow and defend -- anger to confront, fear to flee, sadness to withdraw -- joy broadens and builds (Fredrickson).

## Direction

Outward and up -- toward engagement, approach, play, and connection.
Joy does not defend or withdraw; it opens: it widens attention, loosens the grip of the moment's problem, and moves the persona toward others and toward the world.
This is broaden-and-build -- the momentary widening of thought and action that, over time, lays down lasting resource.

## Lever

The appraisal of a good, and of the safety to enjoy it -- something is well, and nothing presses.
What joy spends is the guardedness of the negative emotions: it lowers the defensive crouch, trading vigilance for openness on the read that here, now, all is well enough to expand.
Its texture is the uplift, the lightness, the smile, the loosening.

## Echo

The good is savoured and the self is built -- joy widens the moment and, over many moments, broadens the person: the play that builds skill, the warmth that builds bonds, the openness that builds discovery (Fredrickson).
Fleeting by design, it is meant to pass and recur, not to persist.
It runs as [pleasure](process_pleasure.md), [elation](process_elation.md), [contentment](process_contentment.md), or [relief](process_relief.md), and is tuned by a standing [sanguine](position_sanguine.md) temperament. The many appraisal-specific joys are their own engines -- the wonder of awe, the achievement of pride, the benefactor of gratitude, the incongruity of amusement, the moral beauty of elevation; the anticipatory joy of a hoped-for good is the hope engine's; the wanting that precedes the liking is the desire engine's; the valuation of outcomes over time is the reward engine's; the prolonging of a good moment is the savoring engine's; the appraisal taxonomy of the basic emotions is the emotion engine's. This engine owns the felt uplift itself.
