# Process: Contentment

## Taxonomy

[Joy](process_joy.md), the low-arousal form.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: joy

## Initiated by

Things are well and nothing presses -- needs met, safety assured, the world in order -- and a low, calm, settled positive tone suffuses the persona: the ease, the sufficiency, the quiet gladness of enough.
This is contentment: joy at low arousal, the serene pole of positive affect (Fredrickson's serenity) -- the well-being that does not leap but rests.
It is the joy of the goal met and the wanting quieted, the good simply had.

## Direction

Inward and settled -- toward savouring the present as it is, integrating the good rather than pursuing more.
Contentment does not reach; it rests in the sufficient, its direction a kind of stillness -- the letting-be of a moment that asks for nothing further.
It turns the persona toward the present rather than the next thing.

## Lever

The appraisal that the present is good and complete enough -- no gap between what is and what is wanted, nothing lacking to chase.
What contentment spends is striving itself: it is the joy that arrives only where desire has, for now, come to rest, and it eases the reaching that the other emotions keep alive.

## Echo

The good is rested in and the person, quietly, broadened -- contentment is the joy most open to reflection, integration, and the sense that a life is well.
Its risk is not excess but complacency, the settledness that stops seeking what still might be sought.
Sustained as a resting tone, contentment shades from the emotion into the mood engine's calm background; here it is the low, settled joy of a present that is enough.
