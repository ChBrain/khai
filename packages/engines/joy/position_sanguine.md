# Position: Sanguine

## Taxonomy

Affect / the trait of positive affectivity -- the standing readiness beneath [Joy](process_joy.md) (the sanguine temperament; trait Positive Affect, Watson & Clark; positive emotionality, Tellegen).

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: joy

## Has

A high resting positive tone and a ready access to joy.
Trait positive affect, as against the state: a disposition to feel good readily, to lift easily, and to return to a bright baseline soon -- the persona gladdened by more, sooner, and longer, carrying a set-point nearer the high.
It is the sanguine humour the old physicians named, the cheerful pole of dispositional affect and the affective core of extraversion.

## Orders

Run from here, ahead of the good.
The sanguine persona reads outcomes as goods, recalls the glad more readily, expects the pleasant, and finds the bright in the mixed; and the tilt reaches into a life -- trait positive affect predicting sociability, resilience, and, through its broaden-and-build harvest, health and longevity.
It colours the read before the event, the way a mood does, but on the axis of the good.

## Loses

The edge of vigilance the darker temperaments keep.
The sanguine persona, meeting a world already lit, can under-read a real threat or loss, discount the warning, and miss the danger the anxious would catch -- the bright baseline has its blind spot.
What is lost is the productive suspicion: the readiness to take a bad sign seriously before it proves itself.

## Drives

On the loss of that edge:
rides the sanguine tone -- trusts the brightness, approaches, expects the good -- or works against it, checking the optimism and attending the warning it would skip, at a cost in ease.
The set-point holds underneath either way, a resting level the persona manages rather than moves.
