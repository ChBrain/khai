# Process: Unbidden

## Taxonomy

[Mind-Wandering](process_mind_wandering.md), the drift the persona did not mean to have.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: mind-wandering

## Initiated by

The persona means to stay on the task, and the mind goes anyway.
There is no decision to disengage: the persona is trying to read, to listen, to work, and a train of thought arrives unasked and pulls attention off. This is spontaneous mind-wandering, the drift that starts without intent (Seli), the mind slipping its leash while the persona still means to hold it.
What enters is a wandering the persona would not have chosen, beginning against its intention to stay.

## Direction

Off the task, against the persona's will to remain on it.
The unbidden drift carries attention away while the persona is still, in intention, committed to the task -- so it runs as a kind of failure of control, the mind doing what the persona did not sanction. It is the mode most tied to the costs: the error slipped in, the instruction missed, the sense afterward of a mind that got away from its owner.
The direction is away, and the persona's stated aim was to stay.

## Lever

The strength of the intention to stay, against the pull of the self-generated thought.
Whether the unbidden drift takes hold turns on whether the persona's control can hold the task over a thought that arrives on its own -- a pressing preoccupation, a dull task, a tired or depleted mind all tilt it toward the drift winning. The persona did not open the door; the thought came through it.
Strengthen the hold on the task and the unbidden thought is resisted; let control lapse and the mind is gone before the persona notices the choice was never made.

## Echo

On that unbidden drift:
rides it -- lets the unasked thought take over and carries off the task, control quietly lost -- or catches it, noticing the mind has gone and hauling it back, or letting the noticed drift become an owned one.
The mode holds underneath: a drift that began without the persona's intent, set apart from the knowingly-chosen [tuning out](process_tuning_out.md) and defined against it by intention, and from [zoning out](process_zoning_out.md) by whether the drift is ever noticed at all. That the drift is self-generated and decouples from the world is the [root's](process_mind_wandering.md); the sticky negative circling it is not is the rumination engine's.
