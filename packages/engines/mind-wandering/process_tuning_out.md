# Process: Tuning Out

## Taxonomy

[Mind-Wandering](process_mind_wandering.md), the drift the persona knows it is having, and often chooses.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: mind-wandering

## Initiated by

The persona knows the task will not hold it, and lets the mind go -- knowingly.
Meta-awareness is intact: the persona is aware it has disengaged, and frequently it disengaged on purpose, deciding the task does not need it and turning the mind loose (Schooler's tuning out; deliberate mind-wandering, Seli). The dull meeting, the familiar drive, the undemanding wait are handed to the mind to fill.
What enters is a drift the persona owns -- noticed, and often intended.

## Direction

Off the task, and toward what the mind is let loose to do.
Because the persona knows it has disengaged, the wandering can be steered and used: turned to planning, to rehearsing a coming conversation, to working a problem loose in the background, to simply resting in a pleasant reverie. This is the mode that carries mind-wandering's benefits -- the future prepared, the idea incubated, the dull stretch made bearable -- because a drift the persona is aware of is a drift it can direct and end.
The direction is away from the task but under the persona's eye, disengagement it can account for.

## Lever

The judgment that the task can spare the mind, meeting the choice to let it go.
Whether the persona tunes out turns on reading the task as one that does not need full attention and electing to spend the freed capacity inward -- and on meta-awareness staying online, so the disengagement remains known rather than sliding into the unmonitored. Misjudge the task, and a chosen tuning-out becomes a costly lapse.
Keep the awareness and the task's low demand, and the persona roams by choice; lose the awareness, and the tuning out sinks into zoning out.

## Echo

On that owned drift:
rides it -- disengages knowingly, lets the mind plan and rove and rest, and steers it back when the task needs it -- or loses the thread of awareness and slips into an unmonitored drift, or misjudges the task and pays for a disengagement it should not have taken.
The mode holds underneath: a drift defined by awareness and often intent, set apart from the unnoticed [zoning out](process_zoning_out.md) by whether the persona knows, and from [unbidden](process_unbidden.md) by whether the drift was chosen rather than merely befell. That the drift is self-generated and decouples from the world is the [root's](process_mind_wandering.md); the total absorption in a task rather than the release from it is the flow engine's.
