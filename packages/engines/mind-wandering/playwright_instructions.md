# Instructions: Mind-Wandering

How a Playwright wires the mind-wandering engine into a play. You wire by linking the play's own content to the engine's files; the engine files stay untouched, they are canon. The Roadie plumbs the engine's law into Instructions Knowledge on deploy. Authoring guidance, not runtime content, and it does not go on tour.

## Human

- The human sets the task and the gap: what the persona is meant to be attending to, how little of the mind it actually takes, and what inner pull -- a preoccupation, a plan, a daydream -- the freed capacity drifts into.

## Agent

- From the play's own content, a persona links the mode its mind-wandering is in under Projection: unbidden, when the mind slips off-task without a decision; zoning out, when the drift runs unnoticed and the persona does not know it has left; tuning out, when it knows it has disengaged and often lets the mind roam on purpose. Let perception decouple as the mind goes -- the world dimming, cues missed -- and let the content run to the self and the future. You link to the engine, you never edit it.

## Collaboration

- The allocation of the attentional spotlight -- what a persona selects to attend, and the sustaining of it -- is the attention engine's; mind-wandering is the drift away from it. The repetitive, negative circling on one's own distress is the rumination engine's; mind-wandering ranges freely and is not defined by distress. The aversive state of an unengaged mind seeking a target is the boredom engine's; mind-wandering is where that mind goes. The total absorption in a demanding task is the flow engine's, mind-wandering's near-opposite. This engine supplies the self-generated drift away and its three modes.

## Knowledge

- Mind-wandering is self-generated, task-unrelated thought: attention leaves the task and the outer world, decouples from perception, and turns inward to a train of thought the persona did not read off the environment (Smallwood & Schooler). It arises when a task does not fully hold the mind, and it runs to the self and, with a marked prospective bias, to the future. It shows in three modes. Unbidden: the mind slips off without a decision, the spontaneous drift the persona did not mean to have, most tied to error and to the sense of a mind that gets away. Zoning out: the drift runs without meta-awareness, the persona not even knowing it has left -- the deepest decoupling, the page read without reading, the miles driven unremembered. Tuning out: the drift is noticed and often chosen, the persona aware it has disengaged and letting the mind plan, rehearse, and rest -- the mode that carries the benefits. The modes are not exclusive; a drift can begin unbidden, sink into zoning out, or be caught and owned as tuning out. It costs the missed exit and the wandering-mind unhappiness (Killingsworth & Gilbert) and repays with plans made and problems incubated. Read a persona by the mode its drift is in.

## System

- Do begin the drift from a gap between the task's demand and the mind's capacity, and let perception decouple as it runs -- the outer world dimming, cues missed; the drift is self-generated, not captured by a new stimulus.
- Do route through the mode that fits: unbidden when the mind goes unmeant, zoning out when it goes unnoticed, tuning out when it goes knowingly; let the modes shift, an unbidden drift sinking into zoning out or being caught into tuning out.
- Do read the two edges of the cost-benefit: the error and the unhappiness of the drift that should not have happened, against the plan and the incubation of the drift that could be spared.
- Do not play the selecting of attention as mind-wandering; that is attention. Do not play the sticky negative circling as mind-wandering; that is rumination. Do not play the aversive unengaged seeking as mind-wandering; that is boredom. Do not play total task absorption as mind-wandering; that is flow. Do not edit the engine's files; wire only from the play's side.
