# Process: Mind-Wandering

## Taxonomy

Self-generated thought that drifts off the task.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: mind-wandering

## Initiated by

A persona's attention leaves the task in front of it and turns inward, without the world asking it to.
The trigger is not a new thing in the environment pulling the spotlight -- that is capture -- but the opposite: a train of thought the persona generates itself, unrelated to the task and unprompted by anything in the room. What enters the process is stimulus-independent, task-unrelated thought (Smallwood & Schooler): the mind supplying its own content and following it away from what the persona was doing.
This is the drift the persona did not read off the world; it begins the moment inner content takes over from the task at hand.

## Direction

Inward and away, with perception decoupling behind it.
Mind-wandering does not sharpen the persona on its task; it carries attention off it, and as it goes the outer world dims -- the signature perceptual decoupling, where the eyes still rest on the page but stop taking it in, the road still runs but stops registering. The train of thought runs to the self and, with a marked prospective bias, to the future: plans, rehearsals, imagined conversations, the autobiographical drift.
The direction is off the task and into the inner world, the environment fading as the mind travels.

## Lever

A task that does not fully hold the mind, meeting a mind that supplies its own content.
Whether the drift begins turns on the gap between what the task demands and what the persona has to give: an undemanding, practised, or dull task leaves capacity the mind fills with its own thought, while a task that fully engages leaves no room and the wandering does not start. Executive resources both enable the drift (they generate and sustain the inner train) and, turned on the task, suppress it.
The lever is available capacity meeting self-generated thought; close the gap by engaging the task fully, and the mind is held; leave it open, and the mind wanders into it.

## Echo

The drift shows in one of three modes, by how it is owned and whether it is noticed: [unbidden](process_unbidden.md), where the mind slips off without a decision; [zoning out](process_zoning_out.md), where the drift runs unnoticed, the persona unaware it has even left; or [tuning out](process_tuning_out.md), where the persona knows it has disengaged and often lets the mind roam on purpose.
The modes are not stages and not exclusive; a drift can begin unbidden, sink out of awareness into zoning out, or be caught and owned as tuning out.
What ends the process is the mind returning to the task -- snapped back by a cue, or steered back by the persona -- and what it leaves is double-edged: the cost of the missed exit, the unread page, the error, and the unhappiness of a wandering mind (Killingsworth & Gilbert), against the benefit of the plan made, the problem incubated, the dull stretch survived. The allocation of the spotlight it drifts from is the attention engine's; the sticky negative circling is the rumination engine's; the aversive unengaged seeking is the boredom engine's; the total absorption in the task is the flow engine's. This engine owns the self-generated drift away.
