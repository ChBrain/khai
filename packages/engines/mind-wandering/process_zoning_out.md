# Process: Zoning Out

## Taxonomy

[Mind-Wandering](process_mind_wandering.md), the drift the persona does not even notice.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: mind-wandering

## Initiated by

The persona's mind leaves the task, and the persona does not know it has.
Beyond the drift itself, meta-awareness fails: the persona is not merely off-task but unaware of being off-task, with no inner monitor flagging that the mind has gone (Schooler's zoning out, the lapse of meta-awareness). The persona would, if asked in the moment, say it was still on the task -- it has no access to the fact of its own wandering.
What enters is a drift running under the persona's own radar, undetected from inside.

## Direction

Away, and unmonitored -- the deepest decoupling.
Because nothing is watching the wandering, it runs unchecked and perception decouples most completely: the page is read line by line and nothing is taken in, the miles are driven and not remembered, the speaker is faced and not heard. The persona is not choosing to ignore the task; it has simply, unknowingly, left it, and the absence of a monitor lets the drift run long before anything interrupts it.
The direction is away and unwatched, until an external jolt restores the awareness that was missing.

## Lever

The failure of the monitor that would catch the drift.
Whether the persona zones out turns not on the drift alone but on whether meta-awareness is online to detect it -- fatigue, alcohol, deep absorption in the inner train, and long undemanding tasks all suppress the monitor, letting a drift that might have been caught run undetected. The wandering and the knowing-you-wander are separable, and here the second has failed.
Restore the monitor -- a prompt, a probe, a jolt -- and the persona catches itself with a start; leave it off and the drift runs unknown.

## Echo

On that unnoticed drift:
rides it -- the mind gone and the going undetected, the task running empty until something breaks in -- or is snapped back, a cue or a probe restoring the meta-awareness that failed, often with the small shock of realizing how long and how far the mind had gone.
The mode holds underneath: a drift defined by the lapse of awareness, set apart from the noticed [tuning out](process_tuning_out.md) by whether the persona knows, and from [unbidden](process_unbidden.md) by whether the drift is monitored rather than merely unintended. That the drift is self-generated and decouples from the world is the [root's](process_mind_wandering.md); the aversive unengaged seeking of a target is the boredom engine's.
