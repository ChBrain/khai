# Process: The Partial Upheld

## Taxonomy

[The Outcome](process_outcome_complaint.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: complaint

## Initiated by

Some of it is upheld and some of it is not.
Which is, on the evidence, the correct finding.

## Direction

Toward an answer that satisfies nobody and ends everything.
The two incidents that fit the form are established; the pattern, which was the complaint, is recorded as not evidenced. The respondent reads a document saying most of it was rejected. The complainer reads one saying most of it was rejected. Both readings are correct.
And the matter is now concluded, which means the parts that were not evidenced cannot be raised again.

## Lever

The honesty of a mixed finding against what the mixture does.
The carrying force is that a partial finding is usually the true one and a panel that upheld everything would be doing something worse: the evidence really is stronger on some points than others, saying so is what integrity looks like, and a complainer who was believed on everything regardless would have been given a verdict rather than a hearing; the resistance is that a partial upholding is treated by everybody afterwards as a substantially failed complaint, and the finality attaches to the whole of it, including the parts the procedure was never able to look at.
The Partial Upheld is the honest finding and functions as a rejection.

## Echo

An accurate letter that both parties read as a loss.
It shows the procedure at its most scrupulous and least useful.
The Partial Upheld is dated to the letter and closes the matter permanently.
