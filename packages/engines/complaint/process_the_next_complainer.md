# Process: The Next Complainer

## Taxonomy

[The Outcome](process_outcome_complaint.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: complaint

## Initiated by

Somebody else has the same problem, and has been watching.
They saw what the last year looked like from the outside.

## Direction

Toward a decision not to file, taken on excellent evidence.
What the watcher observed was not a cover-up. They observed a proper process, correctly run, that took fourteen months, cost a colleague their standing and eventually their job, and ended in a letter that mostly said no.
They are not intimidated and nobody has threatened them. They have simply performed the calculation, and it comes out the same way it did for everybody else who performed it.

## Lever

The rationality of the decision against what it leaves standing.
The carrying force is that not complaining is often the right choice and is not cowardice: the persona has a career, a mortgage, and finite years, the expected value of filing really is poor, and telling somebody they are obliged to spend themselves on a procedure that will not fix the thing would be an outrageous demand to make of them; the resistance is that the deterrent was manufactured by a process nobody designed to deter -- every safeguard was protective, every delay was proper, and the sum of them is a demonstration that the channel is not worth using.
The Next Complainer declines on the evidence, and the evidence was produced by fairness.

## Echo

An open channel with nobody in it.
It shows the loop closing, and why an institution with excellent procedures can receive very few complaints and conclude that there is very little wrong.
The Next Complainer is dated from the last case and is never recorded anywhere.
