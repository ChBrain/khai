# Process: The Slow Care

## Taxonomy

[The Procedure](process_procedure_complaint.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: complaint

## Initiated by

It takes months.
Because doing it properly takes months, and there is a queue.

## Direction

Toward a timescale set by the process and lived by the person.
The investigator has other cases, the respondent is entitled to time to answer, witnesses are on leave, the panel meets quarterly, the policy is under review. Each delay is defensible and most are unavoidable, and rushing any of them would be the actual injustice.
Meanwhile the complainer works alongside the respondent, or does not work, and has this as the organising fact of their year.

## Lever

The rightness of taking time against who is holding the case while it is taken.
The carrying force is that speed is the enemy of fairness here and everybody involved knows it: a rushed investigation reaches unsafe findings, the respondent's right to answer properly is not negotiable, and an institution that resolved these in a fortnight would be resolving them badly; the resistance is that the two parties experience the same calendar differently -- for the institution the case is one file among many, and for the complainer it is the thing their life is currently about -- so a duration that is reasonable at the institution's scale is not survivable at the person's.
The Slow Care is the right speed for the process and the wrong speed for a life.

## Echo

A year of somebody's life inside a reasonable timetable.
It shows the cost being paid without any decision to impose one.
The Slow Care is dated from the opening and is extended twice.
