# Process: The Filing

## Taxonomy

[Complaint](process_complaint.md), how a grievance becomes a case.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: complaint

## Initiated by

The persona decides to make it official.
The Filing runs through three narrowings: [an informal word](process_the_informal_word.md) suggested first, [a form](process_the_form.md) with categories, and [a named respondent](process_the_named_respondent.md) who must be somebody.

## Direction

Toward a claim shaped by what the channel can receive.
Most injuries never become grievances, most grievances are never voiced, and most voiced grievances are never filed -- the attrition at each step is enormous and is mostly invisible, because everybody who dropped out did so for their own good reasons.
What survives to become a case has been translated three times, and each translation removed the part that was hardest to state.

## Lever

The need for a procedure to have a shape against what the shaping leaves out.
The carrying force is that a channel has to have categories to work at all: an institution cannot investigate an atmosphere, the respondent is entitled to know precisely what is alleged, and a complaint that could not be stated in specifics would be impossible to answer and unfair to answer against; the resistance is that the serious grievances are usually about a pattern, a manner, an accumulation -- and the form has no field for accumulation, so the persona submits the two incidents that fit and leaves out the thing that was actually happening.
The Filing converts what happened into what can be alleged.

## Echo

A case that is a subset of a grievance.
It is the movement in which the complaint quietly becomes winnable and beside the point at the same time.
The Filing is dated to the submission and has been running for months before it.
