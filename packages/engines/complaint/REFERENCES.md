# Complaint: Reference

## Line of Work

Complaint as **process**: a grievance about something that has already happened, taken through the
procedure of the institution it is about.

The engine's subject is a channel that works and a use of it that does not. Ahmed's study is the spine,
and it identifies the thing precisely: what complainers describe is not obstruction but procedure, and
the damage is done by steps that are individually correct. The doors are open, the policies exist, the
staff are decent, and the people who go through it come out the other side having lost their position,
their colleagues, or their career, with the conduct they complained about still in place. Once that is
taken seriously the engine stops looking for a villain and starts reading the machine.

The boundary with what khai already carries is clean. The harm signalled before it arrives, and the
scrutiny that lands on whoever signalled it, are **warning**'s, **credibility**'s, and the **raising**
composite's; this engine reads a wrong that has already happened and a channel built to receive it. The
choice between leaving and speaking up is **loyalty**'s. What a fair hearing buys and what institutional
acknowledgement does are the **heeding** composite's and **recognition**'s. The wrong itself and what
repairs it are **repair**'s and **forgiveness**'s, and the mark a persona carries afterwards is
**stigma**'s.

**The filing** converts what happened into what can be alleged. Most injuries never become grievances
and most grievances are never filed, and the attrition happens for good reasons at every step. **The
informal word** is the largest single point of loss: trying informally is genuinely better where it
works, the advice is kindly meant, and it is given to everybody regardless of the matter. **The form**
asks for incidents from somebody complaining about a climate. **The named respondent** turns a claim
about a place into a quarrel between two people, because institutions cannot be respondents to their own
grievance procedures.

**The procedure** is fair in each of its parts and unequal in its distribution of practice. **The
confidence seal** protects everybody and connects nobody, so four identical complaints sit in four
sealed boxes. **The slow care** is the right speed for the process and the wrong speed for a life.
**The single case** is the fairest available treatment of the least useful unit, since the pattern is
the evidence and the pattern is exactly what a fair hearing must exclude. Underneath all three sits
Galanter's asymmetry: the institution is a repeat player and the complainer is running this once.

**The cost** is carried entirely on one side and recorded on neither. **The second job** makes the
complaint a resource question, available to whoever has the evenings and the stamina. **The changed
room** is made of consideration and feels like exile. **The known complainer** is described accurately
and read as a warning.

Hence the **twist**, at the root. **A complaint changes the complainer more reliably than it changes
anything they complained about.** No step is corrupt and no safeguard is a pretence: confidentiality
protects accuser and accused, thoroughness takes time because rushing would be unjust, and each case is
heard on its own merits because that is what fairness means. Every one of those protects somebody, and
their sum leaves the complainer isolated, slow, unable to see the pattern, and eventually known for one
thing.

The engine is built so that nobody has to be covering anything up, and a play should keep it that way.
The investigator was conscientious. The safeguards were real. The finding was defensible.

**On the evidence, stated plainly.** The most important thing to say about this engine's evidence is
what is wrong with the shape of it, and it is the same defect the engine describes. **Ahmed's material
is selected on the outcome.** _Complaint!_ is built from the testimony of people who complained and
were willing, years later, to talk about it -- which is to say, overwhelmingly, people whose complaints
went badly, in universities, in Britain and Australia, mostly about harassment and discrimination. It is
a superb account of that population and it is not a sample of complaints. An engine built on it will
describe the bad case and should not be read as describing the ordinary one. **The engine says nothing
about complaints that work, and most complaints work.** The great majority are small, factual, and
fixable, they are resolved quickly by people doing their jobs, and the channel does what it was built
to do; this engine reads the serious contested case and is silent on the rest, which is a deliberate
scope and not a finding about frequency. **The dispute-pyramid attrition is a durable framework rather
than a fixed quantity.** That injuries become grievances become claims, and that most drop out at each
step, is one of the oldest and best-travelled findings in law and society; the actual rates vary
enormously by domain, and no number here should be treated as measured. **Galanter's repeat-player
advantage is among the firmest legs available**, sustained across four decades of work, and it is doing
more of the load-bearing here than any single account of any single complaint. It is used in this engine
and owned by the law engine. **The endogeneity literature supports a narrower claim than the engine
makes.** That organisations build grievance structures which courts then treat as evidence of
compliance is measured, mostly in United States employment law; that those structures exhaust the
individuals who use them is a different proposition, and the second does not follow from the first.
**The next-complainer loop is an inference.** That watching a colleague's case deters the following one
is consistent with everything above and with what complainers report, and nobody has measured a
deterrence rate, because the people it works on leave no record by definition. What should not be taken
from this engine is that grievance procedures are a sham, that the people who run them are cynics, or
that a persona who declines to complain has been beaten. The claim is narrow: the channel narrows the
grievance, the safeguards isolate the user, the cost falls on one side, and the ending redistributes
almost nothing.

## Origin

The study of what happens to people who use official channels, wired as one process on a persona. The
twist is Ahmed's; the asymmetry underneath the procedure movement is Galanter's.

## Restrictions

What the engine refuses to model, and to whom it delegates.

- **The harm not yet arrived (warning, credibility, raising)**: A harm signalled before it happens, the
  account whose only evidence is the one making it, and the scrutiny that follows the person who raised
  it are those packages'. This engine reads a wrong that has already occurred and a channel built to
  receive it.
- **Going instead of speaking (loyalty, membership, role-exit)**: The choice between exit and voice,
  what belonging costs, and what leaving a role does to a persona are those engines'. The settlement
  member reads only the arrangement, not the departure.
- **Being properly heard (heeding, recognition, credibility)**: What a fair hearing buys, what
  institutional acknowledgement does, and the credit given to an account are those packages'. This
  engine reads the procedure rather than the hearing inside it.
- **The wrong and its repair (repair, forgiveness, moral-account, apology)**: What was done, what
  restores it, and how an account is offered and received are those packages'. The engine is
  indifferent to the merits and reads the channel.
- **The mark afterwards (stigma, gossip, reputation)**: How a discrediting attribute is carried, and how
  talk about a person circulates, are those engines'. The known-complainer member names the label's
  arrival and hands the mechanism back.
- **The law itself (law, document, register)**: Statutes, tribunals, limitation periods, the burden of
  proof, and the records institutions keep are those engines' and the trade's. They set the format;
  this engine reads what the format does to somebody using it.
- **The institution's own conduct (org, hierarchy, power, drift)**: How organisations are structured,
  how authority is held, and how a tolerated exception becomes the norm are those engines'. This engine
  reads one persona's passage through a procedure.
- **A grievance as authored symbol (the play's pitch)**: A complaint staged in order to **stand for** a
  theme -- the case that mirrors a motif of justice -- is refused: that is authored key, the play's
  pitch. Complaint sets what the process required and what it cost; the meaning is the play's.

## Encoding

Source to constraint. A process tree: one root, four movements, twelve forms.

- **[complaint](process_complaint.md)** (the root): a grievance taken through the procedure of the
  institution it is about, read on what the process cost, carrying the twist -- a complaint changes the
  complainer more reliably than it changes anything they complained about. Anchored by Ahmed (2021).
- **[the filing](process_filing_complaint.md)**: how a grievance becomes a case. Anchored by Felstiner,
  Abel & Sarat (1980) and Kolb & Bartunek (1992). [the informal word](process_the_informal_word.md),
  [the form](process_the_form.md), [the named respondent](process_the_named_respondent.md).
- **[the procedure](process_procedure_complaint.md)**: what the institution does with it. Anchored by
  Ahmed (2021) and Galanter (1974), whose repeat-player thesis belongs to the law engine.
  [the confidence seal](process_the_confidence_seal.md), [the slow care](process_the_slow_care.md),
  [the single case](process_the_single_case.md).
- **[the cost](process_cost_complaint.md)**: what the complainer is spending while it runs. Anchored by
  Ahmed (2021). [the second job](process_the_second_job.md), [the changed room](process_the_changed_room.md),
  [the known complainer](process_the_known_complainer.md).
- **[the outcome](process_outcome_complaint.md)**: how it ends and what has changed, where the engine is
