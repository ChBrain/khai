# Process: The Informal Word

## Taxonomy

[The Filing](process_filing_complaint.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: complaint

## Initiated by

The persona is advised to try informally first.
By somebody sympathetic, who is giving them good advice.

## Direction

Toward a route that is offered to everybody and completed by almost nobody.
The informal route is genuinely better where it works: quicker, less damaging to everybody, and capable of fixing a thing before it hardens. It is also the point at which most complaints stop, because trying it means raising the matter directly with the person concerned, or with their friend, and then living with however that goes.
A persona who tries informally and gets nowhere has also used up their surprise, given warning, and started the clock on any deadline the formal route imposes.

## Lever

The real superiority of the informal fix against what attempting it costs the person attempting it.
The carrying force is that the advice is correct and kindly meant: most matters are better settled by a conversation, formal processes damage relationships that could have survived, the adviser has seen formal complaints ruin people, and steering somebody away from one is often the most protective thing they can do; the resistance is that the same advice is given to everybody regardless of the matter, so the cases that most need a formal record are diverted at the door by people acting entirely in the complainer's interest.
The Informal Word is good advice that functions as a filter.

## Echo

A complaint that never became one, for excellent reasons.
It shows the largest single point of attrition, and the one nobody records.
The Informal Word is dated to the first conversation and is where most of this engine's cases end.
