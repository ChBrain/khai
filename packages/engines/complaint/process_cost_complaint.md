# Process: The Cost

## Taxonomy

[Complaint](process_complaint.md), what the complainer is spending while it runs.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: complaint

## Initiated by

The persona is doing the complaint as well as their life.
The Cost is what that takes: [a second job](process_the_second_job.md) of documentation, [a changed room](process_the_changed_room.md), and [a known complainer](process_the_known_complainer.md) where a colleague used to be.

## Direction

Toward an expenditure nobody budgeted and nobody is reimbursing.
The institution's side of the case is done in working hours by people who are paid to do it and who go home. The complainer's side is done in the evenings, out of their own reserves, in addition to the job that is being made harder by the thing they complained about.
None of this appears anywhere in the file.

## Lever

The seriousness of the matter against what pursuing it consumes.
The carrying force is that the effort is proportionate and the persona would make the same choice again: the wrong was real, letting it stand would be worse, and a complaint pursued properly needs exactly this much work if it is going to survive contact with a procedure; the resistance is that the asymmetry is structural rather than anybody's doing -- the institution spends staff time and the persona spends their life, and there is no mechanism anywhere in the process that notices the difference.
The Cost is carried entirely on one side and recorded on neither.

## Echo

A case file that contains none of what it took.
It is the movement the procedure has no field for, and the reason so many complaints are withdrawn by people who were right.
The Cost is dated from the filing and compounds monthly.
