# Process: The Second Job

## Taxonomy

[The Cost](process_cost_complaint.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: complaint

## Initiated by

The persona starts keeping records.
Dates, times, who was present, printed emails, a chronology in a document that keeps growing.

## Direction

Toward unpaid work at professional standard, done alone.
The complaint has to be evidenced, so the persona becomes their own investigator: assembling, cross-referencing, drafting responses to the respondent's account, preparing for meetings, reading the policy properly for the first time in their life.
It is real work, it is done well, and it is done at eleven at night by somebody who has to be functional in the same building in the morning.

## Lever

The necessity of the documentation against what producing it requires.
The carrying force is that the work is the right work and skipping it loses the case: procedures run on records, an unevidenced allegation fails and deserves to, and the persona who documents carefully is doing exactly what any adviser would tell them to do; the resistance is that the requirement is set by the process and the capacity is set by the person, so the complaint is effectively available to whoever has the evenings, the literacy, the stamina, and nothing else going wrong in their life.
The Second Job makes the complaint a resource question.

## Echo

A chronology of forty pages and a person who is very tired.
It shows who can afford to use the channel that is open to everybody.
The Second Job is dated from the decision to file and does not pause.
