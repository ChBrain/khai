# Process: The Settlement

## Taxonomy

[The Outcome](process_outcome_complaint.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: complaint

## Initiated by

An arrangement is reached and the persona goes.
With a sum, a reference, and a clause about what may be said.

## Direction

Toward the most common resolution, which removes the complaint by removing the complainer.
Nobody set out to achieve this. The persona wanted it dealt with and now wants to be somewhere else; the institution wants certainty and an end to the risk; the lawyers on both sides regard the terms as reasonable, and they are. Everybody signs something they can live with.
The conduct stays where it is, with nobody now in a position to describe it.

## Lever

The reasonableness of the deal for both parties against what it takes out of circulation.
The carrying force is that a settlement is frequently the right answer for the person and refusing one can be a kind of self-harm: the persona gets money, a clean reference, an end to a process that was consuming them, and a chance to work somewhere they are not the one who complained -- their adviser is right to recommend it; the resistance is that the terms make the account unsayable, so the single most informative record of what happened leaves with the person who made it, and the next persona in the same position starts from nothing.
The Settlement is a good outcome for the complainer and the reason the next one has no history to consult.

## Echo

A departure that is nobody's defeat and everybody's arrangement.
It shows where the account goes.
The Settlement is dated to the signature and is binding on one side's speech.
