# Complaint

Complaint as process: a grievance about something that has already happened, taken through the procedure of the institution it is about, read on what the process cost rather than on what it concluded -- not the harm signalled before it arrives, which is warning's and the raising composite's, and not the choice between leaving and speaking up, which is loyalty's -- across four movements: the filing (an informal word that is good advice and functions as a filter, a form that asks for incidents from somebody complaining about a climate, and a named respondent that turns a claim about a place into a quarrel between two people), the procedure (a confidence seal that protects everybody and connects nobody, slow care that is the right speed for the process and the wrong speed for a life, and a single case heard on its own merits, which is the fairest treatment of the least useful unit), the cost (a second job of documentation that makes the complaint a resource question, a changed room made of consideration that feels like exile, and a known complainer described accurately and read as a warning), and the outcome (a partial upholding that is the honest finding and functions as a rejection, a settlement that is a good outcome for the complainer and the reason the next one has no history to consult, and a next complainer who declines on evidence produced by fairness) -- with the twist that a complaint changes the complainer more reliably than it changes anything they complained about.

This engine is defined by its [manifest](package.json), which the canon renders as the WIRES card. The manifest is the single source of truth; this README is generated, not edited by hand.
