# Process: The Known Complainer

## Taxonomy

[The Cost](process_cost_complaint.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: complaint

## Initiated by

The persona acquires a new most-important attribute.
Whatever else they are, they are the one who complained.

## Direction

Toward an identity assigned by an act, and never afterwards mislaid.
It travels. It reaches people who have no idea what the complaint was about, in a version with no detail: there was something, it was messy, they took it further. It reaches the next employer through the reference that is careful, and the panel that is choosing between two candidates, and the corridor conversation about who to put on the committee.
The people passing it on are not condemning them. They are being accurate: this person did complain, and that is a true and useful thing to know about a colleague.

## Lever

The truth of the description against what it displaces.
The carrying force is that nobody is defaming anybody and the fact is a fact: the persona did make a formal complaint, colleagues are entitled to know the shape of a serious matter, and a workplace where such things were unmentionable would be worse; the resistance is that the label is sticky in a way the original conduct never was -- the respondent's behaviour, even where established, becomes an episode, and the complaint becomes a property of the person who made it.
The Known Complainer is described accurately and read as a warning.

## Echo

One durable attribute where a reputation used to be.
It shows what the persona is left holding after the file closes.
The Known Complainer is dated from the filing and outlasts everybody's memory of the case.
