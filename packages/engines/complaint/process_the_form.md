# Process: The Form

## Taxonomy

[The Filing](process_filing_complaint.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: complaint

## Initiated by

The grievance has to be written into boxes.
Dates, incidents, policies allegedly breached, remedy sought.

## Direction

Toward the parts of the wrong that have a category, and away from the rest.
The persona sits down with something that has been happening for two years and finds fields for events. So they produce events: the meeting in March, the email in June -- each of which, taken alone, is small, and each of which they must now argue was serious.
The thing they are actually complaining about has no field, and the version they submit reads thinner than the truth to them and thinner still to a reader.

## Lever

The fairness of requiring specifics against what specifics cannot hold.
The carrying force is that the demand is just and protects the accused: nobody can defend themselves against an impression, an investigator needs something to establish or fail to establish, and vagueness in an allegation is a serious unfairness to the person it is about; the resistance is that the wrongs that most need a procedure are cumulative and tonal, so the requirement that makes the process fair is the same requirement that removes the substance of the complaint before it is read.
The Form asks for incidents from somebody complaining about a climate.

## Echo

A true document that understates its own case.
It shows why serious complaints so often read as petty on the file.
The Form is dated to the submission and is the only version anybody later reads.
