# Process: The Single Case

## Taxonomy

[The Procedure](process_procedure_complaint.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: complaint

## Initiated by

The complaint is considered on its own merits.
As it should be, and as the persona was assured it would be.

## Direction

Toward a fairness that dismantles the evidence the complaint needed.
Judged alone, an incident is ambiguous: a remark could have been misread, a decision had other reasons, a manner is a matter of style. Judged alongside eleven others it is not ambiguous at all -- and the eleven others are inadmissible, because this hearing is about this allegation and it would be improper to hold unproven matters against a respondent.
So the complainer's strongest evidence is the one thing the fairest possible procedure must exclude.

## Lever

The protection of the accused against the erasure of the pattern.
The carrying force is that the rule is a bedrock protection and abandoning it would be monstrous: nobody should be found against on the basis of accusations that were never established, a respondent facing an accumulated reputation cannot defend themselves, and the persona would want exactly this rule if they were the one accused; the resistance is that the harm being complained about is frequently a pattern, and the pattern is only visible in aggregate, so the procedure is built to be blind in precisely the dimension the complaint lives in.
The Single Case is the fairest available treatment of the least useful unit.

## Echo

An incident found ambiguous, twelve times.
It shows why complaints about a person can each fail while all of them are true.
The Single Case is dated to the terms of reference.
