# Process: The Procedure

## Taxonomy

[Complaint](process_complaint.md), what the institution does with it.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: complaint

## Initiated by

The case is opened and the machine starts.
The Procedure runs on three safeguards: [a confidence seal](process_the_confidence_seal.md), [slow care](process_the_slow_care.md), and [a single case](process_the_single_case.md) heard on its own merits.

## Direction

Toward a process that is correct at every step and produces isolation as a by-product.
Nothing here is a trick. Confidentiality, thoroughness, and individual assessment are the three things anybody would specify if asked to design a fair grievance procedure from scratch, and each was argued for by people trying to protect somebody.
The engine reads what they do when they run together on a complaint about the conduct of the place.

## Lever

The necessity of every safeguard against what their sum does to one party.
The carrying force is that removing any of them makes the process worse for everybody: without confidentiality nobody would report anything and the accused would be tried in the corridor, without thoroughness the findings would be worthless, and without individual assessment the persona would be judged on somebody else's case; the resistance is that the institution has run this hundreds of times and the complainer is running it once, so protections that are symmetrical on paper are used symmetrically by only one side.
The Procedure is fair in each of its parts and unequal in its distribution of practice.

## Echo

A correct process with a repeat player on one side of it.
It is the movement that convinces everybody watching that the complaint was handled properly, which it was.
The Procedure is dated from the acknowledgement letter and runs longer than anybody said.
