# Instructions: Complaint

How a Playwright wires the complaint engine into a play. You wire by linking the play's own content to the engine's files; the engine files stay untouched, they are canon. The Roadie plumbs the engine's law into Instructions Knowledge on deploy. Authoring guidance, not runtime content, and it does not go on tour.

## Human

- The human keeps everybody conscientious: the engine needs no cover-up, no villain, and no bad-faith investigator, and stops working the moment it gets one. Make each safeguard real and let each one visibly protect somebody, because the mechanism is their sum rather than any one of them. And decide what the complaint was actually about, since the version on the form will be smaller and that gap is the engine.

## Agent

- From the play's own content, a persona links under Projection where they stand to it: whether they filed, were named, investigated, sat on the panel, or watched somebody else go through it and decided not to. Read the engine on what the process cost, never on what it concluded. Keep it the channel: the harm signalled before it arrives is warning's, the scrutiny of the person who raised it is the raising composite's, the choice between going and speaking is loyalty's. You link to the engine, you never edit it.

## Collaboration

- The harm signalled before it arrives, the account with no second source, and the scrutiny that follows the warner are warning's, credibility's, and the raising composite's; the choice between exit and voice is loyalty's and membership's; what a fair hearing buys and what institutional acknowledgement does are the heeding composite's and recognition's; the wrong itself and what restores it are repair's, forgiveness's, and the moral-account composite's; the mark carried afterwards and the talk that carries it are stigma's and gossip's; how organisations are structured and how a tolerated exception becomes the norm are org's, hierarchy's, power's, and drift's. Statutes, tribunals, limitation periods, and the burden of proof are the trade and set the format. Whether a production's procedure was scrupulous or merely correct, and whether a settlement was a relief or a removal, are the team's review.

## Knowledge

- A complaint is a grievance about something that has already happened, taken through the procedure of the institution it is about, and assessed by people who work there under rules that institution wrote. It is not warning's harm signalled before it arrives, not the raising composite's scrutiny of the warner, and not loyalty's choice between going and speaking. It wires on a persona at Projection and runs in four movements. The filing converts what happened into what can be alleged: the informal route is genuinely better where it works, is advised to everybody regardless of the matter, and is where most cases end; the form has fields for incidents and none for accumulation, so a persona complaining about two years submits two events; and because an institution cannot be the respondent to its own procedure, a structural claim acquires a name and becomes a quarrel between two colleagues. The procedure is fair in each of its parts and unequal in its distribution of practice: confidentiality protects accuser and accused and keeps four identical complaints in four sealed boxes; thoroughness takes months, which is the right speed for the process and the wrong speed for a life; and hearing each case on its own merits is a bedrock protection of the accused and excludes the pattern, which is where the harm lived. The cost is carried entirely on one side and recorded on neither -- the documentation is a second job, the colleagues become careful rather than hostile, and the persona acquires one durable attribute. The engine's twist is at the root: a complaint changes the complainer more reliably than it changes anything they complained about, because every safeguard protects somebody and their sum leaves the complainer isolated, slow, and eventually known for one thing.

## System

- Do keep the investigator conscientious and the safeguards real. The engine needs no conspiracy, and staging one replaces the mechanism with a thriller.
- Do let the informal route be offered kindly, by somebody who means well and has seen formal complaints ruin people.
- Do show the form being filled in. Two incidents that fit the boxes, from two years that do not, written by somebody who can feel the version thinning as they type.
- Do make each safeguard protect a named person on stage. Confidentiality shielding the accused, the delay allowing a proper answer, the single case sparing somebody an accumulated reputation.
- Do keep the colleagues decent. Nobody shuns anybody; they are careful, they do not ask, they stop mentioning things, and the persona eats lunch alone.
- Do let the finding be honest and partial. The evidenced parts upheld, the pattern recorded as not evidenced, and both parties reading it as a loss.
- Do put the settlement on reasonable terms. Money, a clean reference, a clause -- recommended by a good adviser, accepted for good reasons, and it takes the account out of circulation.
- Do close on the watcher rather than the letter. Somebody with the same problem performs the calculation, declines, and is not being a coward.
- Do not model the harm not yet arrived (warning, credibility, raising), exit versus voice (loyalty, membership), the fair hearing itself (heeding, recognition), the wrong and its repair (repair, forgiveness, moral-account), the mark afterwards (stigma, gossip), or the institution's structure (org, hierarchy, power, drift). Do not make the case stand for a theme; that is authored key, the play's pitch. Do not edit the engine's files; wire only from the play's side.
