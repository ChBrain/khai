# Process: The Named Respondent

## Taxonomy

[The Filing](process_filing_complaint.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: complaint

## Initiated by

The procedure needs somebody for the complaint to be against.
Institutions cannot be respondents to their own grievance procedures.

## Direction

Toward a claim about a place converted into an accusation of a person.
What the persona wanted to say is that this is how things are done here. What the form requires is a name, so the name goes in -- usually the person who did the most visible thing, who may not be the person the complaint is really about.
From that moment the matter is a dispute between two colleagues, which is a smaller and much more manageable object than the one that was submitted.

## Lever

The necessity of a respondent against what naming one does to the claim.
The carrying force is that somebody has to be answerable and the alternative is nothing: a complaint against no one cannot be investigated, the accused has rights that only exist once they are identified, and a system that let people allege wrongdoing without naming anybody would be intolerable; the resistance is that the naming reclassifies the grievance -- a structural claim becomes an interpersonal one, the remedy shrinks to what can be done to one employee, and the persona is now in a conflict with a colleague rather than in a dispute with an institution.
The Named Respondent turns a claim about a place into a quarrel between two people.

## Echo

A structural complaint with one person's name on it.
It shows how the largest complaints become the smallest cases.
The Named Respondent is dated to the form and determines everything after it.
