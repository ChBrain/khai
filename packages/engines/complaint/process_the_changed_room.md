# Process: The Changed Room

## Taxonomy

[The Cost](process_cost_complaint.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: complaint

## Initiated by

Colleagues become careful.
Not hostile. Careful.

## Direction

Toward an isolation produced entirely by reasonable behaviour.
People stop mentioning the respondent in front of the persona, in case it is awkward. They stop inviting them to things the respondent will be at, to spare everybody. They are warm and slightly formal, and they do not ask how it is going, because that would be prying into a confidential matter.
Every one of those is a considerate choice. Their sum is a person eating lunch alone.

## Lever

The decency of the colleagues against what their decency produces.
The carrying force is that nobody here is shunning anybody and the care is genuine: the colleagues are trying not to make it worse, they are respecting a confidentiality they have been instructed to respect, several of them privately believe the persona, and taking sides publicly would help nobody and could cost them their own position; the resistance is that the persona cannot distinguish tact from distance from the inside, so support delivered as carefulness arrives as withdrawal -- and the one thing that would fix it, an ordinary conversation about the thing, is the thing the process forbids.
The Changed Room is made of consideration and feels like exile.

## Echo

A workplace where everybody is being kind and nobody is talking.
It shows why complainers describe the aftermath as worse than the original conduct.
The Changed Room is dated from the week the complaint became known and does not revert.
