# Process: The Confidence Seal

## Taxonomy

[The Procedure](process_procedure_complaint.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: complaint

## Initiated by

The persona is told the matter is confidential.
And that they should not discuss it with colleagues.

## Direction

Toward a protection that is real and that severs every connection the complainer had.
Confidentiality shields the accused from rumour before anything is established, shields the complainer from gossip, and is the condition on which witnesses will speak at all. It also means the persona cannot ask a colleague whether the same thing happened to them, cannot compare their treatment with anybody else's, and will not learn -- possibly ever -- that three other people have filed about the same person.
The institution knows about the other three. It is not permitted to say so, and would be wrong to.

## Lever

The protection confidentiality gives against the comparison it forecloses.
The carrying force is that the seal is not a device and is owed to everybody: an accusation loose in a workplace destroys people before any finding, witnesses will not speak without it, and a complainer whose case became office conversation would be in a far worse position than one whose case is quiet; the resistance is that the pattern lives in the comparison, so a rule that protects each person individually is the same rule that keeps every complaint about the same conduct in a separate sealed box.
The Confidence Seal protects everybody and connects nobody.

## Echo

Four identical complaints, none of which knows about the others.
It shows why an institution can be handling a pattern correctly, case by case, and never see it.
The Confidence Seal is dated to the opening letter and holds afterwards.
