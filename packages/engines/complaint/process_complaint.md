# Process: Complaint

## Taxonomy

A grievance taken through an institution's own procedure (no khai file of its own yet).

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: complaint

## Initiated by

Something was done, and the persona takes it to the body responsible for the place it was done in.
Complaint starts wherever a wrong that has already happened is put into an official channel: the grievance at work, the report to the university, the letter to the trust, the case opened with the regulator, the form submitted to the housing office.
It is not the harm signalled before it arrives, which is warning's and the raising composite's, and not the choice between leaving and speaking up, which is loyalty's. It is what a procedure does with a grievance, and what it does to the person who brought it.

## Direction

Toward a process that runs correctly and moves almost nothing except the person who started it.
The engine reads four movements: [the filing](process_filing_complaint.md), how a grievance becomes a case; [the procedure](process_procedure_complaint.md), what the institution does with it; [the cost](process_cost_complaint.md), what the complainer is spending while it runs; and [the outcome](process_outcome_complaint.md), the twist -- how it ends and what has changed.
The four run once per case and select who complains next.

## Lever

The necessity of having somewhere to take a wrong against what the channel is made of.
The carrying force is that complaint procedures are a genuine achievement and the alternative is far worse: before them a persona had no route at all except leaving or enduring, the safeguards inside them were each fought for, the people who staff them are usually conscientious, and most complaints -- the small, factual, fixable ones -- are handled quickly and well; the resistance is that the serious complaint is about the conduct of the place, so it is filed with the body it is about, assessed by people who work there, against a person who has colleagues, under a procedure that body wrote.
Complaint is read on what the process cost, never on what it concluded.

## Echo

Somebody who was right, and is now the person who complained.
Complaint does not close; it is a grievance put through a machine, gathered into one reading -- a filing, a procedure, a cost, an outcome.
The engine's twist is here. **A complaint changes the complainer more reliably than it changes anything they complained about.** No step in the process is corrupt and no safeguard is a pretence: confidentiality protects the accused and the accuser both, thoroughness takes time because rushing would be unjust, and each case is heard on its own merits because that is what fairness means. Every one of those protects somebody, and their sum leaves the complainer isolated, slow, unable to see the pattern, and eventually known for one thing.
It hands off what it is not: the harm signalled before it arrives and the scrutiny that follows are warning's, credibility's, and the raising composite's; the choice between going and speaking is loyalty's; the standing a fair hearing confers is heeding's and recognition's; the wrong itself and what repairs it are repair's and forgiveness's; the mark a persona carries afterwards is stigma's; and a grievance staged to **stand for** a theme is authored key, the play's pitch.
The persona is dated from the day they filed.
