# Process: Forbear

## Taxonomy

[Face](process_face.md), the strategy this is one form of.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: face

## Initiated by

The threat is weighed too great to risk, and the persona does not do the act at all.
The request goes unmade, the correction unsaid, the disagreement swallowed -- the want set aside rather than pay the face-cost that doing it, in any form, would carry.

## Direction

Away from the act; face kept whole by leaving it undone.
The persona chooses the want's frustration over the threat's delivery: better the thing ungained than the face -- theirs, or the other's -- spent to reach for it.
It is the fifth option and the floor of the ladder: not a way of doing the act, but the decision not to.

## Lever

The face-cost of any doing outweighs the want.
When even the most redressed form -- the deepest deference, the most deniable hint -- would still cost more face than the persona is willing to spend, the act is withheld: too great a distance, too much power on the other side, too large an imposition, or too fragile a tie to risk.
The calculation is the same as the others'; only here it comes out below the line where any act is worth its threat.

## Echo

Face is spared, and the want goes unmet.
The unsaid thing sits between them -- a favour never asked, a wrong never named, an objection never raised -- and what it reads as depends on whether the other senses it: tact where the restraint is felt and valued, cowardice where the persona knows they should have spoken, an invisible absence where the other never knew there was anything to say.
It is the quietest of the five and often the most consequential, for what is forborne does not go away; it accumulates.
