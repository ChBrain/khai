# Process: Bald

## Taxonomy

[Face](process_face.md), the strategy this is one form of.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: face

## Initiated by

The persona does the act on the record and without softening: "shut the door," "you're wrong," "give me that."
No redress is paid -- the face-threat is left bare, the want stated in the clearest, most direct terms.

## Direction

Toward efficiency and clarity, the threat borne openly.
The persona says exactly what they mean in the plainest form, spending nothing on protecting the other's face; the act is done in the fewest words and the least mistakable way.
It is the strategy that treats getting the thing done as worth more than dressing it.

## Lever

Low threat, high power, or urgent need.
Bald is chosen when the imposition is trivial, or when the persona's standing is high enough that they need not redress, or when the moment demands speed -- an emergency, a command, an instruction where softening would cost lives or clarity.
Where none of those hold, the same bare form reads as an insult, because the redress the weight called for was withheld.

## Echo

The act lands plainly, and how it reads depends on the weight it ignored.
Between intimates at ease it is frankness; from an authority to a subordinate it is command; from below, or between strangers, or where the imposition was large, it is rudeness -- the face-threat delivered without the softening the situation asked for.
It is the zero point the other four strategies pay to move away from.
