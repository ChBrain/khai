# @chbrain/khai-engine-face

## 0.1.1

### Patch Changes

- 013802d: Add the face engine: the public self-image a persona claims in interaction, and how they manage an act that threatens it. The root (`process_face.md`) declares face as two wants at once -- positive face (to be approved of and included) and negative face (to be unimpeded and autonomous) -- threatened by ordinary acts (a request, a refusal, a correction, a disagreement), with the redress paid in proportion to the threat's weight (social distance, relative power, the ranking of the imposition). Five strategies are the ordered ways of doing a face-threatening act: `bald` (on the record, no redress), `solidarity` (positive politeness -- draw near, claim common ground), `deference` (negative politeness -- hedge, minimise, give an out), `hint` (off the record, said so it can be denied), and `forbear` (do not do the act at all, the threat too great to risk).

  Pairs with `speech-act` the way `register` does -- speech-act owns the illocutionary deed being softened, this owns the redress around it. Bounded against `status`/`power` (the rank that feeds a threat's weight), `shame` (the felt collapse when face is lost), `recognition` (the granting of the approval that positive face wants), `self-esteem` (the private self-worth beneath the public face), and `status-move` (the tactical status play that may ride along). Warranted (LORE) on Goffman (face and face-work), Brown & Levinson (the two faces, the FTA, and the strategies), Lakoff (the rules of politeness), Leech (the politeness principle and its maxims), and Spencer-Oatey (rapport management). Set at patch as the free level; a new engine may warrant a minor at the maintainer's `bump:minor` label.
