# Process: Face

## Taxonomy

Pragmatics / the interaction order.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: face

## Initiated by

A persona is about to do something that touches another's standing self-image: ask a favour, refuse a request, correct a mistake, disagree, impose.
Both hold a face they want kept -- the public self-image each claims and expects the other to honour -- and it has two sides: the want to be approved of and included, and the want to be unimpeded and left one's own.
The act about to be done threatens one or both, on one side or the other, and the persona knows it before they open their mouth.

## Direction

Toward doing the act while keeping face -- the other's and their own -- from being lost more than the act requires.
The persona does not usually abandon the act; they dress it, so the threat it carries is softened, deflected, or borne openly.
Face-work is mutual: each is expected to protect the other's face as they protect their own, and the room reads a breach at once.

## Lever

The weight of the threat, which sets how much redress the persona pays.
The weight rises with the social distance between them, with the other's power over the persona, and with the size of the imposition itself; a small ask of an intimate is light, a large ask of a stranger with power over you is heavy.
The heavier the threat, the more the act is softened or turned aside; the lighter, the barer it can be done -- so the same request is barked at a peer and hedged for a superior, not from feeling but from the sum of the weights.

## Echo

The act is done with much redress, little, or none -- or not done at all -- and face is kept, spent, or lost.
The choice is read: bluntness as command or as rudeness, warmth as friendship or as presumption, deference as respect or as coldness, a hint as tact or as evasion, silence as care or as cowardice.
The process continues as [bald](process_bald.md), [solidarity](process_solidarity.md), [deference](process_deference.md), [hint](process_hint.md), or [forbear](process_forbear.md) -- the five strategies, from the barest to the unspoken. The act being softened -- the request, the refusal, the correction -- is the speech-act engine's; this engine holds the redress around it.
