# Process: Solidarity

## Taxonomy

[Face](process_face.md), the strategy this is one form of.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: face

## Initiated by

The persona wraps the act in closeness, redressing the other's want to be liked and included.
The threat is softened by drawing near: the ask comes bundled with warmth, a joke, a compliment, a "we're in this together" that affirms the tie even as it imposes on it.

## Direction

Toward the other's positive face -- their want to be approved of.
The persona does the act while claiming common ground: naming shared wants, using the language of friends, treating the other as one whose needs the persona already shares.
The redress runs by affirming the bond, so the imposition arrives inside a gesture of belonging.

## Lever

Positive politeness: soften the threat by closing the distance.
Claim common ground, seek agreement, avoid disagreement, joke, use the in-group's names and its shorthand, attend to the other and their wants -- all of it says "you are one of us, and I like you," which cushions the act it carries.
It works where a tie exists or can be plausibly claimed, and turns the imposition into a moment of the relationship rather than a demand on it.

## Echo

The act is done and the bond affirmed.
Where the closeness is real, it reads as warmth and the ask lands easily; where it is presumed but not there -- the stranger who calls you buddy to sell you something -- it reads as over-familiar, and the borrowed intimacy grates.
Its opposite in aim is [deference](process_deference.md): where solidarity redresses by drawing near, deference redresses by giving room.
