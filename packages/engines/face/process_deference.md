# Process: Deference

## Taxonomy

[Face](process_face.md), the strategy this is one form of.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: face

## Initiated by

The persona wraps the act in respect for the other's autonomy, redressing their want not to be imposed on.
The threat is softened by giving room: the ask is hedged, made small, apologised for, and handed over with an out, so the other is left as free as the imposition allows.

## Direction

Toward the other's negative face -- their want to be unimpeded.
The persona does the act while working not to presume: not assuming the yes, not pressing, marking the imposition as regretted and the other's freedom as theirs to keep.
The redress runs by holding back, so the act arrives as a request the other may decline rather than a demand they must meet.

## Lever

Negative politeness: soften the threat by not presuming.
Be indirect, hedge the force ("if it's not too much trouble," "you might possibly"), minimise the imposition, apologise for intruding, defer to the other, give them a way to say no without cost -- all of it says "I know this asks something of you, and I leave it yours to grant."
It is the strategy of distance and respect, the register of the formal, the careful, and the outranked.

## Echo

The act is done and the other's freedom acknowledged.
Where the weight was real -- a large ask, a superior, a stranger -- it reads as proper respect and the other's autonomy is honoured; overdone where the weight was light, it reads as cold, stiff, or as empty ceremony that keeps a distance the moment did not need.
Its opposite in aim is [solidarity](process_solidarity.md): where deference redresses by giving room, solidarity redresses by drawing near.
