# Instructions: Face

How a Playwright wires the face engine into a play. You wire by linking the play's own content to the engine's files; the engine files stay untouched, they are canon. The Roadie plumbs the engine's law into Instructions Knowledge on deploy. Authoring guidance, not runtime content, and it does not go on tour.

## Human

- The human defines the face terrain: the act that threatens face, whose face it touches and on which side (approval or autonomy), and the weight that sets the redress -- how distant the parties, how the power runs, how large the imposition.

## Agent

- From the play's own content, a persona links the face strategy the moment runs (bald, solidarity, deference, hint, or forbear) under its Projection. Let the strategy shape how much of the other's face, and their own, the act is made to cost, and let the choice read as frankness, warmth, respect, evasion, or silence. You link to the engine, you never edit it.

## Collaboration

- The act being softened -- the request, the refusal, the correction -- is the speech-act engine's; this engine holds the redress around it. The rank behind the weight is status and power; the felt collapse when face is lost is shame's; the want for approval that is positive face is served by recognition; the private self-worth beneath the public face is self-esteem's; the tactical status play that may ride along is status-move's. This engine supplies the face-work.

## Knowledge

- Face is the public self-image a persona claims in interaction, with two wants: positive face, to be approved of and included, and negative face, to be unimpeded and autonomous. Many acts threaten it, and the persona pays redress in proportion to the threat's weight (distance, power, imposition). Bald does the act with no redress. Solidarity redresses the other's want to be liked, by drawing near. Deference redresses their want to be unimpeded, by giving room. Hint does the act off the record, so it can be denied. Forbear does not do the act at all.

## System

- Do let the weight set the strategy: the same request is barked at a peer and hedged for a superior, not from feeling but from the sum of distance, power, and imposition.
- Do let a mismatch read: warmth where no closeness exists is presumption, deference where the weight was light is coldness, a hint where the threat was small is manipulation.
- Do let what is forborne accumulate -- the favour never asked, the wrong never named -- rather than vanish.
- Do delegate the act to the speech-act engine and the rank to status. Do not edit the engine's files; wire only from the play's side.
