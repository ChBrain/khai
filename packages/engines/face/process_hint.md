# Process: Hint

## Taxonomy

[Face](process_face.md), the strategy this is one form of.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: face

## Initiated by

The persona does the act off the record, so that it can be denied.
Rather than state the want, they say something that implies it -- "it's cold in here" for "close the window," "I'm not sure this is finished" for "do it again" -- and leave the hearer to take the meaning or not.

## Direction

Toward doing the act without being on the record for it.
The persona puts the want where the hearer can find it but does not commit to having asked; both are left an out -- the speaker can disown the demand, the hearer can miss it without refusing.
The redress runs by deniability: no face is openly spent because, on the surface, no threat was made.

## Lever

Off the record: mean past what is said, and leave the uptake to the hearer.
The persona speaks in hints, understatement, and implication, trusting the hearer to infer the want by presuming the remark is relevant -- the safest strategy for the speaker's own face, and the least certain of landing.
It is chosen when the threat is grave enough that being on the record would cost too much, or when the persona wants the thing but not the responsibility of having asked.

## Echo

The act may land, or be missed, or be caught and pointedly not taken.
Where the threat was grave, the deniability reads as tact and delicacy; where it was not, the same indirection reads as evasive, passive, or manipulative -- a demand smuggled in so its maker cannot be held to it.
The burden of catching the meaning falls on the hearer, and how they meet the implicature -- the meaning past the said, which the speech-act engine's cooperative reading recovers -- decides whether the unspoken ask was granted, refused, or never registered at all.
