# Process: The Clock Unset

## Taxonomy

[The Hold](process_hold_germination.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: germination

## Initiated by

The seed does not know what month it is.
It has no calendar, no memory of a date, and no way to be told.

## Direction

Toward a decision that has to be made entirely on present conditions.
Everything the seed will ever learn about the world arrives as temperature, moisture, gas, light, and the chemistry dissolved around it. From that it has to infer something it cannot observe: whether the season ahead will support a seedling for long enough.
A warm wet week in autumn is indistinguishable from a warm wet week in spring on the evidence available, which is exactly the mistake the hold exists to prevent.

## Lever

The reliability of present conditions as data against what they cannot tell the seed.
The carrying force is that the available signals are real and largely trustworthy: moisture and temperature genuinely constrain what a seedling can do, they are measured directly rather than inferred, and a seed responding to them is responding to the thing that will actually kill it or not; the resistance is that the question is about the future and the data are about now, so the seed is obliged to use proxies -- and a proxy is only as good as the correlation that made it, which a changed climate or a moved seed can break without notice.
The Clock Unset forces a forecast out of a thermometer.

## Echo

A decision about a season, made from a moment.
It shows what the cues in the next movement are for.
The Clock Unset is dated to every hour and is never resolved.
