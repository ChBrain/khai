# Process: The Smoke

## Taxonomy

[The Cue](process_cue_germination.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: germination

## Initiated by

Compounds from burnt vegetation reach the seed in water.
Not heat, and not the fire itself -- the residue, dissolved and washed down.

## Direction

Toward a signal that arrives after the danger has passed.
The active chemistry is produced by combustion and carried into the soil by the first rain afterwards, which means the seed is not responding to the fire. It is responding to evidence that a fire happened and is over, at the moment when the canopy is gone, the ground is open, and competition is at its lowest.
It is one of the more exact signals in biology: specific compounds, at very low concentrations, doing nothing else.

## Lever

The quality of the timing against how narrow the window it opens is.
The carrying force is that the cue is superbly placed and is worth waiting decades for: the post-fire ground is the best opportunity the species will ever get, the signal cannot be produced by anything other than burnt plant matter, and seeds that answer it arrive in a cleared field with no shade and a flush of nutrients; the resistance is that the opportunity closes quickly as the survivors regrow, so the entire hedge of a long bank is spent into a window of a season or two, and a seed that answers slightly late finds the ground already taken.
The Smoke is a precise signal for a brief opening.
The fire that produced it, and the cone that opens in it, are combustion's.

## Echo

A bank emptying into a burnt field.
It shows the strongest version of a cue, and the cost of a cue that is this specific.
The Smoke is dated to the first rain after the fire.
