# Process: The After

## Taxonomy

[The Commit](process_commit_germination.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: germination

## Initiated by

The leaves open and the seed is gone.
An empty coat in the soil, and an organism that now lives on light.

## Direction

Toward a thing that is no longer subject to any of this.
The moment the first true leaves are working, everything the engine has been reading stops applying. There is no dormancy available, no store, no coat, no cue to wait for. The plant is now exposed continuously to conditions it can neither refuse nor sit out, which is the ordinary situation of everything that is not a seed.
What it has gained is the ability to make more of itself. What it has lost is the only strategy that ever let it wait.

## Lever

The capability the plant has gained against the protection it has given up.
The carrying force is that the exchange is the point and is overwhelmingly worth making: a seed that never becomes a plant achieves nothing at all, growth is the only route to another generation of seeds, and every advantage of the held state was only ever in service of reaching this; the resistance is that the held state was genuinely the safer one, so the organism has traded a strategy that survives decades of bad conditions for one that must be fed every day.
The After is the end of every option the seed had.

## Echo

A plant where a seed was, with nothing held in reserve.
It shows the loop closing, since this plant will spend a season packing the next generation into the same suspension.
The After is dated to the first true leaf, and the ground it now stands in is the world's.
