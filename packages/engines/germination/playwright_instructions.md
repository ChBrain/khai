# Instructions: Germination

How a Playwright wires the germination engine into a play. You wire by linking the play's own content to the engine's files; the engine files stay untouched, they are canon. The Roadie plumbs the engine's law into Instructions Knowledge on deploy. Authoring guidance, not runtime content, and it does not go on tour.

## Human

- The human keeps the refusal active rather than sleepy: a dormant seed is doing something, and the engine stops working the moment it is staged as merely inert. Let the cue be specific and let it certify something other than itself -- a winter that happened, a fire that is over, a soil that was turned. And keep the failure blameless, because nothing malfunctions in this engine and the seed that dies underground read its conditions correctly.

## Agent

- From the play's own content, a piece links under Apparent which movement the seed is in, and a place links under Shown what it supplies: the cold accumulated, the fire it had, the canopy overhead, the soil that was disturbed. Read the engine on what was required, never on what grew. Keep it the held case: matter losing its imposed order is decay's, directed spoilage is fermentation's, and the fire itself is combustion's. You link to the engine, you never edit it.

## Collaboration

- Rot, rust, and crumble are decay's; decay pointed at a wanted outcome is fermentation's; the burn, the flame front, and the serotinous cone are combustion's and fire's; the plant after its first true leaf is the world's; soil chemistry, rainfall, and the climate that set the correlations the cues rely on are weather's, climate's, and the world's. Stratification schedules, scarification, sowing depth, and germination testing are the trade and set the format. Whether a production's cue was genuine or a false spring, and whether a bank was spent or merely thinned, are the team's review.

## Knowledge

- Germination is a whole organism held in suspension and the conditions under which it stops being held. It is not decay's matter losing its imposed order, not fermentation's directed spoilage, and not combustion's fire. It wires on a piece at Apparent and a place at Shown, and runs in four movements. The pack is provisioned once by a parent who cannot see the conditions: a complete plant already built and dried, a finite store that must cover the whole distance to light, and a coat that permits the long wait by sealing the seed and must be destroyed from outside for anything to happen. The hold keeps the seed alive by keeping it from living -- dormancy is a maintained block rather than an inability, since a viable seed at a workable temperature with adequate water declines to germinate and will go within hours if the block is lifted chemically; the seed has no calendar and must infer a season from a moment; and a bank of thousands, holding at different strengths, spreads one line's attempts across decades at the cost of nearly every individual in it. The cue reads a reliable stand-in for a fact the seed cannot observe: an accumulated cold total certifies that a winter happened, smoke chemistry in the first rain certifies that a canopy burnt and the fire is over, and red light certifies a workable depth while the ratio of red to far-red certifies that nothing is overhead. The engine's twist is at the root: a seed spends nearly all of its life refusing to grow, and the one time it agrees there is no way back -- water uptake looks identical on both sides of the point of return, the store is spent reaching light, and there is no dormancy available part-way.

## System

- Do stage the refusal as work. Warm, wet, viable, and not growing, with something actively holding it down.
- Do let the conditions be adequate and refused. If the scene shows a seed that cannot grow, it is the wrong scene.
- Do make the cue specific and indirect. Not warmth but a winter completed; not fire but its residue in the first rain; not space but the colour of the light.
- Do put the bank on stage. Thousands in the soil, holding at different strengths, most of which will never come up.
- Do cross the threshold without an event. The rain continues one day longer than it might have, and nothing announces that the crossing has happened.
- Do spend the store on an unmeasured distance. A stone, a crust, a depth, and a shoot that stops with the fuel gone.
- Do let the after be a loss as well as an arrival. The plant has gained the ability to make more of itself and has given up the only strategy that let it wait.
- Do not model undirected decay (decay, erosion), directed spoilage (fermentation), fire itself (combustion, fire), the plant after its first leaf, or the season and soil that supply the conditions (weather, climate). Do not treat the engine as a horticulture guide. Do not make the seed stand for a theme; that is authored key, the play's pitch. Do not edit the engine's files; wire only from the play's side.
