# Process: The Light Read

## Taxonomy

[The Cue](process_cue_germination.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: germination

## Initiated by

Light of the right kind reaches the seed.
Which means, in practice, that the soil above it has been disturbed.

## Direction

Toward a depth gauge built out of a pigment.
Many small seeds require light to germinate, and the requirement is a way of asking how deep they are: darkness means too far down for the store to reach the surface, so the answer is not yet.
The system also reads the quality of the light. A high proportion of far-red means leaves overhead, absorbing the red and passing the rest, so the seed is being told there is already a canopy and it would be germinating into shade.

## Lever

The elegance of the measurement against how easily it is triggered.
The carrying force is that the mechanism solves two problems with one pigment and solves them accurately: it prevents a small seed from starting at a depth its store cannot cross, it detects competition without seeing it, and it responds within minutes; the resistance is that any disturbance supplies the signal -- a plough, a hoof, a boot, a mole -- so a bank that has waited thirty years for a gap in the canopy can be emptied by somebody turning the soil in the wrong month.
The Light Read measures depth and competition with the same instrument.

## Echo

A seed answering a question about where it is.
It shows why cultivation produces weeds that nobody planted.
The Light Read is dated to the disturbance and is answered in minutes.
