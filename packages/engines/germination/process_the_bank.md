# Process: The Bank

## Taxonomy

[The Hold](process_hold_germination.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: germination

## Initiated by

There are thousands of them in the soil, not hundreds.
And they are not identical in how strongly they are holding.

## Direction

Toward a population that spreads one plant's attempts across years instead of places.
Seeds from the same parent differ in dormancy depth, so a cohort does not respond to a good spring as a unit: some germinate this year, some the next, some in eleven years, and some never. The soil under an old wood holds decades of accumulated attempts.
The effect is that no single season, however catastrophic, can take the whole line -- which is a hedge, and is paid for by the majority of seeds never growing at all.

## Lever

The insurance the spread provides against what the insurance costs.
The carrying force is that variable dormancy is the strategy's whole defence against a bad year: a lineage that committed everything to one spring would be extinguished by one drought, and the seeds held back are the reason a burnt or ploughed ground comes back at all; the resistance is that the premium is enormous and is paid by individuals -- most seeds in a bank rot, are eaten, or lose viability, and the hedge is only visible from the population, which no seed occupies.
The Bank buys the line a future by spending nearly all of its members.

## Echo

A soil full of unspent attempts.
It shows where the engine's arithmetic actually sits, which is not in any one seed.
The Bank is dated across decades and is depleted every year.
