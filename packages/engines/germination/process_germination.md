# Process: Germination

## Taxonomy

A held life released by a cue (no khai file of its own yet).

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: germination

## Initiated by

A seed is somewhere, and the conditions around it are or are not the ones it is waiting for.
Germination starts wherever a viable seed sits in a place that could support it: in soil, in a crack, in a bank of thousands under a wood, in a packet in a drawer, in the gut of a bird, under a burnt canopy.
It is not matter losing the order imposed on it, which is decay's, and not decay pointed somewhere, which is fermentation's. It is a whole organism held in suspension, and the conditions under which it stops being held.

## Direction

Toward a single irreversible commitment made on evidence that cannot be checked.
The engine reads four movements: [the pack](process_pack_germination.md), what the seed carries; [the hold](process_hold_germination.md), the refusal that is most of its life; [the cue](process_cue_germination.md), the specific unlocking; and [the commit](process_commit_germination.md), the twist -- the point past which there is no drying back.
The four run once for each seed, and almost every seed stops at the second.

## Lever

The safety of not starting against the fact that not starting is also fatal.
The carrying force is that refusal is the seed's competence and not its failure: a seed that sprouted whenever it was wet would be killed by the first warm spell in January, dormancy is what keeps a population alive through bad years, and the great majority of seeds that never germinate are doing exactly what they are built to do; the resistance is that a seed cannot wait indefinitely -- the food store degrades, the coat is attacked, and the bank thins every year -- so the refusal that protects it is also a clock running against it.
Germination is read on what was required, never on what grew.

## Echo

An empty coat in the soil, and a plant that cannot go back.
Germination does not close; it is a suspension being ended, gathered into one reading -- a pack, a hold, a cue, a commit.
The engine's twist is here. **A seed spends nearly all of its life refusing to grow, and the one time it agrees there is no way back.** Dormancy is not sleep and not inactivity; it is an enforced refusal, held against conditions that would otherwise be sufficient, and it is what the organism is doing for almost the whole of its existence. The commitment that ends it is made on cues -- a winter passed, smoke in the water, light at the right wavelength -- which are proxies for a future the seed cannot observe, and past a certain hour of water uptake it cannot be reversed. The store is spent reaching the surface, and there is no second attempt with the same seed.
It hands off what it is not: matter losing its imposed order is decay's; directed spoilage is fermentation's; fire and the cone it opens are combustion's; the ground it sits in is the world's; and a seed staged to **stand for** a theme is authored key, the play's pitch.
The seed is dated from the year it fell.
