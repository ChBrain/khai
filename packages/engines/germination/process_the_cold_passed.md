# Process: The Cold Passed

## Taxonomy

[The Cue](process_cue_germination.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: germination

## Initiated by

The seed has been through a winter.
Weeks of cold and damp, accumulated, at a temperature range the species counts.

## Direction

Toward a requirement that a period of time be completed rather than a condition met.
Stratification is not a switch thrown by cold; it is a total that has to be reached. The seed accumulates cold hours, and the dormancy relaxes in proportion, so a short cold spell moves the count a little and a full winter releases it.
This makes the mechanism resistant to the exact thing it is guarding against: a warm week in November cannot supply the count, and a cold snap in October cannot fake it.

## Lever

The reliability of a counted requirement against what happens when the count is not available.
The carrying force is that requiring an accumulated total is an excellent design and is very hard to trick: it cannot be satisfied by a single event, it scales with the severity of the winter, and it puts germination in spring almost without fail in the climate the species evolved in; the resistance is that it is a fixed requirement in a moving world, so where winters warm the count is not reached, and a seed with an unmet requirement does not germinate late -- it does not germinate.
The Cold Passed certifies a season by requiring one to have happened.

## Echo

A tally completed in the dark.
It shows how a seed reasons about a calendar it cannot read.
The Cold Passed is dated across a winter and is spent in one spring.
