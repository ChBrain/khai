# Process: The Coat

## Taxonomy

[The Pack](process_pack_germination.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: germination

## Initiated by

The seed is closed.
Hard, sometimes waxy, sometimes impermeable to water and gas alike.

## Direction

Toward armour that is also the lock on the door.
The coat does two jobs with one structure. It keeps out fungi, insects, and drying, which is what allows the long wait. It also keeps out water, which is what prevents germination, so in many species the coat alone is the whole of the dormancy.
Breaking it is therefore a requirement rather than an accident: abrasion in gravel, passage through a gut, freezing and thawing, fire, or the slow work of soil microbes.

## Lever

The protection the coat provides against the barrier it is at the same time.
The carrying force is that the seal is what makes long storage possible and a permeable seed would be no use: the coat is the reason a seed can cross a desert, sit in a bank for forty years, or survive being eaten, and none of that works with a soft covering; the resistance is that the same seal has to be destroyed for anything to happen, and the seed has no way to destroy it from inside -- the world has to do it, and the world is not obliged to.
The Coat protects the seed from everything including its own future.

## Echo

A container that must be damaged to be used.
It shows why so much of germination is a matter of what happened to the seed rather than what the seed did.
The Coat is dated to the seed's making and is worn away from outside.
