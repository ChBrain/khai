# Process: The One Shot

## Taxonomy

[The Commit](process_commit_germination.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: germination

## Initiated by

The store starts being spent.
Root down, shoot up, and a fixed amount of fuel for both.

## Direction

Toward a single attempt that cannot be paused or repeated.
The seedling is running on reserves until its own leaves reach light and open. There is no second store, no dormancy available part-way, and no way to stop and wait for better conditions -- a shoot that halts underground has spent the fuel and is finished.
Depth, soil density, a stone, a dry crust: any of these can be more than the budget covers, and none of them was known when the budget was set.

## Lever

The adequacy of the reserves in an ordinary case against the absence of any margin for an unusual one.
The carrying force is that the provisioning is well matched and usually sufficient: species are sized to the depths and soils they normally meet, most seedlings reach light comfortably, and the arrangement has been selected over a very long time to work; the resistance is that the budget is fixed and the conditions are not, so the seedling has no capacity to respond to a surprise -- it cannot slow down, cannot retreat, and cannot ask for more.
The One Shot spends a fixed store on an unmeasured distance.

## Echo

A shoot that will reach the surface or will not.
It shows what the irreversibility actually costs.
The One Shot is dated to the emergence and is decided within days.
