# Germination: Reference

## Line of Work

Germination as **process**: a whole organism held in suspension, and the conditions under which it stops
being held.

The engine's subject is a state that almost nothing else in biology occupies. Bewley's account is the
spine, and it makes the distinction the whole engine turns on: germination is not the start of growth
but the resumption of it, and dormancy is not an inability but a block -- a viable seed, adequately
supplied and at a workable temperature, that does not germinate because something is holding it down.
Remove the block in a laboratory and the seed goes within hours, which is the demonstration that
everything else was ready the entire time. Once dormancy is read as an activity rather than an absence,
the rest follows.

The boundary with what khai already carries is clean. Matter losing the order a maker imposed on it is
**decay**'s. Decay pointed at an outcome somebody wants is **fermentation**'s. Fire, and the cone that
opens in it, are **combustion**'s, whose serotiny member reads the release of seed by heat where this
engine reads what the seed does about it afterwards. Degradation through use is **wear**'s, and the
ground the plant then stands in is the world's.

**The pack** is provisioned once, by a parent who cannot see the conditions. **The embryo** waits
without resting: metabolism is close to zero and not zero, damage accumulates the whole time, and repair
cannot begin until water returns. **The store** is spent on a distance nobody measured in advance, which
is why seed size sets how deep a species can afford to start. **The coat** protects the seed from
everything including its own future, since the same seal that permits a forty-year wait has to be
destroyed from outside for anything to happen.

**The hold** keeps the seed alive by keeping it from living. **The refusal** is maintained rather than
passive, tuneable, and set at different strengths in different seeds from one plant. **The clock unset**
forces a forecast out of a thermometer: a warm wet week in autumn is indistinguishable from a warm wet
week in spring on the evidence available, which is the exact error the hold exists to prevent. **The
bank** buys the line a future by spending nearly all of its members, and the hedge is only visible from
the population, which no seed occupies.

**The cue** reads a reliable stand-in for a fact the seed has no access to. A completed cold period
certifies that a winter happened. Smoke chemistry in the first rain certifies that a canopy burnt and
the fire is over. Red light certifies a workable depth, and the ratio of red to far-red certifies that
nothing is overhead. Each is expensive to counterfeit, which is what makes it trustworthy -- and each
holds only as long as the correlation that made it.

Hence the **twist**, at the root. **A seed spends nearly all of its life refusing to grow, and the one
time it agrees there is no way back.** Water uptake looks identical on both sides of the point of
return; past it, desiccation is death rather than a return to storage. The store is spent reaching
light, there is no dormancy available part-way, and nothing about the cue guaranteed the season would
hold.

The engine is built so that nothing has to have malfunctioned for any of it to run, and a play should
keep it that way. Nothing failed. The cue was read correctly. The seed did exactly what it is built to
do.

**On the evidence, stated plainly.** This engine sits on well-established plant physiology, so the
caveats are about how much a single shape can carry and about the vocabulary it is written in. **The
mechanism is measured, not argued.** That dormancy is an enforced block, that it is released by specific
cues, and that imbibition has a reversible phase and an irreversible one are standard findings with
laboratory and field support, and the engine is on firm ground for all of them. **Species variation is
enormous and the engine flattens it.** The reason the reference works in this field are book-length
classification systems is that physical, physiological, morphological, and combinational dormancy are
different mechanisms with different releases; a single four-movement shape is a convenience, and many
species have no dormancy worth the name and germinate on water alone. **The seed-bank hedge is an
evolutionary interpretation.** That variable dormancy within a cohort is bet-hedging against variable
years is well modelled and supported in several systems, and it is a reading of why the variation
persists rather than a description of what any seed is doing. **The cue accounts are strongest where
they are most specific.** The smoke chemistry is exceptionally well characterised, down to named
compounds at very low concentrations; the light and cold requirements are equally solid; the general
claim that cues are proxies for unobservable futures is the engine's framing rather than a finding.
**The engine's vocabulary is deliberately wrong.** Refusing, agreeing, declining, and deciding are the
khai grammar, and no seed does any of them -- there is no decision anywhere in this process, only a
mechanism whose behaviour is well described by those words and not produced by anything resembling
them. A reader who takes the language literally will get the biology wrong. **This is not a horticulture
guide.** Stratification periods, scarification methods, and sowing depths are species-specific and
belong to the practice. What should not be taken from this engine is that dormancy is a failure to
grow, that a seed is inert, or that germination is a beginning. The claim is narrow: the pack is fixed
before the conditions are known, the refusal is the principal activity, the cue certifies something
adjacent, and the commitment is one-way.

## Restrictions

What the engine refuses to model, and to whom it delegates.

- **Matter losing its imposed order (decay, erosion, palimpsest)**: Rot, rust, crumble, and the slow
  undoing of what a maker arranged are those engines'. A seed's ageing in the bank is read here only as
  the cost of waiting.
- **Directed spoilage (fermentation)**: Decay aimed at an outcome somebody wants, and the conditions
  that rig a microbial contest, are that engine's. The two share a lane and not a mechanism.
- **Fire and what it opens (combustion, fire)**: The burn itself, the serotinous cone, and the
  behaviour of the flame front are combustion's. The smoke form reads only the residue arriving in
  water afterwards.
- **The plant after the first leaf (the world)**: Growth, competition, flowering, and the setting of
  the next generation of seed are outside this engine, which ends where dormancy stops being available.
- **The ground and the season (the world, weather, climate)**: Soil chemistry, rainfall, and the
  climate that set the correlations the cues rely on are those engines' and the world's. They supply
  the conditions; this engine reads what the seed requires of them.
- **The practice (the world)**: Stratification schedules, scarification, sowing depth, storage
  protocols, and germination testing are matters of horticulture and agronomy. They set the format;
  this engine reads the mechanism.
- **A seed as authored symbol (the play's pitch)**: A germination staged in order to **stand for** a
  theme -- the sprout that mirrors a motif of hope -- is refused: that is authored key, the play's
  pitch. Germination sets what was required and what it cost; the meaning is the play's.
