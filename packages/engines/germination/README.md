# Germination

Germination as process: a whole organism held in suspension, and the conditions under which it stops being held, read on what was required rather than on what grew -- not matter losing the order imposed on it, which is decay's, and not decay pointed somewhere, which is fermentation's -- across four movements: the pack (an embryo that waits without resting, a store spent on a distance nobody measured in advance, and a coat that protects the seed from everything including its own future), the hold (a refusal the seed is doing rather than having done to it, a clock unset that forces a forecast out of a thermometer, and a bank that buys the line a future by spending nearly all of its members), the cue (a cold passed that certifies a season by requiring one to have happened, smoke that is a precise signal for a brief opening, and light read as a depth gauge built out of a pigment), and the commit (an imbibition that looks the same on both sides of the point of no return, one shot at a distance the store was set before anybody measured, and an after that is the end of every option the seed had) -- with the twist that a seed spends nearly all of its life refusing to grow, and the one time it agrees there is no way back.
