# Process: The Cue

## Taxonomy

[Germination](process_germination.md), the specific unlocking.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: germination

## Initiated by

Something specific arrives and the block comes off.
The Cue is whichever one this species answers: [a cold passed](process_the_cold_passed.md), [smoke](process_the_smoke.md) in the water, or [light read](process_the_light_read.md) at the right wavelength.

## Direction

Toward signals chosen because they certify something the seed cannot see.
Each cue is a proxy for a condition that matters and cannot be measured directly. A completed cold period certifies that a winter has happened, so the warmth now arriving is spring rather than a mild November. Smoke chemistry certifies that a canopy has burnt, so there is light and bare ground. Red light certifies proximity to the surface, and a particular ratio of red to far-red certifies that no leaf is between the seed and the sky.
None of these is the thing the seed needs. Each is a reliable correlate of it.

## Lever

The precision of the signal against its status as a stand-in.
The carrying force is that the cues are remarkably well chosen and hard to counterfeit: a winter cannot be faked by a cold night, smoke chemistry is not produced by anything except combustion, and the red to far-red ratio is a direct physical consequence of leaves being overhead -- these are expensive signals to imitate and are therefore trustworthy; the resistance is that a proxy holds only as long as the world that made it holds, so a seed moved to another hemisphere, a fire regime that stops, or a warming winter can break the correlation while the mechanism goes on working perfectly.
The Cue reads a reliable stand-in for a fact it has no access to.

## Echo

A block released by a signal about something else.
It is the movement that decides which seeds in a bank wake this year.
The Cue is dated to the event that supplies it and works within days.
