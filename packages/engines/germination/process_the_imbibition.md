# Process: The Imbibition

## Taxonomy

[The Commit](process_commit_germination.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: germination

## Initiated by

The seed takes up water.
Fast at first, then hardly at all, then fast again.

## Direction

Toward a threshold hidden inside an ordinary-looking process.
The first phase is physics: dry tissue draws water in and would give it back if the surroundings dried. Membranes reassemble, damage from the years in storage begins to be repaired, and the enzymes restart. Through all of this the seed can still be dried and returned to storage without loss.
The third phase is different in kind. Cell expansion begins, the root pushes through the coat, and there is no longer a dry state to go back to.

## Lever

The reversibility of the early phases against the invisibility of where they stop.
The carrying force is that the staged design is protective and does real work: a seed wetted by a shower and dried by the afternoon loses nothing, the repair accomplished during the plateau improves its chances substantially, and the arrangement lets a seed sample the conditions before it is committed; the resistance is that the boundary between sampling and committing is not marked and is not decided by the seed, so a longer wet spell simply carries it across, and nothing in the seed announces that the crossing has happened.
The Imbibition looks the same on both sides of the point of no return.

## Echo

A threshold passed without an event.
It shows why the commitment cannot be described as a decision.
The Imbibition is dated to the rain and completes in hours or days.
