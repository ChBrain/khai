# Process: The Embryo

## Taxonomy

[The Pack](process_pack_germination.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: germination

## Initiated by

The plant is already there.
Root tip, shoot tip, and the first leaves, folded and dried and waiting.

## Direction

Toward an organism that is alive, complete, and doing almost nothing.
Metabolism in a dry seed is not zero and it is very close to it: repair continues at a crawl, damage accumulates slowly, and nothing grows. The tissue is neither dead nor active, which is a state very little else in biology occupies.
It can hold this for a season, a decade, or in some species for centuries, and it is the same individual at the end of it.

## Lever

The durability of the held state against the damage it cannot outrun.
The carrying force is that suspension is a genuine solution to time and nothing else does it as well: the embryo survives freezing, drought, and transport that no seedling would live through, and the trick is simply the removal of water; the resistance is that the removal of water does not stop chemistry, only slow it, so the membranes and the genetic material take damage the whole time and the repair cannot start until the water comes back.
The Embryo waits without resting.

## Echo

A whole plant, viable, and not growing.
It shows that dormancy is a condition of something living rather than an absence of life.
The Embryo is dated to the seed's making and ages the entire time.
