# Process: The Pack

## Taxonomy

[Germination](process_germination.md), what the seed carries.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: germination

## Initiated by

The seed leaves the parent with everything it is going to get.
The Pack is three things sealed together: [an embryo](process_the_embryo.md) already built, [a store](process_the_store.md) that is finite, and [a coat](process_the_coat.md) that protects and locks.

## Direction

Toward a complete organism packed for an unknown wait.
A seed is not a starting point. It is a plant that has already been made -- root, shoot, and first leaves present in miniature -- and then stopped, dried to a few per cent water, and closed.
Everything after this point is the same organism resuming, which is why the vocabulary of beginning fits it so badly.

## Lever

The completeness of what is packed against the fact that nothing can be added to it.
The carrying force is that the packing is extraordinarily good and is what makes the whole strategy possible: a seed can survive desiccation that would kill any other stage, it can travel in a gut or on a boot, it can sit for decades, and it carries its own first meal; the resistance is that the pack is closed, so every resource the seedling will have until it reaches light was decided by the parent plant in a season that may bear no relation to the one it wakes into.
The Pack is provisioned once, by somebody who cannot see the conditions.

## Echo

A finished organism in a sealed container.
It shows why the engine reads a resumption rather than a birth.
The Pack is dated to the parent's season and does not change afterwards.
