# Process: The Hold

## Taxonomy

[Germination](process_germination.md), the refusal that is most of its life.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: germination

## Initiated by

Conditions are adequate and nothing happens.
The Hold is why: [a refusal](process_the_refusal.md) that is maintained rather than passive, [a clock unset](process_the_clock_unset.md) with no access to the date, and [a bank](process_the_bank.md) of thousands doing the same thing at different rates.

## Direction

Toward an active suppression of something the seed is otherwise ready to do.
Dormancy is not an inability. A dormant seed is viable, hydrated enough, warm enough, and does not germinate, because the germination is being held down -- by hormonal balance, by an impermeable coat, by an embryo that is not finished, or by several of these at once.
Remove the block and the seed goes immediately, which is the demonstration that everything else was ready the whole time.

## Lever

The protection of not starting against the cost of a wait that is never free.
The carrying force is that the hold is the single most valuable thing the seed does: without it a warm October would empty a population into a killing frost, a single good week would commit every seed a plant ever made, and the strategy of surviving bad years would not exist; the resistance is that waiting has a price paid continuously -- the store degrades, the coat is breached by things that are not cues, predators find it, and the proportion of a bank that is still viable falls every year.
The Hold keeps the seed alive by keeping it from living.

## Echo

A viable organism declining adequate conditions.
It is the movement the seed spends almost its entire existence inside.
The Hold is dated from the seed's making and ends for most seeds only by their death.
