# Process: The Store

## Taxonomy

[The Pack](process_pack_germination.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: germination

## Initiated by

There is a fixed quantity of food.
Starch, oil, protein, packed around or inside the embryo, and no more coming.

## Direction

Toward a single budget that has to cover the whole journey to the surface.
The store buys one thing: the distance between where the seed is and the first light its own leaves can use. Everything before that -- root down, shoot up, cotyledons out -- is spent from reserves, and the account is closed when they are gone.
The size of the store therefore sets how deep a seed can afford to be, which is why small seeds must be near the surface and large ones can start further down.

## Lever

The sufficiency of the ration against the fact that it cannot be topped up.
The carrying force is that the provisioning is well matched to the strategy and mostly adequate: a seed's reserves are sized to the depth and conditions its species usually meets, the margin is real, and most failures are not failures of supply; the resistance is that the budget is fixed before any of the conditions are known, so a seed that has to travel further than its store allows dies with the shoot still underground and nothing available to change course with.
The Store is spent on a distance nobody measured in advance.

## Echo

A ration for a journey of unknown length.
It shows what the commitment is actually risking.
The Store is dated to the parent and is drawn down in days.
