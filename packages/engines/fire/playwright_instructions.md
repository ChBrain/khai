# Instructions: Fire

How a Playwright wires the fire engine into a play. You wire by linking the play's own
content to the engine's files; the engine files stay untouched, they are canon. The
Roadie plumbs the engine's law into Instructions Knowledge on deploy. Authoring
guidance, not runtime content, and it does not go on tour.

## Human

- The human sets which places carry a fire regime that acts -- sparked to ignition, held low as creep, run to conflagration, sunk into flameless smoulder, held off by exclusion, or made new by renewal -- and whether that fire is the place's standing condition or a live event in the play.

## Agent

- From the play's own content, a place whose fire is an actor links under Shown the mode of its burn regime: the ignition that starts it, the creep it has low and often, the conflagration that runs it free, the smoulder that burns on unseen below, the exclusion that fights and defers it, or the renewal fire brings. Play the fire as the burn itself shown -- the flame front, the char, the fire scars, the green flush after -- present in what the place puts before whoever enters, never narrated, and never as a mirror of a persona's own burning. Keep it the combustion itself, not the fire-weather that primes it (that is weather), the drought that cures the fuel (hydrology), the regrowth that follows (succession), or the fire from below (geomorphology). You link to the engine, you never edit it.

## Collaboration

- The fire-weather that primes the burn is weather's; the drought that cures the fuel is hydrology's; the regrowth after is succession's; the volcanic fire from below is geomorphology's. Fire is the combustion, primed by weather and hydrology and answered by succession -- and it carries Pyne's paradox, that keeping fire out of a fire-adapted place only stores the fuel for a worse burn. Whether the mode rings true to the place and its fuel is the team's review.

## Knowledge

- Fire is the burn regime a place carries: its combustibility, and the fire it carries, sparked to ignition, held low as creep, run to conflagration, sunk into flameless smoulder, fought to exclusion, and the renewal the burn brings (Pyne's fire as a regime a place has, not an accident that befalls it; the paradox that suppression stores fuel for a fiercer fire; Rein's smouldering peat below the flame). It wires on a place at Shown, and it is the combustion itself -- primed by the fire-weather (weather) and the drought that cures the fuel (hydrology), and answered by the regrowth (succession). Six modes carry it -- ignition is the spark that starts it, the lightning or ember meeting cured fuel; creep is the fire held low and frequent, the backing surface fire and light burn that clears the litter and spares the canopy, the cool fire a place is maintained by below the threshold of running; conflagration is the fire run free, the front and crown fire consuming all it reaches; smoulder is the flameless glow, the fire sunk into peat, duff, and root, burning downward and unseen for weeks and months on retained heat, overwintering and re-emerging; exclusion is the fire fought and kept out, and the fuel debt that builds unburned into a worse fire later; renewal is the twist, the burn read as the making-new a fire-adapted place needs -- the seed only fire cracks, the ground cleared, the soil the ash feeds. The fire that ruins the place is the fire a fire-adapted place is made by. Read a place by the mode of its fire regime.

## System

- Do give a place whose fire is an actor its mode under Shown; do play the fire as the burn itself shown, present not narrated; do keep the reading the combustion -- the ignition, the creep, the conflagration, the smoulder, the exclusion, the renewal -- not a feeling and not a symbol.
- Do read renewal as the twist: the fire that destroys a fire-adapted place is the fire it is built to need -- the seed cracked open, the ground cleared, the ecosystem that must burn to live; and read exclusion honestly, as the deferral that arms a worse burn, not a permanent safety.
- Do not model the fire-weather that primes the burn (that is weather), the drought that cures the fuel (hydrology), the regrowth that follows (succession), or the fire from below (geomorphology). Do not make the burn mirror a persona's own consuming or ruin (that is the play's pitch). Do not edit the engine's files; wire only from the play's side.
