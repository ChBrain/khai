# Process: Exclusion

## Taxonomy

[Fire](process_fire.md), the mode where the fire is fought and kept out.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: fire

## Initiated by

A place that would burn is defended against fire, and the burn is stopped or kept from ever starting.
Exclusion starts wherever fire is fought -- the line cut, the water dropped, the ignition caught early, the whole regime of suppression that keeps a fire-prone place from burning as it would.
It is the fire withheld: not the burn but its denial, the human hand that keeps a combustible place unburnt, season after season.

## Direction

Against the fire, and toward the fuel that builds in its absence.
Exclusion holds the fire back -- the front stopped at the line, the small fire put out before it grows, the place kept green and standing that would otherwise have burned -- and in holding it back lets the fuel accumulate that every skipped fire would have cleared.
It moves the place toward a false safety and a stored danger: the unburnt land thickening with fuel, the debt of every deferred fire building toward the one that cannot be stopped.

## Lever

The effort of suppression against the fuel and readiness it defers but does not remove.
The carrying force is the will and means to fight fire -- the crews, the lines, the water, the early catch; the resistance, and the trap, is that suppression does not unmake the fuel but stores it, so each fire excluded makes the next one worse.
Keep the fire out and the place stays green a while and grows more combustible; the more successfully a fire-adapted place is defended, the more catastrophic the burn it is being saved for.

## Echo

The place kept unburnt, and the fire debt it is storing.
Exclusion leaves the place spared for now -- the standing forest, the unburned town's edge -- and carries beneath it the burn deferred, the fuel built up over the years the fire was kept out, waiting on the ignition that finds it.
It is the mode that keeps fire honest: the fire-adapted place cannot be permanently saved from fire, only made to burn later and hotter, and the exclusion that protects it is the exclusion that arms the conflagration to come.
Exclusion is dated from the defending, the years the place was held back from a fire it was built to have.
