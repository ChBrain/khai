# Process: Conflagration

## Taxonomy

[Fire](process_fire.md), the mode where the fire runs free.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: fire

## Initiated by

The fire finds fuel, wind, and slope enough to carry itself, and it runs beyond stopping.
Conflagration starts where a caught fire crosses from something that can be beaten out to something that makes its own weather -- the front broad and fast, the crown fire leaping tree to tree, the firestorm drawing its own wind, the burn no line can hold.
It is the fire at full power: not the spark but the sheet of flame, consuming everything combustible in its path.

## Direction

Outward on the wind and up the slope, taking all the fuel it reaches.
Conflagration spreads as a front -- driven by the wind, racing uphill, throwing embers ahead to start new fires, the flame front tall enough to kill the canopy and everything beneath.
It moves the place to ash and char: the forest crowned out, the town in the fire's path taken house by house, the whole watershed blackened, the fire stopped only when it runs out of fuel, weather, or ground to burn.

## Lever

The fuel, wind, and slope driving the fire against everything that would starve or stop it.
The carrying force is the fire feeding itself -- the heat drying the fuel ahead of the front, the wind driving it, the slope pulling it up, the embers seeding it beyond any line; the resistance is the break that starves it -- the cleared line, the rock, the river, the rain, the fuel simply spent.
Past a certain size and heat the place cannot temper it at all: the fire makes its own conditions, and only the running-out of what feeds it brings it down.

## Echo

The place burned through, blackened and spent.
Conflagration leaves the place consumed -- the canopy gone, the ground char, the standing things down, the fire spent where the fuel or the weather failed it -- and hands the cleared and ashed ground to the renewal that follows and the life that returns to it.
It is the fire at its most ruinous and, on the fire-adapted land, its most necessary: the total burn that levels everything is the burn the sequoia and the chaparral are built to meet and need.
Conflagration is dated from the run, the stretch when the fire was beyond any hand to hold.
