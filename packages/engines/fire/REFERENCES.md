# Fire: Reference

## Line of Work

Fire as **process**: the burn regime of a place -- its combustibility, and the fire it
carries, sparked to ignition, run to conflagration, fought to exclusion, and the renewal
the burn brings -- shown in the burned and the burnable before anyone names it. It wires
on a place at Shown, the place's physical self-presentation, because a fire-prone place
puts its fire regime in front of whoever enters: the char, the fuel, the fire scars, the
green flush after. The domain does not model the fire-weather that primes the burn -- the
heat, the wind, the low humidity -- which is the weather engine's; nor the drought that
cures the fuel, which is hydrology's; nor the biological regrowth that follows, which is
succession's; nor the fire from below, the eruption, which is geomorphology's. It models
the combustion itself as a regime a place lives within, and reads it two ways: as the
ruin that consumes the place, and as the renewal a fire-adapted place cannot do without.

The spine is Pyne's argument that fire is not an accident that befalls a place but a
regime a place has -- and that the modern attempt to exclude it has made fire worse, not
gone. Pyne read the whole history of humanity's relationship with fire, and named the
central paradox: that suppressing fire in a fire-adapted land does not remove it but
stores its fuel for a fiercer burn. Christensen made the ecology plain -- the systems
that require fire, the seeds that need it, the succession it resets. And Davis read the
politics of the burn, the built place that insists on standing where the fire regime
guarantees it will burn. So the engine gives fire three modes of the burn -- ignition
that starts it, conflagration that runs it, exclusion that fights and defers it -- and
keeps a fourth, renewal, the same fire read as the making-new a fire-adapted place is
built around.

## Restrictions

What the engine refuses to model, and to whom it delegates.

- **The fire-weather that primes the burn (weather)**: The heat, the wind, the low
  humidity, the dry lightning that make a place ready to burn are the weather engine's;
  fire is the combustion that readiness allows. Weather sets the fire weather in the hour;
  fire is the burn it enables. The Santa Ana wind is weather's; the fire it drives is fire's.
- **The drought that cures the fuel (hydrology)**: The long dry that turns the living fuel
  to tinder is hydrology's -- the deficit in the water regime; fire is what that cured fuel
  then does when lit. Drought readies the fuel; fire burns it. The two meet in the fire
  season, but the dry is hydrology's and the flame is fire's.
- **The regrowth that follows (succession)**: After the fire clears the ground, the
  sequence of life returning -- the pioneers, the flush, the forest rebuilt -- is
  succession's. Fire's renewal mode reads the burn _as_ the trigger and the clearing the
  regrowth needs; the returning life itself is succession's. Fire resets the sequence;
  succession is the sequence that then runs.
- **The fire from below (geomorphology)**: The volcanic fire -- magma, the eruption -- is
  geomorphology's, the ground's own violence from within. Fire is the combustion of fuel on
  the surface. An eruption may ignite a wildfire, but the magma is geomorphology's and the
  burning forest is fire's.
- **A persona's feeling, and the burn as authored symbol**: How the fire makes a persona
  feel -- the terror of the front, the awe of the burn -- is the persona's own engines';
  fire delivers the combustion, not the feeling. And the fire made to **stand for** a
  persona's own burning, the wildfire that mirrors a consuming passion or a razed life, is
  refused: that is authored key, the play's pitch, not the force on the place.
- **The mode itself is not engine content**: The engine declares the four modes exist;
  which one a given place shows lives on that place, linked under its Shown chapter. The
  engine refuses to fix a place's fire for it.
