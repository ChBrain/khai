# Process: Creep

## Taxonomy

[Fire](process_fire.md), the mode where the fire stays low and frequent, below the threshold of running.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: fire

## Initiated by

The fire burns but does not take off -- it stays low, cool, and beatable, and the place has it often and lightly.
Creep starts wherever a place carries a low-intensity fire as its baseline -- the surface fire backing against the wind, the flame creeping through the cured grass, the light burn that runs the litter and spares the canopy, the frequent small fire that never crosses into the run.
It is the fire kept below the threshold: not the spark and not the conflagration, but the low, slow, self-limiting burn a fire-adapted place lives with as a matter of course.

## Direction

Low across the surface, and toward the fuel it keeps from ever building.
Creep works the fine surface fuels and stops there -- flame short, front slow, residence long enough to consume the litter but too cool to kill the standing trees or leap to the crowns -- so the place burns lightly and often rather than rarely and total.
It moves the place toward being maintained rather than reset: the savanna kept open, the prairie greening richer for the frequent burn, the understory cleared of the load that a rarer fire would have made catastrophic -- the light fire doing continuously what the total fire would otherwise have to do all at once.

## Lever

The frequent light burn against the fuel and the threshold it keeps the place below.
The carrying force is that a low-intensity fire, had often, consumes the fine fuel before it accumulates -- so each light burn is the reason the next one stays light, and the place is held in a regime of cool frequent fire; the resistance is the same threshold conflagration crosses, held down only while the fuel stays low and the burn stays cool, and lost the moment the load or the weather lets a creep become a run.
Let the place burn light and often and it maintains itself, self-limiting; stop the frequent fire and the fuel builds until the next fire is no creep at all.

## Echo

The place kept, not consumed, by the fire it has low and often.
Creep leaves the place maintained -- the litter cleared, the canopy spared, the fuel never let to build -- and hands that lightened ground back to the regime of frequent cool fire that keeps it, and forward to the conflagration it becomes when the light fire is withheld.
It is the other half of the burn regime the engine names: where conflagration is the rare total fire that resets a place, creep is the frequent light fire that maintains it -- and the fire a place is denied in exclusion is, most often, exactly this one.
Creep is dated from the low burn, the light and frequent fire the place is held in and kept by.
