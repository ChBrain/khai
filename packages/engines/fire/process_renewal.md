# Process: Renewal

## Taxonomy

[Fire](process_fire.md), the mode where the burn is read as the place made new.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: fire

## Initiated by

The fire passes, and the ground it cleared and the ash it left become the making of what grows next.
Renewal starts wherever the burn is not only loss but the trigger a fire-adapted place needs -- the serotinous cone that only fire's heat cracks open, the seed that germinates in smoke, the ground cleared of the old growth that shaded out the new, the soil the ash enriches.
It is fire read the other way: not the place destroyed but the place reset, the burn the ecosystem is built around and cannot live without.

## Direction

Toward the flush of new growth on the cleared and fertilized ground.
Renewal turns the char to seedbed -- the fireweed and the morel in the ash, the aspen and the lodgepole rising thick where the fire opened the canopy, the prairie greening richer for the burn, the meadow kept open only because it burns.
It moves the place from consumed to renewed: the old cleared, the seeds released, the nutrients returned, the fire-dependent life doing exactly what the fire is for.

## Lever

The renewal the fire confers against the reflex to read every burn as only ruin.
The carrying force is that fire, in the fire-adapted place, is regeneration -- unfakeable, earned only by the burn, the release and the clearing and the enrichment that nothing else provides; the resistance is the impulse to prevent all fire, which starves the very land that needs it and, kept up, dooms it to the catastrophic burn instead of the renewing one.
Renewal rides the knife-edge the whole engine names: the fire that is the place's ruin is, on the land built for it, the place's renewal.

## Echo

The place made new by the fire that burned it.
It shows a place regenerating -- the green flush, the released seed, the opened canopy, the meadow kept -- and carries beneath it the land's-forces twist, the sibling of the family's ambivalence: that fire is not only the place's destruction but, on the land adapted to it, the making it depends on.
Renewal is dated from the burn it rose out of, and it is the mode where fire stops being only the enemy of the place and becomes the thing the place is built to need.
