# Process: Ignition

## Taxonomy

[Fire](process_fire.md), the mode where the fire starts.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: fire

## Initiated by

A source of heat meets fuel dry enough to catch, and the fire begins.
Ignition starts at the spark and the first flame -- the lightning strike, the ember on the wind, the dropped match, the spark off the rail, the coal that never went out -- finding fuel cured and air enough to take hold.
It is the threshold mode: before it the place is only combustible, after it the place is alight, and everything the fire does follows from this first catch.

## Direction

From the point of the spark, spreading to the fuel around it.
Ignition finds the fine, dry fuel first -- the grass, the needle-litter, the bark -- and builds from the smoulder to the flame that can carry itself, reaching for the heavier fuel and the wind that will run it.
It moves the place from unlit to lit: the single point of flame that, given fuel and air, becomes a front; or the spark that finds nothing and dies where it fell.

## Lever

The heat of the source against the fuel's readiness to catch.
The carrying force is the ignition source and the state of the fuel it meets -- the dryness, the fineness, the arrangement that lets a spark become a flame; the resistance is anything that denies the catch -- the damp fuel, the green wood, the bare ground, the spark that lands where nothing will take.
Dry the fuel and lay it fine and the least spark takes; wet it, or clear it, and the same lightning strikes cold.

## Echo

The place lit, or the spark gone cold.
Ignition leaves the place with a fire started -- the first flame that will run to conflagration if the fuel and wind allow, or the smoulder that spreads slow, or the strike that found no purchase and died.
It is the mode that decides whether there is a fire at all: the spark is common, but the catch needs the fuel ready, and the readiness is the place's, built by the drought and the seasons and the fuel let stand.
Ignition is dated from the catch, the moment the combustible place became a burning one.
