# Position: Narcissism

## Taxonomy

Personality psychology / the entitled self-importance and its self-regulation.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: narcissism

## Has

An inflated sense of self-importance the persona holds and must keep held.
A felt entitlement -- to admiration, to special treatment, to being the exception -- and a low, ready empathy for what that costs others.
Beneath the height, a fragility: a self-image that reality keeps threatening, so the whole standing is organised around defending it.

## Orders

Everything is read for what it does to the inflated self.
Praise, slight, rival, and audience are all weighed by a single question -- does this confirm or threaten my grandiosity? -- and the persona's attention, warmth, and aggression are apportioned by that answer.
The disposition subordinates the ordinary business of relating to the standing task of maintaining the self-image, so others become suppliers, mirrors, and threats before they are anything else.

## Loses

The option of a self that could rest.
Because the grandiosity is inflated past what reality will confirm, it cannot be set down and left safe; it must be continually propped, defended, and re-won, and the persona discovers there is no ground on which the self is simply, quietly enough.
What is lost is repose: the entitled self is a debt that must be serviced without end.

## Drives

On the loss of that repose:
runs the self-regulation that holds the inflated self up -- seeking admiration to feed it, turning rivalry on what threatens it, and flaring into injury when it is punctured -- across whichever type the persona is.
The type sets the face and the aim: grandiose (overt dominance), vulnerable (covert fragility), communal (grandiosity through virtue), or malignant (grandiosity fused with antagonism); each runs the same defence toward a different end. The comparison of narcissism against the aversive triad's other traits is the dark-triad engine's; the resting global self-attitude is the self-esteem engine's; the discrete emotion of hubristic pride is the pride engine's. This engine owns the entitled self-importance and the work of defending it -- its types, their aims, and the self-regulation they share.
