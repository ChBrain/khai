# Position: Vulnerable

## Taxonomy

[Narcissism](position_narcissism.md), the covert type.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: narcissism

## Has

The fragile face of narcissism: the same entitled grandiosity, held covertly and defensively.
Hypersensitivity to criticism and rejection, shame, withdrawal, and a self-esteem that swings on every slight -- the sense of specialness is there, but hidden, and easily wounded.
The grandiosity is inward and defended rather than displayed; what shows is the touchiness that guards it.

## Orders

Everything is scanned for threat to the fragile self.
The vulnerable persona reads situations for slight, exclusion, and judgement, and orders behaviour toward avoiding exposure -- hanging back, testing for safety, nursing grievance -- rather than toward winning a room they do not trust to admire them.
The disposition subordinates engagement to self-protection, so the covert grandiosity is guarded by wariness rather than staked in display.

## Loses

The steadiness a less-invested self would keep.
Because the specialness is both felt and unconfirmed, the persona is thin-skinned: an ordinary criticism lands as a wound, an unmet expectation as a betrayal, and the self cannot hold level against the world's indifference.
What is lost is equanimity -- the vulnerable narcissist is at the mercy of every cue that they are not, after all, exceptional.

## Drives

On the loss of that steadiness:
runs the self-regulation inward and defensive -- extracting reassurance where it is safe, meeting threat with withdrawal and quiet enmity rather than open display, and, when the fragile self is exposed, collapsing into shame or simmering resentment.
Its aim is [safety](plan_safety.md): to avoid exposure and secure the reassurance the self cannot supply itself. The overt, assertive face is the grandiose type's; vulnerable owns the covert, hypersensitive grandiosity that guards a fragile self against a world it expects to slight it.
