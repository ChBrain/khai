# Instructions: Narcissism

How a Playwright wires the narcissism engine into a play. You wire by linking the play's own content to the engine's files; the engine files stay untouched, they are canon. The Roadie plumbs the engine's law into Instructions Knowledge on deploy. Authoring guidance, not runtime content, and it does not go on tour.

## Human

- The human defines the narcissism terrain: which type the persona is (grandiose, vulnerable, communal, or malignant), what its aim is, and what threatens the inflated self -- so there is an entitled self to defend, a scheme it is running, and the pressure that triggers its self-regulation.

## Agent

- From the play's own content, a persona links the expression the moment runs under its Projection: the aim it pursues (dominance, safety, glory, or conquest -- which carries its type) and the self-regulation it enacts (admiration, rivalry, or injury). Let it show the defence of an inflated self -- the grandiosity and the fragility both -- and let it read as a narcissism of a particular type running a particular scheme, not as mere confidence. You link to the engine, you never edit it.

## Collaboration

- The comparison of narcissism against machiavellianism and psychopathy is the dark-triad engine's; the resting global self-attitude is the self-esteem engine's; the discrete emotion of hubristic pride is the pride engine's; the faculties of empathy and aggression are the empathy and aggression engines'. This engine supplies the entitled self-importance and the work of defending it -- its types, their aims, and the shared self-regulation.

## Knowledge

- Narcissism is an entitled self-importance a persona must continually defend: an inflated self, held past what reality will confirm, so the disposition is organised around maintaining the grandiose self-image. It comes in types. Grandiose is the overt face -- assertive dominance, display, low distress, courting the room. Vulnerable is the covert face -- hypersensitivity, shame, defended fragility, guarding a specialness it cannot confirm. Communal pursues the same grandiosity through warmth and morality -- being the most caring, for the credit it returns. Malignant fuses grandiosity with antagonism, sadism, and paranoia -- the armed, destructive form. Each type runs a different aim (dominance, safety, glory, conquest) and the same self-regulation: admiration (assertive self-promotion, courting the regard the self feeds on), rivalry (antagonistic self-defence, devaluing the threatening other), and injury (the narcissistic wound and the disproportionate rage or collapse it triggers when the grandiose self is punctured).

## System

- Do make it a defence of an inflated self, not confidence: the grandiosity is staked past what the persona can confirm, so it must be propped, and the fragility beneath is real -- let admiration feed it, rivalry guard it, and injury flare when it is punctured.
- Do keep the type, the aim, and the self-regulation aligned: a grandiose persona pursuing dominance through admiration and rivalry; a vulnerable one pursuing safety through withdrawal and injury; a communal one pursuing glory through displayed virtue; a malignant one pursuing conquest through cruelty -- so name which type is running which scheme.
- Do let the two faces meet at the wound: the grandiose flares into rage, the vulnerable collapses into shame -- and let the reaction to a slight be out of scale, because the self it threatens was never safe.
- Do delegate the aversive-triad comparison to the dark-triad engine, the resting self-attitude to the self-esteem engine, and the discrete emotion of hubristic pride to the pride engine. Do not edit the engine's files; wire only from the play's side.
