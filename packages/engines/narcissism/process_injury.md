# Process: Injury

## Taxonomy

[Narcissism](position_narcissism.md), the wound route.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: narcissism

## Initiated by

The inflated self is punctured -- by a criticism, a slight, a failure, a rival's success -- and the fragile core beneath the grandiosity is exposed.
This is narcissistic injury: the wound that lands when reality contradicts the grandiose self, and the disproportionate reaction it triggers.
The height was never secure; the injury is the moment its insecurity is laid bare.

## Direction

Toward a reaction sized to the fragility, not the offence: rage, or collapse.
Injury reveals the paradox at narcissism's core -- that the grandiose self is also a fragile one -- so a small slight can produce a large response, the persona flaring into narcissistic rage to punish the source and restore the height, or collapsing into shame and withdrawal where the defence fails.
The direction is restoration or ruin: the punctured self must be re-inflated at once, by attacking what deflated it or by retreating from the exposure, because it cannot simply absorb the blow.

## Lever

The threat to the grandiose self, spent as a reaction that defends or drops it.
Injury runs on the gap between the inflated self and the reality that contradicts it: because the self is staked past what it can confirm, a contradiction is not information but a wound, and the persona is driven to close the gap -- by rage that unmakes the threat, or by a shame-collapse that hides the exposed self.
The lever is the fragility beneath the height: the same over-inflation that makes admiration necessary makes injury inevitable, and the reaction is proportioned to the fear the height was hiding.

## Echo

The wound exposes the fragility the grandiosity was built to hide, and the reaction restores or ruins the self.
It is where the two faces meet -- the grandiose flaring into rage, the vulnerable collapsing into shame -- and the reason narcissistic reactions to criticism are so out of scale: the offence is small, but the self it threatens was never safe.
It is [admiration](process_narcissistic_admiration.md) and [rivalry](process_narcissistic_rivalry.md)'s counterpart -- the wounding where they are the building and the defending, and the driver that keeps both running. Every type runs it, the vulnerable most visibly; injury owns the narcissistic wound and the rage or collapse it triggers.
