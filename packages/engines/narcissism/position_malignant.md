# Position: Malignant

## Taxonomy

[Narcissism](position_narcissism.md), the antagonistic-severe type.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: narcissism

## Has

The severe form: grandiosity fused with antagonism, sadism, and paranoia.
To the entitled self-importance are added ego-syntonic aggression -- cruelty the persona feels entitled to and untroubled by -- antisocial disregard, and a suspicious, persecutory reading of others as enemies.
The grandiosity is armed: the self is defended not only by seeking admiration but by dominating, exploiting, and destroying what stands against it.

## Orders

Everything is read as a contest of domination and threat.
The malignant persona orders behaviour toward control and conquest -- exploiting where useful, punishing where crossed, striking pre-emptively at suspected enemies -- and takes an ego-syntonic satisfaction in the power exercised over others.
The disposition subordinates every relation to mastery and safety-through-dominance, so warmth is instrumental and cruelty is licensed.

## Loses

Any brake the ordinary conscience or fear would supply.
Because the aggression is ego-syntonic and the worldview persecutory, the persona feels no self-censure for harm done and reads restraint as weakness, so the checks that would slow the others -- shame, empathy, the wish to be liked -- are turned off or turned to contempt.
What is lost is the limit: the malignant narcissist will go where the grandiose, held by vanity, and the vulnerable, held by fear, will not.

## Drives

On the loss of that limit:
runs the self-regulation at its most destructive -- courting admiration as power, meeting threat with pre-emptive rivalry and calculated cruelty, and answering any wound to the grandiose self with a rage that seeks not just to restore the self but to annihilate the source.
Its aim is [conquest](plan_conquest.md): to subjugate and to destroy what threatens the self. It is grandiose narcissism's severe extension, and its fusion with antagonism, sadism, and paranoia is what sets it apart; malignant owns the armed, destructive defence of the entitled self.
