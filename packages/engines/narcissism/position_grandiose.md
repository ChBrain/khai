# Position: Grandiose

## Taxonomy

[Narcissism](position_narcissism.md), the overt type.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: narcissism

## Has

The loud face of narcissism: overt self-aggrandisement worn with confidence.
Social dominance, exhibitionism, a sense of superiority carried openly, and a low distress about it -- the persona feels, and shows, that they are exceptional.
The grandiosity is on the surface, assertive and approach-oriented, hungry for an audience.

## Orders

Everything is staged for admiration and rank.
The grandiose persona reads the room for where the attention is and moves to hold it, treating situations as arenas to win and others as an audience to impress or a field to dominate.
The disposition orders behaviour toward display, competition, and being seen as the best, subordinating warmth and caution to the standing bid for superiority.

## Loses

The cover the quieter types keep.
Because the grandiosity is overt, it is exposed: staked in public, it can be publicly punctured, and the persona who lives by the room's admiration is at the room's mercy when the admiration fails.
What is lost is deniability -- the grandiose narcissist cannot claim they did not care, having so plainly cared to be seen above.

## Drives

On the loss of that cover:
runs the self-regulation loud and forward -- courting admiration by display and charm, meeting threat with rivalry and open aggression, and, when the superiority is challenged, flaring into narcissistic rage.
Its aim is [dominance](plan_dominance.md): to be admired, superior, and unrivalled. The covert, fragile face is the vulnerable type's; grandiose owns the overt, assertive self-aggrandisement that seeks the room's admiration and rank.
