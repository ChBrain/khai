# Narcissism: Reference

## Line of Work

Narcissism as a **multi-type** engine: the entitled self-importance a persona must continually defend, taken across three altitudes -- the type the persona is (**position**: grandiose, vulnerable, communal, malignant), the aim that type pursues (**plan**: dominance, safety, glory, conquest), and the self-regulation every type runs to hold the inflated self up (**process**: admiration, rivalry, injury). The domain does not model the comparison of narcissism against the aversive triad's other traits, which is the dark-triad engine's; nor the resting global self-attitude a persona carries, which is the self-esteem engine's; nor the discrete emotion of hubristic pride, which is the pride engine's. It models the entitled, inflated self and the work of defending it: its types, their aims, and the shared dynamic they run.

The spine is the social-cognitive and clinical psychology of narcissism. Morf and Rhodewalt's dynamic self-regulatory processing model recast narcissism from a static trait into a process: the overarching goal is to maintain a grandiose self, pursued through intra- and inter-personal strategies and triggered especially by threat. Back and colleagues' Narcissistic Admiration and Rivalry Concept (NARC) split grandiose self-regulation into two routes -- admiration (assertive self-promotion, which wins early social potency) and rivalry (antagonistic self-defence, which brings later social cost). The structure of narcissism resolves into two broad phenotypes -- grandiose and vulnerable -- that Krizan and Herlache's Narcissism Spectrum Model treats as expressions of one entitled core, with communal narcissism (Gebauer et al.) pursuing the same grandiosity through prosocial means and malignant narcissism (Kernberg) fusing it with antagonism, sadism, and paranoia. Narcissistic injury -- the disproportionate wound and rage when the grandiose self is contradicted -- is the fragility the whole structure defends against. The three altitudes are those: types, aims, and self-regulation.

## Restrictions

What the engine refuses to model, and to whom it delegates.

- **Dark triad**: The engine models narcissism in its own depth -- its types, aims, and self-regulation -- not its place beside the other aversive traits. The comparison of narcissism against machiavellianism and psychopathy, the shared cold self-advancement of the triad read as one dispositional core, is owned by the dark-triad engine; this engine owns what narcissism is internally, dark-triad owns where it sits among its dark siblings. The two compose: dark-triad keeps narcissism as one corner of the triad; this engine unfolds that corner.
- **Self-esteem**: The engine models the defence of an inflated, entitled, contingent self, not the resting global attitude toward the self. The persona's durable standing self-regard, positive or negative, is owned by the self-esteem engine; narcissism is not high self-esteem but a self-esteem that is inflated past what it can confirm and so must be continually propped -- a defence, not a level. The engine delegates the resting self-attitude to self-esteem and keeps the defence of the inflated one.
- **Pride**: The engine models the narcissistic syndrome, not the discrete self-conscious emotion. Hubristic pride -- the arrogant "I am superior" that authentic pride is distinguished from -- is owned by the pride engine and is a component narcissism recruits, not the whole; narcissism is the standing entitled self and its self-regulation, of which a flash of hubristic pride is one episode. The engine delegates the discrete emotion to pride.
- **Empathy and Aggression**: The engine models narcissism's low empathy and its rivalry-and-conquest aggression as parts of the self-regulation, not the faculties themselves. The capacity to feel with another is owned by the empathy engine, and the general dynamics of aggression by the aggression engine; narcissism turns empathy low and recruits aggression in service of defending the self, but the faculties are theirs to model. The engine delegates empathy and aggression proper to those engines.

- **[injury](process_injury.md)** (process, shared): The narcissistic wound and the rage or collapse it triggers. Anchored by Pincus & Lukowitsky and Morf & Rhodewalt (threat-reactivity).
