# Process: Rivalry

## Taxonomy

[Narcissism](position_narcissism.md), the self-defence route.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: narcissism

## Initiated by

The persona moves to protect the inflated self by putting others down.
This is the antagonistic, defensive half of narcissistic self-regulation: devaluing rivals, striving for supremacy, and answering threat with hostility, all aimed at keeping the grandiose self above the others who might eclipse it.
Where admiration builds the self up, rivalry guards it by pulling others down.

## Direction

Toward a self kept supreme by the devaluation of those who threaten it.
Rivalry works by antagonistic self-protection: the persona treats others as competitors for a scarce supremacy, and defends the height by derogating, distrusting, and fighting -- quick to see rivals, quick to diminish them.
The direction is against the threat: not courting regard but repelling the persons and comparisons that would deflate the self, so the grandiosity is preserved by the fall of others rather than the rise of the self.

## Lever

The devaluation of the threatening other, spent as the defence of the self's supremacy.
Rivalry runs on the zero-sum reading of standing: because another's height threatens the persona's own, the self is defended by lowering the rival -- through contempt, aggression, and the refusal of any regard that is not owed upward.
The lever is the derogation of the challenger: the self stays supreme not by proving itself but by unseating whoever might outshine it, so the defence is paid in others' diminishment.

## Echo

The self is kept high by keeping others low, and the goodwill the charm won is spent.
It is the route that makes narcissism costly over time -- the antagonism that turns first admiration into conflict, the reason the narcissist who dazzled the room comes to be resented by it -- the supremacy defended at the price of the relationships that supplied it.
It is [admiration](process_admiration.md) and [injury](process_injury.md)'s counterpart -- the defending where they are the building and the wounding. Every type runs it, the malignant most destructively; rivalry owns the antagonistic self-defence by which narcissism guards the inflated self against those who threaten it.
