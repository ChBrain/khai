# Position: Communal

## Taxonomy

[Narcissism](position_narcissism.md), the prosocial-means type.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: narcissism

## Has

The same grandiose self, won through communal rather than agentic means.
The persona is grandiose about their warmth, morality, and helpfulness -- "I am the most caring, the most generous, the best listener" -- pursuing the identical need for admiration and specialness through the domain of virtue.
The self-importance is real and inflated; only its currency is communal, dressed as selflessness.

## Orders

Everything caring is staged for the credit it returns.
The communal persona reads situations for chances to be seen as good, and orders behaviour toward visible generosity, moral display, and being recognised as the most devoted -- help given where it is witnessed, virtue announced, need met in a way that redounds to the giver.
The disposition subordinates the beneficiary to the self-image: the good deed serves the grandiosity that motivates it.

## Loses

The very selflessness it claims.
Because the communal grandiosity needs an audience for its virtue, its care is contingent on credit, and the persona cannot simply be good unwitnessed; the moment the giving stops paying in admiration, the generosity thins.
What is lost is the reality of the virtue the persona most insists on -- the caring is a means, and means run out when the return does.

## Drives

On the loss of that reality:
runs the same self-regulation in a communal key -- courting admiration through displays of goodness, meeting threats to the moral self-image with wounded reproach or righteous rivalry, and flaring when the credit for virtue is denied or a rival claims to be kinder.
Its aim is [glory](plan_glory.md): to be recognised as the best and most virtuous. The overt agentic display is the grandiose type's and the fusion with antagonism the malignant type's; communal owns grandiosity pursued through the currency of warmth and morality.
