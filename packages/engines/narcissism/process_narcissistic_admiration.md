# Process: Admiration

## Taxonomy

[Narcissism](position_narcissism.md), the self-promotion route.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: narcissism

## Initiated by

The persona moves to feed the inflated self by drawing admiration from others.
This is the assertive, approach half of narcissistic self-regulation: charm, self-promotion, grandiose fantasy, and the striving for uniqueness, all aimed at winning the regard that confirms the grandiose self.
The self is maintained by supply, and admiration is the supply sought.

## Direction

Toward a self propped up by the regard of others, courted actively.
Admiration works by assertive self-enhancement: the persona presents themselves as brilliant, special, and worthy, and does so with a real social potency -- narcissists are often liked and admired on first acquaintance, precisely because this route makes them charming and bold.
The direction is outward and forward: not defending the self against a threat but building it up on a stream of regard, the grandiosity fed rather than guarded.

## Lever

The regard of others, courted, spent as the confirmation the inflated self runs on.
Admiration runs on supply: because the grandiose self is inflated past what the persona can confirm alone, it needs others to mirror it, and the persona works to keep the mirrors flattering -- performing, impressing, and steering attention to sustain the flow.
The lever is the self's dependence on external confirmation: the height cannot be held from within, so it is held on the admiration the persona is always, actively, drawing.

## Echo

The self is fed on admiration won, and thins the moment the supply runs dry.
It is the route that makes narcissism attractive before it makes it costly -- the charm that draws the room early -- and the reason the grandiose persona is so bound to their audience: the admiration that feeds the self is a supply that must keep coming.
It is [rivalry](process_narcissistic_rivalry.md) and [injury](process_injury.md)'s counterpart -- the building where they are the defending and the wounding. Every type runs it in its own key; admiration owns the assertive self-promotion by which narcissism courts the regard the inflated self depends on.
