# Plan: Glory

## Taxonomy

A **personal** scheme (a persona): the standing aim a communal narcissist runs. The class, not its owner or its focus.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: narcissism

## Direction

To be recognised as the best and most virtuous. The communal self seeks the same admiration and specialness as the grandiose, but in the currency of warmth and morality -- to be seen as the most caring, the most generous, the most devoted -- so the aim is moral pre-eminence, glory won through goodness witnessed.

## Orders

- Give where it is seen: stage generosity, care, and sacrifice for the credit they return.
- Announce the virtue: make the goodness legible, so the recognition attaches to the self.
- Guard the moral standing against rivals who claim to be kinder, and against any charge that the care is self-serving.

## Implementation

The scheme runs on an audience for virtue: it needs witnesses to the goodness and beneficiaries whose gratitude confirms it, and spends the persona's care as a means to admiration. It is bounded by the self-regulation the type runs -- admiration through goodness, reproach or righteous rivalry when the credit is denied -- and by the contradiction that its care, being contingent on credit, thins when unwitnessed.

## Targets

- [ ] Be recognised as the most caring or virtuous
- [ ] Convert a witnessed good deed into admiration for the self
- [ ] Best a rival who claims greater goodness
- [ ] Secure a moral standing that confirms the self's specialness
