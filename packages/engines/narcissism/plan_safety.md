# Plan: Safety

## Taxonomy

A **personal** scheme (a persona): the standing aim a vulnerable narcissist runs. The class, not its owner or its focus.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: narcissism

## Direction

To avoid exposure and secure the reassurance the self cannot supply. The vulnerable self, holding a specialness it cannot confirm and cannot bear to have denied, aims not to win a room but to survive it -- to dodge the criticism that wounds, and to draw, from safe quarters, the confirmation that steadies the fragile self.

## Orders

- Avoid the arenas where the self could be exposed and judged; hang back, test for safety first.
- Extract reassurance from those judged safe, without staking the specialness openly enough to be refused.
- Guard against slight: read for judgement, withdraw from threat, and nurse the grievance rather than risk the confrontation.

## Implementation

The scheme runs on avoidance and selective disclosure: it needs safe others and low-exposure settings, and spends the persona's vigilance on not being caught ordinary. It is bounded by the self-regulation the type runs -- injury and withdrawal where the grandiose would attack -- and by the same unconfirmable specialness, which makes every ordinary slight a threat to manage.

## Targets

- [ ] Avoid the exposure that would wound the fragile self
- [ ] Secure reassurance from a safe source
- [ ] Deflect or survive the criticism that lands as injury
- [ ] Reach a footing where the self feels, for now, safe from being found ordinary
