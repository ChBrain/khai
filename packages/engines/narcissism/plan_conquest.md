# Plan: Conquest

## Taxonomy

A **personal** scheme (a persona): the standing aim a malignant narcissist runs. The class, not its owner or its focus.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: narcissism

## Direction

To subjugate, and to destroy what threatens the self. Where the grandiose aims to be admired above others, the malignant aims to dominate and, where crossed, to annihilate -- safety sought through mastery, the grandiose self defended by making enemies unable to threaten it. The intent is control secured by power and, against the suspected enemy, destruction.

## Orders

- Dominate the field: exploit where useful, control where possible, and take the ego-syntonic satisfaction of power exercised.
- Strike the threat: move pre-emptively and without compunction against those read as enemies.
- Answer the wound to the self not by restoring it but by destroying its source.

## Implementation

The scheme runs on power and a persecutory field: it needs those it can dominate and those it can cast as enemies, and spends calculated cruelty as freely as charm. It is bounded by the self-regulation the type runs -- rivalry turned to conquest, injury turned to annihilating rage -- and unbounded where the ordinary checks (empathy, shame, fear) that hold the other types are, in this one, switched off.

## Targets

- [ ] Establish dominance over the contested field
- [ ] Neutralise or destroy the suspected enemy
- [ ] Punish the crossing that wounded the self
- [ ] Secure a control no rival can threaten
