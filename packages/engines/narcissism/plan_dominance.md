# Plan: Dominance

## Taxonomy

A **personal** scheme (a persona): the standing aim a grandiose narcissist runs. The class, not its owner or its focus.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: narcissism

## Direction

To be admired, superior, and unrivalled. The grandiose self seeks confirmation of its exceptionality through the room's attention and rank -- to be seen as the best, to win the arena, to have no one above. The intent is the standing bid for superiority that the overt grandiosity is built to press.

## Orders

- Court the audience: charm, display, and self-promotion to draw admiration.
- Take the high ground: compete for status and position openly, treating situations as contests to win.
- Answer any rival who claims the height, and any slight that dims the shine, before it spreads.

## Implementation

The scheme runs on an audience and an arena: it needs onlookers to admire and rivals to best, and spends the persona's charm, energy, and aggression on being seen above. It is bounded by the self-regulation the type runs -- admiration to feed the self, rivalry to defend it -- and by the reality it inflates past, which keeps threatening the height it claims.

## Targets

- [ ] Win the room's admiration
- [ ] Hold visible superiority over rivals
- [ ] Repel the slight or challenge that threatens the height
- [ ] Secure a standing that needs no further proving
