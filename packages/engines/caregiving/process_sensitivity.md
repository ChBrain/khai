# Process: Sensitivity

## Taxonomy

[Caregiving](process_caregiving.md), the attuned form.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: caregiving

## Initiated by

The persona reads the other's need as it actually is -- its signal caught, its size judged, its kind understood -- and answers that, neither more nor less.
This is sensitivity: attuned care, the response fitted to the real need rather than to the caregiver's idea of it.
It is the responsiveness that lets the other feel both held and free.

## Direction

Toward the need the other actually has, answered on its own terms.
Sensitivity tracks the other's signals -- reads the cry, the silence, the turning-away -- and calibrates the care to what they mean, giving support when it is wanted and room when it is not, so the help matches the moment.
The direction is set by the other's state, accurately perceived: the care follows the need rather than leading it or overriding it.

## Lever

The accurate reading of the signal, promptly answered.
Sensitivity spends attention first and provision second: the caregiver perceives the other's cues, interprets them rightly, and responds in time and in measure -- the four beats of noticing, reading, answering, and fitting the answer to the need.
The lever is the fit between signal and response: care that arrives when needed and withdraws when not, so the other learns their need will be met without being managed.

## Echo

The other is held and left free, and the bond is made secure.
It is the care that builds a secure base -- the responsiveness from which trust and, in time, autonomy grow -- because the other comes to know their signals are read and their need will be answered without being seized.
It is [overprotection](process_overprotection.md) with the overshoot corrected and [burnout](process_burnout.md) held off by the care being fitted rather than boundless -- the answer measured to the need, which spends the giver less than care that never stops. The feeling-with beneath it is the empathy engine's; this owns the attuned answering of the need.
