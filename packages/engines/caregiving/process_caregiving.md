# Process: Caregiving

## Taxonomy

Attachment / the behavioral system that answers another's need.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: caregiving

## Initiated by

A persona reads a vulnerable other -- a child, the sick, the old, anyone whose need or distress registers -- and is moved to protect and provide for them.
This is caregiving: the behavioral system that answers a need it did not choose, roused by the other's vulnerability rather than by the persona's own want.
It is the reciprocal of attachment: where the attached reach toward a secure base under threat, the caregiver is the base reached for -- the one who steps toward the distress rather than away from it.

## Direction

Toward the other's need, and its relief.
Caregiving orients on the state of the one cared for, not the one caring: it reads what the other requires -- safety, comfort, provision, a hand -- and moves to supply it, taking the other's welfare as the aim the persona's action serves.
The direction is outward and downward-tending: toward the one who cannot, in this, fend for themselves, and toward closing the gap between their need and its meeting.

## Lever

The other's vulnerability, felt as a call to answer.
What caregiving spends is the persona's own resource -- attention, effort, patience, time, the body's and the heart's supply -- given over to a need that is not theirs, because the other's distress moves them to give it.
The lever is the disposition itself: a standing readiness to be roused by need and to expend the self on it, so that the sight of vulnerability pulls provision the way threat pulls the attachment system toward its base.

## Echo

The other is tended, and the persona is spent in the tending.
Caregiving is the giving side of the bond -- the counterpart to being cared for -- and its quality turns on whether the care meets the real need: held right, the other is safe and still free; held wrong, they are smothered or failed, and the giver is worn.
It runs as [sensitivity](process_sensitivity.md), [overprotection](process_overprotection.md), or [burnout](process_burnout.md) -- the attuned answer, the answer that overshoots, the giver emptied by the giving. The persona's own reaching for a secure base is the attachment engine's; the feeling-with that moves the care is the empathy engine's; the discrete transfer of a resource under a norm of return is the gift engine's. This engine owns the disposition to answer another's need itself.
