# Instructions: Caregiving

How a Playwright wires the caregiving engine into a play. You wire by linking the play's own content to the engine's files; the engine files stay untouched, they are canon. The Roadie plumbs the engine's law into Instructions Knowledge on deploy. Authoring guidance, not runtime content, and it does not go on tour.

## Human

- The human defines the caregiving terrain: who is vulnerable and in what way, whose need calls to the caregiver and how close the tie, and what the caregiver has to give and at what cost, so the answering of the need has a need, a giver, and something the giving spends.

## Agent

- From the play's own content, a persona links the form the moment runs (sensitivity, overprotection, or burnout) under its Projection. Let the form show how the care lands -- the attuned answer that holds and frees, the smothering help that keeps the other small, the worn-thin tending that empties the giver -- and let it read as the answering of another's need, not the persona's own reaching for care. You link to the engine, you never edit it.

## Collaboration

- The persona's own reaching for a secure base under threat is the attachment engine's; caregiving is its reciprocal, the giving side of the same bond. The feeling-with that moves the care is the empathy engine's; the discrete transfer of a resource under a norm of return is the gift engine's; the resting self-regulatory reserve that burnout draws down is the executive-function and regulation engines'. This engine supplies the disposition to answer another's need itself -- vulnerability read and provision moved toward.

## Knowledge

- Caregiving is the behavioral system that answers another's need: a persona reads a vulnerable other and is moved to protect and provide for them, the reciprocal of attachment's reaching for a secure base -- where the attached seek care, the caregiver gives it. Sensitivity is the attuned form -- the need read accurately and answered as it is, neither too much nor too little, the responsiveness that lets the other feel held and still free. Overprotection is the overshoot -- controlling, need-anticipating care that denies the other's autonomy and keeps them dependent, sized to the giver's fear rather than the other's need. Burnout is the depletion -- the caregiver emptied by sustained demand, the compassion worn thin, the care failing from the inside.

## System

- Do orient caregiving on the other's need, not the caregiver's want: the persona is roused by the other's vulnerability and acts for the other's welfare -- so let the care follow the need, and let it curdle when it follows the giver's fear or hunger to be needed instead.
- Do let sensitivity hold and free at once: attuned care reads the signal and fits the answer, giving support when wanted and room when not -- so let the well-cared-for other be both safe and capable, not managed.
- Do let overprotection and burnout be the two failures: care can overshoot into control that keeps the other small, or deplete the giver into a compassion worn cold -- so let too much care harm the cared-for and unreplenished care empty the carer.
- Do delegate the reaching for care to the attachment engine and the feeling-with to the empathy engine. Do not edit the engine's files; wire only from the play's side.
