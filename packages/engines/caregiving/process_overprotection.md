# Process: Overprotection

## Taxonomy

[Caregiving](process_caregiving.md), the overshooting form.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: caregiving

## Initiated by

The persona gives more care than the need requires -- shielding, anticipating, doing-for where the other could do themselves.
This is overprotection: care that overshoots, protection extended past the point the other needs it, help that will not let the other be capable.
It answers a need that is partly the caregiver's own -- to be needed, to avert all harm -- as much as the other's.

## Direction

Toward a need magnified, and an autonomy foreclosed.
Overprotection reads the other as more fragile than they are, or refuses the risk their growth requires, and so intervenes where it should wait -- smoothing every obstacle, forbidding every danger, meeting needs before they are felt.
The direction is control dressed as care: the other kept safe by being kept dependent, their room to act closed in the name of protecting them.

## Lever

The caregiver's discomfort with the other's risk, spent to prevent it.
Overprotection runs on the giver's own need -- to avert harm, to be indispensable, to still their own anxiety about the other -- so the care is sized to the caregiver's fear rather than the other's requirement.
The lever is the intolerance of the other's vulnerability and autonomy: rather than bear the risk of letting the other cope, the persona forecloses it, and the help becomes a cage.

## Echo

The other is kept safe and kept small, and resents the hand that holds them.
It is care that undermines what care is for -- the other's capacity -- because a need met before it is felt is a capacity never built, and protection that never lifts becomes a limit the other must fight or submit to.
It is [sensitivity](process_sensitivity.md) with the reading of the need inflated, and a fast road to [burnout](process_burnout.md), since care sized to the giver's fear has no natural stopping point. The controlling stance and its power over the other shade toward the power engine's; this owns the caregiving that overshoots the need.
