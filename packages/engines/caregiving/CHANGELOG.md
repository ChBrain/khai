# @chbrain/khai-engine-caregiving

## 0.1.1

### Patch Changes

- 24031ca: Add the caregiving engine: the behavioral system through which a persona is moved to protect and provide for a vulnerable other whose need is felt -- the reciprocal complement to `attachment`'s care-seeking. The root (`process_caregiving.md`) declares Bowlby's caregiving behavioral system: where the attached reach toward a secure base under threat, the caregiver is the base reached for, roused by the other's vulnerability rather than by the persona's own want. Three forms carry it: `sensitivity` (the attuned answer -- the need read accurately and met as it is, the responsiveness that lets the other feel held and still free), `overprotection` (the overshoot -- controlling, autonomy-denying care sized to the giver's fear rather than the other's need), and `burnout` (the depletion -- the caregiver emptied by sustained demand, compassion worn thin, care failing from the inside).

  The natural mate to `attachment`, filling in the giving side of the bond it models only the seeking side of. No homonym gate: the sole `care` stem is `moral-judgment`'s care/harm foundation, a different phenomenon, and the slug `caregiving` sidesteps it. Bounded against `attachment` (the seeking of care vs the giving), `empathy` (the feeling-with that moves the care vs the tending itself), `gift` (a discrete transfer under a norm of return vs a standing disposition to answer need), and `executive-function`/`regulation` (the resting self-regulatory reserve vs its depletion through caring). Warranted (LORE) on Bowlby (the caregiving behavioral system, 1969), George & Solomon (_The Caregiving System_, 2008), Ainsworth (maternal sensitivity, 1978), Collins & Feeney (responsive care as safe haven, 2000), and Figley (compassion fatigue, 1995). Set at patch as the free level; a new engine may warrant a minor at the maintainer's `bump:minor` label.
