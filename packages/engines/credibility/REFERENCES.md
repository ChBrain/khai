# Credibility: Reference

## Line of Work

Credibility as **process**: the credit extended to a first-person account, and what a shortfall does
to the persona giving it.

The engine's subject is a class of claim whose only evidence is the person making it -- what a body
feels, what was said with nobody else in the room, what somebody meant by a look. Fricker's account is
the spine, and it supplies both halves. **Testimonial injustice** is a credibility deficit applied to a
speaker because of a prejudice about who they are; **hermeneutical injustice** is a gap in the shared
interpretive resources that leaves an experience unintelligible, so that it cannot be reported at all.
The first is a wrong done in the hearing. The second is a wrong that precedes the hearing and is
nobody's individual doing.

The boundary with what khai already carries is clean. The bet on another's reliability, placed by
accepting vulnerability with a stake at risk, is **trust**'s -- a wager about future conduct, where
this engine reads the weight given to a report about the past. Holding a proposition true against
disconfirming evidence is **belief**'s. What being properly heard by a procedure buys, and whether
compliance follows, is the **heeding** composite's; that reads the fairness of the process, and this
reads the credit given to the speaker inside it. The discrediting attribute a persona carries is
**stigma**'s. Whether a rumour is believed as it travels is the **grapevine** composite's, which is
about a claim with no identifiable author. And the account required rather than offered is
**confession**'s, where the standing runs the other way.

**The report** sets a difficulty that is nobody's fault. The **first person** knows and cannot show,
and the knowing does not help: their certainty is not a reason for anybody else, and the strength of
their conviction reads to a doubter as intensity. The **corroboration** is asked for automatically and
sensibly, and the situation has already ruled it out -- so a general standard, applied to a class of
claims that cannot meet it, becomes a filter on the class. The **manner** is then all that is left,
and every register can be read either way: composure as evidence it cannot be serious, distress as
evidence of unreliability, fluency as rehearsal, hesitation as invention.

**The discount** is a setting rather than a conclusion. The **deficit** attaches to a category and
travels with anybody in it, across topics and across hearers who have never met -- undetectable case by
case and unmistakable in aggregate. The **sincere hearer** is the ordinary case: the discount does not
arrive as a reason, it arrives as an impression of how solid the account feels, and by the time the
hearer reaches for reasons the impression has selected which ones look relevant. So a conscientious
listener produces the same outcome and a better justification for it. The **track record** compounds
it, because an unbelieved true report and a false one leave the same trace.

**The missing word** is the harder case and precedes the hearer entirely. The **unnamed** pattern can
only be produced in parts, and in parts it sounds trivial, so the persona is required to argue for it
out of instances that individually contradict them. The **private fault** is then the best available
explanation: the words that exist for a difficulty nobody else seems to have are all about the person
having it, and the persona's own conscientiousness delivers the wrong verdict. The **coining** repairs
the future and closes none of the old cases.

Hence the **twist**, at the root. **Being disbelieved does not leave a persona holding an unheard
truth. It leaves them holding less of one.** The intuitive picture is of somebody who knows what
happened, cannot get it across, and waits intact for a better listener. Repeated discounting does not
produce that. It produces a persona who hedges before speaking, revises the account toward whatever
might be credited, checks their own memory against the doubt of people who were not there, and in the
limit declines to form a view -- so the discount does not merely fail to receive the report, it
degrades it at the source.

The engine is built so that nobody has to be behaving badly for any of it to run, and a play should
keep it that way. The hearers were reasonable. The persona was right. Nothing was done maliciously.

**On the evidence, stated plainly.** This engine joins a philosophical framework to a measured
literature, and the join is where the care is needed. **Fricker's _Epistemic Injustice_ is philosophy,
not measurement**: a conceptual account, widely adopted and generative, and it is not a finding. Its
two halves are also of different kinds -- testimonial injustice names something that has since been
measured in several domains, and hermeneutical injustice is an interpretive claim about collective
resources that nobody has operationalised and it is not obvious how anybody would. The missing-word
movement is therefore the engine's least evidenced and, in this author's reading, most important
material, and it is stated as an argument. **The credibility-deficit measurements are the strongest
leg and they are domain-specific.** Where the deficit has been looked for carefully -- pain assessment
and treatment recommendation being the best-studied case -- it turns up consistently and with
measurable size, and the mechanisms behind it have been probed rather than assumed. Those studies are
mostly clinical, mostly recent, and mostly one country's; the engine takes the direction as
well-established and does not import any particular effect size. **The claim that discounting damages
a persona as a knower is the twist and is the least directly tested part of it.** It follows from
Fricker's argument, it is consistent with a large literature on how confidence in one's own judgement
is maintained socially, and nobody has run the study that would show the degradation happening -- which
is a hard study to design, since the thing being lost is the persona's grip on what they knew.
**Manner effects are real and are messier than the member implies.** The finding that demeanour drives
credibility judgements is robust; the finding that every register can be read either way is a
generalisation from a scattered literature rather than a single result. **The coining case is
historical rather than experimental.** That a name changes what can be reported is well attested in
particular cases and is not a general law, and the engine does not claim that every unnamed wrong is
waiting for a word. What should not be taken from this engine is that doubting an account is a wrong,
that hearers are usually prejudiced, or that a persona's report is correct because it was discounted.
The claim is narrow: the class of claim has no second source, the weighting precedes the content, the
vocabulary is thinnest where it was most needed, and the damage lands on the knower.

persona. The twist and both movements of injustice are Fricker's.

## Restrictions

What the engine refuses to model, and to whom it delegates.

- **The bet on another's reliability (trust)**: Extending vulnerability to somebody whose conduct
  cannot be controlled, and updating on what they do, is the trust engine's. That is a wager about
  future conduct; this engine reads the weight given to a report about something that has already
  happened.
- **Holding a proposition true (belief)**: How a persona maintains, revises, defends, or abandons a
  belief when the world answers is the belief engine's. This engine reads what a hearer does with
  somebody else's account before any of that begins.
- **What a fair procedure buys (heeding, recognition)**: Procedural justice, being properly heard, and
  what institutional acknowledgement does are those packages'. Those read the fairness of the process;
  this reads the credit given to the speaker inside it, which a scrupulously fair procedure does not
  correct.
- **The discrediting attribute (stigma, carrying, forgoing)**: The mark itself, what concealing it
  costs, and help forgone to avoid a label are those packages'. A mark frequently supplies the prejudice
  behind a deficit, and the engine reads only the deficit.
- **A claim with no identifiable author (grapevine, rumor)**: Whether an unsourced story is believed
  as it travels is those packages'. This engine requires a speaker who can be doubted.
- **The account demanded rather than offered (confession)**: Telling under a relation of authority,
  where the listener has standing the speaker does not, is the confession engine's. There the standing
  extracts the account; here it prices one that was volunteered.
- **The sensation itself (body, illness)**: The integrity set-point and its alarm are the body
  engine's; the sick role and the legitimacy an illness confers are illness's. The engine reads the
  report and not the thing reported.
- **Deciding whether the claim is true (the world)**: Investigation, adjudication, evidence law, and
  clinical diagnosis are practices with their own standards. They settle the case; this engine reads
  what happened to the account and the persona on the way to them.
- **The disbelief as authored symbol (the play's pitch)**: A report dismissed in order to **stand
  for** a theme -- the unheeded speaker who mirrors a motif of blindness -- is refused: that is authored
  key, the play's pitch. Credibility sets what the account cost and lost; the meaning read into it is
  the play's.

## Encoding

Source to constraint. A process tree: one root, four movements, twelve forms.

- **[credibility](process_credibility.md)** (the root): the credit extended to a first-person account,
  read on what the hearer did before listening, carrying the twist -- being disbelieved leaves a
  persona holding less of a truth rather than an unheard one. Anchored by Fricker (2007).
- **[the report](process_report_credibility.md)**: an account with no second source. Anchored by Scarry
  (1985) and Jones (1998). [the first person](process_the_first_person.md),
  [the corroboration](process_the_corroboration.md), [the manner](process_the_manner.md).
- **[the discount](process_discount_credibility.md)**: the credit withheld before the content is heard.
  Anchored by Fricker (2007) and Hoffman et al. (2016). [the deficit](process_the_deficit.md),
  [the sincere hearer](process_the_sincere_hearer.md), [the track record](process_the_track_record.md).
- **[the missing word](process_missing_word_credibility.md)**: where the experience has no name.
  Anchored by Fricker (2007) and Pohlhaus (2012). [the unnamed](process_the_unnamed.md),
  [the private fault](process_the_private_fault.md), [the coining](process_the_coining.md).
- **[the aftermath](process_aftermath_credibility.md)**: what it does to the persona reporting, and
  where the engine is seen for one thing. Anchored by Dotson (2011) and Fricker (2007).
  [the self-doubt](process_the_self_doubt.md), [the belated](process_the_belated.md),
  [the owing back](process_the_owing_back.md).
