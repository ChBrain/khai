# Process: The Owing Back

## Taxonomy

[The Aftermath](process_aftermath_credibility.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: credibility

## Initiated by

Somebody realises they did not believe a person who was telling the truth.
A hearer, looking back, with no procedure available for what to do about it.

## Direction

Toward a debt with no instrument for discharging it.
There is a word for a wrong done and an apology for it, and neither quite fits: the hearer did not lie, break a rule, or act in bad faith -- they weighed an account and got it wrong, which is the thing hearers are supposed to do and sometimes get wrong.
So the acknowledgement, when it comes, tends to arrive as a general regret rather than as an account of what happened, and the persona receives sympathy where they were owed a correction.

## Lever

The decency of the hearer's regret against how little of the debt it reaches.
The carrying force is that the regret is genuine and worth having: hearers who realise this are usually shaken by it, they change how they listen afterwards, and demanding that every misjudgement be treated as a wrong would make anybody unwilling to judge anything; the resistance is that the persona lost something specific -- an interval, and some of their own confidence -- and there is no form of words that returns either, so the exchange ends with the hearer's conscience settled and the persona's ledger open.
The Owing Back is felt by the one who owes and is not paid to the one who is owed.

## Echo

An acknowledgement that lands on the wrong side of the room.
It shows why the operation never concludes.
The Owing Back is dated from the realisation and is discharged by nobody.
