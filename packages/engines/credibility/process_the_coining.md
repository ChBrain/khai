# Process: The Coining

## Taxonomy

[The Missing Word](process_missing_word_credibility.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: credibility

## Initiated by

Somebody names it, and the name spreads.
A term arrives that gathers the incidents, and within a few years everybody has it.

## Direction

Toward a retrospective reorganisation of a great many lives.
The word does not describe a new phenomenon; it makes an old one sayable, and the effect on people who lived through it unnamed is immediate and disorienting. They can suddenly present a case, and they can suddenly see that it was never them.
It also arrives too late for what it explains, and it lands on the other side as an accusation for something that was not describable when it was done.

## Lever

The relief of the naming against what the naming cannot repair.
The carrying force is that the word is a genuine good and its arrival is a real advance: people who could not be heard can now be heard, the thing can be studied and legislated against, and everybody involved is better off for the vocabulary existing; the resistance is that a name changes the present and not the past, so the years spent as a private fault stay spent, and the persona's relief at the word is mixed with a grievance that has nowhere to go.
The Coining fixes the future case and closes none of the old ones.

## Echo

A word that arrives after the people who needed it.
It shows the shortfall being repaired at the level it existed on, which is nobody's individual level.
The Coining is dated from its first published use and back-dates nothing.
