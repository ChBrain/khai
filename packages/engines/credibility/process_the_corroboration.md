# Process: The Corroboration

## Taxonomy

[The Report](process_report_credibility.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: credibility

## Initiated by

The hearer asks whether anybody else saw it.
Reasonably, and usually kindly, and as the first question.

## Direction

Toward a standard the account was never able to meet.
Asking for a second source is sound practice everywhere else, which is why it comes automatically -- and here it is a request for something the situation excludes by construction, since the events that need this treatment are precisely the ones with no other witness.
So the question functions as a finding. The persona answers no, and the no is heard as weakness in the case.

## Lever

The soundness of the standard against its unavailability here.
The carrying force is that corroboration is the right thing to look for and hearers are not being obtuse: a second source really does strengthen a claim, checking is what a careful listener does, and abandoning the practice would leave every account equally weighted regardless of support; the resistance is that applying a general standard to a class of claims that cannot meet it converts the standard into a filter on the class, so the reports most in need of being heard are the ones structurally certain to fail the test.
The Corroboration asks for what the situation has already ruled out.

## Echo

A gap in the case that was there before anybody spoke.
It shows a good practice producing a bad result without anybody departing from it.
The Corroboration is dated at the first question and is not recovered from.
