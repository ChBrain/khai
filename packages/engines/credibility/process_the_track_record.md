# Process: The Track Record

## Taxonomy

[The Discount](process_discount_credibility.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: credibility

## Initiated by

The persona has reported before, and it went badly.
Not resolved against them -- just not taken up, or taken up and dropped, or found inconclusive.

## Direction

Toward a history that is treated as information about the reporter.
An unresolved report becomes, in the record and in memory, an occasion on which this persona said something that did not stand up. Nobody decided that; it is simply what an inconclusive outcome leaves behind.
So each discounted account raises the discount on the next, and the persona accumulates a reputation assembled entirely out of not having been believed.

## Lever

The relevance of a history against what this history actually records.
The carrying force is that past reports are legitimately relevant and a hearer should weigh them: somebody who has been mistaken before may be mistaken now, patterns of complaint are real information, and ignoring history would make every hearing start from nothing; the resistance is that what is being weighed is a record of outcomes rather than a record of accuracy -- an unbelieved true report and a false one leave the same trace -- so the history compounds the original error instead of correcting it.
The Track Record is built out of the discount and is used to justify it.

## Echo

A reputation made of unresolved reports.
It shows the discount becoming self-supporting without anybody adding to it.
The Track Record is dated from the first unresolved case and grows without new evidence.
