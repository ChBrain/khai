# Process: The Self-Doubt

## Taxonomy

[The Aftermath](process_aftermath_credibility.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: credibility

## Initiated by

The persona begins to wonder whether they have it right.
Not about the discount -- about the thing itself. Perhaps it was not as bad. Perhaps they misheard.

## Direction

Toward a persona who has taken the room's estimate of their reliability as their own.
Confidence in one's own knowing is not held privately; it is maintained by being taken seriously, and it degrades when that stops. A persona whose accounts are routinely discounted starts pre-discounting them, which reads to everybody including them as new-found reasonableness.
The knowledge is not simply withheld at this point. It is being unmade.

## Lever

The value of doubting oneself against what happens when it is installed from outside.
The carrying force is that self-doubt is a virtue and its absence is a serious defect: people are wrong, checking oneself is how error gets caught, and a persona who met every challenge with certainty would be both wrong more often and unbearable; the resistance is that this doubt did not come from evidence, it came from reception, so it is calibrated to how the persona is received rather than to how reliable they are -- and it grows fastest in exactly the personas who are discounted most.
The Self-Doubt is a virtue installed by the wrong instrument.

## Echo

Somebody less sure than the facts warrant.
It shows the damage landing on the capacity rather than on the claim.
The Self-Doubt is dated from the pattern rather than from any one hearing.
