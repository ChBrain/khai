# Instructions: Credibility

How a Playwright wires the credibility engine into a play. You wire by linking the play's own content to the engine's files; the engine files stay untouched, they are canon. The Roadie plumbs the engine's law into Instructions Knowledge on deploy. Authoring guidance, not runtime content, and it does not go on tour.

## Human

- The human decides whether the report is true and keeps it off the page, because the engine runs on nobody in the room being able to tell and the play must not settle it for them. Give both sides -- the hearer is a position in the engine, not an obstacle in it. And keep the hearers conscientious: the mechanism does not need a dismissive one and stops being interesting the moment it gets one.

## Agent

- From the play's own content, a persona links under Projection where they stand to the account: whether they are reporting or receiving, what corroboration is being asked for, what prior is in the room, whether there is a word for the thing at all, and what previous reports left behind. Read the engine on what the hearer did before listening, never on what they concluded. Keep it the credit given to an account: the bet on another's reliability is trust's, holding a proposition is belief's, what a fair procedure buys is heeding's. You link to the engine, you never edit it.

## Collaboration

- The bet on another's reliability with a stake at risk is the trust engine's; holding a proposition against disconfirming evidence is belief's; what being properly heard by a procedure buys is the heeding composite's and recognition's; the discrediting attribute is stigma's and the carrying composite's; help forgone to avoid a label is the forgoing composite's; whether an unsourced story is believed as it travels is the grapevine composite's and rumor's; the account demanded rather than offered is confession's; the sensation itself is body's and illness's. Investigation, adjudication, and diagnosis settle the case and are not modelled here. Whether a production's hearer discounted or weighed, and whether a persona's revision came from evidence or from reception, are the team's review -- and the second is a judgement the persona cannot make either.

## Knowledge

- Credibility is the credit extended to a first-person account, and what a shortfall does to the persona giving it. It is not trust's bet with a stake at risk, not belief's hold on a proposition, and not heeding's fair procedure. It wires on a persona at Projection and runs in four movements. The report sets a difficulty that is nobody's fault: the only witness is the one it happened to, so their certainty is not a reason for anybody else and reads to a doubter as intensity; corroboration is asked for sensibly and the situation has already ruled it out, so a general standard becomes a filter on the class of claims that cannot meet it; and with nothing else available the hearer reads manner, where composure says it cannot be serious, distress says unreliable, fluency says rehearsed, and hesitation says invented. The discount is a setting rather than a conclusion: a deficit attaches to a category and travels with anybody in it, undetectable case by case and unmistakable in aggregate; the sincere hearer is the ordinary case, because the discount arrives as an impression of how solid the account feels and deliberation is handed its material afterwards; and each unresolved report builds a record, since an unbelieved true report and a false one leave the same trace. The missing word precedes the hearer entirely: an unnamed pattern can only be produced in parts and in parts sounds trivial, the words available for a difficulty nobody else seems to have are all about the person having it, and the coining repairs the future and closes none of the old cases. The engine's twist is at the root: being disbelieved does not leave a persona holding an unheard truth, it leaves them holding less of one -- they hedge, revise toward what would be credited, check their memory against people who were not there, and eventually decline to form a view.

## System

- Do decide whether the report is true and never settle it on stage. If the play answers the question, the engine stops.
- Do keep the hearers conscientious. They want to get it right, they are not enjoying the doubt, and they can give a complete honest account of their reasoning in which every item is true.
- Do not stage a dismissive hearer. That replaces the mechanism with a villain and describes a rarer and less interesting phenomenon.
- Do ask for corroboration early and reasonably. It is the first question anybody asks, it is sound practice everywhere else, and here it functions as a finding.
- Do make every delivery wrong. Whatever register the persona chooses, give somebody in the room a reading of it that undercuts the account.
- Do write the unnamed as a list of small things. Each one true, each one minor, and the persona unable to say why they add up.
- Do let the persona conclude it is them. That is the correct inference from a defective set of options, and it is what conscientiousness produces when the right word does not exist.
- Do close on the damage to the knower rather than on the verdict. Somebody hedging, revising, and no longer sure about their own afternoon.
- Do not model trust, belief, procedural fairness (heeding, recognition), the mark (stigma, carrying), rumour (grapevine), the demanded account (confession), or the sensation (body, illness). Do not make the disbelief stand for a theme; that is authored key, the play's pitch. Do not edit the engine's files; wire only from the play's side.
