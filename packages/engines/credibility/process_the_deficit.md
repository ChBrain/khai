# Process: The Deficit

## Taxonomy

[The Discount](process_discount_credibility.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: credibility

## Initiated by

The persona is given less credit than their account is worth, because of what they are.
Not because of anything they said, and not on any evidence about their reliability.

## Direction

Toward a shortfall that follows the persona rather than the claim.
The deficit attaches to a category and travels with anybody in it, across topics and across hearers, so a persona subject to one is under-credited about their own body, their own work, and their own afternoon by people who have never met each other.
It is measurable where anybody has bothered to measure it, and the measurements are consistent enough that its existence is not the contested part.

## Lever

The invisibility of the shortfall to the hearer against its consistency across hearers.
The carrying force is that no individual instance is provable and the hearer in front of the persona may genuinely be the exception: any single decision has a defensible reading, hearers vary, and treating every doubting listener as prejudiced would be both unfair and paralysing; the resistance is that a pattern with no visible instances is still a pattern, and the persona experiences it as a run of unrelated disappointments while it is in fact one thing happening repeatedly.
The Deficit is undetectable case by case and unmistakable in aggregate.

## Echo

A persona under-credited across unrelated rooms.
It shows the mechanism at the point where it is most often denied.
The Deficit is dated from whatever the persona is taken to be and does not depend on any hearer's intent.
