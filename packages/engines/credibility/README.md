# Credibility

Credibility as process: the credit extended to a first-person account, and what a shortfall does to the persona giving it -- not the bet on another's reliability with a stake at risk, which is trust's, and not the holding of a proposition under pressure, which is belief's -- across four movements: the report (a first person who is the only witness, a corroboration the situation has already ruled out, and a manner that supplies whatever the hearer brought with them), the discount (a deficit undetectable case by case and unmistakable in aggregate, a sincere hearer who is the ordinary case and is not improved by being sincere, and a track record built out of the discount and used to justify it), the missing word (an unnamed pattern that is strongest as a whole and can only be produced in parts, a private fault that is the best available explanation and is not true, and a coining that fixes the future case and closes none of the old ones), and the aftermath (a self-doubt that is a virtue installed by the wrong instrument, a belated confirmation that cannot return the reporter, and an owing back felt by the one who owes and not paid to the one who is owed) -- with the twist that being disbelieved does not leave a persona holding an unheard truth, it leaves them holding less of one.
