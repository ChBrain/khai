# Process: The Belated

## Taxonomy

[The Aftermath](process_aftermath_credibility.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: credibility

## Initiated by

Years later, it turns out to have been true.
The fault is found, the pattern is documented, somebody else says the same thing and is believed, the word arrives.

## Direction

Toward a vindication that does not land as one.
The persona expected relief and receives something more complicated: the confirmation proves they were right and also measures the interval during which nobody thought so, and the interval is the part that is felt.
It also raises a question that has no good answer, which is what the years were.

## Lever

The genuineness of the vindication against its arrival time.
The carrying force is that being believed at last is real and better than the alternative: the record is corrected, the persona is no longer alone with it, and treating late recognition as worthless would be both wrong and a counsel of despair; the resistance is that credibility restored is not knowledge restored -- the persona spent the interval revising, hedging, and doubting, and none of that is undone by a later finding -- so what arrives is an acknowledgement addressed to a person who is no longer quite the one who reported.
The Belated confirms the account and cannot return the reporter.

## Echo

A correction that has come too far to reach.
It shows why the engine's harm is not repaired by the engine's own best outcome.
The Belated is dated from the confirmation and is measured backwards.
