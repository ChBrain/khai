# Process: The Private Fault

## Taxonomy

[The Missing Word](process_missing_word_credibility.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: credibility

## Initiated by

The persona concludes the problem is them.
Too sensitive, not tough enough, reading things into it, unable to let things go.

## Direction

Toward the only explanation the vocabulary makes available.
Something is wrong and there is no word for the wrong, so the persona reaches for the words that do exist -- and the ones available for a difficulty that nobody else seems to have are all about the person having it.
This is not weakness of character. It is the correct inference from a defective set of options, and the persona is reasoning well with what they were given.

## Lever

The soundness of the reasoning against the absence of the right answer.
The carrying force is that self-examination is a virtue and the persona is doing the right thing: checking whether one is the problem before blaming a situation is exactly what a fair-minded person does, it is frequently the correct conclusion, and somebody who never asked it would be insufferable; the resistance is that the check is being run against an incomplete list, so the honest answer to are you sure it is not you comes back yes it is probably me, and the persona's own conscientiousness is what delivers the wrong verdict.
The Private Fault is the best available explanation and it is not true.

## Echo

Somebody carrying a structural problem as a personal defect.
It shows the missing word doing its damage before anybody has disbelieved anything.
The Private Fault is dated from the failure to explain it and is held privately.
