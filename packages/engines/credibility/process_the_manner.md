# Process: The Manner

## Taxonomy

[The Report](process_report_credibility.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: credibility

## Initiated by

How it is said decides what happens to it.
Calm, upset, precise, rambling, apologetic, insistent, rehearsed, halting.

## Direction

Toward a judgement of the content made from the delivery.
With no corroboration available, the hearer falls back on the only other signal present, which is the persona's bearing -- and the readings are contradictory. Composure reads as evidence that it cannot be serious; distress reads as evidence that the persona is unreliable; fluency reads as rehearsal; hesitation reads as invention.
There is no delivery that reads as credible, because every register is available to be read the other way.

## Lever

The reasonableness of reading manner against the fact that it does not carry information.
The carrying force is that manner is not nothing and hearers are right to attend to it: people do behave differently when describing something that happened to them, experienced listeners genuinely pick things up, and refusing to consider how something is said would be a strange kind of deafness; the resistance is that the mapping runs both ways for every register, so whatever the persona does can be read as confirming or undermining, and the reading that gets applied is the one the hearer was already inclined toward.
The Manner supplies whatever the hearer brought with them.

## Echo

An account graded on its delivery.
It shows why coaching a persona on how to tell it does not reliably help.
The Manner is dated at the telling and cannot be got right.
