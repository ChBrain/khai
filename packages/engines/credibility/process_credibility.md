# Process: Credibility

## Taxonomy

Testimony (no khai file of its own yet).

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: credibility

## Initiated by

A persona says something only they could know, and somebody decides what it is worth.
Credibility starts wherever a first-person account is the whole of the evidence: the patient describing a pain, the employee reporting what was said, the child explaining what happened, the customer insisting the fault is real, the witness who was the only one there.
It is not the bet on another's reliability with a stake at risk, which is trust's, and not the holding of a proposition under pressure, which is belief's. It is the credit extended to an account, and what a shortfall does to the persona giving it.

## Direction

Toward a report that will be discounted or accepted before it is examined.
The engine reads four movements: [the report](process_report_credibility.md), an account with no second source; [the discount](process_discount_credibility.md), the credit withheld by a hearer who would deny withholding it; [the missing word](process_missing_word_credibility.md), where the experience has no name and cannot be made intelligible at all; and [the aftermath](process_aftermath_credibility.md), the twist -- what all of it does to the persona who was reporting.
The four are one operation, and only the second is ever noticed.

## Lever

The necessity of weighing an account against what the weighing costs the person giving it.
The carrying force is that discounting is a legitimate and unavoidable faculty: hearers must weigh what they are told, some accounts are mistaken and a few are false, a listener who credited every report equally would be useless as a judge of anything, and the practice of assessing testimony is what makes testimony worth having at all; the resistance is that the weighting is applied before the content is examined, on who is speaking rather than on what they said, and the persona has no instrument for distinguishing a hearer weighing their evidence from a hearer weighing them.
Credibility is read on what the hearer did before listening, never on what they concluded.

## Echo

An account in the world at a discount nobody set deliberately.
Credibility does not close; it is a report being priced, gathered into one reading -- a report, a discount, a missing word, an aftermath.
The engine's twist is here. **Being disbelieved does not leave a persona holding an unheard truth. It leaves them holding less of one.** The natural picture is of somebody who knows what happened and cannot get it across, waiting intact for a better listener. That is not what the operation produces. A persona whose account is discounted repeatedly loses confidence in their own capacity to know things, revises the account toward what would be credited, and in the limit stops being able to form the knowledge at all -- so the discount does not merely fail to receive the report, it degrades it at the source.
It hands off what it is not: the bet on another's reliability is trust's; holding a proposition against disconfirming evidence is belief's; what a fair procedure buys is heeding's; the discrediting attribute a persona carries is stigma's; whether a rumour is believed as it travels is the grapevine composite's; the account required rather than offered is confession's; and a disbelief staged to **stand for** a theme is authored key, the play's pitch.
The persona is dated from the last thing they knew and stopped saying.
