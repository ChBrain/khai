# Process: The Missing Word

## Taxonomy

[Credibility](process_credibility.md), where the experience has no name.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: credibility

## Initiated by

There is no word for what happened, so it cannot be said at all.
The Missing Word starts below the discount, at a shortfall in the shared vocabulary: [the unnamed](process_the_unnamed.md) experience, [the private fault](process_the_private_fault.md) it is read as instead, and [the coining](process_the_coining.md), which changes everything and arrives late.

## Direction

Toward a wrong that cannot be reported because it is not yet describable.
This is the harder case and it precedes the hearer entirely. A persona subject to something for which the collective has no concept cannot make it intelligible to anybody, including themselves: the account comes out as a list of incidents that sound minor, a complaint about a manner, a difficulty they cannot quite specify.
The hearer is not discounting. There is nothing yet to discount.

## Lever

The completeness of the shared vocabulary for most things against what it leaves out.
The carrying force is that the vocabulary is enormous and mostly adequate: nearly everything anybody needs to say has words available, language grows to fit what people need to describe, and the absence of a term is rare rather than routine; the resistance is that the gaps are not distributed evenly -- a concept goes missing where the people who needed it were not in the rooms where meanings are made -- so the vocabulary is thinnest exactly where it would have done the most work.
The Missing Word is a shortfall in the collective, borne by an individual.

## Echo

An experience that cannot be brought into a conversation.
It is the movement nobody in it can identify while it is running.
The Missing Word is dated from before the persona and ends when somebody names it.
