# Process: The First Person

## Taxonomy

[The Report](process_report_credibility.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: credibility

## Initiated by

The only witness is the one it happened to.
Inside the body, inside the room with the door shut, inside a life the hearer was not present for.

## Direction

Toward an asymmetry that cannot be repaired.
The persona has direct access and no way to transmit it; the hearer has no access and full authority over what to do with the report. Neither position can be moved toward the other, and both parties are stuck with the arrangement.
This is not a communication failure that better description would solve. It is a property of the kind of fact in question.

## Lever

The completeness of the persona's knowledge against its total unavailability.
The carrying force is that first-person access is genuine knowledge and should be treated as such: the persona is not guessing about their own pain or their own afternoon, they are the best-placed observer there will ever be, and a hearer who discounts that on principle is discarding the only information available; the resistance is that the access does not travel, so the persona's certainty about what they know is not itself a reason for anybody else, and the strength of their conviction reads to a doubter as intensity rather than as evidence.
The First Person knows and cannot show, and the knowing does not help.

## Echo

A fact with one observer.
It shows where the domain's difficulty is located, which is not in anybody's conduct.
The First Person is dated from the event and has no witnesses to add.
