# Process: The Discount

## Taxonomy

[Credibility](process_credibility.md), the credit withheld before the content is heard.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: credibility

## Initiated by

The account is weighted before it is examined.
The Discount starts at a prior applied to the speaker rather than the statement: [a deficit](process_the_deficit.md) attached to who they are, [a sincere hearer](process_the_sincere_hearer.md) who would deny applying one, and [a track record](process_the_track_record.md) that compounds it.

## Direction

Toward a report that arrives already reduced.
The weighting is not a conclusion reached at the end of listening. It is a setting that was in place beforehand, produced by what the hearer takes the speaker to be -- young, unwell, foreign, junior, angry, previously mistaken -- and it operates on the credit extended rather than on the argument made.
So the persona is not losing a debate. They are being given less to work with.

## Lever

The necessity of priors against what a prior does to a person.
The carrying force is that weighting by source is rational and everybody does it: a hearer who ignored everything they knew about a speaker would be worse at judging, some sources really are less reliable, and asking listeners to arrive with no expectations is asking them to be less intelligent than they are; the resistance is that a prior about a category is applied to an individual who is not the category, before their account has had a chance to be anything, and the resulting discount is invisible to the hearer because it feels like judgement rather than like a setting.
The Discount is applied to the speaker and lands on the statement.

## Echo

A report received at less than it is worth by somebody who was being careful.
It is the movement everybody can see when it happens to somebody else.
The Discount is dated before the first sentence.
