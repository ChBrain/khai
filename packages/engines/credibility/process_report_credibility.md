# Process: The Report

## Taxonomy

[Credibility](process_credibility.md), an account with no second source.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: credibility

## Initiated by

Somebody says what happened to them.
The Report starts where the evidence is a person: [the first person](process_the_first_person.md), who is the only witness; [the corroboration](process_the_corroboration.md) that is asked for and does not exist; and [the manner](process_the_manner.md) it is delivered in, which decides more than the content.

## Direction

Toward a claim whose only support is the one making it.
There is a class of things that can only be reported: what a body feels, what was said when nobody else was in the room, what somebody meant by a look. For these the account is not evidence about the fact -- it is the fact's only appearance.
So the hearer's decision is not between competing accounts. It is whether to take this one.

## Lever

The unavoidability of the situation against the impossibility of improving it.
The carrying force is that most such reports are accurate and most hearers know it: people rarely invent pains or conversations, ordinary life runs almost entirely on taking each other's word, and a world in which nobody was credited without corroboration would grind to a halt in an afternoon; the resistance is that the persona cannot strengthen their case by any means available to them, since everything they might add is more of the same testimony, so a hearer inclined to doubt cannot be moved by anything the speaker does.
The Report is either taken or it is nothing.

## Echo

An account resting entirely on its author.
It is the movement that sets the whole difficulty, and it is not anybody's fault.
The Report is dated at the telling and cannot be supplemented.
