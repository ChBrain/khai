# Process: The Unnamed

## Taxonomy

[The Missing Word](process_missing_word_credibility.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: credibility

## Initiated by

The persona tries to describe it and produces a list of small things.
He stands too close. She keeps saying it in front of people. It is always my file that goes missing.

## Direction

Toward an account that is accurate and sounds trivial.
Without a term that gathers the incidents, each has to be reported on its own, and on its own each is minor -- so the persona is forced to present the weakest possible version of a real pattern, and to do it repeatedly.
The hearer's response is reasonable: taken one at a time, these are not much, and the persona cannot say why they are.

## Lever

The truth of each incident against the unavailability of the thing they make.
The carrying force is that hearers are right to treat small incidents as small: most of them are, escalating every minor friction would make workplaces and families unliveable, and asking somebody to see a pattern that has not been articulated is asking a lot; the resistance is that a pattern with no name has no way to be presented as a pattern, so the persona is required to argue for it out of instances that individually contradict them.
The Unnamed is strongest as a whole and can only be produced in parts.

## Echo

A real thing that arrives as a series of small complaints.
It shows why the account gets weaker the more honestly it is given.
The Unnamed is dated from the first incident and accumulates without a total.
