# Process: The Sincere Hearer

## Taxonomy

[The Discount](process_discount_credibility.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: credibility

## Initiated by

The person applying the discount would deny applying one, and mean it.
They are conscientious, they take the report seriously, and they have concluded that it does not add up.

## Direction

Toward a prejudice operating below the level anybody could report.
The hearer is not lying about their own reasoning, because the discount does not arrive as a reason. It arrives as an impression of how solid the account feels, and by the time the hearer reaches for reasons the impression has already selected which ones look relevant.
So the hearer can supply a complete, honest, and internally coherent account of why they were not persuaded, and every item in it will be true.

## Lever

The genuineness of the hearer's care against what the care cannot inspect.
The carrying force is that most hearers are exactly as decent as they believe: they want to get it right, they are not enjoying the doubt, they would be appalled to be told what the pattern of their decisions looks like, and an engine that made them villains would be describing a rarer and less important phenomenon; the resistance is that sincerity is no protection at all here, since the mechanism runs before deliberation and hands deliberation its material, so the most careful hearer produces the same outcome and a better justification for it.
The Sincere Hearer is the ordinary case and is not improved by being sincere.

## Echo

A conscientious judgement with a bias inside it.
It shows why the engine does not need anybody to behave badly.
The Sincere Hearer is dated at every hearing and is never available for inspection.
