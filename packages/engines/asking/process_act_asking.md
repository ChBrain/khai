# Process: The Asking

## Taxonomy

[Asking](process_asking.md), the act and its form.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: asking

## Initiated by

The request is finally put.
The Asking starts at the sentence itself: [the request put](process_the_request_put.md) and what it has to contain, [a reason given](process_the_reason_given.md) whether or not it is a good one, and [a free refusal](process_the_free_refusal.md) that both parties are required to treat as real.

## Direction

Toward a short exchange in which most of the work is form.
What a request needs is not persuasion. It needs to be specific, answerable, addressed to a person, and to leave the other one somewhere to stand -- and requests fail far more often on vagueness than on size.
The content matters less than everybody expects. A large clear ask outperforms a small unclear one.

## Lever

The smallness of the act against the weight the persona put on it.
The carrying force is that this is a genuinely light moment and usually a pleasant one: it takes a sentence, the other person is not upset, and the dread carried into it is not visible from the far side of the desk; the resistance is that the lightness is itself misleading, because the persona reads an easy yes as evidence that this particular request was unusually reasonable rather than as evidence about asking.
The Asking is easier than the reluctance and is not credited against it.

## Echo

A request in the world with somebody holding it.
It is the shortest of the four movements and the only one with a witness.
The Asking is dated to the sentence.
