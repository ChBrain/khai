# Process: The Reluctance

## Taxonomy

[Asking](process_asking.md), everything that happens instead of asking.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: asking

## Initiated by

The persona would rather not.
The Reluctance starts before any request exists: [an imposition](process_the_imposition.md) imagined in detail, [a workaround](process_the_workaround.md) taken instead, and [a hedging](process_the_hedging.md) that wraps the request if one is finally made.

## Direction

Toward a great deal of effort spent avoiding a short conversation.
The reluctance is not shyness and does not require any particular temperament. It is a calculation -- what this will cost them, what they will think of me, what I will owe -- run quickly and confidently by people who are otherwise perfectly forward.
Most of it terminates in no request at all, which is why the movement is the largest and leaves no trace.

## Lever

The decency of not wanting to impose against what the decency costs.
The carrying force is that restraint is a virtue and a great deal runs on it: other people's time is finite, a persona who asked for everything they wanted would be exhausting, and the fact that most people hesitate is why being asked registers as something rather than nothing; the resistance is that the hesitation is priced off a figure that is measurably wrong, so restraint that would be correct at the true cost is applied at several times it.
The Reluctance is a good habit running on bad numbers.

## Echo

Work done alone that did not need to be.
It is the movement nobody sees, including the persona, who does not experience not asking as an event.
The Reluctance is dated from the first time asking cost them something.
