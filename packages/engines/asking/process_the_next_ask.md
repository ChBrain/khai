# Process: The Next Ask

## Taxonomy

[The Granting](process_granting_asking.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: asking

## Initiated by

The persona needs something again.
And it is exactly as hard as it was the first time.

## Direction

Toward a mechanism that does not teach.
Having been helped, thanked, and told sincerely that it was no trouble, the persona approaches the next request with the same estimate, the same reluctance, and the same workaround available.
The evidence was delivered and was not filed. What gets stored is the occasion -- that person, that favour, that day -- rather than the general fact.

## Lever

The clarity of the evidence against the persistence of the model.
The carrying force is that the persona is being properly cautious rather than obtuse: one yes from one person is genuinely weak evidence about the next request to somebody else, and treating a favour granted as a licence is the failure mode that makes people insufferable; the resistance is that the caution is applied to every case without exception, so a lifetime of yeses never accumulates into a revised expectation, and the reluctance survives all of its own disconfirmations.
The Next Ask is where the engine shows that it does not learn.

## Echo

The same difficulty again, with more evidence against it.
It shows why the operation never concludes.
The Next Ask is dated from the next need and starts from the same number.
