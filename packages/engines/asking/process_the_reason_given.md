# Process: The Reason Given

## Taxonomy

[The Asking](process_act_asking.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: asking

## Initiated by

The persona explains why.
Because I am behind, because my train leaves at six, because I need to make copies.

## Direction

Toward a request that has been made into a request rather than a demand.
A reason attached to a small ask raises compliance sharply, and it does so even when the reason carries no information at all -- though the same trick fails once the favour is large, which is the half of the finding that rarely gets quoted.
What a reason does is mark the request as reasoned rather than arbitrary, and give the helper something to be helping with.

## Lever

The power of a stated reason against how little of it is doing the work.
The carrying force is that reasons are honest and belong there: the persona does have one, the helper is owed it, and requests with no account at all are received as entitlement; the resistance is that a form which works whether or not it carries content is a form that can be filled with anything, so the persona giving a real reason and the persona giving a hollow one are operating the same lever, and only the size of the ask tells them apart.
The Reason Given works as a shape, up to a point.

## Echo

A request the other person knows what to do with.
It shows how much of the act is grammar.
The Reason Given is dated with the request and is rarely examined.
