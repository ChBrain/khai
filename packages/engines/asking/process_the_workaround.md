# Process: The Workaround

## Taxonomy

[The Reluctance](process_reluctance_asking.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: asking

## Initiated by

The persona does it the hard way instead.
They stay late, they take the long route, they work it out from first principles, they buy the thing rather than borrow it.

## Direction

Toward an outcome reached at several times the cost of a sentence.
The workaround is not a failure of nerve. It is preferred, and it feels like competence -- and the persona is not aware of choosing it over a request, because the request was never a live option to choose against.
It also works, which is the difficulty: a workaround that succeeds supplies no evidence that asking would have been better.

## Lever

The self-sufficiency of the workaround against what it costs to run.
The carrying force is that doing things yourself is genuinely valuable: it builds capability, it keeps a persona from becoming a drag on everybody near them, and much of what looks like reluctance is somebody simply getting on with it; the resistance is that the workaround is taken without a comparison ever being made, so a persona can spend a career paying a large premium for a request they never priced.
The Workaround succeeds and is therefore never audited.

## Echo

A problem solved expensively and alone.
It shows the reluctance producing real work rather than mere hesitation.
The Workaround is dated at every occasion and is counted by nobody.
