# Process: The Free Refusal

## Taxonomy

[The Asking](process_act_asking.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: asking

## Initiated by

The other person could say no.
And both parties have to proceed as though that were entirely true.

## Direction

Toward a fiction that has to hold for the thing to work at all.
A request from somebody with standing is not fully declinable, a request between equals is more declinable than either will admit, and in both cases the parties treat the refusal as free, because the alternative is to say aloud what is actually in the room.
The fiction is load-bearing. Remove it and the request becomes an instruction, and the yes stops meaning anything.

## Lever

The necessity of the free refusal against the fact that it is partly untrue.
The carrying force is that it is mostly true and should be treated as true: people do decline, declining is ordinary, and a persona who assumes their requests cannot be refused becomes somebody whose requests should not be granted; the resistance is that standing does not switch off for the length of a favour, so an asker with more of it collects a yes worth less than they think and never finds out.
The Free Refusal makes the grant meaningful and is not equally available to everybody.

## Echo

A yes that could have been a no.
It shows why the engine's read depends on who is asking whom.
The Free Refusal is dated at the request and is assumed by both.
