# Process: The No Expected

## Taxonomy

[The Misestimate](process_misestimate_asking.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: asking

## Initiated by

The persona is fairly sure they will be turned down.
Not certain, and not anxious about it -- simply confident, the way one is confident about the weather.

## Direction

Toward a prediction that is wrong in a knowable direction.
The asker reasons from the helper's costs, which they can see, and not from the helper's position, which they cannot: that refusing a direct request to somebody's face is socially expensive, that most people would rather help than manage the awkwardness of declining, and that yes is frequently the path of least resistance.
This makes the prediction wrong for a reason the asker would find uncomfortable, which is part of why it goes uncorrected.

## Lever

The plausibility of the prediction against its consistency.
The carrying force is that the asker is not being pessimistic and is doing what any careful person does: estimating from the information available, which genuinely points that way; the resistance is that the estimate is off in the same direction every time, and somebody reliably wrong in one direction is not making an error of judgement so much as reading an instrument with a bias in it.
The No Expected is a forecast, not a fear.

## Echo

A number that decides whether anything happens.
It shows the engine as an estimation problem rather than a courage problem.
The No Expected is dated before the request and determines whether there is one.
