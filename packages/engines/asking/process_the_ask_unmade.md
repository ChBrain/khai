# Process: The Ask Unmade

## Taxonomy

[The Misestimate](process_misestimate_asking.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: asking

## Initiated by

The request is decided against and never becomes an event.
Nothing happens. Nobody declines anything. The persona does not experience having been refused, because they were not.

## Direction

Toward a large invisible population of yeses that were available.
Every unmade ask is a decision taken on a forecast and closed without a result, so there is no record and nothing to learn from, and the persona keeps the model that produced it intact.
This is where nearly all of the engine's volume sits, and it is the only part with no evidence trail whatsoever.

## Lever

The zero cost of not asking against the invisibility of what it cost.
The carrying force is that not asking is genuinely free at the moment it happens, imposes on nobody, and is the right call a great deal of the time, since most things a persona would like are not worth anybody's afternoon; the resistance is that a decision with no outcome cannot be evaluated, so unmade asks accumulate without ever producing the one thing that would revise the forecast, which is a yes.
The Ask Unmade is the engine's largest term and leaves no trace.

## Echo

Help that was available and stayed where it was.
It shows why the misestimate is stable across a lifetime.
The Ask Unmade is dated continuously and is recorded nowhere.
