# Process: Asking

## Taxonomy

Requesting (no khai file of its own yet).

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: asking

## Initiated by

A persona needs something they cannot get alone, and somebody else could give it.
Asking starts wherever help has to be requested rather than owed: the introduction, the loan, the cover for a shift, the read of a draft, the lift to the airport, the sentence that begins I don't suppose you could.
It is not the transfer that binds by naming no price, which is gift's, and not the obligation that transfer creates, which is debt's and the owing composite's. It is the request itself, put to somebody free to refuse.

## Direction

Toward a persona doing something they have been dreading and that is nearly always granted.
The engine reads four movements: [the reluctance](process_reluctance_asking.md), everything that happens instead of asking; [the misestimate](process_misestimate_asking.md), where the arithmetic goes wrong and in which direction; [the asking](process_act_asking.md), the act and its form; and [the granting](process_granting_asking.md), the twist -- what a request does to the person who receives it.
The four are one operation, and the first is much the largest.

## Lever

The cost the asker is certain they are imposing against the cost they are actually imposing.
The carrying force is that the reluctance is decent rather than neurotic: nobody wants to be a burden, other people's time is genuinely finite, and a persona who asked for everything they wanted would be an imposition and would be treated as one -- the restraint is a social virtue and it is part of why a request from them is worth granting; the resistance is that the restraint is priced off a figure the asker computes wrongly, in the same direction, every time, and the requests that were never made are invisible to everybody including them.
Asking is read on what was not asked for, never on what was.

## Echo

Somebody helped, and somebody pleased to have been asked.
Asking does not close; it is a request being placed, gathered into one reading -- a reluctance, a misestimate, an asking, a granting.
The engine's twist is here. **Asking feels like taking and lands like giving.** The asker experiences the request as incurring a debt, and the receiver experiences it as being handed something: a statement that they were judged capable, trusted, and worth approaching, followed by the particular satisfaction of having been useful. The two readings of the same act never meet, and the asker's is the one that decides whether it happens at all.
It hands off what it is not: the transfer that binds by naming no price is gift's; the obligation it creates is debt's and the owing composite's; the sustained care of somebody who needs it is caregiving's and compassion's; the techniques by which compliance is engineered are persuasion's and the swaying composite's; why nobody moves when a stranger collapses is bystander-effect's; and a request made to **stand for** a theme is authored key, the play's pitch.
The persona is dated from the last thing they needed and did without.
