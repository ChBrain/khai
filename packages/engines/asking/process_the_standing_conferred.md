# Process: The Standing Conferred

## Taxonomy

[The Granting](process_granting_asking.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: asking

## Initiated by

Being asked says something about the person asked.
That they were judged capable of this, trusted with it, and worth approaching.

## Direction

Toward a compliment that nobody had to pay.
A request is a public estimate of somebody's competence and reliability, issued by a person with no incentive to flatter, which makes it a more credible statement of regard than any compliment.
This is why being left out of a request is felt more sharply than being left out of a thank-you, and why the asker is handing over something valuable while apologising for it.

## Lever

The value of the estimate against the fact that standing is conserved.
The carrying force is that the asker did mean it in the only way that counts -- they picked this person out of everybody available because they thought they could do it, and a judgement made for practical reasons is the honest kind; the resistance is that the same act which raises the helper marks the asker as the one who could not, so a persona whose competence is under evaluation pays for a request that costs a secure persona nothing, and the engine's cheerful arithmetic is not equally available to everybody.
The Standing Conferred is the real content of the request and is transferred one way.

## Echo

Somebody told they are competent by being asked to prove it.
It shows why the engine runs opposite to the asker's expectation, and why it does not run the same for everybody.
The Standing Conferred is dated at the request and is never acknowledged.
