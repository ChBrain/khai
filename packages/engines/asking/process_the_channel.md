# Process: The Channel

## Taxonomy

[The Misestimate](process_misestimate_asking.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: asking

## Initiated by

The persona decides how to ask.
In person, or by message, or by a mail that can be ignored without anybody having to do anything about it.

## Direction

Toward a medium chosen to reduce an exposure, which reduces the request instead.
Asking face to face is far more likely to be granted than asking in writing, and askers do not know it -- because the thing that makes a request effective in person is exactly the thing they were trying to avoid.
So the persona optimises for their own comfort along a dimension they take to be neutral, and pays for it in the answer.

## Lever

The considerateness of the low-pressure channel against what it removes.
The carrying force is that a written request is genuinely more considerate: it lets the helper answer in their own time, decline without a scene, and think before committing, and a persona who cornered people in corridors would collect more yeses and fewer friends; the resistance is that those same properties are what make it declinable at no cost, so the persona has quietly traded the outcome for the manner and does not know a trade was made.
The Channel is chosen for the asker and paid for by the request.

## Echo

A request sent by the route least likely to work.
It shows the misestimate reaching into the mechanics.
The Channel is dated at the moment of sending.
