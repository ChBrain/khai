# Process: The Request Put

## Taxonomy

[The Asking](process_act_asking.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: asking

## Initiated by

The persona says what they want.
Or does not quite, which is the more common failure.

## Direction

Toward a specification somebody can act on.
A request has to name the thing, the size, and the deadline, and it has to be addressed to a person rather than to a room. Every one of those is uncomfortable to supply, because each converts a vague hope into something that can be refused precisely.
So requests degrade toward the general, and the general cannot be granted.

## Lever

The safety of vagueness against its uselessness.
The carrying force is that a soft request is considerate and often correct: it lets the other person offer what suits them, it does not presume on their week, and a persona who issues specifications to their friends is a persona with fewer friends; the resistance is that nobody can act on a hope, so the vague request produces a warm reply and nothing else, and both parties file the exchange as having gone well.
The Request Put has to be refusable to be grantable.

## Echo

Something specific enough to be done.
It shows the act's real difficulty, which is precision rather than nerve.
The Request Put is dated to the specific version.
