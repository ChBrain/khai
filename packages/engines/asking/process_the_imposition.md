# Process: The Imposition

## Taxonomy

[The Reluctance](process_reluctance_asking.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: asking

## Initiated by

The persona pictures what the request would cost the other person.
The interruption, the hour, the awkwardness, the thing they would have to move.

## Direction

Toward a vivid and one-sided estimate.
The asker can see their own request in full detail and cannot see the other side of it at all: how small it is against the helper's day, how often the helper does this sort of thing, how little they will remember it by Friday.
So the cost is imagined at high resolution and the ease is not imagined at all.

## Lever

The accuracy of the picture against the incompleteness of it.
The carrying force is that the imposition is real and the persona is right to weigh it: an hour is an hour, the interruption is genuine, and somebody who never considered it would be the person everybody learns to avoid; the resistance is that the weighing is done entirely from the asker's side of the table, where the request is the whole foreground, and the helper's side -- where it is one item among many and a fairly agreeable one -- is not available to be weighed.
The Imposition is calculated with only one of the two figures it needs.

## Echo

A cost that is real and overstated.
It shows the reluctance's arithmetic at the point where it goes wrong.
The Imposition is dated from the moment help is needed.
