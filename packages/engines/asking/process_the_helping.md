# Process: The Helping

## Taxonomy

[The Granting](process_granting_asking.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: asking

## Initiated by

Somebody does the thing.
And finds, slightly to their own surprise, that they enjoyed it.

## Direction

Toward a helper in a better mood than they started in.
Doing somebody a favour lifts the doer: it produces the particular satisfaction of competence spent well, and it tends to make the helper think better of the person they helped than they did before, which runs opposite to everybody's expectation.
The effect does not require the favour to be large. It requires it to have been asked for.

## Lever

The genuineness of the helper's pleasure against how completely it is discounted.
The carrying force is that it is real rather than a consolation: helpers report it, they volunteer again, and the person who says it was no trouble at all is usually reporting accurately rather than being polite; the resistance is that the asker holds a strong prior saying otherwise, so the helper's evident good humour is filed as manners and the correction never lands.
The Helping is visible to everybody except the person who needed to see it.

## Echo

A favour that improved the day of the person who did it.
It shows the twist at its plainest.
The Helping is dated from the doing.
