# Process: The Misestimate

## Taxonomy

[Asking](process_asking.md), where the arithmetic goes wrong, and in which direction.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: asking

## Initiated by

The asker predicts the answer, and predicts it low.
The Misestimate starts at the engine's best-measured fact: [a no expected](process_the_no_expected.md) far more often than a no arrives, [asks never made](process_the_ask_unmade.md) on the strength of it, and [a channel](process_the_channel.md) chosen to reduce an exposure that was not there.

## Direction

Toward a systematic error that runs one way.
People asked to predict how many strangers will agree to a small request underestimate, substantially and repeatedly, across favours, donations, and requests for help at work.
The error is not noise. It is directional, it is large, and it survives the persona having been wrong about it before.

## Lever

The reasonableness of expecting a no against the evidence that it does not come.
The carrying force is that expecting refusal is a sensible prior in a world of busy people, protects the asker from becoming a nuisance, and is exactly the caution that makes an unhedged request from them worth taking seriously; the resistance is that the prior is measurably wrong and the persona has no way to find out, because the counter-evidence -- the yes they would have received -- is produced only by asking.
The Misestimate is self-sealing: it prevents the experiment that would correct it.

## Echo

An accurate person with an inaccurate number.
It is the movement the engine's evidence actually rests on.
The Misestimate is dated from the first refusal and is not revised by any subsequent yes.
