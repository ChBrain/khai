# Position: General

## Taxonomy

[Just-World Belief](position_just_world_belief.md), the world assumed fair to all -- the judgment on the other.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: belief-in-a-just-world

## Has

A specific [just-world belief](position_just_world_belief.md): the conviction that the world is fair to people in general -- that others, too, get what they deserve (Dalbert's general just-world belief).
The harsher, other-directed face of the worldview: not a trust about oneself but a verdict on everyone's fate, that the fortunes and misfortunes of others are earned.
It is the face that justifies the way things are and hardens the persona toward those who suffer.

## Orders

Read the other's fate as earned.
The general belief orders the persona to credit the successful with deserving their success and the suffering with having brought it on themselves -- to endorse the standing order as basically fair, and to meet the disadvantaged with less sympathy and more suspicion of fault.
It settles the world's inequalities into a ledger already balanced, each station occupied by desert.

## Loses

The sight of undeserved suffering, and the pull to relieve it.
Holding the world fair to all, the persona cannot see the innocent victim as innocent -- confronted with a suffering it cannot undo, it defends the belief by finding the fault that must be there, derogating or blaming the victim to keep the world just -- and forgoes the compassion and the sense of injustice that the plain sight of undeserved harm would move.
What it trades is the accurate, uncomfortable view of a partly-unjust world for a world whose order needs no reproach, and pays in hardness toward its casualties.

## Drives

On that general belief:
rides it -- endorses the order, credits the fortunate, and, meeting an innocent victim, preserves the world's fairness by concluding the victim deserved it -- or works against it, admitting that harm falls unearned and the order is not just, and paying the disquiet and the demand for redress that admission brings.
The other-directed baseline holds underneath either way, the world assumed fair to all rather than, like the [personal](position_personal.md) face, to oneself. The specific attribution by which the victim is searched for the flaw is the bias engine's just-world-hypothesis member; this face owns the standing general belief that drives it.
