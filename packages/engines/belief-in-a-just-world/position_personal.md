# Position: Personal

## Taxonomy

[Just-World Belief](position_just_world_belief.md), the world assumed fair to me -- the self's resource.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: belief-in-a-just-world

## Has

A specific [just-world belief](position_just_world_belief.md): the conviction that the world is fair to me -- that my own efforts will be justly rewarded and my own fate is deserved (Dalbert's personal just-world belief).
The adaptive, self-directed face of the worldview: not a judgment on others but a trust about oneself, that the world will settle its accounts in one's own case.
It is one of the more robust psychological resources there is -- a buffer that sustains wellbeing, hope, and trust under strain.

## Orders

Trust the world with your effort.
The personal belief orders the persona to invest and to wait: to defer gratification, bear a present cost, and commit to long effort, confident the world will repay it justly -- and to meet its own setbacks as temporary or deserved rather than as proof the game is rigged.
It steadies the persona from within, letting it strive as if the return were assured.

## Loses

The guardedness of a persona that expects no fairness for itself.
Holding the world fair to it, the persona forgoes the wariness that would protect it from a world that may not repay -- it can be blindsided when its just deserts do not come, and slow to see a system genuinely stacked against it -- and it stakes its striving on a trust that reality does not always honour.
What it trades is defensive realism for the resource of hope: the buffer holds it up, and can also set it up.

## Drives

On that personal belief:
rides it -- trusts, invests, defers, and bears strain on the confidence that its own effort will be justly repaid -- or, when its own deserts fail to come, works to preserve the belief, reading the setback as temporary or earned, or at cost gives it up and carries the harder view that its own fate may be undeserved.
The self-directed baseline holds underneath either way, the world assumed fair to oneself rather than, like the [general](position_general.md) face, to all.
