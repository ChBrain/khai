# Position: Just-World Belief

## Taxonomy

Worldview / a persona's standing conviction that the world is fair (no khai file of its own).

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: belief-in-a-just-world

## Has

A standing assumption that the world is fair -- that people get what they deserve and deserve what they get.
Not a single verdict but a resting worldview (Lerner): effort is rewarded, suffering is earned, the moral books balance -- the belief a persona leans on to invest in the future and trust its world, and defends when reality threatens it.
It runs to two audiences at once: the world is fair to me, and the world is fair to people in general -- validated as separable beliefs, the one a resource for the self, the other a judgment on the other.

## Orders

Read fortune and misfortune as deserved.
The belief tilts the persona to credit the lucky with having earned their luck and to search the unlucky for the fault that explains their fate -- and, turned inward, to trust that its own effort will be justly repaid, so it can defer reward and bear a present cost.
The room reads the assumption of a fair world beneath the persona's readings, before it names a particular judgment.

## Loses

The plain fact of undeserved fortune and undeserved harm.
A persona holding the world fair cannot leave a misfortune sitting unexplained by its victim, nor a windfall unearned -- it forgoes the vertigo of a world where the innocent suffer and the wicked prosper for no reason -- and buys, at that price, the security and the motive to strive that the belief supplies.
What it trades is an accurate view of a partly-random world for a world it can trust and invest in, and defends the trade when an innocent victim puts it in question.

## Drives

On that standing belief:
rides it -- trusts, invests, defers, and reads desert into fortune both ways -- or, when an innocent victim's suffering it cannot undo threatens the belief, defends it, most often by concluding the victim deserved it, or works against it, holding that harm can fall unearned and paying the insecurity that admission costs.
The conviction holds underneath either way, in one of two faces: [personal](position_personal.md), the world fair to me, the self's resource; or [general](position_general.md), the world fair to all, the judgment on the other. The specific victim-derogating attribution -- the sufferer searched for the flaw -- is the bias engine's just-world-hypothesis member; the pleasure at a deserved misfortune is the schadenfreude engine's; the neutral evaluation of one's standing against others is the comparison engine's; the fairness intuition applied to a particular act is the moral-judgment engine's. This engine owns the standing belief that the world is fair, and its two faces.
