# Process: The Distrust

## Taxonomy

[The Warrant](process_warrant_measure.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: measure

## Initiated by

Somebody's word is not going to be enough.
The Distrust starts at Porter's observation: quantification concentrates where personal judgement is suspect, where the decider is a stranger to those affected, and where a decision will have to be defended to people who were not there.

## Direction

Toward objectivity of a particular kind -- the kind that means the absence of a person.
This is mechanical objectivity: following rules rather than exercising discretion, precisely so that the answer does not depend on who is answering.
It is adopted by the weak far more often than imposed by the strong. A profession under attack reaches for numbers to defend itself, and the numbers are the terms of the surrender.

## Lever

The protection of a rule-governed answer against what adopting it concedes.
The carrying force is that this is a real defence and frequently the right one: an official who can show a figure cannot be accused of favouritism, an applicant is spared the whim of whoever is at the desk, and a public entitled to check is given something that can be checked -- discretion is not obviously better and is much harder to appeal; the resistance is that the arrangement's whole purpose is to make the practitioner's judgement dispensable, so adopting it announces that their judgement was not going to be trusted, and demonstrating competence afterwards cannot retract the announcement.
The Distrust is the engine's twist arriving as an institutional decision.

## Echo

An answer nobody has to be trusted for.
It shows why measurement arrives when it does, which is almost never at a moment of confidence.
The Distrust is dated from the controversy that preceded the metric, which everybody has forgotten.
