# Process: The False Precision

## Taxonomy

[The Cut](process_cut_measure.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: measure

## Initiated by

The number has more decimal places than the thing has definition.
The False Precision starts wherever the output's exactness exceeds the input's: a rank to a single place computed from judgements that were approximate, a score to two decimals resting on a category somebody assigned by eye.

## Direction

Toward confidence produced by format.
Precision reads as accuracy. A figure given closely is treated as known closely, and the intermediate steps -- the estimate, the proxy, the assumption, the missing data filled by an average -- do not travel with it.
By the time it is used, it is one number.

## Lever

The usefulness of a definite figure against the definiteness being manufactured.
The carrying force is that a decision needs a single answer and somebody has to produce one, so collapsing uncertainty into a point estimate is not dishonest, it is what makes the figure actionable -- an interval nobody can act on is not more truthful in practice; the resistance is that the collapse is invisible downstream, so a figure assembled from three guesses and a proxy arrives looking exactly like one that was counted, and there is nothing about its appearance that would let a reader tell.
The False Precision is where the format outranks the provenance.

## Echo

An exact answer to a question that was never that exact.
It is the movement's smallest member and the one that does most of the work in an argument.
The False Precision is dated from the report, and its assumptions are dated from a spreadsheet nobody opens.
