# Process: The Common Scale

## Taxonomy

[The Cut](process_cut_measure.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: measure

## Initiated by

Things that had no relation are given one.
The Common Scale starts at the translation: a hospital and a hospital, a candidate and a candidate, a harm and a harm, expressed in a unit that applies to both.

## Direction

Toward comparison where none was previously possible.
The scale does something genuinely new. Before it there were incommensurable particulars and afterwards there is an ordering, and the ordering supports arguments, allocations, and appeals that simply could not be made before.
It also feels like a discovery rather than a construction, which is part of how it works.

## Lever

The new relation against the fact that somebody made it.
The carrying force is that the relation is useful and frequently more honest than what preceded it: an unexaminable claim of quality is not more subtle than a crude index, it is merely unaccountable, and the scale gives an outsider a purchase they had no other way of getting; the resistance is that a constructed relation presents as a found one -- nobody encounters the scale alongside the decisions that produced it, so the choice of unit becomes a fact about the world within a very short time.
The Common Scale is a decision that stops looking like one.

## Echo

An ordering that now seems to have been there all along.
It is the movement's product and the thing every later member operates on.
The Common Scale is dated from its adoption and remembered as having no author.
