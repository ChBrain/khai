# Process: Goodhart's Collapse

## Taxonomy

[The Turn](process_turn_measure.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: measure

## Initiated by

The measure has become a target.
Goodhart's Collapse starts the moment a figure acquires consequences: pay, ranking, funding, promotion, survival.

## Direction

Toward an indicator that no longer indicates.
The relationship the measure relied on held while nobody was optimising against it. Once they are, effort moves to the indicator, and the correlation between the indicator and the thing it stood for comes apart.
The measure does not become inaccurate about itself. It becomes accurate about itself and uninformative about anything else.

## Lever

The necessity of consequences against what consequences do to a measure.
The carrying force is that a metric nobody acts on is a metric that changes nothing, so the bite is the point -- and a well-chosen target genuinely does redirect effort to something that was being neglected, which is why the technique keeps being used and frequently works for a while; the resistance is that the bite recruits everybody's ingenuity toward the indicator, so a measure's usefulness and its decay come from the same mechanism and cannot be had separately.
Goodhart's Collapse means a good measure has a shelf life set by how seriously it is taken.

## Echo

A number still being reported, no longer reporting.
It is the best-known member of the engine and the least often acted on.
Goodhart's Collapse is dated from the first time the figure mattered.
