# Process: The Practical Knowledge Lost

## Taxonomy

[The Territory](process_territory_measure.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: measure

## Initiated by

The thing that was holding it together has no column.
The Practical Knowledge Lost starts at the category the grid cannot register: Scott's metis, the local, unsystematic, experience-built knowing that tells somebody when to plant, which stretch floods, who can be relied on, and what this machine does when it makes that sound.

## Direction

Toward a system that works less well and cannot say why.
The knowledge was never written down, was held by particular people in particular places, and was invisible to any survey -- so it is not defended when the reorganisation comes, because nobody can produce it as evidence.
It leaves with the people. Nothing marks its departure.

## Lever

The reality of the knowledge against its total unavailability as an argument.
The carrying force is that this knowledge is genuine, effective, and irreplaceable in exactly the cases that matter, and that its holders are usually right -- which is why the same finding turns up wherever anybody looks closely at how work actually gets done; the resistance is that it cannot be entered into the record on its own behalf: to defend it you must state it, stating it makes it something else, and until it is stated it will lose every argument against a figure.
The Practical Knowledge Lost is the residue again, at the scale of a society.

## Echo

A reorganised system, and nobody left who could have said what was wrong with it.
It shows the engine's cost in the only form the engine can show it: as an absence discovered later.
This is where the measure engine and the expertise engine meet, since what is lost here is the same tacit competence that could never be handed over in words.
The Practical Knowledge Lost is dated from the reorganisation and noticed at the first thing that goes wrong for no reason.
