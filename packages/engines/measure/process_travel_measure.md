# Process: The Travel

## Taxonomy

[The Warrant](process_warrant_measure.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: measure

## Initiated by

The finding has to go somewhere the finder cannot.
The Travel starts at distance: a judgement that must be acted on by people who did not make it, in a place the maker has never been, about a case they will never see.

## Direction

Toward a form that survives transport.
A number moves and a judgement does not. It can be aggregated, filed, compared with another organisation's, revisited in ten years, and used by somebody with no way to evaluate the person who produced it.
This is the property that makes large-scale coordination possible at all.

## Lever

The reach of a portable finding against what portability requires it to shed.
The carrying force is that nothing else travels: an organisation of any size, a state, a market, or a science is only possible because findings can be moved without their makers, and every attempt to run something large on unmediated judgement collapses at the second remove; the resistance is that portability is bought by stripping the context that would let a recipient know when the figure does not apply -- so the further a number travels the more confidently it is used and the less anybody along the way can tell whether it should be.
The Travel is why the people best placed to doubt a figure are the ones who never see it.

## Echo

A finding in use a long way from anybody who could check it.
It shows measurement's genuine indispensability, which is the half of the engine most easily lost.
The Travel is dated from the moment the figure left the room it was made in.
