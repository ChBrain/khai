# Process: The Simplification

## Taxonomy

[The Territory](process_territory_measure.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: measure

## Initiated by

An authority needs to see something it cannot stand next to.
The Simplification starts at distance again, but on the ground rather than in a report: a state, an agency, or a head office needing to count, tax, allocate, or plan across a territory it cannot inspect.

## Direction

Toward a version of the world with a small number of legible facts in it.
Local practice is irregular by nature -- names that vary by village, measures that change with the crop, tenure that depends on who remembers what. None of it can be administered.
So a standard is imposed: fixed surnames, uniform units, registered title, mapped plots. The world becomes countable by becoming different.

## Lever

What legibility makes possible against the irregularity it must destroy.
The carrying force has to be said first and said fully: standardisation is the precondition of nearly everything worth defending about modern life -- public health, non-arbitrary taxation, land title a stranger can rely on, relief that arrives where the famine is, contracts between people who never meet. Every one of them requires seeing at a distance, and there is no seeing at a distance without simplifying; the resistance is that the simplification is an instrument rather than a lens, so the irregularity is not merely unrecorded, it is removed -- and the irregularity was frequently doing something nobody had written down.
The Simplification is indispensable and it is not free.

## Echo

A territory that can now be governed.
It is the movement's premise and the reason the engine will not be read as a complaint about counting.
The Simplification is dated from the survey.
