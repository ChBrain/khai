# Process: The Warrant

## Taxonomy

[Measure](process_measure.md), why a number rather than a judgement.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: measure

## Initiated by

Somebody needs an answer that does not rest on their word.
The Warrant starts at the question the engine's twist answers: why quantify at all, when the person doing the quantifying frequently knows the answer already?
Three reasons carry it -- [distrust](process_distrust_engine.md), [travel](process_travel_measure.md), and [answerability](process_answerability.md).

## Direction

Toward a decision that can be defended without the decider.
A number is a judgement stripped of the person who made it, which is precisely its value: it can be sent, audited, compared with another organisation's, and defended in a room where nobody knows or trusts the author.
That is why quantification concentrates in exactly the places one might expect judgement to be strongest -- large organisations, professions under scrutiny, decisions that will be contested.

## Lever

The protection a number offers against what reaching for it concedes.
The carrying force is that this is defensive and legitimate and mostly good: an official who can point to a rule-governed figure is protected from the accusation of favouritism, the applicant is protected from the official's whim, and a public entitled to check is given something checkable -- objectivity of this kind was fought for and is worth having; the resistance is that the whole apparatus is built to make the practitioner's judgement unnecessary, so adopting it says publicly that their judgement was not going to be enough, and no amount of subsequent good practice retracts that.
The Warrant is a defence that is also an admission.

## Echo

An answer that will survive being questioned by strangers.
It shows why measurement spreads and why it spreads fastest where suspicion is highest.
The Warrant is dated from the first time somebody's word was not accepted.
