# Process: The Reactivity

## Taxonomy

[The Turn](process_turn_measure.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: measure

## Initiated by

The measured have reorganised themselves around the measurement.
The Reactivity starts where Espeland and Sauder found it: institutions being ranked did not merely present themselves differently, they rebuilt -- changing admissions, spending, staffing, and priorities to move on a scale nobody inside them endorsed.

## Direction

Toward an organisation shaped by an index it does not believe in.
The mechanism is not cynicism. Administrators who thought the ranking crude still had to answer for it, so it became the thing decisions were justified against, and after enough years the institution was genuinely different.
Self-fulfilling and reverse-engineered at once: the rank changed the place, and the place then confirmed the rank.

## Lever

The accountability a public ranking creates against the reorganisation it forces.
The carrying force is that external measurement of powerful institutions is one of the few levers outsiders have, and its arrival frequently ends genuine complacency -- the institutions complaining loudest about being ranked are not always the ones with the best case; the resistance is that the reorganisation follows the index rather than the purpose, so a place can improve steadily on a scale while getting worse at what it is for, and every internal argument for the neglected part now has to be made against a number.
The Reactivity is where measurement stops observing and starts governing.

## Echo

An institution that has become what it was scored on.
It shows the turn at its largest scale and is the bridge to the territory.
The Reactivity is dated from the first published table.
