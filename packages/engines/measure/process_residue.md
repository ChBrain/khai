# Process: The Residue

## Taxonomy

[The Cut](process_cut_measure.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: measure

## Initiated by

Something did not survive the translation.
The Residue starts with whatever about the compared things would not go onto the scale: the difficult intake, the slow work, the case that took a year, the quality nobody has a unit for.

## Direction

Toward absence rather than acknowledged omission.
The residue is not recorded as a caveat. It is recorded nowhere, so within a short time the scale is the object and what fell off it has to be argued back into the conversation by somebody who remembers it existed.
And the argument is structurally weak, because it is a claim without a number against a number.

## Lever

The necessity of leaving things out against what leaving them out does to them.
The carrying force is that every measure must exclude and this is not a flaw: a scale that captured everything would capture nothing, and the discipline of deciding what matters is exactly what makes measurement useful; the resistance is that exclusion and non-existence become the same thing operationally -- resources, attention, and defence all follow the recorded, so what is unrecorded is not merely undervalued, it is unfunded, unexamined, and unavailable to anybody arguing for it.
The Residue is the engine's quietest damage and its most reliable.

## Echo

A category that has stopped being a category.
It is what a play should show and what the personas inside the play cannot see.
The Residue is dated from the same moment as the scale, and it is noticed only when something fails.
