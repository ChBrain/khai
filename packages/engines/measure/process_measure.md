# Process: Measure

## Taxonomy

Knowledge (no khai file of its own yet).

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: measure

## Initiated by

Somebody has decided that a quality will be handled as a quantity.
Measure starts wherever unlike things are put on one scale so they can be compared, ranked, allocated between, or answered for -- the score, the rating, the index, the target, the league table.
It is not the general equivalent that converts anything into anything, which is money's, and not the readable image of a place, which is legibility's. It is the operation of turning a judgement into a number, and everything that follows from having done so.

## Direction

Toward a world reorganised around what it turned out to be possible to count.
The engine reads four movements: [the cut](process_cut_measure.md), what is put on the scale and what is left off it; [the warrant](process_warrant_measure.md), why a number is reached for rather than an opinion; [the turn](process_turn_measure.md), what measuring does to the thing measured; and [the territory](process_territory_measure.md), the map that ends up rebuilding the ground.
The four run together and none of them is optional once the first has happened.

## Lever

The reach of a number against what it had to leave behind to travel.
The carrying force is that measurement is one of the great instruments and the engine should not be read as against it: a common scale lets strangers compare, lets resources go where they are needed rather than where somebody is liked, exposes discrimination that testimony could not, and permits an argument to be settled by something other than who is speaking; the resistance is that the scale is made by discarding whatever would not fit it, and what was discarded does not become visible again -- it becomes, for every purpose the number is used for, absent.
Measure is read on what fell off the scale, never on the accuracy of what stayed.

## Echo

A world in which the countable has quietly become the whole of what there is.
Measure does not close; it is a quality being converted, gathered into one reading -- a cut, a warrant, a turn, a territory.
The engine's twist is here, and it is Porter's. **A number is trusted in proportion to how little the people producing it are trusted.** Quantification is a technology of distrust: it flourishes exactly where personal judgement has become suspect, where the decider is a stranger, or where a decision must be defended to somebody who was not there -- so the arrival of metrics is a symptom of a collapse in confidence rather than a gain in rigour. And it cannot be undone by competence, because not having to rely on anybody's competence is what the number was brought in for.
It hands off what it is not: the general equivalent and its earmarking are money's; the readable image of a place is legibility's; measurement as a form of control in the effort bargain is employment's; the expert's own reading of a hazard is risk's; the practical knowledge that cannot be stated is expertise's; a persona measuring themselves against a standard is comparison's and selfhood's; and a metric made to **stand for** a theme is authored key, the play's pitch, not the force.
The persona is dated from the first time somebody asked them for the figure.
