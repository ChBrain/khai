# Process: The Turn

## Taxonomy

[Measure](process_measure.md), what measuring does to the measured.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: measure

## Initiated by

The thing being measured has noticed.
The Turn starts as soon as the scale has consequences: [Goodhart's](process_goodhart.md) collapse of a measure that becomes a target, the [reactivity](process_reactivity.md) of the measured reorganising around the metric, and the [self-fulfilment](process_self_fulfilling.md) of a number that produces the reality it claimed to report.

## Direction

Toward a measure that is no longer measuring what it was chosen for.
Nothing here requires anybody to cheat. A persona told what counts will attend to what counts, which is the correct response to an instruction, and the attending is what degrades the measure.
The better the incentive is aligned, the faster the degradation.

## Lever

The usefulness of a measure that moves people against the fact that moving people is what breaks it.
The carrying force is that a metric with no consequences is a metric nobody acts on, so a measure has to bite to be worth having -- and biting genuinely does redirect effort toward things that were being neglected, which is frequently the entire point and frequently works; the resistance is that the same bite recruits the ingenuity of everybody measured, and their ingenuity is applied to the indicator rather than to the thing, so a measure's usefulness and its decay are produced by one mechanism and cannot be separated.
The Turn means a good measure has a shelf life set by how seriously it is taken.

## Echo

An indicator that is still being reported and no longer reports anything.
It shows why measurement regimes churn: each metric is replaced not because it was wrong but because it worked.
The Turn is dated from the first time the number had a consequence attached.
