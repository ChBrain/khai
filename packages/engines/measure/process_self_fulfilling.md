# Process: The Self-Fulfilment

## Taxonomy

[The Turn](process_turn_measure.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: measure

## Initiated by

The number is producing what it claimed to describe.
The Self-Fulfilment starts wherever the measure's own consequences generate the evidence for it: the credit score that determines the terms that determine the repayment, the risk rating that raises the cost that causes the failure, the assessment that sets the placement that produces the result.

## Direction

Toward a prediction that has arranged its own confirmation.
The loop is closed and nothing about it looks wrong from inside: the figure was computed correctly, the consequence was applied consistently, and the outcome matched.
The validation is real and it is circular, and the two are indistinguishable in the record.

## Lever

The genuine predictive record against how the record was produced.
The carrying force is that these systems often are predictive and are not superstition: the model found something, the something is real, and abandoning it would return the decision to whim -- which was worse and was frequently worse in the same direction; the resistance is that once the figure allocates the resources, it cannot be tested against a world in which it did not, so nobody can distinguish a measure that predicts an outcome from one that produces it, and the better the record looks the less informative it is.
The Self-Fulfilment is where a measure becomes unfalsifiable by working.

## Echo

A model with an excellent record and no way to be wrong.
It is the turn's hardest member and the one with the most consequences outside the play.
The Self-Fulfilment is dated from the first decision the number was allowed to make.
