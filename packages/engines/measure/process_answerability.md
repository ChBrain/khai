# Process: The Answerability

## Taxonomy

[The Warrant](process_warrant_measure.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: measure

## Initiated by

Somebody is going to have to account for this.
The Answerability starts wherever a decision must be justified afterwards -- to an auditor, a court, a board, a public -- and the justification must take a form the challenger will accept.

## Direction

Toward decisions shaped by what will defend well.
Once a persona knows what they will be asked for, they begin producing it in advance: the documented reason, the scored assessment, the figure that shows the process was followed.
The decision and the defence of the decision stop being separable, and the second increasingly determines the first.

## Lever

The value of being answerable against what answerability selects for.
The carrying force is that accountability is not bureaucratic overhead but the thing that makes power bearable: an authority that need not justify itself is worse than one drowning in paperwork, and the ability to demand a reason is among the more important protections anybody has; the resistance is that only some reasons are acceptable currency, so a persona facing a decision where the defensible option and the right option differ is under real pressure to take the defensible one -- and will be praised for it either way the case turns out.
The Answerability makes power justify itself and teaches it which justifications work.

## Echo

A decision that will survive review, whatever it does in the world.
It is the movement's most double-edged member and the one closest to ordinary working life.
The Answerability is dated from the last inquiry, and it tightens after every one.
