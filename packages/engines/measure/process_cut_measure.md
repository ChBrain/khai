# Process: The Cut

## Taxonomy

[Measure](process_measure.md), what goes on the scale and what does not.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: measure

## Initiated by

Unlike things are about to be made comparable.
The Cut starts at commensuration: the decision that these different things share a dimension, and that their differences along it are the differences that matter.
Hospitals by mortality, schools by results, candidates by score, harms by cost.

## Direction

Toward a [common scale](process_common_scale.md), a [residue](process_residue.md), and a [precision](process_false_precision.md) the underlying thing does not have.
Commensuration is a genuine intellectual achievement and an act of destruction in the same motion: it produces a relation between things that had none, and it does so by suppressing everything about them that would have prevented the relation.
The suppression is not concealed. It is simply not recorded anywhere.

## Lever

The power of comparability against the cost of having produced it.
The carrying force is that comparison is what makes fairness operable: without a common scale there is no way to say that this hospital is worse than that one, no way to allocate by need rather than by pull, and no way for an outsider to check anything at all -- the alternative to a crude measure is usually not a subtle one but an unexaminable claim; the resistance is that the differences dropped in the translation do not persist as caveats, they persist as nothing, so within a year the scale is the object and the residue is a thing people have to be reminded exists.
The Cut buys a relation and pays for it in everything that was not the relation.

## Echo

Two things now comparable, and both of them thinner.
It is the movement that makes the other three possible, and the only one that is a choice.
The Cut is dated from whenever somebody first decided what would count.
