# Process: The Territory

## Taxonomy

[Measure](process_measure.md), the map that rebuilds the ground, and where the engine is seen for one thing.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: measure

## Initiated by

The ground is being altered to match the record of it.
The Territory starts where Scott found it: an authority that needs to see at a distance imposes a [simplification](process_simplification.md), lays down an [imposed grid](process_imposed_grid.md), and finds the world easier to administer because the world has been rebuilt to fit the abstraction.
And what the grid cannot see is [the practical knowledge](process_metis_lost.md) that was doing the work.

## Direction

Toward a territory that has come to resemble its own map.
The sequence is the engine's largest claim and its best evidenced: the standardised measure does not merely describe badly, it succeeds -- the forest is replanted in rows, the names are regularised, the plots are squared, and afterwards the simplification is accurate because reality was made to agree with it.
The map won by changing the ground.

## Lever

What legibility makes possible against what it takes out of the world.
The carrying force is genuinely large and must be said first: standardised measure is the precondition of most things worth having -- public health, taxation that is not extortion, land title a stranger can rely on, famine relief that arrives, a currency, a contract enforceable between people who have never met. Every one of those requires seeing at a distance, and seeing at a distance requires simplifying; the resistance is that the simplification is not a lens but an instrument, so what it cannot register gets removed rather than merely overlooked -- and the local practical knowledge that was quietly holding the system together is exactly the category the grid has no column for.
The Territory rides the engine's keystone knife-edge: a number is trusted in proportion to how little the people producing it are trusted, so the measure arrives where local judgement is doubted and then removes the conditions under which local judgement could be demonstrated.

## Echo

A world that now confirms the account somebody made of it.
It shows the four movements complete -- a cut, a warrant, a turn, and finally a ground rebuilt to match -- and it is where the engine stops being about numbers.
This is why the Territory is the hardest of the four to stage and the only one worth closing on: the reform must be well intentioned, the simplification must be genuinely necessary, the administrators must be competent, and the audience must be able to see what has gone missing without anybody in the play being able to name it.
A play that supplies a cynical official has replaced the mechanism with a motive.
The Territory is dated from the survey, and it is discovered a generation later by somebody wondering why nothing works.
