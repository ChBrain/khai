# Instructions: Measure

How a Playwright wires the measure engine into a play. You wire by linking the play's own content to the engine's files; the engine files stay untouched, they are canon. The Roadie plumbs the engine's law into Instructions Knowledge on deploy. Authoring guidance, not runtime content, and it does not go on tour.

## Human

- The human says what the number is for before saying what it costs. Without the case for measurement staged first, the engine reads as a complaint about counting, which is neither what the sources say nor what is true. Then decide what fell off the scale, since that is where the drama is: the thing nobody can argue for because it has no figure. And remember whoever introduced the metric was almost certainly acting defensively and in good faith.

## Agent

- From the play's own content, a persona links under Projection their position in the operation: what they are measured on, what they measure, and which movement is doing the work on them. Read the engine on what was discarded rather than on whether the figure is accurate. Keep it the operation: the general equivalent is money's, the readable image of a place is legibility's, measurement as a control form in the wage relation is employment's, and a persona scoring themselves against a standard is comparison's. You link to the engine, you never edit it.

## Collaboration

- The general equivalent and its earmarking are the money engine's; the readable image of a place is legibility's, which is Lynch's sense of the word rather than Scott's; measurement as one way an employer closes the effort bargain is employment's; the expert's count of a hazard is risk's; knowledge that cannot be stated is expertise's; a persona measuring themselves against a standard is comparison's and selfhood's; tilts in handling numbers are bias's and availability's; what being watched and counted makes of a persona is discipline's and subjection's. Whether a production's metric has actually collapsed under Goodhart, and whether a lost competence was practical knowledge or merely habit, are the team's review.

## Knowledge

- Measure is the operation of turning a judgement into a number and everything that follows from having done so. It wires on a persona at Projection and runs in four movements, together rather than in sequence. The cut is commensuration: a common scale produces a relation between things that had none by suppressing everything that would have prevented it, and because nobody encounters the scale alongside the decisions that made it, a construction is read as a discovery within a very short time. What would not translate is the residue, and it is not recorded as a caveat but as nothing -- so exclusion and non-existence become the same thing operationally, since resources, attention, and defence all follow the recorded. False precision completes it: format outranks provenance, and a figure assembled from estimates arrives looking like one that was counted. The warrant answers why quantify at all: a number is a judgement stripped of the person who made it, so it travels where a judgement cannot -- which makes coordination at scale possible and strips the context that would tell a distant user when it does not apply -- and it supports answerability, which makes power justify itself and teaches it which justifications are currency. Above all it answers distrust: mechanical objectivity is adopted by the weak more often than imposed by the strong, and a profession under attack reaching for numbers is accepting the terms of a surrender. The turn is what measuring does to the measured: Goodhart's collapse, where a measure's usefulness and its decay come from one mechanism so a good measure has a shelf life set by how seriously it is taken; reactivity, where ranked institutions rebuild around an index nobody inside them endorses, not from cynicism but because they must answer for it; and self-fulfilment, where a measure that allocates resources can no longer be tested against a world in which it did not, so prediction and production become indistinguishable and the better the record looks the less it tells you. The territory is Scott's: simplification is the precondition of public health, non-arbitrary taxation, reliable title, and relief that arrives -- and the imposed grid stops describing and starts prescribing, so afterwards the map is accurate because the ground was rebuilt to agree with it. What the grid has no column for is practical knowledge, which cannot be entered into the record on its own behalf, since to defend it you must state it and stating it makes it something else. The engine's twist is at the root: a number is trusted in proportion to how little the people producing it are trusted, so the arrival of metrics is a symptom of collapsed confidence rather than a gain in rigour, and cannot be undone by competence.

## System

- Do make the case for the measure first and fully, in every movement. A play that skips it has staged a grievance rather than the engine.
- Do put the drama in the residue: somebody arguing for a thing that has no number, against a thing that does, and losing for structural reasons rather than because they are wrong.
- Do let the metric bite. A measure with no consequences changes nothing, and the same bite that makes it useful is what recruits everybody's ingenuity toward the indicator -- so the decay should look like competence rather than cheating.
- Do let reactivity happen without cynics: administrators who think the ranking crude still have to answer for it, and after enough years the institution is genuinely different.
- Do stage the territory as a success before it is a cost. The grid yields more, the resettlement gets people water, and the failures arrive together and late, when the knowledge that would have caught them has already gone.
- Do keep the metric's author sympathetic. They are almost certainly defending themselves or their institution against a real accusation, and a play that supplies a cynical administrator has replaced the mechanism with a motive.
- Do not model the general equivalent (money), the readable image of a place (legibility), metric control in the wage relation (employment), the expert's count (risk), tacit knowledge from the practitioner's side (expertise), self-scoring (comparison, selfhood), or number-handling tilts (bias, availability). Do not make the metric stand for a theme; that is authored key, the play's pitch. Do not edit the engine's files; wire only from the play's side.
