# Instructions: Analogy

How a Playwright wires the analogy engine into a play. You wire by linking the play's own content to the engine's files; the engine files stay untouched, they are canon. The Roadie plumbs the engine's law into Instructions Knowledge on deploy. Authoring guidance, not runtime content, and it does not go on tour.

## Human

- The human sets the pairing: the target the persona is trying to understand, the source it reaches for, and what is at stake in whether the analogy maps deep structure or merely surface.

## Agent

- From the play's own content, a persona links the subprocess its analogical reasoning is in under Projection: retrieval, when a source is being called to mind from the target; mapping, when the two structures are being aligned into correspondences; inference, when a prediction is carried across the alignment; schema, when the shared structure is abstracted into a general pattern. Let the reasoning turn on relational structure -- and let a surface-only match be the error it risks. You link to the engine, you never edit it.

## Collaboration

- The rhetorical shaping of a claim, including framing by metaphor to persuade rather than to reason, is the framing engine's; analogy is the structure-mapping and transfer beneath a comparison. The general retrieval of memories -- episodes, facts, procedures -- is the memory engine's; this engine's retrieval owns only the analog-access step and its surface-versus-structure bias. The abstraction of psychological distance, high versus low construal, is the construal engine's; this engine's schema owns relational abstraction. This engine supplies the reasoning-and-transfer mechanism of analogy.

## Knowledge

- Analogy is reasoning by structure-mapping: a persona aligns the relational structure of a known source with a target and carries inferences across the alignment (Gentner). What makes it sound is systematicity -- the analogy should map a system of deep, interconnected relations, above all causal ones, not a shared surface; a good analogy transfers relations, not resemblance. It runs by four subprocesses. Retrieval: a source is called to mind from the target, driven notoriously by surface similarity even where sound mapping needs relational similarity, so the structurally right source is often missed. Mapping: the alignment itself, one-to-one correspondences under parallel connectivity and systematicity, the deep causal structure preferred over the loose attribute. Inference: the source's unmatched relations projected across the alignment onto the target as candidate inferences -- new predictions, held provisionally. Schema: the shared structure abstracted into a domain-general pattern that outlives the pair and makes the next transfer easier (Gick & Holyoak). Read a persona by the subprocess it is in and whether its analogy maps structure or surface.

## System

- Do let the analogy turn on relational, especially causal, structure, and prize systematicity -- and let a match built on surface resemblance be the error the reasoning risks and sometimes commits.
- Do route through the subprocess that fits: retrieval when a source is sought, mapping when structures are aligned, inference when a prediction is transferred, schema when the pattern is abstracted; the four run in sequence but a scene can turn on any one.
- Do hold candidate inferences as provisional -- predictions the analogy proposes, not facts it proves -- and let a bad mapping be how a false inference is exported with false confidence.
- Do not play the rhetorical framing of a claim as analogy; that is framing. Do not play general memory retrieval as analogy's retrieval; that is memory. Do not play construal-level abstraction as analogy's schema; that is construal. Do not edit the engine's files; wire only from the play's side.
