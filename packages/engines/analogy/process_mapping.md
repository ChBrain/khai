# Process: Mapping

## Taxonomy

[Analogy](process_analogy.md), the subprocess where the two structures are aligned.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: analogy

## Initiated by

The persona holds a source and a target together, and sets their elements in correspondence.
With a base in hand, the core work begins: aligning the two so that what plays a role in the source is matched to what plays the same role in the target. What enters is a pair of structures to be put into register -- the sun and the nucleus, the planets and the electrons -- element by element and, more importantly, relation by relation.
This is the heart of structure-mapping: not noticing that two things are alike, but building the correspondence that says exactly how.

## Direction

Toward a structurally consistent alignment.
The persona seeks a mapping that obeys the constraints Gentner names: one-to-one correspondence, each source element matched to a single target element, and parallel connectivity, matched relations having matched arguments -- so the alignment holds together as a system rather than a scatter of matched parts. Guided by systematicity, it favours the alignment that carries the deepest connected structure, the causal spine over the loose attribute.
The direction is toward the maximal consistent match of relations, not the maximal match of features.

## Lever

Structural consistency and systematicity, against the pull of surface matches.
Whether the mapping is good turns on holding to relational alignment under the one-to-one and parallel-connectivity constraints, and preferring the systematic, causally-connected correspondence -- while resisting the easy but shallow matches that surface similarity offers. A forced mapping that pairs things because they look alike, breaking consistency, is the failure mode.
Hold the constraints and prize the deep relations, and the alignment is sound; relax them for surface convenience, and the mapping distorts both source and target.

## Echo

An alignment set between source and target, consistent and systematic or forced and shallow.
Because the mapping fixes which elements correspond, it determines everything downstream -- what can be transferred, and whether the transfer is warranted; a sound alignment is the whole basis of the analogy.
It receives the source that [retrieval](process_retrieval.md) supplied and grounds the [inference](process_inference.md) that projects across it, and repeated over cases it yields the [schema](process_schema.md). That the alignment must be of relational structure and not surface is the [root's](process_analogy.md); the rhetorical use of a comparison to frame a claim is the framing engine's.
