# Process: Inference

## Taxonomy

[Analogy](process_analogy.md), the subprocess where the transfer is carried across.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: analogy

## Initiated by

The alignment is set, and the source knows something the target has not yet been asked.
Once the correspondences hold, the source carries relations the target's side of the map leaves blank -- a further connection, a prediction, a mechanism the persona knows in the base but has not checked in the target. What enters is an aligned pair with unmatched structure on the source side, ripe to be projected.
This is where analogy earns its keep: it does not just describe the target by the source, it tells the persona something new about the target.

## Direction

Toward the source's unmatched relations projected onto the target.
Guided by the alignment, the persona carries the source's leftover structure across the map as candidate inferences -- if the target is built like the source here, it should also hold there -- generating hypotheses about the target it did not start with. These are candidate inferences, not certainties: predictions the analogy proposes and the world must confirm.
The direction is from the filled-in source to the blanks in the target, structure flowing across the alignment as new claims.

## Lever

The soundness of the alignment the inference rides on, and the discipline to hold its output as provisional.
Whether the transfer is warranted turns entirely on the mapping beneath it: candidate inferences projected across a systematic, consistent alignment are good bets, while those carried across a forced or surface mapping are where analogy misleads -- the false prediction, the overreach, the structure assumed to hold where it does not. And even a sound projection yields a hypothesis, not a fact.
Project across deep structure and hold the result open to test, and the inference is powerful; project across surface or take it as proven, and it deceives.

## Echo

New claims about the target, carried from the source across the alignment -- to be tested, kept, or discarded.
Because this is the step that transfers, it is both the payoff of analogy and its danger: the same move that lets a known domain illuminate an unknown one lets a bad mapping export a falsehood with the confidence of structure.
It depends on the [mapping](process_mapping.md) that grounds it and the [retrieval](process_retrieval.md) that supplied the source, and the inferences that survive across cases harden into a [schema](process_schema.md). That the transfer must ride on relational structure is the [root's](process_analogy.md); the evaluation of a candidate inference as a belief is the reasoning done in the play, reviewed by the team.
