# Process: Soundmark

## Taxonomy

[Soundscape](process_soundscape.md), the facet of the place's unique signature sound.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: soundscape

## Initiated by

A place has one sound that belongs to it and to nowhere else, and that sound marks it as itself.
Soundmark starts wherever a place is identified by a specific sound the way it is by a landmark -- this bell and no other, this particular whistle, this fountain, this bird, the one sound a returning resident knows home by before they see it.
It is the signature of the field, named from "landmark": not any bell but the community's bell, the sound that says this exact place.

## Direction

Toward the place made recognizable and cherished by its own sound.
Soundmark fixes the place's identity in a sound -- the specific timbre and pattern that no other place has, the aural equivalent of a skyline, the sound residents defend and visitors remember.
It moves the place from a type to a particular: not a coastal town but this one, known by the exact cry of its gulls and the exact note of its harbor bell; the sound that, once learned, is unmistakable and irreplaceable.

## Lever

The uniqueness of the sound against the homogenizing noise that would erase it.
The carrying force is that the soundmark is singular and rooted -- earned by long association with this place, unfakeable elsewhere; the resistance is the lo-fi flattening of the modern soundscape, the generic traffic and machine noise that masks and displaces the particular sounds, so that soundmarks, like species, can go extinct.
Protect the soundmark and the place keeps its acoustic identity; let the general noise swallow it and the place sounds like anywhere, its signature lost.

## Echo

The place known and held by its own particular sound.
Soundmark leaves the place with an aural identity -- the one sound that names it, worth keeping the way a monument is -- and hands the listener the recognition and the belonging that sound carries.
It is the field's signature and its most vulnerable feature: the sound that makes a place itself, and the first thing lost when a place is drowned in generic noise.
Soundmark is dated from the long association, the time it took the sound to become the place's own.
