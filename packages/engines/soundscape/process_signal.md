# Process: Signal

## Taxonomy

[Soundscape](process_soundscape.md), the facet of the foreground sound.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: soundscape

## Initiated by

A sound rises out of the ground and is listened to for what it means.
Signal starts wherever a place's sound carries information the ear attends to -- the bell, the horn, the siren, the whistle, the call, the alarm, the announcement -- the foreground sound that stands out of the keynote and demands to be heard.
It is the figure to the keynote's ground: not the sound the place is always making but the sound it makes to say something, and that something is heard.

## Direction

Up out of the ground, toward the ear it is meant to reach.
Signal cuts through the field -- pitched, timed, or shaped to be noticed against the background -- the church bell over the town, the ship's horn through the fog, the siren clearing the road, the factory whistle marking the shift.
It moves the place from ambient to addressing: the signal is a sound aimed at the listener, and the place that sends it is a place speaking, ordering the day, warning, calling, timing.

## Lever

The signal's cut against the keynote and noise that would mask it.
The carrying force is the signal's design to be heard -- loud, pitched, or patterned to stand out; the resistance is the masking of a crowded soundscape, where too much sound swallows the signal and it must grow louder or sharper to carry, or is lost.
In a hi-fi place a soft signal carries far and clear; in a lo-fi place the signals compete and escalate, the sirens and horns and alarms all shouting over each other and each meaning less.

## Echo

The place heard as sending its meaningful sounds.
Signal leaves the place punctuated by the sounds that carry -- the bells and horns and calls that order and warn and time -- and hands the listener the information they bear.
It is the field's foreground: the sounds a place makes on purpose, or that mean something when they come, the acoustic events that a listener tracks and reads.
Signal is dated from the sounding, the moment the foreground sound rose out of the ground to be heard.
