# Process: Sleep

## Taxonomy

Body (no khai file of its own yet).

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: sleep

## Initiated by

A persona has to stop being a persona for a while.
Sleep starts wherever the day's requirement to be present, watchful, and available runs out and has to be set down -- and the setting down is the whole difficulty, because it cannot be performed.
It is not the tiredness that pulls on attention until it is met, which is the body engine's; that is the demand. This is what the demand is for, what it requires of a persona before it will be met, and what happens when it is not.

## Direction

Toward an absence the persona will not witness arriving.
The engine reads four movements: [the surrender](process_surrender_sleep.md), the ceasing of vigilance and what it requires; [the debt](process_debt_sleep.md), what accumulates when it does not happen and why the persona cannot read the total; [the effort](process_effort_sleep.md), the attempt that prevents it; and [the schedule](process_schedule_sleep.md), the clock the persona lives by against the one they are made of.
The four are not stages of a night. They are four ways the same thing goes wrong.

## Lever

The absoluteness of the need against the impossibility of meeting it deliberately.
The carrying force is that sleep is not optional and not negotiable: it is the largest recurring requirement a persona has, nothing substitutes for it, and every capacity the play cares about -- judgement, patience, memory, the ability to be decent to somebody -- degrades without it in a way that is measurable and fast; the resistance is that it is the one need effort cannot serve, since attending to it, deciding on it, and working at it are precisely the mechanism by which it fails.
Sleep is read on what the persona did to bring it about, because whatever they did is the reason it did not come.

## Echo

A persona lying still, working hard, at the one task where working is disqualifying.
Sleep does not close; it is a surrender being attempted, gathered into one reading -- a surrender, a debt, an effort, a schedule.
The engine's twist is here. **Sleep is the only thing a persona cannot obtain by trying to.** Hunger answers to eating, cold to warmth, a task to work, a wound to care -- every other need in the catalogue responds to effort in the direction of it. This one inverts: attention to it, intention toward it, and effort at it are the documented mechanism of insomnia, so the persona who most urgently needs it is thereby disqualified from the one move that works everywhere else. And the only route in is to stop minding whether it comes, which also cannot be done on purpose.
It hands off what it is not: the physical demand pulling on attention is body's; the drift of self-generated thought is mind-wandering's; the repetitive dwelling on distress is rumination's; the mending of a worn object is restoration's; content outside awareness is the-unconscious's; the felt passage of duration is time-perception's; and a sleepless night made to **stand for** a theme is authored key, the play's pitch, not the force.
The persona is dated from the last time they slept without deciding to.
