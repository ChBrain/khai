# Process: The Trying

## Taxonomy

[The Effort](process_effort_sleep.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: sleep

## Initiated by

The persona is working at it.
The Trying starts at the effort itself: breathing deliberately, staying still, emptying the mind, doing the technique, willing it -- applying force to a target.

## Direction

Toward arousal, which is the opposite of the state required.
Effort is physiologically arousing. A persona working hard at falling asleep is producing the alertness that prevents it, and they are producing more of it in proportion to how much they care.
This is why the paradoxical instruction -- lie there and try to stay gently awake -- works better than any amount of trying, and why it cannot be taken as one more thing to do well.

## Lever

The persona's competence against the one place competence is disqualifying.
The carrying force is that trying harder is right almost everywhere and the persona is applying a skill that has served them well: effort is the general solution, they have used it successfully on every other problem, and there is no signal telling them this problem is different; the resistance is that this is the exception, so the persona's strength is the mechanism -- and the failure teaches them that they did not try hard enough, which is the most reliable way to guarantee another one.
The Trying is a competent person being defeated by their competence.

## Echo

Full effort, correctly applied, at the one task where it cannot work.
It shows the engine's twist at its sharpest and completes Espie's sequence.
A play should not release the persona by having them decide to stop trying, because deciding to stop trying is trying.
The Trying is dated from the moment sleep became something to achieve.
