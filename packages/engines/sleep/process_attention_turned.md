# Process: The Attention Turned

## Taxonomy

[The Effort](process_effort_sleep.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: sleep

## Initiated by

The persona has started watching themselves fall asleep.
The Attention Turned starts at the monitoring: noticing whether one is drifting, checking the clock, tracking how long it has been, listening for the change.

## Direction

Toward a process disrupted by being observed.
The transition requires attention to loosen from the world, and the persona has just pointed it at the transition itself -- so the one faculty that has to stand down is the one now on duty.
The checking is also self-confirming: every check finds them awake, which is evidence that it is going badly.

## Lever

The reasonableness of monitoring something that matters against monitoring being incompatible with it.
The carrying force is that attending to an important process is ordinarily exactly right, and a persona who has had several bad nights has good reason to be watching this one -- the vigilance is earned; the resistance is that this process is destroyed by observation, so the persona's most sensible response to a run of failure is the first step of the mechanism that produces more.
The Attention Turned is where an appropriate concern becomes the illness.

## Echo

Somebody keeping careful track of not sleeping.
It is Espie's first step and the one a play can show most easily: a persona and a clock.
The Attention Turned is dated from the first night the persona checked the time.
