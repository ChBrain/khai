# Process: The Debt

## Taxonomy

[Sleep](process_sleep.md), what accumulates, and why nobody can read the total.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: sleep

## Initiated by

The sleep that did not happen has not gone away.
The Debt starts wherever the shortfall repeats: [the accumulation](process_accumulation.md) across nights, [the blunted gauge](process_blunted_gauge.md) that stops registering it, and [the microsleep](process_microsleep.md) that takes the seconds anyway.

## Direction

Toward impairment the persona cannot see.
This is the movement's real finding and it is unusually clean. Under sustained restriction, objective performance keeps degrading night after night in a dose-dependent way -- and subjective sleepiness flattens out after the first few days.
So the persona feels roughly as bad on the tenth day as on the third, and is performing very much worse. Their own report has stopped tracking their own state.

## Lever

The reliability of the deficit against the unreliability of the persona reporting it.
The carrying force is that the body is keeping an accurate account and will collect: the deficit is real, it compounds, and recovery does happen -- a persona who sleeps properly for long enough does come back, which is why the arrears metaphor is worth keeping at all; the resistance is that the instrument the persona would use to check -- how sleepy they feel -- has decoupled from the quantity it is supposed to measure, so the persona who is most impaired is the one least able to tell you so, and will sincerely insist they are fine.
The Debt is an accurate ledger with a broken display.

## Echo

A persona operating badly, in good faith, at what they take to be normal.
It shows why sleep loss is the hazard that never gets treated as one: nobody involved is aware anything is wrong.
The Debt is dated from the first short night and is settled only by long ones.
