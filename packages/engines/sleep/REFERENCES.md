# Sleep: Reference

## Line of Work

Sleep as **process**: the one need effort cannot serve.

The engine's boundary is with the body engine and it is worth stating first. Body carries the physical
state making its demands felt -- hunger, fatigue, pain -- pulling on attention until it is met. That is
the demand. This engine is what meeting it **requires of a persona**, what accrues when it is not met,
and why the ordinary response to an unmet need is here the mechanism of failure.

**The surrender** is the precondition nobody states. Sleep is the deliberate suspension of vigilance:
hours in which a persona accepts that they will not notice what happens to them. The **letting go**
itself has no controls and no first-person account of its middle, which is why a persona for whom it
fails has no action available and will invent one. **Safe enough** is the condition, and it is judged
below the level of decision -- a body that is not satisfied keeps a channel open regardless of what the
persona has concluded about the risk. And the **kept watch** is the common partial failure: sleeping
with an ear open for a child, a patient, a phone. The consequence the engine cares about is
distributive. The requirement is graded by circumstance rather than by need, so the persona in the
unsafe house, the shared ward, the cell, or the first weeks with a baby gets the least sleep at the
point they most need it, and every deficit that follows is attributed to them personally.

**The debt** carries the movement's cleanest finding. Under sustained restriction, objective
performance declines in a cumulative, dose-dependent way -- and **subjective sleepiness flattens out
after the first few days**. So a persona feels roughly as bad on the tenth night as on the third and is
performing very much worse: the instrument they would use to check has decoupled from the quantity it
measures. They are not in denial. They will sincerely decline the break, insist they are fine to drive,
and be baffled by anybody who disagrees. The **microsleep** then collects what was not given, in
seconds that leave no gap in the persona's account of the interval. This is why sleep loss is unlike
every other impairment a persona might notice in themselves.

**The effort** is Espie's attention-intention-effort pathway and it is the engine's centre. Noticing
whether one is drifting off requires the monitoring that prevents drifting off. Deciding to sleep
converts a state into a target, and a target comes with a deadline, which supplies a reason to check.
Working at a target recruits effort, and effort is arousal. Each step is the reasonable response to a
real problem, and the three compound: by the third the persona is at their most awake precisely because
they are at their most motivated. Nobody arrives here by being foolish. They arrive by applying the
general solution -- try harder -- to the single case where it is contraindicated, and each failure
teaches them they did not try hard enough.

**The schedule** is the social face. Chronotype is substantially heritable and shifts across a life, so
early and late personas are running different clocks rather than exhibiting discipline and its absence
-- and only one of those clocks is compatible with the hours the world keeps. The **gap** follows: an
alarm on working days at an hour the body has not reached, catch-up at the weekend that is genuine
relief and pushes the clock further out, and a persona permanently a little out of phase who considers
themselves an ordinary sleeper. **Night work** is the extreme, and its finding is that most night
workers do not adapt -- daylight on the commute and a daytime life pull the clock back, so the
misalignment is chronic rather than resolved.

Hence the **twist**, at the root. **Sleep is the only thing a persona cannot obtain by trying to.**
Hunger answers to eating, cold to warmth, a task to work, a wound to care: every other need in the
catalogue responds to effort in its own direction. This one inverts, so the persona who most urgently
needs it is thereby disqualified from the one move that works everywhere else. And the only route in is
to stop minding whether it comes -- which is why the paradoxical instruction to lie there and try to
stay gently awake outperforms any amount of trying, and why it cannot be taken as one more thing to do
well. A play should not release a persona by having them decide to stop trying, because deciding to
stop trying is trying.

**On the evidence, stated plainly.** This is the most experimentally grounded engine khai has built in
a while, and the parts are still unequal. **The restriction findings are the strongest**: Van Dongen
and colleagues' dose-response work, and the subjective/objective dissociation in particular, are clean,
replicated, and the basis for a great deal of applied safety practice -- and they come from young
healthy adults in controlled laboratory conditions, which is not the population or the setting the
engine describes. That same work found **large, stable individual differences in vulnerability**, so
the averages conceal personas who are genuinely much less affected and personas who are much more, and
neither can tell which they are. **The debt metaphor is looser than the engine's own movement title
suggests**, and the member says so: recovery is partial rather than a repayment, the exchange rate is
not one to one, some deficits appear to persist, and the tidiness of the accounting picture invites a
persona to think they can borrow deliberately and settle later. **Espie's attention-intention-effort
model is a clinical framework rather than an experimental result** -- it is well regarded, it organises
the phenomenon convincingly, and the paradoxical-intention technique it motivates has trial support that
is real but modest and mostly from small older studies. **Chronotype and social jetlag are real
constructs with good measurement**; the heritability is well established, and the associations between
social jetlag and health outcomes are correlational, confounded with everything that correlates with
working hours, and considerably weaker than popular accounts suggest. **The shift-work
non-adaptation finding is solid for circadian markers** and much softer for the long-run health claims
built on it. What should not be taken from this engine is that sleeplessness is always the persona's
doing, that it is never anything but circumstance, or that any of this constitutes clinical guidance.
The claim is narrow: the surrender has preconditions unequally available, the deficit hides behind a
gauge that stops working, effort is the mechanism rather than the remedy, and the timetable charges
some bodies more than others.

separately sourced; the twist follows from the effort movement.

## Restrictions

What the engine refuses to model, and to whom it delegates.

- **The physical demand (body)**: Hunger, fatigue, pain, arousal, and sickness as bodily states pulling
  on attention until they are met are the body engine's. That engine says the persona is tired and it
  is pulling on them; this one says what answering it requires, what the shortfall accrues, and why
  trying is the wrong instrument.
- **Thought drifting inward (mind-wandering, rumination)**: Self-generated task-unrelated thought is
  mind-wandering's, and repetitive dwelling on distress is rumination's. Both frequently occupy a
  sleepless persona and neither is what the effort movement models -- attention turned onto one's own
  falling asleep is a specific monitoring, not a drift and not a brooding.
- **Content outside awareness (the-unconscious)**: The dynamic, adaptive, and preconscious kinds of
  mental content that shape a persona without being available to them are the-unconscious's. This
  engine does not model dreams, and a play wanting dream content should look there and to imagination.
- **The mending of a worn thing (restoration)**: The stance taken toward a broken object's identity and
  history is the restoration engine's, which is about objects. The word overlaps and the phenomena do
  not.
- **The felt passage of duration (time-perception, waiting)**: How long the night felt is
  time-perception's; time placed at somebody else's disposal is waiting's. A persona lying awake is
  doing both, and this engine reads only what is happening to their sleep.
- **Effortful self-management (self-control, coping, regulation)**: Overriding an impulse, handling a
  stressor, and intervening on one's own emotion are those engines'. They are the general competences
  the effort movement shows failing, and their failure here is specific to this case rather than a
  fact about them.
- **A standing condition and its social handling (illness)**: What a condition makes of a persona
  socially and biographically -- the ticket, the role, the telling -- is the illness engine's. Chronic
  insomnia is a diagnosable condition and a persona seeking recognition for it is in that engine; this
  one models the mechanism rather than the standing.
- **Being required to be available (waiting, employment)**: Standing by, on-call availability, and the
  night shift as a term of the effort bargain are the waiting and employment engines'. The night-work
  member reads what the rota does to a body, not what the rota is.
- **The sleepless night as authored symbol (the play's pitch)**: Insomnia made to **stand for** a theme
  -- the wakefulness that mirrors a motif of guilt -- is refused: that is authored key, the play's
  pitch. Sleep sets what the night cost; the meaning read into it is the play's.

## Encoding

Source to constraint. A process tree: one root, four movements, twelve forms.

- **[sleep](process_sleep.md)** (the root): the setting down of the day's requirement to be present,
  read on what the persona did to bring it about, carrying the twist -- it is the only thing a persona
  cannot obtain by trying to. Anchored by Espie (2006).
- **[the surrender](process_surrender_sleep.md)**: the ceasing of watch and what it costs to afford.
  Anchored by Tamaki et al. (2016) and Rattenborg. [the letting go](process_letting_go.md),
  [safe enough](process_safe_enough.md), [the kept watch](process_kept_watch.md).
- **[the debt](process_debt_sleep.md)**: what accumulates, and why nobody can read the total. Anchored
  by Van Dongen et al. (2003). [the accumulation](process_accumulation.md),
  [the blunted gauge](process_blunted_gauge.md), [the microsleep](process_microsleep.md).
- **[the effort](process_effort_sleep.md)**: the attempt that prevents it. Anchored by Espie (2006) and
  Ascher & Efran (1978). [the attention turned](process_attention_turned.md),
  [the intending](process_intending_sleep.md), [the trying](process_trying.md).
- **[the schedule](process_schedule_sleep.md)**: the clock lived by against the clock made of. Anchored
  by Roenneberg et al. (2012) and Åkerstedt. [the body clock](process_body_clock.md),
  [the gap](process_social_jetlag.md), [the night work](process_night_work.md).
