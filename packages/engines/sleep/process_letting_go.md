# Process: The Letting Go

## Taxonomy

[The Surrender](process_surrender_sleep.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: sleep

## Initiated by

The persona stops holding on.
The Letting Go starts at the transition itself: attention loosening from the world, thought becoming uncoupled and strange, the body's checks standing down one by one.

## Direction

Toward an event the persona is not present for.
Nobody witnesses their own falling asleep. The last thing available to report is the approach, and the crossing is only ever known afterwards by inference -- which is why personas are so unreliable about how long it took them.
It is the only ordinary experience with no first-person account of its middle.

## Lever

The ease of it when it is easy against the absence of any handle when it is not.
The carrying force is that for most personas most nights this simply happens and requires nothing: the mechanism is old, robust, and does not need supervision, and the ordinary case is that a persona lies down and is gone; the resistance is that there is no move available if it does not -- no muscle to contract, no procedure to run, nothing that can be done more of -- so a persona for whom it fails has no action to take and will invent one.
The Letting Go has no controls, which is why the effort movement exists.

## Echo

A crossing nobody attends.
It is the movement's simplest member and the one that makes every other problem in the engine possible.
The Letting Go is dated by the clock and not by the persona, who will guess wrongly in both directions.
