# Process: The Accumulation

## Taxonomy

[The Debt](process_debt_sleep.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: sleep

## Initiated by

The shortfalls are adding up.
The Accumulation starts across nights rather than within one: an hour missing on Monday and Tuesday and Wednesday, none of them alarming, compounding into something that is.

## Direction

Toward a deficit that grows in a straight line while nothing feels different.
Restriction produces cumulative, dose-dependent decline: the more nights and the shorter they are, the worse the performance, and the decline does not level off just because the persona has got used to the routine.
Recovery is real but slower and less complete than the arithmetic suggests -- one long night does not clear a fortnight.

## Lever

The usefulness of the debt metaphor against how loosely it actually fits.
The carrying force is that the accounting picture gets the important part right: the loss is cumulative rather than nightly, it does not vanish by being ignored, and extended recovery sleep does substantially repay it, which is why the metaphor has survived and why it is worth acting on; the resistance is that sleep is not a ledger -- repayment is partial, some deficits appear to persist, the exchange rate is not one to one, and the metaphor's tidiness invites a persona to believe they can borrow deliberately and settle later at a time of their choosing.
The Accumulation is real and does not behave like money.

## Echo

An arrears nobody is tracking, in a currency nobody can convert.
It is the movement's premise and the thing the next member makes undetectable.
The Accumulation is dated from the first short night and keeps running.
