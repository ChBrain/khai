# Process: The Gap

## Taxonomy

[The Schedule](process_schedule_sleep.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: sleep

## Initiated by

The persona lives on two clocks that disagree.
The Gap starts at the ordinary arrangement: waking by alarm on working days at an hour the body has not reached, and sleeping to the body's own timing at the weekend.

## Direction

Toward a chronic small deficit, and a shift back and forth every week.
The persona is not merely short of sleep; they are repeatedly re-phasing, which is the same imposition a traveller takes on a flight and which most working populations sustain permanently without calling it anything.
The weekend catch-up is genuine relief and it also widens the gap, since sleeping late resets the clock further from the working schedule.

## Lever

The relief of the catch-up against the catch-up making the next week worse.
The carrying force is that the recovery is real and should be taken: the extra hours do repay part of the deficit, and a persona who forces themselves up at the same hour every day of the week is paying a price too; the resistance is that the same hours push the clock later, so Monday is harder than it would otherwise have been, and the persona is oscillating rather than settling.
The Gap is a deficit maintained by the thing that relieves it.

## Echo

A persona permanently a little out of phase and entirely used to it.
It shows why the debt movement runs constantly in people who consider themselves ordinary sleepers.
The Gap is dated from the working timetable and measured against a body nobody consulted.
