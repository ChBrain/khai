# Process: The Effort

## Taxonomy

[Sleep](process_sleep.md), the attempt that prevents it, and where the engine is seen for one thing.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: sleep

## Initiated by

The persona has begun to try.
The Effort starts wherever sleep becomes a task: [attention turned](process_attention_turned.md) onto one's own falling asleep, [intending](process_intending_sleep.md) it, and then [trying](process_trying.md) -- which is Espie's sequence and is the engine's centre.

## Direction

Toward the exact state that makes sleep impossible.
Each step is reasonable on its own and the three compound. Noticing whether one is drifting off requires the monitoring that prevents drifting off. Deciding to sleep converts a state into a goal. Working at a goal recruits effort, and effort is arousal.
By the third step the persona is at their most awake precisely because they are at their most motivated.

## Lever

The reasonableness of trying against the fact that trying is the mechanism.
The carrying force is that the persona is doing what works for literally everything else, and their reasoning is sound: a need is unmet, the need matters, effort is what one applies to needs, and refusing to try would be strange -- nobody arrives at insomnia by being foolish; the resistance is that this is the single exception, so the whole apparatus of competence a persona brings to a problem is, here, the problem -- and the harder they work the more reliably they fail, which teaches them the need to work harder.
The Effort rides the engine's keystone knife-edge: sleep is the only thing a persona cannot obtain by trying to.

## Echo

Somebody at full stretch, in the dark, going nowhere.
It shows the three other movements' stakes concentrated into one night: the surrender that cannot be performed, the debt making the next attempt more urgent, the schedule setting an hour by which it must have happened.
This is why the Effort is the hardest of the four to stage and the only one worth closing on: the persona must be competent, the reasoning must be sound, nothing must be wrong with them, and the audience must be able to see that the striving is the illness.
And the way out cannot be staged as a decision, since the only route in is to stop minding whether it comes -- which is exactly the thing that cannot be done on purpose.
A play that has the persona relax and drift off has staged the one move the engine says is unavailable.
The Effort is dated from the first night they lay there and noticed they were awake.
