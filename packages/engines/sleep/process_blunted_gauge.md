# Process: The Blunted Gauge

## Taxonomy

[The Debt](process_debt_sleep.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: sleep

## Initiated by

The persona has stopped being able to tell.
The Blunted Gauge starts a few days into sustained restriction, when the sense of sleepiness flattens out while the actual impairment continues to grow.

## Direction

Toward sincere and confident misreporting.
The persona is not in denial and is not exaggerating their resilience: they genuinely feel about the same as they did on day three, and they are performing very much worse.
So they will decline the offer of a break, insist they are fine to drive, and be honestly baffled by anybody who suggests otherwise.

## Lever

The persona's right to be believed about their own state against the fact that their instrument has failed.
The carrying force is that first-person report is ordinarily the best evidence about an internal condition and should not be casually overridden -- a rule that treats people as unable to know how they feel is a bad rule with a long history; the resistance is that this is the documented exception, and it is exactly the case where being wrong is dangerous, so the ordinary courtesy of taking somebody's word for it is the thing that lets a badly impaired persona keep operating.
The Blunted Gauge is why sleep deprivation is unlike every other impairment a persona might notice in themselves.

## Echo

Somebody dangerous, and honest, and wrong.
It is the movement's centre and the reason the debt is a hazard rather than a discomfort.
The Blunted Gauge is dated from around the third night, and it does not recalibrate until after recovery.
