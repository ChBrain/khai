# Process: The Night Work

## Taxonomy

[The Schedule](process_schedule_sleep.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: sleep

## Initiated by

The persona is awake when their body says otherwise, for a living.
The Night Work starts at the shift: hours inverted relative to the light, sustained over weeks or years, with the body's own timing pulling the other way throughout.

## Direction

Toward a phase that never fully arrives.
Most night workers do not adapt. Daylight on the commute, the demands of a daytime life, and days off spent on everybody else's schedule all pull the clock back, so the persona is chronically misaligned rather than settled into a new rhythm.
Daytime sleep is also shorter and lighter, so the deficit runs in both directions.

## Lever

The necessity of somebody being awake against what being that somebody costs.
The carrying force is that night work is not a lifestyle error and mostly is not a choice: hospitals, transport, power, and safety all require continuous cover, the people who provide it are doing something the rest of the world depends on, and many of them value the work; the resistance is that the arrangement asks a body to do the one thing it is least able to do, the misalignment does not resolve with practice, and every consequence -- the fatigue, the error, the health record -- attaches to the individual rather than to the schedule.
The Night Work is a collective requirement billed to particular bodies.

## Echo

A persona doing necessary work in a permanently wrong phase.
It is the schedule's extreme case and shows the whole movement without any subtlety.
The Night Work is dated from the rota, and it does not adjust.
