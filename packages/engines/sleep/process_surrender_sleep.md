# Process: The Surrender

## Taxonomy

[Sleep](process_sleep.md), the ceasing of watch, and what it costs to afford.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: sleep

## Initiated by

The persona has to stop guarding themselves.
The Surrender starts at the precondition nobody states: sleep is the deliberate suspension of vigilance, so it requires a persona to accept, for hours, that they will not notice what happens to them.
[Letting go](process_letting_go.md) is the act, [safe enough](process_safe_enough.md) is the condition, and [the kept watch](process_kept_watch.md) is what happens when the condition fails.

## Direction

Toward an unguarded body in a place the persona has decided to trust.
The trust is doing all the work and it is mostly invisible, because most personas most nights have it without noticing.
Where it is absent -- an unsafe house, a shared cell, a strange room, a night on call, a child who might wake -- the body does not simply stay awake. It sleeps shallowly, wakes often, and monitors, which is a different state from being awake and is not restorative in the way the untroubled version is.

## Lever

The necessity of dropping the guard against everything that makes dropping it unwise.
The carrying force is that the surrender is the mechanism and not a metaphor: sleep only works if vigilance is genuinely off, and a body that keeps a channel open is doing exactly what it should where the danger is real -- the light sleeper in the unsafe house is not failing at sleep, they are succeeding at survival; the resistance is that the cost falls on whoever has least reason to feel safe, so the persona in the worst position gets the worst sleep, and the deficits that follow will be read by everybody as a fact about them.
The Surrender is a requirement priced by circumstance.

## Echo

A persona asleep in a place they trusted, or half-asleep in one they did not.
It is the movement that decides whether any of the others get a chance to run.
What it hands upward is not sleep but the possibility of it, which is unequally distributed and almost never mentioned.
The Surrender is dated from whatever the persona last had reason to listen for.
