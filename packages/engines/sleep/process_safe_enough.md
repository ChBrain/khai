# Process: Safe Enough

## Taxonomy

[The Surrender](process_surrender_sleep.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: sleep

## Initiated by

The persona has to be somewhere they will accept not watching.
Safe Enough starts at the condition sleep silently requires: hours of unconsciousness in a place, among people, and under circumstances the body is willing to stop monitoring.

## Direction

Toward a judgement made below the level of decision.
The persona does not choose this and cannot override it. A body that is not satisfied keeps a channel open, and it keeps it open regardless of what the persona has concluded about the risk.
Which is why an anxious persona in a genuinely safe room sleeps badly, and why a tired persona in a genuinely dangerous one sleeps at all.

## Lever

The intelligence of the requirement against who can meet it.
The carrying force is that the condition is not squeamishness but a sound and ancient piece of engineering: an animal that surrendered vigilance regardless of circumstance would be a short-lived animal, and the mechanism is right to be conservative; the resistance is that it is graded by circumstance rather than by need, so the persona in the unsafe house, the shared ward, the cell, the new country, or the first weeks with a baby gets the least sleep at the point they most need it, and every consequence will be attributed to them personally.
Safe Enough distributes rest by security, which is not how it is distributed by need.

## Echo

A condition met invisibly by most and unmeetable by some.
It is where the engine touches everything else about a persona's situation.
Safe Enough is dated from whatever last made the persona's body decline to stand down.
