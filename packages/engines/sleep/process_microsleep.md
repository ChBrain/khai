# Process: The Microsleep

## Taxonomy

[The Debt](process_debt_sleep.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: sleep

## Initiated by

The body takes what it was not given.
The Microsleep starts wherever the deficit is large enough that sleep begins intruding into waking: a few seconds gone, eyes open or not, with no experience of having been away.

## Direction

Toward an absence with no gap in the persona's account of it.
The seconds do not feel like anything and do not leave a hole. The persona's sense of the interval is continuous, so they are not aware of having lost it, and will describe the period as ordinary.
It happens most where the task is monotonous and the stakes make it worst.

## Lever

The body's insistence on collecting against the persona having no way to notice the collection.
The carrying force is that this is the mechanism doing exactly what it should -- the requirement is not optional, and a body that simply took what it needed is a body protecting itself; the resistance is that it takes it wherever the persona happens to be, and the persona's continuous account of the interval means neither they nor anybody else will know it happened until something goes wrong.
The Microsleep is the debt collecting without a notice.

## Echo

A few seconds missing from a story that has no gap in it.
It shows the movement's cost arriving in the world rather than in the persona's feeling.
The Microsleep is dated from nothing, since there is nothing to date it from.
