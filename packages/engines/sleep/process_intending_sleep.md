# Process: The Intending

## Taxonomy

[The Effort](process_effort_sleep.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: sleep

## Initiated by

The persona has decided to sleep.
The Intending starts when it becomes a goal: tonight I must sleep, I need to be asleep by midnight, I have to get seven hours before that meeting.

## Direction

Toward a state converted into a target.
Sleep does not behave as a target. It arrives when it is not being sought, which means an explicit goal changes the persona's relationship to it in exactly the wrong direction -- and the goal comes with a deadline, which supplies a reason to check.
Being asleep by midnight is not something anybody has ever accomplished.

## Lever

The legitimacy of the goal against the goal being the wrong shape.
The carrying force is that the persona has real requirements and setting a goal is how requirements are ordinarily met: they do need to be able to work tomorrow, the meeting is real, and treating a need as a target is the standard competent move; the resistance is that this need cannot be represented as a target without changing it, since a goal implies effort and monitoring, and both are contraindicated -- so competence here consists in not doing the thing competence usually consists in.
The Intending is where a legitimate requirement becomes an impossible instruction.

## Echo

A deadline attached to something that does not accept deadlines.
It is Espie's second step and the one that makes the third inevitable.
The Intending is dated from whatever the persona has to do tomorrow.
