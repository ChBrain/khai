# Process: The Kept Watch

## Taxonomy

[The Surrender](process_surrender_sleep.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: sleep

## Initiated by

Part of the persona stays on duty.
The Kept Watch starts wherever the surrender is partial: sleeping with an ear open for a child, a patient, a phone, a door -- present enough to be woken by the one sound that matters and by little else.

## Direction

Toward a state that is neither sleep nor waking and is counted as sleep.
The persona is horizontal, unconscious for most of the interval, and monitoring. They wake more often, go less deep, and rise unrested from hours that will be recorded as a night's sleep by anybody counting.
And the watch is usually not chosen. It is what the body does when something depends on being heard.

## Lever

The usefulness of the open channel against what leaving it open costs.
The carrying force is that the watch is doing something necessary and irreplaceable: somebody has to hear the child, and a persona who slept through would be failing at the thing they most care about -- the vigilance is love as much as it is fear; the resistance is that the cost is invisible and uncredited, because the hours look identical from outside, so the persona is short of sleep in a way that will not appear in any account they or anybody else gives of their week.
The Kept Watch is unpaid work performed while unconscious.

## Echo

A full night that was not one.
It shows the surrender failing quietly rather than dramatically, which is the common form.
The Kept Watch is dated from whoever started depending on being heard.
