# Process: The Body Clock

## Taxonomy

[The Schedule](process_schedule_sleep.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: sleep

## Initiated by

The persona's timing is not a preference.
The Body Clock starts at chronotype: a substantially heritable internal phase that determines when this particular body becomes sleepy and when it wakes, shifting across a lifetime and not much movable by resolve.

## Direction

Toward hours that suit some bodies and not others.
The distribution is wide. Genuinely early and genuinely late personas differ by hours in when their systems will cooperate, and the difference is not effort -- adolescents run late for developmental reasons and are legislated at on the assumption that they are choosing it.
A persona can override the clock and cannot change it.

## Lever

The reality of the constitutional difference against how completely it is read as character.
The carrying force is that the clock is real, measurable, and largely given, so the early riser's virtue and the late riser's slovenliness are mostly a description of two body clocks meeting one timetable -- which is worth knowing because it removes a moral judgement that was never earned; the resistance is that knowing it changes very little, since the timetable does not move, and the persona with the inconvenient clock is left with an explanation rather than a remedy.
The Body Clock exonerates without helping.

## Echo

A phase the persona did not choose and cannot argue with.
It is the movement's premise and the reason the gap exists at all.
The Body Clock is dated from birth and drifts with age.
