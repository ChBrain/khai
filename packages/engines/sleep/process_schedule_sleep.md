# Process: The Schedule

## Taxonomy

[Sleep](process_sleep.md), the clock lived by against the clock made of.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: sleep

## Initiated by

The persona's hours are set by somebody who did not consult their body.
The Schedule starts at the mismatch: a [body clock](process_body_clock.md) that is largely constitutional, a social clock that is not negotiable, and the [gap](process_social_jetlag.md) between them -- with [night work](process_night_work.md) as the extreme case that never resolves.

## Direction

Toward a persona chronically out of phase and treated as though they had chosen it.
Chronotype is substantially heritable and shifts across a life, so the early riser and the late one are not exhibiting discipline and its absence, they are running different clocks -- and only one of those clocks is compatible with the hours the world keeps.
The late chronotype is therefore short of sleep on every working day and catches up at the weekend, which is the gap in the plainest form.

## Lever

The necessity of a shared schedule against the cost of imposing one clock on many bodies.
The carrying force is that coordination requires common hours and there is no way around it: shifts must be covered, schools must open, and a society where everybody slept to their own rhythm would be a society that could not arrange anything -- the schedule is not an oppression, it is the price of doing things together; the resistance is that the price is not shared evenly, since the same schedule is comfortable for one chronotype and a permanent deficit for another, and the second is read as lazy on the strength of a constitutional difference.
The Schedule charges some bodies more than others for the same arrangement.

## Echo

A persona in deficit on a timetable everybody else finds reasonable.
It shows the engine's social face, and it explains why the debt movement runs constantly in populations that believe themselves ordinary.
The Schedule is dated from the alarm, and it is renewed every working day.
