# Piece: Reach

## Taxonomy

[Ergonomics](piece_ergonomics.md), the facet this is a part of. Reach models what the body can get to on the object without strain -- the control within the arm's arc, the shelf at hand height, the pedal the foot meets without stretch. The reach envelope of anthropometrics: the zone a seated or standing body can comfortably work within. It is distinct from clearance (the space the object gives the body to fit) and from anthropometry (the sizing that sets the envelope): reach is whether the things the body must operate fall inside the arc it can work in.

## Place

At every control, handle, or surface the body must operate -- and at its distance from where the body is fixed while operating it. Reach lives in the gap between the seated hip and the far switch, the standing shoulder and the high shelf, measured against the arc the limb can sweep.

## Load Bearing

What reach holds up is whether the body can work the object from where it must stand or sit. Put the essential control outside the arc and the use is not impossible but strained -- the operator half-rises, twists, over-extends, and does it a thousand times. Remove good reach and the object forces the body out of its stable posture to meet it; the work still gets done, on the body's account, in the slow accumulation of over-stretching.

## Apparent

Reach shows in the body's posture as it works -- the easy economy of everything falling to hand, or the lean, the stretch, the rise onto toes that a badly-placed control demands. To the eye the layout looks flat; to the working body it reads as near or far, in reach or just beyond it. What is apparent is a placement; what is borne is the arc the body must break to meet it.

## Yearbook

The history of reach is the record of the stretches the object demanded -- the strains logged, the shortcuts worn where operators moved things into reach the design left out of it, the tall and the short each accommodating differently. Over time the object's placements are judged by the postures they forced, and the far control is dated from the shoulders it wore down.
