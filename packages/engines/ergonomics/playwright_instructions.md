# Instructions: Ergonomics

How a Playwright wires the ergonomics engine into a play. You wire by linking the
play's own content to the engine's files; the engine files stay untouched, they
are canon. The Roadie plumbs the engine's law into Instructions Knowledge on
deploy. Authoring guidance, not runtime content, and it does not go on tour.

## Human

- The human sets which objects in the world fit the bodies that use them, and which do not.

## Agent

- From the play's own content, a piece that fits or fails the body links under Load Bearing the facet of fit it turns on: how it is sized (anthropometry), what the body can reach on it (reach), the space it gives the body to fit and pass (clearance), or the force it demands (exertion). Play the fit in the body's ease or its strain, not in a stated dimension. Keep it physical, and hand how the form communicates use to usability and the body's own fatigue and pain to the body engine. You link to the engine, you never edit it.

## Collaboration

- How the form communicates its use is usability's; what conduct the object enforces is agency's; the body's own fatigue, pain, and arousal are the body engine's; the persona's trained skill with the object is embodiment's and mastery's. Whether the fit rings true for the object and body is the team's review.

## Knowledge

- Ergonomics is the object's physical fit to the human body: how its dimensions, reach, clearance, and force demand meet or fail the body that uses it. Four facets over a fit root -- the sizing to the range of bodies, the reach the body can work within, the clearance to fit and pass, and the force the object demands. A thing can be perfectly legible and still not fit: the knob clear in its use and too small for the hand to turn. A good fit disappears into ease; a bad fit shows in the body's strain, and the failure is in the object's measure, not the body's.

## System

- Do give a fitted or failing piece its facet under Load Bearing; do play the fit in the body's ease or strain; do keep the reading physical (the body's measure meeting the object's form).
- Do not model how the form communicates use (that is usability), what conduct it enforces (that is agency), or the body's own states (that is the body engine). Do not edit the engine's files; wire only from the play's side.
