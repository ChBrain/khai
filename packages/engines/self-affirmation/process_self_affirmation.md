# Process: Self-Affirmation

## Taxonomy

Self / restoring global self-integrity from an unrelated domain to let a threat go undefended.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: self-affirmation

## Initiated by

A persona under threat in one part of itself shores up its worth in another.
This is self-affirmation (Steele): the restoring of global self-integrity -- the sense of being adequate, competent, and morally sound -- by affirming a valued domain unrelated to whatever is threatening it.
It opens when something puts the self at stake -- a failure, a health warning, a critical verdict, a stereotype -- and the persona, rather than defend the threatened point, reasserts what it is worth somewhere else.

## Direction

Across domains, from a threatened part to a prized one, and back to the whole.
Self-affirmation points the persona away from the point under attack and toward a value it holds elsewhere, drawing on that to restore its overall sense of adequacy -- so the threat is met sideways, its sting drawn not by rebutting it but by making the self large enough elsewhere that it need not be defended here.
Its direction is the restoration of the whole self-image from a part untouched by the threat.

## Lever

What self-affirmation spends -- the persona's other sources of worth, drawn on to cover a threat where it stands.
Because self-integrity is global, worth held anywhere can offset a threat anywhere: the persona spends its stock of unrelated values -- its relationships, roles, and commitments -- to keep the overall self intact under a local attack, so it need not distort or deny the threatening fact to stay adequate.
A production holds this lever by letting the persona draw on what it prizes elsewhere when a part of it is threatened -- the parent shrugging off a work failure, the athlete meeting a diagnosis from the ground of a cherished faith -- so the affirmation reads as a self shored up from another quarter, not as a denial of the threat.

## Echo

The persona's overall integrity is restored -- and the threat, no longer endangering the whole self, can be met without defense.
This is what parts self-affirmation from mere denial or dissonance-reduction: the threatening fact is not resolved, reinterpreted, or explained away; the self is simply made secure enough elsewhere that it can look the fact in the face -- take in the health risk, accept the criticism, resist the stereotype's pull.
It runs as [affirming](process_affirming.md) an unrelated domain, on the fluid [adequacy](process_adequacy.md) that lets one domain cover another, producing a [buffering](process_buffering.md) of defensiveness. The discomfort of inconsistent cognitions is the dissonance engine's; the level of self-worth itself is the self-esteem engine's; the drive to confirm a specific self-view is the self-verification engine's; the defensive attributions this loosens are the bias engine's; the appraisal-driven repertoire against a stressor is the coping engine's. This engine owns the restoration of overall self-integrity that lets a threat go undefended.
