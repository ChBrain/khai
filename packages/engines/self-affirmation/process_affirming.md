# Process: Affirming

## Taxonomy

[Self-Affirmation](process_self_affirmation.md), the move -- reasserting worth in a valued domain unrelated to the threat.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: self-affirmation

## Initiated by

The persona brings forward a value it holds, apart from what is threatening it.
This is the affirming move (Steele; Sherman & Cohen): reflecting on a core value, a cherished relationship, a prized identity or role, and reasserting the persona's worth there -- the concrete act by which self-affirmation is done.
It opens when a threat is present and the persona reaches, not to the threatened point, but to something else it prizes and can stand on.

## Direction

Toward a valued domain, named and affirmed.
Affirming turns the persona's attention onto what it holds dear elsewhere -- what kind of person it is at its best, whom it loves, what it is committed to -- and lets it dwell there and reassert it, so that the value is made present and active rather than merely held.
Its direction is into the prized domain -- the value brought up and leaned on, deliberately and away from the threat.

## Lever

What affirming spends -- a value made salient and reasserted, at the cost of dwelling on the threat.
The move works by importance: the domain affirmed must be one the persona genuinely prizes, and unrelated to the threat, for a value it does not hold or one too near the attack will not restore the whole -- so affirming spends real, held values, and spends them best when they are not the ones under fire.
A production holds this lever by letting the persona turn to what it actually treasures when threatened -- naming it, recalling it, standing on it -- so the affirming reads as a genuine reaching for a held value, not as a slogan or a boast.

## Echo

A valued domain is made present and the persona's worth in it reasserted -- and the raw material of restoration is in hand.
The affirming is the act; what it does depends on the self being a system where worth reasserted in one place counts for the whole -- the fluid [adequacy](process_adequacy.md) it feeds -- and on the [buffering](process_buffering.md) that the restored integrity then produces.
This form owns the move itself: the deliberate bringing-forward of an unrelated value, the doing of self-affirmation.
