# @chbrain/khai-engine-self-affirmation

## 0.1.1

### Patch Changes

- ea8d1d1: Add the self-affirmation engine (process) -- restoring a persona's global self-integrity by affirming a valued domain unrelated to a threat, so the threat need not be defended. The gap analysis (Tier-2 #19) found this present only as a prose footnote in `dissonance`, though it operates far beyond it (health, feedback, stereotype threat) and is coupled to `dissonance`/`self-esteem` but not reducible to either.

  - **Root (process):** `self-affirmation` -- restoring overall self-integrity from an unrelated valued domain, letting a threat go undefended.
  - **Moments (process):**
    - `affirming` -- the move: bringing forward and reasserting a valued domain unrelated to the threat (a core value, relationship, or identity).
    - `adequacy` -- the fluid global integrity that makes the move work: the self as one whole, so worth reasserted in any domain offsets a threat in another rather than answering it point-for-point.
    - `buffering` -- the consequence: defensiveness lowered, threatening information admitted, stress and stereotype threat buffered.

  Warranted (LORE) on Steele (1988, the founding), Sherman & Cohen (2006) / Cohen & Sherman (2014, the mechanism and reach), Steele & Liu (1983, dissonance reduction as one case of integrity maintenance), and Cohen et al. (2006, the stereotype-threat field intervention). Bounded against `dissonance` (the reduction of a contradiction -- one instance of what self-affirmation does, Steele's own finding being that affirming elsewhere removes the need to reduce dissonance at all), `self-esteem` (the level of worth -- self-affirmation works without raising esteem in the threatened domain), `self-verification` (confirming a specific self-view vs restoring global adequacy), the `bias` engine's defensive members (the distortions self-affirmation _loosens_), and `coping` (the appraisal-driven repertoire vs securing the self that faces the stressor). Set at patch as the free level. No whitelist required -- the `self_affirmation`/`affirming`/`adequacy`/`buffering` stems are unique (`substitution`/`compensation` were avoided as bias compound heads).
