# Process: Adequacy

## Taxonomy

[Self-Affirmation](process_self_affirmation.md), the fluid global integrity that lets one domain cover another.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: self-affirmation

## Initiated by

The persona treats its worth as one whole, not a ledger of separate accounts.
This is the fluidity of the self-system (Steele): the sense of being adequate overall is global, so a threat to one domain endangers the whole self-image, and an affirmation in any domain can restore it -- integrity is a single resource, drawn on and replenished across the parts.
It is what makes the affirming move work at all: only because worth is fungible across domains does reasserting it elsewhere offset a threat here.

## Direction

Across the parts, toward the standing of the whole.
Adequacy runs the accounting by which a gain in one domain answers a loss in another: the self does not defend each part on its own terms but keeps an overall sense of being good enough, so worth banked in a prized value can cover a debit struck against a threatened one.
Its direction is the pooling of worth -- the local threat weighed against the global resource, not against the local defense.

## Lever

What adequacy spends -- the point-for-point defense of the threatened domain, given up for the standing of the whole.
Because integrity is global, the persona need not win the specific argument, disprove the failure, or rebut the stereotype; it spends the demand to defend the exact point, trading it for a self kept adequate overall -- which is why an unrelated affirmation suffices where a direct rebuttal is not even attempted.
A production holds this lever by letting the persona's worth be one pool rather than many walls -- a threat to one part met from the standing of the rest -- so the self reads as a system that keeps its whole rather than a set of positions each defended alone.

## Echo

The self is kept adequate overall, though the threatened point is left unanswered -- and that suffices.
This fluid, global integrity is the mechanism beneath the whole engine: it is why [affirming](process_affirming.md) an unrelated domain restores the self, and why the restoration then yields a [buffering](process_buffering.md) of defensiveness rather than a defense of the point.
This form owns the fluidity itself -- the self as one adequacy restorable from any of its parts, the theoretical crux that a threat anywhere can be met by worth anywhere.
