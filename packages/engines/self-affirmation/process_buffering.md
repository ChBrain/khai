# Process: Buffering

## Taxonomy

[Self-Affirmation](process_self_affirmation.md), the consequence -- defensiveness lowered, the threat borne without distortion.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: self-affirmation

## Initiated by

The persona, its integrity restored, lowers its guard against the threat.
This is the buffering effect (Sherman & Cohen): with the self shored up elsewhere, the threat no longer endangers the whole, so the defenses that would have distorted it relax -- and the persona can take the threatening thing in.
It follows the affirmation: what was too dangerous to face while the whole self was at stake becomes bearable once the self is secure from another quarter.

## Direction

Toward openness -- the threatening fact admitted rather than fended off.
Buffering turns the persona from defense to reception: it can accept critical feedback without dismissing it, take in a health risk without denying it, resist the drag of a stereotype rather than confirm it under threat, and meet a stressor with less distortion -- the guard down because the self behind it is safe.
Its direction is the letting-in -- the threat met squarely because it no longer threatens the whole.

## Lever

What buffering spends -- the protection of the defenses, given up because it is no longer needed.
The defenses cost accuracy: to fend off a threat the persona would have to distort it, deny the risk, discount the critic, dismiss the failure. Buffering spends that protection -- the persona gives up the comfort of the distortion because, shored up elsewhere, it can afford the truth.
A production holds this lever by letting the affirmed persona be the more open one -- hearing the hard feedback, weighing the risk, holding under the stereotype -- so the buffering reads as a self secure enough to be undefended, not as indifference or as the threat's having vanished.

## Echo

The persona takes in what it would otherwise have fended off -- and is changed, or steadied, by admitting it.
This opening is what makes self-affirmation matter beyond the moment: the buffered persona learns from the criticism, acts on the risk, performs under the stereotype instead of buckling to it -- the downstream reach across health, feedback, and threat that a mere easing of discomfort would not produce.
It rests on the [affirming](process_affirming.md) that restored the self and the fluid [adequacy](process_adequacy.md) that let an unrelated domain do it. The defensive attributions and distortions that buffering relaxes are the bias engine's; this form owns the lowering of defense itself -- the openness a restored integrity buys.
