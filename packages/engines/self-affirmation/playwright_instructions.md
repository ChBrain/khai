# Instructions: Self-Affirmation

How a Playwright wires the self-affirmation engine into a play. You wire by linking the play's own content to the engine's files; the engine files stay untouched, they are canon. The Roadie plumbs the engine's law into Instructions Knowledge on deploy. Authoring guidance, not runtime content, and it does not go on tour.

## Human

- The human sets the self-affirmation terrain: a threat to the persona in one domain, and the unrelated values, relationships, or identities it prizes elsewhere -- so a moment can run on the self shored up from another quarter rather than defending the point attacked.

## Agent

- From the play's own content, a persona links the self-affirmation a moment runs under its Projection: affirming, bringing forward a valued domain unrelated to the threat; adequacy, the overall sense of worth that an affirmation anywhere restores; and buffering, the lowered defensiveness that lets it take the threat in. Let the persona meet a threat sideways -- steadied by what it prizes elsewhere -- and so be more open than a cornered self would be. You link to the engine, you never edit it.

## Collaboration

- The discomfort of inconsistent cognitions and its reduction is the dissonance engine's; the level of self-worth itself is the self-esteem engine's; the drive to confirm a specific self-view is the self-verification engine's; the defensive attributions and distortions self-affirmation loosens are the bias engine's; the appraisal-driven repertoire against a stressor is the coping engine's. This engine supplies the restoration of global self-integrity -- the affirming, the fluid adequacy, and the buffering.

## Knowledge

- Self-affirmation is the restoring of a persona's global self-integrity -- its sense of being adequate, competent, and morally sound -- by affirming a valued domain unrelated to whatever is threatening it (Steele). It runs in three moments. Affirming is the move: bringing forward a core value, a cherished relationship, or a prized identity and reasserting worth there. Adequacy is the mechanism: the self as one fluid whole, so worth reasserted in any domain restores the overall self, and a threat in one part is met from the standing of the rest. Buffering is the consequence: with integrity restored, defensiveness falls and the persona can take in threatening information -- accept criticism, weigh a health risk, resist a stereotype -- without distorting it. Its signature is that an unrelated affirmation opens a persona that a direct rebuttal would only harden. Read a persona by what it affirms under threat and how the restored whole lowers its guard.

## System

- Do keep self-affirmation the restoring of global integrity from an unrelated domain, not the defense of the threatened point: the persona is made secure elsewhere so it need not answer the threat directly -- and it operates far beyond dissonance, across health, feedback, and stereotype threat.
- Do make the affirmed domain a genuinely held value and unrelated to the threat: a value the persona prizes, and away from the point attacked -- an affirmation too near the threat, or of a value not really held, will not restore the whole.
- Do let the fluid adequacy do the work: worth banked in a prized value covering a debit struck against a threatened one, the self kept adequate overall rather than defended part by part -- which is why an unrelated affirmation suffices where no direct rebuttal is attempted.
- Do let the buffering read as openness, not indifference: the affirmed persona admitting the hard feedback, weighing the risk, holding under the stereotype -- undefended because the self behind the guard is safe, not because the threat has vanished. Delegate the defenses it relaxes to the bias engine and the level of worth to self-esteem. Do not edit the engine's files; wire only from the play's side.
