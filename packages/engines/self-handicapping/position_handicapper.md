# Position: Handicapper

## Taxonomy

Self / the trait of self-handicapping -- the standing disposition beneath [Self-Handicapping](process_self_handicapping.md) (Rhodewalt's Self-Handicapping Scale).

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: self-handicapping

## Has

A standing readiness to reach for a handicap under evaluative threat.
Trait self-handicapping, as against any single obstacle erected: at the high end, the chronic handicapper who meets each test already reaching for its cover -- the effort quietly withdrawn, the excuse pre-placed -- and at the low end, the persona who takes its tests uncovered, letting the result fall as it will.
It runs highest where the persona's sense of its own ability is both prized and uncertain, so that a clean verdict feels too dangerous to risk, and it tends to entrench, each handicap that spares a clean failure making the next one likelier.

## Orders

Run from here, into every test of ability.
The chronic handicapper meets an evaluative moment with the cover already half-built -- inclined to hedge, to leave itself an out, to protect the ability-image before the result rather than stake it clean; the low handicapper meets the same moment exposed, its effort fully spent and the verdict left to stand.
It sets the default reach for an out, before any single test calls for one.

## Loses

At the high end, the growth and the clean wins that only an uncovered test allows; at the low end, the shelter a handicap affords.
The chronic handicapper pays dearly -- the covers it builds really cost it outcomes, it seldom learns what it could have done at full effort, and its hedging tends to erode the very ability it guards; the persona who never hedges keeps its chances whole but faces each clean verdict undefended.
What each trades is the other's good -- the protection of a covered test against the growth and the honest measure of an uncovered one.

## Drives

On that standing readiness:
rides it -- the chronic handicapper hedging each test as a matter of course, the low handicapper taking each one clean -- or works against it, the handicapper learning to spend an effort undefended and bear the verdict, the exposed one learning where a hedge is only prudence.
The disposition holds underneath either way, a resting readiness to cover that the persona builds and manages more than a move that merely comes and goes. It expresses in the [acquired](process_acquired.md) and [claimed](process_claimed.md) handicaps a moment runs.
