# Process: Claimed

## Taxonomy

[Self-Handicapping](process_self_handicapping.md), an impediment merely asserted before the outcome.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: self-handicapping

## Initiated by

The persona claims an obstacle it has not actually created.
This is claimed, or self-reported, self-handicapping (Leary & Shepperd): the persona announces an impediment ahead of the test -- feeling ill, anxious, stressed, underslept, off its game -- laying down a verbal excuse in advance without genuinely degrading its performance.
It opens where the persona wants the cover of a handicap but not its real cost, and so asserts the burden rather than taking it on.

## Direction

Toward a stated excuse, set before the result but off the performance itself.
Claimed handicapping moves the persona to say, ahead of the outcome, that conditions are against it -- so a poor result can be hung on the stated impediment -- while leaving the actual performance untouched, the excuse riding alongside the effort rather than eating into it.
Its direction is into the record, not the performance -- the reason pre-placed in words, ready to be pointed to whichever way the result falls.

## Lever

What claimed handicapping spends -- credibility, not the outcome.
This is the cheap form: because nothing real is impaired, the persona keeps its full chance at success and pays only in the weakness and cost of the excuse -- a claim that persuades less than a real obstacle would, and that, overused or seen through, reads as the excuse-making it is.
A production holds this lever by letting the persona lay its verbal cover -- the pre-announced headache, the mention of a bad week -- without dropping its effort, so the claim protects the ability-image at the price of conviction, not of the result.

## Echo

The persona enters the outcome with an excuse already on record -- and its performance unspent.
A failure can be pointed at the claimed impediment, and a success stands undiminished since nothing real was given up; the cover is cheaper and weaker than a built obstacle, and leans on being believed.
Its costlier cousin actually creates the obstacle -- that is [acquired](process_acquired.md) handicapping; this form owns the merely asserted impediment, the excuse placed in words before the test with the performance left intact.
