# Instructions: Self-Handicapping

How a Playwright wires the self-handicapping engine into a play. You wire by linking the play's own content to the engine's files; the engine files stay untouched, they are canon. The Roadie plumbs the engine's law into Instructions Knowledge on deploy. Authoring guidance, not runtime content, and it does not go on tour.

## Human

- The human sets the self-handicapping terrain: an evaluative test whose result matters to the persona, an ability it prizes but is unsure of, and the obstacles it could build or claim before the outcome -- so a moment can run on a genuine pre-emptive cover.

## Agent

- From the play's own content, a persona links the self-handicapping a moment runs under its Projection: acquired, really creating an obstacle (withdrawing effort, procrastinating, undertraining) so a failure has an external cause; claimed, merely asserting an impediment (illness, anxiety, bad sleep) without degrading the performance; and how disposed it is to reach for one at all. Let the cover be set before the outcome, and let the acquired kind really cost the result. You link to the engine, you never edit it.

## Collaboration

- The after-the-fact attribution that credits success and blames failure once the result is known is the bias engine's self-serving member; the post-hoc account offered for a completed act is the repair engine's excuse; the belief in one's own capability is the self-efficacy engine's; the level of self-worth being defended is the self-esteem engine's. This engine supplies the anticipatory obstacle -- the acquired and claimed handicaps and the handicapper's disposition.

## Knowledge

- Self-handicapping is the erecting of an obstacle before an evaluative outcome, so the outcome attaches to the obstacle rather than to the persona's ability (Berglas & Jones). It works a double-bind in advance: a failure is discounted (the obstacle, not a lack of ability) and a success augmented (won despite the burden). It runs in two forms. Acquired is a real obstacle created -- effort withdrawn, practice skipped, a substance taken -- genuinely impairing the performance, the costly and stronger cover. Claimed is an impediment merely asserted -- illness, anxiety, stress reported in advance -- a verbal excuse without real cost, the cheaper and weaker cover. And the handicapper is the disposition: a standing readiness to reach for a handicap, highest where ability is prized and uncertain. Read a persona by the cover it builds before the test and how readily it reaches for one.

## System

- Do keep self-handicapping anticipatory and behavioral: the obstacle is put in place before the outcome, not the attribution offered after it -- let the after-the-fact crediting and blaming be the bias engine's self-serving member, and the excuse for a completed act the repair engine's.
- Do stage the double-bind: the handicap discounting a failure and augmenting a success, so the ability-image is shielded whichever way the result falls -- and let it be reached for where ability is prized and the outcome uncertain.
- Do keep the two forms distinct in their cost: the acquired handicap really impairing the performance and so really lowering the odds -- a strong cover bought with a worse chance -- and the claimed handicap leaving the performance intact but persuading less, a cheap cover that leans on being believed.
- Do let the handicapper disposition entrench: each cover that spares a clean failure making the next likelier, the chronic handicapper seldom learning what it could do at full effort. Delegate the belief in ability to the self-efficacy engine and the worth defended to the self-esteem engine. Do not edit the engine's files; wire only from the play's side.
