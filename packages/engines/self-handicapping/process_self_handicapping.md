# Process: Self-Handicapping

## Taxonomy

Self / erecting an obstacle before an outcome to control what its result will mean.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: self-handicapping

## Initiated by

A persona, facing a test of its ability, builds an obstacle for itself before the result is in.
This is self-handicapping (Berglas & Jones): the erecting of an impediment ahead of an evaluative outcome, so that the outcome can be read against the impediment rather than against the persona's ability.
It is reached for where ability is on the line and uncertain, and where the persona's sense of being able matters enough to protect -- so it lays down, in advance, a reason the result might not mean what it seems.

## Direction

Toward the attribution the outcome will carry, arranged before the outcome.
Self-handicapping points the persona's action at the meaning of a result not yet known: it sets an obstacle in place so that a failure will attach to the obstacle and a success will shine the brighter for having overcome it.
Its direction is pre-emptive -- the excuse built before the test, the frame set before the verdict, so the ability-image is guarded whichever way the result falls.

## Lever

What self-handicapping spends -- the persona's real chance at the outcome, traded for control of what the outcome will mean.
The double-bind is bought at a price: an obstacle strong enough to excuse a failure also makes the failure likelier, so the persona spends its odds of success to secure the attribution -- protecting the image of ability by risking the ability's actual showing.
A production holds this lever by letting the persona pay for its cover -- withdrawing the effort, laying the claim, taking on the burden -- and get, in return, a result that can no longer simply indict its ability, so the handicap reads as a trade of the outcome for its meaning, not as mere weakness or bad luck.

## Echo

The result comes in against a handicap the persona set -- and cannot cleanly measure the ability the persona feared it would.
Fail, and the handicap discounts it: not unable, only burdened. Succeed, and the handicap augments it: able enough to win even so. Either way the ability-image is shielded, at the cost of the chance that was spent to shield it.
It runs as an [acquired](process_acquired.md) obstacle really created or a [claimed](process_claimed.md) one merely asserted, and some personas reach for it as a standing [handicapper](position_handicapper.md). The after-the-fact attribution that credits success and blames failure once the result is known is the bias engine's self-serving member; the post-hoc account offered for a completed act is the repair engine's excuse; the belief in one's own capability is the self-efficacy engine's; the level of self-worth being defended is the self-esteem engine's. This engine owns the obstacle a persona raises before the outcome to control what its result will mean.
