# Instructions: Social Identity

How a Playwright wires the social-identity engine into a play. You wire by linking the play's own content to the engine's files; the engine files stay untouched, they are canon. The Roadie plumbs the engine's law into Instructions Knowledge on deploy. Authoring guidance, not runtime content, and it does not go on tour.

## Human

- The human defines the group terrain: which group the persona draws their "we" from, where it stands in the order of the scene, and whether the boundary out of it is permeable and the order legitimate and stable -- the reads that select the stance.

## Agent

- From the play's own content, a persona links the social-identity stance it holds (identification, mobility, creativity, or competition) under its Projection. Let the stance shape how the persona reads the line between us and them and what they do when the in-group's standing is threatened. You link to the engine, you never edit it.

## Collaboration

- The personal self-story is the identity engine's "I"; this is the group-belonging "we" it delegates. The in-group bias on perception -- favouring one's own, seeing the out-group as all alike -- is the bias engine's. The allegiance that raises the cost of leaving is loyalty's; the group's development over time is the group engine's; the rank order itself is status and hierarchy; a discrediting mark is stigma's. This engine supplies the self drawn from the group and the stance held toward its standing.

## Knowledge

- Social identity is the part of the self a persona draws from group membership: which categories they fall in, what those categories are worth, and the drive to see the in-group compare well because the self is lifted when the group is. Identification is the secure merger with the in-group -- solidarity and favouritism. Mobility is the individual exit into a higher-standing group, where boundaries are permeable. Creativity is the reframe that recovers group pride by changing the terms of comparison. Competition is the collective contest to change the actual order, where it is read as illegitimate and unstable.

## System

- Do let the read of the structure select the stance: a permeable boundary invites mobility, a closed but legitimate order invites creativity, an illegitimate and unstable one invites competition.
- Do let the stance move the arc -- a persona who turns from creativity to competition is one for whom the order has become intolerable.
- Do leave the in-group bias on the perception to the bias engine; this engine holds the self and the stance, not the slant.
- Do not edit the engine's files; wire only from the play's side.
