# Position: Competition

## Taxonomy

[Social Identity](position_social_identity.md), the stance this is one form of.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: social-identity

## Has

A group, and a grievance about its standing read as both wrong and changeable.
The persona takes the order that places the in-group below as illegitimate -- it should not be so -- and as unstable -- it need not stay so; the boundary is closed, so there is no leaving, and reframing no longer satisfies.

## Orders

Contest.
The position turns the persona to change the actual status relations, with the group and against the out-group -- direct comparison, collective action, the open challenge to the order that keeps the in-group down.
The aim is not to feel better about the group's place but to move it.

## Loses

The quiet, and the individual escape.
Competition names the out-group as rival and the order as unjust, and forecloses passing -- the persona's lot is bound to the group's, win or lose -- so it trades the safety of exit and the comfort of the reframe for the risk of the contest.
There is no standing apart once the line is drawn.

## Drives

Toward changing the real order through collective action.
The persona mobilises with the in-group to overturn the status relations rather than to reinterpret them; the strategy of a boundary read as closed and an order read as illegitimate and unstable -- the point where social identity turns into intergroup conflict.
It is the contest [creativity](position_creativity.md) declines and [mobility](position_mobility.md) sidesteps: the group's standing changed in fact, not in framing, and not by leaving.
