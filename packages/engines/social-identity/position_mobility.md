# Position: Mobility

## Taxonomy

[Social Identity](position_social_identity.md), the stance this is one form of.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: social-identity

## Has

A hold on the group loose enough to leave, and boundaries read as permeable.
The persona belongs to a lower-standing group but does not take that belonging as fixed: the line between groups looks crossable, and standing looks like something an individual can change by changing sides.

## Orders

Pass.
The position turns the persona to move themselves rather than the group -- to cross into the higher-standing group as an individual, to be taken for one of them, to leave the old category behind rather than lift it.
The effort goes into the self's passage, not the group's cause.

## Loses

The group as a home, and the solidarity that came with it.
Mobility means distancing from the low-standing in-group -- playing down the marks of it, keeping the old ties quiet -- so the persona gains a better standing at the cost of the belonging they leave, and is not fully safe in the new group either.
They cannot both pass and stand with those who cannot.

## Drives

Toward individual exit and passage into a better-standing group.
The persona improves their position by changing groups, not by changing the group's lot; the strategy of permeable boundaries, available to the one who can cross and closed to the one who cannot.
Where the boundary is read as closed rather than open, exit is barred and the drive turns instead to holding and reframing the group ([creativity](position_creativity.md)) or contesting the order with it ([competition](position_competition.md)).
