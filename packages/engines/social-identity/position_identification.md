# Position: Identification

## Taxonomy

[Social Identity](position_social_identity.md), the stance this is one form of.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: social-identity

## Has

A strong merger of the self with the in-group.
The group is not a thing the persona belongs to but a thing they are: its name is theirs, its history theirs, its enemies theirs.
This is the baseline stance, the one the other three depart from -- the self read primarily as a group member, the "we" running ahead of the "I".

## Orders

Be us.
The position turns the persona to act as an instance of the group rather than an individual -- to hold what the group holds, feel its wins and wounds as their own, and stand with it by default.
The more the group is salient or threatened, the more the persona's own view narrows to the group's.

## Loses

The separateness that would let the persona stand apart from the group's fate.
Their standing rises and falls with the group's; a slight to the in-group lands as a personal wound, and dissent from within feels like betrayal rather than difference.
The persona cannot easily be neutral about the group, because they are not outside it.

## Drives

Toward in-group solidarity and favouritism, and toward differentiation from the out-group.
The persona defends the group, prefers its members, and reads the world in its terms -- the depersonalised self, standing with its own. Held securely, when the group's standing is good, this rests in belonging and pride.
When the standing is not good, staying here -- doubling down rather than leaving, reframing, or contesting -- is itself the choice, and the strain of it is what pushes some toward [mobility](position_mobility.md), [creativity](position_creativity.md), or [competition](position_competition.md).
