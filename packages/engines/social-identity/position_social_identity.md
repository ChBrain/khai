# Position: Social Identity

## Taxonomy

Self-concept / group membership -- the group-belonging "we" to the identity engine's "I".

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: social-identity

## Has

The part of the self a persona draws from the groups they belong to.
A "we" carried beside the "I": a standing sense of which categories they fall in -- a nation, a faith, a class, a team, a side -- and what those categories are worth in the order the persona reads around them.
It arrives already sorted, the world divided into an in-group the persona is part of and out-groups they are not, before any particular person is met.

## Orders

Read from the group.
The position tilts the persona to see themselves and others through the line between us and them, and to take the in-group's standing as bearing on their own worth.
A win for the group lifts the persona; a slight to it is felt as a slight to them; a stranger is read first as a member of a category and only then as a person.

## Loses

The option of meeting others as pure individuals, and of a self-worth held apart from any group's.
Once a category is salient the persona cannot un-see it: people arrive tagged in-group or out, and the persona's own standing is hostage to the group's -- lifted when it rises, threatened when it falls.
The self is no longer theirs alone to value; part of it is on loan from the group.

## Drives

Toward positive distinctiveness: the in-group compared favourably against the out-group, because the self is raised when the group is.
When the comparison comes out well, the drive rests in belonging and pride. When it comes out badly -- the group the persona belongs to found wanting against another -- the drive to restore standing takes one of four stances: [identification](position_identification.md) doubling down on the group, [mobility](position_mobility.md) leaving it, [creativity](position_creativity.md) reframing the comparison, or [competition](position_competition.md) contesting the order.
The in-group bias on the perception itself -- seeing the out-group as all alike, the in-group as right -- is the bias engine's, not asserted here.
