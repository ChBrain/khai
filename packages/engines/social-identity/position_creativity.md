# Position: Creativity

## Taxonomy

[Social Identity](position_social_identity.md), the stance this is one form of.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: social-identity

## Has

A group held onto, and a comparison the persona treats as open to redefinition.
The boundary is read as closed -- there is no passing out -- but the terms on which the group is judged are read as movable: what is compared, against whom, and how the attribute is valued.

## Orders

Reframe.
The position turns the persona to recover a positive social identity without changing the actual order -- by finding a new dimension on which the in-group compares well, choosing a different group to compare against, or revaluing the very attribute that marked the group down.
The move is on the terms of comparison, not the facts of standing.

## Loses

The straight contest, and the simplicity of the old scale.
Creativity leaves the real status order in place and wins on new ground instead -- "we may have less of what they prize, but more of what matters"; "the mark once carried in shame is worn now with pride" -- so it buys dignity without buying power, and can be read from outside as consolation.
The persona gives up changing the facts in exchange for changing their meaning.

## Drives

Toward a positive distinctiveness found by shifting the comparison rather than the order.
The persona recovers group pride by remaking the terms -- a new axis, a new referent, a reclaimed attribute -- the strategy available when boundaries are closed but the order is not yet contested.
Where the order comes to be read as illegitimate and unstable, the reframe can harden into the direct challenge of [competition](position_competition.md); where a way out opens, some take [mobility](position_mobility.md) instead.
