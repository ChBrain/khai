# Process: The Recalibration

## Taxonomy

[The Repetition](process_repetition_warning.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: warning

## Initiated by

The receiver revises their estimate, and the revision is correct.
Having seen forty alerts and no incidents, they now hold that an alert means very little, which is what the evidence supports.

## Direction

Toward a well-founded belief that guarantees the wrong action once.
This is the sharpest form of the problem, because nothing here is a failure. The persona has updated on evidence, the update is in the right direction and roughly the right size, and their estimate is better than the one they started with.
It is also the estimate that will produce inaction on the occasion the system exists for, and no better reasoning would have prevented that.

## Lever

The soundness of the inference against the shape of what it is about.
The carrying force is that revising on evidence is the correct thing to do and the alternative is superstition: a persona who kept treating every alert as urgent after forty had come to nothing would not be careful, they would be failing to learn; the resistance is that the events being estimated are rare and severe, so a correct estimate of the average case is a terrible guide to the tail, and there is no procedure available to the persona that separates the two from inside.
The Recalibration is good reasoning arriving at a lethal answer.

## Echo

Somebody who has learned the system accurately.
It shows why alarm fatigue is not a lapse of attention and cannot be trained away.
The Recalibration is dated from the accumulated record and is revised only by a disaster.
