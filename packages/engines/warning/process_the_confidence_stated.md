# Process: The Confidence Stated

## Taxonomy

[The Issuing](process_issuing_warning.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: warning

## Initiated by

The warner has to say how sure they are.
Thirty percent chance, elevated risk, possible, likely, we advise -- and none of those survives contact with a person deciding whether to leave the house.

## Direction

Toward a probability that will be heard as a promise.
A warning carries uncertainty and the receiver needs an action, so the uncertainty is converted somewhere between the two into a yes or a no. If the warner hedges, the hedge is read as weakness and the warning is discounted; if they do not, the warning is read as a prediction and they will be held to it.
There is no register in which a probability arrives as a probability.

## Lever

The honesty of stating uncertainty against the uselessness of what arrives.
The carrying force is that stating it is the right thing to do and the alternative is worse: a warner who conceals their uncertainty is misleading people, forecasters who publish confidence intervals are being straight with the public, and the practice has improved considerably; the resistance is that the receiver is not running a probability calculation, they are deciding whether to move, so the careful figure is compressed into a verdict by the person it was given to, and the warner is then judged on the verdict.
The Confidence Stated is accurate on issue and binary on arrival.

## Echo

A number that became a claim in transit.
It shows why warners are so often accused of having said something they did not say.
The Confidence Stated is dated at the bulletin and is remembered as a forecast.
