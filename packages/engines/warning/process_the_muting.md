# Process: The Muting

## Taxonomy

[The Repetition](process_repetition_warning.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: warning

## Initiated by

Somebody turns it off.
The volume down, the limits widened, the notification disabled, the pager rule that suppresses this class of alert overnight.

## Direction

Toward a deliberate act by a competent person with a good reason.
Muting is not laziness and it is rarely rule-breaking: the alarm is genuinely interfering with the work, the false-alarm rate genuinely is enormous, and the person silencing it is frequently the most experienced one there.
And it is invisible afterwards, because a suppressed alert produces no record of what it would have said.

## Lever

The soundness of the local decision against the invisibility of the aggregate.
The carrying force is that the muting is usually correct on the day: the alarms are mostly artefacts, the noise degrades the attention available for everything else, and a unit that silenced nothing would be worse at care rather than better; the resistance is that each mute is decided locally with the full context and reviewed globally with none of it, so the practitioner who acted sensibly on Tuesday is the one named in the report, and the rate that made it sensible is not in evidence.
The Muting is defensible one at a time and indefensible in a list.

## Echo

A safety system with a part of it switched off by the people it protects.
It shows why the fix is never at the point where the suppression happened.
The Muting is dated from the shift it started on and outlives whoever started it.
