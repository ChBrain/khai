# Process: The Milling

## Taxonomy

[The Receiving](process_receiving_warning.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: warning

## Initiated by

The persona goes to find out whether it is true.
They ring a relative, check a second source, ask a neighbour, look at what the people on the street are doing.

## Direction

Toward a decision made socially rather than informationally.
Milling is not hesitation and it is not stupidity: a single message from one source is weak evidence, and a persona who acted on every one of those would spend their life responding to things that never happened.
What resolves it is other people. The persona acts when somebody they trust has acted, and the message's content matters less at this point than the movement around them.

## Lever

The soundness of seeking confirmation against what confirmation costs to obtain.
The carrying force is that this is good reasoning under uncertainty and warning systems that ignore it are badly designed: multiple consistent sources genuinely are better evidence, checking catches errors, and the persona doing it is behaving as anybody sensible would; the resistance is that everybody is checking simultaneously, so each is waiting on evidence the others are also waiting to produce, and a whole population can hold still for an hour while every individual in it does the sensible thing.
The Milling is a search for a signal that only exists once somebody stops searching.

## Echo

A crowd of people each waiting for one of the others.
It shows why warnings are amplified far more by a neighbour moving than by any improvement to the message.
The Milling is dated from the first message and ends when somebody breaks it.
