# Process: Warning

## Taxonomy

Signalling (no khai file of its own yet).

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: warning

## Initiated by

Somebody says a bad thing may be coming, to somebody who would have to act.
Warning starts wherever a harm is forecast rather than met: the siren, the alert on the ward, the memo nobody circulated, the friend who says you should get that looked at, the forecast, the light on the dashboard.
It is not the hazard and its assessment, which is risk's, and not the persona's response to a present threat, which is fear's. It is the signal itself, and what a run of them does to everybody involved.

## Direction

Toward a decision taken early on information that is not yet good.
The engine reads four movements: [the issuing](process_issuing_warning.md), where somebody sets a threshold and accepts one kind of error to avoid the other; [the receiving](process_receiving_warning.md), what a warned persona actually does, which is rarely what anybody plans for; [the repetition](process_repetition_warning.md), what a run of warnings does to the signal; and [the aftermath](process_aftermath_warning.md), the twist -- what happens when the warning works.
The four are one operation, and the last is the one nobody builds for.

## Lever

The value of acting early against the impossibility of knowing whether it was needed.
The carrying force is that warning is one of the better things people have built and it saves an enormous number of lives: forecasts, alarms, screening, and the friend who says something have all moved harm out of the future, they work, and an account that treats every alert as noise has mistaken the phenomenon; the resistance is that every warning is issued before the fact is settled, so it is always possible that nothing was coming -- and the better the warning system, the more often that will be exactly how it looks.
Warning is read on what happened next, which is the one thing it makes unreadable.

## Echo

A signal spent, and a credibility either raised or lowered by something nobody can determine.
Warning does not close; it is a signal being issued and priced, gathered into one reading -- an issuing, a receiving, a repetition, an aftermath.
The engine's twist is here. **A warning that works looks exactly like one that was wrong.** If the persona acts and the harm does not arrive, what remains is a disruption with no disaster: the evacuation that turned out to be unnecessary, the shutdown that cost a fortune for nothing, the treatment for something that never developed. The successful warning and the false alarm are the same event from the outside, so the only warnings that clearly earn their credibility are the ones nobody acted on.
It hands off what it is not: the hazard, its size, and how it is estimated are risk's; the persona's response to a threat in the room is fear's; anticipatory dread with no object is apprehension's; whether the source is believed is trust's; the design of the instrument is usability's and ergonomics's; the number the forecast is expressed in is measure's; and a warning made to **stand for** a theme is authored key, the play's pitch.
The persona is dated from the last alert they ignored and were right to.
