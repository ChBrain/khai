# Process: The Next Warning

## Taxonomy

[The Aftermath](process_aftermath_warning.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: warning

## Initiated by

The next one goes out, into whatever the last one left.
Same instrument, same threshold, same wording -- and a different reception, because the audience has a record now.

## Direction

Toward a signal whose strength was set by the previous outcome rather than by this hazard.
A warning's power is not in its content. It is the credit the source has left, and that credit is spent by every alert and repaid only by a disaster that follows one.
So a system that has been right and quiet is weaker than one that has been wrong and vindicated, and neither of those facts is about the danger now approaching.

## Lever

The urgency of the present hazard against the balance the sender is working from.
The carrying force is that the audience is entitled to use the record and could not do otherwise: past performance is the only information they have about a source, weighting it is basic and sensible, and a public that ignored a body's track record would be easier to mislead rather than safer; the resistance is that the record was assembled by a process that cannot distinguish the successes from the errors, so a source is being priced on a history that contains no usable information about how good it is.
The Next Warning is heard at a volume set by events that had nothing to do with it.

## Echo

A signal arriving pre-discounted.
It shows why the operation never resolves, and why credibility in this domain is spent rather than accumulated.
The Next Warning is dated from the last all-clear and starts from whatever was left.
