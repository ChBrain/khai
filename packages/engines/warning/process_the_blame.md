# Process: The Blame

## Taxonomy

[The Aftermath](process_aftermath_warning.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: warning

## Initiated by

Somebody is held to account, and it depends entirely on which error occurred.
A miss produces an inquiry, a name, and a finding. A false alarm produces a grumble and an invoice.

## Direction

Toward an asymmetry that everybody in the system can read.
The two errors are punished on completely different scales, and the difference is not proportional to the harms -- it is proportional to how identifiable the victim is. A missed harm has a person attached to it; a false alarm's cost is spread across thousands who each lost an afternoon.
Everybody who will ever set a threshold watches this happen and draws the obvious conclusion.

## Lever

The justice of holding somebody to account against what the accounting selects for.
The carrying force is that inquiries are right and necessary: a missed warning that killed somebody should be examined, the families are owed it, and systems have genuinely improved because of findings like these; the resistance is that only one of the two errors is ever examined this way, so the review process is a one-sided instrument that reliably pushes thresholds down, and the fatigue it manufactures is nobody's finding because fatigue has no victim with a name.
The Blame corrects one error by amplifying the other.

## Echo

A lesson learned by everybody who sets a threshold.
It shows the loop from the aftermath back to the issuing, which is where the drift comes from.
The Blame is dated from the report and is remembered by the profession.
