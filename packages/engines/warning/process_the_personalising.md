# Process: The Personalising

## Taxonomy

[The Receiving](process_receiving_warning.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: warning

## Initiated by

The persona works out whether this one is about them.
Not whether the danger is real -- whether it is theirs: this address, this building, this body, this afternoon.

## Direction

Toward the step that everything turns on.
Across the field research, personalisation predicts protective action better than belief in the warning, understanding of it, or trust in the source. A persona can accept every word of an alert, rate the hazard as serious, and do nothing, because they have not placed themselves inside it.
Specificity is what does the work: a named street moves people that a named region does not.

## Lever

The accuracy of exempting oneself against how the exemption is reached.
The carrying force is that most warnings genuinely are not about most recipients -- an alert covers a wide area to be safe, the persona is usually outside the part that matters, and a persona who took every general warning personally would be immobilised; the resistance is that the judgement is made on the same optimism that makes people underrate their own odds generally, so the exemption is reached before the evidence is examined and is then defended with whatever comes to hand.
The Personalising decides the outcome and is made in a second.

## Echo

A warning accepted in full and applied to somebody else.
It shows where the design effort belongs, which is not in the urgency of the message.
The Personalising is dated at the moment of reading and rarely revised.
