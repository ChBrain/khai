# Process: The Averted

## Taxonomy

[The Aftermath](process_aftermath_warning.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: warning

## Initiated by

Nothing happened, and it is not clear why.
The town was evacuated and the flood came to the edge of the road. The system was patched and no breach followed. The drug was withdrawn and the deaths did not occur.

## Direction

Toward an outcome with no observable difference from an overreaction.
A prevented harm leaves the same trace as an absent one, which is none. There is no version of the record in which the disaster appears with a line through it, and the counterfactual is not available to anybody, including the people who acted.
So the strongest evidence a warning system can produce is a nothing that somebody has to be persuaded to interpret.

## Lever

The size of what was prevented against its complete unavailability as evidence.
The carrying force is that the prevention is usually real and sometimes vast: the modelling was sound, the mechanism was understood, the action plausibly worked, and treating every averted harm as an imagined one would dismantle every preventive institution there is; the resistance is that the case rests entirely on a model of what would have happened, and models are exactly what the sceptic is disputing, so the argument for the warning's success is made in the same currency as the argument that it was never needed.
The Averted is the engine's best outcome and its weakest exhibit.

## Echo

A success that has to be argued for.
It shows why preventive work is chronically underfunded by people who are not being foolish.
The Averted is dated from the day nothing occurred and has no record of its own.
