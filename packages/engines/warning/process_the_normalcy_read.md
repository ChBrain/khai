# Process: The Normalcy Read

## Taxonomy

[The Receiving](process_receiving_warning.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: warning

## Initiated by

The persona looks around and everything appears fine.
The street is as it was, the colleagues are working, nobody is running, and the room contains no evidence of anything at all.

## Direction

Toward the present being taken as a forecast.
A warning is about a future state and the persona is standing in the current one, so the most available evidence is the strongest evidence against the warning -- and it is available continuously, at no cost, in high resolution.
The signal is a sentence. The absence of trouble is an entire environment.

## Lever

The reliability of the ordinary against what the ordinary cannot show.
The carrying force is that reading normality is right nearly all the time and is how anybody functions: the vast majority of moments are exactly what they appear to be, a persona who treated calm surroundings as concealing a catastrophe would be unwell, and the inference is one of the best-calibrated things people do; the resistance is that the class of events warnings exist for is precisely the class in which the surroundings are normal until they are not, so the inference is at its most confident exactly where it is worthless.
The Normalcy Read is correct on almost every occasion and fails on all the ones that matter.

## Echo

A room that vouches for itself.
It shows why the evidence against a warning is always closer to hand than the evidence for it.
The Normalcy Read is dated continuously and is never wrong until it is.
