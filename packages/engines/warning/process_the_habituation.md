# Process: The Habituation

## Taxonomy

[The Repetition](process_repetition_warning.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: warning

## Initiated by

The signal stops arriving as a signal.
It is still audible, still legible, still there -- and it has become part of the room rather than an event in it.

## Direction

Toward a stimulus that no longer interrupts anything.
Habituation is not a decision and cannot be undone by resolve: a repeated stimulus that has not been followed by consequence stops recruiting attention, and this happens below the level anybody can inspect.
The persona will report that they heard it. They will not have attended to it, and those are different things.

## Lever

The efficiency of tuning out against what gets tuned out with it.
The carrying force is that habituation is a necessary faculty and its absence is a disorder: an organism that responded to every repeated stimulus with full arousal would exhaust itself in an afternoon, and the filtering is what makes a busy environment workable at all; the resistance is that the filter operates on the signal's frequency rather than on its meaning, so a warning that fires often is filtered whether or not this particular firing is the real one.
The Habituation cannot tell the ninth false alarm from the tenth true one.

## Echo

An alert that is being heard and not received.
It shows the mechanism running below anybody's judgement.
The Habituation is dated from repetition and does not require any belief about the signal.
