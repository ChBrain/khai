# Process: The Issuing

## Taxonomy

[Warning](process_warning.md), where the signal is decided.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: warning

## Initiated by

Somebody has to say something before they know.
The Issuing starts at a choice that is always made in advance of the facts: [a threshold set](process_the_threshold_set.md) somewhere, [a confidence stated](process_the_confidence_stated.md) in a form that does not carry it, and [a liability](process_the_liability.md) that falls unevenly on the two ways of being wrong.

## Direction

Toward a signal whose settings are a decision about which error to make.
There is no neutral threshold. Set it low and the system warns often, catches nearly everything, and is wrong most times it fires; set it high and it is right when it fires and misses the early ones.
Whoever sets it is choosing between two kinds of harm on behalf of people who will never see the choice, and the choice will be judged only by whichever error actually occurred.

## Lever

The necessity of picking a threshold against the fact that any pick is arguable.
The carrying force is that somebody has to decide and the deciders are usually careful: the trade-off is understood, the settings are argued over by people who know the domain, and refusing to warn until certain would defeat the entire purpose of warning; the resistance is that a threshold is a moral choice wearing a technical form, so it is made by whoever owns the instrument, defended in the language of accuracy, and reviewed only after somebody has been hurt by the error it accepted.
The Issuing decides in advance who will be failed.

## Echo

A signal with a setting nobody outside can see.
It is the movement everything else inherits, and the one that never appears in the account afterwards.
The Issuing is dated from the specification and is revisited only after an inquiry.
