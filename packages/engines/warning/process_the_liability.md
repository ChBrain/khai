# Process: The Liability

## Taxonomy

[The Issuing](process_issuing_warning.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: warning

## Initiated by

The two ways of being wrong do not cost the warner the same.
A miss is a scandal with a name attached. A false alarm is an inconvenience spread across everybody and charged to nobody.

## Direction

Toward over-warning by people who are behaving rationally.
The warner is exposed asymmetrically: the harm they failed to flag will be traced back to them in detail, and the harm they flagged unnecessarily will be forgotten by everybody except the people who lost a day.
So the incentive runs one way, the thresholds drift down, and every individual decision that moved them is defensible on its own.

## Lever

The reasonableness of protecting against the worse error against what protecting produces.
The carrying force is that the asymmetry in the incentives mostly matches a real asymmetry in the harms -- a missed diagnosis is worse than an unnecessary scan, and a warner who weights them equally is making a mistake; the resistance is that the incentive is stronger than the harm asymmetry warrants and is felt personally rather than actuarially, so the system warns more than the trade-off alone would justify, and the cost of that lands on the receivers as fatigue rather than on the warner as a finding.
The Liability is carried by one party and paid for by another.

## Echo

A system that leans toward the alarm, for reasons nobody would defend out loud.
It shows why over-warning is structural rather than a failure of nerve.
The Liability is dated from the last inquiry and is renewed by each one.
