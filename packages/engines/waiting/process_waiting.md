# Process: Waiting

## Taxonomy

Time (no khai file of its own yet).

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: waiting

## Initiated by

A persona's time has been placed at somebody else's disposal.
Waiting starts wherever one party's schedule is made to accommodate another's: the appointment, the queue, the application, the reply that has not come, the room with chairs in it.
It is not the feeling of anticipation, which is suspense's, and not the sense of duration, which is time-perception's. It is a relation -- who may make whom wait -- and a transfer, since the time given up by the one waiting is time saved by the one waited upon.

## Direction

Toward a settled account of whose time is worth more, arrived at without anybody saying so.
The engine reads four movements: [the summons](process_summons.md), how the wait is set up and whether it has an edge; [the hold](process_hold_waiting.md), what makes the interval feel as long as it does; [the order](process_order_waiting.md), who goes before whom and what happens when that is broken; and [the schooling](process_schooling.md), what a long habit of waiting makes of the persona who does it.
Nothing is announced. The arrangement states itself by operating.

## Lever

The necessity of some waiting against what waiting silently declares.
The carrying force is that queues are not an insult and mostly are a fairness: a scarce service handled first-come-first-served is about the best allocation anybody has invented, waiting rooms exist because demand is lumpy and staff are finite, and a persona who has never had to wait for anything has been served by other people's waiting; the resistance is that every arrangement to wait also ranks -- the party who is waited upon has had their time protected and the party waiting has had theirs spent -- and the ranking is enacted rather than argued, so there is nothing in it to disagree with.
Waiting is read on who is available to whom, never on how long it took.

## Echo

A hierarchy demonstrated by a room with chairs in it.
Waiting does not close; it is time being transferred, gathered into one reading -- a summons, a hold, an order, a schooling.
The engine's twist is here. The instrument is not duration, it is **uncertainty**: a persona told to come back at four can spend the intervening hours as their own, while a persona told they will be called cannot leave, cannot commit to anything, and must hold the whole interval available -- so an unbounded wait consumes the time it did not use. And it is unanswerable in a way a refusal is not, because nothing has been refused. There is no decision to appeal, nobody has said no, and the persona complaining is complaining that something has not yet happened.
It hands off what it is not: the anticipatory hold on a possible bad outcome is suspense's; the felt passage of duration is time-perception's; the unengaged mind seeking a target is boredom's; the opportune moment is kairos's; the threshold between identities is liminality's; rank itself is status's and hierarchy's; and a wait made to **stand for** a theme is authored key, the play's pitch, not the force.
The persona is dated from when they were told to be available, which is earlier than when the waiting started.
