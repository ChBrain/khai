# Process: The Queue Norm

## Taxonomy

[The Order](process_order_waiting.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: waiting

## Initiated by

A line has formed and nobody organised it.
The Queue Norm starts on its own: personas arriving at a scarce thing sort themselves first-come-first-served, without instruction, without an official, and with near-total compliance.

## Direction

Toward an allocation everybody accepts and nobody administers.
The norm is remarkably robust. It works among strangers, holds when unobserved, is understood by children, and requires no shared anything except the arrival order.
It is one of the few genuinely universal solutions to a scarce good, and it is enforced by the participants at their own cost.

## Lever

The elegance of the norm against what it takes as given.
The carrying force is that first-come-first-served is transparent, cheap, adjudicator-free, and treats every persona's claim as equal -- for allocating a scarce thing among people with no other relationship, nobody has improved on it; the resistance is that equal claims on the queue are not equal claims on the day: arriving first requires being able to arrive first, so a fair sequence over an unfair distribution of free time produces an outcome that is procedurally impeccable and substantively skewed.
The Queue Norm is fair from the door inward.

## Echo

A just allocation of what happens after everybody arrived.
It is the arrangement the other two members of the movement press against.
The Queue Norm is dated from the first arrival, and it re-forms instantly wherever it is broken.
