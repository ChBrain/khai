# Process: The Summons

## Taxonomy

[Waiting](process_waiting.md), how the wait is set, and whether it has an edge.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: waiting

## Initiated by

Somebody has told the persona to be available.
The Summons starts before any waiting is done: at the terms -- [an appointed hour](process_appointed_hour.md), [an open call](process_open_call.md), or [standing by](process_standing_by.md) -- which decide almost everything about what the wait will cost.

## Direction

Toward an interval the persona either owns or does not.
The Summons reads one distinction and treats it as the important one: whether the wait has an announced edge.
A bounded wait leaves the persona in possession of the time before it. An unbounded one takes the time before it as well, since availability cannot be part-held.

## Lever

The convenience of not committing against what not committing transfers.
The carrying force is that vagueness is frequently honest and rarely malicious: an institution genuinely does not know when it will get to somebody, a promised time it cannot keep is worse than no time, and the open call is often the truthful answer; the resistance is that the honesty is one-sided -- the institution's uncertainty is resolved by making the persona absorb it, and absorbing somebody else's uncertainty is exactly what it costs to hold yourself available.
The Summons is where the wait's real price is set, before a minute of it has passed.

## Echo

An interval already spent, before it has begun.
It is the movement that decides whether the other three are ordinary inconvenience or something heavier.
What it hands upward is a persona who is either free until four o'clock or not free at all.
The Summons is dated from the letter, the call, or the sentence "we'll be in touch".
