# Process: The Open Call

## Taxonomy

[The Summons](process_summons.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: waiting

## Initiated by

The persona has been told they will be contacted.
No time, no window, no position -- an assurance that something will happen and no information about when.

## Direction

Toward a persona who cannot make any other plan.
The open call transfers the institution's uncertainty onto the persona whole. Not knowing when means not being able to be elsewhere, not being able to commit, and checking -- so the interval is consumed at full cost regardless of how much of it the institution actually needed.
The persona is not waiting for an hour. They are holding every hour until it comes.

## Lever

The honesty of not promising against what not promising costs the other party.
The carrying force is that the vagueness is frequently truthful: the institution genuinely does not know, a time it cannot keep would be worse, and personas asked to choose usually prefer an honest uncertainty to a broken commitment -- the open call is often the considerate answer and is meant as one; the resistance is that uncertainty does not vanish when it is not stated, it relocates, and the party it relocates onto is the one with less capacity to absorb it and no way to price it back.
The Open Call is honest about the institution's position and silent about the transfer.

## Echo

An interval spent in full and used in part.
It is the form the engine's twist runs on, and it is almost never chosen for that reason.
The Open Call is dated from the assurance, and it has no end until it ends.
