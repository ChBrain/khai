# Waiting

Waiting as process: a persona's time placed at somebody else's disposal -- not the anticipatory hold on an outcome, which is suspense's, and not the felt passage of duration, which is time-perception's, but a relation, since the time given up by the one waiting is time saved by the one waited upon -- across four movements: the summons (an appointed hour, an open call, or standing by, which decides almost everything about what the wait will cost), the hold (empty time, an unexplained delay, and the unfair wait, which stretch the interval far more than its length does), the order (the queue norm that forms without an official, legitimate precedence that is invisible from the chairs, and intrusion, objected to only by those directly displaced), and the schooling (remaining available, nothing refused, and the learned patience the repetition produces) -- with the twist that the instrument is uncertainty rather than duration, since an unbounded wait consumes the time it did not use and cannot be appealed, because nothing has been refused.
