# Process: Empty Time

## Taxonomy

[The Hold](process_hold_waiting.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: waiting

## Initiated by

There is nothing to do.
Empty Time starts wherever the wait supplies no occupation: a room, chairs, a wall, and an interval with no content of its own.

## Direction

Toward an interval that stretches.
Unoccupied waiting is reliably experienced as longer than occupied waiting of the same length, and the difference is substantial -- which is why anything at all placed in the interval shortens it: a form to complete, a screen, a walk to a further gate, a queue that moves.
Attention with nothing to attend to attends to the waiting.

## Lever

The kindness of occupying the interval against what occupation conceals.
The carrying force is that filling the time genuinely helps, costs almost nothing, and is not manipulation when it is done honestly -- a persona who spent the wait doing something has actually had a better hour; the resistance is that the same instrument works when nothing has improved, so an institution can hold its service constant, add an occupation, and receive credit for a shorter wait that did not shorten.
Empty Time is where the cheapest remedy in the engine is also the most usable one.

## Echo

A stretched interval, or a filled one, at identical length.
It is the plainest of the three conditions and the one every institution addresses first.
Empty Time is dated from the moment there is nothing left to look at.
