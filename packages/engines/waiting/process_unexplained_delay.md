# Process: The Unexplained Delay

## Taxonomy

[The Hold](process_hold_waiting.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: waiting

## Initiated by

Nobody has said why.
The Unexplained Delay starts wherever the wait exceeds what the persona expected and no account is given -- the clinic running late with no announcement, the application with no status, the room where nobody comes out.

## Direction

Toward a persona filling the silence themselves.
Without an explanation the persona supplies one, and the supplied explanations are worse than the real one almost every time: they have been forgotten, the file is lost, something has gone wrong, nobody is coming.
An unexplained wait is not only longer, it is a different kind of wait -- one in which the persona is also managing a fear.

## Lever

The cost of explaining against the cost of not.
The carrying force is that explanation is nearly free and works remarkably well: a sentence about why and roughly how much longer converts an ordeal into an inconvenience, and institutions that do it are rated far better on waits of identical length; the resistance is that explaining commits somebody -- to a reason that may be embarrassing and an estimate that may be wrong -- and the silence is not usually a decision to withhold, it is the absence of anybody whose job it is to say.
The Unexplained Delay is mostly nobody's doing, which is why it persists.

## Echo

A wait carrying an invented reason.
It is the condition that turns an interval into an anxiety, and the one most cheaply removed.
The Unexplained Delay is dated from the moment the expected time passed with nothing said.
