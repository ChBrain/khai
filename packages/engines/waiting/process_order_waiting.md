# Process: The Order

## Taxonomy

[Waiting](process_waiting.md), who goes before whom.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: waiting

## Initiated by

There is a sequence, and everybody in it knows what it is.
The Order starts wherever more than one persona is waiting for the same thing: [the queue norm](process_queue_norm.md) forms almost immediately, [precedence](process_precedence.md) may legitimately override it, and [intrusion](process_queue_intrusion.md) is what happens when neither is respected.

## Direction

Toward a sequence enforced by the people standing in it.
The queue is one of the most reliably self-policing arrangements there is: no official is needed, the norm is understood without instruction, and the person who objects to a jumper is almost always the one directly behind them rather than anybody further back.
That detail is the movement's whole finding -- the defence is of a position, not of a principle.

## Lever

The fairness of first-come-first-served against how little of the room it actually mobilises.
The carrying force is that the norm works and is worth defending: it is transparent, needs no adjudicator, gives everybody an equal claim on a scarce thing, and personas honour it overwhelmingly even when nobody is watching; the resistance is that its defence is local, so an intrusion is answered by the one or two people it directly displaces and by nobody else -- which means the norm holds against the casual jumper and fails completely against anybody who is admitted at the front by the institution itself.
The Order polices the queue and cannot see the door.

## Echo

A sequence honoured by everyone in it, and irrelevant to whoever is let in another way.
It shows the arrangement's fairness and the limit of the fairness in the same act.
What it hands upward is the material for the sharpest grievance in the engine, since being passed is felt far more keenly than being delayed.
The Order is dated from whoever arrived first, and it is renegotiated at every interruption.
