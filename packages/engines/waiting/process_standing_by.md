# Process: Standing By

## Taxonomy

[The Summons](process_summons.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: waiting

## Initiated by

The persona is required to be reachable.
Standing by is availability held as a standing condition rather than for a particular event: on call, on shift-with-no-shift, carrying the phone, ready if needed.

## Direction

Toward time that is neither working nor free.
The persona is not doing anything and is not at liberty either: they cannot drink, cannot go far, cannot start what would be hard to stop.
The hours pass and are not theirs, and nothing has occurred that anybody would count.

## Lever

The small cost of being ready against the fact that readiness is the whole of it.
The carrying force is that on-call arrangements are how genuinely irregular demand gets met at all, and the alternative -- staffing continuously for a peak that rarely arrives -- is worse for everybody including the persona; the resistance is that availability is the service being provided, and it is the one part of the arrangement that is generally treated as costing nothing: a night on call in which nothing happened is recorded as a night in which nothing happened.
Standing By supplies the thing nobody counts.

## Echo

A life held at readiness, with nothing to show for the holding.
It shows the summons in its purest form, since there is not even an event to be waiting for.
Standing By is dated from the start of the rota, and it accumulates in a way the persona feels long before they can name it.
