# Process: The Hold

## Taxonomy

[Waiting](process_waiting.md), what makes the interval feel as long as it does.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: waiting

## Initiated by

The persona is in it, and the clock is behaving strangely.
The Hold starts wherever the wait is being experienced rather than arranged, and it runs on the well-documented finding that felt duration tracks the conditions of the wait far more than its length.

## Direction

Toward a wait that is longer or shorter than it is.
Three conditions do most of the work: [empty time](process_empty_time.md), which stretches; [an unexplained delay](process_unexplained_delay.md), which stretches further; and [an unfair wait](process_unfair_wait.md), where somebody has gone ahead, which stretches furthest of all and is the only one that produces anger rather than fatigue.

## Lever

The cheapness of managing the experience against what managing it leaves untouched.
The carrying force is that the management is genuine and mostly kind: occupying the time, explaining the delay, and giving a position in the queue all reduce real suffering at almost no cost, and an institution that does them is treating people better than one that does not; the resistance is that all three improve the wait without shortening it -- so the most effective thing an institution can do about waiting is to make it feel shorter, and the incentive that creates is not toward serving people faster.
The Hold is where comfort and remedy come apart.

## Echo

An interval got through, at a length that was never the real one.
It is the movement most visible to the persona and least connected to the arrangement producing it.
What it hands upward is a persona's account of how bad it was, which will be about the explanation and the queue-jumper rather than about the hours.
The Hold is dated from the moment they sat down.
