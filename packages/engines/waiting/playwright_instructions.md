# Instructions: Waiting

How a Playwright wires the waiting engine into a play. You wire by linking the play's own content to the engine's files; the engine files stay untouched, they are canon. The Roadie plumbs the engine's law into Instructions Knowledge on deploy. Authoring guidance, not runtime content, and it does not go on tour.

## Human

- The human sets the summons first, because an appointed hour and an open call are different plays: one leaves the persona in possession of their day and the other does not. Then decide whether this is a single wait or a repeated one, since the schooling needs repetition and nothing else in the engine does. Remember nobody in the arrangement has to be unkind for any of it to work.

## Agent

- From the play's own content, a persona links under Projection the terms of their wait -- above all which form of summons they are under, since that sets the cost before any of the interval has passed. Read the wait as a relation rather than a feeling: the question is whose schedule accommodates whose, and the time one party gives up is time the other saves. Keep it the arrangement: the hold on a possible bad outcome is suspense's, the felt passage of duration is time-perception's, the unengaged mind is boredom's. You link to the engine, you never edit it.

## Collaboration

- The anticipatory apprehension of a possible bad outcome is the suspense engine's; the felt passage of duration is time-perception's; the unengaged mind seeking a target is boredom's; the opportune moment is kairos's; the threshold between identities is liminality's; rank itself is status's and hierarchy's; the official's discretion at the counter is the law engine's gap axis and the employment engine's; what being governed makes of a persona over a lifetime is the subjection composite's and discipline's. Whether a production's precedence was legitimate, and whether a persona's equanimity is learned patience or ordinary composure, are the team's review.

## Knowledge

- Waiting is a persona's time placed at somebody else's disposal, read as a relation rather than a feeling: the time given up by the one waiting is time saved by the one waited upon, and every arrangement to wait also ranks -- enacted rather than argued, so there is nothing in it to disagree with. It wires on a persona at Projection and runs in four movements. The summons sets the terms before any waiting is done, and the distinction that matters is whether the wait has an announced edge: an appointed hour returns the interval before it and makes somebody accountable for the edge; an open call takes that interval too, because availability cannot be part-held, so the persona is not waiting for an hour but holding every hour until it comes; standing by is availability as a standing condition, neither working nor free, which is the service being provided and the part nobody counts. The hold is what makes the interval feel as long as it does: empty time stretches, an unexplained delay stretches further and has the persona supply a reason worse than the real one, and an unfair wait stretches furthest and is the only condition producing anger rather than fatigue, because the complaint has reorganised from wanting to be seen sooner to wanting to be seen in the right order. All three remedies improve the wait without shortening it. The order is who goes before whom: the queue norm forms without instruction and holds among strangers, precedence is usually correct and almost never explained so a sound rule and a favour look identical from the chairs, and intrusion is objected to by the one or two directly displaced and by nobody further back -- so the norm holds against the rude individual and is silent about anybody admitted at the front by the institution itself. The schooling is what repetition makes: remaining available, which is the cost that is not duration and appears in no accounting; nothing refused, which is why there is no decision to appeal and complaining sounds like impatience; and learned patience, a competence that genuinely serves the persona and is indistinguishable from a virtue. The engine's twist is at the root: the instrument is uncertainty rather than duration, since an unbounded wait consumes the time it did not use, and an outcome deferred indefinitely is functionally an outcome denied, delivered in a form nobody has to defend.

## System

- Do set the summons before anything else, and let it do the work: a persona told to come back at four has an afternoon, and a persona told they will be called has nothing until the call comes.
- Do put the anger on sequence rather than on duration. Being passed is felt far more keenly than being delayed, and a persona will take a longer wait in turn over a shorter one out of it.
- Do let precedence be genuinely correct and genuinely unreadable: the rule is sound, nobody explains it at the moment it operates, and the persona in the chair has to choose between a fair reason and a favour with no information.
- Do keep every official reasonable and every delay genuine. The schooling works without a villain, and a play that supplies a hostile clerk has replaced the mechanism with a scene.
- Do let learned patience be dignified. The competence is real and serves the persona; what a play should show is what it cost to acquire, not that it is a defeat.
- Do stage the cost that is not duration -- the work not taken, the trip not made, the course not begun -- since the ordinary accounting of a wait contains none of it.
- Do not model the hold on an outcome (suspense), the felt passage of duration (time-perception), the unengaged mind (boredom), the opportune moment (kairos), the identity threshold (liminality), or rank itself (status, hierarchy). Do not make the delay stand for a theme; that is authored key, the play's pitch. Do not edit the engine's files; wire only from the play's side.
