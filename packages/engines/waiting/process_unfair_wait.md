# Process: The Unfair Wait

## Taxonomy

[The Hold](process_hold_waiting.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: waiting

## Initiated by

Somebody who came later has gone first.
The Unfair Wait starts at the observation -- a name called out of turn, a door opened for another, a case handled ahead -- and it is the only condition in the movement that produces anger rather than fatigue.

## Direction

Toward a grievance about sequence rather than about time.
The persona's complaint reorganises instantly: what they now want is not to be seen sooner but to be seen in the right order, and they will accept a longer wait taken in turn over a shorter one taken out of it.
The reaction is about standing rather than about the clock, which is why it does not respond to being told the delay was small.

## Lever

The legitimacy of exceptions against how an exception reads from the chair.
The carrying force is that most out-of-turn service is entirely proper -- the emergency, the two-minute question, the appointment that was booked while others walked in -- and an institution unable to make exceptions would serve everybody worse; the resistance is that the reason is almost never given, so a legitimate precedence and a favour are indistinguishable from the waiting room, and the persona is left with the only reading available, which is that somebody counted for more.
The Unfair Wait is caused by the exception and made by the silence about it.

## Echo

A wait that has become an insult.
It shows the movement's sharpest edge and the engine's shortest path to a scene.
The Unfair Wait is dated from the name that was called, and it is remembered long after the interval is forgotten.
