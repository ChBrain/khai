# Process: The Appointed Hour

## Taxonomy

[The Summons](process_summons.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: waiting

## Initiated by

A time has been given.
The persona is to be somewhere at four, and both parties have named the same number.

## Direction

Toward an interval the persona still owns.
The appointment does something the other summons cannot: it returns the time before it. The persona can work, travel, commit, arrange -- because the claim on them has an edge and everything outside the edge is theirs.
It also creates an obligation that runs both ways, which is why a broken appointment is felt as a small breach rather than as ordinary delay.

## Lever

The freedom a bounded claim returns against the promise it commits somebody to.
The carrying force is that this is the decent form and is not difficult: naming a time costs the institution only the discipline of keeping it, and it converts an open claim on a life into a bounded one; the resistance is that the institution must now be wrong in public when it runs late, which an open call never is -- so the honest arrangement is the one that generates complaints, and the vague one generates none.
The Appointed Hour is the form that can fail, which is precisely why it is worth more.

## Echo

A claim with an edge, and somebody accountable for the edge.
It is the engine's baseline, and the thing the other two forms are measured against.
The Appointed Hour is dated from the number both parties agreed, and it is the only form in which the persona can be kept waiting rather than simply waiting.
