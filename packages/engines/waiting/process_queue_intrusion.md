# Process: The Intrusion

## Taxonomy

[The Order](process_order_waiting.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: waiting

## Initiated by

Somebody has stepped into the line.
The Intrusion starts at the breach itself, and what follows is the movement's most useful finding: objection comes overwhelmingly from the one or two people directly behind the point of entry, and almost never from further back.

## Direction

Toward a defence that is local and therefore limited.
Those displaced object; those not displaced watch. The norm is defended by the persona whose position was taken rather than by the room whose rule was broken.
And objection is costly -- it requires a confrontation with a stranger in public, which most personas will pay a great deal to avoid.

## Lever

The strength of the norm against the narrowness of who will enforce it.
The carrying force is that the local defence is generally sufficient: casual jumping is rare because the person behind is right there and will say something, and that alone keeps most queues honest at almost no cost to anybody; the resistance is that a defence that only mobilises the directly displaced is no defence at all against entry that does not displace anybody visibly -- the side door, the call-ahead, the arrangement made at the desk -- so the norm holds against the rude individual and is silent about the institution.
The Intrusion shows a rule with strong enforcement pointed at the smallest violation of it.

## Echo

A queue that polices its own line and not its own door.
It is the movement's conclusion and the reason the order axis says more about legitimacy than about fairness.
The Intrusion is dated from the step, and it is over in seconds either way.
