# Process: The Schooling

## Taxonomy

[Waiting](process_waiting.md), what a habit of waiting makes, and where the engine is seen for one thing.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: waiting

## Initiated by

The persona has done this many times and expects to do it again.
The Schooling starts wherever waiting stops being an event and becomes a condition: the benefits office, the clinic, the permit queue, the tribunal -- places a persona returns to, where the answer is always that they will be called.
It is the face of the engine that is not about any particular wait.

## Direction

Toward a persona shaped by having to remain available.
The Schooling runs through three: [remaining available](process_remaining_available.md), which is the cost that is not duration; [nothing refused](process_no_refusal.md), which is why there is nothing to appeal; and [learned patience](process_learned_patience.md), the disposition the repetition produces, which the institution will read as cooperation and the persona will experience as having no other move.

## Lever

The reasonableness of every individual delay against what the sequence of them teaches.
The carrying force is that no step in this is malicious and most of it is not even deliberate: the office really is overloaded, the delay really is unavoidable, the clerk really does not know, and any single instance is entirely defensible -- there is no villain to find and a play that supplies one has misread it; the resistance is that a persona who has learned that outcomes arrive when they arrive, that pressing does not help, and that being available is the only reliable contribution they can make has been taught a political lesson by an administrative process, and taught it more thoroughly than any argument could.
The Schooling rides the engine's keystone knife-edge: the cheapest form of control is not making somebody wait long but declining to say how long.

## Echo

A persona who has become good at waiting.
It shows the three earlier movements complete -- an unbounded summons, a hold nobody explains, an order that cannot see the door -- and adds the only thing they were missing, which is repetition.
This is why the Schooling is the hardest of the four to stage and the only one worth closing on: every official must be reasonable, every delay must be genuine, nobody must be cruel, and the audience must be able to see the persona learning something anyway.
A play that supplies a hostile clerk has replaced the mechanism with a scene.
The Schooling is dated from the first visit and is renewed every time they are told to take a seat.
