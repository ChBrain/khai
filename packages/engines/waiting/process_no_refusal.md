# Process: Nothing Refused

## Taxonomy

[The Schooling](process_schooling.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: waiting

## Initiated by

Nobody has said no.
Nothing Refused starts at the absence of a decision: the persona has not been turned down, their claim has not been rejected, no adverse finding has been made -- the matter is simply not yet resolved.

## Direction

Toward a grievance with nothing to attach to.
Every remedy the persona might use requires a decision to point at. An appeal needs a ruling, a complaint needs an act, a case needs a defendant.
A delay supplies none of these, so a persona who is being comprehensively failed has nothing they can name as the failure -- and saying so out loud sounds like impatience.

## Lever

The genuine absence of any decision against the fact that the absence is doing the work.
The carrying force is that this is usually exactly what it appears to be: no decision has been made because no decision has been made, the file is in a queue, and nobody is withholding anything -- attributing intent here is generally wrong; the resistance is that intent is not required for the effect, since an outcome deferred indefinitely is functionally an outcome denied, delivered in a form that cannot be contested and that nobody has to defend.
Nothing Refused is a refusal's effect without a refusal's accountability.

## Echo

A denial that nobody made.
It shows why the schooling is so effective: there is no moment to resist, and resistance would be directed at nothing.
Nothing Refused is dated from every day it continues, and each day is individually unremarkable.
