# Process: Learned Patience

## Taxonomy

[The Schooling](process_schooling.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: waiting

## Initiated by

The persona has stopped expecting it to be otherwise.
Learned Patience starts after enough repetitions: they arrive early, bring something to do, do not ask, do not press, and are pleasant to whoever is at the desk.

## Direction

Toward a disposition that reads as cooperation and was arrived at as arithmetic.
The persona has worked out what actually helps -- being there, being agreeable, being ready -- and what does not, which is everything else. So they do the first and stop doing the second.
From the institution's side this is a good applicant. From the persona's side it is the residue of having tried the alternatives.

## Lever

The genuine competence of the practised waiter against what the competence is made of.
The carrying force is that the skill is real and serves them: a persona who knows how the office works, when to come, and how to be with the staff gets better outcomes than one who arrives angry, and the equanimity is not a defeat -- much of it is ordinary maturity about things one cannot change; the resistance is that they have also learned something they were not meant to be taught, which is that pressing does not work and that their own urgency is not a factor -- and a persona who has learned that about one institution has learned a disposition, not a fact.
Learned Patience is the engine's product, and it is indistinguishable from a virtue.

## Echo

Somebody who has become good at this.
It shows the schooling complete, and it is the point at which the arrangement no longer needs to do anything.
A play should let the patience be genuinely dignified, because it is -- and should let the audience see what it cost to acquire.
Learned Patience is dated from the visit on which they stopped asking.
