# Process: Remaining Available

## Taxonomy

[The Schooling](process_schooling.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: waiting

## Initiated by

The persona has to be there when it happens.
Remaining Available starts wherever the requirement is presence rather than action: be reachable, be in the area, be able to come in, do not be halfway through something.

## Direction

Toward a life organised around a call that may not come.
The persona shapes everything else around the possibility: work not taken, trips not made, courses not started, a second commitment refused because the first might land.
None of this appears anywhere. The wait is recorded as the time between the summons and the service, and the cost is everything that could not be begun.

## Lever

The reasonableness of asking somebody to be there against what being there forecloses.
The carrying force is that the request is not unreasonable and the alternative is often worse: an institution that cannot rely on people turning up wastes the slot, and the persona who is unavailable loses their place, so availability genuinely serves both; the resistance is that availability is a full-time claim charged as a part-time one, and it falls hardest on personas whose other commitments are least flexible -- the one with a job that does not forgive absences, the one who cannot afford the second trip.
Remaining Available prices a whole interval at the rate of its used portion.

## Echo

A calendar cleared for something that took twenty minutes.
It is the cost the engine exists to make visible, because nothing in the ordinary accounting of a wait contains it.
Remaining Available is dated from the summons and billed at the end, to somebody who was never told the rate.
