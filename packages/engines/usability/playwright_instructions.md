# Instructions: Usability

How a Playwright wires the usability engine into a play. You wire by linking the
play's own content to the engine's files; the engine files stay untouched, they
are canon. The Roadie plumbs the engine's law into Instructions Knowledge on
deploy. Authoring guidance, not runtime content, and it does not go on tour.

## Human

- The human sets which pieces in the world carry a design that teaches or defeats their use.

## Agent

- From the play's own content, a piece that carries designed use links under Apparent the facet it expresses: what its form affords (grip), what cue says where to act (signifier), what confirms the act took (feedback), or what makes the wrong act hard (constraint). Show the design in the form, read before the manual. Keep it cognitive, and hand the object's social program to agency. You link to the engine, you never edit it.

## Collaboration

- The affordance of the room is space's; the object's history is biography's and patina's; the object as a social delegate enforcing a program is agency's; the persona's skill with the thing is embodiment's and mastery's. Whether the design rings true for the piece is the team's review.

## Knowledge

- Usability is the design of an object acting on its user: how the form communicates and governs its use. Four facets over a conceptual model -- the action the form affords, the cue that signals where to act, the response that confirms the act took, and the shape that forbids the wrong act. A well-designed piece looks like what it does; a badly-designed one looks like something else, and the gap between the two readings is where the user fails. The form is the first instruction, and the object teaches its own use or does not.

## System

- Do give a designed piece its facet under Apparent; do show the design in the form, read before the manual; do keep the reading cognitive (how the form communicates operation).
- Do not model what conduct the object enforces on whom; that is agency, not usability. Do not confuse the object's design with the room's affordance (space's) or with the user's skill (persona-side). Do not edit the engine's files; wire only from the play's side.
