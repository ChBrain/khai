# Usability: Reference

## Line of Work

Usability as **piece**: how a piece's designed form communicates and governs its
use -- what it affords, signals, confirms, and forbids -- the design of everyday
things read on the object. The domain does not model the affordance of the setting,
which the space engine owns; nor the object's history, which biography and patina
own; nor the object's social agency, which the agency engine owns. It models the
design of the object read on the object itself: the form as the first instruction,
before any manual.

The spine is Norman's account of everyday design: an object communicates its own
operation through the action its form affords, the cue that signals where to act,
the response that confirms the act took, and the shape that forbids the wrong act,
all over a conceptual model the form conveys. Gibson's affordance is the root
perception the apparatus is built on; the engine takes Norman's four facets and
gives each its own reading, so a piece can declare not only that it is designed for
use but by which part of that design it teaches its use.

## Restrictions

What the engine refuses to model, and to whom it delegates.

- **The affordance of the setting (space)**: The space engine owns what a _place_
  affords -- the room that scripts conduct, the church that hushes. This engine owns
  what the _object_ affords -- the thing that teaches its own use. The `affordance`
  stem is space's (`place_affordance.md`), and this engine names the object-affordance
  `grip` precisely to hold the line: one reads a `place`, one reads a `piece`, and the
  bearer is the whole distinction.
- **The object's history (biography, patina)**: What the object has _carried_ and
  accreted over time is the history family's. Usability is what the object _invites_ by
  its present form, not what it bears from its past. The one seam is convention -- a
  learned signifier, a cultural constraint -- which is historical; usability keeps the
  present cue and hands the accretion to the history family.
- **The object's social agency (agency, Latour)**: The object as a social delegate
  enforcing a program on whom -- the speed bump that polices the driver, the cap that
  keeps children out -- is the agency engine's. Usability is how the form communicates
  operation to a user's perception. A constraint's _legibility_ is usability; its
  _social purpose_ is agency. The line is held at Verbeek (design mediation) and handed
  at Latour (the politics of artifacts).
- **A persona's skill with the object**: The competence to use a demanding thing well
  -- the violinist's skill, the pilot's training -- is persona-side (`embodiment`,
  `mastery`). Usability is in the object; the skill is in the person. The design is
  playable; the playing is the persona's, which is why this engine reads a `piece` and
  not a persona.
- **The facet is not engine content**: The engine declares the four facets exist;
  which one a given piece expresses lives on the piece, linked under its Apparent
  chapter. The engine refuses to fix a piece's design for it.

## Encoding

Source to constraint, per file.

- **[usability](piece_usability.md)** (the root): the conceptual model, the whole
  design communicating operation over Norman's Gulfs of Execution and Evaluation.
  Anchored by Norman (the model) and Krippendorff (the object communicating meaning).
- **[grip](piece_grip.md)** (affordance): the action the form makes possible.
  Anchored by Gibson (affordance) and Norman with Gaver (perceived and complex
  affordances).
- **[signifier](piece_signifier.md)**: the perceivable cue that says where and how to
  act. Anchored by Norman (the signifier) and Krippendorff (product semantics).
- **[feedback](piece_feedback.md)**: the response that confirms the action registered.
  Anchored by Wiener (cybernetic feedback, upstream) and Norman with Nielsen (the Gulf
  of Evaluation, visibility of status).
- **[constraint](piece_constraint.md)**: the form that makes the wrong use hard or
  impossible. Anchored by Norman (the constraint taxonomy) and Verbeek (design
  mediation), bounded against the agency engine.
