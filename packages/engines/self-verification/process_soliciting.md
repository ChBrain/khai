# Process: Soliciting

## Taxonomy

[Self-Verification](process_self_verification.md), seeking self-confirming feedback and building a self-confirming world.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: self-verification

## Initiated by

The persona seeks out the confirmation it wants.
This is the soliciting route (Swann): the persona actively looks for feedback that verifies its self-view, and builds around itself a world that will supply it -- choosing partners, friends, roles, and settings that see it as it sees itself, and asking, where it can, for the appraisals that confirm.
It opens whenever the persona has a say over whom it deals with and what it hears about itself, and steers those choices toward the self-confirming.

## Direction

Toward a self-verifying environment, chosen and solicited.
Soliciting moves the persona to select its opportunity structures -- the relationships and contexts that will reflect its self-view back to it -- and to draw out confirming feedback within them: gravitating to those who see it accurately by its own lights, and away from those who do not, over time assembling a world that agrees with it.
Its direction is the construction of confirmation -- the persona arranging whom it hears from so that what it hears fits.

## Lever

What soliciting spends -- the persona's freedom to choose its company and its feedback.
Where the persona can select, it trades the wider field of possible relationships and verdicts for the narrower one that confirms: the negatively-self-viewing persona forgoing partners who would think well of it in favor of those who share its low estimate, because those are the ones with whom it feels known.
A production holds this lever by letting the persona pick its people and its rooms by fit-to-self -- staying where it is seen accurately, leaving where it is not -- so the world it ends up in reads as one it solicited, not merely one it landed in.

## Echo

The persona comes to live in a world that confirms it -- its relationships and settings selected to verify the self-view it holds.
Once built, that world does much of the verifying on its own, feeding back the appraisals the persona selected it for, so the self-concept is held steady by the company it keeps.
What the world will not supply on its own the persona [signals](process_signaling.md) for, and what slips through against the self-view it meets with [resistance](process_resistance.md). Soliciting is the choosing and seeking that stacks the world toward confirmation.
