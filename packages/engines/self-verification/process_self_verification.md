# Process: Self-Verification

## Taxonomy

Self / the drive to confirm one's existing self-concept.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: self-verification

## Initiated by

A persona seeks to be seen as it already sees itself.
This is self-verification (Swann): the drive to confirm the existing self-concept, to seek out and hold onto information that matches how the persona takes itself to be, so that the self stays stable, coherent, and predictable.
It runs on the content of the self-view, not its favorability -- a persona verifies who it thinks it is, which is comfortable when the self-view is good and costly when it is not, but the pull is toward the fit, not the flattery.

## Direction

Toward the match between the world's verdict and the self-view.
Self-verification points the persona's dealings at its own self-concept and works to bring the two into line: to elicit, notice, and keep the appraisals that confirm what it already believes about itself, and to fend off the ones that do not.
Its direction is confirmation -- the self-view held steady by a world made, read, and pruned to agree with it.

## Lever

What self-verification spends -- the persona's need for a stable, knowable self over a merely flattering one.
Because a coherent self-concept is what lets a persona predict itself and be predicted by others, it will pay in comfort to keep it intact: a persona with a negative self-view prefers the partner who confirms the low view to the one who overrates it, holds onto the sting that fits over the balm that does not, because the fit secures the self and the balm unsettles it.
A production holds this lever by letting the persona defend its self-concept as itself -- welcoming what confirms it, resisting what would revise it -- so verification reads as the keeping of a self, sharpest where it costs the persona a kinder verdict it could have had.

## Echo

The persona's self-view is confirmed -- and the self holds steady, whether or not the confirmation was kind.
This is what parts self-verification from self-enhancement: where enhancement seeks the best possible verdict, verification seeks the true-to-self one, and when a self-view is negative the two pull opposite ways, verification keeping the low self intact against the flattering correction.
It runs as [soliciting](process_soliciting.md) the confirmation, [signaling](process_signaling.md) for it, and [resistance](process_resistance.md) to what disconfirms. The level of self-worth is the self-esteem engine's; the content of the self-story is identity's; the opposing pull toward flattering feedback is the self-serving member of the bias engine's; the passive cognitive slant toward self-confirming information is the bias engine's; the general drive to reconcile inconsistent cognitions is the dissonance engine's. This engine owns the motive to be known as one knows oneself.
