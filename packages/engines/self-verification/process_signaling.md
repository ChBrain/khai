# Process: Signaling

## Taxonomy

[Self-Verification](process_self_verification.md), eliciting self-confirming reactions from others.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: self-verification

## Initiated by

The persona works to draw the confirming reaction out of others.
This is the signaling route (Swann's identity negotiation): the persona displays identity cues, presents itself, and behaves so as to pull from those around it the responses that match its self-view -- dressing the part, announcing the role, acting in ways that invite others to treat it as it takes itself to be.
It opens whenever the persona cannot simply select confirming company and must instead make its present company confirm it.

## Direction

Toward a reaction shaped to fit the self-view.
Signaling moves outward from the self-concept into display and conduct that cue others to respond in kind: the cues that advertise who the persona is, the self-presentation that sets the terms, the behavior that provokes the confirming answer -- so that even a fresh audience is steered toward seeing the persona as it sees itself.
Its direction is the eliciting of confirmation -- the persona managing its appearance and action to make others verify it.

## Lever

What signaling spends -- the persona's self-presentation, put to the service of the self-view.
The persona invests effort in cueing and performing its identity so that others' reactions come back confirming, and it spends the ease of being taken however others might have taken it -- shaping the impression toward the self-view rather than leaving it free, negotiating until the other treats it as it means to be treated.
A production holds this lever by letting the persona work its identity cues and conduct to pull the confirming response -- and, tellingly, do so even to secure a self-view that is unflattering, insisting on being met as the person it believes itself to be.

## Echo

Others react as the persona has cued them to -- and their confirming response verifies the self-view from outside.
The reaction the persona has drawn out becomes evidence for the self it holds, the identity negotiated into place for this audience as it was selected into place across [soliciting's](process_soliciting.md) world.
Where the elicited reaction still comes back disconfirming, the persona meets it with [resistance](process_resistance.md). Signaling is the active drawing-out of the confirmation that soliciting could not simply select.
