# Instructions: Self-Verification

How a Playwright wires the self-verification engine into a play. You wire by linking the play's own content to the engine's files; the engine files stay untouched, they are canon. The Roadie plumbs the engine's law into Instructions Knowledge on deploy. Authoring guidance, not runtime content, and it does not go on tour.

## Human

- The human sets the self-verification terrain: what the persona firmly believes about itself -- favorable or not -- and what feedback its world offers, so a moment can run on the persona working to be seen as it sees itself.

## Agent

- From the play's own content, a persona links the self-verification a moment runs under its Projection: soliciting, seeking confirming feedback and choosing partners and settings that verify it; signaling, eliciting confirming reactions through identity cues and self-presentation; or resistance, pushing away feedback that does not fit, even when it is favorable. Let the drive show sharpest where verification and flattery part -- the persona with a low self-view holding onto the confirming sting over the disconfirming balm. You link to the engine, you never edit it.

## Collaboration

- The level of self-worth is the self-esteem engine's; the content of the self-story is the identity engine's; the opposing pull toward flattering feedback is the self-serving member of the bias engine's; the passive cognitive slant toward self-confirming information is the bias engine's; the general drive to reconcile inconsistent cognitions is the dissonance engine's. This engine supplies the active drive to confirm the self-concept -- soliciting, signaling, and resistance.

## Knowledge

- Self-verification is a persona's drive to confirm its existing self-concept -- to seek, elicit, and hold onto feedback that matches how it already sees itself, so the self stays stable and predictable (Swann). Its signature is that it runs on the content of the self-view, not its favorability: a persona with a negative self-view verifies it too, preferring feedback that confirms the low view to feedback that flatters but disconfirms it, so verification and enhancement pull apart. It runs in three routes. Soliciting is seeking confirming feedback and building a confirming world -- choosing partners, roles, and settings that see the persona as it sees itself. Signaling is eliciting confirming reactions -- identity cues and self-presentation that pull matching responses from others. Resistance is rejecting self-discrepant feedback, even favorable -- discounting, avoiding, or leaving what does not fit. Read a persona by which self-view it confirms and how.

## System

- Do keep self-verification the drive for confirmation, not for flattery: it seeks the true-to-self verdict, not the best one -- so let it align with enhancement when the self-view is positive and part from it when the self-view is negative, verification then keeping the low self intact against the kinder correction.
- Do stage the three routes: the persona choosing confirming company and soliciting confirming feedback, working its identity cues and self-presentation to draw the confirming reaction, and discounting or withdrawing from what disconfirms -- and let the resistance bite even against a favorable verdict.
- Do make the signature legible: the negatively-self-viewing persona distrusting praise, preferring the partner who shares its low estimate, sometimes leaving the one who overrates it -- because a self it cannot recognize unsettles more than a low self it can.
- Do keep it the drive and its enactment: delegate the level of self-worth to the self-esteem engine, the self-story's content to identity, the opposing flattery-motive and the passive cognitive slant to the bias engine, and the general consistency drive to dissonance. Do not edit the engine's files; wire only from the play's side.
