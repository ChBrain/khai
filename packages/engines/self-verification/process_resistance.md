# Process: Resistance

## Taxonomy

[Self-Verification](process_self_verification.md), rejecting self-discrepant feedback, even when favorable.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: self-verification

## Initiated by

The persona meets feedback that does not fit its self-view, and pushes it away.
This is the resistance route (Swann): the persona discounts, avoids, disbelieves, or withdraws from appraisals that clash with how it sees itself -- and, in the signature case, does so even when the appraisal is favorable, resisting a verdict that is too good to fit a negative self-view as it would resist an unjust one.
It opens whenever a self-discrepant judgment reaches the persona that soliciting and signaling did not head off.

## Direction

Away from the disconfirming, back toward the self-view.
Resistance turns the persona against the ill-fitting appraisal: doubting its accuracy, attributing it to the other's error or motive, avoiding the source, or leaving the relationship that keeps delivering it -- so that the self-concept is protected from the revision the feedback would force.
Its direction is the fending-off of confirmation's opposite -- the mismatch discounted so the self-view need not move.

## Lever

What resistance spends -- a verdict the persona could have kept, when that verdict does not fit.
The persona pays by refusing feedback it might have used or enjoyed: the negatively-self-viewing persona turning from praise, distrusting the partner who thinks too well of it, sometimes leaving the relationship that overrates it, because a self it cannot recognize is more unsettling than a low self it can.
A production holds this lever by letting the persona resist the ill-fitting even at a cost -- discounting the kind word that does not land, pulling away from the too-flattering mirror -- so the resistance reads as the defense of a known self over a better-looking stranger.

## Echo

The disconfirming feedback is turned aside -- and the self-view survives the challenge intact.
What soliciting and signaling could not prevent, resistance repairs after the fact, discounting or escaping the mismatch so the self-concept holds; it is the last guard of the verifying drive, and the clearest place its logic shows, since here the persona pays in a kinder verdict to keep a truer-to-self one.
The general drive to reconcile inconsistent cognitions is the dissonance engine's, and the pull toward the flattering verdict that resistance here refuses is the self-serving member of the bias engine's; this route owns the specifically self-verifying rejection -- the disconfirming appraisal pushed away to keep the self-view that [soliciting](process_soliciting.md) sought and [signaling](process_signaling.md) drew out.
