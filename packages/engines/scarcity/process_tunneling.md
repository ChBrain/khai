# Process: Tunneling

## Taxonomy

[Scarcity](process_scarcity.md), the attention form.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: scarcity

## Initiated by

The pressing lack seizes the persona's attention, drawing them so hard onto the shortfall in front of them that the field of view narrows to a tunnel around it.
This is tunneling: the shortage capturing the mind so completely that the persona attends only to the urgent lack and lets fall away everything the tunnel does not contain.
Inside the tunnel the persona is not distracted but the opposite -- singularly, forcibly focused, the lack itself doing the focusing.

## Direction

Toward a hard focus on the pressing shortfall bought at the cost of neglecting all outside it.
Tunneling has two faces that come together: a focus dividend, the persona working the urgent lack with a concentration the unpressed never bring to it; and tunnel neglect, the important-but-not-urgent -- the bill not yet due, the maintenance, the long plan -- dropped from view because it lies outside the tunnel's walls.
The direction is a trade the persona does not choose: the more the lack captures, the sharper the work on it and the deeper the neglect of what the capture leaves out.

## Lever

The capture of attention by the pressing lack, spent as focus within and neglect without.
Tunneling runs on attention as a beam the shortfall commands: what is urgent and short pulls the beam onto itself, and a beam is a thing that lights one place by darkening the rest, so the very intensity on the pressing lack is the mechanism of the neglect of everything else.
The lever is the involuntary narrowing: the persona does not decide to ignore the important, they are focused off it -- the tunnel's inside lit exactly because its outside is dark.

## Echo

The urgent lack is worked with everything, and the important is left in the dark.
It is why the person short on time makes the meeting but misses the checkup, why the person short on money pays today's crisis with tomorrow's rent, why the crash-dieter thinks of food and the lonely of the next contact -- the tunnel's focus real and useful and blinding at once.
It is [bandwidth](process_bandwidth.md) and [trap](process_trap.md)'s partner, working on the aim of attention where they work on its capacity and its compounding. The mental faculty of directing attention is the attention engine's; tunneling owns the shortfall's seizure of that beam -- the focus dividend inside and the neglect outside that the capture buys together.
