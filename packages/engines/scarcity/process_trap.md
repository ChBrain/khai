# Process: Trap

## Taxonomy

[Scarcity](process_scarcity.md), the compounding form.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: scarcity

## Initiated by

The persona, pressed by the present lack, borrows against the future to meet it -- and so deepens the future's lack, which presses in its turn.
This is the scarcity trap: the neglect and the tax not merely coexisting but compounding, each round of coping with today's shortfall making tomorrow's larger, so the persona is caught in a spiral that keeps the scarce scarce.
The persona is not improvident; they are trapped, each move a reasonable answer to the present that mortgages the future the present already threatens.

## Direction

Toward a self-perpetuating shortfall, the coping deepening the condition it copes with.
The trap turns on borrowing in the widest sense: the payday loan taken against next month, the urgent task done at the cost of the important that becomes urgent later, the sleep or the health or the relationship spent now and owed back with interest.
The direction is a spiral, not a slope: because tunneling neglects the important and bandwidth taxes the planning that might break out, each turn arrives with less slack and less mind to escape, and the lack renews itself faster than the persona can close it.

## Lever

The borrowing against the future to meet the present lack, spent as a deepening of the future's.
The trap runs on the compounding of scarcity's own two motions: the tunnel's neglect leaves the important to fester into the next crisis, and the bandwidth tax spends the capacity that might have planned around it, so the persona meets each shortfall with a move that buys the present by selling the future.
The lever is the absence of slack: with a buffer, a shock is absorbed; without one, every shock must be borrowed against the next period, and the borrowing is the mechanism by which the scarce stay scarce.

## Echo

Each answer to the present lack writes the next one larger, and the spiral holds.
It is why the poor pay more and stay poor, why the perpetually late fall further behind, why "just this once" against the future becomes the standing condition -- the trap not a failing of the trapped but the arithmetic of coping without slack.
It is [tunneling](process_tunneling.md) and [bandwidth](process_bandwidth.md)'s partner, the two of them compounding into it -- the neglect and the tax becoming the spiral. The resource borrowed and owed is the money and capital engines'; the strain of the bind is the stress engine's; trap owns the self-perpetuation itself, the way scarcity's coping deepens scarcity's lack.
