# Instructions: Scarcity

How a Playwright wires the scarcity engine into a play. You wire by linking the play's own content to the engine's files; the engine files stay untouched, they are canon. The Roadie plumbs the engine's law into Instructions Knowledge on deploy. Authoring guidance, not runtime content, and it does not go on tour.

## Human

- The human defines the scarcity terrain: what resource the persona feels short of -- money, time, food, company -- how pressing the lack is, and what important things lie outside the pressing shortfall -- so the felt lack has a mind to capture and a capacity to tax.

## Agent

- From the play's own content, a persona links the form the moment runs (tunneling, bandwidth, or trap) under its Projection. Let it show the felt lack reorganizing the persona -- the tunnel narrowing onto the shortfall while the important is neglected, the bandwidth spent on the nagging lack so the persona thinks the worse for it, the spiral of borrowing against a future the present already threatens -- and let it read as the condition working on the mind, not as a failing of the person. You link to the engine, you never edit it.

## Collaboration

- The resource itself that is short is the money and capital engines'; the mental faculties the lack taxes -- attention, planning, impulse control -- are the attention and executive-function engines'; the strain of demand exceeding resources is the stress engine's; the need that motivates pursuit is the goal engine's. This engine supplies the capture and taxing of the mind by the felt lack itself.

## Knowledge

- Scarcity is the psychology of having less than one feels one needs: the felt lack of a resource capturing the mind and taxing it. Tunneling is the attention route -- the pressing lack seizes the beam of attention, so the persona works the shortfall with a focus dividend, singularly sharp, while the important-but-not-urgent outside the tunnel is neglected, the same narrowing that lights the lack darkening all else. Bandwidth is the capacity route -- the nagging of the lack loads the mind, occupying working memory and self-control, so that fluid intelligence and executive function measurably fall while one is short, the errors the load and not the person, the capacity returning when the lack lifts. Trap is the compounding route -- the neglect and the tax spiral, the persona borrowing against the future to meet the present lack and so deepening the future's, each reasonable answer to today mortgaging a tomorrow the shortfall already threatens, the scarce kept scarce for want of slack.

## System

- Do make the felt lack the force, not a flaw in the persona: the mind is captured and taxed by the shortfall -- so let a persona who would judge well with enough judge worse while short, and let the capacity return when the lack lifts.
- Do route the capture to the right mechanism: tunneling works on the aim of attention, bandwidth on its capacity, trap on the compounding over time -- so name which the lack is doing, the tunnel or the tax or the spiral, rather than a vague pressure.
- Do let tunneling cut both ways: the focus on the pressing lack is real and useful, and the neglect of the important outside the tunnel is its price -- let the persona be sharp on the shortfall and blind to what it excludes, together.
- Do delegate the resource itself to the money and capital engines, the taxed faculties to the executive-function and attention engines, and the demand-exceeds-resources strain to the stress engine. Do not edit the engine's files; wire only from the play's side.
