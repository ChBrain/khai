# Process: Bandwidth

## Taxonomy

[Scarcity](process_scarcity.md), the capacity form.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: scarcity

## Initiated by

The nagging of the lack loads the mind, so that the persona has less mental capacity left for everything else than they would with enough.
This is the bandwidth tax: scarcity consuming cognitive resources -- the shortfall running in the background, pulling at attention, filling the mind with the arithmetic of not-enough -- and what it consumes is not returned to the task at hand.
The persona is not less able; they are, while short, running on a mind a part of which the lack has already claimed.

## Direction

Toward a measurable fall in the capacity to think and to hold, for as long as the lack presses.
The bandwidth tax works on two systems: fluid intelligence, the raw ability to reason and solve in the moment; and executive function, the capacity to plan, resist impulse, and control the self -- both lower while the mind is loaded by the lack, the same person scoring worse short than they do with enough, the drop on the order of a night's lost sleep.
The direction is a load, not a trait: it lifts when the lack lifts, the capacity returning when the shortfall stops nagging -- which is why the tax is the condition's and not the person's.

## Lever

The consumption of cognitive capacity by the nagging lack, spent as reduced bandwidth for all else.
Bandwidth runs on the mind as a finite resource the shortfall taxes: preoccupation with the lack is not free, it occupies working memory and attention, and what it occupies cannot also do the reasoning and the self-control the rest of life requires.
The lever is the background load: the lack need not be consciously dwelt on to tax -- it hums beneath the task, and the humming is the cost, the persona thinking and choosing worse because a part of the mind is already spent.

## Echo

The mind is taxed by the lack it must think its way out of, and thinks the worse for it.
It is why poverty impedes cognitive function and busyness impairs judgment, why the same farmer reasons better after harvest than before, why the tired-seeming errors of the hard-pressed are the load and not the laziness -- the shortfall taxing the very faculty that might have relieved it.
It is [tunneling](process_tunneling.md) and [trap](process_trap.md)'s partner, working on the mind's capacity where they work on its aim and its compounding. The faculties that are taxed -- planning, impulse control, the holding of goals -- are the executive-function engine's; the demand-exceeds-resources strain is the stress engine's; bandwidth owns the shortfall's consumption of that capacity, the tax the lack levies on the mind.
