# Process: Scarcity

## Taxonomy

Behavioral science / the cognitive psychology of having less than one feels one needs.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: scarcity

## Initiated by

A persona has less of something -- money, time, food, company -- than they feel they need, and the felt lack begins to reorganize the mind around itself.
This is scarcity: not the shortfall in the world alone but the psychology it produces, the sense of not-enough capturing attention and pressing on the capacity to think.
The persona is not simply poor or rushed; they are, by the lack, made to attend and to spend their mind differently than they would with enough.

## Direction

Toward a mind narrowed onto the lack and taxed by it, so that the very shortage that demands good judgment erodes the capacity for it.
Scarcity works in two motions at once: it captures -- pulling attention hard onto the pressing shortfall, sharpening the persona on it -- and it loads, the nagging of the lack consuming the mental capacity left for all else.
The direction is a paradox that deepens: the more one lacks, the more the lack fills the mind, and the less mind remains to escape the lack -- capture and tax compounding as the resource falls.

## Lever

The subjective sense of not-enough, spent as the capture and taxing of the mind.
What scarcity spends is the felt lack itself: a shortfall the persona registers as pressing seizes attention without permission, and the seized attention is attention withdrawn from everything the lack does not touch, and the withdrawal is a real cost paid in cognitive capacity and self-control.
The lever is the gap between have and need as the mind reads it: not the objective amount but the felt insufficiency, which is why the same hour or the same dollar weighs differently to one who has few of them than to one who has many.

## Echo

The lack governs the mind that must escape it, and the shortage feeds itself.
Scarcity is why the last hours before a deadline are the sharpest and the rest of the project the most neglected, why the poor and the busy and the lonely can act against their own longer interest not from failing but from a mind a shortfall has captured and taxed -- the condition, not the person, doing the work.
It runs as [tunneling](process_tunneling.md), [bandwidth](process_bandwidth.md), or [trap](process_trap.md) -- the mind narrowed onto the lack, the capacity spent on it, the shortage compounding into a spiral. The resource that is short is the money and capital engines'; the mental capacity the lack taxes is the executive-function and attention engines'; the strain of demand exceeding resources is the stress engine's. This engine owns the capture and taxing of the mind by the felt lack itself.
