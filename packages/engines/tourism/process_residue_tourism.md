# Process: The Residue

## Taxonomy

[Tourism](process_tourism.md), what is left once everybody has gone home.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: tourism

## Initiated by

The visit ends, and something stays on each side.
The Residue starts after departure: [a souvenir](process_the_souvenir.md) carried away, [an account home](process_the_account_home.md) that will replace the visit, and [a place after](process_the_place_after.md) that has to live with what it became.

## Direction

Toward two records of the same fortnight, neither of which is the fortnight.
The visitor goes home with objects and a story, and the story hardens quickly into the version that will be told for years. The place keeps the arrangements, the prices, the season, and the reputation, all of which outlast any individual visitor by decades.
The two residues do not communicate, and only one of them can be revised by the person holding it.

## Lever

The lightness of what the visitor keeps against the weight of what the place keeps.
The carrying force is that this is an exchange rather than an extraction and it usually leaves both sides better off: the visitor is genuinely enlarged, the money is genuinely spent locally, and treating an ordinary holiday as a wound to a community is both wrong and patronising to the community; the resistance is that the two sides keep entirely different things -- one keeps a fortnight, the other keeps a structure -- so the transaction is symmetrical in the moment and asymmetrical in every year after it.
The Residue is a story on one side and a settlement on the other.

## Echo

A trip remembered and a place committed.
It is the movement nobody is present for.
The Residue is dated from the departure and runs on afterwards without anybody watching.
