# Process: The Account Home

## Taxonomy

[The Residue](process_residue_tourism.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: tourism

## Initiated by

The visitor tells people where they went.
A handful of episodes, in an order, with a best one, told enough times to take a shape.

## Direction

Toward a version that replaces the fortnight it describes.
Two weeks does not fit in a conversation, so the trip is compressed into four or five tellable moments, and the compression is permanent: what is not in the account stops being available, and the persona ends up remembering their own telling.
The moments that survive are the ones that told well -- the mishap, the coincidence, the unexpected kindness -- which are not the same as the ones that mattered.

## Lever

The necessity of an account against what accounting costs the trip.
The carrying force is that telling is part of the good of going and should not be treated as a loss: the story is a gift to whoever hears it, the persona is not exaggerating, and a trip nobody could describe would be a strange thing to have taken; the resistance is that the telling selects on tellability rather than on significance, so the quiet afternoon that was the actual reason it was worth going has no place in any version and is gone within a year.
The Account Home is true in every detail and is not the trip.

## Echo

Five episodes standing in for a fortnight.
It shows why people who travel together disagree about what happened.
The Account Home is dated from the first telling and is fixed by the third.
