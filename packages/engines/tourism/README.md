# Tourism

Tourism as process: the arrangement between a place and the people who come to look at it -- not the conversion of a stranger into a guest, which is hospitality's, and not living from one's own values rather than a false front, which is authenticity's -- across four movements: the marking (a marker that creates the significance it reports, a prior image the visit confirms rather than encounters, and an itinerary that protects the trip and rules out the best of it), the seeking (a real sought that cannot be handed over without ceasing to be it, a tourist disowned by every tourist, and an off path found by the people who close it), the staging (a staged back that is genuine and is not the back, a host performing their own life in the shape that keeps it alive, and an authenticity sold, which is the least dishonest step and the one that reads worst), and the residue (a souvenir that stores nothing and reminds well, an account home true in every detail and not the trip, and a place after organised around people who are elsewhere) -- with the twist that the demand for the unstaged is what builds the stage, since any back region a visitor can reach has been made reachable.

This engine is defined by its [manifest](package.json), which the canon renders as the WIRES card. The manifest is the single source of truth; this README is generated, not edited by hand.
