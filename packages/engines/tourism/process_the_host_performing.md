# Process: The Host Performing

## Taxonomy

[The Staging](process_staging_tourism.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: tourism

## Initiated by

Somebody does their own life while being watched doing it.
Mending the net, laying the fire, making the bread, speaking the language they speak.

## Direction

Toward an ordinary act that has acquired an audience and therefore a shape.
The host is not pretending. They are doing the thing they would be doing, and they are also doing it a little more slowly, a little more legibly, at the hour when people are around to see.
Over time the adjusted version becomes the version, including for them, because it is the one performed most often.

## Lever

The honesty of the act against what an audience does to it.
The carrying force is that the host is doing nothing false and has every right to the income: the craft is theirs, the knowledge is theirs, showing it is a reasonable way to make a living from a thing that might otherwise not survive, and visitors who value it are supporting something worth supporting; the resistance is that a practice repeated for watchers drifts toward what watchers respond to, so what is preserved is the version that shows well, and the parts that do not show well are the parts that go.
The Host Performing keeps the practice alive in the shape that keeps it alive.

## Echo

A tradition maintained by the demand that changed it.
It shows the cost falling on the people the visitors most admire.
The Host Performing is dated from the first paying watcher and is renewed daily.
