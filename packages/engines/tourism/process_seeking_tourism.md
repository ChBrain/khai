# Process: The Seeking

## Taxonomy

[Tourism](process_tourism.md), what the visitor is actually after.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: tourism

## Initiated by

The visitor wants something the sight cannot supply.
The Seeking starts at a want that is never for the marked thing: [the real sought](process_the_real_sought.md) behind the display, [the tourist disowned](process_the_tourist_disowned.md) because nobody is one, and [the off path](process_the_off_path.md), which is where the real is presumed to be.

## Direction

Toward the life of the place rather than its exhibits.
What the visitor came for is not the cathedral. It is to see how people live here -- what they eat, how they talk to each other, what a Tuesday is -- and the cathedral is the excuse that got them onto the plane.
This is why the best moment of a trip is almost never on the list, and why visitors describe it as luck.

## Lever

The decency of the want against the fact that it is a want.
The carrying force is that the wanting is honourable and the world is better for it: somebody who travels in order to see how others live is doing something worth doing, the interest is genuine, and a visitor who cared only about monuments would be the poorer sort of traveller; the resistance is that a want at scale is a market, so a sincere demand for unstaged life is an order placed with the people whose life it is, and it will be filled.
The Seeking is a good impulse arriving as a specification.

## Echo

A visitor looking past the thing they paid to see.
It is the movement that explains why improving the attraction never satisfies anybody.
The Seeking is dated from arrival and intensifies with every marked thing ticked off.
