# Process: The Prior Image

## Taxonomy

[The Marking](process_marking_tourism.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: tourism

## Initiated by

The visitor has seen it before they see it.
On a poster, in a film, on a friend's wall, in the search results they looked at while booking.

## Direction

Toward an arrival that is a comparison rather than an encounter.
The prior image sets the angle, the light, the season, and the absence of other people, and the actual place is then assessed against it -- usually unfavourably, because the picture had no queue in it.
The visitor's task on arrival becomes matching: standing where the photograph was taken, waiting for the light, and taking the same picture.

## Lever

The pleasure of recognition against what recognition displaces.
The carrying force is that anticipation is a real part of the good of travelling, the image is what made the persona want to come, and knowing what a place looks like before arriving is a benefit rather than a loss; the resistance is that the encounter has been pre-scripted, so the visitor spends the visit confirming an image instead of looking at a place, and the confirmation is what they will report.
The Prior Image is the thing the visit is compared to and the thing it produces again.

## Echo

A view already familiar to somebody who has never been.
It shows why the same three photographs come back from every visit to a place.
The Prior Image is dated from before departure and is reproduced on arrival.
