# Process: The Souvenir

## Taxonomy

[The Residue](process_residue_tourism.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: tourism

## Initiated by

Something is carried home that only means anything because of where it came from.
A shell, a bowl, a bottle, a ticket stub, a thing that is ordinary there and is not ordinary here.

## Direction

Toward an object whose entire value is a claim about the owner.
A souvenir does not represent the place; it represents having been to it. This is why it works best when it is slightly useless and locally unremarkable, and why the same object bought at home would be worthless.
It also fades on a schedule: the meaning is carried by the owner's memory, so the object outlasts the thing it stands for and becomes, in a drawer, a small unexplained item.

## Lever

The sincerity of the keeping against how little the object carries.
The carrying force is that the impulse is affectionate and harmless: people want a physical trace of somewhere they were happy, the bowl is a good bowl, and a life with no such objects in it would be a colder one; the resistance is that the object holds nothing on its own -- everything it means is in the owner and travels only as far as the owner's account of it -- so the trace requires exactly the memory it was supposed to secure.
The Souvenir stores nothing and reminds well.

## Echo

An ordinary thing with an origin attached.
It shows the visit trying to leave itself a physical receipt.
The Souvenir is dated from the purchase and is unreadable to whoever clears the drawer.
