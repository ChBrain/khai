# Process: Tourism

## Taxonomy

Visiting (no khai file of its own yet).

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: tourism

## Initiated by

Somebody travels to see a place they do not live in, and the place knows they are coming.
Tourism starts wherever a place is visited on purpose and at scale: the viewpoint with a rail, the old quarter, the market that has a name in the guidebook, the village that features on a poster, the museum's one famous room.
It is not the conversion of a stranger into a guest, which is hospitality's, and not the psychological alignment of a self with its expression, which is authenticity's. It is the arrangement between a place and the people who come to look at it.

## Direction

Toward a place that has begun to be what it is seen as.
The engine reads four movements: [the marking](process_marking_tourism.md), how somewhere becomes a sight; [the seeking](process_seeking_tourism.md), what the visitor is actually after, which is never the sight; [the staging](process_staging_tourism.md), the twist -- what the place does in answer; and [the residue](process_residue_tourism.md), what is left on both sides once everybody has gone home.
The four are one operation, and it runs continuously in both directions.

## Lever

The sincerity of the visitor against what the sincerity commissions.
The carrying force is that tourism is mostly good and mostly meant well: people travel because they want to see how others live, the money reaches places that need it, the curiosity is genuine, the hosts are frequently glad of the visitors, and an account that treats every traveller as a vandal has mistaken the phenomenon; the resistance is that the wanting is a demand, and a demand for something unstaged is still an order that somebody has to fill, so the place answers it -- with nobody lying and nobody being fooled.
Tourism is read on what the place had to become, never on what the visitor took away.

## Echo

A place that is now partly an account of itself.
Tourism does not close; it is a place being visited and answering, gathered into one reading -- a marking, a seeking, a staging, a residue.
The engine's twist is here. **The demand for the unstaged is what builds the stage.** The visitor does not want the show; they want the real life behind it, and they say so, and they mean it. The place hears that, sincerely and often gratefully, and produces a back region for them to find -- a working kitchen they may enter, a family that will have them to supper, a route away from the crowds. Nothing about it is a deception: the family really does live there, the fisherman really does fish. But any back region a visitor can reach has been made reachable, so the search cannot terminate, and the harder it is pressed the more thoroughly the place is converted into a performance of itself.
It hands off what it is not: the conversion of a stranger into a guest is hospitality's; the front and back regions of a performance are backstage's; living from one's own values rather than a false front is authenticity's; what a place holds and what it does to whoever is in it are place-attachment's and third-place's; the displacement of residents by incomers is gentrification's; the passing of a place's past into its present is heritage's and palimpsest's; and a journey made to **stand for** a theme is authored key, the play's pitch.
The persona is dated from the last place they went to see.
