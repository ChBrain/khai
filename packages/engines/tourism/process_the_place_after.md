# Process: The Place After

## Taxonomy

[The Residue](process_residue_tourism.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: tourism

## Initiated by

The season ends, and the place is what the visiting made it.
Shops stocked for people who are not there, a square that empties, an economy with a shape, prices set by outsiders' wallets.

## Direction

Toward somewhere that now depends on being visited.
A place that receives visitors reorganises around them, and the reorganisation is not reversible on any short timescale: the bakery that became a cafe does not become a bakery again in November, and the young people who left for the season have a season-shaped year now.
The dependence is genuine and cuts both ways, which is why places that resent visitors also fear their absence.

## Lever

The value of what the visiting brought against the shape it left.
The carrying force is that the money is real and frequently the difference between a living place and an emptying one: incomes rose, the young stayed, buildings were repaired that nobody could otherwise have repaired, and residents are not confused about the trade they made; the resistance is that the settlement is structural rather than seasonal -- what is built for visitors stays built, what stopped being provided for residents stays gone -- and the people who bear it are not the people who negotiated it.
The Place After keeps the arrangement long after the reason for it has moved on.

## Echo

Somewhere organised around people who are elsewhere.
It shows the engine's cost falling where the engine's benefits also fell, on the same households, in different years.
The Place After is dated from the first good season and is not dated from any single decision.
