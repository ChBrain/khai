# Process: The Real Sought

## Taxonomy

[The Seeking](process_seeking_tourism.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: tourism

## Initiated by

The visitor asks where the locals go.
Not the restaurant in the guide -- the one round the corner with no menu in translation.

## Direction

Toward a request for access to something that stops being itself once granted.
The visitor wants the unperformed life of the place, which by definition is the part not arranged for visitors. Being admitted to it is therefore a contradiction: an unperformed room with a stranger in it who came to see it is a room that now has an audience.
This is not the visitor's fault and it is not a trick played on them. It is a property of what they are asking for.

## Lever

The genuineness of the wish against the impossibility of granting it.
The carrying force is that the wish is the right one and its opposite is worse: a visitor content with the arranged version learns nothing, the desire to meet the actual place is exactly what makes travel worth doing, and hosts frequently want to show it as much as visitors want to see it; the resistance is that the presence of somebody who came to look changes what is being looked at, so what can be shown is always a version, and the sincerity on both sides makes the version more convincing rather than less.
The Real Sought cannot be handed over without ceasing to be it.

## Echo

An access granted to something that has adjusted.
It shows the engine's central impossibility stated as a request.
The Real Sought is dated at the moment of asking and is answered generously.
