# Process: The Off Path

## Taxonomy

[The Seeking](process_seeking_tourism.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: tourism

## Initiated by

The visitor goes where the guidebook does not.
The next village along, the street behind the square, the bar with nobody in it, the island the ferry stops at twice a week.

## Direction

Toward a frontier that moves ahead of whoever reaches it.
Somewhere unvisited becomes visited by being reached, and the account of it goes home, into a blog, into the next edition -- so the off path is a position rather than a location, and it relocates every year at roughly the speed of the people looking for it.
The visitor who finds it genuinely does find something, and is genuinely the reason it will not be there next time.

## Lever

The reality of what is found against what finding it does.
The carrying force is that the discovery is real and is the best of travelling: the place is as unarranged as it looked, the visitor's experience of it is not an illusion, and there is nothing wrong with wanting it or with telling people afterwards; the resistance is that the telling is the mechanism, so a hundred people each honestly reporting a quiet place produce a queue, and each of them was the first.
The Off Path is found by the people who close it.

## Echo

A place with two years left.
It shows the seeking operating as a front that advances.
The Off Path is dated from the first account of it and is exhausted from there.
