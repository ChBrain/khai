# Process: The Marking

## Taxonomy

[Tourism](process_tourism.md), how somewhere becomes a sight.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: tourism

## Initiated by

A place is designated worth looking at, before anybody arrives to look.
The Marking starts off site: [a marker](process_the_marker.md) that makes the sight rather than describing it, [a prior image](process_the_prior_image.md) the visitor has already seen, and [an itinerary](process_the_itinerary.md) that turns the visit into a list.

## Direction

Toward a view whose value is entirely in the sign attached to it.
Nothing is intrinsically worth seeing. A hillside becomes a viewpoint when somebody puts a name and a rail on it, and an unmarked hillside a mile away, identical in every respect, is not a sight and nobody stops.
So the visitor is not discovering anything. They are visiting a designation, and the designation is what they came for, though they will describe the view.

## Lever

The usefulness of the marker against what it does to the looking.
The carrying force is that markers are a service and the alternative is worse: a stranger cannot know what is here, the plaque genuinely tells them, the guidebook saves a day of wandering, and a place with nothing marked is not more authentic, it is just illegible; the resistance is that the mark is doing the seeing -- the visitor's attention goes where it has been sent, the unmarked is passed without being registered, and what gets preserved and restored is whatever has a marker on it.
The Marking makes the sight and is mistaken for a description of it.

## Echo

A place with certain parts of it made visible.
It is the movement that happens before anybody sets out, and the one nobody counts as part of the trip.
The Marking is dated from the first guidebook and compounds with every edition.
