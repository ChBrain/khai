# Process: The Tourist Disowned

## Taxonomy

[The Seeking](process_seeking_tourism.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: tourism

## Initiated by

The visitor explains that they are not a tourist.
They are a traveller, they are staying a while, they are here for a wedding, they know somebody.

## Direction

Toward the one attitude every tourist shares.
Contempt for tourists is the tourist's own signature position, held by everybody in the queue about everybody else in it. Nobody in the history of the practice has described themselves as one without irony.
The disowning does real work: it licenses the visitor to press further, since somebody who is not a tourist is entitled to the parts tourists do not get.

## Lever

The truth of the distinction against the universality of drawing it.
The carrying force is that there are genuine differences and the visitor is usually pointing at a real one: staying a month is not the same as staying a day, learning the language matters, and a persona who takes the trouble to arrive differently is in fact treated differently; the resistance is that everybody makes the claim and it cannot be true of everybody, so the distinction functions as a licence rather than as a description, and the pressure it authorises falls on the same small number of households.
The Tourist Disowned is the position from which the deepest access is demanded.

## Echo

A visitor asking for what visitors are not shown.
It shows why the least tourist-like visitors put the heaviest load on a place.
The Tourist Disowned is dated at the first conversation and is maintained throughout.
