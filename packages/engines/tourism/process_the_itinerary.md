# Process: The Itinerary

## Taxonomy

[The Marking](process_marking_tourism.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: tourism

## Initiated by

The marked things are assembled into a list, and the list has to be got through.
Six days, eleven sights, a route between them, and a sense of what would be a waste.

## Direction

Toward a visit governed by completion rather than by interest.
The itinerary converts a place into a set of items, and once it exists the visitor is accountable to it: time spent somewhere unlisted is time that will cost them something later, and the pleasure of an afternoon doing nothing has to be defended.
So the trip is run against a schedule assembled by strangers, and the visitor experiences the schedule as their own plan.

## Lever

The efficiency of the list against what the list forbids.
The carrying force is that a plan is sensible and the visitor is right to make one: the time is short, the money was real, missing the thing everybody comes for would be a genuine loss, and an unplanned trip mostly means seeing less rather than seeing more deeply; the resistance is that the list has no entry for the part that would have been remembered -- the wrong turning, the long lunch, the conversation -- and there is no way to plan for those, so they are what gets cut when the schedule slips.
The Itinerary protects the trip and rules out the best of it.

## Echo

A visit with a completion rate.
It shows the marking's authority reaching all the way into the visitor's hour-by-hour choices.
The Itinerary is dated from booking and is audited on the way home.
