# Process: The Authenticity Sold

## Taxonomy

[The Staging](process_staging_tourism.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: tourism

## Initiated by

The unarranged is priced.
The authentic experience, the local guide, the home-cooked meal, the not-for-tourists tour, all bookable in advance.

## Direction

Toward the moment everybody can see what has happened.
This is the point at which the arrangement becomes legible, because it has a price on it, and the visitor's reaction is embarrassment -- not at the seller, but at their own position in a transaction they had understood as an encounter.
The price is not the corruption. It is the disclosure.

## Lever

The fairness of charging against what charging reveals.
The carrying force is that the money is owed and everybody knows it: the host's time is worth something, unpaid hospitality at scale is extraction rather than warmth, and a visitor who wants the real thing for free is asking a poorer person to subsidise their trip; the resistance is that a price makes the arrangement visible as an arrangement, so the same access that felt like being let in feels, once it is listed, like being sold something -- and the only thing that changed was that somebody was honest about the terms.
The Authenticity Sold is the least dishonest step and the one that reads worst.

## Echo

A transaction that both parties can now see.
It shows the whole movement in a single line item.
The Authenticity Sold is dated from the first fee and is what everybody objects to.
