# Process: The Staged Back

## Taxonomy

[The Staging](process_staging_tourism.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: tourism

## Initiated by

A room behind the room is opened.
The kitchen visitors may enter, the workshop with the door ajar, the yard, the family table, the part that is not on the ticket.

## Direction

Toward an interior arranged for people who came to see an interior.
The room is real, the work in it is real, and it has also been tidied, lit, and made passable -- because people come through it, and a room people come through cannot be a room nobody comes through.
So the boundary the visitor believes they have crossed has been moved to accommodate them, and the crossing is the experience they came for.

## Lever

The reality of the room against the fact that it was opened.
The carrying force is that nothing has been fabricated and the visitor is not being cheated: this is where the work happens, the people in it are the people who do it, and showing somebody your workshop is an ordinary courtesy rather than a performance; the resistance is that being shown is the whole difference, so the room the visitor sees is a room that receives visitors, and the one they were looking for is wherever the current boundary now sits.
The Staged Back is genuine and is not the back.

## Echo

An interior that is exactly what it appears to be and is not what was wanted.
It shows the front-and-back distinction bending rather than breaking, which is why nobody in it detects anything wrong.
The Staged Back is dated from the day the door was first left open.
