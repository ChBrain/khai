# Process: The Marker

## Taxonomy

[The Marking](process_marking_tourism.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: tourism

## Initiated by

Something is put beside the thing, saying that the thing matters.
A plaque, a listing, a star in a guide, a viewing platform, a rope, a queue.

## Direction

Toward an object that has acquired significance from outside itself.
The marker and the sight arrive as a pair and the pair is what is consumed: visitors read the plaque and then look at the stone, and if the plaque were moved to a different stone they would look at that one with the same attention.
This is not gullibility. It is the only way a stranger can tell what they are looking at, and the alternative is looking at everything equally, which is looking at nothing.

## Lever

The necessity of the marker against its power to create what it labels.
The carrying force is that marking is honest work and usually accurate: somebody who knows the place has decided what a newcomer should not miss, the information is real, and the visitor is better off with it than without; the resistance is that a designation is self-fulfilling, so the marked thing becomes the important thing -- funding follows it, restoration follows it, crowds follow it -- and after a generation the marker's claim is true because it was made.
The Marker creates the significance it reports.

## Echo

A thing everybody stops at.
It shows the sight being manufactured at the cheapest possible point.
The Marker is dated from its installation and outlives the reason for it.
