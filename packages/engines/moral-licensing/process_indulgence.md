# Process: Indulgence

## Taxonomy

[Moral Licensing](process_moral_licensing.md), the licensing direction -- banked moral credit spent on a lapse.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: moral-licensing

## Initiated by

The persona spends the moral credit its recent good has banked.
This is the licensing direction (Monin & Miller; Nisan's moral balance): a prior good deed, or a run of them, leaves the persona feeling it has a surplus of goodness, and on the strength of that surplus it permits itself a later act it would otherwise hold back from.
It opens whenever the persona has lately established its virtue -- given, helped, declared the right value, resisted once -- and then meets a temptation it now feels entitled to indulge.

## Direction

Downward, from a moral surplus to a licensed lapse.
Indulgence draws on the banked record to loosen the present rule: having proved itself good, the persona treats the next questionable act as affordable, charging it against the credit rather than judging it on its own terms -- the donation that licenses a later slight, the one fair choice that licenses the next unfair one.
Its direction is the drawing-down of moral capital, the good record cashed for present latitude.

## Lever

What indulgence spends -- the sense that virtue banked can pay for a lapse.
The persona does not tell itself the bad act is good; it tells itself it has earned the room for it, so the lever is the felt commensurability of past good and present bad -- the assumption that a moral surplus is a currency the lapse can be bought with.
A production holds this lever by letting the good deed come first and do quiet work on the second -- the persona more willing to cut a corner just after it has been visibly good, the latitude drawn from a credit it feels it holds -- so the lapse reads as afforded, not as justified.

## Echo

The persona takes the latitude -- and the balance falls back toward its set-point.
Having spent the credit, the persona is no longer in surplus; a further lapse would now cost it, and the same self-regulation that licensed this one will check the next.
The credentialing reframe that makes an ambiguous act read as already cleared -- a permit on file from one clean act -- is the bias engine's moral-credential member; indulgence here is the spending of a genuine moral balance, the persona affording a lapse it still counts as a lapse, against the good it banks. Its opposite number is [cleansing](process_cleansing.md), the repair of a deficit rather than the spending of a surplus.
