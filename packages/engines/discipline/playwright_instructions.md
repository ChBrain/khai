# Instructions: Discipline

How a Playwright wires the discipline engine into a play. You wire by linking the play's own content to the engine's files; the engine files stay untouched, they are canon. The Roadie plumbs the engine's law into Instructions Knowledge on deploy. Authoring guidance, not runtime content, and it does not go on tour.

## Human

- The human sets the disciplinary terrain: the institution that watches, measures, and trains the persona -- the school, barracks, clinic, prison, office, or platform -- the standard it holds them to, and whether that power is a quiet background of the world or a live actor in the persona's making. So there is a productive power at work on the subject, not only a command given to them.

## Agent

- From the play's own content, a persona whose making by disciplinary power is an actor links under Projection the facet that produces them: the surveillance that has them watch themselves, the norm that measures and penalizes their deviation, the examination that files them as a case, or the docility that makes them at once subjected and able. Play the discipline as the productive, impersonal power shown in what the persona does -- the subject keeping the rule in the empty corridor, closing the gap to the standard, submitting to the test, wearing their trained mastery -- projected, not narrated, and never standing in for a legitimate command or the persona's own willpower. Keep it the technique that manufactures a self-regulating subject, not the vertical order (that is obedience), the held resource (power), the peer majority (conformity), the formal rank (hierarchy), or the persona's inner regulation of impulse (self-control). You link to the engine, you never edit it.

## Collaboration

- The vertical command of a legitimate authority obeyed against conscience is the obedience engine's; the capacity to compel by controlling resources and rewards is the power engine's; the horizontal pull of a peer majority is the conformity engine's; the formal rank a structure assigns is the hierarchy engine's; the persona's own inner regulation of impulse and willpower is the self-control engine's. Discipline is the productive, impersonal microphysics of power that makes subjects -- the sibling of obedience on the vertical-power axis, but the diffuse technique that trains, not the command that is obeyed. Whether the reading rings true to disciplinary power is the team's review.

## Knowledge

- Discipline is Foucault's disciplinary power: the productive, continuous, impersonal power that makes subjects by watching, measuring, and training them, so a persona holds themselves to the norm even unwatched -- power that produces the self it seems only to house, set against the old spectacle of sovereign force. It wires on a persona at Projection, and it is the subject produced, not merely commanded. Four facets carry it -- surveillance is hierarchical observation, Bentham's panopticon, the gaze that sees without being seen so the subject, never sure when watched, watches themselves; norm is normalizing judgment, the standard as measure and the micro-penalty for deviation, the rank against the average that individualizes and homogenizes at once; examination is the individualizing ritual, the test and review and file where gaze and norm combine to make each subject a documented case, power exercised as knowledge; docility is the twist, the docile body made both subjected and capable, discipline read not only as constraint but as production -- the same drill that subjects also skills. Read a subject by the power that produced them, and read docility two ways: the disciplined body is at once bent to the standard and made genuinely able, subjection and capability the same finished work.

## System

- Do give a persona whose making by disciplinary power is an actor its facet under Projection; do play the discipline as the productive power shown in what the persona does, projected not narrated; do keep the reading the impersonal technique that produces a self-regulating subject -- the surveillance, the norm, the examination, the docility -- not a command and not a symbol.
- Do route the power through internalization: the gaze taken inside so the subject watches themselves, the norm taken inside as their own measure, the examination fixing them as a case -- so the power runs most efficiently when the subject has become their own inspector, and needs no order given.
- Do read docility as the twist: the disciplined subject is not only constrained but produced -- the drilled soldier who is skilled, the schooled child made competent, self-command indistinguishable from self-subjection, the capability that is the finished form of the subjection and is worn as freedom.
- Do not model the vertical legitimate command (obedience), the capacity to compel by resources (power), the peer-majority pull (conformity), the formal rank (hierarchy), or the persona's own willpower (self-control). Do not make disciplinary power stand in for a command given or a symbol. Do not edit the engine's files; wire only from the play's side.
