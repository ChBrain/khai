# Discipline

Discipline as process: Foucault's disciplinary power -- the productive, continuous, impersonal power that makes subjects by watching, measuring, and training them, so a persona holds themselves to the norm even unwatched. The surveillance that sees without being seen, the norm that measures and penalizes deviation, the examination that makes each subject a documented case, and the twist of the docile body read as capable rather than only subjected.
