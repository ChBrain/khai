# Process: Examination

## Taxonomy

[Discipline](process_discipline.md), the ritual that joins the gaze and the norm.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: discipline

## Initiated by

The persona is made to submit to a ritual that looks and measures at once, and is written down -- turned from a body into a documented, comparable case.
Examination starts wherever an institution stages the moment that combines observation and the norm -- the test, the inspection, the review, the assessment, the intake, the audit -- and records its result: the mark entered, the file opened, the chart hung at the foot of the bed, the record that follows the subject on.
It is discipline's third technique, the examination: the ceremony where the gaze that watches and the norm that measures meet on a single subject and produce knowledge of them, individual by individual.

## Direction

Toward a subject made into a case -- observed, measured, documented, and filed among the rest.
Examination works by fixing the subject in writing: the persona is looked at, scored against the standard, and then inscribed -- named, numbered, described, entered into a register that can be compared, aggregated, and retrieved -- so that they become a case, a knowable individual with a documented past and a place in a distribution.
The direction is individualization by objectification: the examination makes each subject uniquely visible precisely by measuring them, so that power over them becomes knowledge of them, and the file, once opened, precedes and outlasts the person it describes.

## Lever

The ritual that turns visibility into knowledge against the record that then holds the subject.
Examination runs on the union of the gaze and the norm made durable in writing: the persona is seen and scored in a single ceremony, and the result is documented, so that a fleeting observation becomes a permanent, comparable, retrievable fact about them; the resistance is the weight of the record -- the mark that will not be unmade, the file that follows, the case that a subject cannot step outside of once it is written.
Hold the examination and the subject is known, ranked, and filed; contest the record -- what it measured, what it left out, what it fixed too early -- and the objectivity of the case, its air of simply recording what is there, comes into doubt.

## Echo

The subject known as a case, carried in a file that precedes them.
Examination leaves the persona documented -- measured, inscribed, comparable, retrievable -- and hands that record to the disciplinary field that acts on the case it has made.
It is the technique that binds power to knowledge: the examination does not merely observe the subject, it produces them as an object of knowledge, the individual made legible by being measured, the file the modern form of a hold on a person.
Examination is dated from the ritual, the moment the subject was looked at and scored and written down, and became a case.
