# Process: Surveillance

## Taxonomy

[Discipline](process_discipline.md), the technique of hierarchical observation.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: discipline

## Initiated by

The persona is made visible to a watching they cannot see, and the possibility of being seen does the work that a guard never could.
Surveillance starts wherever a place is arranged so that its subjects are observable and the observer is not -- Bentham's panopticon, the tower at the center of the ring of lit cells, the one-way glass, the camera, the open-plan floor, the login that logs -- the eye that sees without being seen.
It is discipline's first technique, hierarchical observation: the gaze built into the arrangement of space, so that supervision is continuous, anonymous, and unverifiable, and the watched can never be sure they are not being watched.

## Direction

Toward a subject who behaves as if always observed, because they can never confirm they are not.
Surveillance works by making the watching visible but unverifiable: the persona sees the tower, the camera, the possibility of the eye, but cannot see whether it is trained on them now -- and so, to be safe, behaves at every moment as though it were.
The direction is the transfer of the watch inward: the subject, unable to catch the gaze off duty, installs it in themselves, becoming the principle of their own subjection -- the prisoner who keeps the rule in an empty corridor, the worker productive in the unwatched hour, the self that performs for an audience it can never see leave.

## Lever

The certainty of visibility against the impossibility of verifying the watch.
Surveillance runs on asymmetry: the subject is wholly seeable and the observer wholly hidden, so the burden of the uncertainty falls entirely on the watched, who must assume the eye is on them because they can never prove it is not; the resistance is the effort of that unrelenting self-presentation, and the fantasy of the blind spot, the unwatched moment the subject is never quite sure they have found.
Build the asymmetry and the guard becomes unnecessary -- the architecture watches, the subject watches themselves; break it, let the watched see the watcher off duty, and the spell that made continuous supervision cheap collapses.

## Echo

The subject watched into watching themselves.
Surveillance leaves the persona carrying the gaze inside -- self-corrected, self-presenting, behaving under the assumption of an eye they can neither see nor be sure is absent -- and hands that internalized watching to the whole disciplinary field built on it.
It is the technique that makes power economical: one possible watcher, or none, in place of an army of guards, because the uncertainty is enough; the modern extension is Deleuze's dispersal of the gaze beyond the walls, the continuous tracking that follows the subject out of the enclosure and into the open.
Surveillance is dated from the arrangement, the moment the subject was made seeable to a watching they could not see and began, to be safe, to watch themselves.
