# Process: Docility

## Taxonomy

[Discipline](process_discipline.md), the facet where subjection is read as capability.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: discipline

## Initiated by

The persona, watched and normed and examined, is made docile -- and the same making that bends them to the standard also trains them into a real ability.
Docility starts wherever discipline finishes its work: the drilled soldier who is also a skilled one, the schooled child who is also a competent adult, the trained worker whose subjection is inseparable from their craft, the self-disciplined person who has taken the overseer inside and, with it, a genuine mastery.
It is discipline read the other way: not only the constraint that subjects the body but the production that makes it able -- the docile body that is, in the same motion, a capable one.

## Direction

Toward a subject who is at once subjected and made able, the discipline that binds them the same discipline that skills them.
Docility deepens discipline by owning its productivity: the timetable and the drill and the correction do not merely repress -- they compose a capability, the trained hand, the sure procedure, the self that can, precisely because it was disciplined into it; and the internalized gaze that makes the subject their own inspector also makes them their own master, self-command indistinguishable from self-subjection.
The direction is the double face: the persona holds their trained ability as their own achievement and freedom, and it is at the same time the deepest mark of the power that produced them -- the capability that is subjection, worn as competence.

## Lever

The value of the capability against the subjection that is its underside.
The carrying force is that discipline genuinely makes able -- the skill is real, the mastery is real, the self-command is real and prized -- so that the subject reads their discipline not as a loss but as a competence, a freedom, an achievement they would not give back; the resistance, and the underside, is that this same capability is the finished form of their subjection, the norm internalized as taste, the gaze internalized as conscience, so that the most disciplined subject is the freest-feeling and the most produced.
Read one way docility is subjection, the body bent to the standard and made to run itself; read the other it is capability, the trained mastery a person owns -- and disciplinary power is most complete exactly where the two are the same thing.

## Echo

The subject made able by the very power that subjects them.
It shows a persona whose competence and self-command are genuine and prized -- and carries beneath it the discipline family's twist, the sibling of the ambivalence: that the docile body is not only constrained but produced, subjection and capability the same finished work, the internalized gaze worn as skill and self-mastery rather than felt as a chain.
Docility is dated from the completion, the moment the disciplined subject became able, and could no longer tell their mastery from their making.
