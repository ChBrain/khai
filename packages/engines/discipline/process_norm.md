# Process: Norm

## Taxonomy

[Discipline](process_discipline.md), the technique of normalizing judgment.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: discipline

## Initiated by

The persona is measured against a standard, and every small departure from it is scored, corrected, and pressed back toward the average.
Norm starts wherever an institution sets a standard of the correct -- the right pace, the right posture, the right level, the expected result -- and turns a continuous, fine-grained judgment on the gap between each subject and that measure: the late minute, the low mark, the odd manner, the missed target.
It is discipline's second technique, normalizing judgment: a whole micro-penalty system that punishes not the crime but the deviation, and holds up the norm not as a law to break but as a mean to approach.

## Direction

Toward a subject who closes the gap to the norm and is ranked by how near they stand to it.
Norm works by comparison and correction: the persona is placed on a scale, measured against the standard and against everyone else, and the distance from the mean is met with a graded pressure -- the correction, the remedial, the mark, the rank -- that is not punishment for an offense but a nudge toward the average.
The direction is normalization: the subject learns to read themselves as a distance from the norm and to work continually to close it, so that the standard, at first imposed from outside, becomes the measure they hold themselves to -- normal not as who they are but as what they must approach.

## Lever

The standard as measure against the deviation it continuously scores.
Norm runs on the double power of comparison: it individualizes, marking each subject's exact distance from the mean, and it homogenizes, pressing all subjects toward the same standard -- so that the persona is at once singled out by their deviation and pulled into line with everyone else; the resistance is the cost of the endless small corrections, and the subject who reads their own difference not as deviation to be closed but as a value to be kept.
Set the norm and every difference becomes a gap to be measured and reduced; question it -- whose standard, made how, serving what -- and the mean loses the naturalness that let it pass for simple correctness.

## Echo

The subject who measures themselves as a distance from the norm.
Norm leaves the persona holding the standard as their own measure -- ranked, corrected, working to close the gap to an average that came to feel like simple rightness -- and hands that internalized standard to the disciplinary field that scores against it.
It is the technique by which the norm quietly replaces the law: not "you have broken a rule" but "you fall below the standard," Canguilhem's norm made a social instrument, the mean turned into a demand.
Norm is dated from the measuring, the moment the subject was scored against a standard and began to work to close the distance to it.
