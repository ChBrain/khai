# Discipline: Reference

## Line of Work

Discipline as **process**: Foucault's disciplinary power -- the productive, continuous,
impersonal power that makes subjects by distributing, observing, normalizing, and examining
them, so a persona comes to hold themselves to the norm even when no one is watching. It
wires on a persona at Projection, the subject produced as much as constrained, because
disciplinary power is legible in what the subject does -- the rule kept in the empty
corridor, the gap closed to the standard, the case carried in the file, the trained mastery
worn as freedom. The domain does not model the vertical command of a legitimate authority
obeyed against conscience, which is obedience's; nor the capacity to compel by controlling
resources and rewards, which is power's; nor the horizontal pull of a peer majority, which
is conformity's; nor the formal rank a structure assigns, which is hierarchy's; nor the
persona's own inner regulation of impulse and willpower, which is self-control's. It models
the diffuse technique that manufactures a self-regulating subject.

The spine is Foucault's analytics of disciplinary power. Against the old sovereign power
that ruled by spectacle and the threat of death, Foucault traced a newer power that works
in the opposite direction: quiet, continuous, productive, exercised not on a territory but
on bodies, and aimed not at punishing the offender but at producing the useful, obedient,
capable subject. He named its means of correct training: hierarchical observation, the gaze
built into arrangements of space so that supervision is continuous and anonymous, for which
Bentham's Panopticon is the perfect diagram -- visibility made a trap; normalizing judgment,
the standard against which every deviation is scored and corrected, the norm that Canguilhem
had shown to be a made thing turned here into a social instrument; and the examination, the
ritual that fuses the gaze and the norm and writes the result down, making each subject a
documented, comparable case and binding power to knowledge. Its product is the docile body
-- and Foucault's decisive turn is that this body is not merely repressed but produced: the
same discipline that subjects it also makes it able, so capability and subjection become the
same finished work. Deleuze extended the analysis past the enclosure, to the dispersed,
continuous control of the societies that followed. So the engine gives discipline three
techniques and a twist -- surveillance, norm, examination -- and keeps a fourth, docility,
the subjection read not only as constraint but as the capability it also produces.

## Restrictions

What the engine refuses to model, and to whom it delegates.

- **The vertical legitimate command (obedience)**: A superior taken as legitimate commands
  the persona to do a thing their conscience refuses, and they obey because the order came
  from a rightful authority -- that vertical override is the obedience engine's. Discipline is
  not a command obeyed but a technique that trains: it produces the self-regulating subject
  who needs no order, where obedience is the subject who follows one.
- **The capacity to compel by resources (power)**: The base a persona holds -- reward,
  coercion, the control of what others need -- from which they can move others is the power
  engine's. Discipline is not a resource one holds over another but a diffuse, impersonal
  technique that acts through arrangements and norms, often with no one in particular holding
  it; power is a base one has, discipline a machinery one is inside.
- **The horizontal peer majority (conformity)**: The pull of a group of equals whose standard
  bends what the persona shows or believes is the conformity engine's. Discipline's norm is
  not a peer's pressure but the impersonal institutional standard, enforced by the gaze and the
  micro-penalty and internalized as one's own measure -- vertical in its source, not the sway
  of the crowd beside the subject.
- **The formal rank (hierarchy)**: The place a structure assigns a persona in its order -- who
  directs, who answers -- is the hierarchy engine's. Discipline uses ranking as a technique of
  normalization, but the formal standing itself, the position on the chart, is hierarchy's;
  discipline is the power that ranks, not the rank.
- **The persona's own regulation of impulse (self-control)**: The inner exercise of willpower,
  the persona restraining their own impulse toward a goal they hold, is the self-control
  engine's. Discipline is the external, productive power that manufactures self-regulation; the
  two meet in docility, where the gaze is internalized, but the raw capacity to regulate one's
  own impulse from within is self-control's, and the power that installed the overseer is
  discipline's.
- **The facet itself is not engine content**: The engine declares the four facets exist; which
  one a given persona shows lives on that persona, linked under its Projection chapter. The
  engine refuses to fix a subject's disciplinary making for them.

## Encoding

Source to constraint, per file.

- **[discipline](process_discipline.md)** (the root): the productive, impersonal power that
  makes subjects by watching, measuring, and training them, so the persona holds themselves to
  the norm unwatched. Anchored by Foucault (disciplinary power against sovereign power).
- **[surveillance](process_surveillance.md)**: hierarchical observation -- the gaze that sees
  without being seen, the panopticon whose watching is unverifiable, so the subject watches
  themselves. Anchored by Bentham (the Panopticon) and Foucault, extended by Deleuze (the
  dispersed gaze of control).
- **[norm](process_norm.md)**: normalizing judgment -- the standard as measure, the
  micro-penalty for deviation, the rank against the average that individualizes and
  homogenizes. Anchored by Foucault (normalizing judgment) and Canguilhem (the norm as a made
  thing).
- **[examination](process_examination.md)**: the individualizing ritual -- the test, review,
  and file where the gaze and the norm combine to make each subject a documented case, power
  exercised as knowledge. Anchored by Foucault (the examination).
- **[docility](process_docility.md)**: the docile body made both subjected and capable --
  discipline read not only as constraint but as production, the same drill that subjects also
  skills. The engine's twist; anchored by Foucault (docile bodies, the productive body).
