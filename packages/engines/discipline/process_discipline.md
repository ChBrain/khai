# Process: Discipline

## Taxonomy

Social theory / Foucault's analytics of disciplinary power.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: discipline

## Initiated by

A persona is placed inside an institution that does not so much command them as arrange them, watch them, measure them, and train them -- and by being seen, ranked against a standard, and examined, they come to hold themselves to that standard even when no one is watching.
This is disciplinary power: the productive, continuous, impersonal power that makes subjects by distributing, observing, normalizing, and examining them -- Foucault's discipline, the microphysics that manufactures docile and capable bodies out of the material it is given.
The force is neither vertical nor horizontal: not a legitimate authority's command obeyed against conscience, not the pull of a peer majority, not a resource held over the persona -- but a diffuse technique that trains the body and is taken inside as self-government.

## Direction

Toward a subject who regulates themselves to the norm because they might, at any moment, be seen.
Discipline works through the arrangement of bodies -- enclosure, partition, rank -- and the control of activity -- the timetable, the drill -- but above all through the means of correct training: the gaze that observes, the norm that measures, the examination that documents.
The direction is internalization: the persona, watched and ranked and filed, comes to carry the overseer inside, correcting themselves toward the standard so that the power need no longer be applied from without -- it runs, most efficiently, when the subject has become their own inspector.

## Lever

The visibility that traps crossed with the norm that measures.
Discipline runs on the panoptic economy: power made visible in the tower yet unverifiable in its watching, so the persona, never certain when they are seen, assumes the burden of their own subjection and behaves as if always observed; and on the norm, the standard against which every deviation is scored, penalized in a hundred small corrections, and closed toward the average.
The lever is the internalized gaze: because the subject cannot know when the eye is on them, they act under its assumption always, and the ranked, examined, documented self becomes a case that measures itself against the norm without an order ever being given.

## Echo

The persona is made a subject -- watched into self-watching, measured into self-measuring, produced as much as constrained.
Disciplinary power is why the modern school and barracks and clinic and office produce the selves they seem only to house, why the subject who believes themselves freely self-made was in fact distributed, observed, and normed into being, why power that never commands can nonetheless make a person -- the productive microphysics that Foucault set against the old spectacle of sovereign force.
It runs as [surveillance](process_surveillance.md), [norm](process_norm.md), [examination](process_examination.md), or [docility](process_docility.md) -- the gaze that sees without being seen, the standard that measures and penalizes, the ritual that files each subject as a case, the docile body made at once subjected and able. The vertical command of a legitimate authority obeyed against conscience is the obedience engine's; the capacity to compel by controlling resources is the power engine's; the horizontal pull of a peer majority is the conformity engine's; the formal rank a structure assigns is the hierarchy engine's; the persona's own inner regulation of impulse is the self-control engine's. This engine owns the productive, impersonal power that makes a self-regulating subject.
