# Place: Numerical

## Taxonomy

[NP-complete](place_np_complete.md). The Numerical chamber, where weights and capacities must be balanced.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: search-space

## Shown

A room containing a large scale and a pile of weighted, valued items. A traveler stands before it, holding a bag with a strict weight capacity, trying to select a combination of items that maximizes value without breaking the bag or the scale.

## Holds

Numerical and knapsack-type problems, including the search for [the knapsack](piece_the_knapsack.md).

## Offers

The trade-off of value and budget. The room offers a persona the challenge of allocating a fixed capacity to the most valuable combination of items.

## Withheld

The exact optimum. The room withholds the precise combination of items that maximizes value without exceeding capacity, leaving the traveler to use greedy approximations or pack too light.
