# Place: PSPACE

## Taxonomy

[The Search Space](place_the_search_space.md). The region of polynomial-space complexity.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: search-space

## Shown

A vast library of infinite corridors where the width is strictly limited, but the traveler is allowed to re-read and erase their notes as many times as they wish. The air is still, and time is irrelevant here: the only pressure is the rigid boundary of the workspace, a time-free zone where memory is the only currency.

## Holds

Problems that can be solved using a polynomial amount of memory, regardless of how many steps or eons they require to finish.

## Offers

Unlimited time within a bounded workspace. The library offers a persona the capacity to simulate complex games, verify infinite branches, and search deep trees as long as they stay within the allocated storage boundary.

## Withheld

The time guarantee. The library withholds any promise that the search will finish within the lifetime of the traveler, meaning that while the space is bounded, the time required to escape it may be exponential or worse.
