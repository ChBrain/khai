# Place: NP-complete

## Taxonomy

[NP](place_np.md). The core gallery of NP-completeness.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: search-space

## Shown

A grand gallery containing five main chambers. Each chamber is locked with a massive, distinct door, yet the walls are lined with mirrors that reflect the same lock mechanisms. The air is tense with the realization that the doors are structurally bound together: a key that opens one will open every room in the gallery.

## Holds

The five classic families of combinatorial difficulty: [Packing](place_packing.md), [Covering](place_covering_family.md), [Sequencing](place_sequencing.md), [Partitioning](place_partitioning.md), and [Numerical](place_numerical.md).

## Offers

The power of reduction. The gallery offers a persona the ability to transform any hard problem in NP into another, proving they share a single, unified difficulty.

## Withheld

The general key. The gallery withholds the polynomial-time algorithm that would unlock all doors at once, forcing the persona to construct approximations or accept local optima.
