## Place

## Load Bearing

Without the Subset Sum, we cannot model exact partitioning of resources or values. It is the core of numerical target matching: if we cannot find a subset that sums exactly to the target, resources will remain unbalanced. The Subset Sum is load bearing because it is the baseline decision problem for numerical constraints, from which Knapsack and partition problems are derived.

## Apparent

A subset of integers that sums exactly to a target value. It looks like a balanced scale, a collection of weights that matches the target perfectly.

## Yearbook

Proven NP-complete by Richard Karp in 1972. It represents the foundation of cryptographic knapsack systems and basic financial partition theory.
