## Load Bearing

Without the Cover, we cannot optimize constraint satisfaction with minimal resources. It is the core model for scheduling, camera placement, and network security: if we cannot find the minimum cover, we cannot protect the network without wasting capacity. The Cover is load bearing because it represents the minimum cost of safety in a connected system.
