# Place: Packing

## Taxonomy

[NP-complete](place_np_complete.md). The Packing chamber, where items must fit without conflict.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: search-space

## Shown

A room filled with geometric frames and complex networks. The task is to select elements (nodes, blocks, schedules) that are mutually compatible, placing them inside a restricted space where they must not touch or overlap.

## Holds

Packing and subset selection problems, including the search for [the clique](piece_the_clique.md).

## Offers

The search for independent subsets. The room offers a persona the capacity to identify compatible groupings and isolate non-conflicting elements.

## Withheld

The maximum configuration. The room withholds the largest possible subset of mutually compatible choices, leaving the persona to guess or settle for smaller, suboptimal groups.
