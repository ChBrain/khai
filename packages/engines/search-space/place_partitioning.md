# Place: Partitioning

## Taxonomy

[NP-complete](place_np_complete.md). The Partitioning chamber, where adjacent nodes must be kept distinct.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: search-space

## Shown

A room containing a large network of vertices connected by edges. The task is to assign colors or groups to each vertex so that no two adjacent vertices share the same group, keeping conflicts separated.

## Holds

Partitioning and coloring problems, including the search for [the coloring](piece_the_coloring.md).

## Offers

The division of resources. The room offers a persona the capacity to split a domain into non-conflicting parts or allocate schedules without adjacent overlaps.

## Withheld

The chromatic threshold. The room withholds the minimum number of colors or partitions required to avoid all adjacent conflicts, forcing the persona to use too many groups or allow a conflict to stand.
