# Place: The Search Space

## Taxonomy

None. This is the root place of the computational complexity engine.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: search-space

## Shown

A silent, boundless terrain of potential configurations. There are no walls or physical gates, but the very air is thick and heavy, making every step forward require energy. To an observer, the space appears as an infinite web of choices, stretching in all directions under a cold, mathematical light.

## Holds

The landscape of computational difficulty, structured into distinct regions: the well-lit hall of [P](place_p.md), the foggy plains of [NP](place_np.md) and its locked gallery [NP-complete](place_np_complete.md), the dark abyss of [NP-hard](place_np_hard.md), and the circular labyrinth of [PPAD](place_ppad.md).

## Offers

A unified coordinate system for modeling search, optimization, and resolution problems. It offers a persona a way to measure the resistance of any goal, identifying whether a task is a matter of simple steps or a structural trap.

## Withheld

The general key to the space: a proof that P equals NP, or that they are forever separate. The space withholds the shortcut that would bypass the need for search, forcing every persona to pay in time or memory for every step they take.
