# Place: P

## Taxonomy

[The Search Space](place_the_search_space.md). The region of polynomial-time tractability.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: search-space

## Shown

A bright, narrow corridor where every turn is marked and every step is predictable. The floor is smooth, and the distance to any visible destination is short, measured in a polynomial number of paces relative to the size of the task.

## Holds

Tractable problems and efficient paths, including the search for [the path](piece_the_path.md). It holds the baseline configurations where answers are found on schedule.

## Offers

Immediate progress and guaranteed resolution. The corridor offers a persona clear instructions and quick movement, allowing them to find the optimal path without hesitation or exhaustion.

## Withheld

The wider horizon. The corridor withholds the complexity of the outside world, keeping the persona safe within its clean walls but offering no tools when the problem escapes its boundaries.
