# Place: NP

## Taxonomy

[The Search Space](place_the_search_space.md). The region of non-deterministic polynomial-time verification.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: search-space

## Shown

A foggy plain where countless structures and combinations lie half-buried in the mist. To inspect any single configuration is immediate: if a traveler is handed a candidate solution, the fog clears around it, and they can verify its correctness at a glance. But the overall plain is wide, and the correct solution is not highlighted.

## Holds

The machinery of verification. The plain holds the mathematical systems that can double-check any claim, but cannot generate the claim themselves.

## Offers

Cheap verification and the promise of a solution. The plain offers a persona the ease of checking work, so that once a candidate is found, its truth is undeniable and free to prove.

## Withheld

The direction of search. The plain withholds the path to find the correct configuration, leaving the persona to wander through an exponential forest of possibilities unless they are handed the right candidate.
