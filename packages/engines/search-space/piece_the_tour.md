# Piece: The Tour

## Taxonomy

[Sequencing](place_sequencing.md). The Traveling Salesman Problem (TSP), representing the optimal sequence.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: search-space

## Place

Located in the Sequencing chamber, [Sequencing](place_sequencing.md), laid across the network nodes.

## Load Bearing

Without the Tour, we cannot optimize logistics or resource sequencing. It is the model for logistics delivery, circuit design, and assembly planning: if we cannot find the optimal tour, the cost of transportation or time delays will collapse the margins of the plan. The Tour is load bearing because it balances sequence against cost.

## Apparent

A cyclic path that visits every node exactly once and returns to the start. It looks like a simple, closed route on a map, a clean loop connecting all cities.

## Yearbook

Studied in the 1800s by William Rowan Hamilton and Thomas Kirkman, and formulated as the TSP by Merrill Flood in 1937. It was proven NP-complete by Richard Karp in 1972, and remains the primary benchmark for optimization heuristics.
