# Place: NP-hard

## Taxonomy

[The Search Space](place_the_search_space.md). The region of ultimate computational difficulty.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: search-space

## Shown

A dark, howling abyss that lies beyond the boundaries of the NP plain. The air is cold and empty: unlike the NP plain, there is no fog that clears when a candidate is presented. A traveler standing at the edge can drop any candidate solution into the void, but the abyss does not check it or return a confirmation.

## Holds

Problems that are at least as hard as any in NP, but do not promise easy verification or bounded search.

## Offers

The boundary of the unknown. The abyss offers a persona the realization that some tasks are so difficult that even confirming a correct answer is beyond tractable reach.

## Withheld

The verification. The abyss withholds the easy verification that NP offers, leaving the persona with no way to prove an answer is correct without running the entire search again.
