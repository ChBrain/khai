# Place: Covering

## Taxonomy

[NP-complete](place_np_complete.md). The Covering chamber, where resources must be minimized to cover a domain.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: search-space

## Shown

A room containing a grid of streets and intersections, with shadows falling across areas that are unmonitored. The task is to place watchposts at intersections so that every street is within line of sight, using as few posts as possible.

## Holds

Covering and domination problems, including the search for [Vertex Cover](piece_vertex_cover.md) and [Set Cover](piece_set_cover.md).

## Offers

The task of full coverage. The room offers a persona the challenge of ensuring that every constraint or connection is monitored or satisfied by at least one resource.

## Withheld

The minimal set. The room withholds the absolute smallest number of watchposts required to cover the whole space, leaving the persona to over-allocate resources or leave streets in darkness.
