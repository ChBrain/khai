# Piece: The Equilibrium

## Taxonomy

## Owner

## Place

Laid at the center of the circular labyrinth, [PPAD](place_ppad.md), where all paths balance.

## Load Bearing

Without the Equilibrium, we cannot model stable states in markets or games. It is the core model for economic balance and strategic interaction: if we cannot find the equilibrium vector, we cannot predict where players or markets will settle, and any plan that assumes stable prices or strategies will collapse into instability. The Equilibrium is load bearing because it is the state where no player has incentive to deviate.

## Apparent

A vector of strategies, one for each player, where each is a best response to the others. It looks like a quiet market, a stable state of balance where all activity has settled into a routine.

## Yearbook

John Nash proved in 1950 that a mixed strategy equilibrium always exists for any finite game. In 2006, Constantinos Daskalakis, Paul Goldberg, and Christos Papadimitriou proved that computing this equilibrium is PPAD-complete, establishing that finding Nash's balance is structurally intractable.
