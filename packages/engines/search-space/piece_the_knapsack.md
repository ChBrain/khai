## Place

## Load Bearing

Without the Knapsack, we cannot model optimal resource allocation within a budget. It is the baseline model for investment portfolio selection, cargo loading, and resource budgeting: if we cannot solve the knapsack problem, our plans will exceed capacity or waste valuable space. The Knapsack is load bearing because it balances value against weight.

## Apparent

A selected subset of items, each with a weight and value, that does not exceed a defined capacity. It looks like a well-packed bag, a collection of valuable assets that fits within the budget.

## Yearbook

Formulated as an optimization problem by George Dantzig in 1957. It was proven NP-complete by Richard Karp in 1972, and remains the primary example of a problem that is solvable in pseudo-polynomial time via dynamic programming.
