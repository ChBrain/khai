# Place: PPAD

## Taxonomy

[The Search Space](place_the_search_space.md). The region of polynomial parity arguments on directed graphs.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: search-space

## Shown

A circular labyrinth where every path has an entrance and an exit, guaranteeing that a destination must exist. The walls are smooth and identical, and although the traveler knows that a stable equilibrium point exists somewhere in the maze, every path looks the same, and they wander in circles.

## Holds

Equilibrium and fixed-point problems, including the search for [the equilibrium](piece_the_equilibrium.md).

## Offers

The topological guarantee of existence. The maze offers a persona the certainty that a solution is there before they even begin to look, preventing them from searching for something that does not exist.

## Withheld

The direction to the stable point. The maze withholds the shortcut to the equilibrium, leaving the persona to search through the paths without a compass, proving that finding balance is structurally hard even when it is guaranteed.
