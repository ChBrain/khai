# Position: Moral Conviction

## Taxonomy

Metacognition / a persona's sense that an attitude is a moral mandate (no khai file of its own).

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: moral-conviction

## Has

A metacognitive stamp on an attitude: the sense that a position is a moral mandate -- grounded in a core conviction about right and wrong -- and not a preference, a taste, or a convention (Skitka).
It is orthogonal to what the attitude is about: any position, on any concern, can carry the stamp or lack it, and the same person can hold one view as a mandate and another, held just as strongly, as mere preference.
Two properties mark the stamp and set a mandate apart from a strong opinion: it is held as an objective fact, universally and self-evidently true, and it is held as independent of authority -- owing nothing to any rule, law, or command, and standing in judgment over them rather than under them.

## Orders

Treat the marked position as an imperative, not a want.
The stamp orders the persona to hold the attitude as fact rather than opinion, to grant it a force no preference commands -- to act on it, defend it, and refuse to trade it away -- and to measure authorities and rules by whether they honour it, not the other way round.
The room reads the mandate status of a persona's positions before it reads their content: which of its views are simply-right, and which are only-preferred.

## Loses

The negotiability of a mere preference.
A position held as a moral mandate cannot be split the difference on, deferred to authority, or set down when it is costly -- the persona forgoes the ordinary give of a preference, the room to compromise, to be overruled, to let it go -- and pays in the rigidity, the friction with rule and authority, and the closure toward dissent that the mandate exacts.
What it trades is the flexibility of holding a view as opinion for the force of holding it as truth: the same stamp that lets the persona stand bravely on principle also stops it bending where bending would be wise.

## Drives

On that stamp:
runs on it -- holds the position as objective fact and above authority, and lets it move conduct with a force no preference has -- or lacks it, holding the same view as a strong preference that can still be traded, deferred, or overruled.
Where the mandate is present it shows in two faces, the double edge of the same conviction: [mobilization](position_mobilization.md), the mandate as fuel that drives the persona to act, sacrifice, and refuse to fold; or [intolerance](position_intolerance.md), the mandate as wall that closes it against those who disagree and can license hard means. Which moral concern the attitude runs on -- care, fairness, loyalty, authority, sanctity, liberty -- is the moral-judgment engine's; this engine owns only the stamp that turns a position from a preference into an imperative.
