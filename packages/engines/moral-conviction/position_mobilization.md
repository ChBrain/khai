# Position: Mobilization

## Taxonomy

[Moral Conviction](position_moral_conviction.md), the mandate as fuel -- the edge that moves the persona to act.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: moral-conviction

## Has

A specific face of the [moral mandate](position_moral_conviction.md): its power to move -- the way a position held as simply-right drives the persona to do something about it (Skitka).
The energizing edge of the stamp: moral conviction is the strongest known predictor of political and civic engagement -- the mandate turns a held view into a felt obligation to act on it, to turn out, to speak, to sacrifice for it, and to refuse to be bought off or worn down.
It is the face that puts the persona on its feet: what it holds as morally true it cannot merely believe from a chair.

## Orders

Act on the mandate, and hold the line for it.
The mobilizing face orders the persona to convert conviction into conduct -- to engage, to give effort and cost and risk that no preference would justify, and to stand firm under pressure to compromise, defer, or fold -- and to draw its resolve from the sense that the position is not its opinion but the truth, so that backing down would be not a concession but a betrayal.
It steadies the persona in the fight: it can be outnumbered and outranked and still not yield, because the mandate does not answer to numbers or rank.

## Loses

The ease of sitting a thing out.
Holding the position as a mandate to act on, the persona forgoes the option a preference leaves open -- to hold the view privately, to let it go when acting is costly, to defer to those who would rather it did nothing -- and it spends real energy, risk, and standing on a cause it cannot, in conscience, decline.
What it trades is the comfort and safety of the bystander for the moral force of the actor: the fuel that lets it stand bravely on principle also commits it to fights a merely-preferring persona could walk past.

## Drives

On that mobilizing mandate:
rides it -- acts, engages, sacrifices, and refuses to fold, drawing resolve from holding the position as truth rather than taste -- or spends it, letting the mandate move it past what is prudent into fights it cannot win or cannot afford, or, at the other end, lets the fuel gutter and holds the conviction without ever acting on it.
The mandate holds underneath either way, felt as fact and above authority; this face owns its power to move the persona to act, where the [intolerance](position_intolerance.md) face owns its power to close the persona against those who disagree. That the position is a mandate at all rather than a preference is the [anchor's](position_moral_conviction.md); which moral concern it runs on is the moral-judgment engine's.
