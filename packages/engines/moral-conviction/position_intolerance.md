# Position: Intolerance

## Taxonomy

[Moral Conviction](position_moral_conviction.md), the mandate as wall -- the edge that closes the persona against dissent.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: moral-conviction

## Has

A specific face of the [moral mandate](position_moral_conviction.md): its power to divide -- the way a position held as simply-right sets the persona against those who hold it otherwise (Skitka).
The corrosive edge of the stamp: moral conviction predicts greater social distance from attitudinally dissimilar others, less trust and goodwill toward them, and a reading of dissent as not merely mistaken but wrong -- a difference of character, not of opinion.
It is the face that raises a wall: a preference lets the persona differ and still sit at the same table; a mandate makes the other's disagreement a mark against them.

## Orders

Refuse the disagreeing other, and the ground of compromise.
The intolerant face orders the persona to distance itself from those who take the other side, to withhold from them the benefit of the doubt a mere difference of opinion would keep, and to treat compromise as complicity -- a splitting of what cannot be split -- rather than as reasonable settlement.
It hardens the persona against authority and procedure too when they cross the mandate: a legitimate rule that violates what is simply-right forfeits its claim, and at the far edge the certainty of the end can license hard means to reach it.

## Loses

The tolerance that lets difference stand.
Holding the position as a mandate the other has failed, the persona forgoes the civic patience that would let it grant the dissenter good faith, share a table with them, and bend to a legitimate procedure it dislikes -- it loses the disagreeing other as a partner and keeps them only as an opponent -- and it risks the escalation, the contempt, and the licensed hard means that certainty of the right can carry.
What it trades is the pluralist's forbearance for the purist's clarity: the same stamp that lets it refuse to compromise its principles also refuses the person who does not share them.

## Drives

On that intolerant mandate:
rides it -- distances from the dissenter, refuses compromise as complicity, subordinates authority and procedure to the mandate, and, at the extreme, licenses hard means to a certain end -- or resists it, holding the conviction as firmly while still granting the other good faith and keeping the table open, and paying the tension of a mandate held without the closure it invites.
The mandate holds underneath either way, felt as fact and above authority; this face owns its power to close the persona against those who disagree, where the [mobilization](position_mobilization.md) face owns its power to move the persona to act. That the position is a mandate at all rather than a preference is the [anchor's](position_moral_conviction.md); which moral concern it runs on is the moral-judgment engine's.
