# Process: Drought

## Taxonomy

[Hydrology](process_hydrology.md), the mode where the water fails in destructive deficit.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: hydrology

## Initiated by

Less water arrives than the place draws, and the store runs down.
Drought starts wherever the input falls short for long enough to matter -- the rains that did not come, the season that stretched dry, the snowpack that failed, the river shrinking to a thread -- and the place spends its water faster than it is refilled.
It is water in deficit: not a single dry day but the slow drawing-down, the balance tipped the other way from flood, the place going thirsty.

## Direction

Down, and toward the parched.
Drought lowers the water everywhere the place holds it -- the reservoir dropping, the well deepening its reach, the soil cracking, the crop wilting, the river bed showing its stones.
It moves the place toward the dry: the green gone brown, the wet ground gone dust, the flow gone still, the life that the water carried failing with it -- and if it holds, the land itself hardening, the dust ready for the wind.

## Lever

The place's draw against the water left to draw on.
The carrying force is the demand -- what the land, the life, and the people take from the water; the resistance is the store -- the reservoir, the soil moisture, the groundwater the dry falls back on -- lasting only as long as it holds against a refill that is not coming.
Deepen the well and it buys time against the dry; empty the store and there is nothing left to temper the deficit, and the place fails.

## Echo

The place parched, and the store drawn down.
Drought does not close like a storm; it deepens as long as the input stays short, handing the place a lowered water everywhere and, at its worst, the cracked ground and the failed harvest.
The drought that parches is the reason the groundwater matters: what the place saved below is what it lives on when the surface fails, and the dry teaches the place the worth of the store it drew on.
Drought is dated from the deficit, the long shortfall that empties what the place had put by.
