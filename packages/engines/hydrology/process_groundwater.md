# Process: Groundwater

## Taxonomy

[Hydrology](process_hydrology.md), the mode where the water is held hidden below.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: hydrology

## Initiated by

Water soaks into the ground and is held in the rock and soil below the surface.
Groundwater starts wherever the water that fell or flowed sinks in and gathers -- the rain soaking to the table, the river losing to its bed, the aquifer filling in the wet years -- and the place carries a store of water out of sight, below the ground it walks on.
It is the water the place holds in reserve: not on the surface where it is seen but beneath it, in the pores and the cracks, rising and falling as it is recharged and drawn.

## Direction

Down into the ground, and out where the table meets the surface.
Groundwater fills from above and moves slowly through the rock toward wherever the land cuts down to it -- surfacing as the spring, the seep, the marsh, the base flow that keeps the river running through the dry, the well that reaches down to find it.
It moves the place's water out of sight and back: the surplus of the wet years stored below, drawn on in the dry, the hidden half of the water the place has.

## Lever

The recharge that fills the store against the draw that empties it.
The carrying force is the water sinking in -- the rain and the river feeding the table; the resistance, and the danger, is the draw -- the wells pumping it out faster than it refills, the aquifer that took ages to fill emptied in a lifetime, the table dropping beyond the reach of the roots and the wells.
Recharge it and the store buffers the place against drought; overdraw it and the ground itself may compact and sink, the water gone and the space it held collapsed.

## Echo

The place's hidden store, rising or falling out of sight.
Groundwater surfaces as the spring and the base flow, and stands beneath as the reserve the place falls back on -- the buffer that keeps the river alive through the drought and the well wet through the dry.
It is the slow, unseen half of the regime: the flood is spectacular and the drought is felt, but the store below decides whether the place survives the one and endures the other.
Groundwater is dated from the recharge, the water put by below in the years of plenty against the years of want.
