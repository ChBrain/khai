# Hydrology: Reference

## Line of Work

Hydrology as **process**: the water regime of a place -- how much water it has, where,
and when -- the water in destructive excess, in destructive deficit, in hidden store,
and as the place's own life, shown in the place before anyone gauges it. It wires on a
place at Shown, the place's physical self-presentation, because the water a place stands
in -- the river, the flood, the dry, the spring -- is among the first things it puts in
front of whoever enters. The domain does not model what the water does to the ground,
the scour and the cut, which is the erosion engine's; nor the rain-event that falls from
the sky in the hour, which is the weather engine's; nor the ground the water runs on and
carves, which is geomorphology's; nor the long climatic regime that governs whether a
place tends wet or dry, which is climate's. It models the water itself as the place's
standing balance, and reads it two ways: as the flood and drought that unmake the place,
and as the water it lives by.

The spine is the recognition that water is the force a place is most made and unmade by,
and that its excess and its deficit are the two faces of one regime. McPhee read the
long war against the water -- the Mississippi held off its own delta, the river that
builds and drowns the land it made. Mike Davis read the Southern California cycle of
drought and flood and fire as one regime a city refuses to live within. And Kelman read
a city's whole history as its relation to its river, the water that made New Orleans and
periodically takes it back. So the engine gives the water regime four modes -- flood in
excess, drought in deficit, groundwater in hidden store -- and keeps a fourth,
wellspring, the same water read as the place's life, the gift the flood and drought are
the failures of.

## Restrictions

What the engine refuses to model, and to whom it delegates.

- **What the water does to the ground (erosion)**: Hydrology is the water regime -- how
  much, where, when; erosion is what the moving water does to the surface it runs over,
  the scour and the cut. The two meet in the eroding river: the flow, its volume, its
  flood are hydrology's; the ground the flow cuts is erosion's. Hydrology supplies the
  agent; erosion is the wearing the agent works.
- **The rain-event from the sky (weather)**: The storm, the downpour, the rain as it
  falls are the weather engine's; hydrology is the water balance the place holds over the
  season and the year, after the rain has fallen. Weather delivers the input in the hour;
  hydrology is what the place makes of that input over time -- the downpour is weather's,
  the flood it swells is hydrology's, the drought of the rains that never came is
  hydrology's too.
- **The ground the water runs on (geomorphology)**: The landform -- the valley, the
  basin, the channel cut by the earth's own violence -- is geomorphology's; hydrology is
  the water that fills and moves through it. The canyon read as landform is
  geomorphology's; the river read as water is hydrology's. Geomorphology shapes the vessel;
  hydrology is what fills it.
- **The long regime that governs the balance (climate)**: Climate is the standing,
  long-run tendency -- arid, humid, monsoonal -- that sets whether a place's water runs to
  surplus or shortfall; hydrology is the actual water and its extremes within that
  tendency. Climate is why the place tends wet or dry over the decades; hydrology is the
  flood this year and the drought the next. Climate sets the odds; hydrology is the water
  that comes.
- **A persona's feeling, and the water as authored symbol**: How the flood or the drought
  makes a persona feel -- the terror of the rising water, the despair of the dry -- is the
  persona's own engines'; hydrology delivers the water, not the feeling. And the water
  made to **stand for** a persona's own state, the drought that mirrors an emotional
  barrenness, is refused: that is authored key, the play's pitch, not the force on the
  place.
- **The mode itself is not engine content**: The engine declares the four modes exist;
  which one a given place shows lives on that place, linked under its Shown chapter. The
  engine refuses to fix a place's water for it.
