# Instructions: Hydrology

How a Playwright wires the hydrology engine into a play. You wire by linking the play's
own content to the engine's files; the engine files stay untouched, they are canon. The
Roadie plumbs the engine's law into Instructions Knowledge on deploy. Authoring
guidance, not runtime content, and it does not go on tour.

## Human

- The human sets which places stand in a water regime that acts -- drowned by flood, parched by drought, held up by a hidden store, or made livable by their water -- and whether that water is the place's standing condition or a live event in the play.

## Agent

- From the play's own content, a place whose water is an actor links under Shown the mode of its regime: the flood of destructive excess, the drought of destructive deficit, the groundwater held below, or the wellspring that gives it life. Play the hydrology as the water itself shown -- the risen river, the cracked reservoir, the spring, the teeming wetland -- present in what the place puts before whoever enters, never narrated, and never as a mirror of a persona's own flooding or thirst. Keep it the water itself, not what the water does to the ground (that is erosion), the rain-event from the sky (weather), the ground the water runs on (geomorphology), or the long regime that governs it (climate). You link to the engine, you never edit it.

## Collaboration

- What the moving water does to the surface is erosion's; the rain-event from the sky is weather's; the ground the water runs on is geomorphology's; the long climatic tendency that governs the balance is climate's. Hydrology supplies the water erosion works and inherits the input weather delivers -- it is the place's standing water balance over the season and the year. Whether the mode rings true to the place and its water is the team's review.

## Knowledge

- Hydrology is the water regime of a place: how much water it has, where, and when -- the water in destructive excess, in destructive deficit, in hidden store, and as the place's own life, shown in the place before anyone gauges it (Davis's drought-flood cycle as one regime; Kelman's city read as its relation to its river). It wires on a place at Shown, and it is the water itself -- it supplies the agent erosion works and inherits the input weather delivers, but reads the balance over time, not the rain of the hour. Four modes carry it -- flood is water in destructive excess, the river over its banks and the low ground drowned; drought is water in destructive deficit, the parched land and the store drawn down; groundwater is the hidden store below, the aquifer and the base flow recharged in the wet and drawn in the dry; wellspring is the twist, the same water read as the place's life -- the perennial river, the oasis, the reason a place is settled. The same water that floods and parches is the water the place lives by. Read a place by the mode of its water regime.

## System

- Do give a place whose water is an actor its mode under Shown; do play the hydrology as the water itself shown, present not narrated; do keep the reading the water regime -- the flood, the drought, the store, the life -- not a feeling and not a symbol.
- Do read wellspring as the twist: the water that floods in excess and fails in drought is, held in balance, the very life of the place -- the river town, the oasis, the settled spring.
- Do not model what the water does to the ground (that is erosion), the rain-event from the sky (weather), the ground the water runs on (geomorphology), or the long regime that governs it (climate). Do not make the flood or drought mirror a persona's own state (that is the play's pitch). Do not edit the engine's files; wire only from the play's side.
