# Process: Relief

## Taxonomy

[Addiction](process_addiction.md), the drive this is one form of.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: addiction

## Initiated by

Pain is present or near -- the ache of exertion, the pressure of distress, the raw edge of a feeling the persona cannot sit inside.
The organism can dampen it: the behaviour releases the currency that masks the hurt and buys a stretch of ease.
The worse the baseline feels, the louder the offer of relief.

## Direction

Toward the covering of pain, not the winning of a prize.
The persona acts to blunt what hurts -- to push through, to numb, to reach the release on the far side of effort or excess.
The aim is a floor, not a ceiling: not to feel good but to stop feeling bad.

## Lever

The relief is real and it works as negative reinforcement.
The currency is the body's own analgesia -- [endorphin](piece_endorphin.md), the opioid that masks pain rather than adds pleasure -- and the organism adapts: the baseline sinks so that going without the behaviour now feels worse than before it ever started, and the persona needs the release simply to return to level.
What was reached for to feel better is now reached for to avoid feeling its withdrawal.

## Echo

The persona keeps returning to the source of relief, and the relief keeps buying less.
The gap that the behaviour once filled has widened underneath it; the escape has become the thing that must be escaped.
Where the analgesia has become the baseline, stopping is not the loss of a pleasure but the arrival of the pain the behaviour was holding off, and that pain is what pulls the persona back.
