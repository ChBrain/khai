# Process: Craving

## Taxonomy

[Addiction](process_addiction.md), the drive this is one form of.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: addiction

## Initiated by

A cue appears that the organism has learned predicts the reward -- the notification, the open door, the first sip, the name on the screen.
The reward itself has not arrived; only its promise has.
The signal is enough: the pursuit fires before there is anything to receive.

## Direction

Toward the getting, not the having.
The persona is pulled to seek, chase, check, and repeat -- the effort is aimed at closing the gap to the reward, and the closing is where the charge lives.
When the reward lands the charge is already fading; the next cue is what the system reaches for.

## Lever

The wanting has come loose from the liking.
The currency here is anticipation, carried on [dopamine](piece_dopamine.md), the signal that marks a cue as worth pursuing, and repetition sensitizes it: the cue-triggered pull grows stronger over time while the pleasure of the payout dulls, so the persona wants it most keenly right when it satisfies least.
Unpredictable payout sharpens the pull further -- a reward that might come tightens the seeking more than one that reliably does.

## Echo

The persona pursues compulsively and gets little from arriving.
The chase is the point and the point keeps receding; the loop runs on the promise, and the promise is renewed by the next cue before the last reward is spent.
Where the wanting has fully outrun the liking, the persona keeps reaching for what no longer pleases them, and can see the emptiness of it without being able to stop the reach.
