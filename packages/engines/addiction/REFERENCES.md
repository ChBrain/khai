# Addiction: Reference

## Line of Work

Addiction as **process**: how a persona comes to pursue a behaviour past its cost, and how the pull shifts from wanting the reward to needing it. The domain does not model the valuation of an outcome across time -- that is the reward engine's territory -- nor the automaticity of a repeated act -- that is the habit engine's. It models the _compulsion_: what happens when the organism's reward chemistry is recruited past the point of choice, so the same behaviour is sought for less and less return at more and more cost, and going without it is felt as a deficit to be corrected rather than a pleasure foregone.

The spine is the shift from positive to negative reinforcement, and the dissociation of wanting from liking. A behaviour that began as a source of pleasure becomes a source of relief from the dysphoria of its own absence; and the cue-triggered pursuit of it sensitizes and grows even as the pleasure it delivers flattens. The four drives name the currency the compulsion is denominated in -- craving, relief, acclaim, closeness -- each carried on the neurochemistry of a distinct reward system (dopamine, the endogenous opioids, serotonin, oxytocin), because the same compulsive shape runs differently depending on which reward the organism has come to need.

## Restrictions

What the engine refuses to model, and to whom it delegates.

- **Reward**: The engine models the compulsion that recruits reward chemistry past choice, not the valuation of outcomes across time. How much a future good is worth at its current distance, and the discount that shapes it, is owned by the reward engine; this engine governs what happens when that system is over-recruited into need.
- **Habit**: The engine models the compulsive pull toward a needed currency, not the automaticity of a repeated response. A behaviour that runs without intention because a stable context cues it is the habit engine's domain; this engine governs the behaviour that is pursued _against_ intention because its absence is felt as a deficit.
- **Motivation**: The engine models the recruitment of a reward into need, not the source or direction of a drive. Why the persona acts -- intrinsic interest, external pressure, or its absence -- is the motivation engine's; this engine governs the drive that has escaped the persona's endorsement of it.
- **Mood** and **Emotion**: The engine models the standing or the bond as a currency a compulsion is spent toward, not the felt tone or the discrete feeling itself. The persona's baseline affect is the mood engine's, and joy, panic, or shame as emotions are the emotion engine's; this engine governs only how the pursuit of the currency has become a need.
- **Desire**: The engine models wanting that has outlived its reward and become need, not the resting style of wanting a persona brings toward an object. How a persona characteristically loves or longs is the desire engine's; this engine governs the compulsion, not the appetite.

## Encoding

Source to constraint, per file.

- **[addiction](process_addiction.md)** (the root): The behaviour is pursued past its cost because the organism has come to need what it pays out; the pull shifts from wanting to needing. Anchored by Koob & Volkow (allostasis and the shift to negative reinforcement) and Berridge & Robinson (wanting outliving liking).
- **[craving](process_craving.md)** (drive, carried on dopamine): A cue predicting reward fires the pursuit; the wanting sensitizes and grows while the liking fades, so the chase is compulsive and the arrival empty. Anchored by Berridge & Robinson (incentive salience and the wanting/liking dissociation).
- **[relief](process_relief.md)** (drive, carried on the endogenous opioids): The behaviour masks pain and buys relief; the baseline sinks so the persona needs it to reach level, and stopping returns the withheld pain. Anchored by Pert & Snyder (the endogenous opioid system) and Koob & Volkow (negative reinforcement in withdrawal).
- **[acclaim](process_acclaim.md)** (drive, carried on serotonin): Standing lifts mood and loss drops it; the persona chases the marker of rank to hold the mood up, and the comparison never closes. Anchored by Raleigh & McGuire (serotonergic function tracking social dominance).
- **[closeness](process_closeness.md)** (drive, carried on oxytocin): The bond settles the organism and its threat alarms it; the tie becomes load-bearing and the persona clings past what it returns. Anchored by Young & Wang (oxytocin in pair bonding and the deficit of its loss).
- **[dopamine](piece_dopamine.md)** (piece): The currency of the craving drive -- the wanting signal that predicts reward and sensitizes with repetition. Anchored by Berridge & Robinson; carries the wanting the [endorphin](piece_endorphin.md) liking does not, and runs against the steadying tone of [serotonin](piece_serotonin.md).
- **[endorphin](piece_endorphin.md)** (piece): The currency of the relief drive -- the body's endogenous opioid, the analgesia that masks pain and delivers the liking dopamine only points at. Anchored by Pert & Snyder; its comfort overlaps [oxytocin](piece_oxytocin.md)'s bond.
- **[serotonin](piece_serotonin.md)** (piece): The currency of the acclaim drive -- the background tone that tracks social rank and pegs mood to standing. Anchored by Raleigh & McGuire; secure, it counters the [dopamine](piece_dopamine.md) chase.
- **[oxytocin](piece_oxytocin.md)** (piece): The currency of the closeness drive -- the chemistry of the bond, released in contact and felt as its settling. Anchored by Young & Wang; a secure bond can quiet the [dopamine](piece_dopamine.md) chase and its comfort shades into [endorphin](piece_endorphin.md).
