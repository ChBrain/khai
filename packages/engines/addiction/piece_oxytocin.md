# Piece: Oxytocin

## Taxonomy

[Addiction](process_addiction.md): the currency the [closeness](process_closeness.md) drive spends.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: addiction

## Place

In the bond, released in the contact that makes one. Oxytocin rises with touch, with holding, with the gaze and the return of it, with the acts that mark a tie as real. It lives between two, not inside one: it is produced in the connection and felt as the settling the connection brings, and it is at its loudest in the presence -- and its absence -- of the specific person the tie is with.

## Load Bearing

Remove it and the tie loses its settle. Closeness stops quieting anything; the [closeness](process_closeness.md) drive reaches for a warmth that is no longer delivered, and separation stops raising the alarm that used to pull the persona back. Its counter-office is buffering: a secure bond can quiet the [dopamine](piece_dopamine.md) chase -- connection standing in where craving was -- and its comfort overlaps the [endorphin](piece_endorphin.md) relief, the two soothing systems that a person can lean on in place of one another. When the bond itself becomes the thing that must not be lost, this is the currency the compulsion is spending.

## Apparent

It wears the name of love -- "the bonding hormone," "the cuddle chemical." That is the warm surface. Underneath it is narrower and more exacting: a signal that binds to a _particular_ other and, in binding, draws the line between the one inside the tie and everyone outside it. The same molecule that settles the bond can sharpen the wall around it.

## Yearbook

It is the chemistry of pair-bonding and of birth and nursing -- the tie between mates in the species that keep one, the tie between parent and infant. It builds the attachment that a life is organised around and that its loss can unmake. Carried into adult love and dependence, it is what makes a connection load-bearing: the reason a bond can be needed past what it returns, and the reason its threatened loss is felt as an alarm out of all proportion to the fact of it.
