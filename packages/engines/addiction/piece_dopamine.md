# Piece: Dopamine

## Taxonomy

[Addiction](process_addiction.md): the currency the [craving](process_craving.md) drive spends.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: addiction

## Place

In the gap between the cue and the reward. Dopamine is not released where the reward lands but where it is _predicted_ -- along the pathway that lights when a signal says something worth having is near. It lives in the anticipation, the half-second before, the reach rather than the grasp; by the time the reward arrives the release has already moved to whatever predicts the next one.

## Load Bearing

Remove it and the pursuit loses its motor. Not the pleasure -- the _pull_: the seeking, the checking, the reaching that [craving](process_craving.md) is made of. What dopamine carries is the wanting, and wanting is separable from liking -- the enjoyment itself runs on [endorphin](piece_endorphin.md), the body's own opioid. In addiction the two come apart: the dopamine wanting sensitizes and grows while the opioid liking flattens, so the persona is driven hardest toward what pleases them least. That dissociation is the whole engine, and dopamine is the half that does not quit.

## Apparent

It wears the name of pleasure -- "the reward chemical," the molecule of joy. That is the costume. What it actually encodes is prediction and pursuit: the signal that a cue is worth chasing, sharpened when the payout is uncertain. Mistaken for the reward, it is only ever the promise of one.

## Yearbook

It began as the learning signal that pointed an organism at food, mate, safety -- marking what predicted survival so the body would go back for it. Every engineered reward since -- the machine, the feed, the drug -- borrows that same circuitry and pays it more reliably than the world ever did. Over a life the steady tone of [serotonin](piece_serotonin.md), the contentment of secure rank, is what would quiet the chase; as the chase runs, that counter-tone thins, and the reach is left with nothing to pull against.
