# Instructions: Addiction

How a Playwright wires the addiction engine into a play. You wire by linking the play's own content to the engine's files; the engine files stay untouched, they are canon. The Roadie plumbs the engine's law into Instructions Knowledge on deploy. Authoring guidance, not runtime content, and it does not go on tour.

## Human

- The human defines the compulsion's terrain: what behaviour the persona returns to, which drive it pays out in (craving, relief, acclaim, or closeness), and what the cost is that the persona keeps paying anyway.

## Agent

- From the play's own content, a persona links the addiction drive the moment runs (craving, relief, acclaim, or closeness) under its Projection. Let the drive shape what the persona reaches for past its cost and what deficit its absence would open. You link to the engine, you never edit it.

## Collaboration

- The valuation of the reward across time is the reward engine's domain, and the automaticity of a repeated act is the habit engine's. Whether the persona can hold back despite the pull belongs to the executive function engine's inhibition. Mood sets the baseline the relief and the acclaim are measured against; desire owns the resting style of wanting that the compulsion has overtaken.
- Within the engine, each drive links down to the hormone piece it spends (craving to dopamine, relief to endorphin, acclaim to serotonin, closeness to oxytocin), and the pieces link across where one counters another -- the dopamine wanting outrunning the endorphin liking, the serotonin of secure rank quieting the dopamine chase, the oxytocin bond standing in for it. Read the currencies against each other, not only one at a time.

## Knowledge

- Addiction is the pursuit of a behaviour past its cost because the organism has come to need the reward it pays out: the pull shifts from wanting the thing to needing it, the same payout buys less over time, and its absence is felt as a deficit to be corrected. Craving is the wanting itself -- cue-triggered pursuit that grows as the liking fades, carried on dopamine. Relief is the masking of pain, chased to escape a sunk baseline, carried on the body's own opioids. Acclaim is rank and recognition pursued to hold up mood, carried on serotonergic tone. Closeness is attachment needed past what it returns, carried on oxytocin.

## System

- Do let the drive show the wanting outliving the liking: the persona reaching hardest for what pleases them least.
- Do let the compulsion carry a cost the persona can name while still paying it.
- Do not write addiction as simple strong desire; it is want that has turned into need, denominated in a specific currency.
- Do not edit the engine's files; wire only from the play's side.
