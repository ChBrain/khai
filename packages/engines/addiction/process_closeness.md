# Process: Closeness

## Taxonomy

[Addiction](process_addiction.md), the drive this is one form of.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: addiction

## Initiated by

A bond is the source -- a person, a nearness, a belonging whose presence settles the persona and whose absence unsettles them.
The organism releases the currency of attachment in the connection, and it has learned where to find it.
The pull is toward the one who supplies the settling.

## Direction

Toward the connection, and toward keeping it, past the point it is returned.
The persona seeks the nearness -- contact, reassurance, the proof of the tie -- because the bond is where the settling lives and its loss is where the panic waits.
The aim is not the relationship's good but the persona's need of it.

## Lever

The currency is belonging, carried on [oxytocin](piece_oxytocin.md), released in the bond, and the tie has become load-bearing.
Connection settles the organism; its threat alarms it, and repetition makes the bond the thing that holds the persona level, so its withdrawal is felt as an alarm out of proportion to the loss.
The need can outgrow what the bond returns: the persona keeps spending toward a closeness that no longer settles them, because going without it is worse than the little it now gives.

## Echo

The persona clings to the connection past the point it serves them.
The bond that was reached for as warmth is now held onto against the panic of its absence; reassurance quiets the alarm briefly and the alarm returns.
Where the belonging has become a need rather than a want, the persona cannot let go of a tie whose cost they can name, because the letting go is the very absence the attachment was holding off.
