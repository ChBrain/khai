# Process: Addiction

## Taxonomy

Motivation / Reinforcement.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: addiction

## Initiated by

A behaviour has paid out in a reward currency the organism can produce -- the surge of pursuit, the masking of pain, the lift of standing, the warmth of a bond -- and the payout has been repeated until the organism has come to count on it.
The question is no longer whether the thing is worth its cost.
It is whether the persona can be without it, and the answer has started to be no.

## Direction

Toward the behaviour, again, at a cost the persona would not have paid at the start.
The pull no longer tracks how good the reward is; it tracks how bad its absence has become.
What began as moving toward a pleasure has become moving away from a lack, and the persona returns to the source whether or not it still delivers.

## Lever

The gap between wanting a thing and needing it.
Repetition recalibrates the organism: the currency that once arrived as a bonus becomes the baseline the persona must reach just to feel level, so the same payout buys less and the cost of going without climbs.
The system learns the cues that predict the currency and fires for them directly -- the wanting sensitizes and grows even as the liking flattens, so the persona is pulled hardest toward what pleases them least.

## Echo

The persona pursues the source past the point it makes sense to, and can name the cost while paying it.
Addiction is not weak want; it is want that has outlived its reward and turned into need, denominated in whichever currency the behaviour pays out.
The process continues as [craving](process_craving.md), [relief](process_relief.md), [acclaim](process_acclaim.md), or [closeness](process_closeness.md), each a distinct currency the compulsion is spent toward.
