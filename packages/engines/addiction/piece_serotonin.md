# Piece: Serotonin

## Taxonomy

[Addiction](process_addiction.md): the currency the [acclaim](process_acclaim.md) drive spends.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: addiction

## Place

In the tone of the whole system, not a single site. Serotonin sets a background level rather than a spike, and that level rides with social rank: it sits high in the one whose standing is secure and drops in the one who has just lost it. It lives less in a moment than in a mood -- the steadiness, or the lack of it, that the persona carries into the room.

## Load Bearing

Remove it and mood loses its peg. The lift that a win, a rank, a nod of regard delivers has nothing to land on, and the [acclaim](process_acclaim.md) drive is chasing a currency that no longer pays. Its other office is restraint: a secure serotonergic tone is what would steady the restless pull of [dopamine](piece_dopamine.md) -- contentment quiets the chase. Low, it does the reverse; a persona who has lost standing is both flatter in mood and more impulsive in pursuit, the counter-weight lifted off the reach.

## Apparent

It wears the name of happiness -- "the contentment chemical," the thing a good day is made of. That is the surface reading. Underneath it is closer to _status_: a signal of where the persona sits in the order, felt as mood, so that rising registers as wellbeing and falling as a threat that has not yet been named.

## Yearbook

It is old enough to predate the primate: in creatures across the tree of life its level tracks dominance, rising as an animal ascends and falling as it is put down. In the troop, the one at the top carries the highest tone, and losing the position lowers it within days. Carried into a world of endless comparison -- every rung visible, every rank contestable -- the peg that once settled at a stable place in a small group is left chasing a standing that never closes.
