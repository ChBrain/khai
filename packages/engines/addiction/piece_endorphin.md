# Piece: Endorphin

## Taxonomy

[Addiction](process_addiction.md): the currency the [relief](process_relief.md) drive spends.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: addiction

## Place

At the site of the hurt. Endorphin -- the body's endogenous opioid -- is released where pain is, in exertion, injury, and distress, binding the same receptors an outside opiate would take. It lives on the far side of effort and the underside of overwhelm: the calm that arrives once the strain has gone on long enough, the numbness that closes over a wound before the mind has caught up.

## Load Bearing

Remove it and pain has no floor. No relief, no runner's calm, no settling of raw feeling -- the [relief](process_relief.md) drive has nothing left to spend. It is also the liking that [dopamine](piece_dopamine.md) only points at: the wanting reaches for a pleasure, and the opioid is the pleasure it reaches for. When a behaviour is chased for the numbing rather than the gain, it is this currency being spent -- and because the body answers relief by lowering its own baseline, the floor keeps dropping out from under it.

## Apparent

It wears the name of a high -- the natural painkiller, the reward of the hard workout. That is the surface. Underneath it is subtraction, not addition: it does not raise the good, it lowers the bad, and a relief that only cancels a deficit is invisible until the deficit is all that is left.

## Yearbook

It began as the body's triage -- the analgesia that lets an injured animal keep moving, the calm that follows survived danger. The same system carries the comfort of touch and holding, so it shades into [oxytocin](piece_oxytocin.md)'s bond: to be soothed by a person and to be soothed by the body's own opioid are not far apart, and either can stand in for the other. Opiates from outside walk straight into this inheritance and pay it at a scale the body cannot match, and then cannot answer.
