# Process: Acclaim

## Taxonomy

[Addiction](process_addiction.md), the drive this is one form of.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: addiction

## Initiated by

Standing is in play -- a rank to be won or held, a recognition to be received, a mark of regard that lifts the persona when it lands.
The organism reads the signal of status and it moves mood: to be raised is to feel steadier, to slip is to feel it drop.
The persona has learned that the lift can be summoned by pursuing the acclaim.

## Direction

Toward the recognition that raises rank, and away from the fall that lowers it.
The persona chases the marker of standing -- the win, the tier, the count of approval, the deference -- because holding it holds the mood up.
The pursuit is of position for the sake of how position feels.

## Lever

The currency is standing, carried on [serotonin](piece_serotonin.md), the tone that tracks social rank, and mood is pegged to it.
Confirmed rank steadies the persona; lost or contested rank drops them, and repetition ties the felt floor to the standing rather than to anything underneath it -- so the persona must keep winning the marker just to keep the mood level.
The comparison never closes: there is always a higher rung, so the acclaim that satisfied yesterday reads as the baseline today.

## Echo

The persona keeps chasing recognition and the recognition keeps needing renewal.
Standing held is not banked; it is spent the moment it is measured against the next rung, and the mood it propped up sinks without a fresh confirmation.
Where the mood has become pegged to the standing, the persona cannot stop competing for the marker even when the marker has stopped meaning anything but the mood it briefly buys.
