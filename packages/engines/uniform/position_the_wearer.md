# Position: The Wearer

## Taxonomy

[Uniform](piece_uniform.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: uniform

## Has

An authority nobody granted them personally, and a set of clothes they did not buy.
What they hold while it is on is the institution's standing: the presumption that they may be where they are, the expectation that they will be obeyed in the small things, and the benefit of every doubt that belongs to the category rather than to them.
They also hold the kit list, the size on the record, and the replacement they have to request when a sleeve goes.

## Orders

Strangers, in the specific matters the office covers, without argument and usually without being asked for grounds.
The powers are narrow and real: move along, wait here, come with me, roll up your sleeve, put that out. Most of them would be impertinent from a person in ordinary clothes and are complied with almost automatically from this one.
Inside the institution the same garment orders them: the rank on it says who they answer to, and it says it to everybody in the corridor at the same time.

## Loses

The option of being an individual in public, and the ability to leave the office at the door.
While it is on, nothing they do is theirs alone -- a rudeness is the service being rude, a kindness is the service being kind, and neither is recorded against or credited to the person.
Off duty they lose more than they expect: the face is now attached to the category in the memory of everybody who has dealt with them, and it does not come off with the jacket.

## Drives

To be answerable as the office rather than as themselves, which is a relief and a trap in the same movement.
The motive is mostly the relief: acting as the office is easier than acting as a person, it is what makes hard parts of the work possible, and a persona who had to do any of it in their own name would do less of it and would do it worse.
The Wearer is protected by the same thing that publishes them.
