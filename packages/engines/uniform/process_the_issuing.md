# Process: The Issuing

## Taxonomy

[Uniform](piece_uniform.md), how it arrives and on whose terms.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: uniform

## Initiated by

The persona is measured and given clothing.
Two of these, three of those, a size that is nearly right, and a form to sign.

## Direction

Toward a wardrobe supplied by somebody else and answerable to them.
The garment is not a purchase and not a gift. It is on loan for the duration of the post: the institution decides the specification, the number issued, the replacement cycle, whether alterations are permitted, and what happens to it at the end.
The persona wears it more hours than anything they own and has almost no say over any of it.

## Lever

The practical necessity of standard issue against what standardisation removes.
The carrying force is that the standard is the point and personalising it would break it: recognisability requires sameness, safety specifications are not a matter of taste, an issued garment means nobody is spending their own money to be employable, and the persona is spared the daily question of what to wear; the resistance is that the one item of clothing they wear most is the one item over which they have no discretion, so the ordinary channel through which people say something about themselves is closed for the majority of their waking hours.
The Issuing dresses the persona in something that is not theirs.

## Echo

A wardrobe on loan, signed for.
It shows the ownership question that everything else in the engine follows from.
The Issuing is dated to the first day and is repeated at every size change.
