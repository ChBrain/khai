# Process: The Taking Off

## Taxonomy

[Uniform](piece_uniform.md), what does not come off with it.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: uniform

## Initiated by

The shift ends and the persona changes.
And is still, to everybody who saw them in it, the person who wears that.

## Direction

Toward a constraint that continues after the garment stops.
Institutions know the uniform travels and write rules for it: not to be worn in a bar, not on a demonstration, not in a photograph with a drink in the frame. The rules are an admission that the office does not switch off when the clothing does.
Beyond the rules, the town has seen the face. In a small place the persona is the officer, the teacher, the doctor, permanently and to people they have never spoken to, and ordinary private conduct is watched at a standard the office set.

## Lever

The reasonableness of expecting the office to be honoured against the hours it claims.
The carrying force is that the expectation is fair and the wearer usually shares it: an office worth having is worth not embarrassing, the public's willingness to obey the garment tomorrow depends on how the person in it behaved tonight, and a wearer who wanted the authority and refused the standard would be taking one half of a deal; the resistance is that the standard has no boundary in time or place, applies to family members by proximity, and is enforced by strangers who saw a photograph -- so the persona is on duty in the sense that matters, permanently.
The Taking Off removes the garment and none of the standard.

## Echo

Ordinary clothes on somebody who is still recognisable.
It shows what the licence cost, on a bill presented in a supermarket.
The Taking Off is dated to the end of the shift and does not complete.
