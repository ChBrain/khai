# Process: The Wearing

## Taxonomy

[Uniform](piece_uniform.md), what it does while it is on.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: uniform

## Initiated by

The persona goes out in it and is dealt with differently.
Immediately, by everybody, including people who know them.

## Direction

Toward an exchange conducted between a public and an office.
Strangers approach the garment. They ask it questions, obey it, complain to it, and occasionally shout at it, and they do all of that without any information about the person inside beyond what the category supplies.
The wearer answers as the office, which is what everybody in the exchange wants: the member of the public needs a function, not an acquaintance, and the wearer needs not to be personally at stake in a difficult five minutes.

## Lever

The licence the office confers against the disappearance of the person holding it.
The carrying force is that the substitution works for both sides and most of the work would be impossible without it: the wearer can do things -- give an instruction, refuse an entry, deliver bad news -- that no private person could do to a stranger, the public gets somebody accountable rather than somebody unknown, and the persona is protected from having their own character be the subject of every encounter; the resistance is that the protection runs in both directions, so praise and abuse both land on the category, and a persona can complete a whole shift of demanding human contact without one exchange in which they were present as themselves.
The Wearing supplies an authority and withdraws a person.

## Echo

A day of encounters with somebody who was never introduced.
It shows the licence and the erasure arriving in the same garment.
The Wearing is dated to the shift and is switched on the moment it is buttoned.
