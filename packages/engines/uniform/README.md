# Uniform

Uniform as piece: the garment that replaces a person with an office, read on what it licensed and what it costs -- not the stripping of a self at the door of a closed institution, which is total-institution's, and not the front a persona manages for an audience, which is presentation's. The object is the piece; the members are what happens around it. One position names who is in it: a wearer holding an authority nobody granted them personally and a wardrobe they did not buy, who may order strangers in the narrow matters the office covers, who loses the option of being an individual in public, and who is protected by the same thing that publishes them. Three processes name what it does: the issuing, which dresses the persona in something that is not theirs and closes the ordinary channel through which people say something about themselves; the wearing, which supplies an authority and withdraws a person, since praise and abuse both land on the category; and the taking off, which removes the garment and none of the standard. The twist: a uniform lets its wearer do what they could not and stops them doing what they could.
