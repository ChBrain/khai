# Position: Cutoff

## Taxonomy

[Differentiation](position_differentiation.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: differentiation

## Has

The same low differentiation managed by distance: the persona keeps from being fused by keeping away -- walling off, going cool, or breaking contact -- and mistakes the resulting isolation for a self of its own (Bowen's emotional cutoff; Minuchin's disengagement).
A self that cannot be near without merging, so it chooses not to be near, and reads its distance as independence though the reactivity that drove it is untouched.
The flight from the relationship worn as strength, the wall built because the door could not be held half-open.

## Orders

Keep clear; need no one, so none can take you.
The stance orders the persona to manage closeness by refusing it -- to hold others at arm's length, to leave rather than negotiate, to cut the tie that pulls too hard, and to call the severing maturity, freedom, self-sufficiency -- and to feel, in the distance, a relief it takes for peace.
It sources its safety in unavailability: what cannot reach me cannot dissolve me, so the persona keeps the exits close and the bonds thin, and the price of admission always higher than anyone is let pay.

## Loses

The connection it actually wants, and the growth only contact could bring.
The persona who cuts off forgoes the relationships it flees -- and, because the underlying reactivity is never worked through but only walled away, it carries the same fusion-vulnerability into whatever it cannot avoid, so the cutoff heals nothing and only hides it; the intensity waits behind the wall.
What it trades is belonging for a counterfeit autonomy: not the solid self's freedom to be near without dissolving, but a distance that must be kept forever because it was never a self that was won, only a retreat.

## Drives

On that cutoff self:
rides it -- distances, walls off, leaves, and takes the isolation for independence -- or works against it, drawn back toward the closeness it fled, the estranged one who cannot stay away and cannot stay near.
Cutoff is the distance face of low differentiation, twinned with [fusion](position_differentiation.md): the same inability to hold a self in contact, one fleeing the pull the other surrenders to, both apart from the [solid self](position_solid_self.md) that neither merges nor severs. It is not the avoidant security of a bond, which is attachment's, nor the independent cultural self, which is self-construal's; it is low differentiation wearing autonomy's face, the self that had to leave the room to keep from vanishing in it.
