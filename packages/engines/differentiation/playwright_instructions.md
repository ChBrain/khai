# Instructions: Differentiation

How a Playwright wires the differentiation engine into a play. You wire by linking the play's own content to the engine's files; the engine files stay untouched, they are canon. The Roadie plumbs the engine's law into Instructions Knowledge on deploy. Authoring guidance, not runtime content, and it does not go on tour.

## Human

- The human sets each persona's differentiation: how strongly it can hold its own self under the pull of the people it is close to -- whether it fuses into them, cuts off from them, or holds a solid self among them -- as authored character architecture, never a diagnosis of a real person.

## Agent

- From the play's own content, a persona links under Projection the differentiation it runs on: fusion, the self dissolved into the relationship, reactive, unable to hold a position the beloved opposes; cutoff, the flight from closeness walled off and mistaken for autonomy; or solid self, its own thinking held while it stays in genuine contact. Read it in how the persona meets closeness under pressure -- who surrenders a position to keep the peace, who leaves rather than negotiate, who can disagree with a loved one and stay warm. Keep it the capacity to hold a self, not the cultural weighting of I against we (self-construal), the bond's security (attachment), or the three-party bind it drives (triangulation). You link to the engine, you never edit it.

## Collaboration

- The cultural boundary of the self is self-construal's; the security of the bond is attachment's; the three-party structure low differentiation drives is the triangulation engine's; the present self-story is identity's. Differentiation is the persona-side sibling of triangulation in the family-systems tradition -- the capacity whose weakness makes a persona fuse, cut off, and triangle. Whether the differentiation rings true to the persona, and stays authored architecture rather than diagnosis, is the team's review.

## Knowledge

- Differentiation is a persona's resting capacity to hold a self -- its own thinking, principles, and course -- under relational and emotional pressure (Bowen's differentiation of self, the central variable of family-systems theory). It wires on a persona at Projection, and it is the strength of the self among the people it is close to. Three stances carry it -- fusion is the self dissolved into the relationship, the togetherness pole, a borrowed pseudo-self that takes its feelings and worth from others and cannot hold a position they oppose (Bowen's fusion, Minuchin's enmeshment); cutoff is the same low differentiation managed by distance, the flight from closeness walled off and mistaken for autonomy though the reactivity that drove it remains (Bowen's emotional cutoff, Minuchin's disengagement); solid self is the differentiated pole, the self held within the tie, close without fusing and separate without cutting off, its own thinking kept under pressure. Read a persona by which stance it holds, and read the low pole two ways: fusion and cutoff are two faces of one condition, not opposites -- the one who merged and the one who fled are equally unable to be a self in the room.

## System

- Do give each persona a differentiation under Projection; do read it in conduct under relational pressure -- the surrender of a position, the flight from contact, the line held while warmth is kept -- shown, not narrated.
- Do read fusion and cutoff as two faces of the same low differentiation, not as opposites: the cutoff persona is not more differentiated than the fused one, only managing the same weakness by distance instead of merger.
- Do distinguish the solid self from the cutoff that mimics it: the solid self stays in contact while holding its own; the cutoff self holds its distance because it cannot stay in contact and hold its own at once.
- Do not model the cultural I/we self-boundary (self-construal), the bond's security (attachment), the three-party bind (triangulation), or the self-story (identity). Do not cast differentiation as a diagnosis of a real person or family; it is authored character architecture. Do not edit the engine's files; wire only from the play's side.
