# Differentiation: Reference

## Line of Work

Differentiation as **position**: a persona's resting capacity to hold a self -- its own
thinking, principles, and course -- under relational and emotional pressure, taken across
the stances a persona rests in on that axis. It wires on a persona at Projection, the
resting self the persona brings to every close relationship, because differentiation is a
standing property of the self, read before any particular quarrel. The domain does not model
where a persona draws its self-boundary as a cultural default, which is the self-construal
engine's; nor the security of the bond itself, which is attachment's; nor the three-party
structure that low differentiation drives, which is triangulation's; nor the present
self-story, which is identity's. It models how strongly a persona can be a self among the
people it is close to.

The spine is Bowen's differentiation of self, the central variable of his family-systems
theory. Bowen set a scale from fusion to differentiation: at the low end, a person's
intellectual and emotional systems are merged and the self is fused with the people around
it -- reactive, unable to hold a position under the group's pull, its self borrowed and
negotiable (a pseudo-self); at the high end, a solid self keeps its own thinking and course
while staying in emotional contact, able to be close without merging and separate without
severing. His crucial observation is that the two low-end presentations are one condition,
not opposites: fusion (seeking togetherness, dissolving into the other) and emotional cutoff
(fleeing closeness, walling off) are both low differentiation, the second a way of managing
the first, so the one who merged and the one who fled are equally unable to be a self in the
room. Minuchin, mapping the family's boundaries, named the same continuum in structural
terms -- the enmeshed family whose members are over-involved, and the disengaged family whose
members are walled from each other -- and Kerr systematized the scale. So the engine gives
differentiation three stances -- fusion, cutoff, and the solid self -- the low pole's two
faces and the high pole they fall short of.

## Restrictions

What the engine refuses to model, and to whom it delegates.

- **Self-construal**: The engine models the strength of the self under pressure, not the
  cultural boundary of the self. Where a persona draws the line around its self as a default --
  independent (defined from within) or interdependent (defined through ties), both healthy
  cultural orientations (Markus & Kitayama) -- is the self-construal engine's; differentiation
  is a different axis of a different lineage: the Bowenian capacity to hold that self under
  relational anxiety. The two are orthogonal -- an independent self can be poorly differentiated
  (cut off, reactive) and an interdependent self highly differentiated (connected and solid) --
  and must not be read as the same. Self-construal is where the boundary sits; differentiation
  is how well the self behind it holds.
- **Attachment**: The engine models the self's autonomy-within-connection, not the bond's
  security. How a persona seeks or gives up on closeness to a secure base under threat --
  secure, anxious, avoidant, disorganized -- is the attachment engine's; the two run parallel
  (anxious near fusion, avoidant near cutoff) but differ: attachment is the security of the tie,
  differentiation the strength of the self within it. A bond can be secure while the self is
  poorly differentiated.
- **Triangulation**: The engine models the persona's capacity, not the three-party structure
  that capacity drives. When a two-person tension recruits a third to hold it, the structure is
  the triangulation engine's (a plot); differentiation is the persona-side property -- low
  differentiation is what makes a persona fuse, cut off, and triangle -- but the triangle itself
  is cast on the plot, not read on the person.
- **Identity and the self-story**: The engine models a capacity, not a content. The present
  self-narrative and its coherence are the identity engine's; differentiation is not what the
  self says it is but how firmly it can hold whatever that is under pressure.
- **The stance itself is not engine content, and it is not a diagnosis**: The engine declares
  the three stances exist; which one a persona holds lives on that persona, under Projection. It
  is authored persona-architecture for a play -- fusion, cutoff, and the solid self as
  dispositions a character runs, in the register of temperament -- never a diagnostic instrument
  or a claim about a real person or family.

## Encoding

Source to constraint, per file.

- **[differentiation](position_differentiation.md)** (the anchor): a persona's resting
  capacity to hold its own self under relational pressure -- the scale from fusion to the solid
  self. Anchored by Bowen (differentiation of self) and Kerr (the scale).
- **[fusion](position_fusion.md)** (stance): the self dissolved into the relationship -- the
  togetherness pole, the borrowed pseudo-self, emotional reactivity, the inability to hold a
  position the beloved opposes. Anchored by Bowen (fusion) and Minuchin (enmeshment).
- **[cutoff](position_cutoff.md)** (stance): the same low differentiation managed by distance --
  the flight from closeness, walled off, mistaken for autonomy though the reactivity remains.
  Anchored by Bowen (emotional cutoff) and Minuchin (disengagement).
- **[solid-self](position_solid_self.md)** (stance): the differentiated pole -- the self held
  within the tie, close without fusing and separate without cutting off, its own thinking kept
  under pressure. Anchored by Bowen (the solid self versus the pseudo-self).
