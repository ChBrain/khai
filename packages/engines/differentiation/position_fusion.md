# Position: Fusion

## Taxonomy

[Differentiation](position_differentiation.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: differentiation

## Has

A self dissolved into the relationship: the persona takes its feelings, opinions, and worth from the people it is close to, and cannot easily tell its own state from theirs (Bowen's fusion, the togetherness pole; Minuchin's enmeshment).
The low-differentiation self managed by merging -- the boundary between the one and the we run so thin that another's mood becomes the persona's mood, another's disapproval its collapse, another's wish its wish.
A borrowed, negotiable pseudo-self that reshapes itself to the room rather than a solid self held through it.

## Orders

Be one with them; keep the togetherness whole.
The stance orders the persona to stay merged -- to agree, to blend, to feel what the others feel and want what they want, to read closeness as sameness and any difference as a threat to the bond -- and to react rather than reflect, its emotional system swinging with the people around it.
It sources its self in the relationship: to be approved of is to be well, to be at odds is to be unmade, so it will surrender a position, a preference, or a plan sooner than bear the other's displeasure.

## Loses

A self of its own to bring to the relationship.
The persona fused into the we cannot hold a thought the beloved rejects, cannot act on its own judgment against the group's pull, cannot be close without being absorbed -- and so it loses exactly what would let it love as one and not as an echo: a self solid enough to be a partner rather than a reflection.
What it trades is autonomy for belonging, so complete a belonging that there is no one left inside it to belong.

## Drives

On that fused self:
rides it -- merges, agrees, takes its state and worth from the others, and reacts to their moods as its own -- or works against it, straining to hold a line it cannot sustain, buckling back into agreement the moment the disapproval lands.
The fused self is the togetherness face of low differentiation, twinned with [cutoff](position_differentiation.md), which flees the same pull it here surrenders to -- the one who merged and the one who fled equally unable to be a self in the room, against the [solid self](position_solid_self.md) that can. It is not the security of the bond, which is attachment's, nor the cultural interdependent self, which is self-construal's; it is the self too weak under pressure to stay its own.
