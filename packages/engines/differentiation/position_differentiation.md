# Position: Differentiation

## Taxonomy

Self / a persona's capacity to hold its own self under relational pressure (no khai file of its own).

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: differentiation

## Has

A resting capacity to hold a self -- its own thinking, principles, and course -- while staying in contact with people who press it to merge, agree, or react.
A read on a single axis: how far the persona can be an "I" inside a "we," keeping its own position when those it is bound to want it fused with them or fear it lost to them (Bowen's differentiation of self).
The strength of the self under emotional pressure that the persona brings to every close relationship, before any particular quarrel or crisis.

## Orders

Hold your self among the people who matter to you -- or lose it to them, or flee them to keep it.
The capacity tilts how the persona meets closeness: whether it can stay itself in contact, or dissolves into the other and takes its self from the relationship, or manages the pull to dissolve by distance and cutoff -- and how much of its energy the holding, the merging, or the fleeing costs.
The room reads the differentiation beneath the conduct: who can disagree and stay warm, who cannot hold a line the beloved opposes, who keeps the peace by keeping away.

## Loses

The option of a self that costs nothing to hold.
Low on the axis, the persona forgoes a self of its own -- fused into the we or walled off from it, unable to be near without merging or separate without severing; high on the axis, it forgoes the ease of belonging without remainder, paying in the effort and the loneliness of holding its own thinking against people who would rather it did not.
What no stance escapes is the pull between the self and the tie: to keep the one always taxes the other.

## Drives

On that capacity to hold a self:
rides it -- the solid self keeping its own course in contact, or the low-differentiated persona fusing or cutting off under the pull -- or works against it, the fused one straining to hold a line it cannot sustain, the cutoff one drawn back toward the closeness it fled.
The baseline holds underneath either way, in one of three stances: [fusion](position_fusion.md), the self dissolved into the we; [cutoff](position_cutoff.md), the flight from closeness mistaken for autonomy; or [solid self](position_solid_self.md), the self held within the tie. Fusion and cutoff are two faces of the same low differentiation, not opposites. The three-party bind that low differentiation drives is the triangulation engine's; the cultural weighting of I against we is self-construal's; the security of the bond itself is attachment's; the present self-story is identity's. This engine owns how strongly a persona can be a self under the pressure of the people it is close to. It is authored persona-architecture for a play, never a diagnosis of a real person.
