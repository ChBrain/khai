# Position: The Outsider

## Taxonomy

[Politics](process_politics.md), the footing that is the absence of the others.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: politics

## Has

No seat, no bloc, no veto, and nobody to speak for. The persona is affected by what the body decides and is not part of how it decides, which is the ordinary condition of most people in relation to most bodies that govern them.

## Orders

Change the size of the room. [Scope-shifting](process_scope_shifting.md) is the one lever this footing carries, and it carries it fully: a persona with nothing inside can still make the fight bigger than the room, and a fight that has left the room is being decided by an audience the insiders did not select.

## Loses

Everything that works on the inside, and the presumption of good faith. A persona with no standing cannot agenda-set, cannot trade, cannot stall, and cannot vote; and when they widen the scope they will be described as going outside the proper channels, which is accurate and is also the point, since the proper channels are what they do not have.

## Drives

On the loss of the inside:
widens the conflict until the body must answer to somebody it does not control, and is sometimes given a seat to stop them doing it again,
or is contained -- ignored, discredited, or answered privately -- and the fight stays the size the insiders chose.
