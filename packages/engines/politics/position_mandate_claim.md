# Position: The Mandate

## Taxonomy

[Politics](process_politics.md), the footing that claims to speak for people who are not in the room.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: politics

## Has

A constituency invoked. The members, the staff, the residents, the customers, the country: the persona speaks not as themselves but on behalf, and the weight of the claim is the weight of the people named rather than of the persona making it.

## Orders

Speak in the plural and stay unfalsifiable. The footing works by keeping the represented at exactly the right distance -- present enough to lend their weight, absent enough that nobody in the room can check what they actually think.

## Loses

The ability to be personally convinced. A persona speaking for others cannot concede a point in their own name, cannot be argued round without appearing to have sold out those they speak for, and cannot compromise beyond what they can carry back -- so the mandate that gives them standing also takes away their freedom to move, and is often invoked for exactly that reason.

## Drives

On the loss of personal movement:
holds the line and is respected as somebody who cannot simply be bought,
or is contradicted by the people they claim -- who turn out to want something else, or to have been consulted by somebody else -- and the footing collapses in a single sentence.
