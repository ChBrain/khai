# Process: Scope-Shifting

## Taxonomy

[Politics](process_politics.md), the lever that changes who is watching.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: politics

## Initiated by

A contest is being lost among the people currently in it. Schattschneider's observation is that every fight has an audience, that the audience decides most fights, and that the surest way to change a result is to change how many people are involved.

## Direction

Outward for the loser, inward for the winner. The party losing a private fight makes it public -- takes it to the wider body, the membership, the press, the street -- because a new audience brings new sympathies and the previous balance dissolves. The party winning does the reverse: keeps it technical, keeps it in committee, keeps it between the two of us, and settles it before anybody else has cause to look.

## Lever

The size of a fight is not fixed by the fight. It is set by whether somebody with an interest in changing it can reach anybody else, which is why containment is the standing work of every winning side and why a persona with no other footing still has this one.

## Echo

The same disagreement, in a room of a different size, coming out differently. Nobody's view changed; the set of people whose views counted did.
