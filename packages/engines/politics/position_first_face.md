# Position: The Open Contest

## Taxonomy

[Politics](process_politics.md), the first face: the contest that is visible, on a question already on the table.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: politics

## Has

A question tabled, sides taken, and a rule that will settle it. Everybody can see what is being decided and roughly who is for what. This is the face political life is usually pictured at, and it is the only one that leaves a record of itself: a vote, a minute, a count, an argument anybody could go back and read.

## Orders

Get the numbers. Where the question is fixed and the rule is fixed, the persona's task is arithmetic and assembly -- find who agrees, find who can be brought, find what the waverers need, and be holding a majority when the rule is applied.

## Loses

Everything upstream. A persona working only at this face takes the question as given, which means they are playing on a board somebody else laid out, and they will win or lose on the merits of a choice they did not get to define. The face is honest and it is shallow.

## Drives

On the loss of the upstream:
fights well and openly, winning what can be won by numbers on questions as they arrive,
or notices that the questions keep arriving in a shape that suits somebody, and starts working at the face above.
