# Process: Stalling

## Taxonomy

[Politics](process_politics.md), the lever that spends the clock instead of the argument.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: politics

## Initiated by

A persona cannot win a question and does not need to. Sessions end, terms expire, budgets close, attention moves, and a decision not taken by a date is often a decision taken.

## Direction

Toward the calendar rather than the vote. Refer it for study, request the fuller paper, take it in parts, seek the further legal opinion, hold the floor, or simply let the item sit below the line until the sitting rises. None of these is a refusal, each is individually reasonable, and their sum is a no that nobody cast.

## Lever

Bodies have less time than business and cannot admit it. Every procedure that protects deliberation -- notice periods, quorum, the right to be heard, the referral -- is also a way to spend time, so the lever is built from the body's own safeguards and cannot be removed without removing them.

## Echo

A question that did not fail and was not carried. There is nothing on the record to appeal, nobody to hold responsible, and by the time it returns the situation that made it urgent has usually changed.
