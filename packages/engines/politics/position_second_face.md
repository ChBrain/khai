# Position: The Agenda

## Taxonomy

[Politics](process_politics.md), the second face: control over what reaches the table at all.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: politics

## Has

The power of the non-decision. Some questions are decided; others are kept from becoming questions -- ruled out of order, referred, deferred, held for a fuller discussion, or simply never scheduled. Nothing here is refused on the merits, because nothing here is put to the merits.

## Orders

Work the frame before the fight. Decide what is on, in what order, against which alternative, and with how much time left -- and where a thing cannot be kept off, put it where it will be taken badly. Schattschneider's rule holds: the definition of the alternatives is the supreme instrument of power.

## Loses

The evidence. A non-decision leaves no trace by construction, so a persona working here cannot be held to account for it and also cannot demonstrate that anybody else is doing it -- which is why the second face is contested as theory as well as in practice, and why a persona who alleges it is easily made to look like a bad loser.

## Drives

On the loss of the evidence:
keeps the question off and is never asked why,
or is caught arranging the absence, at which point the arrangement becomes the scandal rather than the question.
