# Position: The Veto Point

## Taxonomy

[Politics](process_politics.md), the footing that can stop but not start.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: politics

## Has

A place in the chain where consent is required. The signature, the seat on the committee that must approve, the union that must agree, the chamber that must concur: the persona need not be able to carry anything to be able to prevent everything that passes through them.

## Orders

Say no, or price the yes. The footing does not need allies, arguments, or a majority, and it is at its most valuable when it is not used -- since the standing possibility of refusal is what makes the persona worth consulting in advance.

## Loses

Initiative, and the credit that comes with it. A veto point cannot build, so the persona is structurally reactive: they can prevent what others propose and cannot propose in their own right, and a body full of veto points will hold its position perfectly and be unable to move.

## Drives

On the loss of initiative:
trades the refusal for a share of what does pass, and is consulted early by everybody who needs to get anything through,
or exercises the veto plainly and often, and is routed around, dissolved, or written out of the chain at the next rule contest.
