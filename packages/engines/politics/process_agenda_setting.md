# Process: Agenda-Setting

## Taxonomy

[Politics](process_politics.md), the lever that decides what is voted on and in what order.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: politics

## Initiated by

A body has more business than time, and somebody has to say what comes first. The persona who holds the pen, chairs the meeting, drafts the motion, or controls the order paper has been handed the strongest lever in the engine and often does not think of it as one.

## Direction

Toward an order of business that produces the wanted result from unchanged preferences. Where a question has more than one dimension, a sequence of pairwise votes can be arranged so that each step is genuinely preferred by the body and the destination is whatever the setter chose -- McKelvey's result, and the reason agenda control is not a housekeeping matter. Lesser versions do most of the work in practice: putting a rival's item last, pairing a strong proposal against a stronger one first, or scheduling a decision for a session the opposition cannot attend.

## Lever

Order is invisible as a choice. A body will contest a motion fiercely and accept the order of motions without comment, because the order looks administrative and somebody has to decide it. The lever costs nothing, requires no allies, and is rarely recognised as a move even by those it defeats.

## Echo

A decision the body reached by preferring, at every step, what it actually preferred -- and would not have reached in a different order. The record shows a series of legitimate choices, because that is what it was.
