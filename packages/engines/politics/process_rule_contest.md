# Process: The Rule Contest

## Taxonomy

[Politics](process_politics.md), the lever that fights over the procedure rather than the question.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: politics

## Initiated by

A persona sees that the rule will decide the outcome, and that the rule is a text somebody wrote. Thresholds, quorums, who may propose, what counts as carried, who is eligible, and what may be reopened are all settled somewhere, and all of them can be argued about.

## Direction

Toward a procedure under which the persona's preferences win. This runs from the small and immediate -- a point of order, a challenge to the chair, a question about eligibility -- up to the constitutional, where the rule for changing the rule is itself the prize. The move is usually made in the language of fairness and consistency, and often sincerely, since a persona rarely has to lie about a procedure to find one that suits them.

## Lever

Procedure has to be argued in general terms and applies in particular ones. A persona proposing a threshold cannot propose it only for this vote, so every rule change is made under a veil and taken by whoever guesses best about the votes to come -- which is what keeps the contest honest enough to be worth having and what makes losing it expensive for a long time.

## Echo

A settled question about how questions are settled, which will decide contests nobody has thought of yet. This is the deepest of the levers, the slowest, and the only one whose winner keeps winning after they have stopped paying attention.
