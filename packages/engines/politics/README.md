# Politics

Politics as process: how a persona gets an outcome out of a body that decides collectively, where the rule for deciding is itself in play -- across the three faces a contest can sit at (the open contest, the agenda, the settled premise), the seven levers that work on the situation rather than on anybody's mind (agenda-setting, dimension-splitting, scope-shifting, coalition-building, logrolling, stalling, the rule contest), and the five footings that make a lever bite (the veto point, the broker seat, the bloc, the mandate, the outsider) -- with the twist that the most complete victory produces the least visible activity (Dahl; Bachrach & Baratz; Lukes; Schattschneider; Riker).
