# Process: Coalition-Building

## Taxonomy

[Politics](process_politics.md), the lever that assembles enough, and no more than enough.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: politics

## Initiated by

The rule requires a threshold and the persona is below it. Others want things too, and some of those things are compatible with the persona's.

## Direction

Toward a bloc that clears the threshold and stops. Riker's size principle is the counter-intuitive half: a rational coalition-builder aims at the minimum winning coalition rather than the largest one, because the spoils are divided among the members and every surplus partner is a partner whose price was paid for nothing. The instinct to gather everybody is an instinct to weaken one's own share.

## Lever

Winning is a threshold and not a quantity. Above it, additional support buys nothing that the rule recognises; below it, all support is worthless. So the whole art is in the counting, and the persona who miscounts either loses or overpays.

## Echo

A majority that is narrower than it could have been, and more expensive to hold than it looks. Minimum coalitions win and are fragile by construction, since every member is pivotal and every member knows it.
