# Process: Dimension-Splitting

## Taxonomy

[Politics](process_politics.md), the lever that introduces a second question so a new majority exists.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: politics

## Initiated by

A persona is losing on the question as posed, and cannot win it by argument or by numbers. The majority against them is stable on that one dimension, so the dimension has to change.

## Direction

Toward a cross-cutting question that splits the winning side. The persona finds an issue on which the opposing coalition is divided and their own side is not, and gets it into the room -- attached to the motion, raised as a principle, or made the price of support. The old majority was a majority about one thing; asked about two, it is no longer the same set of people.

## Lever

Coalitions are held together by agreement on the question in front of them, not by agreement in general. Riker's heresthetic point is that the persona need persuade nobody: every member of the split coalition keeps exactly the views they came in with, and the arithmetic changes because the question did.

## Echo

A different majority, assembled out of the same people. The side that was winning is now arguing with itself about something it had not intended to discuss, and the original question is either carried or has become somebody else's problem.
