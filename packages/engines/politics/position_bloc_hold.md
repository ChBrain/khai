# Position: The Bloc

## Taxonomy

[Politics](process_politics.md), the footing that can deliver others.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: politics

## Has

A reliable set. The persona can say how a number of votes will go and be right, which converts them from a participant into a quantity -- and a quantity is what every coalition-builder is counting.

## Orders

Deliver, and be seen to. The footing is maintained by never being wrong about one's own people, which means the persona must know what the bloc will not wear and must spend real effort holding it, since a bloc that fails to deliver once is worth much less than the sum of its members.

## Loses

Freedom of movement. A persona who commands a bloc is also commanded by it: they can go only where it will follow, must justify each turn to it, and are answerable for outcomes their members did not choose -- so the same thing that makes them worth dealing with is what narrows what they can agree to.

## Drives

On the loss of freedom:
trades the bloc's weight for a share of the outcome and holds its members' loyalty by returning something,
or takes a position the bloc will not follow, discovers the votes were never theirs, and is left claiming a quantity they cannot produce.
