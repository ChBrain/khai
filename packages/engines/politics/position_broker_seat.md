# Position: The Broker Seat

## Taxonomy

[Politics](process_politics.md), the footing that sits between blocs neither of which can reach the other directly.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: politics

## Has

A position in the middle of a gap. The persona commands nothing and is needed by everybody, because they are the point through which two sides that do not talk can be brought to an arrangement -- the go-between, the pivot, the small party neither large party can govern without.

## Orders

Keep the gap open and stay in it. The broker's value is exactly the distance between the parties, so the footing is worked by carrying, translating, and reassuring, and never by closing the distance so completely that the two find they can deal directly.

## Loses

Any position of its own. A broker who is known to want a particular outcome stops being trusted by the side it disfavours and ceases to be a broker; so the footing costs the persona the ability to be openly for anything, and pays them in access instead.

## Drives

On the loss of a position:
extracts a price from both sides for a service both need, and is indispensable for exactly as long as the gap lasts,
or is found to have a preference, or the gap closes, and the seat disappears without anybody having to remove them from it.
