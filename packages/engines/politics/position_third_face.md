# Position: The Settled Premise

## Taxonomy

[Politics](process_politics.md), the third face and the engine's twist: what nobody raises because nobody has a way to want it raised.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: politics

## Has

A settled world. Here there is no grievance, no suppressed question, and no unhappy party keeping quiet -- there is a body in which the thing simply is not at issue, and everyone in it would say so honestly if asked. What has been arranged is not the outcome and not the agenda but the sense of what is normal, possible, and worth wanting.

## Orders

Arrange the premise and then do nothing. A persona who has won here has no further work: they need not attend, need not vote, need not intervene, and would look faintly ridiculous if they did.

## Loses

Visibility, and with it the possibility of being resisted or credited. Nobody can oppose what is not a question, which is the face's whole strength -- and nobody can be shown to have arranged it either, which is why the third face is the most contested claim in the literature: to say a persona's wants are not their own requires a standard of real interests, and whoever supplies that standard is doing something rather like the thing they are objecting to.

## Drives

On the loss of visibility:
holds the premise, and the body's quiet reads to everyone in it as consent, including to the persona who arranged it,
or the premise is named out loud by somebody with nothing to lose, and becomes, for the first time, an ordinary question at the face below.
