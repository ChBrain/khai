# Process: Logrolling

## Taxonomy

[Politics](process_politics.md), the lever that trades support across questions.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: politics

## Initiated by

Two personas each care intensely about a different question and hardly at all about the other's. A vote counts the same either way, which means each of them holds something cheap to give and dear to receive.

## Direction

Toward an exchange of votes rather than of arguments. I will back yours, you will back mine, and neither of us has to think the other is right. Where the trade is made openly it is a bargain; where it is made by attaching both items to one motion it is a package, and the body votes on a thing no member would have chosen alone.

## Lever

A counting rule is blind to intensity. It records that a persona was for or against and not how much they minded, so the difference between mild preference and burning conviction is available to be traded and is invisible in the result. This is the lever's defence -- it lets a body register intensity that its rule ignores -- and its indictment, since a package can carry items a majority would reject one by one.

## Echo

Two outcomes, each supported by a majority that did not want it, delivered by a majority that wanted something else. The record shows two carried motions and no trade.
