# Process: Grudge

## Taxonomy

[Schadenfreude](process_schadenfreude.md), the antipathy form.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: schadenfreude

## Initiated by

The persona sees a disliked other fall -- a rival, an enemy, a hated group defeated -- and takes pleasure in it for its own sake, needing neither justice nor envy to license the joy.
This is grudge: schadenfreude from antipathy, the misfortune of one we are against enjoyed simply because we are against them.
The dislike is enough; the fall of the disfavored is sweet without further reason.

## Direction

Toward the disliked's harm, enjoyed as the setback of one's side's opponent.
Grudge reads the misfortune through allegiance: the target is an antagonist -- personally resented or a rival group -- and any harm to them registers as a gain for the persona or their side, so the fall is enjoyed as a win in a standing opposition, sharpest between groups where the rival's every loss is one's own gain.
The direction is partisan: not the balancing of comeuppance nor the relief of leveling, but the plain satisfaction of an opponent undone.

## Lever

The antipathy toward the target, spent as partisan pleasure.
Grudge runs on prior hostility: because the persona already stands against the target, the target's misfortune needs no separate justification to please -- the dislike does the licensing, and the pleasure follows the enmity, deepened when the target is a rival group and their loss the in-group's gain.
The lever is the standing opposition: the enemy's harm is the ally's good, so a fall that would be pitiable in a friend is enjoyable in a foe.

## Echo

The opponent is undone and the persona's side is gladdened, the fall enjoyed as a partisan win.
It is the least defensible and most tribal schadenfreude -- the reason a rival team's injury draws cheers, a hated figure's disgrace delights, a rival group's failure lights the reward of one's own -- the pleasure of an antagonist's harm, worn most openly among fellow partisans and hidden most from the target.
It is [comeuppance](process_comeuppance.md) without the desert and [leveling](process_leveling.md) without the envy, the naked enjoyment of a disliked other's loss. The between-group enmity it feeds on is the intergroup composite's, and the active wish to inflict the harm is the aggression engine's; this owns the pleasure taken in a disliked other's misfortune itself.
