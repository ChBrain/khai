# Process: Comeuppance

## Taxonomy

[Schadenfreude](process_schadenfreude.md), the justice form.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: schadenfreude

## Initiated by

The persona sees a deserved misfortune land -- the cheat caught, the arrogant humbled, the hypocrite exposed -- and takes pleasure in it as justice done.
This is comeuppance: schadenfreude licensed by desert, the fall enjoyed because it was earned.
The joy feels not petty but righteous, the world set briefly right.

## Direction

Toward the deserved fall, enjoyed as the balancing of an account.
Comeuppance reads the misfortune against what the target had coming: a wrong committed, an unfair advantage, an unearned height, and when the fall answers the wrong, the persona feels the satisfaction of a debt paid -- the comeuppance not merely a loss but a correction.
The direction is toward restored balance: the pleasure tracks the fit between the misfortune and the desert, sharpest when the punishment mirrors the crime.

## Lever

The deservingness of the fall, spent as righteous pleasure.
Comeuppance runs on a judgment of desert: the persona appraises the target as having merited misfortune, and that appraisal converts the fall from a pitiable loss into a satisfying settlement, the same event drawing sympathy for the innocent and pleasure for the guilty.
The lever is the perceived justice: because the misfortune is read as earned, the pleasure feels warranted, and the persona need not hide it as they would a baser joy.

## Echo

The account is felt settled, and the persona's pleasure wears the face of justice.
It is the most defensible schadenfreude -- the reason audiences cheer the villain's fall and the tax cheat's audit, the reason "they had it coming" turns a misfortune into a satisfaction openly shared -- the joy of a wrong redressed.
It is [leveling](process_leveling.md) with desert in place of envy and [grudge](process_grudge.md) with justice in place of mere dislike, the licensed and least guilty of the three. The judgment of desert that licenses it is the moral-judgment engine's; this owns the pleasure taken in a fall read as deserved.
