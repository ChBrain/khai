# Process: Leveling

## Taxonomy

[Schadenfreude](process_schadenfreude.md), the envy form.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: schadenfreude

## Initiated by

The persona sees an envied superior brought low, and feels the painful gap close -- the tall poppy cut down, the one who stood above returned to the persona's level or below.
This is leveling: schadenfreude born of envy, the fall of the envied enjoyed as relief.
What ached as inferiority eases as the better is unmade.

## Direction

Toward the envied's descent, enjoyed as the self's relative rise.
Leveling reads the misfortune through comparison: the persona was diminished by the target's superiority, and the target's fall lifts the persona by the same measure, so the loss is enjoyed not for justice but for the comparison it improves -- the distance the persona could not close by rising closed instead by the other's falling.
The direction is a relative ascent: the self raised not by its own gain but by the envied's loss, the gap eased from the top down.

## Lever

The closing of the envied gap, spent as comparison-relief.
Leveling runs on the same upward comparison that fed the envy: because the persona's standing was hurt by the target's advantage, the removal of that advantage restores them, and the misfortune is enjoyed in exact proportion to how much it levels -- the more the fallen had been envied, the sweeter the fall.
The lever is the relief of a painful comparison: the envied down is the self up, and the ache of the gap is paid off by the loss that erases it.

## Echo

The gap is eased and the persona lifted, the envied fall banked as private relief.
It is the schadenfreude most tightly bound to envy -- the reason the success we resent draws not sympathy but a secret gladness when it fails, the reason the neural reward of an envied rival's misfortune fires where their triumph stung -- envy's pain answered by its mirror pleasure.
It is [comeuppance](process_comeuppance.md) with envy in place of desert and [grudge](process_grudge.md) with comparison in place of mere antipathy. The upward pain that precedes and prepares it, and its malicious pole, are the envy engine's; this owns the pleasure taken in an envied superior's fall.
