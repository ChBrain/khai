# Process: Schadenfreude

## Taxonomy

Emotion / the social psychology of pleasure at another's misfortune.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: schadenfreude

## Initiated by

A persona learns of another's misfortune -- a fall, a failure, a comeuppance -- and feels, uninvited, a small pleasure at it.
This is schadenfreude: joy at another's pain, taken without having caused it.
The persona did not push, yet the falling gladdens them, and the gladness is theirs whether or not they own it.

## Direction

Toward the other's downfall, met with a private, often guilty joy.
Schadenfreude turns another's loss into the persona's gain: the misfortune of someone deserving, envied, or disliked registers not as sympathy but as satisfaction, a lift the persona feels and frequently hides, knowing the pleasure unbecoming.
The direction is a reversal of fellow-feeling: where sympathy would fall with the fallen, schadenfreude rises, the other's descent felt as the self's small ascent.

## Lever

The other's misfortune, spent as the persona's pleasure.
What schadenfreude spends is a fall it need not have caused: the pleasure is licensed by the target -- that they had it coming, that they stood above, that they were an enemy -- so the same misfortune that would draw pity for a sympathetic other draws joy for a deserving, envied, or disliked one.
The lever is the target's standing with the persona: comeuppance for the deserving, comparison-relief for the envied, and antipathy's satisfaction for the disliked each convert a loss that is not the persona's into a gladness that is.

## Echo

The other is fallen and the persona is gladdened, the misfortune banked as a private gain.
Schadenfreude is the mirror of envy -- where envy aches at another's good, schadenfreude delights in another's ill -- and it is why the mighty humbled, the rival beaten, the smug undone give a pleasure people rarely admit and reliably feel; the neural signature of reward lighting at an envied other's loss.
It runs as [comeuppance](process_comeuppance.md), [leveling](process_leveling.md), or [grudge](process_grudge.md) -- the deserved fall enjoyed as justice, the envied fall enjoyed as relief, the disliked fall enjoyed for itself. The upward pain at another's good is the envy engine's; the deservingness judgment that licenses comeuppance is the moral-judgment engine's; the standing cold down-look on another is the contempt engine's; the active wish to do the harm is the aggression engine's. This engine owns the pleasure taken in another's misfortune itself.
