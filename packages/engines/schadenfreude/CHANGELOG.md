# @chbrain/khai-engine-schadenfreude

## 0.1.1

### Patch Changes

- aef40b1: Add the schadenfreude engine: the pleasure a persona takes in another's misfortune, felt without having caused it -- the fall of the deserving, the envied, or the disliked registering as a small, often guilty joy. The root (`process_schadenfreude.md`) declares schadenfreude as the mirror of `envy`: where envy aches at another's superior good, schadenfreude delights in another's ill, and the two are linked -- the fall of an envied other is especially enjoyed. Three forms carry it, the tripartite motivational model: `comeuppance` (the justice route -- a deserved fall enjoyed as balance restored, the cheat caught, the arrogant humbled, the licensed and openly shared one), `leveling` (the envy route -- an envied superior brought low, the painful comparison eased by the loss, the self lifted by the other's fall), and `grudge` (the antipathy route -- a disliked other's or rival group's fall enjoyed for its own sake, needing no justice or envy to license it, the most tribal).

  The natural mirror of `envy`, and a well-warranted "dark" emotion. Bounded against `envy` (the upward pain that precedes it vs the pleasure at the fall), `moral-judgment` (the deservingness verdict that licenses comeuppance), `contempt` (the standing cold down-look vs the pleasure at a misfortune), `aggression` (the active wish to inflict harm vs joy at a fall not caused), and the `intergroup` composite (the between-group enmity grudge feeds on). No homonym gate: `schadenfreude` and the form stems `comeuppance`/`leveling`/`grudge` are all free (`rivalry` and `enmity` were already taken). Warranted (LORE) on Smith (_The Joy of Pain_, 2013; Smith et al. 1996), van Dijk & Ouwerkerk (the Cambridge volume, 2014), Feather & Sherman (deservingness, 2002), Leach & Spears (intergroup schadenfreude, 2008), and Wang, Lilienfeld & Rochat (the tripartite model, 2019). Set at patch as the free level; a new engine may warrant a minor at the maintainer's `bump:minor` label.
