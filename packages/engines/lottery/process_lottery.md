# Process: Lottery

## Taxonomy

Allocation by chance (no khai file of its own yet).

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: lottery

## Initiated by

Something has to be given to some people and not others, and no reason can be defended.
Lottery starts wherever a good is allocated deliberately at random: the school place, the visa, the conscription number, the ballot for the ward, the clinical trial arm, the jury pool, the housing list drawn by number.
It is not the contest in which the keenest party takes it, which is bidding's, and not the reading of an outcome as deserved, which is belief-in-a-just-world's. It is the decision to let chance decide, and what a decision with no reasons in it does to the people it lands on.

## Direction

Toward an allocation nobody has to justify and nobody can accept.
The engine reads four movements: [the impasse](process_impasse_lottery.md), the situation that makes chance the least bad instrument; [the drawing](process_drawing_lottery.md), a mechanism whose whole virtue is that it admits nothing; [the verdict](process_verdict_lottery.md), what the winner and the loser are each handed; and [the filling](process_filling_lottery.md), the twist -- what everybody puts into the space where the reason should have been.
The four are one operation, and the third is the shortest and the least survivable.

## Lever

The fairness of giving no reasons against the impossibility of living without one.
The carrying force is that the lottery is frequently the best available answer and is chosen by serious people for good motives: where claims genuinely cannot be ranked, any reasoned allocation is a disguised preference, and drawing lots is the one method that cannot be lobbied, bribed, or quietly bent -- its indifference is precisely what protects the weakest claimant; the resistance is that a decision with nothing in it offers nothing to the person it goes against, so the instrument that most reliably prevents injustice is the one that leaves the loser with least to hold.
A lottery is read on what it excluded, never on what it produced.

## Echo

Somebody holding a place they cannot account for, and somebody else with nothing to appeal.
Lottery does not close; it is a decision being emptied of reasons, gathered into one reading -- an impasse, a drawing, a verdict, a filling.
The engine's twist is here. **A lottery works by admitting no reasons, which is why everybody it touches invents one.** The mechanism is built to keep every consideration out, including the bad ones, and that emptiness is its entire value. It is also unbearable: the winner cannot say why it was them and feels chosen anyway, the loser has nothing to argue with and concludes something about themselves regardless, and both readings are manufactured by the one instrument designed to make them impossible.
It hands off what it is not: the contest that awards to the highest estimate is bidding's; the reading of an outcome as deserved is belief-in-a-just-world's; the link between an act and an unrelated result is superstition's; the hazard and its size are risk's; the sense that a procedure was fair is heeding's and recognition's; and a draw staged to **stand for** a theme is authored key, the play's pitch.
The persona is dated from the last number they were given.
