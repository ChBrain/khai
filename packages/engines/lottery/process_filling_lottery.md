# Process: The Filling

## Taxonomy

[Lottery](process_lottery.md), what goes into the space where the reason should have been, and where the engine is seen for one thing.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: lottery

## Initiated by

Nobody leaves the space empty.
The Filling starts once the verdict has been absorbed: [a desert read](process_the_desert_read.md) applied to an outcome that contains none, [an omen](process_the_omen.md) found in a draw that means nothing, and [the next draw](process_the_next_draw.md), entered with everything the last one supplied.

## Direction

Toward reasons manufactured to occupy a vacancy the mechanism created on purpose.
The lottery's achievement is an outcome with no considerations in it. That achievement lasts about as long as the announcement, because a person cannot hold a large fact about their life with nothing attached to it, so an account is assembled from whatever is nearby: character, fate, a sign, a sense of how things have always gone for them.
The engine reads only that this happens and that the design cannot prevent it. The reading of an outcome as deserved is belief-in-a-just-world's, and the link between an act and an unrelated result is superstition's; both mechanisms are theirs and both arrive here uninvited.

## Lever

The necessity of an account against the emptiness that was the point.
The carrying force is that supplying one is not a weakness and cannot be trained out: people are built to explain what happens to them, an unexplained outcome of this size would be intolerable to sit with, and the accounts are usually harmless and sometimes consoling; the resistance is that the accounts reintroduce exactly what the mechanism spent all its effort excluding -- desert, character, and destiny -- so within a month the allocation has acquired the meanings it was designed to have none of, and nobody did anything wrong.
The Filling rides the engine's keystone knife-edge: a lottery works by admitting no reasons, which is why everybody it touches invents one.

## Echo

An empty outcome with a full explanation attached.
It shows the three earlier movements complete -- an impasse that exhausted the reasons, a drawing built to hold them out, a verdict that handed two people nothing -- and adds the thing the design could not reach, which is what people do afterwards.
This is why the Filling is the hardest of the four to stage and the only one worth closing on: the process must have been clean, the draw must have been honest, nobody must have been cheated, and the audience must be able to watch meaning arrive in a place that was deliberately cleared for it.
A play that stages the lottery as rigged has replaced the mechanism with a fraud, and one that stages the winner as gloating has replaced it with a fable.
The Filling is dated from days after the draw and is complete within a season.
