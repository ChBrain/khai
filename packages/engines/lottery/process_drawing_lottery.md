# Process: The Drawing

## Taxonomy

[Lottery](process_lottery.md), a mechanism whose whole virtue is that it admits nothing.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: lottery

## Initiated by

The draw is made, and it is made where people can see it.
The Drawing starts at the ceremony rather than the algorithm: [visible chance](process_the_visible_chance.md) that has to be watched to work, [no reasons](process_the_no_reasons.md) admitted of any kind, and [a single run](process_the_single_run.md) that cannot be repeated.

## Direction

Toward a procedure designed to be empty.
Everything about a lottery is arranged to keep considerations out. The capsules are drawn in public, the numbers are sealed in advance, the operator has no discretion, and the software is audited by people with no stake -- and all of that effort goes into ensuring that nothing about the claimants reaches the outcome.
This is the opposite of every other allocation, which is built to let the relevant facts in.

## Lever

The value of what the emptiness excludes against the value of what it also excludes.
The carrying force is that the emptiness is the entire point and it works: a mechanism that admits no reasons admits no bad ones, so it cannot be lobbied, cannot be bent by whoever is best connected, and cannot quietly encode an advantage nobody would defend out loud -- which is why lotteries protect the claimant with no advocate better than any judgement would; the resistance is that the same barrier holds out the good reasons, so a claimant whose case genuinely is stronger is refused by a device that was built not to hear it.
The Drawing keeps out the corruption and the merit together, because it cannot tell them apart.

## Echo

A decision with nothing inside it.
It is the movement where the instrument's design is visible, and it is designed against itself.
The Drawing is dated to the moment of the draw and contains no information.
