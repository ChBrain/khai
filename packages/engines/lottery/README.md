# Lottery

Lottery as process: a good allocated deliberately at random, and what a decision with no reasons in it does to the people it lands on -- not the contest that awards to the highest estimate, which is bidding's, and not the reading of an outcome as deserved, which is belief-in-a-just-world's -- across four movements: the impasse (a short supply that turns a number into a series of denials, tied claims unequal in every way except the one that would decide, and an exposed decider relieved by the instrument that leaves the claimant with nobody to ask), the drawing (a visible chance trusted for its theatre, a no-reasons rule that is a virtue which cannot be applied selectively, and a single run that is binding and tells nobody anything), the verdict (a winner needing a story the lottery cannot supply, a loser spared a judgement and left with a hole, and no appeal, which protects the process and removes the hearing), and the filling (a desert read that attaches a character to a coin, an omen that is sound reasoning applied to a source with nothing in it, and a next draw entered or abandoned on a record with no content) -- with the twist that a lottery works by admitting no reasons, which is why everybody it touches invents one.

This engine is defined by its [manifest](package.json), which the canon renders as the WIRES card. The manifest is the single source of truth; this README is generated, not edited by hand.
