# Process: The Desert Read

## Taxonomy

[The Filling](process_filling_lottery.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: lottery

## Initiated by

The outcome is read as having been about the person.
They got it because they are the sort of thing goes right for; they did not because they never get a break.

## Direction

Toward character inferred from a number in a drum.
Both parties do it and neither notices doing it. The winner is a little more inclined to think of themselves as fortunate in a way that reflects on them; the loser adds the refusal to a running account of how things go, and the account has other entries in it that were also chance.
The mechanism supplies no support for either reading, which is why they are so hard to argue with.

## Lever

The usefulness of a self-account against what this one is made of.
The carrying force is that a persona needs a coherent story of their own life and cannot function without one: the reading is how people integrate an event, most versions of it are mild, and demanding that somebody hold a major outcome as pure noise is asking more than anybody manages; the resistance is that the material here is a random number, so what gets integrated is a fact about a drum, and the persona has revised their estimate of themselves on evidence with no content.
The Desert Read attaches a character to a coin.

## Echo

A self-estimate updated on nothing.
It shows the vacuum being filled with the first thing available, and the mechanism belongs to belief-in-a-just-world.
The Desert Read is dated within days and is not experienced as an inference.
