# Process: The Tied Claims

## Taxonomy

[The Impasse](process_impasse_lottery.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: lottery

## Initiated by

The claimants cannot be ranked.
Not because nobody has tried, but because the attempts produce different orders depending on which reasonable criterion is used.

## Direction

Toward a set of cases that every rule sorts differently.
Rank by need and one order appears; by benefit expected, another; by time waited, a third; by contribution made, a fourth. Each is defensible and each favours a different subset, and there is no meta-rule for choosing between them that is not itself a preference.
The claims are not equal. They are incommensurable, which is a harder problem and looks from outside like indecision.

## Lever

The reality of the differences between claimants against the absence of a common scale.
The carrying force is that the differences are genuine and worth attending to: these people are not interchangeable, their situations vary enormously, and a system that treated them as identical would be crude and would be resented for it; the resistance is that acknowledging the differences does not produce an order, because the dimensions do not convert -- so more information about the claimants makes the ranking harder rather than easier, and a thorough process ends further from an answer than a hasty one.
The Tied Claims are unequal in every way except the one that would decide.

## Echo

A field that resists ordering.
It shows why the impasse is a property of the case rather than a failure of the committee.
The Tied Claims are dated from the first serious attempt to rank them.
