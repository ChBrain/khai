# Process: The Winner Unearned

## Taxonomy

[The Verdict](process_verdict_lottery.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: lottery

## Initiated by

The persona has the place and no claim to it.
They did nothing, they were not chosen, and there is no sense in which it was theirs.

## Direction

Toward a good held without a story to hold it by.
Ordinarily a persona can say why they have what they have -- they worked, they waited, they paid, somebody judged them suitable. Here none of that is available, and the persona is standing in a place that four hundred people wanted and that they have no account of occupying.
Most of them find this uncomfortable rather than delightful, and most resolve it quickly.

## Lever

The genuineness of the good fortune against the absence of any ground for it.
The carrying force is that being lucky is not a fault and the persona owes nobody an apology: they entered a fair process on the same terms as everybody, they are entitled to the place, and a winner who refused it out of scruple would help nobody and would only shift the discomfort one place down the list; the resistance is that a persona cannot hold something valuable for long without an account of why, so the account gets assembled -- and the only materials available are the ones the mechanism was built to exclude.
The Winner Unearned needs a story the lottery cannot supply.

## Echo

Somebody in possession and looking for grounds.
It shows the vacuum forming on the side that is doing well.
The Winner Unearned is dated from the announcement and is resolved within weeks.
