# Process: The Next Draw

## Taxonomy

[The Filling](process_filling_lottery.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: lottery

## Initiated by

The ballot opens again, and the persona decides whether to enter.
With everything the last one gave them, which is nothing, and which now feels like a great deal.

## Direction

Toward a decision made on an accumulated record that records nothing.
The persona weighs their history with this ballot -- three refusals, or one easy admission -- and the weighing is the ordinary and sensible thing to do with a history. Here it is a history of independent draws, so the only correct summary of it is the number of entries.
And because each round adds another entry to a story that is not about them, the personas most repeatedly refused are the ones most likely to stop entering.

## Lever

The rationality of not persisting forever against what the persistence was actually costing.
The carrying force is that giving up is often right and is not a defeat: the entries cost time and hope, the odds really are poor, and a persona who spent a decade on ballots they never won would have been better served by doing something else with the years; the resistance is that the decision to stop is made on a false reading -- the persona believes the record is telling them something, and it is telling them nothing -- so the selection of who eventually drops out is made by accumulated bad luck rather than by anything about the claim.
The Next Draw is entered or abandoned on a record with no content.

## Echo

A field that thins by discouragement.
It shows why the operation never concludes and how a fair mechanism produces an unfair population over time.
The Next Draw is dated from the next opening and is decided by the last one.
