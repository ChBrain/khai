# Process: The Verdict

## Taxonomy

[Lottery](process_lottery.md), what the winner and the loser are each handed.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: lottery

## Initiated by

The numbers are read out and everybody learns which side they are on.
The Verdict starts at the announcement: [a winner unearned](process_the_winner_unearned.md) holding something they cannot account for, [a loser unanswered](process_the_loser_unanswered.md) holding nothing at all, and [no appeal](process_the_no_appeal.md) available to either.

## Direction

Toward two positions that the same emptiness produces differently.
The winner receives a good and no story about why they should have it; the loser receives a refusal and no story about why they should not. These are not symmetrical, because a good with no story is easy to keep and a refusal with no story is very hard to hold.
So the mechanism's neutrality is experienced as neutrality by exactly one of the two parties.

## Lever

The equality of the treatment against the asymmetry of what it leaves.
The carrying force is that treating the claimants identically is the fairest thing available and both parties knew the terms: nobody was ranked, nobody was preferred, the loser was not judged and should take some comfort in that, and a reasoned refusal would have hurt more in most cases; the resistance is that a person refused something they needed cannot do anything with the information that no reason was involved, since what they wanted was the thing and what they were left with is an absence where an explanation would ordinarily sit.
The Verdict treats everybody equally and lands on them unequally.

## Echo

Two people holding the same nothing to different effect.
It is the shortest of the four movements and the one everything else exists to arrange.
The Verdict is dated to the announcement and is over immediately.
