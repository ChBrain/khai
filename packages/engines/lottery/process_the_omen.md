# Process: The Omen

## Taxonomy

[The Filling](process_filling_lottery.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: lottery

## Initiated by

The draw is taken as a sign.
Of how the year will go, of whether this was meant, of whether to try again or take the hint.

## Direction

Toward a random result treated as information about the future.
The number is read forward: a persona who missed the ballot three times concludes something about whether this is for them; a persona who got in on the first attempt concludes that the door was open.
Neither conclusion has any support, and both feel like the sober lesson of experience rather than like a reading of entrails.

## Lever

The reasonableness of learning from outcomes against the emptiness of these ones.
The carrying force is that updating on results is the correct general policy and the persona is applying it properly: outcomes usually carry information, treating one's own history as evidence is how anybody navigates anything, and a persona who learned nothing from three refusals would be strange; the resistance is that these particular outcomes are constructed to carry no information whatsoever, so the one domain where the general policy is worthless is the one where it feels most like hard-won realism.
The Omen is sound reasoning applied to a source with nothing in it.

## Echo

A forecast built from a draw.
It shows why lotteries so reliably attract ritual, and the mechanism belongs to superstition.
The Omen is dated from the result and points forward.
