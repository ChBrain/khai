# Process: The No Reasons

## Taxonomy

[The Drawing](process_drawing_lottery.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: lottery

## Initiated by

Nothing about anybody enters the decision.
Not need, not merit, not effort, not who they know, not how long they waited.

## Direction

Toward an emptiness that is the mechanism's actual product.
A lottery does not produce a fair allocation in the sense of one that matches the claims. It produces an allocation with no reasons in it, and that is a different and narrower good: it guarantees that no consideration which should not have counted did count.
Everything else people admire about lotteries follows from this and nothing else.

## Lever

The completeness of the exclusion against the completeness of the exclusion.
The carrying force is that keeping every reason out is the only reliable way to keep the wrong ones out: a rule admitting good reasons must specify them, a specification can be argued around, and the arguing is exactly where advantage re-enters -- so a mechanism that hears nothing is the only one that cannot be talked to; the resistance is that the same feature discards the considerations everybody agrees are relevant, so a lottery treats a desperate claimant and a casual one identically and is not embarrassed by it.
The No Reasons is a virtue that cannot be applied selectively.

## Echo

A decision that is clean because it is empty.
It shows the sanitising effect at the point where its cost is also visible.
The No Reasons is dated from the design and holds for every draw.
