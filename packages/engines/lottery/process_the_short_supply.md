# Process: The Short Supply

## Taxonomy

[The Impasse](process_impasse_lottery.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: lottery

## Initiated by

There are forty places and four hundred people who want one.
The number is fixed, the demand is not, and no effort by anybody changes either.

## Direction

Toward a situation with no version where everybody is served.
Scarcity here is not a signal that clears at a price -- it is a hard count, set by beds, seats, kidneys, or a statute, and it cannot be expanded by wanting it more or by anybody being more deserving.
So the question is never how to satisfy the claims. It is which of them to refuse, and the refusing is the whole of the work.

## Lever

The plainness of the arithmetic against what the arithmetic requires of somebody.
The carrying force is that the shortage is usually nobody's fault and is often being worked on: the places are limited for real reasons, the people running the allocation would give everybody one if they could, and treating the shortage as a scandal helps nobody standing in front of it today; the resistance is that a fixed count converts a resource problem into a moral one, since every place granted is a place refused, and the refusals have to be made by a person to a person.
The Short Supply turns a number into a series of denials.

## Echo

A count that cannot be argued with.
It shows why the domain is about refusal rather than about distribution.
The Short Supply is dated from whatever fixed the number.
