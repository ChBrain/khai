# Process: The Visible Chance

## Taxonomy

[The Drawing](process_drawing_lottery.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: lottery

## Initiated by

The randomness has to be seen.
Capsules in a drum, numbers on television, a witness from each side, a published seed.

## Direction

Toward a ceremony carrying more weight than the mathematics.
A cryptographically sound draw conducted privately is more random than a man pulling balls from a barrel and is trusted less. What people need is not a guarantee that the process was random but an occasion at which nobody could have interfered, and those are different requirements.
So the visible, clumsy, theatrical version does the job the elegant one cannot.

## Lever

The soundness of the mathematics against what the mathematics fails to supply.
The carrying force is that the demand for visibility is reasonable rather than superstitious: claimants have no way to audit a procedure they cannot see, trust in an unwatched process has to rest on somebody's word, and the history of unwatched allocations is not encouraging; the resistance is that a spectacle can be arranged to look fair while being anything at all, and the qualities that make a draw convincing -- the drum, the sealed envelope, the dignitary -- are precisely the qualities that can be staged.
The Visible Chance is trusted for its theatre and can be produced without any.

## Echo

A procedure believed because it was watched.
It shows the lottery depending on a performance in order to be an absence.
The Visible Chance is dated at the draw and is the only part anybody remembers.
