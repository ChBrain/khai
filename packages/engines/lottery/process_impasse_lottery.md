# Process: The Impasse

## Taxonomy

[Lottery](process_lottery.md), the situation that makes chance the least bad instrument.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: lottery

## Initiated by

There is not enough, everybody has a case, and somebody has to decide.
The Impasse starts at three facts arriving together: [a short supply](process_the_short_supply.md) with more claimants than places, [tied claims](process_the_tied_claims.md) that cannot be ranked without a fight, and [an exposed decider](process_the_exposed_decider.md) who will be blamed for any answer they give.

## Direction

Toward a point where every reasoned allocation is worse than none.
The lottery is not chosen because chance is good. It is chosen because each of the alternatives -- merit, need, order of arrival, price, judgement -- has been examined and found either indefensible on the facts or corruptible in the hands available.
So the decision to randomise is itself a reasoned one, taken by people who have exhausted the reasons.

## Lever

The seriousness of the attempt to decide properly against what the attempt turns up.
The carrying force is that the effort to find a principled rule is honest and usually thorough: the committee genuinely tries to rank the claims, the criteria are debated, and in most allocations somewhere a defensible rule is found and a lottery is not needed; the resistance is that in the hard cases every candidate rule turns out to favour somebody in the room or to encode an advantage the claimants did not earn, so the search for a good reason is what produces the decision to use none.
The Impasse is arrived at by trying hardest.

## Echo

A decision that has run out of defensible answers.
It is the movement that justifies the whole apparatus and is forgotten the moment the draw is announced.
The Impasse is dated from the point where the criteria failed.
