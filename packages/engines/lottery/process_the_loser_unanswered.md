# Process: The Loser Unanswered

## Taxonomy

[The Verdict](process_verdict_lottery.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: lottery

## Initiated by

The persona is refused and there is nothing to be told.
No criterion they failed, no shortfall to work on, no person who decided.

## Direction

Toward a refusal that offers nothing to do next.
A reasoned refusal is painful and is also usable: it names a gap, which can be closed, contested, or at least understood. A refusal by lot names nothing, so there is no version of next time that is different from this time, and no account the persona can give of what happened to them beyond the fact that it happened.
This is the position the whole engine is built around, and it is the one the design is least able to help.

## Lever

The kindness of not being judged against the cost of not being told.
The carrying force is that not being ranked is a genuine mercy and the alternative is worse for most people: a reasoned refusal would have required somebody to say this claimant's need was smaller, and being spared that verdict is worth something real, which people who have received both will confirm; the resistance is that mercy of this kind is only legible from outside, since from inside the persona has lost the thing and gained an absence, and an absence cannot be argued with, improved on, or reported to anybody.
The Loser Unanswered was spared a judgement and left with a hole.

## Echo

Somebody refused by nothing in particular.
It shows where the mechanism's cost is concentrated, which is on the party it was designed to protect.
The Loser Unanswered is dated from the announcement and does not resolve.
