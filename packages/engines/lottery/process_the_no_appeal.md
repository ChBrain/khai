# Process: The No Appeal

## Taxonomy

[The Verdict](process_verdict_lottery.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: lottery

## Initiated by

There is nothing to appeal against.
No decision was made about the persona, so there is no decision to review.

## Direction

Toward a finality with no surface to push on.
An appeal needs a claim that something was decided wrongly, and a lottery decides nothing about anybody -- so the only available challenges are that the draw was rigged or that a lottery should not have been used, and both are challenges to the system rather than to the outcome.
The persona who wants to contest their own case discovers that their case was never considered, which is what they were promised.

## Lever

The integrity of a process with no appeals against what having no appeal removes.
The carrying force is that unappealable is what makes it work and everybody benefits from it: an allocation that could be revisited would be revisited by whoever had the resources to revisit it, and the absence of that channel is exactly what protects the claimant with no lawyer; the resistance is that appeal is not only a remedy, it is the form in which a person is heard at all -- so the persona loses not just the chance of reversal but the occasion on which somebody would have had to consider them.
The No Appeal protects the process and removes the hearing.

## Echo

A finality that cannot be addressed.
It shows why fairness in this domain is not experienced as fairness by the person it was for.
The No Appeal is dated from the design and is discovered at the loss.
