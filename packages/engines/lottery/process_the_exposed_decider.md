# Process: The Exposed Decider

## Taxonomy

[The Impasse](process_impasse_lottery.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: lottery

## Initiated by

Somebody has to sign it, and they will be answerable for whatever they sign.
A name on the allocation, a person who can be written to, telephoned, and taken to a tribunal.

## Direction

Toward a decider who would rather not be the reason.
Any allocation with a person behind it invites a case: the disappointed claimant can ask why, the answer will be a criterion, and the criterion can be attacked as the wrong one, applied wrongly, or applied to them and not to somebody else.
The lottery removes the person from the answer, and the relief that offers is a real part of why it is adopted.

## Lever

The legitimacy of wanting to be out of it against what going out of it costs the claimants.
The carrying force is that the decider's position is genuinely impossible and their reluctance is not cowardice: they are being asked to rank people's lives on criteria that do not converge, they will be attacked whatever they choose, and a system that leaves such choices to an individual's discretion is a system that will eventually be captured; the resistance is that the same removal takes away the one thing a refused claimant could have had, which is somebody who will explain -- so the decider's protection and the claimant's account are the same item, and only one party can hold it.
The Exposed Decider is relieved by the instrument that leaves the claimant with nobody to ask.

## Echo

A signature nobody has to defend.
It shows the lottery being chosen partly for what it does for the people running it.
The Exposed Decider is dated from the first appeal against a previous method.
