# Process: Lingering

## Taxonomy

[Street-life](process_street-life.md), the facet where idle staying is read as the proof of a place worth being in.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: street-life

## Initiated by

People stay when they have no reason to.
Lingering starts wherever a public place holds people past their errand -- the sitting, the leaning, the watching, the hanging around, the staying-on with nothing to do -- the optional presence that no task requires and that comes only where a place is good enough to want to be in.
It is street-life read the other way: not the busy, purposeful use but the idle staying, the loitering that looks like nothing being done and is in truth the surest sign the place has succeeded.

## Direction

Toward staying, past the point the errand ends.
Lingering keeps people in the place for its own sake -- the bench taken all afternoon, the steps sat on, the corner leaned against, the plaza where people come to watch other people, the optional and social life that appears only where the necessary business could have long since sent everyone home.
It moves the place from used to loved: not crossed on the way to something but chosen as the something, the staying that is the highest verdict a public place can earn and the one no amount of grandeur can buy if the place is cold.

## Lever

The value of the idle staying against the eye that reads it as waste.
The carrying force is that lingering is the proof of quality -- optional and social activity comes only where the place is good, so the loiterers and sitters and hangers-on are the surest measure of a place that works; the resistance is the reading that takes staying for waste or menace, the design and the policing that move people along, the hostile bench and the swept plaza that drive off the very lingering that would have marked the place as alive.
Welcome the lingering and the place fills with the life that chooses it; forbid it, and the place keeps only those with somewhere to be, and loses the proof that anyone would stay.

## Echo

The place vindicated by the people who will not leave.
Lingering leaves the place with its stayers -- the idle, optional, unhurried presence of people who have no reason to be there but want to be -- and carries beneath it the street-life twist, the sibling of the family's ambivalence: that the purposeless staying is not the waste it looks like but the richest thing a public place can host, the loitering read as the proof the place is worth a life.
Lingering is dated from the staying-on, the moment use turned to the wish to remain.
