# Process: Street-life

## Taxonomy

Urbanism.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: street-life

## Initiated by

A public place is entered, and it is read by the people in it before anything else.
Street-life starts the moment a place is meant for people to be in together -- the sidewalk, the plaza, the market, the stoop, the square -- and the first thing it shows is whether anyone is there and what they are doing: the place alive with use or dead and empty.
Every public place has a social life, a way it is lived in that is as particular as its look; street-life is that life as an actor, not the still backdrop a setting is usually taken for.

## Direction

Into use, from the people the place gathers.
Four facets compose the life: [Presence](process_presence.md) is the people out and using the place, [Mingling](process_mingling.md) the casual exchange between the strangers it brings together, [Watch](process_watch.md) the eyes on the street that a peopled place keeps over itself, and [Lingering](process_lingering.md) the twist -- the idle staying read as the proof of a place worth being in.
A place is not read as one flat measure of busy or empty but as a life: who is out, how they meet, how they mind the place, and how they stay -- or, where the life has failed, a dead place that people cross but never use, its sidewalks bare and its windows blind.

## Lever

The place's pull to be used against everything that empties it.
The carrying force is the human draw of a good public place -- people come where people are, and each one out draws the next; the resistance is everything that kills the street, the design and use that give people no reason to stop, no eyes on the walk, nowhere to sit, so the life drains and the place goes dead, unsafe because unused and unused because unsafe.
A living street, dense and mixed and watched, holds its people and makes more; a dead one, however grand, is crossed and shunned, its social life gone and not easily brought back.

## Echo

The place known by its life, and remembered by it.
Street-life does not close; it is the standing social character of the place, gathering its people into a life that is as much the place as its shape.
It hands off what it is not: the layout and standing mood are space's, the sound the place makes is soundscape's, the weather over it is weather's, and a persona's own errand or sociability is theirs.
The place is read by its life -- a setting is not only somewhere seen but somewhere lived, and a place is alive or dead by the people in it and what they do there.
