# Process: Presence

## Taxonomy

[Street-life](process_street-life.md), the facet of the people out and using the place.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: street-life

## Initiated by

People are out, and the place is used.
Presence starts wherever a public place has people in it doing the ordinary things a place is for -- walking, shopping, waiting, passing, sitting, working the stall -- the plain fact of occupancy that is the ground of every other part of the life.
It is the base of the field: not yet the meeting or the watching or the staying, only that people are there, in number and in rhythm, the place peopled rather than empty.

## Direction

Out into the open, in numbers that draw more numbers.
Presence fills the place with its users -- the flow of the sidewalk, the crowd of the market, the thinning at night and the swelling at noon, the rhythm of a place used across the day rather than emptied after the one use that built it.
It moves the place from empty to peopled, and the peopling is self-feeding: people come where people already are, the busy corner pulling the next passerby in, the occupied plaza filling because it is occupied, the crowd its own advertisement.

## Lever

The draw of an occupied place against everything that empties it.
The carrying force is that people seek the used place -- the busy street feels alive and safe and worth being on, and each person out is a reason for the next; the resistance is the single-use, the dead hour, the design that gives no reason to be there, so the place empties and, once empty, repels: the bare plaza no one crosses, the street abandoned after five.
Mix the uses and the place is peopled around the clock; zone it to one and it lives an hour and dies the rest.

## Echo

The place set to its measure of use.
Presence leaves the place with its people -- the plain standing fact of who is out and how many, the occupancy the rest of the life is built on -- and hands that ground to every meeting and every watch that follows.
It is the least remarked and most necessary of the facets: the sheer being-there of people, without which there is no contact to have, no eyes to keep, no one to linger.
Presence is dated from the using, the standing fact of a place peopled rather than empty.
