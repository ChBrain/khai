# Needs: Reference

## Line of Work

Needs as **process**: how a persona's plural needs differ in prepotency -- the more basic pressing harder when unmet and claiming rough priority -- and split into deficiency needs that quiet when filled and growth needs that do not. The domain does not model the quality or source of a persona's motivation -- whether a pursuit is owned or imposed, intrinsic or external -- which is the motivation engine's; nor the single desired end-state a persona is oriented toward, which is the goal engine's; nor the stance a persona holds toward whether their life matters, which is the meaning engine's. It models the structure of the needs themselves: their plurality, their soft prepotent ordering, and their division into deficiency and growth, working through the routes by which that structure governs the persona.

The spine is Maslow's theory of human needs, taken in its empirically defensible form. Maslow held that human motivation is organized around a hierarchy of needs -- physiological, safety, belonging, esteem, and self-actualization -- ordered by prepotency, the more basic pressing harder when unmet, and distinguished deficiency needs (which motivate by lack and quiet when filled) from being- or growth-needs (which grow with feeding). The strict rank-ordering has not held up: large cross-cultural work by Tay and Diener across 123 countries found the needs to be universal and to predict well-being, but to operate somewhat independently rather than in a rigid sequence -- higher needs can be pursued and met before lower ones are fully satisfied. Kenrick and colleagues offered an evolutionary renovation of the structure. So the engine keeps what is warranted -- plural, universal needs; a real but soft prepotency; the deficiency/growth split -- and drops the rigid staircase. Three routes carry it: prepotency, deficiency, actualization.

## Restrictions

What the engine refuses to model, and to whom it delegates.

- **Motivation**: The engine models the structure of the needs -- their plurality, ordering, and deficiency/growth split -- not the quality or source of the motivation to pursue them. Whether a pursuit is owned or imposed, intrinsic or external -- the autonomy of the motive -- is owned by the motivation engine (self-determination theory); the needs engine says which need presses and how it behaves when fed, not whether the persona pursues it freely or under pressure. The two compose: a need supplies the what, motivation the how-owned.
- **Goal**: The engine models the standing needs that press on a persona, not the single desired end-state they are oriented toward. A concrete goal a persona holds and pursues is owned by the goal engine; a need is broader and more basic -- a class of want like safety or belonging -- from which particular goals are formed. The engine delegates the specific pursued end to goal and keeps the underlying need.
- **Meaning**: The engine models the growth-need's pull toward fuller being, not the persona's verdict on whether their life matters. The stance of meaning present, searched-for, or lost is owned by the meaning engine; actualization is a need that pulls toward becoming, where meaning is a held position on significance -- the two are neighbors at the top of the structure but distinct, one a motive and the other an appraisal. The engine delegates the significance-verdict to meaning.

## Encoding

Source to constraint, per file.

- **[needs](process_needs.md)** (the root): The plurality of needs, their soft prepotent ordering, and their split into deficiency and growth -- the more basic pressing harder when unmet, the ordering real but not rigid. Anchored by Maslow (a theory of human motivation) and Tay & Diener (needs and well-being around the world).
- **[prepotency](process_prepotency.md)** (form): The ordering route -- a more basic unmet need pressing harder and claiming rough priority, reasserting over a higher one when it fails, the sequence soft rather than strict. Anchored by Maslow (prepotency) and Tay & Diener (needs pursued somewhat independently).
- **[deficiency](process_deficiency.md)** (form): The lack-driven route -- D-needs that motivate by absence and quiet once filled, the drive spent by its own satisfaction. Anchored by Maslow (deficiency needs, _Toward a Psychology of Being_).
- **[actualization](process_actualization.md)** (form): The growth route -- being-needs that grow with feeding and never satiate, pulling toward the fuller expression of what the persona could become. Anchored by Maslow (being-needs and self-actualization).
