# Process: Needs

## Taxonomy

Behavioral science / the psychology of human needs and their ordering.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: needs

## Initiated by

A persona wants not one thing but many -- to be safe, to be fed, to belong, to be esteemed, to become what they might -- and these wants are not equal: the more basic press harder when they go unmet.
This is the structure of needs: a plurality of motives ranked by prepotency, so that a persona short of safety attends to safety before esteem, and a persona short of nothing basic is freed to reach for more.
The persona is moved not by a single drive but by whichever need is both unmet and basic enough to claim them.

## Direction

Toward whichever need is most prepotent -- but along a soft ordering, not a rigid staircase.
The needs press in a rough sequence, the basic before the higher, and a lower need left unmet can reassert itself over a higher one already in play; yet the ordering is probabilistic, not strict -- needs are pursued in parallel, and higher ones can be reached before lower ones are wholly filled.
The direction is real but supple: universal needs, found across cultures to press and to matter for well-being, that a persona works at once and in rough priority rather than one rung fully at a time -- the pyramid honest only if drawn with soft edges.

## Lever

What is unmet crossed with how basic it is, spent as the priority a need claims.
The structure runs on two facts about a need: whether it is filled, and where it sits in prepotency -- and a need commands the persona in proportion to being both unmet and basic, so that a deficit low in the order outranks a deficit high in it, but a higher need can still pull once the lower is even roughly secured.
The lever is prepotency softened by parallelism: the ordering biases which need governs without dictating it absolutely, so the same persona can be worked by several needs at once, weighted by how basic and how unmet each is.

## Echo

The persona is governed by the need most basic and least met, and freed toward the higher as the lower fill.
The need-structure is why the starving think of food before dignity, why safety bought lets belonging matter, why a life with the basics secured turns to meaning and growth -- and why, when a basic need fails, the higher pursuits it had enabled give way to it again.
It runs as [prepotency](process_prepotency.md), [deficiency](process_deficiency.md), or [actualization](process_actualization.md) -- the ordering that sets priority, the needs that quiet when filled, the growth that never does. The quality and source of a persona's motivation -- whether a pursuit is owned or imposed -- is the motivation engine's; the single desired end-state a persona is oriented toward is the goal engine's. This engine owns the plurality of needs, their soft prepotent ordering, and their split into deficiency and growth.
