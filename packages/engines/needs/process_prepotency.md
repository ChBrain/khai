# Process: Prepotency

## Taxonomy

[Needs](process_needs.md), the ordering form.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: needs

## Initiated by

Two needs go unmet at once, and one takes the persona first: the more basic presses harder, and claims priority over the higher.
This is prepotency: the ordering of needs by urgency, so that a deficit low in the structure -- safety, sustenance -- outranks a deficit high in it, and governs the persona until it is at least roughly secured.
When the basic fails, it does not wait its turn; it comes forward and takes the front.

## Direction

Toward the most basic unmet need -- but as a soft priority, needs pursued in parallel and the order supple.
Prepotency biases which need governs without dictating it: the sequence is real -- the basic tends to press before the higher, and a lower need left unmet can reassert itself over a higher one already engaged -- yet it is probabilistic, not a rigid staircase, so a persona can work several needs at once and reach a higher one before a lower is wholly filled.
The direction is priority, not lockstep: the ordering weights the persona's attention toward the basic-and-unmet without forbidding the higher, the pyramid drawn with soft edges.

## Lever

The rank of a need crossed with whether it is met, spent as the priority it claims on the persona.
Prepotency runs on the interaction of order and deficit: a need commands in proportion to being both basic and unmet, so a low deficit outweighs a high one, but a secured lower need releases its claim and lets the higher pull.
The lever is the reassertion: because the ordering is standing rather than spent, a basic need that fails after a higher one has taken hold comes back to the front and reclaims the persona -- priority restored to the basic the moment it is threatened.

## Echo

The basic unmet need takes the front, and the higher waits -- or is released to pull once the basic is secured.
It is why threat to safety eclipses ambition, why the secured lower needs let the higher ones matter, why a crisis low in the order pulls a persona back down from the pursuits it had climbed to -- the ordering setting priority, softly but really.
It is [deficiency](process_deficiency.md) and [actualization](process_actualization.md)'s partner, the ordering where they are the two kinds of need ordered. The quality of the motivation behind a pursuit is the motivation engine's; prepotency owns the soft ranking that decides which need governs.
