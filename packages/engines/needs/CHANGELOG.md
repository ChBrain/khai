# @chbrain/khai-engine-needs

## 0.1.1

### Patch Changes

- b7e23f2: Add the needs engine: how a persona's plural needs differ in prepotency -- the more basic pressing harder when unmet and claiming rough priority -- and split into deficiency needs that quiet when filled and growth needs that do not. The root (`process_needs.md`) declares Maslow's theory of human needs in its empirically defensible form: plural, universal needs ordered by a prepotency that is real but soft, not a rigid staircase -- needs pursued in parallel, higher ones reachable before lower ones are fully filled. Three forms carry it: `prepotency` (the ordering route -- a more basic unmet need claiming rough priority and reasserting when it fails, the sequence soft rather than strict), `deficiency` (the lack-driven route -- D-needs that motivate by absence and quiet once filled, spent by their own satisfaction), and `actualization` (the growth route -- being-needs that grow with feeding and never satiate, pulling toward the fuller expression of what the persona could become).

  Built on the defensible science, not the wall-poster pyramid: keeps plural universal needs, a real-but-soft prepotency, and the deficiency/growth split; drops the strict rank-order that has not held up. Bounded against `motivation` (the quality and source of motivation -- self-determination theory's autonomy of the motive -- vs the structure of the needs themselves), `goal` (the single desired end-state a persona pursues vs the broader standing need it forms from), and `meaning` (the verdict on whether one's life matters vs the growth-need's pull toward becoming). No homonym gate: `needs` and the form stems `prepotency`/`deficiency`/`actualization` are all free. Warranted (LORE) on Maslow ("A Theory of Human Motivation", 1943; _Motivation and Personality_, 1954; _Toward a Psychology of Being_, 1962), Tay & Diener (needs and subjective well-being across 123 countries -- universal but somewhat independent, JPSP, 2011), and Kenrick et al. (the evolutionary renovation of the pyramid, 2010). Set at patch as the free level; a new engine may warrant a minor at the maintainer's `bump:minor` label.
