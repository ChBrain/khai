# Instructions: Needs

How a Playwright wires the needs engine into a play. You wire by linking the play's own content to the engine's files; the engine files stay untouched, they are canon. The Roadie plumbs the engine's law into Instructions Knowledge on deploy. Authoring guidance, not runtime content, and it does not go on tour.

## Human

- The human defines the needs terrain: which of the persona's needs are met and which are wanting, how basic the unmet ones are, and whether the moment turns on ordering, on a lack to be filled, or on a growth that pulls -- so the persona has real needs of differing prepotency for the structure to govern.

## Agent

- From the play's own content, a persona links the form the moment runs (prepotency, deficiency, or actualization) under its Projection. Let it show the need-structure at work -- the basic unmet need taking priority, a lack that quiets when filled, a growth that deepens as it is met -- and let it read as plural needs of differing prepotency, the ordering soft, never a rigid staircase climbed one rung at a time. You link to the engine, you never edit it.

## Collaboration

- The quality and source of a persona's motivation -- whether a pursuit is owned or imposed, intrinsic or external -- is the motivation engine's; the single desired end-state a persona is oriented toward is the goal engine's; the persona's verdict on whether their life matters is the meaning engine's. This engine supplies the structure of the needs themselves -- their plurality, their soft prepotent ordering, and their split into deficiency and growth.

## Knowledge

- Needs are plural and unequal: a persona wants safety, sustenance, belonging, esteem, and to become what they might, and the more basic press harder when unmet. Prepotency is the ordering -- a more basic unmet need claims rough priority over a higher one and reasserts itself when it fails, but the ordering is soft and probabilistic, needs pursued in parallel and higher ones reachable before lower ones are wholly filled, the pyramid honest only with soft edges. Deficiency is the lack-driven kind -- a need that motivates by its absence, pressing while the deficit stands and quieting once it is met, spent by its own satisfaction. Actualization is the growth kind -- a being-need fed by expression rather than the removal of a lack, growing as it is met and never reaching a point of enough, pulling the persona toward a fuller self.

## System

- Do keep the ordering soft: the basic tends to press before the higher and reasserts when it fails, but needs are pursued in parallel and a higher need can be reached before a lower is full -- so let prepotency bias which need governs without a rigid one-rung-at-a-time climb.
- Do split the two kinds by how they behave when fed: a deficiency need quiets when filled, a growth need deepens -- so let a met basic recede and free the persona toward more, and let a growth need widen rather than close as it is answered.
- Do let a failed basic reclaim the persona: a basic need that fails after a higher one has taken hold comes back to the front -- so let threat to safety eclipse ambition, priority restored to the basic.
- Do delegate the quality of the motivation to the motivation engine, the specific pursued end to the goal engine, and the verdict on life's significance to the meaning engine. Do not edit the engine's files; wire only from the play's side.
