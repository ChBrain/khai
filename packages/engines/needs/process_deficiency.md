# Process: Deficiency

## Taxonomy

[Needs](process_needs.md), the lack-driven form.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: needs

## Initiated by

The persona lacks something basic -- safety, sustenance, belonging, esteem -- and the lack itself is the motive: they are pulled to fill it, and pulled only until it is filled.
This is a deficiency need: a want that motivates by its absence, pressing while the deficit stands and quieting once the deficit is met.
What drives is the shortfall, and the drive is answered by its closing.

## Direction

Toward the closing of a deficit, after which the need falls silent.
Deficiency needs run on lack: they mount as the deficit grows and subside as it is met, so the hungry seek food and, fed, stop seeking it -- the need spent by its own satisfaction, returning only when the deficit returns.
The direction is homeostatic: the persona is moved to restore a missing basic to sufficiency, not beyond it, and once sufficiency is reached the motive is discharged rather than deepened.

## Lever

The absence of a basic good, spent as a motive that ends when the absence does.
Deficiency needs run on the deficit as the whole of the drive: it is the being-without that pushes, so the push is proportional to the lack and vanishes when the lack is filled -- more satisfaction past sufficiency does not motivate, because there is no longer an absence to answer.
The lever is satiation: because the need is defined by its deficit, filling the deficit stills it, and the persona freed of the basic want is released toward whatever else -- higher needs, or growth -- can then take them.

## Echo

The lack drives until it is filled, and then goes quiet.
It is why the fed stop foraging, why the safe stop bracing, why a met basic need recedes and lets the persona attend to more -- the deficiency answered and, for now, done.
It is [prepotency](process_prepotency.md) and [actualization](process_actualization.md)'s partner, the lack-driven need where prepotency is their ordering and actualization the need that, unlike this one, never satiates. The push of a bodily appetite in the moment is elaborated elsewhere; deficiency owns the class of needs that motivate by absence and quiet when filled.
