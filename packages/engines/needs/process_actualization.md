# Process: Actualization

## Taxonomy

[Needs](process_needs.md), the growth form.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: needs

## Initiated by

With the basics roughly secured, the persona is pulled not to fill a lack but to become more -- to realize a capacity, to grow into what they could be -- and this pull is not stilled by being answered.
This is the actualization or being-need: a growth-motive that feeds on its own satisfaction, deepening rather than discharging as it is met.
What draws is not a deficit but a possibility, and meeting it opens more of the same.

## Direction

Toward the fuller expression of what the persona could become, without a point of enough.
Growth needs run opposite to deficiency: where a lack quiets when filled, a being-need grows with feeding -- the more one creates, understands, or becomes, the more one is drawn to -- so satisfaction is not an endpoint but a widening.
The direction is open: the persona is pulled forward by what they might yet be rather than back toward a missing basic, and because the possibility has no ceiling, the motive does not satiate but renews itself richer.

## Lever

A possibility of fuller being, spent as a pull that satisfaction deepens rather than ends.
Actualization runs on growth as its own reward: the being-need is fed by expression, not by the removal of a deficit, so answering it does not discharge it but enlarges it -- the appetite for becoming grows by what it feeds on.
The lever is the absence of a ceiling: because there is no fixed sufficiency to reach, the motive is sustained by its own fulfillment, pulling the persona onward toward a becoming that has no final enough.

## Echo

The growth answered grows, and the persona is drawn on toward a fuller self without end.
It is why the secured basics free a persona toward meaning and mastery, why creating begets the wish to create, why the pursuit of becoming does not close the way hunger does -- the being-need widening as it is met.
It is [prepotency](process_prepotency.md) and [deficiency](process_deficiency.md)'s partner, the growth-need where prepotency is their ordering and deficiency the need that, unlike this one, quiets when filled. The stance a persona holds toward whether their life matters is the meaning engine's, and the quality of the motivation behind the pursuit is the motivation engine's; actualization owns the class of needs that grow with feeding and never satiate.
