# Process: OCD

## Taxonomy

Anxiety and its disorders / obsessive-compulsive presentation (no khai file of its own yet).

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: ocd

## Initiated by

A thought arrives that the persona did not choose and cannot regard as noise.
Everybody has intrusions -- the impulse at the platform edge, the image of harm coming to someone loved, the doubt about the lock. What begins this loop is not the intrusion but the reading of it. In this persona the thought is not traffic; it is testimony. It appears to say something about what they are, and the first move is not dismissal but examination -- which is the one response that guarantees the thought will come back.

## Direction

From an unbidden thought, through a duty to answer it, toward an act that ends the distress and thereby preserves it.
The loop runs in a fixed order and needs all three of its movements. [Thought-action fusion](process_thought_action_fusion.md) makes the intrusion mean something, so it cannot be let go. [Inflated responsibility](process_inflated_responsibility.md) makes answering it the persona's own duty, so it cannot be delegated or deferred. [Neutralizing](process_neutralizing.md) supplies the act that discharges the demand -- and the discharge is genuine, which is exactly the problem: relief arrives, the feared outcome does not, and the persona is left with evidence that the act was necessary. Nothing in the sequence is irrational at any single step. What is broken is that the loop has no exit that does not require running it again.

## Lever

Whether the persona can leave the demand unanswered long enough for the world to answer it instead.
The lever is tolerance of an unresolved state, not insight -- the persona usually has the insight and it does not help, because knowing a fear is disproportionate is not the same as being able to act as though it were. Stage the lever as duration: the seconds between the doubt arriving and the check being performed, and what it costs to let those seconds run. Every performed act shortens the next interval; every unperformed one lengthens it.

**The twist is that the relief is real, and that is what makes the trap.** If the checking did not work, it would extinguish on its own. It works every time -- the anxiety drops, the house does not burn, the loved one is fine -- and each success is recorded as a reason the act was needed. The persona is not maintaining a superstition against the evidence; they are being taught by evidence they themselves generate.

## Echo

The sealed loop runs across three movements: [thought-action fusion](process_thought_action_fusion.md), which makes an ordinary intrusion mean something about the thinker; [inflated responsibility](process_inflated_responsibility.md), which makes answering it non-transferable; and [neutralizing](process_neutralizing.md), which discharges the demand and blocks the disconfirmation that would end it. Magical causality shared by a culture belongs to `superstition`; collective action that marks or transforms its participants belongs to `ritual`; automatized repetition acquired by reinforcement belongs to `habit`; contamination and its avoidance belong to `disgust`. This engine owns only the private loop whose relief is the reason it repeats -- authored as character architecture for dramatic production, never diagnosed onto a living human.
