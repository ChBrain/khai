# OCD: Reference

## Line of Work

OCD as **process**: a loop sealed by its own relief. The domain owns the sequence -- an intrusion read as testimony, a duty to answer it that cannot be transferred, and an act that discharges the duty and thereby prevents the fear from ever being disproved -- and not the pieces that resemble it from outside. Magical causality, ceremony, automatized repetition and contamination are each owned elsewhere and delegated to by name.

The spine is three findings that compose into a mechanism. Rachman's thought-action fusion supplies the reading: intrusions occur at the same rate in everyone, and what distinguishes this persona is the appraisal that having the thought says something about the thinker or raises the chance of the event. Salkovskis's inflated responsibility converts that meaning into an obligation: a pivotal duty to prevent harm, with no upper bound and no criterion for enough. Gillan's work on the balance between goal-directed and habitual control describes the end state: the compulsion drifts from the belief that created it and runs past the point where the persona still endorses the reason -- which is why argument aimed at the belief stops working long before the behaviour does.

## Restrictions

What the engine refuses to model, and to whom it delegates.

- **Superstition**: The engine models a private loop the persona knows is disproportionate, not a magical causality a community shares and endorses. Culturally transmitted belief in a link between act and outcome is owned by the `superstition` engine, which disclaims the obsessive-compulsive ritual by name; this engine owns what that disclaimer leaves.
- **Ritual**: The engine models a repeated private act performed to discharge a demand. Collective action that crosses a threshold and marks or transforms its participants is owned by the `ritual` engine; nothing here is ceremonial, witnessed, or shared.
- **Habit**: The engine models the demand that creates the act and the disconfirmation its success prevents. Automatized stimulus-response repetition acquired through reinforcement is owned by the `habit` engine -- and the drift toward it is the neutralizing movement's end state, cited here and delegated there rather than restated.
- **Disgust**: The engine models the loop, not one of its contents. Contamination, its appraisal and its avoidance are owned by the `disgust` engine; washing appears here only as one form the neutralizing act takes.
- **Emotional processing and exposure**: Foa and Kozak's _"Emotional processing of fear"_ (1986) is already carried by the `ptsd` engine, and the mechanism of therapeutic exposure is a treatment account rather than a persona's. This engine cites neither and models the blocked disconfirmation from the persona's side only.
- **Diagnosis**: The engine models a persona's loop for dramatic production. It is authored character architecture, never a clinical assessment, screening instrument, or diagnosis of a living person, and it carries no criteria, thresholds, or duration rules.

## Encoding

Source to constraint, per file.

- **[ocd](process_ocd.md)** (the root): The loop whose relief is the reason it repeats, running in a fixed order and requiring all three movements to close. Anchored by Rachman (the appraisal that starts it) and Salkovskis (the duty that sustains it).
- **[thought-action fusion](process_thought_action_fusion.md)** (movement): An ordinary intrusion appraised as testimony about the thinker, morally or probabilistically. Anchored by Rachman.
- **[inflated responsibility](process_inflated_responsibility.md)** (movement): A pivotal duty to prevent harm, non-transferable and without a threshold at which it is discharged. Anchored by Salkovskis.
- **[neutralizing](process_neutralizing.md)** (movement): The act that discharges the demand, blocks the disconfirmation, and drifts from the belief that created it. Anchored by Gillan.
