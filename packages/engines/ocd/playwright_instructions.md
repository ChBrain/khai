# Instructions: OCD

How a Playwright wires the ocd engine into a play. You wire by linking the play's own content to the engine's files; the engine files stay untouched, they are canon. The Roadie plumbs the engine's law into Instructions Knowledge on deploy. Authoring guidance, not runtime content, and it does not go on tour.

## Human

- The human sets what the loop is about: which movement the persona runs, what the intrusion's content is and therefore what the persona most fears being, who is placed to offer an exit and how the loop closes it -- and decides, as author, where the scene lets the cost show without turning the persona into their diagnosis.

## Agent

- From the play's own content, a persona links the ocd movement it runs (thought-action fusion, inflated responsibility, or neutralizing) under its Projection. Let the movement set what the persona cannot leave alone and what it costs them in time, not merely how anxious they appear. Route what surrounds the loop -- the contamination content, the automatized drift, the exhausted overriding -- through the disgust, habit and self-control engines, which own it. You link to the engine, you never edit it.

## Collaboration

- The superstition engine owns magical causality a community shares and endorses; this engine's persona knows the belief is disproportionate and cannot act on that knowledge. The ritual engine owns collective action that marks or transforms; nothing here is ceremonial or witnessed. The habit engine owns automatized repetition acquired by reinforcement, which is where a long-running compulsion drifts -- cite the drift, do not restate the mechanism. The disgust engine owns contamination; washing is one content this loop can take, not the loop itself.

## Knowledge

- OCD is a loop sealed by its own relief, and the order of its movements is the mechanism. Thought-action fusion reads an intrusion everybody has as testimony about the person having it, so it cannot be dismissed. Inflated responsibility makes answering it a pivotal duty that cannot be transferred, deferred, or declared finished. Neutralizing supplies an act that discharges the demand -- and because the act works, the feared outcome never arrives to disprove the fear, while the act itself drifts from the belief that started it and runs on its own.

## System

- Do stage the relief as genuine. The check works, the anxiety drops, the harm does not occur; that is the trap, not a failure of the persona's reasoning.
- Do give the persona their insight. They usually know the fear is disproportionate, and knowing does not help -- a scene where explanation cures the loop contradicts the engine.
- Do make the cost time. The loop is expensive in hours and in what those hours were owed to, and that is more legible on stage than visible distress.
- Do let the content track the values: intrusions land on what the persona would least want to be, so a caring persona gets thoughts of harm. Do not play the content as a wish.
- Do not write the loop as a checklist of symptoms or carry criteria, thresholds or duration rules; it is a character's loop in a scene, never a diagnosis of a living person. Do not edit the engine's files; wire only from the play's side.
