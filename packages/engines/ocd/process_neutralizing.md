# Process: Neutralizing

## Taxonomy

[OCD](process_ocd.md), the seal -- an act that discharges the demand and, by working, prevents the fear from ever being disproved.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: ocd

## Initiated by

The duty is unbearable and there is something the persona can do about it right now.
The act may be visible or entirely internal: the lock checked, the hands washed, the message reread, the phrase repeated silently, the mental review of what was said at the meeting. What all of them share is a function rather than a form -- each one ends a demand that had become intolerable, and each one delivers. The relief is not imagined. It arrives, quickly, and it is the last honest thing in the loop.

## Direction

From an intolerable demand, through an act that discharges it, toward a fear that can no longer be tested.
The movement runs on a mechanism the persona cannot see from inside, because seeing it would require the one experiment they never run. Every performed act removes the occasion on which the feared outcome would have failed to happen anyway. The house was never going to burn; the check is credited with the house not burning. So the belief is never disconfirmed, and the act is reinforced by an outcome it did not cause. Gillan's finding adds the second drift: over time the act detaches from the belief that started it, becoming habit-like and running past the point where the persona still endorses the reason. Asked why they are doing it, a persona late in this movement may not have an answer that satisfies even them.

## Lever

Whether the act can be left undone long enough for the world to supply the disconfirmation.
The lever is the experiment, and it is unpleasant by design: not performing the act and remaining in the unresolved state while nothing happens. Stage the cost accurately -- the anxiety does not fall gently, it rises first, and the persona has to sit inside a rising curve on the promise that it turns. Stage too the pull of the alternative, which is a single small act that ends it now.

**The twist is that the compulsion outlives the belief that created it.** By the time it is entrenched, the persona may no longer think the thought is true and may still be unable to stop, because the act is no longer answering the fear -- it is running on its own. That is what makes reassurance and argument fail so completely at this stage: they are addressed to a belief the behaviour has already left behind.

## Echo

Neutralizing discharges what [thought-action fusion](process_thought_action_fusion.md) made meaningful and [inflated responsibility](process_inflated_responsibility.md) made obligatory, and by discharging it keeps both alive. Automatized stimulus-response repetition acquired by reinforcement belongs to `habit`, which owns the drift this movement ends in but not the demand that starts it; collective action that marks or transforms its participants belongs to `ritual`; contamination and its avoidance belong to `disgust`; the effortful override of an urge belongs to `self-control`. This movement owns only the act performed to discharge an obsession, and the disconfirmation that its success prevents.
