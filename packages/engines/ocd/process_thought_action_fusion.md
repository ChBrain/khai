# Process: Thought-Action Fusion

## Taxonomy

[OCD](process_ocd.md), the reading -- an intrusion taken as evidence about the person who had it.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: ocd

## Initiated by

An unwanted thought arrives, and the persona asks what it means that they thought it.
Rachman's account is precise about the two moves that follow, and they are separable. In the moral form, having the thought is treated as close to doing the deed: to picture harming someone is to have discovered something about one's character. In the likelihood form, having the thought is treated as raising the chance of the event: to imagine the crash makes the crash nearer. Either way the intrusion stops being mental weather and becomes information, and information must be dealt with.

## Direction

From an ordinary intrusion, through an appraisal of what having it says, toward a content the persona cannot put down.
The movement is an interpretation running on material everybody produces. Non-clinical populations report the same intrusions at the same frequency; what differs is the reading. Once the thought is testimony, suppression follows, and suppression raises its rate -- so the persona now has more of the thought they most wanted less of, which they read as further evidence. The content sharpens toward whatever the persona would least want to be: harm to a child, violence toward someone loved, a blasphemy, an act against their own values. The intrusions are ego-dystonic by construction. They are drawn from the exact material the persona would never endorse, which is why they land.

## Lever

Whether the thought can be granted the status of noise while still being unwelcome.
The lever is not reassurance about character -- reassurance is consumed and returns nothing, because it answers the content rather than the reading. What shifts is the status of the thought itself. Stage the failure of reassurance explicitly: a persona asking to be told they are not a monster, being told, and needing to ask again within the hour.

**The twist is that the thoughts are worst where the values are strongest.** A persona indifferent to a child's safety does not generate intrusions about harming one. The content tracks what the persona most fears being, so the intrusions are, read correctly, evidence of exactly the opposite of what they seem to prove -- and that argument, however true, does not dislodge the reading, because the reading is not a conclusion the persona reached by argument.

## Echo

Thought-action fusion supplies the meaning that [inflated responsibility](process_inflated_responsibility.md) then makes the persona's own to answer, and that [neutralizing](process_neutralizing.md) discharges. A magical link between act and outcome shared as a cultural belief belongs to `superstition`; the general looping of distressing content belongs to `rumination`; the suppression of unwanted material belongs to `the-unconscious`. This movement owns only the appraisal of an intrusion as testimony about the thinker.
