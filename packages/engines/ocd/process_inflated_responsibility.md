# Process: Inflated Responsibility

## Taxonomy

[OCD](process_ocd.md), the duty -- a pivotal obligation to prevent harm that nobody assigned and nobody can take back.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: ocd

## Initiated by

The persona finds themselves pivotally placed to prevent an outcome, and cannot find the person who placed them there.
Salkovskis's move is the one that turns a distressing thought into an obligation: the persona believes they have power to bring about or prevent a crucial negative outcome, so failing to act is not neutral -- it is causing. The asymmetry is total. An omission that leads to harm counts fully; a precaution that prevents nothing costs, in the persona's reckoning, almost nothing. Under that arithmetic the rational act is always to check once more.

## Direction

From a possible harm, through a duty that cannot be transferred, toward an obligation with no threshold at which it is discharged.
The movement runs by removing exits. Others cannot be trusted to have checked, because their not-checking would still be the persona's fault. Probability offers no relief, because the standard is not likelihood but preventability: if it _could_ be stopped and was not, the persona did it. And there is no criterion for enough. A lock checked once is checked; a lock checked because harm must not happen has no count at which harm becomes impossible, so the persona is searching for a certainty the world does not issue.

## Lever

Whether responsibility can be shared, bounded, or declined at all.
The lever is the transferability of the duty, and it should be staged as social rather than internal: a partner who says they locked it, a colleague who signs off, a rule that assigns the task elsewhere. Each offers an exit, and the movement's work is to close it -- the partner might be wrong, the sign-off does not cover this case, the rule does not know what the persona knows.

**The twist is that the persona is not selfish or fearful for themselves.** The harm they are preventing is almost always someone else's, and the duty they cannot decline is a duty of care. This movement takes the most creditable thing about a persona -- that they will not let harm come to others through their inaction -- and gives it no upper bound. Played correctly it is exhausting to watch precisely because the persona is being good.

## Echo

Inflated responsibility takes what [thought-action fusion](process_thought_action_fusion.md) made meaningful and makes it the persona's own to answer, which is what [neutralizing](process_neutralizing.md) then discharges. Duty owed to a role or an office belongs to `obligation`; the anticipation of self-blame for an outcome belongs to `guilt`; the general appraisal of a threat against one's resources belongs to `coping`. This movement owns only the unbounded, non-transferable duty to prevent.
