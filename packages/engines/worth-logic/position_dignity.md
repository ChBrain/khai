# Position: Dignity

## Taxonomy

[Worth-Logic](position_worth_logic.md), worth inherent and inalienable -- the self anchored from within.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: worth-logic

## Has

A specific [worth-logic](position_worth_logic.md): the conviction that worth is inherent, equal, and inalienable -- each person's simply by being a person, and not in anyone else's gift (Leung & Cohen's dignity culture).
Because worth is intrinsic, it is internally anchored: the persona's value does not rise with praise or fall with insult, and its standard is its own conscience, not the crowd's opinion.
It is the logic of the sticks-and-stones creed -- words cannot touch what does not depend on words.

## Orders

Hold your worth as your own, and let the affront slide.
The dignity logic orders the persona to locate its value within, unmoved by others' regard: to meet an insult as powerless -- the fault of the one who throws it, not a wound to be answered -- and to take its disputes to inner standards and impartial law rather than to personal combat.
It steadies the persona from within, free of the need to be granted or to defend its worth.

## Loses

The vigilance and the responsiveness the other logics keep.
Holding worth inviolable, the persona forgoes the honor-holder's readiness to answer a challenge and the face-holder's fine attunement to its standing in the group -- it can be slow to see that in another's world an unanswered insult really does cost, and can read as weakness or coldness what those worlds read as necessity or grace.
What it trades is the social sensitivity of the other logics for an inner freedom -- and it may leave real reputational damage unaddressed because, by its own logic, damage should not be possible.

## Drives

On that inherent worth:
rides it -- holds its value from within, deflects the insult, and trusts conscience and law over the duel -- or, under a logic-crossing pressure, is drawn toward defending a worth its own creed says needs no defense, or wounded by a slight it believes should not be able to wound.
The dignity baseline holds underneath: worth inherent, not [honor's](position_honor.md) precarious claim nor [face's](position_face_worth.md) granted standing. That the softening of an interactional face-threat is elsewhere is the [anchor's](position_worth_logic.md) note; this face owns the inherent-worth logic.
