# Position: Face

## Taxonomy

[Worth-Logic](position_worth_logic.md), worth conferred by position -- the self held by propriety and harmony.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: worth-logic

## Has

A specific [worth-logic](position_worth_logic.md): worth as conferred by others according to one's place in a stable social hierarchy, held by meeting the obligations of one's role (Leung & Cohen's face culture).
Because worth is granted by the group and indexed to position, it is neither inherent like dignity nor won by combat like honor: it is maintained by propriety, humility, and the smooth working of the order, and it is diminished by disrupting harmony or failing the duties of one's station.
It is the logic of face given and kept -- a standing the group extends, and the person upholds by knowing and filling their place.

## Orders

Keep your place, and keep the peace that holds it.
The face logic orders the persona to maintain its standing by propriety and role: to show the humility and deference the order expects, to avoid the open conflict and self-assertion that would breach harmony, and to protect not only its own face but the face of others, since worth is a fabric the whole group holds. An affront is met less by retaliation than by restoring the order -- saving face on both sides, repairing the harmony the slight tore.
It holds the persona within the pattern, its worth secured by fitting it.

## Loses

The self-assertion and the inner independence the other logics allow.
Holding worth as granted by position, the persona forgoes the honor-holder's licensed retaliation and the dignity-holder's indifference to others' regard -- it cannot breach the order to defend or declare itself without endangering the very face it seeks to keep, and it can be bound by hierarchy and propriety where another logic would push back.
What it trades is the freedom to assert against the group for a worth woven into the group's harmony -- and it can be held in an unjust order by a logic that makes disruption itself the disgrace.

## Drives

On that granted worth:
rides it -- keeps its place, upholds propriety and humility, protects its own and others' face, and meets a slight by restoring harmony rather than trading blows -- or, under strain, breaches the order to assert itself and forfeits the standing that breach costs, or upholds a place its own interest chafes against.
The face baseline holds underneath: worth conferred by position, not [dignity's](position_dignity.md) inherent worth nor [honor's](position_honor.md) worth-by-defense. The interactional face-work of softening a face-threatening act in the moment is the face engine's; this face owns the cultural worth-logic that face-culture runs on.
