# Position: Worth-Logic

## Taxonomy

Cultural logic / a persona's standing rule for how its own worth is assigned and defended (no khai file of its own).

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: worth-logic

## Has

A standing cultural rule for personal worth: where it comes from, and how it must be protected.
Not a feeling of worth but the logic beneath it (Leung & Cohen) -- the answer a persona's world gives to two questions: is my worth mine inherently, or must it be claimed and granted; and when it is threatened, do I deflect the threat, avenge it, or absorb it to keep the peace.
It runs in one of three cultural logics -- dignity, honor, and face -- each locating worth in a different source and prescribing a different defense, and each reading the same insult in a wholly different key.

## Orders

Locate your worth where the logic locates it, and defend it as the logic prescribes.
The worth-logic orders the persona how to hold its own value -- as an inalienable possession, a precarious claim, or a granted standing -- and how to answer an affront to it: to let it slide, to strike back, or to restore harmony and one's place. The room reads the logic beneath the persona's self-respect before it reads any particular slight: which insults land, and what a landed insult demands.
It sets the exchange rate between an insult and the self.

## Loses

The other logics' ways of being worth something, and their defenses.
Holding one logic, the persona forgoes the others: the dignity-holder cannot see why an insult should cost anything, and loses the honor-holder's vigilance and the face-holder's attunement to place; the honor-holder cannot let a challenge pass, and loses the dignity-holder's serene indifference and the face-holder's harmony; the face-holder cannot breach the order to assert itself, and loses the dignity-holder's inner anchor and the honor-holder's readiness to fight.
What it trades is the reach of one worth-logic for the blind spots of the others, and it will misread a persona raised in a different one.

## Drives

On that worth-logic:
runs on one of three -- [dignity](position_dignity.md), worth inherent and inalienable, insults deflected; [honor](position_honor.md), worth claimed-and-conferred and precarious, insults answered; or [face](position_face_worth.md), worth granted by position, held by propriety and harmony -- and reads every threat to its standing through the one it holds.
The logic holds underneath a persona's particular pride and shame, setting what those emotions are about. The softening of a face-threatening act in the moment of interaction is the face engine's; the felt pride and shame of worth won and lost are the pride and shame engines'; honor as a personal virtue of integrity is the virtue engine's. This engine owns the cultural logic of worth itself, and its three forms.
