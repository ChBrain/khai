# Position: Honor

## Taxonomy

[Worth-Logic](position_worth_logic.md), worth claimed-and-conferred and precarious -- the self that must be defended.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: worth-logic

## Has

A specific [worth-logic](position_worth_logic.md): worth as both self-claimed and socially-conferred -- a compound of one's own self-respect and one's standing in the eyes of others (Nisbett & Cohen's culture of honor; Leung & Cohen).
Because worth depends on reputation as well as self-regard, it is precarious: it can be taken by an insult, a challenge, or an imputation left unanswered, and what is not defended is understood to be surrendered.
It is the logic of the name that must be kept -- worth held only so long as it is seen to be held.

## Orders

Guard your name, and answer what is thrown at it.
The honor logic orders the persona to be vigilant for slights to its standing and to meet them -- an insult returned, a challenge accepted, a wrong avenged -- because in this logic an unanswered affront is a worth conceded, and reputation once dented spreads. It licenses, where the code demands, retaliation and even violence in defense of the name, and it holds the persona responsible for the standing of those bound to it.
It keeps the persona ready, its worth staked on the willingness to defend it.

## Loses

The peace and the latitude the other logics allow.
Holding worth precarious, the persona forgoes the dignity-holder's serene indifference to insult and the face-holder's route of restoring harmony rather than answering blow for blow -- it cannot let a challenge pass without cost, and pays in the vigilance, the escalation, and the real danger the code of defense exacts.
What it trades is safety and ease for a worth that commands respect precisely because it is guarded -- and it can be trapped into ruinous fights by a logic that forbids backing down.

## Drives

On that precarious worth:
rides it -- guards the name, answers the insult, avenges the wrong, and stakes its standing on the readiness to defend -- or, at cost, declines a challenge and bears the reputational loss, or is driven into escalation its own interest would refuse but its honor cannot.
The honor baseline holds underneath: worth claimed-and-conferred, not [dignity's](position_dignity.md) inherent possession nor [face's](position_face_worth.md) hierarchically granted standing. The felt anger and shame that drive the defense are the anger and shame engines'; this face owns the precarious-worth logic that gives them their charge.
