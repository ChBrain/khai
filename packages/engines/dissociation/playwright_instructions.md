# Instructions: Dissociation

How a Playwright wires the dissociation engine into a play. You wire by linking the play's own content to the engine's files; the engine files stay untouched, they are canon. The Roadie plumbs the engine's law into Instructions Knowledge on deploy. Authoring guidance, not runtime content, and it does not go on tour.

## Human

- The human sets the depth and its history: which movement the persona runs, what closed both exits and how early, who is present now and whether the present is steady enough for anything to be assembled -- and decides, as author, where the scene shows the cost without turning the persona into a spectacle.

## Agent

- From the play's own content, a persona links the dissociation movement it runs (depersonalization, an amnesic barrier, or a structural split) under its Projection. Let the movement govern what reaches the persona and what they can report, not how distressed they appear. Route the trauma's own sequelae -- the numbing, the intrusion, the estrangement -- through the ptsd engine, which owns them. You link to the engine, you never edit it.

## Collaboration

- The-unconscious owns material held down by repression in one unified mind; this engine owns pieces that never arrive together, which is the opposite mechanism. The ptsd engine owns what follows a trauma; this engine owns the disconnection during and after it. The defense engine owns the category and its maturity ordering; this engine owns the mechanism at depth. The absorption composite owns attention narrowed onto something engrossing, the ordinary end of the same scale; this engine begins where the narrowing stops being chosen.

## Knowledge

- Dissociation is a failure of integration under an experience that can neither be held nor left, and its three movements are depths rather than stages. Depersonalization is a step outside the self taken while fully aware, with reality testing intact -- the persona can give a complete account and feel they were not in it. An amnesic barrier is a stretch of experience encoded in one state and unreachable from another, so time goes missing rather than memory going vague. A structural split is division into parts that do not share a first person, arising where integration was never assembled rather than where a formed person broke.

## System

- Do stage it as flat from inside and alarming only from outside. The persona's account of the worst hour will be calm, detailed, and told as though about somebody else; the fear belongs to whoever is listening.
- Do let it have worked. This is the response that let a child stay attached to someone they could not afford to see clearly. A scene that plays it as pure damage has misread it.
- Do make the cost structural rather than dramatic: the covering, the inferring, the avoidance of situations where a gap might show, and what those hours were owed to.
- Do treat the difficult part as protective when staging a structural split. The part that frightens people is usually the one fixed in the original defence, and a scene that treats it as the problem to remove has misread the system.
- Do not write it as spectacle or as a reveal, and do not carry criteria, thresholds or duration rules; it is a character's disconnection in a scene, never a diagnosis of a living person. Do not edit the engine's files; wire only from the play's side.
