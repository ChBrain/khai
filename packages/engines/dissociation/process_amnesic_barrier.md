# Process: Amnesic Barrier

## Taxonomy

[Dissociation](process_dissociation.md), the middle depth -- a stretch of experience walled from the state that could reach it.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: dissociation

## Initiated by

The experience is encoded in a state the persona is not in afterwards.
Putnam's account is what makes this movement precise, and it is not a theory of forgetting. Experience is laid down within discrete behavioural states, and access runs along the state rather than along the timeline. What happened in one state is not degraded or overwritten; it is simply not reachable from another. The persona has not lost the material. They are standing on the wrong side of a wall, and there is no felt sensation of a wall -- only a stretch of clock with nothing attached to it.

## Direction

From an experience encoded in one state, through a barrier that does not announce itself, toward a life with load-bearing holes in it.
The movement runs by removing continuity rather than content. Hours are missing, then evenings; the persona finds work they did and does not recall doing it, is told about a conversation they held, arrives somewhere without the journey. Because there is no sensation of forgetting, the gaps are usually noticed from outside first -- by someone who says "you were there" and gets a look in return. The persona learns to cover: to infer, to agree, to reconstruct from evidence, and to avoid situations where the covering might fail. That labour becomes a substantial and invisible part of how they live.

## Lever

Whether the persona can be brought into the state that holds the material, safely and by consent.
The lever is state, not effort -- trying harder to remember does nothing, and pressing on the gap directly tends to widen it. What reaches it is context: a place, a smell, a posture, a mood that belongs to the far side. Stage the danger of that honestly. The material arrives with the state, at its own intensity and on its own schedule, and it does not check whether the present moment is a good time.

**The twist is that the persona is not looking for the missing time.** From inside, the account is continuous; the mind reports smoothly across the gaps the way vision reports smoothly across the blind spot. So the persona is rarely troubled by what is absent -- they are troubled by the consequences of its absence, and they experience those as unreliability, or as other people being strange about things they insist did not happen.

## Echo

The amnesic barrier is the middle depth of [dissociation](process_dissociation.md), deeper than [depersonalization](process_depersonalization.md) and short of [a structural split](process_structural_split.md), which is what the barrier becomes when both sides hold a person. Material kept from awareness by repression belongs to `the-unconscious`, where the mechanism is a push and the content is one mind's; ordinary encoding, storage and decay belong to `memory`; the intrusive re-experiencing of a trauma belongs to `ptsd`. This movement owns only the state-bound unavailability of what was fully experienced.
