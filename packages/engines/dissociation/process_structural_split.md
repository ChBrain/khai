# Process: Structural Split

## Taxonomy

[Dissociation](process_dissociation.md), the deepest -- a division into parts that do not share a first person.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: dissociation

## Initiated by

The division has to hold, because the pressure started before there was one person to divide and did not stop.
Van der Hart and colleagues describe the shape: a part organised to carry on with daily life, avoiding whatever would breach it, and one or more parts fixed in the defensive response of the original events. The condition is developmental. Integration is not something a person is born holding; it is assembled, and it is assembled out of continuity and safety. Where those are absent early enough and long enough, the assembly does not complete, and what results is not a person who broke into parts but a person who was never gathered into one.

## Direction

From a division that never resolved, through parts with their own vantage and their own memory, toward a life run by more than one first person.
The movement runs through ownership. The part carrying daily life does not merely avoid the material; it experiences the other parts' actions as not-mine, and can say so honestly. Handwriting differs, preferences differ, a promise made by one is not felt as binding by another. The parts are not characters the persona is playing and not moods -- each has a perspective from which its own account is simply what happened. Staged carelessly this becomes spectacle; staged correctly it is mostly mundane and mostly hidden, because the part organised for daily life is organised precisely to keep it so.

## Lever

Whether the parts can come to know of one another and negotiate, rather than one being eliminated.
The lever is cooperation, and the direction of care matters: nothing here is improved by a part being overridden, evicted or argued out of existence, because each is carrying something the system needed carried. Stage the work as diplomatic and slow -- information passed across, agreements about who holds what, a gradual widening of what any one part can afford to know.

**The twist is that the part everyone finds difficult is the one doing the protecting.** The angry part, the reckless one, the one that frightens people, is typically the part fixed in the original defence -- still running the response that was correct at the time. It is not the damage; it is the thing that absorbed the damage so the rest could go to school. A scene that treats it as the problem to be removed has misread the system it is in.

## Echo

The structural split is the deepest reach of [dissociation](process_dissociation.md), and it is what [the amnesic barrier](process_amnesic_barrier.md) becomes when both sides of the wall hold a person rather than a stretch of time. The category of defensive operations and their maturity belongs to `defense`; material repressed from a single unified mind belongs to `the-unconscious`; the continuity of a self across time belongs to `identity`. This movement owns only the division into parts that do not share a first person.
