# Process: Depersonalization

## Taxonomy

[Dissociation](process_dissociation.md), the shallowest depth -- a step outside the self that is having the experience, taken while fully aware.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: dissociation

## Initiated by

The experience continues and the persona stops being inside it.
There is no gap and no confusion. The persona sees, hears and understands everything; they can answer questions, follow instructions, and later give a detailed account. What has changed is the vantage. They are watching from a little way off -- above and behind, commonly -- and the body doing the talking is legible as theirs without being inhabited. The world may go with it: surfaces flatten, colour drains, the room acquires the quality of a set. Reality testing is intact throughout, which is why the persona can say the sentence "I know this is happening to me" and mean both halves of it.

## Direction

From full presence, through a widening distance from the self in the scene, toward an account of one's own life given in the third person.
The movement runs on availability rather than on collapse. Because it costs nothing visible, it can be maintained indefinitely, and it generalises: what began at the worst hour becomes the ordinary way of attending. Persons in this state function -- often conspicuously well, because the feeling that would interfere is at a distance too. The trouble surfaces sideways: an inability to say what they want, a sense of watching their own choices being made, a flatness they report as more frightening than fear because there is nothing in it to push against.

## Lever

Whether anything can land closely enough to be felt as happening to _them_ rather than observed.
The lever is contact, not confrontation, and it should be staged as small and physical: cold water, another person's hand, a task that requires the body to be present. Argument does not reach it, because the persona is not mistaken about anything and will agree with every word from the same distance.

**The twist is that it is invisible and often rewarded.** A persona depersonalizing through a crisis is the calm one, the capable one, the person everybody is glad was there. They will be thanked for it. Nothing in the room registers that they were not present for the thing they handled so well, and they may not have language for it themselves until years later.

## Echo

Depersonalization is the shallowest depth of [dissociation](process_dissociation.md), shading toward [the amnesic barrier](process_amnesic_barrier.md) when the distance takes the memory with it. Attention narrowed onto something engrossing belongs to `absorption`; the emotional numbing that follows a trauma belongs to `ptsd`; the flat unavailability of pleasure belongs to `anhedonia`. This movement owns only the vantage stepping outside while awareness stays whole.
