# Process: Dissociation

## Taxonomy

Trauma and its sequelae / failures of integration (no khai file of its own yet).

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: dissociation

## Initiated by

An experience arrives that cannot be held and cannot be left.
The entry condition is not intensity alone but the closing of both exits. Something is happening that the persona has no capacity to absorb, and no permission or means to escape -- a child in a house they cannot leave, a body in a room it cannot get out of, a fact about someone needed that cannot be known and survived at the same time. Fight and flight are unavailable. What remains is a third option the mind takes without being asked: it stops assembling the experience into one continuous person.

## Direction

From an unabsorbable experience, through a self that stops arriving in one piece, toward a life organised around a discontinuity.
The movement is subtraction of integration, not addition of a defence, and it runs at three depths that are not stages. [Depersonalization](process_depersonalization.md) takes the persona a step outside the one having the experience; [an amnesic barrier](process_amnesic_barrier.md) walls a stretch of it off from the state that could reach it; [a structural split](process_structural_split.md) divides the person into parts that do not share a first person. A persona may run only the shallowest for a lifetime and never approach the others. What runs deeper is not a worse episode but a longer history: pressure that started early, came from someone needed, and did not stop.

## Lever

Whether there is anywhere safe enough, for long enough, that the pieces can be assembled without the assembly itself being unbearable.
The lever is not insight and not the recovery of a memory. It is conditions -- a present that stays predictable long enough for integration to be affordable. Stage it as slow and as reversible: a persona who has begun to arrive in one piece will leave again under pressure, and the leaving is not a relapse in character but the same mechanism doing the same job.

**The twist is that it worked.** This is the hardest thing to stage honestly and the thing that makes the persona legible. Dissociation is not a malfunction that befell them; it is the response that let a child stay attached to a caregiver they could not afford to see clearly, or let a body get through an hour it could not have got through present. The cost arrives decades later, in a life organised around a gap. The persona is carrying a solution that outlived its problem, and asking them to give it up is asking them to trust that the problem is over.

## Echo

The failure of integration runs at three depths: [depersonalization](process_depersonalization.md), the step outside the self that is living it; [the amnesic barrier](process_amnesic_barrier.md), the stretch walled off from the state that could reach it; and [the structural split](process_structural_split.md), the division into parts that do not share a first person. Material kept from awareness by repression belongs to `the-unconscious`; the numbing and estrangement that follow a trauma belong to `ptsd`; the category of defensive operations and their maturity belongs to `defense`; attention narrowed onto something engrossing belongs to `absorption`. This engine owns only the failure of the pieces to arrive together -- authored as character architecture for dramatic production, never diagnosed onto a living human.
