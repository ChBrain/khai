# Position: Structural Model

## Taxonomy

Metapsychology / the tripartite psyche in dynamic conflict (no khai file of its own).

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: structural-model

## Has

A standing three-way structure beneath a persona's inner life: three agencies in conflict (Freud's second topography).
Not the conscious self a persona tells itself it is, but the deeper architecture the identity rests on -- the id that wants, the superego that forbids, and the ego caught between them and the world, brokering what can actually be done.
The three are not equals at peace but forces in tension: a drive presses for discharge, a conscience presses back, and reality permits only some of what either demands -- and the persona's inner life is the working-out of that pressure.

## Orders

Read the persona as a conflict of agencies, not a single will.
The structural model orders the room to see three claims at once behind a persona's act -- what the drive demands, what the conscience forbids, and what the ego can broker with reality -- and to locate a given moment in which agency is pressing hardest. The persona is not of one mind; it is a negotiation, often unconscious, among three.
It sets the deep structure the persona's surface choices are the compromise of.

## Loses

The simplicity of a persona that simply wants what it wants.
Held as three agencies, the persona forgoes the clean picture of a unified chooser -- every act becomes a compromise-formation, pulled by a drive it may not own, checked by a conscience it did not consciously write, and settled by an ego serving a reality neither of the others respects. Nothing the persona does is purely its own.
What it trades is the fiction of a single self for the truth of an inner parliament, and pays in the conflict that never fully resolves.

## Drives

On that three-agency structure:
runs through whichever agency presses hardest -- the [id](position_id.md), the reservoir of drives ruled by the pleasure principle; the [superego](position_superego.md), the internalized conscience and ideal that forbids and judges; or the [ego](position_ego.md), the executive that serves reality and mediates the other two -- while the standing conflict among all three holds beneath.
The structure is metapsychological, not the conscious self-story the identity engine owns. The ego's specific impulse-override is the self-control engine's, its defensive manoeuvres the defense engine's; the moral trait the superego internalizes is the moral-identity engine's; the reward value of what the id craves is the reward engine's. This engine owns the three-agency structure and its standing conflict.
