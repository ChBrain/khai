# Position: Ego

## Taxonomy

[Structural Model](position_structural_model.md), the executive that serves reality -- the agency that mediates.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: structural-model

## Has

The executive agency, ruled by the reality principle: the mediator that stands between the id, the superego, and the outer world (Freud).
It is the only agency that touches all three -- it feels the id's demand for discharge, hears the superego's prohibition, and reckons with what reality will actually permit -- and its task is to find, moment by moment, a course that serves the drive without wrecking the persona on reality or conscience.
It defers, redirects, and defends; where the id knows only the pleasure principle, the ego knows delay, detour, and the cost of things.

## Orders

Broker a course that reality will bear.
The ego orders the persona to mediate -- to check the id's demand against the world's facts and the superego's rules, to postpone gratification until it is safe, to find a substitute path when the direct one is barred, and to defend against a demand or a judgment too painful to face head-on. It serves the drive, but by the reality principle: it wants what the id wants, only survivably.
It is the agency that acts, because it is the only one that reckons with consequence.

## Loses

The peace of answering to only one master.
Caught between three claimants, the ego forgoes the id's freedom to simply want, the superego's certainty in simply forbidding, and any settled rest -- it serves three demands that do not agree, and is anxious in proportion to their conflict, for it is the ego that feels the danger when a drive threatens to break through or a judgment threatens to crush. It is, in Freud's image, a rider on a horse it only partly controls.
What it trades is inner peace for agency: it alone can act, and pays in the strain of brokering what will not be reconciled.

## Drives

On that mediating executive:
brokers the conflict -- checking the [id's](position_id.md) demand and the [superego's](position_superego.md) prohibition against reality, deferring, redirecting, and defending -- or is overwhelmed when the pressure from either exceeds what it can hold, and the compromise breaks down.
The ego is the central agency of the [structural model](position_structural_model.md), the mediator beneath the persona's every act. Its specific in-the-moment override of an impulse is the self-control engine's, and its unconscious defensive manoeuvres against anxiety are the defense engine's; this face owns the whole mediating executive those are functions of.
