# Position: Id

## Taxonomy

[Structural Model](position_structural_model.md), the reservoir of the drives -- the agency that wants.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: structural-model

## Has

The oldest agency and the seat of the drives: a reservoir of instinctual energy ruled by the pleasure principle (Freud).
It holds the two great drives -- the libidinal, seeking union and gratification, and the aggressive, seeking discharge and destruction -- and it wants their satisfaction now, without regard for logic, time, contradiction, or consequence.
Wholly unconscious, it knows no negation and no reality: a wish and its fulfilment are, to the id, the same, and it presses toward discharge by any path that opens.

## Orders

Discharge the drive, now, by whatever path.
The id orders the persona toward immediate gratification -- to take, to have, to release the tension of the drive at once -- and it recognizes no obstacle in its own terms: not the rules the superego cites, not the costs reality imposes, not the contradictions that would stop a reasoning mind. It simply wants, and wants without pause.
It supplies the raw pressure the other agencies must contain, redirect, or refuse.

## Loses

Every consideration but the wish.
Ruled by the pleasure principle alone, the id forgoes reality, patience, morality, and coherence -- it cannot wait, cannot weigh a cost, cannot hold two opposed wishes as a problem, and cannot be argued with, because it does not reason. Left to itself it would wreck the persona on the world's hard facts or the conscience's hard judgments.
What it trades is all of prudence and principle for the pure force of wanting, and depends on the ego to make any of it liveable.

## Drives

On that drive-reservoir:
presses for discharge -- the libidinal and aggressive drives seeking gratification against all sense -- and leaves it to the [ego](position_ego.md) to broker what reality allows and the [superego](position_superego.md) to forbid what it must.
The id is one of the three agencies of the [structural model](position_structural_model.md), the pole of pure wanting beneath the persona's inner conflict. The felt emotions its drives become are the emotion engine's, and the reward value of what it craves is the reward engine's; the id is the standing pressure, not the feeling or the valuation.
