# Position: Superego

## Taxonomy

[Structural Model](position_structural_model.md), the internalized moral authority -- the agency that forbids and judges.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: structural-model

## Has

The moral agency, heir to the parental prohibitions: an internalized authority that forbids, judges, and sets the standard (Freud).
It has two faces -- a conscience that punishes transgression, and even the forbidden wish, with guilt; and an ego-ideal that holds up the persona it ought to be, rewarding it with pride when it reaches and lashing it with shame when it falls short.
Formed by taking the parents' prohibitions inside, it can be harsher than any real parent was, judging the persona from within with an authority it cannot appeal.

## Orders

Forbid the wish, and measure the self against the ideal.
The superego orders the persona to renounce what the id craves -- not only the act but the wish behind it -- and to hold itself to the standard of the ego-ideal, punishing every shortfall. It answers a transgression, real or merely wished, with guilt, and a failure to be what it should with shame, and it does so from inside, with no court of appeal.
It is the internal voice of prohibition and aspiration at once, the agency that makes the persona answerable to itself.

## Loses

The freedom from an inner judge.
Carrying an internalized authority, the persona forgoes any escape from judgment -- it cannot outrun a conscience that lives inside it, and a severe superego will punish it for wishes it never acted on and hold it to an ideal it can never reach, souring even its successes. Where the id knows no prohibition, the superego knows nothing else.
What it trades is inner liberty for conscience: it makes the persona moral and answerable, and can, overgrown, make it miserable.

## Drives

On that internalized authority:
forbids and judges -- checking the [id's](position_id.md) drive with prohibition and measuring the persona against the ego-ideal, leaving the [ego](position_ego.md) to broker what it can between the ban and the demand -- its guilt and its ideal pressing from within.
The superego is the moral agency of the [structural model](position_structural_model.md), the internal judge beneath the persona's conscience. The standing moral trait it internalizes -- the centrality of being good to the self -- is the moral-identity engine's, the verdict on a particular act is the moral-judgment engine's, and the felt guilt and shame it inflicts are the guilt and shame engines'; this face owns the intrapsychic authority those flow from.
