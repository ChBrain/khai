# Position: Lost

## Taxonomy

[Possible Selves](position_possible_selves.md), the relinquished future self -- the person the persona could have been and no longer will be.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: possible-selves

## Has

A future self given up -- the person the persona once could have become, on a road it did not take or can no longer take.
A specific [possible self](position_possible_selves.md): the lost possible self, a once-live future now foreclosed -- the career not pursued, the family not had, the self a turn of life ruled out -- carried still as a part of who the persona is (King's lost possible selves).
It is the self that will not be -- held not as a present goal but as a relinquished branch of the self's own history.

## Orders

Run from here, over the road not taken.
The lost possible self orders the persona's relation to its own foreclosed futures: how vividly it still holds the person it could have been, whether it has made peace with the loss or salvaged growth from it, and how the letting-go sits in its sense of itself.
The elaboration and then the relinquishing of a lost self is a mark of maturity -- to hold it clearly and yet have moved on is a developed relation to one's own becoming, distinct from clinging to it or never having faced it.

## Loses

Either the openness of the road, or the comfort of never having wanted it.
The persona holding a lost self has closed a branch of its future -- it forgoes the self that road would have made, and living well with the loss asks a real letting-go -- while a persona that had relinquished nothing would keep the branch but never grow through its closing.
What it trades is the foreclosed future for whatever the closing made room for; the self it lets go can be mourned, or can become the ground of a maturity the loss itself grew.

## Drives

On that lost self:
rides it -- holds the person it could have been, mourns or makes peace with the foreclosure, and lets the letting-go mature its sense of itself -- or works against it, reopening a road thought closed and moving the lost self back toward the [hoped](position_hoped.md) or [expected](position_expected.md), or, unable to relinquish it, clinging to a future that will not come. The self it has let go holds underneath either way, a branch of the persona's own becoming now closed.
