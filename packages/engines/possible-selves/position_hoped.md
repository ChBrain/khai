# Position: Hoped

## Taxonomy

[Possible Selves](position_possible_selves.md), the desired future self -- what the persona would like to become.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: possible-selves

## Has

A vision of the self it wants to be -- the successful, admired, capable, or loved persona it reaches for.
A specific [possible self](position_possible_selves.md): the hoped-for self, the future the persona desires and works toward, its aspiration made a picture of a person rather than a bare goal (Markus & Nurius).
It is the self the persona is trying to become -- who it would be if things went as it wishes.

## Orders

Run from here, toward the wanted future.
The hoped-for self draws the present forward: the persona measures its steps by whether they carry it toward this self, invests effort in what closes the distance, and reads progress as becoming-more-itself.
It pulls -- but a hope alone, unbalanced by the fear of its opposite and unlinked to a plausible path, pulls weakly, more daydream than engine (Oyserman).

## Loses

The comfort of wanting nothing in particular of oneself.
The persona holding a hoped-for self makes its own present insufficient against that self -- it can be disappointed in who it is, strained by the reach, and stung when the hope proves out of range -- and so it forgoes the ease of a self with no aspiration to fall short of.
What it trades is contentment with the present self for a pull toward a better one; the hope that lifts it also indicts where it stands.

## Drives

On that hoped-for self:
rides it -- reaches, invests, and orients its becoming toward the wanted self, keenest when the hope is paired with the [feared](position_feared.md) self it would escape and a route it can see -- or works against it, revising the hope down toward the [expected](position_expected.md), or letting it slip into the [lost](position_lost.md) when the reach is abandoned. The self it wants holds underneath either way, the future the persona is trying to grow into.
