# Position: Feared

## Taxonomy

[Possible Selves](position_possible_selves.md), the dreaded future self -- what the persona is afraid of becoming.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: possible-selves

## Has

A vision of the self it dreads being -- the failed, alone, ruined, or diminished persona it moves to avoid.
A specific [possible self](position_possible_selves.md): the feared self, the future the persona does not want and steers away from, the negative image that names what is at stake in going wrong (Markus & Nurius).
It is the self the persona is trying not to become -- who it would be if things went as it dreads.

## Orders

Run from here, away from the dreaded future.
The feared self pushes the present: the persona reads some paths as slides toward this self and swerves, spends effort to keep clear of it, and feels the pull of avoidance where the hoped-for self would pull toward.
It is often the sharper motivator -- a vivid dread of what one could become moves harder than a distant hope -- and it works best paired with the hope it is the shadow of, the two together giving both a destination and a cliff.

## Loses

The peace of a future that holds no threat.
The persona holding a feared self lives with a live danger in its own becoming -- it can be anxious about backsliding, driven by dread rather than desire, and narrowed by what it will not risk -- and so it forgoes the ease of a self with nothing to avoid.
What it trades is untroubled openness to its future for a guarding of it; the fear that protects it also haunts it, and unbalanced by hope can drive avoidance without direction.

## Drives

On that feared self:
rides it -- swerves, guards, and spends effort to stay clear of the dreaded self, sharpest when it is paired with the [hoped](position_hoped.md) self it is the shadow of, so avoidance has a direction and not only a cliff -- or works against it, letting the dread shade the [expected](position_expected.md) when it comes to seem likely, or disarming it as the threat recedes. The self it dreads holds underneath either way, the future the persona is trying not to grow into.
