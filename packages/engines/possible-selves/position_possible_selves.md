# Position: Possible Selves

## Taxonomy

Self / the future-oriented representations of what a persona could become (no khai file of its own).

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: possible-selves

## Has

A repertoire of future selves -- the persona it could become, hopes to become, dreads becoming, or has given up becoming.
The forward face of the self-concept: cognitive representations of the self in a future state, the bridge between who the persona is and what it does about who it might be (Markus & Nurius).
It is not the present self-story, nor the present gap between the self and its standards, but the self projected into a future the persona moves toward or away from.

## Orders

Run from here, beneath every reach and every retreat.
Possible selves set the standing pull and push of the future on the present: a hoped-for self draws effort toward it, a feared self drives effort away from what it dreads, and both work strongest when a hope is balanced by its feared counterpart in the same domain and tied to a strategy the persona can actually walk (Oyserman).
Under no particular scene, this is how the persona is oriented by the selves it might yet be.

## Loses

The freedom of a self with no stake in its future.
A persona that holds a possible self cannot be indifferent to its own becoming -- the hoped-for self makes present shortfall a shortfall against something, the feared self makes some paths threats, and the lost self makes a road not taken a real absence -- and so it forgoes the ease of a self accountable only to now.
What it trades is present sufficiency for a future that lays claim: to carry a possible self is to be answerable to a person one is not yet.

## Drives

On that repertoire of future selves:
rides it -- reaches for the hoped-for, flees the feared, plans by the expected, mourns or is freed by the lost -- or works against it, revising which selves it holds, balancing a bare hope with the fear that sharpens it, or letting a possible self go.
The repertoire holds underneath either way, in the standing a moment runs on: [hoped](position_hoped.md) for a self desired, [feared](position_feared.md) of a self dreaded, [expected](position_expected.md) toward a self likely, or [lost](position_lost.md) for a self relinquished. The present gap between the actual self and its ideal or ought guides is the self-discrepancy engine's; the present self-story is identity's; a specific end-state pursued is the goal engine's; the counterfactual ache over a past choice already made is the regret engine's. This engine owns the selves a persona could yet become.
