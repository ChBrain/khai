# Position: Expected

## Taxonomy

[Possible Selves](position_possible_selves.md), the probable future self -- what the persona realistically anticipates becoming.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: possible-selves

## Has

A vision of the self it actually expects to be -- neither the dream nor the dread but the likely, the future the persona anticipates given its trajectory.
A specific [possible self](position_possible_selves.md): the expected or probable self, what the persona genuinely believes it is on the way to becoming, as against what it wishes or fears (Markus & Nurius).
It is the self the persona sees coming -- who it will be if the present line simply continues.

## Orders

Run from here, along the anticipated future.
The expected self sets the baseline the persona plans by: it reads its likely course, calibrates its effort to close or hold the distance to what it hopes or away from what it fears, and takes the expected as the realistic anchor between the two.
It is the reality against which hope is measured for reach and fear for danger -- the self the persona would bet on, and the gap between it and the hoped-for self is much of what a persona is working to close.

## Loses

The license of a future known only as wish or dread.
The persona holding an expected self cannot hide its likely course behind its hopes -- it must reckon with where it is actually headed, which may fall short of the hoped-for or drift toward the feared -- and so it forgoes the comfort of a future imagined only as one wants or dreads it.
What it trades is the freedom of pure aspiration for a realistic reckoning; the honesty that grounds it can also dishearten, when the expected sits far below the hoped.

## Drives

On that expected self:
rides it -- plans by the likely course, measures the [hoped](position_hoped.md) self's reach and the [feared](position_feared.md) self's danger against it, and treats it as the realistic anchor -- or works against it, revising the expectation up as a hope comes within range or down as it recedes, and, where a path is closed for good, moving what it expected into the [lost](position_lost.md). The self it anticipates holds underneath either way, the future the persona actually sees coming.
