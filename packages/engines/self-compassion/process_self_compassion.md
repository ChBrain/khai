# Process: Self-Compassion

## Taxonomy

Affect / the reflexive turn of compassion onto the suffering self.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: self-compassion

## Initiated by

A persona meets its own suffering, failure, or inadequacy -- and turns toward it with warmth rather than attack.
This is self-compassion (Neff): compassion reflexively directed at oneself, the persona treating its own pain the way it would a suffering friend, in the very moment it has fallen short.
Where self-esteem asks _am I worthy, am I better than others_, self-compassion asks nothing of the sort -- it is extended unconditionally, precisely because one is suffering, and needs no success and no comparison to earn it.

## Direction

Inward, toward the self in pain -- to soothe it, not to judge it.
Self-compassion turns the care system on its owner: the impulse that would comfort another in distress is bent back onto the self, softening the inner voice from critic to comforter.
Its direction is the reflexive arc of care -- the same warmth compassion sends outward, sent instead to the one feeling it.

## Lever

The reflex of self-judgment -- the harsh inner critic that meets every failure with attack.
What self-compassion spends is that reflex: the habit of turning on oneself in the moment of inadequacy, adding the second arrow of self-blame to the first of the pain itself.
It fires on the same appraisal that would otherwise trigger the critic -- a shortfall, a loss, a flaw exposed -- and answers it not with _you are not enough_ but with the warmth, connection, and steadiness a friend would offer.

## Echo

The suffering is met with care, and the second arrow is not fired -- the pain remains, but the self is not made its enemy.
Its lack is the harsh self-critic, for whom every failure is an indictment; its counterfeit is self-pity, which isolates and magnifies rather than connects and steadies, and self-indulgence, which spares discomfort rather than meeting it with care.
It runs as [kindness](process_kindness.md), [humanity](process_humanity.md), and [balance](process_balance.md), tuned by a standing [self-compassionateness](position_self_compassionate.md). The judgment of one's own worth is the self-esteem engine's; the compassion turned outward on another is the compassion engine's; the release of a specific self-transgression is the forgiveness engine's; the repetitive dwelling on one's pain is the rumination engine's; the present-moment awareness as a practice is the grounding engine's. This engine owns the reflexive turn of compassion onto the suffering self.
