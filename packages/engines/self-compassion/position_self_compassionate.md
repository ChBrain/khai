# Position: Self-Compassionate

## Taxonomy

Affect / the trait of self-compassionateness -- the standing disposition beneath [Self-Compassion](process_self_compassion.md) (Neff's Self-Compassion Scale).

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: self-compassion

## Has

A resting readiness to meet one's own suffering with compassion rather than attack.
Trait self-compassion, as against the state of any one moment: at the high end, a persona who reliably turns kind, connected, and balanced toward its own failures; at the low end, one whose reflex is the harsh critic, the isolating shame, the being swept away.
It tracks the standing balance between the three components and their negatives -- self-kindness over self-judgment, common humanity over isolation, mindful balance over over-identification.

## Orders

Run from here, into every failure the persona meets.
The highly self-compassionate persona meets a shortfall already tilted toward warmth -- the kind word comes first, the sense of shared humanity is near, the pain is held rather than magnified; the harshly self-critical persona meets the same shortfall braced for the lash.
It sets the default response to one's own suffering, before any deliberate turn -- the standing tone the self takes toward the self.

## Loses

At the low end, the resilience that self-compassion buys; at the high end, little that its critics fear.
The harshly self-critical persona loses the steadiness that meeting failure with care provides, paying in shame, avoidance, and the fear of failing again; the self-compassionate persona is often supposed to lose drive and standards, but the disposition does not in fact license complacency -- it meets failure with care while still seeing it clearly, which is what makes trying again bearable.
What the self-critic trades away is the ground from which one recovers.

## Drives

On that standing tilt:
rides the disposition -- the self-critic keeps meeting failure with attack, the self-compassionate with care -- or works against it, the harsh persona learning to offer itself the warmth it would give a friend, the self-compassionate holding the stance when shame would pull it back to the critic.
The threshold holds underneath either way, a resting level the persona cultivates or neglects rather than a state that merely comes and goes.
