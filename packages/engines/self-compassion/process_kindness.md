# Process: Kindness

## Taxonomy

[Self-Compassion](process_self_compassion.md), the first component -- self-kindness against self-judgment.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: self-compassion

## Initiated by

The persona, having fallen short, speaks to itself with warmth instead of with the lash.
This is self-kindness (Neff's first component): meeting one's own inadequacy, failure, or pain with care and understanding rather than harsh criticism -- the gentle inner voice in place of the punishing one.
It is the warmth of the stance, the tone the self takes toward the self when things go wrong.

## Direction

Toward comfort -- the active offering of care to oneself.
Self-kindness does not merely withhold the criticism; it supplies its opposite, the reassuring word, the understanding, the soothing a friend would give -- so the persona is actively comforted, not merely spared attack.
Its direction is the warm turn inward, care given where judgment would have been.

## Lever

What kindness spends -- the harsh inner critic and the belief that self-punishment is deserved or useful.
The self-judging reflex holds that failure warrants attack, that being hard on oneself is what keeps one in line; self-kindness spends that belief, meeting the failure with warmth on the understanding that care, not the lash, is what actually steadies a person in pain.
A production holds this lever by letting the persona's inner voice audibly soften -- the reflexive self-attack replaced by something a friend would say -- so the kindness reads as an active choice against the critic, not mere absence of it.

## Echo

The self is comforted, and the critic is quieted -- the failure met with warmth rather than one more injury added to it.
Without the other components it can thin toward mere self-soothing; it is genuine self-kindness only when joined to the sense of [humanity](process_humanity.md) that keeps it from isolating and the [balance](process_balance.md) that keeps it from either wallowing or denial.
Its negative pole, the harsh self-judgment it replaces, is the ordinary reflex most personas bring; kindness is the warmth deliberately turned against it.
