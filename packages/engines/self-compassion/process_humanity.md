# Process: Humanity

## Taxonomy

[Self-Compassion](process_self_compassion.md), the second component -- common humanity against isolation.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: self-compassion

## Initiated by

The persona, in its failure, sees that failing is part of being human -- not a flaw that sets it apart.
This is common humanity (Neff's second component): recognizing that suffering, inadequacy, and imperfection are shared across all people, so that one's own shortfall connects one to others rather than cutting one off.
It is the widening of the frame from _something is wrong with me_ to _this is part of what it is to be a person_.

## Direction

Toward connection -- the self placed among others rather than singled out.
Common humanity turns the appraisal from the personal to the shared: this failure is not mine alone but one instance of a thing everyone meets, and in that recognition the isolation of shame loosens.
Its direction is outward into belonging, even as the pain is one's own.

## Lever

What humanity spends -- the isolating conviction that one's failure is uniquely disqualifying.
The isolating reflex holds that _everyone else has it together and I alone am broken_; common humanity spends that conviction, setting the shortfall against the plain fact that imperfection is universal, so the failure stops being evidence of a private defect.
A production holds this lever by letting the persona locate its failure in the shared human condition -- others have stood here, this is what people do -- rather than as a mark that isolates it from the rest.

## Echo

The failure is felt as human, and the isolation loosens -- the persona still hurting, but no longer alone in a way that compounds the hurt.
This is the component that most sharply separates self-compassion from self-pity: self-pity says _poor me, why me_, magnifying the sense of being singled out; common humanity says _this too is human_, dissolving it.
Joined to [kindness](process_kindness.md) it keeps the warmth from curdling into self-absorption, and it rests on the same recognition of shared vulnerability that the outward compassion engine turns toward others; here it is turned back to include the self among them.
