# Touch: Reference

## Line of Work

Touch as **process**: the haptic field of a place -- the whole of what it feels like against
the body, read by the skin before the eye has finished looking, as particular to a place as
its look, the one sense that cannot hold a place at a distance, and as much an actor as its
scenery. It wires on a place at Shown, the place's self-presentation to whoever enters,
because a place is felt as much as seen: the surface grain the skin reads, the warmth or
chill it holds, the give and resistance the body meets, and the touch it withholds. The
domain does not model the weather that supplies some of the feel -- the wind, the sun, the
damp -- which is weather's; nor the layout and standing mood of the setting, which is
space's; nor a persona's own body and touch-memory, the feels that move them because of who
they are, which is theirs; nor the symbolic warmth or weight a felt quality is read to mean,
which is embodied-metaphor's; nor touch between persons, the handshake and the embrace and
the blow, which is theirs. It models the feel the place itself has against the body.

The spine is the haptic study of place -- the argument that a setting is known through the
skin and the body as much as through the eye, and that modern building has starved that
knowing. Pallasmaa set the case against ocularcentrism: architecture is met by the whole
body, the hand and the skin and the sole, and the flat, machine-smooth, climate-neutral
surface of the modern world numbs the haptic sense a place should wake. Gibson showed touch
to be active and exploratory -- the hand that finds by moving what a still hand cannot, the
haptic system that reads the world by meeting it. Paterson mapped the registers of the
haptic -- the cutaneous, the thermal, the kinaesthetic, the felt -- the several ways the
body reads what it touches. And Classen traced the cultural history of touch, including the
forbidden touch: how the museum's "do not touch," the sacred relic, and the pristine
untouchable turn the withholding of touch into a charge of its own. So the engine gives
touch three feels and a twist -- texture, thermal, press -- and keeps a fourth, untouchable,
the withheld touch read not as the absence of the field but as one of its states, the
presence a place takes on the moment it may not be touched.

## Restrictions

What the engine refuses to model, and to whom it delegates.

- **The weather that feeds the feel (weather)**: The wind on the skin, the sun's warmth, the
  damp in the air -- weather is one of the haptic field's sources, but the weather itself is
  weather's engine. Touch takes the feel the weather makes as part of the field; it does not
  model the storm. The wind is weather's; the feel of the wind, gathered into the place's
  field, is touch's.
- **The layout and standing mood (space)**: How a place is arranged, how its size and shape
  and standing feel read -- the room's proportions, the street's enclosure -- is space's.
  Touch is the place's feel, not its shape; the two meet in a place (a low ceiling's press
  and its closeness) but are not the same force. Space is how the place is laid out; touch is
  how it feels against the body.
- **A persona's own body and touch-memory (the persona's engines)**: The feel that undoes a
  particular persona, the touch that carries their private past, what they feel because of who
  they are and what they have lived -- that is the persona's, not the place's. Touch is the
  place's feel, met by any body that enters; it never stands in for a persona's own
  body-sense, though the place's texture is exactly what such memory catches on.
- **The symbolic warmth or weight (embodied-metaphor)**: The warm person, the weighty
  decision, the hard heart, the rough patch -- the felt qualities of touch read as metaphors
  for character and circumstance are embodied-metaphor's. Touch is the literal feel of the
  place against the body, the stone cold and the bench hard as facts of the place; the warmth
  or weight a quality is taken to mean is embodied-metaphor's, not the place's own.
- **Touch between persons (theirs)**: The handshake, the embrace, the hand on the shoulder,
  the shove, the caress -- touch as it passes between people, with its consent and intimacy
  and violation, is the personas' and their interaction's, not the place's. Touch is the
  haptic field of the setting, the feel of its materials and air and give; the touch of one
  body by another is a different force.
- **The facet itself is not engine content**: The engine declares the four facets exist;
  which one a given place shows lives on that place, linked under its Shown chapter. The
  engine refuses to fix a place's feel for it.

## Encoding

Source to constraint, per file.

- **[touch](process_touch.md)** (the root): the haptic field of a place, the whole of what it
  feels like against the body taken in before the eye has finished looking, the place felt as
  much as seen. Anchored by Pallasmaa (the architecture of the skin) and Gibson (the active
  haptic system).
- **[texture](process_texture.md)**: the surface grain -- what the skin and hand and foot read
  of a place's materials, rough or smooth, worn or new: the stone, the plaster, the rail, the
  cobbles, the grain the body knows. Anchored by Gibson (active exploratory touch) and
  Pallasmaa (materiality).
- **[thermal](process_thermal.md)**: the warmth or chill the place holds against the body --
  the sun-warmed wall, the cold crypt, the humid heat, the felt temperature of the place and
  not the meteorological one. Anchored by Paterson (the thermal register of the haptic).
- **[press](process_press.md)**: the give and resistance the body meets -- the hard bench, the
  deep carpet, the crush of the crowd, the yield of the ground, the place's push-back on the
  body's weight. Anchored by Paterson (the kinaesthetic register of the haptic).
- **[untouchable](process_untouchable.md)**: the touch a place withholds read as a presence --
  the relic behind glass, the pristine unworn, the sterile, the sacred kept from the hand, at
  once revered and estranging. The engine's twist; anchored by Classen (the forbidden touch)
  and Pallasmaa (the untouchable smooth).
