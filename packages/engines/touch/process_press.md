# Process: Press

## Taxonomy

[Touch](process_touch.md), the facet of the give and resistance the body meets.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: touch

## Initiated by

A place pushes back on the body, and the body reads the place by how it yields or holds.
Press starts wherever the place meets the body's weight and force -- the hard bench that will not give, the deep carpet that swallows the foot, the crush of the packed crowd, the mud that grips the boot, the give of the old mattress, the solid floor that throws the step back.
It is the pressure register of the haptic field: not what a surface feels like to the fingertip but what the place does against the body's weight -- the mechanical push-back, the give and the resistance the whole body reads.

## Direction

Against the body, as yield or resistance to its weight.
Press gives the place its firmness -- hard or soft, solid or yielding, the ground that holds and the ground that swallows, the wall that presses in and the room that gives space -- the place felt through the force it returns to the body that leans, sits, stands, or pushes through it.
It moves the body toward ease or effort: the soft that receives and rests, the hard that holds and wears, the crush that hems the body in, the give that lets it sink -- each a way the place acts on the body's weight, felt not at the skin's surface but in the muscle and bone that meet it.

## Lever

The place's push-back against the body's weight that must meet it.
The carrying force is that a place answers the body with force -- the pew that punishes, the earth that yields, the throng that presses, the floor that holds -- so that the body reads the place by leaning into it and feeling what it gives; the resistance is the body's own limit, for press is the facet felt in fatigue and effort, the hard seat that aches, the soft that traps, the crowd whose pressure the body cannot escape.
Meet the press and the place is known in the muscles -- restful or punishing, spacious or crushing; ignore it and the place is read only at the fingertips, its whole force on the body unfelt.

## Echo

The place set to its give and resistance.
Press leaves the place answering the body with force -- the hardness and softness, the yield and the push-back the whole body meets -- and hands that resistance to the feel of the place read through it.
It is the facet felt in the body's weight rather than its skin: the place that rests or punishes, holds or swallows, gives room or hems in, known by how it meets the body that presses into it.
Press is dated from the leaning, the moment the body put its weight against the place and felt what the place gave back.
