# Process: Touch

## Taxonomy

Haptics.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: touch

## Initiated by

A place is entered, and it is felt as much as seen.
Touch starts the moment a place meets the body at all -- the floor underfoot, the cold of the wall, the rail worn smooth, the crush of the crowd, the give of the ground -- and the skin reads it before the eye has finished looking.
Every place has a haptic field, a way it feels that is as particular as its look and the one sense that cannot hold the place at a distance; touch is that field as an actor, not the untouched scenery a setting is usually taken for.

## Direction

Into the skin, from the whole surface of the body at once.
Four facets compose the field: [Texture](process_texture.md) is the surface grain the skin reads, [Thermal](process_thermal.md) the warmth or chill the place holds against the body, [Press](process_press.md) the give and resistance the body meets in it, and [Untouchable](process_untouchable.md) the twist -- the touch a place withholds read as a presence.
A place is not felt as one flat surface but as a composition: the grain of its materials, the temperature it keeps, the push-back the body meets, and the touch it will not give -- and touch alone is reciprocal, for the hand that reads the place is read by it in the same instant.

## Lever

The place's feel against the skin numbed by a world made smooth.
The carrying force is everything at the place that meets the body -- the rough and the smooth, the warm and the cold, the hard and the yielding; the resistance is the ocular habit that looks and does not touch, and the flat, machine-smooth, temperature-controlled modern surface that gives the skin nothing to read, numbing the haptic sense the place should wake.
A place still rich in feel is known in the body, in the register that memory keeps longest; a place planed to a numb sameness is felt as a blank, its texture and thermal and press erased into the seamless.

## Echo

The place known by its feel, and held in the body.
Touch does not close; it is the standing haptic character of the place, gathering its feels into a field as much the place as its look, and kept in the body's own memory -- the hand that knows a banister in the dark, the feet that know a floor.
It hands off what it is not: the weather that supplies some of the feel is weather's, the layout and standing mood are space's, a persona's own body and touch-memory are theirs, the symbolic warmth or weight a felt quality stands for is embodied-metaphor's, and touch between persons is theirs.
The place is dated from its feel, and read by it -- a setting is not only somewhere seen but somewhere felt, and the body keeps a place the eye lets go, because touch is the sense that will not be held at arm's length.
