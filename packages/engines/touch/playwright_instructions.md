# Instructions: Touch

How a Playwright wires the touch engine into a play. You wire by linking the play's own
content to the engine's files; the engine files stay untouched, they are canon. The Roadie
plumbs the engine's law into Instructions Knowledge on deploy. Authoring guidance, not
runtime content, and it does not go on tour.

## Human

- The human sets which places are felt as much as seen -- the surface grain the skin reads, the warmth or chill the place holds against the body, the give and resistance the body meets in it, or the touch it withholds -- and whether that feel is the place's quiet background or a live actor in the play.

## Agent

- From the play's own content, a place whose feel is an actor links under Shown the facet of its haptic field: the texture the skin reads, the thermal it holds, the press the body meets, or the untouchable it withholds. Play the touch as the place's feel present in what it puts before whoever enters -- the worn stone of the step, the cold of the crypt, the crush of the crowd, the relic behind glass -- felt, never narrated, and never standing in for a persona's own body-sense or for the symbolic warmth a quality is read to mean. Keep it the feel of the place, not the weather that feeds it (that is weather), the layout and standing mood (space), a persona's own body and touch-memory (the persona's engines), the metaphorical warmth or weight it stands for (embodied-metaphor), or touch between persons (theirs). You link to the engine, you never edit it.

## Collaboration

- The weather that supplies some of the feel is weather's; the layout and standing mood is space's; a persona's own body and touch-memory is theirs; the symbolic warmth or weight a felt quality stands for is embodied-metaphor's; touch between persons is theirs. Touch is the third sensory sibling of soundscape and smellscape -- the place felt as much as heard or smelled -- and the feel of the place itself, gathered into a field and wired, like its siblings, on the place at Shown. Whether the facet rings true to the place and its feel is the team's review.

## Knowledge

- Touch is the haptic field of a place: the whole of what it feels like against the body, read by the skin before the eye has finished looking, as particular to a place as its look and the one sense that cannot hold a place at a distance (Pallasmaa's architecture of the skin and its critique of the numbing smooth surface; Gibson's active exploratory touch; Paterson's registers of the haptic; Classen's cultural history of touch and the forbidden touch). It wires on a place at Shown, and it is the place felt as much as seen. Four facets carry it -- texture is the surface grain the skin and hand and foot read, rough or smooth, worn or new, the cutaneous material of the place; thermal is the warmth or chill the place holds against the body, the sun-warmed wall and the cold crypt, the felt temperature and not the meteorological one; press is the give and resistance the body meets, the hard bench and the deep carpet and the crush of the crowd, the place's push-back on the body's weight; untouchable is the twist, the touch a place withholds read not as a lack but as a presence, the relic behind glass and the pristine unworn and the sterile, at once revered and estranging. Read a place by its feel, and read untouchable two ways: the withheld touch is not the failure of the haptic field but one of its states, reverence and estrangement in the same forbidden distance.

## System

- Do give a place whose feel is an actor its facet under Shown; do play the touch as the place's feel shown, present not narrated; do keep the reading the feel of the place -- the texture, the thermal, the press, the untouchable -- not a feeling and not a symbol.
- Do remember touch is reciprocal: the body that reads the place is read by it in the same instant, and touch is the sense that will not be held at arm's length -- the register the body keeps longest, the hand that knows a rail in the dark.
- Do read untouchable as the twist: the withheld touch is not the absence of the haptic field but one of its states -- the relic kept whole and the art unspoiled and the sacred honored by distance, and the same withholding turned cold, the sealed sterile place that holds every body at arm's length and calls it care.
- Do not model the weather that feeds the feel (weather), the layout and standing mood (space), a persona's own body and touch-memory (the persona's engines), the symbolic warmth or weight it stands for (embodied-metaphor), or touch between persons (theirs). Do not make the feel stand in for a persona's body-sense or for a metaphor. Do not edit the engine's files; wire only from the play's side.
