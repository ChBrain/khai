# Process: Thermal

## Taxonomy

[Touch](process_touch.md), the facet of the warmth or chill a place holds.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: touch

## Initiated by

A place holds a temperature against the body, and the skin reads it as a feel, not a number.
Thermal starts wherever a place's warmth or cold meets the skin -- the sun-warmed wall, the cold stone of the crypt, the close humid heat of the glasshouse, the dry heat of the kiln room, the chill that lives in an unheated hall, the warmth banked in a hearth.
It is the thermal register of the haptic field: not the meteorological temperature that weather keeps, but the felt warmth or chill the place holds against the body, the heat it gives or takes at the skin.

## Direction

Toward the skin, as warmth given or heat drawn away.
Thermal gives the place its temperature-as-feel -- the wall that gives back the day's sun, the stone that pulls the warmth out of a hand, the room that wraps the body in heat, the hall whose cold no coat quite keeps out -- the place felt as a thermal presence on the skin.
It moves the body toward the warm and away from the cold, and it colors the whole feel of a place before a single surface is named: the cold church solemn by its chill, the warm kitchen kind by its heat, the same room transformed by whether it holds warmth or leaches it.

## Lever

The felt warmth of the place against the meteorology that is not its business.
The carrying force is that a place holds its own thermal character -- the thick-walled house cool in summer and warm in winter, the cellar's steady cold, the forge's heat -- the temperature the place keeps at the body, distinct from the weather outside it; the resistance is the climate-controlled sameness that holds every place at one neutral degree, giving the skin no thermal read, and the meteorological temperature, which is weather's and not the place's to model.
Feel the thermal and the place's warmth or cold is part of its character; flatten it to a controlled neutral and the place loses a register the body reads first and remembers longest.

## Echo

The place set to its warmth or chill.
Thermal leaves the place holding its temperature against the body -- the heat it gives, the cold it draws -- and hands that warmth or chill to the whole feel of the place read through it.
It is the register the skin reads before any surface is touched, felt across the air of the room: the place that warms or chills the body the moment it is entered, coloring everything the other facets add.
Thermal is dated from the meeting of skin and air, the moment the place's warmth or cold was felt as a quality of the place and not a reading on a glass.
