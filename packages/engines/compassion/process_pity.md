# Process: Pity

## Taxonomy

[Compassion](process_compassion.md), the distanced shadow.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: compassion

## Initiated by

Another's suffering is felt for, but from above and apart -- the sufferer regarded as lesser, distant, or other, and the feeling carries a tint of superiority.
This is pity: compassion's condescending shadow, the feeling-for that lacks the fellow-feeling, where the sufferer is an object of concern rather than an equal met in their pain.
It is care with the distance kept.

## Direction

Down and toward, but at a distance held -- pity inclines toward the sufferer without joining them.
The regard flows from a self that remains above and untouched; it may give, but it does not stand with, and it keeps the gap between the comfortable and the afflicted intact.
The direction is a reaching-down that does not close the distance it reaches across.

## Lever

The perceived distance and inequality between the self and the sufferer.
Pity spends little of the self and stakes no fellow-feeling; it holds the sufferer as beneath or apart, which is why it so often wounds the one it is aimed at.
What distinguishes it from compassion is exactly this: compassion meets the sufferer as a fellow who could be oneself, pity holds them as other.

## Echo

The sufferer is regarded and perhaps helped, but diminished in the helping -- pity's aid can shame, because it confirms the gap it is given across.
It is the compassion of the comfortable for the distant, curdling most easily where the sufferer is a stranger, a category, or a supposed lesser.
The downward look that judges another beneath, at its cold extreme, is the contempt engine's; pity is the softer look-down, care that keeps the sufferer below.
