# Process: Tenderness

## Taxonomy

[Compassion](process_compassion.md), the warm form.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: compassion

## Initiated by

The sight of another's vulnerability or suffering softens the persona -- the gaze gentles, the voice drops, a warm and protective fondness rises.
This is tenderness: the affective heart of compassion, the soft warmth toward the fragile and the hurt (Goetz -- the tender appraisal).
It is what compassion feels like from the inside, the melting toward the one who suffers or is small.

## Direction

Toward the sufferer, gently -- to be soft with, to hold, to soothe.
Tenderness does not rush to fix; it draws near and warms, the caring presence that eases by its very softness.
The direction is closeness and gentleness, the guard lowered to meet the fragile with care.

## Lever

The perceived vulnerability of the other, and the safety to be soft.
Tenderness spends the persona's hardness, lowering the guard to meet the fragile; it is cued strongly by the young, the weak, and the wounded -- the caregiving warmth the baby-schema calls up -- and it is the feeling that makes help feel like care rather than duty.
What it stakes is the openness to be moved and made gentle.

## Echo

The sufferer is met with warmth, and the tie is deepened -- tenderness is the emotional glue of care, what turns aid from a transaction into a comfort.
Its excess is the sentimentality that feels warmly but never helps, or the softness that cannot bear the other's pain and turns away.
The behavioral providing it warms is the caregiving engine's; tenderness is the warm feeling that moves it, compassion's heart made soft.
