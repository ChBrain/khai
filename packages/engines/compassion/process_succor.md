# Process: Succor

## Taxonomy

[Compassion](process_compassion.md), the helping form.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: compassion

## Initiated by

The suffering demands not just feeling but doing, and compassion turns to the impulse to relieve -- to soothe, aid, rescue, provide.
This is succor: compassion's action tendency (Goetz -- the caregiving motivation), the reaching to end or ease the other's pain.
It is what separates compassion from a sympathy that only feels: the emotion that moves the hands.

## Direction

Toward the relief of the suffering -- at the source where it can be removed, at the sufferer where it can be soothed.
Succor is instrumental: it aims at the pain's end and moves the persona's resources toward it, the wish to help become the act of helping.
The direction is the suffering answered, not merely witnessed.

## Lever

The felt responsibility and the perceived ability to help.
Succor fires where the persona feels both moved to relieve and able to, and it spends the self's time, effort, and resources on the other's behalf; where the ability is absent, it can curdle into helplessness or the distress that withdraws.
What it stakes is the self's own means, given over to the other's relief.

## Echo

The sufferer is aided, and compassion completes itself in the act -- succor is the emotion made useful, the point at which care becomes help.
Its shadow is the over-reaching that helps past what is wanted, or the helping that serves the helper's need to feel good more than the sufferer's need.
The behavioral system that carries the help, and its over- and under-shoots, is the caregiving engine's; succor is the compassionate impulse that drives it.
