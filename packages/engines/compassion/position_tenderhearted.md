# Position: Tenderhearted

## Taxonomy

Affect / the trait of tenderheartedness -- the standing readiness beneath [Compassion](process_compassion.md) (dispositional compassion; the compassionate temperament).

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: compassion

## Has

A low threshold and a wide reach for another's suffering -- a disposition to be moved by need, to feel warmth toward the vulnerable, and to reach to relieve.
Trait compassion, as against the state: the persona readily moved by suffering, near or far, familiar or strange, where another meets the same suffering unmoved or turns away.
Its core is a warm, other-focused concern, and it tracks agreeableness and the caregiving temperament.

## Orders

Run from here, ahead of the suffering.
The tenderhearted persona notices need that others miss, extends care widely, and is drawn toward the hurt rather than away; and the tilt shapes a life of caregiving, helping, and moral concern -- though it also raises the exposure to distress and burnout.
It colours the read before the event, on the axis of others' suffering.

## Loses

The protection of distance.
The tenderhearted persona, meeting a world full of pain, can take on more than it can bear, be moved past its resources, and slide toward the distress and exhaustion that wide-open compassion courts.
What is lost is the buffer that keeps the other's suffering the other's, not one's own.

## Drives

On the loss of that buffer:
rides the tenderheartedness -- feels widely, gives freely, bears the exposure -- or works against it, regulating the channel, setting the boundary, and keeping the other's pain theirs, at a cost in the easy warmth.
The threshold holds underneath either way, a resting level the persona manages rather than moves.
