# Process: Compassion

## Taxonomy

Emotion / the psychology of the response to another's suffering.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: compassion

## Initiated by

A persona witnesses another's suffering -- pain, loss, hardship, need -- and is moved: a warm, other-focused concern arises, with the impulse to relieve it.
This is compassion (Goetz, Keltner & Simon-Thomas): the emotion of suffering witnessed, keyed on the appraisal of another's serious and undeserved hardship and turning outward toward their relief.
Where sadness answers one's own loss, compassion answers another's; and where empathy merely shares the state, compassion adds the wish to help.

## Direction

Outward and toward -- toward the sufferer, to soothe, aid, and relieve.
Compassion is an approach emotion: it closes the distance to the one who suffers rather than withdrawing from the discomfort of their pain, and it moves toward action on their behalf.
The direction is the sufferer met and their suffering answered.

## Lever

The appraisal of another's suffering as real, serious, and undeserved -- met not with the self's own distress but with warmth and care.
What compassion spends is the self's comfort and resources, extended toward the other's relief; its felt core is tenderness, and its motivation is the reaching to help.
It rests on the empathic channel but is not it: empathy takes in the state, compassion answers it with care.

## Echo

The sufferer is helped, or at least met and not left alone -- and the compassionate self is, at its sustainable best, warmed rather than drained.
But the response is fragile: it can collapse into the self-focused distress that withdraws and burns out, or cool into the distanced pity that looks down, or fatigue under chronic exposure.
It runs as [tenderness](process_tenderness.md), [succor](process_succor.md), [distress](process_distress.md), and [pity](process_pity.md), and is tuned by a standing [tenderhearted](position_tenderhearted.md) disposition. The capacity to take in another's inner state is the empathy engine's; the behavioral system that protects and provides, and its burnout, is the caregiving engine's; the response to one's own loss is the sadness and grief engines'; the uplift at another's virtue is the elevation engine's. This engine owns the warm response to another's suffering and the motivation to relieve it.
