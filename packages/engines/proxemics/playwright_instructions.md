# Instructions: Proxemics

How a Playwright wires the proxemics engine into a play. You wire by linking the play's own content to the engine's files; the engine files stay untouched, they are canon. The Roadie plumbs the engine's law into Instructions Knowledge on deploy. Authoring guidance, not runtime content, and it does not go on tour.

## Human

- The human defines the proxemics terrain: who stands how near to whom, what tie each distance is meant to encode, and what presses the gap -- a stranger too close, a friend held off, a setting that forbids the comfortable distance -- so the space between bodies has a relationship to signal and a boundary to guard.

## Agent

- From the play's own content, a persona links the form the moment runs (zone, intrusion, or equilibrium) under its Projection. Let it show the distance between persons doing work -- the band that admits or holds off, the encroachment that breaches the bubble, the pull-back that compensates a lean-in -- and let it read as the gap between people carrying meaning, not as a claim on a place. You link to the engine, you never edit it.

## Collaboration

- The claiming and marking of a region a persona makes their own is the space engine's territory; proxemics is the portable bubble, distance between people, not a region held. The mass of bodies as an environment is the crowd engine's; the intimacy the closeness signals is the self-disclosure engine's; distance as a driver of abstraction is the construal engine's. This engine supplies the regulation of the physical distance between persons itself -- the gap held, breached, and negotiated.

## Knowledge

- Proxemics is the regulation of the distance between persons: a persona carries a portable bubble of personal space and admits others only as near as the relationship warrants, so how near they let another stand says how close the tie is. Zone is the graded bands -- intimate, personal, social, public -- each admitting a different degree of tie, so the distance kept declares the relationship. Intrusion is the breach -- another too close for the tie, felt as discomfort and arousal and the urge to restore the gap, the bubble felt where it is broken. Equilibrium is the trade-off -- distance balanced against gaze, angle, and other intimacy signals so the total stays comfortable, one persona's approach met by the other's compensating pull-back.

## System

- Do make the distance a signal, not a happenstance: how near a persona lets another stand says what that other is to them -- so let closing the gap claim a tie and holding it refuse one, and let a distance out of step with the tie be read at once as wrong.
- Do let the bubble be felt where it is breached: personal space registers most sharply at the moment of intrusion -- so let a too-close body fire discomfort and the push to step back, and where the setting forbids the step, let the discomfort persist.
- Do let closeness be conserved across channels: when distance cannot be restored, let the persona compensate on another channel -- drop the gaze, angle away, fall silent -- to bring the total intimacy back to bearable.
- Do delegate the claiming of a region to the space engine and the intimacy the closeness signals to the self-disclosure engine. Do not edit the engine's files; wire only from the play's side.
