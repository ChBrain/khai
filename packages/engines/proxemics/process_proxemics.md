# Process: Proxemics

## Taxonomy

Communication / the social psychology of interpersonal distance.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: proxemics

## Initiated by

A persona keeps a physical distance from other persons, and the distance is not idle -- it carries a portable bubble of personal space and admits others into it only as near as the relationship warrants.
This is proxemics: the regulation of the space between bodies, the gap held or closed reading and setting how close the tie is.
How near a persona lets another stand is a statement, made without words, about what that other is to them.

## Direction

Toward a distance that fits the relationship, held and adjusted moment to moment.
Proxemics matches the gap to the tie: the intimate are admitted close, the stranger held far, and a persona reads the closeness others keep as a claim about the bond and answers it by holding, yielding, or closing the distance in turn.
The direction is a continual setting of the gap -- neither fixed like a border nor tied to a place, but carried on the body and negotiated afresh with each person and each moment.

## Lever

The distance itself, spent as a signal and felt as a boundary.
What proxemics spends is the space between: the same gap that tells another how close they are held also guards the persona, so closing it claims intimacy and holding it refuses, and a distance out of step with the tie -- too near for a stranger, too far for a friend -- is read at once and felt as wrong.
The lever is the meaning distance carries: because the gap tracks the relationship so reliably, setting the gap sets, or contests, the relationship.

## Echo

The tie is signalled and the self is guarded, both through the gap.
Proxemics is why a stranger too close is alarming and a friend too far is cold, why an elevator forces a hundred small adjustments, why cultures that stand close and cultures that stand far each read the other as rude -- the distance between persons is dense with meaning, and mis-set, it misspeaks.
It runs as [zone](process_zone.md), [intrusion](process_intrusion.md), or [equilibrium](process_equilibrium.md) -- the graded bands that encode the tie, the breach of the bubble, the trade of distance against other intimacy. The claiming and marking of a region is the space engine's; the mass of bodies as an environment is the crowd engine's; the intimacy the closeness signals is the self-disclosure engine's. This engine owns the regulation of the distance between persons itself.
