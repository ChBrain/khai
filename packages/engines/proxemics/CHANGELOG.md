# @chbrain/khai-engine-proxemics

## 0.1.1

### Patch Changes

- 27b7b6f: Add the proxemics engine: the regulation of the physical distance a persona keeps from other persons -- a portable bubble of personal space whose size encodes the relationship and guards the self. The root (`process_proxemics.md`) declares Hall's proxemics: how near a persona lets another stand is a wordless statement about the tie, and a distance out of step with it is read at once as wrong. Three forms carry it: `zone` (the graded bands -- intimate, personal, social, public -- each encoding a degree of tie, so the distance kept declares the relationship), `intrusion` (the breach -- another too close for the tie, felt as discomfort and arousal and the push to restore the gap, the bubble felt where it is broken), and `equilibrium` (the trade-off -- distance balanced against gaze, angle, and other intimacy signals so the total stays comfortable, approach met by compensating pull-back).

  The still-open sibling to `space`'s territory, pre-bounded when space landed: territory is claiming a region, proxemics is the portable bubble carried between persons. Bounded against `space` (a region held vs the distance between people), `crowd` (the mass of bodies as an environment vs the dyadic gap and its breach; crowding as chronic intrusion), `self-disclosure` (the intimacy the closeness signals vs the spatial channel that signals it), and `construal`/`body` (psychological distance and bodily state vs the literal interpersonal gap). No homonym gate: `proxemics` and the form stems `zone`/`intrusion`/`equilibrium` are all free. Warranted (LORE) on Hall (_The Hidden Dimension_, 1966), Sommer (_Personal Space_, 1969), Felipe & Sommer (invasions of personal space, 1966), Argyle & Dean (equilibrium theory, 1965), and Hayduk (the personal-space review, 1983). Set at patch as the free level; a new engine may warrant a minor at the maintainer's `bump:minor` label.
