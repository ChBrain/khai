# Proxemics: Reference

## Line of Work

Proxemics as **process**: the regulation of the physical distance a persona keeps from other persons -- a portable bubble of personal space whose size encodes the relationship and guards the self. The domain does not model the claiming and marking of a region, which is space's territory; nor the mass of bodies as an environment, which is crowd's; nor the persona's own bodily state, which is body's; nor the intimacy the closeness signals, which is self-disclosure's; nor psychological distance as a driver of abstraction, which is construal's. It models the gap between persons itself: how near another is held, what that distance says about the tie, and how it is defended and negotiated.

The spine is Hall's proxemics and the personal-space tradition. A person carries an invisible bubble of space and admits others into it only as near as the relationship warrants, sorting distance into graded zones -- intimate, personal, social, public -- each encoding a degree of tie. The bubble is felt most sharply when breached: intrusion into personal space produces discomfort, arousal, and compensation. And closeness is regulated as a sum across channels: by equilibrium theory, distance is traded against gaze and other intimacy signals to hold a comfortable total. The three forms are those: zone, intrusion, equilibrium.

## Restrictions

What the engine refuses to model, and to whom it delegates.

- **Space (territory)**: The engine models the distance between persons, not the claiming of a region. The bounding, marking, and defending of a place a persona makes their own is owned by the space engine's territory; proxemics is the portable bubble carried on the body and negotiated with each person, not a fixed claim on ground -- distance between people, not a region held.
- **Crowd**: The engine models the dyadic bubble and its breach, not the mass of bodies as an environment. The press of a whole throng that acts on a persona before anyone speaks -- density, growth, the crowd prior to its members -- is owned by the crowd engine; proxemics owns the individual gap and the intrusion into it, crowding as the chronic breach, but not the mass itself.
- **Self-disclosure**: The engine models the distance that signals the tie, not the intimacy it signals. The mutual opening that deepens a bond is owned by the self-disclosure engine; proxemics owns the spatial channel by which closeness is claimed, refused, and read, not the closeness itself.
- **Construal and Body**: The engine models literal interpersonal distance, not psychological distance or the body's state. Distance as a driver of abstraction -- temporal, spatial, social, hypothetical -- is owned by the construal engine; the persona's own hunger, fatigue, and pain are body's. Proxemics owns the physical gap between persons, not distance-as-abstraction or the flesh.
