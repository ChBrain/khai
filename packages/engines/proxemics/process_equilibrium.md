# Process: Equilibrium

## Taxonomy

[Proxemics](process_proxemics.md), the trade-off form.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: proxemics

## Initiated by

Two personas hold a comfortable total intimacy by trading distance against the other signals of closeness -- when one comes nearer, the other pulls back on gaze, angle, or touch to keep the sum steady.
This is equilibrium: the mutual regulation of closeness, distance and the other intimacies balanced so the whole stays where both can bear it.
Nearness on one channel is paid for by reserve on another.

## Direction

Toward a steady total, approach on one channel met by compensation on the rest.
Equilibrium treats intimacy as a sum across channels -- distance, eye contact, body angle, touch, topic -- and holds that sum near a comfortable level: lean in, and the other looks away or turns aside; step back, and they meet your eyes more, so the total intimacy is conserved though its distribution shifts.
The direction is a continual counter-balancing: each persona reads the closeness the other offers and adjusts their own channels to keep the joint intimacy tolerable to both.

## Lever

The tradeable channels of intimacy, spent to hold the sum.
Equilibrium runs on the substitutability of the signals: because distance is one of several ways closeness is expressed, a surplus on it can be offset by a deficit on another, and the persona dials whichever channel is freest -- dropping the gaze in the crowded lift, angling away on the shared bench -- to bring an unwanted nearness back to comfort.
The lever is the conserved total: it is not any single channel the persona defends but the sum, so distance and gaze and angle move together to keep it steady.

## Echo

The closeness stays bearable, held level across the shifting channels.
It is why strangers forced near avert their eyes and face front, why a sudden lean-in draws a lean-back, why two who want different degrees of closeness negotiate it silently through a dozen small adjustments -- the equilibrium keeps the total intimacy where both can stand it, or breaks when it cannot be held.
It is the [zone](process_zone.md) defended by other means when distance alone cannot hold it, and the compensation that [intrusion](process_intrusion.md) forces when the gap is lost. The intimacy the balance regulates the level of is the self-disclosure engine's, and the mutual gaze it trades on is the contagion and grounding engines'; this owns the trade of distance against the other channels to hold closeness steady.
