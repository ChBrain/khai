# Process: Zone

## Taxonomy

[Proxemics](process_proxemics.md), the graded-distance form.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: proxemics

## Initiated by

The persona holds others at one of a few graded distances, each band admitting a different degree of tie -- the intimate near, the friendly a step back, the formal further, the public furthest of all.
This is the zone: the distance sorted into bands that encode the relationship, so where a persona stands says how close they are.
The gap is not continuous in feel but stepped, each step a different kind of nearness.

## Direction

Toward the band that fits the tie, and the tie the band declares.
The zone maps distance onto relationship in stages: the intimate distance for embrace and confidence, the personal for friends and easy talk, the social for acquaintance and business, the public for the stranger and the address to many -- and to take a given distance is to claim the tie that distance is for.
The direction runs both ways: the relationship sets the band a persona takes, and the band a persona takes announces the relationship they claim.

## Lever

The banded distance, read as a code for the tie.
The zone runs on a shared, mostly wordless code: a culture's members agree, closely enough, on which distance means which degree of closeness, so standing in the personal band with a stranger over-claims and standing in the social band with a lover under-claims, each misread at once.
The lever is the code's reliability: because the bands map so steadily onto ties, stepping into a nearer band is a bid for a closer tie, and holding a further one is a refusal of it.

## Echo

The distance kept declares the relationship, in a language everyone reads.
It is the grammar of interpersonal space -- the reason a step closer can be a courtship or an aggression and a step back a cooling, the reason the bands drawn differently by different cultures make each read the other as pushy or cold -- the zone a persona takes is a sentence about the tie.
It is the calm structure that [intrusion](process_intrusion.md) breaches and [equilibrium](process_equilibrium.md) trades within. The intimacy that a nearer band signals and invites is the self-disclosure engine's; this owns the graded distances that encode the tie.
