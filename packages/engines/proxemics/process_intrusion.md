# Process: Intrusion

## Taxonomy

[Proxemics](process_proxemics.md), the breach form.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: proxemics

## Initiated by

Another comes closer than the relationship warrants, pressing into the persona's bubble, and the persona feels it as a breach -- discomfort, tension, the urge to withdraw or restore the gap.
This is intrusion: the encroachment of another into personal space, the too-close body that the persona registers as a violation before they reason about it.
Personal space is felt most clearly exactly here, at the moment it is breached.

## Direction

Toward discomfort, and the move to repair the distance.
Intrusion crosses the gap the tie did not license, and the persona answers with an aversive charge -- arousal, unease, sometimes anger or flight -- and with compensations: a step back, a turned shoulder, an averted gaze, an object interposed, the closing of the self where the distance cannot be reclaimed.
The direction is a pushing-back against the press: the persona works to restore the breached distance, or, where they cannot, to withdraw behind other barriers.

## Lever

The breach of the bubble, spent as an aversive signal.
Intrusion runs on the boundary function of personal space: the bubble guards the persona, so its penetration reads as threat or over-claim and fires the discomfort that motivates repair, an alarm that trips the nearer the intruder comes and the less the tie warrants it.
The lever is the violated boundary itself: because the too-close body is felt as a boundary crossed, the persona is pushed to re-establish it, and when the setting forbids that -- the packed lift, the crowded train -- the discomfort persists as the boundary stays breached.

## Echo

The gap is defended or the self withdraws, and the breach is felt throughout.
It is why a stranger's nearness alarms, why the crowded elevator produces its frozen stares and rigid avoidance, why a persistent encroacher is experienced as aggressive whatever their words -- the intrusion is registered as a wrong to be righted, and where it cannot be, as a discomfort endured.
It is the violation of the [zone](process_zone.md) and the collapse of [equilibrium](process_equilibrium.md) when compensation fails. The chronic press of a whole mass of bodies as an environment is the crowd engine's; this owns the breach of the personal bubble by another and the push to restore it.
