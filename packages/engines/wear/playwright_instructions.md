# Instructions: Wear

How a Playwright wires the wear engine into a play. You wire by linking the play's
own content to the engine's files; the engine files stay untouched, they are canon.
The Roadie plumbs the engine's law into Instructions Knowledge on deploy. Authoring
guidance, not runtime content, and it does not go on tour.

## Human

- The human sets which objects in the world are being used up, how hard they are worked, and whether the use wears them out or wears them in.

## Agent

- From the play's own content, a used object links under Load Bearing the mode its use spends: the surface rubbed away by friction (abrasion), the part worked to failure by repeated stress (flexure), the consumable spent to nothing (depletion), or the surface improved and individualized by handling (seasoning). Play it as the object bearing what its use took -- the bald tread, the snapped cable, the empty tank, the broken-in boot -- and keep it degradation by use, not by time, and never a persona's own exhaustion. Hand the use-independent entropy to the decay family, the mending to restoration, and the standard-shift to obsolescence. You link to the engine, you never edit it.

## Collaboration

- The rust and rot that take an object whether or not it is used are the decay family's; the mending that answers wear is restoration's (its continuation stance is the standing reply); the working object made merely out of date is obsolescence's; the designed fit of the object to the hand is ergonomics'. Seasoning is the shared edge with decay's patina -- but wear's is laid down by use, where patina is laid down by time. Whether the mode rings true for the object and its use is the team's review.

## Knowledge

- Wear is the degradation an object takes from use rather than from time -- decay's complement: the unused thing rots where it sits, the used thing wears in the hand. Four modes carry the use, three of loss and one twist. Abrasion is the surface rubbed away by friction, the tread gone bald, the edge dulled, the finish worn through. Flexure is the part weakened and cracked by repeated stress, the hinge and cable and spring worked until they fail -- the hidden wear that gives no surface warning. Depletion is the consumable spent, the fuel and ink and charge drawn down to nothing, use that leaves nothing to mend. Seasoning is the twist: the same use that ruins one object improves another -- the boot broken in, the handle worn to the hand, the tool made better and more its owner's the more it is used. Read an object by the mode its use spends.

## System

- Do give a used object its mode under Load Bearing; do play it as the object bearing what its use spent of it; do keep the reading degradation by use, not by time, and not a feeling.
- Do read seasoning as use-made-value: the boot broken in, the handle worn to the grip, the object better and more its owner's for the wear -- wear's twist against the three modes of loss, and use's counterpart to decay's patina.
- Do let flexure fail from within without warning: the surface can look whole while the part is spent, and the break come at the flex point, not the worn one.
- Do not model the use-independent entropy (that is the decay family), the mending (that is restoration), the standard-shift (that is obsolescence), or the designed fit (that is ergonomics). Do not make the worn object mirror a persona's own exhaustion (that is the play's pitch). Do not edit the engine's files; wire only from the play's side.
