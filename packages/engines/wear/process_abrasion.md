# Process: Abrasion

## Taxonomy

[Wear](process_wear.md), the mode this use spends.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: wear

## Initiated by

Two surfaces move against each other, and the friction takes material away.
Abrasion starts wherever the object rubs or is rubbed -- the sole on the pavement, the tyre on the road, the blade on the whetstone, the cuff at the desk edge -- and each pass carries off a little of the surface.
It is the most visible wear and the most literal: the object is, grain by grain, smaller than it was.

## Direction

Off the working surface, thinning it.
The tread shallows and goes bald, the edge rounds and dulls, the finish wears through to the base, the printed face rubs to a ghost; the loss is where the use touches, and its shape is the record of how the object was held and moved.
Abrasion works fastest at the point of contact and the point of most use, so the object wears to the pattern of its own handling.

## Lever

The surface's hardness against the friction that removes it.
The carrying force is the rubbing -- the load, the speed, the grit between the surfaces; the resistance is the hardness and the finish that resist being carried off, and the lubrication that keeps the surfaces apart.
Harden the surface or ease the friction and the abrasion slows; work it bare and loaded and the surface goes, and with it the function the surface carried.

## Echo

The surface worn away, and the function with it.
It shows the bald tread, the dull edge, the worn-through finish, the smoothed grip, and carries beneath it the loss of what the surface did -- the tyre that will not grip, the tool that will not bite, the tread that will not hold the road.
Abrasion is dated from the distance rubbed, and it is wear at its plainest: the object made less, one contact at a time, until the surface it needed is gone.
