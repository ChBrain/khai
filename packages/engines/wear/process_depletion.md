# Process: Depletion

## Taxonomy

[Wear](process_wear.md), the mode this use spends.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: wear

## Initiated by

The object's substance is itself the thing consumed, and use spends it directly.
Depletion starts wherever the object is a store to be drawn down -- the fuel in the tank, the ink in the pen, the charge in the cell, the lead in the pencil, the match, the candle, the round -- and each use takes not from its surface or its structure but from its quantity.
This is the consumable: an object whose whole purpose is to be used up.

## Direction

Down the store, toward empty.
The tank drains, the cartridge empties, the wick shortens, the charge falls; the object does not wear thin or crack but simply runs low and then out, its function intact until the moment the substance is gone.
Depletion moves in one direction only, from full to spent, and the object's remaining life is read not in its condition but in how much of it is left.

## Lever

The size of the store against the rate the use draws it.
The carrying force is the consumption -- the burn, the draw, the discharge; the resistance is the capacity, the reserve the object was charged with, and the efficiency that makes a given charge last.
A larger store or a lighter use lasts longer; but unlike the other modes there is no durability that saves it -- the consumable is meant to be spent, and every use is a withdrawal that does not return.

## Echo

The object used up, its store gone to nothing.
It shows an empty vessel -- the dry tank, the spent cell, the burnt-out match, the pencil worn to a stub -- and carries beneath it the plainest truth of use: that some objects are consumed entirely by the doing of their work, and are whole only until they are needed most.
Depletion is dated from the fraction remaining, and it is wear taken to its limit: not the object worn but the object spent, use that leaves nothing to mend.
