# Process: Wear

## Taxonomy

Use.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: wear

## Initiated by

An object is used, and the use spends it.
Wear starts not when the object is neglected but when it is worked -- the hand on the handle, the road under the tread, the current through the cell, the blade to the stone -- and every use takes a little of the object's capacity to do its work.
It is decay's complement: the unused thing rots where it sits; the used thing wears in the hand.

## Direction

Onto the object's Load Bearing, spending the function.
Four modes carry the use:
[Abrasion](process_abrasion.md) rubs the surface away, [Flexure](process_flexure.md) works the parts to failure, [Depletion](process_depletion.md) spends the substance to nothing, and [Seasoning](process_seasoning.md) is the twist where the same use improves the thing.
Each takes from what the object can do -- until, in three of the four, it can no longer do it.

## Lever

The object's capacity for work against the use that draws it down.
The carrying force is the using itself -- the friction, the cycling, the consumption; the resistance is the object's durability, the material and the making that let it bear the use for a while before it is spent.
Use it and it goes; the harder or longer the use, the faster the object reaches the point where it no longer answers -- and only maintenance, or replacement, or the mend that restoration brings, can keep it in service past that.

## Echo

The object spent by its own usefulness.
Wear is dated not from a day but from a tally of use -- the miles, the cycles, the hours, the reams -- and it reads in what the Load Bearing can no longer bear: the tool that will not cut, the part that will not hold, the cell that will not power.
And in the fourth mode it inverts: the object worn not out but in, made better and more its owner's by the very use that would have spent another -- wear's own twist, the boot broken in where the blade is dulled.
