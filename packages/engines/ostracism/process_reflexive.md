# Process: Reflexive

## Taxonomy

[Ostracism](process_ostracism.md), the first stage -- the immediate, source-independent pain.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: ostracism

## Initiated by

The persona registers the exclusion and is stung at once, before any thought about it.
This is the reflexive stage (Williams): a fast, involuntary social pain that fires the moment ostracism is detected, and the sharp felt threat to the four needs -- belonging, control, self-esteem, and meaningful existence -- all at once.
It runs on the pain system that registers social injury as it does physical (Eisenberger's social-pain overlap): the hurt is immediate and bodily, not reasoned.

## Direction

Inward and immediate -- the sting landing before the mind can frame it.
The reflexive stage points nowhere useful yet; it simply hurts, flooding the persona with the felt loss of place, control, worth, and mattering, a raw signal that something fundamental has just been threatened.
Its direction is the alarm itself, the pain that announces the exclusion before any coping begins.

## Lever

What the reflexive stage spends -- the persona's ability to appraise its way out of the hurt.
This is the stage's signature: the pain is source-independent, firing even when the ostracism is trivial, expected, explicitly accidental, or by a group the persona despises -- appraisals that should blunt it do not, because the detection system reacts first and asks later.
A production holds this lever by letting the sting land regardless of context -- the persona hurt by an exclusion it knows is meaningless -- so the reflexive pain reads as the automatic alarm it is, not a considered response.

## Echo

The pain is felt, and the four needs are thrown into deficit -- and only now can the persona begin to do anything about it.
This is why even a trivial snub can wound, and why the ostracized so often cannot talk themselves out of the hurt in the moment: the alarm precedes the reasoning.
What the persona does with the deficit -- whether it moves to repair the needs prosocially or antisocially -- is the [reflective](process_reflective.md) stage; the reflexive stage owns only the immediate, unbidden sting and the fresh threat to the four needs.
