# Instructions: Ostracism

How a Playwright wires the ostracism engine into a play. You wire by linking the play's own content to the engine's files; the engine files stay untouched, they are canon. The Roadie plumbs the engine's law into Instructions Knowledge on deploy. Authoring guidance, not runtime content, and it does not go on tour.

## Human

- The human defines the ostracism terrain: who excludes the persona and how -- the silent treatment, the turned back, the unanswered bid, the being left out -- and whether the exclusion can be reversed, so a moment holds a real act of being cut off and needs it threatens.

## Agent

- From the play's own content, a persona links the ostracism a moment runs under its Projection: what exclusion is appraised, which of the four needs it most threatens (belonging, control, self-esteem, meaningful existence), and which stage the persona is in -- the reflexive sting, the reflective coping, or the resignation of the chronically excluded. Let it read as the pain of being unseen and the scramble to restore what the exclusion took, and keep it the event done by others, not the persona's own chronic sense of disconnection. You link to the engine, you never edit it.

## Collaboration

- The chronic, subjective sense of insufficient connection, whatever its cause, is the loneliness engine's; the discrediting mark that invites others to exclude one is the stigma engine's; the violation of trust by an intimate who was supposed to be on one's side is the betrayal engine's; the lowered, withdrawn affective state that resignation feeds is the sadness engine's. This engine supplies being excluded and the staged response to it -- the reflexive pain, the reflective coping, and the resignation of prolonged exclusion.

## Knowledge

- Ostracism is being ignored and excluded, and the staged response it triggers (Williams). It threatens four fundamental needs at once -- belonging, control, self-esteem, and meaningful existence -- and runs a temporal arc. Reflexive is the first stage -- an immediate, source-independent social pain that hurts even when the ostracism is trivial, expected, accidental, or by a despised group, because the detection system reacts before it appraises. Reflective is the second -- the coping, where the persona appraises the exclusion and moves to fortify the threatened needs, prosocially (reconnecting, attending to others, conforming) when belonging seems recoverable, or antisocially (aggression, control-reassertion) when it does not. Resignation is the chronic pole -- when the exclusion is prolonged and inescapable, the coping is spent and gives way to alienation, helplessness, and depression. Read the production in which stage the persona is, and which needs the exclusion most threatens.

## System

- Do keep ostracism the excluding event, not the chronic subjective gap: it is done to the persona by others, where loneliness is felt by the persona about its own connection -- let the exclusion be an act with an agent.
- Do let the reflexive sting be source-independent: the persona hurt even by an exclusion it knows is meaningless, trivial, or by a group it despises -- the alarm precedes the reasoning, which is the reflexive stage's signature.
- Do fork the reflective coping by which need is threatened and whether reinclusion seems possible: belonging recoverable turns the persona prosocial and eager to reconnect; belonging beyond reach turns it antisocial and controlling -- so the same exclusion can make one persona ingratiate and another lash out.
- Do let resignation be earned by real, prolonged, unrelieved exclusion -- coping that genuinely fails -- not staged as quick despair, and let it hand the settled disconnection to loneliness and the depressive affect to sadness. Do not edit the engine's files; wire only from the play's side.
