# Process: Reflective

## Taxonomy

[Ostracism](process_ostracism.md), the second stage -- the coping that fortifies the threatened needs.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: ostracism

## Initiated by

The persona, stung and its needs in deficit, begins to appraise the exclusion and to act on it.
This is the reflective stage (Williams): the coping that follows the reflexive pain, where the persona assesses what the ostracism means and moves to fortify the needs it threatened.
Here, unlike the reflexive stage, context matters -- who excluded, why, whether it can be reversed -- and shapes which way the coping turns.

## Direction

Toward the threatened needs -- and which needs are most threatened forks the response two ways.
When belonging and self-esteem are the wound and reconnection seems possible, the persona turns prosocial: attending closely to others, working to be included, complying, conforming, mimicking, becoming newly eager to please.
When control and meaningful existence are the wound and belonging feels beyond reach, it turns antisocial: reasserting control through aggression, provocation, or withdrawal that refuses the excluders the power to hurt further.

## Lever

What the reflective stage spends -- the persona's resources, spent to buy back the threatened needs.
Coping is effortful, and its direction is set by which needs are most in deficit and whether inclusion looks recoverable: fortify belonging by seeking reconnection, or, when that road is shut, fortify control by force -- the same exclusion driving the ingratiating and the aggressive down different roads.
A production holds this lever by setting which needs the ostracism most threatens and whether reinclusion is possible, and letting the coping fork accordingly -- so the response reads as a bid to restore a specific deficit, not a generic reaction.

## Echo

The needs are shored up, or the effort fails -- and the fork here decides whether ostracism produces the eager conformist or the lashing-out excluded.
It is why the same act of exclusion can make one persona work harder to belong and another turn hostile: the coping follows which need is threatened and whether belonging still seems within reach.
When the coping is repeatedly defeated and the exclusion will not lift, the effort itself is exhausted, and the response gives way to [resignation](process_resignation.md); the reflective stage owns the coping while it can still be mustered.
