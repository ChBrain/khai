# Process: Resignation

## Taxonomy

[Ostracism](process_ostracism.md), the chronic stage -- the coping spent, the exclusion surrendered to.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: ostracism

## Initiated by

The persona, ostracized long enough and unable to make the coping work, gives out.
This is the resignation stage (Williams): the chronic pole of prolonged, inescapable ostracism, where the resources the reflective stage spent are depleted and the effort to restore the four needs collapses.
It is what remains when exclusion has held past the point the persona can keep fighting it.

## Direction

Away from the effort -- from coping to giving up.
Resignation is the settling of the deficits into a stable state: belonging abandoned, control surrendered, self-esteem worn down to a sense of unworthiness, meaningful existence eroded toward a feeling of being invisible or as good as dead to others.
Its direction is out of the struggle, into alienation, helplessness, and depression -- the exclusion no longer fought but inhabited.

## Lever

What resignation spends -- the persona's remaining will to belong.
Every defeated coping attempt raises the cost of the next, and resignation is what happens when the cost exceeds what the persona has left: the fortifying stops, and the four needs are left in chronic deficit rather than defended.
A production holds this lever by letting the exclusion genuinely persist and the coping genuinely fail -- help that never comes, reinclusion that never arrives -- so the slide into resignation is earned by real, unrelieved ostracism, not staged as quick despair.

## Echo

The struggle ends and the deficits settle -- the chronically ostracized persona alienated, helpless, and worn to a sense of not mattering.
This is ostracism's gravest end, the state that long exclusion, bullying, and exile produce, and it is where the acute event finally shades into something enduring.
The chronic, self-perceived gap in connection, held as a standing subjective state rather than the endpoint of an excluding act, is the loneliness engine's -- resignation is where prolonged ostracism hands off to it; the flat depressive lowering itself, as an affective state, is the sadness engine's. This stage owns the collapse of coping under exclusion that will not lift.
