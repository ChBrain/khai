# Process: Ostracism

## Taxonomy

Social / being excluded, and the staged response to it.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: ostracism

## Initiated by

A persona is ignored and excluded -- the group turns its back, the silent treatment falls, the bid for contact goes unanswered.
This is ostracism (Williams): being cut off by others, and the threat it lands at once on four fundamental needs -- belonging, control, self-esteem, and meaningful existence.
Where loneliness is the persona's own chronic sense of a gap between the connection it has and the connection it wants, ostracism is the acute event of being shut out by others, and its response runs a characteristic arc over time.

## Direction

Toward the restoration of what the exclusion threatened -- the four needs the persona moves to shore up.
Ostracism does not resolve in the moment; it opens a sequence -- the reflexive sting, then the reflective effort to recover the threatened needs, then, if the exclusion holds, the resignation of the chronically shut-out.
Its direction is back toward inclusion and standing, pursued through whatever the persona has left to pursue it with -- and, when that is spent, away from the effort altogether.

## Lever

The appraisal of being excluded -- ignored, unseen, cut from the group.
What ostracism spends is the assumed security of belonging: the persona had a place, and the exclusion removes it, threatening at a stroke the sense of being connected, in control, worthy, and real to others.
It fires on the barest cue -- ostracism stings even when the excluding group is despised, the exclusion trivial, or the snub certainly accidental -- because the detection system is tuned to miss no real exclusion, and pays for that by sounding at every possible one.

## Echo

The exclusion is felt, coped with, and -- if it lifts -- recovered from, or -- if it holds -- surrendered to.
It runs as [reflexive](process_reflexive.md) pain, [reflective](process_reflective.md) coping, and, at the chronic pole, [resignation](process_resignation.md), the three stages of the temporal need-threat model.
The chronic, subjective sense of insufficient connection, whatever its cause, is the loneliness engine's; the discrediting mark that invites others to exclude one is the stigma engine's; the violation of trust by an intimate who was supposed to be on one's side is the betrayal engine's; and the diffuse pain of not belonging, absent an excluding act, is loneliness's again. This engine owns being excluded and the staged response the exclusion sets running.
