# Instructions: Decay

How a Playwright wires the decay engine into a play. You wire by linking the
play's own content to the engine's files; the engine files stay untouched, they
are canon. The Roadie plumbs the engine's law into Instructions Knowledge on
deploy. Authoring guidance, not runtime content, and it does not go on tour.

## Human

- The human sets which places and objects in the world are losing their fabric or material, in what mode, and whether the mark reads as ruin or as prized age.

## Agent

- From the play's own content, a decaying place links under Shown, or a decaying object under Apparent, the mode its fabric or material shows: the metal returning to oxide (rust), the organic breaking down under damp (rot), the mineral disintegrating grain by grain (crumble), or the surface aged into a prized skin (patina). Play the decay as the place or thing enacting the loss -- the stain, the sag, the shed grain, the earned sheen -- shown in the first beat, never narrated, and never made to mirror a persona's feeling. Keep it use-independent material entropy, and hand the atmospheric load to weather, the worn-from-outside surface to erosion, the owner's withdrawal to dereliction, the degradation of use to wear, and the intervention to reclamation (place) or restoration (object). You link to the engine, you never edit it.

## Collaboration

- The setting itself is space's; the live front is weather's; the surface abraded from outside is erosion's; the authority that stopped maintaining is dereliction's; the degradation an object takes from use is wear's; the repair that arrests the fall is reclamation's on a place and restoration's on an object. Patina is the shared seam with that intervention: whether to keep the age or wipe it. Whether the mode rings true for the place or thing and its material is the team's review.

## Knowledge

- Decay is the material entropy a place or a thing undergoes: matter losing the order a maker imposed on it, shown in the fabric of a place or the material of an object once the making stops holding it (Simmel's ruin; Trigg's memory in the failing fabric). It is one phenomenon on two types -- on a place at Shown, on an object at Apparent, since rust is rust on a rail or a blade. Four modes work the matter, each on its own material -- rust returns the metal to oxide, staining and thinning it from within; rot breaks the organic down under damp and fungus, softening the fibre to soil; crumble disintegrates the mineral under freeze-thaw and salt, shedding the surface grain by grain; patina is the same entropy laid down slowly as a surface time comes to prize, decay read as the value of age rather than loss (Riegl's age-value; DeSilvey's curated decay). Decay is slow, one-way, and use-independent -- it takes the object left in the drawer, where wear takes the one worked in the hand. Read the place or thing by the mode it shows.

## System

- Do give a decaying place its mode under Shown, or a decaying object its mode under Apparent; do play the decay as the fabric or material enacting its own breakdown, shown not narrated; do keep the reading material -- the entropy in the matter, not a feeling and not a symbol.
- Do read patina as value, not only loss: the same process that ruins one thing enriches another, and the choice to keep or wipe the age is the seam handed to reclamation or restoration.
- Do not model the setting itself (that is space), the live front (that is weather), the surface worn from outside (that is erosion), the owner's withdrawal (that is dereliction), the degradation of use (that is wear), or the intervention that arrests it (that is reclamation on a place, restoration on an object). Do not make the decay mirror a persona's fall (that is the play's pitch). Do not edit the engine's files; wire only from the play's side.
