# Process: Rust

## Taxonomy

[Decay](process_decay.md), the mode this force works in.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: decay

## Initiated by

Metal meets air and water, and begins to give itself back to oxide.
Rust starts wherever the coating fails -- the scratch, the joint, the underside the paint missed -- and the bare iron finds the damp.
It is the metal returning to the ore it was smelted from, the making undone at the atom.

## Direction

Outward from the flaw and inward through the section.
The orange spreads across the surface as a stain, and the scale lifts and flakes; underneath, the sound metal thins, so the loss is both the mark shown and the strength quietly gone from within.
Rust travels the water's path -- down the runs, into the seams, wherever wet sits and will not dry.

## Lever

The metal's reactivity against the dryness or the coating that holds it off.
The carrying force is the iron's hunger for oxygen; the resistance is the paint, the galvanising, the shelter that keeps the water off.
Break the coating and rust runs; keep the metal dry, or make it of a metal that skins over rather than flakes, and the reaction stalls at the surface.

## Echo

The stain spread and the strength gone.
The place shows the bleed first -- the streak down the concrete, the weeping bolt -- and carries the structural loss beneath it, the rail that will not bear, the beam eaten at the flange.
Rust is dated from the wet it was left in: a stain that is only a stain, or a section so far gone the metal is lace.
