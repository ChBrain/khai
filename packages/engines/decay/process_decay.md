# Process: Decay

## Taxonomy

Entropy.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: decay

## Initiated by

The making stops holding, and the matter begins to return to its lower order.
Decay starts not when someone notices the rot but when the maintenance that kept the material in its imposed form -- the paint, the roof, the dry, the care -- lapses, and the matter is left to itself.
Every made thing carries the fall in it from the day it is made; decay is only the fall let run.

## Direction

Inward and downward, through the material itself.
Four modes work the fabric of a place or the material of a thing, each on its own matter:
[Rust](process_rust.md) takes the metal, [Rot](process_rot.md) the organic, [Crumble](process_crumble.md) the mineral, and [Patina](process_patina.md) is the slow surface the same process lays down and time comes to prize.
A place or an object does not decay evenly: one face rusts while another rots, and a threshold wears to patina while a blade crumbles to scale.

## Lever

The matter's entropy against the maintenance that holds it off.
The carrying force is the material's own tendency to its lower state -- the metal to oxide, the wood to soil, the stone to grit; the resistance is the upkeep the place or thing is given.
Remove the upkeep and decay runs; remove the matter's susceptibility, or the time, and there is nothing to decay.
It runs whether or not the thing is used -- that is what parts it from wear: decay takes the object left in the drawer, wear the one worked in the hand.
The rate is slow and the direction is one way: maintenance can arrest decay but not undo the years already spent.

## Echo

The place or thing diminished, or aged into value.
Decay does not close like a front passing; it accrues, and it hands the matter onward.
Left with no one to arrest it, the thing is given over -- on a place, the withdrawal that lets it run is dereliction's; the surface it wears may be prized rather than mourned, which is patina's; the intervention that arrests it is reclamation's on a place, restoration's on an object.
Decay carries the years forward in the matter, dating the place or thing from the time no upkeep can return.
