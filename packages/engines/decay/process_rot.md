# Process: Rot

## Taxonomy

[Decay](process_decay.md), the mode this force works in.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: decay

## Initiated by

Moisture and living things enter the organic fabric, and the material begins to break down into soil.
Rot starts where the wet gets into the wood, the fabric, the fibre and does not leave -- the leak, the rising damp, the trapped condensation -- and the fungus and the bacteria take hold.
It is the fastest of the modes, because the matter is already food.

## Direction

Through the fibre, softening as it goes.
The wood turns from sound to punk, spongy and dark, crumbling to the touch where it was solid; the fabric perishes, the leather cracks and powders, the paper foxes and falls apart.
Rot follows the damp into the hidden places first -- the joist end in the wall, the sill under the sill, the underside no one sees -- so the surface can look whole over a core already gone.

## Lever

The material's integrity against the dryness or the treatment that preserves it.
The carrying force is the appetite of the organisms and the water they need; the resistance is the dry, the airflow, the preservative, the seasoning that denies them.
Keep the organic fabric dry and rot cannot start; let the damp stand and it runs until the fibre is soil.

## Echo

The load-bearing gone soft, and the smell of it.
The place shows the sag, the give underfoot, the bloom of mould, and carries the failure beneath -- the beam that will not hold, the floor that opens, the whole timber returned to the ground it grew from.
Rot is dated from the damp it fed on: a musty corner, or a structure eaten hollow behind a sound face.
