# Process: Crumble

## Taxonomy

[Decay](process_decay.md), the mode this force works in.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: decay

## Initiated by

Water, frost, and salt work the mineral fabric until it loses its cohesion.
Crumble starts in the pore and the crack -- the rain drawn in, the freeze that widens it, the salt that crystallises and pushes -- and the stone, the brick, the mortar, the concrete begins to shed itself.
It is the slowest of the modes and the most patient, measured in seasons of freeze and thaw.

## Direction

Off the surface and down, grain by grain.
The stone spalls and flakes, the face powders to sand under the thumb; the mortar turns to dust and the joints open; the concrete cracks, and the water reaches the steel, and the rust within bursts the mineral from inside.
Crumble works from the weather-side in and from the joint out, thinning the wall toward the rubble it will become.

## Lever

The fabric's cohesion against the pointing, the shelter, and the shedding of water that spares it.
The carrying force is the freeze-thaw and the salt in the pore; the resistance is the sound mortar, the overhang, the drain that keeps the water moving off rather than sitting in.
Keep the water out of the fabric and crumble stalls; let it soak and freeze and the mineral returns to grit.

## Echo

The surface shed and the wall thinning.
The place shows the sand at the foot of the wall, the rounded edge where the arris was sharp, the open joint, and carries the loss beneath -- the section thinned past its strength, the parapet loosened, the fabric on its way back to the ground it was quarried from.
Crumble is dated from the seasons it stood unsheltered: a softened edge, or a wall gone to a heap of its own stone.
