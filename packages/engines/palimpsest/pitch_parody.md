# Pitch: Parody

## Taxonomy

[Palimpsest](pitch_palimpsest.md), the mocking transform.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: palimpsest

## Tenor

The derisive-playful key: a serious source re-pitched to comic deflation. Parody keeps the source's shape -- its plot, its manner, its solemn gestures -- and retunes them to tease, so the grand line lands as a wink and the noble pose as a pratfall. The events need not change; the key does, tilting the familiar tale toward laughter by holding it a half-step off its own straight face.

## Undertow

The affection beneath the mockery. One parodies what one knows closely and, usually, half-loves -- so under the teasing runs a real intimacy with the source, and the tone curdles if that warmth is lost: without it, parody hardens into mere ridicule and the palimpsest thins to an attack. The counter-current is the fondness that makes the mockery a caress as much as a jab, the source worth teasing because it is worth keeping.

## Nerve

The delighted recognition of the target under the tease. Parody plays for the audience that knows the source and catches it being gently turned -- the pleasure of the double take, of seeing the solemn thing and its deflation in one beat. It presses for complicity: the reader is flattered to be in on the joke, to hold the original and its send-up together and enjoy the gap.

## Echo

The source teased loose from its solemnity, harder to take quite straight again. Once a tale is parodied well, its earnest key carries a faint comic overtone ever after -- the wink lingers on the grand gesture, the pratfall shadows the pose. What parody leaves is not the source destroyed but the source lightened, its pomp made spendable, its straight face never wholly straight to those who have heard it teased.
