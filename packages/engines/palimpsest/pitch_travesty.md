# Pitch: Travesty

## Taxonomy

[Palimpsest](pitch_palimpsest.md), the debasing transform.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: palimpsest

## Tenor

The burlesque-coarse key: a noble tale re-clothed in low, vulgar register. Where parody teases the source's manner, travesty keeps the noble matter and drops it into a base idiom -- the hero speaks like a lout, the epic deed is told in gutter terms, the sublime is dressed in rags. The events hold; the diction falls, and the fixed tale is played in a key that drags its dignity down through the words it is given.

## Undertow

The grandeur still glimpsed under the debasement. Travesty works only while the noble original is felt beneath the vulgar dress -- the fall is funny and pointed because it is a fall, and if the source's height is forgotten there is no descent, only crudeness. The counter-current is the remembered dignity pressing up against the low register, the height the coarseness needs in order to have something to bring down.

## Nerve

The gleeful scandal of the high brought low. Travesty plays for the audience's transgressive delight -- the charge of hearing the sacred spoken profanely, the sublime undignified, the thing-above dragged to the level of the body and the street. It presses on the pleasure of irreverence: the reader is made complicit in the debasement, enjoying the affront to a dignity they are invited, for the moment, to stop respecting.

## Echo

The grand tale's pomp punctured, its solemnity made spendable. Once a noble source is travestied, its high register carries the memory of its own debasement -- the epic line can be heard in the gutter key ever after, the sublime pose shadowed by its coarse double. What travesty leaves is a dignity that no longer sits quite secure: the height re-accented by the fall, the reverence loosened by having once been so cheerfully withheld.
