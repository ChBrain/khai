# Palimpsest: Reference

## Line of Work

Palimpsest as **pitch**: how a fixed tale is re-pitched into a new key by its relation to a prior telling -- Genette's literature in the second degree -- the source showing through the new tone without the events changing. The domain does not model the science of how an affective key is established and sustained in an audience, which is the tone engine's; nor the amusement mechanism a comic re-pitching may trigger, which is the humor engine's; nor the tilt a presentation frame puts on judgment, which is the framing engine's; nor the structure of the fixed events themselves, which the play engines own. It models the transformative key: the run tuned to a felt debt to an earlier telling, working through the transforms by which the prior telling is made to show through -- teased, debased, or transplanted.

The spine is Gérard Genette's theory of hypertextuality and the wider study of a text re-toned by its relation to another. In _Palimpsests_ Genette maps the ways a hypertext derives from a hypotext without commenting on it directly -- by transformation rather than imitation -- and distinguishes the playful (parody), the satirical or debasing (travesty), and the serious (transposition) regimes of that transformation. Bakhtin's reaccentuation names the situational face of the same phenomenon: a work re-toned by a new context or era, a comedy heard as tragedy across time -- the pitch retuned not by an author but by the situation of the run. Linda Hutcheon reframes parody as repetition with critical difference, and Raymond Queneau's _Exercises in Style_ -- ninety-nine retellings of one trivial event in ninety-nine keys -- is the field's artistic proof that a fixed event can be pitched any number of ways. Three transforms carry it: parody, travesty, transposition.

## Restrictions

What the engine refuses to model, and to whom it delegates.

- **Tone**: The engine models a specific transformative key -- a tale re-pitched by its relation to a prior telling -- not the general science of how an affective key is cued and sustained. How redundant cues establish and hold a mood in an audience is owned by the tone engine; palimpsest is one particular way to choose and define a key (by transformative debt), and it rides on tone's mechanism to land. The engine delegates the affective-key mechanism to tone and keeps the transform relation.
- **Humor**: The engine models the mocking or debasing transform, not the amusement it may produce. Parody and travesty run in a comic register, but the mechanism of laughter -- incongruity, superiority, release -- is owned by the humor engine; palimpsest owns the transformative relation to a source, which may raise a laugh or a shudder or a tear, not the machinery of the laugh itself. The engine delegates the comic mechanism to humor.
- **Framing**: The engine models the retuning of a fixed tale's felt key, not the tilt a presentation frame puts on judgment. How the same content, framed as a gain or a loss or through a metaphor, shifts a persona's evaluation is owned by the framing engine; palimpsest works on the tone and the transformative relation, framing on the verdict -- one changes how a tale is keyed, the other how a claim is judged. The engine delegates the presentation-frame's tilt on judgment to framing.
- **Play engines (dramatic-arc, monomyth, morphology)**: The engine models the key a fixed tale is re-pitched to, not the structure of the events. The whole-story shapes -- the dramatic arc, the monomyth, the tale's morphology -- are owned by the play engines; palimpsest changes the tone without changing what happens, and a transposition re-fixes the events on new ground but does not alter their structure. The engine delegates the event-structure to the play engines and keeps the retuning.

## Encoding

Source to constraint, per file.

- **[palimpsest](pitch_palimpsest.md)** (the root): The doubled, second-degree key -- a run played so a prior telling shows through it, the events fixed and only the tone retuned, honoured and altered at once. Anchored by Genette (_Palimpsests_) and Bakhtin (reaccentuation).
- **[parody](pitch_parody.md)** (transform): The mocking route -- a serious source re-keyed to comic deflation, teased loose from its solemnity with an affection beneath the mockery. Anchored by Genette (the playful regime) and Hutcheon (parody as repetition with critical difference).
- **[travesty](pitch_travesty.md)** (transform): The debasing route -- a noble tale re-clothed in low, vulgar register, its dignity pulled down while the grandeur is still glimpsed beneath. Anchored by Genette (the satirical regime, travesty).
- **[transposition](pitch_transposition.md)** (transform): The serious route -- the tale transplanted to new ground and re-keyed in full earnest, the ancient pattern lending the new setting its weight. Anchored by Genette (serious transposition) and the _Ulysses_/_Odyssey_ paradigm.
