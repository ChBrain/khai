# Pitch: Transposition

## Taxonomy

[Palimpsest](pitch_palimpsest.md), the serious transform.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: palimpsest

## Tenor

The grave-earnest key: the tale transplanted to new ground and re-pitched in full seriousness. Unlike parody and travesty, transposition does not tease or debase -- it moves an old pattern into a new setting and plays it straight, so the ancient story lives again in modern dress without a wink, as the Odyssey walks through a single Dublin day in Ulysses. The events are re-fixed on new soil; the key stays earnest, and the source lends the new telling its gravity rather than its comedy.

## Undertow

The ancient pattern felt beneath the new setting, and the strain of the fit. Transposition is alive only while the old myth is sensed under the modern ground -- too faint and it is a plain new story, too literal and it is costume. The counter-current is the friction between then and now: the archetype pressing up through the changed circumstance, the modern setting resisting the old shape, and the tone lives in how nearly, and how imperfectly, the two are made to rhyme.

## Nerve

The recognition of the old myth carrying the new story's stakes. Transposition plays for the audience that senses the ancient pattern beneath the present tale and feels the present dignified by it -- the ordinary day made mythic, the modern figure shadowed by a hero, the small life given the weight of the archetype it re-enacts. It presses for resonance, not laughter: the grave pleasure of seeing the timeless move again in time.

## Echo

The present dignified by the archetype it re-enacts, and the source renewed by its new ground. Once a tale is transposed in earnest, the modern setting keeps the myth as an undertone and the old story gains a new instance to be read through -- each renewing the other. What transposition leaves is continuity across the gap of time: the pattern shown still living, the new tale weighted with the old, the source proved able to speak again in a world it never knew.
