# Instructions: Palimpsest

How a Playwright wires the palimpsest engine into a play. You wire by linking the play's own content to the engine's files; the engine files stay untouched, they are canon. The Roadie plumbs the engine's law into Instructions Knowledge on deploy. Authoring guidance, not runtime content, and it does not go on tour.

## Human

- The human defines the palimpsest terrain: what prior telling the run is written over, and which transform it is tuned by -- the source teased, debased, or transplanted -- so there is a fixed tale to re-pitch and a felt debt to an earlier telling for the new key to play against.

## Agent

- From the play's own content, the production links, under its Arc, the transform its run is tuned to (parody, travesty, or transposition). Let it re-pitch the fixed events by their relation to the source -- the prior telling showing through the new key -- and let it read as a retuning of how the tale lands, never as a change to what happens. You link to the engine, you never edit it; and the Director may tune the run to another transform of the same palimpsest.

## Collaboration

- The science of how an affective key is cued and sustained in an audience is the tone engine's; the mechanism of amusement a comic re-pitching may trigger is the humor engine's; the tilt a presentation frame puts on judgment is the framing engine's; the structure of the fixed events is the play engines' (dramatic-arc, monomyth, morphology). This engine supplies the transformative key -- a fixed tale re-pitched by its relation to a prior telling.

## Knowledge

- A palimpsest is a fixed tale re-pitched into a new key by its relation to a prior telling: the source shows through the new tone the way old writing shows through a scraped-and-reused page, and the events do not change -- only how they land. Parody is the mocking transform: a serious source re-keyed to comic deflation, teased loose from its solemnity by one who knows and half-loves it, with an affection beneath the mockery that keeps it from mere ridicule. Travesty is the debasing transform: a noble tale re-clothed in low, vulgar register, its dignity pulled down while its grandeur is still glimpsed beneath, playing on the scandal of the high brought low. Transposition is the serious transform: the tale transplanted to new ground and re-keyed in full earnest -- no wink, no debasement -- the ancient pattern lending the new setting its weight, as the Odyssey carries Ulysses.

## System

- Do keep the events fixed and retune only the key: a palimpsest changes how the tale lands, not what happens -- so let the transform re-pitch the tone while the plot holds, and let the prior telling show through as an overtone rather than being rewritten.
- Do keep both tellings audible: the pitch lives in the doubling, the new key heard against the source -- so let the original be sensed beneath, and lose the palimpsest the moment either the source or the new tone drops out.
- Do route by transform: parody teases the source's manner toward comedy, travesty drops noble matter into a low register, transposition transplants the tale and plays it straight -- so name which the run is tuned by rather than a vague "reworking."
- Do delegate the affective-key mechanism to the tone engine, the comic mechanism to the humor engine, and the event-structure to the play engines. Do not edit the engine's files; wire only from the play's side, and leave the Director free to tune the run to another transform.
