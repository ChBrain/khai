# Process: Breadth

## Taxonomy

[Self-Disclosure](process_self_disclosure.md), the movement this is one form of.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: self-disclosure

## Initiated by

The persona opens more areas of themselves -- work, family, tastes, history, the day's small doings -- while keeping each one fairly shallow.
The reveal widens across topics rather than reaching down into any of them; a new subject is unlocked, not a deeper layer of an old one.

## Direction

Toward a wider surface shared.
The persona spreads the self across many topics, so the two come to know a great deal about each other's outer lives -- what they do, like, and have done -- without yet touching the core of any of it.
Early on, this is also how uncertainty is reduced: the more areas are opened, the more predictable and safe the other becomes.

## Lever

Breadth is the range of the self unlocked, held at a safe depth.
Widening is low-risk and easily reciprocated -- surface facts cost little to give and little to hold -- so a relationship can grow broad quickly, touching many areas lightly, long before it grows deep in any.
It is the natural early movement and the steady state of the wide, easy acquaintance who knows your whole surface and none of your depths.

## Echo

The two know many things about each other and few of them deeply.
Broad-and-shallow is comfortable and versatile -- there is always something to talk about -- but it is friendliness, not confidence; a wide surface with no depth beneath it stays warm and stays light.
Its complement is [deepening](process_deepening.md), which trades range for reach: the one topic taken to the core rather than the many kept at the skin.
