# Self-Disclosure: Reference

## Line of Work

Self-disclosure as **process**: how a persona reveals themselves to another over time, and how a relationship deepens, holds, or re-closes by what is opened. The domain does not model the particular fact held back -- the withheld thing that carries weight -- which is the secret engine's; nor the bet on another's reliability that a disclosure rests on, which is the trust engine's; nor the bond and its dynamics under threat, which is the attachment engine's. It models the revealing itself: the layers of the self opened across topics and inward toward the core, the reciprocity that paces the descent, and the depenetration that reverses it.

The spine is social penetration: the self is a layered structure, wide across many topics and deep from a public surface to a private core, and a relationship develops as the two open those layers to each other -- penetrating from the outer, safe layers inward, only as fast as reward outweighs cost and disclosure is returned in kind. The same process runs in reverse -- depenetration -- when the closeness stops being worth its price or is betrayed. The four movements are the ways the self is opened and closed: breadth, deepening, reciprocity, retreat.

## Restrictions

What the engine refuses to model, and to whom it delegates.

- **Secret**: The engine models the process of revealing the self, not the particular fact kept back. The withheld fact that carries more weight than it shows is owned by the secret engine; this engine governs the opening and closing of the self's layers, and a specific concealment within it is secret's.
- **Trust**: The engine models the disclosure and the closeness it builds, not the bet on the other's reliability. The persona's wager on whether another can be relied on -- extended, tested, betrayed -- is owned by the trust engine; disclosure is gated by trust and builds it, but the bet itself is trust's.
- **Attachment**: The engine models the revealing that deepens a tie, not the bond and its dynamics under threat. How a persona seeks or gives up on closeness to a secure base is owned by the attachment engine; this engine governs the disclosure by which closeness is built, not the attachment system beneath it.
- **Identity and Narrative**: The engine models the revealing of the self, not the self being revealed. The self-concept and the life-story a persona holds are owned by the identity and narrative engines; this engine governs which layers of that self are opened to whom, not the content of the self.
- **Speech act**: The engine models the relational movement of opening the self, not the utterance-act that carries a given disclosure. The assertive that tells and the expressive that confides are owned by the speech-act engine; this engine governs the penetration those utterances accomplish over time.
- **Face**: The engine models the disclosure, not the redress around a reveal that threatens face. The politeness work that softens a disclosure's face-cost is owned by the face engine; this engine governs the opening of the self, that one the face-work around it.

## Encoding

Source to constraint, per file.

- **[self-disclosure](process_self_disclosure.md)** (the root): The revealing of the layered self that builds a relationship -- across topics and inward toward the core, penetrated as reward and reciprocity allow. Anchored by Altman & Taylor (social penetration) and Jourard (self-disclosure and its dyadic effect).
- **[breadth](process_breadth.md)** (movement): Widen the self across many topics while each stays shallow -- the broad, easy acquaintance; also how early uncertainty is reduced. Anchored by Altman & Taylor (breadth) and Berger & Calabrese (uncertainty reduction).
- **[deepening](process_deepening.md)** (movement): Reach inward to an intimate, vulnerable core layer on a topic -- the narrow, deep confidant; closeness bought with risk. Anchored by Altman & Taylor (depth) and Collins & Miller (disclosure and liking).
- **[reciprocity](process_reciprocity.md)** (movement): Match the other's opening in kind -- the dyadic dance that paces the descent and grows liking; unmet, it exposes and unbalances. Anchored by Jourard (the dyadic effect) and Collins & Miller (the reciprocity effect).
- **[retreat](process_retreat.md)** (movement): Depenetration -- re-close the layers as costs mount or a disclosure is betrayed, thinning the tie to a colder, narrower state. Anchored by Altman & Taylor (depenetration) and Derlega et al. (the regulation and withdrawal of disclosure).
