# Process: Deepening

## Taxonomy

[Self-Disclosure](process_self_disclosure.md), the movement this is one form of.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: self-disclosure

## Initiated by

The persona reveals a more intimate, core, vulnerable layer on some topic -- a fear, a shame, a longing, a wound -- reaching past the surface of it toward the centre.
This is not a new subject opened but an old one taken deeper: the same topic, unlocked to a layer that costs something to show.

## Direction

Inward, toward the core of the self on a given topic.
The persona moves from what is safe to say to what is not -- the thing usually kept back -- and the tie deepens exactly where the reaching goes, becoming intimate on that topic even if it stays shallow on others.
Depth on one thing can outweigh breadth across many: to be known deeply somewhere is closer than to be known widely everywhere.

## Lever

Depth is how far toward the core the disclosure reaches, and the inner layers are both riskier and more binding.
The core layers are more defining and more particular -- fewer people hold them, so sharing one confers a closeness the surface cannot -- and reaching them is trust spent, because what is shown from the core can wound if it is used.
This is the confidant's movement: narrow and deep, the one who knows the thing about you that no one else does.

## Echo

The tie is intimate where the depth went.
The vulnerability shared binds -- shared depth is the substance of closeness -- and also exposes, for the core once opened is out of the persona's keeping and in another's; depth is closeness bought with risk.
It is the complement of [breadth](process_breadth.md), and it advances safely only when met -- deepening that outruns [reciprocity](process_reciprocity.md) leaves the discloser over-exposed and alone with what they showed.
