# Process: Reciprocity

## Taxonomy

[Self-Disclosure](process_self_disclosure.md), the movement this is one form of.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: self-disclosure

## Initiated by

One persona's disclosure invites the other's in return, at a like level.
A confidence offered opens a small debt: the other is drawn to answer it with a confidence of similar depth, and the exchange proceeds by turns rather than by one person's pouring out.

## Direction

Toward a matched, mutual opening that paces the deepening.
The two advance in step -- a surface fact met by a surface fact, a vulnerability met by a vulnerability -- so that neither is far ahead of the other in what they have risked.
The matching is the mechanism of pacing: it is how a relationship deepens without either party getting dangerously exposed.

## Lever

The norm of reciprocity applied to the self.
A disclosure calls for a return of comparable intimacy, and the dyadic dance of matched revealing both paces the descent inward and grows the liking between them -- to be disclosed to is to be trusted, and trust returned deepens the bond. The tempo matters: an answer too shallow reads as a rebuff, too deep too fast as a lunge.
Early and shallow, the matching is easy and quick; deep, it slows, each waiting to see the other's opening honoured before risking the next.

## Echo

The relationship deepens in step, each opening met by an opening.
Where reciprocity holds, disclosure is safe and the tie grows evenly; where it fails -- one reveals and the other does not answer -- the discloser is left exposed and the relationship lopsided, one person deep in and the other still at the surface.
Unmet, the imbalance is felt as a debt or a snub, and the over-discloser usually retreats to restore the balance, tipping the movement toward [retreat](process_retreat.md).
