# Process: Retreat

## Taxonomy

[Self-Disclosure](process_self_disclosure.md), the movement this is one form of.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: self-disclosure

## Initiated by

A tie cools, is hurt, or is threatened, and the persona re-closes the layers they had opened.
The sharing that had widened and deepened begins to reverse: topics that were open fall quiet, depths that were reached are not returned to, and the self draws back toward the surface.

## Direction

Outward again, back toward the safe layers.
The persona withdraws what they had been revealing -- talking of less, and less deeply -- so the relationship thins to a narrower, shallower state than it held at its fullest.
The retreat may be mutual, cooling on both sides, or one-sided, one person closing while the other is left holding an openness no longer met.

## Lever

Depenetration: the penetration run in reverse as costs come to outweigh rewards.
When the closeness stops being worth its price -- the effort, the exposure, the risk -- or when a disclosure is betrayed and the openness feels unsafe, the layers close in reverse order, breadth and depth both shrinking, and the relationship recedes.
The rate reads as clearly as it did opening: a slow drift apart is different from an abrupt slamming shut after a wound.

## Echo

The tie recedes to a shallower, narrower state, or ends.
What was shared is not unshared -- the other still holds the confidences given when the closeness warranted them -- so the retreat is guarded, and after a betrayal, often bitter: the persona has withdrawn the closeness but cannot withdraw what it already revealed.
It is the reversal of [deepening](process_deepening.md) and [breadth](process_breadth.md), and it leaves the relationship not where it started but somewhere colder -- a known distance, kept on purpose.
