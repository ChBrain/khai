# Process: Self-Disclosure

## Taxonomy

Interpersonal communication / relationship development.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: self-disclosure

## Initiated by

A persona chooses how much of themselves to reveal to another -- which parts, how deep, how fast.
The self is layered, an onion: an outer skin of the safe and public, and beneath it, layer by layer, the more particular, the more private, the more defining, down to a core few are shown.
Every exchange offers the choice to open a layer or keep it closed, and the choice, made over and over, is what builds the relationship or holds it where it is.

## Direction

Toward a relationship deepened -- or held shallow, or re-closed -- by what is revealed.
The persona opens the self from the outer layers inward, and the tie grows with the opening: to be known more, and more deeply, is to be closer.
The movement is not only theirs; the other is opening too, or not, and the relationship advances to the depth the two of them jointly reach.

## Lever

The breadth and depth of the self opened, penetrated only as far as reward and reciprocity allow.
Breadth is how many areas of the self are unlocked; depth is how far toward the core each goes -- and a tie can be broad and shallow (many topics, lightly) or narrow and deep (one thing, to the bone). Penetration runs from the safe layers inward, each step riskier, and continues while the rewards of closeness outweigh its costs and the other opens in turn.
Disclosure is gated: too much too soon breaks the pacing and alarms; too little keeps the tie on the surface. The rate is as legible as the content.

## Echo

The relationship is as broad and deep as the disclosure that built it.
What is revealed can bind -- shared vulnerability is the stuff of intimacy -- and can expose, for what is opened can be used, so each layer is trust spent as much as closeness bought.
The process continues as [breadth](process_breadth.md), [deepening](process_deepening.md), [reciprocity](process_reciprocity.md), or [retreat](process_retreat.md) -- the movements by which the self is opened and closed to another. The particular fact held back is the secret engine's; the bet each disclosure rests on is the trust engine's.
