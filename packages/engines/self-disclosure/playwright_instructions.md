# Instructions: Self-Disclosure

How a Playwright wires the self-disclosure engine into a play. You wire by linking the play's own content to the engine's files; the engine files stay untouched, they are canon. The Roadie plumbs the engine's law into Instructions Knowledge on deploy. Authoring guidance, not runtime content, and it does not go on tour.

## Human

- The human defines the disclosure terrain: what the persona has to reveal and at what depth, whom they are opening to, and where the relationship stands -- how broad and how deep it has already grown.

## Agent

- From the play's own content, a persona links the disclosure movement the moment runs (breadth, deepening, reciprocity, or retreat) under its Projection. Let the movement shape how much of the self is opened or closed and how far the tie deepens or thins, and let the pacing read -- too much too soon alarms, too little keeps it shallow. You link to the engine, you never edit it.

## Collaboration

- The particular fact held back is the secret engine's; the bet a disclosure rests on is trust's; the bond under threat is attachment's. The self being revealed -- its story and concept -- is identity's and narrative's; the utterance that confides is the speech-act engine's; the politeness around a face-threatening reveal is face's. This engine supplies the revealing itself, the opening and closing of the self's layers over time.

## Knowledge

- Self-disclosure is how a persona reveals themselves and deepens a relationship layer by layer: the self is an onion, wide across topics and deep from a public surface to a private core, and a tie grows as the layers are opened, as far as reward and reciprocity allow. Breadth widens the self across many topics kept shallow. Deepening reaches inward to an intimate, vulnerable layer. Reciprocity matches the other's opening in kind, pacing the descent. Retreat is depenetration -- re-closing the layers as a tie cools or a disclosure is betrayed.

## System

- Do let breadth and depth come apart: a bond can be broad and shallow (many topics, lightly) or narrow and deep (one thing, to the bone), and they read very differently.
- Do let reciprocity pace it: a deep disclosure that is not returned leaves the discloser exposed and usually triggers retreat.
- Do let what was revealed outlast the retreat -- the other still holds it -- so a withdrawal after a betrayal is guarded, not clean.
- Do delegate the withheld fact to the secret engine and the bet to trust. Do not edit the engine's files; wire only from the play's side.
