# @chbrain/khai-engine-self-disclosure

## 0.1.1

### Patch Changes

- 1b1117c: Add the self-disclosure engine: how a persona reveals themselves to another over time and deepens a relationship layer by layer. The root (`process_self_disclosure.md`) declares social penetration -- the self as a layered onion, wide across topics and deep from a public surface to a private core, opened from the outer layers inward only as fast as reward outweighs cost and disclosure is returned in kind. Four movements are the ways the self is opened and closed: `breadth` (widen across many topics kept shallow -- the broad acquaintance, and how early uncertainty is reduced), `deepening` (reach inward to an intimate, vulnerable core layer -- the narrow, deep confidant, closeness bought with risk), `reciprocity` (match the other's opening in kind -- the dyadic dance that paces the descent and grows liking; unmet, it exposes and unbalances), and `retreat` (depenetration -- re-close the layers as costs mount or a disclosure is betrayed, thinning the tie to a colder, narrower state).

  Pairs with `secret` as its complement -- secret owns the particular fact withheld, this owns the process of revealing the self. Bounded against `trust` (the bet a disclosure rests on, which it is gated by and builds), `attachment` (the bond under threat), `identity`/`narrative` (the self being revealed vs the revealing of it), `speech-act` (the utterance that confides), and `face` (the redress around a face-threatening reveal). Warranted (LORE) on Altman & Taylor (social penetration), Jourard (self-disclosure and its dyadic effect), Berger & Calabrese (uncertainty reduction), Collins & Miller (the disclosure-liking and reciprocity effects), and Derlega et al. (the regulation and withdrawal of disclosure). Set at patch as the free level; a new engine may warrant a minor at the maintainer's `bump:minor` label.
