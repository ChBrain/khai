# Process: Evasion

## Taxonomy

[PTSD](process_ptsd.md), the cluster that flees the reminders -- and by fleeing, maintains the disorder.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: ptsd

## Initiated by

The pain of re-experiencing drives the persona to keep the trauma at bay.
This is DSM-5's criterion C, and it is the pivot of the whole mechanic. Having felt the [reexperiencing](process_reexperiencing.md) tear the past into the present, the persona does the intelligible thing: it tries not to go there again. What initiates the evasion is the ordinary wish to avoid pain -- and the tragedy of the disorder is that this reasonable wish is exactly what keeps it alive.

## Direction

From the wish to escape, toward a widening perimeter of avoidance, inner and outer.
The evasion runs two ways. Outwardly, the persona avoids the external reminders -- the people, places, conversations, activities, and objects that might trigger the memory, the perimeter often widening over time as more of the world becomes associated with the trauma. Inwardly, it avoids the internal reminders -- the thoughts, feelings, and memories themselves -- pushing them down, refusing to speak or think of the event, numbing to stay clear of them. Each act of avoidance brings immediate relief: the distress drops, the memory recedes. But the relief is purchased at the cost of recovery, because the persona never stays with the memory long enough for it to be processed and filed as past. The direction is a life quietly narrowing around the places the trauma must not be touched.

## Lever

Whether the avoidance is reinforced by relief while it forecloses repair. This cluster carries the disorder's cruelest turn, and staging it means staging the trap, not merely the flinch. Avoidance is negatively reinforced: it works in the moment, the distress falls, so it is learned and repeated -- and precisely because the persona escapes before the memory can update, the fear structure never extinguishes and the raw trace is preserved intact for the next cue (Foa's emotional processing; Ehlers & Clark's maintaining behaviors). The very manoeuvre meant to end the suffering is what perpetuates it. Stage the persona as reasonable, not weak: it is doing what pain teaches, and the teaching is a trap. The lever of repair is its exact reversal -- the staying-with, the exposure, that lets the memory finally be processed -- which this cluster marks the need for by being its obstacle.

## Echo

On that self-defeating flight:
shuts out the trauma's reminders, inner and outer, and wins a relief that reinforces the shutting-out and, by it, keeps the memory unprocessed and the loop turning. Evasion is one of four clusters, the flight from the trauma, set against the [reexperiencing](process_reexperiencing.md) it flees, the [estrangement](position_estrangement.md) its numbing feeds, and the [hyperarousal](process_hyperarousal.md) it cannot outrun. That the avoidance is the mechanism maintaining the disorder is the [root's](process_ptsd.md); the deliberate in-the-moment override of an impulse is the self-control engine's, and the ego's unconscious defensive manoeuvres are the defense engine's, where this cluster owns the specific trauma-avoidance that forecloses the memory's processing.
