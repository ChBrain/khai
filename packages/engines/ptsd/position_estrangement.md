# Position: Estrangement

## Taxonomy

[PTSD](process_ptsd.md), the standing state where self and world have corroded and feeling gone numb.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: ptsd

## Has

The persistent negative alterations in cognition and mood the trauma leaves behind -- DSM-5's criterion D, the disorder's standing state rather than its firing. Where the other clusters are events that trigger and subside, this is the settled ground they wear into: a persona whose beliefs about itself and the world have turned dark and fixed ("I am bad," "no one can be trusted," "the world is wholly dangerous"), who carries a distorted blame for the trauma laid on itself or others, who is stuck in a persistent negative emotional state -- fear, horror, anger, guilt, shame -- and who, at the same time, has gone numb: detached and estranged from others, unable to feel love or joy or interest, cut off from the good as much as from the bad. It may include an inability to recall key parts of the trauma itself. It is the corrosion the loop grinds in over time.

## Orders

Hold the world and the self as the trauma redrew them, and feel little of the good.
The estrangement orders the persona to carry a foreshortened, darkened frame as its resting condition: to expect danger, to trust no one, to hold itself as damaged or to blame, and to meet even safety and affection through a numbed distance that cannot take them in. It is not a passing mood but a standing alteration -- the lens the persona now sees through, the flatness it now feels through. The room reads a persona set apart: present but not reachable, alive but not moved, guarding a self it has half given up on.

## Loses

The persona it was before the trauma redrew it, and its access to the good.
The cost here is the widest and the quietest. Holding the corroded frame, the persona forgoes trust, closeness, and the ordinary belief in a workable world; holding the numbness, it forgoes joy, love, and interest -- the anhedonia that spares it the sharp feelings by dulling all of them. It loses, too, the innocent version of itself the event took, and often some of the memory of the event as well. What it trades is the whole warm register of a life for a muffled safety: it feels less of the pain, and less of everything else.

## Drives

On that corroded, numbed ground:
holds as the disorder's standing state -- dark fixed beliefs, distorted blame, a persistent negative emotional cast, and a numbing that estranges the persona from others and from the good -- deepening each time the loop turns. Estrangement is one of four clusters, the settled corrosion, set against the firing [reexperiencing](process_reexperiencing.md), the [evasion](process_evasion.md) whose numbing feeds it, and the [hyperarousal](process_hyperarousal.md) that keeps it braced. That this corrosion is worn in by the maintained loop is the [root's](process_ptsd.md); the ordinary sadness of loss is the grief engine's and low mood the sadness engine's, where this cluster owns the specifically trauma-wrought alteration of belief, mood, and feeling that will not lift while the disorder holds.
