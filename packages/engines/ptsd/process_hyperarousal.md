# Process: Hyperarousal

## Taxonomy

[PTSD](process_ptsd.md), the cluster where the body stays braced for a danger already past.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: ptsd

## Initiated by

The trauma leaves the persona's alarm system set too high, and it will not stand down.
This is DSM-5's criterion E, the marked alterations in arousal and reactivity. Having met a danger that overwhelmed it, the persona's threat-detection is recalibrated to treat the world as still dangerous -- the amygdala-driven alarm running hot, the body kept mobilized (van der Kolk, the body keeping the score). What initiates it is not a present threat but a nervous system that has not learned the threat is over; it is braced for an ambush that has already happened.

## Direction

From a lowered threshold, toward a persona perpetually on guard and easily set off.
The hyperarousal shows in a cluster of the always-ready: hypervigilance, the constant scanning for threat, sitting with its back to the wall, reading every exit; an exaggerated startle response, flinching hard at the sudden or the unexpected; irritability and angry outbursts, the fuse burned short; and reckless or self-destructive behavior, the recalibrated sense of danger gone the other way. It shows, too, in what it costs the body over time: disturbed sleep, the guard that will not lower even for rest, and impaired concentration, the attention forever split between the task and the perimeter. And when a trauma cue lands, this is the system that spikes -- the bodily reactivity that rides with the [reexperiencing](process_reexperiencing.md). The direction is a persona whose body is fighting a war the world has stopped waging.

## Lever

Whether the arousal is keyed to a past danger rather than a present one. The cluster turns on the mismatch: the persona is not responding to a real current threat in proportion, which would be ordinary vigilance, but carrying a mobilization left over from a danger that is gone. Stage the always-on quality -- the scanning, the startle, the short fuse, the sleepless guard -- as a body that cannot believe the event is over, not as a reasonable response to the room. The more the persona's setting is stuck high, the more the ordinary world reads to it as threat, and the more cues fire the whole loop. The lever of repair is the nervous system relearning safety -- the arousal finally standing down -- which this cluster marks by its absence.

## Echo

On that stuck alarm:
holds the persona mobilized -- hypervigilant, startling, irritable, sleepless -- for a danger already past, and spikes in bodily reactivity when a cue fires the [reexperiencing](process_reexperiencing.md). Hyperarousal is one of four clusters, the body's stuck alarm, set against the [reexperiencing](process_reexperiencing.md) it rides with, the [evasion](process_evasion.md) it cannot outrun, and the [estrangement](position_estrangement.md) it keeps braced. That the alarm stays high because the danger was never marked as over is the [root's](process_ptsd.md); the general physiology of bodily arousal is the body engine's, and the acute stress response to a present demand is the stress engine's, where this cluster owns the specifically trauma-stuck arousal that will not stand down.
