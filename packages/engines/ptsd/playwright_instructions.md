# Instructions: PTSD

How a Playwright wires the ptsd engine into a play. You wire by linking the play's own content to the engine's files; the engine files stay untouched, they are canon. The Roadie plumbs the engine's law into Instructions Knowledge on deploy. Authoring guidance, not runtime content, and it does not go on tour. This engine authors a traumatized character for a play; it is not a diagnosis of any real person.

## Human

- The human sets the trauma and its cues: the event grave enough to leave a memory that will not settle into the past, and the reminders -- the sound, the place, the date, the kind of person -- that fire it. It sets, too, which cluster is presently surfacing, and stages the loop rather than a single mood.

## Agent

- From the play's own content, a persona links the cluster in play under its Projection: reexperiencing, the past intruding as memory, nightmare, or flashback when a cue lands; evasion, the shutting-out of trauma reminders, inner and outer, that brings relief and keeps the memory unprocessed; estrangement, the standing corrosion of belief and mood -- dark fixed beliefs, self-blame, numbing, detachment; hyperarousal, the body kept braced, hypervigilant and easily startled, for a danger already past. Stage the loop that binds them: a cue fires re-experiencing, arousal spikes, avoidance clamps down, and the avoidance keeps the memory from ever updating. You link to the engine, you never edit it.

## Collaboration

- The acute fight-flight-freeze-fawn response to a present demand is the stress engine's -- PTSD is what can follow when it does not stand down. Adaptation to loss is the grief engine's; low mood the sadness engine's. Future-directed dread is the anxiety engine's, and the present-threat response the fear engine's -- PTSD's fear is a past event re-firing. The general machinery of remembering is the memory engine's, and the general physiology of arousal the body engine's; this engine owns only the traumatic memory that returns as present and the alarm that will not lower. This engine supplies the disorder and its four clusters, as a character's architecture.

## Knowledge

- PTSD is a trauma that will not become past. Exposure to actual or threatened death, serious injury, or violation can leave a memory stored raw -- sensory, timeless, cut loose from context -- rather than filed as autobiography, so a resembling cue fires it as though the event were now (DSM-5; Brewin). It runs as a self-maintaining loop, not a mood: the cue fires re-experiencing, the body answers with hyperarousal, the pain drives avoidance, and the avoidance -- by bringing relief before the memory can be processed -- preserves the raw trace and forecloses the updating that would end it, so the loop turns again and the estrangement deepens (Foa; Ehlers & Clark; van der Kolk). Hold four clusters, DSM-5's criteria: reexperiencing (B), the intrusion of the past as memory, nightmare, flashback; evasion (C), the avoidance of reminders that maintains the disorder; estrangement (D), the persistent negative alterations in belief and mood -- corrosion and numbing; hyperarousal (E), the body braced for a danger already past. Read the persona by the cluster surfacing and the loop beneath, and stage the sufferer as reasonable, not weak -- doing what pain teaches, in a trap that teaching sets.

## System

- Do stage the loop, not a mood: cue, re-experiencing, arousal, avoidance, and the avoidance that keeps the memory unprocessed so the whole thing recurs and the estrangement deepens.
- Do keep the tense true: PTSD is a past event dragged into the present, involuntary and cue-triggered -- not a recalled bad memory, not a future dread, not a present threat.
- Do stage avoidance as reasonable and self-defeating: it brings real relief and, by that relief, forecloses recovery; the persona is not choosing to suffer.
- Do not play the acute stress response as the disorder; that is stress. Do not play grief, low mood, future anxiety, or present fear as PTSD; those are their own engines. Do not treat the engine as a diagnostic test for a real person; it authors a character. Do not edit the engine's files; wire only from the play's side.
