# Process: Reexperiencing

## Taxonomy

[PTSD](process_ptsd.md), the cluster where the past is forced into the present -- intrusion, nightmare, flashback.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: ptsd

## Initiated by

A cue resembling the trauma reaches the persona, and the unprocessed memory fires.
This is DSM-5's criterion B, the hallmark of the disorder. Because the trauma was stored raw rather than filed as past, it has no when-and-where to hold it back: a matching cue -- a sound, a smell, a face, an angle of light, an anniversary -- can trigger it directly, bypassing ordinary recollection (Brewin's sensory representation, triggered perceptually rather than retrieved deliberately). What initiates it is not a decision to remember but a resemblance the persona did not choose to notice.

## Direction

From the cue, the memory returns not as recollection but as recurrence.
The re-experiencing runs along a spectrum of intensity. At the lighter end, intrusive memories arrive unbidden, vivid and unwanted, breaking into the day. In sleep, the trauma returns as nightmare. At the sharpest end is the flashback: a dissociative re-living in which the persona loses the present and is, for a moment, back inside the event -- seeing it, feeling it, reacting to it as though it were happening now. And whether or not the memory reaches awareness fully, a resembling cue brings intense psychological distress and bodily reactivity -- the heart, the breath, the flinch -- the [hyperarousal](process_hyperarousal.md) firing in concert. The direction is the collapse of the distance between then and now: the past does not stay behind the persona but arrives in front of it.

## Lever

Whether the memory returns as present rather than as past. The cluster turns on a single distinction: this is not a persona recalling a bad thing that happened, which is ordinary memory, but a persona to whom the thing is, in the returning moment, happening again. The mark of re-experiencing is the loss of the past tense -- the involuntariness, the sensory vividness, the felt nowness. Stage the intrusion as something that seizes the persona rather than something it summons, and pitch its severity honestly, from the unwanted image to the full flashback. The stronger and more cue-saturated the persona's world, the more often it fires; the lever is the resemblance between the present scene and the stored trauma.

## Echo

On that returning memory:
fires when a cue resembles the trauma -- intrusion, nightmare, or flashback -- and drags the past into the present, the [hyperarousal](process_hyperarousal.md) rising with it and the pain of it driving the [evasion](process_evasion.md) that will try to shut the cues out. Reexperiencing is one of four clusters, the intrusion of the past, set against the [evasion](process_evasion.md) that flees it, the [estrangement](position_estrangement.md) it deepens, and the [hyperarousal](process_hyperarousal.md) that answers it. That the memory returns as present because it was never processed as past is the [root's](process_ptsd.md); the general machinery of encoding and deliberate retrieval is the memory engine's, where this cluster owns only the involuntary, cue-triggered return of the trauma.
