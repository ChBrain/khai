# Process: PTSD

## Taxonomy

Trauma disorder / a traumatic memory that fires as present and maintains itself (no khai file of its own).

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: ptsd

## Initiated by

A persona meets a trauma -- actual or threatened death, serious injury, or violation -- and the memory of it does not settle into the past.
Ordinary events are encoded, given context, and filed as autobiography: over and done, retrievable in the past tense. A trauma-grade event can fail to be filed that way. Overwhelmed, the persona stores it raw -- sensory, fragmentary, timeless, cut loose from the where-and-when that would mark it finished (Brewin's dual representation; Ehlers & Clark's poorly elaborated memory). What enters is not a bad memory but an unprocessed one: an event that, because it was never made past, can be triggered as though it were present.

## Direction

From a cue, through re-experiencing and the body's alarm, to the avoidance that seals the loop shut.
The disorder runs as a self-maintaining circuit, and staging it is staging the circuit, not a mood. A cue that resembles the trauma -- a sound, a smell, a posture, a date -- fires the [reexperiencing](process_reexperiencing.md): the memory returns not as recollection but as recurrence, and the body answers with [hyperarousal](process_hyperarousal.md), braced as if the danger were here. The pain of that drives [evasion](process_evasion.md) -- the persona shuts out the reminders, inner and outer -- and the evasion works, in the moment: the distress drops. But relief is the trap. Because the persona never stays with the memory long enough for it to be processed, contextualized, and filed as past, the raw trace is preserved intact, ready to fire at the next cue (Foa's emotional processing; the extinction that avoidance forecloses). Round the loop grinds, and with each turn the [estrangement](position_estrangement.md) deepens -- the corroded view of self and world, the numbing, the distance from others. The direction is not toward resolution but toward a groove worn deeper.

## Lever

Whether the memory stays unprocessed -- and the avoidance is what keeps it so. The whole disorder turns on one mechanic: the trauma memory has not been integrated, and the persona's own defense against the pain is precisely what blocks the integration. Avoidance brings immediate relief and, by that reward, is reinforced; but it forecloses the corrective experience -- the staying-with that would let the memory update, gain context, and become past -- so the fear structure never extinguishes (Foa & Kozak; Ehlers & Clark's maintaining behaviors). This is why time alone does not heal it and why the sufferer is not choosing to dwell: the mechanism is a loop that feeds on the attempt to escape it. Stage that trap -- the relief that costs recovery -- because it is the engine of the thing. Its converse is the lever of repair: exposure, processing, the memory finally stayed with and filed, which is the work of recovery this engine marks the need for but does not itself perform.

## Echo

The disorder surfaces through four clusters, DSM-5's criteria: [reexperiencing](process_reexperiencing.md), the past forced into the present as intrusion, nightmare, and flashback; [evasion](process_evasion.md), the effortful avoidance of all that is trauma-linked, the manoeuvre that maintains the loop; [estrangement](position_estrangement.md), the persistent negative alterations in cognition and mood -- corroded belief, self-blame, numbing, detachment; and [hyperarousal](process_hyperarousal.md), the body kept on a war footing, hypervigilant and easily startled. The acute fight-flight-freeze-fawn response to a present demand is the stress engine's, and PTSD is what can follow when the response does not stand down; adaptation to irreversible loss is the grief engine's; future-directed dread of what has not happened is the anxiety engine's, where PTSD's fear is a past event re-firing; and the general machinery of encoding and retrieval is the memory engine's, where this engine owns only the traumatic memory that will not become past. PTSD is a trauma that stays present, maintained by the flight from it -- read across the four clusters and the loop that binds them, and authored as a character's architecture, never diagnosed onto a real person.
