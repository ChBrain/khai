# Position: Tuning

## Taxonomy

[Self-Monitoring](position_self_monitoring.md), the high-monitor's expressive control.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: self-monitoring

## Has

The high self-monitor's ability and habit of adjusting the presented self to fit: the expressive control that takes what the scanning found and shapes conduct, manner, and self to match it.
The persona has a self that is not one thing but a range -- the warmth turned up here, the edge shown there, the opinion voiced or withheld by the room -- a chameleon fluent across situations.
It is the acting half of self-monitoring: the read made real in a fitted presentation.

## Orders

Order the self toward the situation's reward. The persona modulates expression to suit the read -- becoming the confident one where confidence lands, the deferential one where deference does, the funny one, the serious one, as each room calls -- and does it skillfully enough that the fit rarely shows as effort.
It treats its own presentation as adjustable, a tool suited to the moment rather than a fixed report of what it is.
Whatever the room rewards, the persona can generally produce.

## Loses

The anchor of a single self. The persona that can be anything for any room loses the ground of being one thing regardless -- across enough tunings, the question of which self is real grows hard to answer, and others may sense a manner fitted rather than felt.
It loses, too, the moral simplicity of the consistent: a self tuned to advantage can shade toward the ingratiating, and sincerity becomes something the persona must choose rather than simply have.

## Drives

On the loss of that anchor: spends the fluency, fitting each room and winning what fitting wins, or holds a line it will not tune past -- a core kept back from the adjusting, so the chameleon is not all surface.
It acts on what [scanning](position_scanning.md) reads and is the active opposite of [consistency](position_consistency.md), which will not tune. The fitting of a role's required feeling for a wage is the emotional-labor engine's, and the image the tuning protects is the face engine's; this owns the trait-level habit of adjusting self-presentation to the situation.
