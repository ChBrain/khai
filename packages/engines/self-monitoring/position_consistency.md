# Position: Consistency

## Taxonomy

[Self-Monitoring](position_self_monitoring.md), the low-monitor's inner direction.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: self-monitoring

## Has

The low self-monitor's inner-directed constancy: a presentation that flows from the persona's own attitudes, feelings, and dispositions and holds steady across situations, the same self shown in every room whether or not it fits.
The persona has one face -- not from an inability to read rooms so much as an indifference to fitting them, its conduct reported from within rather than tailored without.
It is the pole that does not scan and does not tune, because it is not in the business of matching.

## Orders

Order conduct from the inside out. The persona acts on what it actually holds -- says what it thinks, shows what it feels, keeps its manner constant -- and lets the situation take that self as it finds it, treating consistency between the inner and the shown as the thing to protect.
Where the high self-monitor asks what the room wants, this persona asks only what it holds, and presents that.
The audience changes; the self does not.

## Loses

The rewards of fitting. The persona that will not tune loses the social smoothness, the situational advantage, the eased path that a matched presentation buys -- it pays in friction where the fitted self would have slid through, misreading rooms not because it cannot read them but because it will not act on the reading.
It loses the flexibility to be what a moment needs, and can seem rigid, tactless, or out of step where a chameleon would have charmed.

## Drives

On the loss of that fit: holds the one self and pays the friction, wearing the constancy as integrity, or bends -- just here, just once -- to a situation whose cost of rigidity is finally too high, and feels the bend as a small betrayal of a self that does not usually move.
It is the opposite pole to [scanning](position_scanning.md) and [tuning](position_tuning.md): the self that will not read to fit and will not adjust to match. The stable self-concept it expresses is the identity engine's, and the authenticity it prizes shades toward that engine's territory; this owns the low-monitor trait of holding one presentation across situations.
