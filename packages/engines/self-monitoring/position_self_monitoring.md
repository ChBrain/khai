# Position: Self-Monitoring

## Taxonomy

Personality / the social psychology of expressive self-control.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: self-monitoring

## Has

A characteristic degree of self-monitoring: how much the persona watches its own expressive behavior and self-presentation and tunes it to what the situation calls for, versus letting the presented self flow from inner states held constant across situations.
High on the trait, the persona is a reader of rooms and a tuner of selves -- attentive to what each situation wants and able to become it.
Low on the trait, the persona is one self everywhere -- guided from within by attitude and feeling, the same in every room whether or not it fits.

## Orders

Order attention and presentation by the trait's level. High, the persona orders its gaze outward to social cues and its conduct toward whatever presentation fits, treating the self shown as a thing to be suited to the audience.
Low, the persona orders its conduct from within, treating the self shown as an expression of what is actually felt and believed, and holding it steady as the audience changes.
The trait sets, before any single situation, whether the persona asks _what does this call for?_ or _what do I hold?_

## Loses

What the trait's level costs at each end. High, the persona loses the guarantee that the self shown is the self felt -- and, across enough rooms, a settled sense of which self is the real one, worn thin by the changing.
Low, the persona loses the fit: the social smoothness of matching the room, the advantage of the presentation each situation rewards, paid in friction where a tuned self would have slid through.
Neither end holds both the fit and the constancy; the trait spends one to buy the other.

## Drives

On the loss its level imposes: the high self-monitor leans into the fit, becoming what each room rewards and paying in a self spread thin, or checks the tuning against a core it refuses to trade away. The low self-monitor holds its one self and pays the friction, or bends, just here, to a situation that a rigid constancy would have cost too much.
The trait sets the resting point; the drive is how the persona spends or defends it when fit and constancy pull apart.
