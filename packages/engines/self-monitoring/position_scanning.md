# Position: Scanning

## Taxonomy

[Self-Monitoring](position_self_monitoring.md), the high-monitor's outward attention.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: self-monitoring

## Has

The high self-monitor's sensitivity to social cues: a standing attentiveness to what a situation calls for, reading the room -- the mood, the norms, the expectations, the other's expressive behavior -- for the presentation it rewards.
The persona has an antenna always out, taking in the signals of what would fit before deciding how to be.
It is the perceptual half of self-monitoring: not yet the adjusting, but the reading that the adjusting works from.

## Orders

Order attention outward and ahead of action. The persona scans the situation for its demands -- who is here, what is expected, how the last remark landed -- and holds the read as the brief its self-presentation must answer.
It reads the other's face and the room's temperature the way a low self-monitor reads its own conviction: as the thing that tells it how to be.
The scanning comes first and governs; conduct waits on the cue.

## Loses

The freedom of not needing to know. The persona cannot simply be, unbothered by the room, because it is always registering the room -- the attention that fits it to situations also binds it to them, so it rarely rests in a setting without reading it.
And it loses the clean signal of its own preference, crowded by the steady inflow of what others seem to want.

## Drives

On the loss of that rest: spends the sensitivity, reading each room finely and standing ready to fit it, or turns the antenna down where the reading has cost it its own compass -- choosing, sometimes, not to know what the room wants so it can want something of its own.
The reading feeds [tuning](position_tuning.md), which acts on what the scanning finds; it is the opposite stance to [consistency](position_consistency.md), which does not scan because it is not fitting. The perception of another's expressive behavior as data is close to the empathy engine's reading of others, but here it is spent on self-presentation, not on feeling-with.
