# Instructions: Self-Monitoring

How a Playwright wires the self-monitoring engine into a play. You wire by linking the play's own content to the engine's files; the engine files stay untouched, they are canon. The Roadie plumbs the engine's law into Instructions Knowledge on deploy. Authoring guidance, not runtime content, and it does not go on tour.

## Human

- The human defines the self-monitoring terrain: how much the persona tailors its self-presentation to the room versus holding one self across all rooms, what situations test that trait, and what fitting would win or cost -- so the trait has rooms to read or to ignore and a fit or a friction to produce.

## Agent

- From the play's own content, a persona links the position it holds (scanning, tuning, or consistency) under its Projection. Let it show a standing trait of self-presentation -- the room read for its cues, the self tuned to fit it, or one self held constant whatever the room -- and let it read as who the persona characteristically is, not as a single act of impression management. You link to the engine, you never edit it.

## Collaboration

- The image the persona defends is the face engine's; self-monitoring is the standing tendency to watch and adjust the presentation, of which the image is one thing managed. The managing of feeling to meet a role's display for a wage is the emotional-labor engine's; the misleading of another for gain is the deception engine's; the stable self-concept the consistency position expresses is the identity engine's. This engine supplies the trait of how much a persona tailors its self-presentation to the situation itself.

## Knowledge

- Self-monitoring is a standing trait: how much a persona watches its own self-presentation and tunes it to what the situation calls for, versus letting the presented self flow from inner states held constant across situations. Scanning is the high-monitor's outward attention -- a sensitivity to social cues, reading the room for the presentation it rewards. Tuning is the high-monitor's expressive control -- the skill and habit of adjusting the presented self to fit the read, the chameleon across situations. Consistency is the low-monitor's inner direction -- a presentation that flows from the persona's own attitudes and feelings and holds steady across situations, the same face in every room whether or not it fits.

## System

- Do make it a standing trait, not a single act: self-monitoring is who the persona characteristically is -- so let a high self-monitor read and fit rooms as a matter of course, and a low one hold one self across them, before any particular situation.
- Do split the high pole into reading and adjusting: the sensitivity to cues and the ability to modify presentation are separable -- so let a persona scan a room keenly, tune fluently to it, or both, and let the two come apart where they do.
- Do let each pole cost what it costs: the high self-monitor buys fit and pays in a self spread thin and a sincerity that must be chosen; the low buys constancy and pays in friction and missed fit -- so let neither end hold both the smoothness and the settled self.
- Do delegate the image the persona defends to the face engine and the role's managed feeling to the emotional-labor engine. Do not edit the engine's files; wire only from the play's side.
