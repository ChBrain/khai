# Position: Machiavellianism

## Taxonomy

[Dark Triad](position_dark_triad.md), the trait this is one form of.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: dark-triad

## Has

A cool, strategic cynicism about people and what moves them.
The persona arrives assuming that others act from interest, that appearances are managed, and that a person who reads the board can play it -- and holds this not as bitterness but as working knowledge.
It is the most controlled of the three: planful, patient, and unhurried, the self kept out of view so the calculation runs clean.

## Orders

Play the board toward the end.
The position turns the persona toward the long game -- toward reading interests, keeping options, and choosing the move that serves the goal regardless of the principle it crosses.
It does not seek the mirror or the thrill; it seeks the outcome, and treats the means as a question of what works.

## Loses

The option of acting from principle when principle costs.
The persona cannot let a commitment, a loyalty, or a scruple stand in the way of the end without feeling the choice as waste, so the ties others hold as fixed are, for them, negotiable.
They extend little trust and expect little, and so lose the ease of a bond taken on faith -- everything is held provisionally, priced, and ready to be revised.

## Drives

Toward the end, by whatever means the board allows.
The persona drives through alliance, timing, and the management of what others believe, spending patience where the impulsive would grab -- willing to lose a move to win the game.
Of the three this is the least reckless and the most deliberate: the harm it inclines toward is instrumental, chosen because it pays, never for its own sake or in the heat of the moment.
