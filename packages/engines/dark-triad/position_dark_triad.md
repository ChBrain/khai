# Position: Dark Triad

## Taxonomy

Disposition / dark personality (no khai file of its own yet).

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: dark-triad

## Has

A resting disposition of cold self-advancement, held before any particular act.
The persona arrives reading others as terrain rather than ends -- useful, obstructive, or irrelevant -- with the empathy and the restraint that would slow such a reading turned low.
It is a socially aversive tilt that stays inside the ordinary range, not a diagnosis: the shared core beneath three variants, which differ in how the self is served and how little the others weigh.

## Orders

Serve the self; the others are means.
The disposition decides no single act, but it tilts every one toward advantage for the persona and discounts the cost to anyone else.
It runs beneath the surface manner, which may be warm, plain, or charming; the tilt is what the manner is for.

## Loses

The trust that only reciprocity earns.
Once the disposition is read, the persona cannot be met as safe, and the goods that require being trusted -- the loyalty freely given, the benefit of the doubt, the durable tie -- close to them.
They also lose the check that others' welfare places on most people; nothing internal argues the other side, so the argument, when it comes, must come from outside.

## Drives

Toward getting ahead at a cost carried by someone else.
Which shape the drive takes depends on what the self needs and what the disposition is missing: the admiration a self must have to stay inflated ([narcissism](position_narcissism.md)), the strategic patience that plays a person toward an end ([machiavellianism](position_machiavellianism.md)), or the cold, unbraked reach for the near reward ([psychopathy](position_psychopathy.md)).
The disposition inclines the persona toward harm; the harm itself -- the lie told, the trust broken, the force used -- is carried by the deception, betrayal, and aggression engines, not asserted here.
