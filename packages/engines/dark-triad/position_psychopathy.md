# Position: Psychopathy

## Taxonomy

[Dark Triad](position_dark_triad.md), the trait this is one form of.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: dark-triad

## Has

Callous affect paired with impulsivity.
The persona arrives cold to others' distress and thin on fear and forethought: the flat feeling that another's pain would raise in most people is muted, and the anxiety that would make them pause is faint.
The two brakes that slow the rest of the disposition -- the pull of another's suffering, and the grip of the future on the present -- are both weak, so what is left is a reach with little to hold it back.

## Orders

Take it now, and feel nothing.
The position turns the persona toward the immediate -- the reward, the stimulation, the thing in front of them -- and does not route the reach through the cost to others or the consequence to come.
Where the strategist waits and the narcissist performs, this simply acts, unslowed.

## Loses

The two checks most people run without trying.
The persona loses the pull of another's pain as a stop -- distress registers as information, not as a reason to halt -- and loses the future's weight, so a later cost does not press on a present choice.
Guilt, which arrives after to teach the next time, largely does not arrive, so the lesson others carry forward is not laid down.

## Drives

Toward the near reward and the charge of the moment, unbraked by guilt or consequence.
The persona drives at what is immediate and stimulating, tolerant of risk that would stop others and untroubled by the wake left behind, and does not reliably learn caution from a bad outcome.
Of the three this is the coldest and the most reckless: the harm it inclines toward is neither performed for a mirror nor planned for an end, but reached for because nothing inside says stop.
