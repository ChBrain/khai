# @chbrain/khai-engine-dark-triad

## 0.1.1

### Patch Changes

- 3265db5: Add the dark-triad engine: the aversive dispositional core a persona may arrive carrying -- a resting tilt toward cold self-advancement, others read as means, with the empathy and restraint that would slow it turned low. It is the dark complement of the temperament engine's bright five, and it models a `position` (a trait held), never the harmful acts it inclines toward. The root (`position_dark_triad.md`) declares the shared core; three traits are the shapes it resolves into: `narcissism` (a grandiose self that needs admiration and is wounded when it is withheld), `machiavellianism` (a cool, strategic cynicism that plays people toward an end -- the most controlled of the three), and `psychopathy` (callous affect and impulsivity, unbraked by others' pain or by consequence).

  Framed descriptively and dramaturgically throughout: the engine supplies the disposition and routes the act it inclines toward -- the lie, the broken trust, the force -- to the `deception`, `betrayal`, and `aggression` engines that own it, never performing the harm itself. Bounded against `temperament` (the normal-range bright five), `empathy` (the capacity the trait runs low on), `pride` (hubristic pride as the momentary emotion vs the standing trait), `power`/`status-move`/`persuasion` (the tactics of influence), and `moral-judgment` (how an onlooker reads the conduct). Warranted (LORE) on Paulhus & Williams (the paper that named the triad), Raskin & Hall (narcissism inventory), Christie & Geis (the Mach-IV), Hare (the two-factor picture of psychopathy), and Jones & Paulhus (the SD3). Triad, not tetrad -- sadism is left to a later decision. Set at patch as the free level; a new engine may warrant a minor at the maintainer's `bump:minor` label.
