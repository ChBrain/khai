# Dark Triad: Reference

## Line of Work

The dark triad as **position**: the aversive dispositional core a persona may arrive carrying -- a resting tilt toward cold self-advancement, others read as means, with the empathy and restraint that would slow it turned low. The domain models a _trait_, the standing shape the room comes to read, not the acts it inclines toward: the lie, the broken trust, the force used are owned by the deception, betrayal, and aggression engines, and this engine routes to them rather than performing the harm itself.

The spine is the personality-psychology finding that three socially aversive but non-clinical traits -- narcissism, Machiavellianism, and psychopathy -- are distinct yet share a callous, self-serving core, and can be measured in ordinary populations. So the engine is the dark complement of the temperament engine's bright five: where temperament models the normal-range disposition the room reads when nothing is happening, this models the aversive-range core beneath the same surface. The three traits are the shapes that core resolves into -- the self that must be admired, the mind that plays the board, the reach that feels no brake.

## Restrictions

What the engine refuses to model, and to whom it delegates.

- **Temperament**: The engine models the aversive, dark-range dispositional core, not the normal-range personality. The bright five -- how far a persona reaches out, how steadily they hold, how widely they range, how hard they push, how warmly they meet -- is owned by the temperament engine; this engine is its dark complement, not a replacement.
- **Deception, Betrayal, Aggression**: The engine models the disposition that inclines toward harm, not the harmful acts themselves. Creating a false belief is owned by the deception engine, violating a trust by the betrayal engine, and mobilising hostile force by the aggression engine; this engine routes the act to them and never performs it.
- **Empathy**: The engine models the trait that runs low on fellow-feeling, not the capacity itself. The baseline ability to take in another's inner state is owned by the empathy engine; this engine governs the disposition in which that capacity is turned down, not the capacity's own range.
- **Pride**: The engine models narcissism as a standing trait, not the momentary emotion. Hubristic pride -- the self-evaluation that credits a success to the whole self -- is owned by the pride engine; this engine governs the resting disposition beneath such moments, not the state.
- **Power, Status-move, Persuasion**: The engine models the disposition that reaches for influence and advantage, not the tactics of exercising it. The base a persona moves others from, the discrete status tactic, and the attempt to change what another does are owned by those engines; this engine governs the tilt toward using them, not the moves.
- **Moral judgment**: The engine models the persona's own disposition, not how an onlooker reads its conduct. The moral lens another applies to the persona's acts is owned by the moral-judgment engine.

## Encoding

Source to constraint, per file.

- **[dark triad](position_dark_triad.md)** (the root): A resting disposition of cold self-advancement, others read as means, the slowing empathy and restraint turned low -- the shared core of three variants. Anchored by Paulhus & Williams (the unified triad) and Jones & Paulhus (the shared core kept distinct).
- **[narcissism](position_narcissism.md)** (trait): A grandiose self that needs admiration to stay inflated and is wounded when it is withheld; the drive toward superiority and its restoration. Anchored by Raskin & Hall (the narcissism inventory) and Paulhus & Williams.
- **[machiavellianism](position_machiavellianism.md)** (trait): A cool, strategic cynicism that plays people toward an end by whatever the board allows; the most controlled and least impulsive of the three. Anchored by Christie & Geis (the Mach-IV and construct).
- **[psychopathy](position_psychopathy.md)** (trait): Callous affect and impulsivity, the pull of others' pain and the grip of consequence both weak; the cold, unbraked reach for the near reward. Anchored by Hare (the two-factor picture) and Paulhus & Williams.
