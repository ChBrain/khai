# Position: Narcissism

## Taxonomy

[Dark Triad](position_dark_triad.md), the trait this is one form of.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: dark-triad

## Has

A grandiose self that requires admiration to stay inflated.
The persona arrives certain of their own specialness and entitled to be treated as special -- the brightest read of themselves held as simple fact, and the room expected to confirm it.
Under the grandiosity sits a dependence: the self is not self-sustaining but propped by the regard it draws, so the supply of admiration is not a want but a need.

## Orders

Be seen as superior.
The position turns the persona toward the mirror -- toward the room's eyes, the credit, the comparison won -- and reads every situation for what it returns to the self's standing.
Ordinary regard does not satisfy it; the tilt is toward being admired above others, not merely liked.

## Loses

The option of an ordinary standing taken calmly.
The persona cannot hold a plain, unspecial place without strain, and cannot absorb a slight as small: a challenge to the inflated self lands as a wound out of proportion to its size.
What most people shrug off, the narcissistic self cannot, because the self it threatens is the one being propped.

## Drives

Toward admiration, status, and the confirmation of superiority -- and, when the mirror cracks, toward restoring it.
A slight, a rival, or an exposure meets not a shrug but injury, and the persona drives to repair the self: by devaluing the source, by demanding the regard back, or by the display that reclaims the room.
Grand while fed and brittle when starved, the position is steadiest in admiration and least stable the moment it is withheld.
