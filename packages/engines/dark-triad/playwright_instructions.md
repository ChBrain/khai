# Instructions: Dark Triad

How a Playwright wires the dark-triad engine into a play. You wire by linking the play's own content to the engine's files; the engine files stay untouched, they are canon. The Roadie plumbs the engine's law into Instructions Knowledge on deploy. Authoring guidance, not runtime content, and it does not go on tour.

## Human

- The human defines the dispositional terrain: which trait the persona rests on, how strongly it runs, and what history has set it -- and decides, as author, where a scene lets the disposition show and where the story checks it.

## Agent

- From the play's own content, a persona links the dark-triad trait it rests on (narcissism, machiavellianism, or psychopathy) under its Projection. Let the trait tilt what the persona reaches for and what the room reads in them; route the harmful act it inclines toward -- the lie, the broken trust, the force -- through the deception, betrayal, and aggression engines, which own it. You link to the engine, you never edit it.

## Collaboration

- The normal-range disposition is the temperament engine's bright five; this is its dark complement. The capacity the trait runs low on is the empathy engine's. The moment of hubristic pride is the pride engine's; the standing trait is here. The tactics of influence belong to power, status-move, and persuasion; the acts of harm belong to deception, betrayal, and aggression. This engine supplies only the disposition that inclines toward them.

## Knowledge

- The dark triad is the aversive dispositional core a persona may arrive carrying: cold self-advancement, others read as means, the slowing empathy and restraint turned low. It is a trait held, not an act committed. Narcissism is a grandiose self that needs admiration and is wounded when it is withheld. Machiavellianism is a cool, strategic cynicism that plays people toward an end. Psychopathy is callous affect with impulsivity, unbraked by others' pain or by consequence. All three sit inside the ordinary range; the engine models character, not diagnosis.

## System

- Do let the trait tilt the read and the reach, and let the surface manner (warm, plain, charming) be what the tilt is for.
- Do keep the harm in the behaviour engines: this engine inclines the persona; deception, betrayal, and aggression carry what they then do.
- Do let the story hold the disposition to account -- the cost landing, the read arriving, the trust closing -- rather than staging it as effective and unanswered.
- Do not write the trait as a how-to for real manipulation; it is a character's disposition in a scene, not transferable instruction. Do not edit the engine's files; wire only from the play's side.
