# Process: Anxiety

## Taxonomy

Emotion / the apprehension of an uncertain future threat.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: anxiety

## Initiated by

A persona is readied against a danger that has no clear object, is not yet present, and may never come.
This is anxiety: the diffuse, anticipatory apprehension of an uncertain future threat -- a bracing for the possible rather than a reaction to the actual (Barlow; LeDoux).
Where fear fires sharp and cued at an imminent, identified threat, anxiety is sustained and objectless, running on a distinct defensive system -- the sustained-threat circuitry of the BNST against the amygdala's phasic alarm (Davis), the behavioral inhibition system that halts and scans when threat is possible but unconfirmed (Gray & McNaughton).

## Direction

Toward a future that has not arrived -- and, in the body, toward readiness held with no release.
Anxiety does not discharge against a present danger as fear does; it braces against a coming one, keeping the system primed -- scanning, worrying, muscles ready -- for a threat it cannot locate or resolve.
Its direction is forward and inward, into the uncertain what-might-be, the alarm sustained because the danger is never confirmed enough to fight or flee, nor absent enough to stand down.

## Lever

The appraisal of an uncertain future threat -- something bad that might happen, unlocatable and unresolved.
What anxiety spends is the ease of the untroubled present: the persona cannot rest in a now that is, in fact, safe, because its regard is pulled to a possible harm ahead.
It fires precisely where the threat is uncertain -- knowable danger draws fear and a plan, but the open question, the unconfirmed risk, the not-knowing, draws anxiety, which is why it thrives on ambiguity and cannot be argued down by a present that offers no danger to point to.

## Echo

The threat never comes, or comes and is survived -- but the bracing exhausts, because a readiness with no release wears the system down.
Its adaptive edge is preparation, the cost of a false alarm cheap against the cost of a real one missed; its excess is the chronic apprehension of the anxious, worn thin by dangers that never arrive.
It runs as [worry](process_worry.md), [vigilance](process_vigilance.md), and [looming](process_looming.md), tuned by a standing [intolerance of uncertainty](position_uncertainty.md). The sharp, cued response to a present, identified threat is the fear engine's; the physiological mobilization to a present stressor is the stress engine's; the repetitive dwelling on a past or present loss is the rumination engine's; the perceptual-attentional baseline that filters what is noticed is the apprehension engine's. This engine owns the apprehension of an uncertain future threat.
