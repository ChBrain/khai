# Position: Intolerance of Uncertainty

## Taxonomy

Affect / the trait of intolerance of uncertainty -- the standing vulnerability beneath [Anxiety](process_anxiety.md) (Dugas & Ladouceur; Carleton's "fear of the unknown").

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: anxiety

## Has

A standing inability to bear the not-knowing -- a disposition to find uncertainty itself threatening, distressing, and to be resolved at any cost.
Trait intolerance of uncertainty, as against the state of any one anxiety: the persona for whom an open question is unbearable, ambiguity reads as danger, and "it might be fine" gives no relief because it is not "it is fine."
It is argued to be the core cognitive vulnerability beneath anxiety (Carleton) -- the disposition that turns the ordinary uncertainty of life into a standing supply of threat.

## Orders

Run from here, into every open question.
The intolerant persona meets ambiguity by trying to close it -- seeking reassurance, over-preparing, checking, avoiding the unresolvable -- and where it cannot close it, worries; the tolerant persona meets the same open question and lets it stay open, untroubled by the not-knowing.
It sets how much uncertainty the persona can hold before the worry, vigilance, and looming fire, the standing dial that tunes the whole state.

## Loses

The ease of the person who can live with an open outcome.
The highly intolerant persona meets a world thick with unbearable uncertainty -- every unanswered question a threat, every ambiguous sign read as danger -- and loses the rest that only comes from accepting that much cannot be known; it pays in reassurance-seeking, over-control, and the exhaustion of trying to make a probabilistic world certain.
What narrows is the range of open questions the persona can leave open.

## Drives

On the loss of that ease:
rides the intolerance -- keeps seeking certainty, over-preparing, avoiding the unresolvable -- or works against it, learning to sit with the open question, to let "might" be enough, to tolerate the not-knowing that no amount of checking will close.
The threshold holds underneath either way, a resting level the persona manages rather than a state that comes and goes.
