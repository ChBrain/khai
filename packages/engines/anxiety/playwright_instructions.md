# Instructions: Anxiety

How a Playwright wires the anxiety engine into a play. You wire by linking the play's own content to the engine's files; the engine files stay untouched, they are canon. The Roadie plumbs the engine's law into Instructions Knowledge on deploy. Authoring guidance, not runtime content, and it does not go on tour.

## Human

- The human defines the anxiety terrain: what uncertain future threat hangs over the persona -- a possible loss, an unresolved risk, an open question that will not close -- so a moment holds a danger that is anticipated, not present, and cannot be simply located and faced.

## Agent

- From the play's own content, a persona links the anxiety a moment runs under its Projection: what uncertain threat is appraised, which forms are present -- the worried stream of what-ifs, the vigilant scanning, the sense of the harm looming closer -- and how much uncertainty its standing disposition can bear. Let it read as the bracing against a danger not yet arrived, sustained and objectless, and keep it distinct from the sharp cued jolt of fear. You link to the engine, you never edit it.

## Collaboration

- The sharp, cued response to a present, identified threat -- and its panic, phobia, and terror -- is the fear engine's; the physiological fight/flight/freeze/fawn mobilization to a present stressor is the stress engine's; the repetitive dwelling on a past or present loss is the rumination engine's; the perceptual-attentional baseline that filters what is noticed is the apprehension engine's; the pessimistic resting expectation of a bad future is the hope engine's dread stance. This engine supplies the apprehension of an uncertain future threat -- the worry, the vigilance, the looming, and the intolerance of uncertainty beneath them.

## Knowledge

- Anxiety is the diffuse, anticipatory apprehension of an uncertain future threat: the persona braces against a danger that has no clear object, is not yet present, and may never come (Barlow; LeDoux). Where fear is phasic and cued -- a reaction to an imminent, identified threat -- anxiety is sustained and objectless, running on a distinct system (the BNST and Gray's behavioral inhibition system, against the amygdala's phasic alarm). It runs in forms. Worry is the verbal-cognitive stream -- the chain of future what-ifs, thought in words, that both engages and avoids the feared (Borkovec). Vigilance is the attentional-somatic readiness -- hypervigilance, the attentional bias toward threat, the startle set high, the body kept primed. Looming is the anticipatory core -- the threat felt as approaching, mounting, closing in (Riskind). Intolerance of uncertainty is the trait -- a standing inability to bear the not-knowing, the core vulnerability that turns open questions into threats (Carleton).

## System

- Do keep anxiety diffuse and anticipatory: it braces for a possible future harm, objectless and unresolved, not a present identified one -- let the persona be unable to locate or face the threat, which is what makes it anxiety rather than fear.
- Do stage the forms legibly: the worried stream of what-ifs, the vigilant scanning body, the sense of the threat looming closer -- and let the worry run at the future (where rumination runs at the past) and the vigilance live in the body, not only the thoughts.
- Do let intolerance of uncertainty set the threshold: the intolerant persona cannot bear the open question and worries, over-prepares, and seeks reassurance; the tolerant one lets the not-knowing stay open. Let anxiety thrive on ambiguity and resist being argued down by a safe present.
- Do let the bracing exhaust: a readiness held with no release wears the persona down, which is anxiety's cost. Do delegate the cued jolt of a present threat to the fear engine and the autonomic mobilization to the stress engine. Do not edit the engine's files; wire only from the play's side.
