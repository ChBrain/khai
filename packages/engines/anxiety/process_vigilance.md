# Process: Vigilance

## Taxonomy

[Anxiety](process_anxiety.md), the attentional-somatic form -- the scanning, readied body.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: anxiety

## Initiated by

The persona's attention and body are held ready for a danger that has not shown.
This is hypervigilance, anxiety's attentional-somatic form: the scanning of the environment for threat, the attentional bias that snags on any cue of danger, the startle set high and the muscles kept primed -- the output of the behavioral inhibition system, halting and checking when threat is possible (Gray & McNaughton).
It is anxiety in the body and the eyes -- the readiness itself, before any threat is confirmed.

## Direction

Outward, sweeping -- toward wherever a threat might appear.
Vigilance turns the persona into a sensor: the gaze flicks to exits and faces, the ear catches the ambiguous sound, attention is captured by the possible sign of danger and held there, biased toward threat and slow to release it.
Its direction is the perimeter, scanned and rescanned for the harm that uncertainty says could be anywhere.

## Lever

What vigilance spends -- the ease of the unguarded body and the settled attention.
The readied state is metabolically and attentionally costly: the startle kept high, the muscles tensed, the mind unable to rest its regard, all held against a threat that has not arrived; vigilance spends that reserve to buy early detection, trading calm for the chance of catching the danger first.
A production holds this lever by letting the body carry the anxiety -- the flinch at the sound, the scan of the room, the inability to settle -- so the state reads as physical readiness, not only worried thought.

## Echo

Nothing appears, or something ambiguous does and is read as threat -- and either way the vigilance confirms itself, since a quiet perimeter proves the scanning worked and an ambiguous cue proves it was needed.
It is why anxiety is exhausting and self-sustaining: the readied body finds threat cues everywhere and stands down nowhere.
The cued, phasic alarm and flight when a real threat is identified is the fear engine's, and the full autonomic mobilization to a present stressor is the stress engine's; vigilance is the sustained pre-threat readiness that precedes both, the scanning that [worry](process_worry.md) narrates and [looming](process_looming.md) feels closing in.
