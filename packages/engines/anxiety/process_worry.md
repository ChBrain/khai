# Process: Worry

## Taxonomy

[Anxiety](process_anxiety.md), the verbal-cognitive form -- the stream of what-ifs.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: anxiety

## Initiated by

The persona runs a chain of future catastrophes in words -- what if this, then that, then the worse thing after.
This is worry, anxiety's cognitive form (Borkovec): a predominantly verbal-linguistic stream of thought about possible negative future events, run as talk to oneself rather than as vivid image.
It is anxiety thinking -- the mind rehearsing the threat forward, link by link, toward outcomes that have not happened and mostly will not.

## Direction

Forward, down the chain -- from the present question to the ramifying harms it might lead to.
Worry runs the catastrophe outward: each answer breeds a further what-if, the thought never resting on a resolution but extending into the next contingency, the next thing that could go wrong.
Its direction is the branching future, followed in words further than any real likelihood warrants.

## Lever

What worry spends -- and, paradoxically, what it defends.
Worry feels like problem-solving and is often its counterfeit: the verbal stream, being abstract and wordy, dampens the vivid imagery and the somatic jolt that a fully-pictured threat would bring, so worrying is in part an avoidance of feeling the fear it circles (Borkovec's avoidance theory).
It spends the peace of the unrehearsed mind and buys a thin sense of preparedness, while keeping the threat verbal enough not to be truly faced.

## Echo

The catastrophes are rehearsed and mostly do not come -- but the rehearsal is negatively reinforced each time the feared thing fails to happen, so worry teaches itself that it works.
It is the engine of generalized anxiety, the mind that cannot stop running the what-ifs, and it is future-facing where its cousin looks back.
The repetitive dwelling on a loss or wrong that has already happened is the rumination engine's -- rumination broods over the past, worry runs at the future; the two share a repetitive, verbal, self-focused form, and this one is anxiety's, aimed ahead at [vigilance](process_vigilance.md)'s scanned-for threats.
