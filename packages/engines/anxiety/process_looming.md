# Process: Looming

## Taxonomy

[Anxiety](process_anxiety.md), the anticipatory core -- the threat felt as approaching.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: anxiety

## Initiated by

The persona feels an uncertain harm not as static but as drawing nearer -- mounting, closing in, about to arrive.
This is the looming form (Riskind's looming vulnerability; Grupe & Nitschke on the anticipation of uncertain threat): the appraisal of a threat as rapidly rising in likelihood or proximity, a danger on an approaching trajectory rather than a fixed possibility.
It is the anticipatory heart of anxiety -- not that something bad exists, but that it is coming.

## Direction

Toward the approaching edge -- the future harm sensed as accelerating in.
Looming gives anxiety its temporal shape: the threat is read as a trajectory, gaining, so the persona feels time running out and the danger swelling, a sense of being closed in upon that no static appraisal of risk would produce.
Its direction is the collapsing distance between now and the feared thing, felt as movement even when nothing has changed.

## Lever

What looming spends -- the sense of time and room to act.
A threat held as static leaves space; a threat felt as looming steals it, converting an uncertain future into an emergency that is almost here, which is why anxiety feels urgent out of proportion to any confirmed danger.
A production holds this lever by giving the threat a felt trajectory -- the deadline rushing up, the dreaded meeting nearing, the sense that it is gaining -- so the anxiety reads as an approaching harm, not a distant one.

## Echo

The looming thing arrives and is met, or dissolves as its imagined approach outruns any real one -- and the persona learns, or fails to learn, that the closing-in was the appraisal, not the world.
It is what makes anxiety a state of anticipation rather than reaction: the harm's motion is felt before its substance is known.
The pessimistic expectation that the future will go badly, held as a resting orientation rather than a felt approach, is the hope engine's dread stance; looming is the live sense of that bad future accelerating in, which [worry](process_worry.md) puts into words and [vigilance](process_vigilance.md) braces the body against, all sharpened by a low tolerance for the [uncertainty](position_uncertainty.md) that leaves the trajectory open.
