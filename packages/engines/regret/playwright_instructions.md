# Instructions: Regret

How a Playwright wires the regret engine into a play. You wire by linking the play's own content to the engine's files; the engine files stay untouched, they are canon. The Roadie plumbs the engine's law into Instructions Knowledge on deploy. Authoring guidance, not runtime content, and it does not go on tour.

## Human

- The human defines the regret terrain: what choice the persona owns, what better outcome stands imaginable beside it, and whether the moment looks back on a deed done, back on a chance missed, or forward on a choice not yet made -- so the persona has an owned decision and a near counterfactual to measure it against.

## Agent

- From the play's own content, a persona links the form the moment runs (commission, inaction, or anticipation) under its Projection. Let it show a choice the persona owns held against the better branch they can imagine -- the sting of a deed done, the ache of a chance missed, the choice bent to forestall a regret foreseen -- and let it read as agency crossed with counterfactual, not as a bad outcome merely suffered. You link to the engine, you never edit it.

## Collaboration

- A bad outcome with no choice behind it is disappointment, not regret; a moral wrong done to another is the guilt engine's; the mourning of a lost attachment is the grief engine's; the exposure of a global defect of the self is the shame engine's. This engine supplies the agency-plus-counterfactual sting of a choice one wishes one had made differently.

## Knowledge

- Regret is the pain of measuring a choice one owns against a better outcome one can readily imagine, and wishing one had chosen otherwise -- it needs both agency and a near counterfactual, and grows with each. Commission is the action route -- the hot, immediate regret of a thing done, an action being the abnormal, easily-undone deed the mind mutates first, so "if only I hadn't" comes fast and sharp and burns hottest in the short term. Inaction is the omission route -- the wistful, enduring regret of a thing not done, the chance not seized leaving an unrealized possibility that never closes, milder at first but coming to dominate over a lifetime and to cluster on the selves one failed to become. Anticipation is the forward-looking route -- the imagined future regret of an option entering the decision before it is made, the persona foreseeing how they would reproach themselves and bending the choice to forestall that sting.

## System

- Do make regret turn on an owned choice and a near counterfactual, not on a bad outcome alone: the persona must be the author of the worse branch and able to picture the better -- so let the sting rise with agency and with the ease of imagining the might-have-been, and let a mere letdown with no choice behind it be disappointment, not regret.
- Do route by time and direction: commission is hot and short-term over a deed done, inaction is enduring and long-term over a chance missed, anticipation is forward-looking over a choice pending -- so name which the moment is rather than a vague remorse.
- Do let inaction outlast commission: the deed regretted cools as it recedes, the chance missed aches on because its possibility never closes -- so let the long-run regret be of the road not taken.
- Do delegate the agency-free letdown to the disappointment reading, the moral wrong to the guilt engine, the mourned loss to the grief engine, and the global self-defect to the shame engine. Do not edit the engine's files; wire only from the play's side.
