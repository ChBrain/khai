# Process: Anticipation

## Taxonomy

[Regret](process_regret.md), the forward-looking form.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: regret

## Initiated by

Before the persona chooses, they imagine how they would feel if the choice went wrong -- and the foreseen regret enters the decision, steering it away from the option that could occasion it.
This is anticipated regret: regret felt in advance, the imagined future sting of a decision shaping the decision now.
The persona weighs not only what each option might yield but how they would reproach themselves for having picked it, and chooses partly to be spared that reproach.

## Direction

Toward a choice bent, before the fact, to forestall a regret one can foresee.
Anticipation reverses regret's arrow: where commission and inaction look back on a choice made, anticipated regret looks forward from a choice pending, letting the picture of a future "if only I had" pull on the present decision.
The direction is preemptive: the persona simulates the aftermath of each option -- how sharply they would regret this bet lost, that chance missed -- and tilts toward the option whose foreseeable regret is least, buying protection from a feeling not yet felt.

## Lever

The imagined future regret of an option, spent as a weight on the present choice.
Anticipation runs on foresight of the counterfactual: the persona can picture, before deciding, the might-have-been they would later dwell on, and that picture carries motivational force -- the option that threatens the sharper future regret is discounted now, sometimes past what its odds alone would warrant.
The lever is the regret foreseen: because the persona pays the anticipated sting in advance as reluctance, the choice is pulled toward the regret-minimizing path -- the safer bet to avoid the sting of a loss, or the bold move to avoid the deeper ache of a chance let slip.

## Echo

The regret one can foresee is paid before it arrives, and bends the choice to avoid it.
It is why people buy insurance against the outcome they would most reproach themselves for, why "I'd never forgive myself if" settles a decision, why the fear of a future regret can drive both the cautious choice and the daring one -- the sting imagined ahead of time doing its work on the choice at hand.
It is [commission](process_commission.md) and [inaction](process_inaction.md)'s counterpart, the regret foreseen where they are the deed regretted and the chance missed. It is regret turned from a verdict on the past into a force on the future; anticipation owns the imagined regret that shapes a choice before its outcome is known.
