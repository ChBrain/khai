# Process: Inaction

## Taxonomy

[Regret](process_regret.md), the omission form.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: regret

## Initiated by

The persona did not do something -- did not take the chance, speak the word, seize the opening -- and the road not taken comes to ache: they wish they had acted.
This is inaction regret: the regret of a thing not done, an omission measured against the fuller life the seized chance might have brought.
Quiet at first, it lingers and grows, the unrealized possibility keeping its hold.

## Direction

Toward a wistful, enduring regret over a chance missed that time does not dull but deepens.
Inaction regret runs opposite to commission across time: milder in the short term, because an omission is the normal, unremarkable non-event, it comes to dominate over the long run, so that the biggest regrets of a life are mostly of things not done.
The direction is slow and lasting: as the sharp regrets of deeds fade, the omissions endure, clustering on the selves one failed to become -- the education not pursued, the love not declared, the risk not taken -- because an unrealized possibility has no end, and stays open to imagine.

## Lever

The omission as an open, unrealized possibility, spent as a counterfactual that never closes.
Inaction regret runs on the endlessness of the road not taken: a deed done has a fixed result to reckon with and recede from, but a chance not seized leaves the better branch forever unbounded and forever imaginable, so the regret has nothing to complete it and no ceiling on the good it might have held.
The lever is the durability of the unfulfilled: because what one did not do is never finished, its regret is not spent by time but fed by it, the unlived possibility aching on where the done deed's regret would have cooled.

## Echo

The chance missed aches long after the deed regretted has cooled, the unlived branch left open.
It is why, asked what they most regret, people name the risks not taken and the words not said, why "I wish I had" outlasts "I wish I hadn't," why the omission's regret is the one that keeps -- the road not taken with no end to imagine down.
It is [commission](process_commission.md) and [anticipation](process_anticipation.md)'s counterpart, the enduring regret of the omission where they are the hot regret of the deed and the regret foreseen. It rises as commission's fades, overtaking it over a lifetime; inaction owns the wistful, lasting regret of a chance not seized.
