# Process: Commission

## Taxonomy

[Regret](process_regret.md), the action form.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: regret

## Initiated by

The persona did something -- took the bet, sent the message, made the move -- and it turned out badly, and the regret is sharp and immediate: they wish they had not acted.
This is commission regret: the sting of a thing done, an action taken that a better outcome, plainly imaginable, condemns.
The deed is theirs and its cost is fresh, and the hand that acted is the hand they now blame.

## Direction

Toward a hot, salient regret over a deed whose undoing is easy to imagine.
Commission regret is fierce in the short term because an action is the abnormal thing -- it departs from the do-nothing baseline, and the mind mutates the departure most readily, summoning with ease the world in which one simply had not acted.
The direction is acute and near: the regret spikes soon after the deed, vivid because the alternative -- inaction, the status quo left alone -- is the effortless counterfactual, and the persona sees at once the untouched world they traded away.

## Lever

The action as the easily-mutated abnormal, spent as a counterfactual close at hand.
Commission regret runs on the mutability of the deed: because acting is a change the persona made, the imagination undoes it with little effort -- "if only I hadn't" is a short step from what happened -- and the nearer the better branch, the hotter the regret.
The lever is the salience of the act: a done thing is concrete, authored, and pointed-to, so its regret is intense and immediate, the cost of the move weighed against the vivid ease of never having made it.

## Echo

The deed done stings now, and the world one could have left alone stands plainly by.
It is why the rash act regretted burns hottest in its first hours and days, why "I never should have" comes so fast and so sharp, why acting wrongly feels worse in the moment than failing to act -- the commission the mind can most easily imagine undone.
It is [inaction](process_inaction.md) and [anticipation](process_anticipation.md)'s counterpart, the hot short-term regret of the deed where they are the enduring regret of the omission and the regret foreseen. Its intensity fades as the deed recedes and the inaction regret rises to overtake it; commission owns the sharp, immediate sting of a thing done that one wishes undone.
