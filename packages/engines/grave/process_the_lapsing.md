# Process: The Lapsing

## Taxonomy

[Grave](piece_grave.md), the ending nobody decides.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: grave

## Initiated by

A visit is missed because of the weather.
Then one because of a Tuesday, then a year in which there were two, then a year in which there was the anniversary only.

## Direction

Toward an ending arrived at by a sequence of reasonable omissions.
The people move away, the ones who knew the name die, the surviving cousin has a hip. The renewal notice goes to the address on the file, which has been wrong for two decades. The stone tilts, is assessed as unsafe, and is laid flat by a groundsman who has never heard of anybody in this story.
Where the law allows it the plot is eventually reclaimed and reused, which most families never learn has happened.

## Lever

The reasonableness of each omission against what their sum is taken to mean.
The carrying force is that no single absence is a failure and defending them individually is easy: the visit was skipped for good reasons, the distance is real, the person would not have wanted anybody driving four hours in February, and a life cannot be organised around a plot; the resistance is that the object records only the sum, so a family whose reasons were all good is left with a green stone and an untended bed, and anybody who walks past reads the one thing the plot can say.
The Lapsing is a verdict assembled out of individually innocent weeks.

## Echo

A grave that has been let go by nobody in particular.
Nobody neglected it. Nobody decided to stop. The visits thinned, the years did the rest, and each of the people involved was behaving reasonably at the time.
The Lapsing is dated from the first missed visit and is noticed, if at all, by a stranger.
