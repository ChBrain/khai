# Process: The Visiting

## Taxonomy

[Grave](piece_grave.md), the relationship conducted as maintenance.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: grave

## Initiated by

Somebody goes, and takes something with them.
Water, secateurs, a cloth, replacement flowers, and increasingly a specific and unvarying route through the cemetery.

## Direction

Toward a bond carried on through small horticultural tasks.
The ethnographies of grave visiting keep finding the same thing: the visit is mostly work. The visitor weeds, scrubs the lettering, empties and refills the container, tidies the neighbouring plot if it has been let go, and talks aloud while doing it. Very little of the time is spent standing still.
The tasks are what makes the visit possible. A person who arrives with nothing to do stands at a stone for four minutes and leaves.

## Lever

The relief of having something to do for them against the fact that the task renews.
The carrying force is that this is real consolation and is not a substitute for anything: after a death there is nothing left that can be done for the person, and the plot is the one place where an ordinary act of care still reaches something of theirs -- visitors describe the tending as the part that helps, not the standing; the resistance is that flowers die on a schedule and grass grows back, so the one comfort available is the one that reissues itself, and the relationship stays open by means of a chore that cannot be completed.
The Visiting keeps the bond in the only material the bond has left.

## Echo

A duty with a calendar and no end date.
It shows the mercy and the trap arriving in the same act, which is why nobody in it experiences it as a trap.
The Visiting is dated from the burial and thins on a curve nobody chooses.
The internal bond that the visiting expresses is grief's; this reads the going and the doing.
