# Process: The Burying

## Taxonomy

[Grave](piece_grave.md), the day the location becomes permanent.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: grave

## Initiated by

The funeral finishes and something has to be done with the body.
The rite itself is ritual's; what this engine reads is the moment the choice of ground stops being reversible.

## Direction

Toward an address selected in a fortnight and binding for a century.
The decisions are taken by exhausted people in a week when they are least able to take any: which cemetery, which section, single or double depth, how many spaces, which term. Most of them are made on cost and on what the funeral director suggests.
None of them can be undone. Moving a body afterwards requires a licence, a fee, and a reason the authorities will accept, and almost nobody does it.

## Lever

The urgency of having somewhere to put them against the permanence of what gets decided.
The carrying force is that the decision genuinely cannot wait and the pressure is not anybody's fault: there is a body, there is a limited time, the family needs the thing to happen, and postponing it would be its own cruelty; the resistance is that the choice sets where a family will be required to travel for the next forty years, and it is made by people who cannot yet imagine having a next forty years, on a criterion -- proximity to the crematorium, availability that Thursday -- that will look arbitrary long before it stops binding.
The Burying fixes an address at the worst possible moment for choosing one.

## Echo

A location that cannot be revised.
It shows why so many families end up with a grave in a town none of them still live in, and why nobody involved was careless.
The Burying is dated to the day and is discovered to be permanent much later.
