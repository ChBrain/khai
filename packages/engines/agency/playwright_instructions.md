# Instructions: Agency

How a Playwright wires the agency engine into a play. You wire by linking the
play's own content to the engine's files; the engine files stay untouched, they
are canon. The Roadie plumbs the engine's law into Instructions Knowledge on
deploy. Authoring guidance, not runtime content, and it does not go on tour.

## Human

- The human sets which objects in the world carry a program of conduct and enforce it on whom.

## Agent

- From the play's own content, a piece that carries a social program links under Load Bearing the facet it enforces: the scenario inscribed in its form (script), the conduct it requires and forbids (prescription), the absent maker's will it carries (delegation), or the users it conscripts (enlistment). Play the program as borne and mostly invisible, felt when the object is met or missed, never announced. Keep it political -- what conduct it enforces on whom -- and hand how its form communicates use to usability. You link to the engine, you never edit it.

## Collaboration

- How the object's form communicates its use is usability's; a persona's own capacity to act is self-efficacy's; a setting's ambient program is space's; the social norm the object enforces, and the persona's compliance with it, are the norm and obedience engines'. Whether the program rings true for the object is the team's review.

## Knowledge

- Agency is the object as a social delegate: a designed thing that carries a program of action and enforces it on whoever meets it, in its maker's absence. Four facets over a delegate root -- the scenario inscribed in the form, the conduct it requires and forbids, the absent will it carries, the users it drafts into its network. The politics are borne, not shown: a good delegate reads as mere design, and its program is invisible until the object is gone and the conduct it held reverts. The speed bump is neither the traffic law nor the driver's obedience; it is the object that carries the law into the road and enforces it with no one there.

## System

- Do give a delegate piece its facet under Load Bearing; do play the program as borne and invisible, felt in the object's presence or its absence; do keep the reading political (what conduct it enforces on whom).
- Do not model how the form communicates use; that is usability. Do not confuse the object's social force with a persona's own agency (self-efficacy's) or a setting's ambient program (space's). Do not edit the engine's files; wire only from the play's side.
