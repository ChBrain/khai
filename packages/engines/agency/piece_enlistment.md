# Piece: Enlistment

## Taxonomy

[Agency](piece_agency.md), the facet this is a part of. Enlistment models the reach of the program: how the object conscripts whoever meets it into a network or program they did not choose -- the turnstile that makes everyone who enters a payer, the form that makes every applicant a case, the platform that makes every user a source of data. Callon's enrollment and translation (the sociology of translation), with Latour and Law. It is distinct from prescription (the demand on the one user before it): enlistment is the drafting of all who meet the object into its politics -- not what it forbids you, but what it makes you a part of by your using it at all.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: agency

## Place

At the object's threshold, wherever it stands between a person and what they came for -- the gate before the platform, the form before the service, the sign-up before the tool. Enlistment sits at the point of entry, where meeting the object at all is already to be enrolled.

## Load Bearing

What enlistment holds up is the network the object builds out of its users. Remove it and the conscription lapses: the users disperse back into people who merely passed by, no longer payers or cases or data-sources. The object bears the quiet conversion of everyone who meets it into a role in a system they never agreed to join -- and where it is gone, the system loses the members it was silently recruiting, and must find them some other way.

## Apparent

The enrollment is mostly unfelt; the user thinks they are simply doing the thing -- entering, applying, using -- and does not see that in doing it they have been made a node in someone's network. A good enlistment is frictionless and invisible, which is exactly its reach: to draft without the drafted noticing. What is apparent is a service used; what is borne is a person enrolled.

## Yearbook

The history of an enlistment is the record of everyone the object drew in -- the numbers passed through the gate, filed on the form, logged by the platform -- and the network that grew from them. Over time the sheer accumulation of the enrolled becomes the object's real weight: not what it did to any one user, but the system it built, one unnoticing recruit at a time.
