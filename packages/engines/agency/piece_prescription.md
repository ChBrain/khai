# Piece: Prescription

## Taxonomy

[Agency](piece_agency.md), the facet this is a part of. Prescription models the deontic force of the object -- what conduct it requires and what it forbids: "you must slow," "you cannot pass without the key," the door that will not stay open, the turnstile that will not turn without the fare. Latour's prescription and proscription, the must and the cannot held as one demand with two poles, with Winner (the artifact that embodies a politics) and Verbeek (moralizing technology, the enforcement). It is distinct from usability's constraint: a constraint governs by legible form so the user gets the operation right; a prescription enforces a policy on the user, whether or not they understand it and whether or not they consent.

## Place

At the point where the object meets the conduct it governs -- the road where the bump sits, the gate where the turnstile stands, the sill where the door refuses to hold. The demand is made exactly where the forbidden act would otherwise be taken.

## Load Bearing

What the prescription holds up is the compliance the object exacts. Remove it -- take away the bump, unlock the gate, prop the door -- and the required conduct is no longer required: the must and the cannot lapse, and the users do as they would have without it. The object bears the rule against the user's own inclination; where it is gone, the inclination returns unopposed, and the policy exists only on paper if it exists at all.

## Apparent

The demand is felt more than seen -- the jolt that says slow, the bar that says stop, the resistance that will not give. Some prescriptions announce themselves (the sign, the light), but the force is in the form's refusal, not the notice: the object that merely cannot be used wrongly enforces without a word. What is apparent is the wall met; what is borne is the policy that built the wall.

## Yearbook

The history of a prescription is the tally of the forbidden act prevented and the compliance compelled -- every driver slowed, every fare paid, every entry refused, and the record of who fought the demand and at what cost. Over time the object's must and cannot become the settled shape of conduct in its reach, and the rule it enforced is remembered as the way things simply are, its origin as a demand forgotten.
