# Agency: Reference

## Line of Work

Agency as **piece**: the object as social delegate -- how a designed thing
prescribes and forbids conduct as a political actor, enforcing a program on whom.
The domain does not model how the form communicates its use, which the usability
engine owns; nor a persona's own capacity to act, which self-efficacy and its
neighbours own (read on a position, not a piece); nor a setting's ambient
behavioral program, which the space engine owns. It models the politics of the
artifact read on the object itself: the program it carries and enforces in its
maker's absence.

The spine is the actor-network account of the artifact: things are the "missing
masses" of social order, carrying delegated programs of action and pressing them
on whoever meets them. The engine takes the object's social program four ways --
the scenario inscribed in the form (script), the demand it makes (prescription),
the absent will it carries (delegation), and the users it conscripts (enlistment)
-- so a piece can declare not only that it acts on conduct but by which part of
its politics it does so.

## Restrictions

What the engine refuses to model, and to whom it delegates.

- **The object's usability (the cognitive pole)**: The usability engine owns how
  the form communicates its use -- legible design, read on Apparent. This engine owns
  what the object enforces on conduct -- the social program, borne on Load Bearing. The
  childproof cap is the joint case: its difficulty _as the form communicates it_ is
  usability; its _keep-children-out purpose, and whom it targets_, is agency. The line
  is Verbeek split cleanly -- design mediation (_What Things Do_) to usability, moralizing
  technology (_Moralizing Technology_) to agency.
- **A persona's own agency (self-efficacy and neighbours)**: A persona's capacity or
  sense of acting -- capability belief (`self-efficacy`), causation (`locus-of-control`),
  the agentic life-theme (`narrative`), autonomy (`motivation`) -- is a position or
  persona reading. This engine is the _object's_ social force, not a person's capacity.
  Same word, different science, different type: agency reads a `piece`, personal agency
  reads a `position`.
- **A setting's behavioral program (space)**: The space engine owns the affordance of
  the _setting_ -- an ambient behavioral field acting on the co-present (the church that
  hushes). This engine owns the _object_ -- a portable, discrete delegate carrying a
  specific maker's program into every hand it meets. The bearer test tells them apart: a
  discrete or portable object is agency, an ambient setting is space. A bench bolted into
  a plaza sits on the seam and is adjudicated where `defensible-space` is built.
- **The object's history (biography, patina, sign-value)**: What the object has carried
  and accreted over time is the history family's. Agency is the present form's social
  enforcement, not its accrued past. (The `fetish` composite reads `sign-value` +
  `agency` + `biography` together -- the boundary is a seam, not a wall.)
- **The norm and the compliance (obedience, role, conformity)**: The object _enforcing_
  a norm is distinct from the norm itself and from the persona's yielding to it. Agency
  owns the object-as-enforcer; the norm and role engines own the social rule; the persona
  engines own the compliance. `delegation` is precisely the handoff -- from a social norm
  (persona-side) to a material artifact (piece-side) that carries it into the world.
- **The facet is not engine content**: The engine declares the four facets exist; which
  one a given piece enforces lives on the piece, linked under its Load Bearing chapter.
  The engine refuses to fix a piece's program for it.

## Encoding

Source to constraint, per file.

- **[agency](piece_agency.md)** (the root): the object as a social delegate, bearing a
  program of conduct enforced in the maker's absence. Anchored by Latour (the missing
  masses) and Gell (the object as social agent).
- **[script](piece_script.md)**: the scenario of roles, sequence, and competences
  inscribed in the object's form. Anchored by Akrich (the script and its de-scription).
- **[prescription](piece_prescription.md)**: the deontic force -- what the object
  requires and forbids, must and cannot. Anchored by Latour (prescription and proscription)
  and Verbeek (moralizing technology), with Winner.
- **[delegation](piece_delegation.md)**: the absent maker's will handed to the object to
  enforce in their stead. Anchored by Latour ("Where Are the Missing Masses?"), with Akrich.
- **[enlistment](piece_enlistment.md)**: the conscription of whoever meets the object into
  a program they did not choose. Anchored by Callon (enrollment and translation), with
  Latour and Law.
