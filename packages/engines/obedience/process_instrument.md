# Process: Instrument

## Taxonomy

[Obedience](process_obedience.md), the agentic form.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: obedience

## Initiated by

Under the authority's command, the persona stops feeling the author of their acts and comes to feel a tool carrying out another's will.
This is the agentic shift: the move from an autonomous state, in which one acts on one's own purposes and owns the result, into an agentic state, in which one sees oneself as the instrument of a superior.
The hand is still the persona's, but the deed feels the authority's.

## Direction

Toward a self that executes rather than decides, with responsibility passed upward to the one who commands.
The agentic shift redefines the persona's own role: they are no longer the source of the action but its channel, so the moral weight of the deed is felt to belong to the authority who directed it, not to the agent who performed it.
The direction is a divestment of authorship: the persona attends to how well they carry out the instruction rather than to whether the instruction is right, and the conscience that would judge an owned act falls quiet before an act experienced as merely relayed.

## Lever

The self recast as the authority's instrument, spent as the displacement of responsibility upward.
The agentic route runs on the felt transfer of authorship: because the persona construes themselves as executing another's will, the act's ownership -- and with it its guilt -- is charged to the commander, leaving the agent free to perform what their own conscience would forbid.
The lever is the relocation of responsibility: it is not that the persona ceases to know the act is harmful, but that they cease to feel it is theirs, and an act that is not one's own does not summon one's own restraints.

## Echo

The persona acts as a tool and feels the deed belongs to the hand that wields it.
It is the psychological heart of destructive obedience -- the reason "I was only following orders" describes a genuine state and not merely an excuse, the reason a subject can pull a lever they would never pull on their own account, the reason authorship, once ceded, takes the conscience with it.
It is [legitimacy](process_legitimacy.md) and [binding](process_binding.md)'s core, the agentic shift where they are the authority's right and the factors that hold the persona in. The self-exemption by which a wrongdoer silences their own conscience for a harm they still own is the moral-disengagement engine's; instrument owns the prior shift by which the persona ceases to feel the author of the act at all.
