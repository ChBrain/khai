# Process: Legitimacy

## Taxonomy

[Obedience](process_obedience.md), the authority form.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: obedience

## Initiated by

The persona takes the one giving the order to have the right to give it -- by role, by setting, by the marks of office -- and it is that perceived right that lends the command its force.
This is legitimacy: the authority's accepted standing to command in this context, which the persona obeys rather than the person themselves.
Strip the standing and the same words lose their power; grant it and they compel.

## Direction

Toward a command obeyed because its source is recognized as entitled to issue it.
Legitimacy works through the signs that mark a rightful authority: the institution behind them, the title and coat, the setting that frames the encounter, the appearance of a proper chain of command -- and the persona reads these as the credential that makes the order binding.
The direction is deference to the office: the persona obeys not because the commander is strong or persuasive but because they occupy a position the persona accepts as authorized, so the command's force rests on the role and falls when the role is doubted.

## Lever

The perceived right of the authority to command, spent as the bindingness of the order.
Legitimacy runs on recognition of entitlement: the persona grants the authority a standing -- earned by institution, role, and symbol -- and that granted standing is what converts a mere request into an order that must be obeyed, so the same instruction carries weight from a legitimate source and none from an illegitimate one.
The lever is the credential of office: because the persona obeys the position rather than the man, the authority need only be seen as entitled, not admired or feared, and obedience collapses the moment the entitlement is exposed as false.

## Echo

The order binds because its giver is seen as entitled to give it, and loosens the moment that entitlement is doubted.
It is why a coat and a clipboard can command what a stranger cannot, why obedience falls when the authority's credentials crack or a rival authority contradicts them, why the office and not the person is obeyed -- the legitimacy the license the command runs on.
It is [instrument](process_instrument.md) and [binding](process_binding.md)'s condition, the authority's right where they are the agentic shift and the factors that hold the persona in. The capacity to compel by controlling resources and rewards, whatever the legitimacy, is the power engine's; legitimacy owns the perceived right to command that gives an order its binding force.
