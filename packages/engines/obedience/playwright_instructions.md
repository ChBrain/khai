# Instructions: Obedience

How a Playwright wires the obedience engine into a play. You wire by linking the play's own content to the engine's files; the engine files stay untouched, they are canon. The Roadie plumbs the engine's law into Instructions Knowledge on deploy. Authoring guidance, not runtime content, and it does not go on tour.

## Human

- The human defines the obedience terrain: what authority commands the persona and by what marks it is taken as legitimate, what the command asks that the persona's conscience resists, and how near the harm and the authority stand -- so there is a legitimate order to override conscience and a gradient to tune it.

## Agent

- From the play's own content, a persona links the form the moment runs (instrument, legitimacy, or binding) under its Projection. Let it show the command of a legitimate authority overriding the persona's own restraints -- the agentic shift that displaces responsibility, the perceived right that licenses the order, the binding factors that hold them in -- and let it read as vertical obedience, not as the pull of peers nor raw coercion. You link to the engine, you never edit it.

## Collaboration

- The matching of a horizontal peer majority is the conformity engine's; the capacity to compel by control of resources and rewards is the power engine's; the moral self-exemption by which a wrongdoer silences conscience for a harm they still own is the moral-disengagement engine's; the sunk-cost persistence in a failing venture is the escalation engine's. This engine supplies the override of conscience by a legitimate authority's command.

## Knowledge

- Obedience to authority is compliance with the command of a superior taken as legitimate, carried out even against the persona's own conscience -- the force vertical, not the pull of equals. Instrument is the agentic shift: the persona ceases to feel the author of their acts and comes to feel a tool carrying out the authority's will, so responsibility passes upward and the deed feels not theirs -- "I was only following orders" as a real state, not merely an excuse. Legitimacy is the authority's perceived right to command, backed by role, setting, and the marks of office: the persona obeys the position and not the person, so the order binds while the entitlement holds and loosens the moment it is doubted or a rival authority contradicts it. Binding is what holds the persona in once they have begun: the sequential creep of small steps, the awkwardness of breaking off, the weight of prior compliance -- and around these the proximity gradient, obedience falling as the victim is brought closer and the authority made distant.

## System

- Do make the pressure vertical and the authority legitimate: the persona obeys a superior's command because its source is accepted as entitled -- so let the order override conscience without threat or persuasion, and let obedience fall when the legitimacy cracks.
- Do route the override through the agentic shift: let the persona cease to feel the author of the act, responsibility relocated to the one who commanded -- so they can do on order what they would refuse on their own account, protesting yet continuing.
- Do let binding and proximity tune it: the moment to refuse never quite arrives because each step is small and the break is costly, and let the strain rise as the victim nears and the authority withdraws -- so obedience is held by inertia and distance, not zeal.
- Do delegate peer-majority matching to the conformity engine, coercive leverage to the power engine, and the moral self-exemption to the moral-disengagement engine. Do not edit the engine's files; wire only from the play's side.
