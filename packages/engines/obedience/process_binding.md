# Process: Binding

## Taxonomy

[Obedience](process_obedience.md), the maintenance form.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: obedience

## Initiated by

Having begun to obey, the persona finds it strangely hard to stop -- each further step small, defiance awkward, the prior compliance already committing them.
This is binding: the set of factors that hold the persona in the obedient course once it has started, making the break harder than the next increment.
It is not why they began but why they cannot easily quit.

## Direction

Toward continued obedience held in place by the friction of stopping and the graded conditions around the act.
Binding works through several holds: the sequential creep of demands raised in small steps, so no single one seems the moment to refuse; the awkwardness of breaking off, which would affront the authority and disrupt the situation; and the self-justifying weight of having already complied. Around these, the situation grades the obedience -- it falls as the victim is brought closer and the persona must face the harm, and as the authority is made distant and its presence thins.
The direction is a course made sticky: each step lowers the threshold for the next while raising the cost of the first refusal, and the proximity of victim and authority tunes how much of the strain the persona feels.

## Lever

The friction of breaking off crossed with the proximity gradient, spent as the persistence of obedience.
Binding runs on the asymmetry between the next step and the break: the increment is trivial and the rupture is not, so the persona keeps going less from zeal than from the difficulty of stopping -- and the nearer the victim, the harder the strain to override, while the nearer the authority, the stronger the pull to comply.
The lever is the cost of the first "no": because defiance means disrupting the frame, confronting the authority, and repudiating one's own prior steps, the persona is held by inertia and awkwardness as much as by the command, and the situational distances set how heavy that hold is.

## Echo

The persona is kept obeying by how hard it is to stop, and by how near or far the harm and the authority stand.
It is why the moment to refuse never quite arrives, why obedience drops sharply when the victim is brought into the room and the authority sent out of it, why breaking off feels like a rupture and continuing like the path of least resistance -- the binding factors and the proximity gradient holding the course.
It is [instrument](process_instrument.md) and [legitimacy](process_legitimacy.md)'s support, the maintenance where they are the agentic shift and the authority's right. The sequential creep by which a small first commitment eases a larger later one is elaborated by the escalation engine; binding owns the factors and the proximity gradient that hold a persona in an obedient course once begun.
