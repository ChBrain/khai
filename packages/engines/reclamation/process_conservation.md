# Process: Conservation

## Taxonomy

[Reclamation](process_reclamation.md), the stance this intervention takes.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: reclamation

## Initiated by

The intervention decides the age is the thing to keep, and moves to arrest the decay without erasing it.
Conservation starts from Ruskin's refusal: we have no right to restore, for the marks of the years are not ours to wipe -- so the hand shores, stabilizes, shelters, and does the least it can.
It reaches in only to stop the fall, and leaves the fallenness legible.

## Direction

Onto the Shown, holding the fabric as found.
The roof is made tight over the ruin, the wall is pinned before it sheds, the surface is left with its patina and its scars; new work, where it must be done, is kept honest and distinct, never disguised as old.
Conservation moves the place from falling to held, and no further -- the age preserved exactly at the state the intervention found it.

## Lever

The trust in the age against the impulse to make the place whole again.
The carrying force is the belief that the years are the value -- that a place's age-worth is destroyed the moment it is renewed; the resistance is the pull to complete, to tidy, to return the place to a prime it has left.
Hold the minimum and the age stands; reach further and it is reconstruction or facsimile, not conservation.

## Echo

The place stopped falling, its age intact.
It shows the stabilized ruin, the honest patch, the fabric kept exactly as the years made it, and carries beneath it the wager the stance makes -- that the truest respect for a place is to let its age remain visible, arrested but not erased.
Conservation is dated from the day the falling stopped, and it is the stance that keeps faith with decay's patina: the marks of time preserved as worth, not wiped for a newness.
