# Process: Facsimile

## Taxonomy

[Reclamation](process_reclamation.md), the stance this intervention takes.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: reclamation

## Initiated by

The intervention reaches in to save the place and produces, instead, a lie that reads as old.
Facsimile starts where reclamation loses faith in the actual past and manufactures a better one: the age scrubbed off and a fake antiquity applied, the fabric replaced wholesale and called original, the history curated into a heritage that flatters the present.
It is the stance Ruskin dreaded -- restoration as "a destruction, a Lie from beginning to end."

## Direction

Onto the Shown, staging a past the place never had.
The genuine ruin is smoothed into a picturesque one, the mongrel accretions replaced by a coherent invented "period," the working district turned to a themed quarter, the reproduction set where the real once stood and labelled as the thing itself.
Facsimile moves the place from a real past to a consumable one -- Lowenthal's heritage in place of history, the past staged for the visitor rather than kept for its truth.

## Lever

The comfort of the manufactured past against the truth of the real one.
The carrying force is the appetite for a legible, flattering, purchasable history -- older-looking than the old, cleaner than the true; the resistance is the fact the facsimile erases, the actual years it paves over, the authenticity it can only imitate.
The deeper the fake, the more it satisfies and the less of the real place remains -- and at the limit the place becomes a reproduction of itself, indistinguishable to the visitor and empty of the thing it copies.

## Echo

The place saved into a counterfeit of itself.
It shows the too-perfect old town, the heritage plaque on the rebuilt facade, the antiqued surface that never weathered, and carries beneath it the loss the rescue performed -- a past manufactured over the one it destroyed, so convincing it is mistaken for real.
Facsimile is dated from the fabrication, and it closes the family's ambivalence: where decay's patina was loss that read as value and gentrification was revival that carried loss, facsimile is the rescue that falsifies -- even the saving hand able to produce a lie.
