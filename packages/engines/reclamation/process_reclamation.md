# Process: Reclamation

## Taxonomy

Intervention.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: reclamation

## Initiated by

Someone decides the fallen place is worth taking back, and moves to arrest its decay.
Reclamation starts where dereliction and decay leave off: a hand reaches into the decline -- to shore, to repair, to rebuild, to reuse -- and the place stops falling.
But the moment it reaches in, it faces the question the whole field turns on: what to do with the age the place has gathered, the very marks the years put on it.

## Direction

Onto the place's Shown, re-presenting what the fabric shows of its past.
Four stances carry the intervention:
[Conservation](process_conservation.md) keeps the fabric and its age as found, [Reconstruction](process_reconstruction.md) rebuilds it whole to an ideal, [Reuse](process_reuse.md) keeps it by giving it a living new function, and [Facsimile](process_facsimile.md) fakes a past it never had.
Where decay wrote decline on the place's Shown, reclamation overwrites it -- but each stance shows a different answer to the same inherited age.

## Lever

The place's gathered age against what the intervention decides that age is worth.
The carrying force is the will to take the place back; the resistance is the age itself -- the patina, the accretions, the marks of every year -- which any intervention must keep, complete, use, or fake, and cannot leave untouched once it reaches in.
This is the debate the field was built on: to conserve is to trust the age, to reconstruct is to override it for the design, and between them lies every choice about whose past a place is allowed to show.

## Echo

The place taken back, and an age either kept, completed, used, or counterfeited.
Reclamation closes the neighborhood-cycle it inherits: it arrests the decay that ran in the fabric, reverses or accepts the dereliction that let it, and stands apart from gentrification by what it serves -- the place kept for its age, not withheld for its yield, though the reuse stance rides close to that line.
The place is dated from the intervention and from the stance it took toward its own past: the honest arrest, the confident rebuild, the living reuse, or the lie that reads as old and is not.
