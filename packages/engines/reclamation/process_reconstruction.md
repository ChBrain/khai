# Process: Reconstruction

## Taxonomy

[Reclamation](process_reclamation.md), the stance this intervention takes.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: reclamation

## Initiated by

The intervention decides the design is the thing to keep, and moves to make the place whole again.
Reconstruction starts from Viollet-le-Duc's ambition: to restore is to reinstate the place in a condition of completeness that may never have existed -- to realize the idea the fabric was reaching for, over the accidents of its decay.
It reaches in to rebuild, not merely to hold.

## Direction

Onto the Shown, returning the place to an ideal whole.
The missing spire is raised, the lost wing rebuilt, the worn detail recut to the pattern, the additions of centuries stripped back to the chosen moment; the place is made legible and complete, its design finished as it perhaps never was.
Reconstruction moves the place from fragment to whole -- and in doing so must choose one past to complete, erasing the others.

## Lever

The clarity of the restored design against the age it overrides to reach it.
The carrying force is the will to make the place whole and legible again; the resistance is everything the wholeness costs -- the patina wiped, the accretions removed, the honest fragment replaced by a confident guess at the original.
Complete the design and the place reads whole; but the more completely it is restored, the less of the actual years remain in it, and the choice of which moment to restore to is a choice against every other.

## Echo

The place made whole, and one of its pasts chosen over the rest.
It shows the finished tower, the recut stone, the building returned to a prime -- and carries beneath it the loss the wholeness hides: the centuries edited to a single legible moment, the real age traded for the clear design.
Reconstruction is dated from the rebuilding, and it is the stance that answers decay's patina by wiping it -- the newness restored, the age overridden for the idea.
