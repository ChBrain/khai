# Process: Affiliation

## Taxonomy

[Admiration](process_admiration.md), the drawing-near form.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: admiration

## Initiated by

Admiration prompts the wish to be near the admired -- to connect, to join, to stand in the light of the excellence and be known by it.
This is affiliation: admiration's approach tendency (Onu, Kessler & Smith), the pull toward the admired person, not only their skill.
It is the emotion reaching past the excellence to the one who bears it.

## Direction

Toward the admired, to close the social distance -- to befriend, follow, apprentice to, or simply be around the one whose excellence draws.
Admiration is centripetal: it gathers people around the excellent, and affiliation is the gathering, the persona moving into the orbit of the one it admires.
The direction is toward proximity and belonging with the admired.

## Lever

The desire for connection with the source of excellence, and the standing that nearness to it confers.
What affiliation spends is distance and reserve: the persona moves toward the admired, sometimes past what the tie warrants, for proximity to excellence is itself rewarding and lends a borrowed shine.
The greater the excellence, the stronger the pull to be counted among those near it.

## Echo

The admired gathers a following, and excellence becomes a social center -- affiliation is why the skilled attract students, fans, and allies, and why standing near the admired confers status.
Its excess is the loss of self in the following, the affiliation that tips into [adoration](process_adoration.md) when nearness becomes devotion.
The durable bond that may form from the approach is the attachment engine's; affiliation is the admiration-driven drawing-near that may begin it.
