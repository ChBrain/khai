# Instructions: Admiration

How a Playwright wires the admiration engine into a play. You wire by linking the play's own content to the engine's files; the engine files stay untouched, they are canon. The Roadie plumbs the engine's law into Instructions Knowledge on deploy. Authoring guidance, not runtime content, and it does not go on tour.

## Human

- The human defines the admiration terrain: whose excellence the persona witnesses, what kind it is -- a skill, a talent, an achievement -- and whether it reads as reachable or beyond reach, so there is a real excellence to admire, a model to rise toward or revere, and a distance to cross or keep.

## Agent

- From the play's own content, a persona links the admiration the moment runs under its Projection: which form (inspiration, affiliation, or adoration), what excellence is appraised, and how admiring the persona's standing disposition is. Let it read as a warm upward regard that lifts and draws toward the excellent -- not as mere approval, and not as the envy the same superiority provokes in the resentful. You link to the engine, you never edit it.

## Collaboration

- The witnessing of moral excellence is the elevation engine's; the warm response to a benefit received is the gratitude engine's; the accommodation of a vastness beyond the frame is the awe engine's; the painful, contrastive response to another's superiority is the envy engine's; the rank excellence earns is status's, and the bond affiliation may grow into is attachment's. This engine supplies the warm upward response to another's excellence -- the regard that lifts and the wish to rise toward it.

## Knowledge

- Admiration is the warm upward response to witnessed excellence: a persona meets another's skill, talent, or achievement -- a non-moral excellence -- and warms toward them, moved to rise rather than diminished, saying not you are beneath me (contempt) or that should be mine (envy) but I would become like that. It is one of the other-praising emotions (with gratitude and elevation), and the benign face of upward comparison -- assimilative where envy is contrastive. It runs in forms. Inspiration is the emulative form -- admiration firing striving toward a reachable model, the engine of social learning. Affiliation is the drawing-near form -- the pull to connect with and stand in the light of the admired. Adoration is the reverent form -- admiration of an excellence felt beyond reach, tipping into worship and devotion, the idol and the hero, fragile when it falls. Admiring is the trait -- a standing readiness to meet excellence with admiration rather than envy.

## System

- Do make admiration lift and draw upward, not merely approve: the persona is moved by the excellence toward the excellent and toward its own climb, warmed rather than flattened -- so let it read as regard that rises.
- Do keep it distinct from envy: the same superiority draws admiration in one persona and envy in another; let the admiring meet it with the wish to rise, not the wish to pull down or take.
- Do let the form follow attainability: a reachable excellence fires inspiration and striving, an unreachable one tips into adoration and reverence, and affiliation draws the persona near either way -- and let adoration be fragile, the idol that shatters rather than descends when it falls.
- Do let admiringness set the threshold: the admiring persona finds models and is inspired, and can over-defer or over-credit; the envious meets the same excellence with resentment. Do delegate moral excellence to the elevation engine and benefit received to the gratitude engine. Do not edit the engine's files; wire only from the play's side.
