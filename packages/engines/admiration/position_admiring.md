# Position: Admiring

## Taxonomy

Affect / the trait of admiringness -- the standing readiness beneath [Admiration](process_admiration.md), the disposition to meet excellence with admiration rather than envy.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: admiration

## Has

A ready openness to another's excellence -- a disposition to find models, to be moved and lifted by skill and achievement, and to grant superiority freely rather than resent it.
Trait admiringness, as against the state: the persona that readily admires, holds heroes, and is inspired, where another meets the same excellence with envy or a shrug.
It is the assimilative pole of upward comparison, the standing readiness to be bettered by others.

## Orders

Run from here, ahead of the excellence.
The admiring persona scans for the admirable, learns readily by emulation, gathers around the skilled, and reads another's superiority as a path rather than a threat; and the tilt shapes a life of apprenticeship and role-models, a disposition that supports learning and social cohesion (Onu).
It colours the read before the event, on the axis of others' excellence -- where the envious meet the same superiority with pain, the admiring meet it with regard.

## Loses

The guardedness of the self before another's superiority.
The admiring persona, meeting a world full of betters, can lose itself in the following, over-defer to the admired, and mistake a fallen idol's fall for its own -- what is lost is the reserve that keeps admiration a lift rather than a surrender.
The plainer cost is the ease of over-crediting: the readiness to admire can outrun the excellence that earns it.

## Drives

On the loss of that reserve:
rides the admiringness -- finds models, is inspired, defers to the great -- or works against it, keeping the self a potential equal, admiring without worshipping, learning without following, at a cost in the easy warmth.
The set-point holds underneath either way, a resting level the persona manages rather than moves.
