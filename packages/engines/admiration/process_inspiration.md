# Process: Inspiration

## Taxonomy

[Admiration](process_admiration.md), the emulative form.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: admiration

## Initiated by

The admired excellence reads as a model the persona could rise toward, and admiration fires striving -- the pull to work, practice, and become more like what was witnessed.
This is inspiration: admiration's self-improvement tendency (Thrash & Elliot; Onu), the emotion turned into motivation, the excellence taken as a summons rather than a rebuke.
It is the moment regard for another becomes fuel for one's own climb.

## Direction

Toward the skill, and toward the self improved.
Inspiration points the admiration at one's own effort -- it does not rest in regard for the other but converts it into a reaching of one's own, the model ahead and the persona moving to close on it.
The direction is the persona's own ascent, lit by another's height.

## Lever

The attainability of the excellence -- inspiration fires where the model is felt to be reachable, so the gap reads as a path rather than a wall.
What it spends is complacency: the settled sense that one's own level is enough, dissolved by the sight of what is possible.
The more the persona believes it could get there, the harder the striving; where belief fails, the striving does too.

## Echo

The persona strives, and skill propagates -- inspiration is the engine of apprenticeship and a reason cultures accumulate competence: we admire the skilled and copy them.
Where the excellence is felt unreachable, inspiration gives way to [adoration](process_adoration.md), the striving replaced by reverence for a height one will not climb.
The goal-striving itself, once lit, is the goal and grit engines' to sustain; inspiration is the lighting of it by another's excellence.
