# Process: Adoration

## Taxonomy

[Admiration](process_admiration.md), the reverent form.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: admiration

## Initiated by

The excellence is felt to be beyond reach -- so far above the persona that emulation seems impossible -- and admiration tips into reverence: the idol, the hero, the star, adored rather than emulated.
This is adoration (Schindler et al.): admiration of an unattainable excellence, which draws worship and devotion where inspiration draws striving.
It is admiration that has stopped believing it could climb, and begun to kneel.

## Direction

Upward only -- toward the admired placed above, and the self kept below in willing deference.
Adoration does not close the gap; it consecrates it, holding the admired on a height the persona does not expect to reach.
The direction is veneration: the eyes up, the distance kept and honored rather than crossed.

## Lever

The perceived unattainability of the excellence -- adoration fires where the model reads as a wall, not a path, so the response is reverence rather than emulation.
What it spends is the sense of oneself as a potential equal: the adored is set apart, and the persona takes its place as follower, fan, or devotee.
The further above the excellence is felt, the deeper the deference and the more complete the devotion.

## Echo

The admired is exalted, and a following becomes a cult of the figure -- adoration is the engine of hero-worship, celebrity devotion, and the veneration of the great.
It is the most fragile admiration: the idol who falls does not descend to peer but shatters, the adoration curdling to disillusion or betrayal, for a height that was consecrated cannot be quietly lowered.
The reverence toward the sacred proper is the awe engine's; adoration is the reverence toward an excellent person felt above one.
