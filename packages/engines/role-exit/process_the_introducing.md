# Process: The Introducing

## Taxonomy

[The Ex-Role](process_ex_role.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: role-exit

## Initiated by

The persona has to say who they are to somebody new.
And the sentence that works contains the role they left.

## Direction

Toward a self-description built around an absence.
There is no other efficient answer. I used to be is shorter than any account that avoids it, it explains the persona's competence, their contacts, and their age in one clause, and the listener immediately knows what to do with it.
So the persona reissues the role at every introduction, and each reissue is voluntary, useful, and slightly against their own interest.

## Lever

The convenience of the shorthand against what the shorthand fixes.
The carrying force is that it is efficient and honest: the persona did hold the role, it is the most informative thing about their history, and avoiding it would be both awkward and a small lie; the resistance is that a self-description is a claim, and this one keeps the vacated role at the front of it, so the persona spends years supplying the evidence for a placing they would rather not have.
The Introducing is performed by the persona and works against them.

## Echo

A former role, kept current by its holder.
It shows the twist running through the persona's own mouth.
The Introducing is dated at every meeting.
