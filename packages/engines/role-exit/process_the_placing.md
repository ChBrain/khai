# Process: The Placing

## Taxonomy

[The Ex-Role](process_ex_role.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: role-exit

## Initiated by

Other people file the persona under what they used to be.
Not to diminish them -- because it is the most useful thing anybody knows about them.

## Direction

Toward a classification that is accurate, kind, and permanent.
An ex is legible in a way a change of job is not. Everybody has a model of what an ex-priest, an ex-soldier, an ex-dancer is like, the models are largely sympathetic, and they are applied instantly and without hostility.
There is no mechanism for revoking one. Roles have exits; ex-roles do not, because nobody administers them.

## Lever

The kindness of the classification against its permanence.
The carrying force is that being placed is mostly a benefit and is mostly meant well: it opens doors, it explains the persona to strangers faster than they could themselves, and the people doing it are being interested rather than reductive; the resistance is that a classification nobody issued cannot be appealed, so the persona's only instrument is to stop mentioning it, which fails, because somebody else will mention it for them.
The Placing is done by people who like the persona and cannot be asked to stop.

## Echo

A permanent entry made out of a former life.
It shows why the ex-role is a status rather than a gap.
The Placing is dated from the first introduction and is maintained by everybody else.
