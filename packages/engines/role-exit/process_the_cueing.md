# Process: The Cueing

## Taxonomy

[The Doubts](process_doubts_exit.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: role-exit

## Initiated by

The persona floats it without asking anything.
A joke about quitting, a hypothetical raised at dinner, an idle question about what somebody else did, a remark left hanging to see who picks it up.

## Direction

Toward information gathered without a commitment being made.
Cueing is how a persona finds out what leaving would cost before making themselves liable for wanting to. It requires deniability: if the response is alarm, the remark was a joke, and if the response is interest, the conversation can go further.
The replies are the data. A partner who laughs, a colleague who says quietly that they have thought about it too, a superior who does not hear it at all -- each moves the process without the persona having declared a thing.

## Lever

The safety of the deniable against what deniability costs.
The carrying force is that this is prudent rather than devious: nobody should announce a change of this size before knowing what it would mean, the ambiguity protects the people who would be affected as much as it protects the persona, and somebody who declared every passing thought would be impossible to live with; the resistance is that a question nobody can answer straight gets answered inaccurately, so the persona calibrates a decision of this size on responses to something they never actually said.
The Cueing gathers evidence about a question that was not put.

## Echo

A set of reactions to something deniable.
It shows the exit already running through the persona's relationships before it exists in their account of themselves.
The Cueing is dated from the first joke.
