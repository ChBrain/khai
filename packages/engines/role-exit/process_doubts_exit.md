# Process: The Doubts

## Taxonomy

[Role Exit](process_role_exit.md), where the role stops fitting.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: role-exit

## Initiated by

Something is wrong and it is not yet about leaving.
The Doubts start well below the level of a decision: [a first doubt](process_the_first_doubt.md) that is not a thought about quitting, [cueing](process_the_cueing.md) that tests the water without asking anything, and [a reinterpreting](process_the_reinterpreting.md) of everything that came before.

## Direction

Toward a persona who has been leaving for some time without knowing it.
The doubts do not arrive as an argument. They arrive as fatigue, as a stray thought after a bad week, as a growing difficulty in saying the things the role requires saying, and each of them is individually dismissible.
By the time the persona notices, the process is usually years old, which is why people describe the decision as sudden and the record does not support them.

## Lever

The reasonableness of each doubt against what the doubts are doing together.
The carrying force is that doubting is healthy and does not mean anything: everybody in every role has bad weeks, most doubts go nowhere, and a persona who treated each one as a verdict would leave everything they ever joined; the resistance is that doubts of this kind accumulate rather than resolve, and the accumulation is not available to the persona, who can inspect any one of them and none of the set.
The Doubts do their work by being individually unalarming.

## Echo

A role that has stopped fitting and has not been reported.
It is the movement that takes longest and is remembered as the shortest.
The Doubts are dated years earlier than the persona will say.
