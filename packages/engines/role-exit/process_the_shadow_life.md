# Process: The Shadow Life

## Taxonomy

[The Alternatives](process_alternatives_exit.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: role-exit

## Initiated by

The other life is specified.
Not a fantasy about being somebody else -- a version with a town in it, an income, a schedule, and a first month.

## Direction

Toward an alternative detailed enough to be compared and not enough to be tested.
The shadow life is built out of the parts of the current one that hurt, so it is precisely the negative of the role: no meetings if meetings are the problem, no travel if travel is, no standing if standing is what exhausted them.
It is therefore always better, and it will stay better until it is entered.

## Lever

The comfort of the rehearsal against its immunity to disappointment.
The carrying force is that this is imagination doing its job, it is how anybody works out what they want, and a persona who has never pictured another life has less information about themselves rather than more; the resistance is that an unentered life accrues no faults, so the comparison being run is not between two lives but between a life and a description of one.
The Shadow Life wins every comparison it is in.

## Echo

A second life kept in good condition.
It shows why the alternatives movement holds so many people for so long.
The Shadow Life is dated from the first version and improves with age.
