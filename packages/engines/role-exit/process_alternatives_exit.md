# Process: The Alternatives

## Taxonomy

[Role Exit](process_role_exit.md), the other life carried alongside this one.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: role-exit

## Initiated by

The persona begins living a second version in parallel.
The Alternatives start where doubt becomes specification: [a shadow life](process_the_shadow_life.md) rehearsed in detail, [side bets](process_the_side_bets.md) holding the persona in place for reasons unrelated to the role, and [a weighing](process_the_weighing.md) that cannot be done accurately.

## Direction

Toward a persona holding two lives at once and committed to neither.
The alternative is not a plan. It is a rehearsed possibility, revisited often, refined, and mostly not acted on, and its function is to make the current role survivable rather than to end it.
Most alternatives are never taken. The ones that are usually turn out to have been rehearsed for years.

## Lever

The relief of having somewhere to go against what having it does.
The carrying force is that an imagined alternative is one of the few genuinely free goods available to somebody in a hard role: it costs nothing, harms nobody, and makes the present bearable, and telling a persona not to picture leaving is telling them to stop coping; the resistance is that a rehearsed alternative runs a continuous comparison against the actual role, in which the actual role has costs and the alternative has none, because it has never been tried.
The Alternatives make staying possible and make the role look worse every year.

## Echo

Two lives, one of them untested.
It is the movement most people never leave.
The Alternatives are dated from the first serious version and are revised for as long as they are not taken.
