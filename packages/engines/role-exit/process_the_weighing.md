# Process: The Weighing

## Taxonomy

[The Alternatives](process_alternatives_exit.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: role-exit

## Initiated by

The persona tries to work out whether to go.
Lists are made, pros are set against cons, people are consulted.

## Direction

Toward a calculation the persona is not equipped to run.
What is being compared is a life known in every detail against one that exists only as a description, and a self who holds the role against a self who does not and cannot be consulted.
So the weighing is not a decision procedure. It is the form the deliberation takes while the decision is made elsewhere, by accumulation.

## Lever

The seriousness of the effort against what the effort can reach.
The carrying force is that the weighing is not wasted and should not be skipped: it surfaces the stakes, it forces the persona to say what they actually want, and the people consulted often supply the one fact that was missing; the resistance is that the two sides of the scale are made of different material -- lived cost against imagined benefit -- so the exercise produces confidence rather than an answer, and the persona will credit it with the decision.
The Weighing organises the reasons and does not produce them.

## Echo

A decision that will be attributed to the wrong process.
It shows why people who leave cannot say why they left when they did.
The Weighing is dated late and is remembered as the cause.
