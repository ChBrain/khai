# Process: The Announcing

## Taxonomy

[The Turning Point](process_turning_point_exit.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: role-exit

## Initiated by

The persona says it out loud to somebody who counts.
The resignation, the papers, the conversation with the head of the house, the sentence that cannot be taken back.

## Direction

Toward a status changed by the saying.
Announcing is the one irreversible act in the sequence and it works immediately: from the moment it is heard the persona is somebody who is leaving, and everybody adjusts before anything has actually changed.
The role begins withdrawing at once -- fewer long-term matters, a slight distance, a reallocation nobody discusses -- and the persona reads the withdrawal as confirmation that they were right.

## Lever

The relief of having said it against what the saying starts.
The carrying force is that the announcement is honest, usually overdue, and owed to the people it affects, and the persona is generally treated well: institutions and families mostly manage departures decently, and the kindness is not a performance; the resistance is that the kindness is also a handling -- a departure is smoothed so that it does not become a scene -- and the smoothing removes the persona from the role's future while everybody is being nice about it.
The Announcing is the last act performed from inside.

## Echo

A persona present and no longer counted.
It shows the role closing over the space before the space is vacant.
The Announcing is dated to the sentence and is irreversible from it.
