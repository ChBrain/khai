# Process: The Vacuum

## Taxonomy

[The Turning Point](process_turning_point_exit.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: role-exit

## Initiated by

The role ends, and nothing takes its place.
Not the next job and not the new life -- a period, often months, in which the persona is not anything in particular.

## Direction

Toward free fall that nobody gave any warning about.
The exit was planned as a move from one thing to another, and what arrives is an interval with no role in it: the days have no shape, the automatic answer to what do you do is gone, and the persona is surprised by how much of their week was structure they had not chosen.
This is the part missing from every account given beforehand, including the persona's own.

## Lever

The expectation of a transition against the reality of an interval.
The carrying force is that the vacuum is temporary, survivable, and frequently productive: it is where people find out what they want when nothing is being asked of them, and those who come through it rarely wish they had skipped it; the resistance is that it arrives unannounced in a sequence everybody described as a transition, so the persona reads a normal stage as evidence of a mistake, and some go back for that reason alone.
The Vacuum is a stage and is experienced as a verdict.

## Echo

A persona between two things with only one of them behind them.
It shows why a rite that reincorporates is worth so much, and why modern exits so rarely have one.
The Vacuum is dated from the last day and has no announced end.
