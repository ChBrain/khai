# Role-exit

Role exit as process: the leaving of a role that supplied a large part of who somebody is, and what the leaving leaves -- not the between-state itself, which is liminality's, and not the rite that carries somebody across, which is ritual's and the passage composite's -- across four movements: the doubts (a first doubt too small to raise and therefore never answered, a cueing that gathers evidence about a question that was not put, and a reinterpreting that makes the exit retrospectively inevitable), the alternatives (a shadow life that wins every comparison because it has never been entered, side bets never wagered on the role and forfeited with it, and a weighing that organises the reasons without producing them), the turning point (a last straw accurate about the timing and misleading about everything else, an announcing that is the last act performed from inside, and a vacuum that is a stage and is experienced as a verdict), and the ex-role (a residual that does not switch off, an introducing performed by the persona against their own interest, and a placing done kindly by people who cannot be asked to stop) -- with the twist that a role can be quit and being its ex cannot, because there is nobody to resign to and no procedure for it.

This engine is defined by its [manifest](package.json), which the canon renders as the WIRES card. The manifest is the single source of truth; this README is generated, not edited by hand.
