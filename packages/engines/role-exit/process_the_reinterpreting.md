# Process: The Reinterpreting

## Taxonomy

[The Doubts](process_doubts_exit.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: role-exit

## Initiated by

The past starts reading differently.
The years in the role are gone over again, and what was a good stretch with difficult patches becomes a long accumulation of signs.

## Direction

Toward a history that makes leaving obvious.
This is not dishonesty and the persona is not building an alibi. It is the ordinary work of making a decision coherent, and the material genuinely supports the new reading, because the difficult patches were real.
What changes is which facts are load-bearing, and afterwards the persona cannot recover the version in which the same events were simply a job.

## Lever

The necessity of a coherent account against what coherence destroys.
The carrying force is that nobody can act without a story about why, the story here is true in every particular, and a persona who could not assemble one would be stuck indefinitely in a role they had already stopped believing in; the resistance is that the assembly runs one way -- once the past reads as a build-up to leaving, the evidence for staying is not outweighed but invisible -- and the persona will describe the decision as something they finally saw.
The Reinterpreting makes the exit retrospectively inevitable.

## Echo

A history pointing at the door.
It shows where the point of no return actually sits, which is well before anybody notices one.
The Reinterpreting is dated late and back-dates everything.
