# Process: The Turning Point

## Taxonomy

[Role Exit](process_role_exit.md), the event that makes leaving sayable.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: role-exit

## Initiated by

Something happens, and it is finally enough.
The Turning Point starts at the event that carries the accumulation: [a last straw](process_the_last_straw.md) usually too small to explain the exit, [an announcing](process_the_announcing.md) that changes the persona's status the moment it is heard, and [a vacuum](process_the_vacuum.md), because what follows the role is not the next thing.

## Direction

Toward a decision that becomes a fact in public.
The turning point does not cause the exit; it licenses it. Its work is to give the persona and everybody around them an account that fits in a sentence, in place of a decade that does not.
Once the account exists, the exit is no longer a possibility being weighed. It is a thing that is happening.

## Lever

The usefulness of a sayable reason against how little of the truth it holds.
The carrying force is that a sayable reason is necessary and largely benign: nobody can announce a decade of accumulated doubt, the short version protects colleagues who would otherwise be implicated, and everybody understands the convention; the resistance is that the short version becomes the record, so the persona is afterwards required to defend a reason that was not the reason, and anybody who disputes the last straw appears to have disproved the exit.
The Turning Point supplies a cause after the fact and is believed.

## Echo

An exit with a date and a story on it.
It is the movement everybody remembers and the one that did the least.
The Turning Point is dated to the day and is not where the decision was made.
