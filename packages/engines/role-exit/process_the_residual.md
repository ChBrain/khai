# Process: The Residual

## Taxonomy

[The Ex-Role](process_ex_role.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: role-exit

## Initiated by

The role left things behind in the persona.
Reflexes, vocabulary, a way of reading a room, the habit of checking something that no longer needs checking, an hour the body still wakes at.

## Direction

Toward equipment with nothing to operate on.
A role held for a long time is not a set of duties; it is a trained way of perceiving, and perception does not resign. The ex-officer still reads the exits, the ex-teacher still counts the room, the ex-nun still keeps the hours.
Most of it is useful. All of it is a reminder.

## Lever

The value of the training against the fact that it does not switch off.
The carrying force is that the residual is a genuine asset and is why exits are survivable at all: the persona takes years of competence out with them, it transfers further than they expect, and being told their old training is a burden would be both wrong and insulting; the resistance is that the equipment keeps running whether or not there is anything to apply it to, so the persona is reminded of the role by their own reflexes at moments that have nothing to do with it.
The Residual is the role's last piece of tenure.

## Echo

Skills that outlast their occasion.
It shows why nobody returns to who they were before, in the most literal way available.
The Residual is dated from the training and fades slowly or not at all.
