# Process: The Last Straw

## Taxonomy

[The Turning Point](process_turning_point_exit.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: role-exit

## Initiated by

A small event does what large ones did not.
A meeting that went badly, a request too many, a remark from somebody who did not matter.

## Direction

Toward an event carrying more weight than it could possibly deserve.
The last straw is selected by its timing rather than its size, and it is usually trivial, which is why exits so often look disproportionate from outside and feel exactly proportionate from inside.
Its size is a feature. A large event would have been arguable; a trivial one cannot be argued with, because nobody would defend it.

## Lever

The triviality of the event against its finality.
The carrying force is that the persona is not overreacting and knows the event is small -- they will say so themselves, and what they are reporting is a threshold crossed rather than a grievance lodged; the resistance is that a trivial cause invites a trivial remedy, so everybody around them offers to fix the meeting, and the persona has to refuse a reasonable offer without being able to explain why it is beside the point.
The Last Straw is accurate about the timing and misleading about everything else.

## Echo

A departure attributed to a Tuesday.
It shows the accumulation converting itself into an event.
The Last Straw is dated precisely and explains nothing.
