# Process: The Side Bets

## Taxonomy

[The Alternatives](process_alternatives_exit.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: role-exit

## Initiated by

Things accumulate that make leaving expensive and have nothing to do with the role.
A pension that vests, a house near the office, a reputation that means something here and nothing elsewhere, a partner's job, children in a school, a title people use.

## Direction

Toward a persona held by stakes they never placed deliberately.
Every year in a role lays down commitments that were incidental at the time and load-bearing later. Not one of them was chosen as a reason to stay, and together they are the reason.
This is why leaving gets harder in a way the persona experiences as getting more serious about the work.

## Lever

The ordinariness of each stake against the hold they form together.
The carrying force is that all of it is good: a pension is prudent, roots are valuable, a reputation is earned, and a persona with no accumulated stakes anywhere has not been living so much as passing through; the resistance is that stakes laid down for their own sake still function as a hold, and the persona cannot tell commitment to the role apart from the cost of leaving it, because from inside the two are identical.
The Side Bets were never wagered on the role and are forfeited with it.

## Echo

A position that is expensive to vacate.
It shows why exits cluster at the points where the stakes happen to reset.
The Side Bets are dated continuously and are counted only on the way out.
