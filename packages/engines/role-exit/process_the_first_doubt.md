# Process: The First Doubt

## Taxonomy

[The Doubts](process_doubts_exit.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: role-exit

## Initiated by

A small thing does not sit right.
Not a crisis and not a grievance -- an instruction that seemed odd, a colleague's remark, a morning that was harder than it should have been.

## Direction

Toward a crack that will not be repaired because nobody will name it.
A first doubt is too small to raise. Raising it would require making a case, and there is no case: there is a feeling about one incident, and the incident will not carry it.
So it goes unspoken and stays available, and the next one lands beside it rather than replacing it.

## Lever

The insignificance of the doubt against its durability.
The carrying force is that it genuinely is insignificant, the persona is right to let it go, and a role in which nothing ever felt slightly wrong would be a role nobody was actually doing; the resistance is that a doubt that cannot be raised also cannot be answered, so it is retained in full, where a stated grievance would have been dealt with and disposed of.
The First Doubt survives because it was too small to argue with.

## Echo

One unanswered thing.
It shows the mechanism starting far below the level anybody monitors.
The First Doubt is dated exactly and is recognised only in hindsight.
