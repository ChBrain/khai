# Process: Exemplar

## Taxonomy

[Framing](process_framing.md), the operation this is one form of.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: framing

## Initiated by

The persona presents an issue through a single vivid case, or through the abstract pattern -- one family at the shelter, or the poverty statistics; the named victim, or the crime rate.
This is exemplar, or episodic-versus-thematic, framing: the same issue told at the level of the particular instance, or at the level of the general condition.
The persona chooses which altitude the hearer sees it from.

## Direction

Toward the level at which the hearer assigns cause and cure.
The episodic frame -- the case -- pushes the hearer to attribute the problem to the individual in it; the thematic frame -- the pattern -- pushes attribution toward systems, conditions, and society.
Shown one struggling family, the hearer asks what that family did; shown the rate, the hearer asks what the conditions are -- the same issue, a different place to lay responsibility.

## Lever

Concretion versus abstraction.
The vivid particular is accessible and moving -- it grips, it is remembered -- but it strips the context that would place it in a pattern; the abstract pattern supplies the context but does not grip, and slides off.
Which is shown sets whether the hearer feels the issue as one person's story or sees it as a structural fact, and feeling and seeing lead to different verdicts.

## Echo

The locus of responsibility shifts with the level of the telling.
The case persuades by presence and the pattern by scope, and a persona can move a hearer's whole account of who is to blame without changing a single fact, only the altitude.
Where the responsibility then lands -- on the person or the conditions -- is the attribution engine's to model; which framing tipped it there is [framing](process_framing.md)'s. It is distinct from [valence](process_valence.md), [emphasis](process_emphasis.md), and [metaphor](process_metaphor.md) in working through the grain of the telling -- the particular or the general -- rather than sign, dimension, or likeness.
