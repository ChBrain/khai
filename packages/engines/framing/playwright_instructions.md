# Instructions: Framing

How a Playwright wires the framing engine into a play. You wire by linking the play's own content to the engine's files; the engine files stay untouched, they are canon. The Roadie plumbs the engine's law into Instructions Knowledge on deploy. Authoring guidance, not runtime content, and it does not go on tour.

## Human

- The human defines the framing terrain: what content is being presented, to whom, and what judgment is at stake -- so that the choice of frame has a target, and the shift the frame produces has somewhere to land.

## Agent

- From the play's own content, a persona links the framing operation the moment runs (valence, emphasis, metaphor, or exemplar) under its Projection. Let the operation shape how the same content is put -- the sign it is stated in, the dimension foregrounded, the metaphor it is likened to, the case or the pattern it is told at -- and let the reader feel the judgment tip with the framing, though the facts hold still. You link to the engine, you never edit it.

## Collaboration

- The resolving of the choice a frame tilts is the decision engine's; the standing tilt a persona brings before any framing is bias's. The change-of-mind campaign a frame may serve is persuasion's; where responsibility lands once an exemplar frame has shifted it is attribution's; the metaphor as a device of the language is the language engine's. This engine supplies the presentation-form itself and its effect on judgment -- sign, salience, likeness, altitude.

## Knowledge

- Framing is how the same content, put in a different frame, yields a different judgment though the facts are unchanged -- there being no frameless telling. Valence states the same outcome as a gain or a loss, flipping risk preference by sign alone. Emphasis foregrounds one of an issue's true dimensions, so the salient consideration drives the verdict. Metaphor construes the target through a source domain, importing its inferences. Exemplar presents a vivid case or the abstract pattern, shifting where cause and cure are assigned.

## System

- Do keep the facts still: the power of a frame is that it moves the judgment without changing the information, so let the reader see the same content land differently, not a different content.
- Do let valence be the sharpest -- logically equivalent wordings ("ninety percent survive" / "ten percent die") that still flip the choice -- and let it work through a reference point, losses looming larger than gains.
- Do let a metaphor import its whole logic (the beast wants cages, the virus wants a cure) and mostly go unnoticed as the cause of the view it produced.
- Do delegate the choosing that follows to the decision engine and the standing lean to bias. Do not edit the engine's files; wire only from the play's side.
