# Process: Metaphor

## Taxonomy

[Framing](process_framing.md), the operation this is one form of.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: framing

## Initiated by

The persona construes the target through a source domain -- crime as a beast preying on the city, or crime as a virus infecting it; an argument as war, a nation as a household, time as money.
This is metaphorical framing: the target is not described but likened, and the likeness is not decoration -- it is the lens the hearer is handed to think through.
The persona chooses the source, and in choosing it chooses a way of reasoning about the target.

## Direction

Toward the inferences the source domain carries.
A metaphor imports a whole structure: "beast" brings hunting, capture, and cages, and the hearer reaches for enforcement; "virus" brings sources, spread, and cure, and the hearer reaches for reform -- from the same crime figures, different remedies.
The hearer reasons about the target with the source's logic, following entailments they were never told to accept.

## Lever

Cross-domain mapping.
Metaphor is not ornament laid over literal thought but a mapping that structures the thought itself: the source's relations, roles, and entailments are carried onto the target, so whatever is true-by-inference in the source becomes available as a conclusion about the target.
Choose the source and the conclusion is pre-loaded; the mapping does the arguing before any argument is made.

## Echo

The reasoning is the source domain's, and mostly invisible as such.
Hearers moved by a metaphor rarely name it as the cause of their view -- they cite facts and figures, not the beast or the virus -- so it steers judgment without being noticed as a frame, which is much of its force.
The metaphor as a device of the language -- the moving of meaning across the gap between words -- is the language engine's; this engine owns the framing effect of the likeness, the inferences it imports into a judgment. It differs from [emphasis](process_emphasis.md) in that it does not select among the target's own dimensions but lends it a foreign one.
