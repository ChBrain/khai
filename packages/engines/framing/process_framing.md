# Process: Framing

## Taxonomy

Communication / cognitive psychology of judgment.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: framing

## Initiated by

A persona presents content -- a fact, a choice, an issue -- and cannot present it without a frame: which aspect to put first, in what terms, against what baseline, likened to what.
There is no frameless telling; every presentation selects some part of the whole and makes it salient, and leaves the rest in shadow.
And the frame, not only the facts, is what the hearer judges from -- so the persona who chooses how to put a thing is already shaping what will be concluded about it, whether they mean to or not.

## Direction

Toward a judgment shaped by the presentation, not only by the substance.
The same information, framed one way and then another, arrives as a different thing to weigh -- a gain or a loss, a safety question or a liberty one, a beast or a plague -- and the hearer's conclusion tracks the frame more than the underlying fact.
The move is not to falsify: the facts can be identical and complete under both frames, and the judgment still diverges.

## Lever

Selection and salience.
To frame is to select some aspects of a situation and make them more salient, so that a particular reading, weighing, or remedy is the one that comes to mind. What is foregrounded, what baseline it is measured against, what it is likened to, whether it is shown as a case or a pattern -- each steers the inference while the information stays the same.
Framing works on what is accessible and applicable when the hearer judges, not on the argument they would endorse; it is a lever on the premises, not the logic.

## Echo

The judgment is as the frame made it.
Framing bites even when the hearer, shown the framer's conclusion stated plainly, would reject it -- because it operates below the level of the claim, on what the mind reaches for first.
The process continues as [valence](process_valence.md), [emphasis](process_emphasis.md), [metaphor](process_metaphor.md), or [exemplar](process_exemplar.md) -- the operations by which the same content is shaped toward a different judgment. The resolving of the choice that a frame tilts is the decision engine's; the standing tilt a persona brings before any framing is the bias engine's.
