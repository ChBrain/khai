# Process: Valence

## Taxonomy

[Framing](process_framing.md), the operation this is one form of.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: framing

## Initiated by

The persona states the same outcome in positive or negative terms -- as what is saved or what is lost, as "ninety percent survive" or "ten percent die," as a discount kept or a surcharge paid.
This is valence, or equivalence, framing: the two descriptions are logically identical -- each entails the other -- and differ only in sign.
Nothing in the facts has moved; only the side of the ledger the persona has named.

## Direction

Toward a reversal of the hearer's risk preference by sign alone.
Cast as a gain, the same prospect makes people risk-averse -- they take the sure thing; cast as a loss, it makes them risk-seeking -- they gamble to avoid the loss. Same numbers, opposite choice.
The direction is set not by the magnitude but by which side of a reference point the wording places the outcome.

## Lever

The reference point and the sign, read through prospect theory.
Outcomes are not valued in absolutes but as gains or losses from a reference point, and losses loom larger than equivalent gains -- so a frame that puts the outcome on the loss side pulls harder than the same outcome framed as a foregone gain.
Move the reference point -- call the same price a "cash discount" or a "credit surcharge" -- and the identical number changes what the hearer will do.

## Echo

The choice is set by wording that carries no information.
Because the two framings are equivalent, a difference in response is a pure framing effect -- the cleanest demonstration that the frame moves judgment independently of fact, the finding [framing](process_framing.md) is built on.
It is the sharpest of the operations because it needs no suppression and no likeness -- only a sign -- where [emphasis](process_emphasis.md) works by selecting among true considerations and [metaphor](process_metaphor.md) by importing a foreign logic.
