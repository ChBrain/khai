# Position: Tight

## Taxonomy

[Tightness-Looseness](position_tightness_looseness.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: tightness-looseness

## Has

A specific [normative regime](position_tightness_looseness.md): strong norms, narrow latitude -- clear rules, strongly enforced, with little tolerance for stepping out of them (Gelfand; the tight pole).
A world of pervasive monitoring and swift sanction, where the range of the allowable is narrow and known, and a departure is noticed and answered.
The field drawn taut: the persona lives under high situational constraint, its conduct closely bounded by what the world will permit.

## Orders

Hold the line, and watch it.
The regime orders the persona to meet each act already constrained -- to know the rule, keep within it, monitor itself and others, and expect a departure to be marked and corrected; it rewards self-regulation, impulse control, and reliability, and treats deviance as a threat to an order worth protecting.
It buys coordination, predictability, and safety through constraint, and the room feels the narrowness of the permitted before the persona acts.

## Loses

The openness and variety a wider latitude would allow.
A tight world forgoes the room to improvise, to differ, to try the unsanctioned -- it can be rigid, intolerant of the novel and the deviant, and costly to those who do not fit -- and it trades the creativity and adaptability of the loose for the order it keeps.
What it gives up is latitude: the reliability it secures is bought with the range of lives and acts it will not allow.

## Drives

On that tight regime:
holds it -- enforces, monitors, sanctions, and keeps the narrow order, most firmly where a threat makes coordination worth the constraint -- or loosens it, the latitude widening as the threat recedes and the enforcement comes to cost more than it buys.
The strong-norm baseline holds underneath either way, the world drawn taut rather than let [loose](position_loose.md).
