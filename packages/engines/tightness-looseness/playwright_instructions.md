# Instructions: Tightness-Looseness

How a Playwright wires the tightness-looseness engine into a play. You wire by linking the play's own content to the engine's files; the engine files stay untouched, they are canon. The Roadie plumbs the engine's law into Instructions Knowledge on deploy. Authoring guidance, not runtime content, and it does not go on tour.

## Human

- The human sets the normative terrain: how strong the norms of the persona's world are and how much deviance it tolerates -- so a moment can read the pressure the persona acts under, the tight world watching and sanctioning or the loose world letting a departure pass.

## Agent

- From the play's own content, a persona links the normative regime it acts under its Projection: tight, meeting each act already constrained by clear rules strongly enforced, or loose, meeting it with room to improvise and depart. Let the regime show as the severity of the field beneath the conduct -- what the world will notice and answer, and what it will let pass -- not as the persona's own choice to conform or rebel. You link to the engine, you never edit it.

## Collaboration

- The persona's yielding to a norm is the conformity engine's; its yielding to an authority is the obedience engine's; the formation of a particular group's norm is the group engine's; the persona's own self-monitoring and impulse control are its own engines'; the individual self-boundary of independence or interdependence is the self-construal engine's. This engine supplies the strength of the norm field -- the tight and loose regimes and the latitude each grants.

## Knowledge

- Tightness-looseness is the strength of the social norms a persona's world enforces and the latitude it grants for stepping out of them (Gelfand). It is a property of the normative regime, not of the persona's yielding -- upstream of conformity, it sets how much pressure there is to conform. It runs in two regimes. Tight is strong norms, narrow latitude -- clear rules strongly enforced, pervasive monitoring and swift sanction, high situational constraint met with more self-regulation; the regime buys order and coordination at the cost of openness. Loose is weak norms, wide latitude -- few or weakly enforced rules, high tolerance for deviance, little monitoring, room to improvise; the regime buys openness and variety at the cost of order. The field tends to tighten under a world's history of threat and to relax in its absence. Read a scene by how strong its norms are and how much it lets a persona stray.

## System

- Do keep tightness-looseness the strength of the norm field, not the persona's yielding: it is how severe the rules are and how much deviance the world tolerates, set before anyone conforms or refuses. Let the persona's bending to a norm be the conformity engine's and to an authority the obedience engine's.
- Do let a tight regime constrain: clear rules, pervasive monitoring, swift sanction, a narrow range of the allowable met with self-regulation -- and let it buy order and coordination at the cost of openness, and cost those who do not fit.
- Do let a loose regime grant room: few or weak rules, little monitoring, a wide range of the allowable, departures that mostly pass -- and let it buy variety and creativity at the cost of order and predictability.
- Do tie the regime to the world's conditions: tightness rising where a history of threat makes coordination worth the constraint, looseness where no such threat presses. Delegate the individual self-boundary to the self-construal engine (the two form a culture cluster but are separate axes) and a particular group's norm-making to the group engine. Do not edit the engine's files; wire only from the play's side.
