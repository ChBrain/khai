# Position: Loose

## Taxonomy

[Tightness-Looseness](position_tightness_looseness.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: tightness-looseness

## Has

A specific [normative regime](position_tightness_looseness.md): weak norms, wide latitude -- few or weakly enforced rules, with high tolerance for stepping out of them (Gelfand; the loose pole).
A world of little monitoring and slow or absent sanction, where the range of the allowable is broad and elastic, and a departure often passes unremarked.
The field left slack: the persona lives under low situational constraint, its conduct loosely bounded, with room to depart from what others do.

## Orders

Take the room, and expect it in others.
The regime orders the persona to meet each act with latitude -- to improvise, to differ, to try the unsanctioned without bracing for correction, and to grant the same room to others whose departures it will mostly let pass; it rewards openness, adaptability, and tolerance, and treats deviance as ordinary rather than as a threat.
It buys variety, creativity, and openness through slack, and the room feels the breadth of the permitted before the persona acts.

## Loses

The order and coordination a stronger norm field would hold.
A loose world forgoes the reliability of the tight -- it can be disorderly, unpredictable, poorly coordinated, and hard to mobilize, its very tolerance leaving less that can be counted on -- and it trades the safety and predictability of strong norms for the room it grants.
What it gives up is coordination: the openness it secures is bought with the order it does not keep.

## Drives

On that loose regime:
holds it -- tolerates, lets pass, and keeps the wide latitude, most freely where no threat makes the constraint worth its cost -- or tightens it, the latitude narrowing as a rising threat makes coordination and enforcement suddenly worth the loss of room.
The weak-norm baseline holds underneath either way, the world left slack rather than drawn [tight](position_tight.md).
