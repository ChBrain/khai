# Position: Tightness-Looseness

## Taxonomy

Culture / the strength of the norms a persona's world enforces (no khai file of its own).

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: tightness-looseness

## Has

A standing strength of the norm field a persona lives under, and the latitude its world grants for stepping out of it.
A read on a single axis: how clear and strongly enforced the rules are, and how much deviance is tolerated before sanction falls (Gelfand).
It is a property of the normative regime, not of the persona's yielding to it -- the severity of the field the persona acts within, set before anyone conforms or refuses.

## Orders

Act under this pressure.
The regime sets how much room the persona has to depart from the norm: a tight world watches, sanctions, and narrows the range of the allowable, so the persona meets each act already constrained; a loose world lets much pass, so the persona meets each act with room to improvise.
The room reads the severity of the field beneath the conduct, before the persona chooses to keep the norm or break it.

## Loses

The option of a world that is at once ordered and free.
A normative regime cannot maximize both: the tighter the field, the more order and coordination it buys and the less openness and variety it allows; the looser, the more openness and room it grants and the less predictability and coordination it holds.
What a world trades is the other pole's good -- the reliability of the tight against the latitude of the loose -- and the persona lives inside whichever trade its world has made.

## Drives

On that norm strength:
a world holds its regime -- the tight enforcing and monitoring, the loose tolerating and letting pass -- or shifts it, tightening under a rising threat that makes coordination worth the constraint, loosening as the threat recedes and the constraint comes to chafe.
The strength of the field holds underneath either way, in one of two regimes: [tight](position_tight.md), strong norms strongly enforced with little latitude, or [loose](position_loose.md), weak norms weakly enforced with much. The persona's yielding to a norm is the conformity engine's; its yielding to an authority is the obedience engine's; the formation of a particular group's norm is the group engine's; the individual self-boundary of independence or interdependence is the self-construal engine's. This engine owns how strong the norms are and how little or much a world lets a persona stray from them.
