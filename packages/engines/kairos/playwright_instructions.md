# Instructions: Kairos

How a Playwright wires the kairos engine into a play. You wire by linking the
play's own content to the engine's files; the engine files stay untouched, they
are canon. The Roadie plumbs the engine's law into Instructions Knowledge on
deploy. Authoring guidance, not runtime content, and it does not go on tour.

## Human

- The human sets which moments in the play are charged -- the beats a scene turns on, the windows a plan must strike through, the thresholds a dynamic tips at -- and whether each is met or missed.

## Agent

- From the play's own content, an unfolding links under its own moment-chapter the facet of the opportune moment it turns on: a plot at Cue, a plan at Implementation, a process at Initiated by. Link the window that opens and shuts (window), the decisive turn the outcome pivots on (juncture), the skill of the well-timed seizing (timing), or the pressing moment read as come full (ripeness). Play the kairos as the occasion itself -- the way open, the fork live, the time ripe -- present in the unfolding, never narrated, and never as a persona's choosing (that is agency's and decision's). Keep it the fittingness of the moment, not the measured flow (chronos), the countdown (ticking-clock), the imposed deadline (deadline), or the crossing (phase-shift). You link to the engine, you never edit it.

## Collaboration

- The measured passage the moment is cut from is chronos's; the suspense of a depleting clock is ticking-clock's; the imposed deadline is deadline's; the regime-crossing is phase-shift's; the persona's choice to seize the moment is agency's and decision's. Ripeness is the shared seam with chronos's maturation, and they are kept distinct: maturation is the object matured by much time, ripeness is the moment ripe at the right time. Whether the moment rings true as opportune is the team's review.

## Knowledge

- Kairos is the qualitative, opportune moment cut from time's even flow: the fitting instant when an act can be done that a moment before or after it cannot (Onians's weaver's opening and archer's aperture; Kermode's moment charged with significance, against chronos, mere passing time). It is the family's multi-cargo force, wiring on three unfolding types at once -- a plot at Cue, a plan at Implementation, a process at Initiated by -- because the fittingness of a moment is one phenomenon whatever unfolds. Four facets carry it -- window is the opening that opens and shuts, the gap the act can pass through; juncture is the decisive turn the outcome pivots on, many futures before it and one after; timing is the skill of the seizing, the fitting act at the fitting instant; ripeness is the twist, the same pressing moment read not as the window closing but as the time come full (Tillich's fullness of time, distinct from chronos's maturation). Kairos is knife-edged and cannot be stored -- a moment unread in its span is gone, not banked. Read the unfolding by the facet of the moment it turns on.

## System

- Do give each charged unfolding its facet under the right moment-chapter -- a plot's beat at Cue, a plan's window at Implementation, a process's threshold at Initiated by; do play the kairos as the occasion itself, present not narrated; do keep the reading the fittingness of the moment -- the way open, the outcome pivoting, the time ripe -- not a persona's choice and not a symbol.
- Do read ripeness as the twist: the same fleeting, pressing moment is also the ripe one, the occasion come full -- the sibling of chronos's maturation, kept distinct (the moment ripe at the right time, not the object matured by much time).
- Do not model the measured passage (that is chronos), the depleting countdown (that is ticking-clock), the imposed deadline (that is deadline), the regime-crossing (that is phase-shift), or the persona's choice to seize (that is agency and decision). Do not make a taken or missed moment mirror a persona's one chance at life (that is the play's pitch). Do not edit the engine's files; wire only from the play's side.
