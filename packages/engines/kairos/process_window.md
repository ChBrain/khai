# Process: Window

## Taxonomy

[Kairos](process_kairos.md), the facet of the opening.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: kairos

## Initiated by

The flow parts and a gap appears through which the act can pass.
Window starts wherever the unfolding opens a way that was not there and will not stay -- the shuttle's shed in the weave, the archer's target come clear, the guard dropped, the door left ajar, the market open before it closes.
It is kairos read as an aperture: not the whole flow but the one span of it the act can go through.

## Direction

Open, widen, narrow, shut.
The window is a span with a near edge and a far one: it comes open, holds for a while, and closes, and the act is possible only between the two edges.
It moves toward its own closing -- every window is passing even as it holds, and to read it is to read not only that it is open but how long it will stay so.

## Lever

The openness of the way against its closing.
The carrying force is that right now the way is clear -- the obstacle gone, the path free, the act unblocked; the resistance is that it will not remain clear, and the closing is often unannounced.
The window rewards the ready and punishes the late: to be poised when it opens is to pass through; to notice only as it shuts is to find the way closed.

## Echo

The act passed through, or the way gone.
Window leaves the unfolding either advanced through the opening -- the shot taken, the move made, the message sent while it still could be -- or stalled before a closed way, the chance had and lost.
It is the plainest face of kairos: that the way is not always open, and the open way does not wait.
