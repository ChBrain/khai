# Process: Timing

## Taxonomy

[Kairos](process_kairos.md), the facet of the skilled seizing.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: kairos

## Initiated by

An open window or a live juncture meets a reader of the moment, and the instant to act must be judged.
Timing starts wherever the moment is there to be taken and the taking is a skill -- the fitting word at the fitting time, the archer's loose, the comedian's beat, the strike neither rushed nor held.
It is kairos read as an art: not that the moment exists but that meeting it well is done, or bungled.

## Direction

Toward the act released on the moment's own tempo.
Timing matches the act to the instant -- not too early, before the moment is ripe and the way clear, and not too late, after the window has begun to shut; it is the felt sense of when, more than the knowledge of what.
It moves toward the well-timed release: the move that lands because it met the moment squarely, the trained eye and hand that find the instant the untrained miss.

## Lever

The judgement of the instant against the cost of misjudging it.
The carrying force is the practised reading of the moment -- the prudence that knows ripe from unripe and open from closing; the resistance is the ease of being a beat off, for early spends the moment before it comes and late lets it pass, and both fail though the moment was real.
Timing is where kairos is a competence and not only a condition: the same open window is passed through by the well-timed and missed by the ill-timed.

## Echo

The act that landed because it was timed, or the mistimed one that failed though the moment was there.
Timing leaves the unfolding marked by the quality of the meeting -- the deft seizing that makes the most of the occasion, or the fumble that had the chance and squandered it on being early or late.
It is the face of kairos that can be learned: the reading of the moment as a skill, dated from the release that met it or missed it.
