# Process: Juncture

## Taxonomy

[Kairos](process_kairos.md), the facet of the decisive turn.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: kairos

## Initiated by

The unfolding reaches a point where it can go more than one way, and what is done now decides which.
Juncture starts at the fork -- the crossroads, the tipping point, the moment past which one course is set and the others foreclosed: the order given or withheld, the line crossed, the offer taken.
It is kairos read as the pivot: not merely an opening but the instant the whole downstream hangs on.

## Direction

Toward the branch taken, the rest foreclosed.
The juncture concentrates the run into one instant's turn: before it, many futures are live; after it, one is real and the others are gone, unrecoverable.
It moves the unfolding from open to settled -- the point of no return once passed, the decision that cannot be un-made, the moment the story or the plan or the dynamic commits.

## Lever

The weight of the moment against the wish to defer.
The carrying force is that the outcome pivots here and nowhere else -- delay does not preserve the choice but spends it, for the un-taken juncture chooses by default and often for the worse; the resistance is the pull to wait for more certainty, which the moment does not grant.
To meet a juncture is to act into it knowing the branches close; to miss it is to have it decided for you by the passing.

## Echo

The course set, the branch locked, the run dated from the turn.
Juncture leaves the unfolding committed -- one future made real and the alternatives closed -- and everything after reads from the turn that was or was not taken here.
It is the heaviest face of kairos: the moment that does not merely pass but decides, and cannot be returned to once it has.
