# Process: Kairos

## Taxonomy

Occasion.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: kairos

## Initiated by

The even flow of time opens a moment that fits, and an act becomes possible that a moment before or after it is not.
Kairos starts not by the clock but when the unfolding -- a scene, a plan, a dynamic -- reaches an instant that is ripe to act on: the shed the weaver's shuttle can pass, the opening the archer's target gives, the beat when the thing can be done.
It is cut from chronos's measured passage: where chronos runs the flow evenly, kairos is the one moment in it charged with the chance to act.

## Direction

Toward the moment seized, or the moment let pass.
The occasion works four facets of the unfolding, read under its own moment-chapter -- a plot at Cue, a plan at Implementation, a process at Initiated by: [Window](process_window.md) is the opening that opens and shuts, [Juncture](process_juncture.md) the decisive turn the outcome pivots on, [Timing](process_timing.md) the skill of the seizing, and [Ripeness](process_ripeness.md) the twist -- the pressing moment read as come full.
A moment does not present evenly: a window may be wide but the timing missed, a juncture ripe but read as mere pressure, the occasion at hand yet let go.

## Lever

The fittingness of the moment against its fleetingness.
The carrying force is that right now everything lines up for the act -- the way is clear, the outcome pivots here, the time is ripe; the resistance is that it will not hold -- the window shuts, the juncture passes, and the same act a beat later fails or costs more.
Kairos is knife-edged and cannot be stored the way chronos accrues: a moment unread in its span is not banked but gone, and there is no returning to it -- only, sometimes, waiting for another.

## Echo

The moment taken and the act landed, or the moment missed and the chance closed.
Kairos does not accrue; it opens, presses, and shuts, handing the unfolding onward changed by whether its moment was met.
It hands off what it is not: the measured passage it was cut from is chronos's, the suspense of a depleting clock is ticking-clock's, the imposed deadline is deadline's, the regime-crossing is phase-shift's; kairos keeps only the fittingness -- whether this was the moment, and whether it was seized.
What follows is dated from the moment taken or lost, and the run bends around it.
