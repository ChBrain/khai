# Process: The Offering

## Taxonomy

[The Taking In](process_taking_in.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: hospitality

## Initiated by

Something is put in front of the stranger.
The Offering starts at the substance: bread, salt, water, tea, a share of the fire, a seat at the table -- given without being asked for and frequently without a word.

## Direction

Toward a bond sealed by consumption rather than agreement.
Accepting is what makes the guest, and the accepting is the whole ceremony. It has to be ingested or received bodily, which is why it is nearly always food, drink, or warmth rather than words or objects.
Once taken, both parties are bound, and neither can undo it by changing their mind.

## Lever

The completeness of the seal against the guest's inability to decline it.
The carrying force is that this is an elegant piece of social machinery: it is instant, requires no common language, cannot be faked, and produces a binding on both sides that survives everything except a breach -- a persona who has eaten in a house is safe in it, and that is worth a great deal in a world without other guarantees; the resistance is that the guest has no way to accept the shelter and decline the bond, since the offering is indivisible -- so a persona who wants only a night's protection has, by taking the cup, entered a relation with obligations they did not want and cannot now inspect.
The Offering binds by being impossible to take partially.

## Echo

A cup taken, and two people now under rules.
It is the conversion's actual mechanism, and the moment the clock starts.
The Offering is dated from the first swallow.
