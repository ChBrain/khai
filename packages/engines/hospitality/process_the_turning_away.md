# Process: The Turning Away

## Taxonomy

[The Breach](process_breach_hospitality.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: hospitality

## Initiated by

The door does not open.
The Turning Away starts at the refusal: a stranger at the threshold who is not taken in, whatever the reason and however good it is.

## Direction

Toward a resolution that is the other one.
There is no neutral refusal. A stranger who is not converted into a guest remains unplaceable, and the household has declared that it will not resolve them -- which leaves the second resolution standing.
The refused persona is not simply somewhere else. They are outside, unaccounted for, and now know something about this house.

## Lever

The right to refuse against there being no innocent way to exercise it.
The carrying force is that refusal is sometimes necessary and sometimes right: a household with nothing to give, or with reason to fear, cannot be obliged to open, and a rule that forbade refusal absolutely would make hospitality a hostage arrangement rather than a gift; the resistance is that the arrangement supplies no gentle form for it, since taking in is the only way to resolve a stranger and everything else means declining to -- so a refusal for the best of reasons arrives at the door as the same act as a refusal for the worst.
The Turning Away has no polite version, which is why it is felt so keenly by both.

## Echo

A door that stayed shut and a person who is still a stranger.
It is the breach that is frequently not a wrong at all, and is experienced as one anyway.
The Turning Away is dated from the moment nobody came.
