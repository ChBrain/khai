# Process: The Overstay

## Taxonomy

[The Clock](process_clock_hospitality.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: hospitality

## Initiated by

The term has run out and the guest is still here.
The Overstay starts at an invisible boundary: nothing was announced, nothing changed on the surface, and the persona's status has altered.

## Direction

Toward a guest who has become a burden and is still being treated as a guest.
The provision continues, because stopping it would be a breach. The honours continue, for the same reason. What changes is underneath: the giving is now counted, the household's patience is being spent, and a relation that was outside accounting has entered it.
The guest is usually the last to know, since every visible signal is unchanged.

## Lever

The host's obligation to keep giving against what the giving has become.
The carrying force is that the continued provision is genuinely honourable and the host is not being false: they are keeping a rule that binds them regardless of how they feel, and a host who threw somebody out at the exact hour would be a worse person than one who quietly resents them for a week; the resistance is that the arrangement gives the host no legitimate way to end it, so the only instruments available are indirect ones -- coolness, hints, third parties, sudden inconvenient plans -- and a guest reading them correctly must pretend they did not.
The Overstay makes both parties dishonest by giving neither a way to be direct.

## Echo

A welcome being extended and no longer offered.
It is the movement's centre and the engine's most stageable failure.
The Overstay is dated from a moment neither party can identify afterwards.
