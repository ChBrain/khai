# Process: The Guest's Duty

## Taxonomy

[The Terms](process_terms_hospitality.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: hospitality

## Initiated by

The guest must not become a second authority.
The Guest's Duty starts as a set of prohibitions: do not rearrange, do not invite others, do not go where you were not shown, do not comment on how the household runs, do not refuse what is offered.

## Direction

Toward conduct that leaves the household's order untouched.
Almost every item is negative. The guest's obligations are not things to perform but things to refrain from, and what they all protect is a single fact: there is one authority in this house and it is not them.
A guest who is helpful in the wrong way -- who tidies, who directs, who answers the door -- has broken it as surely as one who is rude.

## Lever

The lightness of the duties against what they add up to.
The carrying force is that the restraint is easy, courteous, and mostly invisible: a guest who observes it costs nothing and is welcome again, and the rules are so intuitive that most personas keep them without ever having been taught -- there is nothing oppressive about not rearranging somebody's kitchen; the resistance is that the sum of them is a suspension of the persona's ordinary agency for the duration, so a competent adult becomes somebody who must be shown where things are, and the more thoroughly they are hosted the less they may do.
The Guest's Duty is a small set of courtesies that together amount to a status.

## Echo

An adult being careful in somebody else's house.
It is where the engine's twist is already visible to anybody watching for it.
The Guest's Duty is dated from the offering and does not relax with familiarity.
