# Process: The Host's Betrayal

## Taxonomy

[The Breach](process_breach_hospitality.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: hospitality

## Initiated by

A guest has been harmed under the roof that took them in.
The Host's Betrayal starts at the act: violence, robbery, or delivery to an enemy, done to somebody who had eaten in the house.

## Direction

Toward a wrong held apart from all other wrongs.
Societies with very different moralities converge here, and the framing is remarkably consistent: the offence is not merely against the guest but against something above both parties, so it is described in terms of pollution, curse, or sacrilege rather than in terms of injury.
The same harm done to the same person in the road is a crime. Done under the roof it is something else.

## Lever

The proportionality of the horror against its unarguability.
The carrying force is that the horror is doing necessary work: nothing else protects a guest, who has surrendered every defence by entering, so the arrangement stands entirely on the act being unthinkable -- and where it is genuinely unthinkable, travel is possible for people with no other protection; the resistance is that a rule enforced by horror cannot be reasoned with or applied in degrees, so it is absolute where absoluteness is right and equally absolute where it is not, and it has been used to bind hosts to guests who did not deserve the covering.
The Host's Betrayal shows why the arrangement's protection and its rigidity are the same fact.

## Echo

An act the parties experience as belonging to a different category from ordinary harm.
It is the most severe thing the engine contains and the reason the whole arrangement is stable.
The Host's Betrayal is dated from the act and told about for generations.
