# Process: The Welcome

## Taxonomy

[The Clock](process_clock_hospitality.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: hospitality

## Initiated by

The arrangement is in its good period.
The Welcome starts after the conversion and runs for as long as the term does: the guest honoured, the host giving, both conducting themselves correctly, and nobody counting.

## Direction

Toward a stretch of time that feels open and is not.
Within the welcome everything is warm and nothing is measured. The guest is pressed to stay, told there is no hurry, and assured that the house is theirs -- and every one of those statements is sincere.
The term exists anyway, running underneath, known to nobody in a form they could state.

## Lever

The sincerity of the open invitation against the boundary it does not mention.
The carrying force is that the warmth is not a pretence and the host is not lying: within the term they genuinely do want the guest to stay, genuinely are not counting, and would be offended to be thanked too formally -- the welcome is the real thing and it is the reason the arrangement is worth having; the resistance is that a sincere open invitation and a bounded one are indistinguishable while the term is running, so the guest has no way to calibrate from the host's manner, which is exactly as warm on the last good day as on the first.
The Welcome tells the guest nothing about how long it lasts.

## Echo

A period both parties will remember fondly and differently.
It is the state the other two members of the movement are departures from.
The Welcome is dated from the offering and ends without a signal.
