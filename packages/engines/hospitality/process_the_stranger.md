# Process: The Stranger

## Taxonomy

[The Taking In](process_taking_in.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: hospitality

## Initiated by

Somebody is here who fits nowhere.
The Stranger starts at the arrival of a person with no place in the local order: not kin, not neighbour, not enemy, not client -- owing nothing, owed nothing, and belonging to no category the household keeps.

## Direction

Toward a condition the order cannot tolerate for long.
A person without a status is not merely unclassified, they are unaccountable: there is no way to predict them, no one answerable for them, and no established way to treat them.
Which is why the arrival is felt as a small emergency even where the household is entirely well disposed.

## Lever

The interest a stranger carries against the trouble they represent.
The carrying force is that strangers are also valuable and known to be: they bring news, goods, marriage possibilities, and word of the world, and a household that could not receive them would be poorer as well as ruder -- the eagerness at the door is not merely duty; the resistance is that the same unplaceability makes them dangerous, and the household has no information with which to distinguish the two, so the welcome is extended under uncertainty and would be extended to the wrong person just as readily.
The Stranger is an opportunity and a hazard in one body, and nobody can tell which.

## Echo

A person at the edge of an order that has no slot for them.
It is the movement's premise and the reason the conversion is performed so quickly.
The Stranger is dated from the moment they were seen approaching.
