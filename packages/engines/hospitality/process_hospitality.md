# Process: Hospitality

## Taxonomy

Household (no khai file of its own yet).

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: hospitality

## Initiated by

Somebody who does not belong here is at the door.
Hospitality starts at the anomaly a stranger presents: a person in a space that is not theirs, owing nothing and owed nothing, belonging to no category the household has -- and the arrangement exists because that condition cannot be left standing.
It is not the gift that binds by naming no price, which is the gift engine's, and not the neutral ground where nobody is host or guest, which is third-place's. It is the conversion of a stranger into a guest, and the terms the conversion carries.

## Direction

Toward a stranger resolved into somebody with a place, for a while.
The engine reads four movements: [the taking in](process_taking_in.md), the conversion at the door; [the terms](process_terms_hospitality.md), what each side then owes; [the clock](process_clock_hospitality.md), the period the arrangement holds and what happens past it; and [the breach](process_breach_hospitality.md), the violations, which every society treats as unusually grave.
The four are one arrangement, and it is among the oldest any society keeps.

## Lever

The honour done to the guest against what accepting it concedes.
The carrying force is that hospitality is genuinely generous and is not a trick: the host gives real protection, real provision, and real precedence, frequently at cost and frequently to somebody who can do nothing for them -- and the arrangement solves a hard problem, since it lets strangers move through a world with no other guarantee of their safety; the resistance is that the giving is also a demonstration, because only somebody who owns a place can extend it, so every kindness is a statement about whose house this is.
Hospitality is read on who may say when it ends, which is never the guest.

## Echo

A stranger exalted, fed, protected, and placed under somebody.
Hospitality does not close; it is a conversion being performed and maintained, gathered into one reading -- a taking in, a set of terms, a clock, and the breaches.
The engine's twist is here. **Hospitality honours the guest by demonstrating whose house it is.** To give is to have; to be given to is to be, however kindly, in somebody's power. So the guest is raised and subordinated in one motion: they cannot refuse without insult, cannot reciprocate here because a return must wait for their own house, and cannot stay past the term without the honour turning into dependence. And the more lavish the host, the more complete the demonstration -- which is why the most generous welcome can be the least comfortable to receive, and why nobody can say so.
It hands off what it is not: the transfer that binds by naming no price is gift's; the informal ground where nobody is host or guest is third-place's; the kept flame of a household is hearth's; the person held by a place is dwelling's; the distances bodies keep is proxemics's; the public self-image managed in interaction is face's; and a welcome made to **stand for** a theme is authored key, the play's pitch, not the force.
The persona is dated from the moment they were let in, and the clock started then.
