# Process: The Host's Duty

## Taxonomy

[The Terms](process_terms_hospitality.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: hospitality

## Initiated by

The household now owes the guest.
The Host's Duty starts on conversion: to feed, to shelter, to protect -- including against the household's own -- and to do it without visible calculation and without asking what the guest can offer in return.

## Direction

Toward provision that must not look like a transaction.
The giving has to be unstinting and it has to be unpriced. A host who counts, who asks about payment, or who is seen to be measuring what the guest is worth has not been ungenerous, they have failed at the thing itself.
And the protection extends further than seems reasonable: in the strong versions the host defends the guest against enemies, against kin, and at real cost.

## Lever

The genuine cost of the duty against the standing that discharging it produces.
The carrying force is that the burden is real and frequently heavy, is borne for people who can do nothing in return, and is met -- this is one of the more impressive things societies routinely accomplish, and treating it as merely strategic misses what is actually happening at most doors; the resistance is that the duty is also the mechanism by which a household displays what it has and what it can afford to give away, so discharging it well is indistinguishable from advertising, and the most generous host in a valley is also its most conspicuous.
The Host's Duty costs and it also announces.

## Echo

A household that has given openly and has been seen to.
It is the half of the terms that looks like pure virtue and mostly is.
The Host's Duty is dated from the offering and runs while the clock does.
