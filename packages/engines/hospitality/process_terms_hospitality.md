# Process: The Terms

## Taxonomy

[Hospitality](process_hospitality.md), what each side then owes.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: hospitality

## Initiated by

Both parties are now under obligations neither negotiated.
The Terms start immediately on conversion: [the host's duty](process_host_duty.md) to protect and provide, [the guest's duty](process_guest_duty.md) not to usurp, and [the precedence](process_the_precedence.md) that the guest is given and must accept.

## Direction

Toward an arrangement in which the guest is served and constrained by the same rules.
The obligations are asymmetric and both are heavy. The host must feed, shelter, and defend somebody who may be nothing to them; the guest must accept what is offered, refrain from anything that would make them a second authority, and pass no comment on how the household is run.
Neither set is stated, and both are known.

## Lever

The protection the terms extend against what they forbid the protected.
The carrying force is that the terms are what make the arrangement work at all: a guest who can rely on being fed and defended can travel, and a host who can rely on not being challenged can take in strangers without risk -- the mutual restraint is the mechanism, and it is why hospitality is one of the few arrangements that reliably survives a language barrier; the resistance is that the guest's half consists almost entirely of things they must not do, so being well treated and being disempowered arrive as the same package, and the guest who notices is not permitted to mention it.
The Terms give the guest everything except a say.

## Echo

Two people conducting themselves correctly, unequally, without discussion.
It shows the arrangement working, which is the ordinary case and the one a play should establish before disturbing.
The Terms are dated from the conversion and hold until the clock runs out.
