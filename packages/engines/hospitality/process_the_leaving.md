# Process: The Leaving

## Taxonomy

[The Clock](process_clock_hospitality.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: hospitality

## Initiated by

The guest goes.
The Leaving starts at the departure and is a ceremony in its own right: the pressing to stay longer, the refusal, the provisions for the road, the accompaniment to the edge of the property, the parting gift.

## Direction

Toward a relation that outlives the visit.
A departure taken in good time converts the arrangement into something durable: the parties are now bound by a tie that persists, obliges reciprocally, and can be claimed years later or by their children.
The pressing to stay is part of it and is not a test. Both parties know the guest is going; performing the reluctance is how the ending is made to look like the host's loss rather than the guest's exit.

## Lever

The warmth of a good parting against the fact that its warmth depends on its timing.
The carrying force is that this is the arrangement at its best and its most useful: hospitality properly closed produces a lasting bond between households that had none, which is how a network of obligation gets built across strangers, and the ceremony that closes it is doing real work; the resistance is that everything about the leaving depends on the guest having judged the term correctly -- the same ceremony performed a week late is a relief with warmth painted over it, and the persona cannot tell which one they are receiving.
The Leaving is the only exit that keeps the relation, and it is available only to somebody who guessed right.

## Echo

A departure that made two households into something.
It is the movement's good ending and the reason the term matters.
The Leaving is dated from the parting and is claimed decades later.
