# Process: The Guest's Usurpation

## Taxonomy

[The Breach](process_breach_hospitality.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: hospitality

## Initiated by

The guest has behaved as though the house were theirs.
The Guest's Usurpation starts at any act that puts them in the host's position: inviting somebody else in, directing the household, disposing of what is not theirs, or answering for the house to a caller.

## Direction

Toward a challenge that need not have been intended as one.
Most usurpations are accidents of helpfulness. The guest tidies, offers a visitor a drink, moves a chair, takes an initiative -- and has, without meaning anything by it, exercised an authority they do not hold.
The offence is structural rather than psychological, which is why it lands even when everybody can see it was well meant.

## Lever

The innocence of the act against the seriousness of what it displaces.
The carrying force is that hosts mostly absorb it and should: the guest meant well, the harm is symbolic, and a household that took offence at every misplaced kindness would be intolerable -- most usurpations are quietly repaired and never mentioned; the resistance is that the arrangement's whole architecture rests on there being one authority here, so a repeated or public usurpation is not a discourtesy but a claim, and the host cannot let it stand without the terms dissolving for everybody in the house.
The Guest's Usurpation is usually harmless and always the same act.

## Echo

A kindness that was a claim, and everybody pretending otherwise.
It is the engine's most ordinary breach and the easiest to stage without a villain.
The Guest's Usurpation is dated from the chair that was moved.
