# Process: The Precedence

## Taxonomy

[The Terms](process_terms_hospitality.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: hospitality

## Initiated by

The guest is put first.
The Precedence starts with the honours: the better seat, the first serving, the best of what there is, the household's own portion given away if there is not enough.

## Direction

Toward a guest elevated above the people who live here.
The honour is real and is frequently expensive -- children go without, the good dish is opened, the host eats after or not at all.
And it must be accepted. A guest who declines the seat, refuses the best portion, or insists on being treated as ordinary has not been modest, they have refused the host's right to confer.

## Lever

The generosity of the elevation against the guest's obligation to receive it.
The carrying force is that this is hospitality at its most admirable and is not performance: households genuinely do go without for people they will never see again, and the guest who is placed at the head of the table has been given something that costs the giver; the resistance is that only somebody with authority can raise another, so being honoured is being ranked -- and the guest cannot decline the ranking without insulting the ranker, which means the one thing they may not do is treat the exchange as between equals.
The Precedence exalts the guest by exercising the very authority the guest is being kept from.

## Echo

Somebody in the best chair who did not choose to sit there.
It is the engine's twist compressed into a seating arrangement.
The Precedence is dated from the first meal and repeated at every one.
