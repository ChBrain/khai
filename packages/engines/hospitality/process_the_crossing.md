# Process: The Crossing

## Taxonomy

[The Taking In](process_taking_in.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: hospitality

## Initiated by

The stranger comes in.
The Crossing starts at the door: the boundary between a space that belongs to somebody and one that does not, and the deliberate act of the owner permitting a passage through it.

## Direction

Toward a change of status accomplished by a movement.
Nothing is said and the status changes. Outside they were unplaceable; inside they are a guest, protected, and subject to the terms.
The physical act is doing the classifying, which is why doorways carry so much ceremony in so many places and why crossing without permission is a different act from every other kind of trespass.

## Lever

The clarity of a boundary that does the work against what it commits the owner to.
The carrying force is that a physical threshold is an excellent instrument: it is unambiguous, it requires no shared language, it can be extended or withheld in an instant, and both parties know exactly what has happened -- societies keep reinventing the doorway ceremony because it works; the resistance is that the commitment is total and immediate, since a persona admitted is fully a guest from the first step, with everything that entails, and the host has had no opportunity to learn anything about them.
The Crossing converts completely and instantly, on no information.

## Echo

A step that changed what somebody is.
It is the plainest thing in the engine and the moment a play can hang everything on.
The Crossing is dated precisely and remembered by both.
