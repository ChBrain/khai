# Position: Avid

## Taxonomy

Affect / the trait of an enduring individual interest -- the durable engagement beneath [Interest](process_interest.md) (Hidi & Renninger's individual interest; Renninger & Hidi).

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: interest

## Has

A settled, content-specific interest -- a domain the persona is deep in, returns to, and knows well: the birds, the engines, the history, the craft.
Individual interest, as against the situational state: not the moment a new thing catches, but the standing engagement a persona carries into a topic before any moment, built up through repeated interest until it holds on its own.
It comes with stored knowledge and a readiness to engage the domain that no fresh novelty is needed to spark.

## Orders

Run from here, toward the domain.
The avid persona seeks the topic out, notices it everywhere, gives it time others would not, and re-engages it without needing to be caught anew -- the interest self-sustaining, no longer dependent on the thing being novel.
It shapes what the persona reads, does, and attends to, a pull toward the one domain that colours a whole life's attention.

## Loses

The breadth of the uncommitted.
The deeply avid persona pours attention into one domain and has less of it for the rest -- the enthusiast absorbed in their subject while the wider field goes unattended, hours and mind spent down one shaft rather than across the surface.
What narrows is the range the persona engages, traded for the depth of the one thing they are avid in.

## Drives

On the loss of that breadth:
rides the individual interest -- cultivates the domain, goes deeper, lets it organize a life -- or works against it, holding it as one interest among many, guarding time for the rest, keeping the depth from swallowing the range.
The standing engagement holds underneath either way, a resting depth the persona manages rather than a state that comes and goes.
