# Process: Broaden

## Taxonomy

[Interest](process_interest.md), the function -- the widening of attention that builds knowledge over time.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: interest

## Initiated by

Interest, once fired, does its work: it widens.
This is interest as a positive emotion in the broaden-and-build sense (Fredrickson) and as the primary motivator of learning (Tomkins; Izard): the engagement does not merely register the object but opens the persona to it, drawing attention wider, sustaining exploration, and, across time, building competence and knowledge.
It is the forward face of the emotion -- not the moment interest strikes, but what interest is for.

## Direction

Outward and onward -- attention widened now, capacity built later.
In the moment, broaden is the widening of the attentional field, the persona taking in more of the object and its surround than a narrower state would allow; over time, it is the accumulation the widening leaves -- the skills practiced, the facts gathered, the domain slowly filled in.
Its direction runs from the live engagement to the durable competence it deposits.

## Lever

What broaden spends -- the narrowness of the disengaged mind.
Boredom and indifference keep attention tight and the world unexplored; interest opens it, and the opening is the mechanism by which a persona learns things they were not required to learn and gathers resources they did not set out to gather.
A production holds this lever by letting interest change what the persona does -- reading further, trying the thing, returning to it -- so the emotion visibly builds something across the run, rather than sitting as a static state.

## Echo

The engagement leaves a deposit -- knowledge, skill, a domain more filled in than before.
This is the bridge from the state to the trait: repeated broadening in one domain is how a passing situational interest becomes an enduring [avid](position_avid.md) one, the emotion crystallizing into a standing engagement (Hidi & Renninger's development of individual interest).
The total absorption where the widening collapses into a single matched challenge and self-consciousness drops away is the flow engine's; broaden is the ordinary widening interest works by, the open attention that learning rides on.
