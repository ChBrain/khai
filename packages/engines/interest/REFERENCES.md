# Interest: Reference

## Line of Work

Interest as a **multi-type** engine: the knowledge emotion of engagement with the new-yet-graspable, taken across its two appraisals, its broadening function, and the enduring individual interest it develops into. The domain does not model the resting inclination toward the unknown that precedes any object, which is the curiosity engine's; nor the total absorption in a challenge matched to skill where self-consciousness drops away, which is the flow engine's; nor the momentary startle at the unexpected, which is the surprise engine's; nor the vastness that overwhelms the frame and demands accommodation, which is the awe engine's. It models the emotion itself: the felt pull of engagement when a persona meets something novel and complex and senses it can be understood, and what that emotion does -- widen attention, sustain exploration, and build knowledge over time.

The spine is the psychology of interest as a distinct emotion. Against the folk view that interest is merely the absence of boredom or a synonym for curiosity, the differential-emotions and knowledge-emotions traditions treat interest as a basic positive emotion in its own right (Tomkins's interest-excitement; Izard). Silvia's appraisal account gives it structure: interest arises from two appraisals in sequence -- a novelty-complexity check (is this new, complex, unexpected?) and a coping-potential check (can I understand this?) -- and only their conjunction fires the emotion, which is what forks interest from confusion when the same complexity outruns the coping. Berlyne's collative variables (novelty, complexity, uncertainty, conflict) supply the first appraisal's content. Fredrickson's broaden-and-build theory names its function: interest widens the momentary attentional field and, over time, builds knowledge and competence. Hidi and Renninger's four-phase model traces its development from a triggered situational state to a durable, content-specific individual interest. The altitudes are those: the emotion, its two appraisals, its broadening function, and its trait.

## Restrictions

What the engine refuses to model, and to whom it delegates.

- **Curiosity**: The engine models the emotion of engagement, not the resting inclination toward the unknown. Curiosity -- the standing posture at the edge of the understood, before any particular object or gap appears -- is owned by the curiosity engine (which models it as a position, with its epistemic, perceptual, social, and exploratory forms); interest is the emotion that fires when a specific new-yet-graspable object is met, the disposition made live. The two compose: curiosity is the readiness, interest the state it readies for.
- **Flow**: The engine models the engaged attention of interest, not the total absorption of flow. The complete merging of action and awareness in a challenge matched to skill, where self-consciousness and the sense of time drop away, is owned by the flow engine; interest is the ordinary widening of attention that can precede or feed flow but is not it -- one can be deeply interested while still self-aware and clock-watching.
- **Surprise**: The engine models the appraised novelty that catches interest, not the startle that registers the unexpected. The brief, appraisal-light interruption when something violates expectation is owned by the surprise engine; surprise may trigger interest, but interest is what follows when the novel thing is appraised as graspable and worth engaging, not the initial jolt.
- **Awe**: The engine models the new-yet-graspable, not the vast-yet-unassimilable. Awe -- the response to perceived vastness that overwhelms the current frame and demands accommodation -- is owned by the awe engine; interest lives where the thing can be grasped with the resources at hand, and where it cannot but overwhelms instead, the emotion is awe's, not this engine's.
- **Boredom and confusion**: The engine models interest, and marks its two failure edges without owning them. Where the object is too familiar to be novel, the state is boredom, the world gone flat; where it is too complex to be grasped, the state is confusion, the knowledge emotion of the not-yet-comprehended. Interest is the band between, and the neighboring knowledge emotions at each edge are their own domains where those exist.

- **[avid](position_avid.md)** (position, trait): An enduring, content-specific individual interest the emotion develops into -- the persona settled deep in a domain, the interest self-sustaining. Anchored by Hidi & Renninger (the four-phase model; individual interest).
