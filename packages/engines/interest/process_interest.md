# Process: Interest

## Taxonomy

Emotion / the knowledge emotion of engagement with the new-yet-graspable.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: interest

## Initiated by

A persona meets something new, complex, unexpected, or uncertain -- and, sensing it can be understood, leans in.
This is interest: the knowledge emotion, the felt pull of engagement toward what departs from the already-known (Silvia; Tomkins's interest-excitement; Izard's differential emotions).
It is appraised on two dials, not one -- how novel and complex the thing is, and whether the persona can cope with it, make sense of it -- and only where both read high does the emotion fire: the new that is also graspable.

## Direction

Toward the object, and into it -- attention drawn, widened, and held.
Interest is an approach emotion: it moves the persona closer, to look longer, ask, handle, and explore, rather than away or on.
Its direction is deeper into the thing that caught it -- the sustained engagement that keeps a persona reading, tinkering, or turning a problem over long after a less interested one would have moved on.

## Lever

The two appraisals that gate it -- novelty-complexity, and coping potential (Silvia's two-appraisal structure).
What interest spends is the ease of the familiar: the settled, fully-understood world draws no interest, and neither does the world so complex it cannot be grasped -- the first is not new, the second not comprehensible.
Interest fires in the band between, where a thing is new enough to matter and graspable enough to try, and it is this appraisal, not the object alone, that sets whether the same complexity draws interest, confusion, or indifference.

## Echo

The thing is engaged -- and, engaged, it is learned: interest is the emotion that motivates exploration and the growth of knowledge and skill, the affective engine of curiosity's disposition made live.
Its absence is boredom, the world gone flat and unengaging; its overreach, when the complexity outruns the coping, is confusion, and past that, anxiety.
It runs as [novelty](process_interest_novel.md), [grasp](process_interest_grasp.md), and [broaden](process_interest_broaden.md), settling over time into an enduring [avid](position_avid.md) interest. The resting inclination toward the unknown, before any object appears, is the curiosity engine's; the total absorption in a challenge matched to skill is the flow engine's; the momentary startle at the unexpected is the surprise engine's; the vastness that overwhelms the frame and demands accommodation is the awe engine's. This engine owns the knowledge emotion of engagement with the new-yet-graspable.
