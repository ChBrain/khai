# @chbrain/khai-engine-interest

## 0.1.1

### Patch Changes

- 52ff8fc: Add the interest engine (multi-type: process, position) -- the knowledge emotion of engagement with the new-yet-graspable. Against the folk view that interest is just the absence of boredom or a synonym for curiosity, this models interest as a distinct emotion (Tomkins's interest-excitement; Izard) with the two-appraisal structure Silvia gives it: a novelty-complexity check and a coping-potential check, whose conjunction fires the emotion and forks it from confusion when the complexity outruns the coping.

  - **Root (process):** `interest` -- the felt pull of engagement when a persona meets something novel and complex and senses it can be understood, appraised on two dials and moving the persona to attend and explore.
  - **Forms (process):** `novelty` (the first appraisal -- the collative pull toward what departs from the expected, on Berlyne's novelty/complexity/uncertainty/conflict), `grasp` (the second appraisal -- coping potential, the sense the thing is comprehensible, the fork with confusion), `broaden` (the function -- interest as the emotion that widens attention and builds knowledge and competence over time, on Fredrickson's broaden-and-build and Tomkins/Izard's interest-as-motivator-of-learning).
  - **Trait (position):** `avid` -- an enduring, content-specific individual interest the emotion crystallizes into (Hidi & Renninger's four-phase model), the persona settled deep in a domain, the interest self-sustaining.

  Warranted (LORE) on Silvia (the two-appraisal account of interest, 2005/2006), Tomkins and Izard (interest as a basic positive emotion), Berlyne (the collative variables), Fredrickson (broaden-and-build), and Hidi & Renninger (individual-interest development). Bounded against `curiosity` (the resting inclination toward the unknown, modeled there as a position -- interest is the emotion that disposition readies for), `flow` (total absorption in a matched challenge, self-consciousness gone -- interest is the ordinary widening that can precede it), `surprise` (the startle that registers the unexpected -- interest is what follows when the novel thing is appraised graspable), and `awe` (the vast-yet-unassimilable -- interest lives where the thing can be grasped). Set at patch as the free level. No whitelist required -- the `interest` root, `interest_*` form stems, and `avid` trait stem are unique.
