# Process: Novelty

## Taxonomy

[Interest](process_interest.md), the first appraisal -- the novelty-complexity that catches it.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: interest

## Initiated by

The persona meets something that departs from the expected -- new, complex, unexpected, ambiguous, or mysterious.
This is the first of interest's two appraisals (Silvia), and it runs on what Berlyne called the collative variables: novelty, complexity, uncertainty, conflict -- the properties a thing has by how it compares against what the persona already knows.
It is the catch: the deviation from the familiar that makes a thing a candidate for interest in the first place.

## Direction

Toward the departure itself -- the point where the thing stops matching the known.
The novelty appraisal orients the persona to what is unexpected in the object: the surprising detail, the pattern that does not fit, the gap where understanding runs out.
Its direction is at the edge of the known, where the new begins.

## Lever

What novelty spends -- the sufficiency of the expected.
A thing fully predicted draws no interest; the collative pull is precisely the mismatch between what was expected and what is met, and it scales with the size of that mismatch up to a point.
A production holds this lever by making the object genuinely depart from the persona's frame -- not merely new to the audience, but new to the persona, appraised against what that persona already holds -- so the novelty is real from where they stand.

## Echo

The thing is marked as new -- but novelty alone does not make interest; it makes only a candidate.
The unexpected that cannot be grasped does not draw interest but confusion or unease; the novel appraisal must be met by the second, the sense that the thing is comprehensible, before the emotion fires.
The bare registering of the unexpected, the startle before any appraisal of coping, is the surprise engine's; novelty here is interest's first gate, the collative pull that opens the question the [grasp](process_interest_grasp.md) appraisal must then answer.
