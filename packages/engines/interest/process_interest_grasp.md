# Process: Grasp

## Taxonomy

[Interest](process_interest.md), the second appraisal -- the coping potential that forks it from confusion.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: interest

## Initiated by

The persona, having met something novel and complex, appraises whether they can make sense of it.
This is the second of interest's two appraisals (Silvia's coping-potential appraisal): not is this new, but can I understand this -- do I have the skills, knowledge, or footing to follow it.
It is the appraisal that decides whether the novelty becomes interest or something else, and it is what makes interest, unlike raw curiosity, a bet the persona judges winnable.

## Direction

Toward the persona's own resources, held against the thing's demand.
The grasp appraisal turns from the object to the self's capacity for it -- measuring the complexity met against the understanding available, and reading whether the gap is one the persona can close.
Its direction is inward, to the question of whether this can be handled, before the leaning-in commits.

## Lever

What grasp spends -- the assumption that novelty dictates the feeling.
The same complex object draws interest from the persona who senses they can grasp it and confusion, then anxiety, from the one who cannot: hold the coping high and the novelty becomes engaging; let the demand outrun the resources and the identical thing becomes overwhelming.
A production holds this lever by setting the persona's footing against the object -- an expert and a novice meeting the same complexity feel different emotions, because the fork is in the coping, not the thing.

## Echo

The novelty is judged graspable, and interest fires -- or judged beyond reach, and it does not.
This is the fork that distinguishes interest from its neighbors in the knowledge emotions: met with coping, complexity draws interest; met without it, the same complexity draws confusion, the knowledge emotion of the not-yet-comprehended.
When the coping is so complete that the thing no longer challenges, interest fades toward the ease of the mastered and the flatness of boredom; interest lives in the moving band where the thing is graspable but not yet grasped, and holding it there over a real engagement is the [broaden](process_interest_broaden.md) the emotion builds toward.
