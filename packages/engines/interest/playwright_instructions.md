# Instructions: Interest

How a Playwright wires the interest engine into a play. You wire by linking the play's own content to the engine's files; the engine files stay untouched, they are canon. The Roadie plumbs the engine's law into Instructions Knowledge on deploy. Authoring guidance, not runtime content, and it does not go on tour.

## Human

- The human defines the interest terrain: what novel, complex, or uncertain things the persona meets, and where the persona's understanding stands against them -- so a moment has something new to engage and a footing from which it can be grasped or not.

## Agent

- From the play's own content, a persona links the interest a moment runs under its Projection: what is appraised as novel and complex, whether it reads as graspable, and, where a durable engagement has formed, the domain the persona is avid in. Let it read as the leaning-in of engagement -- attention widened and held, the exploration sustained -- and keep it the emotion, not the disposition: the resting inclination toward the unknown is the curiosity engine's. You link to the engine, you never edit it.

## Collaboration

- The resting inclination toward the unknown, before any object appears, is the curiosity engine's; the total absorption in a challenge matched to skill, where self-consciousness drops away, is the flow engine's; the momentary startle at the unexpected is the surprise engine's; the vastness that overwhelms the frame and demands accommodation is the awe engine's. This engine supplies the knowledge emotion of engagement with the new-yet-graspable -- the two appraisals, the widening, and the individual interest it develops into.

## Knowledge

- Interest is the knowledge emotion of engagement with the new-yet-graspable: a persona meets something novel, complex, or uncertain, senses it can be understood, and leans in. It is appraised on two dials, not one (Silvia): a novelty-complexity check -- is this new, complex, unexpected? -- and a coping-potential check -- can I understand this? -- and only their conjunction fires it, which is what forks interest from confusion when the complexity outruns the coping. It runs in forms. Novelty is the first appraisal -- the collative pull toward what departs from the expected (Berlyne). Grasp is the second -- the sense that the thing is comprehensible, the fork with confusion. Broaden is the function -- interest as the emotion that widens attention and builds knowledge and competence over time (Fredrickson; Tomkins). Avid is the trait -- an enduring, content-specific individual interest the emotion crystallizes into (Hidi & Renninger), the persona settled deep in a domain.

## System

- Do keep interest the emotion and curiosity the disposition: interest fires when a specific new-yet-graspable object is met, curiosity is the standing readiness that precedes it -- let the persona's interest attach to something, not float.
- Do gate interest on both appraisals: novelty alone is not enough, and neither is comprehensibility alone -- let the same complex thing draw interest from the persona who can grasp it and confusion from the one who cannot, so the fork reads as the coping, not the object.
- Do let interest broaden and build: let it change what the persona attends to and does -- reading further, trying the thing, returning to it -- so the emotion visibly widens attention and, across the run, accumulates knowledge or skill rather than sitting static.
- Do let a repeated situational interest crystallize into an avid one where the play earns it, the persona grown deep in a domain. Do delegate the total absorption of a matched challenge to the flow engine and the resting inclination toward the unknown to the curiosity engine. Do not edit the engine's files; wire only from the play's side.
