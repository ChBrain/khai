# Position: Peripheral

## Taxonomy

[Moral Identity](position_moral_identity.md), both dimensions low -- morality not central to the self.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: moral-identity

## Has

A self not built around being moral -- morality present but off to the side.
A specific [moral identity](position_moral_identity.md): moral traits neither central to the private self-concept (internalization low) nor expressed as a moral image (symbolization low), the moral self peripheral to who the persona takes itself to be.
It is not immorality -- the persona may know and even follow the rules -- but morality is one consideration among many, not the ground of the self.

## Orders

Run from here, weakly.
The peripheral moral self exerts little self-regulatory pull: conduct is governed by other anchors -- advantage, belonging, safety, pleasure -- and the moral self, being small, is easily outweighed when it conflicts with them.
It reads a moral choice as a cost-benefit like any other, not as a question of who it is.

## Loses

The internal brake that a central moral self supplies.
The persona for whom morality is peripheral is more available to cut the moral corner when it pays, since doing so costs no piece of the self -- there is little moral self to violate -- and so relies on external constraint, incentive, and oversight to behave well, where the custodian is held by its own identity.
What it lacks is the self-consistency motive that makes goodness self-enforcing.

## Drives

On that peripherality:
rides it -- keeps morality one factor among many, behaves well when it pays or is watched and cuts the corner when it does not -- or, through some reckoning or role, begins to build the moral self up, moving toward the [internalized](position_internalized.md) or [symbolic](position_symbolic.md) standing as being good becomes more a part of who it is. The centrality holds underneath either way, low and off to the side.
