# Position: Custodian

## Taxonomy

[Moral Identity](position_moral_identity.md), both dimensions high -- moral self central and enacted.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: moral-identity

## Has

A moral self both deeply held and openly enacted -- the integrated moral identity.
A specific [moral identity](position_moral_identity.md): morality central to the private self-concept (internalization high) and expressed through visible moral action (symbolization high) at once, the two dimensions aligned.
It is the moral self at full weight -- being good is core to who the persona is, and its life is visibly organized around it.

## Orders

Run from here, governing.
The custodian moral self is the strongest self-regulator: conduct is anchored hard to the moral self-image, the self-consistency pull at its most forceful, so the persona holds the moral line under pressure and temptation where a peripheral self would fold.
It reads every significant choice against the moral person it is committed to being, in private and in public alike.

## Loses

The latitude to fail cheaply, and the ease of moral flexibility.
The custodian cannot take the convenient exception without a wound to the self -- a lapse is a betrayal of who it is, not a minor cost -- and it can tip into moral rigidity, self-righteousness, or the strain of a standard held too hard, judging others by the bar it holds itself to.
What it trades is all moral wiggle-room for a self it can stand behind.

## Drives

On that integrated centrality:
rides it -- lets the moral self govern conduct through pressure and cost, the person whose word holds and whose line stays -- or lets it stiffen into rigidity and judgment, or, worn down by the cost of holding the line alone, erodes toward the merely [symbolic](position_symbolic.md) or the [internalized](position_internalized.md). The centrality holds underneath either way, at its full and governing weight.
