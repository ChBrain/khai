# Position: Symbolic

## Taxonomy

[Moral Identity](position_moral_identity.md), the outward display high and the private rootedness lower.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: moral-identity

## Has

A moral self expressed and enacted outwardly, more shown than privately held.
A specific [moral identity](position_moral_identity.md): the persona expresses its moral self through visible action, membership, and self-presentation (Aquino & Reed's symbolization high), where the private centrality runs lower.
It is the public moral self -- goodness worn, signalled, and performed, the moral image projected to others.

## Orders

Run from here, outward.
The symbolic moral self governs conduct by its visibility: the persona does the right thing where it is seen and read as moral, curates the moral image, joins and displays and announces -- the audience is part of the point.
It reads its conduct against how moral it appears at least as much as against a private standard.

## Loses

The self-consistency that private rootedness would supply.
The persona whose moral self is more symbolized than internalized is more exposed to the gap between the shown and the felt -- moral display without deep private centrality can slide toward moral licensing, the credit of the visible good act spent on a hidden lapse, or toward hypocrisy when the performance outruns the self.
What it risks is the moral image coming loose from the moral substance.

## Drives

On that outward expression:
rides it -- lets the moral image motivate real good done in the open, uses visibility to hold itself to account -- or lets the display drift from the substance, symbolizing more than it internalizes, until the moral self is a presentation. Deepened by private centrality, it becomes the [custodian](position_custodian.md); hollowed of it, it approaches the [peripheral](position_peripheral.md) self wearing a moral face. The centrality holds underneath either way, turned toward the audience.
