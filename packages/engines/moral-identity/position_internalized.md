# Position: Internalized

## Taxonomy

[Moral Identity](position_moral_identity.md), the private dimension high and the outward display low.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: moral-identity

## Has

A moral self rooted deep in the private self-concept, not worn on the sleeve.
A specific [moral identity](position_moral_identity.md): morality central to who the persona is inwardly (Aquino & Reed's internalization high), but not made visible or symbolized outwardly.
It is the quiet moral self -- being good matters intensely to the persona's sense of itself, and it needs no audience for that to be true.

## Orders

Run from here, inward.
The internalized moral self governs conduct by private consistency: the persona does the right thing because not to would violate who it is, whether or not anyone sees, and takes little care to be seen doing it.
It reads its own conduct against its moral self-image first, and the world's regard second or not at all.

## Loses

The credit that outward moral display would earn.
The quietly moral persona is often not recognized as moral -- it does not advertise, signal, or perform its goodness -- and so loses the reputational return that the symbolic moral self collects, sometimes read as cold or indifferent for its lack of display.
What it trades is moral visibility for moral integrity unwitnessed.

## Drives

On that private rootedness:
rides it -- holds the line unseen, lets conscience govern where no one is watching, keeps the goodness real rather than shown -- or, feeling unrecognized, begins to symbolize, moving toward the [custodian](position_custodian.md) who does both, or resents the display of others who symbolize more than they internalize. The centrality holds underneath either way, private and deep.
