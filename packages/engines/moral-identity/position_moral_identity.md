# Position: Moral Identity

## Taxonomy

Moral self / the centrality of being moral to the self-concept (no khai file of its own).

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: moral-identity

## Has

A self built, to some degree, around being a moral person.
How central the moral traits -- caring, fair, honest, kind, compassionate -- are to how the persona defines itself, a self-schema it holds strongly or weakly (Aquino & Reed).
It is not whether the persona knows right from wrong, but how much being good is part of who it takes itself to be.

## Orders

Run from here, beneath every choice with a moral edge.
The moral self does not decide a single case; it sets the standing pull toward staying consistent with the moral person the persona believes itself to be, the pressure Blasi called self-consistency.
Under no particular test, this is how much the persona's conduct is anchored to its moral self-image.

## Loses

The freedom of a self not staked on being good.
The persona for whom moral identity is central cannot act against it cheaply -- a dishonest or cruel act costs it a piece of who it is, not merely an external penalty -- and so it forgoes the latitude the morally uncommitted keep.
What it trades is ease of exception: the more central the moral self, the more a breach of it is a breach of the self.

## Drives

On the standing centrality of the moral self:
rides it -- lets being good govern conduct, holds the line where the self is at stake, works to stay consistent with the moral person it means to be -- or lets it sit peripheral, morality one consideration among many, easily outweighed.
The centrality holds underneath either way, and it is expressed inwardly, outwardly, in both, or in neither: as [internalized](position_internalized.md), [symbolic](position_symbolic.md), [custodian](position_custodian.md), or [peripheral](position_peripheral.md). The firing of a virtue as a moment's event is the virtue engine's; the reading of another's conduct against a moral standard is the moral-judgment engine's; the acute self-conscious emotions of guilt, shame, and pride are the self-conscious composite's; the general self-concept is the identity engine's. This engine owns how central being moral is to who the persona is.
