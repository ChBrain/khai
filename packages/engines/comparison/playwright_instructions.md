# Instructions: Comparison

How a Playwright wires the comparison engine into a play. You wire by linking the play's own content to the engine's files; the engine files stay untouched, they are canon. The Roadie plumbs the engine's law into Instructions Knowledge on deploy. Authoring guidance, not runtime content, and it does not go on tour.

## Human

- The human defines the comparison terrain: on what dimension the persona wants to know where they stand -- ability, opinion, wealth, progress -- and who is around to be measured against, above, below, or alongside, so there is a real self-evaluation to run against a real other.

## Agent

- From the play's own content, a persona links the form the moment runs (upward, downward, or similar) under its Projection. Let it show the self measured against others -- the look upward that informs or threatens, the look downward that enhances, the similar other chosen as the diagnostic yardstick -- and let it read as evaluative measuring, not as the emotion it provokes nor the standing itself. You link to the engine, you never edit it.

## Collaboration

- The pain at another's superiority is the envy engine's; the malicious pleasure at another's misfortune is the schadenfreude engine's; the persona's rank within an order is the status engine's; the resting global self-attitude is the self-esteem engine's; the catalog of comparison tilts held as static positions is the bias engine's. This engine supplies the evaluative measuring of the self against others.

## Knowledge

- Social comparison is the drive to evaluate one's own opinions and abilities by measuring against others, so the self is known relative to others where no objective standard is at hand. Upward is comparison with the better-off: the gap to a superior other informs and can inspire the reach to improve, but the same gap can threaten -- breeding inadequacy or envy -- when the domain matters and the higher mark seems out of reach; a reachable superior lifts, an unreachable one wounds. Downward is comparison with the worse-off: measuring against those below enhances the self by contrast, a defense drawn on most when the self is threatened, the worse-off other a reminder that things could be worse. Similar is the selection of the target: a persona compares most tellingly with those like themselves, because only a like other -- near on the relevant dimensions -- yields a diagnostic read on where they truly stand.

## System

- Do make the self relative: what the persona judges themselves to be is read off the gap to a chosen other, so the same fact reads well or badly by whom it is set beside -- let a strong peer deflate and a weak one flatter without any change in the persona.
- Do route by direction and target: upward is the look at the better-off (spur or wound), downward the look at the worse-off (enhancement under threat), similar the choice of a like other (the diagnostic yardstick) -- so name which the moment is.
- Do let the upward look cut both ways: a reachable superior inspires and instructs, an unreachable one threatens and diminishes -- so tie the effect to whether the persona believes the gap can be closed.
- Do delegate the pain at another's superiority to the envy engine, the rank itself to the status engine, and the settled self-attitude to the self-esteem engine. Do not edit the engine's files; wire only from the play's side.
