# Process: Upward

## Taxonomy

[Comparison](process_comparison.md), the better-off form.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: comparison

## Initiated by

The persona measures themselves against someone better off or more able -- richer, more skilled, further along -- and reads the self against that higher mark.
This is upward comparison: the self evaluated by looking at those above, the gap to the better a source of both information and threat.
Where the target stands is where the persona sees they do not yet.

## Direction

Toward a self read against a superior other, informing the reach upward or wounding by the shortfall.
Upward comparison cuts two ways: as information and inspiration, the better other showing what is possible and pulling the persona to improve toward it; and as threat, the same gap exposing a deficiency, breeding inadequacy, resentment, or envy when the domain matters and the distance is large.
The direction turns on the reading: taken as a model, the upward look lifts; taken as a verdict, it lowers -- the better other either a promise of what one might become or a measure of what one is not.

## Lever

The gap to a superior other, spent as either a spur to improve or a wound to the self.
Upward comparison runs on that gap as double-edged: because the target is above, the comparison always shows a shortfall, and whether the shortfall reads as a route or a rebuke depends on whether the persona feels the better other's level is attainable and shares their frame.
The lever is the attainability of the higher mark: a reachable superior inspires and instructs, an unreachable one threatens and deflates -- the same gap motivating or diminishing by whether the persona believes they can close it.

## Echo

The look upward shows what one is not yet, and either draws the persona toward it or presses them down.
It is why a mentor inspires and a rival stings, why seeing someone excel can light a fire or start a spiral, why the reachable model motivates where the unreachable one only wounds -- the better other read as promise or as reproach.
It is [downward](process_downward.md) and [similar](process_similar.md)'s counterpart, the look at those above where they are the look below and the choice of a like other. The pain that a large, threatening gap can breed is the envy engine's; upward owns the self-evaluation against a superior other and its double reading as spur or wound.
