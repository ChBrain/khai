# Process: Downward

## Taxonomy

[Comparison](process_comparison.md), the worse-off form.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: comparison

## Initiated by

The persona measures themselves against someone worse off or less able, and the gap the other way -- being ahead of them -- lifts the self.
This is downward comparison: the self enhanced by looking at those below, the persona feeling better by the contrast with the worse.
Where the target falls short is where the persona sees they do not.

## Direction

Toward a self enhanced by contrast with a worse-off other, a defense drawn on most under threat.
Downward comparison serves self-enhancement: measuring against those doing poorly reassures the persona of their own standing, and the move is invoked most when the self is threatened -- after a setback or a loss, the persona seeks a lower other to feel restored beside.
The direction is protective: where the upward look can wound, the downward look salves, the worse-off other a reminder that things could be worse and that the persona is, by this measure, doing well.

## Lever

The gap below to a worse-off other, spent as a lift to the threatened self.
Downward comparison runs on that gap as reassurance: because the target is below, the comparison shows the persona ahead, and this contrast is recruited to repair or protect self-regard, especially when some threat has lowered it.
The lever is the self-enhancing contrast: the worse-off other, without changing anything about the persona, raises how they feel about where they stand -- a comfort bought by measuring against those who have less.

## Echo

The look downward shows what one is spared, and lifts the self by the distance from the worse.
It is why misfortune loves company that is worse off, why we feel better hearing of those doing poorly, why a threatened person seeks someone lower to stand beside -- the worse-off other a balm to self-regard.
It is [upward](process_upward.md) and [similar](process_similar.md)'s counterpart, the look at those below where they are the look above and the choice of a like other. The malicious pleasure at another's misfortune is the schadenfreude engine's; downward owns the self-enhancing evaluation against a worse-off other, the comfort of the contrast.
