# Process: Similar

## Taxonomy

[Comparison](process_comparison.md), the target-selection form.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: comparison

## Initiated by

The persona, seeking to know where they truly stand, compares not with just anyone but with those like themselves -- the same age, training, or circumstance.
This is the similar-other principle: a persona compares most tellingly with those similar to them, because only a like other gives a diagnostic read on their own standing.
The choice of yardstick is the choice of a peer.

## Direction

Toward a comparison target near enough to make the reading meaningful.
The similar-other principle governs whom a persona selects: a comparison with someone very unlike them -- a novice against a master, a child against an adult -- says little about the self, so the persona seeks a target close on the relevant dimensions, whose standing actually bears on their own.
The direction is toward diagnosticity: the more the other resembles the persona in the ways that matter, the more the gap between them means, so meaningful self-evaluation pulls the persona to compare within their own class.

## Lever

The similarity of the chosen other, spent as the diagnosticity of the comparison.
The similar form runs on relevance: because a comparison informs the self only to the degree the other is a fair standard, similarity on the pertinent attributes is what makes the reading valid, and the persona selects targets accordingly -- peers, not extremes.
The lever is the fair standard: a like other converts a raw difference into information about the self, where a mismatched one yields a number that cannot be trusted, so the drive to know oneself is a drive to compare with one's own kind.

## Echo

The peer is the true mirror, and it is against one's own kind that one's standing is read.
It is why the runner measures against runners of their level, why "compared to someone like me" is the honest comparison, why an unlike target -- far above or far below one's class -- tells the self little -- the like other the only diagnostic yardstick.
It is [upward](process_upward.md) and [downward](process_downward.md)'s ground, the choice of target where they are the looks above and below. The persona's rank within an order is the status engine's, and the assumption that others are more like oneself than they are is the bias engine's; similar owns the selection of a like other as the diagnostic standard for self-evaluation.
