# Process: Testimony

## Taxonomy

[Restoration](process_restoration.md), the stance this mend takes.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: restoration

## Initiated by

The mend decides the break is an event to be honoured, not hidden, and makes the repair visible.
Testimony starts where the seam is declared rather than erased: kintsugi's crack traced in gold, the boro cloth patched in contrasting indigo, the darn worked as a feature -- the mend shown, and the object's history of damage carried openly on its face.
Its target is not the counterfactual whole but the true, marked thing.

## Direction

Onto the Apparent, adding the mend as a new stratum the object wears.
The gold runs the fracture, the patch sits proud, the join is the ornament; the object shows both what it was and what befell it, the break become a line in its biography rather than a blemish erased from it.
Testimony moves the object forward into a marked state it did not have before, and often values it more for the mark.

## Lever

The authority of the object's material history against the appeal of the unblemished.
The carrying force is the sense that the object's true here-and-now includes its wounds -- Benjamin's aura, the authenticity that lives in what the thing has been through; the resistance is the pull toward the flawless and the new, which testimony refuses on principle.
A production holds this lever by letting the mended object be worth more than the unbroken one -- the break honoured as fate accepted, wabi-sabi's beauty in the imperfect and impermanent -- so the repair adds rather than subtracts.

## Echo

The object marked and prized for the mark.
It shows the gilded seam, the honest patch, the wound made worth, and carries beneath it the claim the stance makes -- that a thing's breaks are part of what it is, and a repair that adds a stratum is truer than one that cancels a chapter.
Testimony is dated from the mend that declared itself, and it is restoration's twist: where reversion hides the break as loss, testimony shows it as value -- rescue that enriches rather than erases.
