# Process: Restoration

## Taxonomy

Mending.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: restoration

## Initiated by

An object is broken, worn, or damaged, and a hand moves to mend it.
Restoration starts the moment the hand reaches in -- and with it comes the question the whole field turns on: how much of the thing may be renewed before it is no longer that thing, and should the mend vanish or declare itself.
The reach is never neutral; every mend is already a ruling on what the object is.

## Direction

Onto the object's Apparent, deciding what the mend makes the object show.
Five stances carry the intervention:
[Reversion](process_reversion.md) erases the break, [Testimony](process_testimony.md) gilds it, [Stabilization](process_stabilization.md) arrests it and touches no more, [Continuation](process_continuation.md) keeps the thing working through part-turnover, and [Fidelity](process_fidelity.md) serves the maker's hand.
Each shows a different answer to the same broken object, and beneath them all runs the constitution question -- the ship whose every plank is replaced.

## Lever

The object's aesthetic instance against its historical instance.
The carrying force is the will to make the object whole again -- its form, its function, its use; the resistance is everything that will cost -- the marks of the years, the original fabric, the honest record of the break, the maker's own hand.
Brandi's rule holds the two: a mend may commit neither a forgery of the aesthetic (inventing what was lost, passing new for old) nor an erasure of the historical (wiping the passage of time for a false as-new), and every stance is a ruling on where between them to stand.

## Echo

The object mended, and its identity resolved one way.
The mend dates the object and answers, for now, whether it is still the same thing -- reverted to an unbroken original, testified with its wound made worth, stabilized in its damage, continued in use though its matter has turned over, or held faithful to the hand that made it.
Restoration is the object's own reclamation, at the scale of the thing rather than the place; and whether the reach saved the object or replaced it is read in what the Apparent now shows, and in how much of the original still answers to the name.
