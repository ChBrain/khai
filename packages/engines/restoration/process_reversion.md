# Process: Reversion

## Taxonomy

[Restoration](process_restoration.md), the stance this mend takes.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: restoration

## Initiated by

The mend decides the break is a deviation to be cancelled, and returns the object toward its unbroken original.
Reversion starts from the ideal of the invisible repair: the fill, the inpaint, the reweave, the re-glaze that leaves no seam, so the object reads as it did before it was ever hurt.
Its target is the counterfactual whole -- the thing as if the damage had not happened.

## Direction

Onto the Apparent, erasing the break from view.
The loss is filled and toned to its neighbours, the crack closed, the chip made good, the tear rewoven, until the eye passes over the mend without catching -- the object returned to a prior, undamaged state.
Reversion moves the object back toward its origin, and the more complete the return, the less of the damage's record remains to be seen.

## Lever

The wholeness recovered against the honesty of the object's history.
The carrying force is the wish for the intact object -- newness-value, the appeal of completeness; the resistance is what the erasure hides, the passage of time wiped and, at the limit, the line where an invisible mend becomes an invented one.
Reversion done well recovers the image; reversion pressed too far invents it, and the seamless fill that cannot be told from the original has crossed from repair into forgery.

## Echo

The object made whole again, its break unseen.
It shows a surface with no visible wound, and carries beneath it the wager the stance makes -- that the object's worth is its intact image, and the damage a thing best forgotten.
Reversion is dated from the mend that vanished, and it is the stance that answers the break by cancelling it -- the risk of which is that, pressed to invisibility, it erases the very authenticity it meant to restore.
