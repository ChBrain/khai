# Process: Stabilization

## Taxonomy

[Restoration](process_restoration.md), the stance this mend takes.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: restoration

## Initiated by

The mend decides to arrest the damage and do no more, leaving the object as it is found.
Stabilization starts from the minimal-intervention ethic: consolidate the flaking, halt the active corrosion, house the object against further harm, and touch nothing that is not failing -- the remedial act that stops the loss without restoring the look.
Its target is not a prior state but a held present.

## Direction

Onto the Apparent, holding the object exactly at the condition found.
The rot is stopped and the surface left, the tear backed and not rewoven, the corrosion neutralised and the patina kept; new work, where it must be done, is honest and reversible and does not pretend to be old.
Stabilization moves the object from deteriorating to held, and no further -- the damage arrested and left legible.

## Lever

The trust in the object as-found against the impulse to make it whole or beautiful.
The carrying force is the belief that the least intervention is the truest -- that every added touch risks the object and forecloses a better-informed later hand; the resistance is the pull to fill, to tidy, to return the thing to a prime it has left.
Do the minimum and the object endures with its history intact; reach further and it is reversion or continuation, not stabilization -- the stance is defined by the restraint.

## Echo

The object stopped from failing, its damage kept.
It shows the arrested wound -- the stabilized crack, the halted corrosion, the surface preserved as found -- and carries beneath it the ethic the stance keeps: that the object is not ours to complete, only ours to keep from getting worse.
Stabilization is dated from the day the loss was stopped, and it is the stance of humility -- the conservator's least hand, keeping faith with the object exactly as time has left it.
