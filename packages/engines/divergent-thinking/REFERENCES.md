# Divergent thinking: Reference

## Line of Work

Divergent thinking as a **process** engine: the deliberate generation of many varied, novel
ideas from one open-ended prompt -- the generative act creative cognition turns on, taken
across the four dimensions along which an ideational pool is read. It wires on a persona at
Projection, the persona shown by what it produces, because a creative persona is read by
what its generating throws up. The domain does not model the sudden restructuring of one
stuck problem, which is the insight engine's aha; nor the structure-mapping transfer of one
source onto one target, which is the analogy engine's; nor the convergent choosing that
selects the useful ideas from the pool, which is the decision engine's. It models the
fan-out itself -- the many novel responses from the one prompt, and how that pool is scored.

The spine is Guilford's distinction between divergent and convergent production and
Torrance's operationalizing of it. Guilford, in his 1950 address to the American
Psychological Association and then in the Structure of Intellect model, set divergent
production -- generating many responses where the task has no single correct answer -- apart
from the convergent production the standard intelligence test measures, and made it the core
of creative aptitude; his Alternate Uses Task (name every use for a common object) became the
canonical probe. Torrance built the standard measure, the Torrance Tests of Creative
Thinking, scoring an open response not for correctness but along four dimensions: fluency
(the number of ideas), flexibility (the number of categories), originality (the statistical
rarity), and elaboration (the detail worked out). Runco carried the theory forward: divergent
thinking is an estimate of creative potential rather than creativity itself, its originality
found in the later, rarer reaches of a persona's ideational pool, and its output only half of
creativity -- the novel and the many, which a convergent valuation of usefulness must still
gate. So the engine gives divergent thinking Guilford's four dimensions as four equal facets,
carrying no forced twist -- one act of generating is scored on all four at once.

## Restrictions

What the engine refuses to model, and to whom it delegates.

- **The sudden restructuring of one stuck problem (insight)**: The engine models deliberate
  generation of many ideas, not the discontinuous aha. The sudden reorganization of a single
  problem at impasse, the solution arriving whole with no warmth ramp, is the insight engine's;
  divergent thinking is the effortful fanning-out across many ideas, not the leap on one.
  Insight restructures one frame; divergent thinking populates a field. A creative act may use
  both, but they are different events.
- **The structure-mapping of one source onto one target (analogy)**: The engine models the
  many-from-one, not the transfer between two. Carrying the relational structure of one domain
  onto another -- the source mapped to the target -- is the analogy engine's; divergent
  thinking generates a pool, and analogy may be one move within it, but the mapping of a single
  source is not the generation of the many.
- **The convergent choosing of the good ideas (decision)**: The engine models the novel and
  the many, not the selection among them. Picking the useful, workable, or best idea out of the
  pool -- the convergent narrowing to an answer -- is the decision engine's; divergent thinking
  supplies the candidates, and creativity requires that novel be paired with useful, but the
  usefulness is settled by the choosing that follows, not by the generating. Divergent thinking
  fans out; decision narrows in.
- **The trait, state, and drive around the generating (temperament, flow, curiosity)**: The
  engine models the generative act, not the person or condition that hosts it. The openness of
  disposition that makes a persona ideationally fertile is a trait (temperament, and the charted
  `openness`); the absorbed state generation can run inside is flow's; the appetite that sent the
  persona to the prompt is curiosity's. Divergent thinking is the producing itself, whoever does
  it and in whatever state.
- **The dimension itself is not engine content**: The engine declares the four dimensions
  exist; which one a given persona shows lives on that persona, linked under its Projection
  chapter. The engine refuses to fix a persona's generating for it.

- **[originality](process_originality.md)** (process, dimension): the statistical rarity of the
  ideas, their distance from the common answers. Anchored by Torrance (originality scoring) and
  Runco (originality in the later reaches of the pool).
- **[elaboration](process_elaboration.md)** (process, dimension): the detail and development the
  ideas are worked out with, the notion carried toward a design. Anchored by Torrance (elaboration
  scoring).
