# Process: Elaboration

## Taxonomy

[Divergent thinking](process_divergent-thinking.md), the dimension of the detail the ideas are worked out with.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: divergent-thinking

## Initiated by

An idea is had, and it is filled in.
Elaboration starts wherever a persona's generating is read for the detail it develops -- not how many ideas or how rare, but how far each is carried past the bare statement, the additions and specifics and working-out that turn a one-line notion into a fleshed thing.
It is the dimension of development: the sketch taken toward a design, the "a chair, but --" and everything that follows the but, the amount of elaborating detail a persona adds to an idea once it has one.

## Direction

Deeper into one idea, adding detail and development.
Elaboration works an idea out rather than reaching for the next -- the persona building on the response it has, specifying, extending, ornamenting, answering the questions the bare idea leaves open, carrying it from notion toward plan.
It moves the pool from stated to developed: where fluency and flexibility and originality all reach outward for more and different and rarer ideas, elaboration reaches inward and downward into a single idea, and a persona strong here produces fewer ideas but each one further along, where a persona weak here lists many that stay one line each.

## Lever

The impulse to develop against the pull to move on.
The carrying force is the persona's readiness to stay with an idea and build it out -- to find the detail latent in it, to specify and extend rather than abandon it for the next; the resistance is the pull of generation itself toward the next idea, the mind that lists and lists but never stops to work any one out, and the effort a developed idea costs against the ease of a bare one.
Stay and build, and the ideas gain body and reach; rush on, and the pool is a heap of undeveloped starts, none carried far enough to be usable.

## Echo

The generating read as its development.
Elaboration leaves the pool measured by its worked-out detail -- how far the ideas are carried past their bare statement, the body they are given -- and hands that development to the reading of the persona as one who not only has ideas but builds them.
It is the dimension that completes the four: fluency the count, flexibility the range, originality the rarity, and elaboration the depth of working-out -- one act of generating scored on all four at once, and a persona may be high on any and low on the rest.
Elaboration is read from the detail, the persona known by how fully it works its ideas out once it has them.
