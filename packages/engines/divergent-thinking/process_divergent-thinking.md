# Process: Divergent thinking

## Taxonomy

Creative cognition, the generative act.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: divergent-thinking

## Initiated by

A persona meets a prompt with no single answer and begins to generate.
The trigger is an open, ill-defined task -- name every use for a brick, every consequence of a world without sleep, every way this could be done -- the kind of problem Guilford set against the convergent test-item that has one right key. What enters the process is not a puzzle to be solved but a field to be filled: a prompt that rewards the many over the correct, and a persona willing to run past the first idea into the pool of others behind it.
This is the ground the generative act needs: not a stuck problem waiting to give, but an open one waiting to be populated.

## Direction

Outward, from one prompt into many ideas.
Divergent thinking fans out where convergent thinking narrows in: it produces a pool of responses rather than homing on the answer, and its motion is the widening branch, the one prompt throwing up idea after idea, each opening room for the next. Guilford named it divergent production and set it at the heart of creative aptitude; Torrance built the standard measure of it, scoring not whether an answer is right but how many, how varied, how rare, and how worked-out the answers are.
The direction is proliferation held just short of nonsense: the many, but the many that still bear on the prompt.

## Lever

The pull to keep generating against the pull to stop at the first good idea.
Whether the pool grows turns on the persona pushing past early, obvious, high-frequency responses -- which come first and come to everyone -- into the later, rarer reaches of its ideational pool, where originality lives (Runco). The resistance is fixation and satisficing: the mind that takes its first workable idea and quits, or that stays inside one category mining variations, never gets to the far ideas. The freeing is suspending judgment -- generating now, choosing later -- so the evaluation that would kill an odd idea early is held off until the pool is full.
The lever is not a better single idea but a refusal to stop at one; keep the gate to judgment shut and the pool deepens, open it early and generation collapses back into choosing.

## Echo

The pool is read along four dimensions, Guilford's classic scoring of divergent production: [fluency](process_fluency.md), the sheer number of ideas; [flexibility](process_flexibility.md), the spread across categories; [originality](process_originality.md), the statistical rarity of the ideas; and [elaboration](process_elaboration.md), the detail they are worked out with.
The four are not stages and not a sequence; one act of generating is scored on all four at once, and a persona can be high on some and low on others -- fluent but unoriginal, original but sparse.
What this engine owns is the generation itself, the novel-and-many. It is not the sudden restructuring of one stuck problem, which is the insight engine's aha; not the structure-mapping of one source onto one target, which is analogy's; and not the convergent choosing that selects the useful ideas from the pool, which is decision's. Divergent thinking supplies the novel and the many; the useful is settled by the choosing that comes after.
