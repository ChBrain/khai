# Process: Fluency

## Taxonomy

[Divergent thinking](process_divergent-thinking.md), the dimension of the sheer number of ideas.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: divergent-thinking

## Initiated by

The prompt is met, and ideas come in quantity.
Fluency starts wherever a persona's generating is read for its raw count -- how many responses it throws up, the length of the list, the ideas per minute, the many against the few.
It is the base dimension of divergence: before variety or rarity or detail, the plain productivity of the pool, the fluent mind that fills the page where a blocked one stalls at two or three.

## Direction

Up in number, the pool growing idea by idea.
Fluency piles response on response -- not yet asking whether they differ or how rare they are, only that they keep coming, the persona naming a tenth use for the brick where most stop at three.
It moves the generating toward volume: quantity first, on the working assumption that a large pool is the ground the good ideas are later found in -- the count that gives originality and elaboration something to be scored against, since the rare and the worked-out are drawn from the many, not from the first few.

## Lever

The flow of ideas against the early stall.
The carrying force is the persona's readiness to keep producing -- to run past the first workable answer into the ones behind it, treating the task as fill-the-field and not find-the-answer; the resistance is the quick stop, the mind that offers two ideas and is done, blocked by fixation, by self-censoring each idea as it forms, or by taking the first good-enough response as sufficient.
Suspend judgment and the count climbs; judge each idea as it arrives and the flow chokes, the pool staying shallow.

## Echo

The generating read as its quantity.
Fluency leaves the pool measured by its size -- the many ideas the persona produced, the raw count the other dimensions are read against -- and hands that volume to the flexibility, originality, and elaboration scored over it.
It is the most basic and most enabling dimension: high fluency does not guarantee good ideas, but low fluency starves the pool, and the rare far idea is rarely reached by a mind that stopped at the near ones.
Fluency is read from the count, the persona known by how many ideas its generating throws up.
