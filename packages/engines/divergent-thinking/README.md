# Divergent-thinking

Divergent thinking as process: the deliberate generation of many varied, novel ideas from one open prompt -- the sheer count of ideas (fluency), the spread across categories (flexibility), the statistical rarity of the ideas (originality), and their working-out into detail (elaboration) -- the generative act creativity turns on, apart from the sudden aha and the convergent choosing that selects among what it makes.
