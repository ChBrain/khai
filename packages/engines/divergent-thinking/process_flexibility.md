# Process: Flexibility

## Taxonomy

[Divergent thinking](process_divergent-thinking.md), the dimension of the spread across categories.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: divergent-thinking

## Initiated by

The ideas come, and they come in different kinds.
Flexibility starts wherever a persona's generating is read for its variety -- not how many ideas but how many categories of idea, the number of distinct frames the pool ranges over, the jumps from one kind of answer to another.
It is the dimension that tells a broad pool from a deep rut: twenty uses for a brick that are all "build a wall, build a house, build a path" score low flexibility however high the count; twenty that range from building to weapon to paperweight to heat-store to art score high.

## Direction

Sideways, across frames, breaking out of the vein being mined.
Flexibility spreads the generating over categories -- the persona shifting the kind of answer, not just adding another of the same, leaving the obvious frame for a distant one, reading the prompt now this way and now that.
It moves the pool from deep to broad: where fluency asks only for more, flexibility asks for different, and the two can diverge -- a persona can be fluent within one category, pouring out variations on a single theme, and still be inflexible, never making the jump that opens a whole new region of ideas.

## Lever

The readiness to switch frames against the pull to stay in one.
The carrying force is the persona's willingness to abandon a productive vein and look from a wholly different angle -- to treat the brick as heat, as mass, as colour, as tool, not only as building-block; the resistance is perseveration and set, the mind that finds one category and mines it, each new idea a minor variant of the last, fluent but stuck in a groove.
Break the set and the pool ranges wide; hold it and the pool, however large, stays clustered in one corner of the possible.

## Echo

The generating read as its range.
Flexibility leaves the pool measured by its spread -- the number of different categories the ideas fall into, the breadth the variety is scored by -- and hands that range to a reading of how freely the persona moves between frames.
It is the dimension that keeps fluency honest: a large pool of near-identical ideas is fluent but narrow, and it is flexibility that catches the difference, rewarding the jump between kinds over the pile within one.
Flexibility is read from the categories, the persona known by how many different frames its generating ranges across.
