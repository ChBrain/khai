# Instructions: Divergent thinking

How a Playwright wires the divergent-thinking engine into a play. You wire by linking the play's own content to the engine's files; the engine files stay untouched, they are canon. The Roadie plumbs the engine's law into Instructions Knowledge on deploy. Authoring guidance, not runtime content, and it does not go on tour.

## Human

- The human sets the open prompt and what the persona generates against it: the ill-defined task with no single answer, and whether the persona's generating is a quiet background trait or a live act in the play -- the mind filling the field with ideas.

## Agent

- From the play's own content, a persona whose generating is an actor links the dimension its divergence turns on under Projection: fluency, the sheer number of ideas it throws up; flexibility, the spread of its ideas across categories; originality, the statistical rarity of the ideas; or elaboration, the detail it works them out with. Play the divergent thinking as the persona producing a pool of many varied ideas from one prompt -- shown in what it generates, present, not narrated -- and keep judgment suspended: the generating is the many and the novel, and the choosing of which idea is good comes after. Keep it the generation, not the sudden aha on one stuck problem (that is insight), the mapping of one source onto one target (analogy), or the selection of the best idea (decision). You link to the engine, you never edit it.

## Collaboration

- The sudden restructuring of one problem at impasse is the insight engine's aha; the structure-mapping of one source onto one target is analogy's; the convergent choosing of the useful idea from the pool is decision's; the trait that makes a persona fertile is temperament's, the absorbed state is flow's, and the appetite that sent it to the prompt is curiosity's. Divergent thinking is the generative core of the creativity cluster -- the producing of the novel and the many. Whether the generating rings true to the persona is the team's review.

## Knowledge

- Divergent thinking is the deliberate generation of many varied, novel ideas from one open-ended prompt: the generative act creative cognition turns on, set apart from convergent production by Guilford (the Alternate Uses Task; divergent vs convergent) and measured by Torrance along four dimensions. It wires on a persona at Projection, and it is the fan-out, the many from the one. Four dimensions carry it -- fluency is the sheer number of ideas, the raw count of the pool; flexibility is the spread across categories, the number of different kinds of idea and the jumps between frames; originality is the statistical rarity of the ideas, found in the later reaches of the pool once the common answers are past (Runco); elaboration is the detail the ideas are worked out with, the notion carried toward a design. One act of generating is scored on all four at once, and a persona can be high on some and low on others -- fluent but unoriginal, original but sparse. Read a persona by the dimension its generating turns on, and remember that divergent thinking supplies only the novel and the many; the useful is settled by the convergent choosing that follows, so novel-and-useful is a pair split across two engines.

## System

- Do stage the open prompt first -- the task with no single answer -- so the generating reads as a fan-out into many ideas, not a search for the one right one; suspend judgment while the pool fills.
- Do route through the dimension that fits: fluency for the sheer count, flexibility for the range of kinds, originality for the rare and unexpected, elaboration for the worked-out detail; one act may be read on more than one.
- Do let originality come late: the obvious ideas arrive first and to everyone, and the rare ones are reached only by generating past them -- a persona that stops at its first good idea never reaches its original ones.
- Do not play the sudden aha on a stuck problem as divergent thinking; that is insight. Do not play the mapping of one source onto one target as generation; that is analogy. Do not play the choosing of the best idea as divergent thinking; that is decision, and the usefulness gate is convergent. Do not edit the engine's files; wire only from the play's side.
