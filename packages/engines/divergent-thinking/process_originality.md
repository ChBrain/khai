# Process: Originality

## Taxonomy

[Divergent thinking](process_divergent-thinking.md), the dimension of the statistical rarity of the ideas.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: divergent-thinking

## Initiated by

The ideas come, and some are ones few others would give.
Originality starts wherever a persona's generating is read for the rarity of its ideas -- how far they sit from the obvious and common, how unusual against the pool everyone else produces, the answers that are the persona's own and not the crowd's.
It is the dimension creativity most prizes: not how many ideas or how varied, but how novel -- the response that is statistically infrequent, the one that would not appear on most people's lists.

## Direction

Away from the common, toward the rare and unexpected.
Originality pushes the generating past the high-frequency answers -- the ones that come first and come to everyone -- into the low-frequency reaches where the surprising ideas live; the persona reaching the uncommon use, the odd consequence, the answer that makes a reader pause.
It moves the pool from expected to novel, and its ideas come late: the obvious responses are produced first and quickly, and originality is found further down the list (Runco), in the ideas a persona reaches only by generating past the ones that came easily.

## Lever

The reach for the rare against the gravity of the common.
The carrying force is the persona's push into the infrequent -- suspending the pull toward the safe and the usual, staying in the generating long enough to get past the obvious and into the strange; the resistance is the commonness of first ideas, the high-frequency responses that crowd in early and satisfy, and the self-censor that reads an odd idea as a wrong one and drops it.
Reach past the common and the pool gains its rare ideas; stop at the first answers, or judge the strange ones out as they form, and the pool stays wholly ordinary however large.

## Echo

The generating read as its novelty.
Originality leaves the pool measured by its rarity -- the ideas that few others would give, the distance from the obvious the pool reaches -- and hands that novelty to the reading of the persona as a generator of the new.
It is the dimension nearest to what creativity means, and the one most dependent on the others: originality is drawn from a pool made large by fluency and broad by flexibility, and the rare idea is still only half of creativity -- novel, but not yet shown to be useful, which the convergent choosing that follows must settle.
Originality is read from the rarity, the persona known by how far its ideas sit from the answers everyone gives.
