# Process: Dereliction

## Taxonomy

Withdrawal.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: dereliction

## Initiated by

The authority responsible for a place stops maintaining it.
Dereliction starts not when the roof falls but when the keeping stops -- the owner who defers, the institution that departs, the capital that is redlined away -- and the place is left to the decay that upkeep held off.
Unlike the decay it unleashes, dereliction is not a thing matter does; it is a thing a person, or a system, chooses not to do.

## Direction

Onto the place's Offers, which begin to lapse.
Four forms carry the withdrawal:
[Disrepair](process_disrepair.md) is care reduced while the place is still held, [Abandonment](process_abandonment.md) is care ended and the place left, [Disinvestment](process_disinvestment.md) is care pulled from a whole area at once, and [Ruin](process_ruin.md) is the place given fully over.
What the place freely offered -- its use, its shelter, its function -- is withdrawn form by form, until only unsanctioned offers remain.

## Lever

The place's need for care against the authority's willingness to give it.
The carrying force is the withdrawal -- the deferral, the departure, the flight of capital; the resistance is the maintenance, the tenancy, the investment that would keep the place in its use.
Remove the care and the place slides; restore the care, or the capital, and the slide arrests -- for the withdrawal, unlike decay, is a decision, and a decision can be reversed while the fabric still stands.

## Echo

The place given over, and what fills the vacuum it leaves.
Dereliction hands the place onward: to decay, which now runs unchecked in the fabric; to the biological reclaiming that follows once human use withdraws, which is succession's; to the capital re-entry that later profits from the depressed value, which is gentrification's, re-entering through the rent-gap the disinvestment opened; and to the deliberate arrest, which is restoration's.
The withdrawal is a human act, and the place is dated from the day the keeping stopped, not the day the fabric failed.
