# Process: Disrepair

## Taxonomy

[Dereliction](process_dereliction.md), the form this withdrawal takes.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: dereliction

## Initiated by

The party responsible for a place reduces its care below what the place needs, while still holding it.
Disrepair starts with the deferred repair -- the leak left, the paint gone, the boiler nursed one more winter -- because keeping the place up costs more than the keeper will spend on it now.
The place is still in use, still nominally kept; it is simply no longer maintained.

## Direction

Onto the Offers, quietly, degrading them.
The place still offers its use, but offers it worse: the lift that works when it feels like it, the heat that is unreliable, the stair with the loose tread, the room that can no longer be let.
Disrepair does not close the place; it lets each thing it offers slip a grade, so the use continues on a slope.

## Lever

The cost of upkeep against the willingness to bear it.
The carrying force is the economy of deferral -- not fixing is cheaper today; the resistance is the knowledge that it is dearer tomorrow, the small failure that becomes the large one left long enough.
Spend again and disrepair reverses with the fabric still sound; keep deferring and the small failures compound past the point where spending can still catch them.

## Echo

The place worn but working, on a slope.
It shows the accumulation of small untended failures -- the patched, the propped, the making-do -- and carries beneath it the choice not yet forced: the early, reversible phase of the withdrawal.
Disrepair is dated from the first deferral, and it is the seam where a decision to spend again arrests the slide, or a decision to stop tips the place toward abandonment.
