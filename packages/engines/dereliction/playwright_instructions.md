# Instructions: Dereliction

How a Playwright wires the dereliction engine into a play. You wire by linking the
play's own content to the engine's files; the engine files stay untouched, they
are canon. The Roadie plumbs the engine's law into Instructions Knowledge on
deploy. Authoring guidance, not runtime content, and it does not go on tour.

## Human

- The human sets which places in the world are being let go, by whom, and how far the withdrawal has run -- from a deferred repair to a district written off to a place given fully over.

## Agent

- From the play's own content, a declining place links under Offers the form its dereliction takes: care reduced while still held (disrepair), care ended and the place left (abandonment), care pulled from a whole area at once (disinvestment), or the place given fully over to a second disordered life (ruin). Play it as the place enacting the lapse of what it offered -- the use degraded, the doors sealed, the district thinned, the offers of the working place traded for the unsanctioned offers of the ruin -- and keep it a human withdrawal, a choice reversible by a choice, never a persona's feeling mirrored in the stones. Hand the material rot to decay, the returning wild to succession, the capital re-entry to gentrification, and the arrest to restoration. You link to the engine, you never edit it.

## Collaboration

- The material entropy the withdrawal unleashes is decay's; the biology that reclaims the emptied place is succession's; the capital that re-enters the rent-gap and displaces is gentrification's; the intervention that arrests or keeps the ruin is restoration's. Ruin is the shared seam with restoration: whether the second life is cleared or preserved. Whether the form rings true for the place and its keeper is the team's review.

## Knowledge

- Dereliction is the decline a place undergoes when the authority responsible for it withdraws its care: decline is done, not merely suffered -- a place falls because someone with the power to keep it chooses not to (Beauregard's produced decline). Four forms carry the withdrawal, borne as the place's offers lapse. Disrepair is care reduced while the place is still held, the deferred repair, the reversible early phase. Abandonment is care ended, the place vacated and boarded, the offers withdrawn wholesale. Disinvestment is care pulled from a whole area at once, redlining and planned shrinkage, the district declined together and the rent-gap opened. Ruin is the place given fully over, the sanctioned offers gone and unsanctioned ones opened, the second disordered life Edensor prized as excess and not only loss -- dereliction's own age-value. Read a place by the form its decline takes.

## System

- Do give a declining place its form under Offers; do play it as the place enacting the lapse of what it offered; do keep the reading social -- a withdrawal of care, a choice, reversible while the fabric still stands.
- Do read ruin as excess, not only loss: the given-over place is full of memory and of what comes after, not merely empty -- the sibling of decay's patina, and the seam handed to restoration.
- Do not model the material entropy (that is decay), the returning wild (that is succession), the capital re-entry (that is gentrification), or the arrest (that is restoration). Do not make the derelict place mirror a persona's ruin (that is the play's pitch). Do not edit the engine's files; wire only from the play's side.
