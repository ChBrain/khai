# Process: Ruin

## Taxonomy

[Dereliction](process_dereliction.md), the form this withdrawal takes.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: dereliction

## Initiated by

The withdrawal is complete, and time enough has passed that the place is no longer kept in any sense.
Ruin starts where abandonment ends and the years take over: the roof gone, the place open to the sky and the weather and the trespasser, its former order dissolved.
But ruin is not emptiness -- it is the given-over place come into a second, disordered life.

## Direction

The sanctioned Offers gone entirely, and unsanctioned ones opened in their place.
Where the working place offered its single purpose, the ruin offers many unlicensed ones: the trespass, the exploration, the shelter, the canvas, the shortcut, the wild -- the alternative uses a place freed from its ordering comes to hold.
Ruin moves the place from one offer strictly given to a hundred offers loosely taken.

## Lever

The place's lost order against the new life that fills it.
The carrying force is the removal of the ordering that told the place what it was for; the resistance is the memory of that order -- the machines still bolted down, the signage, the worn paths -- which the ruin keeps even as it exceeds.
The ruin is not less than the working place but other than it: emptied of purpose and filled with meaning, the thing Edensor prized as excess rather than mourned as loss.

## Echo

The place returned, thick with what it was and open to what reclaims it.
It shows the roofless hall, the tree in the floor, the graffiti over the machine, and carries beneath it the doubled life -- the memory of the work and the disordered present grown over it.
Ruin is dereliction's own age-value, the given-over read as excess and not only loss, the sibling of decay's patina; and it hands the fabric to decay running free, to the biological reclaiming of succession, and to restoration -- which must decide whether this second life is a thing to arrest and clear, or a thing to keep.
