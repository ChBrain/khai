# Process: Abandonment

## Taxonomy

[Dereliction](process_dereliction.md), the form this withdrawal takes.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: dereliction

## Initiated by

The party responsible for a place leaves it entirely.
Abandonment starts when the keeper departs -- the factory shuttered, the house vacated and boarded, the branch closed, the block written off -- and the place is no longer even nominally held.
It is the withdrawal completed: not care reduced but care ended.

## Direction

Onto the Offers, wholesale, withdrawing them.
The doors are locked, the utilities cut, the windows boarded; the place is closed to the use it was made for, and offers nothing sanctioned any longer.
Where disrepair let the offers slip, abandonment takes them all at once, and the place stands sealed against its own purpose.

## Lever

The value of staying against the value of leaving.
The carrying force is the calculation that the place is worth less than the cost of keeping it -- the rent uncollectable, the repair unrecoverable, the use gone; the resistance is whatever tie, obligation, or return would hold the keeper in place.
When leaving pencils out over staying, the place is let go -- and the vacancy, unlike disrepair, is not a slope but a cliff already gone over.

## Echo

The vacant place, boarded and dark, its purpose ended.
It shows the sealed doors and the blind windows and carries the vacuum beneath -- a place with no keeper, waiting on whatever reclaims it.
Abandonment is dated from the day the keeper walked, and it opens the place to the ruin that time will make of it and to whatever fills a vacuum: the trespasser, the squatter, the biology, or the speculator watching the rent-gap widen.
