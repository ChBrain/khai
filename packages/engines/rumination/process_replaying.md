# Process: Replaying

## Taxonomy

[Rumination](process_rumination.md), the rewind form.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: rumination

## Initiated by

The persona runs a specific event through their mind again and again -- the argument, the slight, the mistake, the moment it went wrong -- rewinding and re-watching what cannot be changed.
This is replaying: rumination fixed on a particular episode, the mental rerun that alters nothing but the depth of the groove.
The scene will not close, so the mind keeps reopening it.

## Direction

Toward the unresolved episode, returned to and re-lived.
Replaying loops a concrete event tied to a hurt or an unmet goal: the persona rehearses what was said and what they should have said, relives the failure or the wound, plays the tape forward to the same bad end each time -- a recurrence driven by the event's unfinished business, its discrepancy still open.
The direction is a return to the scene: not toward its resolution but toward its repetition, the same footage run until it wears a channel.

## Lever

The unclosed episode, spent as recurrence.
Replaying runs on the mind's pull toward the unresolved: an event that leaves a goal blocked or a wrong unrighted keeps recurring to attention precisely because it is not settled, and each rerun re-presents the distress without discharging it, the loop sustained by the very incompleteness it fails to complete.
The lever is the open loop: because the episode remains unfinished in the persona's account of it, it recurs unbidden, and the recurring keeps it raw.

## Echo

The event stays vivid and unhealed, sharpened by each rerun rather than dulled.
It is the rumination of the specific -- the reason a single harsh remark can be relived a hundred times, the reason a mistake loops through the sleepless night, the reason some hurts sharpen with retelling instead of fading -- the replay keeping the wound open by reopening it.
It is [brooding](process_brooding.md) anchored to an event rather than the self, and the raw material a [reflection](process_reflection.md) could work through if it turned from rewinding to resolving. The recurrent pull of a blocked goal is the goal engine's territory; this owns the repetitive re-living of the unresolved episode itself.
