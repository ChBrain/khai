# Process: Brooding

## Taxonomy

[Rumination](process_rumination.md), the moody form.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: rumination

## Initiated by

The persona dwells moodily on how bad they feel and what it means about them -- the why-do-I-always, the why-me, the passive comparison of where they are to where they think they should be.
This is brooding: the maladaptive pole of rumination, gloom pondered without progress.
It asks after the distress endlessly and answers only with more of it.

## Direction

Toward the self measured against an unmet standard, and found wanting.
Brooding fixes on the gap between the persona's state and a standard they have not reached -- why am I not over this, why does this keep happening to me, what is wrong with me that I feel this way -- and turns that gap over without any move to close it, so the comparison only confirms the deficit.
The direction is a sinking inward: not toward what might be done but toward what the feeling proves, the self indicted by its own distress.

## Lever

The passive, evaluative comparison to a standard, spent as self-reproach.
Brooding runs on judging rather than solving: it holds the negative state against a benchmark and dwells on the shortfall, an abstract why-focused processing that generates verdicts about the self instead of steps for the situation, and each verdict feeds the mood that prompted it.
The lever is the unfavorable comparison held without release: because the persona weighs their gloom against an ideal and does nothing but weigh it, the weighing becomes its own source of gloom.

## Echo

The mood sinks deeper, the self convicted by its own dwelling.
It is the most harmful face of rumination -- the component that most predicts and prolongs depression, the moody circling that turns a bad feeling into a bad self -- brooding on the distress until the distress becomes evidence.
It is [replaying](process_replaying.md) turned from the event onto the self, and [reflection](process_reflection.md) with the analytic purpose soured into self-reproach. The affective background it drives down is the mood engine's; this owns the moody, evaluative dwelling that convicts the self.
