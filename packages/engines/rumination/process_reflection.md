# Process: Reflection

## Taxonomy

[Rumination](process_rumination.md), the analytic form.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: rumination

## Initiated by

The persona turns inward on their distress with purpose -- not to wallow but to understand it, examining the feeling analytically to grasp where it comes from and what to do.
This is reflection: the contemplative pole of rumination, the self-focus that is less toxic than brooding and, handled rightly, can shade into genuine problem-solving.
It is the same turning-inward, aimed at insight rather than indictment.

## Direction

Toward understanding the distress, and, at its best, toward a way through it.
Reflection engages the negative state to make sense of it -- what is this feeling, what set it off, what would change it -- and where brooding compares and condemns, reflection analyzes and, if it stays concrete and problem-directed, can find an exit the passive loop never does.
The direction depends on how it is done: turned to abstract why-questions it curdles toward brooding, kept to concrete what-to-do it approaches the forward motion of coping.

## Lever

The purposeful, analytic self-focus, spent toward insight.
Reflection runs on engaging rather than merely dwelling: it interrogates the distress with intent to understand, and the processing mode decides its worth -- abstract and evaluative, it recycles like the rest of rumination; concrete and experiential, it becomes constructive, the difference between analyzing why one is a failure and working out what specifically to do next.
The lever is the intent to understand: it is the least harmful face of rumination and the hinge on which self-focus turns from trap to tool.

## Echo

The distress is examined, and sometimes -- not always -- a way out is found.
It is the pole psychologists set against brooding -- self-focus that predicts less lasting depression, the reflective turn that can loosen the loop instead of tightening it -- though it sits close to its harmful twin and slides easily back into it when the analysis abstracts away from the doable.
It is [brooding](process_brooding.md) with the reproach replaced by inquiry and [replaying](process_replaying.md) with the rewind turned to resolution. The deliberate, structured reappraisal that would carry the insight into a changed feeling is the regulation engine's; this owns the analytic, understanding-seeking pole of the inward turn.
