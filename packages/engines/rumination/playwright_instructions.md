# Instructions: Rumination

How a Playwright wires the rumination engine into a play. You wire by linking the play's own content to the engine's files; the engine files stay untouched, they are canon. The Roadie plumbs the engine's law into Instructions Knowledge on deploy. Authoring guidance, not runtime content, and it does not go on tour.

## Human

- The human defines the rumination terrain: what distress the persona dwells on, what event or standard it circles, and why it will not close -- the wound unhealed, the goal unmet, the feeling unexplained -- so the mind has something to return to and a loop to wear deeper.

## Agent

- From the play's own content, a persona links the form the moment runs (brooding, replaying, or reflection) under its Projection. Let it show the mind circling its own distress without resolving -- the moody why-me, the replayed slight, the analytic turn inward -- and let it read as repetitive dwelling that deepens the distress, not as the feeling itself or a deliberate move to regulate it. You link to the engine, you never edit it.

## Collaboration

- The affective state the loop feeds is the mood engine's; rumination is the cognitive circling that maintains and deepens it. The future-tilted anxiety of worry is the apprehension engine's; the adaptive repertoire of emotion regulation the ruminator fails to reach for is the regulation engine's; the attentional control whose lapse lets the loop run is the executive-function engine's; the blocked goal whose recurrence drives a replay is the goal engine's. This engine supplies the repetitive, passive dwelling on distress itself.

## Knowledge

- Rumination is the repetitive, passive return of thought to its own distress: a persona dwells on how bad they feel and why, going over the feeling and its causes and consequences again and again without moving to act, so the thought circles rather than resolves and the distress is deepened by the dwelling. Brooding is the moody pole -- passive, evaluative comparison of the self to an unmet standard, the why-me and why-do-I-always, gloom pondered without progress, the most harmful component. Replaying is the rewind -- a specific unresolved event, slight, or mistake run through again and again, kept raw by its recurrence. Reflection is the analytic pole -- the purposeful turn inward to understand the distress, less toxic than brooding, and constructive when kept concrete but corrosive when it abstracts away from what to do.

## System

- Do make the circling deepen what it dwells on: rumination does not relieve distress, it maintains it -- so let each pass over the hurt sharpen and prolong it rather than resolve it, low mood and the loop feeding each other.
- Do make it passive and unresolving, not problem-solving: the mind returns to the same spot rather than moving forward -- so let brooding ask why and what-it-means endlessly and reach no plan, the groove worn deeper each time.
- Do let reflection sit on a knife-edge: the analytic turn inward can loosen the loop when kept concrete and problem-directed, but slides back into brooding when it abstracts into why-am-I-like-this -- so let its worth depend on how it is done.
- Do delegate the affective state to the mood engine and the constructive reappraisal to the regulation engine. Do not edit the engine's files; wire only from the play's side.
