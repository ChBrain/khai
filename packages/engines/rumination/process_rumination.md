# Process: Rumination

## Taxonomy

Cognition / the social psychology of repetitive negative thought.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: rumination

## Initiated by

A persona turns their mind onto their own distress and cannot turn it off -- dwelling on how bad they feel, and why, going over the feeling and its causes and consequences again and again without moving to act on them.
This is rumination: the repetitive, passive return of thought to its own pain.
The mind circles the hurt rather than passing through it, and the circling is not idle -- it deepens the very distress it examines.

## Direction

Toward the distress, again and again, and downward as the circling feeds it.
Rumination keeps attention fixed on the negative feeling and its meaning -- what it says about the self, why it came, how long it will last -- and each pass over the ground, far from resolving it, sharpens and prolongs it, so the loop tends downward, low mood recruiting the rumination that deepens the low mood.
The direction is recursive and unresolving: not the forward motion of thinking a problem through to a plan, but a return to the same spot, the groove worn deeper each time.

## Lever

The passive, repetitive dwelling itself, spent against the distress it feeds.
What rumination spends is attention held on the negative without discharge into action: because the mind attends to the pain abstractly -- asking why and what-it-means rather than what-to-do -- it finds no exit, and the same material is processed over and over, each cycle amplifying the affect and dimming the capacity to problem-solve.
The lever is the perseveration: a thought that recurs without resolving keeps its object alive and present, so dwelling on distress sustains it the way looking away would not.

## Echo

The distress is prolonged and deepened, the loop feeding what it dwells on.
Rumination is why low mood outlasts its cause, why a slight replayed grows sharper not duller, why turning the problem over and over yields more misery and less solution -- the passive circling maintains the very state it seems to be trying to understand.
It runs as [brooding](process_brooding.md), [replaying](process_replaying.md), or [reflection](process_reflection.md) -- the moody why-me, the rewound event, the analytic turn inward. The affective state the loop feeds is the mood engine's; the future-tilted anxiety of worry is the apprehension engine's; the adaptive repertoire of emotion regulation the persona fails to reach for is the regulation engine's; the attentional control whose lapse lets the loop run is the executive-function engine's. This engine owns the repetitive, passive dwelling on distress itself.
