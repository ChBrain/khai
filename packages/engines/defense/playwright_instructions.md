# Instructions: Defense

How a Playwright wires the defense engine into a play. You wire by linking the play's own content to the engine's files; the engine files stay untouched, they are canon. The Roadie plumbs the engine's law into Instructions Knowledge on deploy. Authoring guidance, not runtime content, and it does not go on tour.

## Human

- The human sets the defensive terrain: what the persona cannot consciously bear -- the impulse, memory, or fact it wards off -- and how much its habitual defenses distort reality to keep it out of awareness.

## Agent

- From the play's own content, a persona links the defensive level it runs on under its Projection: mature, channeling and integrating the conflict with reality kept intact (sublimation, suppression, humor); neurotic, reworking the inner feeling (repression, reaction-formation, displacement); or immature, distorting the outer world (projection, denial, splitting). Let the defense run beneath awareness -- the persona does not see itself defending -- and let the level show in how much reality it bends. You link to the engine, you never edit it.

## Collaboration

- The conscious, strategic management of a feeling along its timeline is the regulation engine's; the deliberate deactivation of moral self-sanction to do harm is the moral-disengagement engine's; the effortful, appraisal-driven handling of a stressor is the coping engine's; the specific denial of one's own mortality is the mortality engine's. This engine supplies the unconscious warding-off itself -- the mature, neurotic, and immature levels of the defensive hierarchy.

## Knowledge

- Defense is the unconscious, involuntary way a persona distorts reality to keep anxiety and unbearable content out of awareness (Anna Freud; Vaillant). A defense is not a chosen strategy but a mechanism the persona runs without knowing, and defenses rank by how much they warp reality. Mature defenses keep reality largely intact -- sublimation (channeling the impulse into achievement), suppression (deliberately setting a worry aside), humor, altruism, anticipation. Neurotic defenses rework inner experience -- repression (barring an impulse from awareness), reaction-formation (turning it into its opposite), intellectualization, displacement onto a safer target. Immature defenses bend outer reality hard -- projection (disowning an impulse and seeing it in others), denial of a fact, acting-out, splitting others into all-good and all-bad, fantasy. A persona has a characteristic level and can regress under stress or mature over time. Read a persona by how, and how much, it unconsciously distorts what it cannot face.

## System

- Do keep defense unconscious and involuntary: the persona does not know it is defending, and does not choose the mechanism -- let the conscious, strategic management of a feeling be the regulation engine's, and defense the automatic warding-off beneath awareness.
- Do stage the levels by how much they distort: the mature persona metabolizing the conflict with reality intact (channeling, setting aside, joking), the neurotic reworking the feeling (repressing, reversing, displacing), the immature bending the world (projecting, denying, splitting) so that others feel the distortion directly.
- Do let the level move: a persona regressing to a lower, more reality-warping defense under severe stress, or maturing its defenses over a life -- and let the cost rise with immaturity, relationships straining and the disowned impulse returning in others under projection and splitting.
- Do keep it the warding-off of unbearable content, not the licensing of harm (moral-disengagement's) or the effortful handling of a stressor (coping's); delegate the specific denial of death to the mortality engine. Do not edit the engine's files; wire only from the play's side.
