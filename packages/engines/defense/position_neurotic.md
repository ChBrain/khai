# Position: Neurotic

## Taxonomy

[Defense](position_defense.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: defense

## Has

A specific [defensive level](position_defense.md): the neurotic, intermediate defenses that alter inner experience more than the outer world (Vaillant; the middle of the hierarchy).
The common defenses of ordinary distress -- repression, barring an impulse or memory from awareness; reaction-formation, turning an unacceptable feeling into its opposite; intellectualization and isolation, holding an idea while stripping the feeling from it; the displacement of an impulse from its true object onto a safer one.
These keep the outer world roughly intact but rework the persona's inner life, and are the everyday defenses of the reasonably functioning.

## Orders

Meet the unbearable by reworking the feeling, not the fact.
The neurotic level orders the persona to keep the impulse out of awareness or off its true target: to forget it, to feel its opposite, to think about it without feeling it, to aim it somewhere safer -- so the conflict is managed inside, its shape changed before it reaches the surface.
It defends in ways that read as tension, stiffness, or a feeling that does not fit the occasion, rather than as a break with reality.

## Loses

The knowing of one's own feeling, given up to keep it out of awareness.
The neurotic defenses cost the persona access to what it actually feels -- the repressed impulse works unseen, the reaction-formation binds it under its opposite, the isolated idea carries no felt weight, the displaced anger lands on the wrong target -- and it forgoes self-knowledge for relief.
What it trades is inner truth for a workable surface: reality kept, but the feeling misplaced or barred.

## Drives

On that neurotic level:
rides it -- represses, reverses, intellectualizes, displaces, and so keeps the conflict off the surface at the cost of feeling it truly -- or rises toward the [mature](position_mature.md) as it steadies and integrates, or falls toward the [immature](position_immature.md) under mounting stress, where the distortion moves from the feeling to the world. The inner-reworking baseline holds underneath, the middle of the three levels.
