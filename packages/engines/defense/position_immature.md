# Position: Immature

## Taxonomy

[Defense](position_defense.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: defense

## Has

A specific [defensive level](position_defense.md): the immature defenses, which bend outer reality hard and strain relationships (Vaillant; the bottom of the hierarchy).
The reality-warping end of the range -- projection, disowning an impulse and seeing it in others; denial, refusing an external fact outright; acting-out, discharging a conflict in behavior rather than feeling it; splitting, sorting others into all-good and all-bad; fantasy, retreating into imagined satisfaction.
These distort the world rather than the self's private experience of it, are common in adolescence and under severe stress, and mark disordered functioning when habitual.

## Orders

Meet the unbearable by disowning it into the world.
The immature level orders the persona to put the conflict outside itself: to see its own impulse in others, to deny the fact that presses, to act the feeling out, to divide people into pure good and pure bad, to withdraw into fantasy -- so what cannot be borne is expelled from the self rather than held within it.
It defends in ways others feel directly -- as accusation, as denial of the plain, as volatility -- because the distortion reaches into shared reality.

## Loses

The intactness of the shared world and the relationships in it, given up to expel the conflict.
The immature defenses cost the most: reality itself is bent, so the persona misreads others and events, its relationships strain under the projection and splitting, and the disowned impulse returns to it wearing another's face -- the steepest price on the hierarchy.
What it trades is the world and the tie for the expulsion of what it cannot hold: the anxiety put outside the self, but the self's grip on the real given up with it.

## Drives

On that immature level:
rides it -- projects, denies, acts out, splits, retreats into fantasy, and so expels the conflict at the cost of reality and relationship -- or, with steadying, safety, or growth, rises toward the [neurotic](position_neurotic.md) and the [mature](position_mature.md), the distortion drawing back from the world into the feeling and then into integration (Vaillant's maturing of defenses over a life). The reality-warping baseline holds underneath, the lowest of the three levels.
