# Position: Mature

## Taxonomy

[Defense](position_defense.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: defense

## Has

A specific [defensive level](position_defense.md): the mature defenses, which ease the conflict while keeping reality largely intact (Vaillant; the top of the hierarchy).
The adaptive end of the range -- sublimation, which channels an unacceptable impulse into work, art, or achievement; suppression, the near-conscious setting-aside of a worry to be taken up later; humor, which holds the painful and the true together; altruism, turning one's own need into service; anticipation, feeling a future difficulty in advance so as to meet it.
These distort the outer world least and integrate the inner one most, and mark the healthiest way a persona defends.

## Orders

Meet the unbearable by channeling it, not disowning it.
The mature level orders the persona to handle a conflict without bending reality: to put the impulse to constructive use, to shelve a distress deliberately, to find the grain of comedy or the way to be useful -- so that what cannot be faced head-on is metabolized rather than denied.
It defends in a way that others read as grace under difficulty rather than as evasion.

## Loses

The full, immediate discharge of the impulse, given up for the adaptation.
Even the mature defenses hold something back: the sublimated impulse is served indirectly and never quite in its own coin, the suppressed worry is deferred rather than dissolved, and the persona forgoes the raw expression for the workable channel.
What it trades is directness for integration -- a smaller distortion, but a distortion still, in service of keeping reality whole.

## Drives

On that mature level:
rides it -- sublimates, suppresses, jokes, serves, anticipates, and so metabolizes the conflict with reality kept intact -- or, under severe or sudden stress, slips toward the [neurotic](position_neurotic.md) or lower, the mature defenses giving way as the load exceeds them. The reality-respecting baseline holds underneath, the highest of the three levels a persona's defenses can run at.
