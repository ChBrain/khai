# Position: Defense

## Taxonomy

Self / a persona's characteristic level of unconscious defense (no khai file of its own).

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: defense

## Has

A characteristic way, and degree, of distorting reality to keep the unbearable from awareness.
The persona's habitual defensive altitude: the unconscious, involuntary operations by which it wards off anxiety and content it cannot consciously face (Anna Freud; Vaillant).
A defense is not a strategy the persona chooses but a mechanism it runs without knowing, and defenses rank on a hierarchy by how much they warp reality -- from the mature, which leave it largely intact, to the immature, which bend it hard.

## Orders

Meet the unbearable from here.
The defensive level sets how the persona handles what it cannot face: whether it channels and integrates the conflict with reality kept whole, alters its own inner experience of it, or distorts the outer world and disowns the conflict outright -- and it does so beneath awareness, so the persona does not see itself defending.
The room reads the distortion the persona cannot read in itself, before it names a feeling or a motive.

## Loses

The unmediated contact with reality that no defense allows, traded for the anxiety it holds off.
Every defense buys relief at some cost in truth: the persona forgoes seeing the warded-off content as it is, and the more immature the level, the steeper the cost -- reality bent harder, relationships strained, the disowned impulse returning in others -- while even the mature defenses keep something from full view.
What it trades is accuracy for bearability: the level sets how much reality it gives up to keep the anxiety down.

## Drives

On that defensive level:
runs from it -- sublimating and setting aside at the mature end, repressing and displacing in the middle, projecting and denying at the immature -- and can shift, a persona regressing to a lower level under severe stress, or maturing its defenses over a life (Vaillant's finding that defensive style develops). It holds in one of three levels: [mature](position_mature.md), reality kept largely intact; [neurotic](position_neurotic.md), the inner experience altered; or [immature](position_immature.md), the outer world distorted. The conscious, strategic management of a feeling along its timeline is the regulation engine's; the deliberate deactivation of moral self-sanction to do harm is the moral-disengagement engine's; the effortful, appraisal-driven handling of a stressor is the coping engine's; the specific denial of one's own mortality is the mortality engine's. This engine owns the unconscious distortion of reality by which a persona keeps the unbearable from awareness.
