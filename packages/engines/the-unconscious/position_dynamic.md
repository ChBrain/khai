# Position: Dynamic

## Taxonomy

[The Unconscious](position_the_unconscious.md), the repressed -- content kept out of awareness because it is unbearable.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: the-unconscious

## Has

A specific kind of [the unconscious](position_the_unconscious.md): the dynamic, Freudian unconscious of repression.
Its content is not merely absent from awareness but actively held out of it -- a forbidden wish, an intolerable memory, a shameful impulse -- because to know it consciously would be unbearable. It is motivated and defended, kept down by effort, and it does not rest.
Barred from consciousness, it presses for expression and returns in disguise: in the slip of the tongue, the dream, the symptom, the inexplicable feeling -- the return of the repressed.

## Orders

Bury what cannot be borne, and let it surface in disguise.
The dynamic unconscious orders the persona to keep the unbearable from awareness at all costs, and orders the buried content, in turn, to press back -- so the persona is moved by a wish it does not know it has, and betrayed by it in a slip, a dream, a symptom it cannot explain. What is repressed is not gone; it is working, out of sight, and leaking.
It sets the hidden motive beneath a persona's puzzling acts.

## Loses

Access to the very content that most moves it.
Because the material is repressed, the persona forgoes any conscious grasp of it -- it cannot examine, argue with, or resolve the wish or memory driving it, and pays in the symptom that stands in for it and the energy spent holding it down. What is defended against cannot be worked through while it stays unconscious.
What it trades is the pain of knowing for the cost of not knowing, and carries a motive it cannot reach shaping a life it cannot quite explain.

## Drives

On that repressed content:
presses and returns -- the buried wish or memory pushing toward expression and surfacing disguised in slip, dream, and symptom, while the persona keeps it down -- until, sometimes, it is made conscious and can be faced.
The dynamic is one kind of [the unconscious](position_the_unconscious.md), the motivated and defended kind, set apart from the [adaptive](position_adaptive.md) unconscious that was never repressed and the [preconscious](position_preconscious.md) that is merely inattentive. The mechanisms that do the repressing are the defense engine's; the intrapsychic conflict the repression serves is the structural-model engine's. This face owns the repressed content and its return.
