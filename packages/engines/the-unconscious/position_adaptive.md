# Position: Adaptive

## Taxonomy

[The Unconscious](position_the_unconscious.md), the automatic -- fast, efficient processing that was never conscious.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: the-unconscious

## Has

A specific kind of [the unconscious](position_the_unconscious.md): the cognitive or adaptive unconscious that modern psychology documents.
Its content is out of awareness not because it is repressed but because it was never conscious to begin with -- the vast, fast, efficient machinery that reads a face, sizes up a situation, forms a first impression, and sets a course before the conscious self is consulted (Kihlstrom; Wilson's adaptive unconscious).
It is a mental butler: it does most of the work of perceiving, evaluating, and deciding silently and quickly, and hands the conscious self a result, not a process.

## Orders

Judge and act first, and let awareness arrive after.
The adaptive unconscious orders the persona to run on automatic -- to perceive, appraise, and often decide outside awareness, efficiently and at speed -- and to present consciousness with an outcome it did not watch being made: the snap judgment, the gut feeling, the fluent skill, the choice already leaning before the reasons are marshalled. The conscious self then explains a decision it did not consciously reach.
It sets the fast, silent processing beneath a persona's seemingly deliberate acts.

## Loses

Insight into how its own judgments were made.
Because the processing was never conscious, the persona forgoes any view of it -- it gets the verdict without the deliberation, and when asked why, it confabulates a plausible reason rather than reporting the real cause it never had access to (Nisbett & Wilson). Its introspective reports of its own mental processes are, in this domain, unreliable.
What it trades is transparency for speed and efficiency: the adaptive unconscious lets the persona function without deliberating everything, and pays in a self-account that is often invented after the fact.

## Drives

On that automatic machinery:
runs fast and silent -- perceiving, evaluating, and deciding outside awareness, handing consciousness a result it then rationalizes -- the everyday, functional unconscious the persona lives on far more than it knows.
The adaptive is one kind of [the unconscious](position_the_unconscious.md), the never-repressed, functional kind, set apart from the motivated [dynamic](position_dynamic.md) unconscious and the merely-inattentive [preconscious](position_preconscious.md). The monitor that would report on it, and fails, is the metacognition engine's; the automatic evaluations it forms are read as feeling by the emotion engine's. This face owns the fast out-of-awareness processing itself.
