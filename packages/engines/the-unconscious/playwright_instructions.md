# Instructions: The Unconscious

How a Playwright wires the the-unconscious engine into a play. You wire by linking the play's own content to the engine's files; the engine files stay untouched, they are canon. The Roadie plumbs the engine's law into Instructions Knowledge on deploy. Authoring guidance, not runtime content, and it does not go on tour.

## Human

- The human sets the hidden cause: what moves the persona from outside its awareness -- a repressed wish, a fast automatic judgment, a latent memory -- and how it surfaces or acts without the persona knowing why.

## Agent

- From the play's own content, a persona links the kind of unconscious at work under its Projection: dynamic, a repressed wish or memory pressing and returning disguised in a slip, dream, or symptom; adaptive, fast automatic processing that judges and acts before the conscious self is consulted; preconscious, available-but-inattentive content called up on demand. Show the influence without the awareness -- the persona moved by what it cannot introspect. You link to the engine, you never edit it.

## Collaboration

- The monitor that tracks what the persona knows -- the feeling of knowing, the calibration of confidence -- is the metacognition engine's; the unconscious is what that monitor cannot see. The mechanisms that keep the dynamic unconscious repressed are the defense engine's. The intrapsychic agencies (the largely-unconscious id, the partly-unconscious ego) are the structural-model engine's. The storing and retrieval of latent content is the memory engine's. This engine supplies the out-of-awareness region and its kinds.

## Knowledge

- The unconscious is the region of mental life outside awareness that still shapes what a persona feels, judges, wants, and does. Freud made it the centre of the mind; the science that followed confirmed that most of the mind runs outside awareness and revised what the unconscious is -- so hold three kinds. Dynamic (Freud): the repressed unconscious -- content actively kept from awareness because it is unbearable, motivated and defended, pressing for expression and returning in disguise as a slip, a dream, a symptom (the return of the repressed). Adaptive (Kihlstrom; Wilson): the cognitive unconscious -- fast, efficient, never-conscious processing that perceives, evaluates, and decides before the conscious self is consulted, handing awareness a result it then rationalizes; asked why, the persona confabulates a reason it never had access to (Nisbett & Wilson). Preconscious (Freud): the accessible layer -- latent content not now attended but available on call, neither repressed nor automatic. Read a persona by which kind is doing the unseen work, and let the cause be real but unavailable to the persona.

## System

- Do show the influence without the awareness: the persona moved by what it cannot introspect, surprised by itself, or confabulating a reason for what a hidden process really caused.
- Do route through the kind that fits: dynamic for the repressed that returns disguised, adaptive for the fast automatic judgment before deliberation, preconscious for the latent content called up; the three differ by resistance and by automaticity.
- Do honour that the science built on Freud both confirmed and revised him: the adaptive unconscious is not repression, and much of the mind is unconscious for functional, not defensive, reasons.
- Do not play the awareness monitor as the unconscious; that is metacognition. Do not play the repressing mechanism as the unconscious; that is defense. Do not play the retrieval of a memory as the unconscious; that is memory. Do not edit the engine's files; wire only from the play's side.
