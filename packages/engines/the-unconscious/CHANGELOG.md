# @chbrain/khai-engine-the-unconscious

## 0.1.1

### Patch Changes

- 6ad4c56: Add the the-unconscious engine (position: anchor + expressions) -- the region of mental life outside awareness that still shapes what a persona feels, judges, wants, and does. Third engine of the Freud effort, and the construct where modern science most visibly built on _and revised_ Freud.

  - **Anchor (position):** `the-unconscious` -- the out-of-awareness region; the conscious self is not the author of its acts but their (often mistaken) commentator.
  - **Faces (position)** -- the three kinds, staging exactly the Freud-then-science arc:
    - `dynamic` -- Freud's unconscious of **repression**: content actively kept from awareness because it is unbearable, motivated and defended, pressing and returning disguised in slips, dreams, symptoms (the return of the repressed).
    - `adaptive` -- the modern **cognitive/adaptive** unconscious: fast, efficient, _never-conscious_ processing that perceives, evaluates, and acts before the conscious self is consulted, handing awareness a result it then rationalizes (Kihlstrom; Wilson's "mental butler"; Nisbett & Wilson's confabulation).
    - `preconscious` -- Freud's **accessible** layer: latent content not now attended but available on call, neither repressed nor automatic.

  **Warrant is a lineage, not solo work.** Freud (_The Unconscious_, 1915; _The Interpretation of Dreams_, 1900) founded it, but the modern warrant is experimental: Kihlstrom (1987, "The Cognitive Unconscious," _Science_), Wilson (2002, _Strangers to Ourselves_), and Nisbett & Wilson (1977, "Telling More Than We Can Know") re-founded the unconscious on evidence and revised what it is -- confirming most of the mind runs outside awareness while showing much of that is functional, not defensive. (Neuropsychoanalysis, Solms, works to reconcile the two.)

  **Scope note.** The awareness monitor (feeling-of-knowing, confidence calibration) stays with `metacognition` -- the unconscious is what it cannot see; the repressing mechanisms stay with `defense`; the intrapsychic agencies (the largely-unconscious id, partly-unconscious ego) stay with `structural-model` (the unconscious cross-cuts them); latent-content storage/retrieval stays with `memory`. Set at patch. No whitelist required -- the `the_unconscious` / `dynamic` / `adaptive` / `preconscious` stems are unique.
