# Position: The Unconscious

## Taxonomy

Depth psychology / the region of mind outside awareness that still shapes the persona (no khai file of its own).

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: the-unconscious

## Has

A standing region of mental life the persona cannot introspect, yet which shapes what it feels, judges, wants, and does.
Freud placed the unconscious at the centre of the mind and made consciousness the exception; the science that followed both confirmed his central claim -- that most of the mind runs outside awareness -- and revised his account of what that unconscious is.
So the persona carries not one unconscious but a layered one: the dynamic unconscious of the repressed, the adaptive unconscious of automatic processing, and the preconscious that lies accessible between them and awareness.

## Orders

Let the unseen do its work, and the persona not know it did.
The unconscious orders the room to read a persona as moved by more than it can report -- a judgment made before the reasons, a feeling whose cause is hidden, a slip that says what was not meant -- and to locate which kind of out-of-awareness process is doing the shaping. The persona acts, and cannot fully say why; the cause is real but not available to it.
It sets the depth beneath the persona's conscious account of itself.

## Loses

The transparency of a self that knows its own mind.
Carrying an unconscious, the persona forgoes full self-knowledge -- it cannot introspect the springs of much of what it does, will confabulate reasons for choices it made on hidden grounds, and can be surprised by itself. The conscious self is not the author of its acts but their commentator, often mistaken.
What it trades is the illusion of a wholly-known self for the truth of a mind most of which it cannot see, and pays in the errors of a narrator who does not have the story.

## Drives

On that out-of-awareness region:
runs through whichever kind is doing the unseen work -- the [dynamic](position_dynamic.md), repressed content pressing and returning disguised; the [adaptive](position_adaptive.md), fast automatic processing judging and acting before the conscious self is consulted; or the [preconscious](position_preconscious.md), available content called up from latency.
The region holds beneath the persona's conscious life. The monitor that knows what the persona knows is the metacognition engine's; the mechanisms that keep the dynamic unconscious repressed are the defense engine's; the intrapsychic agency the id is largely unconscious of is the structural-model engine's. This engine owns the out-of-awareness region itself, and its three kinds.
