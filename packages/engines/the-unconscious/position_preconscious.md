# Position: Preconscious

## Taxonomy

[The Unconscious](position_the_unconscious.md), the accessible -- content not now in mind but available to it.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: the-unconscious

## Has

A specific kind of [the unconscious](position_the_unconscious.md): the preconscious, the accessible antechamber between the depths and awareness.
Its content is out of awareness only in the descriptive sense -- not repressed, not automatic, but simply not currently attended to: a name, a fact, a memory the persona is not thinking of this moment but can call up at will.
It is the vast store of latent knowledge that stands ready, the reservoir consciousness draws from without effort when it turns that way (Freud's descriptive preconscious).

## Orders

Hold available, and surface on call.
The preconscious orders the persona's latent content to wait accessible -- neither pressing like the repressed nor running like the automatic, but ready -- and to come into awareness the moment attention turns to it, a memory recalled, a fact retrieved, a name found. It is the easy traffic between what the persona knows and what it happens to be thinking.
It sets the accessible reserve just beneath a persona's present awareness.

## Loses

Little -- and that is what marks it.
Because its content is merely inattentive rather than defended or never-conscious, the persona forgoes almost nothing: what is preconscious it can have for the asking, and the only cost is that it is not all present at once. This is the shallowest of the three kinds, and the one nearest the surface.
What it trades is simply the finitude of attention -- the persona cannot hold everything it knows in mind at once -- for a store it can draw on freely.

## Drives

On that accessible store:
holds latent and surfaces on call -- content available to awareness the moment attention turns, drawn up without effort or resistance.
The preconscious is one kind of [the unconscious](position_the_unconscious.md), the accessible kind, set apart from the defended [dynamic](position_dynamic.md) unconscious and the automatic [adaptive](position_adaptive.md) one -- the layer nearest awareness, from which the other two are told apart by resistance and by automaticity. The storing and retrieval of that latent content is the memory engine's; this face owns its standing availability just below awareness.
