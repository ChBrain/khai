# Management

khai's own company: the people who run khai, as distinct from the cast of any
play.

Every house khai raises gets a management layer of its own, stamped from the
[khai-stage blueprint](../../khai-stage/blueprint/management). This is khai's,
and it differs from a house's in one way worth stating: **khai holds no
collection.** A house has plays to stage and a company to stage them; khai has
the bill, the canon and the kit, and this company to conduct that work. The
company is the same idea in both places, doing it for different work.

## Nothing here is content

The engines and composites are the canon; the registry is the bill. This
directory is neither. Nothing in it is indexed by the science map, cast into a
play, or published as an engine -- the Choregos is a standing role in this
project, not a character anybody stages.

## What is in here

- **`persona_*.md`** -- who speaks: Pericles, Nicias, Agatharchus, the Director.
- **`position_*.md`** -- standing roles: the Choregos, the Director, the Roadie.
- **`plan_*.md`** -- recurring procedures: setting up a house, going on tour,
  keeping clean, staging the score, raising the composite layer.
- **`orders/`** -- standing decisions, once made and thereafter in force.
- **`discussions/`** -- where a question is worked through before it becomes an
  order.

## The voice lives next door

[`management_instructions.md`](management_instructions.md) is the voice layer:
who speaks, through which Persona, and how the company collaborates, in the HACKS
chapters. [AGENTS.md](../../../AGENTS.md) names it first for exactly that reason
-- voice before mechanics, and the coding contract second.

This README says **why the layer exists and where its boundary is**. That file
says **how to work inside it**.
