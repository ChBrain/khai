# Order: The Repertoire

## Direction

The canon adopts **the repertoire** (`architecture/repertoire.md`, mnemonic
LOAN): a house declares the castable world in reach (its own collection, the
houses it depends on, the engines it depends on), and every play inside it may
cast members it did not write. Borrow against build is computed from link
resolution, never judged; names are API; qualification follows the composite
convention (package plus member file). The concept generalises two rulings the
system already made: engines as content dependencies with laws, and the
package-specifier link resolution the composite layer order mandated.

It also names a lifecycle. A **scenario** is a play that is mostly Library and
thinly Own: prepared by the Playwright, produced as a tour artifact by
khai-tour in the Roadie's hands, performed at a venue under the Director, and
ephemeral by default. If a scenario is meant to stay, it is housed: committed
to a collection like any play, conformant under the same rule. What a run
produces flows to khai-writing when kept; what a run teaches about a lender
returns to that lender as an ordinary change.

The proof exists: an intercultural scenario (Interreg Flensburg, six personas
from three cultures plus a fourth culture's language, the language engine's
ladder injected, instructions composed from the spine, packed flat) was staged
from the Cultures house and performed at a foreign venue from a single upload.

## Orders

Adopt the concept; land the mechanism lane by lane, each on its own gate:

- **The canon file** (khai-arch, `arch/` lane): `repertoire.md` rides with
  this order; status draft until the mechanism below proves it.
- **The catalog** (khai-tests, its lane): the registry build ships a
  `members[]` list per entry with casting facets (kind, persona type, claimed
  position, languages), derived at build, so a registry is a catalog and the
  Playwright casts by query, never by walking shelves.
- **The resolution** (khai-tests, its lane): conformance resolves a Company
  link against the repertoire (the play's folder, the house collection, the
  declared houses, the declared engines) and answers borrow, build, fail, or
  qualify. In-house cross-collection links become checked; cross-house links
  resolve through node_modules per the composite ruling.
- **The tour station** (khai-tour, its lane): `khai-tour scenario <folder>`
  resolves the Company, widens by the engine ladders the laws demand,
  composes the deployed instructions (Standard plus House Rules plus the
  scenario Adaption, engine laws at Knowledge, links as names), gates on
  conformance, strips frontmatter, points beyond-bundle links home, and packs
  one flat zip. The Roadie composes and stages, never authors.
- **The fragments** (engine lanes): spine ships the scenario Adaption; each
  engine exports its law as a computed bullet, the language engine first.
- **The raising** (khai-stage, its lane): a stamped house may declare house
  dependencies as it declares engines, and carries an ignored `scenarios/`
  working directory; a workshop is a house whose collection may be empty and
  whose repertoire is rich.
- **The voices** (khai-skills, `skills/` lane): the Playwright resolves
  against the repertoire before authoring and casts from the catalog; the
  Roadie carries the guest-production tour; the Impresario curates a house's
  repertoire from the bill. The Director is untouched: a ready play is a
  ready play.

- **The performance type** (khai-arch, `arch/` lane, foreseen): a
  Performance content type, mnemonic TO LOAN: the claim ticket a run leaves
  riding beside the play, its written record lodged with khai-writing and the
  in-band file permanently on loan from it, borrowable onward by later
  productions the way a plot carries history. Minted as its own architecture
  change when the Director's first capture wants writing down; not before.

Execution is deferred to those lanes; this order records the shape so every
later change lands under a directive instead of a judgment call.

## Implementation

Each item above lands in its own lane, on its own branch, with its own
changeset (patch unless the maintainer labels otherwise), source and tests as
separate PRs with the tests dormant until their source merges. The golden
fixture for the whole chain is the Interreg Flensburg scenario: six personas
from three cultures plus a fourth culture's language, the language ladder
injected, instructions composed from the spine, one flat zip. The tour
station must reproduce it from the authored layer and a cultures checkout;
what it packs is what a venue plays.
