# Process: Love-Hate, Turn

## Taxonomy

[Love-Hate](process_love_hate.md), the bridge where one sign crosses into the other.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: love-hate

## Initiated by

A bond that was one thing begins to become its opposite.
Where holding keeps [love](@chbrain/khai-composite-love/process_love.md) and [hate](@chbrain/khai-composite-hate/process_hate.md) at full together, the turn is the motion between them -- love curdling toward hate, or, more rarely, hate thawing toward love.
It opens most fiercely at a betrayal: the sharpest hate is reserved for a former intimate, because the negation of intimacy that hate is built on is love's own intimacy inverted -- the closer the love was, the more there is to invert.

## Direction

From one sign toward the other, across the shared substrate.
The turn moves the bond along the near path between love and hate: the trust of intimacy becoming the revulsion of its negation, the passion of attraction becoming the passion of hostility, the loyalty of commitment becoming the commitment to devalue -- each corner of love crossing to its dark twin.
Its direction is the crossing itself -- the same bond, resigned from one sign and taking up the other.

## Lever

What the turn spends -- the investment the love had built, converted rather than lost.
A love betrayed does not fade to indifference; its intimacy, passion, and commitment are still there, inverted -- which is why the turn produces a hate hotter than one built from scratch, drawing its heat from the love it was.
A production holds this lever by letting a broken love arm the hate that follows it -- the ex-lover's specific, intimate cruelty, the disciple's hatred of the mentor -- so the turn reads as a conversion of a real bond, not a fresh enmity.

## Echo

The bond has crossed -- the love now hate, or the hate now love, and marked by the sign it came from.
A hate born of a turned love carries the love's knowledge and its wound, sharper and more particular than a stranger's; a love won back from hate carries the hate's caution. The crossing can complete, or it can stall halfway and settle back into the [holding](process_love_hate_holding.md) of both at once, or lock into a [bind](process_love_hate_bind.md) the persona cannot leave.
The composition of the resulting hate -- which corners it takes -- is the hate composite's; this bridge owns the crossing, the conversion of a bond from one sign to its opposite.
