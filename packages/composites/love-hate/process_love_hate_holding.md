# Process: Love-Hate, Holding

## Taxonomy

[Love-Hate](process_love_hate.md), the bridge where love and hate run at full toward one object at once.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: love-hate

## Initiated by

A single object that is, at once, cherished and resented.
Each atom answers as if its composition ran alone -- [love](@chbrain/khai-composite-love/process_love.md) draws toward, [hate](@chbrain/khai-composite-hate/process_hate.md) pushes away; neither, alone, sees the ambivalence their co-firing makes.
When both are read from the same person at the same time -- the parent adored and resented, the sibling loved and envied, the partner needed and blamed -- the two run together and the result is the ambivalent bond: a feeling denser and more binding than either, the reaching and the recoiling in one motion.

## Direction

Toward the object held loved and hated together -- clung to and struck at in the same breath.
Holding does not alternate love then hate; it keeps them concurrent, so the persona cannot point to a single feeling for the object and every gesture toward it carries both the pull and the push.
Its direction is the sustained coexistence, not a settling to one sign.

## Lever

What holding spends -- the coherence of a single-valence bond.
Love alone lets the persona simply cherish; hate alone lets them simply reject; holding denies both, keeping the drawing-toward and the pushing-away at once so neither can be felt clean, and the bond is binding precisely because it will not reduce.
A production holds this lever by letting both composites run live from the same object rather than choosing one, and reading the ambivalence -- not any single feeling -- as what governs the tie.

## Echo

The object is felt in both signs at once -- and the ambivalent bond is the signature of it, the density and the hold that a purely loving or purely hating reading would lose.
It is why the fiercest bonds are so often the most tormented, and why a flattening to one valence -- all devotion, all loathing -- reads as thinner than the truth.
Holding can persist as a standing ambivalence, or it can begin to move, one sign gaining on the other; that motion, if the production stages it, is a [turn](process_love_hate_turn.md) -- a crossing, not the sustained coexistence holding names.
