# Process: Love-Hate, Bind

## Taxonomy

[Love-Hate](process_love_hate.md), the bridge where the ambivalence locks the persona in place.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: love-hate

## Initiated by

An object matters too much to leave and hurts too much to hold.
Where holding keeps [love](@chbrain/khai-composite-love/process_love.md) and [hate](@chbrain/khai-composite-hate/process_hate.md) alive together, the bind is what that coexistence does when the stakes are highest: the love forbids the leaving that the hate demands, and the hate forbids the peace that the love wants, so the persona is caught.
It opens in the closest and most inescapable ties -- the parent, the child, the long marriage, the homeland -- where neither the drawing-toward nor the pushing-away can win, and both press at once.

## Direction

Toward the lock -- approach and avoidance at full, cancelling into paralysis.
The bind moves the persona to reach and recoil in the same instant and be able to complete neither: to stay and resent, or leave and grieve, with no move that does not cost the other feeling its due.
Its direction is the standstill of a bond that cannot be resolved and cannot be dropped -- the ambivalence at its most binding.

## Lever

What the bind spends -- the persona's freedom to act cleanly on the object.
Because both feelings are at full and aimed at one target, every course forecloses a real good: to honour the love is to swallow the hate, to act the hate is to betray the love, so the persona pays whichever way it moves and often, therefore, does not move.
A production holds this lever by letting the persona be genuinely stuck -- unable to leave the tie or make peace with it, its energy spent holding the two apart -- so the bind reads as a real trap, not indecision, and the object's mattering is what closes it.

## Echo

The persona is locked -- held by a love it cannot honour and a hate it cannot act, spending itself where it cannot move.
This is ambivalence at its most costly: the tie that will not resolve drains the persona, and the way out is rarely a clean choice but a loosening -- the object's hold reduced, the stakes lowered, or the bond mourned and released. Until then the [holding](process_love_hate_holding.md) is not a still poignancy but a trap, and a [turn](process_love_hate_turn.md) that completed might at least free the persona to one sign.
The acute grief of finally releasing the bond is the grief engine's; the approach-avoidance of an insecure attachment its own engine's. This bridge owns the lock itself -- the ambivalence that binds where the object matters too much to leave.
