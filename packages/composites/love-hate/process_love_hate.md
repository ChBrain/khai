# Process: Love-Hate

## Taxonomy

A composite reading over two atom composites (no canon file of its own): the love and hate composites this package declares as dependencies.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: love-hate

## Initiated by

A persona loves and hates the same object at once -- and both hold, neither cancelling the other.
The love is the love composite's, the hate is the hate composite's; what neither, alone, can see is the ambivalence they make when they run from the same object.
This is the ambivalence Freud placed at the heart of the closest ties -- the coexistence of love and hate toward one person, which he found in mourning, in the child's bond to the parent, in obsessional love -- and it rests on more than metaphor: Zeki and Romaya find love and hate share a common subcortical circuit, so they are near neighbours a single bond can hold together, not distant opposites.
The parent maddened by the child adored, the lover who cannot forgive and cannot leave -- [love](@chbrain/khai-composite-love/process_love.md) and [hate](@chbrain/khai-composite-hate/process_hate.md) held toward one object, not traded clean for one another.

## Direction

Toward the object held loved and hated at once -- reached for and recoiled from in the same breath.
The composite does not net the two to a single verdict; it keeps the love and the hate both alive toward one target, so the persona is drawn to and against the same person, and every move toward them carries its opposite.
Its direction is the both-at-once, rarely resolved down to clean love or clean hate.

## Lever

What the love-hate spends -- the assumption that a bond runs one way toward its object.
Love alone lets the persona simply cherish; hate alone lets them simply reject; ambivalence denies both, holding the drawing-toward and the pushing-away together so that neither runs clean, and the bond is more binding and more painful than either would make it.
It rests on the shared substrate the two feelings run on: because love and hate are near neighbours, the very intensity of a love is what a betrayal can invert, and the closest bonds are the ones most able to hold both.

## Echo

The bond is felt in both signs at once -- loved and hated together, and the more binding for it.
The atoms each run their own composition and cannot see the family: the [holding](process_love_hate_holding.md) where love and hate run at full toward one object at once, the ambivalent bond; the [turn](process_love_hate_turn.md) where one crosses into the other, a love betrayed hardening to its sharpest hate; and the [bind](process_love_hate_bind.md) where the object matters too much to leave and hurts too much to hold, the approach-and-avoid that cannot resolve.
The general capacity to hold any opposed attitudes toward one object -- attitudinal ambivalence beyond love and hate -- and the wider reaches of Freud's account are their own domains; the approach-avoidance of an insecure bond is the attachment engine's. This composite owns the love and hate of one object held together, and the shapes that coexistence takes.
