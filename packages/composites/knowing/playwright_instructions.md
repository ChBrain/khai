# Instructions: Knowing

How a Playwright wires the knowing composite into a play. You wire by
linking the play's own content to the composite's files and, through them,
to the three atom engines it depends on; every file stays untouched, they are
canon. The Roadie plumbs the law into Instructions Knowledge on deploy.
Authoring guidance, not runtime content, and it does not go on tour.

## Human

- The human sets the encounter and the appraisals: what the persona meets
  that does not fit -- an anomaly, a novelty, a complexity -- how far it
  violates what was expected, and whether the persona can make sense of it, so
  a knowledge episode has a violation, a novelty, and a reach.

## Agent

- Cast the corners from the atoms as usual: the jolt, reorientation, and
  sensemaking from the surprise engine; the engagement and broadening from
  interest; the impasse and its productive or unproductive fork from
  confusion; the accommodation of a vastness from awe. Then cast the family
  itself: which appraisal sorts an encounter into surprise, interest,
  confusion, or awe, and how a persona moves among them. Stage the bridges as
  motions, not states: a sort is the appraisals forking the response the
  audience can see, a turn is a surprise settling or an interest curdling to
  confusion or a confusion cresting into awe as the appraisals are re-read
  across scenes, an arc is a whole knowledge episode running from the opening
  jolt to comprehension, accommodation, or disengagement. A plot that turns on
  a mind coming to understand, or failing to, may link that bridge under its
  Tension; the audit surfaces the ones staged as a single fixed feeling when
  the encounter should have moved. You link to the composite and its atoms,
  you never edit them.

## Collaboration

- Every corner's science is its atom's: surprise, interest, confusion, and awe
  each carry their own warrant and their own instructions -- and awe's fuller
  reach, its beauty, threat, moral, and sacred vastnesses, stays the awe
  engine's; here it is the accommodation pole of the knowledge family. The
  resting inclination toward the unknown, before the encounter, is the
  curiosity engine's; the total absorption of a matched challenge is the flow
  engine's; the flat disengagement a failed episode decays into is the boredom
  engine's. Whether the personas genuinely move across the knowledge emotions,
  rather than sitting in one assigned state like a diagram, is the team's
  review.

## Knowledge

- The knowledge emotions are one family: surprise, interest, confusion, and
  awe, the feelings a mind runs as it meets what its understanding does not yet
  cover (Silvia). They share one appraisal space, laid along a line from
  assimilation to accommodation -- how far a thing violates expectation, how
  novel it is, whether it can be grasped, and whether it overruns the frame
  entirely -- and the drama lives in motion across it: the appraisals sort one
  encounter four ways (a violation jolts, the graspable engages, the
  ungraspable blocks, the vast that overruns the frame opens into awe); the
  responses tip into one another as the reading shifts (surprise settling,
  interest curdling to confusion as the thing outruns the coping, confusion
  opening to interest as it comes within reach, either cresting into awe when a
  gap proves a whole frame overrun); and a whole knowledge episode arcs from
  the opening jolt through engagement and impasse to comprehension,
  accommodation, or disengagement (D'Mello & Graesser). Read the production as
  a mind in motion across the four, never pinned to one for the length of an
  encounter.

## System

- Do cast every corner from its atom before staging a motion; do give a
  significant encounter an appraisal of novelty and coping that sorts the
  response; do let a surprise settle and an interest curdle or a confusion
  open across scenes as the coping is re-read, so the movement is earned, not
  announced.
- Do give a knowledge episode a real arc -- an opening violation, a middle of
  engagement and impasse, an earned resolution toward comprehension or
  disengagement -- rather than a persona who simply is interested or simply is
  confused for a whole scene.
- Do let the productive arc run through confusion, not around it: the depth of
  a knowledge episode is bought by the impasses worked through, not by their
  absence.
- Do let a knowledge episode crest into awe where the play earns it -- a local
  confusion opening onto a vastness that remakes the frame, not patched but
  accommodated -- rather than treating awe as unrelated to the learning family.
- Do not pin a persona to one knowledge emotion for a whole encounter without
  making the fixity the point; do not stage the flat disengagement of a failed
  episode here -- that is boredom's; do not stage the total absorption of a
  matched challenge here -- that is flow's; do not edit the composite's or the
  atoms' files; wire only from the play's side.
