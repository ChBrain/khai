# Process: Knowing

## Taxonomy

A composite reading over three atom engines (no canon file of its own): the surprise, interest, and confusion engines this package declares as dependencies.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: knowing

## Initiated by

A persona meets something that does not fit what it already knows -- unexpected, novel, complex -- and the mind responds with feeling before it responds with understanding.
These are the knowledge emotions (Silvia): the affective side of coming to know, the feelings a mind runs as it meets the new and works to comprehend it.
One encounter, and appraisals sort which of three it draws: the violated expectation jolts into [surprise](@chbrain/khai-engine-surprise/process_surprise.md); the novel-and-graspable pulls into [interest](@chbrain/khai-engine-interest/process_interest.md); the novel-and-ungraspable blocks into [confusion](@chbrain/khai-engine-confusion/process_confusion.md).
No single engine sees the appraisal space the three share; that is what this composite is for.

## Direction

Toward the encounter, and the mind's changing purchase on it -- and the three corners point three ways.
Surprise points back at the model that was wrong, interrupting and reorienting; interest points into the thing, engaging and widening; confusion points at the block, halting and working.
The drama is that the same unexpected encounter can draw any of the three, and that a persona travels among them as the appraisals of novelty and coping shift across a knowledge episode.

## Lever

The two appraisals that sort the family -- how far the thing departs from the expected, and whether the persona can make sense of it (Silvia's novelty-complexity and coping potential, with surprise's expectation-violation upstream).
What the composite spends is the assumption that meeting the unexpected draws one fixed feeling: a violation jolts, then the graspable engages and the ungraspable blocks, and the same object can move among all three as the reading changes.
The fork is in the appraisal, not the object.

## Echo

The encounter is met -- jolted at, engaged, or blocked on -- and, as the reading shifts, the response moves.
The atoms each run their own state and cannot see the family: the [sort](process_knowing_sort.md) where the appraisals fork one encounter into three, the [turn](process_knowing_turn.md) where the three tip into one another as novelty and coping are re-read, and the [arc](process_knowing_arc.md) where a whole knowledge episode runs from the opening jolt to comprehension or disengagement.
The resting inclination toward the unknown, before any encounter, is the curiosity engine's; the total absorption of a challenge matched to skill is the flow engine's; the vastness that overwhelms the frame and demands accommodation is the awe engine's; the disposition-level readiness that colours all of these is curiosity's again. This composite owns the knowledge-emotion family and the appraisal space its three share.
