# Process: Knowing

## Taxonomy

A composite reading over four atom engines (no canon file of its own): the surprise, interest, confusion, and awe engines this package declares as dependencies.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: knowing

## Initiated by

A persona meets something its current understanding does not yet cover -- unexpected, novel, complex, or vast -- and the mind responds with feeling before it responds with comprehension.
These are the knowledge emotions (Silvia): the affective side of coming to know, the feelings a mind runs as it meets what exceeds its frame and works to take it in.
One encounter, and appraisals sort which of four it draws: the violated expectation jolts into [surprise](@chbrain/khai-engine-surprise/process_surprise.md); the novel-and-graspable pulls into [interest](@chbrain/khai-engine-interest/process_interest.md); the novel-and-ungraspable blocks into [confusion](@chbrain/khai-engine-confusion/process_confusion.md); the vast-that-overruns-the-whole-frame opens into [awe](@chbrain/khai-engine-awe/process_awe.md).
No single engine sees the appraisal space the four share; that is what this composite is for.

## Direction

Toward the encounter, and the mind's changing purchase on it -- and the four corners point four ways.
Surprise points back at the model that was wrong, interrupting and reorienting; interest points into the thing, engaging and assimilating; confusion points at the block, halting and working a piece that will not fit; awe points past the frame itself, accommodating a vastness the frame cannot hold.
The drama is that the same encounter with what exceeds understanding can draw any of the four, and that a persona travels among them as the appraisals shift across a knowledge episode.

## Lever

The appraisals that sort the family, laid along one line from assimilation to accommodation.
A violated expectation flags the mismatch (surprise); then novelty-complexity and coping potential fork what follows -- the graspable assimilates into the existing frame (interest), a piece that will not assimilate blocks it (confusion), and a vastness that overruns the frame entirely forces the frame itself to change (awe, the demand for accommodation).
What the composite spends is the assumption that meeting what exceeds understanding draws one fixed feeling: the fork is in the appraisal -- how far the thing departs, whether it can be taken in, and whether it overruns the frame -- not in the object.

## Echo

The encounter is met -- jolted at, engaged, blocked on, or accommodated -- and, as the reading shifts, the response moves.
The atoms each run their own state and cannot see the family: the [sort](process_knowing_sort.md) where the appraisals fork one encounter into four, the [turn](process_knowing_turn.md) where the four tip into one another as novelty, coping, and vastness are re-read, and the [arc](process_knowing_arc.md) where a whole knowledge episode runs from the opening jolt to comprehension, accommodation, or disengagement.
The resting inclination toward the unknown, before any encounter, is the curiosity engine's; the total absorption of a challenge matched to skill is the flow engine's; the flat disengagement a failed episode decays into is the boredom engine's. This composite owns the knowledge-emotion family and the appraisal space its four share, from the smallest mismatch to the vastness that remakes the frame.
