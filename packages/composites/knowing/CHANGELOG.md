### Patch Changes

- 4ffcf66: Add the knowing composite -- the knowledge emotions read as one family. Surprise, interest, confusion, and awe are the feelings a mind runs as it meets what its understanding does not yet cover, and they share one appraisal space, laid along a line from assimilation to accommodation: how far a thing violates expectation, how novel it is, whether it can be grasped, and whether it overruns the frame entirely. The composite authors no emotion of its own: it declares the `surprise`, `interest`, `confusion`, and `awe` engines as dependencies, wires their members by hard package links, and adds only the shared axis and the three motions across it.

  - **Root:** `knowing` -- the family as one reckoning with what exceeds understanding, sorted by appraisal into surprise (violation → jolt), interest (novel + graspable → assimilate), confusion (novel + ungraspable → block), or awe (vast + overruns the frame → accommodate).
  - **Bridges (the drama the atoms cannot stage alone):** `knowing, sort` (one encounter forked four ways by expectation-violation and the novelty/coping/scale appraisals -- Silvia's structure, Keltner & Haidt on accommodation, Meyer et al. on surprise), `knowing, turn` (the four tipping into one another as the appraisals shift -- surprise settling, interest curdling to confusion, confusion opening to interest, either cresting into awe -- D'Mello & Graesser's transitions), `knowing, arc` (the trajectory of a whole knowledge episode from the opening jolt through engagement and impasse to comprehension, accommodation, or disengagement -- the affect dynamics of learning, and why the productive arc runs _through_ confusion, not around it).

  The capstone over Silvia's knowledge-emotions quartet, now that `surprise`, `interest`, `confusion`, and `awe` all stand as engines. The Piagetian line makes the family legible: interest is where the novel assimilates into the frame, confusion where a piece will not, awe where a vastness overruns the frame and forces it to change, and surprise the violation that flags the mismatch upstream. Warranted (LORE, integrative) on Silvia (the knowledge emotions and their appraisal structure), D'Mello & Graesser (the affect dynamics of learning), Keltner & Haidt (awe's need-for-accommodation appraisal), Meyer/Reisenzein/Schützwohl (surprise's expectation-violation), Pekrun (epistemic emotions), and D'Mello et al. 2014 (confusion beneficial for learning). Bounded against `curiosity` (the resting disposition beneath the episodic emotions), `flow` (total absorption of a matched challenge), and `boredom` (the flat state the failed arc decays into); awe's fuller reach -- beauty, threat, moral, sacred -- stays the awe engine's, this composite reading only its place as the accommodation pole. Set at patch as the free level. No whitelist required -- the `knowing` root and `knowing_*` bridge stems are unique.
