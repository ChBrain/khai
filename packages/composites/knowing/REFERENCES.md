# Knowing: Reference

## Line of Work

The knowledge emotions as **composite**: surprise, interest, confusion, and awe, the feelings a mind runs as it meets what its understanding does not yet cover, read as one family sharing an appraisal space. No single atom engine sees the family, because each authors one emotion and the shared axis is a property of the set. The jolt at a violated expectation is the surprise engine's; the engagement with the new-yet-graspable is the interest engine's; the impasse of the not-yet-comprehended is the confusion engine's; the accommodation of a vastness that overruns the frame is the awe engine's. This package authors none of those again. It declares the four engines as dependencies, wires their members by hard package links, and adds only what the atoms cannot hold: the appraisal space the four share -- laid along one line from assimilation to accommodation, with expectation-violation upstream -- and the three motions across it, the sort where the appraisals fork one encounter four ways, the turn where the responses tip into one another as the appraisals shift, and the arc where a whole knowledge episode runs from the opening jolt to comprehension, accommodation, or disengagement.

The warrant is integrative, not primary: the sources below establish that these emotions form a family with a common appraisal structure, not the individual emotions, which their own engines warrant. The spine is Silvia's account of the knowledge emotions -- interest, confusion, surprise, and awe as a set of emotions about knowledge, tied together by appraisals of novelty-complexity, comprehensibility (coping potential), and the demand for accommodation. The family runs a line the Piagetian frame makes legible: interest is where the novel assimilates into the existing frame, confusion where a piece will not assimilate, awe where a vastness overruns the frame and forces the frame itself to change (Keltner & Haidt's need-for-accommodation appraisal), and surprise the expectation-violation that flags the mismatch upstream of them all. The dynamics are D'Mello and Graesser's: the affective states of learning succeed one another in a characteristic sequence -- a jolt, engagement or impasse, and resolution, accommodation, or disengagement -- so that a knowledge episode has an arc, not a steady state. It is the knowledge-emotion counterpart within the affect family: where each atom engine owns one feeling of coming-to-know, this composite reads the appraisal space they share and the episode they compose.

## Restrictions

What the composite refuses to model, and to whom it delegates.

- **The four atoms**: The composite authors no emotion of its own. Surprise -- the jolt at a violated expectation, and the reorienting sensemaking that follows -- is the surprise engine's, whole; interest -- the engagement with the new-yet-graspable -- is the interest engine's; confusion -- the impasse of the not-yet-comprehended -- is the confusion engine's; awe -- the accommodation of a vastness that overruns the frame -- is the awe engine's. This package reads over them and adds only the shared appraisal space and its motions; every corner is an atom's ground, cited by hard link, never restated. Awe's fuller reach -- the beauty, threat, moral, and sacred vastnesses it runs -- stays the awe engine's; this composite reads only awe's place in the knowledge-emotion family, the accommodation pole opposite interest's assimilation.
- **Curiosity**: The composite reads the episodic knowledge emotions, not the resting disposition beneath them. Curiosity -- the standing inclination toward the unknown, before any encounter -- is owned by the curiosity engine (as a position); this composite reads the emotions that fire when an encounter is met, the states the disposition readies for, not the readiness itself.
- **Flow**: The composite reads the family of coming-to-know, not the total absorption of a matched challenge. Flow -- action and awareness merged, self-consciousness gone -- is owned by the flow engine; a knowledge episode's sustained interest can feed flow but is not it, and the composite hands the absorption across.
- **Boredom**: The composite reads the knowledge episode up to its decay, not the flat state past it. Boredom -- the unengaged mind seeking any target -- is owned by the boredom engine; the unproductive arc decays toward boredom, and the composite owns the sequence up to the disengagement, handing the flat state that follows across.
- **Learning and reasoning**: The composite models the emotions of a knowledge episode, not the cognition that runs it. The actual mechanisms of comprehension, inference, and skill acquisition are owned by the learning and reasoning engines where those exist; this composite supplies the affective arc, not the machinery of the knowing.

Source to constraint, per file.

- **[knowing](process_knowing.md)** (the root): The knowledge emotions as one family -- surprise, interest, confusion, awe -- sharing the appraisal space from a violated expectation to a vastness that demands accommodation. Anchored by Silvia (the knowledge emotions) and D'Mello & Graesser (the dynamics).
- **[knowing, sort](process_knowing_sort.md)** (bridge): One encounter forked four ways, by expectation-violation and the novelty, coping, and scale appraisals. Anchored by Silvia (the appraisal structure), Keltner & Haidt (accommodation), and Meyer et al. (surprise's violation).
- **[knowing, turn](process_knowing_turn.md)** (bridge): The four tipping into one another as the appraisals shift -- surprise settling, interest curdling to confusion, confusion opening to interest, either cresting into awe. Anchored by D'Mello & Graesser (the transitions).
- **[knowing, arc](process_knowing_arc.md)** (bridge): The trajectory of a whole knowledge episode -- the opening jolt, the middle of engagement and impasse, and the resolution toward comprehension, accommodation, or disengagement. Anchored by D'Mello & Graesser (the affect dynamics) and D'Mello et al. (confusion beneficial for learning).
