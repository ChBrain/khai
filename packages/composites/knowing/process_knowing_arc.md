# Process: Knowing, Arc

## Taxonomy

[Knowing](process_knowing.md), the bridge where the four sequence into a whole episode.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: knowing

## Initiated by

A stretch of coming-to-know long enough to have a shape.
The atoms fire in the moment -- [surprise](@chbrain/khai-engine-surprise/process_surprise.md) at the violation, [interest](@chbrain/khai-engine-interest/process_interest.md) at the graspable, [confusion](@chbrain/khai-engine-confusion/process_confusion.md) at the block, [awe](@chbrain/khai-engine-awe/process_awe.md) at the vastness; none of them sees the sequence they compose across a learning episode.
A knowledge episode has a characteristic trajectory (D'Mello & Graesser's affect dynamics of learning): a jolt of surprise opens it, interest or confusion carries the middle, and it lands either in the deep comprehension a worked-through confusion yields, the accommodation an awe forces, or the disengagement a stalled confusion decays into.

## Direction

Across the episode, from the opening jolt toward one of its ends.
The arc runs a course, not a moment: surprise breaks the smooth flow, the appraisal sends the persona into interest or confusion, the turns carry them back and forth as they work the material, and the whole resolves -- toward comprehension if the confusions are broken and the interest sustained, toward accommodation if the material proves vast enough to remake the frame, toward disengagement if the impasses stall and the interest flattens to boredom.
Its direction is the shape of the whole, the sequence the single emotions cannot hold.

## Lever

What the arc spends -- the idea that a knowledge episode is one steady state.
Learning is not a mood but a sequence, and the arc is where the composite reads it as one: the productive episode is not confusion-free but confusion-resolved, its depth bought by the impasses worked through, and its largest turns are the moments a local confusion opens onto a vastness and crests into awe, the frame not patched but remade.
A production holds this lever by giving a knowledge episode a real trajectory -- an opening violation, a middle of engagement and impasse, and an earned resolution -- rather than a persona who simply is interested, or confused, or awed, for the length of a scene.

## Echo

The episode resolves -- into an understanding deeper than it began with, a frame remade to hold what overran it, or a mind that has given the material up.
Read whole, the arc is why the same difficulty is a breakthrough in one episode and an abandonment in another: not the difficulty, but the sequence of responses to it, and whether the confusions along the way were worked, abandoned, or opened onto something vast.
The single crossings that make up the arc are the [turn](process_knowing_turn.md); the appraisal that sets each response the arc strings together is the [sort](process_knowing_sort.md); the flat disengagement the failed arc decays into is the boredom engine's, downstream of where this composite hands off.
