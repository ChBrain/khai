# Process: Knowing, Turn

## Taxonomy

[Knowing](process_knowing.md), the bridge where the three tip into one another.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: knowing

## Initiated by

A knowledge response that will not hold its place as the appraisals shift beneath it.
The atoms read their own states -- [surprise](@chbrain/khai-engine-surprise/process_surprise.md)'s jolt, [interest](@chbrain/khai-engine-interest/process_interest.md)'s engagement, [confusion](@chbrain/khai-engine-confusion/process_confusion.md)'s block; what they cannot read is one becoming the next.
Surprise settles into interest when the violation proves graspable, or into confusion when it does not; interest curdles to confusion as the thing outruns the coping; and confusion resolves back to interest as the impasse comes within reach -- each turn a re-reading of novelty or coping made live.

## Direction

Among the three, both ways -- the family in motion, not the darkening slope of the harsher composites.
Unlike the one-way souring of admiration into contempt, the knowing turn runs in both directions: coping rising carries confusion up into interest, coping falling carries interest down into confusion, and the initial surprise can resolve either way.
Its direction is wherever the appraisal moves next -- the response caught mid-crossing as the reading changes.

## Lever

What the turn spends -- the expectation that a knowledge response, once set, stays.
Each crossing is legible only against the state it came from: interest that hardens into confusion carries the memory of the engagement it lost, and confusion that opens into interest the relief of the block that broke.
A production holds this lever by choosing when the coping appraisal shifts -- the moment the graspable proves too hard, or the impasse suddenly yields -- and letting the prior state still colour the new one, so the turn reads as a mind's purchase changing, not a new feeling appearing.

## Echo

The response has crossed -- and the mind now runs on a different knowledge emotion than it began with, interest gone to confusion or confusion opened to interest, the encounter the same but the reading of it moved.
Whoever clocks only the endpoint misses the crossing that shaped it, the way a confusion resolved is not the same as an interest that never met a block.
Strung across a whole episode, these turns are the [arc](process_knowing_arc.md); the still fork that sets the first response, before any crossing, is the [sort](process_knowing_sort.md).
