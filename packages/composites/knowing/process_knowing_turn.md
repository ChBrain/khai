# Process: Knowing, Turn

## Taxonomy

[Knowing](process_knowing.md), the bridge where the four tip into one another.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: knowing

## Initiated by

A knowledge response that will not hold its place as the appraisals shift beneath it.
The atoms read their own states -- [surprise](@chbrain/khai-engine-surprise/process_surprise.md)'s jolt, [interest](@chbrain/khai-engine-interest/process_interest.md)'s engagement, [confusion](@chbrain/khai-engine-confusion/process_confusion.md)'s block, [awe](@chbrain/khai-engine-awe/process_awe.md)'s accommodation; what none can read is one becoming the next.
Surprise settles into interest when the violation proves graspable or into confusion when it does not; interest curdles to confusion as the thing outruns the coping; confusion resolves back to interest as the impasse comes within reach; and interest or confusion crests into awe when what seemed a graspable thing or a local gap proves instead a vastness that overruns the whole frame.

## Direction

Among the four, most ways -- the family in motion, not the darkening slope of the harsher composites.
Unlike the one-way souring of admiration into contempt, the knowing turn runs in many directions: coping rising carries confusion up into interest, coping falling carries interest down into confusion, scale opening carries either up into awe, and the initial surprise can resolve toward any of the three.
Its direction is wherever the appraisal moves next -- the response caught mid-crossing as the reading of novelty, coping, or scale changes.

## Lever

What the turn spends -- the expectation that a knowledge response, once set, stays.
Each crossing is legible only against the state it came from: interest that hardens into confusion carries the memory of the engagement it lost, confusion that opens into interest the relief of the block that broke, and interest or confusion that crests into awe the moment the frame gave way to something larger than the question first posed.
A production holds this lever by choosing when the appraisal shifts -- the moment the graspable proves too hard, the impasse suddenly yields, or the thing turns out to be vast -- and letting the prior state still colour the new one, so the turn reads as a mind's purchase changing, not a new feeling appearing.

## Echo

The response has crossed -- and the mind now runs on a different knowledge emotion than it began with, interest gone to confusion, confusion opened to interest, or either lifted into awe, the encounter the same but the reading of it moved.
Whoever clocks only the endpoint misses the crossing that shaped it, the way a confusion that crests into awe is not the same as an awe met head-on.
Strung across a whole episode, these turns are the [arc](process_knowing_arc.md); the still fork that sets the first response, before any crossing, is the [sort](process_knowing_sort.md).
