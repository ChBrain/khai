# Process: Knowing, Sort

## Taxonomy

[Knowing](process_knowing.md), the bridge where one encounter is met three ways.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: knowing

## Initiated by

A single encounter with the unexpected that more than one response could meet.
The atoms each answer as if their response were given -- [surprise](@chbrain/khai-engine-surprise/process_surprise.md) jolts, [interest](@chbrain/khai-engine-interest/process_interest.md) engages, [confusion](@chbrain/khai-engine-confusion/process_confusion.md) blocks -- but which meets a given encounter is not given, it is sorted.
The appraisals do the sorting (Silvia): a violated expectation draws the surprise jolt first; then novelty-complexity and coping potential fork what follows -- novel and graspable draws interest, novel and ungraspable draws confusion.

## Direction

From the encounter to a response, by the persona's reading of novelty and coping.
The same anomaly draws surprise from the one whose model it violates, interest from the one who finds it new and within reach, and confusion from the one who finds it new and beyond reach -- and often surprise first, then one of the other two as the coping appraisal lands.
The direction is set at the fork, and the fork is in the appraisal.

## Lever

What the sort exposes -- that the response was never in the encounter but in the reading brought to it.
The composite spends the illusion that meeting the unexpected dictates one feeling: hold the thing graspable and interest fires, read it as beyond reach and confusion does, and let it merely violate an expectation and surprise does, briefly, before the coping appraisal sends it on.
Where the outdone composite's reading turns on desert and reach, the knowing sort turns on novelty and coping -- the two appraisals that place any encounter in the knowledge-emotion space.

## Echo

The encounter is met in the key the appraisals set -- and a production that stages the sort, rather than fixing one response, shows why the same anomaly lands differently on different minds: a jolt to one, a fascination to another, a wall to a third, each reading the novelty and the coping their own way.
The reasoned judgment of whether the thing truly is graspable is beyond this bridge; it owns only that the response follows the appraisal.
When the coping is read as open, the sort tends toward interest and the [turn](process_knowing_turn.md) can still run either way; when it closes, confusion takes it, and the darkening toward disengagement can begin.
