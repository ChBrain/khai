# Process: Knowing, Sort

## Taxonomy

[Knowing](process_knowing.md), the bridge where one encounter is met four ways.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: knowing

## Initiated by

A single encounter with what exceeds understanding that more than one response could meet.
The atoms each answer as if their response were given -- [surprise](@chbrain/khai-engine-surprise/process_surprise.md) jolts, [interest](@chbrain/khai-engine-interest/process_interest.md) engages, [confusion](@chbrain/khai-engine-confusion/process_confusion.md) blocks, [awe](@chbrain/khai-engine-awe/process_awe.md) accommodates -- but which meets a given encounter is not given, it is sorted.
The appraisals do the sorting (Silvia): a violated expectation draws the surprise jolt first; then novelty, coping, and scale fork what follows -- novel and graspable draws interest, novel and ungraspable draws confusion, and vast enough to overrun the whole frame draws awe.

## Direction

From the encounter to a response, laid along the line from assimilation to accommodation.
The same anomaly draws surprise from the one whose model it violates, interest from the one who takes it into their frame, confusion from the one for whom a piece will not fit, and awe from the one for whom the whole frame is overrun -- and often surprise first, then one of the other three as the deeper appraisal lands.
The direction is set at the fork, and the fork is in the appraisal.

## Lever

What the sort exposes -- that the response was never in the encounter but in the reading brought to it.
The composite spends the illusion that meeting what exceeds understanding dictates one feeling: hold the thing graspable and interest fires, read it as a piece that will not fit and confusion does, meet it as a vastness that overruns the frame and awe does, and let it merely violate an expectation and surprise does, briefly, before the deeper appraisal sends it on.
Where the outdone composite's reading turns on desert and reach, the knowing sort turns on novelty, coping, and scale -- the appraisals that place any encounter along the line from what the frame can take in to what remakes it.

## Echo

The encounter is met in the key the appraisals set -- and a production that stages the sort, rather than fixing one response, shows why the same thing lands differently on different minds: a jolt to one, a fascination to another, a wall to a third, a vastness that humbles a fourth, each reading the novelty, coping, and scale their own way.
The reasoned judgment of whether the thing truly is graspable, or truly vast, is beyond this bridge; it owns only that the response follows the appraisal.
When the coping is read as open, the sort tends toward interest and the [turn](process_knowing_turn.md) can still run any way; when a piece will not fit, confusion takes it; when the frame itself is overrun, awe does.
