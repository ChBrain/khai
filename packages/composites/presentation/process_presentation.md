# Process: Presentation

## Taxonomy

The self performed before an audience.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: presentation

## Initiated by

A persona is read not as simply being itself in company but as performing a self before an audience, and the performing is work.
Presentation starts wherever a persona puts a self before others -- the [face](@chbrain/khai-engine-face/process_face.md) it claims in interaction, the [frontstage](@chbrain/khai-engine-backstage/place_frontstage.md) region where that claim must be held, the [self-monitoring](@chbrain/khai-engine-self-monitoring/position_self_monitoring.md) by which it watches and tunes the display, and the [emotional labor](@chbrain/khai-engine-emotional-labor/process_emotional_labor.md) it spends to feel or feign what the part demands.
This composite adds no fifth engine; it reads what the four make together: self-presentation as labour rather than display, a self held up before others at a cost.

## Direction

Toward the whole labour of being seen, read through its parts.
The composite wires the four engines and reads their working in three shapes: [the Front](process_presentation_front.md), the face claimed and held; [the Tuning](process_presentation_tuning.md), the watching and adjusting that fits it to the room; and [the Cost](process_presentation_cost.md), the twist -- the feeling managed to sustain it, and what that management takes.
A persona before others is not merely seen: it claims an image, holds it in a region where it can be seen, adjusts it to the room, and pays in managed feeling to keep it up.

## Lever

The self held up before an audience against the effort that holding it demands.
The carrying force is that a persona must be someone in company -- an image is claimed the moment it is seen, and the claim commits it to sustaining what it has put forward; the resistance is the labour of the sustaining, the watching, the adjusting, the feeling worked up or worked down, and the region needed to set it all down.
Presentation is read wherever a persona is granted its performance: not a self simply on display but a self at work, doing something to be seen as it means to be.

## Echo

The persona revealed as a self at work before an audience.
Presentation does not close; it is the persona going on performing, gathering the four engines into one reading -- a self claiming a face, holding a region, tuning to the room, and paying in feeling.
It hands off what it is not: the truth-status of what a self lets through -- withheld, feigned, or revealed -- is the opacity composite's; whether the self is true to itself is the authenticity engine's, read in the self-relation composite; the rank the performance competes for is the standing composite's; the felt quality of a connection working is the rapport composite's; and the performance made to stand for a story is authored key, the play's pitch, not the force.
The persona is dated from its own performing, and read as a self that must be someone before others, and works to be.
