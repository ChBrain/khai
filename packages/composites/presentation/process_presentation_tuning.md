# Process: Presentation, the Tuning

## Taxonomy

[Presentation](process_presentation.md), the part where the performance is watched and fitted to the room.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: presentation

## Initiated by

The persona reads the room and adjusts what it is showing -- or does not, and shows the same self everywhere.
The Tuning starts wherever [self-monitoring](@chbrain/khai-engine-self-monitoring/position_self_monitoring.md) governs the display -- the [scanning](@chbrain/khai-engine-self-monitoring/position_scanning.md) of the situation for what it calls for and the [tuning](@chbrain/khai-engine-self-monitoring/position_tuning.md) of the performance to fit it, against the [consistency](@chbrain/khai-engine-self-monitoring/position_consistency.md) of a self let to flow the same from room to room.
It is the fit: not the image claimed nor what sustaining it costs, but how much the persona watches and adjusts.

## Direction

Toward the performance fitted to the room, or held constant against it.
The Tuning reads the persona at the dial -- the high self-monitor reading what each room rewards and becoming it, fluent and different in every company; the low one letting the same self show regardless, legible and unchanging and sometimes wrong for the room.
It moves the persona from a claimed image to a calibrated one: the same self shown differently by audience, or the same self shown identically and left to fit or not.

## Lever

The fit won by adjusting against the coherence lost in adjusting.
The carrying force is that a performance tuned to its audience lands -- the room read, the register matched, the self shown in the form this company can receive; the resistance is what the tuning costs in coherence, a persona so fitted to each room that no room sees the same self, against a persona so constant that some rooms cannot receive it at all.
The Tuning is presentation at its most adaptive: the dial between fitting the room and being the same person in every room, each setting buying one at the other's expense.

## Echo

The performance fitted to its audience, or held the same across them.
The Tuning leaves the persona calibrated -- scanning and adjusting, or flowing constant -- and carries the presentation's shared shape: a self at work before others, here the work of fitting.
It is the calibration of the performance: how much a persona lets the room decide what it shows.
The Tuning is dated from the reading, the moment the persona took the room's measure and decided how much to become what it asked for.
