# Presentation: Reference

## Line of Work

Presentation as **composite**: the self performed before an audience, read as labour rather
than display. It is not a new force but a wiring of four: the **face** engine supplies the
image claimed in interaction and the redress that protects it; the **backstage** engine
supplies the regions -- the frontstage where the claim must be held and the backstage where
it can be dropped; the **self-monitoring** engine supplies the watching and tuning that fits
the performance to the room; and the **emotional-labor** engine supplies the management of
feeling that sustains it. This composite adds no fifth engine; it reads what the four make
together -- a self held up before others at a cost -- and stages that into three parts of
the work, closing on the discovery that the performance which succeeds best is the one that
reaches deepest into the performer.

The domain does not model any single force -- the claimed image and its redress are the face
engine's, the regions are backstage's, the calibration is self-monitoring's, the managed
feeling is emotional-labor's, and one alone is a process, place, or position, not the whole
labour. Nor is it the truth-status of what a self lets through -- withheld, feigned, or
revealed -- which is the opacity composite's; nor whether a self is true to itself, which is
the authenticity engine's, read in the self-relation composite; nor the rank a performance
competes for, which is the standing composite's; nor the felt quality of a connection
working, which is the rapport composite's. It owns the performance itself: the persona at
work being seen, read not as a self simply on display but as one doing something to be taken
as it means to be.

The spine is the dramaturgical reading of social life -- the self as something performed
rather than merely possessed. Goffman set the stage: the presentation of self as a
performance before an audience, with front and back regions, a claimed line the performer is
committed to, and the constant risk of the discrepant fact. Brown and Levinson formalised
the claim itself -- face as positive and negative want, and the redress a speaker offers
when an act threatens it. Snyder read the dial -- self-monitoring as the characteristic
degree to which a person scans the situation and tunes the performance, against a self let to
flow constant. And Hochschild read the cost and the composite's turn: emotional labour, the
management of feeling for a wage or a part, surface acting that strains and deep acting that
succeeds by making the feeling real -- and the self-estrangement that follows when the work
reaches the heart it was meant only to cover. The three bridges are those readings staged:
the front, the tuning, and the cost.

## Origin

The dramaturgy and sociology of self-presentation, wired as a composite over the face,
backstage, self-monitoring, and emotional-labor engines.

| :------------------------------------ | :----------------------------------------------------- | :--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Erving Goffman**                    | _The Presentation of Self in Everyday Life_ (1959)     | The self as performance before an audience, front and back regions, the line claimed and the risk of its breaking. The root and the front bridge.                |

## Restrictions

What the composite refuses to model, and to whom it delegates.

- **The single engines (the atoms)**: The composite wires the four but owns none. The claimed
  image and its redress -- deference, solidarity, hint, forbearance, bald -- is the face
  engine's; the frontstage, backstage, and outside regions are backstage's; the scanning,
  tuning, and consistency of the display are self-monitoring's; the masking, method, and
  genuine feeling are emotional-labor's. Presentation reads only what the four make as one
  labour; a single engine alone is a process, place, or position, not the whole.
- **The truth-status of what is let through (opacity)**: Whether the self shown is withheld,
  feigned, or revealed -- the secret kept, the false belief built, the true self opened -- is
  the opacity composite's. Presentation is the performance itself and what it costs, not the
  question of whether what is shown is true; a wholly sincere performance is still work.
- **Whether the self is true to itself (authenticity / self-relation)**: The persona's fidelity
  to its own values and feeling is the authenticity engine's, read in the self-relation
  composite. Presentation reads the labour of being seen; whether the performer is betraying
  or expressing itself in the performing is authenticity's judgment, not this composite's.
- **The rank the performance competes for (standing)**: The capital, hierarchy, power, status,
  and status-moves a persona is ranked by are the standing composite's. Presentation is how a
  self is put before others, not the position it wins by it.
- **The quality of the connection (rapport)**: The felt sense of a connection working --
  synchrony, grounding, mutual disclosure -- is the rapport composite's. Presentation is one
  side's labour of showing, not the dyad's achieved rapport.
- **The performance as authored symbol (the play's pitch)**: A persona's self-presentation made
  to **stand for** a story -- the mask that mirrors a theme of falseness, the emotional labour
  that mirrors a moral about work -- is refused: that is authored key, the play's pitch, not
  the force on the persona. Presentation delivers the persona's own labour of being seen; the
  meaning read into it is the play's.

## Encoding

Source to constraint. A composite root over four engine atoms it declares as dependencies,
with three bridges, the parts of the labour of being seen.

- **[presentation](process_presentation.md)** (the root): the self performed before an audience
  -- the face claimed, the region held, the display tuned, the feeling managed. Anchored by
  Goffman (the presentation of self).
- **[the front](process_presentation_front.md)** (bridge): the image claimed and defended, held
  on the frontstage where the audience can see. Anchored by Goffman (regions) and Brown &
  Levinson (face and redress).
- **[the tuning](process_presentation_tuning.md)** (bridge): the watching and adjusting that
  fits the performance to the room, against a self let to flow constant. Anchored by Snyder
  (self-monitoring).
- **[the cost](process_presentation_cost.md)** (bridge): the feeling managed to sustain the
  display -- masked on the surface or induced until real -- and the backstage that would give
  the self back. Anchored by Hochschild; the composite's twist and keystone, that the mask worn
  deeply enough stops being a mask.
