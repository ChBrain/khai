# Process: Presentation, the Cost

## Taxonomy

[Presentation](process_presentation.md), the part where the feeling is managed to sustain the display, and the mask reaches the self.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: presentation

## Initiated by

The persona must not only show the right face but feel, or seem to feel, what the part demands -- and the seeming is work on its own heart.
The Cost starts wherever [emotional labor](@chbrain/khai-engine-emotional-labor/process_emotional_labor.md) sustains the display -- the [masking](@chbrain/khai-engine-emotional-labor/process_masking.md) that paints the required feeling on a face that does not hold it, the [method](@chbrain/khai-engine-emotional-labor/process_method.md) that induces the feeling until it is really there, and the [genuine](@chbrain/khai-engine-emotional-labor/process_genuine.md) moment when what is displayed is simply felt -- with the [backstage](@chbrain/khai-engine-backstage/place_backstage.md) as the region where it can all be set down.
It is what the performance takes: the management of the heart that keeps the front standing.

## Direction

Toward the feeling worked into place, and the self that the working reaches.
The Cost reads the persona managing its own heart for the part -- surface work that strains because the face and the feeling disagree, and deep work that resolves the strain by making the feeling real -- and the backstage where the performance is meant to be dropped and the self returned.
It moves the persona from displaying a feeling to having one on demand: the labour succeeding exactly to the degree it stops being labour, and the backstage shrinking as the part reaches further in.

## Lever

The mask that must convince against the self that convincing it costs.
The carrying force is that a role demands a feeling, and the persona can produce it -- painting it on, or inducing it until it is genuinely there; the resistance is that the two ways fail differently: the mask that stays a mask strains and shows and exhausts, while the mask that works reaches into the heart it was covering, so the persona that performs warmth well enough is no longer sure which warmth is its own, and the backstage that was to give the self back has less and less self waiting in it.
The Cost rides the composite's keystone knife-edge: the performance that succeeds best is the one that reaches deepest -- the mask worn deeply enough stops being a mask, and what makes the display convincing is precisely what makes it no longer a display.

## Echo

The feeling managed until it is real, and the self less separable from the part.
It shows a persona working its own heart to hold up a front -- masking, inducing, sometimes simply feeling -- and carries beneath it the composite's keystone: that self-presentation is labour paid in feeling, and its best success is a kind of loss, the performer no longer able to find the line between the part and the person.
The Cost is the note the whole composite closes on: presentation is not a costume put on and taken off but work that reaches the worker, and the backstage where the self would be returned is only as large as the part has left it.
The Cost is dated from the managing, the moment the persona began working on its own feeling to keep the front standing.
