# Process: Presentation, the Front

## Taxonomy

[Presentation](process_presentation.md), the part where a self claims a face and holds it before an audience.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: presentation

## Initiated by

The persona puts an image of itself before others and is thereafter committed to it.
The Front starts wherever a persona claims a [face](@chbrain/khai-engine-face/process_face.md) in interaction -- the want to be approved of and the want to be unimpeded -- and works to protect it and the faces of others, softening a threat with [deference](@chbrain/khai-engine-face/process_deference.md), [solidarity](@chbrain/khai-engine-face/process_solidarity.md), or a [hint](@chbrain/khai-engine-face/process_hint.md), [forbearing](@chbrain/khai-engine-face/process_forbear.md) the act altogether, or going [bald](@chbrain/khai-engine-face/process_bald.md) and letting it land; and it is held on the [frontstage](@chbrain/khai-engine-backstage/place_frontstage.md), the region where the audience can see.
It is the claim: the image put forward, and the region in which it must be maintained.

## Direction

Toward the image claimed and defended, wherever the audience can see.
The Front reads the persona holding its claim -- each act that could threaten a face redressed or risked, each stretch of frontstage a stretch where the performance cannot lapse -- the claim standing only as long as it is maintained before those who can see it.
It moves the persona from unseen to committed: an image put forward that others now hold it to, and a region in which it must go on being who it said it was.

## Lever

The image a persona claims against the constant threat of its being contradicted.
The carrying force is that a self in company must claim something, and the claim once made is a standing commitment others will read it against; the resistance is everything that would break it -- the act that threatens a face, the slip in the frontstage, the moment the claimed image and the visible conduct do not match.
The Front is presentation at its most exposed: the claim that gives a persona a self to be, and gives every witness a claim to catch it failing.

## Echo

The face claimed, defended, and held wherever it can be seen.
The Front leaves the persona committed to an image before its audience -- redressing what threatens it, holding the frontstage -- and hands that claim to the tuning that will fit it and the cost that will sustain it.
It is the claim of the performance: the self put forward, and the region in which the putting-forward cannot stop.
The Front is dated from the claim, the moment the persona put an image of itself before those who could see it.
