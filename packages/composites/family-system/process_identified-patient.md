# Process: Identified-patient

## Taxonomy

[Family-system](process_family-system.md), the bridge where one member carries the whole's tension as a symptom.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: family-system

## Initiated by

The family names one member the problem -- and that member is carrying the trouble of all.
The system's unspoken conflict concentrates in one: the child who acts out, the one who sickens, the one who fails, made by a [detour](@chbrain/khai-engine-triangulation/plot_detour.md) the place the couple's strain is sent, held there by a [double bind](@chbrain/khai-engine-double-bind/plot_double_bind.md) that forbids naming what is really wrong, and least able, from low [differentiation](@chbrain/khai-engine-differentiation/position_differentiation.md), to refuse the role.
This is the identified patient: the seam where the whole system speaks through one member, whose symptom is the family's and not their own alone.

## Direction

Inward onto one, the tension of all concentrated in the symptom-bearer.
The load converges by the atoms meeting on a single member: the [detour](@chbrain/khai-engine-triangulation/plot_detour.md) routes the couple's conflict onto them, the [double bind](@chbrain/khai-engine-double-bind/plot_no_metacomment.md) forbids the system from naming the real trouble so the symptom must speak it instead, and the member's fusion leaves them without a self solid enough to decline the assignment.
The direction is concentration: the family's diffuse, unspeakable strain gathered into one person's visible trouble, so the system has a problem it can point to that is not the problem it has.

## Lever

The coupling of all three atoms onto one member that none stages alone.
[Triangulation](@chbrain/khai-engine-triangulation/plot_triangulation.md) supplies the detour that selects the member; [double-bind](@chbrain/khai-engine-double-bind/plot_double_bind.md) supplies the ban on naming that makes the symptom necessary; [differentiation](@chbrain/khai-engine-differentiation/position_differentiation.md) supplies the low self-capacity that makes the member unable to refuse. The bridge is their convergence: the identified patient is produced by the whole system, so their symptom lifts only to return, or move to another member, unless the system that made it changes -- the reason treating the patient alone fails.
See the symptom as the system's and the family can change; see it as the patient's own and the family keeps its scapegoat and its balance both.

## Echo

One member carries the symptom, and the family calls them the problem they are only expressing.
Identified-patient leaves the system with its symptom-bearer -- the whole's tension worn by one, the family's trouble located in a person so it need not be faced as the system's -- and hands the reader the turn the whole tradition rests on: the patient is not sick apart from the family; the family is sick through the patient.
The convergence itself is the composite's; the detour is triangulation's, the bind double-bind's, the low self-capacity differentiation's. This bridge is their meeting on one member.
Identified-patient is dated from the naming, the moment the system fixes its trouble in one of its own.
