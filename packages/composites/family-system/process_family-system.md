# Process: Family-system

## Taxonomy

The household read as one emotional system -- a composite over triangulation, differentiation, and double-bind.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: family-system

## Initiated by

A household is read not as a set of separate people but as one emotional system, whose members' troubles are properties of the whole.
This is the family system: the insight that a person's symptom is not theirs alone but the system's, produced and held by the pattern of the family around them -- and it is never one thing but three running together, the [triangles](@chbrain/khai-engine-triangulation/plot_triangulation.md) that route the family's tension, the members' [differentiation](@chbrain/khai-engine-differentiation/position_differentiation.md) that holds or fails to hold a self within it, and the [binds](@chbrain/khai-engine-double-bind/plot_double_bind.md) a low-differentiated system lays on its own.
The composite adds no fourth engine. It wires the whole-system properties the three become: patterns inherited down the generations, a resistance to any member's change, and the one made to carry the tension of all.

## Direction

Toward the system holding its shape -- against every attempt, from inside or out, to change it.
Where the three run together, the family becomes a self-maintaining whole: the triangles bind the tension, the low differentiation reproduces it, the binds enforce it, and the system settles into a pattern that resists its own disturbance.
It is read the way the whole family-therapy tradition read it -- Bowen, Minuchin, Haley, Jackson -- the individual's trouble relocated into the system that makes and keeps it, so that to change the person you must change the system, which does not want to change.

## Lever

The three composing a whole no member can be understood apart from.
Triangulation routes the strain through thirds; differentiation sets how much each member can be a self against the pull; the double bind traps where the system forbids the naming of its own contradictions. Together they make the family a system with properties of its own -- an inheritance across generations, a homeostasis that resists change, an identified patient who carries the load -- none of which is any one atom's.
No single engine stages this: the family system lives in the seams, the whole the three become that none is alone.

## Echo

The family holds as a system -- its symptom the whole's, its shape defended against change.
The system runs as [inheritance](process_inheritance.md), [homeostasis](process_homeostasis.md), or [identified-patient](process_identified-patient.md) -- the pattern passed down the generations, the resistance to any member's change, and the one made to carry the tension of all.
It hands off what it is not: the emotions that run inside the system -- the jealousy, the grief, the guilt -- are those engines'; the socialization by which a child takes on the family's world is socialization's; the attachment bonds among its members are attachment's. This composite owns the system itself: the family as a unit that makes and keeps its members' troubles, and it is authored dramatic architecture for a play, never a diagnosis of a real family.
