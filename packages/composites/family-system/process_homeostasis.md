# Process: Homeostasis

## Taxonomy

[Family-system](process_family-system.md), the bridge where the system resists any member's change.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: family-system

## Initiated by

The family has a balance, and it defends it.
When any member changes -- differentiates, recovers, steps out of their assigned role -- the whole system registers the disturbance and pulls to restore the old equilibrium: the member who starts to hold a self is met with pressure to fuse back, the symptom that lifts is replaced, the [stabilizer](@chbrain/khai-engine-triangulation/plot_stabilizer.md) that was carrying the strain is not let go.
This is homeostasis: the seam where the system resists change, Jackson's family homeostasis -- the family behaving like a self-correcting whole that treats one member's growth as a threat to be neutralized.

## Direction

Back toward the old balance, against the member who would change it.
The resistance runs by the atoms defending the pattern: a member's move toward a [solid self](@chbrain/khai-engine-differentiation/position_solid_self.md) is answered by the [fusion](@chbrain/khai-engine-differentiation/position_fusion.md) pressure of those who need them fused -- the guilt, the crisis, the sudden need that pulls them back -- and the [triangle](@chbrain/khai-engine-triangulation/plot_triangulation.md) whose third was holding the tension will not release them, because the pair cannot yet take the strain back.
The direction is restoration: the change-back-to-change, the family that gets one member well only to make another the problem, keeping its total pattern constant.

## Lever

The coupling of the triangle's stabilizing and the members' fusion that neither stages alone.
[Triangulation](@chbrain/khai-engine-triangulation/plot_triangulation.md) shows one dyad steadied by one third; [differentiation](@chbrain/khai-engine-differentiation/position_differentiation.md) shows one member's capacity to hold a self. The bridge is the whole system's resistance built from both: the fused members pull the differentiating one back, and the triangles rebuild around any that dissolves, so the family as a whole holds its shape even as its parts shift -- a homeostasis no single triangle or single member's differentiation accounts for.
Push one member to change and the system pushes back; the family will spend its scapegoat, its crisis, its guilt to keep the balance it knows.

## Echo

The system holds its shape, the changing member pulled back into the pattern.
Homeostasis leaves the family self-restoring -- its equilibrium defended against growth, its old balance reasserted whenever a part tries to move -- so that lasting change requires changing the system, not the member, because the system will otherwise absorb or reverse the member's change.
It is the seam Jackson named: not one triangle's stabilizer and not one member's fusion, but the whole's resistance, the family as a system that keeps itself the same.
Homeostasis is dated from the disturbance, the moment a member's change calls out the system's defense of its balance.
