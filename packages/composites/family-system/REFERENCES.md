# Family-system: Reference

## Line of Work

Family-system as a **composite**: the household read as one emotional system rather than a
set of individuals, whose members' troubles are properties of the whole -- taken across the
system-level patterns the atoms compose. It wires over three atoms -- triangulation (the
three-party binds that route the system's tension), differentiation (the members' capacity to
hold a self within it), and double-bind (the communicational trap a low-differentiated system
lays on its own) -- and adds no fourth engine; its own content is the three whole-system
properties the atoms become that none is alone. It reads the emotions that run inside the
system (jealousy, grief, guilt), the socialization by which a child takes on the family's
world, and the attachment bonds among its members as neighbours it names but does not own.

The spine is the family-therapy tradition that relocated the individual's trouble into the
system around them. Bowen made the family an emotional unit and gave it a mechanism across
time -- the multigenerational transmission process, by which a family's differentiation level
and its triangles are handed down, so the pattern reproduces itself in each generation.
Jackson, from the Palo Alto group, named family homeostasis -- the family behaving as a
self-correcting system that resists change and pulls to restore its equilibrium when a member
moves. Minuchin mapped the structures whose rigidity concentrates a system's stress, and the
whole tradition -- Bowen, Minuchin, Haley, Jackson, Satir -- converged on the identified
patient: the member whose symptom is produced and held by the system, expressing for the
family what it cannot own. So the composite gives family-system three bridges -- inheritance,
homeostasis, and identified-patient -- the properties of the family as a whole that emerge
only when its triangles, its members' differentiation, and its binds are read together.

## Restrictions

What the composite refuses to model, and to whom it delegates.

- **The emotions inside the system**: The composite models the system, not the feelings that
  run through it. The jealousy of a rivalrous pair, the grief of a loss, the guilt a member
  carries are those engines'; the family system is the pattern that produces and holds trouble,
  and the particular emotions it routes are owned elsewhere.
- **Socialization**: The composite models the family as an emotional system, not the transfer
  of the social world into the child. How a newcomer internalizes the ready-made world handed to
  it is the socialization engine's; family-system's inheritance bridge is the transmission of the
  emotional pattern -- the differentiation level and the triangles -- not the cultural content.
- **Attachment**: The composite models the system, not the bonds within it. The security of a
  member's tie to a caregiver is the attachment engine's; the family system reads how the members
  hold selves and route tension, and the bond's own security is attachment's.
- **The atoms**: The composite adds no content its atoms already own. The three-party binds are
  the triangulation engine's; the members' self-capacity is the differentiation engine's; the
  communicational traps are the double-bind engine's. Family-system owns only the seams -- the
  whole-system properties the three become that none is alone.
- **A diagnosis of a real family (not composite content)**: The composite casts authored
  dramatic architecture for a play, never a diagnostic instrument or a claim about a real person
  or family, and its content says so.

## Encoding

Source to constraint, per file.

- **[family-system](process_family-system.md)** (the root): the household read as one emotional
  system whose members' troubles are the whole's -- triangles, differentiation, and binds
  composing a self-maintaining unit. Anchored by Bowen (the family as an emotional unit) and the
  whole family-therapy tradition.
- **[inheritance](process_inheritance.md)** (bridge): the system's differentiation level and its
  triangles passed down the generations, the pattern reproducing itself. Wires differentiation to
  triangulation across time. Anchored by Bowen (multigenerational transmission).
- **[homeostasis](process_homeostasis.md)** (bridge): the system's resistance to any member's
  change, the whole pulling to restore its equilibrium. Wires the triangle's stabilizing to the
  members' fusion. Anchored by Jackson (family homeostasis).
- **[identified-patient](process_identified-patient.md)** (bridge): the whole's tension
  concentrated in one member, whose symptom is the system's and not their own. Wires the detour,
  the bind, and low differentiation onto one member. Anchored by Minuchin, Haley, and Jackson (the
  symptom-bearer as the system speaking).
