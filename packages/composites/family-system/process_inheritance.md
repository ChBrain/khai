# Process: Inheritance

## Taxonomy

[Family-system](process_family-system.md), the bridge where the system's patterns pass down the generations.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: family-system

## Initiated by

The family's shape is not made fresh each generation; it is handed down.
A fused, triangling household hands its children the same low [differentiation](@chbrain/khai-engine-differentiation/position_differentiation.md) it runs on and the same readiness to route tension through [triangles](@chbrain/khai-engine-triangulation/plot_triangulation.md) -- so the child raised inside the pattern arrives already primed to repeat it, to marry at a like level of differentiation and to triangle its own children in turn.
This is inheritance: the seam where the system reproduces itself across time, Bowen's multigenerational transmission -- the family's emotional level and its structures passed down as surely as its name.

## Direction

Down the generations, the pattern reproducing itself.
The transmission runs by the atoms compounding over time: a member's [fusion](@chbrain/khai-engine-differentiation/position_fusion.md) is learned from a fused parent and taught to a fused child; a [triangle](@chbrain/khai-engine-triangulation/plot_triangulation.md) that bound one generation's tension is rebuilt in the next; even a member's [cutoff](@chbrain/khai-engine-differentiation/position_cutoff.md) -- the flight that looks like escape -- returns as the next generation's fusion-vulnerability, the unworked reactivity handed on intact.
The direction is repetition across time: the family becomes the thing that makes more of itself, the parent's unfinished business the child's inheritance.

## Lever

The coupling of differentiation and triangulation across generations that neither stages alone.
[Differentiation](@chbrain/khai-engine-differentiation/position_differentiation.md) is a persona's own capacity, read in one lifetime; [triangulation](@chbrain/khai-engine-triangulation/plot_triangulation.md) is a structure cast in one plot. The bridge is their compounding down the line: a low differentiation and its triangles, transmitted, become the next generation's starting condition, so the pattern is neither one person's trait nor one plot's structure but a lineage's -- the family projection process by which parents hand their own level to the child they focus on most.
Break the transmission -- one member differentiating enough to not pass it on -- and the line changes; leave it unworked, and the pattern descends unbroken.

## Echo

The pattern is handed down, the family reproduced in its children.
Inheritance leaves the system extended across time -- its differentiation level and its triangles carried into the next generation, the parent's pattern become the child's ground.
It is the seam Bowen set across the generations: not a member's differentiation and not a single triangle, but the transmission that makes the family a thing that outlives and remakes its members.
Inheritance is dated from the handing-down, the pattern passing from the generation that held it to the one that will.
