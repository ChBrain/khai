# Instructions: Family-system

How a Playwright wires the family-system composite into a play. You wire by linking the play's own content to the composite's files; the composite and its atoms stay untouched, they are canon. Installing the composite brings its atoms as dependencies and their laws with them. The Roadie plumbs the composite's law into Instructions Knowledge on deploy. Authoring guidance, not runtime content, and it does not go on tour.

## Human

- The human sets the family as a system: what pattern it runs, what it hands down, what it refuses to let change, and which member it makes carry the whole's tension -- as authored dramatic architecture, never a diagnosis of a real family.

## Agent

- From the play's own content, a plot that stages a family system may link under Tension the bridge it turns on: inheritance, the differentiation level and triangles passed down the generations; homeostasis, the system's resistance to any member's change, the whole pulling to restore its balance; or identified-patient, the one member made to carry the whole's tension as a symptom. Cast the system from the atoms -- its triangles from triangulation, its members' self-capacity from differentiation, its binds from double-bind -- and stage the seams, not a fourth engine. Read the family as a system, never as separate characters: the identified patient's symptom is the family's, and the family fights to keep its shape. You link to the composite, you never edit it.

## Collaboration

- The emotions that run inside the system -- jealousy, grief, guilt -- are those engines'; the socialization by which a child takes on the family's world is socialization's; the attachment bonds among its members are attachment's. Family-system is the capstone of the family-systems track -- the whole that triangulation, differentiation, and double-bind compose. Whether a production's family reads as a genuine system rather than a set of separate people, and stays authored architecture rather than diagnosis, is the team's review.

## Knowledge

- Family-system is the household read as one emotional system rather than a set of individuals, whose members' troubles are properties of the whole (Bowen's emotional unit; Jackson's homeostasis; Minuchin's structures; the family-therapy tradition's identified patient). It wires over three atoms and adds no fourth: triangulation supplies the three-party binds that route the system's tension, differentiation the members' capacity to hold a self within it, and double-bind the communicational trap a low-differentiated system lays on its own. Three bridges are its own content -- inheritance is the multigenerational transmission, the differentiation level and triangles handed down so a fused, triangling family reproduces itself in its children (cutoff in one generation becoming fusion-vulnerability in the next); homeostasis is the system's resistance to change, the whole pulling to restore its equilibrium when a member differentiates or a symptom lifts, so lasting change means changing the system, not the member; identified-patient is the one member made to carry the whole's tension as a symptom, produced by a detour that selects them, a bind that forbids naming the real trouble, and a low differentiation that leaves them unable to refuse. Read the family as a system, and read the identified patient two ways: they are not sick apart from the family; the family is sick through them.

## System

- Do stage the family as a system, not a set of characters: its symptom the whole's, its pattern inherited, its balance defended -- shown in how the members bind, hold, and trap one another, never narrated.
- Do route through the bridge that fits: inheritance for the pattern handed down the generations, homeostasis for the system resisting a member's change, identified-patient for the one carrying the whole's tension.
- Do read the identified patient as the system speaking: treat their symptom as the family's and it can change; treat it as their own and the family keeps its scapegoat and its balance both -- and expect homeostasis to replace a lifted symptom unless the system changes.
- Do not add a fourth engine's content: the triangles are triangulation's, the self-capacity differentiation's, the binds double-bind's; the emotions inside are those engines', the socialization socialization's, the bonds attachment's. Do not cast the family system as a diagnosis of a real family; it is authored architecture. Do not edit the composite's or the atoms' files; wire only from the play's side.
