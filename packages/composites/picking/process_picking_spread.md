# Process: Picking, the Spread

## Taxonomy

[Picking](process_picking.md), the alternatives moving apart, and where the three are seen for one thing.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: picking

## Initiated by

The choice is made, and the side that lost is still worth what it was worth.
The Spread starts wherever [dissonance](@chbrain/khai-engine-dissonance/process_dissonance.md) begins working on the commitment -- the [free-choice](@chbrain/khai-engine-dissonance/process_free_choice.md) route above all, where the forgone good is disposed of by revaluing it, with [induced compliance](@chbrain/khai-engine-dissonance/process_induced_compliance.md) and [earning](@chbrain/khai-engine-dissonance/process_earning.md) standing alongside as the other ways an act rewrites an attitude.
It is the face of the composite where the persona stops being divided.

## Direction

Toward a version of the object in which the choice was obvious.
The Spread reads the movement rather than the verdict: the chosen side rises, the rejected side falls, and the two separate until the gap between them is wide enough that no reasonable person would have hesitated -- and the persona does not experience any of this as movement, only as having finally seen the thing clearly.
It moves the evaluation to fit the act already taken, and it moves quickly.

## Lever

The relief of a settled attitude against the accuracy of the one it replaced.
The carrying force is that the cheapest thing to change is the belief: the choice is made, the act cannot be recalled, and the only cognition still free to move is what the persona thinks the two options were worth -- so it moves, and the persona ends up holding a position more one-sided than any they arrived with and reports it as what they always thought; the resistance is the one condition that keeps the fence standing, which is not having had to come off it -- an unmade choice spreads nothing, which is why a persona who can genuinely leave a question open keeps an accuracy that a persona who was made to answer it has already spent.
The Spread rides the composite's keystone knife-edge: the conviction is manufactured by the choice rather than the choice by the conviction, and the harder the call was, the wider the spread that follows, because more had to be closed.

## Echo

A certainty, and the fork it is made of.
It shows the three engines complete -- a real division, a fork that will take only one answer, and an attitude revised to fit what was done -- and carries beneath it the composite's keystone: the confidence a persona has in a position is evidence about how difficult it was to take, not about how right it is, so the firmest convictions in a play are often the ones with the most given up underneath them.
This is why the Spread is the one to stage and the hardest to stage honestly: the persona must be sincere, must not be able to see the movement, and must be able to give a good account of their position -- and a play that lets them wink at the audience has replaced the mechanism with self-knowledge nobody has.
The Spread is the note the whole composite closes on: what a persona had to give up is legible in how sure they became, and nowhere else without a discount.
The Spread is dated from the commitment, and never from the conviction.
