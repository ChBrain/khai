# Process: Picking

## Taxonomy

What a genuinely two-sided evaluation does when a choice is required of it.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: picking

## Initiated by

A persona holds both sides of something, and then has to pick.
Picking starts wherever that is happening: two full charges toward one object, through [ambivalence](@chbrain/khai-engine-ambivalence/position_ambivalence.md); a fork that has to be answered, through [decision](@chbrain/khai-engine-decision/process_decision.md); and the tidying that follows a commitment, through [dissonance](@chbrain/khai-engine-dissonance/process_dissonance.md).
This composite adds no fourth engine. It reads the one thing the three make together, which none of them is: a divided persona put under a fork, and what the fork leaves behind.

## Direction

Toward a position the persona did not hold before they were asked.
The composite wires the three engines and reads their working in three shapes: [the Fence](process_picking_fence.md), both sides held and costing nothing; [the Call](process_picking_call.md), the fork that makes them press together; and [the Spread](process_picking_spread.md), the twist -- the alternatives moving apart after the commitment until the persona is more certain than they ever were.
The three are not three degrees of conviction. They are one episode, and the conviction is its residue.

## Lever

The genuineness of a divided evaluation against the fact that a choice will not take one.
The carrying force is that both sides are real and stay real: a persona strong on the positive and strong on the negative is not confused and not indifferent, and nothing in the episode informs them -- no fact arrives, no argument lands, the object does not change; the resistance is that a fork admits one answer, so whichever way it goes something the persona actually valued is given up, and the giving up has to be made bearable afterwards by the only means available, which is revising what the thing was worth.
Picking is read across the whole episode and never at a single point, because a persona read only at the end looks like somebody who simply made up their mind.

## Echo

A firm position, and the fork that manufactured it.
Picking does not close; it is one pass of a divided persona through a choice, gathered into one reading -- a fence, a call, a spread.
It hands off what it is not: a persona repairing their account after their own **conduct** is the squaring composite's, which wires this same dissonance engine on behaviour where this runs it on a choice between two goods; how much room a persona has to decide with at all is the slack composite's, which wires this same decision engine under scarcity; what a persona holds and on what footing is assent's; the standing modes and errors of judgement are estimation's; the circling that never reaches a fork is rumination's, under unmoored; what is mourned after the fact is longing's; and a divided persona made to **stand for** a theme of doubt is authored key, the play's pitch, not the force.
The persona is dated from the fork, and from how certain they became afterwards.
