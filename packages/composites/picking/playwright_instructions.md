# Instructions: Picking

How a Playwright wires the picking composite into a play. You wire by linking the play's
own content to the composite's files; the files stay untouched, they are canon. The
composite installs the ambivalence, decision, and dissonance engines as dependencies, and
their laws arrive with them. Authoring guidance, not runtime content, and it does not go
on tour.

## Human

- The human sets which questions in the play a persona is genuinely divided on, which of those the play forces to an answer, and how firm the persona is about it afterwards.

## Agent

- From the play's own content, read a persona who holds both sides of something and link under Projection what is running: the fence while two opposed evaluations are held toward one object, present whether or not they are being felt (from ambivalence), the call while a fork is bringing both sides to mind at once and will take only one answer, whichever mode the persona reaches for (from decision), the spread while the alternatives are moving apart after the commitment and the chosen is rising as the rejected falls (from dissonance). Keep both sides genuinely wanted, since a persona who was secretly sure all along has nothing here to run, and keep the persona unable to see the movement. You link to the composite, you never edit it.

## Collaboration

- The potential and felt forms are the ambivalence engine's; the five decision modes are decision's; induced compliance, free choice, and earning are dissonance's -- one alone is a position or a process, not an episode. A persona repairing their account after their own conduct is the squaring composite's, which wires this same dissonance engine on behaviour where this runs it on a choice between two goods; how much room a persona has to decide with at all is slack's, which wires this same decision engine under scarcity; what a persona holds and on what footing is assent's; the catalogued errors of judgement are estimation's; circling that never reaches a fork is rumination's, under unmoored; what is mourned once it is gone is longing's, and the spread is what a persona does so that there is nothing to mourn; a bind where every move is punished is double-bind's, under family-system, and picking's fork has two live answers both genuinely wanted. This composite owns the episode -- and the spread bridge carries the keystone: the conviction is manufactured by the choice rather than the choice by the conviction.

## Knowledge

- Picking is a persona read while a division of theirs is put under a fork -- braiding the state (ambivalence: a strong positive and a strong negative held at once toward one object, on separable dimensions, which is neither a lukewarm midpoint nor indifference) with the demand (decision: a choice that admits one answer) and with what follows (dissonance: an evaluation revised to fit an act already taken). The join is van Harreveld's and it is a claim none of the three atoms makes alone: ambivalence is not in itself aversive, and a persona can hold a division for years without it costing anything, because the two sides are rarely brought to mind together. What makes it hurt is a choice that is imminent and unavoidable, and the source of the hurt is anticipated loss rather than confusion -- whichever way it goes, something actually valued is given up. It runs in three faces. The fence is the division standing, and often the correct read: an object with real goods and real costs is answered honestly by a divided evaluation, and most such divisions sit latent and are never felt. The call is the fork, which is precisely the device that makes both charges accessible at the same moment and then refuses to accept both; the mode the persona runs is a symptom rather than a style, since a pressed persona defers, discovers that more information is needed, reopens a closed question, takes a rule and lets the rule carry the blame, or satisfices on the first thing that clears -- and where none of that is available, the paralysed mode is the fence made visible. The spread is the twist: the choice is made, the act cannot be recalled, and the only cognition still free to move is what the persona thinks the options were worth, so it moves -- the chosen rises, the rejected falls, and the two separate until no reasonable person would have hesitated. Here the three are seen for one thing: the persona comes out holding a position more one-sided than any they went in with, reports it as what they always thought, and the width of the spread tracks how hard the call was rather than how good the answer is.

## System

- Do read the whole episode and never a single point: name whether the fence (both sides held), the call (a fork pressing them together), or the spread (a commitment being defended) is running, and cast each from its engines. A persona read only at the end looks like somebody who simply made up their mind.
- Do make both sides genuinely wanted and both real, since a persona who secretly knew all along is not ambivalent and the composite has nothing to do in that scene.
- Do make the choice unavoidable: a fork the persona can dodge forever produces no discomfort and no spread, and the paralysed mode is what a dodgeable fork looks like.
- Do put no new information in the episode: nothing arrives, no argument lands, the object does not change -- the whole movement happens on the persona's side, and a fact introduced to settle it has replaced the mechanism with a plot.
- Do let the persona be sincere and unable to see the movement, and able to give a good account of their position afterwards -- a play that lets them wink at the audience has replaced the mechanism with self-knowledge nobody has.
- Do let the hardest calls produce the firmest convictions, because that is the keystone: the confidence is evidence about difficulty, not about correctness.
- Do not model an account repaired after wrongdoing (that is squaring, wiring the same engine on conduct), the room a persona has to decide with (that is slack, wiring the same engine under scarcity), the footing of belief in general (that is assent), the errors as a family (that is estimation), circling with no fork (that is rumination), what is mourned once gone (that is longing, and the spread is what prevents it), or a bind where every move is punished (that is double-bind). Do not make a dividedness stand for a theme (that is the play's pitch). Do not edit the composite's files; wire only from the play's side.
