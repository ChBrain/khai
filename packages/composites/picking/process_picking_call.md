# Process: Picking, the Call

## Taxonomy

[Picking](process_picking.md), the fork that brings both sides to mind at once.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: picking

## Initiated by

An answer is now required, and it can only be one answer.
The Call starts wherever [decision](@chbrain/khai-engine-decision/process_decision.md) is running on a divided persona -- the fork taken [intuitively](@chbrain/khai-engine-decision/process_intuitive.md), worked through [deliberately](@chbrain/khai-engine-decision/process_deliberate.md), settled by a [heuristic](@chbrain/khai-engine-decision/process_heuristic.md), closed by [satisficing](@chbrain/khai-engine-decision/process_satisficing.md) on the first option that clears, or suspended in [paralysis](@chbrain/khai-engine-decision/process_paralysed.md).
It is the face of the composite where the fence stops being free.

## Direction

Toward a commitment, through whatever will make committing bearable.
The Call reads the discomfort and locates it correctly, which is the composite's first real claim: the aversiveness does not belong to the ambivalence, which sat harmlessly for years, and it does not belong to the choice, which an undivided persona takes without distress -- it belongs to the two together, because a fork is precisely the device that makes both charges accessible at the same moment and then refuses to accept both.
It reads the mode as a symptom rather than a style, since a divided persona under a fork does not run whichever mode they normally run: they defer, they discover that new information is needed, they reopen a question that was closed, they take a rule and let the rule carry the blame, or they satisfice on the first thing that clears and stop looking -- and where none of that is available, the paralysed mode is the fence made visible, a persona standing in front of a choice with two full charges and no way to discharge either.
It moves the persona toward relief, and only incidentally toward an answer.

## Lever

The demand for one answer against a persona who honestly has two.
The carrying force is that the fork will not wait and cannot be split: the persona anticipates that whichever way they go, something they actually valued is given up, and the anticipation is what produces the discomfort rather than any doubt about the facts -- so the search that follows is doing a different job than it appears to, gathering not what would decide the question but what would make one side easier to let go of; the resistance is that the discomfort is relieved by commitment itself, so almost any answer beats standing there, which is why the mode a persona reaches for under this pressure says more about how badly they want the pressure to end than about what they think.
The Call spends the persona's honesty about their own reasons, and hands them a decision they will have to live with.

## Echo

A choice made, and something real given up to make it.
It is the bridge that supplies the debt the last bridge pays: at the instant of commitment the persona has picked one side of an evaluation they held whole, and the other side is still there, still true, and now attached to nothing.
What it hands upward is exactly that -- a forgone good, fully valued, with no home -- and there is only one place the persona can put it.
The Call is dated from the moment the choice became unavoidable, not from when it first appeared.
