# Picking

Picking as composite: what a genuinely two-sided evaluation does when a choice is required of it -- both charges held at once and costing nothing (the fence), the choice that makes them press together and cannot be answered without a loss (the call), and the alternatives moving apart after the commitment until the persona holds a conviction more one-sided than any they arrived with (the spread) -- wired over the ambivalence, decision, and dissonance engines.

This engine is defined by its [manifest](package.json), which the canon renders as the WIRES card. The manifest is the single source of truth; this README is generated, not edited by hand.
