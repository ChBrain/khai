# Process: Picking, the Fence

## Taxonomy

[Picking](process_picking.md), both sides held, and no bill for holding them.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: picking

## Initiated by

A persona wants the thing and does not want it, and both are true.
The Fence starts wherever [ambivalence](@chbrain/khai-engine-ambivalence/position_ambivalence.md) is standing -- opposed evaluations co-active toward one object, which the evaluative-space account makes possible by keeping positivity and negativity on separable dimensions -- in its [potential](@chbrain/khai-engine-ambivalence/position_potential.md) form where the conflict is present and measurable whether or not it is being felt, and in its [felt](@chbrain/khai-engine-ambivalence/position_felt.md) form where the persona is experiencing the strain.
It is the face of the composite where nothing is being decided and nothing is going wrong.

## Direction

Toward a stable state that has no reason to end on its own.
The Fence reads the division as a division and not as a deficiency: it is not a lukewarm midpoint, not indifference, and not a failure to have got the facts -- two charges at full strength, the way a persona loves and resents the same parent, wants the job and dreads it, believes the project and can see exactly what is wrong with it.
It moves the persona nowhere, and that is its whole character.

## Lever

The accuracy of holding both against the cost of holding both, which is usually nothing.
The carrying force is that the fence is often the correct read and cheap to keep: an object with real goods and real costs is answered honestly by a divided evaluation, and the persona pays for it only when the two sides are brought to mind at the same time -- so most ambivalence sits latent, is not experienced, and would never need to be resolved; the resistance is that potential and felt come apart, so a persona can be strongly divided and know nothing about it until something makes both sides accessible at once, at which point the strain arrives as if it were new.
The Fence spends nothing, and is quietly load-bearing.

## Echo

A division, standing, at rest.
It is the bridge that fixes what the composite starts from, and the fixing matters: a persona who was secretly sure all along is not on the fence, and there is nothing here for them to run.
What it hands upward is the gap between the two forms -- that the conflict is present long before it is felt, and that something has to bring the sides together for the felt form to fire.
The Fence is dated from whenever both evaluations became true, which is usually not a moment anybody noticed.
