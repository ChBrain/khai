# Process: Overriding, the Call

## Taxonomy

[Overriding](process_overriding.md), the moment this case looks different.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: overriding

## Initiated by

The practitioner reads the output and something is wrong with it.
The Call starts where the [expert](@chbrain/khai-engine-expertise/process_expert_stage.md) sees the case whole -- situation and response arriving together, with no interval in which anything was weighed -- and what arrives does not match what the instrument returned.
The information they are acting on is frequently real and frequently [unstateable](@chbrain/khai-engine-expertise/process_tacit.md), which is what makes the moment so difficult to adjudicate.

## Direction

Toward a departure that is easy to justify and hard to evaluate.
The practitioner can always give reasons, and the reasons are usually good ones: something they observed, something the form does not capture, a pattern they have met before.
And the reasons are reconstructions, since the judgement did not arrive by reasoning -- so what is offered in defence of the override is not the process that produced it, and neither party in the conversation can tell.

## Lever

The reality of what the practitioner saw against the unreliability of their sense that it matters.
The carrying force is that the perception is genuine and the case for discretion is strong: a rule cannot see the thing that arrived after the form was filled, the practitioner frequently can, and Meehl himself insisted on the exception -- if you know the man has a broken leg you do not consult the actuarial table on whether he will go to the cinema; the resistance is that the felt strength of the signal does not track its validity, since it is produced by fluency and pattern-match, and in a [domain that cannot teach](@chbrain/khai-engine-expertise/process_low_validity.md) it fires just as strongly with nothing behind it.
The Call feels identical whether it is a broken leg or a Tuesday.

## Echo

A departure taken with confidence, on grounds that cannot be examined.
It is the bridge where the two engines are actually in contact, and where a play will want to sit.
What it hands upward is not one decision but a rate, since a practitioner who overrides at all overrides regularly -- and the next bridge is about the rate.
The Call is dated from the moment the file was opened, which is before the reasons were assembled.
