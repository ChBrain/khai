# Overriding

Overriding as composite: what a practitioner does when a standardised instrument and their own judgement disagree -- the rule, which wins on average not by insight but by consistency, weighting the same factors the same way every time, so that even simple models built from the practitioner's own stated criteria outperform the practitioner applying them by hand (the rule); the moment the case is seen whole and does not match what the instrument returned, acting on information that is frequently real and frequently unstateable, with reasons offered afterwards that are reconstructions rather than the process (the call); and the tally of departures, where practitioners given the instrument and the freedom to depart do worse than the instrument alone, not because the exceptions are all wrong but because the good ones are outnumbered (the reckoning) -- wired over the measure and expertise engines, and closing on the exception that is real and cannot be identified in advance.

This engine is defined by its [manifest](package.json), which the canon renders as the WIRES card. The manifest is the single source of truth; this README is generated, not edited by hand.
