# Process: Overriding, the Rule

## Taxonomy

[Overriding](process_overriding.md), the instrument, and why it wins on average.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: overriding

## Initiated by

An answer has been produced without anybody's judgement in it.
The Rule starts wherever a standardised instrument is in use: a scoring sheet, a risk model, a triage protocol, a checklist that yields a decision -- built on a [common scale](@chbrain/khai-engine-measure/process_common_scale.md), able to [travel](@chbrain/khai-engine-measure/process_travel_measure.md) to people who did not make it, and [answerable](@chbrain/khai-engine-measure/process_answerability.md) in a way a person's word is not.
It is the face of the composite where nobody has done anything clever.

## Direction

Toward a decision that is worse than the best human one and better than the average human one.
The instrument's advantage is not insight. It is consistency: it weights the same factors the same way every time, does not get tired, does not remember the last case, and is not moved by how the applicant presents.
Most of what beats the practitioner is the elimination of noise rather than the addition of knowledge -- which is why remarkably simple rules, even ones built from the practitioner's own stated criteria, outperform the practitioner applying those criteria by hand.

## Lever

The reliability of a fixed weighting against everything the weighting cannot see.
The carrying force is that this finding is robust, old, and deeply unwelcome: mechanical prediction has matched or beaten clinical judgement across decades and scores of domains, the margin is not small, and it holds where the practitioners are experienced and sincere -- so a system that hands the ordinary case to the instrument is not being philistine, it is being accurate; the resistance is that the instrument's inputs are whatever somebody thought to record, so it is blind by construction to the [residue](@chbrain/khai-engine-measure/process_residue.md) -- and the residue is exactly where the case that does not fit will show up.
The Rule is better on average and structurally unable to notice when it is not.

## Echo

A decision that is defensible, consistent, and occasionally plainly wrong.
It is the bridge that installs the standard the next two are measured against.
What it hands upward is not merely an answer but an entitlement: once the instrument exists, departing from it is an act somebody has to account for.
The Rule is dated from its validation study, and it does not know what year it is.
