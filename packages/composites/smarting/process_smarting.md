# Process: Smarting

## Taxonomy

What a favourable self-view does when the room declines to confirm it.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: smarting

## Initiated by

A persona thinks well of themselves, and somebody has just indicated otherwise.
Smarting starts wherever that is running: a resting regard held securely or staked on something that must keep delivering, through [self-esteem](@chbrain/khai-engine-self-esteem/position_self_esteem.md); an inflated case actively defended, through [narcissism](@chbrain/khai-engine-narcissism/position_narcissism.md); and force mobilised into harm, through [aggression](@chbrain/khai-engine-aggression/process_aggression.md).
This composite adds no fourth engine. It reads the one path the three make together, which the received story about self-regard gets backwards.

## Direction

Toward somebody being hurt, usually not the one who did it.
The composite wires the three engines and reads their working in three shapes: [the Claim](process_smarting_claim.md), a regard put where it can be answered; [the Puncture](process_smarting_puncture.md), the contradiction landing; and [the Return](process_smarting_return.md), the twist -- the force sent back to whoever is nearest rather than to whoever caused it.
The three are not three degrees of temper. They are one path, and it starts in self-regard rather than in self-loathing.

## Lever

The favourable view a persona holds of themselves against the room's willingness to ratify it.
The carrying force is that the view has to be put out to be worth anything: a regard nobody can see confirms nothing, so the persona claims it in what they say, what they take as theirs by right, and what they expect to be granted without asking -- and the claiming is what makes it contradictable; the resistance is that the room grants what it grants, on its own reckoning, so a claim the room will not meet stands exposed and stays exposed, and every ordinary interaction is a chance for it to be answered.
Smarting is read on the gap and never on the height, because a persona's regard for themselves is dangerous only in relation to what the room will confirm.

## Echo

Somebody hurt, and a self-picture intact.
Smarting does not close; it is one pass of an exposed self-regard through a contradiction, gathered into one reading -- a claim, a puncture, a return.
It hands off what it is not: the persona's whole relation to their own self -- evaluating it, caring for it in suffering, being true to it -- is the self-relation composite's, which wires this same self-esteem engine as a stance held inward where this reads what it does outward; hostility between groups is the intergroup composite's, which wires this same aggression engine on the us/them line where this runs it on one persona's regard; the moral emotions aimed at a wrongdoer are condemnation's and hate's; a wanting produced by somebody else's advantage is outdone's; a rank being contested is standing's; the account a persona gives afterwards is squaring's; damage laid down early is cptsd's; and a fit of temper made to **stand for** a theme is authored key, the play's pitch, not the force.
The persona is dated from what they claimed, and from what the room would not give them.
