# Process: Smarting, the Claim

## Taxonomy

[Smarting](process_smarting.md), a regard put out where the room can answer it.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: smarting

## Initiated by

A persona holds a view of themselves, and acts as though it were settled.
The Claim starts wherever [self-esteem](@chbrain/khai-engine-self-esteem/position_self_esteem.md) is resting -- [high](@chbrain/khai-engine-self-esteem/position_high.md) and met without defence, [low](@chbrain/khai-engine-self-esteem/position_low.md) and met with doubt, or [contingent](@chbrain/khai-engine-self-esteem/position_contingent.md) and holding only while a staked domain keeps delivering -- and, in the inflated case, wherever [narcissism](@chbrain/khai-engine-narcissism/position_narcissism.md) is running its [admiration](@chbrain/khai-engine-narcissism/process_admiration.md) route, the assertive self-promotion by which a [grandiose](@chbrain/khai-engine-narcissism/position_grandiose.md) regard goes out to be met.
It is the face of the composite where nothing has gone wrong yet.

## Direction

Toward exposure, which is the price of the regard counting for anything.
The Claim reads what is being asserted and how far out it is put: not the private opinion, which is safe, but the version in the room -- what the persona takes as theirs by right, expects to be granted without asking, and would be surprised to be refused.
It moves the regard from something held to something answerable, and the move is what the rest of the composite needs.

## Lever

The worth of a regard that others confirm against the exposure that confirming requires.
The carrying force is that the claim is not vanity but the ordinary means by which a self-picture gets any purchase: a persona who never advances a view of themselves is never confirmed in one, so the claiming is normal, mostly proportionate, and mostly met -- and the composite does not begin to run until a claim is made that the room will not meet; the resistance is that the room reckons independently and does not consult, so the same act of claiming that could be ratified can also be refused, and the persona has no way to make the claim and keep it safe.
The Claim spends nothing, and puts something where it can be broken.

## Echo

A self-picture, out in the open, awaiting an answer.
It is the bridge that sets the quantity the whole composite runs on, and it is a difference rather than a level: not how well the persona thinks of themselves, but how far that sits from what the room will grant.
What it hands upward is exactly that distance -- and the warning that the height alone predicts nothing, since a large claim the room agrees with is stable and a modest one it will not meet is not.
The Claim is dated from whenever the persona began expecting to be taken at their own estimate.
