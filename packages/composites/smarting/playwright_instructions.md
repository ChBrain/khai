# Instructions: Smarting

How a Playwright wires the smarting composite into a play. You wire by linking the play's
own content to the composite's files; the files stay untouched, they are canon. The
composite installs the narcissism, self-esteem, and aggression engines as dependencies, and
their laws arrive with them. Authoring guidance, not runtime content, and it does not go on
tour.

## Human

- The human sets which personas claim more than the room will grant them, what the room does about it, and who ends up paying.

## Agent

- From the play's own content, read a persona whose view of themselves is exposed and link under Projection what is running: the claim while a regard is being put out where the room can answer it, secure or low or staked on a domain that must keep delivering, and in the inflated case actively promoted (from self-esteem and narcissism), the puncture while a contradiction is landing and the injury is turning to antagonism rather than to revision (from narcissism), the return while force is being aimed -- hot at the source, cold as a means, through the bond, through what is withheld, or displaced onto somebody safer (from aggression). Read the gap between the claim and what the room grants, never the height of the claim alone. You link to the composite, you never edit it.

## Collaboration

- High, low, and contingent are the self-esteem engine's; the four types and the admiration, rivalry, and injury routes are narcissism's; the five forms of force are aggression's -- one alone is a position or a process, not the path. A persona's whole relation to their own self is the self-relation composite's, which wires this same self-esteem engine inward where this reads it outward; hostility between groups is intergroup's, which wires this same aggression engine on the us/them line where this runs it on one persona's regard; the emotions aimed at a wrongdoer are condemnation's and hate's, and nothing here has been transgressed except an estimate; a wanting produced by somebody else's advantage is outdone's, and needs a rival where this needs none; a rank being contested is standing's; the account given afterwards is squaring's; damage laid down early is cptsd's. This composite owns the path -- and the return bridge carries the keystone: what does the damage is the distance between the regard and what the room will confirm.

## Knowledge

- Smarting is a persona read while a favourable view of themselves meets a room that will not ratify it -- braiding the regard (self-esteem: high and met without defence, low and met with doubt, or contingent and holding only while a staked domain keeps delivering) with the inflated case that actively defends itself (narcissism: the grandiose claim promoted through admiration, and the wound answered through rivalry) and with what the mobilised force becomes (aggression: hostile, instrumental, relational, passive, or displaced). The join is Baumeister, Smart and Boden's, and it corrects the received story: violence was long said to come from thinking badly of oneself, and the argument runs the other way -- what precedes hostility is a favourable self-view meeting a contradiction, and the operative quantity is the gap between what a persona claims and what their situation will confirm. Bushman and Baumeister put all three atoms into one experiment, whose title names them: after a scathing evaluation, narcissism predicted how hard participants retaliated where self-esteem alone did not, and in the companion study the aggression appeared again against somebody who had done nothing. It runs in three faces. The claim is the regard put out where it can be answered, which is not vanity but the ordinary way a self-picture gets any purchase, and which exposes it -- and the discipline here is the difference rather than the level, since Kernis found hostility highest in regard that was high and unstable and lowest in regard that was high and steady. The puncture is the contradiction landing, sized by the distance it travels rather than by the remark, which is why the trigger looks trivial from outside and enormous from inside, and why accepting the room's estimate is not a small concession but the adoption of a lower self from now on. The return is the twist: the charge has to go somewhere, the source of it is often the worst available target -- more powerful, better placed, or gone -- so the force is carried until something cheaper presents itself, which is usually whoever is nearest and least able to answer back. Here the three are seen for one thing: what does the damage is not the height of the self-regard but its distance from what the room will confirm, so the remedy most often prescribed, raising the persona's opinion of themselves, widens the very gap it was meant to close.

## System

- Do read the gap and never the height: name whether the claim (a regard being put out), the puncture (a contradiction landing), or the return (force going somewhere) is running, and cast each from its engines. A modest claim the room refuses is more dangerous than a large one it grants.
- Do make the trigger small and the response large, since the composite is at its truest where a remark that would pass through anybody else detonates in this persona.
- Do let the persona explain themselves without mentioning the claim: they are not aware that the size of the response came from what they had staked, and an account that names it has replaced the mechanism with insight.
- Do send the bill to the wrong address: choose the target by who is available and safe rather than by who is responsible, and let a play that has the persona strike their actual contradictor know it has staged a fight instead.
- Do keep the room's refusal ordinary: nobody has to be cruel, and a room that simply declines to be impressed is the whole of what is needed.
- Do not make a persona who thinks well of themselves dangerous by default: the exposure is the condition, and a secure regard that does not need the room's confirmation has nothing here to run.
- Do not model the persona's stance toward their own self (that is self-relation, wiring the same engine inward), hostility between groups (that is intergroup, wiring the same engine on the us/them line), condemnation of a wrongdoer (that is condemnation and hate), a wanting produced by another's advantage (that is outdone), a contest over rank (that is standing), the account given afterwards (that is squaring), or damage laid down early (that is cptsd). Do not make an outburst stand for a theme (that is the play's pitch). Do not edit the composite's files; wire only from the play's side.
