# Process: Smarting, the Return

## Taxonomy

[Smarting](process_smarting.md), the force sent back, and where the three are seen for one thing.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: smarting

## Initiated by

The charge has to go somewhere, and the persona has options about where.
The Return starts wherever [aggression](@chbrain/khai-engine-aggression/process_aggression.md) takes its form -- [hostile](@chbrain/khai-engine-aggression/process_hostile.md) and hot at the source, [instrumental](@chbrain/khai-engine-aggression/process_instrumental.md) and cold as a means, [relational](@chbrain/khai-engine-aggression/process_relational.md) through the social bond, [passive](@chbrain/khai-engine-aggression/process_passive.md) through what is withheld, or [displaced](@chbrain/khai-engine-aggression/process_displaced.md) onto a safer target entirely.
It is the face of the composite where somebody who had nothing to do with it gets hurt.

## Direction

Toward a target, chosen more by availability than by involvement.
The Return reads the aiming and reads it honestly: the source of the contradiction is often the worst available target -- more powerful, better placed, or simply gone -- so the charge is carried until something cheaper presents itself, and what presents itself is usually whoever is nearest, most familiar, and least able to answer back.
It moves the cost off the persona and onto a third party, and it restores the claim without the room ever having agreed to it.

## Lever

The relief of discharging against the accuracy of where it lands.
The carrying force is that returning force does what accepting the verdict cannot: it re-establishes the claim in the only currency still available to the persona, which is what they can make somebody else concede -- and it works, in the short term and on the wrong person; the resistance is the one thing that stops the whole path, which is a regard that did not need the room's confirmation in the first place, so a persona whose good opinion of themselves is not staked on being granted it has nothing to defend and no charge to place.
The Return rides the composite's keystone knife-edge: what does the damage is not the height of the self-regard but the distance between it and what the room will confirm, so the remedy most often prescribed -- raise the persona's opinion of themselves -- widens the very gap it was meant to close.

## Echo

A self-picture restored, and the bill paid by somebody who was not there.
It shows the three engines complete -- a regard put out, a refusal it cannot absorb, and force aimed by convenience -- and carries beneath it the composite's keystone: the direction of the received story is wrong, and it is favourable self-regard rather than self-hatred that is doing the work, which is why the intervention aimed at the wrong end of it makes things worse.
This is why the Return is the one to stage and the easiest to stage badly: the trigger should be small and the response large, the persona should be able to explain themselves without mentioning the claim, and the target should be nearby and undeserving -- and a play that lets the persona strike the one who actually contradicted them has staged a fight rather than this.
The Return is the note the whole composite closes on: what a persona could not get the room to grant is legible in who they took it out on.
The Return is dated from the refusal, and it does not clear the balance.
