# Process: Smarting, the Puncture

## Taxonomy

[Smarting](process_smarting.md), the contradiction landing, sized by the gap and not by the remark.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: smarting

## Initiated by

The room has answered, and the answer is lower than the claim.
The Puncture starts wherever [narcissism](@chbrain/khai-engine-narcissism/process_injury.md) is taking the wound and turning to [rivalry](@chbrain/khai-engine-narcissism/process_narcissistic_rivalry.md), the antagonistic defence of a regard that has just been refused -- and wherever a [contingent](@chbrain/khai-engine-self-esteem/position_contingent.md) esteem finds that the staked domain has stopped delivering.
It is the face of the composite where the size of the response stops matching the size of the cause.

## Direction

Toward a state the persona has to discharge, and cannot discharge by agreeing.
The Puncture reads the disproportion and explains it: the injury is not measured by the insult but by the distance the insult travels, so the same remark passes through a persona who never claimed the thing and detonates in one who did -- and it is this that makes the trigger look trivial from outside and feel enormous from inside.
It moves the persona from exposed to contradicted, and leaves them with something to do about it.

## Lever

The cost of accepting the room's estimate against the cost of rejecting it.
The carrying force is that accepting is not a small concession: to take the contradiction at face value is to hold the lower view from now on, which for a persona whose regard is staked on the point is not a correction but a demolition -- so the response is not proportionate to the remark because it was never about the remark; the resistance is that rejecting it does not work either, since the room does not withdraw its estimate on request, which is why the state persists rather than resolving and has to go somewhere.
The Puncture spends the persona's composure, and leaves a charge with nowhere to sit.

## Echo

A regard refused, and a persona who cannot let the refusal stand.
It is the bridge that carries the composite's correction of the received account: this is where the damage comes from, and it comes from self-regard rather than from self-loathing -- a persona holding themselves low has nothing here to defend, and the paradigm case is somebody who thinks well of themselves and has just been told no.
What it hands upward is a mobilised charge and no legitimate place to put it, which is the last bridge's whole problem.
The Puncture is dated from the refusal, and never from the claim it refused.
