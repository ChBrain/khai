# Process: Opacity, Revealed

## Taxonomy

[Opacity](process_opacity.md), the posture where the self is opened true, at the cost of being seen.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: opacity

## Initiated by

The persona opens its boundary and lets the true self through, and to be known it gives up the safety of not being seen.
Revealed starts wherever a persona opens itself truly -- the [self-disclosure](@chbrain/khai-engine-self-disclosure/process_self_disclosure.md) that widens in [breadth](@chbrain/khai-engine-self-disclosure/process_breadth.md) and [deepens](@chbrain/khai-engine-self-disclosure/process_deepening.md) toward the core, paced by the [reciprocity](@chbrain/khai-engine-self-disclosure/process_reciprocity.md) it is returned, and the [disclosure](@chbrain/khai-engine-secret/process_disclosure.md) of what was kept.
It is the self opened true: not sealed, not faked, but shown as it is -- and shown means exposed.

## Direction

Toward the self made known, and the exposure that being known requires.
Revealed reads the persona opening -- the topic widened, the depth reached, the kept thing told -- each opening returned or not, the boundary lowered layer by layer toward the self that was held.
It moves the persona from unknown to known: and because what is opened cannot be un-known, every revealing is a surrender, the self placed in another's hands.

## Lever

The self given away against the exposure that giving it away requires.
The carrying force is that a persona can only be truly known by opening -- the sealed self is safe and unknown, and there is no being known without being seen; the resistance is that the opening is a surrender, the exposure irreversible, the self placed where it can be hurt.
Revealed rides the composite's keystone knife-edge: the exposure that being known costs is the intimacy that being known gives, the surrender of the boundary and the forming of the bond one and the same act -- to be known at all, a self must give up the safety of not being seen.

## Echo

The self opened true, known and exposed at once.
It shows a persona letting the true self through -- the widening, the deepening, the kept thing finally told -- and carries beneath it the composite's keystone: that a self is known only as much as it is willing to be seen, and the opening that makes it known is the same opening that makes it vulnerable.
Revealed is the note the whole composite closes on: opacity is not a wall to be admired but a boundary a self must lower to be known, and the lowering is both the gift and the risk of every true telling.
Revealed is dated from the opening, the moment the persona let the true self through and could not take it back.
