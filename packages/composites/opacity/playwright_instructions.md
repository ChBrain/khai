# Instructions: Opacity

How a Playwright wires the opacity composite into a play. You wire by linking the play's
own content to the composite's files; the files stay untouched, they are canon. The
composite installs the secret, self-disclosure, and deception engines as dependencies,
and their laws arrive with them. Authoring guidance, not runtime content, and it does not
go on tour.

## Human

- The human sets the persona's information self: which persona is read as managing what others may know of it -- a self held back, shown false, or opened true -- and, the question that governs the read, which posture it takes toward a given fact: withheld, feigned, or revealed.

## Agent

- From the play's own content, read a persona as a self that controls the sight of itself: link under Projection the posture its boundary is in -- withheld while it keeps the true self back (the keeper, the concealment, the retreat, and the exposure when it gets out, from secret), feigned while it shows a false self (the false belief constructed, delivered, and maintained, from deception), revealed while it opens the true self (the breadth, the deepening, the reciprocity, and the kept thing told, from self-disclosure and secret's disclosure). Play the persona as managing its boundary, present in what it projects, never narrated, and never made to stand for a story. You link to the composite, you never edit it.

## Collaboration

- The withheld fact and the work of keeping it are the secret engine's; the layered opening is self-disclosure's; the built false belief is deception's -- one engine alone is a process, not the whole management. The deceptive object made to mislead is guile's, read in the artifice composite, a piece and not a persona; the persona's wider face-work and self-monitoring are their own engines'. This composite owns the information self -- the persona's control of how much of the true self shows -- and the revealed bridge carries the keystone: to be truly known, a self must give up the safety of not being seen.

## Knowledge

- Opacity is a persona read as a self that controls what others may know of it -- braiding the self withheld (secret), the self shown false (deception), and the self opened true (self-disclosure) into one axis, the truth-status of the projected self -- read as a wiring of those three engines, and its question is which posture the self takes toward a given fact (Goffman's managed self; Altman's openness-closedness dialectic; Jourard's transparent self). It runs in three postures. Withheld is the self kept back -- the keeper who holds a secret, the work of concealment, the retreat that pulls the opening closed, the exposure when it gets out despite the keeping, the boundary drawn closed and guarded. Feigned is the self shown false -- the false belief constructed, delivered, and maintained against discovery, the boundary opened onto a fiction the persona is then bound to hold up. Revealed is the twist -- the self opened true, widening in breadth and deepening toward the core, paced by what is returned, the kept thing finally told, the boundary surrendered, where being truly known costs the safety of not being seen. Read a persona as a self known only as much as it chooses to let through, and read each posture by what it costs.

## System

- Do read the persona as managing its own boundary, never as a transparent surface: name whether its self is withheld (sealed), feigned (shown false), or revealed (opened true), and cast each from its engine.
- Do keep the three postures distinct: the withheld self is held back and unshown, the feigned self is shown but false, the revealed self is shown and true -- one boundary, three truth-statuses.
- Do let revealed carry the keystone: the exposure that being known costs is the intimacy that being known gives, the surrender of the boundary and the forming of the bond one and the same act.
- Do not model the single engines' internals (the secret's keeping, the disclosure's layers, the deception's construction -- those are the atoms), the deceptive object made to mislead (that is guile, in the artifice composite), or the persona's wider face-work and self-monitoring (their own engines). Do not make the persona's opacity stand for a story (that is the play's pitch). Do not edit the composite's files; wire only from the play's side.
