# Process: Opacity, Feigned

## Taxonomy

[Opacity](process_opacity.md), the posture where the self is shown false.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: opacity

## Initiated by

The persona opens its boundary onto a fiction, showing others a self that is not the true one.
Feigned starts wherever a persona builds a false belief in another -- the [deception](@chbrain/khai-engine-deception/process_deception.md) [constructed](@chbrain/khai-engine-deception/process_construction.md), [delivered](@chbrain/khai-engine-deception/process_delivery.md), and [maintained](@chbrain/khai-engine-deception/process_maintenance.md) against discovery.
It is the self shown false: the boundary opened, but onto a made thing, the persona seen as it is not.

## Direction

Toward the fiction shown and the work of holding it up.
Feigned reads the persona presenting a false self -- the lie built to fit, delivered to be believed, then maintained, because a deception is not a single act but a standing thing that must be kept consistent as others test it.
It moves the self from sealed or true to falsely shown: the boundary open but the thing behind it manufactured, and the persona bound to the upkeep of what it has made others believe.

## Lever

The false self shown against the labour and the exposure of keeping it up.
The carrying force is that a persona can open its boundary onto a fiction and be taken for what it is not; the resistance is the maintenance a lie demands -- every fact that must fit it, every test it must survive -- and the collapse if the fiction fails.
Feigned is the boundary opened onto a made self: the persona known as it is not, at the mounting cost of holding the made thing together.

## Echo

The false self shown, and the strain of keeping it standing.
Feigned leaves the persona seen as it is not -- the fiction delivered, the story maintained -- and hands the made self toward the collapse if it fails or the confession if it is dropped.
It is the boundary at its most active and its most brittle: not a self kept back nor a self opened true, but a self manufactured and held up under test.
Feigned is dated from the construction, the moment the persona built the false self it would show.
