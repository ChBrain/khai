# Process: Opacity

## Taxonomy

The self as others may know it.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: opacity

## Initiated by

A persona is read not as transparent but as a self it controls the sight of -- how much of what is true it lets others know.
Opacity starts wherever a persona manages the boundary between the self it is and the self others may see, sealing it as a [secret](@chbrain/khai-engine-secret/piece_secret.md) withheld, showing it false as a [deception](@chbrain/khai-engine-deception/process_deception.md) built, or opening it in true [self-disclosure](@chbrain/khai-engine-self-disclosure/process_self_disclosure.md).
This composite adds no new engine; it reads what the three make together: the truth-status of the projected self, one axis running from sealed through faked to revealed.

## Direction

Toward the whole management of what a self lets through, read through its postures.
The composite wires the three engines and reads their working in three postures: [Withheld](process_opacity_withheld.md), the self kept back; [Feigned](process_opacity_feigned.md), the self shown false; and [Revealed](process_opacity_revealed.md), the twist -- the self opened true at the cost of being seen.
A persona is never simply transparent: it holds some of itself back, shows some of itself false, and opens some of itself true, and which posture it takes toward a given fact is the whole of how it is known.

## Lever

The self's control of its own boundary against the assumption that a persona is transparent.
The carrying force is that a persona owns the access others have to it -- it decides what is sealed, what is faked, and what is opened; the resistance is the reflex to read a persona as see-through, to take what is shown for what is true and what is withheld for nothing there.
Opacity is read wherever a persona is granted control of its own visibility: not a transparent self on display but a self that manages, fact by fact, how much of what is true it lets others know.

## Echo

The persona revealed as a self that controls the sight of itself.
Opacity does not close; it is the persona going on managing its boundary, gathering the three engines into one reading -- a self that seals, fakes, and opens what others may know.
It hands off what it is not: the withheld fact and the work of keeping it is the [secret](@chbrain/khai-engine-secret/piece_secret.md) engine's; the layered opening is [self-disclosure](@chbrain/khai-engine-self-disclosure/process_self_disclosure.md)'s; the built false belief is [deception](@chbrain/khai-engine-deception/process_deception.md)'s; the deceptive object made to mislead is the guile engine's, read in the artifice composite, not here; the persona's face-work and self-monitoring are their own engines'; and the hidden self made to stand for a story is authored key, the play's pitch, not the force.
The persona is dated from its own managing, and read as a self that is known only as much as it chooses to let through.
