# Process: Opacity, Withheld

## Taxonomy

[Opacity](process_opacity.md), the posture where the self is kept back.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: opacity

## Initiated by

The persona draws its boundary closed and holds part of the true self out of others' reach.
Withheld starts wherever a persona keeps something back -- the [keeper](@chbrain/khai-engine-secret/position_keeper.md) who holds a [secret](@chbrain/khai-engine-secret/piece_secret.md), the work of [concealment](@chbrain/khai-engine-secret/process_concealment.md) that keeps it, and the [retreat](@chbrain/khai-engine-self-disclosure/process_retreat.md) that pulls the opening closed when it has gone too far.
It is the self sealed: not shown false, not shown true, simply held out of sight, weighed and guarded.

## Direction

Toward the self held out of reach, and the cost of holding it.
Withheld reads the persona keeping its boundary closed -- the fact carried unspoken, the topic steered around, the opening drawn back -- and the pressure that builds behind a thing kept, up to the [exposure](@chbrain/khai-engine-secret/process_exposure.md) when it gets out despite the keeping.
It moves the self from open to sealed: the boundary held, the fact guarded, the persona known less than it is by exactly the measure it withholds.

## Lever

The persona's right to keep itself against the pressure of what is kept.
The carrying force is that a self may hold part of itself back -- privacy is the persona's, and not everything true is owed; the resistance is the weight of the thing kept, the vigilance the keeping costs, and the exposure that waits if the seal fails.
Withheld is the boundary at its most closed: the self a persona keeps is the self others cannot reach, held at the price of guarding it and the risk of its getting out.

## Echo

The self sealed, guarded, and known the less for it.
Withheld leaves the persona holding its boundary closed -- the secret kept, the topic avoided, the retreat made -- and hands the guarded self toward the exposure that may break it or the disclosure that may open it.
It is the self at its most controlled and its most costly: known less than it is, at the price of the keeping.
Withheld is dated from the sealing, the moment the persona drew its boundary closed on the true self.
