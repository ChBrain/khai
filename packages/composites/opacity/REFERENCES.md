# Opacity: Reference

## Line of Work

Opacity as **composite**: the self as others may know it -- how much of the true self a
persona lets through. It is not a new force but a wiring of three: the **secret** engine
supplies the self **withheld** -- the fact held back and the work of keeping it; the
**deception** engine supplies the self **feigned** -- the false belief built and
maintained; and the **self-disclosure** engine supplies the self **revealed** -- the true
self opened layer by layer. This composite adds no fourth engine; it reads what the three
make together: the truth-status of the projected self, one axis running from sealed
through faked to revealed, and stages that into three postures a self takes toward its own
boundary.

The domain does not model any single force -- the withheld fact and its keeping are the
secret engine's, the layered opening is self-disclosure's, the built false belief is
deception's, and one alone is a process, not the whole management. Nor is it the deceptive
**object** made to mislead -- the dark pattern -- which is the guile engine's, read in the
artifice composite, a piece and not a persona. Nor is it the persona's wider face-work,
self-monitoring, or backstage self, which are their own engines'. It owns the information
self: the persona granted control of its own visibility, read not as a transparent thing
on display but as a self that manages, fact by fact, how much of what is true it lets
others know.

The spine is the reading of the self as a managed boundary rather than a transparent
surface. Goffman read the self as a presentation, staged for an audience and vulnerable to
the discrepant fact that breaks it. Altman set the openness-closedness dialectic at the
centre -- privacy as the selective control of access to the self, neither pure disclosure
nor pure withholding but a boundary continually regulated; with Taylor he read the opening
itself as social penetration, widening in breadth and deepening toward the core. Bok
weighed the ethics of the boundary, concealment and revelation as the two faces of what a
self owes. And Jourard read the far pole -- the transparent self, the opening that is a
surrender, where to be known at all is to be made vulnerable. The three bridges are those
readings staged: withheld, feigned, and revealed.

## Origin

The social-psychology and dramaturgy of the self as a managed boundary, wired as a
composite over the secret, self-disclosure, and deception engines.

| Source                           | Key Work                                                      | Scope                                                                                                                                                                  |
| :------------------------------- | :------------------------------------------------------------ | :--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Erving Goffman**               | _The Presentation of Self in Everyday Life_ (1959)            | The self as a presentation staged for an audience, and the discrepant fact that breaks it -- the managed self the whole composite reads. The root.                     |
| **Irwin Altman & Dalmas Taylor** | _Social Penetration_ (1973); privacy regulation theory        | The openness-closedness dialectic -- privacy as the selective control of access to the self -- and disclosure as social penetration. The root and the revealed bridge. |
| **Sissela Bok**                  | _Secrets: On the Ethics of Concealment and Revelation_ (1982) | Concealment and revelation as the two faces of what a self owes -- the ethics of the boundary held closed. The withheld bridge.                                        |
| **Sidney M. Jourard**            | _The Transparent Self_ (1971)                                 | The opening that is a surrender -- to be truly known is to be made vulnerable. The revealed bridge, the composite's keystone.                                          |

## Restrictions

What the composite refuses to model, and to whom it delegates.

- **The single engines (the atoms)**: The composite wires the three but owns none. The
  withheld fact, its keeper, and the work of keeping and telling it are the secret engine's;
  the opening of the self across breadth, depth, reciprocity, and retreat is
  self-disclosure's; the construction, delivery, and maintenance of a false belief is
  deception's. Opacity reads only what the three make as one management of the boundary; a
  single engine alone is a process, not the whole.
- **The deceptive object (guile / artifice)**: The made thing designed to mislead -- the
  dark pattern, the trap in the interface -- is the guile engine's, a piece read on the
  Apparent surface and staged in the artifice composite. Opacity is a persona's management
  of its own boundary, not an object built to deceive a user; the false self a persona shows
  is deception's, the false thing a maker builds is guile's.
- **The wider presented self (face, self-monitoring, backstage)**: The persona's face-work,
  its monitoring of the impression it makes, and its backstage self are their own engines'.
  Opacity is the narrower axis -- the truth-status of what is let through, sealed or faked or
  opened -- not the whole apparatus of self-presentation.
- **The hidden self as authored symbol (the play's pitch)**: A persona's opacity made to
  **stand for** a story -- the secret that mirrors a buried theme, the disclosure that mirrors
  a redemption -- is refused: that is authored key, the play's pitch, not the force on the
  persona. Opacity delivers the self's own managing; the meaning read into it is the play's.

## Encoding

Source to constraint. A composite root over three engine atoms it declares as dependencies,
with three bridges, the postures a self takes toward its own boundary.

- **[opacity](process_opacity.md)** (the root): the self as others may know it -- a persona's
  control of how much of the true self shows, sealed, faked, or opened. Anchored by Goffman
  (the managed self) and Altman (the openness-closedness dialectic).
- **[withheld](process_opacity_withheld.md)** (bridge): the self kept back -- the keeper, the
  concealment, the retreat, and the exposure when it gets out, the boundary drawn closed.
  Anchored by Bok (the ethics of concealment).
- **[feigned](process_opacity_feigned.md)** (bridge): the self shown false -- the false belief
  constructed, delivered, and maintained against discovery, the boundary opened onto a fiction.
  Anchored by Goffman (misrepresentation) and the deception literature.
- **[revealed](process_opacity_revealed.md)** (bridge): the self opened true -- widening in
  breadth and deepening toward the core, the kept thing finally told, the boundary surrendered.
  Anchored by Altman & Taylor (social penetration) and Jourard; the composite's twist and
  keystone, that being known costs the safety of not being seen.
