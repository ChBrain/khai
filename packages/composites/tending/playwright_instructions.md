# Instructions: Tending

How a Playwright wires the tending composite into a play. You wire by linking the play's
own content to the composite's files; the files stay untouched, they are canon. The
composite installs the compassion and caregiving engines as dependencies, and their laws
arrive with them. Authoring guidance, not runtime content, and it does not go on tour.

## Human

- The human sets who in the play meets somebody else's suffering, and what it does to them -- and, the question that governs the read, where the relief they seek is actually aimed.

## Agent

- From the play's own content, read a persona who meets another's need for where their relief is aimed, and link it under Projection: the turn while the response tracks the other's state (the tenderness that softens toward them, the succor that goes to them, the sensitivity that reads the need actually present rather than the one expected, from compassion and caregiving), the flinch while it tracks the persona's own (the distress that is their own alarm and departs when departing is easy, the pity that settles them above, the overprotection sized to their anxiety rather than the other's need), the toll while sustained tending has emptied them (the burnout, landing on a standing tenderheartedness everyone took to be the reason they were good at this). Play the flinch sympathetically: it is not hypocrisy, the feeling is real and often stronger, and the persona cannot tell from inside. You link to the composite, you never edit it.

## Collaboration

- Tenderness, succor, distress, pity, and the tenderhearted trait are the compassion engine's; sensitivity, overprotection, and burnout are caregiving's -- one alone is a process, not the question of where the relief went. What the suffering is like for the sufferer is the tragic composite's, read at the other end; the bond a persona has to somebody is love's, where this needs no bond and fires for strangers; the capacity to absorb stress is resilience's and the room a persona has is slack's, both of which meet this composite at the toll; kindness toward oneself is self-relation's; admiration and gratitude run toward the fortunate where this runs toward the badly-off; the costly sanction paid to make a wrongdoer answer is altruistic-punishment's, inside deserving, and points at a transgressor where this points at a sufferer. This composite owns the aim -- and the toll bridge carries the keystone: what ends the caring is not a shortage of it but an excess of resonance.

## Knowledge

- Tending is a persona read for what happens in them when they meet another's need -- braiding the other-focused state and the two responses that shadow it (compassion) with the behavioural system that acts on a felt need and what running it costs (caregiving) -- read as one system met at its feeling end and its acting end, a join both atoms already declare. Its question is where the relief is aimed, and that question matters because the movement's occurrence carries no information: the system fires whether or not the relief points at the other person, it fires hardest for the close and the visible and the young, and neither the persona nor the room can tell the direction from the fact of being moved. It runs in three faces. The turn is the relief aimed at the other: it tracks their state rather than the persona's, so it stops when the need stops, changes shape when the need changes, and does not require the sufferer to be grateful, improved, or pleasant -- and it is undemonstrative by construction, which is why next to somebody visibly overcome it looks like less. The flinch is the relief aimed at the persona's own state by three routes -- the distress that is their own alarm at another's pain, the pity that relieves by placing the sufferer below, the protection sized to the carer's anxiety rather than the other's need -- each sincerely felt, none hypocritical, and each stopping when the persona feels better rather than when the other does. Its tell is one condition only: Batson found that when escape is easy, personal distress predicts leaving while empathic concern predicts helping anyway, so the two are indistinguishable until the exit appears. The toll is the twist. What sustained tending spends is not a stock of caring: the persona who turned toward need after need is tired the way work makes people tired, and the persona who took each suffering on as their own has been running their own alarm several times a day for years, and it is that which empties them -- warmth toward sufferers goes first and does not come back with a holiday, because the state that produced it was never warmth but resonance, and resonance with pain is pain (Singer and Klimecki, where empathy training raised negative affect and compassion training reversed it). So the flinch and the toll are one failure at two speeds, fast where an exit exists and slow where it does not, and the persona instructed to feel what the sufferer feels has been aimed at the exit. Read a persona by where their relief was going, and never by how moved they appeared.

## System

- Do read the one meeting the need, not the one who has it: name whether the turn (relief aimed outward), the flinch (relief aimed at the persona's own state), or the toll (the cost of having sustained it) is running, and cast each from its engines.
- Do keep the turn quiet: the working form is the calm one, so a play that scores caring by how affected a persona appears has ranked the three exactly backwards.
- Do keep the flinch sincere: the feeling is real, frequently stronger than the turn's, and the persona has no way to tell -- a flinch written as insincerity has become something else entirely.
- Do stage the tell rather than assert it: the difference shows only when leaving is cheap, so give the persona an easy exit and let the audience learn which state they were in from whether they take it.
- Do let the toll carry the keystone: what is spent is resonance, not caring, so the burnt-out carer is neither a failed one to be pitied nor a saint to be honoured -- and both readings will already be in the room.
- Do let the room misjudge it: the other personas, the institution, and the persona themselves will read intensity as depth and exhaustion as caring too much, and each of those readings is honoured in life and is what the composite is for.
- Do not claim more than the evidence: the easy-escape dissociation is firm, the empathy-and-compassion separation is well argued on a modest base, and compassion fatigue as a measured construct is disputed -- so stage the mechanism and quote no magnitudes.
- Do not model the sufferer's end (that is tragic), the bond (that is love, and this needs none), the capacity to absorb (that is resilience, and the room is slack), kindness to oneself (that is self-relation), the response to the fortunate (that is praise and outdone), or the sanction aimed at a wrongdoer (that is altruistic-punishment, inside deserving). Do not make a suffering stand for a story (that is the play's pitch). Do not edit the composite's files; wire only from the play's side.
