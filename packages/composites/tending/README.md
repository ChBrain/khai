# Tending

Tending as composite: what happens in a persona who meets another's need -- the move toward relieving it (the turn), the move toward relieving their own state while appearing to turn toward (the flinch), and what sustained tending costs over time (the toll) -- wired over the compassion and caregiving engines, and closing on the discovery that the two failures are one, and it is not a shortage of caring but an excess of resonance.
