# Process: Tending, the Toll

## Taxonomy

[Tending](process_tending.md), what sustained tending costs, and where the three are seen for one thing.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: tending

## Initiated by

The tending has gone on, and the persona has less of it in them than they had.
The Toll starts wherever [burnout](@chbrain/khai-engine-caregiving/process_burnout.md) is running -- the erosion of a carer who has kept meeting need after need -- and it lands on the persona's standing [tenderheartedness](@chbrain/khai-engine-compassion/position_tenderhearted.md), which is the trait everyone including the persona took to be the reason they were good at this.
It is the face of the composite that arrives late and is read as a character having run out.

## Direction

Toward a carer who cannot any more, and does not know what it was that ran down.
The Toll reads the exhaustion and asks the question the exhaustion cannot answer for itself -- what has been spent? -- and finds that it is not the caring. The persona who turned toward need after need is tired in the ordinary way that work makes people tired. The persona who has been taking each suffering on as their own has been running an alarm on themselves, several times a day, for years, and it is that which has emptied them.
It moves the persona out of the work, and moves the meaning of everything they did before it.

## Lever

The exactness of the cost as a reading against every reason to read it as a virtue exhausted.
The carrying force is that the difference is real and shows in what is actually recoverable: warmth toward the sufferers is what goes first, and it does not come back with a holiday, because the state that produced it was never warmth -- it was resonance, and resonance with pain is pain; the resistance is that the persona will describe it as having cared too much, the institution will describe it as compassion fatigue, and the account will be honoured, which is why the correction is almost never made and the next carer is trained into the same mechanism on purpose.
The Toll rides the composite's keystone knife-edge: the flinch and the toll are the same failure at two speeds, so what ends the caring is not a shortage of it but an excess of resonance -- and the persona told to feel what the sufferer feels has been aimed at the exit.

## Echo

Emptied, and mourning a capacity they may not have had.
It shows the three faces complete -- one aimed outward and sustainable, one aimed inward and quick, one aimed inward and slow -- and carries beneath it the composite's keystone: taking the suffering on is the thing that looks most like compassion, feels most like compassion, and is the thing that terminates it, while turning toward it is quieter, colder-looking, and can be done for thirty years.
This is why the Toll is worth closing on and hardest to stage: the persona who burns out is not a failed carer to be pitied and not a saint to be honoured, and both of those readings are already in the room before the scene begins.
The Toll is the note the whole composite closes on: what a persona was doing when they cared is legible in what is gone afterwards, and nowhere else without a discount.
The Toll is dated from every occasion the persona felt it as their own, none of which anybody counted.
