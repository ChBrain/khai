# Process: Tending, the Turn

## Taxonomy

[Tending](process_tending.md), the relief aimed at the other, and the quietest of the three.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: tending

## Initiated by

The need registers as the other person's, and the persona moves toward it rather than away.
The Turn starts wherever [compassion](@chbrain/khai-engine-compassion/process_compassion.md) is running in its own form -- the [tenderness](@chbrain/khai-engine-compassion/process_tenderness.md) that softens toward the one in trouble and the [succor](@chbrain/khai-engine-compassion/process_succor.md) that goes to them -- and wherever [caregiving](@chbrain/khai-engine-caregiving/process_caregiving.md) is answering with [sensitivity](@chbrain/khai-engine-caregiving/process_sensitivity.md), reading the need that is actually there rather than the one the carer expected.
It is the face of the composite that works, and the one hardest to see working.

## Direction

Toward the other person being better off, by whatever the other person needs.
The Turn reads the response that stays aimed outward -- the question asked instead of the reassurance offered, the practical thing done without being announced, the sitting with somebody who does not want to be fixed -- and reads its defining property, which is that it tracks the other's state rather than the persona's: it stops when the need stops, it changes shape when the need changes, and it does not require the sufferer to be grateful, improved, or even pleasant.
It moves the persona toward somebody and keeps them there while it is useful.

## Lever

The accuracy of an outward aim against the near-total absence of signal that it is outward.
The carrying force is that this is the form that delivers: it can be sustained, it does not need the other to perform recovery, and it survives the moments when the sufferer is difficult, because nothing in it was ever about the persona's own state; the resistance is that it is undemonstrative by construction -- there is no display in it, it does not read as strongly felt, and next to a persona visibly overcome by someone's misfortune it looks, to everyone including both parties, like less.
The Turn spends the appearance of depth.

## Echo

Somebody helped, by a person who did not look especially moved.
It is the bridge that sets the composite's difficulty by being the baseline nobody recognises: the working form is the calm one, so a play that scores caring by how affected a persona appears has ranked the three exactly backwards.
What it hands upward is the standard the other two are measured against -- not intensity of feeling, which the flinch has more of, and not duration of service, which the toll is the exhaustion of, but whether the relief was ever pointed at the other person.
The Turn is dated from the moment the persona attended to what was actually needed, which nobody in the room registers as a moment.
