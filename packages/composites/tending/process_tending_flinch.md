# Process: Tending, the Flinch

## Taxonomy

[Tending](process_tending.md), the relief aimed at the persona's own state while appearing to be aimed outward.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: tending

## Initiated by

The other's suffering has landed as the persona's own, and the persona now needs something to stop.
The Flinch starts wherever compassion's shadows are running -- the [distress](@chbrain/khai-engine-compassion/process_distress.md) that is the persona's own alarm at another's pain, and the [pity](@chbrain/khai-engine-compassion/process_pity.md) that relieves by placing the other somewhere lower -- and wherever [overprotection](@chbrain/khai-engine-caregiving/process_overprotection.md) is answering a need the carer feels rather than the one that is there.
It is the face of the composite that is loudest, most sincerely felt, and aimed the wrong way.

## Direction

Toward the persona's own discomfort ending, by whichever route is available.
The Flinch reads the three exits and their tell -- the persona who is overcome and then, when leaving is easy, leaves; the persona who is very sorry for somebody in a way that settles them above; the persona who wraps the other in a protection that manages the carer's anxiety and takes the other's own footing away -- and reads that each of these tracks the persona's state rather than the sufferer's, which is why each stops when the persona feels better and not when the other is.
It moves the persona out of an unbearable position, and calls the movement care.

## Lever

The genuineness of the feeling against the direction it is pointed.
The carrying force is that none of this is hypocrisy: the distress is real and frequently more intense than anything the turn produces, the pity is sincerely meant, the overprotection is done at real cost by somebody who loves the person they are diminishing -- and the persona has no way to tell from inside, because being strongly affected feels exactly like caring strongly; the resistance is the tell, which shows only under one condition: when leaving is cheap, the flinch leaves and the turn does not, so the difference between the two is invisible until it becomes the only thing that matters.
The Flinch spends the sufferer, to buy the persona a way out.

## Echo

A persona relieved, and somebody left worse off by an act everyone present read as kindness.
It is the bridge that carries the composite's practical warning: intensity is not the measure, and the room -- and the persona, and often the sufferer -- will use it as the measure anyway.
What it hands upward is a suspicion the last bridge confirms: that this is not a lesser grade of caring but a different mechanism entirely, and that what it does quickly under an easy exit it does slowly under a hard one.
The Flinch is dated from the moment the persona's own state became the thing that needed attending to, which was not experienced as a moment at all.
