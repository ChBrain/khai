# Process: Tending

## Taxonomy

What happens in a persona who meets another's need.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: tending

## Initiated by

Somebody is suffering in front of a persona, and something in the persona has already moved.
Tending starts wherever that movement is running: as the other-focused state and the responses that shadow it, through [compassion](@chbrain/khai-engine-compassion/process_compassion.md); and as the behavioural system that acts on a felt need, through [caregiving](@chbrain/khai-engine-caregiving/process_caregiving.md).
This composite adds no third engine. It reads that these are one system met at two ends -- the feeling end and the acting end -- and both atoms say so already: one names the caregiving system as compassion's substrate, the other names compassion's erosion as caregiving's characteristic injury.

## Direction

Toward relief, and toward the question of whose.
The composite wires the two engines and reads their working in three shapes: [the Turn](process_tending_turn.md), the relief aimed at the other; [the Flinch](process_tending_flinch.md), the relief aimed at the persona's own state while appearing to be aimed outward; and [the Toll](process_tending_toll.md), the twist -- what sustained tending costs, and where the second turns out to have been the same thing all along.
The three are not degrees of caring. They are one movement sorted by where the relief is going, which is not visible from the outside and is not reliably visible from the inside either.

## Lever

The plain fact that another's need moves a persona against the fact that being moved says nothing about whom the movement serves.
The carrying force is that the response is real and mostly involuntary: a persona who meets suffering does not decide to be affected, the system fires, and it fires hardest for the close, the visible, and the young -- which is why the movement can be trusted to occur and why its occurrence is worth nothing as evidence; the resistance is that the aim is the whole question and the aim is invisible, so the persona reports caring, the room observes caring, and neither can say from that report whether the other person is going to be better off.
Tending is read on the persona meeting the need, never on the one who has it; what it is like to be the sufferer belongs elsewhere.

## Echo

A persona moved, and a question left open about which way.
Tending does not close; it is the movement running, gathered into one reading -- a turn, a flinch, a toll.
It hands off what it is not: what the suffering is like for the one suffering -- the grief, the pain, the empathy felt with them -- is the tragic composite's, read at the other end; the bond a persona has to somebody, its desire and loyalty and disclosure, is the love composite's, where this needs no bond at all and fires for strangers; the standing capacity to absorb and recover from stress is the resilience composite's, which owns the coping where this owns the aiming; the persona's own kindness toward themselves is the self-relation composite's; the admiration and gratitude felt toward another's worth are praise's, which run toward the fortunate where this runs toward the badly-off; the costly sanction paid to make a wrongdoer answer is the altruistic-punishment engine's, inside deserving, and points at a transgressor where this points at a sufferer; the room a persona has before their own shortfall does their thinking is the slack composite's, which prices what this costs when there is none; and a suffering made to **stand for** a story is authored key, the play's pitch, not the force.
The persona is dated from where their relief was aimed, which is the one thing nobody in the scene can see.
