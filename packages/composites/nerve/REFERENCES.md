# Nerve: Reference

## Line of Work

Nerve as **composite**: what a persona does with an alarm that is still ringing. It is not a third
mechanism but a wiring of two: the **fear** engine supplies the alarm -- threat appraised, the body
readied to escape or avoid -- and the **courage** engine supplies the move made toward the thing
anyway, for a good worth the facing. This composite adds no third engine; it reads the relation the
courage atom already names, and stages it into three bridges, closing on the discovery that the
fear is not the obstacle to courage but the condition of it.

The domain does not model either side alone -- panic, phobia, terror, and the standing fearfulness
are the fear engine's; the physical, conviction, and vital courages are courage's; and one alone is
a process, not the moment of meeting. Nor is it the dread of what has not arrived, which is the
jeopardy composite's; nor the diffuse future unease with no object in the room, which is the
anxiety engine's inside prospection; nor the absorbing of a blow and the coming back, which is
resilience's. It owns the meeting: what happened at the moment the danger was there.

The join is declared inside the courage atom, which anchors Rachman for the empirical separation of
fear and courage. That separation is the composite's whole content. Fear is not one thing but
three loosely coupled ones -- what a persona reports, what their body does, and what they actually
do -- and the three can come apart. Courage is what the third component looks like when it has come
apart from the other two: approach behaviour with the report and the arousal still running. From
which the composite's structure follows. Nothing before the move distinguishes the persona who will
advance from the persona who will run, because the two are identical on the components that are
observable in advance; and courage therefore has no existence as a state, only as a record of
moves. Rachman's populations -- bomb-disposal operators, paratroopers, decorated servicemen -- also
supply the training result the third bridge needs: the behavioural component decouples with
practice while the subjective one persists, so competence at meeting danger is bought and the price
goes on being paid privately long after it stops showing.

Rachman's other finding is what makes the flat bridge more than an observation about poker faces:
fearlessness is a distinct condition rather than the top of the courage scale. A persona in whom
the alarm does not fire is not further along than one who fires and advances; they are off the
scale, because there is nothing being overcome and, more practically, because the alarm is how
danger is learned and an organism that does not register it does not update. So the calm surface
holds two people with opposite futures -- one whose cost is being paid invisibly and whose
competence was purchased, one with no cost, no purchase, and no capacity to learn -- and nothing
visible separates them.

The flanks come from the atoms' own anchors and are load-bearing here. LeDoux and the Blanchards
give the alarm bridge its character: a defensive system that fires on imminence, biases toward the
false positive, and readies the body before any appraisal the persona would recognise as thinking
-- which is why obeying it is cheap and why it is competent about danger and silent about worth.
Barlow's distinction between phasic fear and sustained anxiety is the boundary the fear engine
already keeps and this composite inherits: nerve is read at the meeting, and the diffuse dread of
an absent object belongs elsewhere. Aristotle supplies the courage bridge's shape as a mean rather
than a maximum -- the excess is recklessness, which is not more courage but a failure to register
the danger correctly, and which the flat bridge's second occupant is the standing case of. Putman
and the Pury and Lopez synthesis supply the taxonomy the courage atom runs on.

## Restrictions

What the composite refuses to model, and to whom it delegates.

- **The two sides (the atoms)**: The composite wires the two but owns neither. Panic, phobia,
  terror, and the standing fearfulness are the fear engine's; the physical, conviction, and vital
  courages are courage's. Nerve reads only the moment they meet, and one side alone is a process
  rather than that moment.
- **The waiting (jeopardy)**: Apprehension, stress, and suspense are the jeopardy composite's,
  which owns the **before** -- the dread of a thing that has not arrived and the tension of not
  knowing. Nerve owns the meeting. A play can run both across one scene, and should not read the
  waiting as the test: nothing endured before the danger arrives is evidence about what will happen
  when it does.
- **The unease with no object (prospection)**: Anxiety, diffuse and future-directed, is the anxiety
  engine's inside prospection, and the fear engine already keeps that boundary on Barlow's terms.
  Nerve requires something present or imminent to be met; where there is nothing in the room, there
  is nothing to step toward.
- **The after (resilience)**: Coping and stress, the absorbing of a blow and the coming back from
  it, are the resilience composite's. Nerve closes at the move; what it costs afterwards and
  whether the persona recovers is resilience's reading of the same event.
- **The long haul (striving)**: The achievement motive, regulatory focus, and grit are the striving
  composite's -- persistence over years toward a distant goal, which is a different quantity from a
  move made now into something present. A persona can have a great deal of one and none of the
  other.
- **The belief about oneself (capability)**: Mindset, locus of control, self-efficacy, and
  self-handicapping are the capability composite's. What a persona believes they can do is not what
  they did, and this composite reads only the second -- a persona certain of their own courage who
  has never stepped has nothing here to link.
- **What made it worth facing (conscience, moral-conviction)**: The moral self, the conviction, and
  the commitment a persona will speak against cost for are the conscience composite's. Nerve owns
  the facing and is indifferent to the cause: a step taken for a bad reason is still a step, and
  the composite will not grade it.
- **The danger as authored symbol (the play's pitch)**: A threat made to **stand for** a theme --
  the storm that arrives to mirror a reckoning -- is refused: that is authored key, the play's
  pitch, not the force on the persona. Nerve sets what was met and what was done; the meaning read
  into it is the play's.

## Encoding

Source to constraint. A composite root over two engine atoms it declares as dependencies, with
three bridges.

- **[nerve](process_nerve.md)** (the root): what a persona does with an alarm that is still ringing,
  read at the moment of meeting and never in advance. Anchored by Rachman (1978) on the empirical
  separation of fear and courage.
- **[the alarm](process_nerve_alarm.md)** (bridge): the fear running properly -- competent about
  danger, silent about worth, and predicting nothing about what comes next. Anchored by LeDoux and
  the Blanchards on the defensive system, and by Barlow on the phasic/sustained boundary.
- **[the step](process_nerve_step.md)** (bridge): the move made into the thing while the alarm is
  still sounding, and the only place the two engines separate. Anchored by Aristotle on the mean
  and by Putman and Pury & Lopez on courage as an act rather than a state.
- **[the flat](process_nerve_flat.md)** (bridge): the calm surface, holding the persona whose
  behaviour has decoupled from a fear still running and the persona in whom nothing fires.
  Anchored by Rachman on desynchrony under training and on fearlessness as a distinct condition;
  the composite's twist and keystone, that a persona who feels nothing has performed nothing, so
  the calm that reads as the most courageous carries no information at all.
