# Process: Nerve

## Taxonomy

What a persona does with an alarm that is still ringing.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: nerve

## Initiated by

Something dangerous is present or close, the body has already answered, and there is a thing the persona was going to do.
Nerve starts wherever both are running: the alarm itself, through [fear](@chbrain/khai-engine-fear/process_fear.md); and the move toward the thing anyway, for a good worth the facing, through [courage](@chbrain/khai-engine-courage/process_courage.md).
This composite adds no third engine. It reads the relation the courage atom already names when it anchors Rachman -- that fear and courage are separable rather than opposed -- and follows the separation to the one place it becomes visible.

## Direction

Toward a danger, or away from it, with the same body reporting the same thing either way.
The composite wires the two engines and reads their working in three shapes: [the Alarm](process_nerve_alarm.md), the fear doing its proper work; [the Step](process_nerve_step.md), the move made into it while it is still sounding; and [the Flat](process_nerve_flat.md), the twist -- the calm that holds two people who cannot be told apart.
The three are not degrees of bravery. They are one moment read at its two components and at the case where one of them has gone missing.

## Lever

The reliability of the alarm as a report on the danger against its uselessness as a report on the persona.
The carrying force is that the fear is correct and worth having: it fires on threat, readies the body, and pulls toward the exit, and a persona without it learns nothing from danger and walks into it again -- which is why the alarm is not a weakness to be got rid of; the resistance is that it says nothing about what will happen next, since the persona about to run and the persona about to advance are, up to the moment of the move, reporting and showing the same thing.
Nerve is read at the moment of meeting, never in advance; nothing before the move is evidence about the move.

## Echo

A persona who went in or did not, and a body that had no opinion about which.
Nerve does not close; it is the alarm and the answer running together, gathered into one reading -- an alarm, a step, a flat.
It hands off what it is not: the dread of what has not arrived yet -- the apprehension, the stress, the suspense of the waiting -- is the jeopardy composite's, which owns the before where this owns the meeting; the diffuse future-directed unease with no object in the room is the anxiety engine's, inside prospection, and the fear engine keeps that boundary already; the absorbing of a blow and the coming back from it is the resilience composite's, which owns the after; the persistence over years toward a distant goal is the striving composite's, where this is a move made now; what a persona believes about their own capacity is the capability composite's, and the belief is not the act; what makes a thing worth facing -- the conviction, the moral commitment the step is taken for -- is the conscience composite's, since this owns the facing and never the cause; and a danger made to **stand for** a story is authored key, the play's pitch, not the force on the persona.
The persona is dated from the move, and from nothing they felt before it.
