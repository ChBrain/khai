# Process: Nerve, the Alarm

## Taxonomy

[Nerve](process_nerve.md), the fear doing its proper work, before anything has been decided.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: nerve

## Initiated by

Something dangerous is present or imminent, and the body has answered before the persona has.
The Alarm starts wherever [fear](@chbrain/khai-engine-fear/process_fear.md) is firing -- the threat appraised and the system readied to escape or avoid -- in whatever form it takes: the [panic](@chbrain/khai-engine-fear/process_panic.md) that arrives as a false alarm at full volume, the [phobia](@chbrain/khai-engine-fear/process_phobia.md) aimed at a thing the persona knows is not worth it, the [terror](@chbrain/khai-engine-fear/process_terror.md) of a danger that genuinely is -- and it is tuned by a standing [fearfulness](@chbrain/khai-engine-fear/position_fearfulness.md) that sets how readily any of it fires.
It is the face of the composite that is not optional and not a fault.

## Direction

Toward the exit, at speed, with the reasons arriving afterwards.
The Alarm reads the pull rather than the feeling -- the step not taken toward the edge, the hand that will not go into the dark, the voice that does not raise itself in the meeting -- and reads it as a system working correctly: it fires early, it errs toward the false positive, and it costs a persona almost nothing to obey.
It moves the persona away, or holds them still, and it does so before anything has been weighed.

## Lever

The correctness of the alarm against the cost of always taking its advice.
The carrying force is that fear is a competent instrument and its bias is the right way round -- a persona who treats every rustle as a predator loses a little, and one who treats a predator as a rustle loses everything, so the system is built to over-fire and a persona is right to have it; the resistance is that it does not know what the persona is for: it reports danger and has no access to whether the thing on the other side is worth it, so a life run entirely on it is a life in which nothing worth doing is done.
The Alarm spends nothing and asks for everything.

## Echo

A persona ready to leave, and not yet a persona who has.
It is the bridge that establishes what the next one has to be measured against: fear present, functioning, and pulling -- so that a move made into it is a move made against a working system rather than in the absence of one.
What it hands upward is the composite's first refusal: the alarm predicts nothing. The persona who will run and the persona who will advance are, at this moment, indistinguishable to the room, to the instruments, and to themselves.
The Alarm is dated from the threat, and from no decision at all.
