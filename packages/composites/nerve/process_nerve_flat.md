# Process: Nerve, the Flat

## Taxonomy

[Nerve](process_nerve.md), the calm that shows no alarm, and where the two are seen for one thing.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: nerve

## Initiated by

A danger is present and nothing in the persona appears to have moved.
The Flat starts wherever the surface has gone quiet over one of two entirely different insides: a [fear](@chbrain/khai-engine-fear/process_fear.md) still running whose pull has come loose from the behaviour after enough [steps](process_nerve_step.md) were taken against it, or a standing [fearfulness](@chbrain/khai-engine-fear/position_fearfulness.md) set so low that there is nothing to come loose from.
It is the face of the composite everyone in the room reads as the top of the scale.

## Direction

Toward an appearance that is not evidence of anything.
The Flat reads the two occupants and refuses to choose between them from the outside -- the one who has done this a hundred times and is still afraid privately, quietly, and without expecting it to stop; and the one who simply does not fire, has never learned what the danger costs, and will meet it again the same way -- and reads that the second is not further along the scale than the first but off it altogether, because there is no [courage](@chbrain/khai-engine-courage/process_courage.md) in a move that costs nothing.
It moves nobody. It is what the other two look like once the surface has settled over them.

## Lever

The exactness of the calm as a reading of the surface against its total silence about what is under it.
The carrying force is that the calm is real in both cases and is what the room will use: an unshaking hand is the single most persuasive thing a persona can present at a moment of danger, and it is presented equally well by long practice and by an alarm that never rang; the resistance is that the two have opposite futures -- one has a cost being paid invisibly and a competence that was bought, the other has no cost, no purchase, and no capacity to learn from a danger it does not register.
The Flat rides the composite's keystone knife-edge: the fear is not the obstacle to courage but the condition of it, so a persona who feels nothing has performed nothing, and the calm that reads as the most courageous is the one reading that carries no information at all.

## Echo

Two people, one surface, and no way to tell from here.
It shows the two engines complete -- an alarm that reports the danger and never the persona, and a step that is the only evidence either way -- and carries beneath it the composite's keystone: courage is not a feeling, not a state, and not visible in advance; it exists only as a record of moves made while the alarm was sounding, so it cannot be predicted, cannot be claimed, and disappears the moment the fear does.
This is why the Flat is the hardest of the three to stage and the only one worth closing on: a play must resist telling the audience which of the two it has, because nobody in the scene can know either, and the resolution the audience wants is the one thing the situation does not contain.
The Flat is the note the whole composite closes on: what a persona has in them is legible in what they did while afraid, and nowhere else without a discount.
The Flat is dated from a hundred earlier occasions, or from none, and the surface does not say which.
