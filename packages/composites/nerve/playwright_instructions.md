# Instructions: Nerve

How a Playwright wires the nerve composite into a play. You wire by linking the play's
own content to the composite's files; the files stay untouched, they are canon. The
composite installs the fear and courage engines as dependencies, and their laws arrive
with them. Authoring guidance, not runtime content, and it does not go on tour.

## Human

- The human sets which dangers in the play are met and by whom: what is actually there, what it would cost to go toward it, and what is on the other side that could be worth the facing.

## Agent

- From the play's own content, read a persona at the moment a danger is present and link under Projection what they did with the alarm: the alarm while the fear is running (the threat appraised and the body readied, in whatever form -- the panic that fires as a false alarm, the phobia aimed at a thing the persona knows is not worth it, the terror of a danger that is -- tuned by a standing fearfulness, from fear), the step while the move is being made into it anyway (the physical move into bodily danger, the conviction that speaks when speaking costs, the vital courage of facing what cannot be escaped, from courage), the flat while nothing shows at all. Keep the fear present during the step -- removing it removes the courage -- and never let the alarm predict the step, because it cannot. You link to the composite, you never edit it.

## Collaboration

- Panic, phobia, terror, and the standing fearfulness are the fear engine's; the physical, conviction, and vital courages are courage's -- one alone is a process, not the moment of meeting. The dread of what has not arrived is the jeopardy composite's, which owns the before; the diffuse future unease with no object in the room is the anxiety engine's inside prospection; the absorbing of a blow and the coming back is resilience's, which owns the after; persistence over years toward a distant goal is striving's; what a persona believes they can do is capability's, and the belief is not the act; what makes a thing worth facing is the conscience composite's, since this owns the facing and never the cause. This composite owns the meeting -- and the flat bridge carries the keystone: the fear is not the obstacle to courage but the condition of it.

## Knowledge

- Nerve is a persona read at the moment a danger is present and their alarm is still ringing -- braiding the defensive alarm that appraises the threat and readies the body to escape (fear) with the move made toward the thing anyway, for a good worth the facing (courage). The join is Rachman's, and the courage engine already anchors it: fear is not one thing but three loosely coupled ones -- what a persona reports, what their body does, and what they actually do -- and the three come apart. Courage is what the third looks like when it has come apart from the other two: approach with the report and the arousal still running. It runs in three faces. The alarm is the fear working properly and it is not a fault: it fires on imminence, biases toward the false positive, readies the body before anything the persona would call thinking, and costs almost nothing to obey -- competent about danger and entirely silent about worth, so a life run on it alone is one in which nothing worth doing is done. And it predicts nothing: the persona who will run and the persona who will advance are, until the move, identical on everything anyone can observe. The step is the move made into the thing while the alarm is still sounding, and it is the only place the two engines separate -- the hands still unsteady, the pull still toward the exit, the persona not overriding the fear so much as walking while carrying it. There is no state of courage, only a record of steps, and the move does not get cheaper for having been made before, only more available. The flat is the twist. With enough steps the behaviour comes loose from the feeling, and the persona then looks exactly like somebody who was never afraid -- which is also what somebody who was never afraid looks like. So the calm surface holds two people with opposite futures: one whose cost is still being paid privately and whose competence was purchased, and one in whom the alarm does not fire, who is not further along the scale but off it, since there is no courage in a move that costs nothing and since the alarm is how danger is learned. Here the two are seen for one thing: courage is not a feeling, not a state, and not visible in advance; it cannot be predicted, cannot be claimed, and disappears the moment the fear does. Read a persona by what they did while afraid, and read the unshaking hand as carrying no information.

## System

- Do read the meeting, not the run-up: name whether the alarm (fear running), the step (the move made into it), or the flat (nothing showing) is what the scene has, and cast each from its engines.
- Do keep the fear present during the step: a persona who has stopped being afraid is no longer being courageous, and a play that resolves the fear before the move has written the courage out of it.
- Do not let the alarm predict the step: the frightened persona and the one about to advance look the same, so a play that telegraphs which is which from the trembling has claimed knowledge the situation does not contain.
- Do let the step be expensive every time: practice makes the move available, not free, and a veteran paying nothing is a persona from the flat bridge's second category, whether or not the play means them to be.
- Do let the flat carry the keystone: two people, one surface, opposite futures -- and resist telling the audience which one it has, because nobody in the scene can know either.
- Do keep recklessness out of the courage column: the persona who does not register the danger has not exceeded courage, they have failed a reading, and Aristotle's excess is a defect rather than a surplus.
- Do let a step taken for a bad reason still be a step: this composite owns the facing and never the cause, and it will not grade what the danger was met for.
- Do not model the dread of what has not arrived (that is jeopardy), the diffuse unease with no object (that is anxiety, inside prospection), the coming back afterwards (that is resilience), the long persistence (that is striving), the belief about oneself (that is capability), or what made the thing worth facing (that is conscience). Do not make a danger stand for a story (that is the play's pitch). Do not edit the composite's files; wire only from the play's side.
