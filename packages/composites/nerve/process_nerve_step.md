# Process: Nerve, the Step

## Taxonomy

[Nerve](process_nerve.md), the move made into the thing while the alarm is still sounding.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: nerve

## Initiated by

The alarm is running, the pull is toward the exit, and the persona goes the other way.
The Step starts wherever [courage](@chbrain/khai-engine-courage/process_courage.md) is running -- the [physical](@chbrain/khai-engine-courage/process_physical.md) move into bodily danger, the [conviction](@chbrain/khai-engine-courage/process_conviction.md) that speaks when speaking costs, and the [vital](@chbrain/khai-engine-courage/process_vital.md) courage of facing what cannot be escaped and going on living in it.
It is the face of the composite where the two engines finally come apart, and the only one where they can.

## Direction

Toward the thing, against everything the body has just recommended.
The Step reads the move and nothing else -- the person who went back into the building, the one who said the sentence they could not unsay, the one who got up the next morning knowing what was coming -- and reads its defining property, which is that the alarm has not stopped: the hands are still unsteady, the pull is still toward the exit, and the persona is not overriding the fear so much as walking while carrying it.
It moves the persona into the danger, and leaves the fear exactly where it was.

## Lever

The good on the far side against a system that has no opinion about goods.
The carrying force is that something has been judged worth it and the judgement has been made operative -- which is the whole of the act, since the fear will not concede the point and does not have to; the resistance is that this is expensive in a way nothing before it was, and it is expensive every time: the move does not get cheaper because it was made yesterday, only more available, and a persona who has done it a hundred times is not a persona for whom the hundred-and-first is free.
The Step spends the persona's own alarm, and buys nothing back from it.

## Echo

A move made, and a body still objecting.
It is the bridge that carries the composite's whole discrimination: fear and courage are not opposite ends of one scale, they are two things running at once, and the only place they separate is here. A persona cannot be courageous quietly at home; there is no state of courage, only a record of steps.
What it hands upward is the setup for the last bridge -- that with enough of these steps the behaviour comes loose from the feeling, and once it has, the persona will look, to everybody watching, like somebody who was never afraid.
The Step is dated from the move, and the move is the only evidence there has ever been.
