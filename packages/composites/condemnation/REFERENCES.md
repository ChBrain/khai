# Condemnation: Reference

## Line of Work

The other-condemning moral emotions as **composite**: one condemnation of another's violation, and one dial -- which moral code was broken -- that sets which of three corners the condemnation lands in. No single atom engine sees the code axis the corners share. A wrong to community is the contempt engine's, whole; a wrong to autonomy is the anger engine's; a wrong to divinity is the disgust engine's. This package authors none of those readings again. It declares the three engines as dependencies, wires their members by hard package links, and adds only what the atoms cannot hold: the code axis itself, and the three motions along and across it -- the fusion where codes combine into dehumanization, the hardening where hot anger cools into cold contempt, and the reading where one act is coded now one way, now another.

The warrant is accordingly integrative, not primary: the sources below establish the CAD triad and the three-ethics map the composite reads over, not the individual emotions, which their own engines warrant. The spine is Rozin, Lowery, Imada and Haidt's CAD Triad Hypothesis, which maps Contempt, Anger, and Disgust onto violations of Community, Autonomy, and Divinity (Shweder's three ethics), and Haidt's Moral Foundations, which explains why the same act draws different condemnations from readers who weight the codes differently. It is the other-directed counterpart to the self-conscious composite: there an appraisal of the self against a standard yields guilt, shame, and pride; here an appraisal of another's violation yields contempt, anger, and disgust.

## Restrictions

What the composite refuses to model, and to whom it delegates.

- **The three atoms**: The composite authors no emotion of its own. Contempt -- the downward look at another judged beneath their station -- is the contempt engine's, whole; anger -- the hot approach at an autonomy wrong -- is the anger engine's; disgust -- the recoil from a divinity defilement -- is the disgust engine's. This package reads over them and adds only the code axis and its motions; every corner is an atom's ground, cited by hard link, never restated.
- **Moral judgment**: The composite models the condemnation, not the reasoned verdict beneath it. Which foundation is right, whether a reading is justified, and the adjudication of the wrong are owned by the moral-judgment engine; this composite owns only the emotional condemnation and the fact that it follows the code the reader brings.
- **Self-conscious**: The composite models the other-directed condemnation, not the self-directed appraisal. When the appraisal falls on the self against a standard -- guilt, shame, authentic pride, hubris -- it is the self-conscious composite's; this one is its mirror, the same moral machinery turned outward on another.
- **Aggression and Stigma**: The composite models the condemnation and the dehumanization it can fuse into, not the behavior or the social structure that follows. The aggression the fused condemnation licenses is the aggression engine's, and the durable social mark and exclusion of the condemned is the stigma engine's; this composite owns the emotional othering, not its enactment or its institution.

## Encoding

Source to constraint, per file.

- **[condemnation](process_condemnation.md)** (the root): The other-condemning family as one appraisal with one dial -- which code was broken -- landing in contempt (community), anger (autonomy), or disgust (divinity). Anchored by Rozin et al. (the CAD triad) and Shweder (the three ethics).
- **[code, fusion](process_code_fusion.md)** (bridge): The codes firing together and the violator dehumanized past the reach of amends -- the ANCODI cluster and the contempt-disgust othering. Anchored by Matsumoto et al. (ANCODI) and Fiske et al. (the contempt-disgust prejudice).
- **[code, hardening](process_code_hardening.md)** (bridge): Hot anger cooling into cold contempt as the account stays open -- the act-condemnation eroding into a person-condemnation. Anchored by Gottman (contempt as anger's corrosive terminus).
- **[code, reading](process_code_reading.md)** (bridge): One act coded now as harm, now as betrayal, now as defilement, by the code the reader holds sacred -- and the moralization that re-codes the neutral. Anchored by Haidt's Moral Foundations, Shweder's ethics, and Rozin (moralization).
