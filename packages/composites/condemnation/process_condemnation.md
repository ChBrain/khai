# Process: Condemnation

## Taxonomy

A composite reading over three atom engines (no canon file of its own): the contempt, anger, and disgust engines this package declares as dependencies.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: condemnation

## Initiated by

A persona meets another's violation of a moral order and condemns them.
One condemnation, and one dial sets which face it takes: which moral code the violation broke.
This is Rozin's CAD triad, over Shweder's three ethics -- a wrong to **community** (the hierarchy, duty, or role that binds) draws [contempt](@chbrain/khai-engine-contempt/process_contempt.md), the downward look that places the violator beneath their station; a wrong to **autonomy** (the rights, fairness, or freedom a person is owed) draws [anger](@chbrain/khai-engine-anger/process_anger.md), the hot approach that answers the harm; a wrong to **divinity** (the purity or sanctity of the body and the sacred) draws [disgust](@chbrain/khai-engine-disgust/process_sociomoral.md), the recoil that casts the violator out as unclean.
No single engine sees the axis the three share; that is what this composite is for -- and it is the other-directed mirror of the self-conscious family, where the same appraisal falls on the self rather than on another.

## Direction

Outward, at the violator, along the code axis -- and in more than one code at once.
The atoms each aim their own emotion at their own code; what the family adds is that a single wrong can be read through two or three codes together, and that a condemnation can travel from code to code as the wrong is re-read or the tie sours.
The direction of the drama is across the codes, not within any one of them.

## Lever

The moral code the persona reads as violated -- for the same act is not one condemnation but three possible ones, and which fires depends on which code the reader holds sacred.
Haidt's Moral Foundations name the split: readers who weight care and fairness see an autonomy harm or nothing; readers who weight loyalty, authority, or sanctity see a community betrayal or a divinity defilement where the first saw no wrong at all.
What the composite spends is the assumption that a wrong carries one moral meaning.

## Echo

The violator is condemned -- and, where more than one code fires, dehumanized past the reach of amends.
The atoms each run their own code and cannot see the family: the fusion where the codes combine and strip the other of standing, the hardening where a hot autonomy-anger cools into a cold community-contempt, and the reading where one act is coded now as harm, now as betrayal, now as defilement.
It runs as [code, fusion](process_code_fusion.md), [code, hardening](process_code_hardening.md), and [code, reading](process_code_reading.md). The reasoned adjudication of right and wrong beneath the emotions is the moral-judgment engine's; the self-directed appraisal of guilt, shame, and pride is the self-conscious composite's; this composite owns the other-directed condemnation and the code axis its three emotions share.
