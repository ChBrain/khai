# Process: Code, Hardening

## Taxonomy

[Condemnation](process_condemnation.md), the bridge where hot anger cools into cold contempt.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: condemnation

## Initiated by

An [anger](@chbrain/khai-engine-anger/process_anger.md) that will not resolve.
The anger engine reads a specific autonomy wrong -- hot, aimed at the act, and reparable, wanting the account squared; what it cannot read is the wrong changing kind.
When the account stays open, or the same wrong repeats, or the tie sours, the condemnation cools and hardens: _this act wronged me_ becomes _you are the kind of person beneath my regard_, and the persona has crossed from anger into [contempt](@chbrain/khai-engine-contempt/process_contempt.md) without any new event -- from the hot, act-aimed, autonomy code to the cold, person-aimed, community one.
Gottman drew this as the deadliest turn a bond can take: contempt is where anger goes when it gives up on the other.

## Direction

From act to person, hot to cold, one way by default.
Hardening is the easy slope: an anger left unanswered drifts toward contempt, while contempt almost never softens back to anger on its own -- for anger still grants the other standing enough to be wronged, and contempt has withdrawn it.
This is the other-directed mirror of the self-conscious composite's widening, where guilt's act-verdict erodes into shame's self-verdict; here anger's act-condemnation erodes into contempt's person-condemnation.

## Lever

What the hardening destroys -- anger's whole exit.
Anger's account can be squared: an apology, a repair, a restitution answers the act. Contempt's cannot, because its verdict is on the person, not the deed, and a person cannot be repaired the way an act can.
The moment the condemnation hardens, the roads out stop fitting: the other could still make amends, and no amends is person-sized. A production holds this lever by choosing when the cooling happens -- mid-scene as a fresh slight lands on an old account, or across acts, discovered when an apology that should have worked is met with a cold look instead of a warm one.

## Echo

The persona now runs on contempt's engine -- the downward look replaces the hot objection, and the corrosion Gottman named sets in: the bond, once merely strained by anger, now erodes.
Whoever knew the persona in the anger corner will misread them in the contempt corner, because the temperature and the target both changed while the original wrong did not.
The warming back, if the production stages one, is a scene -- a re-granting of standing -- not a drift.
