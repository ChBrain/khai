# Instructions: Condemnation

How a Playwright wires the condemnation composite into a play. You wire by
linking the play's own content to the composite's files and, through them,
to the three atom engines it depends on; every file stays untouched, they
are canon. The Roadie plumbs the law into Instructions Knowledge on deploy.
Authoring guidance, not runtime content, and it does not go on tour.

## Human

- The human sets the violation and the codes in play: what another has done,
  which moral order it breaks -- the community that binds, the autonomy a
  person is owed, the divinity of the sacred -- and whose codes are being
  applied, so a condemnation has a wrong, a code, and a reader.

## Agent

- Cast the corners from the atoms as usual: the downward look and its form
  from the contempt engine; the flare, the form, and the trait from anger;
  the recoil, the domain, and the sensitivity from disgust. Then cast the
  axis itself: which code each significant persona reads the wrong through,
  and how readily. Stage the bridges as motions, not states: a fusion is
  codes stacking into dehumanization the audience watches build, a hardening
  is a hot anger cooling into cold contempt across scenes, a reading is two
  personas coding the same act differently in the same room. A plot that
  turns on a condemnation fusing or hardening may link that bridge under its
  Tension; the audit surfaces the ones that stage it unnamed. You link to
  the composite and its atoms, you never edit them.

## Collaboration

- Every corner's science is its atom's: contempt, anger, and disgust each
  carry their own warrant and their own instructions. The reasoned verdict
  beneath the condemnation is moral-judgment's; the aggression a fused
  condemnation licenses is the aggression engine's; the durable social mark
  on the condemned is stigma's; the self-directed mirror of this family --
  guilt, shame, pride -- is the self-conscious composite's. Whether the
  personas genuinely move across the codes, rather than sitting in assigned
  corners like a diagram, is the team's review.

## Knowledge

- The other-condemning family is one condemnation with one dial: which moral
  code a violation broke. The three corners -- contempt for a community
  wrong, anger for an autonomy wrong, disgust for a divinity wrong (Rozin's
  CAD triad) -- share one code axis, and the drama lives in motion across it:
  codes fuse, and where two or three fire at once the violator is
  dehumanized past the reach of amends; an unanswered anger hardens, cooling
  from a hot act-condemnation into a cold person-contempt the way guilt
  widens into shame; and one act is read through different codes by different
  readers, so the same scene divides a room. Read the production in which
  codes are firing and which way the condemnation is moving, never in a fixed
  assignment of persona to corner.

## System

- Do cast every corner from its atom before staging a motion; do give each
  significant persona a code tendency; do let a fusion build from stacked
  codes and a hardening cool across scenes, so the dehumanization and the
  contempt are earned, not announced.
- Do not pin a persona to one code for the whole run without making the
  fixity itself the point; do not resolve a hardening by drift when the
  warming back needs a scene; do not let the composite pass a moral verdict
  -- that is moral-judgment's; do not edit the composite's or the atoms'
  files; wire only from the play's side.
