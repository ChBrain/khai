# Process: Code, Fusion

## Taxonomy

[Condemnation](process_condemnation.md), the bridge where the codes fire together and the violator is dehumanized.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: condemnation

## Initiated by

A wrong read through more than one code at once.
Each atom answers a single code -- [contempt](@chbrain/khai-engine-contempt/process_contempt.md) to community, [anger](@chbrain/khai-engine-anger/process_anger.md) to autonomy, [disgust](@chbrain/khai-engine-disgust/process_disgust.md) to divinity -- and none, alone, sees what their combination does.
When a violation is read as harm _and_ betrayal _and_ defilement together, the three fire as one: the anger-contempt-disgust cluster (the "ANCODI" that Matsumoto and colleagues find in the rhetoric preceding intergroup violence), and the contempt-disgust fusion that denies the target full humanity.

## Direction

Toward the violator stripped of standing.
Where anger alone keeps the other an equal who wronged and can make amends, the fused condemnation removes the other from the moral community entirely -- beneath regard, and unclean, and wronging, at once.
Its direction is exclusion: not a wrong to be answered but a person to be cast out.

## Lever

What the fusion destroys -- the reparability each code keeps alone.
Anger's wrong can be answered, contempt's inferior can rise, disgust's taint can sometimes be cleansed; but fused, the codes license what none licenses alone: the other placed outside the circle where amends, rise, or cleansing could apply.
This is the engine of moralized cruelty and the othering that runs ahead of atrocity -- contempt and disgust together the most corrosive pairing (Fiske's map of the prejudice aimed at groups seen as low in warmth and competence).

## Echo

The violator is dehumanized, and the condemnation self-justifies -- the fused state reads its own severity as proof of the wrong.
A production stages this by letting the codes stack rather than choosing one, and by reading the fusion, not any single emotion, as the license for the harm that follows: the mob that is angrier, colder, and more revolted than any one of those alone would warrant.
The un-fusing back, if the production stages one, is a scene -- a re-granting of the standing the fusion withdrew -- not a drift.
