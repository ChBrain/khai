# Process: Learned Helplessness

## Taxonomy

Learned helplessness. The cognitive-behavioral lock-in where the persona learns that their actions have no effect on their environment, leading to profound passivity.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: depression

## Initiated by

Repeated exposure to uncontrollable stressors, failures, or the sustained crushing weight of [despair](@chbrain/khai-engine-sadness/process_despair.md) that cannot be mitigated by action.

## Direction

Forward into a void. The persona anticipates that future outcomes are entirely independent of their voluntary responses. The [anticipatory anhedonia](@chbrain/khai-engine-anhedonia/position_anticipatory.md) drive is extinguished because the engine calculating effort-to-reward ratio concludes that effort always equals zero return.

## Lever

The cessation of effort. The persona stops trying to escape the trap or improve their situation, not out of laziness, but because the cognitive machinery has successfully "learned" that trying is statistically useless. The tragedy is that this passivity persists even when the environment changes and control actually becomes possible again.

## Echo

A generalized surrender. The persona becomes a passenger in their own life, watching the deterioration of their circumstances without raising a hand to stop it, which provides further evidence that they are powerless.
