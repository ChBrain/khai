# Depression: Reference

## Line of Work

Major Depressive Episode as **process**: the synergistic trap where affective, cognitive, and somatic failures lock each other into a self-sustaining cycle. It is not just sadness or fatigue, but the architecture of a state that actively defends itself against resolution.

## Origin

| Source             | Key Work                                                            | Scope                                                                                                                                            |
| :----------------- | :------------------------------------------------------------------ | :----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Beck, A. T.        | Depression: Clinical, experimental, and theoretical aspects. (1967) | The cognitive triad; the fusion of despair and self-judgment that produces worthlessness and hopelessness.                                       |
| Seligman, M. E. P. | Learned helplessness. Annual review of medicine. (1972)             | The behavioral lock-in; the learned passivity and cessation of effort when actions are perceived as futile.                                      |
| Nesse, R. M.       | Is depression an adaptation? Archives of general psychiatry. (2000) | The evolutionary perspective; melancholia and vegetative shutdown as a misfired energy-conservation mechanism in the face of unattainable goals. |

## Restrictions

Delegates the atomic mechanisms to its five dependencies: `sadness` handles the despair, `anhedonia` handles the blunted capacity for reward, `rumination` handles the cognitive looping, `self-esteem` handles the baseline valuation, and `body` handles the fatigue and vegetative state.

The composite models only their **fusion**: the way they lock together. Do not re-author the atoms here. Use hard links to point to the atoms when describing the components of the trap.

This is persona architecture for character authoring. It provides structural constraints for staging a state of lock-in, never a clinical assessment of a real person.
