# Process: Outdone, Sort

## Taxonomy

[Outdone](process_outdone.md), the bridge where one upward comparison is met three ways.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: outdone

## Initiated by

A single superiority that more than one response could meet.
The atoms each answer as if the response were given -- [admiration](@chbrain/khai-engine-admiration/process_admiration.md) warm, [benign envy](@chbrain/khai-engine-envy/process_benign.md) assimilative, [malicious envy](@chbrain/khai-engine-envy/process_malicious.md) hostile -- but which meets a given advantage is not given, it is sorted.
Two appraisals do the sorting (van de Ven, Zeelenberg & Pieters): whether the other's advantage is **deserved**, and whether the persona holds the **control** to attain the same -- deserved and attainable draws admiration or the benign pull to rise, undeserved or out of reach curdles toward the malicious wish to level down.

## Direction

From the comparison to a response, by the persona's reading of desert and reach.
The same colleague's promotion draws admiration from the one who thinks it earned and within their own future grasp, benign envy from the one who wants it and believes they can get it too, and malicious envy from the one who thinks it unfair or forever beyond them.
The direction is set at the fork, and the fork is in the appraisal.

## Lever

What the sort exposes -- that the response was never in the rank but in the reading brought to it.
The composite spends the illusion that another's superiority dictates one feeling: hold the advantage deserved and reachable, and admiration or benign envy fires; read it as unfair or hopeless, and the malicious form does.
Where the praise composite's reading turns on which good a deed reveals, the outdone sort turns on whether an advantage is deserved and within reach.

## Echo

The comparison is met in the key the appraisals set -- and a production that stages the sort, rather than fixing one response, shows why the same success lands differently on different rivals: admired by one, chased by another, resented by a third, each reading the desert and the reach their own way.
The reasoned judgment of whether the advantage truly is deserved is beyond this bridge; it owns only the fact that the response follows the appraisal.
When the reach is read as open, the sort tends toward the benign and the [turn](process_outdone_turn.md) can still run either way; when it closes, the darkening begins.
