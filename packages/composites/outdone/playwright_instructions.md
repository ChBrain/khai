# Instructions: Outdone

How a Playwright wires the outdone composite into a play. You wire by
linking the play's own content to the composite's files and, through them,
to the two atom engines it depends on; every file stays untouched, they are
canon. The Roadie plumbs the law into Instructions Knowledge on deploy.
Authoring guidance, not runtime content, and it does not go on tour.

## Human

- The human sets the advantage and the appraisals: who stands above the
  persona and in what, whether that advantage reads as deserved, and whether
  the persona could attain the same -- so a reckoning has a superior, a
  desert, and a reach.

## Agent

- Cast the corners from the atoms as usual: the warmth, inspiration, and
  affiliation from the admiration engine; the benign and malicious forms and
  the resentment that banks them from envy. Then cast the axis itself: how
  each significant persona meets another's advantage, and on what appraisal.
  Stage the bridges as motions, not states: a sort is the appraisals forking
  the response the audience can see, a turn is a warmth souring or a benign
  envy hardening as the path up closes across scenes, a blend is a persona
  admiring and envying the same rival at once. A plot that turns on a
  response darkening or an ambivalence breaking may link that bridge under its
  Tension; the audit surfaces the ones that stage it unnamed. You link to the
  composite and its atoms, you never edit them.

## Collaboration

- Every corner's science is its atom's: admiration and envy each carry their
  own warrant and their own instructions. The comparing itself -- upward,
  downward, level -- is the comparison engine's; the joy when the envied falls
  is schadenfreude's; the rank the advantage confers is status's and
  hierarchy's; the freely-given regard for another's good, admiration without
  the sting, is the praise composite's. Whether the personas genuinely move
  across the responses, rather than sitting in assigned corners like a
  diagram, is the team's review.

## Knowledge

- The upward-comparison family is one reckoning with another's superior
  standing, and one dial: how the persona meets it. The three corners --
  admiration (warm, rise toward), benign envy (painful, move up), malicious
  envy (hostile, pull down) (van de Ven) -- share one meeting axis, and the
  drama lives in motion across it: the appraisals of deservingness and reach
  sort one comparison into three; the responses tip and darken, admiration
  souring to envy and benign envy hardening to malicious as the path up
  closes, the way anger cools to contempt but here from warm to hostile; and
  a persona can admire and envy the same rival at once, the respected rival
  held in two minds. Read the production in which response is firing and which
  way it is turning, never in a fixed assignment of persona to corner.

## System

- Do cast every corner from its atom before staging a motion; do give each
  significant persona an appraisal of desert and reach that sorts their
  response; do let a warmth sour and a benign envy harden across scenes as the
  path up closes, so the darkening is earned, not announced.
- Do not pin a persona to one response for the whole run without making the
  fixity the point; do not resolve a turn by drift when the lightening back
  needs a scene; do not stage the joy at the rival's fall here -- that is
  schadenfreude's; do not edit the composite's or the atoms' files; wire only
  from the play's side.
