# Process: Outdone, Blend

## Taxonomy

[Outdone](process_outdone.md), the bridge where the responses fire at once and the rival is held in two minds.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: outdone

## Initiated by

A superiority met in more than one way at the same time.
Each atom answers as if its response ran alone -- [admiration](@chbrain/khai-engine-admiration/process_admiration.md) warm, [benign envy](@chbrain/khai-engine-envy/process_benign.md) yearning, [malicious envy](@chbrain/khai-engine-envy/process_malicious.md) hostile; none, alone, sees what their co-firing makes.
When a persona admires and envies the same other at once -- genuinely lifted by their excellence and genuinely stung by it -- the responses blend, and the compound is the ambivalent state the corners cannot hold apart: the respected rival, the friend one cannot quite be glad for, the frenemy.

## Direction

Toward the other held in two minds -- drawn and repelled together.
The blend does not resolve to a corner; it keeps the pull-toward and the pull-against alive at once, so the persona both roots for the rival and braces against them, praises and undercuts in the same breath.
Its direction is the sustained tension, not its release.

## Lever

What the blend spends -- the coherence of a single feeling toward the other.
Admiration alone lets the persona be simply glad; envy alone lets them be simply against; the blend denies both, holding the warmth and the sting together so that neither can be acted on cleanly, and every gesture toward the rival carries its opposite.
A production holds this lever by letting the two responses run together rather than choosing one, and reading the ambivalence, not any single feeling, as what governs the tie.

## Echo

The rival is admired and envied at once -- and the tie runs on a tension that can hold for years or break either way: toward the warmth, if the path up reopens and the sting eases, or toward the malice, if it closes and the admiration is the first thing sacrificed.
It is the positive mirror's shadow: where the praise composite's blend idealizes the good other past their parts, the outdone blend cannot even settle whether the superior other is loved or resented.
The settling, to admiration or to envy, if the production stages one, is a [turn](process_outdone_turn.md) given a direction -- a scene, not a drift.
