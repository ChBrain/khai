# Process: Outdone, Turn

## Taxonomy

[Outdone](process_outdone.md), the bridge where the responses tip into one another and darken.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: outdone

## Initiated by

A response to another's advantage that will not hold its place.
The atoms read their own states -- [admiration](@chbrain/khai-engine-admiration/process_admiration.md)'s warmth, [benign envy](@chbrain/khai-engine-envy/process_benign.md)'s pull to rise; what they cannot read is one turning into the next.
When the advantage begins to frustrate, or reads as less deserved than it seemed, admiration sours toward envy; and when the path up closes -- the persona tries to gain the same and cannot -- the benign envy hardens into [malicious envy](@chbrain/khai-engine-envy/process_malicious.md), the wish to rise curdling into the wish to level down.

## Direction

From warm to painful to hostile, one way by default.
The turn is the darkening slope: a warmth soured, a benign envy hardened, each drifting toward the malicious pole as desert and reach are read down -- while the reverse, malicious softening back to benign or admiring, almost never happens on its own.
It is the outdone family's counterpart to the condemnation composite's hardening, where anger cools to contempt; here admiration cools to envy, and benign envy to malicious.

## Lever

What the turn destroys -- the openness the benign responses need.
Admiration and benign envy both rest on a future in which the gap can be honored or closed; malicious envy is what remains when that future shuts -- when the advantage is fixed, unfair, and out of reach, the only motion left is against the other.
A production holds this lever by choosing when the closing happens -- across a run of blocked attempts that wear a benign envy into malice, or in a single scene where the advantage is suddenly revealed as unearned and the admiration curdles on the spot.

## Echo

The persona now runs on the darker engine -- the pull to rise replaced by the wish to pull down, and, when the envied at last stumbles, the malicious envy pays out as the schadenfreude engine's joy.
Whoever knew the persona in the admiring corner will misread them in the malicious one, because the warmth inverted while the rank that provoked it did not.
The lightening back, malice to benignity to warmth, if the production stages one, is a scene -- a reopening of the path up, a desert re-granted -- not a drift.
