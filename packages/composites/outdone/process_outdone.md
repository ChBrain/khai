# Process: Outdone

## Taxonomy

A composite reading over two atom engines (no canon file of its own): the admiration and envy engines this package declares as dependencies.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: outdone

## Initiated by

A persona reckons with another who stands above them -- better off, more able, further ahead -- and the comparison lands.
One reckoning, and one dial sets which face it takes: how the persona meets the other's advantage.
This is the upward-comparison family (van de Ven, Zeelenberg & Pieters) -- warmth and the wish to rise toward the other draws [admiration](@chbrain/khai-engine-admiration/process_admiration.md); the painful but assimilative pull to gain the same for oneself draws [benign envy](@chbrain/khai-engine-envy/process_benign.md); the painful, hostile wish to see the other lose it draws [malicious envy](@chbrain/khai-engine-envy/process_malicious.md).
No single engine sees the axis the three share; that is what this composite is for -- and it is the reckoning-with-a-superior opposite the praise composite's freely-given regard, the upward comparison met from below.

## Direction

Toward the other's advantage, and the self's standing beside it -- and the three corners point three ways.
Admiration points up and toward, closing the gap by lifting the self; benign envy points at the advantage itself, to gain the same; malicious envy points at the other, to bring them down.
The drama is that the same superiority can draw any of the three, and that a persona can travel among them as the reckoning wears on.

## Lever

The appraisals that sort the meeting -- whether the other's advantage is deserved, and whether the persona could attain the same.
What the composite spends is the assumption that another's superiority draws one fixed feeling: deserved and attainable, it draws admiration or the benign pull to rise; undeserved or out of reach, it curdles toward the malicious wish to level down (van de Ven).
The fork is in the reading, not the rank.

## Echo

The other's advantage is embraced, chased, or resented -- and, unmet, the reckoning darkens.
The atoms each run their own response and cannot see the family: the sort where the appraisals fork one comparison into three, the turn where a warm admiration sours and a benign envy hardens as the path up closes, and the blend where a persona admires and envies the same rival at once.
It runs as [sort](process_outdone_sort.md), [turn](process_outdone_turn.md), and [blend](process_outdone_blend.md). The comparing itself -- upward, downward, or level -- is the comparison engine's; the joy when the envied finally falls is the schadenfreude engine's; the rank the advantage confers is the status engine's; the resentment that banks the malicious form is the envy engine's own. This composite owns the response to being outdone and the meeting axis its three emotions share.
