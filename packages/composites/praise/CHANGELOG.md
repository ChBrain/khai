## 0.1.1

### Patch Changes

- 5a61a66: Add the praise composite -- the other-praising emotions read as one family, the positive mirror of the `condemnation` composite. One warm regard for another's witnessed good, split by which kind of good it is -- a benefit received, a virtue, or a skill -- into gratitude, elevation, and admiration (Algoe & Haidt). The composite authors no emotion of its own: it declares the `gratitude`, `elevation`, and `admiration` engines as dependencies, wires their members by hard package links, and adds only the good-kind axis the three share and the three motions across it.

  - **Root:** `praise` -- the family as one warm regard with one dial (the kind of good witnessed), landing in gratitude (benefit), elevation (virtue), or admiration (skill).
  - **Bridges (the drama the atoms cannot stage alone):** `praise, blend` (the goods firing together and the other idealized past their parts -- the halo, the hero, the idol, fragile when it falls; the positive mirror of condemnation's dehumanizing fusion), `praise, lift` (deed-praise widening into person-praise -- a regard for what was done rising into a regard for who did it, the ascending mirror of anger cooling into contempt), `praise, reading` (one good act read now as a benefit, now a virtue, now a skill, by the good the witness is disposed to see).

  Completes the moral-emotion quadrant across the composites: `self-conscious` (self-directed), `condemnation` (other-directed, to a wrong), and `praise` (other-directed, to a good). Newly wireable now that `admiration` is a standalone engine (gratitude and elevation already were). Warranted (LORE, integrative) on Algoe & Haidt (the other-praising emotions, 2009), Haidt (the shared self-transcendent payoff), Thorndike (the halo effect), Fredrickson (broaden-and-build), and Algoe, Haidt & Gable (gratitude's find-remind-and-bind). Bounded against `joy` (the core felt uplift vs the other-directed regard), `self-conscious` (self- vs other-directed), `condemnation` (the praising vs the condemning response), and `awe`/`attachment` (the vastness and the bond vs the emotion). Set at patch as the free level. No whitelist required -- the `praise` root and `praise_*` bridge stems are unique.
