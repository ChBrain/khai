# Praise: Reference

## Line of Work

The other-praising emotions as **composite**: one warm regard for another's witnessed good, and one dial -- which kind of good it is -- that sets which of three corners the regard lands in. No single atom engine sees the good-kind axis the corners share. A benefit received is the gratitude engine's, whole; a witnessed virtue is the elevation engine's; a witnessed skill is the admiration engine's. This package authors none of those readings again. It declares the three engines as dependencies, wires their members by hard package links, and adds only what the atoms cannot hold: the good-kind axis itself, and the three motions along and across it -- the blend where the goods combine into idealization, the lift where deed-praise widens into person-praise, and the reading where one act is witnessed now as benefit, now virtue, now skill.

The warrant is accordingly integrative, not primary: the sources below establish the other-praising family the composite reads over, not the individual emotions, which their own engines warrant. The spine is Algoe and Haidt's account of the other-praising emotions -- gratitude, elevation, and admiration -- distinguished by their elicitor: a benefit to the self, a witnessed moral excellence, and a witnessed non-moral excellence. It is the positive mirror of the condemnation composite: there another's violation of a moral code draws the condemning emotions contempt, anger, and disgust; here another's good draws the praising ones. The shared payoff the family adds, and the composite holds, is Fredrickson's broaden-and-build turned prosocial: witnessing another's good, of any kind, lifts the witness toward the good themselves -- to thank, to emulate, to become better.

## Restrictions

What the composite refuses to model, and to whom it delegates.

- **The three atoms**: The composite authors no emotion of its own. Gratitude -- the warm turn toward a benefactor for a benefit received -- is the gratitude engine's, whole; elevation -- the uplift at a witnessed virtue -- is the elevation engine's; admiration -- the warm regard for a witnessed skill -- is the admiration engine's. This package reads over them and adds only the good-kind axis and its motions; every corner is an atom's ground, cited by hard link, never restated.
- **Joy**: The composite models the warm regard for another's good, not the core felt uplift beneath it. The hedonic yes that any good occasions -- pleasure, elation, contentment, relief -- is owned by the joy engine; the other-praising emotions each carry that gladness, but this composite owns their other-directed regard and its good-kind axis, not the felt uplift itself.
- **Self-conscious**: The composite models the other-directed praise, not the self-directed appraisal. When the good appraised is one's own against a standard -- authentic pride, hubris -- it is the self-conscious composite's; this one is its positive, other-directed sibling, the warm regard turned outward on another's good rather than inward on one's own.
- **Condemnation**: The composite models the praising response to another's good, not the condemning response to another's wrong. The contempt, anger, and disgust drawn by a violation of community, autonomy, or divinity are the condemnation composite's; praise is its mirror -- the same other-directed moral machinery turned on a good rather than a wrong.
- **Awe and Attachment**: The composite models the emotion, not the vastness that dwarfs the frame or the bond it may grow into. The response to a stimulus so vast it forces the frame to revise is the awe engine's, and the durable tie that praise and affiliation may deepen into is the attachment engine's; praise draws warmly toward the good other, but the accommodation and the bond themselves are theirs.

## Encoding

Source to constraint, per file.

- **[praise](process_praise.md)** (the root): The other-praising family as one warm regard with one dial -- which kind of good is witnessed -- landing in gratitude (benefit), elevation (virtue), or admiration (skill). Anchored by Algoe & Haidt (the other-praising emotions) and Haidt (the shared prosocial payoff).
- **[praise, blend](process_praise_blend.md)** (bridge): The goods firing together and the other idealized past their parts -- the halo, the hero, the idol, fragile when it falls. Anchored by Thorndike (the halo effect) and the idealization literature.
- **[praise, lift](process_praise_lift.md)** (bridge): Deed-praise widening into person-praise -- a regard for what was done becoming a regard for who did it, the benefactor and expert deepening into a revered figure. Anchored by Algoe, Haidt & Gable (gratitude widening to the person and the bond).
- **[praise, reading](process_praise_reading.md)** (bridge): One good act read now as a benefit, now a virtue, now a skill, by the good the witness is positioned and disposed to see. Anchored by Algoe & Haidt (the elicitor-based distinction among the three).
