# Instructions: Praise

How a Playwright wires the praise composite into a play. You wire by
linking the play's own content to the composite's files and, through them,
to the three atom engines it depends on; every file stays untouched, they
are canon. The Roadie plumbs the law into Instructions Knowledge on deploy.
Authoring guidance, not runtime content, and it does not go on tour.

## Human

- The human sets the good and the witness: what another has done or is,
  which kind of good it carries -- a benefit to someone, a virtue, a skill --
  and who is watching, so a praise has a good, a kind, and a witness
  positioned to see it.

## Agent

- Cast the corners from the atoms as usual: the benefaction, indebtedness,
  and appreciation from the gratitude engine; the witness, warmth, and
  emulation from elevation; the inspiration, affiliation, and adoration from
  admiration. Then cast the axis itself: which good each significant persona
  is tuned to see, and how readily. Stage the bridges as motions, not states:
  a blend is goods stacking into idealization the audience watches build, a
  lift is a deed-praise widening into regard for the person across scenes, a
  reading is two witnesses praising the same act in different keys in the same
  room. A plot that turns on a praise idealizing or widening may link that
  bridge under its Tension; the audit surfaces the ones that stage it unnamed.
  You link to the composite and its atoms, you never edit them.

## Collaboration

- Every corner's science is its atom's: gratitude, elevation, and admiration
  each carry their own warrant and their own instructions. The core felt
  gladness under any praise is the joy engine's; the vastness that dwarfs the
  frame is awe's; the bond praise may grow into is attachment's; the
  self-directed mirror of this family -- authentic pride, hubris -- is the
  self-conscious composite's, and its condemning mirror is the condemnation
  composite's. Whether the personas genuinely move across the goods, rather
  than sitting in assigned corners like a diagram, is the team's review.

## Knowledge

- The other-praising family is one warm regard with one dial: which kind of
  good a witness sees in another. The three corners -- gratitude for a
  benefit, elevation for a virtue, admiration for a skill (Algoe & Haidt) --
  share one good-kind axis, and the drama lives in motion across it: goods
  blend, and where two or three fire at once the other is idealized past
  their parts, haloed into a hero or idol, fragile when a fault shows; a
  deed-praise widens, a regard for what was done rising into a regard for who
  did it, the way anger's act-verdict cools into contempt's -- but ascending;
  and one act is read through different goods by different witnesses, so the
  same deed draws gratitude here, admiration there, elevation from the one
  attuned to character. Read the production in which goods are firing and
  which way the regard is moving, never in a fixed assignment of persona to
  corner.

## System

- Do cast every corner from its atom before staging a motion; do give each
  significant persona a good-kind tendency; do let a blend build from stacked
  goods and a lift widen across scenes, so the idealization and the reverence
  are earned, not announced.
- Do not pin a persona to one good for the whole run without making the
  fixity the point; do not resolve a lift by drift when the narrowing back
  needs a scene; do not let the composite pass judgment on whether the good is
  real -- it owns the regard, not the verdict; do not edit the composite's or
  the atoms' files; wire only from the play's side.
