# Process: Praise, Blend

## Taxonomy

[Praise](process_praise.md), the bridge where the goods fire together and the other is idealized.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: praise

## Initiated by

A good witnessed in more than one kind at once.
Each atom answers a single kind of good -- [gratitude](@chbrain/khai-engine-gratitude/process_gratitude.md) a benefit, [elevation](@chbrain/khai-engine-elevation/process_elevation.md) a virtue, [admiration](@chbrain/khai-engine-admiration/process_admiration.md) a skill -- and none, alone, sees what their combination does.
When one figure is benefactor and exemplar and expert together -- helping the persona, and good, and gifted -- the three fire as one, and the compound is more than its parts: the halo (Thorndike), by which one admired quality spreads to a global glow, and the idealization that lifts the other past who they are.

## Direction

Toward the other elevated past their parts.
Where any single praise keeps the other a person with a good deed, a good heart, or a good hand, the blended regard fuses these into a figure -- the hero, the idol, the beloved -- lifted above the ordinary and granted standing no single good would earn.
It is the positive mirror of the condemnation composite's fusion: there the codes combine to strip the other of standing, here the goods combine to grant them excess of it.

## Lever

What the blend spends -- the other's ordinary humanity, gilded over.
Just as fused condemnation removes the other from the circle of amends, fused praise removes them from the circle of the ordinary: the idealized figure is held above, their faults unseen, their goods magnified, and the persona's own judgment surrendered to the glow.
A production holds this lever by letting the goods stack rather than choosing one, and reading the idealization, not any single warmth, as what sets the figure on the pedestal.

## Echo

The other is idealized, and the regard becomes devotion -- the fan, the disciple, the lover who sees only the good.
It is the fragile state: the idol built of blended praise has no ordinary footing to descend to, so when a fault shows, the fall is a shattering, the idealization curdling to disillusion or betrayal -- the positive mirror of dehumanization is idolization, and both undo the other's real proportion.
The un-blending back, if the production stages one, is a scene -- a restoring of the other to human size -- not a drift.
