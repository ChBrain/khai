# Process: Praise, Lift

## Taxonomy

[Praise](process_praise.md), the bridge where deed-praise widens into person-praise.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: praise

## Initiated by

A praise for a deed that will not stay on the deed.
The atoms read their own occasions -- [gratitude](@chbrain/khai-engine-gratitude/process_gratitude.md) a benefit given, [admiration](@chbrain/khai-engine-admiration/process_admiration.md) a skill shown; what they cannot read is the regard changing size.
When the deeds repeat, or the persona's warmth leans that way, the praise widens: _they did a good thing for me_ and _they did a skilled thing_ become _they are a good person_, the benefactor and the expert deepening into a figure the persona reveres -- and the regard crosses toward the person-praise that [elevation](@chbrain/khai-engine-elevation/process_elevation.md) describes, the good witnessed now in the person entire.

## Direction

From deed to person, and upward.
Lift is the family's ascending slope: a deed-praise left to accrue drifts toward the person, a regard for what was done becoming a regard for who did it -- and, unlike its dark mirror, the drift is a rising, not a corrosion.
It is the positive counterpart of the condemnation composite's hardening, where anger's act-verdict cools into contempt's person-verdict; here gratitude's and admiration's act-praise warms into elevation's person-praise.

## Lever

What the lift changes -- the unit of the praise, from the act to the actor.
Gratitude and admiration are, at root, praise of a thing done: a gift, a feat, a skill, each of a size the deed sets. When the praise widens to the person, it leaves that size behind -- the regard is now for a character, a self, which no single deed bounds, and which the persona holds as good or admirable entire.
A production holds this lever by choosing when the widening happens -- across a run of deeds that accrue into a reputation, or in a single scene where one act is suddenly taken as the whole measure of the one who did it.

## Echo

The persona now regards the person, not the deed -- and the warmth deepens toward reverence, loyalty, or love, the elevation and adoration the atoms reach at their far ends.
It is the widening that makes benefactors into heroes and the skilled into idols, and its risk is the [blend](process_praise_blend.md)'s: a person-praise unchecked by the ordinary deed tips into idealization.
The narrowing back, person to deed, if the production stages one, is a scene -- a return of the regard to the human-sized thing that earned it -- not a drift.
