# Process: Praise, Reading

## Taxonomy

[Praise](process_praise.md), the bridge where one good act is read now one way, now another.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: praise

## Initiated by

A single good act that more than one kind of praise could claim.
The atoms each answer a kind of good as given -- but which good a witness sees in an act is not given, it is read.
The same act -- a surgeon saving a life, a stranger returning a wallet, a musician playing -- can be read as a benefit to oneself ([gratitude](@chbrain/khai-engine-gratitude/process_gratitude.md)), as a mark of the doer's virtue ([elevation](@chbrain/khai-engine-elevation/process_elevation.md)), or as a display of their skill ([admiration](@chbrain/khai-engine-admiration/process_admiration.md)), and different witnesses, and the same witness at different moments, read it differently.

## Direction

From the act to a kind of good, by the witness's lights -- and between kinds as it is re-read.
The patient reads the surgery as a benefit and is grateful; the bystander reads it as skill and admires; the one who sees the surgeon choose the poor patient over the rich reads it as virtue and is elevated -- one act, three praises, sorted by what each witness is positioned and disposed to see.
The same witness can move between them as the act is re-read: the gift first felt as a benefit, then seen as a kindness, then as a mastery of giving.

## Lever

What the reading exposes -- that the kind of praise was never only in the act but in the good the witness brought to it.
The composite spends the illusion that a good act has one meaning: change what good is seen, and gratitude, elevation, or admiration fires instead.
Where condemnation's reading turns on which code a wrong offends, praise's reading turns on which good a deed reveals -- the same act a benefit, a virtue, or a feat, by the witness's angle and appetite for each.

## Echo

The act is praised in the key the witness hears it -- and a production that stages the reading, rather than fixing one good, shows why the same deed lands differently on different watchers: the helped feel gratitude where the onlookers feel admiration, and the one attuned to character feels elevation where the others feel neither.
The reasoned judgment of whether the good is real is beyond this bridge; it owns only the fact that the good is a reading, and that the praise follows it.
The core felt gladness under any of the readings is the joy engine's; this bridge owns the sorting of a good act into the kind of praise it draws.
