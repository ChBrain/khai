# Process: Praise

## Taxonomy

A composite reading over three atom engines (no canon file of its own): the gratitude, elevation, and admiration engines this package declares as dependencies.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: praise

## Initiated by

A persona witnesses a good in another and warms toward them.
One warm regard, and one dial sets which face it takes: which kind of good the witness sees.
This is the other-praising family (Algoe & Haidt) -- a **benefit** given to the self draws [gratitude](@chbrain/khai-engine-gratitude/process_gratitude.md), the warm turn toward a benefactor; a witnessed **virtue** draws [elevation](@chbrain/khai-engine-elevation/process_elevation.md), the uplift that moves the witness toward the good; a witnessed **skill** draws [admiration](@chbrain/khai-engine-admiration/process_admiration.md), the warm regard for excellence that moves the witness to rise toward it.
No single engine sees the axis the three share; that is what this composite is for -- and it is the positive mirror of the condemnation composite: there another's violation draws the condemning emotions, here another's good draws the praising ones.

## Direction

Outward, toward the other and the good in them -- and in more than one kind of good at once.
The atoms each aim their own warmth at their own kind of good; what the family adds is that a single act can be witnessed as two or three goods together, and that praise can travel from a deed to the person who did it as the regard deepens.
The direction of the drama is across the good-kinds, and upward from the act to the actor.

## Lever

The kind of good the persona witnesses -- for the same act is not one praise but three possible ones, and which fires depends on what good the witness is tuned to see: a helping hand as a benefit to oneself, as a mark of the helper's goodness, or as a display of their skill.
What the composite spends is the assumption that a good act carries one kind of praise; and what it returns is the family's shared payoff -- that witnessing another's good, of any kind, lifts the witness toward the good themselves.

## Echo

The other is praised -- and, where more than one good fires, idealized past their parts.
The atoms each run their own good-kind and cannot see the family: the blend where the goods combine and halo the other into a hero, the lift where a deed-praise widens into a regard for the person entire, and the reading where one act is witnessed now as benefit, now virtue, now skill.
It runs as [blend](process_praise_blend.md), [lift](process_praise_lift.md), and [reading](process_praise_reading.md). The core felt uplift of any good is the joy engine's; the accommodation of a vastness beyond the frame is the awe engine's; the durable bond that praise may grow into is the attachment engine's; the self-directed appraisal of one's own good against a standard, pride, is the self-conscious composite's. This composite owns the warm regard for another's good and the good-kind axis its three emotions share.
