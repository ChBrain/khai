# Instructions: Creativity

How a Playwright wires the creativity composite into a play. You wire by linking the play's own content to the composite's files; the composite and its atoms stay untouched, they are canon. Installing the composite brings its atoms as dependencies and their laws with them. The Roadie plumbs the composite's law into Instructions Knowledge on deploy. Authoring guidance, not runtime content, and it does not go on tour.

## Human

- The human sets the creative work and its arc: what the persona is trying to make new-and-good, where it stalls, and whether the play stages the whole cycle -- generation, incubation, illumination, verification -- or one bridge of it.

## Agent

- From the play's own content, a plot that stages the creative process may link under Tension the bridge it turns on: incubation, the set-down impasse readied by the drift; illumination, the aha surfacing with a remote map for its vehicle; or verification, the novel pool met by the gate that keeps only the useful. Cast the process from the atoms -- the generation from divergent-thinking, the drift from mind-wandering, the aha from insight, the remote map from analogy -- and stage the seams, not a fifth engine. Read the creative work as the whole cycle turning, never as novelty alone; the striking-but-useless is the counterfeit, not the creative. You link to the composite, you never edit it.

## Collaboration

- The convergent choosing that keeps the useful idea is the decision engine's; the intrinsic drive that sustains the work is motivation's; the representing it draws on is imagination's; the absorbed state it often runs inside is flow's. Creativity is the composite that wires divergent-thinking, mind-wandering, insight, and analogy into one process -- the making of the novel-and-useful. Whether a production's creativity is the whole cycle genuinely turning, rather than novelty asserted, is the team's review.

## Knowledge

- Creativity is the production of the novel-and-useful read as one process, not a single act (Wallas's preparation, incubation, illumination, verification; Amabile's novel-and-appropriate, not novel alone). It wires over four atoms and adds no fifth: divergent-thinking generates the many candidate ideas, mind-wandering supplies the offline drift that incubates them, insight supplies the sudden aha, and analogy supplies the remote structure-map the aha often rides. Three bridges are its own content -- incubation is the seam where effortful generation stalls, is set down, and the drift readies a solution offline (the shower, the walk, the sleep); illumination is the seam where the incubated material surfaces as the aha, a remote source retrieved and mapped to the stuck target its vehicle; verification is the seam where the pool of novel ideas meets the convergent gate that keeps only what is also useful, and creativity is realized -- or the merely-novel, ungated, is mistaken for the creative. Read the creative work by which bridge it stages, and read verification two ways: novelty is necessary and not sufficient, and the original-but-worthless is creativity's counterfeit, not its achievement. The convergent gate is decision's, the drive motivation's, the representing imagination's.

## System

- Do stage the impasse before the aha: generation that stalls and is set down, so incubation reads as offline work and illumination lands as a sudden restructuring, not a gradual approach.
- Do route through the bridge that fits: incubation for the set-down drift, illumination for the aha and its remote map, verification for the gate that keeps the useful; a creative arc may run all three in sequence, and the cycle may turn again.
- Do read verification as the half that completes creativity: novelty is necessary and not sufficient, so the merely-original that skips the gate is the counterfeit -- striking and useless, new and no good -- and genuine creativity is the whole cycle closed.
- Do not add a fifth engine's content: the generation is divergent-thinking's, the drift mind-wandering's, the aha insight's, the map analogy's; the convergent choosing is decision's, the drive motivation's, the representing imagination's. Do not edit the composite's or the atoms' files; wire only from the play's side.
