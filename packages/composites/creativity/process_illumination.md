# Process: Illumination

## Taxonomy

[Creativity](process_creativity.md), the bridge where the incubated material surfaces as the aha, a remote map its vehicle.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: creativity

## Initiated by

The readied problem gives, and the answer arrives whole.
Out of the incubated ground the [aha](@chbrain/khai-engine-insight/process_insight.md) breaks -- the impasse restructuring all at once, the solution appearing complete and obvious in hindsight -- and its vehicle is often a remote [mapping](@chbrain/khai-engine-analogy/process_mapping.md), a far source suddenly [retrieved](@chbrain/khai-engine-analogy/process_retrieval.md) and aligned to the stuck target, the distant domain that turns out to fit.
This is illumination: the seam where the drift's offline work becomes a sudden restructuring, and a remote analogy is the bridge the aha crosses.

## Direction

Up from the incubated ground into the finished insight.
The relaxed frame gives way where effort could not move it: the [constraint relaxation](@chbrain/khai-engine-insight/process_constraint_relaxation.md) the incubation loosened completes in the aha, or a [re-encoding](@chbrain/khai-engine-insight/process_re_encoding.md) reads a part anew, and the remote source the drift surfaced maps onto the problem and carries its inferences across.
The direction is the discontinuous jump -- from a problem readied but unsolved to the solution arriving whole, the analogy's alignment the moment the far thing and the stuck thing become one.

## Lever

The coupling of the aha and the map that neither stages alone.
[Insight](@chbrain/khai-engine-insight/process_insight.md) is the restructuring but not its content; [analogy](@chbrain/khai-engine-analogy/process_analogy.md) is the structure-mapping but not the impasse it breaks. The bridge is their coupling: the incubated impasse gives way when a remote source, retrieved by the drift and mapped to the target, supplies the new frame -- the aha is the map landing, the map is what the aha is made of. Kekule's snake-ring, the many discoveries told as "it came to me like --": the illumination is the analogy arriving as insight.
Ready the impasse and let a remote source map onto it, and illumination fires; without the map there is no new frame to jump to, without the impasse no jump to make.

## Echo

The solution arrives, whole and sudden, out of the incubated ground.
Illumination leaves the persona holding the new idea -- the restructured problem, the remote source mapped home -- and hands it to the gate that will keep it or send it back.
It is the seam Wallas set between incubation and verification: not the ripening and not the testing, but the flash between, the aha that is an [analogy](@chbrain/khai-engine-analogy/process_analogy.md) landing on a problem the [drift](@chbrain/khai-engine-mind-wandering/process_mind_wandering.md) had readied.
Illumination is dated from the break, the moment the readied frame gave and the answer came whole.
