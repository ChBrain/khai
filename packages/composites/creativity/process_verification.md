# Process: Verification

## Taxonomy

[Creativity](process_creativity.md), the bridge where the novel meets the gate that keeps only what is also useful.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: creativity

## Initiated by

The idea is had, and now it must be judged good.
The pool of novel ideas -- the [originality](@chbrain/khai-engine-divergent-thinking/process_originality.md) the [generation](@chbrain/khai-engine-divergent-thinking/process_divergent-thinking.md) reached into, the aha's product -- meets the convergent valuation that asks not "is it new?" but "is it also useful, workable, right?", and keeps only what passes.
This is verification: the seam where creativity's novel half meets the gate that supplies its useful half, and the merely-original is either completed into the creative or exposed as strangeness.

## Direction

Toward the novel-and-useful kept, and the merely-novel discarded.
The gate narrows what the generation and illumination widened: the many ideas are tested against the standard the task sets, the striking-but-wrong set aside, the new-and-good retained -- and the cycle can turn again, a failed idea sent back to generation for another pass.
The direction is the convergent close on the divergent open: novelty is necessary and not sufficient, and verification is where the second, harder half of "novel and useful" is won or the idea falls.

## Lever

The value of the gate against the temptation to skip it.
Divergent generation supplies the novel and the many; the convergent valuation that keeps the useful is the decision engine's -- creativity requires both, and verification is the bridge where the generated pool meets that gate. The resistance is the skip: novelty is vivid and immediate where usefulness is dull and effortful to test, so the ungated pool is easily mistaken for creativity itself, the original-but-worthless passed off as the new-and-good.
Close the gate and the process completes into the creative; leave it open and the merely-strange wears creativity's name, striking and useless, new and no good.

## Echo

The idea is kept as novel-and-useful -- or exposed as novelty alone, and creativity's counterfeit stands revealed.
Verification leaves the process complete when the gate is genuinely closed, the retained idea both new and good; and it is what tells real creativity from its imitation, for the imitation is exactly this bridge skipped -- generation and illumination worn without the gate, the pool's strangeness mistaken for its value.
The convergent choosing itself is the decision engine's, and the intrinsic drive that carried the work this far is motivation's; this bridge is only their seam with the pool -- the moment the novel is made to answer for the useful.
Verification is dated from the judging, the turn from making the new to keeping the good.
