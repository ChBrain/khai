# Process: Incubation

## Taxonomy

[Creativity](process_creativity.md), the bridge where set-down generation drifts offline and readies the aha.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: creativity

## Initiated by

The generating stalls, and the persona sets the problem down -- and the work goes on without it.
Effortful [generation](@chbrain/khai-engine-divergent-thinking/process_divergent-thinking.md) reaches an impasse, the obvious ideas spent and the pool going dry; the persona turns away, and the mind's [drift](@chbrain/khai-engine-mind-wandering/process_mind_wandering.md) takes the problem up offline, the [zoning-out](@chbrain/khai-engine-mind-wandering/process_zoning_out.md) that looks like doing nothing doing the work the effort could not.
This is incubation: the seam where deliberate generation hands off to spontaneous drift, and setting the problem down is not quitting it but continuing it by other means.

## Direction

Toward a solution readied while the persona is not looking.
The set-down interval lets the fixated frame relax off the conscious effort that was holding it -- the drift recombining the pool's elements, surfacing remote associations, loosening the constraint the effort had clamped -- so the problem the persona returns to is not the one they left, but one whose ground has quietly shifted toward giving.
The direction is offline ripening: the pause that is the opposite of idleness, the work done precisely because the effort stopped.

## Lever

The coupling of the generation and the drift that neither stages alone.
[Divergent-thinking](@chbrain/khai-engine-divergent-thinking/process_divergent-thinking.md) fills the pool but, pressed, fixates and dries; [mind-wandering](@chbrain/khai-engine-mind-wandering/process_mind_wandering.md) drifts but, without a prepared problem, drifts to nothing. The bridge is their coupling: a mind stocked by hard generation and then released to drift incubates, where either alone does not -- the pool gives the drift something to work, and the drift gives the pool the offline relaxation effort cannot.
Prepare the pool and then let it go, and incubation runs; skip the preparation, or never let go, and there is nothing to ripen or no room to ripen it.

## Echo

The problem is set down and quietly readied -- the ground laid for the aha to come.
Incubation leaves the impasse loosened, its frame relaxed off the effort, its elements recombined by the drift into a state from which [illumination](process_illumination.md) can break.
It is the seam Wallas set between preparation and illumination: not the generating and not the aha, but the offline interval that carries the one toward the other -- the reason the solution so often comes in the shower, the walk, the sleep, when the persona had stopped trying.
Incubation is dated from the setting-down, the turn away that lets the work go on unwatched.
