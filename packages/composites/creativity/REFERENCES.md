# Creativity: Reference

## Line of Work

Creativity as a **composite**: the production of the novel-and-useful read as one process
rather than a single act -- effortful generation set down to incubate, the aha surfacing
from the drift with a remote map for its vehicle, and the convergent gate that keeps only
what is both new and good. It wires over four atoms -- divergent-thinking (the generation),
mind-wandering (the incubating drift), insight (the aha), and analogy (the remote map) --
and adds no fifth engine; its own content is the three bridges the atoms cannot stage
apart. It reads three neighbours it names but does not own: the convergent valuation that
gates the pool (decision), the intrinsic drive that sustains the work (motivation), and the
representing it draws on (imagination).

The spine is the classic staged account of the creative process and the componential and
systems theories that refined it. Wallas set the four stages the bridges are read between --
preparation, incubation, illumination, and verification -- the effortful work, the offline
interval, the sudden aha, and the testing of the result. Amabile's componential theory made
the point the verification bridge turns on: creativity is not novelty alone but novelty that
is also appropriate, useful, or valuable, so the convergent valuation is constitutive and
not optional. Guilford's divergent/convergent distinction locates the two halves in
different engines -- generation divergent, selection convergent -- and Mednick's associative
account and the incubation literature (Wallas; later empirical work on set-down intervals)
ground the incubation bridge, where a prepared but stalled problem is readied offline. So
the composite gives creativity three bridges -- incubation, illumination, verification --
the seams that turn four separate engines into a process that produces the new-and-good, or
fails and lets the merely-novel pass for it.

## Restrictions

What the composite refuses to model, and to whom it delegates.

- **Decision**: The composite models the seam where the novel pool meets the gate, not the
  convergent choosing itself. The valuation that selects the useful, workable, or best idea
  from the pool is owned by the decision engine; verification is the bridge where the
  generated novelty is made to answer to that gate, but the choosing itself is decision's.
- **Motivation**: The composite models the process, not the drive that sustains it. The
  intrinsic task motivation Amabile made a component -- the appetite that carries a persona
  through preparation, incubation, and verification -- is owned by the motivation engine;
  creativity reads it as its fuel but does not own it.
- **Imagination and flow**: The composite models the creative process, not the faculties and
  states it runs on. The deliberate forming of non-present representations is the imagination
  engine's; the absorbed state generation often runs inside is flow's; creativity draws on
  both and owns neither.
- **The atoms**: The composite adds no content its atoms already own. The generation of the
  many is the divergent-thinking engine's; the offline drift is the mind-wandering engine's;
  the sudden restructuring is the insight engine's; the remote structure-map is the analogy
  engine's. Creativity owns only the seams -- the process the four become that none is alone.

## Encoding

Source to constraint, per file.

- **[creativity](process_creativity.md)** (the root): the making of the novel-and-useful as
  one process -- generation incubated, illuminated, and verified -- or the merely-novel
  mistaken for it. Anchored by Wallas (the four stages) and Amabile (novel-and-useful, not
  novel alone).
- **[incubation](process_incubation.md)** (bridge): effortful generation stalls and is set
  down, and mind-wandering's drift readies the aha offline. Wires divergent-thinking to
  mind-wandering. Anchored by Wallas (incubation) and Mednick (remote association).
- **[illumination](process_illumination.md)** (bridge): the incubated material surfaces as
  insight's aha, analogy's remote map its vehicle. Wires insight to analogy. Anchored by
  Wallas (illumination) and Mednick (the associative leap).
- **[verification](process_verification.md)** (bridge): the novel pool meets the convergent
  gate that keeps only what is also useful, or the merely-novel is exposed. Wires
  divergent-thinking's pool to the decision gate. Anchored by Amabile (appropriateness) and
  Guilford (convergent selection).
