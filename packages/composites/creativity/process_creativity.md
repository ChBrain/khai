# Process: Creativity

## Taxonomy

The making of the novel-and-useful -- a composite over divergent-thinking, mind-wandering, insight, and analogy.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: creativity

## Initiated by

A persona sets out to make something both new and good, and the making runs as a process, not a flash.
This is creativity: the production of the novel-and-useful, and it is never one act but four running in sequence and reinforcing -- the [generation](@chbrain/khai-engine-divergent-thinking/process_divergent-thinking.md) of many candidate ideas, the offline [drift](@chbrain/khai-engine-mind-wandering/process_mind_wandering.md) that incubates them, the sudden [aha](@chbrain/khai-engine-insight/process_insight.md) that resolves the impasse, and the remote [mapping](@chbrain/khai-engine-analogy/process_mapping.md) the aha often rides.
The composite adds no fifth engine. It wires the seams the four make into a process: generation feeds the drift, the drift readies the aha, the aha yields the idea the gate then keeps or discards.

## Direction

Toward the novel-and-useful, built by a cycle that turns -- or toward the counterfeit, where novelty is mistaken for creativity because the cycle never closed.
Where the four genuinely reinforce, creativity completes: a pool is generated, set down to incubate, illuminated by an aha, and verified into the idea that is both new and good -- Wallas's preparation, incubation, illumination, and verification read as the seams between the engines.
It is a cycle, not a line: the gate can send a failed idea back to generation, and the process turns again until the novel-and-useful is reached or abandoned.

## Lever

The four reinforcing, each making the next possible.
Generation fills the pool the drift works on; the drift, off the effortful frame, lets the impasse relax; the relaxed frame gives way in the aha, a remote source mapped to the stuck target; the aha's product meets the gate that keeps only what is also useful. Novelty without the gate is mere strangeness; the gate without the pool has nothing to keep -- the value lives in the whole turning.
No single atom stages this: creativity lives in the seams, the process the four become that none is alone.

## Echo

The persona makes the new-and-good -- or produces the merely-novel and mistakes it for the creative.
The process runs as [incubation](process_incubation.md), [illumination](process_illumination.md), or [verification](process_verification.md) -- the set-down drift that readies the aha, the aha that surfaces from it, and the gate that keeps the novel-and-useful.
It reads its neighbours but does not own them: the convergent valuation that gates the pool is the decision engine's, the intrinsic drive that sustains the work is the motivation engine's, the representing it draws on is the imagination engine's, and the absorbed state it often runs inside is flow's. This composite owns the creative process itself: the cycle from the many to the new-and-good, and whether it turned or only looked new.
