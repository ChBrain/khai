# Process: Dealing

## Taxonomy

Two parties who cannot get what they want without each other and can each impose costs.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: dealing

## Initiated by

Two parties want different things, need the same agreement, and can each make the other worse off.
Dealing starts wherever that condition is live: the pursuit of terms, through [negotiation](@chbrain/khai-engine-negotiation/process_negotiation.md); the exposure accepted on conduct that cannot be controlled, through [trust](@chbrain/khai-engine-trust/process_trust.md); and the loop neither party is steering, through [escalation](@chbrain/khai-engine-escalation/process_escalation.md).
This composite adds no fourth engine. It reads that these are not three relationships but three states of one, and that a pair in this condition is always in exactly one of them.

## Direction

Toward an outcome set more by the state the pair fell into than by what they were dividing.
The composite wires the three engines and reads their working in three shapes: [the Table](process_dealing_table.md), the bargaining itself; [the Bet](process_dealing_bet.md), the exposure accepted or refused; and [the Spiral](process_dealing_spiral.md), the twist -- the loop where every move answers the last.
The three are not three stages in a sequence, though they often run in that order. They are three states one relation can be in, each of which makes itself more likely next time.

## Lever

The gain available from an agreement against the safety each party can buy at the agreement's expense.
The carrying force is that there is something here for both -- neither can reach it alone, and a deal on almost any terms beats no deal on many days; the resistance is that every instrument of self-protection is paid for out of the same pot: a position held firmly narrows the ground an agreement could stand on, an exposure refused turns every term into something that must be verified and enforced, and a capacity to hurt the other, acquired to strengthen a hand, makes the hand not worth playing.
Dealing is read on a pair rather than on a persona; a party who can simply walk away is not in it.

## Echo

A relation in one of three states, and about to make that state more likely again.
Dealing does not close; it is the mixed-motive condition running, gathered into one reading -- a table, a bet, a spiral.
It hands off what it is not: the moral wrong between two and its reckoning -- the felt guilt, the grievance, the forgiveness -- is the moral-account composite's, which owns the injury where this owns the price, so a broken promise is a wrong there and a repricing here; the settlement of accounts that ends a relation is the owing composite's; how the talk itself works, converges, and lands is the crosstalk and utterance composites'; what is withheld or revealed as a matter of truth-status is opacity's; the verdict on what the other party has coming is deserving's, and the standing rank either holds is standing's, where this reads only the leverage in the deal at hand; what happens between groups rather than parties is intergroup's; and a breakdown made to **stand for** a story is authored key, the play's pitch, not the force.
The pair is dated from the state they fell into, and read as two parties each of whom can name the other, correctly, as the reason it failed.
