# Process: Dealing, the Bet

## Taxonomy

[Dealing](process_dealing.md), the exposure one party accepts on conduct the other controls.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: dealing

## Initiated by

One party could protect themselves against what the other might do, and decides how much of that protection to forgo.
The Bet starts wherever [trust](@chbrain/khai-engine-trust/process_trust.md) is being placed or refused -- [extended](@chbrain/khai-engine-trust/process_extend.md) into ground the other party could take, [tested](@chbrain/khai-engine-trust/process_test.md) by what they do with it, [withheld](@chbrain/khai-engine-trust/process_withhold.md) so that nothing is at risk, [betrayed](@chbrain/khai-engine-trust/process_betrayed.md) where it was taken, or [repaired](@chbrain/khai-engine-trust/process_repair.md) at a price.
It is the face of the composite that is never spoken of at the table and prices everything on it.

## Direction

Toward a relation that is cheap to run, or one that is not.
The Bet reads exposure as an instrument rather than a sentiment -- the clause not asked for, the invoice paid before the work is checked, the information handed over that could be used -- and reads what each refusal costs: everything a bet supplies has to be bought some other way when it is absent, in verification, in drafting, in escrow, in the third party who now has to be paid to stand between two people who could have simply said yes.
It moves the pair toward terms that are affordable to write, or toward a deal that is worth doing and too expensive to document.

## Lever

The cheapness of the exposed relation against the plain reality of what exposure can cost.
The carrying force is that no pair can afford to specify everything, so some of the agreement is always resting on conduct nobody can compel, and the party who accepts that runs a relation at a fraction of the price of the one who does not; the resistance is that the risk is real and asymmetric -- the exposure is taken now and the conduct arrives later, the loss falls on the one who extended, and no amount of good judgement about a counterpart makes the bet into something other than a bet.
The Bet spends recourse, and buys the only discount available.

## Echo

A relation running cheaply, or one paying for its own suspicion.
It is the bridge where the composite's arithmetic first shows: a party who protects themselves thoroughly has not made the deal safer, they have made it dearer, and past a certain point they have priced it out entirely while doing nothing anyone could call unreasonable.
What it hands upward is the mechanism the spiral runs on -- a refused bet is read by the other party as a verdict on their character, which is very often exactly what it is, and it is the cheapest way to start a sequence neither of them wanted.
The Bet is dated from the protection that was available and not taken.
