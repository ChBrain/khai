# Process: Dealing, the Table

## Taxonomy

[Dealing](process_dealing.md), the state in which an agreement is being looked for.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: dealing

## Initiated by

Both parties have decided, at least for now, that terms are worth pursuing.
The Table starts wherever [negotiation](@chbrain/khai-engine-negotiation/process_negotiation.md) is running -- the [preparation](@chbrain/khai-engine-negotiation/process_preparation.md) done before either sits down, the [exploration](@chbrain/khai-engine-negotiation/process_exploration.md) of what is actually wanted behind what is being asked for, the [invention](@chbrain/khai-engine-negotiation/process_invention.md) of terms neither arrived with, and the [resolution](@chbrain/khai-engine-negotiation/process_resolution.md) that closes or does not.
It is the face of the composite where the most is still possible.

## Direction

Toward terms, or toward the discovery that there are none.
The Table reads the bargaining as a search over a space that the bargaining itself keeps changing -- the ground on which a deal could stand widening whenever an interest is found under a position, and narrowing every time a party commits publicly to a number they will now have to defend.
It moves the pair toward a settlement neither would have written alone, or toward the plain finding that the ranges do not meet.

## Lever

The value waiting in an agreement against the cost of the moves each party makes to secure their share of it.
The carrying force is that the pie is not fixed and both parties know it: an hour spent on what each side actually needs routinely turns up terms worth more to both than the thing they were arguing over, which is why the search is worth the discomfort of admitting what one wants; the resistance is the negotiator's standing dilemma -- the same candour that finds those terms is what a counterpart can use to take a larger share, so every move that creates value exposes, and every move that protects conceals, and the two cannot be done wholeheartedly at once.
The Table spends the safety of an unstated position.

## Echo

A pair holding a settlement, or holding the knowledge that there is not one.
It is the bridge that sets the composite's baseline: the state where the substance is still what the outcome depends on, and the only one of the three in which that is true.
What it hands upward is the reason the other two matter -- a table can produce a good agreement and still leave a pair who will not do this again, because what was agreed and what the agreeing did to them are different results.
The Table is dated from the first offer that was answered rather than refused.
