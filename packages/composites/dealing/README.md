# Dealing

Dealing as composite: two parties who cannot get what they want without each other and can each impose costs -- the bargaining they do (the table), the exposure they accept on conduct they cannot control (the bet), and the loop neither is steering (the spiral) -- wired over the negotiation, trust, and escalation engines, and closing on the discovery that everything each side does to make itself safe is what puts the agreement out of reach.
