# Process: Raising

## Taxonomy

What happens to somebody who says a harm is coming and can be doubted.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: raising

## Initiated by

A persona says something bad is going to happen, and they are the only source.
Raising starts wherever both are in play: a harm signalled before it arrives to somebody who would have to act, through [warning](@chbrain/khai-engine-warning/process_warning.md); and a first-person account whose only evidence is the one making it, through [credibility](@chbrain/khai-engine-credibility/process_credibility.md).
This composite adds no third engine. It reads the one thing the two make together, which is a claim that nothing can settle except the harm arriving.

## Direction

Toward a question that changes, without anybody noticing, from whether the claim is true to whether the speaker is reliable.
The composite wires the two and reads their working in three shapes: [the Standing](process_raising_standing.md), whose warning counts; [the Substitution](process_raising_substitution.md), the question being swapped for one that can be answered; and [the Proportion](process_raising_proportion.md), the twist -- what decides how hard the swap is worked.
The three are not a sequence of events. They are one arrangement, and a question about where doubt is cheapest to put.

## Lever

The value of hearing a warning early against the cost of acting on one that cannot be checked.
The carrying force is that scrutiny is owed here and the receiver is not being obtuse: an unverifiable claim about a future harm is exactly the kind that should be examined, most such claims are wrong, acting on every one would empty any organisation in a season, and the person weighing it is doing the job they hold; the resistance is that there is nothing in the claim to examine, because the event has not happened -- so the examination goes to the only thing available, which is the person who raised it.
Raising is read on where the scrutiny went, never on what it concluded.

## Echo

Somebody who was right and is now the subject.
Raising does not close; it is a claim placed where it cannot be checked, gathered into one reading -- a standing, a substitution, a proportion.
The twist is here: **the more expensive a warning would be to act on, the more thoroughly the warner is examined instead.** Doubt has to go somewhere and it goes where it costs least. A small warning is cheap to act on, so it is acted on and the speaker is never examined at all; a large one has no cheap response, the claim has no evidence in it yet, and the person is the only surface there is. So the scrutiny scales with the implication rather than with anything about the speaker -- and it rarely looks like retaliation from inside, because it is a search for a reason not to act, conducted by people who would be right to want one.
It hands off what it is not: the threshold, the receiving, the discounting by repetition, and the unrecordable success are warning's; the report with no second source, the deficit, the missing word, and the damage to the knower are credibility's; the standing a fair procedure confers is heeding's; the tolerated exception becoming the norm is drift's; the choice between leaving and speaking up is loyalty's; and a messenger destroyed to **stand for** a theme is authored key, the play's pitch, not the force.
The persona is dated from the last thing they raised.
