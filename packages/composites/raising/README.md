# Raising

Raising as composite: what happens to somebody who says a harm is coming and can be doubted. It is not a third mechanism but a wiring of two -- the warning engine supplies the harm signalled before it arrives, its threshold and its unrecordable success, and the credibility engine supplies the first-person account whose only evidence is the one making it. This composite adds no third engine; it reads the one thing the two make together, which is a claim that nothing can settle except the harm arriving. Three bridges: the Standing, where the same observation enters at a different threshold depending on who noticed it, so the earliest warnings enter at the highest one; the Substitution, where the claim offers no purchase and the speaker offers a great deal, so a careful inquiry goes where the material is and the hazard is left outside it; and the Proportion, the twist -- doubt goes where it costs least, so the more expensive a warning would be to act on, the more thoroughly the warner is examined instead.
