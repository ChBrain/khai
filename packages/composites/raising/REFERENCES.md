# Raising: Reference

## Line of Work

Raising as **composite**: what happens to somebody who says a harm is coming and can be doubted. It is
not a third mechanism but a wiring of two: the **warning** engine supplies the harm signalled before it
arrives, its threshold and its unrecordable success, and the **credibility** engine supplies the
first-person account whose only evidence is the one making it. This composite adds no third engine. It
reads the one thing the two make together, which is a claim that nothing can settle except the harm
arriving.

The warrant is accordingly integrative rather than primary: the sources below are the literature of
what organisations do to people who raise things, where each atom's own REFERENCES carries the primary
science of its side. **Alford is the joining.** His finding is not that whistleblowers are wrong, and
not separately that organisations retaliate; it is that the substance of the claim is characteristically
never addressed at all -- the process that follows a serious warning is an inquiry into the person who
gave it, and the people it happens to describe the absence of any engagement with the facts as the
thing they cannot get anybody to believe afterwards. That is a claim about the two engines together,
and it is why this composite exists.

Both atoms are wired here for the first time. The pairing is structural rather than convenient: a
warning is a first-person claim about an event that has not happened, so it carries both atoms'
difficulties simultaneously. It cannot be corroborated, because there is nothing yet to corroborate;
and it will therefore be evaluated on its speaker, because the speaker is the only thing in the room
with any evidence attached.

**The Standing** is where the two atoms are actually in contact. The warning engine treats the
threshold as a property of the system -- a setting somebody chose, deciding which error it will make.
The credibility engine shows that the setting is in practice a property of the speaker, since the same
observation from a senior engineer and from a contractor in their second week arrives at different
sensitivities, and neither sensitivity is written down anywhere. The consequence runs one way and it is
the reason the composite matters: the people closest to the work see a hazard first, in detail, before
it is countable, and they are frequently the people with the least standing.

**The Substitution** follows from the claim having no purchase. There is nothing to test, no second
source, and no event -- while the speaker offers a record, a manner, a relationship with a manager, and
a reason they might want this to be true. So the inquiry goes where the material is, and every step of
it is defensible in isolation. What makes it hard to see is that a question about the speaker looks
exactly like rigour, so the hazard goes un-examined by a process every participant experiences as
careful.

**The Proportion** is the loop and it is where the composite is seen for one thing. Doubt has to be put
somewhere, and it is put where it is cheapest. A small warning is cheap to act on, so it is acted on
and the speaker is never examined at all. A large one -- stop the line, ground the fleet, withdraw the
product, reopen the finished report -- has no cheap response, and the only available surface is the
person.

Hence the **twist**, at the root. **The more expensive a warning would be to act on, the more
thoroughly the warner is examined instead.** The scrutiny scales with the implication rather than with
anything about the speaker, which means the correct warnings about serious things attract the most of
it and attract it earliest. It rarely looks like retaliation from inside, because it is a search for a
reason not to act, conducted by people who would be right to want one.

The composite is built so that nobody has to be concealing anything for it to run, and a play should
keep it that way. The receiver is conscientious. The warning is right. Nobody is retaliating. The file
is about the person anyway.

**On the evidence, stated plainly.** **Alford's work is interview-based and selected in a way that
matters.** _Whistleblowers_ draws on a support-group population -- people whose cases went badly enough
that they were still talking about them years later -- so it is an account of the tail rather than of
the distribution, and nobody has interviewed the warners who were simply heard, because there is no
population of them to find. The engine's picture is therefore drawn from the cases where it went
wrong, and that limit should be read into every claim here. **Near and Miceli's finding is the closest
thing to a measured version and it is survey work**: retaliation is better predicted by how much the
disclosure threatens the organisation than by whether the disclosure is valid, which is the composite's
mechanism, and it rests on self-reported retaliation in mostly US samples, with the usual problems that
brings. **The twist is an inference and has not been measured.** Nobody has compared scrutiny-of-the-
speaker against cost-of-acting across a set of warnings, and it is not obvious how a study would get a
comparable measure of either; the claim follows from the two atoms plus the case literature and should
be read as a structural argument of the same kind as its atoms' twists. **The case literature has the
same defect the warning engine describes, applied to itself.** Challenger, Piper Alpha, and their
successors are retrospective and selected on disasters that happened, so the warnings that were heard
and acted on leave no case study, and the composite is reasoning from exactly the biased sample its own
parent engine warns against. That is stated here rather than worked around, because there is no way
around it. **Turner's incubation period** -- the long stretch before a failure in which warning signs
are present and discounted -- is a historical synthesis of inquiry reports, influential and not
experimental. What should not be taken from this composite is that receivers are covering things up,
that people who raise concerns are usually right, or that an organisation examining a complainant is
acting in bad faith. The claim is narrow: the threshold varies by speaker, the claim offers nothing to
examine, the person offers plenty, and the effort spent on them is set by what it would cost if they
were right.

## Restrictions

What the composite refuses to model, and to whom it delegates.

- **The signal itself (warning)**: The threshold and which error it accepts, the milling and
  under-response of receivers, the discounting produced by repetition, and the success that leaves no
  record are the warning engine's, whole. This composite reads none of them again; it declares the
  engine and links its members.
- **The account itself (credibility)**: The report with no second source, the deficit applied before
  listening, the missing word, and the damage that lands on the knower are the credibility engine's,
  whole. The composite adds only what happens when the account is about a harm that has not occurred.
- **The tolerated exception becoming the norm (drift)**: Normalization of deviance, and the yardstick
  moving with the practice so that no single step looks like a fall, are the drift engine's. That
  explains why the hazard was not visible as one; this composite starts once somebody has said it is.
- **Leaving instead of speaking up (loyalty, politics, membership)**: Allegiance held through a
  decline, and the choice between exit and voice, are those packages'. The composite reads what happens
  to voice once it has been used, not the decision to use it.
- **What a fair procedure buys (heeding, recognition)**: Procedural justice and institutional
  acknowledgement are those packages'. A scrupulously fair procedure is exactly what the Substitution
  runs inside, which is why fairness does not correct it.
- **The organisation's stance and stages (org, group, psychological-safety)**: How an organisation
  operates, how a group develops, and the climate that decides what can be risked aloud are those
  engines'. The composite is indifferent to the culture and reads the arithmetic.
- **Retaliation as a wrong to be judged (the world)**: Employment law, protected disclosure regimes,
  regulators, and settlements are matters of policy and practice with their own standards. They deal
  with the aftermath; this composite reads the process that produced it.
- **The messenger as authored symbol (the play's pitch)**: A warner destroyed in order to **stand for**
  a theme -- the prophet who mirrors a motif of blindness -- is refused: that is authored key, the
  play's pitch. Raising sets where the scrutiny went; the meaning read into it is the play's.

## Encoding

Source to constraint. Two atoms, one root, three bridges.

- **[raising](process_raising.md)** (the root): a harm reported by somebody who can be doubted, read on
  where the scrutiny went, carrying the twist -- the more expensive a warning would be to act on, the
  more thoroughly the warner is examined instead. Anchored by Alford (2001) over
  [warning](@chbrain/khai-engine-warning/process_warning.md) and
  [credibility](@chbrain/khai-engine-credibility/process_credibility.md).
- **[the Standing](process_raising_standing.md)**: the threshold varying by speaker, so the earliest
  warnings enter at the highest one. Anchored by Turner (1978) and Vaughan (1996).
- **[the Substitution](process_raising_substitution.md)**: the unanswerable question swapped for an
  answerable one, by a process everybody experiences as careful. Anchored by Alford (2001) and Glazer &
  Glazer (1989).
- **[the Proportion](process_raising_proportion.md)**: the scrutiny scaling with the implication, where
  the composite is seen for one thing. Anchored by Near & Miceli (1985, 2016).
