# Process: Raising, the Standing

## Taxonomy

[Raising](process_raising.md), whose warning counts.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: raising

## Initiated by

The warning's weight is set before the hazard is considered.
The Standing starts where warning's [threshold](@chbrain/khai-engine-warning/process_the_threshold_set.md) -- a setting somebody chose, deciding which error the system will make -- meets credibility's [deficit](@chbrain/khai-engine-credibility/process_the_deficit.md), the credit withheld from a speaker for what they are rather than for what they said.
The two produce something neither holds: a hazard whose visibility depends on who noticed it.

## Direction

Toward a warning system whose sensitivity varies by speaker.
An organisation does not have one threshold. It has as many as it has speakers, because the same observation reported by a senior engineer and by a contractor in their second week arrives at different settings, and neither setting was written down.
The consequence runs one way. The people closest to the work see a hazard first, in detail, before it is countable -- and they are frequently the people with the least standing, so the earliest warnings enter at the highest threshold.

## Lever

The reasonableness of weighting by source against what the weighting selects for.
The carrying force is that a source's position is genuine information and ignoring it would be foolish: experience does track judgement, somebody thirty years in the trade is more often right about it, and a receiver who weighted a first-week contractor and a chief engineer identically would be worse at the job rather than fairer; the resistance is that proximity and standing come apart, so the correlation the receiver is relying on inverts at exactly the early edge where a warning is worth most.
The Standing sets the alarm quietest where the hazard is first visible.

## Echo

An early signal entering at the wrong sensitivity.
It is the bridge where the two engines are actually in contact: what warning treats as a property of the system, credibility shows to be a property of the speaker.
What it hands upward is not a rejection but an interval -- the warning is not dismissed, it is held at a lower priority, and the interval is where everything else happens.
The Standing is dated before the warning and is written down nowhere.
