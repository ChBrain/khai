# Instructions: Raising

How a Playwright wires the raising composite into a play. You wire by linking the play's own content to the composite's files; the files stay untouched, they are canon. The composite declares two atoms as dependencies and links their members by name -- you never copy an atom's reading into the play, you point at it. The Roadie plumbs the composite's law into Instructions Knowledge on deploy. Authoring guidance, not runtime content, and it does not go on tour.

## Human

- The human decides what acting on the warning would cost and keeps the figure off the page, because it sets how hard the persona gets examined and nobody in the room will ever name it. Give both sides -- the receiver is a position in the composite, not an obstacle in it. And keep the receiver conscientious: the composite does not need a cover-up and stops working the moment it gets one.

## Agent

- From the play's own content, a persona links under Projection which bridge is running: whether their standing set the threshold before the hazard was considered, whether the questions have moved to them, or whether the scrutiny is scaling with what acting would cost. The link is audited rather than failed, because a persona can be in either atom without being in this arrangement. Read the composite on where the scrutiny went, never on what it concluded. Keep it the joining: the threshold and the unrecordable success are warning's, the report with no second source and the damage to the knower are credibility's. You link, you never edit.

## Collaboration

- The threshold, the receiving, the discounting by repetition, and the success that leaves no record are the warning engine's, whole; the report with no second source, the deficit, the missing word, and the damage to the knower are the credibility engine's, whole; the tolerated exception becoming the norm is drift's; the choice between leaving and speaking up is loyalty's, politics's, and the membership composite's; procedural fairness is the heeding composite's and recognition's; the organisation's stance, the group's stage, and the climate are org's, group's, and psychological-safety's. Employment law, protected disclosure regimes, and regulators deal with the aftermath and are not modelled here. Whether a production examined the hazard or the person, and whether a receiver was being rigorous or looking for a reason to stand down, are the team's review.

## Knowledge

- Raising is what happens to somebody who says a harm is coming and can be doubted. It is not a third mechanism but a wiring of two: the warning engine supplies the harm signalled before it arrives, and the credibility engine supplies the first-person account whose only evidence is the one making it. It adds no third engine and reads the one thing the two make together -- a claim that nothing can settle except the harm arriving, because a warning is a first-person report about an event that has not happened, so it cannot be corroborated and will therefore be evaluated on its speaker. It wires on a persona at Projection and runs in three bridges. The Standing is where the two atoms touch: an organisation has as many thresholds as it has speakers, since the same observation from a senior engineer and from a second-week contractor arrives at different sensitivities and neither is written down -- and because the people closest to the work usually hold the least standing, the earliest warnings enter at the highest setting. The Substitution follows from the claim offering no purchase: nothing to test, no second source, no event, while the speaker offers a record, a manner, and a motive -- so the inquiry goes where the material is, every step is defensible, and a question about the speaker looks exactly like rigour, which is why the hazard goes un-examined by a process everybody experiences as careful. The Proportion is the loop: doubt goes where it costs least, so a small warning is acted on and the speaker is never looked at, and a large one has no cheap response and the person is the only surface there is. The twist is at the root: the more expensive a warning would be to act on, the more thoroughly the warner is examined instead -- the scrutiny scales with the implication rather than with anything about the speaker, and it is a search for a reason not to act, conducted by people who would be right to want one.

## System

- Do decide what acting would cost before anything else, and never say the number. It sets the whole shape and nobody in the play can see it.
- Do keep the receiver conscientious. They are examining the claim seriously, they can defend every step, and they would be appalled to be told what the process amounts to.
- Do not stage a cover-up. That replaces the mechanism with a conspiracy and describes a rarer and less interesting thing.
- Do give the warning to somebody without standing. The person who sees it first is usually the person whose report enters at the highest threshold.
- Do let the questions drift to the person, one reasonable question at a time. Their record, their manner, why they might want this to be true -- each defensible, none about the hazard.
- Do put the scrutiny in proportion to the cost. A cheap fix gets done and nobody asks who raised it; an expensive one gets a thorough inquiry into the persona.
- Do let the file exist. By the end there is a record about the person and none about the risk, and nobody decided to make it that way.
- Do not close on vindication. A play that proves the warner right in the last scene has replaced the mechanism with a courtroom; close on the scrutiny landing where it was cheapest.
- Do not model the signal itself (warning), the account itself (credibility), the tolerated exception (drift), exit and voice (loyalty, politics, membership), procedural fairness (heeding, recognition), or the organisation's culture (org, group, psychological-safety). Do not make the messenger stand for a theme; that is authored key, the play's pitch. Do not edit the composite's or the atoms' files; wire only from the play's side.
