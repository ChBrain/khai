# Process: Peopling, the Loitering

## Taxonomy

[Peopling](process_peopling.md), the presence a place cannot compel, and where the three are seen for one thing.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: peopling

## Initiated by

Somebody is here who does not have to be, and is not going anywhere.
The Loitering starts wherever [lingering](@chbrain/khai-engine-street-life/process_lingering.md) is running -- the staying-on after the use is finished, the sitting on a step, the standing about at a [node](@chbrain/khai-engine-legibility/process_node.md) or under a [landmark](@chbrain/khai-engine-legibility/process_landmark.md) because it is a place worth standing about at, in whatever [tempo](@chbrain/khai-engine-social-time/process_tempo.md) the place keeps slow enough to permit it -- and it draws the [presence](@chbrain/khai-engine-street-life/process_presence.md) that other presence attracts.
It is the face of the composite that no design, budget, or schedule can produce directly.

## Direction

Toward the one reading the place cannot manufacture.
The Loitering reads the optional staying -- the bench occupied all afternoon, the wall people sit on though it was not built to be sat on, the corner where the same conversation seems to be permanently in progress -- and reads it as evidence rather than as ornament: necessary presence arrives whatever the place is like, and this arrives only where the place is good, so it is the single component of the population that is actually reporting.
It moves nobody anywhere, and it is the only face of the three that is about the place at all.

## Lever

The exactness of an unforced reading against every reason to remove it.
The carrying force is that this is the measurement: the loiterers are the instrument, and a place that has them has passed the only test that is not confounded by necessity, which is why an afternoon spent counting who is sitting down tells more than a year of footfall totals; the resistance is that it does not look like a measurement -- it looks like waste, or like menace, and it is read that way by the people with the power to end it, so the hostile bench goes in, the plaza is swept, the seat is angled, the group is moved along.
The Loitering rides the composite's keystone knife-edge: the only presence that reports on a place is the presence the place did not compel, so a place made efficient destroys the sole instrument by which it could have been known to work.

## Echo

The place vindicated, by the people it had least claim on.
It shows the three faces complete -- one that would have happened anywhere, one that mixes habit with choice, one that is pure choice -- and carries beneath it the composite's keystone: presence is informative in inverse proportion to how much it was compelled, so the errand is the loudest reading and the emptiest, and the idler on the step is the quietest and the only one that counts.
This is why the Loitering is the hardest of the three to defend and the only one worth defending: nobody can be scheduled into it, no budget produces it, and the case for it must be made against an audience that can see with its own eyes that these people are doing nothing.
The Loitering is the note the whole composite closes on: what a place is worth is legible in who stays for no reason, and nowhere else without a discount.
The Loitering is dated from the moment the use ended and the person did not leave.
