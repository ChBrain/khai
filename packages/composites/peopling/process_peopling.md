# Process: Peopling

## Taxonomy

A place read by the presence in it.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: peopling

## Initiated by

There are people in a place, and the question is what their being there says about it.
Peopling starts wherever that presence is legible: the place held as an image somebody can move through, via [legibility](@chbrain/khai-engine-legibility/process_legibility.md); the rhythm the place keeps and gathers on, via [social time](@chbrain/khai-engine-social-time/process_social-time.md); and the social occupation it hosts, via [street life](@chbrain/khai-engine-street-life/process_street-life.md).
This composite adds no fourth engine. It reads what the three have in common, which is that none of them is in the buildings: an image is held in a head, a rhythm is kept by people, and an occupation happens or does not, and a maker can build none of the three -- only the conditions under which they might occur.

## Direction

Toward a reading of the place taken from the people who are in it, graded by how much the place had to compel them.
The composite wires the three engines and reads their working in three shapes: [the Errand](process_peopling_errand.md), the crossing that would have happened whatever the place were like; [the Round](process_peopling_round.md), the return kept on a rhythm; and [the Loitering](process_peopling_loitering.md), the twist -- the staying done for no reason at all.
The three are not three kinds of people. They are one population, sorted by what would have kept them away.

## Lever

The exactness of presence as a measurement against the fact that most presence is measuring something else.
The carrying force is that a place cannot be assessed from its plan and can be assessed from its occupants -- an image that fails leaves people lost in it, a rhythm that fails leaves it empty at the hours it should be full, an occupation that fails leaves it crossed and never used -- and all of that is visible to anyone standing there for an afternoon; the resistance is that presence is confounded by necessity, since a street between a station and an office will be full whatever it is like, so the readings crowd toward the bottom of the scale where they carry the least information.
Peopling is read on the place and never on the person in it; what the place means to any one of them belongs elsewhere.

## Echo

A place with its people in it, and a reading only the unforced part of that presence supports.
Peopling does not close; it is the place being occupied, gathered into one reading -- an errand, a round, a loitering.
It hands off what it is not: a persona's own bond with a place -- the one they belong at, the one that restores them, the one they are rooted in -- is the dwelling composite's, read on the person where this is read on the place; a persona altered by the presence of others, their performance changed by being seen, their responsibility divided, themselves massed into a crowd, is the midst composite's, also read on the person; the physical distance a persona keeps from another is the proxemics engine's; the shared quarters of a household or an institution and the order kept in them is subjection's; the built form itself -- what the place is made of, what it affords, what it conceals -- is the place's own chapters and the design engines', where this reads only what happened in front of them; and a square emptied to **stand for** a story is authored key, the play's pitch, not the force.
The place is dated from the people who did not have to be there.
