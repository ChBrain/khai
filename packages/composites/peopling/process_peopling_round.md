# Process: Peopling, the Round

## Taxonomy

[Peopling](process_peopling.md), the presence that comes back, half-obliged and half-chosen.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: peopling

## Initiated by

The same people are here again, at the hour they were here last time.
The Round starts wherever a place is keeping a rhythm and people are keeping it with the place -- the [calendar](@chbrain/khai-engine-social-time/process_calendar.md) it runs on and the [tempo](@chbrain/khai-engine-social-time/process_tempo.md) it runs at, gathered into the [synchrony](@chbrain/khai-engine-social-time/process_synchrony.md) of a shared hour -- and where that recurrence is turning strangers into faces, through the [mingling](@chbrain/khai-engine-street-life/process_mingling.md) of people who now half-know each other and the [watch](@chbrain/khai-engine-street-life/process_watch.md) that comes free with being regularly seen.
It is the face of the composite where obligation and preference are mixed and cannot be separated.

## Direction

Toward a place that has become inhabitable rather than merely passable.
The Round reads the recurring presence -- the market on Saturdays, the school run, the evening walk the whole street takes without arranging it, the same four people at the same café at the same hour -- and reads what recurrence produces that a single crossing cannot: the [district](@chbrain/khai-engine-legibility/process_district.md) known well enough to be held without effort, the strangers become familiars, and the informal watching that only regulars can supply.
It moves the place from a route into somewhere with its own population.

## Lever

The binding of a kept rhythm against the constraint of the same rhythm.
The carrying force is that returning on a cycle does the work no amount of traffic does: it accumulates recognition, so the people here are increasingly here **as themselves**, and a place with regulars is safer, easier, and better-tempered than an identical place with an equal number of strangers; the resistance is that the rhythm is also a demand -- the schedule that gathers is the schedule that constrains -- and the one who keeps the place's time without being gathered by it is more alone in the full square than they were in the empty one.
The Round spends the freedom of being unrecognised.

## Echo

A place with a population, and the beginnings of a claim about its quality.
It is the middle of the composite's scale in both senses: more informative than the errand because something in it was optional, less conclusive than the loitering because something in it was not.
What it hands upward is the reason presence must be graded rather than counted -- the market-day crowd and the rush-hour crowd are not the same evidence, though they may be the same number, and the difference is only the share that would have come anyway.
The Round is dated from the second time, which is the first time anything was chosen.
