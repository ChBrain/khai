# Process: Peopling, the Errand

## Taxonomy

[Peopling](process_peopling.md), the presence a place did not have to earn.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: peopling

## Initiated by

Somebody has to get somewhere, and the place is in the way.
The Errand starts wherever a place is being crossed under obligation -- moved along the [paths](@chbrain/khai-engine-legibility/process_path.md) it offers and steered by whatever [landmark](@chbrain/khai-engine-legibility/process_landmark.md) is visible, timed by the [schedule](@chbrain/khai-engine-social-time/process_schedule.md) that put the person on the street at that hour, and counted in the [presence](@chbrain/khai-engine-street-life/process_presence.md) the place shows at eight in the morning.
It is the face of the composite that will occur under nearly any conditions, which is exactly what is wrong with it as evidence.

## Direction

Toward a place fully occupied and telling you almost nothing.
The Errand reads the compelled crossing -- the station approach, the corridor between the car park and the door, the underpass used because there is no other way over -- and reads what such presence can and cannot support: it can show that the place works as a route, since a person who gets lost is a person the image failed, and it cannot show that the place is any good, since nobody chose it.
It moves people through, on time, and leaves the question open.

## Lever

The volume of necessary presence against its indifference to quality.
The carrying force is that necessity is reliable and legible in its own right: a place that is hard to read costs errand-runners real time, so the [nodes](@chbrain/khai-engine-legibility/process_node.md) and [edges](@chbrain/khai-engine-legibility/process_edge.md) that fail show up as hesitation, doubling back, and the small crowd standing at a junction working out which way is out; the resistance is that the errand will keep coming whatever is done to the place -- brutalise it, wall it, strip every seat from it, and the numbers hold -- so the busiest streets in the world include some of the worst, and the count alone cannot tell them apart.
The Errand spends nothing and proves nothing beyond navigability.

## Echo

A place used and unjudged.
It is the bridge that establishes the composite's scale by sitting at the bottom of it: presence at its highest volume and its lowest information, the reading everyone takes because it is the easiest to take.
What it hands upward is the confound the other two exist to escape -- that a full place and a good place are different claims, and only one of them is settled by counting.
The Errand is dated from the timetable that produced it, and from nobody's preference at all.
