# Process: Freehand, the Room

## Taxonomy

[Freehand](process_freehand.md), the setting deciding the outcome, and where the two are seen for one thing.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: freehand

## Initiated by

The same persona, doing the same thing, in a different kind of room.
The Room starts wherever the conditions decide what [the disposition](@chbrain/khai-engine-dark-triad/position_dark_triad.md) is worth -- whether the encounter is face to face and improvised, whether there is latitude to invent as it goes, and above all whether the [maintenance](@chbrain/khai-engine-deception/process_maintenance.md) of a false impression will ever be tested by anything that outlasts the conversation.
It is the face of the composite where the persona's competence turns out not to be theirs.

## Direction

Toward an outcome that will be attributed to a person and was produced by a setting.
The Room reads the conditions as the variable and the persona as the constant: unstructured, unrecorded, one-off, and improvisational favours the disposition and makes it look like skill; structured, recorded, rule-bound, and repeated exposes it and makes the same conduct look like what it is.
It moves nothing about the persona, and everything about what the persona is worth.

## Lever

What a room permits against what a room remembers.
The carrying force is that latitude is not a defect of a room but usually a feature: rooms are left improvisational because improvisation is valuable, roles are left loose because looseness is efficient, and conversations are left unrecorded because recording them is expensive and insulting -- so the conditions this disposition thrives in are conditions somebody chose for good reasons; the resistance is that the remedy is available and is structural rather than personal, since taking the latitude out -- writing it down, fixing the roles, making the same parties meet again -- disarms it without anybody having to identify who it was aimed at, and the persona cannot adapt because the disposition is not the sort of thing that adapts.
The Room rides the composite's keystone knife-edge: what reads as a superior person is a superior fit, so the apparent competence belongs to the setting and the setting is the only thing anybody can change.

## Echo

A run of wins, in the only kind of room where they were available.
It shows the two engines complete -- a disposition carried unchanged and a repertoire it converts into -- and carries beneath it the composite's keystone: the advantage is narrow and conditional, it does not generalise, and where the record is kept and the parties meet again the same disposition is a liability rather than an edge, which is what the averages say.
This is why the Room is the hardest of the three to stage and the only one worth closing on: the persona must be genuinely effective in the first room, must be identical in the second, must be undone by nothing more than the conditions, and must not understand what changed -- and a play that has them outwitted by a cleverer opponent has staged a duel rather than this.
The Room is the note the whole composite closes on: what a setting was actually permitting is legible in who did well in it.
The Room is dated from whoever set the conditions, and they are usually not in the scene.
