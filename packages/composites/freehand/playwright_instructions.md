# Instructions: Freehand

How a Playwright wires the freehand composite into a play. You wire by linking the play's
own content to the composite's files; the files stay untouched, they are canon. The
composite installs the dark-triad and deception engines as dependencies, and their laws
arrive with them. Authoring guidance, not runtime content, and it does not go on tour.

## Human

- The human sets which personas carry the disposition, which rooms in the play have latitude and no record, and which do not.

## Agent

- From the play's own content, read a persona carrying an aversive disposition and working a situation, and link under Projection what is running: the make while the cold self-advancing core is resting in one of its three shapes (from dark-triad), the play while a false belief is being constructed, delivered, and kept standing in somebody who did not consent to hold it (from deception), the room while the conditions are deciding whether any of it works. Read the room and never the persona alone, since the persona is the same in every scene and only the outcome changes. You link to the composite, you never edit it.

## Collaboration

- Narcissism, machiavellianism, and psychopathy are the dark-triad engine's; construction, delivery, and maintenance are deception's -- one alone is a position or a process, not the conversion. The account a persona gives themselves afterwards is squaring's, and the boundary is substantive: this persona typically needs less of that machinery, because there is less discomfort to resolve. A favourable self-view meeting a contradiction is smarting's, which wires the narcissism family on a refusal where this runs the disposition as a working instrument; what is kept hidden and what holding it costs is opacity's and carrying's; arranging a made thing so a choice goes one way is artifice's, where the maker is absent and here they are in the room; the record that outlives the occasion is writ's, and it is the strongest thing this composite's room can be given; who pays to enforce a shared thing is upkeep's, and this persona is much of what the enforcement is for; the tilts of judgement in general are bias's and estimation's, and the target's failure to check is ordinary rather than biased. This composite owns the conversion -- and the room bridge carries the keystone: what reads as a superior person is a superior fit.

## Knowledge

- Freehand is a persona read while an aversive disposition meets a situation -- braiding what they carry (dark triad: cold self-advancement with the empathy that would slow it turned low, resolving into narcissism, machiavellianism, and psychopathy, held in degree rather than in kind) with what they do with it (deception: a false belief constructed, delivered, and maintained in somebody who did not consent to hold it). The join is Jones and Paulhus's: the three shapes do not deceive more or less than one another, they deceive differently -- the machiavellian strategically and cautiously, picking moments and hedging exposure; the psychopathic impulsively, over-reaching and getting caught; the narcissistic upward, toward a version of the self it would like confirmed rather than toward a gain. So there is no being good at deceiving in the abstract, only at a kind of it. It runs in three faces. The make is the disposition, carried in and not chosen, and it removes a brake most personas cannot remove -- asking for more, changing position, letting somebody else take the loss are all cheaper here, and cheapness is a real capability. But a brake removed is removed everywhere, so the persona brings the same setting into the recorded meeting and the improvised one with nothing that would tell them which they are in. The play is the conversion into conduct: construction and delivery are the easy parts, available to anybody and almost always landing since the target has no reason to be checking -- which is why the early record looks good. Maintenance is the expensive part and the part where the shapes separate, because a false belief must survive every later thing that touches it and its cost rises with every party who knows, every occasion it must be repeated, and every record it must not contradict. The room is the twist, and it is Christie and Geis's: the advantage appears where the encounter is face to face, latitude-rich, and distracting, and vanishes where the situation is structured and the moves are fixed. Here the two are seen for one thing: the persona is the constant across scenes and the conditions are the variable, so what reads as a superior person is a superior fit -- the apparent competence belongs to the setting, the remedy is structural rather than personal, and the persona cannot adapt because the disposition is not the sort of thing that adapts.

## System

- Do read the room and never the persona alone: name whether the make (a disposition resting), the play (a false belief being built and kept), or the room (conditions deciding) is running, and cast each from its engines.
- Do set the room deliberately and let it do the work: the same persona in a recorded, rule-bound, repeat-encounter setting is not formidable, and staging them as formidable everywhere loses the composite entirely.
- Do make the tactic specific to the trait rather than generic cunning, since the manner of the deceit is what identifies which shape is present.
- Do keep the persona identical between the two rooms and let nothing but the conditions undo them -- a play that has them outwitted by a cleverer opponent has staged a duel instead.
- Do put the cost in maintenance rather than in construction: the first move should land easily, and the bill should arrive later, from a party who knows, an occasion that repeats, or a record that will not agree.
- Do not let them understand what changed, because the disposition does not adapt and a persona who diagnoses their own room is a different character.
- Do not use this composite to explain success in general: on average the disposition costs its carrier, and the meta-analytic picture runs against the folk story.
- Do not model the self-justification afterwards (that is squaring, and this persona needs less of it), a punctured self-view (that is smarting), the strain of holding something in (that is opacity and carrying), an arrangement made by an absent maker (that is artifice), the record itself (that is writ), or the enforcement of a shared thing (that is upkeep). Do not make an operator stand for a theme (that is the play's pitch). Do not edit the composite's files; wire only from the play's side.
