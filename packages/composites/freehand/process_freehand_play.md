# Process: Freehand, the Play

## Taxonomy

[Freehand](process_freehand.md), the deceit each shape actually runs.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: freehand

## Initiated by

The persona wants somebody to believe something that is not so.
The Play starts wherever [deception](@chbrain/khai-engine-deception/process_deception.md) is running -- the [construction](@chbrain/khai-engine-deception/process_construction.md) of the false impression, its [delivery](@chbrain/khai-engine-deception/process_delivery.md) into the target's belief, and the [maintenance](@chbrain/khai-engine-deception/process_maintenance.md) that keeps it standing once other things start bearing on it.
It is the face of the composite where the three shapes stop looking alike.

## Direction

Toward a false belief held by somebody who did not agree to hold it.
The Play reads the difference rather than the quantity, which is the composite's first real claim: the three shapes do not deceive more or less than one another, they deceive differently -- the strategic one plans, hedges, and picks its moments; the callous one improvises, over-reaches, and is caught; the grandiose one lies upward, toward a version of itself it would like confirmed rather than toward a gain.
It moves the target's picture of the world, and the manner of the move says which trait is in the room.

## Lever

The usefulness of a false belief against what keeping one costs.
The carrying force is that construction and delivery are the easy parts and are within reach of anybody: the impression can be built quickly, and the target has no reason to be checking, so the first move almost always lands -- which is why the tactic looks effective and why the persona's early record is good; the resistance is maintenance, which is where the shapes separate, since a false belief has to survive every later thing that touches it and the cost of holding it rises with every party who knows, every occasion it must be repeated, and every record it must not contradict.
The Play spends the persona's future room to manoeuvre, in instalments they are not counting.

## Echo

An impression established, and an obligation to keep it standing.
It is the bridge that shows the disposition converted into conduct, and shows the conversion to be specific rather than generic -- there is no such thing as being good at deceiving in the abstract, only at a kind of it.
What it hands upward is the maintenance burden, and the fact that whether the burden is bearable is not a property of the persona at all.
The Play is dated from the first impression given, and it accrues from there.
