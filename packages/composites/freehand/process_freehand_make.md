# Process: Freehand, the Make

## Taxonomy

[Freehand](process_freehand.md), the disposition carried in, before any scene.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: freehand

## Initiated by

A persona arrives already like this, and did not decide to be.
The Make starts wherever [the dark triad](@chbrain/khai-engine-dark-triad/position_dark_triad.md) is resting -- resolving into [narcissism](@chbrain/khai-engine-dark-triad/position_narcissism.md), a grandiose self that needs admiration to stay inflated; [machiavellianism](@chbrain/khai-engine-dark-triad/position_machiavellianism.md), a cool strategic cynicism that plays people toward an end; or [psychopathy](@chbrain/khai-engine-dark-triad/position_psychopathy.md), callous affect unbraked by another's pain or by consequence.
It is the face of the composite where nothing has been done yet.

## Direction

Toward a standing readiness, carried unchanged into every room.
The Make reads the core as continuous rather than categorical: these are traits measurable in ordinary people and held in degree, so the persona is not a type but a position on a scale, and the three shapes overlap without collapsing into one -- each is a different resting arrangement of the same low-empathy self-advancement.
It moves nothing by itself, and is the only part of the composite the persona cannot alter.

## Lever

The freedom of not being slowed against the cost of not being slowed anywhere.
The carrying force is that the disposition removes a brake most personas cannot remove: asking for more, changing position, letting somebody take the loss -- all of it is cheaper here, and cheapness is a real capability in the moments where it matters; the resistance is that a brake removed is removed in every room, so the persona carries the same setting into the recorded meeting and the improvised one, into the first encounter and the fortieth, with no mechanism that would tell them the conditions have changed.
The Make spends nothing, and is not a strategy despite being read as one.

## Echo

A disposition, standing, indifferent to where it has been brought.
It is the bridge that fixes what is constant, and the fixing is what makes the composite's last bridge possible: if the persona varied, the outcome would not be diagnostic of the room.
What it hands upward is a readiness with no plan attached -- and the fact that the three shapes, sharing a core, will not do the same thing with it.
The Make is dated from before the play began, and it will not be revised inside one.
