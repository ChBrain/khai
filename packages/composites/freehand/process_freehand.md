# Process: Freehand

## Taxonomy

What an aversive disposition can do, and the room that decides whether it can.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: freehand

## Initiated by

Somebody who does not much mind what it costs other people is working a situation.
Freehand starts wherever those two are both present: a cold self-advancing core with the empathy that would slow it turned low, through [the dark triad](@chbrain/khai-engine-dark-triad/position_dark_triad.md); and a false belief built in somebody who did not consent to hold it, through [deception](@chbrain/khai-engine-deception/process_deception.md).
This composite adds no third engine. It reads the one question the two make together, which is not what the persona is like but what the room lets that be worth.

## Direction

Toward an outcome the persona will be given credit or blame for, and the setting that produced it.
The composite wires the two engines and reads their working in three shapes: [the Make](process_freehand_make.md), the disposition and its three shapes; [the Play](process_freehand_play.md), the deceit each shape actually runs; and [the Room](process_freehand_room.md), the twist -- the setting deciding whether any of it works.
The three are not a portrait of a villain. They are a disposition, a repertoire, and a set of conditions, and only the last is variable within a scene.

## Lever

What the disposition frees a persona to do against how much the room permits.
The carrying force is that the disposition is a genuine advantage in a narrow band: a persona unslowed by another's discomfort can move faster, ask for more, and change position without cost, and where nothing is written down and nobody checks, this reads as decisiveness; the resistance is that the same freedom is a liability wherever conduct is recorded, roles are fixed, or the same parties meet repeatedly -- since the moves that work once are exactly the moves that accumulate into a file, and the persona has no braking system that would tell them which room they are in.
Freehand is read on the room and never on the persona alone, because the persona is the same in every scene and only the outcome changes.

## Echo

A performance that looked like ability, in a setting chosen to make it look like one.
Freehand does not close; it is a disposition meeting a situation, gathered into one reading -- a make, a play, a room.
It hands off what it is not: the account a persona gives themselves afterwards is squaring's, and this persona often needs less of it; what a persona keeps hidden and at what cost is opacity's and carrying's; a favourable self-view meeting a contradiction is smarting's, which wires the narcissism family on a refusal where this runs it as a working disposition; the arrangement of a made thing so a choice goes one way is artifice's, and there the maker is absent while here they are in the room; the record that outlives the occasion is writ's, and it is the strongest thing the room can be given; who pays to enforce a shared thing is upkeep's, and this persona is what the enforcement is for; the resting tilts of judgement in general are bias's; and an operator made to **stand for** a theme is authored key, the play's pitch, not the force.
The persona is dated from what they carried in, and the scene from what it allowed.
