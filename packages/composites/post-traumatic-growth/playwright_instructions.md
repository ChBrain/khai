# Instructions: Post-Traumatic Growth

How a Playwright wires the post-traumatic-growth composite into a play. You wire by linking the play's own content to the composite's files and, through them, to the four atom engines it depends on; every file stays untouched, they are canon. The Roadie plumbs the law into Instructions Knowledge on deploy. Authoring guidance, not runtime content, and it does not go on tour.

## Human

- The human sets the trauma and the stakes: an event grave enough to shatter the persona's assumptive world -- to break its core beliefs that the world is safe and meaningful and the self worthy -- and the long struggle to rebuild that follows.

## Agent

- Cast the four parts from their atoms: meaning reconstructed (the meaning engine), the life story revised (the narrative engine), mortality confronted (the mortality engine), and the self remade (the identity engine). Then read the transformation as growth beyond the old baseline, not a return to it, along one of two movements: strength, the self made more capable and its possibilities widened; wisdom, the philosophy of life deepened by having faced the worst. A persona transformed along either movement may link it under Projection; the audit surfaces the ones left unnamed. You link to the composite and its atoms, you never edit them.

## Collaboration

- The maintenance of stable functioning across the event -- coming through unbroken rather than remade -- is the resilience composite's, the outcome this one is defined against. Each part of the rebuilding is its atom's: meaning-making the meaning engine's, the revised story the narrative engine's, the confrontation with mortality the mortality engine's, the remade self the identity engine's. The acute stress response and the chronic-dysfunction trajectory are the stress engine's and resilience's; growth can coexist with ongoing distress. The mere return of pleasure to a setpoint is the hedonic-adaptation engine's. Whether a production earns the growth -- a real shattering truly rebuilt -- is the team's review.

## Knowledge

- Post-traumatic growth is positive transformation beyond the prior baseline, wrought through the struggle with trauma (Tedeschi & Calhoun) -- not despite the struggle but through it. It begins where a seismic event shatters the assumptive world (Janoff-Bulman): the core beliefs that the world is benevolent and meaningful and the self worthy break, and must be rebuilt rather than restored. The rebuilding runs through meaning-making, narrative revision, existential confrontation, and the remaking of the self, and through that deliberate, effortful work growth emerges across five domains, which group into two movements. Strength: the self transformed -- personal strength ("I survived that, I can bear what comes") and new possibilities the old life did not hold. Wisdom: the philosophy of life transformed -- a deepened appreciation of life, existential and spiritual change, and a richer valuing of others. Growth is wrought, not automatic or universal, and it can run alongside continuing post-traumatic distress -- it is a separate axis from the disorder, not its opposite. Read the production across the compound and its movement, never in the trauma alone, and keep growth distinct from the mere maintenance of baseline.

## System

- Do stage a shattering deep enough to force a rebuild -- a lesser blow the old frame absorbs yields resilience, not growth -- and cast all four parts of the rebuilding before reading the growth.
- Do read growth as transformation beyond the old baseline, not a return to it: strength for the remade, more-capable self, wisdom for the deepened philosophy of life.
- Do let growth coexist with the wound: a persona can grow and still suffer; do not stage growth as the cancellation of distress, and do not force a growth the struggle did not earn.
- Do not play the maintenance of stable functioning as growth; that is resilience. Do not play the mere return of pleasure to a setpoint as growth; that is hedonic-adaptation. Do not restate the atoms' work; wire only from the play's side, and do not edit the composite's or the atoms' files.
