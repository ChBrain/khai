# Position: Post-Traumatic Growth, Strength

## Taxonomy

[Post-Traumatic Growth](process_post_traumatic_growth.md), the movement where the self is transformed -- strength and new possibilities.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: post-traumatic-growth

## Has

The self-directed movement of growth: a persona that emerges from the struggle more capable and more open than it was. Two of Tedeschi and Calhoun's domains cluster here -- personal strength, the discovery that one is stronger than one knew ("if I survived that, I can bear what comes"), and new possibilities, the paths, purposes, and interests the old life did not hold. The [identity](@chbrain/khai-engine-identity/position_achieved.md) engine's remade, re-achieved self carries it, told through the [narrative](@chbrain/khai-engine-narrative/position_agentic.md) engine's agentic, redemptive story of a low point turned to a source of strength.

## Orders

Own the strength the ordeal revealed, and walk the paths it opened. The strength movement orders the persona to hold itself as remade and more capable -- to meet what comes with the confidence of one who has already survived the unsurvivable -- and to take up the new possibilities the shattering cleared room for: the changed vocation, the risk now worth taking, the self it could not have been on the old path. It is growth as agency: a wider sense of what the persona can do and become.

## Loses

The person it would have been on the unbroken path, and the ease of an untested self. The strength is real but bought at the price of the trauma, and the persona forgoes the self that never needed to discover it -- there is no version of this growth without the wound that forced it. It risks, too, the overreach of a strength narrative that hardens into denial of ongoing pain, or a claimed growth never actually tested. What it trades is the innocence of the untried for a capability it would not have chosen the road to.

## Drives

On that self-transforming movement:
rides it -- holds the remade, stronger self, walks the new paths, and tells the redemptive, agentic story of what the ordeal made of it -- or, paired with the [wisdom](position_growth_wisdom.md) movement, deepens from a changed sense of what it can do into a changed sense of what is worth doing. The strength baseline holds where the growth is self-directed, the remade self rather than, like the wisdom movement, the remade philosophy of life. That growth comes through the struggle and beyond the old baseline is the [root's](process_post_traumatic_growth.md); the remade self it rests on is the identity engine's, and the maintenance of the old self across the storm is the resilience composite's.
