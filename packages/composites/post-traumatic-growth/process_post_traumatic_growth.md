# Process: Post-Traumatic Growth

## Taxonomy

A composite reading over four atom engines (no canon file of its own): the meaning, narrative, mortality, and identity engines this package declares as dependencies.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: post-traumatic-growth

## Initiated by

A persona meets a trauma grave enough to break the frame it lived by, and cannot simply resume.
The compound begins where an ordinary adversity ends: with a seismic event that shatters the persona's assumptive world -- the core beliefs that the world is benevolent and meaningful and the self worthy (Janoff-Bulman). The old frame cannot be restored, and the persona is forced to rebuild rather than return. What enters is not just a wound but the collapse of the structure that made sense of things -- and the struggle to build a new one.

## Direction

From a shattered assumptive world, through the struggle to rebuild, toward a self transformed beyond its prior baseline.
The rebuilding runs through the four atoms: the [meaning](@chbrain/khai-engine-meaning/position_searching.md) engine's search for and reconstruction of meaning, the [narrative](@chbrain/khai-engine-narrative/position_redemptive.md) engine's revision of the life story into one that can hold the trauma, the [mortality](@chbrain/khai-engine-mortality/position_acceptance.md) engine's confrontation with death and the existential, and the [identity](@chbrain/khai-engine-identity/position_moratorium.md) engine's remaking of the self. Tedeschi and Calhoun's finding is that the growth comes through this struggle, not despite it: the distress is the engine, the effortful, deliberate processing of a broken world the very work that builds the new one. The direction is up past the old baseline, not back to it.

## Lever

Whether the shattering is deep enough to force a rebuild, and the persona does the deliberate work of rebuilding. Growth turns on two things: that the event genuinely broke the assumptive world -- a lesser blow the old frame absorbs yields resilience, not growth -- and that the persona, after the first intrusive processing, turns to the effortful, reflective work of making new meaning, revising the story, and remaking the self. Growth is not automatic and not universal; it is wrought. The lever is the struggle carried through to reconstruction, and it can coexist with real ongoing distress -- the growth and the wound are not opposites. Stage a world truly broken and a persona truly rebuilding, because that labour is the whole of the growth.

## Echo

The transformation takes two movements, grouping Tedeschi and Calhoun's domains: [strength](position_growth_strength.md), the self transformed -- personal strength and new possibilities, the remade, more-capable self; and [wisdom](position_growth_wisdom.md), the philosophy of life transformed -- a deepened appreciation of life, existential and spiritual change, and a richer valuing of others. When the assumptive world is rebuilt and the self remade, the atoms resume on new ground. The reconstruction of meaning is the meaning engine's; the revised story is the narrative engine's; the confrontation with mortality is the mortality engine's; the remade self is the identity engine's. The maintenance of stable functioning across the event -- coming through unbroken rather than remade -- is the resilience composite's, the outcome this one is defined against; and the growth this composite reads can run alongside a post-traumatic distress it does not cancel. Post-traumatic growth is a self transformed beyond its baseline, not a self merely held together across the storm.
