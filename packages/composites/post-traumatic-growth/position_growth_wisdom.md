# Position: Post-Traumatic Growth, Wisdom

## Taxonomy

[Post-Traumatic Growth](process_post_traumatic_growth.md), the movement where the philosophy of life is transformed -- appreciation and existential depth.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: post-traumatic-growth

## Has

The world-directed movement of growth: a persona whose sense of what matters is deepened by having faced the worst. Tedeschi and Calhoun's remaining domains cluster here -- a heightened appreciation of life and the small things in it, existential and spiritual change, and a richer valuing of others and of relationship. The [mortality](@chbrain/khai-engine-mortality/position_acceptance.md) engine's confrontation with death, accepted rather than denied, turns the persona toward what is precious, and the [meaning](@chbrain/khai-engine-meaning/position_renewed.md) engine's reconstructed meaning gives that turn its content: a philosophy of life remade around what the ordeal revealed to matter.

## Orders

Live by what the brush with the worst revealed to be precious. The wisdom movement orders the persona to hold its deepened sense of value -- to savour a life it no longer takes for granted, to invest in the relationships and meanings that survived the test, and to carry the existential seriousness of one who has looked at mortality and not looked away. It is growth as depth: not a wider sense of what the persona can do but a truer sense of what is worth doing, and a compassion for others enlarged by its own suffering.

## Loses

The comfort of the unexamined life, and the lightness of never having had to choose what matters. The wisdom is bought at the cost of the illusions the trauma stripped -- the persona forgoes the easy assumption that the world is safe and time unlimited, and carries instead a knowledge that can weigh as much as it lifts. It risks, too, a claimed serenity that masks unresolved grief, or an existential depth performed rather than lived. What it trades is innocence for insight: it sees more truly, and cannot unsee.

## Drives

On that world-transforming movement:
rides it -- savours the life it nearly lost, invests in what matters, and carries its existential depth and enlarged compassion -- or, paired with the [strength](position_growth_strength.md) movement, joins a changed sense of what is worth doing to a changed sense of what it can do. The wisdom baseline holds where the growth is world-directed, the remade philosophy of life rather than, like the strength movement, the remade self. That growth comes through the struggle and beyond the old baseline is the [root's](process_post_traumatic_growth.md); the confrontation with mortality it rests on is the mortality engine's, and the mere return of pleasure to a setpoint, without any remaking of values, is the hedonic-adaptation engine's, not this growth.
