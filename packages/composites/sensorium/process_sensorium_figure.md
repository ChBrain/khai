# Process: Sensorium, the Figure

## Taxonomy

[Sensorium](process_sensorium.md), the shape where something rises from the field and is registered.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: sensorium

## Initiated by

Something crosses the constant field and the body notices it, or something recurs until it becomes the place's own signature.
The Figure starts wherever a sense rises out of its ground -- the drifting [plume](@chbrain/khai-engine-smellscape/process_plume.md) of a foreground smell, the foreground [signal](@chbrain/khai-engine-soundscape/process_signal.md) that must be attended to, the give and resistance of a [press](@chbrain/khai-engine-touch/process_press.md) the body meets -- and wherever one rises and stays, binding the place to memory: the signature [redolence](@chbrain/khai-engine-smellscape/process_redolence.md) and the place's own [soundmark](@chbrain/khai-engine-soundscape/process_soundmark.md).
It is the event against the field: the part of the sensory place that can actually be reported.

## Direction

Toward what the body picks out, and what afterward stands for the place.
The Figure reads the rising -- bread carried on the air, a bell across a valley, the sudden yield of a floorboard -- and the recurring, which does more: the smell that will return a persona to this place decades later, the sound no other place makes, the signature that becomes the place's name in the body.
It moves the place from a field to a thing that can be recalled: an event noticed now, or a mark that will identify this place against every other.

## Lever

The figure that can be noticed against the ground that gave it its meaning.
The carrying force is contrast -- a smell, sound, or pressure registers by differing from the field it arrives in, so the same plume in another ground would not be the same event at all, and the figure is always partly a fact about its background; the resistance is that only figures get reported, so a place recorded by its figures alone loses the ground that made them mean anything, and reads as a list of incidents rather than a surround.
The Figure is the sensorium at its most retrievable: the part a persona can name, and which owes its force to the part it cannot.

## Echo

The event picked out, and the signature that will bring the place back.
The Figure leaves the place marked in the body -- a smell recalled, a sound belonging to nowhere else, a surface the hand remembers -- and carries the sensorium's shared shape: a field, and something rising from it.
It is the reportable sense: what the body noticed, and what will stand for the place afterward.
The Figure is dated from the rising, the moment something crossed the field and the body marked it.
