# Process: Sensorium, the Ground

## Taxonomy

[Sensorium](process_sensorium.md), the shape where the constant field works below notice.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: sensorium

## Initiated by

The place holds a steady field in every non-visual sense, and the body settles into it without registering that it has.
The Ground starts wherever that constant layer is present -- the base [ground](@chbrain/khai-engine-smellscape/process_ground.md) smell a place sits in, the [keynote](@chbrain/khai-engine-soundscape/process_keynote.md) sound heard beneath everything else, and the [texture](@chbrain/khai-engine-touch/process_texture.md) the skin reads and the [thermal](@chbrain/khai-engine-touch/process_thermal.md) warmth or chill the place holds.
It is the field before the event: not what happens in a place but what the place is, continuously, to a body inside it.

## Direction

Toward a body already tuned to a field it never noticed arriving.
The Ground reads the constant working -- the smell that is simply what this building smells like, the hum a room is heard against, the cold of the floor and the grain of the rail -- each unremarked precisely because it does not change, and each setting the terms on which everything else is met.
It moves the body from outside to inside: acclimatised within moments, no longer able to detect what it has adapted to, and reading every later smell, sound, and pressure against a baseline it cannot now perceive.

## Lever

The constancy that makes the field work against the constancy that makes it invisible.
The carrying force is that an unchanging field is a field the body stops attending to and never stops being affected by -- it does not compete for notice, so it colours the whole of a place's feel unopposed; the resistance is that it therefore cannot be reported: ask a persona what a place smelled or sounded like and it will name the events, not the ground, having adapted to the very layer that did the most.
The Ground is the sensorium at its most pervasive and least visible: the part of a place that is always working and never remembered.

## Echo

The body settled into a field it has already stopped noticing.
The Ground leaves the place established in the body -- acclimatised, baselined, unremarked -- and hands that baseline to the figure that will rise against it and the absence that will expose it.
It is the field before the event: what a place is, continuously, to whoever is inside it.
The Ground is dated from the settling, the moment the body adapted to the field and stopped being able to name it.
