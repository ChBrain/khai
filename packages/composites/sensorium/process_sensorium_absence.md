# Process: Sensorium, the Absence

## Taxonomy

[Sensorium](process_sensorium.md), the shape where a sense is withheld and the withholding is what registers.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: sensorium

## Initiated by

The place gives the body nothing in one of its senses, and the nothing is the strongest thing in the room.
The Absence starts wherever a field is withheld -- the [scentless](@chbrain/khai-engine-smellscape/process_scentless.md) room whose lack of smell is read as a presence, the [silence](@chbrain/khai-engine-soundscape/process_silence.md) that is heard rather than merely unfilled, and the [untouchable](@chbrain/khai-engine-touch/process_untouchable.md) surface whose withheld contact organises the whole body around it.
It is the field subtracted: not a sense at rest but a sense taken away, and read as an act.

## Direction

Toward a body reading the missing field as a message.
The Absence reads the withholding at work -- the scentless corridor that says something has been scrubbed, the room that falls silent and thereby speaks, the thing that must not be touched and so becomes the only thing the hand is aware of -- each an emptiness the body treats as deliberate, and attends to harder than it would attend to anything present.
It moves the place from giving to withholding: and the body, finding nothing where a field should be, does not relax but searches, and the searching is itself the experience of the place.

## Lever

The nothing that ought to register as nothing against the body that reads it as something.
The carrying force is that the senses are built for a world that always supplies something -- there is normally a smell, a sound, a surface -- so an absence is not a neutral zero but a violated expectation, and the body treats a violated expectation as information; the resistance is only the assumption that what is not there cannot act, which every scentless room, every silence, and every forbidden surface disproves at once.
The Absence rides the composite's keystone knife-edge: a place is felt hardest where a sense has been taken away -- the withheld field registers more strongly than any field supplied, so the strongest thing a place can do to a body is give it nothing.

## Echo

The missing sense, read as the loudest thing present.
It shows a place withholding -- scentless, silent, untouchable -- and carries beneath it the composite's keystone: that an absence in a sensory field is not the field's zero but its most forceful state, so the emptiest place is not the least felt but often the most.
The Absence is the note the whole composite closes on: a place reaches the body through what it gives and, hardest of all, through what it refuses.
The Absence is dated from the withholding, the moment the body reached for a field and found it taken away.
