# Process: Interval, the Vacancy

## Taxonomy

[Interval](process_interval.md), the hour with no claimant.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: interval

## Initiated by

Nobody owns the time and nobody owns the room.
The Vacancy is the two arriving together: [a hollow hour](@chbrain/khai-engine-commute/process_the_hollow_hour.md) that is not paid, not chosen, and not rest; and [transit](@chbrain/khai-engine-non-place/place_transit.md), a route that cannot be arrived at.

## Direction

Toward a stretch of a life that no institution has a claim on.
The employer does not own it, because it is unpaid. The household does not own it, because nobody in it is present. The persona does not quite own it either, because it was not chosen and cannot be moved.
And the room is arranged to match: it verified the persona at the barrier, has no concept of a returning visitor, and will not have registered that they were in it.

## Lever

The rarity of unclaimed time against the fact that it was never granted to anybody.
The carrying force is that unclaimed time is genuinely scarce and its scarcity is not an illusion: an ordinary day is almost entirely spoken for by work, household, care, and obligation, and a stretch in which no one on earth is entitled to the persona's attention is a real and unusual condition, whatever produced it; the resistance is that it exists because of a gap between two places rather than because anybody decided a person should have an hour, so what looks like freedom is a residue -- and a residue can be removed by a timetable change, an office move, or a policy about working from home.
The Vacancy is unowned because it was left over.

## Echo

An hour with no claimant, produced by an arrangement nobody made for that purpose.
It is where the two atoms touch: a duration that belongs to nobody, inside a space that holds nothing.
The Vacancy is dated to the departure and is repeated twice a day.
