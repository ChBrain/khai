# Interval

Interval as composite: the daily stretch that belongs to no part of a persona's life. It is not a third mechanism but a wiring of two -- the commute engine supplies a cost agreed once and incurred every morning, including an hour that is not paid, not chosen, and not rest, and the non-place engine supplies a space entered by contract that verifies a persona once and has no further use for them. This composite adds no third engine; it reads the one thing the two make together, which is a stretch of time nobody owns, in a room that holds nothing, recurring twice a day for years. Three bridges: the Vacancy, unowned because it was left over rather than granted, so what looks like freedom is a residue a timetable change can remove; the Furnishing, entirely brought by its occupant and credited to the place it was brought to, since every item of it is portable and none of it argues for the room; and the Defence, the twist -- the hour a persona never chose becomes the only one nobody can claim, and defending it is what keeps them making the journey.

This engine is defined by its [manifest](package.json), which the canon renders as the WIRES card. The manifest is the single source of truth; this README is generated, not edited by hand.
