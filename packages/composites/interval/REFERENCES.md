# Interval: Reference

## Line of Work

Interval as **composite**: the daily stretch that belongs to no part of a persona's life. It is not a
third mechanism but a wiring of two: the **commute** engine supplies a cost agreed once and incurred
every morning, including an hour that is not paid, not chosen, and not rest, and the **non-place**
engine supplies a space entered by contract that verifies a persona once and has no further use for
them. This composite adds no third engine. It reads the one thing the two make together, which is a
stretch of time nobody owns, in a room that holds nothing, recurring twice a day for years.

The warrant is accordingly integrative rather than primary: the sources below are the mobilities
literature on what travel time actually contains, where each atom's own REFERENCES carries the primary
work of its side. **Lyons and Urry are the joining.** Their argument is not about commuting's costs and
not separately about the character of transit space; it is that travel time has been misdescribed by
everybody who treats it as a pure cost to be minimised, because travellers use it -- they work, read,
sleep, talk, plan, and above all establish routines inside it -- and a substantial proportion report the
time as usefully spent. That is a claim about the two engines together. Commute supplies a duration
nobody wanted, non-place supplies a room that contributes nothing to it, and the mobilities finding is
that the hour is nevertheless full.

Both atoms are wired here for the first time, and an engine can be in 0..n composites: an atom carries
a phenomenon, and a composite carries a question asked of it. The pairing is structural rather than
convenient, because the two engines supply the same hour from opposite ends -- one a duration with no
owner, the other a room with no memory.

**The Vacancy** is unowned because it was left over. The employer does not own it, since it is unpaid;
the household does not, since nobody in it is present; and the persona does not quite own it either,
since it was never chosen and cannot be moved. The room is arranged to match, having verified them at
the barrier and having no concept of a returning visitor.

**The Furnishing** is entirely brought. The space supplies a seat and nothing else, and the persona
carries in the podcast, the book, the thinking, the habit of considering one thing on the way in and
another on the way back. All of it is real, much of it is excellent, and every item of it is portable.

**The Defence** is true. A persona offered the removal of their journey will often say they would miss
it, and they are not rationalising: the reading is real reading, the transition between two roles has to
happen somewhere, and anybody who has worked from a bedroom for a year can confirm that its abolition
is not straightforwardly a gain.

Hence the **twist**. **The hour a persona never chose becomes the only one nobody can claim, and
defending it is what keeps them making the journey.** The defence converts an unchosen residue into a
benefit of the arrangement, and once it is a benefit it lands on the same side of the ledger as the
extra room -- except that unlike the extra room, it is still being felt. So a genuine good, correctly
identified, becomes the last argument standing for a trade whose other side went quiet years ago.

The composite is built so that nobody has to be deceiving themselves for it to run, and a play should
keep it that way. The persona is right about what they would lose. The alternative really is worse in
the way they say. Nobody is fooling anybody.

**On the evidence, stated plainly.** A composite cannot be firmer than its atoms, and these two were
built with their weaknesses on the page: commute's REFERENCES records that its central paradox is panel
evidence rather than experimental and that mode is an enormous moderator it flattens, and non-place's
records that its category is a theoretical proposal whose strongest criticism is probably right.
Everything here inherits both. **The travel-time-use finding is the firmest thing in this composite.**
That travellers use the time, and report a meaningful fraction of it as worthwhile, comes from large
national travel surveys as well as from qualitative work, and it is the one claim here with real
measurement under it. **What that finding supports is narrower than what this composite does with it.**
It establishes that the hour is used; it does not establish that the hour is defended, that the defence
is load-bearing for the arrangement, or that removing the journey produces the specific loss the third
bridge describes. Those are the composite's own reasoning and should be read as such. **The two atoms
sit in tension and the composite does not resolve it.** Commute's evidence says long journeys track
lower reported well-being; the mobilities evidence says the time inside them is valued. Both can be true
-- a valued hour inside a costly arrangement -- and this composite asserts that combination rather than
demonstrating it, and a reader should know that the field itself has not settled how the two results fit
together. **The composite is written from the same narrow setting as its atoms**: salaried personas with
seats, in rich-country transit systems, who have a choice about where they live. A standing hour on an
unreliable bus, or a journey that is itself unsafe, has a vacancy and no furnishing at all, and the
engine says nothing useful about it. What should not be taken from this composite is that people who
enjoy their commutes are deluded, that the enjoyment is a coping mechanism, or that the honest response
to the defence is to explain it away. The claim is narrow: the hour is unowned because it is residual,
the furnishing is portable, and the defence is accurate and does work its speaker did not intend.

## Origin

The mobilities literature on what travel time contains. Each atom's REFERENCES carries the primary work
of its own side; these are the sources for the joining.

| Source                              | Key Work                                                                          | Scope                                                                                                                                                                    |
| :---------------------------------- | :-------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | :----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Glenn Lyons & John Urry**         | _"Travel Time Use in the Information Age"_ (Transportation Research Part A, 2005) | **The joining.** Travel time is not a pure cost: travellers use it, routinise it, and report a meaningful share of it as worthwhile. The root and the Furnishing bridge. |
| **John Urry**                       | _Mobilities_ (2007)                                                               | Movement as a domain of social life rather than a gap between locations. Used here, also cited by tourism for _The Tourist Gaze_.                                        |
| **Juliet Jain & Glenn Lyons**       | _"The Gift of Travel Time"_ (Journal of Transport Geography, 2008)                | Travel time treated by travellers as a gift: transition, retreat, and time out from other obligations. The Vacancy and the Defence bridges.                              |
| **Glenn Lyons, Juliet Jain et al.** | National travel survey work on what passengers actually do in transit (from 2007) | The distribution of activities across a journey, and what proportion of travellers rate the time as usefully spent. The Furnishing bridge's measured ground.             |

## Restrictions

What the composite refuses to model, and to whom it delegates.

- **The trade and the adaptation (commute)**: The calculation made once, the buffer paid every morning,
  the absorbed house, and the ledger that cannot be audited are the commute engine's own movements. This
  composite links them and reads only the hour itself.
- **The room (non-place)**: The contractual entry, the crowd in which nobody is anybody, and the space
  that holds nothing on behalf of a returning visitor are the non-place engine's. This composite reads
  what the persona does inside it.
- **The return to a baseline (hedonic-adaptation, lasting, savoring)**: How feeling returns toward a set
  point, and what attending to a pleasure does, are those packages'. The defence bridge reads a good
  that is still being felt, and does not model why.
- **Attention restored (restorative-environment, restoration, coping)**: What refills depleted directed
  attention and what a persona does about sustained demand are those engines'. The rest described here
  is relief from obligation rather than recovery of capacity.
- **How the time feels (time-perception, boredom, mind-wandering, flow)**: The stretch and compression
  of a duration, what an unoccupied mind does, and absorption in an activity are those engines'. The
  furnishing bridge reads what is carried in, not what it is like.
- **The job and the household at either end (employment, dwelling, family-system)**: The effort bargain,
  what a home comes to mean, and how a household distributes its work are those packages'. The composite
  begins at the door and ends at the door.
- **The infrastructure (the world)**: Timetables, fares, seat availability, network reliability, and
  transport policy are matters of trade and statute. They set the format; this composite reads the hour.
- **An hour as authored symbol (the play's pitch)**: A journey staged in order to **stand for** a theme
  -- the train that mirrors a motif of suspension -- is refused: that is authored key, the play's pitch,
  not the force.

## Encoding

Source to constraint. A composite tree: one root and three bridges over two atoms.

- **[interval](process_interval.md)** (the root): a stretch of time nobody owns in a room that holds
  nothing, read on what the hour is for, carrying the twist -- the hour a persona never chose becomes the
  only one nobody can claim, and defending it is what keeps them making the journey. Anchored by Lyons &
  Urry (2005).
- **[the Vacancy](process_interval_vacancy.md)**: the hour with no claimant, unowned because it was left
  over. Anchored by Jain & Lyons (2008), linking commute's hollow hour and non-place's transit.
- **[the Furnishing](process_interval_furnishing.md)**: what the persona puts into it, entirely brought
  and entirely portable. Anchored by Lyons & Urry (2005), linking non-place's solitude.
- **[the Defence](process_interval_defence.md)**: what happens when somebody offers to take it away,
  where the composite is seen for one thing. Anchored by Jain & Lyons (2008), linking commute's ledger.
