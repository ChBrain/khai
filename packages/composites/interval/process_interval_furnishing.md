# Process: Interval, the Furnishing

## Taxonomy

[Interval](process_interval.md), what the persona puts into it.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: interval

## Initiated by

The persona brings something to do.
Headphones, a series of podcasts that exist nowhere else in their week, a book read only here, a game, a window, a habit of thinking about one thing on the way in and a different thing on the way back.

## Direction

Toward an hour furnished entirely by its occupant.
The space supplies nothing: [solitude](@chbrain/khai-engine-non-place/place_solitude.md) is proximity without contact, the staff rotate, the crowd is replaced hourly, and nothing that happens leaves a mark on the room. So everything in the hour is what the persona carried in.
And they do carry it in, elaborately. Travel time turns out on measurement not to be empty at all: people work, read, talk, sleep, plan, and above all arrange the hour into a routine, and a substantial proportion describe it as usefully spent.

## Lever

The reality of what gets made in the hour against the fact that the room contributed none of it.
The carrying force is that the furnishing is real and often excellent: the reading done on trains is real reading, the transition between two roles genuinely needs somewhere to happen, and the transport economics that treats travel time as pure cost is measurably wrong about what people do with it; the resistance is that every item of it is portable -- the podcast, the book, the thinking -- so nothing in the furnishing argues for the room, and a persona who values the hour is valuing a set of practices that would survive being moved to a chair at home.
The Furnishing is entirely brought and is credited to the place it was brought to.

## Echo

An hour made habitable by its occupant, in a room that supplied a seat.
It shows why the arrangement is defended by the people inside it and why the defence proves less than it appears to.
The Furnishing is dated to the first week and hardens into a routine within a month.
