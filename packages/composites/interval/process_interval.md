# Process: Interval

## Taxonomy

The daily stretch that belongs to no part of a persona's life.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: interval

## Initiated by

A persona spends an hour a day somewhere that is not home, not work, and not chosen.
Interval starts wherever both are in play: a cost agreed once and incurred every morning, through [commute](@chbrain/khai-engine-commute/process_commute.md); and a space built for passing through, entered by contract and asking nothing of anybody inside it, through [non-place](@chbrain/khai-engine-non-place/place_non_place.md).
This composite adds no third engine. It reads the one thing the two make together, which is a stretch of time that nobody owns, in a place that holds nothing, recurring twice a day for years.

## Direction

Toward an hour that is emptied by design and is then inhabited.
The composite wires the two and reads their working in three shapes: [the Vacancy](process_interval_vacancy.md), the hour with no claimant; [the Furnishing](process_interval_furnishing.md), what the persona puts into it; and [the Defence](process_interval_defence.md), the twist -- what happens when somebody offers to take it away.
The three are not a sequence of events. They are one arrangement, and a question about what an unclaimed hour becomes.

## Lever

The genuine value of an unowned hour against the fact that nobody would have bought one at this price.
The carrying force is that the time is really worth something and the people in it are not deceived: a transition between two roles has to happen somewhere, the space asks nothing and nobody in it wants anything, the podcast and the book and the staring are real uses, and a persona whose journey vanished overnight often finds they have lost something they cannot name; the resistance is that the hour was never on offer as an hour -- it arrived attached to a distance, a fare, and a permanent arrangement, and no persona in the history of the practice has chosen it on its own terms.
Interval is read on what the hour is for, never on what it costs.

## Echo

Somebody with headphones on, in a place that will not remember them, doing the only thing all day that nobody asked for.
Interval does not close; it is an unclaimed hour being furnished, gathered into one reading -- a vacancy, a furnishing, a defence.
The twist is here: **the hour a persona never chose becomes the only one nobody can claim, and defending it is what keeps them making the journey.** The two engines supply the same hour from opposite ends. Commute supplies a duration that is not paid, not leisure, and not home; non-place supplies a room that verifies a persona once and then has no further use for them. Put together they make the only stretch of an ordinary day in which nothing is owed to anybody -- which the persona correctly identifies as valuable, and then defends, and the defence is an argument for the arrangement that produced it.
It hands off what it is not: the trade struck once and the adaptation that erases one side of it are commute's; the contractual entry and the crowd in which nobody is anybody are non-place's; the return of feeling to a baseline is hedonic-adaptation's; what refills depleted attention is restorative-environment's; the stretch of a duration and what an unoccupied mind does are time-perception's, boredom's, and mind-wandering's; and an hour staged to **stand for** a theme is authored key, the play's pitch, not the force.
The persona is dated from the last time somebody offered to shorten it.
