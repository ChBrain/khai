# Process: Befalling, the Strain

## Taxonomy

[Befalling](process_befalling.md), the case the belief cannot absorb.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: befalling

## Initiated by

The misfortune in front of the observer was not earned, and cannot be put right.
The Strain starts wherever [belief in a just world](@chbrain/khai-engine-belief-in-a-just-world/position_just_world_belief.md) is holding under a case that contradicts it -- pressing on the [general](@chbrain/khai-engine-belief-in-a-just-world/position_general.md) conviction that the world at large gives people what they have coming, and on the [personal](@chbrain/khai-engine-belief-in-a-just-world/position_personal.md) one that it will do so for the observer.
It is the face of the composite where nothing is yet being done to anybody.

## Direction

Toward a contradiction the observer has to live inside or resolve.
The Strain reads what the belief is for rather than what it is wrong about -- and it is for a great deal: a persona who holds that outcomes broadly track deserving will invest in long projects, keep to standards nobody is checking, and trust arrangements they cannot verify, all of which require the future to be worth working toward.
Then it reads the case that will not fit: not a large misfortune, which the belief absorbs easily by finding a cause, but an **undeserved** one that stays undeserved, in front of somebody who cannot compensate it.
It moves the observer nowhere, and makes standing still expensive.

## Lever

What the belief buys the observer against what an unearned suffering costs it.
The carrying force is that abandoning the belief is not free and is not obviously right: it is the ground the observer's own effort stands on, and a persona who concludes that outcomes are arbitrary has lost the reason to keep to anything -- so the pressure to preserve it is not vanity, it is the defence of a working assumption; the resistance is the case itself, which is not an argument to be answered but a person standing there, and which presses harder the plainer the innocence, since a sufferer who might have brought it on themselves would have relieved the contradiction on their own.
The Strain spends the observer's composure, and will be relieved by whatever is available.

## Echo

A contradiction, and an observer who needs it to stop.
It is the bridge that supplies the force the whole composite runs on, and locates it correctly: the pressure is not toward cruelty, it is toward resolution, and cruelty is only what resolution costs when the other routes are shut.
What it hands upward is the condition that decides which route is taken -- because there is a route that harms nobody, and it requires the observer to be able to do something.
The Strain is dated from the moment the observer understood that it was undeserved.
