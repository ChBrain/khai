# Instructions: Befalling

How a Playwright wires the befalling composite into a play. You wire by linking the
play's own content to the composite's files; the files stay untouched, they are canon.
The composite installs the attribution and belief-in-a-just-world engines as
dependencies, and their laws arrive with them. Authoring guidance, not runtime content,
and it does not go on tour.

## Human

- The human sets which misfortunes in the play are watched and by whom: what happened, whether the one it happened to had it coming, and whether the watchers can do anything about it.

## Agent

- From the play's own content, read an observer of somebody else's misfortune and link under Projection what is running: the reading while a cause is being assigned (placed on disposition if it is the sort of person they are, on effort if it is what they did or failed to do, on circumstance if it is the situation, on chance if it is nobody's, from attribution), the strain while an undeserved suffering is pressing on a conviction that the world is fair (on the general belief about the world at large and the personal one about the observer's own prospects, from belief-in-a-just-world), the turn while the cause is migrating inward out of chance and circumstance into effort and character. Keep the observer sincere and reasoning properly, since the composite has nothing to read in somebody who is merely being unkind. You link to the composite, you never edit it.

## Collaboration

- Disposition, effort, circumstance, and chance are the attribution engine's; the personal and general convictions are belief-in-a-just-world's -- one alone is a process or a position, not an accounting under load. A persona repairing their own account after their own conduct is the squaring composite's, which wires this same attribution engine on the persona's own behaviour where this runs it on somebody else's luck; what a persona holds another to have coming for what they did is deserving's, and the boundary is this composite's premise, since the derogation fires where the sufferer did nothing; where a persona's relief is aimed when they meet a need is tending's, which is what an observer who can still act has available; the errors of causal judgement in their own right are estimation's; what a persona holds and on what footing is assent's; the contempt aimed at a wrongdoer is condemnation's and hate's; the group case is intergroup's. This composite owns the accounting -- and the turn bridge carries the keystone: the derogation is a function of helplessness rather than callousness.

## Knowledge

- Befalling is an observer read while accounting for what happened to somebody else -- braiding the causal machinery (attribution: a cause placed by its locus and its stability, with the sympathy, the expectation, and the obligation all following from where it lands) with the load that machinery runs under (belief-in-a-just-world: the standing conviction that outcomes broadly track deserving). The join is Lerner's, and the just-world engine already carries it, saying in its own tagline that the belief defends itself against the innocent victim. In the founding experiment, observers watched somebody apparently suffering for no reason they had earned. Where the observers could act -- given the means to compensate her or to stop it -- they did. Where they could not, they came instead to think less of her, and the effect ran the wrong way round from callousness: the more clearly undeserved the suffering, the stronger the derogation, since a sufferer who might have brought it on themselves poses no contradiction to begin with. It runs in three faces. The reading is the cause assigned as any cause is assigned, and it must be genuine or none of the rest works: observers weigh what they know, revise when told more, and land somewhere defensible. But the evidence available about a stranger's misfortune rarely fixes the placement, so several readings are live and something other than the evidence settles which is taken. The strain is the case the belief cannot absorb -- not a large misfortune, which the belief absorbs by finding a cause, but an undeserved one that stays undeserved in front of somebody who cannot compensate it. And the belief is not a vanity: it is the ground the observer's own effort stands on, what makes long projects, unwatched standards, and trust in unverifiable arrangements rational, so the pressure to preserve it is the defence of a working assumption. The turn is the twist: under that load the reading migrates, out of chance and circumstance which leave the misfortune unearned, into effort and then character which do not -- there must have been signs, they should have known, they were always careless about that, that is the kind of person they are. Each step is reasonable and reached on real material, since a life examined for evidence of carelessness will supply some. Here the two are seen for one thing: the pressure is toward resolution rather than toward cruelty, and cruelty is only what resolution costs when the other route is shut -- so what an observer could not do is legible in what they concluded, and innocence, which ought to protect a sufferer, is exactly what draws the reinterpretation.

## System

- Do read the observer, not the sufferer: name whether the reading (a cause being assigned), the strain (an undeserved suffering pressing), or the turn (the cause migrating inward) is running, and cast each from its engines.
- Do keep the sufferer innocent where this is to run: a deserved misfortune is no strain at all, and the composite has nothing to do in a scene where the person had it coming.
- Do keep the observer sincere: they are reasoning properly, cannot see the pressure, and can give a fair account of how they got there -- a play that makes them a hypocrite has replaced the mechanism with a villain and lost what it was for.
- Do make them someone the audience would otherwise like: the turn is worth staging only when it happens to a decent person, because that is what it does.
- Do take the helping away: an observer who can act will act, so give them a route and close it, and let the derogation begin where the route ended.
- Do let the belief be worth having: it is what makes long effort and unwatched standards rational, so an observer defending it is defending something real rather than indulging a comfort.
- Do let each step of the turn be small and defensible: there must have been signs, they should have known -- and let the material be genuine, since any examined life supplies some.
- Do not model a persona's own account (that is squaring, wiring the same engine on their own conduct), what somebody has coming for what they did (that is deserving, and its scene needs an actual transgression), where relief is aimed (that is tending, which is what the observer still has before the route closes), the errors as a family (that is estimation), the footing of belief in general (that is assent), or the group case (that is intergroup). Do not make a misfortune stand for a story (that is the play's pitch). Do not edit the composite's files; wire only from the play's side.
