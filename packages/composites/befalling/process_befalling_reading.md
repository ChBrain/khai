# Process: Befalling, the Reading

## Taxonomy

[Befalling](process_befalling.md), the cause assigned, as any cause is assigned.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: befalling

## Initiated by

An outcome is in front of somebody, and they want to know what produced it.
The Reading starts wherever [attribution](@chbrain/khai-engine-attribution/process_attribution.md) is running -- the cause placed by its locus and its stability, landing on [disposition](@chbrain/khai-engine-attribution/process_disposition.md) if it is taken to be the sort of person they are, on [effort](@chbrain/khai-engine-attribution/process_effort.md) if it is what they did or failed to do, on [circumstance](@chbrain/khai-engine-attribution/process_circumstance.md) if it is the situation they were in, or on [chance](@chbrain/khai-engine-attribution/process_chance.md) if it is nobody's and nothing's.
It is the face of the composite that is simply somebody thinking.

## Direction

Toward a cause, and toward everything that follows from where it sits.
The Reading reads the placement rather than the verdict -- inside the person or outside them, fixed or passing -- and reads what the placement then decides, since these are not four labels for one conclusion: a cause read as disposition says the outcome will recur and the person is the reason, a cause read as chance says nothing about them or about next time, and the sympathy, the expectation, and the obligation all move with the placement.
It moves the observer to an account, and the account to consequences.

## Lever

The competence of ordinary causal reasoning against the range of placements the same evidence permits.
The carrying force is that this is a working faculty and mostly a good one: observers weigh what they know, revise when told more, and land somewhere defensible -- and none of this composite works unless the reading is genuine, since a reasoner who was merely deciding what to think would not need the rest of the machinery; the resistance is that the evidence available about a stranger's misfortune rarely fixes the placement, so several readings are live at once and something other than the evidence will settle which one is taken.
The Reading spends nothing, and leaves a slot that has to be filled by something.

## Echo

A cause assigned, defensibly, out of several that would have done.
It is the bridge that establishes the composite's honesty: nothing here is bad faith, the apparatus is the same one used to work out why a bus is late, and the observer is not aware of choosing.
What it hands upward is the slack the next bridge tightens -- that the evidence underdetermines the placement, so a pressure applied to the reasoner will move the reading without ever presenting itself as a pressure.
The Reading is dated from the outcome, and from whatever the observer happened to know about it.
