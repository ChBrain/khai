# Process: Befalling, the Turn

## Taxonomy

[Befalling](process_befalling.md), the cause moving inward, and where the two are seen for one thing.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: befalling

## Initiated by

The contradiction has to go, and the only thing the observer can change is the reading.
The Turn starts wherever the [attribution](@chbrain/khai-engine-attribution/process_attribution.md) begins to travel under the load -- out of [chance](@chbrain/khai-engine-attribution/process_chance.md) and [circumstance](@chbrain/khai-engine-attribution/process_circumstance.md), which leave the misfortune unearned, and into [effort](@chbrain/khai-engine-attribution/process_effort.md) and then [disposition](@chbrain/khai-engine-attribution/process_disposition.md), which do not.
It is the face of the composite where somebody is harmed by an act of thinking.

## Direction

Toward a version of events in which the world is still fair.
The Turn reads the migration and its stages -- there must have been signs; they should have known; they were always careless about that sort of thing; that is the kind of person they are -- and reads that each step is individually reasonable and reached on real material, since a life examined for evidence of carelessness will supply some.
It moves the cause into the sufferer, and the observer out of the contradiction, and it costs the observer nothing they will notice.

## Lever

The relief of a coherent world against the price paid by the person who is standing in it.
The carrying force is that the belief is restored and the observer can go on: the world gives people what they have coming, this person had something coming, and the future the observer is working toward is safe again -- which is why the turn is stable, satisfying, and very hard to argue somebody out of, since the argument is not with the reading but with what the reading is protecting; the resistance is the one thing that removes the need entirely, which is being able to put the misfortune right. An observer who can compensate compensates, and the belief is served by the fixing rather than by the blaming.
The Turn rides the composite's keystone knife-edge: the derogation is a function of helplessness rather than of callousness, and the plainer the sufferer's innocence, the harder the pressure that produces it.

## Echo

A fair world, restored at somebody's expense, by somebody who meant well.
It shows the two engines complete -- an ordinary causal reading, and a conviction that will not accept one of its outputs -- and carries beneath it the composite's keystone: innocence, which ought to protect a sufferer, is exactly what draws the reinterpretation, because a sufferer who might have deserved it poses no contradiction at all.
This is why the Turn is the hardest of the three to stage and the only one worth closing on: the observer must be sincere, must be reasoning properly, must be a person the audience would otherwise like, and must be able to give a fair account of how they got there -- and a play that makes them a hypocrite has replaced the mechanism with a villain and lost everything it was for.
The Turn is the note the whole composite closes on: what an observer could not do is legible in what they concluded, and nowhere else without a discount.
The Turn is dated from the moment helping stopped being possible.
