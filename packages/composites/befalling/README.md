# Befalling

Befalling as composite: how an observer accounts for what happened to somebody else, when the accounting has a job to do -- the cause assigned along its locus and its stability (the reading), the innocent suffering the worldview cannot absorb (the strain), and the re-reading that moves the cause inward until the one it happened to is holding it (the turn) -- wired over the attribution and belief-in-a-just-world engines.
