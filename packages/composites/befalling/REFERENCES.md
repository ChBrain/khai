# Befalling: Reference

## Line of Work

Befalling as **composite**: how an observer accounts for what happened to somebody else. It is not
a third mechanism but a wiring of two: the **attribution** engine supplies the machinery -- a cause
placed by its locus and its stability, with the emotion, the expectation, and the obligation
following from where it lands -- and the **belief-in-a-just-world** engine supplies the load that
machinery runs under. This composite adds no third engine; it stages what the just-world atom
already names when it says the belief defends itself against the innocent victim, and follows the
defence to where it puts the cost.

It is the second composite to wire the attribution engine. The squaring composite runs it on a
persona's own conduct, where the account being repaired is their own and the repairs go deeper and
show less. Here it runs on somebody else's luck, and the account being protected is not a
self-image but a picture of how the world works. Same engine, two subjects, and neither composite
owns it.

The domain does not model either side alone -- disposition, effort, circumstance, and chance are
the attribution engine's; the personal and general convictions are the just-world engine's; and one
alone is a process or a position, not an accounting under load. Nor is it what a persona holds
another to have coming for what they **did**, which is the deserving composite's; nor where a
persona's relief is aimed when they meet a need, which is tending's; nor the systematic errors of
causal judgement in their own right, which are estimation's. It owns the accounting: what an
observer concludes about a misfortune, and what the concluding was doing.

The spine is Lerner, and the just-world atom already carries him for the founding work and for the
experiment this composite is built on. Lerner and Simmons had observers watch a person apparently
receiving painful shocks for no reason they had earned. Where the observers could act -- where they
were given the means to compensate the sufferer or to end the treatment -- they did. Where they
could not, they came instead to think less of her: she was rated as less admirable, as somehow more
fitting the thing that was happening to her. And the effect went the wrong way round from
callousness: the more clearly undeserved the suffering, the stronger the derogation, because a
sufferer who might have brought it on themselves poses no contradiction to begin with.

That is the whole of the composite's claim, and it needs both atoms to state. From attribution: the
evidence available about a stranger's misfortune rarely fixes a cause, so several placements are
live at once and something other than the evidence settles which is taken. From the just-world
belief: a persona holding that outcomes broadly track deserving has one placement they cannot
arrive at, and the belief is not a vanity but a working assumption -- it is what makes long effort,
unwatched standards, and trust in unverifiable arrangements rational. So the pressure is toward
resolution rather than toward cruelty, and the derogation is what resolution costs when the other
routes are shut.

Hafer and Bègue supply the consolidation and the bounds. The experimental literature on just-world
theory is substantial and the core phenomenon has held up across several decades of paradigms, with
the belief also doing the adaptive work the theory claims -- it buffers, it sustains investment,
and it correlates with wellbeing. Dalbert's separation of the personal belief from the general one
matters here and is in the atom: a persona can hold firmly that the world is fair **to them** while
holding it much less firmly about the world at large, and the two behave differently. Weiner's
dimensional account is what the attribution engine runs on and is why the migration in the third
bridge has a direction: locus and stability are what move, and both move inward.

**On the evidence, stated plainly.** The core derogation effect is well replicated and its
moderation by the observer's ability to compensate is part of the original design rather than a
later gloss, which is what makes the keystone stateable. The scales have a long measurement
literature and the personal/general distinction is well supported. What should not be taken from
this composite is a general theory of why people blame victims: just-world reasoning is one route
and there are others -- motivated defence of a group, prior hostility, and plain ignorance among
them -- and this composite models the route where an otherwise sympathetic observer arrives at
derogation through sincere reasoning under load. The three bridges are the reading, the strain, and
the turn.

## Origin

Just-world theory and attribution research, wired as a composite over the attribution and
belief-in-a-just-world engines. The first row is the **joining** -- the published work that puts a
causal reading under the load of a worldview and measures what comes out -- and the rest anchor the
individual bridges and their bounds.

## Restrictions

What the composite refuses to model, and to whom it delegates.

- **The two sides (the atoms)**: The composite wires the two but owns neither. Disposition, effort,
  circumstance, and chance are the attribution engine's; the personal and general convictions are
  the just-world engine's. Befalling reads only what the first does under the second.
- **A persona's own account (squaring)**: The squaring composite wires this same attribution engine
  on a persona's own conduct -- the cause reassigned, the dissonance settled, the standard loosened,
  each repair deeper and less visible. This runs it on somebody else's misfortune, and what is being
  protected is a picture of the world rather than a picture of oneself. A play may run both on one
  persona, and should not confuse an observer defending their worldview with one defending their
  record.
- **What somebody has coming for what they did (deserving)**: The moral judgment, the costly
  sanction, and the pleasure at a fall are the deserving composite's, and it reads conduct. This
  composite's premise is the opposite case: the derogation fires where the sufferer **did nothing**,
  and the desert is manufactured after the fact rather than found. Where a play has an actual
  transgression, it is deserving's scene and not this one.
- **Where the relief is aimed (tending)**: Compassion and caregiving are the tending composite's.
  The two meet at the strain: an observer who can act still has the whole of tending available to
  them, and the turn is what happens to the accounting when that route is closed. A play that wants
  both should give the observer a way to help and then take it away.
- **The errors in their own right (estimation)**: Anchoring, availability, and representativeness
  are the estimation composite's. Befalling reads one specific pressure on one specific judgement,
  and does not model the biases as a family.
- **What a persona holds and on what footing (assent)**: Belief, superstition, and faith are the
  assent composite's. The just-world conviction is one belief among many and assent owns how any of
  them is held; this composite owns only what happens when this particular one meets a case it
  cannot take.
- **The emotions toward a wrongdoer (condemnation, hate)**: Contempt, anger, and disgust aimed at
  somebody who broke a code are those composites'. What the turn produces is not indignation at a
  transgression, it is a lowered estimate of a person nobody has accused of anything.
- **The group case (intergroup)**: Aggression, bias, and social identity between groups are the
  intergroup composite's. Just-world reasoning runs at group scale too, and where a play stages a
  whole class of people re-read this way, the group machinery is intergroup's and this composite
  supplies only the individual observer's step.
- **The misfortune as authored symbol (the play's pitch)**: A suffering made to **stand for** a
  theme -- the accident that mirrors a motif of judgement -- is refused: that is authored key, the
  play's pitch, not the force. Befalling sets what the watchers concluded; the meaning read into the
  event is the play's.

## Encoding

Source to constraint. A composite root over two engine atoms it declares as dependencies, with
three bridges.

- **[befalling](process_befalling.md)** (the root): how an observer accounts for what happened to
  somebody else, read on the observer and never on the sufferer. Anchored by Lerner (1980) and
  Lerner & Simmons (1966).
- **[the reading](process_befalling_reading.md)** (bridge): the cause assigned along locus and
  stability, defensibly, out of several placements the evidence would have permitted. Anchored by
  Weiner (1986) and by Heider and Jones & Nisbett on lay causal ascription and the observer's tilt.
- **[the strain](process_befalling_strain.md)** (bridge): the undeserved and uncompensated
  suffering the belief cannot absorb, pressing harder the plainer the innocence. Anchored by Hafer
  & Bègue (2005) on what the belief does and Dalbert (2001) on its two forms.
- **[the turn](process_befalling_turn.md)** (bridge): the cause migrating out of chance and
  circumstance and into effort and character, until it rests in the sufferer. Anchored by Lerner &
  Simmons (1966); the composite's twist and keystone, that the derogation is a function of
  helplessness rather than callousness, and that innocence is what draws it.
