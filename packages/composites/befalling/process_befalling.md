# Process: Befalling

## Taxonomy

How an observer accounts for what happened to somebody else.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: befalling

## Initiated by

Something bad has happened to a person, and somebody who was not involved is working out why.
Befalling starts wherever that accounting is running: as a cause assigned by its locus and its stability, through [attribution](@chbrain/khai-engine-attribution/process_attribution.md); and under the load a standing conviction puts on it, through [belief in a just world](@chbrain/khai-engine-belief-in-a-just-world/position_just_world_belief.md).
This composite adds no third engine. It stages what the just-world atom already names when it says the belief defends itself against the innocent victim, and follows the defence to where it puts the cost.

## Direction

Toward an explanation, and toward whoever ends up holding it.
The composite wires the two engines and reads their working in three shapes: [the Reading](process_befalling_reading.md), the cause assigned; [the Strain](process_befalling_strain.md), the case the belief cannot absorb; and [the Turn](process_befalling_turn.md), the twist -- the cause moving inward until it rests in the person it happened to.
The three are not three attitudes toward a victim. They are one accounting, running under a load the observer cannot feel.

## Lever

The ordinary competence of causal reasoning against the fact that one of its outputs is unbearable.
The carrying force is that the reasoning is real and usually good: an observer genuinely weighs what they know, revises on evidence, and reaches a cause the way anybody reaches one -- there is no moment at which they decide to be unfair; the resistance is that a persona who holds the world to be broadly fair has a conclusion they cannot arrive at, since an undeserved and uncompensated suffering says the world is not, and so the search proceeds until it finds something that does not say that.
Befalling is read on the observer and never on the sufferer: nothing in this composite is about what the sufferer did, which is the whole of the point.

## Echo

An explanation held sincerely, and somebody worse off for it.
Befalling does not close; it is the accounting running, gathered into one reading -- a reading, a strain, a turn.
It hands off what it is not: a persona repairing their own account after their own conduct -- the cause reassigned, the dissonance settled, the standard loosened -- is the squaring composite's, which wires this same attribution engine on the persona's own behaviour where this runs it on somebody else's luck; what a persona holds another to have coming for what they **did** is the deserving composite's, and the boundary is the composite's premise, since this fires where the sufferer did nothing; where a persona's relief is aimed when they meet another's need is the tending composite's, which is what an observer who has not turned still has available; the systematic errors of causal judgement in their own right are the estimation composite's; what a persona holds and on what footing is the assent composite's, of which this belief is one instance under load; the contempt and disgust aimed at a wrongdoer are condemnation's and hate's; what happens between groups is intergroup's; and a misfortune made to **stand for** a story is authored key, the play's pitch, not the force.
The observer is dated from the moment they could not help, and from what they concluded afterwards.
