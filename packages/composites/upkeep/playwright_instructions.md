# Instructions: Upkeep

How a Playwright wires the upkeep composite into a play. You wire by linking the play's own
content to the composite's files; the files stay untouched, they are canon. The composite
installs the commons, trust, and altruistic-punishment engines as dependencies, and their
laws arrive with them. Authoring guidance, not runtime content, and it does not go on tour.

## Human

- The human sets which shared things in the play are being drawn on, who is quietly keeping them, and what that costs.

## Agent

- From the play's own content, read a persona standing in a shared thing and link under Projection what is running: the pool while the resource and the rules its users made are in view (from commons), the read while a contribution is being set by what the persona expects the others to do (from trust), the sword while somebody is paying out of their own pocket to make a defector answer (from altruistic-punishment). Read who is paying and never whether the rule is right, since a rule with nobody paying for it is a sentence. You link to the composite, you never edit it.

## Collaboration

- The pool, the boundary, and the sanction are the commons engine's; extend, test, withhold, betrayed, and repair are trust's; second-party and third-party punishment are altruistic-punishment's -- one alone is a place or a process, not the keeping. A bargain between two parties is dealing's, which wires this same trust engine on an agreement; a bond with a rival near it is vigil's, which wires it on one relationship; what somebody has coming for what they **did** is deserving's, which wires this same altruistic-punishment engine as moral desert where this runs it as a running cost, and this composite's punisher may feel nothing in particular about the defector; who counts as us is membership's and intergroup's, and the commons boundary is about who may draw rather than who belongs; the group's own working and its safety to speak are airing's; how a thing spreads through a population is adoption's; a place's own regime is wildland's and neighborhood-cycle's. This composite owns the keeping -- and the sword bridge carries the keystone: a commons that is working looks like one that needs no keeping.

## Knowledge

- Upkeep is a persona read while standing in a shared thing that will last only if enough of them keep paying into it -- braiding the resource (commons: a pool that is subtractable and hard to exclude from, with boundaries, monitoring, and graduated sanctions its own users crafted) with the estimate (trust: a bet placed on people whose conduct cannot be controlled) and with the enforcement (altruistic punishment: a personal price paid, with no material return, to make a defector answer). The join is Ostrom, Walker and Gardner's, and their title states it -- covenants with and without a sword. Put a common-pool resource in a laboratory, let the users talk, and give them a way to sanction each other, and the resource survives; neither element alone does as much. What matters here is what the subjects did with the sanctioning option: they used it at their own expense, recovering nothing. It runs in three faces. The pool is the arrangement, and it is genuinely good -- shared things are not doomed, the tragedy is not a law of nature, and self-governed pools have outlasted the states that ignored them, so this composite starts from a success. But every clause needs a person to execute it, and the document says who may act, never who will bear the cost of acting. The read is what actually sets the contribution: most personas are neither saints nor free-riders but conditional, paying in proportion to what they expect the others to pay -- which is a decent position rather than a mean one, since a persona who keeps paying into a thing everybody else abandoned is exploited rather than virtuous. It makes the arrangement rest on an estimate, and a single visible defection does not remove one share, it revises everybody's estimate at once. The sword is the twist: the sanction is carried out by somebody who pays for it and receives nothing, since the restored contribution goes to everybody equally -- including the ones who watched and did nothing, and including the defector once they resume. And the enforcement has the same structure as the thing it protects, so free-riding on the punishing is available too and it is never anyone's turn. Here the three are seen for one thing: because the cost is private and the benefit general, a commons that is working presents as one that needs no keeping -- the sanctions look unnecessary exactly when they are doing their job, and the only people positioned to see the cost are the ones paying it.

## System

- Do read who is paying and never whether the rule is right: name whether the pool (the resource and its rules), the read (a contribution set by an expectation), or the sword (somebody paying to enforce) is running, and cast each from its engines.
- Do make the defection visible, since the read runs on what personas can see rather than on what is true, and an unseen defection does nothing to the estimate.
- Do name who pays for the enforcement and what it costs them -- time, standing, goodwill, or worse -- and let them gain nothing by it.
- Do put somebody else in the room who could have done it instead and did not, because the second-order problem is the whole of the twist and needs a witness to be visible.
- Do keep the cost invisible to everybody else: nobody thanks the punisher, the arrangement simply continues, and that is the mechanism rather than an injustice added on top.
- Do start from a success where the composite is to run: the arrangement should be working when the play opens, so the audience sees what is being spent only when it stops.
- Do not stage this as punishing being virtuous, or shared things being doomed without it: punishment aimed at cooperators occurs in real populations and destroys cooperation where it does, and the punisher is frequently worse off for it in the end.
- Do not model a two-party bargain (that is dealing), a bond with a rival (that is vigil), moral desert for a transgression (that is deserving, wiring the same engine as a verdict), who belongs (that is membership and intergroup), the group's functioning (that is airing), diffusion through a population (that is adoption), or a place's own regime (that is wildland and neighborhood-cycle). Do not make a collapse stand for a theme (that is the play's pitch). Do not edit the composite's files; wire only from the play's side.
