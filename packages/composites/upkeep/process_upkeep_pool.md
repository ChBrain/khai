# Process: Upkeep, the Pool

## Taxonomy

[Upkeep](process_upkeep.md), the shared thing and the rules its own users made for it.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: upkeep

## Initiated by

There is something several personas draw on, and one persona's drawing leaves less for the rest.
The Pool starts wherever [the commons](@chbrain/khai-engine-commons/place_commons.md) is standing -- the [pool](@chbrain/khai-engine-commons/place_pool.md) itself, subtractable and running low; the [boundary](@chbrain/khai-engine-commons/place_boundary.md) that says who is inside it and who is not; and the [sanction](@chbrain/khai-engine-commons/place_sanction.md) that says what happens to somebody who takes too much.
It is the face of the composite where the arrangement looks like a solved problem.

## Direction

Toward an institution that describes exactly what should happen.
The Pool reads what the users have built rather than what an outsider would prescribe: boundaries drawn where the people who live off the thing know they belong, monitoring done by the same people who are being monitored, sanctions that start small and escalate, and rules that can be changed by those they bind -- an arrangement crafted rather than imposed, and often centuries old.
It moves nothing on its own, which is the point of reading it first.

## Lever

The evident sensibleness of the arrangement against the fact that no part of it runs by itself.
The carrying force is that the design is genuinely good and genuinely works: shared things are not doomed, the tragedy is not a law of nature, and self-governed pools have outlasted the states that ignored them -- so the composite starts from a success and not from a failure; the resistance is that every clause needs somebody to execute it, since a boundary has to be checked by a person, a rule has to be applied by a person, and a sanction has to be imposed by a person -- and the document says who may do these things, never who will bear the cost of doing them.
The Pool spends nothing, and describes a machine with no power source named.

## Echo

An arrangement, well made, waiting on people.
It is the bridge that sets the composite's subject correctly: the interesting question about a commons is not whether its rules are wise, since they usually are, but what keeps them running when running them is expensive.
What it hands upward is a set of obligations and a gap where the motive should be, which the next two bridges fill from opposite directions.
The Pool is dated from whenever the users last agreed, and it has been quietly load-bearing since.
