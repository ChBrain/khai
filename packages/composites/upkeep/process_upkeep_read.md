# Process: Upkeep, the Read

## Taxonomy

[Upkeep](process_upkeep.md), what each persona expects the others to do.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: upkeep

## Initiated by

A persona decides what to put in, and looks sideways before deciding.
The Read starts wherever [trust](@chbrain/khai-engine-trust/process_trust.md) is being placed on the others -- [extended](@chbrain/khai-engine-trust/process_extend.md) by a persona who contributes on the expectation that the rest will, [tested](@chbrain/khai-engine-trust/process_test.md) by one who puts in a little and watches, [withheld](@chbrain/khai-engine-trust/process_withhold.md) by one who will not go first, or [betrayed](@chbrain/khai-engine-trust/process_betrayed.md) and then either [repaired](@chbrain/khai-engine-trust/process_repair.md) or not.
It is the face of the composite where nothing is enforced and everything is decided.

## Direction

Toward a contribution set by a belief about other people rather than by the rule.
The Read reads the conditionality, which is the thing outsiders get wrong about shared arrangements: most personas are neither saints nor free-riders but conditional -- they will pay their share if they believe the others are paying theirs, and will withdraw as that belief falls, so the level of contribution tracks the estimate rather than the obligation.
It moves the whole arrangement up or down together, because everybody is watching the same thing.

## Lever

The willingness to contribute against the need to not be the only one doing it.
The carrying force is that conditional cooperation is a decent position and not a mean one: a persona who keeps paying into a thing everybody else has abandoned is not virtuous but exploited, and the condition is what makes contributing rational at all -- so the arrangement is sustained by a belief that is, most of the time, correct; the resistance is that the belief is fragile in one specific way, since a single visible defection does not merely remove one share, it revises every other persona's estimate at once -- and each revision is itself visible, so the withdrawal is faster than the building and does not need anybody to have decided against the thing.
The Read spends nothing while it holds, and everything at once when it goes.

## Echo

A shared thing held up by what people think of each other.
It is the bridge that explains why the arrangement is unstable without something more, and why that something cannot be another rule: the rule was already there, and it is the estimate that moved.
What it hands upward is the requirement -- that a defection be answered visibly and soon, or the estimate will do the answering -- and the fact that the answering will cost whoever does it.
The Read is dated from the last thing anybody saw somebody else do.
