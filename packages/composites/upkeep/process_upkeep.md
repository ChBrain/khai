# Process: Upkeep

## Taxonomy

What a shared thing costs the people who keep it.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: upkeep

## Initiated by

Several personas draw on one thing, and it will last only if enough of them keep paying into it.
Upkeep starts wherever that is running: a resource anybody can take from and nobody can easily be shut out of, governed by rules its own users made, through [the commons](@chbrain/khai-engine-commons/place_commons.md); a bet placed on people whose conduct cannot be controlled, through [trust](@chbrain/khai-engine-trust/process_trust.md); and a price paid to make a defector answer, through [altruistic punishment](@chbrain/khai-engine-altruistic-punishment/process_altruistic_punishment.md).
This composite adds no fourth engine. It reads the one question the three make together, which is not whether the rules are good but who is paying to run them.

## Direction

Toward a thing that survives, and a bill nobody sees.
The composite wires the three engines and reads their working in three shapes: [the Pool](process_upkeep_pool.md), the resource and the rules around it; [the Read](process_upkeep_read.md), what each persona expects the others to do; and [the Sword](process_upkeep_sword.md), the twist -- the enforcement, and who funds it.
The three are not three views of a rule. They are a rule, the belief that holds it up, and the private cost of the belief being true.

## Lever

What the shared thing is worth to everybody against what keeping it costs somebody in particular.
The carrying force is that the thing is genuinely worth having and nobody has to be persuaded of that: the grazing, the fishery, the roster, the quiet in the room, the standard nobody has written down -- all of it is better for everyone than its absence, and personas will say so freely; the resistance is that the benefit falls on everybody and the cost falls on whoever bears it, so at every moment the rational private move is to draw and let somebody else do the keeping, and this is true for the drawing, true for the monitoring, and true again for the punishing.
Upkeep is read on who is paying and never on whether the rule is right, because a rule with nobody paying for it is a sentence.

## Echo

A thing still standing, and somebody out of pocket for it.
Upkeep does not close; it is the keeping running, gathered into one reading -- a pool, a read, a sword.
It hands off what it is not: a bargain struck between two parties is dealing's, which wires this same trust engine on an agreement where this runs it on a group; a bond with a rival near it is vigil's, which wires the same engine on one relationship; what somebody has coming for what they **did** is deserving's, which wires this same altruistic-punishment engine as moral desert where this runs it as the fee for a shared thing; who counts as us is membership's and intergroup's; the group's own working and its safety to speak are airing's; how a thing spreads through a population is adoption's; a place's own life is wildland's and neighborhood-cycle's; and a collapse made to **stand for** a theme is authored key, the play's pitch, not the force.
The persona is dated from what they took, and from what they were willing to pay to stop somebody else taking it.
