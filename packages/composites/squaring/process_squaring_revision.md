# Process: Squaring, the Revision

## Taxonomy

[Squaring](process_squaring.md), the depth at which the belief itself is edited.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: squaring

## Initiated by

The persona did it, freely, and cannot put the cause anywhere else -- so the belief is what moves.
The Revision starts wherever [dissonance](@chbrain/khai-engine-dissonance/process_dissonance.md) is being reduced -- the [induced compliance](@chbrain/khai-engine-dissonance/process_induced_compliance.md) in which an act done against one's view with too little outside reason to excuse it is covered by coming to hold the view, the [free choice](@chbrain/khai-engine-dissonance/process_free_choice.md) in which the chosen option is upgraded and the rejected one downgraded until a near-tie looks like no contest, and the [earning](@chbrain/khai-engine-dissonance/process_earning.md) in which a goal is prized in proportion to what it cost to reach.
It is the second depth: the deed is fixed, so the cheapest thing left to move is what the persona thinks.

## Direction

Toward a persona who now believes what fits what they did.
The Revision reads the bending -- the dull task that becomes genuinely interesting for a dollar and not for twenty, the car that looks better the day after it was bought than the day before, the group loved most by those whose initiation was worst -- each a mind changed in a way the persona does not experience as a change.
It moves the persona from holding a mismatch to holding a set that coheres: not a lie maintained but a view sincerely arrived at, which is the whole difference.

## Lever

The consistency the persona regains against the fact that they cannot feel it being bought.
The carrying force is that inconsistency is aversive and the discomfort is real -- a self that could act freely against its own beliefs and feel nothing would have no beliefs worth the name, so the pressure to cohere is what makes conviction mean anything; the resistance is that the pressure is indifferent to which cognition yields, and since the act is already committed and the attitude is not, the attitude is what gives -- so the belief bends to the deed rather than the deed to the belief, and the smallest push produces the largest turn.
The Revision is squaring at the depth where argument stops working: there is no insincerity to expose, because the persona means it now.

## Echo

The belief moved to cover the act, and held in good faith.
The Revision leaves the persona coherent again at the price of a view they did not previously have and cannot date the arrival of -- and hands the discrepancy on when even a revised belief will not cover what was done.
That is its limit: a revision works on what the persona thinks, and some acts are wrong under any belief the persona is willing to hold, which is where the suspension is reached for instead.
The Revision is dated from the bending, the moment a mind changed to fit a deed and did not notice.
