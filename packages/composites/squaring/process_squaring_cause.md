# Process: Squaring, the Cause

## Taxonomy

[Squaring](process_squaring.md), the depth at which only the outcome's why is edited.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: squaring

## Initiated by

An outcome has landed and the persona reaches behind it for a cause, and where the cause is put decides what the outcome costs them.
The Cause starts wherever [attribution](@chbrain/khai-engine-attribution/process_attribution.md) does its work -- the reading placed at one of four corners, [disposition](@chbrain/khai-engine-attribution/process_disposition.md) where the cause is the actor's own nature and will hold next time, [effort](@chbrain/khai-engine-attribution/process_effort.md) where it is the actor's but was a one-off, [circumstance](@chbrain/khai-engine-attribution/process_circumstance.md) where it is a standing feature of the world, and [chance](@chbrain/khai-engine-attribution/process_chance.md) where it was the wind.
It is the cheapest repair there is: nothing about the persona has to change if the cause can be put down as passing, or put down elsewhere.

## Direction

Toward an outcome that has stopped being a bare fact and become a thing explained.
The Cause reads the placement being made -- the failure laid on a night's slack rather than a fixed lack, the promotion credited to ability rather than a thin field, the slight read as the other's nature rather than their bad week -- each an account built quickly, from what was seen and what was already believed, and carried forward in place of the outcome itself.
It moves the persona from having a result to having a reason: the emotion tracking where the cause sits and the expectancy tracking whether it will hold.

## Lever

The explanatory work a placement genuinely does against the pull under which it is made.
The carrying force is that causal reading is not a luxury -- an outcome without a cause predicts nothing, and locating the why is how a persona knows whether to try again, and the axes it reads on are real ones that carry real consequences; the resistance is that the weighing happens fast and under the pull of what flatters or protects, so the placement is a reading rather than a proof, and it is made by exactly the party with an interest in where it lands.
The Cause is squaring at its shallowest and most defensible: the persona has moved nothing but the explanation, and the explanation was always going to be a guess.

## Echo

The outcome explained, and the self left standing at no cost.
The Cause leaves the persona with an account they could in principle be argued out of -- how others fared, how this one usually fares, how they fare elsewhere are all evidence somebody else can bring -- and hands the discrepancy on when a placement cannot cover it.
That is the limit: a placement cannot cover a deed the persona freely did and cannot deny, and where it fails, the revision is what is reached for next.
The Cause is dated from the placing, the moment a why was fixed and carried forward instead of the what.
