# Process: Squaring, the Suspension

## Taxonomy

[Squaring](process_squaring.md), the depth at which the standard is edited, and the ladder is seen for what it is.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: squaring

## Initiated by

The act is one the persona's own morals would condemn, and neither a placement nor a revision will cover it -- so the standard is taken out of play instead.
The Suspension starts wherever [moral disengagement](@chbrain/khai-engine-moral-disengagement/process_moral_disengagement.md) is at work, switching off the self-sanction at one of four points: the act relabelled until it is not the thing it was, through [justification](@chbrain/khai-engine-moral-disengagement/process_justification.md); the authorship routed upward to an order or outward into a crowd, through [displacement](@chbrain/khai-engine-moral-disengagement/process_displacement.md); the harm kept unseen, downplayed, or disbelieved until it falls below the threshold that would trip the sanction, through [minimizing](@chbrain/khai-engine-moral-disengagement/process_minimizing.md); and the one harmed put outside the circle the sanction protects, through [blaming](@chbrain/khai-engine-moral-disengagement/process_blaming.md).
It is the last depth, and the only one that does not touch the persona's beliefs at all.

## Direction

Toward a harm done with the self-regard intact and the morals still, in the persona's own account, in force.
The Suspension reads the carve-out being made -- the euphemism laid over the thing, the hand that was only following, the result nobody went and looked at, the person who had it coming -- each a deactivation at one point in a chain that would otherwise have stopped the act before it started.
It moves the persona nowhere at all: the standards stay in place for other acts and other people, and the persona keeps its sense of itself as decent precisely by exempting the one deed from the reckoning that would threaten it.

## Lever

The self-regard preserved against the fact that nothing was conceded to preserve it.
The carrying force is the same self-regulation that ordinarily works: moral conduct is governed by anticipated self-censure, and a persona who could not exempt anything -- an emergency, a duty, a lesser evil genuinely chosen -- would be unable to act under any pressure at all, so the switch has to exist; the resistance is that the switch is operated by the party it protects, at whichever of the four points is easiest that day, and needs no general belief to be revised in order to work.
The Suspension rides the composite's keystone knife-edge: the account is not a lie the persona tells but what the persona now holds -- and here not even that, since nothing is held differently; the standard is intact, the belief is intact, and only this act has quietly been placed where neither reaches it.

## Echo

The harm done, the self spared, and nothing on the record to point at.
It shows the ladder complete -- a cause placed, a belief bent, a standard suspended -- and carries beneath it the composite's keystone: each repair goes deeper and shows less, so the shallowest can be argued with by anyone holding better evidence, the middle one cannot because the persona sincerely means it, and the deepest cannot even be located, because the persona will still tell you the act would be wrong for someone else and will be telling the truth.
This is why no monstrousness is required to explain harm-doing, and why the persona who did it is usually the last available witness to it.
The Suspension is the note the whole composite closes on: what a persona cannot square, they do not carry -- they exempt, and go on being who they were.
The Suspension is dated from the switching-off, the moment a standard stopped applying to one deed and to nothing else.
