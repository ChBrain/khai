# Process: Squaring

## Taxonomy

How a persona squares what happened with who they take themselves to be.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: squaring

## Initiated by

Something has happened that does not fit the persona's picture of itself, and the picture is not what gives way.
Squaring starts wherever the account is being repaired -- the cause placed where it need not disturb the self, through [attribution](@chbrain/khai-engine-attribution/process_attribution.md); the belief bent to fit a deed that cannot be undone, through [dissonance](@chbrain/khai-engine-dissonance/process_dissonance.md); and the moral standard switched off for this act alone, through [moral disengagement](@chbrain/khai-engine-moral-disengagement/process_moral_disengagement.md).
This composite adds no fourth engine; it reads what the three make together: one repair, reached for at three depths, each deeper one taken up when the shallower will not hold.

## Direction

Toward the whole account by which a self survives what it did, read through how deep the edit had to go.
The composite wires the three engines and reads their working in three shapes: [the Cause](process_squaring_cause.md), the outcome's why placed where it costs least; [the Revision](process_squaring_revision.md), the belief moved because the deed cannot be; and [the Suspension](process_squaring_suspension.md), the twist -- the standard itself taken out of play.
The order is not a schedule but a cost ladder: a placement is cheap and often enough, a revision is dearer and needed when the act was freely the persona's own, and a suspension is reached for when neither will cover what was done.

## Lever

The self a coherent account protects against the correction it makes impossible.
The carrying force is that a persona cannot act at all without a working picture of itself -- an account that survives its own conduct is what lets someone keep going after a failure, a compromise, or a hard choice, and a self that revised itself downward at every discrepancy would be unusable; the resistance is that the repair is done by the same persona whose picture is at stake, so the account settles where it flatters or protects, and the persona has no vantage from which to check it.
Squaring is read wherever a persona is granted the story they tell themselves: not a self reporting on its conduct but one already reconciled to it.

## Echo

The persona revealed by the account that survived.
Squaring does not close; it is the repair running, gathering the three engines into one reading -- a cause placed, a belief bent, a standard suspended.
It hands off what it is not: the standing catalogue of tilts held as positions -- the self-serving slant, the correspondence bias -- is the bias engine's, and this reads the operation as it happens rather than the tilt that is always there; the moral self that governs conduct in advance, its identity, its convictions, and the credit it draws on, is the conscience composite's; the wrong and the reckoning between two people is the moral-account composite's, and the self-sanction that fires when a harm is owned rather than exempted is the guilt engine's; restoring the self by affirming a value in an unrelated domain, rather than repairing the account of this one, is the self-affirmation engine's and the selfhood composite's; the persistence in a failing course of action is the escalation engine's; the misleading of another is the deception engine's, where this misleads the self; the lens by which a persona judges someone else's conduct is the moral-judgment engine's; the circling on distress that never resolves is the rumination engine's, where this resolves; and the account made to stand for a story is authored key, the play's pitch, not the force.
The persona is dated from the discrepancy, and read as a self that has already finished explaining.
