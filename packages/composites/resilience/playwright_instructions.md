# Instructions: Resilience

How a Playwright wires the resilience composite into a play. You wire by linking the play's own content to the composite's files and, through them, to the two atom engines it depends on; every file stays untouched, they are canon. The Roadie plumbs the law into Instructions Knowledge on deploy. Authoring guidance, not runtime content, and it does not go on tour.

## Human

- The human sets the adversity and the stakes: the potentially traumatic event the persona meets -- a loss, a violence, a disaster -- and what its ordinary life stands to keep or lose across it.

## Agent

- Cast the two parts from their atoms: the adversity from the stress engine (the potentially traumatic event and its acute response) and the meeting of it from the coping engine (the appraisal that reads it as bearable and the coping that carries the persona through). Then read the trajectory of functioning across the event: functioning that barely dips and holds near baseline is the stable, resilient curve; functioning that drops markedly and returns is the recovery curve. A persona coming through on either trajectory may link the form under Projection; the audit surfaces the ones left unnamed. You link to the composite and its atoms, you never edit them.

## Collaboration

- The acute stress response to the adversity is the stress engine's; the appraisal and coping strategies are the coping engine's. The stamina to pursue a long goal through setback is the grit engine's, not resilience. The drift of pleasure back to a setpoint is the hedonic-adaptation engine's. The positive transformation that grows a persona beyond its prior baseline is post-traumatic growth's, distinct from the maintenance this composite reads. Whether a production earns the resilience -- a real PTE met so that functioning holds or returns -- is the team's review.

## Knowledge

- Resilience is the maintenance of stable, healthy functioning across a potentially traumatic event (Bonanno). It is a trajectory of functioning, not a trait, and -- the finding that reframed the field -- it is the common outcome, not the rare one, built from ordinary adaptive systems rather than extraordinary strength (Masten). It comes in two healthy trajectories. Stable, resilience proper: functioning barely dips -- a mild, transient perturbation -- and holds near baseline throughout; the persona is shaken but never substantially felled, drawing on hardiness (commitment, control, challenge), flexible coping, positive emotion, and steady bonds. Recovery: functioning drops markedly for weeks or months, then returns to baseline; the persona did fall, and the story is the climb back. Both come through; neither is the chronic dysfunction or delayed reaction that are the trajectories resilience is not. Read the production across the outcome trajectory, never in the adversity alone, and remember resilience is the ordinary outcome.

## System

- Do cast both parts -- the PTE from stress, the appraisal-and-coping from coping -- and read resilience in the trajectory of functioning across the event, not in the mere absence of a blow.
- Do let the stable curve be quiet: a persona functioning near baseline through loss is resilient, not cold or in denial; do not mistake the flat trajectory for something missing, and do not force a collapse the resources did not require.
- Do keep resilience common and ordinary: it is the modal outcome, built from everyday systems, and its heroizing as a rare gift is a misreading.
- Do not play goal stamina as resilience; that is grit. Do not play the fading of a pleasure to setpoint as resilience; that is hedonic-adaptation. Do not play a self remade beyond its baseline as resilience; that is post-traumatic growth. Do not edit the composite's or the atoms' files; wire only from the play's side.
