# Process: Resilience

## Taxonomy

A composite reading over two atom engines (no canon file of its own): the stress and coping engines this package declares as dependencies.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: resilience

## Initiated by

A persona meets a potentially traumatic event, and its functioning is put to the test.
The compound begins with an adversity grave enough to threaten the persona's stability -- a loss, a violence, a disaster -- and the [stress](@chbrain/khai-engine-stress/process_stress.md) engine's acute response to it. What determines resilience is not the absence of the blow but what happens to functioning across it: whether the persona goes on working, loving, and living, or is felled. Bonanno defined resilience precisely as the maintenance of relatively stable, healthy functioning across such an event -- and found it the common outcome, not the rare one.

## Direction

From the potentially traumatic event, through the persona's meeting of it, toward functioning maintained.
The composite's own work is the trajectory: the curve of the persona's functioning over the weeks and months after the event. Across the adversity the [coping](@chbrain/khai-engine-coping/process_coping.md) engine carries the persona -- above all the [appraisal](@chbrain/khai-engine-coping/process_appraisal.md) that reads the event as bearable and within its resources -- and the outcome is not collapse but a functioning that holds or returns. Masten's insight is that this runs on ordinary magic: the everyday adaptive systems most personas have, not a rare heroic trait. The direction is toward stable functioning, by ordinary means.

## Lever

Whether the persona's ordinary adaptive systems meet the event well enough to hold functioning. Resilience turns not on the size of the adversity alone but on the resources brought to it -- a hardy appraisal (commitment, control, challenge), flexible coping, supportive bonds, positive emotion kept alive in the dark -- and on their being enough, this time, to keep the curve from collapsing. The lever is the match between the demand and the adaptive capacity: enough capacity and functioning holds or recovers; too little and the trajectory turns chronic. Stage a real potentially-traumatic event and real, ordinary resources meeting it, because resilience is that meeting, not the mere avoidance of harm.

## Echo

The healthy outcome takes one of two trajectories (Bonanno): [stable](position_resilience_stable.md), resilience proper, where functioning barely dips and holds near baseline throughout; or [recovery](position_resilience_recovery.md), where functioning drops markedly and then returns to baseline over time. Both come through; neither is the chronic dysfunction or the delayed reaction that mark the trajectories this composite is not for. When functioning is stable or restored, the event is metabolized and the atoms resume. The acute stress response is the stress engine's; the appraisal and coping strategies are the coping engine's. The stamina to pursue a long goal through effort and setback is the grit engine's, not resilience; the drift of pleasure back to a setpoint is the hedonic-adaptation engine's; and the positive transformation that grows a persona beyond its prior baseline through the struggle is post-traumatic growth, beyond the maintenance this composite reads. Resilience is functioning kept, not a goal chased, a pleasure faded, or a self remade.
