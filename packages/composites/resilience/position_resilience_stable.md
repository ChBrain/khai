# Position: Resilience, Stable

## Taxonomy

[Resilience](process_resilience.md), the trajectory where functioning barely dips and holds -- resilience proper.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: resilience

## Has

The resilient trajectory in its strict sense: a functioning that is shaken but never substantially felled. Across the potentially traumatic event, the [stress](@chbrain/khai-engine-stress/process_stress.md) response fires and a brief perturbation shows -- a few bad days, a wobble -- but the [appraisal](@chbrain/khai-engine-coping/process_appraisal.md) holds the event as bearable and the persona goes on functioning, working, and relating near its baseline throughout. This is the curve Bonanno reserves the word resilience for: not a fall and a climb, but a stability that the blow scarcely interrupts.

## Orders

Carry on, near normal, through the blow. The stable trajectory orders the persona to keep functioning -- to grieve or reel only transiently and to hold its ordinary life largely intact, drawing on hardiness, flexible coping, positive emotion, and steady bonds to keep the curve from dropping. It does not order the suppression of feeling; it orders that feeling not fell the functioning. The persona is knocked and rights itself almost at once.

## Loses

The dramatic arc of a fall and a recovery, and the reading it invites. Because the stable persona never visibly collapses, its resilience can be misread as coldness, denial, or a failure to have loved enough to be hurt -- the flat trajectory is often mistaken for something missing. What it forgoes is the legible story of being broken and made whole that the [recovery](position_resilience_recovery.md) trajectory tells; its strength is quiet and easily overlooked, and it can be pressed to perform a grief its functioning did not require.

## Drives

On that stable trajectory:
rides it -- keeps functioning near baseline, metabolizes the blow with only a transient dip, and returns to ordinary life quickly -- or, if the adversity outruns the resources or a second blow lands, drops into the [recovery](position_resilience_recovery.md) curve or, past that, into chronic dysfunction. The stable baseline holds while functioning is never substantially lost. That resilience is the common outcome built from ordinary systems is the [root's](process_resilience.md); the acute stress response beneath the wobble is the stress engine's.
