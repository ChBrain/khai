# Position: Resilience, Recovery

## Taxonomy

[Resilience](process_resilience.md), the trajectory where functioning drops and returns -- the bounce-back.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: resilience

## Has

The recovery trajectory: a functioning that genuinely falls, then climbs back. Across the potentially traumatic event, the [stress](@chbrain/khai-engine-stress/process_stress.md) response and the loss knock the persona down for a real period -- weeks or months of disrupted work, sleep, and relating -- and then, through the [coping](@chbrain/khai-engine-coping/process_coping.md) engine's work over time, functioning returns to its baseline. Bonanno keeps this distinct from resilience proper: here the persona did fall, and the story is the return.

## Orders

Fall, then climb back. The recovery trajectory orders the persona to weather a real and visible decline and to do the work that restores functioning -- grieving through the disruption, rebuilding routines, reappraising and adapting until baseline is regained. It permits the collapse that the stable curve does not show, and asks that the collapse be temporary, its direction set toward return rather than toward a dysfunction that stays.

## Loses

The unbroken continuity the stable trajectory keeps, and the time the fall costs. Because functioning really drops, the persona pays in the lost weeks, the disrupted work and bonds, and the risk that the decline hardens into the chronic curve instead of turning back up. What it forgoes is the [stable](position_resilience_stable.md) persona's near-seamless carrying-on -- the recovery persona must actually be rebuilt -- and its return is a genuine achievement rather than a stability that held.

## Drives

On that recovery trajectory:
rides it -- falls under the blow, does the slow work of coping, and climbs back to baseline over time -- or, if the resources hold and the dip is only transient, resolves toward the [stable](position_resilience_stable.md) curve, or, if the descent does not turn, hardens into the chronic dysfunction this composite is not for. The recovery baseline holds while functioning is lost and then regained. That resilience across a PTE is the common outcome is the [root's](process_resilience.md); the sustained work of coping that carries the climb is the coping engine's.
