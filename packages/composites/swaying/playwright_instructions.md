# Instructions: Swaying

How a Playwright wires the swaying composite into a play. You wire by linking the play's
own content to the composite's files; the files stay untouched, they are canon. The
composite installs the persuasion and reactance engines as dependencies, and their laws
arrive with them. Authoring guidance, not runtime content, and it does not go on tour.

## Human

- The human sets who in the play is trying to move whom: what position is being pressed, what the presser wants, and how much room the one being pressed is left.

## Agent

- From the play's own content, read an attempt to move a position and link under Projection what is happening to it: the case while the persuader is making it (the appeal assembled and aimed, the reception that decides whether any of it arrives, the processing that runs on the argument or on whatever stands in for it, the outcome that may not survive the week, from persuasion), the kick while the target is answering the pressure rather than the point (the position held harder for being pressed, the discouraged option made more attractive by the discouraging, the messenger attacked instead of the argument, from reactance), the give while the persuader has stopped adding and is conceding instead. Keep the target answering two things at once, and let the persuader's effort be visible to them, because it is. You link to the composite, you never edit it.

## Collaboration

- The appeal, the reception, the processing, and the outcome are the persuasion engine's; defiance, allure, and derogation are reactance's -- one alone is a process, not the attempt and its answer. How the talk works is crosstalk's and utterance's; what a persona holds and on what footing is assent's; matching a group nobody addressed you about is membership's; the command backed by the power to compel is subjection's, and swaying has no such power, which is why the kick is available at all; the mutual pursuit of terms is dealing's; an object built to deceive, hook, or arrange is guile's, captology's, and choice-architecture's, on a piece rather than between people; how an idea travels a whole system is the adoption engine's, a population where this is a pair. This composite owns the attempt on a position -- and the give bridge carries the keystone: an attempt to persuade is itself information, and it argues the other way.

## Knowledge

- Swaying is one persona read while trying to move another's position -- braiding the case made and met (persuasion) with the pushback a pressed freedom produces against the very force that pressed it (reactance) -- and the join is already inside the reactance engine, which anchors Dillard and Shen for the intertwined model: reactance is part of what a persuasive message does, not a separate subject beside it. From which the composite's premise follows: the target is answering two things at once, the content and the attempting, and can only report on the first. Knowles and Linn supply the design consequence. Persuasion proceeds either by alpha moves, which increase the force toward a position, or by omega moves, which reduce the resistance to it -- and nearly the whole history of the field is alpha, because the alpha instruments are the ones a persuader controls and can see. So the persuader is working a lever whose two ends are wired to opposite outcomes. It runs in three faces. The case is the attempt as its maker designed it, and the only face they control: the instruments are real -- evidence, credibility, timing, the plain fact of others having agreed -- but the same message runs through two different machines depending on how much the target has left to think with, and the persuader rarely gets to choose which, so an argument sharpened past the point where anyone is weighing arguments has been sharpened into a cue and reads as effort rather than evidence. The kick is the pushback the attempt produces on its own: indifferent to merit, since a correct message and an incorrect one produce it equally, and proportional to pressure and to how little room the target is left -- so every increment that makes a case more forceful, more insistent, or more complete raises the counter-force too, and past some point the second increment is larger than the first. The give is the twist. If adding force adds resistance, the remaining instrument cannot be an addition: the moves that work are subtractions -- naming the other's freedom to refuse, conceding the counter-argument before it has to be raised, asking for less, letting a silence run -- and not one of them makes the case better. They make the pressing smaller, and the pressing was doing half the damage. The best measured of them is the plainest: adding to a request a sentence saying the other is free to say no substantially raises the chance they do not, strongest face to face and weakening with delay. It gives away nothing that was ever held, since the decision was always theirs, and it removes the reason to refuse that the pressure had been supplying. Read an attempt by what it cost the position, and read the persuader's effort as data the target is using against the conclusion.

## System

- Do read the attempt and its target together: name whether the case (the argument being made), the kick (the pressure being answered), or the give (the persuader subtracting) is running, and cast each from its engines.
- Do let the effort be visible: the target knows they are being worked on, notices earlier than the persuader thinks, and is responding to that as well as to the point.
- Do keep the kick indifferent to merit: it fires on a good argument as readily as a bad one, so a play that makes the resisting persona wrong or stupid has staged something else.
- Do make the arithmetic show: let a persona improve their case and lose ground by it, and let the improvement be genuine rather than a straw version.
- Do let the give look like weakness: it must cost the persuader something they feel, be indistinguishable in the moment from surrender, and never be legible to the audience as a technique -- the moment it reads as a manoeuvre it has stopped being one.
- Do not let a pushed agreement hold: a persona pressed into agreeing has not moved, and the play should collect on that later.
- Do not claim more than the evidence: reactance to persuasive messages is well established, the alpha/omega split is a framework rather than a finding, and the freedom-affirming phrase is measured mostly on modest face-to-face requests and decays with delay -- so stage the mechanism and not a general technique for moving convictions.
- Do not model how the talk works (that is crosstalk and utterance), what a persona holds (that is assent), matching without being addressed (that is membership), the command backed by power (that is subjection), the mutual bargain (that is dealing), the object that deceives or hooks or arranges (those are guile, captology, choice-architecture), or an idea travelling a whole system (that is adoption). Do not make a change of mind stand for a story (that is the play's pitch). Do not edit the composite's files; wire only from the play's side.
