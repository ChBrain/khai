# Process: Swaying, the Kick

## Taxonomy

[Swaying](process_swaying.md), the pushback the attempt produces on its own, regardless of what it said.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: swaying

## Initiated by

The target has noticed that somebody is trying to move them.
The Kick starts wherever [reactance](@chbrain/khai-engine-reactance/process_reactance.md) is running -- the [defiance](@chbrain/khai-engine-reactance/process_defiance.md) that holds the position harder because it was pressed, the [allure](@chbrain/khai-engine-reactance/process_allure.md) that makes the discouraged option more attractive for having been discouraged, and the [derogation](@chbrain/khai-engine-reactance/process_derogation.md) that goes after the persuader instead of the argument.
It is the face of the composite the persuader is not looking at and is themselves producing.

## Direction

Toward the freedom, and away from wherever the pressure was pointing.
The Kick reads the response to being pressed rather than to being told -- the recommendation refused on grounds assembled afterwards, the thing wanted more once it was withheld, the messenger found to be the sort of person who would say that -- and reads its indifference to merit: a correct message and an incorrect one produce it equally, because it is not answering the message.
It moves the target somewhere the persuader did not aim, using energy the persuader supplied.

## Lever

The value of a freedom against the pressure applied to it.
The carrying force is that the freedom is worth defending and the defence is proportionate: the more important the freedom, the more it is threatened, and the less room the target is left, the harder the pushback -- which is why the strongest, clearest, most insistent version of a case reliably produces the most of it; the resistance is only whatever room the target still feels they have, so anything that restores that room reduces the kick without touching the argument at all.
The Kick spends the persuader's own force, and converts it into the target's resolve.

## Echo

A position held harder for having been argued with.
It is the bridge that carries the composite's arithmetic: the two things the target is responding to are wired to opposite outcomes, so every increment the persuader adds to the case adds to the kick as well, and past some point the second is larger than the first.
What it hands upward is the shape of the only remaining move -- if adding force adds resistance, then the instrument that works cannot be an addition.
The Kick is dated from the moment the target registered the attempt, which is earlier than the persuader thinks and is not a stage of the argument.
