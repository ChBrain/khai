# Process: Swaying, the Case

## Taxonomy

[Swaying](process_swaying.md), the attempt as its maker designed it.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: swaying

## Initiated by

A persona has something they want another to hold, and begins to make it hold.
The Case starts wherever [persuasion](@chbrain/khai-engine-persuasion/process_persuasion.md) is running -- the [appeal](@chbrain/khai-engine-persuasion/process_appeal.md) assembled and aimed, the [reception](@chbrain/khai-engine-persuasion/process_reception.md) that decides whether any of it arrives, the [processing](@chbrain/khai-engine-persuasion/process_processing.md) that runs on the argument itself or on whatever stands in for it, and the [outcome](@chbrain/khai-engine-persuasion/process_outcome.md) that may or may not survive the week.
It is the face of the composite the persuader is actually attending to, and the only one they control.

## Direction

Toward a position held differently, by the route the target's attention allows.
The Case reads the attempt as a thing with a route -- the argument weighed on its merits where the target has the motivation and the room to weigh it, and the whole load carried by the source, the manner, and the number of others agreeing where they do not -- and reads that the persuader rarely gets to choose which route runs, since the target's capacity to attend is set by everything else in their day.
It moves the position, or does not, and does so by a mechanism the persuader has to guess at.

## Lever

The real purchase of a better case against the persuader's poor information about which case is better here.
The carrying force is that the instruments work: evidence, credibility, timing, and the plain fact of other people having agreed all shift positions, and a persuader who ignores them is worse off than one who does not; the resistance is that the same message runs through two entirely different machines depending on how much the target has left to think with, and a case built for one arrives at the other -- so an argument sharpened past the point where anyone is weighing arguments has been sharpened into a cue, and reads as effort rather than as evidence.
The Case spends the persuader's attention on the one variable they can set.

## Echo

An attempt made, and a target who has now been attempted on.
It is the bridge that establishes what the composite is not about: the design of the message, on which there is a large and useful literature and to which this composite adds nothing.
What it hands upward is the thing the design cannot cover -- that the attempt has arrived as an event and not only as a content, and the target is about to respond to both.
The Case is dated from the moment the persuader began, which is also the moment the second thing started running.
