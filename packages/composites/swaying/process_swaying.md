# Process: Swaying

## Taxonomy

One persona trying to move another's position.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: swaying

## Initiated by

Somebody wants somebody else to think, value, or do something different, and has begun trying.
Swaying starts wherever that attempt is running: as the case made and met, through [persuasion](@chbrain/khai-engine-persuasion/process_persuasion.md); and as the pushback a pressed freedom produces against the very force that pressed it, through [reactance](@chbrain/khai-engine-reactance/process_reactance.md).
This composite adds no third engine. It reads the relation the reactance atom already names -- that reactance is part of what a persuasive message does rather than a separate subject -- and follows it to the consequence neither atom states alone.

## Direction

Toward a position that has moved, hardened, or moved and will not stay moved.
The composite wires the two engines and reads their working in three shapes: [the Case](process_swaying_case.md), the attempt as its maker designed it; [the Kick](process_swaying_kick.md), the pushback the making of it produces regardless of content; and [the Give](process_swaying_give.md), the twist -- the moves that work by subtraction.
The three are not three tactics. They are one attempt, met by a target who is answering two things at once and can only report on one of them.

## Lever

The force available to a persuader against the fact that force is one of the things being responded to.
The carrying force is that positions do move and the ordinary instruments do work: an argument can be better, a source can be more credible, a request can be timed, and none of that is illusory; the resistance is that every one of those instruments is also an increase in the pressure a target feels, and pressure is answered separately -- so the persuader is operating a lever whose two ends are wired to opposite outcomes, and cannot pull one without the other.
Swaying is read on the attempt and its target together; a persona persuading nobody in particular is making a speech, which is a different thing.

## Echo

A position moved by an amount that is not the amount of effort spent.
Swaying does not close; it is the attempt running, gathered into one reading -- a case, a kick, a give.
It hands off what it is not: how the talk itself works -- the accommodation, the framing, the grounding, the speech act and its implicature -- is the crosstalk and utterance composites'; what a persona actually holds and on what footing is the assent composite's, where this is the attempt on it; the systematic errors of judgement are the estimation composite's; matching a group without anybody having addressed you is the membership composite's, where this requires an attempt aimed at somebody; the command backed by the power to compel, and the order kept by an institution, are the subjection composite's -- swaying has no such power, which is what makes it swaying; the mutual pursuit of terms between two parties who each hold something is the dealing composite's, where this is one-directional and about a position rather than a bargain; an object built to deceive, to hook, or to arrange the options is guile's, captology's, and choice-architecture's, all read on a piece rather than between people; how an idea travels a whole social system is the adoption engine's, which is a population where this is a pair; and a conversion made to **stand for** a story is authored key, the play's pitch, not the force.
The persona is dated from the attempt, and read as somebody who was answering the pressing as well as the point.
