# Process: Crosstalk

## Taxonomy

Interactional miscommunication -- a composite over grounding, framing, and accommodation.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: crosstalk

## Initiated by

Two personas talk, and each is running their own frame of what the exchange is about, grounding what they hear against that frame -- so each takes themselves to be understood while neither is.
This is crosstalk: the talking-past loop, where a mismatch of frames turns every attempt to ground into a fresh misgrounding, and the gap widens turn by turn instead of closing.
The composite adds no fourth engine. It wires the loop the atoms will not stage alone: framing supplies the mismatched frames, grounding supplies the misgrounding, and accommodation supplies the divergence that widens the rift.

## Direction

Toward a shared understanding that never arrives -- and a gap that grows while both believe it is closing.
Because each grounds against a frame the other does not hold, the ordinary evidence of understanding runs backward: a nod confirms the wrong thing, a fitting next turn fits the wrong contribution, and every exchange that should close the gap instead deepens it.
The two move apart on a rail that feels, from inside each frame, like moving together.

## Lever

The frames that will not meet, run through the grounding channel.
Grounding assumes a shared frame it never checks; framing hands the two of them different ones; and the mismatch is invisible precisely because each party's evidence looks right from inside their own frame. As frustration mounts, accommodation turns the screw -- style diverging, or stubbornly held -- so the mismatch reads as ill will and hardens.
No single atom stages this: the loop lives in the seams between them, where a criterion met inside one frame is a criterion missed across two.

## Echo

The two end further apart than they began, each certain the other is being obtuse -- or a clarification aimed at the frame itself catches the loop and re-grounds them at last.
The loop runs as [mismatch](process_mismatch.md), [loop](process_loop.md), or [impasse](process_impasse.md) -- the bridges by which the frames fail to meet, the misgrounding compounds, and the whole thing is caught or hardens.
The conflict-spiral it can ignite -- retaliation raising the stakes toward lose-lose -- is the escalation engine's; the relationship it damages when it hardens is the repair engine's to mend. This composite owns the understanding-spiral itself: the talking-past, and the widening gap the two cannot see.
