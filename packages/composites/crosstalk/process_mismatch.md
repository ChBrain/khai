# Process: Mismatch

## Taxonomy

[Crosstalk](process_crosstalk.md), the bridge where two frames fail to meet and grounding begins to misfire.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: crosstalk

## Initiated by

Two personas enter the exchange under different frames -- one hearing it as [emphasis](@chbrain/khai-engine-framing/process_emphasis.md) on one dimension, the other on another; one construing it through a [metaphor](@chbrain/khai-engine-framing/process_metaphor.md) the other does not hold.
Each grounds what they hear against their own frame, so the evidence of understanding each gives -- the nod, the fitting next turn -- is honest evidence to the giver and false to the receiver.
This is the doorstep of crosstalk: the point where the two are already reading the same words as different messages, and neither has any sign of it.

## Direction

From two frames to a misgrounding that is guaranteed, not accidental.
Because each reads the other through the wrong frame, the [acknowledgment](@chbrain/khai-engine-grounding/process_acknowledgment.md) that should ground a contribution instead grounds a different contribution than the one meant -- [misgrounding](@chbrain/khai-engine-grounding/process_misgrounding.md) by structure. The criterion is met inside each frame and missed across the two.
The talk proceeds smoothly, which is exactly the trouble: nothing feels wrong yet.

## Lever

The frame each grounds against, and never checks.
Grounding's whole economy assumes a shared frame; framing quietly supplies two; and the bridge is the seam where that assumption fails silently. Neither engine stages this alone -- framing gives the two frames but not the false uptake, grounding gives the uptake but assumes one frame -- so the mismatch is the handoff between them, invisible because each party's evidence is valid from inside their own frame.
It is legible to no one in the scene and plain to the audience above it.

## Echo

The misgrounding is seeded, and neither party knows.
What looks like understanding is the first turn of the divergence; the honest nods have laid ground that only one of them stands on.
From here the [loop](process_loop.md) takes over -- each contribution built on the last misgrounding, the gap compounding rather than correcting.
