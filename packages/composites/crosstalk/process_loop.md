# Process: Loop

## Taxonomy

[Crosstalk](process_crosstalk.md), the bridge where the misgrounding compounds instead of correcting.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: crosstalk

## Initiated by

Each misgrounded turn becomes the next turn's input.
One party responds -- reasonably, from inside their own frame -- to what they took the other to mean; the other, hearing that response through their frame, misgrounds it again and answers in kind.
Neither is careless; each is being as clear as they know how, which is why the failure is so hard to feel from inside it.

## Direction

Toward a widening gap, each turn further from shared understanding than the last, and each party more certain the fault is the other's.
This is the punctuation of the sequence: each reads their own move as a response to the other's provocation, so both experience themselves as reacting reasonably to someone being difficult.
As the exchange frustrates, style pulls apart -- [divergence](@chbrain/khai-engine-accommodation/process_divergence.md), or a stubborn [holding](@chbrain/khai-engine-accommodation/process_holding.md) that will not bend toward the other -- and the growing distance in manner reads as hostility, deepening the mismatch it came from.

## Lever

The feedback the atoms will not close.
Grounding stages one contribution's [misgrounding](@chbrain/khai-engine-grounding/process_misgrounding.md); it does not stage the next contribution built upon it. Accommodation stages one style move; it does not stage the divergence that feeds the misunderstanding that feeds the divergence.
The loop is the seam that joins them into a cycle: misgrounding drives divergence, divergence sharpens the mismatch, the sharper mismatch deepens the misgrounding -- a spiral that sustains itself with no new cause needed.

## Echo

The two are now locked in a self-sustaining spiral -- of understanding, not of stakes.
Whether it breaks, and how, is the [impasse](process_impasse.md) bridge's business.
The conflict-spiral it may light -- retaliation and hardening positions toward lose-lose -- is the escalation engine's, a different spiral this one can ignite but is not; crosstalk's spiral is the gap in meaning widening, whether or not the temperature ever rises.
