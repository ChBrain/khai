# Instructions: Crosstalk

How a Playwright wires the crosstalk composite into a play. You wire by linking the play's own content to the composite's files; the composite and its atoms stay untouched, they are canon. Installing the composite brings its three atoms -- grounding, framing, accommodation -- as dependencies, and the Roadie plumbs the composite's law into Instructions Knowledge on deploy. Authoring guidance, not runtime content, and it does not go on tour.

## Human

- The human defines the crosstalk terrain: the two personas, what each takes the exchange to be about (their frames), and what is at stake in their failing to meet -- so the mismatch has real content and the widening gap has somewhere to go.

## Agent

- From the play's own content, a plot links the crosstalk bridge it stages (mismatch, loop, or impasse) under its Tension. Cast the two sides from the atoms -- each with their own frame from framing, each grounding from grounding, each tuning style from accommodation -- and let the bridge show the loop: the frames that fail to meet, the misgrounding that compounds, the catch or the hardening that ends it. You link to the composite and its atoms; you never edit them.

## Collaboration

- The conflict-spiral a crosstalk can ignite -- retaliation toward lose-lose -- is the escalation engine's; the relationship a hardened crosstalk damages is the repair engine's to mend. The frames themselves are framing's, the misgrounding and the clarification that catches it are grounding's, the style divergence is accommodation's. This composite supplies only the seams -- the talking-past loop the three atoms make together but none makes alone.

## Knowledge

- Crosstalk is two personas talking past each other in a self-sustaining loop: each grounds what they hear against their own frame, so every attempt to understand misfires into a fresh misgrounding and the gap widens turn by turn. Mismatch is the start -- two frames that fail to meet, so honest evidence of understanding is false to the other. Loop is the compounding -- each misgrounded turn feeding the next, style diverging, each sure the other is at fault. Impasse is the end -- a clarification aimed at the frame catches it and re-grounds the two, or the loop hardens and they estrange.

## System

- Do keep both personas reasonable: crosstalk is not one party being stupid but two grounding honestly against frames that do not meet, so let the audience see the mismatch the characters cannot.
- Do let the evidence run backward -- the nod that grounds the wrong thing, the fitting next turn that fits the wrong contribution -- so the talk looks smooth while the gap grows.
- Do let the catch be hard: the question that fixes it must reach the frame ("are we even talking about the same thing?"), and from inside the loop that question is hard to reach because each reads the trouble as the other's failure.
- Do delegate the conflict it may ignite to the escalation engine and the relationship it damages to repair. Do not edit the composite's or the atoms' files; wire only from the play's side.
