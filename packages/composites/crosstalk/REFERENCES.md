# Crosstalk: Reference

## Line of Work

Crosstalk as **composite**: two personas talking past each other in a self-sustaining loop, where each grounds what they hear against their own frame of the exchange, so every attempt to understand misfires into a fresh misgrounding and the gap widens turn by turn instead of closing. It is not a new engine but a wiring of three: **framing** supplies the mismatched frames each party grounds against, **grounding** supplies the misgrounding that results, and **accommodation** supplies the style divergence that widens the rift. The composite adds no fourth phenomenon; it stages the loop the three atoms will not stage apart -- the feedback in which a misgrounding drives divergence, divergence sharpens the mismatch, and the sharper mismatch deepens the misgrounding.

The domain does not model the conflict-spiral a crosstalk can ignite -- positions hardening, each retaliation raising the stakes toward lose-lose -- which is the escalation engine's; nor the mending of the relationship a hardened crosstalk damages, which is the repair engine's. It owns the understanding-spiral itself: the talking-past, and the widening gap the two cannot see from inside their own frames.

The spine is the study of systematic miscommunication. Two people can be fluent, cooperative, and entirely mistaken about each other, because understanding is grounded against a frame that is assumed and never checked; where the frames differ, the checks that normally catch a misunderstanding instead certify it, and the interaction punctuates into a loop each experiences as the other's unreasonableness. The three bridges are the loop's course: mismatch, loop, impasse.

## Origin

Interactional sociolinguistics and the pragmatics of human communication, wired as a composite over three engines.

## Restrictions

What the composite refuses to model, and to whom it delegates.

- **Escalation**: The composite models the understanding-spiral, not the conflict-spiral. The self-amplifying stages of a conflict -- positions hardening, the other narrowing to an antagonist, each retaliation raising the stakes toward lose-lose -- are owned by the escalation engine; crosstalk owns the widening gap in meaning, which may run hot or cold and can ignite escalation without being it.
- **Repair**: The composite models the talking-past loop, not the mending of the relationship it damages. The rupture in a tie taken to resolution is owned by the repair engine; when a crosstalk hardens and estranges, what the relationship then undergoes to be mended is repair's, where this owns the misunderstanding that estranged it.
- **The atoms**: The composite adds no content its atoms already own. The frame each party runs is the framing engine's; the misgrounding, and the clarification that could catch it, are the grounding engine's; the style divergence that widens the rift is the accommodation engine's. Crosstalk owns only the seams -- the loop the three make together that none makes alone.

## Encoding

Source to constraint, per file.

- **[crosstalk](process_crosstalk.md)** (the root): Two personas each grounding against their own frame, so understanding misfires into compounding misgrounding and the gap widens while both believe it is closing. Anchored by Gumperz (cross-talk from mismatched contextualization) and Tannen (conversational style and mutual misunderstanding).
- **[mismatch](process_mismatch.md)** (bridge): The two frames fail to meet, so the honest evidence of understanding each gives is false to the other -- misgrounding by structure, invisible because each party's evidence is valid inside their own frame. Wires framing to grounding. Anchored by Gumperz (contextualization cues).
- **[loop](process_loop.md)** (bridge): Each misgrounded turn becomes the next turn's input, style diverging as frustration mounts, the gap compounding while each punctuates the sequence as the other's fault. Wires grounding's misgrounding to accommodation's divergence. Anchored by Watzlawick, Beavin & Jackson (punctuation) and Tannen (compounding style-mismatch).
- **[impasse](process_impasse.md)** (bridge): A clarification that reaches the frame catches the loop and re-grounds the two, or it hardens into estrangement -- crosstalk's catch having to repair a frame, not a word, which is why it persists. Wires grounding's clarification to the exit. Anchored by Laing (the interpersonal knot).
