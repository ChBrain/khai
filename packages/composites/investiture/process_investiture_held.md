# Process: Investiture, the Held

## Taxonomy

[Investiture](process_investiture.md), the width at which one persona's own self is the thing invested.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: investiture

## Initiated by

A persona has something they will not swap for an identical one, and cannot fully say why.
The Held starts wherever the [extended self](@chbrain/khai-engine-extended-self/piece_extended_self.md) has formed -- the [appropriation](@chbrain/khai-engine-extended-self/process_appropriation.md) by which an object is taken in through mastery, making, knowing, gift, or the sheer sediment of time and use, the [possessor](@chbrain/khai-engine-extended-self/position_possessor.md) who holds it as self rather than as goods, and the [dispossession](@chbrain/khai-engine-extended-self/process_dispossession.md) that tears it out again and takes a piece of the person with it.
It is investiture at its narrowest: one self, one thing, and no witness required.

## Direction

Toward a persona enlarged by having, and exposed by it.
The Held reads the extension at work -- the tool through which the persona is competent, the coat that is not any coat, the room's contents that make a life continuous, the cheap object fiercely kept -- each a thing that has stopped being interchangeable for its owner while remaining perfectly interchangeable for everybody else.
It moves the persona from owning to being partly located outside themselves: a store of self kept where it can be stolen.

## Lever

The self a thing can hold against the market's reading of the same thing.
The carrying force is that having is one way of being -- an object can carry memory, capability, taste, and continuity better than a mind alone can, so the self that leans on it really is larger for it; the resistance is that the extension is invisible in the thing and visible only in the having, so the disproportion between an object's price and the distress its loss would cause is the only external evidence there is, and to everyone else the grief looks like a category error.
The Held is investiture at its least defensible and most common: a self that has put part of itself somewhere it can be lost.

## Echo

The persona made larger by a possession, and smaller by its going.
The Held leaves the object charged with a self that no replacement can restore -- an identical thing returns the property and not the person -- and hands that pattern up to the widths where a line and a people do the same.
There is one exit that does not tear: the deliberate divestment, the meaning drawn back out before the thing is let go, so the object leaves empty.
The Held is dated from the taking-in, the moment a good stopped being a good.
