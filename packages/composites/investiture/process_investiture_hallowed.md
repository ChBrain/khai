# Process: Investiture, the Hallowed

## Taxonomy

[Investiture](process_investiture.md), the width at which a collective's own force is the thing invested, and the mechanism is seen for what it is.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: investiture

## Initiated by

A group has put its whole sense of itself into an object with no property that would justify it.
The Hallowed starts wherever the [totem](@chbrain/khai-engine-totem/piece_totem.md) is being kept apart -- the [consecration](@chbrain/khai-engine-totem/process_consecration.md) in which the heat of an assembly settles on a thing and charges it, sacred not by any property of its own but by the collective treating it so; the [adherent](@chbrain/khai-engine-totem/position_adherent.md) for whom what is a scrap of cloth to an outsider is not available as a scrap of cloth; and the [desecration](@chbrain/khai-engine-totem/process_desecration.md) that converts a thing into an injury and forces the group to defend the sacred or watch it fall.
It is investiture at its widest, and its most exposed: the charge fades without renewal, because the assembly is the whole of what supplies it.

## Direction

Toward an abstract we made into a thing that can be saluted, carried, and defended.
The Hallowed reads the disproportion at work -- the carved animal, the coloured cloth, the worn relic, worth little as material and worth dying for -- each an object that commands out of all proportion to what it is, which is precisely the tell that the force in it was put there.
It moves the object across a line: not among ordinary things but on the other side of one, where the distance the profane must keep is part of what the object is, and moving it back into the ordinary ends it.

## Lever

The power of a thing that has no power of its own.
The carrying force is that a collective feeling has nowhere to live between gatherings -- the sense of being one is real only while the group is massed, so it is fixed in something portable, and the object is how a we survives the crowd dispersing; the resistance is that nothing about the object holds it there: remove the investment and it is only a broken object, remove the assembly and nothing charges it, and the whole arrangement runs on people continuing to observe a prohibition whose ground is not in the thing they are observing it toward.
The Hallowed rides the composite's keystone knife-edge: what the object carries is not in the object -- which is why the outsider who sees a scrap of cloth is not making a mistake about the material, and why the adherent who cannot see a scrap of cloth is not making one either.

## Echo

The thing that is nothing, and therefore can be everything.
It shows investiture at its widest -- charged by assembly, cooling without it, felt as the group's own centre -- and carries beneath it the composite's keystone: because the worth was never a property of the object, no identical thing can substitute for it and no damage to it can be answered as damage to a thing; a scratch on the right one is a wound, and a perfect replacement is worthless, and both follow from the same fact.
This is what the narrower widths were doing all along: the persona who cannot swap the coat and the line that will not sell the deed are running the same move on a smaller assembly.
The Hallowed is the note the whole composite closes on: an object can be destroyed without touching what it held, and kept intact long after what it held has gone -- and only the people invested in it can tell which has happened.
The Hallowed is dated from the charging, the moment a gathering set something apart.
