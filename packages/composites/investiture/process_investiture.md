# Process: Investiture

## Taxonomy

How an object comes to carry an identity that is not in it.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: investiture

## Initiated by

A thing is being treated as worth more than it is, and the excess is not in the thing.
Investiture starts wherever an identity has been put into an object -- taken into one persona's own self through the [extended self](@chbrain/khai-engine-extended-self/piece_extended_self.md), withheld from exchange and passed down a line as the [heirloom](@chbrain/khai-engine-heirloom/piece_heirloom.md), or set apart by an assembled collective as the [totem](@chbrain/khai-engine-totem/piece_totem.md).
This composite adds no fourth engine; it reads what the three share: the same move made at three widths, and the same tell each time.

## Direction

Toward the whole of what an object is made to carry, read through whose identity is in it.
The composite wires the three engines and reads their working in three shapes: [the Held](process_investiture_held.md), the thing taken into a single self; [the Handed](process_investiture_handed.md), the thing kept out of the market and passed along a line; and [the Hallowed](process_investiture_hallowed.md), the twist -- the thing set apart by a gathered collective.
The widths differ and the mechanism does not: something that was in a person, a lineage, or a people has been lodged in an object, and the object now answers for it.

## Lever

The durability an identity gains by being put into a thing against the exposure it takes on there.
The carrying force is that an identity kept only in feeling or memory is hard to point at and easy to dispute, so it is lodged in something that can be held, displayed, guarded, and handed on -- which is why a line keeps one possession back from everything it sells and why an abstract we is given a piece of cloth to gather around; the resistance is that the object is now a place the identity can be attacked, and it can be taken, priced, broken, or profaned by people who see only what it materially is.
Investiture is read wherever a persona is granted a thing they cannot be reasonable about: not a self among its property but one with a part of itself out in the room.

## Echo

The persona revealed by what they cannot let go of.
Investiture does not close; it is the charging running, gathering the three engines into one reading -- a thing held as self, a thing kept for the line, a thing set apart by the group.
It hands off what it is not: the object's material life, worn by use and decayed by time and met or not by mending, is the object-cycle composite's, and the marks themselves prized as worth are its treasure; a claim fixed into durable form that travels unchanged and says the same thing everywhere is the document engine's; the obligation a transfer leaves between two persons is the owing composite's; the formation a persona arrives already carrying, and the capital transmitted with it, is the heritage engine's; the acute grief of a loss is the grief engine's; a shared resource governed by its own users is the commons engine's; and the object made to stand for a story is authored key, the play's pitch, not the force.
The persona is dated from the charging, and read as a self with a piece of itself in something breakable.
