# Process: Investiture, the Handed

## Taxonomy

[Investiture](process_investiture.md), the width at which a line's claim to itself is the thing invested.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: investiture

## Initiated by

One possession has been taken out of everything that can be sold, and the taking-out is the point.
The Handed starts wherever the [heirloom](@chbrain/khai-engine-heirloom/piece_heirloom.md) is being kept -- the [heir](@chbrain/khai-engine-heirloom/position_heir.md) who holds it in custody rather than ownership and cannot convert it under hardship without indicting themselves before the whole line, the [bequest](@chbrain/khai-engine-heirloom/process_bequest.md) in which the object moves only if the account moves with it, and the [alienation](@chbrain/khai-engine-heirloom/process_alienation.md) that begins the moment a price attaches.
It is investiture widened to a line: more than one lifetime, and nobody alive owns it.

## Direction

Toward a claim made hard to dispute by being made pointable.
The Handed reads the withholding at work -- the deed brought out at a succession, the title recited over at a funeral, the plain object a stranger passing would not price, the object present at the ceremonies that call for it and absent from the ledger of what may be sold -- each a claim that exists in memory anyway and would be merely a story without the thing.
It moves the object from property to trust: held by one at a time, belonging to a line, and legitimating whoever currently carries it with borrowed standing rather than a possession to spend down.

## Lever

The anchor a line gains against the freedom the current heir gives up.
The carrying force is that descent is exactly the kind of claim that talk cannot settle -- an account recited without an object is contestable and an account recited over one is much less so, which is why the line sets one thing outside the circuit that moves everything else it owns; the resistance is that the withholding is unnatural and under strain the pull to convert the object into the cash it could plainly become is the sharpest test the position faces, and the same market forces that move an ordinary possession along finally reach the one thing that was kept from them.
The Handed is investiture at its most institutional: a refusal maintained across generations by people who did not choose it.

## Echo

The line's claim anchored in a thing, or the anchor gone and the account left reciting a loss.
The Handed leaves the object carrying more than one lifetime of identity -- and when it goes, whoever received it next holds the object without the claim, while the line says we used to have and carries that as the story instead.
The story must travel with the thing or the transfer was never a bequest, only a handover of goods.
The Handed is dated from the setting-apart, the moment one possession stopped being for sale.
