# Process: Conscience, the Core

## Taxonomy

[Conscience](process_conscience.md), the part where being moral is central to who a person is.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: conscience

## Initiated by

The persona holds being moral close to the centre of who it is, and that centrality pulls on its conduct.
The Core starts wherever morality anchors the self -- the [moral identity](@chbrain/khai-engine-moral-identity/position_moral_identity.md) a persona carries, [internalized](@chbrain/khai-engine-moral-identity/position_internalized.md) as the true self or worn as a [symbolic](@chbrain/khai-engine-moral-identity/position_symbolic.md) image before others, made a [custodian](@chbrain/khai-engine-moral-identity/position_custodian.md) of standards, or held only at the [periphery](@chbrain/khai-engine-moral-identity/position_peripheral.md).
It is the anchor of the moral self: not what a persona holds moral nor how it keeps its balance, but how much being moral is who it is.

## Direction

Toward the conduct pulled by how central morality is to the self.
The Core reads the persona anchored in its own morality -- the more being moral is internal to the self, the harder its pull on conduct, and the more it is a symbol worn outward, the more it bends to being seen as moral rather than to being it.
It moves the persona from morality as one attribute among many to morality as the self's centre or its edge: the anchor whose depth sets how much the conduct answers to it.

## Lever

The pull of a moral self-image against the shallowness of a morality held only for show.
The carrying force is that a persona anchored in being moral is governed by it -- the internalized self-image sanctioning its own conduct, the custodian holding a standard it will not cross; the resistance is the morality kept peripheral or merely symbolic, an image maintained for others that does not reach the acts done unseen.
The Core is the moral self at its most foundational: the centrality that decides whether a persona's conduct is truly ruled by its morality or only dressed in it.

## Echo

The persona anchored, deeply or shallowly, in being moral.
The Core leaves the persona set on how central morality is to it -- the self ruled by its moral image or only adorned with it -- and hands that anchor to the mandate that will harden it and the ledger that will trade against it.
It is the moral self's foundation: the depth at which being moral is who a person is, and so the depth at which their conduct answers to it.
The Core is dated from the anchoring, the moment being moral took its place at the centre or the edge of who the persona is.
