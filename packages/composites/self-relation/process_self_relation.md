# Process: Self-Relation

## Taxonomy

A composite reading over three atom engines (no canon file of its own): the self-esteem, self-compassion, and authenticity engines this package declares as dependencies.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: self-relation

## Initiated by

A persona relates to its own self -- and it does so in more than one way at once.
It evaluates its worth, extends or withholds care from itself in suffering, and lives from or against its own truth; these are three modes of one relationship, the self's stance toward itself.
The worth-judgment is [self-esteem](@chbrain/khai-engine-self-esteem/position_self_esteem.md); the warmth toward one's own pain is [self-compassion](@chbrain/khai-engine-self-compassion/process_self_compassion.md); the alignment of the shown self with the felt one is [authenticity](@chbrain/khai-engine-authenticity/process_authenticity.md).
No single engine sees that a whole self-relation is their weave, nor that the three rest on different footings; that is what this composite is for.

## Direction

Toward the self, in three stances at once -- evaluating it, caring for it, being true to it.
Self-relation does not reduce to one mode: a persona can prize its worth while unable to comfort its pain, or be kind to itself while living a false life, or true to itself while judging itself harshly.
The direction is the self held as an object of relationship, met along all three modes together, their particular combination making the persona's overall stance toward itself.

## Lever

What the composite spends -- the assumption that relating to oneself is one thing.
The folk picture runs the self-relation on a single dial, self-regard high or low; the composite spends that picture, splitting it into three modes that vary independently and rest on different footings -- worth staked on success and comparison is fragile, care extended unconditionally is stable, truth to the self is its own ground -- so that two personas with the same self-esteem can relate to themselves utterly differently.
The lever is in the plurality: the stance is a weave, not a level.

## Echo

The self is related to along all three modes -- and how it fares, especially under failure, depends on which footing it rests on.
The atoms each run their own mode and cannot see the family: the [modes](process_self_relation_modes.md) where the three are held together as one stance, the [fault](process_self_relation_fault.md) where they diverge most sharply under a shortfall, and the [weave](process_self_relation_weave.md) where they enable or undercut one another.
The content of who the persona is, the self-concept, is the identity engine's; the self-evaluative emotions of guilt, shame, and pride are the self-conscious composite's; the disordered grandiose-and-fragile self-relation is the narcissism engine's. This composite owns the healthy structure of how a self relates to itself -- the three modes, their stabilities, and their weave.
