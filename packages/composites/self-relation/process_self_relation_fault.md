# Process: Self-Relation, Fault

## Taxonomy

[Self-Relation](process_self_relation.md), the bridge where the modes diverge under failure.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: self-relation

## Initiated by

A shortfall -- a failure, a rejection, an inadequacy exposed -- meeting the persona's self-relation and finding its fault line.
The atoms each answer the failure in their own mode -- [self-esteem](@chbrain/khai-engine-self-esteem/position_self_esteem.md) reckons whether worth survives it, [self-compassion](@chbrain/khai-engine-self-compassion/process_self_compassion.md) whether care is extended through it, [authenticity](@chbrain/khai-engine-authenticity/process_authenticity.md) whether the failure is owned truly; none, alone, sees that failure is exactly where the three modes come apart.
This is the stress test of self-relation: the moment the different footings of the three modes show, because a shortfall loads each differently.

## Direction

Toward the fracture -- where the modes that seemed one under success split under failure.
When worth was staked on the very success that failed, contingent self-esteem collapses (Crocker & Wolfe); self-compassion, whose care needs no success, holds and can catch the fall (Neff & Vonk); and authenticity is forced to a choice -- own the failure as one's own, or defend the image the failure threatens.
Its direction is the divergence, the three modes pulling apart under the load a shortfall puts on each.

## Lever

What the fault bridge spends -- the illusion that a self-relation is one stable thing.
Under success the three modes can look identical, all reading as high self-regard; failure spends that illusion, revealing which footing the persona actually rested on -- the fragile worth that needed the win, the stable care that did not, the authenticity that either deepens by owning the failure or is spent to protect the image.
A production holds this lever by putting a persona through a real failure and letting the modes visibly diverge -- the self-esteem that cracks, the self-compassion that holds, the authenticity forced to choose -- rather than reading its self-relation only in good times.

## Echo

The failure finds the fault, and the persona's true self-relation shows -- which modes held, which broke, which had to be sacrificed to save another.
This is why self-compassion buffers where self-esteem cannot: the one whose worth was contingent is undone by the failure, the one who can be kind to itself meets the same failure and remains whole (Leary; Neff & Vonk).
How the modes, having diverged, then act on one another -- the self-compassion that rescues the collapsed esteem, the image-defense that costs the authenticity -- is the [weave](process_self_relation_weave.md); the fault only exposes where they part.
