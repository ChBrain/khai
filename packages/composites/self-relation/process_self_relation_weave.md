# Process: Self-Relation, Weave

## Taxonomy

[Self-Relation](process_self_relation.md), the bridge where the modes enable or undercut one another.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: self-relation

## Initiated by

The three modes running not side by side but on one another.
The atoms read their own states -- [self-esteem](@chbrain/khai-engine-self-esteem/position_self_esteem.md)'s judgment, [self-compassion](@chbrain/khai-engine-self-compassion/process_self_compassion.md)'s warmth, [authenticity](@chbrain/khai-engine-authenticity/process_authenticity.md)'s alignment; what none can read is how each conditions the others.
Self-compassion makes authenticity safer -- one can afford to see and show the real self when one will not be attacked for what it finds; authenticity can cost contingent self-esteem -- living one's truth risks the approval that staked worth depends on; and a self-esteem bent on protecting its image can block both the honest self-view authenticity needs and the kindness self-compassion offers.

## Direction

Among the modes -- each strengthening or straining the others over time.
The weave runs the interactions: self-compassion underwriting authenticity, authenticity pressuring image-based esteem, image-defense starving both the honest look and the kind word -- a self-relation settling, over a life, into a configuration where the modes have shaped one another.
Its direction is the mutual conditioning, the stance the three modes make by acting on each other rather than in parallel.

## Lever

What the weave spends -- the assumption that the modes are independent.
The modes bridge held them separable; the weave spends that separability at the level of dynamics, showing that though they vary independently they do not run independently -- a change in one shifts the others, so that cultivating self-compassion can free authenticity, and staking worth on an image can corrode both.
A production holds this lever by letting one mode move another across the arc -- the persona who, learning to be kind to itself, can finally be honest with itself; the one whose need to protect its worth slowly hollows its authenticity.

## Echo

The self-relation settles into a configuration the modes have woven -- not three independent readings but a structure where each has shaped the others.
This is the composite's deepest claim: that a healthy self-relation is not maximal self-esteem but a weave in which self-compassion gives a stable floor, authenticity gives an honest object, and worth-judgment sits atop rather than under them (Kernis's optimal, secure self-esteem; Neff & Vonk).
Where the weave curdles into the grandiose self-image defended against a fragile true worth, the self-relation is the narcissism engine's to model; this composite owns the healthy weave, of which that pathology is the collapse.
