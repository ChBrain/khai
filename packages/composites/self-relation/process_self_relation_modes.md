# Process: Self-Relation, Modes

## Taxonomy

[Self-Relation](process_self_relation.md), the bridge where the three modes are held together as one stance.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: self-relation

## Initiated by

A persona met with itself along all three modes at once.
Each atom answers as if its mode ran alone -- [self-esteem](@chbrain/khai-engine-self-esteem/position_self_esteem.md) judging worth, [self-compassion](@chbrain/khai-engine-self-compassion/process_self_compassion.md) extending warmth, [authenticity](@chbrain/khai-engine-authenticity/process_authenticity.md) aligning truth; none, alone, sees that a persona's stance toward itself is their combination.
The modes vary independently: high worth with low self-kindness, warm self-care with a false life, hard self-judgment with fierce authenticity -- each pairing a different way of being with oneself.

## Direction

Toward the self as one object met three ways.
The modes bridge holds evaluate, care, and be-true side by side rather than collapsing them: it reads the persona's self-relation as the particular chord the three strike together, so that the whole stance is legible only across all three, not from any one.
Its direction is the composite self-stance, the three modes summed into how a persona is with itself.

## Lever

What the modes bridge spends -- the single dial of self-regard.
Reduce self-relation to one axis and most of it is lost: the composite spends that reduction, showing that worth-esteem, self-warmth, and self-truth are separable, so a persona high on one can be low on another, and the interesting cases are precisely the mismatches -- the admired achiever who cannot be kind to itself, the self-accepting person living someone else's life.
A production holds this lever by giving a persona a real profile across the three, not a single self-esteem reading, so its way of being with itself is specific.

## Echo

The self-relation is read as a chord, not a note -- the persona's stance toward itself specific to how its three modes combine.
Which combination it is matters most when it is tested: the same three modes that read as a static profile here diverge under a shortfall, at the [fault](process_self_relation_fault.md), and shape one another over time, in the [weave](process_self_relation_weave.md).
The modes bridge only lays the three side by side; that they rest on different footings, and act on one another, is what the other bridges carry.
