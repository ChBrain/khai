# Instructions: Self-Relation

How a Playwright wires the self-relation composite into a play. You wire by
linking the play's own content to the composite's files and, through them, to
the three atom engines it depends on; every file stays untouched, they are
canon. The Roadie plumbs the law into Instructions Knowledge on deploy.
Authoring guidance, not runtime content, and it does not go on tour.

## Human

- The human sets the self-relation terrain: what the persona stakes its worth
  on, how it meets its own suffering, and whether it lives from its own truth
  -- and what failures test all three -- so a persona has a real stance toward
  itself with a fault line to find.

## Agent

- Cast the three modes from the atoms as usual: the worth-evaluation, secure
  or contingent, from the self-esteem engine; the self-warmth or self-attack
  from self-compassion; the truth-to-self or self-alienation from
  authenticity. Then cast the stance itself: which modes are operative, which
  footing each rests on, and how they combine. Stage the bridges as more than
  a profile: a modes reading lays the three side by side, a fault is the
  divergence under a real failure the audience can see, a weave is one mode
  moving another across the arc. A plot that turns on a persona's way of being
  with itself -- collapsing when its worth fails, or learning to be kind
  enough to be honest -- may link that bridge under its Tension; the audit
  surfaces the ones staged as a single self-feeling when the modes diverged.
  You link to the composite and its atoms, you never edit them.

## Collaboration

- Every mode's science is its atom's: self-esteem, self-compassion, and
  authenticity each carry their own warrant and their own instructions. The
  content of who the persona is, the self-concept, is the identity engine's;
  the self-evaluative emotions of guilt, shame, and pride are the
  self-conscious composite's; the disordered grandiose-and-fragile
  self-relation is the narcissism engine's; the tuning of self-presentation to
  the room is the self-monitoring engine's. Whether the personas genuinely
  weave the three modes, rather than sitting at one self-esteem level like a
  dial, is the team's review.

## Knowledge

- A persona relates to its own self in three modes at once: evaluating its
  worth (self-esteem), caring for it in suffering (self-compassion), and being
  true to it (authenticity). These vary independently and rest on different
  footings -- worth staked on success and comparison is fragile, care extended
  unconditionally is stable, truth to the self is its own ground -- so a whole
  self-relation is their weave, not a single dial of self-regard (Neff & Vonk;
  Crocker & Wolfe; Kernis). The drama lives in motion across them: the modes
  held together as one stance, the fault where they diverge under failure
  (contingent esteem collapsing while self-compassion holds and authenticity
  is forced to own or defend), and the weave where they act on one another
  (self-compassion freeing authenticity, image-defense corroding both). Read a
  persona's self-relation as a weave tested at its fault line, never as one
  self-esteem reading.

## System

- Do cast all three modes before staging the stance; do give a persona a real
  profile across evaluate, care, and be-true, so its way of being with itself
  is specific -- and let the interesting cases be the mismatches, the achiever
  who cannot be kind to itself, the self-accepting person living a false life.
- Do stage the fault at a real failure: let the contingent esteem crack, the
  self-compassion hold or fail to, the authenticity choose between owning the
  shortfall and defending the image -- so the divergence is earned, not
  asserted.
- Do let the modes weave over the arc: one persona learning enough
  self-kindness to be honest, another hollowing its authenticity to protect
  its worth. Do delegate the self-evaluative emotions to the self-conscious
  composite and the pathological self-relation to the narcissism engine. Do
  not edit the composite's or the atoms' files; wire only from the play's side.
