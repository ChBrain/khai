# Self-Relation: Reference

## Line of Work

The self's stance toward itself as **composite**: the three modes in which a persona relates to its own self -- evaluating its worth, caring for it in suffering, and being true to it -- read as one weave. No single atom engine sees the whole, because each authors one mode and the stance is a property of their combination. The worth-judgment is the self-esteem engine's; the compassionate warmth toward one's own pain is the self-compassion engine's; the alignment of the shown self with the felt one is the authenticity engine's. This package authors none of those again. It declares the three engines as dependencies, wires their members by hard package links, and adds only what the atoms cannot hold: that a whole self-relation is their weave, that the three rest on different footings, and the dynamics among them -- the modes held together, the fault where they diverge under failure, and the weave where they enable or undercut one another.

The warrant is integrative, not primary: the sources below establish that these modes of self-relation are distinct, differ in stability, and interact, not the individual constructs, which their own engines warrant. The spine is Neff and Vonk's demonstration that self-compassion and global self-esteem are two different ways of relating to oneself -- self-esteem an evaluation of worth, often contingent and comparative and so fragile under failure; self-compassion an unconditional care that holds precisely when one falls short and buffers the self where esteem cannot. Crocker and Wolfe's contingencies of self-worth explain the fragility: self-esteem staked on domains of success rises and falls with them. Kernis's work on optimal, secure self-esteem, and Kernis and Goldman's on authenticity, tie the evaluative mode to the truth-to-self mode -- secure self-esteem is the kind that does not need defending, and authenticity is part of what makes it secure. The third mode, living from the true self, is the authenticity engine's, rooted in Rogers and Deci and Ryan. Together they make a self-relation legible not as one dial of self-regard but as a weave of evaluate, care, and be-true, most revealing where the modes diverge -- under failure -- and where they shape one another over a life. It is the self-relation counterpart within the self family: where each atom engine owns one mode, this composite reads the stance the three compose.

## Restrictions

What the composite refuses to model, and to whom it delegates.

- **The three atoms**: The composite authors no mode of its own. Self-esteem -- the evaluation of the self's worth, earned and comparative -- is the self-esteem engine's, whole; self-compassion -- the unconditional warmth toward one's own suffering -- is the self-compassion engine's; authenticity -- living and acting from the true self -- is the authenticity engine's. This package reads over them and adds only the weave and its dynamics; every mode is an atom's ground, cited by hard link, never restated.
- **Identity**: The composite models how a persona relates to its self, not the content of that self. Who the persona is -- the self-concept, its commitments and its story -- is owned by the identity engine; self-relation is the stance turned upon that self, and takes the self's content as given, reading only the modes of relating to it.
- **The self-conscious composite**: The composite models the standing modes of self-relation, not the self-evaluative emotions. Guilt, shame, and pride -- the acute emotions of appraising the self against a standard -- are read by the self-conscious composite; self-relation is the standing stance beneath such episodes, the evaluative, compassionate, and truthful footings from which those emotions are met, not the emotions themselves.
- **Narcissism**: The composite models the healthy structure of self-relation, not its pathological collapse. The grandiose self-image defended against a fragile true worth -- the disordered weave where esteem is inflated, self-compassion absent, and authenticity sacrificed to the image -- is owned by the narcissism engine; this composite owns the healthy weave, of which narcissism is one failure mode.
- **Self-monitoring**: The composite models the modes of relating to the self, not the tuning of self-presentation. How a persona adjusts how it comes across to fit the room is owned by the self-monitoring engine; self-relation is turned inward, on the self, not outward on its presentation.

## Encoding

Source to constraint, per file.

- **[self-relation](process_self_relation.md)** (the root): The self's stance toward itself as three modes -- evaluate, care, be-true -- woven into one. Anchored by Neff & Vonk (the two ways) extended by authenticity as the third mode.
- **[self-relation, modes](process_self_relation_modes.md)** (bridge): The three modes held together and varying independently, so the whole stance is their combination, not any one. Anchored by Neff & Vonk (self-esteem and self-compassion as distinct) and Harter (the self-system).
- **[self-relation, fault](process_self_relation_fault.md)** (bridge): The modes diverging under failure -- contingent esteem collapsing, self-compassion holding, authenticity forced to own or defend. Anchored by Crocker & Wolfe (contingent self-worth), Neff & Vonk, and Leary et al. (buffering).
- **[self-relation, weave](process_self_relation_weave.md)** (bridge): The modes enabling or undercutting one another -- self-compassion freeing authenticity, image-defense corroding both, secure esteem resting on the other two. Anchored by Kernis (optimal self-esteem) and Kernis & Goldman (authenticity and secure esteem).
