# Instructions: Vigil

How a Playwright wires the vigil composite into a play. You wire by linking the play's own
content to the composite's files; the files stay untouched, they are canon. The composite
installs the jealousy, attachment, and trust engines as dependencies, and their laws arrive
with them. Authoring guidance, not runtime content, and it does not go on tour.

## Human

- The human sets which bonds in the play have a third party near them, whose history decides what that counts as, and what the guarding costs.

## Agent

- From the play's own content, read a persona whose bond has a third party in it and link under Projection what is running: the threshold while a rival's claim is being appraised through a strategy already set by how the base has answered before (from jealousy and attachment), the guard while vigilance, confrontation, or mate-guarding is being mounted over the bond (from jealousy), the proof while reassurance is being sought and a bet is being tested rather than placed (from trust). Read it on the bond and never on the rival, since the rival is the occasion and almost never the cause. You link to the composite, you never edit it.

## Collaboration

- Threat appraisal, the jealous position, and guarding are the jealousy engine's; the four strategies are attachment's; extend, test, withhold, betrayed, and repair are trust's -- one alone is a process, not the episode. A bargain between parties who need not like each other is the dealing composite's, which wires this same trust engine on an agreement where this runs it on a bond; the history that set the strategy is cptsd's and freud's, which wire this same attachment engine on the past where this runs it on tonight; wanting what somebody else has is outdone's, and the boundary is the whole difference -- outdone's persona wants what another holds, vigil's fears losing what they hold; a wrong actually done and reckoned between two is moral-account's, and vigil's premise is that nothing has been established; the desire and disclosure that made the bond are love's; what the other party is concealing and at what cost is opacity's and carrying's. This composite owns the episode -- and the proof bridge carries the keystone: the checking cannot produce what it is looking for.

## Knowledge

- Vigil is a persona read while a bond of theirs has a third party in it -- braiding the threat (jealousy: how real and how close a rival's claim is, the position once it has been read as worth acting on, and the vigilance, confrontation, and mate-guarding that follow) with the setting (attachment: secure, anxious, avoidant, or disorganized, fixed by how the base has answered before) and with the thing actually at stake (trust: a bet placed by accepting vulnerability to somebody whose conduct cannot be controlled). The join is Sharpsteen and Kirkpatrick's: jealousy had been read as a rivalry phenomenon, and they relocated it to the bond, showing that the experience varies with attachment style rather than with the competitor -- so two personas given the same scene are not disagreeing about facts, they are appraising from different histories. It runs in three faces. The threshold is how much of a rival it takes, and the bar was set before the rival arrived: the same late reply is evidence of different weights to two personas, and neither is inventing what they see, since a working model is a summary of a real record and mostly a good one -- but nothing in the model can tell the persona that this bond is a different situation, so the reading arrives fully formed and feels like perception. The guard is what gets done about it, and it is always the wrong thing, because what the persona wants is the bond to be secure and what is available is the schedule, the phone, the seating, and the question asked in a light voice -- so the effort goes into the surroundings of the answer, the relief is real enough to reinforce the behaviour, and the other party receives the guarding as the relationship's content. The proof is the twist: what would settle the question is evidence that the other could have done otherwise and did not, freely and unwatched, with the option genuinely open -- and a persona who closes the option destroys the conditions under which the answer would have counted. Here the three are seen for one thing: the vigilance is read by both parties as the measure of how much the persona cares, which is what licenses it and what makes it so hard to give up, since stopping looks like caring less rather than like trusting more.

## System

- Do read the bond and never the rival: name whether the threshold (a claim being appraised), the guard (a watch being mounted), or the proof (reassurance being sought) is running, and cast each from its engines. A play that makes the third party interesting has staged a triangle instead of this.
- Do set the threshold from the persona's history rather than from the rival's conduct, and make the persona's reading defensible -- a jealousy the audience can dismiss as fantasy is a smaller and different thing.
- Do make sure that whatever the persona asks for, getting it does not help: they must be given the reassurance, must be briefly satisfied, and must be back at the beginning by the next scene.
- Do put the cost on the bond and not on the rival, who is usually unaffected throughout, and let it be paid in instalments neither party is counting.
- Do let the guarding be legible to the other party as a statement about what the persona believes, since a partner told repeatedly that they are suspected acquires a relationship in which they are suspected.
- Do keep the persona sincere: they are not enjoying this and are trying to end it, and each test is offered to themselves as the last one.
- Do not stage a real betrayal here: where the wrong has actually happened, the scene has moved on and belongs to moral-account. Reactive jealousy in the face of a genuine threat is not this mechanism and needs none of it.
- Do not model a bargain being verified (that is dealing, wiring the same engine on an agreement), the history that set the strategy (that is cptsd and freud), wanting what another has (that is outdone), the reckoning after a wrong (that is moral-account), the making of the bond (that is love), or what the other party is concealing (that is opacity and carrying). Do not make a rival stand for a theme (that is the play's pitch). Do not edit the composite's files; wire only from the play's side.
