# Process: Vigil, the Proof

## Taxonomy

[Vigil](process_vigil.md), what the guarding is for, and where the three are seen for one thing.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: vigil

## Initiated by

The persona wants to know, and sets about finding out.
The Proof starts wherever [trust](@chbrain/khai-engine-trust/process_trust.md) is the thing actually at stake -- the bet placed by accepting vulnerability to somebody whose conduct cannot be controlled -- and wherever the persona is [testing](@chbrain/khai-engine-trust/process_test.md) instead of [extending](@chbrain/khai-engine-trust/process_extend.md) it, or [withholding](@chbrain/khai-engine-trust/process_withhold.md) it while they wait for a certainty that is not coming.
It is the face of the composite where the persona gets what they asked for and is no better off.

## Direction

Toward an answer that cannot be made to count.
The Proof reads what the persona is trying to establish and why the method rules it out: what would settle the question is evidence that the other could have done otherwise and did not -- freely, unwatched, with the option genuinely open -- and a persona who has closed the option has destroyed the only conditions under which the answer would have meant anything.
It moves the persona through every available reassurance and leaves them exactly where they started.

## Lever

The certainty the persona is asking for against the vulnerability that would have given it to them.
The carrying force is that the demand is sincere and the persona would accept the answer if it could be made solid: they are not enjoying this, they are trying to end it, and each test is offered to themselves as the last one; the resistance is that a bond cannot be verified into safety -- trust is placed rather than confirmed, and it is placed by leaving something at risk on purpose -- so the persona is looking for reassurance in the one place structurally incapable of supplying it, and the more thoroughly they look, the less there is left to find.
The Proof rides the composite's keystone knife-edge: the checking cannot produce what it is looking for, because what would settle the question is precisely the willingness not to ask it.

## Echo

An answer given, and worth nothing, and asked for again.
It shows the three engines complete -- a threshold set elsewhere, a guard mounted on the surroundings, and a bet the persona will not place -- and carries beneath it the composite's keystone: the vigilance is read by both parties as the measure of how much the persona cares, which is what licenses it and what makes it so hard to give up, since stopping looks like caring less rather than like trusting more.
This is why the Proof is the hardest of the three to stage and the only one worth closing on: the persona must get what they demanded, must be briefly satisfied, and must be back at the beginning by the next scene -- and a play that lets a reassurance land permanently has staged a reconciliation instead of this.
The Proof is the note the whole composite closes on: what a persona could not bring themselves to risk is legible in how much they needed to be told.
The Proof is dated from every time the question was answered.
