# Process: Vigil, the Guard

## Taxonomy

[Vigil](process_vigil.md), the watch mounted over the bond.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: vigil

## Initiated by

The persona has read a threat worth acting on, and starts acting.
The Guard starts wherever [the jealous position](@chbrain/khai-engine-jealousy/position_jealous.md) has settled on the persona and [guarding](@chbrain/khai-engine-jealousy/process_guarding.md) has begun -- the vigilance that reads every arrival and absence, the confrontation that puts the question, and the mate-guarding that works on access rather than on the answer.
It is the face of the composite where the persona is doing something, and it is the wrong something.

## Direction

Toward control of the circumstances, since the disposition cannot be reached.
The Guard reads what the persona is actually operating on, which is never the thing they want: they want the bond to be secure, and what is available to them is the schedule, the phone, the seating, the questions asked in a light voice -- so the effort goes into the surroundings of the answer because the answer itself is somebody else's to give.
It moves the persona into work that can be done, and away from the only thing that would help.

## Lever

The relief of doing something against the cost of what the doing communicates.
The carrying force is that guarding is not irrational and sometimes succeeds at exactly what it aims at: a rival can in fact be kept at a distance, an evening can be sat through, an account can be demanded and given -- and the immediate relief is real, which is why the behaviour is reinforced and returns; the resistance is that every one of these moves is legible to the other party as a statement about what the persona believes, so the bond receives the guarding as its content, and a partner told repeatedly that they are suspected acquires a relationship in which they are suspected.
The Guard spends the bond in order to secure it, in instalments neither party is counting.

## Echo

A watch kept faithfully, and a bond quietly changed by being watched.
It is the bridge where the composite's cost lands, and it lands on the thing being protected rather than on the rival, who is usually unaffected throughout.
What it hands upward is the question the persona thinks all this is for -- whether the bond is safe -- and the fact that they have now made it much harder to answer.
The Guard is dated from the first question asked in a light voice.
