# Process: Vigil

## Taxonomy

What a persona does to a bond once a third party is in the room.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: vigil

## Initiated by

Somebody else has appeared beside a bond the persona already holds.
Vigil starts wherever that is being worked on: a rival's claim appraised and answered, through [jealousy](@chbrain/khai-engine-jealousy/process_jealousy.md); a strategy toward closeness already set by how the base has answered before, through [attachment](@chbrain/khai-engine-attachment/process_attachment.md); and a bet on somebody whose conduct cannot be controlled, through [trust](@chbrain/khai-engine-trust/process_trust.md).
This composite adds no fourth engine. It reads the one thing the three make together, which is that the episode is not about the rival.

## Direction

Toward a reassurance that has to come from the wrong place.
The composite wires the three engines and reads their working in three shapes: [the Threshold](process_vigil_threshold.md), how much of a rival it takes; [the Guard](process_vigil_guard.md), the watch mounted over the bond; and [the Proof](process_vigil_proof.md), the twist -- what the guarding is looking for, and why the looking is what prevents finding it.
The three are not three intensities of jealousy. They are one bond being tested by the person who most wants it to hold.

## Lever

The wish to know the bond is safe against the fact that a bond is a bet and not a fact.
The carrying force is that the persona is not wrong to care: a valued bond genuinely can be lost, a rival genuinely can be real, and reading the room for signs of it is what anybody does with something they cannot afford to lose -- the vigilance is competence turned on the thing it is worst at; the resistance is that the reassurance being sought is of a kind that only arrives unrequested, since a fidelity that has been checked is a fidelity that was not left free, and the persona cannot both secure the answer and let it count.
Vigil is read on the bond and never on the rival, because the rival is the occasion and almost never the cause.

## Echo

A bond held tightly, and worse for it.
Vigil does not close; it is one pass of a threatened bond through its own defence, gathered into one reading -- a threshold, a guard, a proof.
It hands off what it is not: a bargain struck between two parties who need not like each other is the dealing composite's, which wires this same trust engine on an agreement where this runs it on a bond; the developmental damage that set the strategy is cptsd's, and the drive-level account of it is freud's, both of which wire this same attachment engine on the history where this runs it on tonight; a wanting produced by somebody else's advantage is outdone's, which needs no bond of one's own and is the difference between wanting what they have and fearing to lose what one has; a wrong done and reckoned between two is moral-account's, where the betrayal has actually happened; what a persona keeps hidden and at what cost is opacity's and carrying's; the desire and the disclosure that make the bond are love's; and a rival made to **stand for** a theme is authored key, the play's pitch, not the force.
The persona is dated from how the base once answered, and never from the rival's arrival.
