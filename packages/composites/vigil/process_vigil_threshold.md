# Process: Vigil, the Threshold

## Taxonomy

[Vigil](process_vigil.md), how much of a rival it takes.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: vigil

## Initiated by

Something happened that could mean nothing, and the persona has to decide whether it does.
The Threshold starts wherever [threat appraisal](@chbrain/khai-engine-jealousy/process_threat_appraisal.md) is running -- how real and how close a rival's claim on the bond is -- and it runs it through a strategy already in place, since [attachment](@chbrain/khai-engine-attachment/process_attachment.md) has set how this persona moves under threat: [secure](@chbrain/khai-engine-attachment/process_secure.md) and able to wait, [anxious](@chbrain/khai-engine-attachment/process_anxious.md) and reaching harder, [avoidant](@chbrain/khai-engine-attachment/process_avoidant.md) and withdrawing in advance, or [disorganized](@chbrain/khai-engine-attachment/process_disorganized.md) and doing both.
It is the face of the composite where the rival is still ambiguous.

## Direction

Toward a reading of the scene, taken as a reading of the world.
The Threshold reads where the bar sits and where the bar came from: the same late reply, the same warmth toward a colleague, the same unexplained hour is evidence of different weights to two personas, and neither is inventing what they see -- one has a history in which the base answered and one has a history in which it did not, and both are generalising correctly from what they were given.
It moves the persona from a scene to a conclusion, and the distance between them is the composite's real subject.

## Lever

The accuracy of a working model against the fact that it was calibrated somewhere else.
The carrying force is that the model is not a distortion but a summary, and mostly a good one: a persona learns from a record how much closeness can be counted on, and carries the answer forward because carrying it forward is what learning is for -- so a low threshold is a competence in the situation that produced it; the resistance is that the current bond is a different situation, and there is nothing in the model that can tell the persona which of the two they are standing in, so the reading arrives fully formed and feels like perception rather than inference.
The Threshold spends nothing, and decides everything downstream.

## Echo

A rival, now counted, on evidence the persona did not choose how to weigh.
It is the bridge that puts the composite's subject in the right place: this is not a story about how attractive the third party is, and staging it as one loses the mechanism.
What it hands upward is a persona with a conclusion they can defend and did not reason their way to, and a bond that now needs doing something about.
The Threshold is dated from the persona's history, and not from the evening in question.
