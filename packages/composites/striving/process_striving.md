# Process: Striving

## Taxonomy

The standing character a persona brings to pursuit.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: striving

## Initiated by

A persona is read not only by the goal in front of it but by the character it brings to any goal -- why it strives, how it strives, and how long it stays.
Striving starts wherever a persona's dispositions shape its pursuit before the particular aim is set -- the [achievement motive](@chbrain/khai-engine-achievement-motive/position_achievement_motive.md) that energizes which goals it adopts, the [regulatory focus](@chbrain/khai-engine-regulatory-focus/position_regulatory_focus.md) by which it pursues them, and the [grit](@chbrain/khai-engine-grit/position_grit.md) by which it holds on.
This composite adds no new drive; it reads what the three make together: the standing shape of a self's striving, the character that would show on any goal it took up.

## Direction

Toward the whole standing character of a pursuit, read through its dispositions.
The composite wires the three engines and reads their working in three shapes: [the Pull](process_striving_pull.md), why a persona strives; [the Stance](process_striving_stance.md), how it strives; and [the Stay](process_striving_stay.md), the twist -- how long it stays, and what the staying costs.
A persona does not simply want and pursue: it is drawn or driven toward the attempt, it chases in eagerness or in guard, and it holds on for a season or for years -- three standing dispositions that would colour any goal it chose.

## Lever

The persona's standing striving character against the assumption that the goal explains the pursuit.
The carrying force is that how a self pursues is set before the goal arrives -- the motive that made it reach at all, the orientation that shapes the reaching, the stamina that decides how long it lasts; the resistance is the reflex to read a pursuit from its object alone, as though anyone with the same aim would chase it the same way.
Striving is read wherever a persona is granted its dispositional character: not a self defined by what it wants but by how it wants -- the pull, the stance, and the stay it would bring to anything.

## Echo

The persona revealed as a self with a standing way of pursuing.
Striving does not close; it is the persona going on with its own character of pursuit, gathering the three engines into one reading -- a self pulled toward or away, chasing eagerly or vigilantly, staying briefly or for years.
It hands off what it is not: the crossing from wanting to doing -- the goal deliberated, the if-then plan, the executive control that holds the pursuit -- is the volition composite's; the moment-to-moment override of an impulse is the self-control engine's; the belief that ability can grow is the mindset engine's; the particular goal a persona holds is the goal engine's; and the striving made to stand for a story is authored key, the play's pitch, not the force.
The persona is dated from its own manner of pursuit, and read as a self whose way of striving would show on any goal it took.
