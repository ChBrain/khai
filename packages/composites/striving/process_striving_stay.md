# Process: Striving, the Stay

## Taxonomy

[Striving](process_striving.md), the disposition that sets how long a persona holds on, and what the holding costs.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: striving

## Initiated by

The persona keeps at a distant goal through setback, plateau, and the years, and the same stamina that carries it will not let it leave.
The Stay starts wherever [grit](@chbrain/khai-engine-grit/position_grit.md) holds a persona to a far aim -- the [passion](@chbrain/khai-engine-grit/position_passion.md) kept steady across years without wandering to a newer interest, and the [perseverance](@chbrain/khai-engine-grit/position_perseverance.md) of effort held through the setback, the plateau, and the long unrewarding middle.
It is the endurance of striving: not why a persona strives nor how, but how long it stays -- the marathon rather than the sprint.

## Direction

Toward the far goal held across time, and the alternatives foreclosed by holding it.
The Stay reads the persona enduring -- effort held where results have stopped coming, interest held where a newer one beckons, the same aim carried year after year -- and what that endurance forecloses: every path not taken while this one was kept, every signal to change direction outlasted rather than heeded.
It moves the persona through the long middle where achievement is actually made, and past the exit it does not take.

## Lever

The stamina that outlasts setback against the same stamina that cannot quit.
The carrying force is a self that does not stop -- passion steady, effort renewed, the setback treated as weather rather than verdict, and it is this and not talent that carries most distant goals home; the resistance is that the disposition has no brake: the persona who cannot be discouraged by a hard year cannot be informed by one either, and the perseverance that refuses a false signal to quit refuses the true one identically.
The Stay rides the composite's keystone knife-edge: the stamina that makes achievement possible is the stamina that holds a persona to a dead path -- the virtue and the trap one disposition, faithful to the lost cause exactly as faithfully as to the live one.

## Echo

The persona still at it, carried and held by the same endurance.
It shows a persona holding a distant aim through setback and plateau -- the passion unmoved, the effort renewed -- and carries beneath it the composite's keystone: that a self able to persist past discouragement is a self unable to read discouragement as information, so the grit that wins the long game is the grit that will not leave the losing one.
The Stay is the note the whole composite closes on: striving is not a virtue that only rewards but a standing character with a cost, and the persona that cannot quit is the persona that cannot redirect.
The Stay is dated from the holding, the moment the persona kept to the aim past the point where leaving became possible.
