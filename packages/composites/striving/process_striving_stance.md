# Process: Striving, the Stance

## Taxonomy

[Striving](process_striving.md), the disposition that sets how a persona pursues.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: striving

## Initiated by

The persona takes up a goal, and a standing orientation decides whether it chases a gain or guards a loss.
The Stance starts wherever the [regulatory focus](@chbrain/khai-engine-regulatory-focus/position_regulatory_focus.md) shapes the pursuit -- [promotion](@chbrain/khai-engine-regulatory-focus/position_promotion.md), the eagerness for gains to reach and advancement to make, or [prevention](@chbrain/khai-engine-regulatory-focus/position_prevention.md), the vigilance against losses to guard and duties not to fail.
It is the manner of the chase: not why a persona strives nor how long it stays, but the orientation by which the same goal is pursued two entirely different ways.

## Direction

Toward the goal pursued in eagerness or in guard, and the strategies each brings.
The Stance reads the persona chasing by its orientation -- the promotion-focused reaching wide, trying more, accepting a miss to catch a hit; the prevention-focused holding the line, checking the flank, accepting a smaller gain to avoid an error -- the same objective approached as an advance to make or a position to keep.
It moves the persona from having a goal to having a way with it: the strategy, the risk taken, the error it would rather make, all set by which side of the orientation it stands on.

## Lever

The eagerness that reaches against the vigilance that guards.
The carrying force is a standing orientation that fits some pursuits and not others -- eagerness serving the goal that rewards reach and tolerance of misses, vigilance serving the goal that punishes error and rewards holding; the resistance is the mismatch, the persona whose stance does not fit the task, reaching where care was needed or guarding where boldness was, its own manner working against the aim it holds.
The Stance is striving at its most strategic: the disposition that makes a persona good at one kind of pursuit and clumsy at its opposite.

## Echo

The goal pursued in the persona's own manner, eager or vigilant.
The Stance leaves the persona chasing in its standing orientation -- reaching for the gain or guarding against the loss -- and carries the striving's shared shape: a disposition that would colour any goal, here the way the chase is run.
It is the manner of pursuit: the orientation that decides what a persona tries, what it risks, and which failure it can least abide.
The Stance is dated from the orientation, the moment the persona's standing focus set how the goal would be chased.
