# Process: Striving, the Pull

## Taxonomy

[Striving](process_striving.md), the disposition that sets why a persona strives at all.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: striving

## Initiated by

The persona meets a situation where it could succeed or fail, and a standing motive decides whether it reaches for the attempt or shrinks from it.
The Pull starts wherever the [achievement motive](@chbrain/khai-engine-achievement-motive/position_achievement_motive.md) energizes a persona in an achievement situation -- the [hope of success](@chbrain/khai-engine-achievement-motive/position_hope_of_success.md) that draws it toward the attempt, or the [fear of failure](@chbrain/khai-engine-achievement-motive/position_fear_of_failure.md) that pulls it away from the risk of falling short.
It is the motive beneath the goal: not how a persona pursues nor how long it holds, but why it engages the attempt at all.

## Direction

Toward the goals a persona will even adopt, sorted by what its motive seeks or avoids.
The Pull reads the persona energized before the choosing -- the one drawn by hope of success reaching for the goal that can be won and shows something, the one held by fear of failure choosing the safe task that cannot embarrass or the impossible one that excuses the fall.
It moves the persona from a field of possible goals to the ones its motive permits: the aims adopted, ducked, or set at a level that protects it from the verdict of failing.

## Lever

The pull toward the attempt against the pull away from the verdict of failing.
The carrying force is a standing motive that makes achievement matter -- the wish to succeed, to meet the standard, to find out what one can do; the resistance is the other pole of the same disposition, the fear of falling short and being judged by it, which does not remove the striving but bends it, into tasks too easy to fail or too hard to be blamed for.
The Pull is striving at its most originating: the disposition that decides whether a persona enters the contest, and on what terms it dares to.

## Echo

The persona drawn to the attempt, or steering around the risk of falling short.
The Pull leaves the persona energized toward or away from achievement -- the goals it adopts, avoids, or pitches to protect itself -- and hands that motive to the stance that will shape the pursuit and the stay that will test it.
It is the disposition beneath the goal: the reason a persona reaches at all, and the fear that can make the reaching a hedge.
The Pull is dated from the energizing, the moment a persona's standing motive decided whether the attempt was worth making.
