# Process: Carrying

## Taxonomy

What it costs a persona to hold an attribute that would discredit them.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: carrying

## Initiated by

A persona has something about them that, in the world they are standing in, counts against them.
Carrying starts wherever that is being borne: as the attribute and the discounting it draws, through [stigma](@chbrain/khai-engine-stigma/piece_stigma.md); as information about oneself that can be held or let go, through [secret](@chbrain/khai-engine-secret/piece_secret.md); and as what the attribute does inside and how it comes out, through [shame](@chbrain/khai-engine-shame/process_shame.md).
This composite adds no fourth engine. It stages the split the stigma atom already declares -- the mark that is known against the mark that could be -- and reads them as different work rather than as different amounts of the same trouble.

## Direction

Toward a persona living alongside a fact about themselves, by whichever arrangement the fact allows.
The composite wires the three engines and reads their working in three shapes: [the Mark](process_carrying_mark.md), the attribute known; [the Tell](process_carrying_tell.md), the attribute changing hands; and [the Keeping](process_carrying_keeping.md), the twist -- the attribute held.
The three are not stages and not severities. They are three arrangements a persona can be in with respect to one fact, set by who knows it and by whether it could be hidden at all.

## Lever

The weight of a fact that counts against a persona, against the fact that none of the weight is in the fact.
The carrying force is that the discrediting is real and is made entirely by the setting: the same attribute is unremarkable in one room and disqualifying in the next, and the persona carrying it did not set which room they are in, so the cost is imposed and the bearer is not its author; the resistance is that this is invisible to almost everyone including, often, the bearer, who experiences a local judgement as a fact about themselves.
Carrying is read on the persona holding the attribute, and never on the attribute: nothing about a mark makes it one.

## Echo

A persona and a fact about them, in one of three arrangements.
Carrying does not close; it is the holding running, gathered into one reading -- a mark, a tell, a keeping.
It hands off what it is not: the boundary a group keeps and what it costs to be inside or outside it is the membership composite's, which wires this same stigma engine and reads it from the group's side where this reads it from the bearer's; the truth-status of what a self lets through -- withheld, feigned, or revealed -- is the opacity composite's, whose secret may be about anything, where this one is always about the persona and always discrediting; the self-evaluative emotions in their own right, the guilt and the pride beside the shame, are the self-conscious composite's; the answer a room gives a persona who has made a claim on it is the regard composite's, which reads what came back where this reads what was brought in; the general labour of being taken as one means to be is the presentation composite's; the rank a persona holds is standing's; the injury done by a childhood of it is the cptsd composite's; and a mark made to **stand for** a story is authored key, the play's pitch, not the force.
The persona is dated from who knows, and from what that leaves them doing.
