# Instructions: Carrying

How a Playwright wires the carrying composite into a play. You wire by linking the
play's own content to the composite's files; the files stay untouched, they are canon.
The composite installs the stigma, secret, and shame engines as dependencies, and their
laws arrive with them. Authoring guidance, not runtime content, and it does not go on
tour.

## Human

- The human sets which personas carry something the world of the play counts against them, whether it shows, and who knows -- and, the question that governs the read, which arrangement they are in.

## Agent

- From the play's own content, read a persona holding a discrediting attribute and link the arrangement under Projection: the mark while it is known and the discounting is running in the encounter itself (the discrediting that discounts what the bearer says before they say it, met from whatever stance the bearer has taken, from stigma), the tell while it is changing hands (the disclosure the keeper frames and times, or the exposure that takes the framing away, from secret -- then the shame going somewhere through the compass: withdrawal, attack on the self, avoidance, or attack on whoever saw), the keeping while it is held (the passing, the concealment, tuned by a standing shame proneness). Keep the attribute discrediting in the world of the play rather than in yours: nothing about a fact makes it a mark. You link to the composite, you never edit it.

## Collaboration

- The mark, the bearer's stance, the discrediting, and the passing are the stigma engine's; the keeper, the concealment, the disclosure, and the exposure are secret's; the compass and its four directions are shame's -- one alone is a process, not the arrangement. The group's boundary is the membership composite's, which wires this same stigma engine from the group's side where this reads it from the bearer's, so a play may run both; the truth-status of what a self lets through is opacity's, whose secret may be about anything where this one is always about the persona and always discrediting; the self-evaluative emotions in their own right are self-conscious's; the answer a room gives to a claim is regard's, which reads what came back where this reads what was brought in; the general labour of being taken as one means to be is presentation's; the rank is standing's; the injury of a childhood of it is cptsd's. This composite owns the holding -- and the keeping bridge carries the keystone: the capacity to hide is not the light case, it is the unfinished one.

## Knowledge

- Carrying is a persona read for what it costs them to hold an attribute that would discredit them -- braiding the mark and the discounting it draws (stigma), the machinery of holding information about oneself (secret), and what the attribute does inside and how it comes out (shame). The join is Goffman's and the stigma engine already carries it: a discrediting attribute comes in two conditions that make entirely different demands. Where it is evident, the discredited case, the bearer's problem is managing tension in encounters that are already informed. Where it is not evident but could become so, the discreditable case, the problem is managing information -- not how to be treated but who is told and when. Link and Phelan supply what makes a mark at all: labelling, stereotyping, separation, status loss, and discrimination co-occurring in a power situation, which is why nothing about an attribute makes it discrediting -- the same fact is unremarkable in one room and disqualifying in the next, and the bearer did not set which room they are in. It runs in three arrangements, by who knows. The mark is the attribute already known: the discounting runs through the encounter itself, discounting what the bearer says before they have said it, and it is levied in employment, in housing, in medicine, and in every careful kindness -- heavy, external, continuous, and no stance the bearer takes removes it, since it is the room's act. But the question is closed. Nothing is being managed, because nothing can be, and there are others who share it who can be found. The tell is the attribute changing hands, by a disclosure the bearer authors, times, and addresses, or by an exposure that supplies none of those -- and then the shame goes somewhere, out through the compass as withdrawal, as attack on the self that agrees with the verdict, as avoidance that will not have it be about that, or as attack on whoever saw. It runs one way and cannot be taken back. The keeping is the twist: the attribute held, so the persona carries not a mark but a question, asked again in every room -- the story edited on the way to telling it, the question anticipated three exchanges out, the calendar arranged so two rooms never meet, the invitation declined for a reason invented on the spot. None of it is a crisis and all of it is happening. The protection is real and where the room is not safe the keeping is the correct move; what it lacks is any condition under which it is finished. A mark is settled once; a keeping is settled per encounter, forever, with the roster only growing, and it takes from the bearer the one resource the marked bearer has, since the company of others who share it can only be joined by telling. Read a persona by what they are doing when nothing is happening.

## System

- Do read the arrangement, not the attribute: name whether the mark (known), the tell (changing hands), or the keeping (held) is running, and cast each from its engines.
- Do make the room do the discrediting: the same fact is unremarkable elsewhere, so a play in which the attribute is intrinsically shameful has staged the author's judgement rather than a setting's.
- Do let the mark be the expensive settled case: heavy in every encounter and requiring no management, with others who share it -- not a lesser version of the keeping.
- Do keep the tell irreversible and its reception unknown: the bearer has rehearsed the worst version many times and is a poor judge, and the same sentence lands as nothing, as a bond, or as the start of the discounting.
- Do let the keeping carry the keystone: give it no scene and no incident, and put the work in rooms where nothing appears to be occurring -- the tell is a persona doing a great deal in a conversation about nothing.
- Do not resolve the keeping by having the persona simply tell, unless the play has also made the room safe to tell in: an unsafe disclosure staged as relief is a play arguing with its own setting.
- Do not claim the concealable case is worse than the visible one: it is a different job, without a finish, and which costs more depends on the attribute, the setting, and whether telling would be safe.
- Do not model the group's boundary (that is membership, wiring the same engine from the other side -- link both where a play wants both), the truth-status of what is let through (that is opacity), the self-evaluative emotions in their own right (that is self-conscious), the room's answer (that is regard), the general labour of being seen (that is presentation), the rank (that is standing), or the developmental injury (that is cptsd). Do not make a mark stand for a story (that is the play's pitch). Do not edit the composite's files; wire only from the play's side.
