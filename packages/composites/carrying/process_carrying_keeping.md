# Process: Carrying, the Keeping

## Taxonomy

[Carrying](process_carrying.md), the attribute held, and where the three are seen for one thing.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: carrying

## Initiated by

The attribute does not show, so the persona is not carrying a mark but a question, and it is asked again in every room.
The Keeping starts wherever [passing](@chbrain/khai-engine-stigma/process_passing.md) is running -- the bearer taken for someone who has no such attribute -- and it runs on the machinery of [concealment](@chbrain/khai-engine-secret/process_concealment.md), the work of holding information about oneself, tuned by a standing [shame proneness](@chbrain/khai-engine-shame/position_shame_proneness.md) that sets how loudly the thing being held reads from inside.
It is the face of the composite that looks, from every angle, like the lucky one.

## Direction

Toward a life conducted with a permanent second task running underneath it.
The Keeping reads the labour rather than the fear -- the story edited on the way to telling it, the question anticipated three exchanges out, the friendship kept at a distance that will not require it, the calendar arranged so two rooms never meet, the invitation declined for a reason invented on the spot -- and reads that none of it is a crisis and all of it is happening, continuously, in encounters where nothing whatever appears to be occurring.
It moves the persona through their days with part of them permanently assigned elsewhere.

## Lever

The protection the concealment genuinely buys against the terms on which it is bought.
The carrying force is real and should not be talked down: the discounting is avoided, and where the room is not safe the keeping is the correct move and may be the only one; the resistance is what the arrangement lacks, which is any condition under which it is finished. A mark is settled once. A keeping is settled per encounter, forever, with the number of people to be managed only ever rising -- and it takes from the bearer the one thing the marked bearer has, which is other people who know, since the company of the like-marked can only be joined by telling.
The Keeping rides the composite's keystone knife-edge: this is not the lighter case, it is the unfinished one, and its cost is of a different kind -- private, continuous, and carried in rooms where nothing is happening.

## Echo

Safe, and working the whole time.
It shows the three arrangements complete -- one known and settled, one changing hands and irreversible, one held and never closed -- and carries beneath it the composite's keystone: the capacity to hide reads as the fortunate case and is the one with no completion condition in it, so a persona can be spared every instance of the discounting and still be spending more of themselves on the attribute than the persona who is discounted daily.
This is why the Keeping is the hardest of the three to stage and the only one worth closing on: it has no scene, no incident, no line that carries it, and the tell is that a persona is doing a great deal of work in a conversation about nothing.
The Keeping is the note the whole composite closes on: what an attribute costs a persona is legible in what they are doing when nothing is happening, and nowhere else without a discount.
The Keeping is dated from every ordinary room the persona walked into and decided again.
