# Carrying

Carrying as composite: what it costs a persona to hold an attribute that would discredit them -- the mark already known and discounting them in every encounter (the mark), the moment it moves from unknown to known by their choice or against it (the tell), and the mark held privately, decided again in every room, with no condition under which the holding ends (the keeping) -- wired over the stigma, secret, and shame engines.

This engine is defined by its [manifest](package.json), which the canon renders as the WIRES card. The manifest is the single source of truth; this README is generated, not edited by hand.
