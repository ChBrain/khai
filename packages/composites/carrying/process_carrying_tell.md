# Process: Carrying, the Tell

## Taxonomy

[Carrying](process_carrying.md), the attribute changing hands.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: carrying

## Initiated by

Somebody who did not know is about to know.
The Tell starts wherever the [secret](@chbrain/khai-engine-secret/piece_secret.md) stops being kept -- by the [disclosure](@chbrain/khai-engine-secret/process_disclosure.md) the [keeper](@chbrain/khai-engine-secret/position_keeper.md) chooses and times, or by the [exposure](@chbrain/khai-engine-secret/process_exposure.md) that takes the choice away -- and it lands as [shame](@chbrain/khai-engine-shame/process_shame.md), which is what an exposure against a standard produces and which then has to go somewhere.
It is the face of the composite with an actual scene in it.

## Direction

Toward a room that now knows, and a persona who has to be in it.
The Tell reads the transfer and what follows rather than the words -- and reads that the two routes into it are not the same event: a disclosure is authored, timed, and addressed to somebody chosen, so the bearer keeps some of the frame; an exposure is none of those, and the frame is whatever the discoverer supplies.
Then it reads where the shame goes, since it does not stay: through the [compass](@chbrain/khai-engine-shame/process_compass.md), out as [withdrawal](@chbrain/khai-engine-shame/process_compass_withdrawal.md) from the people who now know, as [attack on the self](@chbrain/khai-engine-shame/process_compass_attack_self.md) that agrees with the verdict, as [avoidance](@chbrain/khai-engine-shame/process_compass_avoidance.md) that will not have it be about that, or as [attack on the other](@chbrain/khai-engine-shame/process_compass_attack_other.md) aimed at whoever saw.
It moves the persona from one arrangement to the other, permanently, in one direction only.

## Lever

The relief of not holding it against the irreversibility of having let it go.
The carrying force is that the holding ends: whatever this costs, the persona will not have to decide about this listener again, and where the telling is met well it converts a room from one to be managed into one that can be stood in; the resistance is that it cannot be taken back and its reception cannot be known in advance -- the same sentence lands as nothing at all, as a bond, or as the beginning of the discounting, and the bearer is a poor judge of which, having usually rehearsed the worst version many times.
The Tell spends the option, and is the only move that spends it.

## Echo

Known now, and the shame gone somewhere.
It is the bridge that connects the other two and runs one way: a mark cannot be un-known, so this is the door between the keeping and the mark and there is no door back.
What it hands upward is why the keeping is so hard to leave -- not because telling is worse than keeping, but because telling is a single irreversible act with an unknown reception, and keeping is a series of small reversible ones, and a persona will choose the second almost every time even when the first would have cost less in total.
The Tell is dated from the sentence, or from the moment somebody else found out.
