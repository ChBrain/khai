# Process: Carrying, the Mark

## Taxonomy

[Carrying](process_carrying.md), the attribute already known, and the case where the question is closed.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: carrying

## Initiated by

The attribute is visible, or on record, or already told, and everyone in the room has it.
The Mark starts wherever [stigma](@chbrain/khai-engine-stigma/piece_stigma.md) is running in the open -- the [discrediting](@chbrain/khai-engine-stigma/process_discrediting.md) that runs through the encounter itself, discounting what the bearer says and does before they have said or done it -- and it is met from whatever stance the [bearer](@chbrain/khai-engine-stigma/position_bearer.md) has taken toward it.
It is the face of the composite that costs the most in any given hour and asks the least of the persona's attention.

## Direction

Toward encounters that are all slightly about one thing.
The Mark reads the discounting rather than the attribute -- the help offered before it was needed, the subject changed, the competence assumed absent, the kindness that is a little too careful, the job that went elsewhere -- and reads what the bearer does with an audience that has already decided: nothing is being managed here, because nothing can be, and the work is not concealment but the conduct of a life in front of people who know.
It moves the persona through a world that meets them already informed.

## Lever

The reality of the discounting against the fact that it is settled.
The carrying force is that the cost is heavy, external, and continuous: it is levied in employment, in housing, in medicine, and in every ordinary exchange, and no stance the bearer takes removes it, since the discounting is the room's act and not theirs; the resistance -- and this is what distinguishes the bridge -- is that the question does not have to be answered again. There is no decision to make on entering, nothing to watch for, and no version of the persona that could be blown. And there are others who share it, who can be found, and in front of whom none of this is running.
The Mark spends the persona's standing, and buys back the whole of their attention.

## Echo

Discounted, and not managing anything.
It is the bridge that sets the composite's real axis by being the expensive case that is nevertheless the settled one -- which is what makes the third bridge legible, since the third is not a lighter version of this but a different job.
What it hands upward is the thing that will be missing later: a bearer here has company, and a bearer who is keeping does not.
The Mark is dated from whenever it became known, and from every encounter since that began already decided.
