# Carrying: Reference

## Line of Work

Carrying as **composite**: what it costs a persona to hold an attribute that would discredit them.
It is not a fourth mechanism but a wiring of three: the **stigma** engine supplies the mark and the
discounting it draws, the **secret** engine supplies the machinery of holding information about
oneself, and the **shame** engine supplies what the attribute does inside and the directions it
comes out. This composite adds no fourth engine; it stages the split the stigma atom already
declares and reads the two sides as different work rather than as different amounts of the same
trouble.

This is the second composite to wire the stigma engine. The membership composite reads it from the
group's side, as one of the ways a group keeps a boundary, alongside conformity, loyalty, and
social identity. Here it is read from inside, as something a particular persona is carrying
around. Same engine, two questions, and neither composite owns it.

The domain does not model any single side -- the mark, the bearer's stance, the discrediting, and
the passing are the stigma engine's; the keeper, the concealment, the disclosure, and the exposure
are secret's; the compass and its four directions are shame's; and one alone is a process, not the
arrangement a persona is in. Nor is it the truth-status of what a self lets through, which is the
opacity composite's; nor the self-evaluative emotions in their own right, which are
self-conscious's; nor the answer a room gives to a claim, which is regard's. It owns the holding:
what an attribute costs its bearer, and how that changes with who knows.

The spine is Goffman, and the stigma atom already carries him for the distinction this composite
is built on. A discrediting attribute comes in two conditions that make entirely different demands.
Where the attribute is evident -- the **discredited** case -- the bearer's problem is the
management of tension in encounters that are already informed. Where it is not evident but could
become so -- the **discreditable** case -- the bearer's problem is the management of **information**,
which is a different job: not how to be treated, but who is told, and when, and what is said in the
meantime. Goffman's own terms for the second are passing and covering, and both are in the stigma
engine.

Link and Phelan supply the other flank and the thing that keeps the composite from reading a mark
as a property of a person. Stigma in their account is the co-occurrence of labelling,
stereotyping, separation, status loss, and discrimination, and it requires a power situation to
run at all -- which is why nothing about an attribute makes it discrediting. The same fact is
unremarkable in one room and disqualifying in the next, the bearer did not set which room they are
in, and the cost is imposed rather than authored. That is the claim the root rests on.

What the composite adds is the ordering and the correction it produces. Arranged by who knows, the
three run: known and settled, changing hands, held. Arranged by what a reader assumes about the
weight, they run the other way -- the visible mark looks worst and the concealable one looks like
the lucky case. The correction is not that the second is worse. It is that the second is a
different job with no completion condition in it. The marked bearer answers the question once and
then lives in front of people who know; the keeping bearer answers it in every room, forever, with
the roster of people to be managed only ever growing, and without the one resource the marked
bearer has -- others who share the attribute, whose company can only be joined by telling. The
concealment literature is where this stops being an observation: Pachankis models the load as
cognitive, affective, and behavioural rather than as fear alone, and Quinn and Chaudoir find that
what predicts the outcome for a concealable identity is not whether it is concealed but its felt
centrality, its salience, and the expectation of how disclosure would be received.

**On the evidence, stated plainly.** The Goffman distinction is a conceptual one of long standing
and is what the composite's structure rests on; it is not an empirical claim and does not need to
be. The concealment literature is genuine and the load it describes is well characterised, but the
comparison itself should be handled carefully and this composite deliberately does not make the
strong version of it: it does not claim that concealable stigma is worse than visible stigma.
Visible stigma carries discrimination in employment, housing, and medicine that concealment
avoids, and which of the two arrangements costs a given persona more depends on the attribute, the
setting, and whether disclosure would be safe. What is claimed is narrower and better supported --
that the concealable case is not the light one, that its cost is of a different kind, and that it
has no state in which it is finished. The three bridges are the mark, the tell, and the keeping.

## Origin

The stigma literature, the sociology of secrecy, and the psychology of shame, wired as a composite
over the stigma, secret, and shame engines. The first two rows are the **joining** -- the published
work that splits a discrediting attribute into two conditions and explains what makes one at all --
and the rest anchor the individual bridges and their bounds.

## Restrictions

What the composite refuses to model, and to whom it delegates.

- **The three sides (the atoms)**: The composite wires the three but owns none. The mark, the
  bearer's stance, the discrediting, and the passing are the stigma engine's; the keeper, the
  concealment, the disclosure, and the exposure are secret's; the compass and its directions are
  shame's. Carrying reads only the arrangement a persona is in with respect to one attribute.
- **The group's boundary (membership)**: The membership composite wires this same stigma engine
  alongside conformity, loyalty, and social identity, and reads it as one of the ways a group keeps
  its edge. This reads the same engine from the bearer's side. Both are true of it, a play may run
  both on one scene, and the difference is where the reading is taken from.
- **The truth-status of what is let through (opacity)**: The secret, the disclosure, and the
  deception read as withheld, feigned, or revealed are the opacity composite's, and its secret may
  be about anything at all. This composite's is always about the persona and always discrediting,
  which is what gives it the shame engine and the completion problem opacity has no reason to
  carry.
- **The self-evaluative emotions (self-conscious)**: Guilt, pride, and shame in their own right are
  the self-conscious composite's. Carrying uses shame only as what an exposure produces and where
  it goes, and it does not model the emotion's own family.
- **The room's answer (regard)**: The nod, the spotlight, and the blank -- what a room does with a
  claim put in front of it -- are the regard composite's. Carrying reads what the persona brought
  into the room; regard reads what came back, and a bearer can be marked and nodded to.
- **The general labour of being seen (presentation)**: Face, the front and back regions,
  self-monitoring, and emotional labour are the presentation composite's -- the work of being taken
  as one means to be, which every persona does. Carrying is the special case where a specific fact
  would discredit, and where the labour therefore has a subject.
- **The rank (standing)**: Capital, hierarchy, power, and status are the standing composite's.
  Carrying reads the attribute and its cost, and is indifferent to where the bearer sits otherwise
  -- a mark runs on the high-standing bearer too, and often more expensively.
- **The injury of a childhood of it (cptsd)**: Attachment, trauma, regulation, self-esteem, and
  shame wired as developmental injury are the cptsd composite's. Carrying reads a present
  arrangement and does not model what a lifetime of it makes.
- **The mark as authored symbol (the play's pitch)**: An attribute made to **stand for** a theme --
  the illness that mirrors a motif of corruption -- is refused: that is authored key, the play's
  pitch, not the force on the persona. Carrying sets what is held and who knows; the meaning read
  into it is the play's.

## Encoding

Source to constraint. A composite root over three engine atoms it declares as dependencies, with
three bridges, ordered by who knows.

- **[carrying](process_carrying.md)** (the root): what it costs to hold an attribute that would
  discredit its bearer, read on the persona and never on the attribute. Anchored by Goffman (1963)
  and Link & Phelan (2001) on what makes a mark at all.
- **[the mark](process_carrying_mark.md)** (bridge): the attribute known, so the discounting runs
  in every encounter and nothing is being managed -- expensive, settled, and with others who share
  it. Anchored by Goffman on the discredited and Link & Phelan on status loss and discrimination.
- **[the tell](process_carrying_tell.md)** (bridge): the attribute changing hands, by a disclosure
  the bearer frames or an exposure they do not, and the shame that follows going one of four ways.
  Anchored by Chaudoir & Fisher (2010) and by Nathanson's compass.
- **[the keeping](process_carrying_keeping.md)** (bridge): the attribute held, decided again in
  every room, its work informational and its roster only growing. Anchored by Goffman on the
  discreditable and passing, Pachankis (2007) on the load, and Quinn & Chaudoir (2009) on what
  actually predicts the outcome; the composite's twist and keystone, that the capacity to hide
  reads as the fortunate case and is the one with no completion condition in it.
