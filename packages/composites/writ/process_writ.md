# Process: Writ

## Taxonomy

What a saying becomes once it is written down and sent on.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: writ

## Initiated by

Somebody said a thing that made it so, and then it went on paper.
Writ starts wherever those two meet: an utterance that is a deed rather than a description, through [the speech act](@chbrain/khai-engine-speech-act/process_speech_act.md); and an inscription that fixes a claim and travels unchanged into contexts its author never occupies, through [the document](@chbrain/khai-engine-document/piece_document.md).
This composite adds no third engine. It reads what the second does to the first, which is to make it portable and unexaminable at the same time.

## Direction

Toward a room acting on a piece of paper, correctly, without any way to know.
The composite wires the two engines and reads their working in three shapes: [the Saying](process_writ_saying.md), the act and the conditions that made it valid; [the Fixing](process_writ_fixing.md), the act made durable and set travelling; and [the Reliance](process_writ_reliance.md), the twist -- a room enforcing the record because the act is gone.
The three are not three stages of paperwork. They are one act, and the progressive loss of everything that would let anybody test it.

## Lever

The reach a record gives an act against what the act gives up to be carried.
The carrying force is that this is how anything holds beyond a room: a promise nobody wrote down binds only the people who heard it, and a status conferred face to face is renegotiated at every door -- so fixing an act and sending it on is not bureaucracy, it is the only way a deed survives its own occasion; the resistance is that the conditions which made the act valid were features of that occasion -- who was standing there, with what authority, saying it how -- and none of them travels, because what travels is the inscription and the inscription is the same either way.
Writ is read on the record and never on the person holding it, since the holder is usually the last party with any say in what it says.

## Echo

A deed still in force, and nobody left who could check it.
Writ does not close; it is one act's passage from an occasion into a file, gathered into one reading -- a saying, a fixing, a reliance.
It hands off what it is not: the illocutionary points in their own right, and what an utterance carries past the literal, are the utterance composite's, which wires this same speech-act engine on how a persona talks where this runs it on what a persona's saying became; the bond of relying on somebody whose conduct cannot be controlled is dealing's and vigil's, and this composite's room relies on a thing rather than on a person; the management of another's belief is deception's, and a forged record is a different and smaller story; the two expectations a room brings to a person are unbecoming's; who may draw on a shared thing is upkeep's; how a place is made readable from above is legibility's, under peopling; and a document made to **stand for** a theme is authored key, the play's pitch, not the force.
The record is dated from the act, and it will not say what the act was like.
