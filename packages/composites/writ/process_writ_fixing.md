# Process: Writ, the Fixing

## Taxonomy

[Writ](process_writ.md), the act made durable and set travelling.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: writ

## Initiated by

The act is written down, and starts going places nobody who was present will follow.
The Fixing starts wherever [inscription](@chbrain/khai-engine-document/process_inscription.md) is turning the deed into [a document](@chbrain/khai-engine-document/piece_document.md) and [circulation](@chbrain/khai-engine-document/process_circulation.md) is carrying it unchanged into contexts its author never occupies, with [the bearer](@chbrain/khai-engine-document/position_bearer.md) holding and wielding the authority it now carries.
It is the face of the composite where the act gains everything and loses the rest.

## Direction

Toward reach, bought with the loss of the occasion.
The Fixing reads the trade exactly: the record acquires durability, portability, and the property of saying the same thing everywhere -- and it acquires them by being a different kind of object from the act, one that carries the outcome and not the circumstances, the verdict and not the hearing, the certificate and not the examination.
It moves the deed out of anybody's memory and into a form that outlasts all of them.

## Lever

What the inscription preserves against what it necessarily leaves out.
The carrying force is that the omission is not sloppiness but the design: a record that carried its whole occasion would not travel, would not be comparable with other records, and could not be acted on quickly -- the compression is the point, and the form itself, with its letterhead and seal and signature, is a claim to have been made properly; the resistance is that the compression is total and silent, since a badly issued certificate and a well issued one are typographically identical, and the document has no field in which to record that anything was wrong.
The Fixing spends the occasion, and returns a thing that will be believed at a distance.

## Echo

An act, portable, and stripped of everything that would let it be questioned.
It is the bridge that makes the composite's problem structural rather than accidental: nothing has gone wrong here, and the same operation that makes records useful is the one that makes them uncheckable.
What it hands upward is a durable claim in motion, and the fact that every room it reaches will have strictly less to go on than the room it came from.
The Fixing is dated from the moment of writing, and it does not carry that date's conditions with it.
