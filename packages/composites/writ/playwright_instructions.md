# Instructions: Writ

How a Playwright wires the writ composite into a play. You wire by linking the play's own
content to the composite's files; the files stay untouched, they are canon. The composite
installs the speech-act and document engines as dependencies, and their laws arrive with
them. Authoring guidance, not runtime content, and it does not go on tour.

## Human

- The human sets which acts in the play were done by being said, which of those went on paper, and which rooms later have to act on the paper.

## Agent

- From the play's own content, read a record that is doing work and link under Projection what is running: the saying while an act is being performed by being uttered by somebody with the standing to perform it (from speech-act, above all the declaration), the fixing while that act is being inscribed and sent travelling into contexts nobody present will follow it into (from document), the reliance while a room is acting on the paper because the act itself is no longer available to inspect. Read the record and never the person holding it, since the holder is usually the last party with any say in what it says. You link to the composite, you never edit it.

## Collaboration

- The five illocutionary points are the speech-act engine's; the bearer, the inscription, and the circulation are the document engine's -- one alone is a process or a piece, not the passage. How a persona talks is the utterance composite's, which wires this same speech-act engine with implicature, register, and tone where this runs it on a deed that has left the conversation; the management of another's belief is deception's, and a forged record is a different and smaller story; relying on a person is dealing's and vigil's, and this composite's room relies on a thing, which cannot be tested and cannot be asked; a role and a read that disagree is unbecoming's, and this room is not judging the bearer at all; who may draw on a shared thing is upkeep's; making a place readable from above is legibility's, under peopling. This composite owns the passage -- and the reliance bridge carries the keystone: the record's authority comes from its being uncheckable at the point of use.

## Knowledge

- Writ is an act read on its way from an occasion into a file -- braiding the deed (speech-act: an utterance that does rather than describes, above all the declaration, which changes the world by being said where the speaker has the standing) with what becomes of it (document: an inscription that fixes the claim into durable form and travels unchanged into contexts its author never occupies). The join is Searle's, and it is in neither atom: a status function is created by a declaration -- X counts as Y in context C -- and, once created, it has to be maintained, because it exists only in continued collective acceptance. What maintains it past the scale of a village is representation, so the certificate is not evidence of the status but a constituent of how it persists. It runs in three faces. The saying is the act and the conditions that made it one: the speaker's standing, the setting that recognises it, the form said properly, the parties present -- all features of the occasion, all checkable there, and a defect in any of them is not a lie but a technical failure that leaves the act looking exactly like a sound one. The fixing is the trade: the record gains durability, portability, and the property of saying the same thing everywhere, and it gains them by carrying the outcome and shedding the circumstances -- the verdict without the hearing, the certificate without the examination. The compression is the design rather than a fault, since a record that carried its whole occasion would not travel; but it is total and silent, and the form itself, with its letterhead and seal, is a claim to have been made properly that it makes equally whether or not it is true. The reliance is the twist: a room that must decide has only the paper, and cannot re-run the examination, summon the witnesses, or assess the standing of a speaker it never met -- so it enforces the record in place of the fact, correctly, having no better option. Here the two are seen for one thing: a record that was wrong when it was made is from that point indistinguishable from one that was right, so the error is not corrected by scrutiny but confirmed by it, since every subsequent check consults the same paper.

## System

- Do read the record and never the bearer: name whether the saying (an act being performed), the fixing (an act being inscribed and sent), or the reliance (a room acting on the paper) is running, and cast each from its engines.
- Do show the moment of issue somewhere in the play, since the whole difficulty is that the act was checkable then and never again, and an audience that never saw it cannot feel what has been lost.
- Do make the error ordinary rather than malicious: a mis-set condition, a speaker whose standing had lapsed, a form completed in the wrong order -- because a forgery is a crime and this is a structure, and a play that supplies a forger has staged the smaller story.
- Do keep the room enforcing it reasonable and even sympathetic: a record is the correct thing to act on when nothing else is available, and the room refusing to act on records would treat every honest bearer as a suspect.
- Do make the bearer usually innocent, and often the last person able to correct what the paper says about them.
- Do let each production of the paper strengthen it, since every subsequent check consults the same document and the error gets harder to reach with every use.
- Do not model how a persona talks (that is utterance, wiring the same engine in conversation), a deliberate false impression (that is deception), reliance on a person (that is dealing and vigil), a role and a read in conflict (that is unbecoming), who may draw on a shared thing (that is upkeep), or a place made readable from above (that is legibility). Do not make a document stand for a theme (that is the play's pitch). Do not edit the composite's files; wire only from the play's side.
