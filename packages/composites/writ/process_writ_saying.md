# Process: Writ, the Saying

## Taxonomy

[Writ](process_writ.md), the act done by being said, and the conditions that made it one.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: writ

## Initiated by

Somebody with the standing to do it says the words, and the world is different.
The Saying starts wherever [the speech act](@chbrain/khai-engine-speech-act/process_speech_act.md) is running, and most sharply at the [declaration](@chbrain/khai-engine-speech-act/process_declaration.md) -- the act that changes the world by being uttered where the speaker has the standing -- with the [commissive](@chbrain/khai-engine-speech-act/process_commissive.md) that binds the speaker to a future act and the [assertive](@chbrain/khai-engine-speech-act/process_assertive.md) that commits them to the truth of a claim standing alongside it.
It is the face of the composite where everything necessary is present in the room.

## Direction

Toward a state of affairs that now obtains because somebody said so.
The Saying reads what makes the deed a deed and not a description: the speaker's standing, the setting that recognises it, the form said properly and in the right order, and the parties who are there -- so that a person is married, appointed, discharged, certified, or bound, and none of it was true a moment before.
It moves the world by moving nothing physical, which is what makes the conditions the whole of the matter.

## Lever

The power of a saying that acts against the fragility of what has to hold for it to.
The carrying force is that the machinery is real and mostly reliable: societies run on acts of this kind, personas perform them constantly and correctly, and the standing that licenses them is usually genuine and usually visible to everybody present; the resistance is that every one of the conditions is a feature of the occasion -- the room can see whether the speaker had the authority, whether the form was right, whether anybody objected -- and the occasion is the only place any of that is checkable, since a defect here is not a lie but a technical failure that leaves an act looking exactly like a good one.
The Saying spends nothing, and its validity is entirely local in time.

## Echo

A deed done properly, or improperly, and no difference visible from outside the room.
It is the bridge that establishes what is at stake: everything that would let a later party assess the act is present here and nowhere else.
What it hands upward is a completed deed together with a set of conditions that have already begun to expire, and no way to attach the second to the first.
The Saying is dated from the moment it was uttered, and that is the only moment it can be judged in.
