# Process: Writ, the Reliance

## Taxonomy

[Writ](process_writ.md), the room acting on the paper, and where the two are seen for one thing.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: writ

## Initiated by

A room has to decide something, and what it has is the record.
The Reliance starts wherever [circulation](@chbrain/khai-engine-document/process_circulation.md) has delivered [the document](@chbrain/khai-engine-document/piece_document.md) somewhere that must act on it, and [the bearer](@chbrain/khai-engine-document/position_bearer.md) presents it as the thing that settles the question -- the act itself having been a [declaration](@chbrain/khai-engine-speech-act/process_declaration.md) that nobody present witnessed and nobody present can reconstruct.
It is the face of the composite where an error becomes permanent.

## Direction

Toward enforcement of the record, in place of the fact.
The Reliance reads what the room is actually doing, which is not what it takes itself to be doing: it believes it is acting on a status somebody holds, and it is acting on a claim that somebody holds it -- and these come apart only in the cases where it matters, which are precisely the cases the room cannot detect.
It moves the record from evidence about the world to a substitute for it, and the substitution is invisible because both look identical from here.

## Lever

The correctness of relying on a record against the impossibility of checking one.
The carrying force is that the room is right to do this and has no better option: it cannot re-run the examination, cannot summon the witnesses, cannot assess the standing of a speaker it never met -- and a room that refused to act on records would grind to a halt while treating every honest bearer as a suspect; the resistance is the one thing that would resolve it, which is going back to the occasion -- available in principle, ruinous in practice, and reserved for the rare case somebody has already found reason to doubt, which is exactly the case a clean record prevents anybody from finding.
The Reliance rides the composite's keystone knife-edge: the record's authority comes from its being uncheckable at the point of use, so the property that makes it useful is the property that makes an error in it permanent.

## Echo

A decision taken properly, on a document that might be wrong, by people who could not have known.
It shows the two engines complete -- an act that was valid only in its moment, and an inscription that carried the outcome and shed the moment -- and carries beneath it the composite's keystone: a record that was wrong when it was made is, from here on, indistinguishable from one that was right, so the error is not corrected by scrutiny but confirmed by it, since every subsequent check consults the same paper.
This is why the Reliance is the hardest of the three to stage and the only one worth closing on: the room must be reasonable, the bearer must usually be innocent, the error must be ordinary rather than malicious, and nobody must be available to appeal to -- and a play that supplies a forger has staged a crime rather than this.
The Reliance is the note the whole composite closes on: what an act actually was is legible only where somebody still has standing to say so, and the record is what remains after they stop.
The Reliance is dated from every time the paper is produced, and it gets stronger each time.
