# Process: Bittersweet, Mix

## Taxonomy

[Bittersweet](process_bittersweet.md), the bridge where joy and sadness fire at full at the same instant.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: bittersweet

## Initiated by

A single moment that is, at once, cause for gladness and cause for sorrow.
Each atom answers as if its response ran alone -- [joy](@chbrain/khai-engine-joy/process_joy.md) lifts, [sadness](@chbrain/khai-engine-sadness/process_sadness.md) sinks; neither, alone, sees the compound their co-firing makes.
When both are read from the same object at the same time -- the graduation that is an achievement and a parting, the reunion shadowed by how long it has been, the tears that come at the happy ending -- the two run together and the result is the poignant: a feeling denser than either, the smile and the ache in one breath (Larsen, McGraw & Cacioppo, on the simultaneity of happy and sad).

## Direction

Toward the object held glad and sorrowful together -- savoured and mourned in the same motion.
The mix does not alternate joy then sadness; it holds them concurrent, so the persona cannot point to a single feeling and every response to the moment carries both its lift and its weight.
Its direction is the sustained density, not a settling to one sign.

## Lever

What the mix spends -- the coherence of a single-valence reading.
Joy alone lets the persona simply celebrate; sadness alone lets them simply grieve; the mix denies both, holding the gladness and the sorrow at once so neither can be felt clean, and the moment is poignant precisely because it will not reduce.
A production holds this lever by letting both atoms run live from the same object rather than choosing one, and reading the poignancy -- not any single feeling -- as what governs the moment.

## Echo

The moment is felt in both signs at once -- and the poignant is the signature of it, the density that a purely happy or purely sad reading would lose.
It is why the sweetest moments are often the saddest, and why a flattening to one valence -- all celebration, all grief -- reads as thinner than the truth.
The mix can hold as a still compound, or it can begin to move, one sign gaining on the other; that motion, if the production stages it, is a [turn](process_bittersweet_turn.md) -- a scene, not the still poignancy the mix names.
