# Process: Bittersweet, Turn

## Taxonomy

[Bittersweet](process_bittersweet.md), the bridge where one sign crosses into the other.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: bittersweet

## Initiated by

A moment whose valence will not hold its place.
The atoms read their own states -- [joy](@chbrain/khai-engine-joy/process_joy.md)'s lift, [sadness](@chbrain/khai-engine-sadness/process_sadness.md)'s sinking; what they cannot read is one becoming the other.
When a dreaded thing lifts, sadness gives way to the rush of relief; when a good thing ends, joy gives way to the letdown, the flatness after the peak; and when the two will not settle, the moment oscillates -- gladness and sorrow trading the foreground, each crossing into the other and back.

## Direction

From one sign to the other, and sometimes back -- the crossing, not the blend.
Where the mix holds both at once, the turn moves between them: relief is sadness releasing into joy, the letdown is joy subsiding into sadness, and the oscillation is the pair that will not resolve, swinging as the reading of the moment shifts.
Its direction is the transit itself -- the emotion caught mid-crossing.

## Lever

What the turn spends -- the expectation that a feeling, once set, stays.
The relief spends the dread that preceded it, the letdown the elevation it follows; each is legible only against the sign it crossed from, the joy sharper for the fear it answers, the sadness heavier for the peak it trails.
A production holds this lever by choosing the crossing point -- the instant the dread lifts, the moment the good thing closes -- and letting the prior sign still color the new one, so the relief carries the memory of the fear and the letdown the memory of the height.

## Echo

The moment has crossed -- and the feeling on the far side is marked by the one it came from, relief not the same as plain joy, the letdown not the same as plain sadness.
Whoever reads only the endpoint misses the transit that shaped it, the way relief without its dread is just contentment and a letdown without its peak is just a low mood.
When neither sign will win and the crossing keeps reversing, the turn becomes the oscillation the [mix](process_bittersweet_mix.md) would hold still -- motion where the mix is compound, the same two atoms caught moving instead of held.
