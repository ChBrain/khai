# Process: Bittersweet

## Taxonomy

A composite reading over two atom engines (no canon file of its own): the joy and sadness engines this package declares as dependencies.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: bittersweet

## Initiated by

A persona meets a moment that is glad and sorrowful at once -- and both readings land, neither cancelling the other.
The uplift is the joy engine's, the sinking the sadness engine's; what neither, alone, can see is the compound they make when they fire together.
This is the mixed emotion the evaluative-space model warrants (Cacioppo & Berntson; Larsen, McGraw & Cacioppo): positivity and negativity are separable systems, not the two ends of one dial, so they can be co-activated -- both live at full -- and the folk mind's "you can't feel happy and sad at the same time" is simply wrong.
The graduation, the last day of a good thing, the tears of joy -- [joy](@chbrain/khai-engine-joy/process_joy.md) and [sadness](@chbrain/khai-engine-sadness/process_sadness.md) held together, not traded turn for turn.

## Direction

Toward the moment held in both signs at once -- approached and mourned in the same breath.
The composite does not net the two to a single valence; it keeps the gladness and the sorrow both alive, so the persona savours and grieves the same thing, and every gesture toward the moment carries its opposite.
Its direction is the both-at-once, never resolved down to happy or to sad.

## Lever

What the mix spends -- the assumption that a feeling has one sign.
Joy alone lets the persona simply celebrate; sadness alone lets them simply mourn; the bittersweet denies both, holding the uplift and the sinking together so that neither runs clean, and the moment is richer and heavier than either would make it.
It rests on the evaluative-space fact that the two systems are separable: co-activation is possible, and it is what the folk theory of opposite feelings cannot account for.

## Echo

The moment is felt whole -- glad and sorrowful together, and the poorer if flattened to one.
The atoms each run their own response and cannot see the family: the [mix](process_bittersweet_mix.md) where joy and sadness fire at full at once, the poignant compound; the [turn](process_bittersweet_turn.md) where one sign crosses into the other, relief lifting or a joy closing into loss; and the [meaning](process_bittersweet_meaning.md) where the mix peaks because the moment is bounded and matters -- the ending that draws the two together.
The specific sentimental longing for one's own past is the nostalgia engine's; the mix as a persisting background tone rather than a moment is the mood engine's. This composite owns the mixed emotion of joy and sadness itself, and the shapes the co-activation takes.
