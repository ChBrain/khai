# Position: Relative Deprivation, Egoistic

## Taxonomy

[Relative Deprivation](process_relative_deprivation.md), the grievance pitched at the individual -- the persona deprived as itself.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: relative-deprivation

## Has

A grievance whose comparison is individual: the persona measures itself against other persons and finds itself unfairly short. The [upward comparison](@chbrain/khai-engine-comparison/process_upward.md) runs self-to-other -- this colleague promoted over me, this neighbour with more -- and the [grievance](@chbrain/khai-engine-anger/process_grievance.md) it ignites is the persona's own, on its own behalf. It is the deprivation of the single case: I, personally, have been wrongfully denied what I deserve.

## Orders

Answer the personal wrong, alone. Egoistic deprivation orders the persona to redress its own disadvantage -- to strive harder, to resent the better-off individual, to feel the private sting of a merit unrewarded -- and it turns the grievance inward and toward the particular rival, not toward a cause. It is tied to individual outcomes: personal stress, dissatisfaction, and a self-focused drive to close the gap or to withdraw from the comparison that opened it.

## Loses

The mobilizing force that only the group grievance carries. Pitched at the self, the deprivation forgoes the collective action the [fraternal](position_deprivation_fraternal.md) form drives: the persona resents alone and acts alone, and cannot draw on the solidarity, the shared cause, or the political energy that a group-based grievance supplies. What it trades is collective leverage for a private wound -- keenly felt, but borne and answered by one.

## Drives

On that individual grievance:
rides it -- resents the better-off other, strives to close its own gap, or withdraws from the galling comparison -- or, when the persona re-reads its disadvantage as shared with others like it, the grievance shifts to the fraternal level and its energy turns collective. The egoistic baseline holds while the comparison stays self-to-other, the deprivation the persona's own rather than, like the [fraternal](position_deprivation_fraternal.md) form, its group's. That the grievance requires an injustice appraisal over a comparison is the [root's](process_relative_deprivation.md); the neutral self-to-other evaluation is the comparison engine's.
