# Position: Relative Deprivation, Fraternal

## Taxonomy

[Relative Deprivation](process_relative_deprivation.md), the grievance pitched at the group -- the persona deprived as a member.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: relative-deprivation

## Has

A grievance whose comparison is intergroup: the persona measures its group against other groups and finds its group unfairly short. The [upward comparison](@chbrain/khai-engine-comparison/process_upward.md) runs group-to-group -- my people, my class, my kind against a better-placed other -- and the [indignation](@chbrain/khai-engine-anger/process_indignation.md) it ignites is felt on the group's behalf, a shared wrong rather than a private one. It is the deprivation of the collective: we, as a group, have been wrongfully denied what we deserve.

## Orders

Act with others against the shared wrong. Fraternal deprivation orders the persona toward collective response -- to identify with the aggrieved group, to join its cause, to protest, organize, and press for redress, and, at its harsher edge, to turn hostility on the out-group held responsible. It is this group-based grievance, Smith and Pettigrew's meta-analysis shows, and not the individual kind, that predicts collective action, support for social movements, and intergroup prejudice. The wrong is ours, and so is the answer.

## Loses

The private, self-focused striving the individual grievance keeps. Pitched at the group, the deprivation forgoes the [egoistic](position_deprivation_egoistic.md) form's personal channel: the persona's own particular standing recedes behind the group's, and it may act against its individual interest for the collective cause, or subordinate a private advancement to a shared grievance. What it trades is the personal remedy for collective leverage -- and it can bind the persona to a group's fight that a self-focused reading would decline.

## Drives

On that group grievance:
rides it -- identifies with the aggrieved group, joins the collective action its wrong demands, and turns on the out-group blamed -- or, when group identification weakens or the persona re-reads the disadvantage as merely its own, the grievance falls back to the egoistic level and its energy turns private. The fraternal baseline holds while the comparison stays group-to-group, the deprivation the group's rather than, like the [egoistic](position_deprivation_egoistic.md) form, the persona's own. That the grievance requires an injustice appraisal over a comparison is the [root's](process_relative_deprivation.md); the group identification it presupposes is the social-identity engine's.
