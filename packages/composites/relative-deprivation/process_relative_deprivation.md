# Process: Relative Deprivation

## Taxonomy

A composite reading over two atom engines (no canon file of its own): the comparison and anger engines this package declares as dependencies.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: relative-deprivation

## Initiated by

A persona measures itself, or its group, against a better-off referent, and feels the gap as a wrong.
The compound begins with an [upward comparison](@chbrain/khai-engine-comparison/process_upward.md): the persona sets itself or its group against others who have more, and registers that it falls short. But a bare comparison is not yet deprivation -- Runciman's condition is that the persona wants what the other has, sees it as attainable, and, above all, feels entitled to it, so the shortfall reads not as mere difference but as something wrongfully withheld. What enters is a disadvantage appraised as unjust.

## Direction

From an unfavorable comparison, through the appraisal of injustice, into grievance.
The composite's own work is the middle term the atoms do not supply: the judgment that the disadvantage is undeserved and unfair, which converts a neutral gap into a felt deprivation. Across that appraisal the comparison ignites the anger engine's [grievance](@chbrain/khai-engine-anger/process_grievance.md) and [indignation](@chbrain/khai-engine-anger/process_indignation.md) -- resentment at a wrong, not mere want of a good. The direction is one way and gated on the appraisal: no sense of injustice, no deprivation, only comparison.

## Lever

Whether the shortfall is read as unjust, and at what level the comparison is pitched. A difference merely noted is comparison; a difference resented as deserved-but-denied is relative deprivation, and the lever between them is the injustice appraisal -- entitlement, illegitimacy, and the sense that it could be otherwise. The second lever is level: whether the persona compares as an individual or as a member of a group, because the group comparison, not the individual one, is what moves a persona to act with others. Stage a genuine sense of wrongful disadvantage, pitched at the level the situation calls for, because that appraisal is the whole of the deprivation and the thing a neutral comparison lacks.

## Echo

The grievance is pitched at one of two levels: [egoistic](position_deprivation_egoistic.md), where the persona is deprived as an individual against other individuals, driving personal resentment and self-focused response; or [fraternal](position_deprivation_fraternal.md), where its group is deprived against other groups, driving collective action, protest, and intergroup hostility. Smith and Pettigrew's meta-analysis confirms the split: it is the fraternal, group-based deprivation that predicts collective action and prejudice, not the individual kind. When the injustice appraisal fades or the gap closes, the grievance subsides and the atoms resume, comparison without resentment. The neutral evaluation of standing is the comparison engine's; the resentment and indignation are the anger engine's; the pain at another's specific advantage, absent the injustice-and-group core, is the envy engine's. This composite owns the affective-injustice layer that makes a comparison a deprivation.
