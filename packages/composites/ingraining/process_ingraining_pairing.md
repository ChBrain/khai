# Process: Ingraining, the Pairing

## Taxonomy

[Ingraining](process_ingraining.md), the stage where a cue comes to stand for what follows it.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: ingraining

## Initiated by

Something harmless keeps arriving just before something that matters, and the persona begins answering the harmless thing.
The Pairing starts wherever [conditioning](@chbrain/khai-engine-conditioning/process_conditioning.md) does its work -- the [acquisition](@chbrain/khai-engine-conditioning/process_acquisition.md) by which a neutral cue that reliably precedes a significant outcome comes to evoke the response in advance, the [generalization](@chbrain/khai-engine-conditioning/process_generalization.md) that spreads it to whatever resembles the cue, and the [extinction](@chbrain/khai-engine-conditioning/process_extinction.md) that fades it when the cue stops keeping its promise.
It is the first shortening: the outcome no longer has to arrive to be answered.

## Direction

Toward a persona already responding to the sign before the thing signified.
The Pairing reads the prediction forming -- the sound of the car in the drive, the particular tone that precedes bad news, the smell that means the dentist -- each a cue that began as nothing and now carries the whole weight of what follows, with the response arriving on the cue rather than the event.
It moves the persona from reacting to anticipating: the body braced, lifted, or turned before there is anything yet to brace against.

## Lever

The prediction that buys time against the association that does not check whether it still holds.
The carrying force is that the world is full of reliable precursors, and a mind that answers the precursor is ready when the thing arrives -- this is what makes anticipation possible at all; the resistance is that the link is built from co-occurrence rather than from reasons: it spreads to what merely resembles the cue, it needs repeated disconfirmation to fade, and until it fades the persona goes on answering a sign that no longer signifies.
The Pairing is ingraining at its most basic: a response detached from the outcome and attached to its herald.

## Echo

The sign answered as though it were the thing.
The Pairing leaves the persona responding in advance -- braced by a sound, lifted by a smell, alarmed by a resemblance -- and hands that anticipatory response to the discounting and grooving that will build on it.
It is the first shortening: the outcome dropped from the loop, its cue standing in.
The Pairing is dated from the acquiring, the moment a cue began to carry the weight of what followed it.
