# Process: Ingraining, the Discount

## Taxonomy

[Ingraining](process_ingraining.md), the stage where an outcome is weighed by how near it stands.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: ingraining

## Initiated by

Two outcomes are on offer and the persona does not compare their worth but their distance.
The Discount starts wherever [reward](@chbrain/khai-engine-reward/process_reward.md) applies its rate -- the [immediate](@chbrain/khai-engine-reward/process_immediate.md) outcome arriving at near-full weight because the wait is too short for the discount to run, the [deferred](@chbrain/khai-engine-reward/process_deferred.md) one thinned by the interval until it must compete against present things carrying their whole value, the [delay-averse](@chbrain/khai-engine-reward/process_delay_averse.md) push that treats the waiting itself as the thing to escape, and the [stimulation-seeking](@chbrain/khai-engine-reward/process_stimulation_seeking.md) urge that wants activation now and does not care what it is activation toward.
It is the second shortening: the consequence far enough away barely enters the sum.

## Direction

Toward a persona whose horizon has a slope in it.
The Discount reads the weighing as it happens -- the degree abandoned in its third year, the smaller sum taken today, the wait broken not because the payoff got worse but because the interval got unbearable -- each a choice made against a scale the persona is not consulting and could not report.
It moves the persona from valuing to valuing-at-a-distance: the same outcome worth one thing this afternoon and another thing next spring, with nothing about the outcome having changed.

## Lever

The rate that makes action possible against the rate that makes the far consequence disappear.
The carrying force is that a discount is not a defect -- a future that never arrives should not outweigh a present that has, and an organism that valued every distant thing at full weight would starve deliberating; the resistance is that the rate is a property of the system rather than a judgment, it steepens under stress, scarcity, and unreliability, and at any real steepness the outcome that matters most is precisely the one placed far enough out to be invisible.
The Discount is ingraining's arithmetic: not what a thing is worth, but what it is worth from here.

## Echo

The far consequence thinned until it stops entering the choice.
The Discount leaves the persona acting on a horizon that slopes away -- the near looming, the far faint, the wait itself a cost the observer does not see -- and hands that sloped horizon to the grooving that will build on it.
It is the second shortening: the outcome kept in the loop but weighed so lightly, at range, that it no longer pulls.
The Discount is dated from the weighing, the moment nearness rather than worth decided what the persona did.
