# Process: Ingraining

## Taxonomy

How a response gets installed until it runs without consulting why.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: ingraining

## Initiated by

A persona is read not as choosing each act afresh but as running conduct that was laid down in it over time.
Ingraining starts wherever a response is being installed -- the neutral cue that comes to predict through [conditioning](@chbrain/khai-engine-conditioning/process_conditioning.md), the outcome weighed by how near it stands through [reward](@chbrain/khai-engine-reward/process_reward.md), and the act grooved into its setting through [habit](@chbrain/khai-engine-habit/process_habit.md).
This composite adds no fourth engine; it reads what the three make together: one loop from situation to act, shortening at every stage.

## Direction

Toward the whole installing of conduct, read through the stages that shorten the loop.
The composite wires the three engines and reads their working in three shapes: [the Pairing](process_ingraining_pairing.md), the cue that comes to predict; [the Discount](process_ingraining_discount.md), the outcome weighed by its nearness; and [the Groove](process_ingraining_groove.md), the twist -- the act that no longer needs summoning.
Each stage takes something out of the loop: the pairing means the outcome need not arrive to be answered, the discount means the far consequence barely enters the sum, and the groove means the goal is not consulted at all.

## Lever

The economy of an installed response against the deliberation it quietly replaces.
The carrying force is that a mind cannot decide everything every time -- pairing, discounting, and grooving are how conduct gets fast, cheap, and reliable, and most of what a persona does well it does without choosing; the resistance is that the same installation removes the act from the reach of the reasons that would revise it, so a response laid down under one set of conditions goes on running under another, and the persona experiences its own conduct as simply what it does.
Ingraining is read wherever a persona is granted its installed conduct: not a self deliberating from scratch but one running what was laid down, and mostly unable to see the laying.

## Echo

The persona revealed as conduct that was installed and now runs itself.
Ingraining does not close; it is the installing going on, gathering the three engines into one reading -- a cue that predicts, an outcome discounted, an act grooved.
It hands off what it is not: the pursuit of a behaviour past its cost, in a reward currency the organism has come to need, is the addiction engine's; the standing character a persona brings to any pursuit is the striving composite's; the crossing from wanting to doing is the volition composite's; the moment-to-moment override of an impulse is the self-control engine's; where a drive springs from, and what a persona requires, are the motivation and needs engines'; and the installed conduct made to stand for a story is authored key, the play's pitch, not the force.
The persona is dated from the laying-down, and read as a self whose readiest conduct is the least recently examined.
