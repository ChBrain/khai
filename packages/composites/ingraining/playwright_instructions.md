# Instructions: Ingraining

How a Playwright wires the ingraining composite into a play. You wire by linking the play's
own content to the composite's files; the files stay untouched, they are canon. The
composite installs the conditioning, reward, and habit engines as dependencies, and their
laws arrive with them. Authoring guidance, not runtime content, and it does not go on tour.

## Human

- The human sets the persona's installed conduct: which response is read as having been laid down -- a cue that has come to predict, an outcome weighed by how near it stands, or an act that no longer needs summoning -- and, the question that governs the read, which stage it is in: the pairing, the discount, or the groove.

## Agent

- From the play's own content, read a persona as running conduct that was installed rather than chosen afresh: link under Projection the stage the response is in -- the pairing while a neutral cue is acquiring the power to predict (the acquisition, its spread by generalization, its fading by extinction, from conditioning), the discount while nearness rather than worth is deciding (the immediate at near-full weight, the deferred thinned by the interval, the delay-averse escape from waiting, the stimulation-seeking urge, from reward), the groove while the act has stopped being chosen (the cue that retrieves, the repetition that laid it, the automaticity that runs unbidden, the friction that reveals it, from habit). Play the persona as acting out of what was laid down in them, present in the conduct, never narrated, and never made to stand for a story. You link to the composite, you never edit it.

## Collaboration

- The predictive learning is the conditioning engine's; the temporal weighing is reward's; the context-cued automaticity is habit's -- one stage alone is not the installing. Pursuit past its cost in a needed reward currency is the addiction engine's; the standing character a persona brings to any pursuit is the striving composite's; the crossing from wanting to doing is volition's; the momentary override of an impulse is the self-control engine's; where a drive springs from and what a persona requires are the motivation and needs engines'. This composite owns the pipeline -- what happens to a response between the first pairing and the point where it runs unbidden -- and the groove bridge carries the keystone: what is finally installed no longer consults the reason it was installed for.

## Knowledge

- Ingraining is a persona read as running conduct that was laid down in them -- braiding the predictive cue (conditioning), the outcome weighed by its nearness (reward), and the act grooved into its context (habit) into one loop from situation to act -- read as a wiring of those three engines, and its question is which stage the installing has reached (Rescorla's contingency against mere pairing; Ainslie's hyperbolic discount and preference reversal; Adams and Dickinson's outcome devaluation; Wood and Neal's habit-goal interface). It runs in three stages, and each takes something out of the loop. The pairing is the cue coming to stand for what follows it -- a neutral thing that reliably precedes something significant evokes the response in advance, spreads to whatever resembles it, and fades only under repeated disconfirmation -- the first shortening: the outcome no longer has to arrive to be answered. The discount is the outcome weighed by its distance -- the near looming at near-full weight, the far thinned until it must compete with present things carrying their whole value, and the wait itself an active cost the observer does not see -- the second shortening: the consequence far enough away barely enters the sum, and the rate steepens under stress and scarcity. The groove is the twist -- the response repeated in a stable context until it passes a threshold and comes without being summoned, chunked and retrieved by the setting faster than deliberation can intervene, revealed only when friction removes the cue -- where the pipeline is seen for what it is: a cue can fire when the response is no longer wanted, because the strength of the link lies in the pairing and not in what the act is now worth. Read a persona as mostly unable to see the laying, and read their readiest conduct as the least recently examined.

## System

- Do read the persona as running installed conduct, never as deliberating each act afresh: name whether the pairing (the cue coming to predict), the discount (nearness deciding worth), or the groove (the act no longer summoned) is at work, and cast each from its engines.
- Do keep the stages distinct: the pairing is what the cue has come to mean, the discount is what the outcome is worth from here, the groove is what runs without being asked.
- Do let the groove carry the keystone: what is finally installed no longer consults the reason it was installed for -- the response runs because it is bound, not because it is wanted.
- Do remember the persona cannot report the groove from inside: an installed act does not present as a decision, so a play that has the persona explain it has broken the stage.
- Do remember friction attacks the cue, not the will: a habit is returned to choice by a changed context, not by a persona resolving harder.
- Do not model pursuit past its cost (that is addiction), the standing character of pursuit (that is striving), the crossing from wanting to doing (that is volition), the momentary override (that is self-control), or where the drive springs from and what is required (that is motivation and needs). Do not make the installed conduct stand for a story (that is the play's pitch). Do not edit the composite's files; wire only from the play's side.
