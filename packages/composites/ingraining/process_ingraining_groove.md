# Process: Ingraining, the Groove

## Taxonomy

[Ingraining](process_ingraining.md), the stage where the act stops being summoned and the installing is seen for what it is.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: ingraining

## Initiated by

The persona meets the setting and the act is already underway, ahead of any decision to perform it.
The Groove starts wherever [habit](@chbrain/khai-engine-habit/process_habit.md) has finished its work -- the [cueing](@chbrain/khai-engine-habit/process_cueing.md) by which a stable feature of the context retrieves the response without consulting the goal, the [repetition](@chbrain/khai-engine-habit/process_repetition.md) that lays the pairing down until consistency rather than effort carries it past a threshold, the [automaticity](@chbrain/khai-engine-habit/process_automaticity.md) in which the chunk fires faster than deliberation can intervene, and the [friction](@chbrain/khai-engine-habit/process_friction.md) that reveals the whole arrangement only when the context is taken away.
It is the last shortening: the goal dropped from the loop entirely.

## Direction

Toward a persona whose readiest conduct is the least examined.
The Groove reads the installed act running -- the cigarette lit on the way to the car, the phone in the hand before the thought that reached for it, the route driven to an office left two years ago -- each an act performed cleanly, efficiently, and without an intention in between.
It moves the persona from doing to being done through: the conduct is theirs, it is skilful, it is fast, and it is not currently being chosen by anyone.

## Lever

The efficiency of the chunk against the reason it was built to serve.
The carrying force is that this is what installing is for -- an act that runs without deliberation frees the persona for whatever else is in front of them, and nearly everything a persona does well they do this way; the resistance is that the binding, once deep enough, is its own justification: the response runs because it is bound, not because it is wanted, and the persona may be unable to say why they did it.
The Groove rides the composite's keystone knife-edge: a cue can fire when the response is no longer wanted, because the strength of the link lies in the pairing and not in what the act is now worth -- so what is finally installed no longer consults the reason it was installed for.

## Echo

The conduct that runs itself, and the persona who cannot see the laying.
It shows the pipeline complete -- a cue that predicts, an outcome thinned by distance, an act that has stopped asking -- and carries beneath it the composite's keystone: the installing removes the act from the reach of the reasons that would revise it, so a response laid down under one set of conditions goes on running under another, and only [friction](@chbrain/khai-engine-habit/process_friction.md) -- a context taken away rather than a will applied -- returns it to choice.
The Groove is the note the whole composite closes on: what a persona does most readily is what they last had a reason for.
The Groove is dated from the threshold, the moment the act stopped needing to be summoned.
