# Ingraining

Ingraining as composite: how a response gets installed until it runs without consulting why -- the cue that comes to predict an outcome (conditioning), the outcome valued by how near it is (reward), and the repetition that grooves the act into its context (habit) -- across the pairing, the discount, and the groove, and the drama is that each stage shortens the loop between situation and act until the consequence has dropped out of it altogether.
