# Ingraining: Reference

## Line of Work

Ingraining as **composite**: how a response gets installed in a persona until it runs without
consulting why. It is not a fourth mechanism but a wiring of three: the **conditioning** engine
supplies the predictive link by which a neutral cue comes to stand for what follows it, the
**reward** engine supplies the temporal discount by which an outcome is weighed by how near it
stands, and the **habit** engine supplies the context-cued automaticity by which a repeated act
stops being summoned. This composite adds no fourth engine; it reads the seam the three make
when they run in series -- one loop from situation to act that shortens at every stage -- and
stages that seam into three bridges, closing on the discovery that what is finally installed no
longer consults the reason it was installed for.

The domain does not model any single stage -- acquisition, extinction, and generalization are the
conditioning engine's; the immediate, deferred, delay-averse, and stimulation-seeking modes are
reward's; cueing, repetition, automaticity, and friction are habit's; and one alone is a process,
not the installing. Nor is it the pursuit of a behaviour past its cost in a reward currency the
organism has come to need, which is the addiction engine's; nor the standing character a persona
brings to any pursuit, which is the striving composite's; nor the crossing from wanting to doing,
which is the volition composite's. It owns the pipeline: what happens to a response between the
first pairing and the point where it runs unbidden.

The spine is the convergence of three independently established literatures on a single
observation -- that learning removes the reason from the act. Rescorla reinterpreted Pavlovian
conditioning as the learning of a contingency rather than a pairing, and the Rescorla-Wagner model
made it error-driven, so a cue that predicts is answered in advance of the outcome. Ainslie showed
the discount to be hyperbolic rather than exponential, which is why preference reverses as the near
option comes into view: the far consequence does not merely weigh less, it stops entering the
comparison. Adams and Dickinson supplied the decisive experiment for the third stage: after
extended training, an animal continues to work for an outcome it has been made to find aversive --
outcome devaluation leaves the overtrained response intact, demonstrating that the act has become
independent of the value of what it produces. Wood and Neal gave the architecture that follows
from it, habit as a learned context-response association at the habit-goal interface, and Graybiel
the mechanism, a sequence chunked into a single unit retrieved whole from the cue. The three
bridges are those three shortenings: the pairing, the discount, and the groove.

Associative learning, intertemporal choice, and habit formation, wired as a composite over the
conditioning, reward, and habit engines.

## Restrictions

What the composite refuses to model, and to whom it delegates.

- **The single stages (the atoms)**: The composite wires the three but owns none. Acquisition,
  extinction, and generalization are the conditioning engine's; the immediate, deferred,
  delay-averse, and stimulation-seeking modes are reward's; cueing, repetition, automaticity, and
  friction are habit's. Ingraining reads only what the three make in series; one stage alone is a
  process, not the installing.
- **Pursuit past its cost (addiction)**: The escalation of a behaviour in a reward currency the
  organism has come to need, with tolerance, withdrawal, and pursuit continuing against mounting
  harm, is the addiction engine's. Ingraining models how an ordinary response gets laid down; it
  supplies mechanisms addiction draws on, but stops at the installing.
- **The standing character of pursuit (striving)**: What a persona is like when they want something
  -- the disposition they bring to any goal -- is the striving composite's. Ingraining is the
  installing itself, whoever is being installed in.
- **The crossing from wanting to doing (volition)**: The passage from an intention to an initiated
  act is the volition composite's. Ingraining is what happens to that passage over repetitions,
  until it is no longer needed.
- **The momentary override (self-control)**: The effortful interruption of an impulse in the
  instant is the self-control engine's. Ingraining models what the impulse became before the
  override was required.
- **Where the drive springs from, and what is required (motivation, needs)**: The source and
  direction of drive is the motivation engine's; what a persona must have is the needs engine's.
  Ingraining takes an outcome as given and models how a response to it is installed.
- **The installed conduct as authored symbol (the play's pitch)**: A persona's habit made to
  **stand for** a story -- the ritual that mirrors a theme of entrapment, the tic that signals a
  past -- is refused: that is authored key, the play's pitch, not the force on the persona.
  Ingraining lays the conduct down; the meaning read into it is the play's.

## Encoding

Source to constraint. A composite root over three engine atoms it declares as dependencies, with
three bridges, the stages by which a response is laid down.

- **[ingraining](process_ingraining.md)** (the root): how a response gets installed until it runs
  without consulting why -- one loop from situation to act, shortening at every stage. Anchored by
  Adams & Dickinson (1981) on the decoupling and Wood & Neal (2007) on the habit-goal interface.
- **[the pairing](process_ingraining_pairing.md)** (bridge): a neutral cue that reliably precedes
  a significant outcome comes to evoke the response in advance, spreading by generalization and
  fading by extinction. Anchored by Rescorla (1988) and Rescorla & Wagner (1972); the first
  shortening -- the outcome need not arrive to be answered.
- **[the discount](process_ingraining_discount.md)** (bridge): the outcome weighed by its
  distance, the near looming and the far thinning, with the wait itself a cost. Anchored by Ainslie
  (1992) on hyperbolic discounting and preference reversal; the second shortening -- the far
  consequence barely enters the sum.
- **[the groove](process_ingraining_groove.md)** (bridge): the response repeated in a stable
  context until it runs unbidden, cued by the setting and revealed only when friction removes the
  cue. Anchored by Adams & Dickinson (1981) -- responding survives outcome devaluation -- with
  Graybiel (2008) on chunking and Wood & Neal (2007) on attacking the cue rather than the will; the
  composite's twist and keystone, that what is finally installed no longer consults the reason it
  was installed for.
