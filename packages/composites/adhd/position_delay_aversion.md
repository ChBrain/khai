# Position: ADHD, Delay Aversion

## Taxonomy

[ADHD](process_adhd.md), the interval that is genuinely unpleasant to sit in -- so escaping it is rewarded, not merely tempting.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: adhd

## Has

The second movement, and Sonuga-Barke's insistence that it is not the first one in other clothes: a motivational route to the same presentation, dissociable from the executive one. Delay is not neutral for this persona. Waiting is aversive in itself, so behaviour that ends the wait -- filling it, leaving it, taking the smaller sooner thing -- is negatively reinforced, and reinforcement builds a habit rather than a temptation. This is why the ordinary reading of impulsivity as weak [restraint](@chbrain/khai-engine-self-control/process_restraint.md) misses it: the persona is not failing to resist a pull toward the reward, they are succeeding at escaping a state that is unpleasant to occupy. The [delay](@chbrain/khai-engine-self-control/process_delay.md) itself is the aversive object. Where the choice cannot be escaped the cost lands as distress rather than as a [lapse](@chbrain/khai-engine-self-control/process_lapse.md), and Nigg's hot system names the same split from the reward side: it is the motivational machinery that is differently tuned, not only the cognitive one.

## Orders

End the waiting, by whatever is nearest.
The delay-aversion movement orders the persona to treat an unfilled interval as something to be got out of -- to check the phone in the queue, to start the interesting task instead of the due one, to accept the smaller thing now over the larger thing later, and to feel relief rather than pleasure when they do. The room sees someone who cannot be bored and calls it low tolerance for frustration, or greed for the immediate. It stages a persona for whom empty time is not empty but actively costly, and who is paying to leave it.

## Loses

The future's purchase on the present. Carrying this movement, the persona forgoes the ordinary arrangement in which a larger later reward can outbid a smaller sooner one, and pays in outcomes they genuinely preferred and did not choose: the saved money, the finished degree, the unhurried version of the work. It loses, too, the credibility of its own stated preferences -- having said what it wanted and then done otherwise, repeatedly and in front of witnesses, it forfeits the presumption that its wants mean anything. What it trades is the long horizon for relief from an interval nobody else could see was hard.

## Drives

On that aversive interval:
waiting is escaped rather than endured, and the escape is reinforced each time it works. Delay aversion is one of four movements, the hot and motivational route, set beside the [behavioral inhibition](position_behavioral_inhibition.md) that is its cool counterpart, the [default intrusion](position_default_intrusion.md) that fragments the attempt, and the [hyperfocus](position_hyperfocus.md) where the same ungovernance holds instead of releases. That two dissociable roads reach one presentation is the [root's](process_adhd.md); the effortful override of a present temptation and the machinery of waiting are the self-control engine's, where this movement owns the specifically ADHD case in which delay is not merely hard to hold but unpleasant to inhabit.
