# ADHD: Reference

## Line of Work

ADHD as **composite**: the syndrome read as a compound of four atoms this package declares as dependencies, none of which carries it alone. The composite adds only the read of the compound; every mechanism it names belongs to an atom.

The organising claim, and the reason the composite exists rather than a fifth attention engine: **this is not a shortage of attention but a failure of attention to be governed by intention.** The persona attends -- sometimes completely -- and cannot direct it. That single reframing is Barkley's, and it is what makes four apparently unrelated presentations one thing: a pause that does not arrive, a delay that cannot be sat in, a drift that leaves mid-task, and an attention that will not release.

**The four movements are not one mechanism seen four ways**, and the composite is careful about this because the literature is. **Sonuga-Barke's dual pathway** is load-bearing: an executive route (inhibition, updating, shifting failing together) and a motivational route (delay itself aversive, so escaping it is negatively reinforced) are **dissociable** -- children present with one, the other, or both. **Nigg's** hot/cool distinction names the same split from the reward side. **Behavioral inhibition** takes Barkley's model at its word: the pause is not an executive function but the interval the executive functions require, which is why the disorder is one of performance rather than knowledge, and of time rather than attention -- what fails is the future's ability to be motivationally present now. **Default intrusion** carries the default-mode interference finding (**Castellanos & Proal**): task-unrelated thought that should be suppressed intrudes periodically, which explains the _shape_ of the failure rather than its existence. That shape is the composite's most defensible empirical claim -- **intra-individual variability** is the most robust behavioural signature in the field, more reliable than any mean, and it is why the same persona produces a brilliant hour and a lost one from the same effort.

The **twist** is at the root and the whole composite turns on it. **The capacity is the evidence used against the persona.** Because attention is ungoverned rather than absent, every observer has seen this persona concentrate -- in hyperfocus, on the interesting thing, under a deadline -- and that sighting becomes proof that the failure elsewhere is chosen. _You managed it yesterday._ The persona has no answer, because the observation is true; what is false is the inference from a capacity that appears under some conditions to one available under all of them. And the persona's own memory of their good days makes the inference feel fair from inside as well, so the condition supplies, out of its own best moments, the case for treating it as a character failing.

**On the evidence, stated plainly.** The four movements are not equally well supported and the composite should not imply they are. Barkley's inhibition model and Sonuga-Barke's dual pathway are extensively evidenced and extensively criticised -- no single executive deficit is present in all cases, effect sizes overlap heavily with unaffected populations, and a substantial minority show no executive impairment on testing at all, which is part of why the dual pathway was proposed. Default-mode interference is a well-replicated imaging and reaction-time finding whose causal role remains argued. **Hyperfocus is the weakest**: near-universally reported by adults with the diagnosis and well described (Hupfeld, Abagis & Shah; Ashinoff & Abu-Akel), but it is not a diagnostic criterion, has no settled operational definition, and is not established as specific to ADHD. It is carried because a model that omits it cannot explain the twist, not because the measurement is as good as the others'. What should not be taken from the composite: that these mechanisms are a diagnosis, that their absence rules anything out, or that any of it is a claim about a real person rather than a persona's authored architecture.

## Restrictions

What the composite refuses to model, and to whom it delegates.

- **The control layer itself (executive-function)**: Inhibition, updating, shifting, and planning as a persona's general regulatory machinery belong to the `executive-function` engine. This composite reads only the case where the pause those functions require is what is missing.
- **The effortful override (self-control)**: Resisting a present temptation, the lapse, and the discipline that holds belong to the `self-control` engine. This composite reads only the case where the delay is aversive to occupy rather than merely hard to hold.
- **The spotlight (attention)**: Selecting, dividing, sustaining, capturing, and orienting attention belong to the `attention` engine. This composite reads only ungoverned allocation -- the drift out and the capture that will not release.
- **The departure (mind-wandering)**: Self-generated, task-unrelated thought belongs to the `mind-wandering` engine. This composite reads only its unsuppressed, periodic, task-intruding form.
- **Diagnosis**: The composite is authored persona architecture for a play. It is not a diagnostic instrument, carries no criteria or thresholds, and makes no claim about any real person.

## Encoding

Source to constraint. Barkley's inhibition-as-interval becomes the behavioral-inhibition movement's Has and the root's Initiated by; his performance-not-knowledge formulation becomes the root's Lever. Sonuga-Barke's delay aversion becomes the delay-aversion movement, and the dual pathway's dissociability becomes the root's Direction, which is why the four movements are stated as distinct rather than as one mechanism. Castellanos & Proal's default-mode interference becomes the default-intrusion movement, and intra-individual variability becomes its Loses -- a spiky record rather than a low one. Hyperfocus becomes the fourth movement and, through its Loses, the root's twist: the demonstration of capacity the persona did not choose to give and cannot repeat on request.
