# Adhd

ADHD as composite: not a shortage of attention but a failure of attention to be governed by intention -- a pause that does not arrive (executive-function), a delay that cannot be sat in (self-control), a default-mode drift that intrudes on the task (mind-wandering), and an attention that will not release once caught (attention) -- across its behavioral-inhibition, delay-aversion, default-intrusion, and hyperfocus movements (Barkley; Sonuga-Barke; Nigg; Castellanos & Proal).
