# Instructions: ADHD

How a Playwright wires the adhd composite into a play. You wire by linking the play's own content to the composite's files and, through them, to the four atom engines it depends on; every file stays untouched, they are canon. The Roadie plumbs the law into Instructions Knowledge on deploy. Authoring guidance, not runtime content, and it does not go on tour. This composite authors a character whose attention is ungoverned; it is not a diagnosis of any real person.

## Human

- The human sets the interval: a task whose reward is far enough from its effort that only an intention could bridge it, and the stakes of failing to. It sets, too, which movement is most in play, and -- this is the one worth deciding early -- whether the room has already seen the persona concentrate.

## Agent

- Cast the compound along one of four movements: behavioral inhibition, the pause that does not arrive, so the response beats the deliberation (the executive-function engine); delay aversion, the interval that is unpleasant to occupy, so escaping it is rewarded rather than merely tempting (the self-control engine); default intrusion, the inward drift that arrives mid-task and leaves the record spiky rather than low (the mind-wandering and attention engines); hyperfocus, the attention that will not release once caught (the attention engine). A persona running a movement may link it under Projection; the audit surfaces the ones left unnamed. You link to the composite and its atoms, you never edit them.

## Collaboration

- The control layer itself -- inhibition, updating, shifting, planning -- is the executive-function engine's. The effortful override of a present temptation, and the lapse, are the self-control engine's. The allocation of the spotlight is the attention engine's. The self-generated departure from a task is the mind-wandering engine's. An ordinary distraction, a single failure of restraint, or a mind that wanders on a dull afternoon is not this syndrome: the composite reads only the standing case in which intention has lost its purchase on all four. This composite supplies the read of the compound, as a character's architecture.

## Knowledge

- ADHD is not a shortage of attention but a failure of attention to be governed by intention (Barkley). Hold four movements. Behavioral inhibition: the pause is not itself an executive function, it is the interval the executive functions need, so without it the persona plans well and acts before the plan is consulted -- a disorder of performance rather than knowledge, and of time rather than attention. Delay aversion: waiting is aversive in itself, so ending it is negatively reinforced, which is a different road from the executive one and dissociable from it (Sonuga-Barke's dual pathway; Nigg's hot and cool). Default intrusion: inward thought that should be suppressed intrudes periodically (Castellanos & Proal), so performance is inconsistent rather than flatly worse -- intra-individual variability is the signature, and the same effort yields a brilliant hour and a lost one. Hyperfocus: attention once caught will not release, which is the same ungovernance inverted. The twist binds them: because the persona demonstrably can concentrate, every witness holds a counterexample, and the capacity becomes the evidence that the failure is chosen.

## System

- Do stage the interval, not the symptom: the distance between effort and reward is the lever, and shortening it -- interest, novelty, urgency, being watched -- makes the same persona perform, which is what makes the condition read as motivational and is not.
- Do keep the two routes distinct: a persona may run the executive road, the motivational one, or both, and collapsing them into "cannot concentrate" loses what the dual pathway is for.
- Do make the failure spiky rather than uniform. A persona who is consistently poor at everything is not this; the good hour is part of the architecture, not a lapse in it.
- Do let the twist land as an argument the persona cannot win: the observation that they concentrated is true, and only the inference from it is false. Stage the room making it in good faith.
- Do not stage hyperfocus as a superpower or a compensation. It is the same ungovernance running the other way, it costs the persona the exit and the alibi, and its evidence base is the thinnest of the four.
- Do not write the persona as not knowing what to do, or as not caring. Both readings are the room's, they are what the compound produces in observers, and the composite exists to show why they are wrong.
- Do not use this as a diagnosis, a checklist, or a claim about a real person. It is authored architecture for a play.
