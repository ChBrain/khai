# Position: ADHD, Behavioral Inhibition

## Taxonomy

[ADHD](process_adhd.md), the pause that does not arrive -- and the executive functions that cannot run without it.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: adhd

## Has

The first movement, and in Barkley's model the root of the others: a behavioural inhibition that runs late or not at all. The pause is not itself an executive function; it is the interval the executive functions need in order to happen. Delay the prepotent response and there is room to hold the goal in mind, to consider, to sequence, to speak to oneself before acting. Fail to delay it and the room never opens, so the [inhibition](@chbrain/khai-engine-executive-function/process_inhibition.md) that should suppress a competing pull, the [updating](@chbrain/khai-engine-executive-function/process_updating.md) that should refresh what the goal was, and the [shifting](@chbrain/khai-engine-executive-function/process_shifting.md) between task sets all arrive too late to matter. What the persona loses is not the capacity to plan but the gap in which planning is possible: they can [plan](@chbrain/khai-engine-executive-function/process_planning.md) beautifully and still act before the plan is consulted. This is why the condition presents as a disorder of performance rather than of knowledge, and why advice lands so badly -- the persona already knows.

## Orders

Act before the interval that would have let you not.
The behavioral-inhibition movement orders the persona to arrive at the response ahead of the deliberation -- to answer before the question is finished, to leave before the checking, to send the message and read it afterwards. The room sees speed where it expected consideration, and reads the speed as carelessness or as rudeness, because those are the ordinary causes of it. It stages a persona perpetually one beat ahead of their own judgement, and aware of it, since the judgement does arrive -- immediately after, intact and useless.

## Loses

The interval, and everything that needed the interval. Carrying this movement, the persona forgoes the pause in which an intention could have been consulted, and pays for it in a currency that looks like character: the interruption that was not meant as dominance, the purchase not meant as recklessness, the reply not meant as a dismissal. It loses, too, the ordinary defence -- _I did not think_ is true and sounds like an excuse, because for most people not thinking is a choice about how much care to take. What it trades is the deliberative gap for a speed it did not select and cannot reliably spend.

## Drives

On that missing pause:
the response arrives before the consideration, and the executive layer that depends on a delay runs without one or not at all. Behavioral inhibition is one of four movements, the cool and executive route, set beside the [delay aversion](position_delay_aversion.md) that is its motivational counterpart, the [default intrusion](position_default_intrusion.md) that makes performance inconsistent, and the [hyperfocus](position_hyperfocus.md) that is the same ungovernance inverted. That the failure is of governance rather than of capacity is the [root's](process_adhd.md); the control layer itself -- inhibition, updating, shifting, planning -- is the executive-function engine's, where this movement owns the specifically ADHD case in which the pause those functions require is the thing that is missing.
