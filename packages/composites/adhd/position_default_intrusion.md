# Position: ADHD, Default Intrusion

## Taxonomy

[ADHD](process_adhd.md), the inward drift that arrives mid-task -- and makes the performance inconsistent rather than flatly worse.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: adhd

## Has

The third movement, and the one that explains the shape of the failure rather than its existence. The default-mode network, active when the mind is turned inward and ordinarily suppressed when a task begins, intrudes periodically instead (Castellanos & Proal). What arrives is [mind-wandering](@chbrain/khai-engine-mind-wandering/process_mind_wandering.md) in its [unbidden](@chbrain/khai-engine-mind-wandering/process_unbidden.md) form -- not chosen, not noticed at onset, and often not noticed at all until the page has been read four times. [Sustained](@chbrain/khai-engine-attention/process_sustained.md) attention therefore does not decay smoothly; it drops out in episodes and returns. The consequence is the most robust behavioural signature in the literature and the least intuitive: intra-individual variability. This persona's performance is not uniformly poorer than anyone else's -- their good trials are ordinary and their bad trials are very bad, and it is the _variance_ that separates them, more reliably than any mean. Which is precisely why the condition is so hard to credit from outside, and why the persona's own account of it is inconsistent too.

## Orders

Leave the task without deciding to, and return without knowing you left.
The default-intrusion movement orders the persona to lose the thread mid-sentence, mid-paragraph, mid-instruction -- to surface some seconds later holding nothing, having been elsewhere without a departure. The room sees someone who was not listening, and is not wrong about the fact, only about the act: nothing was withdrawn, because nothing was directed. It stages attention as intermittent rather than absent, so the persona is reliably present and unreliably there, and the failure lands at random intervals a listener cannot predict and neither can they.

## Loses

The evenness that lets a performance be trusted. Carrying this movement, the persona forgoes the ordinary relation between effort and output -- the same effort produces a brilliant hour and a lost one -- and pays in the reputation that variance earns: unreliable, inconsistent, does not apply themselves. It loses the ability to promise, since it cannot forecast which version of itself will arrive, and it loses the diagnostic value of its own good work, which is read as the true measure and the rest as the shortfall from it. What it trades is a stable floor for an average built out of two very different populations of moments.

## Drives

On that intermittent departure:
the task is left without a decision and rejoined without a return, so the record is spiky rather than low. Default intrusion is one of four movements, the one that governs the _shape_ of the failure, set beside the [behavioral inhibition](position_behavioral_inhibition.md) and [delay aversion](position_delay_aversion.md) that supply its two routes, and the [hyperfocus](position_hyperfocus.md) that is its exact inverse. That the signature is variance rather than deficit is the [root's](process_adhd.md); the self-generated departure from a task is the mind-wandering engine's and the holding of a spotlight over time the attention engine's, where this movement owns the specifically ADHD case in which the drift is unsuppressed, periodic, and unnoticed at both ends.
