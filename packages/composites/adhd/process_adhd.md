# Process: ADHD

## Taxonomy

A composite reading over four atom engines (no canon file of its own): the executive-function, self-control, attention, and mind-wandering engines this package declares as dependencies.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: adhd

## Initiated by

A persona meets a task whose reward is later than its effort, and the pause that would bind the two does not arrive.
The compound begins where an ordinary lapse of attention leaves off. What enters is not a moment of distraction but a standing condition: across settings and years, the regulatory layer that would hold a goal against a nearer pull runs late or not at all. The trigger is therefore structural rather than incidental -- an intention formed, a delay imposed between it and its payoff, and no reliable [inhibition](@chbrain/khai-engine-executive-function/process_inhibition.md) to hold the interval open. Barkley's reframing is the entry condition: what presents as inattention begins as a failure of behavioural inhibition, and the executive functions that depend on a pause cannot run without one.

## Direction

From a pause that does not arrive, through a delay that cannot be sat in and a task the mind departs, toward a persona whose attention is real but ungoverned.
The name is the first thing to distrust. This is not a shortage of attention -- the persona attends, sometimes with a completeness the room finds remarkable -- but a failure of attention to be governed by intention. Four movements carry it, and they are not one mechanism seen four ways. Sonuga-Barke's dual pathway is load-bearing: an executive route, where [inhibition](@chbrain/khai-engine-executive-function/process_inhibition.md), [updating](@chbrain/khai-engine-executive-function/process_updating.md) and [shifting](@chbrain/khai-engine-executive-function/process_shifting.md) fail together, and a motivational route, where delay itself is aversive and escaping it is rewarded, run as dissociable roads to the same presentation. Nigg's hot and cool systems name the same split from the other side. Onto both, the default-mode interference finding (Castellanos & Proal) adds a third: task-unrelated thought that should be suppressed intrudes periodically, so performance is not uniformly poorer but _inconsistent_ -- intra-individual variability is the most robust behavioural signature the literature has, more reliable than any mean. And the fourth is the one the models did not predict and the persona reports first: attention that, once caught, will not be released.

## Lever

Whether the task's reward is far enough away that only an intention could bridge it. Everything the compound stages turns on this interval. Shorten it -- make the task interesting, urgent, novel, or watched -- and the persona performs, sometimes exceptionally; lengthen it and the same persona cannot start, cannot hold, cannot finish. That is why the disorder reads as motivational to observers and is not: the capacity is intact and its _governance_ is not, so the persona knows what to do and does not do it. Barkley's formulation is the one to stage -- a disorder of performance rather than of knowledge, and of time rather than of attention, since what fails is the future's ability to be motivationally present now.

**The twist is that the capacity is the evidence used against the persona.** Because the same persona both cannot hold attention on the tax return and cannot release it from the thing that caught them at midnight, every observer has seen them concentrate. That sighting becomes the proof that the failure is chosen: _you managed it yesterday_, _you can focus when you want to_. The persona has no answer, because the observation is true. What is false is the inference -- that a capacity which appears under some conditions is available under all of them -- and it is the persona's own experience of their good days that makes the inference feel fair from inside as well. So the condition supplies, out of its own best moments, the case for treating it as a character failing.

## Echo

The syndrome surfaces through four movements: [behavioral inhibition](position_behavioral_inhibition.md), the pause that does not arrive, and the executive functions that cannot run without it; [delay aversion](position_delay_aversion.md), the interval that is genuinely unpleasant to sit in, so that escaping it is rewarded rather than merely tempting; [default intrusion](position_default_intrusion.md), the inward drift that arrives unbidden mid-task and makes the performance inconsistent rather than flatly worse; and [hyperfocus](position_hyperfocus.md), the attention that will not release, which is the same ungovernance running the other way. The control layer itself -- inhibition, updating, shifting, planning -- is the executive-function engine's; the effortful override of a present temptation is the self-control engine's; the allocation of the spotlight is the attention engine's; and the self-generated departure from the task is the mind-wandering engine's. ADHD is what those four look like when intention has lost its purchase on them -- read across the compound, never in a single symptom, and authored as a character's architecture, never diagnosed onto a real person.
