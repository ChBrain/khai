# Process: Estimation, the Likeness

## Taxonomy

[Estimation](process_estimation.md), the substitution where resemblance stands in for probability, and coherence runs against the odds.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: estimation

## Initiated by

The persona is asked how likely a case is, sees what it looks like, and answers with the resemblance.
The Likeness starts wherever [representativeness](@chbrain/khai-engine-representativeness/process_representativeness.md) supplies a probability -- the case matched to the type it resembles while its [priors](@chbrain/khai-engine-representativeness/process_priors.md) go unweighted, the expectation that [randomness](@chbrain/khai-engine-representativeness/process_randomness.md) must look irregular, and the [conjunction](@chbrain/khai-engine-representativeness/process_conjunction.md) in which a detailed story is judged likelier than the plainer one it contains.
It is the third substitution: not how probable, but how much it looks the part.

## Direction

Toward the well-matched case judged likely and the base rate left out of the sum.
The Likeness reads resemblance doing the work of probability -- the quiet, bookish stranger judged a librarian though there are far more clerks, the run of heads expected to correct itself, the specific and satisfying account preferred to the vague one that must be at least as likely -- each judgment tracking the fit of the picture and ignoring how many of each kind there are.
It moves the persona from weighing frequencies to matching a portrait, with everything the portrait leaves out simply absent from the reckoning.

## Lever

The coherence that makes a case convincing against the improbability that coherence buys.
The carrying force is that resemblance is genuine evidence -- categories exist because members do look alike, and matching to type is a fast, mostly sound way to classify; the resistance is that probability does not work like similarity: each detail added to an account makes it match its type better and makes it strictly less likely to be true, since every specification narrows what would satisfy it, so the story that persuades most is by construction among the least probable on offer.
The Likeness rides the composite's keystone knife-edge: the better a story hangs together, the more probable it feels and the less probable it is -- coherence and likelihood run in opposite directions, and the mind reads the first as the second without ever noticing it has.

## Echo

The likely case chosen for looking right, and the odds never entered.
It shows a persona judging by the fit of the picture -- the type matched, the base rate absent, the richly detailed story preferred over the plain one it contains -- and carries beneath it the composite's keystone: that the qualities which make an account feel probable are precisely the qualities that make it improbable, so conviction is not evidence of likelihood but often the reverse.
The Likeness is the note the whole composite closes on: estimation is the answering of an easier question, and its most convincing answers are the ones that traded the most likelihood for the most coherence.
The Likeness is dated from the matching, the moment the persona read what a case resembled and called it how likely it was.
