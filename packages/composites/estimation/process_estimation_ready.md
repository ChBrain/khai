# Process: Estimation, the Ready

## Taxonomy

[Estimation](process_estimation.md), the substitution where ease of recall stands in for how often.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: estimation

## Initiated by

The persona asks how common a thing is, tries to think of instances, and reads its own fluency as the answer.
The Ready starts wherever [availability](@chbrain/khai-engine-availability/process_availability.md) supplies a frequency -- the [retrievability](@chbrain/khai-engine-availability/process_retrievability.md) of instances already stored, the [searchability](@chbrain/khai-engine-availability/process_searchability.md) that makes some classes easy to scan for and others not, and the [imaginability](@chbrain/khai-engine-availability/process_imaginability.md) that lets a vivid case be constructed where no memory exists.
It is the second substitution: not how often it happens, but how easily it comes to mind.

## Direction

Toward the vivid, the recent, and the well-reported judged common.
The Ready reads the fluency standing in for the count -- the dramatic death judged likelier than the quiet one, the recent failure looming over a long record of successes, the risk that is easy to picture outweighing the risk that is merely frequent -- so the world is estimated from a sample the mind assembled by ease rather than by frequency.
It moves the persona from asking what the rate is to asking how readily an example arrives, and taking the second for the first.

## Lever

The ease that usually tracks frequency against the forces that make it lie.
The carrying force is that ease and frequency really are correlated -- common things are commonly encountered and so commonly recalled, which makes the shortcut work most of the time and makes it worth having; the resistance is everything that decouples them: vividness, recency, reporting, personal involvement, and sheer imaginability all raise ease without raising rate, so the estimate tilts toward whatever is memorable rather than whatever is likely.
The Ready is estimation at its most sampled: a mind reading off its own retrieval and mistaking that for the world.

## Echo

The frequency judged from what happened to surface.
The Ready leaves the persona holding a rate assembled from the easily reached -- the striking overweighted, the ordinary underweighted -- and hands that judgment to the resemblance that will finish it.
It is the second substitution: how easily it comes, standing in for how often it happens.
The Ready is dated from the retrieval, the moment the persona searched its mind and read the searching as the answer.
