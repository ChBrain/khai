# Process: Estimation, the Anchor

## Taxonomy

[Estimation](process_estimation.md), the substitution where the number that arrived first holds the answer.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: estimation

## Initiated by

A figure is in the room before the persona begins, and the estimate comes to rest near it.
The Anchor starts wherever [anchoring](@chbrain/khai-engine-anchoring/process_anchoring.md) draws a numeric judgment toward a reference that entered first -- the [planting](@chbrain/khai-engine-anchoring/process_planting.md) of the value, whether offered as evidence or arriving as noise, the [accessibility](@chbrain/khai-engine-anchoring/process_accessibility.md) it gains by being there, and the [adjustment](@chbrain/khai-engine-anchoring/process_adjustment.md) away from it that stops too soon.
It is the first substitution: not what the quantity is, but what it is near.

## Direction

Toward an estimate that settles short of where the evidence would have put it.
The Anchor reads the pull of the planted figure -- the asking price that sets the offer, the first forecast that fixes the range, the arbitrary number that has no business in the judgment and moves it anyway -- with the persona adjusting outward from the anchor and halting while the adjustment is still incomplete.
It moves the persona from an open question to a bounded one: the estimate produced as a distance from a starting point rather than a reading of the thing itself.

## Lever

The starting point that makes the estimate possible against the pull it never releases.
The carrying force is that a mind needs somewhere to start -- with no anchor there is no procedure at all, and adjusting from a given figure is a real and usually serviceable method; the resistance is that the starting point is rarely earned: an irrelevant number anchors nearly as well as a relevant one, the adjustment is systematically too small, and the persona feels itself to have reasoned outward rather than to have been held.
The Anchor is estimation at its most mechanical: a judgment shaped by whatever happened to be said first.

## Echo

The estimate resting near a number it never justified.
The Anchor leaves the persona holding a figure that is mostly a function of where it began -- adjusted, but not far enough -- and hands that judgment to the recall and the resemblance that will shape the rest.
It is the first substitution: how near, standing in for how much.
The Anchor is dated from the planting, the moment a value entered and the estimate began to be measured from it.
