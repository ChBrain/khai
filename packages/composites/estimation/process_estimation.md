# Process: Estimation

## Taxonomy

Judging what cannot be computed.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: estimation

## Initiated by

A persona is asked how many, how likely, or how long, has no way to work it out, and answers anyway.
Estimation starts wherever a judgment is needed that cannot be calculated -- and an easier question is answered in its place: what number arrived first ([anchoring](@chbrain/khai-engine-anchoring/process_anchoring.md)), what comes to mind readily ([availability](@chbrain/khai-engine-availability/process_availability.md)), and what the case resembles ([representativeness](@chbrain/khai-engine-representativeness/process_representativeness.md)).
This composite adds no fourth engine; it reads what the three make together: the substitution of a tractable question for the one actually asked, made without the persona noticing the exchange.

## Direction

Toward the whole business of judging the incalculable, read through its substitutions.
The composite wires the three engines and reads their working in three shapes: [the Anchor](process_estimation_anchor.md), the number that arrived first; [the Ready](process_estimation_ready.md), what comes to mind easily; and [the Likeness](process_estimation_likeness.md), the twist -- what the case resembles, and the coherence that runs against the odds.
A persona does not decline the question for want of a method: it reaches for whatever is at hand -- a figure already in the room, an instance that surfaces, a type the case looks like -- and returns an answer with no sense of having answered something else.

## Lever

The answer that must arrive against the computation that cannot be done.
The carrying force is that judgment is required on a schedule the world sets -- the estimate is wanted now, from a mind with no access to the base rates, the full distribution, or the time to work them -- and the substitutions deliver: fast, cheap, and mostly serviceable, which is why they are used at all; the resistance is that the substitute question has its own answer, systematically off the true one in a direction the persona cannot feel, so the error is not random noise but a lean, and it arrives wearing the confidence of a real computation.
Estimation is read wherever a persona is granted its shortcuts: not a calculator failing but a mind answering the only question it could, and mistaking that answer for the one it was asked.

## Echo

The persona revealed as a mind that judges by substitution.
Estimation does not close; it is the persona going on estimating, gathering the three engines into one reading -- a judgment anchored, retrieved, and matched.
It hands off what it is not: the named tilts a persona brings to judgment before evidence lands, catalogued as standing positions with their motive families, are the bias engine's -- bias names the lean, estimation works the operation; the weighing of options toward a choice is the decision engine's; the frame an option is presented in is the framing engine's, read in the crosstalk composite; the belief held and defended is the belief engine's; and the misjudgment made to stand for a story is authored key, the play's pitch, not the force.
The persona is dated from its own estimating, and read as a mind that answered what it could and took it for what it was asked.
