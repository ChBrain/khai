# Instructions: Estimation

How a Playwright wires the estimation composite into a play. You wire by linking the play's
own content to the composite's files; the files stay untouched, they are canon. The
composite installs the anchoring, availability, and representativeness engines as
dependencies, and their laws arrive with them. Authoring guidance, not runtime content, and
it does not go on tour.

## Human

- The human sets the persona's estimating: which persona is read as judging what it cannot compute -- held by a number that arrived first, reading its own recall as frequency, or reading resemblance as probability -- and, the question that governs the read, which substitution is being made: the anchor, the ready, or the likeness.

## Agent

- From the play's own content, read a persona as answering an easier question than the one asked: link under Projection the substitution in play -- the anchor while a planted figure holds the estimate (the value that entered first, relevant or arbitrary, and the adjustment away from it that stops too soon, from anchoring), the ready while ease of recall stands in for frequency (the retrievability, searchability, and imaginability that make the vivid and recent seem common, from availability), the likeness while resemblance stands in for probability (the type matched, the priors unweighted, randomness expected to look irregular, the detailed story preferred to the plain one it contains, from representativeness). Play the persona as estimating, present in what it projects, never narrated, and never made to stand for a story. You link to the composite, you never edit it.

## Collaboration

- The dragging reference is the anchoring engine's; the fluency-as-frequency is availability's; the resemblance-as-probability is representativeness's -- one engine alone is not the whole operation. The named tilt as a standing position, catalogued through its motive families, is the bias engine's: bias names that a persona leans and which named lean it is, estimation works how the judgment is actually made -- position and operation, both needed. The weighing toward a choice is the decision engine's; the frame an option arrives in is framing's, in the crosstalk composite; the belief held and defended is belief's. This composite owns the estimating itself -- a mind answering the only question it could -- and the likeness bridge carries the keystone: the better a story hangs together, the more probable it feels and the less probable it is.

## Knowledge

- Estimation is a persona read as judging what it cannot compute -- braiding the reference value that arrived first and drags the answer (anchoring), the ease of recall read as frequency (availability), and the resemblance to a type read as probability (representativeness) into one operation: an easier question answered in place of the one asked, with the exchange unnoticed -- read as a wiring of those three engines, and its question is which substitution is being made (Tversky & Kahneman's three heuristics; Kahneman's attribute substitution; Gigerenzer's bounded mind as the counterweight). It runs in three substitutions. The anchor is how near standing in for how much: a figure planted in the room, relevant or arbitrary, made accessible by being there, and adjusted away from too little, so the estimate is a distance from a starting point rather than a reading of the thing. The ready is how easily it comes standing in for how often it happens: ease and frequency usually track together, which is why the shortcut works, but vividness, recency, reporting, and sheer imaginability raise ease without raising rate, so the striking is judged common. The likeness is the twist -- how much it looks the part standing in for how probable it is: the case matched to its type while base rates go unweighted, randomness expected to look irregular, and the detailed story judged likelier than the plainer one it contains, where the operation is seen for what it is: every detail added makes an account match its type better and makes it strictly less likely, since each specification narrows what would satisfy it, so coherence and likelihood run in opposite directions and the mind reads the first as the second. Read a persona as answering what it could, and read its most convincing judgments as the ones that traded the most likelihood for the most coherence.

## System

- Do read the persona as substituting an easier question, never as computing: name whether the anchor (a number that arrived first), the ready (what comes to mind), or the likeness (what it resembles) is doing the work, and cast each from its engine.
- Do keep the substitutions distinct: the anchor sets where the estimate rests, the ready sets how often it seems, the likeness sets how probable it looks.
- Do let the likeness carry the keystone: conviction is not evidence of likelihood but often the reverse -- the account that persuades most is by construction among the least probable on offer.
- Do keep the heuristics honest as heuristics: they are economical and usually effective, which is why a mind uses them; the error is a systematic lean, not stupidity, and the persona feels itself to have reasoned.
- Do not model the named tilt as a standing position (that is bias), the weighing toward a choice (that is decision), the frame an option arrives in (that is framing, in crosstalk), or the belief held and defended (that is belief). Do not make the persona's misjudgment stand for a story (that is the play's pitch). Do not edit the composite's files; wire only from the play's side.
