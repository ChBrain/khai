# Process: Margin, the Load

## Taxonomy

[Margin](process_margin.md), the reserve spent while the state that would report it does not move.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: margin

## Initiated by

Something is accumulating, and the process is absorbing it without any sign of doing so.
The Load starts wherever [tipping-point](@chbrain/khai-engine-tipping-point/process_tipping-point.md) is running -- the quantity gathering behind an unchanged surface, through [loading](@chbrain/khai-engine-tipping-point/process_loading.md); the process brought to the edge where the least thing decides it, through [criticality](@chbrain/khai-engine-tipping-point/process_criticality.md); the discontinuous shift when a trivial trigger finally arrives, through [snap](@chbrain/khai-engine-tipping-point/process_snap.md); and the [ratchet](@chbrain/khai-engine-tipping-point/process_ratchet.md) that will not let the process back.
It is the face of the composite where the change is entirely in the reserve and not at all in the reading.

## Direction

Toward a state that holds until it does not hold at all.
The Load reads the widening distance between what a process looks like and what it could survive -- the team still delivering while every person on it is at capacity, the ground still standing while the water table under it fills, the relationship still functioning on a stock of goodwill nobody has counted since it was full -- and reads the absorbing itself as the expenditure, since absorbing is what the margin is for.
It moves the process to the brink and leaves the level exactly where it was.

## Lever

The absorbency of the process against the finiteness of what it is absorbing with.
The carrying force is that absorption is invisible by construction -- the whole function of a reserve is to keep the observable steady while it is drawn down, so a process doing its job perfectly and a process nearly out are reporting the same number; the resistance is a reading of something other than the level, and there is one available: near the edge the process comes back more slowly from small knocks and wobbles more widely on the way, which is the reserve reporting itself in the only currency it has.
The Load spends the connection between how a process looks and what it can take.

## Echo

A process at the threshold, with everyone about to misassign the cause.
It is the bridge that arranges the misreading: when the fall comes, the trigger is small and visible and the load is large and was not, so the eye takes the last increment for the weight -- over that? -- and the account written afterwards names the occasion rather than the reason.
What it hands to the rest of the composite is the harder half of the keystone: the slide at least ends up somewhere different, and the Load ends up nowhere at all until it ends up everywhere, so there is not even a slow change to notice.
The Load is dated from the first increment that was absorbed, which is to say from the beginning, and from nothing that looked like anything.
