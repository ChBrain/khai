# Margin: Reference

## Line of Work

Margin as **composite**: what a running process has left before it cannot come back. It is not a
third mechanism but a wiring of two: the **drift** engine supplies the baseline that slides one
unremarked step at a time, and the **tipping-point** engine supplies the load absorbed until a
trivial trigger decides everything. These are the only two engines in the canon that attach to a
running process's Direction, and this composite is the first to read them together. It adds no
third engine; it reads the seam the two make -- not their shape, since one is all slope and the
other is all threshold, but their blindness -- and stages that seam into three bridges, closing on
the discovery that the stretch of readings which say nothing is happening is where all of it
happens.

The domain does not model either mechanism -- normalization, uncoupling, and the gradient are the
drift engine's; loading, criticality, the snap, and the ratchet are tipping-point's; and one alone
is a dynamic, not the question of what is left. Nor is it a decision to change course, since the
whole claim is that nobody chose it; nor a conflict spiral in which each party is answering the
last, which is the escalation engine's and is read on the personas doing the answering; nor the
wear of a made thing or the run of a landscape, which are the object-cycle and wildland
composites'; nor a persona's felt lack and what it does to their thinking, which is the scarcity
engine's, read inside a mind where this is read on a process from outside it. It owns the reserve:
how much a running process has left, and whether anyone inside it could tell.

The spine is the resilience and critical-transitions literature, and the join is published rather
than asserted here. Holling made the distinction the composite rests on and made it first:
**stability and resilience are two different properties**, the one describing how steadily a system
sits at its state and the other how much it can absorb before it moves to another, and a system can
be maximally steady and minimally resilient at the same time. Scheffer carried that into the joined
account this composite wires -- gradual erosion of resilience and abrupt regime shift as one
phenomenon rather than two, across ecological, climatic, financial, and social systems alike, in a
book titled to say so -- and, with colleagues, supplied the reason the calm is readable at all: near
a threshold a system recovers more slowly from small perturbations and its fluctuations widen,
so the approach is legible in the **response dynamics** while the **state** is still reporting
normal. That is the composite's keystone stated as a measurement. Dekker gives the organisational
form of the same joining, where locally rational decisions each defensible on their own terms erode
the margin until the failure arrives suddenly and looks like an aberration; Vaughan gives the
worked case, in which an anomaly accepted repeatedly becomes the standard against which the next
one is judged, until the threshold that was never moved is finally crossed.

What the composite adds on top of that join is the pairing of the two blindnesses and the
consequence that follows. In the slide the instrument moves with the thing it measures, so the
reading is wrong. In the load the instrument is fine and is measuring the wrong quantity, so the
reading is right and irrelevant. Either way the process is watched by something the process is
carrying, and a report of stability is produced with equal fidelity by a system in good health and
a system with nothing left. The third bridge is where those two arrive at the same place: a long
unremarkable stretch, honestly reported, taken by everyone inside as evidence about the reserve --
which it is not, and cannot be. The three bridges are the slide, the load, and the calm.

## Origin

The resilience and critical-transitions literature, together with the organisational-failure work
that carries it into human systems, wired as a composite over the drift and tipping-point engines.
The first two rows are the **joining** -- the published work that sets gradual erosion and abrupt
shift as one phenomenon -- and the rest anchor the individual bridges.

## Restrictions

What the composite refuses to model, and to whom it delegates.

- **The two mechanisms (the atoms)**: The composite wires the two but owns neither. Normalization,
  uncoupling, and the gradient are the drift engine's; loading, criticality, the snap, and the
  ratchet are tipping-point's. Margin reads only the question the two share -- how much is left, and
  whether anyone inside could tell -- and one mechanism alone is a dynamic rather than a reserve.
- **A decision (nothing; it is refused outright)**: A process that was turned has been turned. The
  composite's entire premise is that nobody chose it, so a change with an author, a meeting, or a
  moment is not margin at all, and a play that stages a decision and calls it drift has taken the
  one thing out that made it worth reading.
- **The spiral between parties (escalation)**: A conflict in which each response answers the last,
  positions harden, and the other party narrows to an antagonist is the escalation engine's, read on
  the personas doing the answering. It shares the self-amplifying arithmetic and differs in what it
  needs: escalation requires somebody to be responding, and margin requires nobody to be doing
  anything at all.
- **The clocks that belong to things (object-cycle, wildland)**: The wear, decay, and restoration of
  a made thing is the object-cycle composite's, and a landscape running its own dynamics is
  wildland's. Those have their own tempos and their own endings; margin is the reserve question,
  which can be asked of any running process including theirs.
- **The felt lack (scarcity)**: A persona's experience of not having enough, and what that does to
  their attention and planning, is the scarcity engine's -- read inside a mind. Margin is read on a
  process from outside it, and its premise is precisely that the lack is **not** felt: a process
  running out of reserve is not distressed, it is comfortable, which is the difficulty.
- **The capacity to come back (resilience)**: Coping and stress under load are the resilience
  composite's, which owns the absorbing and the recovering as a persona's standing capability.
  Margin owns the spending of it, and the two meet at the composite's own measurement -- the
  recovery from a small knock is what resilience is, and watching it is how margin is read.
- **The collapse as authored symbol (the play's pitch)**: A failure made to **stand for** a theme --
  the institution that falls to mirror a motif of rot -- is refused: that is authored key, the play's
  pitch, not the force on the process. Margin sets what was spent; the meaning read into it is the
  play's.

## Encoding

Source to constraint. A composite root over two engine atoms it declares as dependencies, with
three bridges.

- **[margin](process_margin.md)** (the root): what a running process has left before it cannot come
  back, read on the process and not on anyone in it. Anchored by Holling (1973) on the
  stability/resilience split and Scheffer (2009) on the joined account.
- **[the slide](process_margin_slide.md)** (bridge): the baseline moving with the yardstick that
  would measure it, so the reading is wrong and honestly given. Anchored by Vaughan (1996) on the
  normalization of deviance and Dekker (2011) on locally rational erosion.
- **[the load](process_margin_load.md)** (bridge): the reserve going while the level holds, ending
  in a trivial trigger that gets read as the cause. Anchored by Bak, Tang & Wiesenfeld (1987) on
  self-organized criticality and Thom (1972) on discontinuity with hysteresis.
- **[the calm](process_margin_calm.md)** (bridge): the long unremarkable stretch where both run and
  neither shows, taken by everyone inside as evidence about the reserve. Anchored by Holling (1973)
  and Scheffer et al. (2009) on critical slowing down; the composite's twist and keystone, that the
  stretch in which nothing is happening is the whole of the change, so the tell is in the recovery
  and never in the level.
