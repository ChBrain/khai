# Process: Margin, the Calm

## Taxonomy

[Margin](process_margin.md), the stretch in which nothing happens, and where the two are seen for one thing.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: margin

## Initiated by

The readings are steady, the process is delivering, and there is nothing to report.
The Calm starts wherever both engines are running and neither is showing: a [drift](@chbrain/khai-engine-drift/process_drift.md) whose [normalization](@chbrain/khai-engine-drift/process_normalization.md) has moved the reference so the numbers still look right, and a [loading](@chbrain/khai-engine-tipping-point/process_loading.md) whose whole function is to keep the surface unchanged while the reserve goes.
It is the face of the composite that costs nothing to live through and is not, by anyone inside, experienced as a face at all.

## Direction

Toward a confidence that is produced by the mechanism it is confidence about.
The Calm reads the long unremarkable stretch -- the years without an incident, the quarter that came in as forecast, the marriage in which nothing is wrong -- and reads what an inside observer does with it, which is to take the steadiness as evidence and to treat the absence of trouble as a report on the reserve.
It moves nobody anywhere. That is the point: it is the only face of the composite with no motion in it, and it is where the whole of the spending occurs.

## Lever

The exactness of a signal that says nothing against the fact that nothing is exactly what a failing process says.
The carrying force is that the calm is honest -- the process really is steady, the readings really are normal, and nobody is being deceived by anybody; the resistance is that stability and reserve are two different quantities and only one of them is being reported, so the same steady output is produced by a process with everything in hand and a process with nothing left, and no amount of care in reading the level will separate them.
The Calm rides the composite's keystone knife-edge: the stretch in which nothing is happening is not the absence of the change but the whole of it, and the reassurance is the mechanism rather than a report on it.

## Echo

Everything spent, nothing recorded.
It shows the two engines complete -- one that moves the reference, one that hides the reserve -- and carries beneath it the composite's keystone: read the response, never the level. A process that comes back slowly from a small knock, or wobbles more widely than it used to on its way back, has told you what its level cannot; a process that returns crisply to a baseline is at least at a baseline it can return to, whatever the baseline has become.
This is why the Calm is the hardest of the three to stage and the only one worth closing on: it has no scene in it, no incident, no line that carries it, and any persona who raises it is asking a room to be alarmed by good news.
The Calm is the note the whole composite closes on: what a process has left is legible in how it recovers, and nowhere else without a discount.
The Calm is dated from nothing, over a long period, which is the injury.
