# Process: Margin

## Taxonomy

What a running process has left before it cannot come back.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: margin

## Initiated by

A process is running, it is holding, and something it will need later is being spent now.
Margin starts wherever that spending is under way: the baseline sliding one unremarked step at a time, through [drift](@chbrain/khai-engine-drift/process_drift.md); or the load gathering behind a state that does not move, through [tipping-point](@chbrain/khai-engine-tipping-point/process_tipping-point.md).
This composite adds no third engine. It takes the two engines that bend a running process's direction -- the only two -- and reads what they have in common, which is not their shape: one is all slope and the other is all threshold.

## Direction

Toward a process that cannot return to where it was, by a route nobody selected.
The composite wires the two engines and reads their working in three shapes: [the Slide](process_margin_slide.md), the standard moving with whatever would measure it; [the Load](process_margin_load.md), the reserve going while the level holds; and [the Calm](process_margin_calm.md), the twist -- the stretch where both are running and nothing whatever is happening.
The three are not three kinds of change. They are one question asked of a running process: how much is left, and would anyone inside it be able to tell.

## Lever

The exactness of a reading that keeps coming back normal against the fact that normal is what it would say either way.
The carrying force is that the loss is real and continuous and cheap at every step -- each exception saves something, each increment is absorbed, and no single one is worth stopping for; the resistance is that the process is being watched by instruments the process is carrying: in the slide the yardstick moves with the thing it measures, and in the load the state is not the quantity that is going, so a report of stability is produced with equal fidelity by a system in fine health and a system with nothing left.
Margin is read on the process rather than on anyone in it, and the composite's premise is that nobody in it can see the reading.

## Echo

A process still running, and shorter of what it will need than any measurement it takes will say.
Margin does not close; it is the reserve going, gathered into one reading -- a slide, a load, a calm.
It hands off what it is not: a decision taken to change course is not this, since the whole claim is that nobody chose it, and a process that turns has turned rather than slid; a conflict spiral in which each party is answering the last is the escalation engine's, read on the personas doing the answering, where this is read on the process and needs no one to be answering anything; the ordinary wear and decay of a made thing is the object-cycle composite's and the run of a landscape's own dynamics is wildland's, both of which have their own clocks; a persona's felt lack of a resource, and what that lack does to their thinking, is the scarcity engine's, read inside a mind where this is read on a process from outside it; the standing capacity to absorb a shock and recover is the resilience composite's, which owns the coping where this owns the spending; and a collapse made to **stand for** a story is authored key, the play's pitch, not the force.
The process is dated from the last reading anyone took, and read as a thing whose steadiest stretch was the one it could least afford.
