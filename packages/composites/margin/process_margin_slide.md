# Process: Margin, the Slide

## Taxonomy

[Margin](process_margin.md), the reserve spent by a standard that moves with whatever would measure it.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: margin

## Initiated by

An exception passes, and the thing that would have caught the next one has already moved.
The Slide starts wherever [drift](@chbrain/khai-engine-drift/process_drift.md) is running -- the yardstick moving with the practice it measures, through [normalization](@chbrain/khai-engine-drift/process_normalization.md); the practice coming loose from the rule that still nominally governs it, through [uncoupling](@chbrain/khai-engine-drift/process_uncoupling.md); and a standing pressure tilting the floor so that every step is downhill, through [gradient](@chbrain/khai-engine-drift/process_gradient.md).
It is the face of the composite with no event in it at all.

## Direction

Toward a standard nobody defends and nobody notices has gone.
The Slide reads the accumulation of unremarked steps -- the check skipped because it has never once caught anything, the tolerance widened to what the work actually produces, the review that became a signature -- and reads each as fully defensible on its own terms, which is why the sequence runs.
It moves the process somewhere no one chose, and does it without a moment anyone could point to.

## Lever

The real local saving of each step against whatever still measures back to the origin.
The carrying force is that every step pays and pays immediately: something is faster, cheaper, quieter, and the cost is a reserve that will not be called on today; the resistance is a measure that still reaches past the current baseline to the one the process started from -- and that is the rare thing, because measures are usually kept by the same people the drift is happening to, using the current baseline as their reference.
The Slide spends the ability to say how far the process has come.

## Echo

A process at a baseline no one set, reading itself as normal, and correct to do so.
It is the bridge where the loss is fully invisible rather than merely unnoticed: not a failure to look, but a look that returns the wrong answer, because the reference travelled with the thing.
What it hands to the rest of the composite is the reason a process can be far from where it began and be in no distress about it -- the gap is not felt as a gap, and the persona who names it will be answered, honestly, that this is how it has always been done.
The Slide is dated from the first exception that went uncorrected, which nobody recorded, because nothing happened.
