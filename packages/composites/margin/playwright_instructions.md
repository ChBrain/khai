# Instructions: Margin

How a Playwright wires the margin composite into a play. You wire by linking the play's
own content to the composite's files; the files stay untouched, they are canon. The
composite installs the drift and tipping-point engines as dependencies, and their laws
arrive with them. Authoring guidance, not runtime content, and it does not go on tour.

## Human

- The human sets which running processes in the play are spending something they will need later, and how much is left -- and, the question that governs the read, which face is showing: the slide, the load, or the calm.

## Agent

- From the play's own content, read a running process for what its margin is doing: link under Direction the face that is showing -- the slide while a standard is moving with whatever would measure it (the yardstick travelling with the practice, the practice coming loose from the rule, a standing pressure tilting every step downhill, from drift), the load while the reserve is going and the level is not (the accumulation absorbed behind an unchanged surface, the edge where the least thing decides it, the snap, the ratchet that will not let it back, from tipping-point), the calm while both are running and nothing whatever is showing. Play it as a process and never as a decision: nobody chose this, there is no meeting where it happened, and any persona inside it who reports normal is reporting honestly. You link to the composite, you never edit it.

## Collaboration

- The sliding baseline and its mechanisms are the drift engine's; the load, the edge, the snap, and the ratchet are tipping-point's -- one mechanism alone is a dynamic, not the question of what is left. A conflict spiral where each party answers the last is the escalation engine's, read on the personas doing the answering, where this needs nobody to be doing anything; the wear of a made thing is the object-cycle composite's and a landscape's own run is wildland's; a persona's felt lack and what it does to their thinking is the scarcity engine's, read inside a mind where this is read on a process from outside it; the standing capacity to absorb and recover is the resilience composite's, which owns the coping where this owns the spending. This composite owns the reserve -- and the calm bridge carries the keystone: read the response, never the level.

## Knowledge

- Margin is a running process read for what it has left before it cannot come back -- braiding the baseline that slides one unremarked step at a time (drift) and the load absorbed until a trivial trigger decides everything (tipping-point) into one question -- read as a wiring of the only two engines that bend a process's direction, and its question is how much is left and whether anyone inside could tell. The join is published: Holling separated stability from resilience, two different properties, so a system can be maximally steady and minimally resilient at once; Scheffer carried gradual erosion and abrupt regime shift into one account across ecological, financial, and social systems, and supplied the measurement -- near a threshold a system recovers more slowly from small knocks and its wobbles widen, so the approach shows in the response dynamics while the state still reads normal; Dekker gives the organisational form, locally rational decisions each defensible on their own terms eroding the margin until failure arrives suddenly; Vaughan gives the worked case, an anomaly accepted once becoming the standard the next is judged against. It runs in three faces. The slide is the loss made invisible by a moving reference: every exception saves something real and costs a reserve nobody will call on today, and the only resistance is a measure that reaches past the current baseline to the original one -- rare, because measures are kept by the people the drift is happening to, using the current baseline as their reference, so a process far from where it started is in no distress and will answer, honestly, that this is how it has always been done. The load is the loss made invisible by the reserve doing its job: absorbing is what a margin is for, so a process with everything in hand and a process with nothing left report the same number, and when the fall comes the trigger is small and visible while the load was large and was not, so the account written afterwards names the occasion rather than the reason. The calm is the twist -- the long unremarkable stretch where both are running and nothing is happening, the years without an incident, the quarter that came in as forecast, the marriage in which nothing is wrong -- and here the two faces are seen for one thing: the steadiness is honest and nobody is being deceived, but stability and reserve are different quantities and only one is being reported, so the reassurance is the mechanism rather than a report on it, and the tell is never in the level and always in the recovery. Read a running process by what it is spending, and read its steadiest stretch as the one it could least afford.

## System

- Do read the process, not the people in it: name whether the slide (the reference moving), the load (the reserve going), or the calm (both, and nothing showing) is running, and cast each from its engines.
- Do keep it authorless: a change with a meeting, a moment, or a decision behind it is not margin, and a play that stages a choice and calls it drift has removed the only thing worth reading.
- Do let the insiders be right: the reading really is normal, the steadiness really is steady, and nobody is lying -- the composite is not about negligence, and a play that makes the calm into somebody's failure to look has flattened it.
- Do let the calm carry the keystone: stability and reserve are two quantities, only one is reported, and a process slow to come back from a small knock has said what its level cannot.
- Do stage the misassignment: when the load finally snaps, the trigger will be small and visible, and everyone will name it as the cause -- over that? -- which is the load bridge working, not a lapse in the play.
- Do remember the calm has no scene in it: no incident, no line that carries it, and any persona who raises it is asking a room to be alarmed by good news, which is why they will not be believed and why it closes the composite.
- Do not model a decision to change course (that is refused outright), a spiral where each party answers the last (that is escalation, on the personas), the wear of a thing or the run of a landscape (that is object-cycle and wildland), a persona's felt lack (that is scarcity, inside a mind), or the standing capacity to recover (that is resilience). Do not make a collapse stand for a story (that is the play's pitch). Do not edit the composite's files; wire only from the play's side.
