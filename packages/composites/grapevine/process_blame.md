# Process: Blame

## Taxonomy

[Grapevine](process_grapevine.md), the seam where an event's account turns into a person's verdict.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: grapevine

## Initiated by

The community, making sense of what happened, does not stop at the event -- it names who did it.
Blame starts wherever a [rumor](@chbrain/khai-engine-rumor/process_rumor.md) about an ambiguous event resolves into a judgment on persons -- the layoffs no one explained becoming the story of whose greed caused them, the accident becoming the tale of whose negligence, the shortage becoming the account of who hoarded.
This is the seam where rumor feeds gossip: the news about an event, improvised to fill a [vacuum](@chbrain/khai-engine-rumor/process_vacuum.md), converts into an entry in gossip's [ledger](@chbrain/khai-engine-gossip/process_ledger.md) and a verdict its [policing](@chbrain/khai-engine-gossip/process_policing.md) enforces.

## Direction

Toward a culprit -- the ambiguous event given a face, and the face charged.
The demand that opens rumor's vacuum is not only to know what happened but to know whom to hold responsible, so the [improvised](@chbrain/khai-engine-rumor/process_improvisation.md) account, as it forms, gravitates toward an agent: a person whose fault would make the event make sense, fitted to the story and then written into the reputational record.
The direction is the conversion of the impersonal into the personal: an event that had no author is given one, the rumor's ambiguity resolved by naming a culprit whom the grapevine then weighs, marks, and punishes as gossip does its own.

## Lever

The need to hold someone responsible against the event's own facelessness.
Rumor makes sense of the event but leaves the community with a happening and no one to hold; gossip weighs persons but needs a charge to weigh; the seam is their coupling -- the event's account supplies the deed, gossip supplies the court, and blame is the verdict neither reaches alone. The resistance is the injustice built into the handoff: the culprit is chosen for fitting the story, not for having done it, so the faceless event finds a face that is convenient rather than guilty.
Let the account name a name and the event becomes a person's crime, entered and enforced; hold the event faceless and the grapevine has news but no verdict, sense but no target.

## Echo

The event given a culprit, and the culprit written into the record.
Blame leaves an ambiguous happening resolved into a person's fault -- the rumor's account converted to gossip's ledger and its policing -- and hands that verdict to the reputation the grapevine will carry.
It is the social-control seam Merry traced between rumor and gossip: the way a community's sensemaking about events becomes its judgment of persons, the scandal born where the news about what happened turns into the talk about who is to blame.
Blame is dated from the naming, the moment the account of an event became the charge against a person.
