# Process: Grapevine

## Taxonomy

The informal communication network -- a composite over gossip and rumor.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: grapevine

## Initiated by

A community runs an unofficial channel alongside its official one, and through it travels everything the official one will not carry.
This is the grapevine: the informal communication network by which a group circulates what it is not told, carrying two cargoes at once -- the evaluative [talk about persons](@chbrain/khai-engine-gossip/process_gossip.md) that is gossip, and the unverified [news about events](@chbrain/khai-engine-rumor/process_rumor.md) that is rumor.
The composite adds no third engine. It wires the seams gossip and rumor make into one network: they run on the same channel, an event's account turns into a person's verdict, and a reputation decides which news will stick.

## Direction

Toward a community that knows and governs itself through its unofficial talk -- persons weighed and events made sense of on the same wire.
The grapevine carries both cargoes together, and they feed each other: the [reputational ledger](@chbrain/khai-engine-gossip/process_ledger.md) gossip keeps supplies the prior by which a [rumor](@chbrain/khai-engine-rumor/process_rumor.md) is believed or dismissed, and the [vacuum](@chbrain/khai-engine-rumor/process_vacuum.md) an ambiguous event opens is filled by an account that soon names names -- Rosnow and Fine's hearsay, the two forms of informal word running as one system.
The direction is a single unofficial epistemics: a persona on the network learns who its people are and what is happening at once, from the same low, trust-graded talk, and passes both on.

## Lever

The two reinforcing, each making the other travel further and land harder.
Gossip and rumor share a channel, so the grooming ties that carry a confidence are the very edges an account travels; an event's ambiguity converts, on that channel, into a verdict on persons; and a person's standing gates which accounts are credited -- none of which either atom stages alone. The value is in the seams: a grapevine is more than gossip plus rumor, it is the network in which each becomes the other's vehicle.
No single atom stages this: the grapevine lives where the talk about persons and the news about events run on one wire and feed each other.

## Echo

The community knows its people and reads its events through one unofficial network, each cargo carrying the other.
The grapevine runs as [channel](process_channel.md), [blame](process_blame.md), or [credence](process_credence.md) -- the shared medium that carries both, the event-account that turns into a person-verdict, and the reputation that decides which news will stick.
It reads its neighbours but does not own them: the exclusion the talk may bring is the ostracism engine's, the felt exposure of being talked about is the shame engine's, and the affect that rides the same channel and catches as it goes is the contagion engine's. This composite owns the informal network itself: the one wire on which a community's talk about persons and news about events travel together and feed each other.
