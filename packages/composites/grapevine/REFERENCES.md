# Grapevine: Reference

## Line of Work

Grapevine as a **composite**: a community's informal communication network read as one
thing rather than two -- the unofficial channel through which evaluative talk about persons
and unverified news about events both travel, converting into each other and gating each
other's belief. It wires over two atoms -- gossip (the talk about absent persons) and rumor
(the news about ambiguous events) -- and adds no third engine; its own content is the three
seams the two cannot stage apart. It reads three neighbours it names but does not own: the
exclusion the talk may bring (ostracism), the felt exposure it induces (shame), and the
affect that rides the same channel and catches as it goes (contagion).

The spine is the social psychology of hearsay and the study of informal communication
networks. Rosnow and Fine set the composite's thesis: rumor and gossip are the two forms of
hearsay, the informal word a community circulates outside its official channels, best
understood together as one system rather than apart. Davis, studying organizations, named
and mapped the grapevine -- the informal network that carries what the official one omits,
faster and further, along the ties of trust and acquaintance. Merry traced how gossip and
rumor work as social control, and how a community's sensemaking about events becomes its
judgment of persons -- the seam where an account of what happened turns into a verdict on
who is to blame. And Turner, following rumor through a community's talk, showed that which
rumors are carried and believed is set by whom they concern and what is already thought of
them -- reputation gating credence. So the composite gives the grapevine three seams --
channel, blame, credence -- the joints that turn two separate engines into one informal
network, each cargo the other's vehicle.

## Restrictions

What the composite refuses to model, and to whom it delegates.

- **Ostracism**: The composite models the informal talk, not the exclusion it may bring. When
  the grapevine's verdict turns into a community shutting a persona out -- the silent
  treatment, the cut -- that acute exclusion and its felt threat is owned by the ostracism
  engine; the grapevine feeds it but does not own it.
- **Shame**: The composite models the network, not the emotion it lands. The hot, sinking
  experience of being talked about, the wish to hide when one learns one is the subject, is
  owned by the shame engine; the grapevine causes it but is not it.
- **Contagion**: The composite models the spread of word, not the spread of affect. The
  feeling that leaps person to person along the same channel -- the panic, the mood that
  catches -- is owned by the contagion engine; the grapevine's accounts may carry affect with
  them, but the affective transmission itself is contagion's.
- **The atoms**: The composite adds no content its atoms already own. The evaluative talk
  about absent persons -- its reputational ledger, grooming bond, policing, and venom -- is the
  gossip engine's; the unverified news about ambiguous events -- its vacuum, improvisation,
  mutation, and hearsay -- is the rumor engine's. Grapevine owns only the seams: the one network
  the two become that neither is alone.

## Encoding

Source to constraint, per file.

- **[grapevine](process_grapevine.md)** (the root): the informal communication network as one
  channel carrying talk about persons and news about events together. Anchored by Rosnow and
  Fine (rumor and gossip as hearsay) and Davis (the grapevine).
- **[channel](process_channel.md)** (seam): gossip and rumor run on the same trust-graded,
  absent-target network, the grooming ties that carry a confidence the very edges an account
  travels. Wires gossip's grooming to rumor's improvisation and mutation. Anchored by Davis (the
  grapevine) and Rosnow and Fine (one hearsay system).
- **[blame](process_blame.md)** (seam): an ambiguous event's account resolves into a person's
  verdict, rumor feeding gossip's ledger and policing. Wires rumor's vacuum and improvisation to
  gossip's ledger and policing. Anchored by Merry (gossip and rumor in social control).
- **[credence](process_credence.md)** (seam): a person's standing in gossip's ledger gates
  whether a rumor about them is believed. Wires gossip's ledger to rumor's hearsay and mutation.
  Anchored by Turner (reputation and which rumors are carried).
