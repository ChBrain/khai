# Process: Channel

## Taxonomy

[Grapevine](process_grapevine.md), the seam where gossip and rumor run on one shared medium.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: grapevine

## Initiated by

The same low, trust-graded, off-the-record talk carries both the word about persons and the word about events.
Channel starts wherever a community's informal network transmits gossip and rumor together -- the confidence shared to bind a friend and the unverified account of the crisis passing along the same wire, to the same people, in the same lowered voice.
This is the shared medium: the grapevine's single channel, on which the [grooming](@chbrain/khai-engine-gossip/process_grooming.md) ties that carry a gossip's confidence are the very edges a [rumor](@chbrain/khai-engine-rumor/process_improvisation.md) travels.

## Direction

Toward one network carrying two cargoes, each riding the paths the other built.
The bonds gossip builds are the topology rumor spreads on: to be trusted with talk about a person is to be on the channel that also carries the news about an event, so the account improvised in rumor's [vacuum](@chbrain/khai-engine-rumor/process_vacuum.md) travels the alliances gossip's grooming laid, and both are reshaped by the same passage -- gossip's tale and rumor's [mutation](@chbrain/khai-engine-rumor/process_mutation.md) worn and bent alike as they move hand to hand.
The direction is a single circulatory system: one network, one set of ties, one trust-graded flow, carrying persons-talk and events-news as one traffic.

## Lever

The shared network against the illusion that gossip and rumor are separate streams.
Gossip alone is talk about persons and rumor alone is news about events, but they do not run on separate wires -- they share the channel, so the same tellers, the same confidences, the same trust decide what of either travels; the coupling is that a person cannot be on the grapevine for one and off it for the other, and an account of any kind moves at the speed and reach the network's ties allow.
Grant the shared channel and gossip and rumor become one traffic, each extending the other's reach; imagine them apart and the grapevine's actual behavior -- the crisis-news and the character-talk arriving together, from the same source -- is lost.

## Echo

The community's persons-talk and events-news carried on one wire, by one set of trusted ties.
Channel leaves gossip and rumor sharing a medium -- the same network, the same reach, the same reshaping in transit -- and hands that shared traffic to the seams where the one cargo becomes the other.
It is Keith Davis's grapevine and Rosnow and Fine's hearsay: not two informal media but one, on which everything the official channel omits travels together.
Channel is dated from the shared passage, the moment the talk about a person and the news about an event were seen to move on the same wire.
