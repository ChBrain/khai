# Process: Credence

## Taxonomy

[Grapevine](process_grapevine.md), the seam where a reputation decides what news will stick.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: grapevine

## Initiated by

The community decides whether to believe a piece of news by who it is about, not only by what it says.
Credence starts wherever a persona's standing in gossip's [ledger](@chbrain/khai-engine-gossip/process_ledger.md) gates whether a [rumor](@chbrain/khai-engine-rumor/process_rumor.md) about them is credited -- the account of wrongdoing that sticks at once to the one already ill-regarded and slides off the one long trusted, the same story believed of one person and laughed off about another.
This is the seam where gossip feeds rumor: the reputational record supplies the prior against which an unverified account is judged plausible, so a name decides what news will carry.

## Direction

Toward belief apportioned by reputation -- the account credited where it fits the record, dismissed where it does not.
The record gossip keeps sets each person's expected character, and rumor's [hearsay](@chbrain/khai-engine-rumor/process_hearsay.md) is weighed against it: an account [assimilates](@chbrain/khai-engine-rumor/process_mutation.md) to the target's standing, gaining credence when it confirms what the ledger already says and losing it when it contradicts a trusted name.
The direction is confirmation by reputation: the grapevine believes of people what it already thought of them, so a rumor's fate is set less by its evidence than by the standing of the one it concerns.

## Lever

The reputational prior against the account's own thin evidence.
Rumor is unverified and needs a reason to be believed; gossip's ledger supplies one -- the target's known character, the standing that makes an account plausible or absurd -- so the seam is their coupling: the record gives the news its credibility, and the news, once credited, writes back into the record. The resistance, and the danger, is the closed loop -- the ill-regarded are believed of anything and so regarded worse, the trusted are disbelieved and so shielded, reputation and rumor confirming each other past correction.
Supply the reputational prior and a rumor's credence is decided before its evidence is weighed; strip it, judge the account alone, and the grapevine loses the shortcut by which a name settles what news will stick.

## Echo

The news believed or dismissed by the standing of the one it concerns.
Credence leaves each account weighed against its target's reputation -- credited where it fits the ledger, dismissed where it does not -- and hands that gated belief back to the record it confirms.
It is the dynamic Turner found in the grapevine: which rumors a community will carry is set by whom they are about and what it already believes of them, reputation and hearsay locked in mutual confirmation.
Credence is dated from the weighing, the moment a rumor's fate was decided by a name rather than by its evidence.
