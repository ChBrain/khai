# Instructions: Grapevine

How a Playwright wires the grapevine composite into a play. You wire by linking the play's own content to the composite's files and, through them, to its atoms; the files stay untouched, they are canon. The Roadie plumbs the law into Instructions Knowledge on deploy. Authoring guidance, not runtime content, and it does not go on tour.

## Human

- The human sets the grapevine terrain: the community whose unofficial network carries what it is not officially told, the persons it weighs and the events it must make sense of, and whether that informal traffic is a quiet background of the world or a live actor -- the channel that carries both, the blame that turns an event into a culprit, the credence a reputation lends the news.

## Agent

- From the play's own content, a plot whose informal network is an actor links under Tension the seam it stages: the channel that carries both cargoes, the blame that turns an event's account into a person's verdict, or the credence by which a reputation decides what news will stick. Play the grapevine as one network carrying gossip and rumor together -- the crisis-news and the character-talk arriving from the same source, the faceless event given a culprit, the rumor believed of the already-suspected -- staged, never narrated, and never standing in for the exclusion the talk may bring or the feeling it may spread. Cast the two cargoes from the atoms: the talk about persons from gossip, the news about events from rumor. You link to the composite and its atoms, you never edit them.

## Collaboration

- The evaluative talk about absent persons is the gossip engine's; the unverified news about ambiguous events is the rumor engine's; the composite owns only the seams the two make into one network -- the shared channel, the event-to-person blame, the reputation-gated credence. The exclusion the talk may bring is the ostracism engine's; the felt exposure of being talked about is the shame engine's; the affect that rides the same channel and catches as it goes is the contagion engine's. Whether the production is the whole network genuinely running -- both cargoes on one wire, each feeding the other -- rather than gossip or rumor alone, is the team's review.

## Knowledge

- The grapevine is a community's informal communication network read as one thing: the unofficial channel through which evaluative talk about persons (gossip) and unverified news about events (rumor) both travel, converting into each other and gating each other's belief (Rosnow and Fine's hearsay, the two forms of informal word; Davis's organizational grapevine; Merry on gossip and rumor in social control; Turner on reputation and which rumors a community carries). It wires over gossip and rumor as atoms and adds no third engine; its own content is the three seams the two cannot stage apart. Channel is the shared medium -- gossip and rumor run on the same trust-graded, absent-target network, the grooming ties that carry a confidence the very edges an account travels, so who is told the one is told the other. Blame is the conversion of an event into a culprit -- rumor's account of an ambiguous happening resolving into a verdict on persons, the event-news feeding gossip's ledger and policing, the scandal born where what happened becomes who is to blame. Credence is the reputation-gated belief -- a person's standing in gossip's ledger deciding whether a rumor about them is credited, the record supplying the prior so the account sticks to the already-suspected and slides off the trusted. Read a production by whether the network carries both at once, each cargo the other's vehicle.

## System

- Do give a plot whose informal network is an actor its seam under Tension; do play the grapevine as one network carrying gossip and rumor together, staged not narrated; do keep the reading the informal channel and its seams -- the channel, the blame, the credence -- not gossip or rumor run alone.
- Do run both cargoes on one wire: the ties that carry a confidence about a person are the same ties an unverified account of an event travels, so let the character-talk and the crisis-news arrive together, from the same trusted source, reshaped by the same passage.
- Do stage blame as the conversion of an event into a culprit and credence as reputation gating belief: let the faceless happening find a face the grapevine then weighs, and let a rumor be believed or dismissed by the standing of the one it concerns -- and read the injustice in both, the culprit chosen for fitting the story, the ill-regarded believed of anything.
- Do delegate the exclusion the talk may bring to the ostracism engine, the felt exposure to the shame engine, and the affect that rides the channel to the contagion engine. Do not make the grapevine stand in for the exclusion or the feeling. Do not edit the composite's or the atoms' files; wire only from the play's side.
