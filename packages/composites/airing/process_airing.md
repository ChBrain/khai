# Process: Airing

## Taxonomy

What a working collective can hear about itself.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: airing

## Initiated by

Something is known inside a collective that has not reached it, and whether it does is not up to the person who knows it.
Airing starts wherever that reaching is being decided -- the stage the shared pattern has arrived at, through [group](@chbrain/khai-engine-group/process_evolve_group.md); the stance the collective is organised to value and what holding it costs, through [org](@chbrain/khai-engine-org/process_operate_org.md); and the interpersonal climate that prices speaking up, through [psychological safety](@chbrain/khai-engine-psychological-safety/place_psychological_safety.md).
This composite adds no fourth engine; it reads what the three make together: a collective read not by what it produces but by what gets back to it.

## Direction

Toward the whole of what reaches a collective, read grain by grain.
The composite wires the three engines and reads their working in three shapes: [the Stage](process_airing_stage.md), where the pattern between people has got to; [the Stance](process_airing_stance.md), what the collective is organised to value; and [the Silence](process_airing_silence.md), the twist -- the climate that decides whether a persona speaks at all.
The three sit at different grains and answer the same question: what does it cost, here, to say the thing that is true.

## Lever

The coordination a collective buys by settling against the news that settling keeps out.
The carrying force is that a working collective has to settle -- a pattern that never stops being negotiated cannot act, a stance held loosely buys nothing, and a room where everything is equally sayable is a room that has not agreed on anything; the resistance is that every settlement prices some sentence out of the room, and the sentences that get priced out are disproportionately the ones the collective would most need: the error that implicates the plan, the dissent that would cost a restart, the question that reveals its asker.
Airing is read wherever a persona is granted a collective to speak inside: not a self choosing candour but one already meeting a price they did not set and cannot see itemised.

## Echo

The collective revealed by what it does not get told.
Airing does not close; it is the reaching running, gathering the three engines into one reading -- a stage, a stance, a climate.
It hands off what it is not: being among others and what their mere presence does is the midst composite's; belonging to a group and what that identification costs is the membership composite's, and what happens between groups the intergroup composite's; the institution's power over the person it forms is the subjection composite's; the mechanics of a conversation, its framing and its common ground, are the crosstalk composite's, and the warmth built between two the rapport composite's; a persona's bet on one other person's reliability is the trust engine's, where this is the climate rather than the wager; what a persona withholds for their own reasons is the opacity composite's, where this is what the room withholds from them; the strength of a culture's norms is the tightness-looseness engine's inside the grain composite, a field far wider than a room; the throwing of more after a failing course is the escalation engine's; the rank a persona holds is the standing composite's; and the silence made to stand for a story is authored key, the play's pitch, not the force.
The collective is dated from what it last managed to hear, and read as an entity whose confidence in itself is highest exactly where nobody can afford to test it.
