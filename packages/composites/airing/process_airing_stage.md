# Process: Airing, the Stage

## Taxonomy

[Airing](process_airing.md), the grain at which the pattern between people is still being settled.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: airing

## Initiated by

Two or more people are in a context that will repeat, and what can be said between them has not been established yet.
The Stage starts wherever [group development](@chbrain/khai-engine-group/process_evolve_group.md) is running -- the [forming](@chbrain/khai-engine-group/process_form_group.md) that costs the revealing of nothing, the [storming](@chbrain/khai-engine-group/process_storm_group.md) that begins the first time someone says what they actually think and the room flinches or does not, the [norming](@chbrain/khai-engine-group/process_norm_group.md) in which the edges become understood and the room settles into a working shape, the [performing](@chbrain/khai-engine-group/process_perform_group.md) that costs the forgetting of how hard the agreeing was, and the [adjourning](@chbrain/khai-engine-group/process_adjourn_group.md) that ends it.
It is the grain at which the price of speaking is not yet fixed, because it is being set.

## Direction

Toward a room that knows where its sharp edges are.
The Stage reads the settling -- the meeting where nobody says the obvious thing, the first disagreement that goes badly and the second that goes better, the team that works so smoothly it can no longer remember what the smoothness cost -- each a moment where what may be said next is being decided by what happened last.
It moves the collective from having members to having a pattern: what can be said freely, what carries cost, who pays which cost.

## Lever

The working shape a settled pattern buys against the settling that only conflict can do.
The carrying force is that a group cannot work until the edges are known, and the knowing is not free -- storming is where the edges get found, so the stage that looks most like dysfunction is the one doing the work, and a group that skips it has not avoided the cost but deferred it; the resistance is that the same settling that makes work possible is what fixes the price of speaking, and by performing the price is invisible: the room is smooth, and nobody present can say what it cost to make it so.
The Stage is airing at its most local and most reversible: a pattern still being made, by people who will not notice the moment it stopped being made.

## Echo

The edges found, and the finding forgotten.
The Stage leaves the collective with a working shape and a price list nobody wrote down -- and hands that pattern on to the grains where the stance and the climate will confirm or contradict it.
The stage a collective is in is not a measure of its quality: a storming room is not a failing one, and a performing room is not a safe one.
The Stage is dated from the first cost paid, the moment someone said the true thing and the room answered.
