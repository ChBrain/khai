# Process: Airing, the Stance

## Taxonomy

[Airing](process_airing.md), the grain at which what the collective is organised to value decides what it will hear.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: airing

## Initiated by

The collective has something it is for, and the thing it is for decides what counts as noise.
The Stance starts wherever the [organisation](@chbrain/khai-engine-org/process_operate_org.md) is operating -- the claim it makes as [clan](@chbrain/khai-engine-org/position_clan_org.md), [adhocracy](@chbrain/khai-engine-org/position_adhocracy_org.md), [market](@chbrain/khai-engine-org/position_market_org.md), or [hierarchy](@chbrain/khai-engine-org/position_hierarchy_org.md), and the processes it runs on that claim: the [collaborating](@chbrain/khai-engine-org/process_collaborate_org.md) that reaches for the room, the [creating](@chbrain/khai-engine-org/process_create_org.md) that reaches for the new, the [competing](@chbrain/khai-engine-org/process_compete_org.md) that reaches for the result, and the [controlling](@chbrain/khai-engine-org/process_control_org.md) that reaches for the standard.
It is the grain that decides which sentences are contributions and which are obstruction.

## Direction

Toward a collective that hears what its stance is tuned to and files the rest.
The Stance reads the tuning at work -- the concern raised in a market org and answered with a deadline, the deviation reported in a hierarchy and closed as a variance, the caution voiced in an adhocracy and heard as a failure of nerve, the hard truth softened in a clan because the room is what is being protected -- each a true thing arriving in a stance that has a category for it or does not.
It moves the collective from having a purpose to having a filter: what the stance buys, and what it buys with.

## Lever

The reliability a held stance delivers against the reporting it depends on and discourages.
The carrying force is that a stance is not a bias but a competence -- a hierarchy exists to make the same thing happen the same way next time, and control runs on the record, since what was done is documented, what was documented is auditable, and what is auditable can be enforced; the resistance is that the record is not collected by the organisation, it is handed over by people, and a stance strong enough to enforce a standard is usually strong enough to make the reporting of a deviation costly to the reporter, so the control that most needs the record is the stance most likely to starve it.
The Stance is airing at the grain where the collective's own strength works against it: the tighter the tuning, the truer the thing that cannot get through.

## Echo

The collective hearing in its own key.
The Stance leaves the true thing categorised before it is weighed -- a contribution, an obstruction, or noise -- and hands what survives to the climate, which decides whether it is offered at all.
The gap between the claim and the demand is where this shows: a stance under pressure that wants its opposite either pays to hold and hears less, or drops and hears differently.
The Stance is dated from the tuning, which was set before the news arrived.
