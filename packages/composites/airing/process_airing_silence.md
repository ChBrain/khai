# Process: Airing, the Silence

## Taxonomy

[Airing](process_airing.md), the grain at which a persona decides whether to speak, and where the whole is seen for what it is.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: airing

## Initiated by

A persona knows the thing, has the words for it, and is running the cost.
The Silence starts wherever the [interpersonal climate](@chbrain/khai-engine-psychological-safety/place_psychological_safety.md) is doing its work -- the shared and never-stated belief about what follows from admitting an error, asking a question, disagreeing, or raising a concern, held as a history of who took the risk and what happened to them.
It runs [high](@chbrain/khai-engine-psychological-safety/place_high.md), where the risk can be taken without calculating; [low](@chbrain/khai-engine-psychological-safety/place_low.md), where it cannot; [fragile](@chbrain/khai-engine-psychological-safety/place_fragile.md), where candour is available but priced above zero, so the speaker tests the temperature and what they cannot afford is what they leave unsaid; or [performative](@chbrain/khai-engine-psychological-safety/place_performative.md), where a declared norm of openness sits over an enforcement history that contradicts it, and the room holds both and does not say so.

## Direction

Toward a collective that receives what it is safe to give it.
The Silence reads the calculating -- the error owned in one team and quietly absorbed in another, the question asked without hedging and the question rehearsed until it is safe, the invitation to speak freely that everyone in the room correctly declines -- each a persona reading an ambient history that is legible to everyone and written down nowhere.
It moves the collective's knowledge of itself from what happened to what was reportable, and nothing marks the difference.

## Lever

The record a safe room produces against the reading that record invites.
The carrying force is that this is measurable and was measured: teams rated better on independent grounds reported _more_ errors, not fewer, and the difference was not how often they erred but whether they would say so -- so the reporting rate is a reading of the climate before it is a reading of the trouble; the resistance is that nobody consuming the record can see that, because a low count and a good outcome look identical from outside, and the room that produces the flattering number is the room where the cost of producing an honest one is highest.
The Silence rides the composite's keystone knife-edge: the collective that reports more trouble is usually the healthier one, so quiet reads as well-run and is generally its opposite.

## Echo

The count that measured the climate and was read as measuring the work.
It shows the three grains complete -- a pattern settled, a stance tuned, a climate priced -- and carries beneath it the composite's keystone: every one of them buys something real by making some sentence expensive, and none of them itemises the bill, so a collective's confidence in itself runs highest exactly where nobody can afford to test it.
Performative safety is the sharpest case and the most common: the policy is the surface and the enforcement history is the structure, and a collective that has declared itself open has usually stopped counting.
The Silence is the note the whole composite closes on: what a collective knows about itself is what it was safe to tell it, and it has no way of knowing the difference from inside.
The Silence is dated from the last time someone spoke and found out what it cost.
