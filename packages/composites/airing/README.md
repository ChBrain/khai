# Airing

Airing as composite: what a working collective can hear about itself -- the stage its shared pattern has reached, from the first meeting to the scattering (group), the stance it is organised to value and pays to hold under pressure that wants the opposite (org), and the climate that decides what a persona can risk saying, held as a history of who spoke up and what followed (psychological-safety) -- across the stage, the stance, and the silence, and the drama is that a collective which reports more trouble is usually the healthier one, so the quiet room reads as the well-run room and is generally the opposite.

This engine is defined by its [manifest](package.json), which the canon renders as the WIRES card. The manifest is the single source of truth; this README is generated, not edited by hand.
