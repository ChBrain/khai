# Instructions: Airing

How a Playwright wires the airing composite into a play. You wire by linking the play's
own content to the composite's files; the files stay untouched, they are canon. The
composite installs the group, org, and psychological-safety engines as dependencies, and
their laws arrive with them. Authoring guidance, not runtime content, and it does not go
on tour.

## Human

- The human sets the collective a persona speaks inside: the stage its shared pattern has reached, the stance it is organised to value, and the climate that prices what can be said -- and, the question that governs the read, which grain is deciding here: the stage, the stance, or the silence.

## Agent

- From the play's own content, read a collective by what gets back to it: link under Projection the grain doing the work -- the stage while the pattern between people is still being settled (the forming that reveals nothing, the storming that starts when someone says what they actually think, the norming that finds the working shape, the performing that forgets what the shape cost, the adjourning that ends it, from group), the stance while what the collective is organised to value is deciding what counts as a contribution (the clan, adhocracy, market, and hierarchy claims and the collaborate, create, compete, and control processes, from org), the silence while a persona is running the cost of speaking (the high climate where the risk needs no calculating, the low where it cannot be taken, the fragile where candour is priced above zero, the performative where a declared openness sits over an enforcement history that contradicts it, from psychological-safety). Play the price as ambient and unstated, present in what nobody raises, never narrated, and never made to stand for a story. You link to the composite, you never edit it.

## Collaboration

- The developmental stage is the group engine's; the competing-values stance is org's; the interpersonal climate is psychological-safety's -- one grain alone is not the whole of what reaches a collective. Being among others is the midst composite's; belonging to a group is membership's and what happens between groups is intergroup's; the institution's power over the person it forms is subjection's; the mechanics of a conversation are crosstalk's and the warmth between two is rapport's; a persona's bet on one other person is the trust engine's, where this is the room rather than the wager; what a persona withholds for their own reasons is opacity's, where this is what the room withholds from them; the strength of a culture's norms is tightness-looseness', inside grain, a field far wider than a room; throwing more after a failing course is escalation's. This composite owns the reaching -- what gets back to a collective about itself -- and the silence bridge carries the keystone: the collective that reports more trouble is usually the healthier one.

## Knowledge

- Airing is a collective read by what reaches it rather than by what it produces -- braiding the stage its shared pattern has arrived at (group), the stance it is organised to value (org), and the climate that prices speaking up (psychological-safety) into one question, what it costs here to say the thing that is true -- read as a wiring of those three engines (Edmondson and Lei on psychological safety across the individual, group, and organizational levels; Edmondson's hospital study and The Fearless Organization; Frazier and colleagues' meta-analysis; Tuckman on the developmental sequence; Quinn and Rohrbaugh and Cameron and Quinn on competing values). It runs at three grains, and each buys something real by making some sentence expensive. The stage is the pattern still being set: storming begins the first time someone says what they actually think and the room flinches or does not, so the stage that looks most like dysfunction is the one finding the edges, and a group that skips it has deferred the cost rather than avoided it -- and by performing the price list is invisible, the room smooth and nobody able to say what it cost to make it so. The stance is what the collective is for, which decides what counts as a contribution and what as obstruction: a hierarchy exists to make the same thing happen the same way next time and control runs on the record, so what was done is documented, what was documented is auditable, and what is auditable can be enforced -- but the record is not collected, it is handed over, and a stance strong enough to enforce a standard is usually strong enough to make handing it over costly. The silence is the twist -- the climate held as a never-stated history of who spoke up and what followed, legible to everyone and written down nowhere -- where the whole is seen for what it is: teams rated better on independent grounds reported more errors, not fewer, and the difference was not how often they erred but whether they would say so, so a reporting rate reads the climate before it reads the trouble, and the room producing the flattering number is the room where an honest one costs most. Read a collective as knowing only what it was safe to tell it, and as unable to see the difference from inside.

## System

- Do read the collective by what does not reach it: name whether the stage (the pattern still settling), the stance (what the collective is for), or the silence (the cost of speaking) is doing the work, and cast each from its engines.
- Do keep the grains distinct: the stage is between these people, the stance is what the collective is organised to value, the silence is the standing price of a sentence.
- Do let the silence carry the keystone: the room that reports more trouble is usually the healthier one, so quiet reads as well-run and is generally its opposite.
- Do remember the stage is not a quality rating: a storming room is not a failing one and a performing room is not a safe one, and a play that reads smoothness as health has made the composite's own mistake.
- Do remember performative safety is the common case: the policy is the surface and the enforcement history is the structure, so a collective that has declared itself open has usually stopped counting.
- Do remember the price is never itemised: no persona in the room can say what it costs to speak, they can only find out, so a play that has a character explain the climate has stepped outside it.
- Do not model the mere presence of others (that is midst), belonging to a group (that is membership), the institution forming the person (that is subjection), the mechanics of talk (that is crosstalk), the bet on one person (that is trust), what a persona withholds for their own reasons (that is opacity), or a culture's norm field (that is tightness-looseness, inside grain). Do not make the silence stand for a story (that is the play's pitch). Do not edit the composite's files; wire only from the play's side.
