# Airing: Reference

## Line of Work

Airing as **composite**: what a working collective can hear about itself. It is not a fourth
mechanism but a wiring of three: the **group** engine supplies the stage its shared pattern has
reached, the **org** engine supplies the stance it is organised to value and what holding that
stance costs, and the **psychological-safety** engine supplies the interpersonal climate that
prices what a persona can risk saying. This composite adds no fourth engine; it reads the seam the
three make -- each grain deciding some part of whether what needs saying gets said -- and stages
that seam into three bridges, closing on the discovery that the record a collective keeps of its
own trouble measures the climate before it measures the trouble.

The domain does not model any single grain -- forming, storming, norming, performing, and
adjourning are the group engine's; the clan, adhocracy, market, and hierarchy stances and the
collaborate, create, compete, and control processes are org's; the high, low, fragile, and
performative climates are psychological-safety's; and one alone is a process or a place, not the
whole of what reaches a collective. Nor is it being among others, which is the midst composite's;
nor belonging to a group, which is the membership composite's; nor the institution's power over
the person it forms, which is the subjection composite's; nor the mechanics of a conversation,
which is the crosstalk composite's. It owns the reaching: what gets back to a collective about
itself, and what it costs to send.

The spine is Edmondson's psychological safety, which is where the joining is argued rather than
asserted here. Edmondson and Lei's review of the construct traces it from the organizational
scholars of the 1960s through its renaissance and sets out contemporary research explicitly at the
individual, **group, and organizational levels of analysis**, identifying psychological safety as
the factor behind voice, teamwork, team learning, and organizational learning -- so the climate,
the group, and the organisation are one literature with one construct running across them, not
three subjects placed side by side. Edmondson's foundational hospital study supplies the finding
this composite closes on, and it arrived as a surprise to its author: teams rated better on
independent grounds reported _higher_ medication-error rates, not lower, because the difference
between units was not how often they erred but whether a member would say so -- in some, errors
were openly acknowledged and discussed; in others, a member kept the knowledge to themselves. The
reporting rate was reading the climate. Frazier and colleagues' meta-analysis of 136 studies gives
the construct its antecedents and outcomes at scale. What this composite adds on top is the
extension of that inversion down and across the other two grains -- that a group's storming stage
and an organisation's control stance run the same trade, buying a real good by making some
sentence expensive -- and the consequence for a persona rather than for a team: that the price is
never itemised, so nobody inside can read it off. The three bridges are those three grains: the
stage, the stance, and the silence.

## Restrictions

What the composite refuses to model, and to whom it delegates.

- **The single grains (the atoms)**: The composite wires the three but owns none. Forming through
  adjourning are the group engine's; the four stances and four processes are org's; the four
  climates are psychological-safety's. Airing reads only what the three make together; one grain
  alone is a process or a place, not the whole of what reaches a collective.
- **Being among others (midst)**: Social facilitation, the bystander effect, and the crowd are the
  midst composite's -- what the mere presence of others does. Airing needs a collective that
  persists and has something at stake in what it knows.
- **Belonging, and between groups (membership, intergroup)**: Conformity, loyalty, social identity,
  and stigma are the membership composite's; what happens between groups is the intergroup
  composite's. Airing reads what a collective can hear, not what belonging to it does to a self.
- **The institution on the person (subjection)**: Socialisation, discipline, obedience, and the
  total institution are the subjection composite's -- power forming the person. Airing reads
  information moving the other way, from the person to the collective.
- **The mechanics of talk (crosstalk, rapport)**: Accommodation, framing, and grounding are the
  crosstalk composite's, and the contagion, grounding, and disclosure that build a tie are
  rapport's. Airing reads whether the sentence is affordable, not how it is built or how it warms.
- **The bet on one person (trust)**: A persona's wager on another's reliability, extended, tested,
  withheld, betrayed, or repaired, is the trust engine's. Airing owns the ambient climate, which is
  a property of the room rather than of any pair in it, and the two are separable -- a persona can
  trust a colleague inside a room where neither can speak.
- **What a persona withholds for their own reasons (opacity)**: The secret kept, the disclosure
  chosen, and the deception practised are the opacity composite's. Airing reads what the room
  withholds from the persona: the cost that makes a sentence unaffordable rather than the motive
  that makes it unwanted.
- **The culture's norm field (tightness-looseness, grain)**: The strength of the norms a persona
  lives under and the latitude a world grants are the tightness-looseness engine's, inside the
  grain composite. That is a field far wider than a room; airing owns the local climate, which can
  and does diverge from it.
- **Throwing more after a failure (escalation)**: Persisting in a losing course of action is the
  escalation engine's. Airing reads why the news that would stop it never arrived.
- **The silence as authored symbol (the play's pitch)**: A quiet room made to **stand for** a story
  -- the meeting that mirrors a theme of complicity -- is refused: that is authored key, the play's
  pitch, not the force. Airing sets what reaches the collective; the meaning read into it is the
  play's.

## Encoding

Source to constraint. A composite root over three engine atoms it declares as dependencies, with
three bridges, the grains at which a collective decides what it will hear.

- **[airing](process_airing.md)** (the root): what a working collective can hear about itself --
  the coordination it buys by settling, against the news that settling keeps out. Anchored by
  Edmondson & Lei (2014).
- **[the stage](process_airing_stage.md)** (bridge): the pattern between people settling over a
  repeating context, with storming the stage that finds the edges and performing the stage that
  forgets what finding them cost. Anchored by Tuckman (1965) and Tuckman & Jensen (1977).
- **[the stance](process_airing_stance.md)** (bridge): what the collective is organised to value,
  and the filter that follows -- control running on a record it does not collect but is handed,
  by people for whom handing it over is costly. Anchored by Quinn & Rohrbaugh (1983) and Cameron &
  Quinn (1999).
- **[the silence](process_airing_silence.md)** (bridge): the climate held as a history of who
  spoke and what followed, running high, low, fragile, or performative. Anchored by Edmondson
  (1999, 2018) and Frazier et al. (2017); the composite's twist and keystone, that the collective
  reporting more trouble is usually the healthier one, so quiet reads as well-run and is generally
  its opposite.
