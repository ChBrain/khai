# Process: Anomaly, the Recoil

## Taxonomy

[Anomaly](process_anomaly.md), what arrives instead of a verdict, and where the three are seen for one thing.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: anomaly

## Initiated by

The sorting has failed, and something else answers in its place.
The Recoil starts wherever [disgust](@chbrain/khai-engine-disgust/process_disgust.md) takes the case -- the rejection and the impulse to withdraw, with [contamination](@chbrain/khai-engine-disgust/process_contamination.md) making the taint spread and cling by contact and by likeness, and a standing [sensitivity](@chbrain/khai-engine-disgust/position_sensitivity.md) setting how low the threshold sits.
It is the face of the composite where the persona is certain, and about the wrong thing.

## Direction

Toward a conclusion about the object, drawn from a fact about the scheme.
The Recoil reads the substitution: no judgement was reached, and what the persona is left holding is a strong feeling, which they do what anybody does with a strong feeling and treat as evidence -- so the anomaly is reported as foul, wrong, or not right rather than as unclassified, and the report is offered in perfect good faith.
It moves the persona out of the unfinished operation, and it does so by settling a question that was never asked.

## Lever

The relief of a resolved case against the accuracy of the resolution.
The carrying force is that the recoil works: it ends the pressure, it licenses avoidance, and avoidance removes the instance from view, so the scheme is never confronted with the case again and comes away confirmed -- and the mechanism supplies its own durability, since a taint that spreads by contact and by likeness cannot be argued off, which is why what a persona finds unclean is so much harder to move than what they believe; the resistance is the one move that dissolves it, which is being made to say which two kinds the thing is between -- because that question is answerable, and answering it converts a contamination into a boundary, which a persona can look at.
The Recoil rides the composite's keystone knife-edge: the strength of the reaction is a measurement of the scheme rather than of the thing, and it presents itself as exactly the opposite.

## Echo

A verdict on an object, which was a report on a boundary.
It shows the three engines complete -- a scheme of kinds, an instance it cannot take, and a rejection standing in for a judgement -- and carries beneath it the composite's keystone: what a persona cannot place, they do not file as unplaced, they file as unclean, so their revulsions map the edges of their categories and not the hazards of their world.
This is why the Recoil is the hardest of the three to stage and the only one worth closing on: the persona must be sincere, must be unable to name what is wrong, must offer a reason that is plainly a reconstruction, and must be somebody the audience would otherwise trust -- and a play that supplies a bigot has replaced a classification with a character and lost what it was for.
The Recoil is the note the whole composite closes on: where a persona's kinds run out is legible in what they cannot stand.
The Recoil is dated from the failed sorting, and it does not know that.
