# Process: Anomaly, the Misfit

## Taxonomy

[Anomaly](process_anomaly.md), the instance that falls between, or into two at once.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: anomaly

## Initiated by

The sorting runs, and returns nothing.
The Misfit starts wherever [the uncanny](@chbrain/khai-engine-uncanny/process_uncanny.md) is firing on an undecidable sort -- most sharply the [animate](@chbrain/khai-engine-uncanny/process_animate.md) blur, where the living and the not-living will not separate, and alongside it the [double](@chbrain/khai-engine-uncanny/process_double.md) where a self that should be singular is not and the [revenant](@chbrain/khai-engine-uncanny/process_revenant.md) where the dead are not wholly gone.
It is the face of the composite where the persona cannot say what is wrong.

## Direction

Toward a sorting that will not finish and will not stop.
The Misfit reads the specific failure and distinguishes it from ignorance: the persona is not short of information, and being told more does not help -- the trouble is that the thing satisfies the criteria of two kinds, or lands in the gap the scheme does not cover, so more evidence produces a better-described anomaly rather than a resolution.
It moves the persona nowhere, and keeps them there with the process still running.

## Lever

The demand for a kind against an instance that will not supply one.
The carrying force is that the persona cannot decline to sort: the machinery is automatic and does not offer an abstention, so it keeps returning to the case, which is what the disturbance is made of -- an unfinished operation that will not release attention; the resistance is that nothing about the object needs to be wrong for this to happen, since the case is produced by the fit between the thing and the scheme, and the same object will pass without incident through a persona whose kinds are cut elsewhere.
The Misfit spends the persona's composure and produces no conclusion to spend it on.

## Echo

An instance still unsorted, and a persona who cannot leave it alone.
It is the bridge that supplies the pressure the last bridge discharges, and it locates it correctly: the difficulty is a relation between a thing and a scheme, and it lives in neither of them by itself.
What it hands upward is an unresolved operation with a charge on it, and the fact that the persona has no vocabulary for what has gone wrong -- so whatever explanation arrives will be about the object, because the object is the only thing they can see.
The Misfit is dated from the moment the sorting failed, which is a moment the persona did not notice.
