# Process: Anomaly

## Taxonomy

What a persona does with a thing that will not sort.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: anomaly

## Initiated by

Something is in front of a persona, and their kinds will not take it.
Anomaly starts wherever that is happening: instances sorted into kinds and the kinds themselves formed, through [categorization](@chbrain/khai-engine-categorization/process_categorization.md); a sort that will not resolve and turns disturbing, through [the uncanny](@chbrain/khai-engine-uncanny/process_uncanny.md); and a charged rejection with a taint that clings, through [disgust](@chbrain/khai-engine-disgust/process_disgust.md).
This composite adds no fourth engine. It reads the one path the three make together, which runs from a scheme of kinds to a feeling nobody experiences as being about kinds.

## Direction

Toward a rejection the persona will offer as a description.
The composite wires the three engines and reads their working in three shapes: [the Scheme](process_anomaly_scheme.md), the kinds already held and sharpening themselves; [the Misfit](process_anomaly_misfit.md), the instance that falls between; and [the Recoil](process_anomaly_recoil.md), the twist -- what arrives instead of a verdict, and what it is actually a report on.
The three are not three strengths of revulsion. They are one classification meeting its own edge, and the feeling is the edge becoming audible.

## Lever

The usefulness of having kinds at all against what having them costs at the seams.
The carrying force is that a scheme of kinds is not optional and mostly not wrong: a persona who could not sort could not act, and every kind they hold earns its place by making the world predictable in the middle of itself -- nothing here is a failure of thought; the resistance is that a scheme is finite and the world is not, so there will be things at its seams, and the machinery has no state for **unsorted** -- it has only kinds, so a thing that fits none of them is not filed under nothing, it is met with something.
Anomaly is read on the scheme and never on the object, because the object is usually doing nothing at all.

## Echo

A revulsion, sincerely felt, describing a boundary rather than a thing.
Anomaly does not close; it is one pass of a classification through a case it cannot take, gathered into one reading -- a scheme, a misfit, a recoil.
It hands off what it is not: disgust aimed at somebody who broke a moral code is condemnation's and hate's, which wire this same disgust engine on a wrongdoer where this runs it on a thing that has done nothing; a discrediting attribute borne by the one who has it is carrying's, and this composite reads the room's side of a much wider case; what happens when the room's role and its read disagree is unbecoming's, and its scissors close on a person in a position rather than on a thing between kinds; the us/them line is intergroup's; awe, confusion, and surprise at what does not fit a model are knowing's, and are the same shape without the charge; the errors of judgement in their own right are estimation's; and a misfit made to **stand for** a theme is authored key, the play's pitch, not the force.
The persona is dated from the kinds they hold, and from what they could not put in any of them.
