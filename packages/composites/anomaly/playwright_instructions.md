# Instructions: Anomaly

How a Playwright wires the anomaly composite into a play. You wire by linking the play's own
content to the composite's files; the files stay untouched, they are canon. The composite
installs the categorization, uncanny, and disgust engines as dependencies, and their laws
arrive with them. Authoring guidance, not runtime content, and it does not go on tour.

## Human

- The human sets which things in the play fall between the kinds the room holds, and what the room does about it.

## Agent

- From the play's own content, read a persona whose kinds have met something they will not take, and link under Projection what is running: the scheme while instances are being sorted by prototype, by remembered exemplars, or by an intuitive theory of why the features go together (from categorization), the misfit while a sorting will not resolve and the disturbance of an undecidable case is firing (from uncanny), the recoil while a charged rejection with a clinging taint has arrived in place of a verdict (from disgust). Read it on the scheme and never on the object, since the object is usually doing nothing. You link to the composite, you never edit it.

## Collaboration

- Prototype, exemplar, and theory are the categorization engine's; the double, recurrence, animism, the animate blur, and the revenant are the uncanny's; pathogen, sexual, sociomoral, contamination, and sensitivity are disgust's -- one alone is a process, not the path. Disgust aimed at somebody who broke a moral code is condemnation's and hate's, which wire this same disgust engine on a wrongdoer where this runs it on a thing that has done nothing; what being unplaceable costs the one who is unplaced is carrying's, read from the bearer's side; a role and a read that disagree about a person in a position is unbecoming's; the us/them line is intergroup's; awe, confusion, surprise, and interest are knowing's, and are the same structural event met with attention instead of rejection; the catalogued errors are estimation's. This composite owns the path -- and the recoil bridge carries the keystone: the strength of the reaction measures the scheme rather than the thing.

## Knowledge

- Anomaly is a persona read while their scheme of kinds meets something it cannot take -- braiding the sorting (categorization: kinds formed and instances placed, membership settled by resemblance to a prototype, by match to stored exemplars, or by fit to an intuitive theory) with the failure (uncanny: the disturbance of a sort that will not decide, most sharply where the living and the not-living will not separate) and with what answers in its place (disgust: rejection, withdrawal, and a taint that spreads by contact and by likeness). The join is Mary Douglas's, and it is a claim none of the three atoms makes: pollution is not a property of substances but a judgement produced by a classification, so dirt is matter out of place and what a persona treats as unclean is what their own scheme cannot accommodate. It runs in three faces. The scheme is the kinds already held and working, and nothing in it is a prejudice -- the same apparatus sorts cutlery, weather, and people with no change of gear, and its successes are invisible because they are constant. But a classification in use does not stay neutral about its own boundaries: differences across a line are read larger and differences within a kind smaller than they are, so a scheme sharpens its seams the longer it runs well, and an edge case gets less tolerable rather than more. The misfit is the instance that satisfies two kinds or falls in the gap, and the persona is not short of information -- being told more produces a better-described anomaly rather than a resolution, and the machinery offers no abstention, so it keeps returning to the case, which is what the disturbance is made of. The recoil is the twist: no judgement was reached, the persona is left holding a strong feeling, and they do with it what anybody does with a strong feeling, which is treat it as evidence -- so the thing is reported as foul or wrong rather than as unclassified, in perfect good faith. Here the three are seen for one thing: what a persona cannot place, they do not file as unplaced, because there is no such state; they file it as unclean, so their revulsions map the edges of their categories rather than the hazards of their world -- and the recoil is durable in a way a belief is not, because a taint that spreads by contact and by likeness cannot be argued off.

## System

- Do read the scheme and never the object: name whether the scheme (an ordinary sorting), the misfit (a sorting that will not resolve), or the recoil (a rejection standing in for a judgement) is running, and cast each from its engines.
- Do make the thing genuinely between kinds rather than merely unpleasant, since a plainly foul thing needs none of this and staging one loses the mechanism entirely.
- Do keep the persona unable to name what is wrong, and let the reason they give be plainly a reconstruction -- a persona who can say which two kinds it is between has already stopped defending the boundary, and that is the only move that dissolves this.
- Do make the persona somebody the audience would otherwise trust, because a play that supplies a bigot has replaced a classification with a character.
- Do let the recoil work: it ends the pressure, licenses avoidance, and removes the case from view, so the scheme comes away confirmed and the persona has no reason to revisit it.
- Do remember the composite reads one route into revulsion and not the whole of it: disgust has a disease-avoidance core that owes nothing to classification, and a play that treats every recoil as this mechanism is making a claim the composite does not.
- Do not model disgust at a wrongdoer (that is condemnation and hate, wiring the same engine on somebody who did something), what being unplaceable costs the unplaced (that is carrying), a role and a read that disagree (that is unbecoming), the us/them line (that is intergroup), or the same failure met with attention instead of rejection (that is knowing, and which of the two fires is not settled here). Do not make a misfit stand for a theme (that is the play's pitch). Do not edit the composite's files; wire only from the play's side.
