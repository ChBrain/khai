# Anomaly: Reference

## Line of Work

Anomaly as **composite**: what a persona does with a thing that will not sort. It is not a fourth
mechanism but a wiring of three: the **categorization** engine supplies the scheme -- kinds formed and
instances sorted, by resemblance to a prototype, by match to stored exemplars, or by fit to an
intuitive theory of why the features go together -- the **uncanny** engine supplies what an undecidable
sort feels like from inside, and the **disgust** engine supplies the charged rejection that arrives in
place of a verdict.

The joining is Mary Douglas's, and it is a claim about the relation between the atoms that none of
them makes. Her argument is that pollution is not a property of substances but a judgement produced by
a classification: dirt is matter out of place, so what a culture treats as unclean is what its own
scheme of kinds cannot accommodate. Her reading of the Levitical abominations is the worked example --
the forbidden creatures are, on her account, the ones that fail the scheme's own definitions of their
element, the things that swarm where they should walk or lack the fins their class requires. The claim
generalises past its ethnography and past the sacred: wherever a persona holds a set of kinds, the
instances at the seams will draw a response the persona experiences as being about the instance.

The composite needs all three atoms to state it. From categorization: the scheme, and the property
that makes it get worse rather than better with use -- Tajfel and Wilkes showed that imposing a
classification on a continuum makes judgements exaggerate differences across the boundary and shrink
them within a class, so a scheme in service sharpens its own seams. From the uncanny: the phenomenology
of the failure, which Jentsch located precisely in the doubt whether a figure is alive, and which the
atom carries as its animate expression. Mori's uncanny valley is the same shape arriving from
engineering rather than from psychoanalysis -- a figure that is nearly human and not quite is met with
revulsion where a plainly mechanical one is not -- and it is the second independent instance the
composite rests on. From disgust: the response itself, and the contagion and similarity laws that make
it spread, cling, and resist correction, which is why a recoil is so much more durable than a belief.

That is the keystone. What a persona cannot place, they do not file as unplaced -- the machinery has no
such state -- they file as unclean, and then read the resulting feeling as information about the
object. So the strength of a recoil measures the tightness of a classification and not the badness of
a thing, and it presents itself as exactly the opposite.

It is the third composite to wire the disgust engine. Condemnation runs it as one of the three
other-condemning moral emotions, keyed to violations of purity, and hate as one of the ingredients of
a durable hostility; both run it on a wrongdoer who has done something. Here it runs on a thing that
has done nothing, which is what makes the mechanism visible without a moral reading on top of it. Same
engine, different question, and no composite owns it.

The domain does not model any side alone -- prototype, exemplar, and theory are the categorization
engine's; the double, recurrence, animism, the animate blur, and the revenant are the uncanny's;
pathogen, sexual, sociomoral, contamination, and sensitivity are disgust's. Nor is it awe, confusion,
and surprise at what does not fit a model, which are knowing's and are the same shape without the
charge. It owns the path: a scheme, a case it cannot take, and a feeling that answers for it.

**On the evidence, stated plainly.** The three atoms are each well founded; the joining is influential
and genuinely contested, and the composite should be read as one route rather than as a theory of
disgust. Douglas's anomaly account has been criticised on the ground that it is neither necessary nor
sufficient -- many powerful disgust elicitors, faeces and rot and decay among them, are not
category-anomalous at all and are far better explained by disease avoidance, and many anomalies produce
curiosity, delight, or reverence rather than revulsion, which is a fact about anomaly the theory does
not predict. Douglas herself qualified the argument in later work. The dominant contemporary account of
disgust is functional and pathogen-first -- it is the account the disgust atom itself carries -- and
nothing here displaces it. Mori's valley is widely cited and its empirical status is mixed: a dip has
been found in careful studies, but the shape of the curve and the category-ambiguity explanation of it
are both actively disputed, with realism inconsistency a live rival. The accentuation effect is solid.
What should not be taken from this composite is that revulsion is generally about classification, or
that a persona's disgust can be argued away by pointing at their categories. The claim is narrow and
conditional: where a thing genuinely falls between a persona's kinds and the sorting will not resolve,
expect a charged rejection rather than a suspended judgement, and expect the persona to read the charge
as a property of the thing.

## Origin

Categorization research, the psychoanalytic literature on the uncanny, and the psychology and
anthropology of pollution, wired as a composite over the categorization, uncanny, and disgust engines.
The first row is the **joining** -- the published work that derives a charged rejection from a
classification rather than from a substance -- and the rest anchor the individual bridges and their
bounds.

## Restrictions

What the composite refuses to model, and to whom it delegates.

- **The three sides (the atoms)**: The composite wires the three but owns none. Prototype, exemplar,
  and theory are the categorization engine's; the double, recurrence, animism, the animate blur, and
  the revenant are the uncanny's; pathogen, sexual, sociomoral, contamination, and sensitivity are
  disgust's. Anomaly reads only the path the three make, and one alone is a process, not the path.
- **Disgust at a wrongdoer (condemnation, hate)**: The condemnation composite wires this same disgust
  engine as one of the three other-condemning moral emotions, keyed to violations of purity and
  divinity, and hate as an ingredient of durable hostility. Both need somebody who has **done**
  something. This composite's premise is the opposite: the thing has done nothing, and the response is
  produced by where it sits rather than by what it did.
- **A mark borne by the one who has it (carrying)**: Stigma, secrecy, and shame are the carrying
  composite's, read from the bearer's side. Anomaly reads the sorting persona's side and does not
  model what being unplaceable costs the one who is unplaced.
- **The room's two expectations (unbecoming)**: The unbecoming composite reads a role and a read that
  disagree, and its scissors close on a person in a position. Anomaly's misfit is between kinds and
  need not be a person at all; where the mismatch is between a role's norms and a group's read, that
  is unbecoming's.
- **The us/them line (intergroup)**: Social identity, bias, and hostility between groups are the
  intergroup composite's. Anomaly does not require two groups and does not run on membership; it runs
  on a scheme meeting its own edge.
- **What does not fit a model, without the charge (knowing)**: Awe, confusion, surprise, and interest
  are the knowing composite's, and they are the same structural event met with attention rather than
  rejection. Which of the two fires is the interesting question and this composite does not settle it;
  where the persona leans in, it is knowing's scene.
- **The errors in their own right (estimation)**: Anchoring, availability, and representativeness are
  the estimation composite's. The accentuation in the scheme bridge is one specific property of a
  classification in use, not a member of that family.
- **The misfit as authored symbol (the play's pitch)**: A thing between kinds made to **stand for** a
  theme -- the outsider who mirrors a motif of purity -- is refused: that is authored key, the play's
  pitch, not the force. Anomaly sets what the persona's scheme did; the meaning read into it is the
  play's.

## Encoding

Source to constraint. A composite root over three engine atoms it declares as dependencies, with
three bridges.

- **[anomaly](process_anomaly.md)** (the root): what a persona does with a thing that will not sort,
  read on the scheme and never on the object. Anchored by Douglas (1966).
- **[the scheme](process_anomaly_scheme.md)** (bridge): the kinds already held, graded and softly
  edged, and the property that makes their seams sharpen with use. Anchored by Rosch and Murphy on
  the structure and Tajfel & Wilkes (1963) on the accentuation.
- **[the misfit](process_anomaly_misfit.md)** (bridge): the instance that falls between two kinds or
  into both, so the sorting returns no answer and will not stop running. Anchored by Jentsch (1906)
  and Freud (1919), and by Mori (1970) as the same shape from another direction.
- **[the recoil](process_anomaly_recoil.md)** (bridge): the charged rejection that arrives in place of
  a verdict, spreading by contact and by likeness. Anchored by Rozin and colleagues on contamination;
  the composite's twist and keystone, that the strength of the reaction measures the scheme rather
  than the thing, and presents itself as exactly the opposite.
