# Process: Anomaly, the Scheme

## Taxonomy

[Anomaly](process_anomaly.md), the kinds already held, and what holding them does.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: anomaly

## Initiated by

A persona meets an instance, and something in them decides what it is an instance of.
The Scheme starts wherever [categorization](@chbrain/khai-engine-categorization/process_categorization.md) is running -- membership settled by resemblance to a [prototype](@chbrain/khai-engine-categorization/process_prototype.md), by match to remembered [exemplars](@chbrain/khai-engine-categorization/process_exemplars.md), or by fit to an intuitive [theory](@chbrain/khai-engine-categorization/process_theory.md) of why the features go together.
It is the face of the composite where everything is working.

## Direction

Toward a world made navigable by being cut up.
The Scheme reads the cutting as a competence rather than a bias: membership is graded and the edges are soft, a robin is a better bird than a penguin without the penguin being in doubt, and the persona is not aware of running a scheme any more than they are aware of running a language.
It moves the persona from an instance to a kind, in almost every case without incident.

## Lever

The predictive value of a kind against the fact that the boundary is a choice.
The carrying force is that the whole point of a kind is to let a persona treat unlike things as alike and act on the middle of the category, so the scheme earns its keep constantly and its successes are invisible; the resistance is that a classification in use does not stay neutral about its own boundaries -- differences that cross a line are read larger and differences inside a kind smaller than they are, so the scheme sharpens itself with every use and the seams get harder rather than softer, which is why an edge case gets less tolerable the longer the scheme has been running well.
The Scheme spends nothing, and quietly makes its own exceptions costly.

## Echo

A working set of kinds, with edges it did not put there and cannot see.
It is the bridge that establishes what the composite is about: nothing in it is a prejudice, and the same apparatus sorts cutlery, weather, and people with no change of gear.
What it hands upward is the seam -- that a finite scheme meets an unbounded world, so there will be instances at the edges, and the machinery has no way to record that it has met one.
The Scheme is dated from long before the instance, and from whoever the persona learned it from.
