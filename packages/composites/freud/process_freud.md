# Process: Freud

## Taxonomy

A composite reading over six atom engines (no canon file of its own): the structural-model, the-unconscious, anxiety, defense, transference, and attachment engines this package declares as dependencies.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: freud

## Initiated by

A persona is taken as a mind divided against itself, and most of the division out of its own sight.
The compound begins wherever the Freudian frame is cast over a persona: not a unitary rational agent but an apparatus in conflict, its drives, its conscience, and its grip on reality pulling against one another, and the greater part of that contention running below awareness. The [ego](@chbrain/khai-engine-structural-model/position_ego.md) stands between the [id](@chbrain/khai-engine-structural-model/position_id.md) and the [superego](@chbrain/khai-engine-structural-model/position_superego.md), and much of what moves the persona it cannot see (the [unconscious](@chbrain/khai-engine-the-unconscious/position_dynamic.md)). What enters is a psyche at war with itself, and largely in the dark about the war.

## Direction

From forbidden material pressing toward awareness, through the ego's warding of it, to its return in disguise and repetition.
The Freudian dynamic runs a circuit the six atoms trace. Drive and wish arise in the [id](@chbrain/khai-engine-structural-model/position_id.md); the [superego](@chbrain/khai-engine-structural-model/position_superego.md) forbids; the [ego](@chbrain/khai-engine-structural-model/position_ego.md), caught between them and reality, registers the danger as [anxiety](@chbrain/khai-engine-anxiety/process_anxiety.md) -- Freud's signal anxiety, the ego's alarm at a threatened breakthrough -- and answers it with [defense](@chbrain/khai-engine-defense/position_defense.md), the manoeuvre that keeps the unbearable out of awareness. But the repressed is not destroyed: held in the [dynamic unconscious](@chbrain/khai-engine-the-unconscious/position_dynamic.md), it presses still and returns disguised, and above all it repeats -- the early [attachment](@chbrain/khai-engine-attachment/process_attachment.md) template replayed and the old relation [transferred](@chbrain/khai-engine-transference/process_transference.md) onto present figures. The direction is that circuit: conflict, defended, returning, repeated.

## Lever

Whether the persona is genuinely divided and defended, and the past genuinely repeated -- not a transparent mind straightforwardly reporting itself. The Freudian read turns on three things being real rather than declared: that the conflict is actual and mostly unconscious, so what moves the persona is not fully available to it; that the defense truly distorts -- the persona spares itself the unbearable at the cost of seeing straight, and does not know it is doing so; and that the relation truly repeats -- the present figure met through the template of an old one rather than seen fresh. Stage a persona that cannot simply introspect its way clear, whose account of itself is partial and motivated, because that opacity is the whole of the frame. Where the mind is transparent to itself and the past does not press, the Freudian read does not apply.

## Echo

The psyche reads across three movements: [apparatus](position_apparatus.md), the standing structure -- the three agencies held largely in the unconscious, who the persona is made of and that the making runs unseen; [warding](position_warding.md), the defensive economy -- signal anxiety and the defenses that answer it, how the persona keeps the unbearable out; and [relation](position_relation.md), the object world -- the early template repeated and the repressed transferred onto present figures, how the past returns. The agencies in conflict are the structural-model engine's; the out-of-awareness region they run in is the the-unconscious engine's; the signal is the anxiety engine's and the warding the defense engine's; the early bond is the attachment engine's and its replay the transference engine's. The specific love and hate borne toward the one object is the love-hate composite's, and the general structure of opposed evaluation the ambivalence engine's -- this composite reads the apparatus and its dynamics, not that particular affect. Freud is a mind at conflict with itself, mostly unseen, defended, and doomed to repeat -- read across the compound, never in one atom alone.
