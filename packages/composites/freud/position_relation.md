# Position: Freud, Relation

## Taxonomy

[Freud](process_freud.md), the movement of the object world -- the past that repeats in present bonds.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: freud

## Has

The return of the past in relationship: how the persona meets present figures through the template of old ones. What is repressed is not gone, and above all it repeats. The earliest bonds lay down a template -- the [attachment](@chbrain/khai-engine-attachment/process_attachment.md) pattern, secure or [anxious](@chbrain/khai-engine-attachment/process_anxious.md) or [avoidant](@chbrain/khai-engine-attachment/process_avoidant.md), formed with the first caregivers (Bowlby; Ainsworth) -- and that template is carried forward and imposed: the persona [transfers](@chbrain/khai-engine-transference/process_transference.md) onto a present figure the feelings, expectations, and reactions that belong to a significant other from the past (Freud; and the relational-self science after him, Andersen & Chen). The present partner, teacher, rival is met not fresh but as a stand-in, loved or fought as the old figure was.

## Orders

Meet the present figure through the old one, and repeat the bond you were formed in. The relation movement orders the persona to import its history into the present tie -- to bring the early template to bear on the new person, so that the reactions fit the old figure more than the one actually present. It stages a persona that responds to a stranger with a familiar pattern, that recreates the relationship it knows even when a different one is on offer, that fights or clings by a script written long before. It is how the past returns: not remembered but re-lived, in the bond with whoever now stands where the old figure stood.

## Loses

The present figure seen clearly, and the freedom to relate outside the pattern. Meeting the new through the old, the persona forgoes the person actually before it -- it reacts to a projection, misreads the present tie by the lights of a past one, and forecloses relationships that do not fit the template. The repetition is costly precisely when the old pattern was painful: the persona recreates the very bond it suffered, mistaking the recreation for the way things simply are. What it trades is a clear, present relationship for the grip of a history it repeats without recognizing.

## Drives

On that object world:
holds the persona meeting present figures through the past -- the early template carried forward, the old relation transferred onto the new -- as the psyche's way of repeating what it cannot simply remember. The relation movement is one of three, how the persona repeats, set after the [apparatus](position_apparatus.md) of who it is made of and the [warding](position_warding.md) of how it defends. That the psyche is a compound doomed to repeat is the [root's](process_freud.md); the early bond is the attachment engine's and its replay onto present figures the transference engine's. The specific love and hate borne toward the one object is the love-hate composite's -- this movement reads the repetition, not that particular affect.
