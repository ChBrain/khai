# Position: Freud, Warding

## Taxonomy

[Freud](process_freud.md), the movement of the defensive economy -- the ego's warding of the unbearable.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: freud

## Has

The ego's management of conflict: how the persona keeps the forbidden out of awareness, and at what cost to seeing straight. When drive and prohibition threaten to break through, the ego takes fright -- Freud's signal [anxiety](@chbrain/khai-engine-anxiety/process_anxiety.md), the small alarm that danger is near, fired before the full flood -- and answers it with [defense](@chbrain/khai-engine-defense/position_defense.md), the unconscious manoeuvre that distorts perception, memory, or feeling to spare the persona the unbearable. The warding runs a range: from the [mature](@chbrain/khai-engine-defense/position_mature.md) that costs little reality to the [neurotic](@chbrain/khai-engine-defense/position_neurotic.md) and [immature](@chbrain/khai-engine-defense/position_immature.md) that cost much (A. Freud; Vaillant). The persona does not know it is doing this: the warding is itself unconscious.

## Orders

Take fright at the forbidden, and ward it off without knowing you do. The warding movement orders the persona to meet the threatened breakthrough not with clear sight but with defense -- to feel the signal anxiety and, beneath awareness, to distort: to repress, deny, project, rationalize, or sublimate, whichever spares it the unbearable. It stages a persona whose account of a charged matter is bent by the very need not to see it, and who is sincere in the bent account because the bending is hidden from it. It is how the persona defends: the unbearable kept out, at the price of a reality quietly reshaped.

## Loses

The clear view of what it wards, and the knowledge that it is warding. The defense buys relief at the cost of accuracy: the persona forgoes seeing the charged matter straight -- the wish it will not own, the memory it will not hold, the feeling it lodges in someone else -- and forgoes, too, awareness of the trade, since the manoeuvre runs unconscious. The cheaper defenses cost little; the dearer ones cost much reality and can build the symptom itself. What it trades is a true but unbearable perception for a bearable but distorted one, and does not know it has traded.

## Drives

On that defensive economy:
holds the ego at its warding -- the signal fired, the defense raised, the unbearable kept from awareness at some cost to reality -- as the psyche's answer to its own conflict. The warding movement is one of three, how the persona defends, set after the [apparatus](position_apparatus.md) of who it is made of and before the [relation](position_relation.md) of how it repeats. That the psyche is a compound defended against itself is the [root's](process_freud.md); the signal is the anxiety engine's and the warding manoeuvres the defense engine's, and the region where the repressed then resides is the the-unconscious engine's.
