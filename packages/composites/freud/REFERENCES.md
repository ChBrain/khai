# Freud: Reference

## Line of Work

Freud as a **composite**: the Freudian psyche read whole across the atoms it spans -- a mind divided against itself and largely unconscious, driven by intrapsychic conflict, defended against its own drives, and doomed to repeat its past in the present. This composite reads the six its atoms hold -- the structural-model engine's agencies (id, ego, superego), the the-unconscious engine's out-of-awareness region, the anxiety engine's signal, the defense engine's warding, the transference engine's replay of the past, and the attachment engine's early relational template -- and adds only what no atom holds: the read of the psyche as this single compound, conflict that is mostly unseen, managed by defense, and repeated in relationship. It declares the six engines as dependencies, wires their members by hard package links, and reads the compound and its three movements.

**The warrant is a lineage, not solo work.** This is the point the maintainer pressed and the point the composite is built to honour: Freud founded the frame, but the psychoanalytic psychology this composite reads is a collaborative science built on, tested against, and revised beyond him, and khai packages and attributes it as such rather than crediting one author. Freud gave the spine -- the unconscious, the structural model, signal anxiety, repression and defense, transference, the repetition of the past -- but the atoms carry the tradition after him: Anna Freud systematized the mechanisms of defense; Heinz Hartmann and ego psychology reworked the ego as an agency with its own conflict-free capacities; George Vaillant put the defenses on an empirical maturity hierarchy; Melanie Klein and object relations turned the field toward the internalized other; John Bowlby and Mary Ainsworth re-founded the earliest bond as attachment on ethological and observational ground, departing from drive theory; and Susan Andersen and Serena Chen re-established transference experimentally as the relational self. The composite reads the psyche these built together. The three movements group the six atoms -- apparatus, the standing structure; warding, the defensive economy; relation, the object world. The sources below are the integrative literature of the Freudian and post-Freudian synthesis; each atom's own REFERENCES carries the primary science of its part.

## Restrictions

What the composite refuses to model, and to whom it delegates.

- **Each part of the apparatus and its dynamics**: The agencies in conflict are the structural-model engine's; the out-of-awareness region they run in is the the-unconscious engine's; the signal of threatened breakthrough is the anxiety engine's; the warding manoeuvres are the defense engine's; the early bond is the attachment engine's; and its replay onto present figures is the transference engine's. The composite restates none of them; a bridge links, it never re-authors. It reads only the compound those six compose.
- **The specific love and hate toward one object (love-hate composite)**: The particular affective ambivalence Freud found at the root of so much conflict -- love and hate borne toward the same person -- is the love-hate composite's, and the general structure of opposed co-activated evaluation is the ambivalence engine's. This composite reads the apparatus and its dynamics, not that specific affect; where the reading is of the mixed feeling toward one object, it delegates to those.
- **The clinical disorders (out of scope)**: The composite reads the ordinary Freudian psyche -- conflict, defense, transference as universal structure -- not the diagnosis of pathology. The specific neuroses, the trauma disorders, and the clinical syndromes are a clinical tier this composite does not model; it reads the apparatus every persona has, not the illness some develop. The line Freud drew from conflict to symptom is real, but the symptom's diagnosis is not this composite's to make.
- **The later revisions that reject the frame (out of domain)**: The composite reads the psychodynamic tradition that built on Freud's spine, not the schools that discard it. Where a later psychology abandons the unconscious, the drive, or the intrapsychic frame entirely, it is outside what this composite reads; the composite holds the Freudian and object-relational lineage, and does not claim the traditions that broke from it.

## Encoding

Source to constraint. A root (the Freudian psyche as intrapsychic conflict) with three movements, each a cluster of atoms, wired by hard package links into the six it spans.

- **[freud](process_freud.md)** (the root): the psyche as a mind divided against itself and largely unconscious -- conflict among the agencies (structural-model), mostly unseen (the-unconscious), defended by warding (anxiety, defense), and repeated in relationship (transference, attachment). Anchored by Freud's spine and the tradition that built on it.
- **[freud, apparatus](position_apparatus.md)** (movement, the standing structure): the three agencies (id, ego, superego) held largely in the unconscious -- who the persona is made of, and that the making runs unseen. Anchored by Freud and Hartmann over the structural-model and the-unconscious members it links.
- **[freud, warding](position_warding.md)** (movement, the defensive economy): signal anxiety and the defenses that answer it, mature to immature -- how the persona keeps the unbearable out, at a cost to seeing straight. Anchored by Freud, A. Freud, and Vaillant over the anxiety and defense members it links.
- **[freud, relation](position_relation.md)** (movement, the object world): the early attachment template repeated and the repressed transferred onto present figures -- how the past returns in present bonds. Anchored by Freud, Bowlby & Ainsworth, and Andersen & Chen over the attachment and transference members it links.
