# Process: Yielding, the Glance

## Taxonomy

[Yielding](process_yielding.md), the recognition that arrives ahead of the reasoning.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: yielding

## Initiated by

The garment is seen and read in well under a second.
The Glance starts before the request does: [what is apparent](@chbrain/khai-engine-uniform/piece_uniform.md) about a uniform is a category, and the category arrives ahead of the person wearing it.

## Direction

Toward compliance that is under way before any assessment has been made.
The measurement is old and behavioural. Confederates in the street made small arbitrary requests -- pick up that bag, move away from that bus stop, give this man a dime for the meter -- dressed either in civilian clothes or as a guard, and the guard's success rate was roughly double.
What makes the finding sharp is that it held when the requester gave the instruction and then walked off before it could be carried out. Nobody was being watched, and they did it anyway.

## Lever

The efficiency of a shortcut that is right almost every time against the fact that it responds to cloth.
The carrying force is that the shortcut is a good one and is not laziness: the base rate of uniformed instructions being legitimate is extremely high, the cost of complying is nearly always trivial, checking would take longer than obeying, and a society in which every marshal had to establish their standing before anybody moved would grind to a stop; the resistance is that the cue being used is manufacturable -- it is a set of clothes, obtainable by anybody, carrying no information about whether the person in it holds the office at all.
The Glance grants the office before establishing there is one.

## Echo

An instruction being followed by somebody who has not yet thought about it.
It is where the two atoms touch, and the touch takes about four hundred milliseconds.
The Glance is dated to the sighting and is complete before the sentence ends.
