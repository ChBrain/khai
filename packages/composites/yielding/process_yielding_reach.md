# Process: Yielding, the Reach

## Taxonomy

[Yielding](process_yielding.md), how far past the office's warrant the compliance extends.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: yielding

## Initiated by

The request is one the office has no power to make.
The Reach starts there: [the wearer](@chbrain/khai-engine-uniform/position_the_wearer.md) may order strangers in the narrow matters the office covers, and what is being asked is not one of them.

## Direction

Toward an authority that is read as general when it was only ever specific.
A security guard has authority over a car park and none at all over a passer-by's wallet. A traffic warden may issue a ticket and may not tell anybody where to stand. An inspector may enter a kitchen and may not require the person in it to answer a question about somebody else.
In every one of those cases the instruction is complied with anyway, and the margin between what the office may require and what the garment gets is not small.

## Lever

The reasonableness of not litigating each request against what the unlitigated margin covers.
The carrying force is that nobody can be expected to know the boundaries and demanding them would be obnoxious: the exact powers of a hospital porter, a school caretaker, a rail employee, and a private security contractor are genuinely obscure, most requests are small and cooperative, and a persona who answered every one with a question about jurisdiction would be difficult rather than principled; the resistance is that the boundary the persona is not litigating is precisely where the office's actual authority ends, so the compliance the garment collects is largest exactly where it is least owed.
The Reach is granted widest where the warrant is narrowest.

## Echo

An instruction obeyed by somebody who did not owe it.
It shows that what the insignia collects is not an assessment of standing but a suspension of the question.
The Reach is dated to the request and is not remembered as unusual.
