# Yielding

Yielding as composite: what a stranger gives an insignia, and how fast. It is not a third mechanism but a wiring of two -- the uniform engine supplies a category readable at a distance and a wearer holding an authority nobody granted them personally, and the obedience engine supplies compliance with the instruction of an authority. This composite adds no third engine; it reads the one thing the two make together, which is compliance collected by an insignia from people it has no claim on. Three bridges: the Glance, where an arbitrary request roughly doubles its success rate when the person making it is in uniform, and keeps working after the requester has walked away, so the office is granted before anybody establishes there is one; the Reach, where the compliance extends past what the office may lawfully require, and is granted widest exactly where the warrant is narrowest; and the Denial, the twist -- the insignia is obeyed by people who correctly believe it has no authority over them.

This engine is defined by its [manifest](package.json), which the canon renders as the WIRES card. The manifest is the single source of truth; this README is generated, not edited by hand.
