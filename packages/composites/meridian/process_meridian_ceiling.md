# Process: Meridian, the Ceiling

## Taxonomy

[Meridian](process_meridian.md), the sky the place actually stands under.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: meridian

## Initiated by

The sun comes up over this place, at this latitude, on this date.
The Ceiling starts wherever [celestial](@chbrain/khai-engine-celestial/process_celestial.md) is showing -- the [sun](@chbrain/khai-engine-celestial/process_sun.md) as governor of the day's arc and the season's swing, the [starfield](@chbrain/khai-engine-celestial/process_starfield.md) as the fixed ceiling and the oldest navigation, and the apparitions that mark a place on their own clocks: the [eclipse](@chbrain/khai-engine-celestial/process_eclipse.md), the [comet](@chbrain/khai-engine-celestial/process_comet.md), the [meteor](@chbrain/khai-engine-celestial/process_meteor.md), the [aurora](@chbrain/khai-engine-celestial/process_aurora.md).
It is the face of the composite that was here first and will not be consulted.

## Direction

Toward a reading anybody standing here could take, with nothing in their hands.
The Ceiling reads the sky as a measurement rather than as scenery: noon is when the sun is highest over this place and not another, the day's length follows from latitude and date, the solstices are the year's hinges, and the stars are a chart that needs no maintenance and belongs to nobody.
It moves on its own schedule, indifferent to what is agreed below it.

## Lever

The sky's authority against its uselessness for agreement.
The carrying force is that it cannot be wrong: there is no version of this measurement that a place could dispute, corrupt, or have taken from it, and for almost all of history it was the only clock there was -- so a place's time was a fact about the place, readable from inside it; the resistance is that this is precisely why it could not survive contact, since every place has its own noon and no two of them are the same, and the moment two places had to keep an appointment one of the two suns had to be given up.
The Ceiling spends nothing, and cannot be shared.

## Echo

A sky doing exactly what it has always done, over a place that has stopped asking.
It is the bridge that establishes the reference, so the rest of the composite has something to be offset from.
What it hands upward is a true local time that nobody uses -- and the fact that its being true locally is what made it impossible to keep.
The Ceiling is dated from the place's own latitude and longitude, and from nothing else.
