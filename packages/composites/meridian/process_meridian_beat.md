# Process: Meridian, the Beat

## Taxonomy

[Meridian](process_meridian.md), the time the place actually keeps.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: meridian

## Initiated by

The place opens at a certain hour, and everybody knows which.
The Beat starts wherever [social time](@chbrain/khai-engine-social-time/process_social-time.md) is running -- the [calendar](@chbrain/khai-engine-social-time/process_calendar.md) as the shared cycle the place repeats on, the [schedule](@chbrain/khai-engine-social-time/process_schedule.md) as the imposed timetable that sorts its life into slots, the [tempo](@chbrain/khai-engine-social-time/process_tempo.md) at which it all runs, and the [synchrony](@chbrain/khai-engine-social-time/process_synchrony.md) in which the whole place moves as one.
It is the face of the composite where none of it comes from the sky.

## Direction

Toward a rhythm the place can be held to.
The Beat reads what the cycles are made of, which is agreement: the week has no celestial referent at all, the working day is a convention with a history, the opening hour is somebody's decision, and the festival falls where the calendar says rather than where the sun does -- so every one of these is enforceable in a way a sunrise is not.
It moves the whole place together, which is the only thing it is for.

## Lever

What a shared beat makes possible against what it is made out of.
The carrying force is that the arbitrariness is the point rather than a defect: a cycle with no natural referent cannot be disputed by appeal to nature, so it binds everybody equally and can be extended to any place that agrees to it -- which is why the week travelled and the local sunrise did not, and why synchrony reads as belonging rather than as constraint; the resistance is that the same property removes the appeal, since a beat that is answerable to nothing outside itself can be lengthened, tightened, or accelerated with no measurement to object -- and a place under it has no ground to stand on except somebody else's agreement.
The Beat spends the place's independence to buy its reach.

## Echo

A place running to time, and none of the time its own.
It is the bridge that makes the composite's gap possible: two working clocks, neither wrong, measuring different things.
What it hands upward is a beat that has no obligation to the sky -- and the fact that nothing in the arrangement would notice if the two came apart.
The Beat is dated from whatever was agreed, and from whoever it was agreed with.
