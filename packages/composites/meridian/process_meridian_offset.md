# Process: Meridian, the Offset

## Taxonomy

[Meridian](process_meridian.md), the distance between the two clocks, and where the two are seen for one thing.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: meridian

## Initiated by

The clock says noon and the [sun](@chbrain/khai-engine-celestial/process_sun.md) is not overhead.
The Offset starts wherever the place's [schedule](@chbrain/khai-engine-social-time/process_schedule.md) is set by a meridian the place does not stand on, and it deepens wherever the place's own light has put out the [starfield](@chbrain/khai-engine-celestial/process_starfield.md) it once steered by -- two severances from the same sky, one of measurement and one of sight.
It is the face of the composite where nothing is broken and the reference is gone.

## Direction

Toward a place that is exactly on time and cannot say what time it is.
The Offset reads the gap and reads that it is unfelt: a place set to a zone runs an hour or a fraction of one away from its own sun, everybody in it is offset by the same amount, and an error shared by everybody present is not experienced as an error at all -- it is experienced as the time.
It moves nothing, and removes the only thing that could have measured it.

## Lever

The coordination the severance bought against the reference it was bought with.
The carrying force is that the trade was worth making and was not a mistake: places that kept their own noon could not run a railway between them, could not keep an appointment across a distance, and could not agree on when anything had happened -- so the sun was given up for the one thing it could never provide, which is agreement with elsewhere; the resistance is that the price is unrecoverable rather than merely paid, since a place that wanted to check now finds the check itself has gone -- the clock is authoritative, the sundial is a decoration, and where the sky is bright enough at night the ceiling that would have oriented anybody has been blinded out by the place's own lamps.
The Offset rides the composite's keystone knife-edge: the reference had to be surrendered to buy the coordination, so the better a place keeps time the less it can tell what time it is.

## Echo

Perfect agreement, resting on nothing outside itself.
It shows the two engines complete -- a sky that measures and a beat that binds -- and carries beneath it the composite's keystone: an offset shared by everybody is invisible to everybody, so the severance is not a loss the place feels but a loss it cannot locate, and the same works that made the coordination possible also erased the ceiling that would have shown the cost.
This is why the Offset is the hardest of the three to stage and the only one worth closing on: no character may notice it, the clocks must all agree, the place must be running well, and the audience must be given the sun once so they can hold it against the hour -- and a play that has somebody explain the time zones has replaced the mechanism with a lecture.
The Offset is the note the whole composite closes on: what a place gave up is legible only in what it can no longer check.
The Offset is dated from the day the place adopted somebody else's noon, which was not a day anybody there remembers.
