# Process: Meridian

## Taxonomy

What a place gave up when it stopped taking its time from its own sky.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: meridian

## Initiated by

A place has a sky over it and a clock in it, and they no longer say the same thing.
Meridian starts wherever both are running: the sky's bodies as the place's own Shown, through [celestial](@chbrain/khai-engine-celestial/process_celestial.md); and the collective structuring of time the place keeps, through [social time](@chbrain/khai-engine-social-time/process_social-time.md).
This composite adds no third engine. It reads the one thing the two make together, which is a gap that opened once, was never closed, and is not felt by anybody standing in it.

## Direction

Toward a place that runs perfectly and is nowhere near the sun.
The composite wires the two engines and reads their working in three shapes: [the Ceiling](process_meridian_ceiling.md), the sky the place actually stands under; [the Beat](process_meridian_beat.md), the time the place actually keeps; and [the Offset](process_meridian_offset.md), the twist -- the distance between them, and what buying it cost.
The three are not two clocks and an error. They are a reference given up, a coordination bought with it, and a place that can no longer check.

## Lever

What the sky can tell a place against what a place needs its time to do.
The carrying force is that the sky was a genuinely good clock and the only one that could not be argued with: the solar arc is set by latitude and date, the seasons swing between fixed hinges, and any place could read them without instrument, permission, or agreement -- which is exactly the property that made them useless once places had to agree with each other; the resistance is that agreement is what time is now for, so a place that kept its own noon would be right about the sun and unable to meet anybody, and the reference had to go in order for the coordination to arrive.
Meridian is read on the gap and never on either clock, because each is correct about what it measures.

## Echo

A place keeping perfect time by a line it does not stand on.
Meridian does not close; it is two accounts of the same day, gathered into one reading -- a ceiling, a beat, an offset.
It hands off what it is not: the long-run regime a place stands in and the day it is having is clime's; the sky's own bodies read for what they show is the celestial engine's; a place read by the presence in it -- the errand, the round, the loitering -- is peopling's, which wires this same social-time engine on who is about where this runs it against the sun; the wearing of a made thing and its going is object-cycle's; a persona's read of duration from inside is absorption's, and this composite is not about how long anything felt; a moment being right rather than merely arriving is kairos's; and a sky made to **stand for** a theme is authored key, the play's pitch, not the force.
The place is dated from the meridian it was assigned, and from the last time anybody checked it against noon.
