# Process: Deserving, the Lift

## Taxonomy

[Deserving](process_deserving.md), the face of the verdict the persona will not avow, and where the three are seen for one thing.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: deserving

## Initiated by

A fall lands that the persona did not cause, and something in them rises.
The Lift starts wherever [schadenfreude](@chbrain/khai-engine-schadenfreude/process_schadenfreude.md) is running -- the [comeuppance](@chbrain/khai-engine-schadenfreude/process_comeuppance.md) of the deserving, enjoyed as justice; the [leveling](@chbrain/khai-engine-schadenfreude/process_leveling.md) of the envied, enjoyed as relief; and the [grudge](@chbrain/khai-engine-schadenfreude/process_grudge.md) satisfied by the fall of the disliked, enjoyed for itself.
It is the face of the verdict that costs nothing, is asked of nobody, and is the one the persona hides.

## Direction

Toward another's descent felt as the persona's own small ascent.
The Lift reads the gladness -- the news of the rival's setback read twice, the tone of the account given to a friend, the pleasure at a public figure's fall that the persona would not put in writing -- each a misfortune that would have drawn pity for a sympathetic other and draws satisfaction for a deserving one.
It moves the persona nowhere they will report: the pleasure is theirs whether or not they own it, and they mostly do not.

## Lever

The exactness of an unpaid reading against the reasons a persona has to disown it.
The carrying force is that this is the same judgment as the other two and arrives cleaner: the pleasure is licensed by the target -- that they had it coming, that they stood above, that they were an enemy -- so it tracks deservingness directly, without an argument to dress it and without a bill to limit it, which is why it is the most exact report of the verdict a persona holds; the resistance is that it is unbecoming, and the persona knows it, so what is most diagnostic is what they are least willing to say and often unwilling to notice.
The Lift rides the composite's keystone knife-edge: the response a persona will not avow is the one that reports their verdict most exactly -- the gladness is not a lapse in the moral sense but a reading of it.

## Echo

The verdict felt, and disowned in the same motion.
It shows the three faces complete -- one stated with reasons fitted afterward, one paid for out of pocket, one enjoyed for free and denied -- and carries beneath it the composite's keystone: the same judgment of desert that licenses the sanction licenses the pleasure, so a persona who denies the pleasure is denying their own verdict rather than resisting a temptation.
This is why it is worth reading and why it cannot be asked for: the persona who is glad and says so is rare, and the persona who is glad is not.
The Lift is the note the whole composite closes on: what someone thinks another has coming is legible in what they enjoy, and nowhere else without a discount.
The Lift is dated from the fall, and from nothing the persona will admit to having wanted.
