# Process: Deserving, the Price

## Taxonomy

[Deserving](process_deserving.md), the face of the verdict the persona will pay for.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: deserving

## Initiated by

A norm has been broken and a persona who will not recover their costs moves to make the breaker answer.
The Price starts wherever [altruistic punishment](@chbrain/khai-engine-altruistic-punishment/process_altruistic_punishment.md) fires -- the [second-party](@chbrain/khai-engine-altruistic-punishment/process_second_party.md) case, where the persona was the one taken from and spends beyond recovering it, and the [third-party](@chbrain/khai-engine-altruistic-punishment/process_third_party.md) case, where they were not involved at all and spend anyway.
It is the face of the verdict that costs something, and the cost is what makes it evidence.

## Direction

Toward a sanction the violator cannot ignore, at a price the persona absorbs.
The Price reads the spending -- the customer who will not return and tells others, the colleague who raises the thing in the meeting where it will cost them, the stranger who intervenes, the review written by someone who is never coming back -- each a persona docking a defector with nothing material coming back and retaliation possible.
It moves the persona from disapproving to enforcing: not the wish that the violator suffer but the readiness to pay to make it so.

## Lever

The norm a cost-bearing enforcer keeps alive against the fact that nobody is obliged to be them.
The carrying force is that a cooperative norm with no enforcer decays -- the free-rider does better than the contributor until someone will pay to change that arithmetic, so the persona who spends is holding up an arrangement they do not personally profit from; the resistance is that the spending is voluntary and finite: reframe the breach as a private matter rather than a violation and it does not fire, or let the cost run past what the persona will spend on a stranger's compliance and they leave it to someone else.
The Price is deserving at its most demonstrated: talk is free and this is not, so what a persona will pay marks the edge of what they actually hold.

## Echo

The verdict enacted, and its holder out of pocket.
The Price leaves the norm answered by somebody who did not have to answer it -- and hands the judgment on to the face that costs nothing at all, where the same reading arrives without the bill and without the avowal.
That is the contrast the last bridge turns on: this face is expensive and admitted, and the next is free and denied.
The Price is dated from the spending, the moment a persona chose to be the one who answers.
