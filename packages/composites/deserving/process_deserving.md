# Process: Deserving

## Taxonomy

What a persona holds another to have coming.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: deserving

## Initiated by

Someone else has done something, and a persona who was not asked has already decided what it is worth.
Deserving starts wherever that decision is showing -- the foundation that fires and names the conduct before the reasons are gathered, through [moral judgment](@chbrain/khai-engine-moral-judgment/position_moral_judgment.md); the price the persona will absorb to make a breaker answer, through [altruistic punishment](@chbrain/khai-engine-altruistic-punishment/process_altruistic_punishment.md); and the small lift when the fall arrives, through [schadenfreude](@chbrain/khai-engine-schadenfreude/process_schadenfreude.md).
This composite adds no fourth engine; it reads what the three make together: one judgment of desert, surfacing as a reading, a payment, and a pleasure.

## Direction

Toward the whole of what a persona holds another to have coming, ordered by what they will avow.
The composite wires the three engines and reads their working in three shapes: [the Lens](process_deserving_lens.md), the verdict stated; [the Price](process_deserving_price.md), the verdict enacted at a cost; and [the Lift](process_deserving_lift.md), the twist -- the verdict felt and disowned.
The three are not three attitudes. They are one judgment about what someone has coming, met by three different levels of willingness to be seen holding it.

## Lever

The order a shared sense of desert keeps against the fact that it is not a reasoned thing.
The carrying force is that a world of cooperators needs someone to answer a defection who was not the one defected against, and needs it enough that people will pay out of pocket to do it -- a persona with no sense of what others have coming would be no use to anyone and easy to take from; the resistance is that the sense arrives ahead of its argument and is fitted with reasons afterwards, so the persona experiences a conclusion as a perception, and the three faces of it can diverge without the persona noticing that they have.
Deserving is read wherever a persona is granted their verdict on somebody else: not a self reasoning about conduct but one already holding a figure for what it is worth.

## Echo

The persona revealed by what they think someone else has coming.
Deserving does not close; it is the judgment running, gathering the three engines into one reading -- a lens, a price, a lift.
It hands off what it is not: the other-condemning emotions themselves, the contempt, anger, and disgust that answer a violated code, are the condemnation composite's, which owns what a persona feels toward the wrongdoer where this owns what they hold the wrongdoer to have coming; the wrong between two people and its reckoning -- the felt debt, the spoken account, the grievance, the repair -- is the moral-account composite's, dyadic and aimed at restoration where this is third-party and aimed at desert; the moral self that governs the persona's own conduct is the conscience composite's, and the repair of their own account is the squaring composite's; the upward ache at another's good is the envy engine's, inside the outdone composite, of which this pleasure is the mirror; the standing conviction that the world is fair and people get what they deserve is the belief-in-a-just-world engine's, a worldview where this is the operation in a particular case; the anger of a group at a gap it did not earn is the relative-deprivation composite's; the active wish to do the harm is the aggression engine's; and the fall made to stand for a story is authored key, the play's pitch, not the force.
The persona is dated from the verdict, and read as a self whose fairest-sounding judgement and least admissible pleasure are the same judgement twice.
