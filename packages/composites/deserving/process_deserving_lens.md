# Process: Deserving, the Lens

## Taxonomy

[Deserving](process_deserving.md), the face of the verdict the persona will state.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: deserving

## Initiated by

Another persona acts, and the conduct arrives already weighed.
The Lens starts wherever [moral judgment](@chbrain/khai-engine-moral-judgment/position_moral_judgment.md) is running -- a standing set of foundations of which one lights first, whether [care](@chbrain/khai-engine-moral-judgment/position_care.md) and the harm done, [fairness](@chbrain/khai-engine-moral-judgment/position_fairness.md) and the exchange broken, [loyalty](@chbrain/khai-engine-moral-judgment/position_loyalty.md) and the side betrayed, [authority](@chbrain/khai-engine-moral-judgment/position_authority.md) and the order flouted, [sanctity](@chbrain/khai-engine-moral-judgment/position_sanctity.md) and the thing degraded, or [liberty](@chbrain/khai-engine-moral-judgment/position_liberty.md) and the freedom pressed on.
It is the face of the judgment the persona is willing to say out loud, and the one they will give reasons for.

## Direction

Toward a verdict laid down before the argument that will be offered for it.
The Lens reads the firing -- the act that is obviously wrong before anyone has said why, the two personas watching the same conduct and disagreeing about what happened rather than about what follows, the argument produced fluently on request and revised without the verdict moving -- each a foundation that fired and a case assembled afterward.
It moves the persona from seeing to having seen: the conduct weighed on arrival, with no plain look left from which to start.

## Lever

The speed that makes a moral world workable against the reasons that arrive too late to have decided anything.
The carrying force is that a persona who had to derive a verdict each time would not have one when it was needed, and a shared set of foundations is what lets a group agree about conduct fast enough to act on the agreement; the resistance is that what fired first is not what gets cited: the persona feels the verdict land and then reaches for the argument, so the account they give is a reconstruction, sincerely offered and not the cause.
The Lens is deserving at its most sayable and least examined.

## Echo

The verdict stated, with reasons attached after the fact.
The Lens leaves the persona holding a figure for what another's conduct is worth -- and hands it on to the faces where the figure gets spent and enjoyed.
Which foundation fired is most of what a disagreement about conduct is really about, and it is the part neither party thinks to name.
The Lens is dated from the firing, which was before the persona began to think.
