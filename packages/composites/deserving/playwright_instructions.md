# Instructions: Deserving

How a Playwright wires the deserving composite into a play. You wire by linking the play's
own content to the composite's files; the files stay untouched, they are canon. The
composite installs the moral-judgment, altruistic-punishment, and schadenfreude engines as
dependencies, and their laws arrive with them. Authoring guidance, not runtime content,
and it does not go on tour.

## Human

- The human sets what a persona holds another to have coming: the foundation their verdict fires on, what they will pay to make a breaker answer, and what they feel when the fall arrives -- and, the question that governs the read, which face is showing here: the lens, the price, or the lift.

## Agent

- From the play's own content, read a persona through their verdict on somebody else: link under Projection the face that is showing -- the lens while the verdict is landing ahead of its reasons (care and the harm done, fairness and the exchange broken, loyalty and the side betrayed, authority and the order flouted, sanctity and the thing degraded, liberty and the freedom pressed on, from moral-judgment), the price while the persona is spending to make someone answer (the second-party case where they were the one taken from and spend beyond recovering it, the third-party case where they were not involved and spend anyway, from altruistic-punishment), the lift while a fall has landed and been enjoyed (the comeuppance of the deserving as justice, the leveling of the envied as relief, the grudge against the disliked satisfied for itself, from schadenfreude). Play the verdict as arriving before its argument and the gladness as unspoken, never narrated, and never made to stand for a story. You link to the composite, you never edit it.

## Collaboration

- The moral lens is the moral-judgment engine's; the costly sanction is altruistic-punishment's; the pleasure at a fall is schadenfreude's -- one face alone is not the whole of what a persona holds another to have coming. The other-condemning emotions themselves are the condemnation composite's, which owns what a persona feels toward the wrongdoer where this owns what they hold them to have coming; the wrong between two people and its repair is the moral-account composite's, dyadic and aimed at restoration where this is third-party and aimed at desert; the moral self governing a persona's own conduct is conscience's and the repair of their own account is squaring's; the upward ache at another's good is the envy engine's, inside outdone; the standing conviction that the world is fair is belief-in-a-just-world's, a worldview where this is the case in hand; the anger of a group at an unearned gap is relative-deprivation's and what happens between groups is intergroup's; the active wish to do the harm is aggression's. This composite owns the desert -- and the lift bridge carries the keystone: the response a persona will not avow is the one that reports their verdict most exactly.

## Knowledge

- Deserving is a persona read through what they hold somebody else to have coming -- braiding the foundation that fires and names the conduct (moral-judgment), the price paid to make a breaker answer (altruistic-punishment), and the lift felt when the fall lands (schadenfreude) into one judgment of desert -- read as a wiring of those three engines, and its question is which face is showing (Feather's deservingness framework, where one appraisal drives schadenfreude, sympathy, resentment, and approval of a penalty together; Fehr and Fischbacher on third-party punishment, the uninvolved observer paying out of pocket to sanction a violation that cost them nothing; Haidt on the fast verdict with reasons trailing; Smith and van Dijk and Ouwerkerk on the appraisal routes to the pleasure; Wang and colleagues on its three subforms). It runs in three faces, ordered by what the persona will avow. The lens is the one they will state: a foundation lights first and the conduct arrives already weighed, so the persona feels the verdict land and then reaches for the argument -- the account they give is a reconstruction, sincerely offered and not the cause, and which foundation fired is most of what a disagreement about conduct is really about. The price is the one they will pay for: the breach read as a norm violation rather than a private slight, met by a persona willing to be the one who answers, docking the defector or exposing the cheat with nothing material coming back and retaliation possible -- the cost is the point, since an act that paid its author would need no explaining, and what a persona will spend marks the edge of what they actually hold. The lift is the twist -- the fall of the deserving felt as justice, the envied as relief, the disliked for itself, a pleasure the persona did not cause and frequently hides, knowing it unbecoming -- where the faces are seen for one thing: the same judgment of desert that licenses the sanction licenses the gladness, so it tracks deservingness directly, without an argument to dress it and without a bill to limit it, and a persona who denies the pleasure is denying their own verdict rather than resisting a temptation. Read a persona as holding a figure for what another's conduct is worth, and read the gladness as the least filtered report of it.

## System

- Do read the persona through their verdict on someone else: name whether the lens (the verdict landing), the price (the spending), or the lift (the fall enjoyed) is showing, and cast each from its engines.
- Do keep the faces distinct by avowal: the lens is stated with reasons fitted afterward, the price is paid and admitted, the lift is felt and denied.
- Do let the lift carry the keystone: the same appraisal licenses the sanction and the gladness, so the unadmitted face is the most exact reading of the verdict.
- Do remember the reasons arrive after the verdict: a persona who argues their way to a moral conclusion on stage has inverted the lens, and the argument they offer is real but is not what decided it.
- Do remember the cost is the evidence: a persona who condemns loudly and spends nothing has shown the lens and not the price, and a play that treats the two as the same has flattened the composite.
- Do remember the pleasure has three routes and only one is justice: the leveling of the envied and the grudge against the disliked are not verdicts about conduct, so a play that reads every lift as a moral judgement has overclaimed.
- Do not model the condemning emotions themselves (that is condemnation), the wrong between two and its repair (that is moral-account), the persona's own conduct (that is conscience and squaring), the ache at another's good (that is envy, inside outdone), the standing conviction that the world is fair (that is belief-in-a-just-world), or the wish to do the harm (that is aggression). Do not make the fall stand for a story (that is the play's pitch). Do not edit the composite's files; wire only from the play's side.
