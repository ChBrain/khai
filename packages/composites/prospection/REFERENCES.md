# Prospection: Reference

## Line of Work

Prospection as **composite**: a persona pulled by the unassured future -- the resting slant
it brings to any horizon and the anticipatory apprehension a looming outcome stirs. It is
not a new emotion but a wiring of two: the **hope** engine supplies the standing
orientation -- the slant across expected valence and felt agency a persona carries toward
what the future holds -- and the **anxiety** engine supplies the active anticipatory
process -- the worry, vigilance, and looming a specific uncertain outcome stirs. This
composite adds no third engine; it reads what the two make together: the prospective reach,
hope and dread as one openness to a future that is not guaranteed, and stages that into
three shapes -- the slant a persona brings, the looming a specific outcome stirs, and the
reach that shows the two as one faculty.

The domain does not model either force alone -- the resting orientation is the hope
engine's and the anticipatory process is anxiety's, and one alone is a position or a
process, not the whole facing. Nor is it the present-cued alarm at a danger already here,
which is the fear engine's; nor the threat-response arc of a danger not yet fallen -- the
baseline apprehension, the anticipatory suspense, and the stress of a demand that exceeds
resources -- which is the jeopardy composite's. It owns the bivalent future-reach: the
persona granted its pull toward an outcome it cannot secure, read not as a hope to be kept
apart from a dread but as one exposure to the unknown future, hoping and dreading with a
single motion.

The spine is the reading of the human as a prospective animal, pulled by the future rather
than only pushed by the past. Gilbert and Wilson set prospection at the centre -- the mind
that continually simulates what has not yet happened -- and Seligman and colleagues named
the species for it, Homo Prospectus, the animal drawn forward by the futures it imagines.
Snyder read the hopeful pole as agency and pathways, a slant with a felt power to reach the
wanted end; Barlow read the anxious pole as anxious apprehension, a future-oriented mood
set to a danger that has not arrived. The composite's own move is to hold the two as one:
that the reach which hopes for an unassured good is the same reach that dreads an unruled-out
harm. The three bridges are those readings staged: slant, looming, and reach.

## Restrictions

What the composite refuses to model, and to whom it delegates.

- **The single engines (the atoms)**: The composite wires the two but owns neither. The
  resting orientation toward the future -- hopeful, optimistic, dreading, defiant, across
  expected valence and felt agency -- is the hope engine's; the anticipatory apprehension a
  looming outcome stirs -- worry, vigilance, looming, tuned by an intolerance of uncertainty
  -- is anxiety's. Prospection reads only what the two make as one reach; a single engine
  alone is a position or a process, not the whole facing of the future.
- **The present danger (fear)**: The alarm at a threat already here and cued -- phasic,
  present, object-bound -- is the fear engine's. Prospection is the reach toward a future not
  yet arrived and not assured, the apprehension of what might come, not the response to what
  has.
- **The threat-response arc (jeopardy)**: The persona under a danger not yet fallen, met with
  the baseline apprehension it brings, the anticipatory suspense, and the stress of a demand
  that exceeds resources, is the jeopardy composite's. Prospection is the bivalent reach --
  hope and dread as one faculty -- not the mono-valent threat and the response it forces.
- **The future as authored symbol (the play's pitch)**: A persona's facing of the future made
  to **stand for** a story -- the hope that mirrors a theme of redemption, the dread that
  mirrors a coming fall -- is refused: that is authored key, the play's pitch, not the force
  on the persona. Prospection delivers the persona's own reach; the meaning read into it is
  the play's.

## Encoding

Source to constraint. A composite root over two engine atoms it declares as dependencies,
with three bridges, the shapes of a persona facing the unassured future.

- **[prospection](process_prospection.md)** (the root): a persona pulled by the future it
  cannot yet know -- the slant it brings and the apprehension a looming outcome stirs, read
  as one reach. Anchored by Gilbert & Wilson and Seligman (the prospective mind).
- **[slant](process_prospection_slant.md)** (bridge): the resting orientation a persona brings
  to any horizon -- hopeful, optimistic, dreading, or defiant, across expected valence and
  felt agency. Anchored by Snyder (hope theory).
- **[looming](process_prospection_looming.md)** (bridge): the active anticipatory process a
  specific uncertain outcome stirs -- the worry, the vigilance, the looming, tuned by an
  intolerance of uncertainty. Anchored by Barlow (anxious apprehension).
- **[reach](process_prospection_reach.md)** (bridge): hope and dread as one faculty -- to reach
  for an unassured good is to be exposed to its not-coming. Anchored by Seligman; the
  composite's twist and keystone, that the openness which lets a persona hope is the one that
  lets it dread.
