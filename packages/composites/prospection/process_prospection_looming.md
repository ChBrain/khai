# Process: Prospection, Looming

## Taxonomy

[Prospection](process_prospection.md), the shape where a specific uncertain outcome stirs the persona.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: prospection

## Initiated by

A particular outcome comes into view, uncertain and not yet arrived, and the persona's apprehension stirs toward it.
Looming starts wherever a specific uncertain future presses on a persona -- the [anxiety](@chbrain/khai-engine-anxiety/process_anxiety.md) of a danger that has not landed and may not, the [worry](@chbrain/khai-engine-anxiety/process_worry.md) that turns it over, the [vigilance](@chbrain/khai-engine-anxiety/process_vigilance.md) that scans for it, and the [looming](@chbrain/khai-engine-anxiety/process_looming.md) sense of it growing near, tuned by a standing [intolerance of uncertainty](@chbrain/khai-engine-anxiety/position_uncertainty.md).
It is the disposition made active: no longer the slant a persona brings but the state a specific unresolved outcome stirs, the apprehension of what might still happen.

## Direction

Toward the outcome held in view, worried, watched, and felt growing near.
Looming reads the persona stirred by a particular uncertain future -- the mind returning to it, the guard raised for it, the sense of it drawing closer and larger the nearer it comes -- sustained by the very uncertainty that keeps it from being settled either way.
It moves the persona from a resting slant to an active apprehension: a specific outcome now holds it, and will not release it until the uncertainty resolves.

## Lever

The unresolved outcome against the persona's need to have it settled.
The carrying force is an outcome that matters and will not resolve -- uncertain short of certainty, near enough to grip and far enough to doubt; the resistance is the persona's intolerance of that open state, the pull to know now, to end the not-knowing even for the worse, because the suspension itself is the burden.
Looming is prospection at its most active: the future not coloured from a distance but pressing close, the apprehension a specific unresolved outcome sustains until it lands.

## Echo

The outcome held in view, worried and watched until it resolves.
Looming leaves the persona gripped by a particular uncertain future -- worrying it, scanning for it, feeling it near -- and hands that active apprehension toward the reach that will show it as the same faculty by which the persona hopes.
It is prospection stirred: not the standing tilt but the live state a specific outcome draws, the mind held on what might still happen.
Looming is dated from the outcome coming into view, the moment a specific uncertain future began to press on the persona.
