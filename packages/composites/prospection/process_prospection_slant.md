# Process: Prospection, Slant

## Taxonomy

[Prospection](process_prospection.md), the shape where a persona brings a resting orientation to any horizon.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: prospection

## Initiated by

The persona meets the future already tilted -- a standing slant it brings before any particular outcome is in view.
Slant starts wherever a persona carries its resting orientation toward what the future holds -- [hopeful](@chbrain/khai-engine-hope/position_hopeful.md) or [dreading](@chbrain/khai-engine-hope/position_dread.md), [optimistic](@chbrain/khai-engine-hope/position_optimistic.md) that things tend to come right or [defiant](@chbrain/khai-engine-hope/position_defiant.md) that they can be made to -- the slant of [hope](@chbrain/khai-engine-hope/position_hope.md) across the two axes of expected valence and felt agency.
It is the disposition before the event: not a response to a specific outcome but the tilt a persona brings to every horizon, colouring what has not yet come.

## Direction

Toward the future coloured by the slant a persona already holds.
Slant reads the persona's resting orientation -- whether it expects good or ill of what comes, and whether it feels able to bend the outcome or only to await it -- the standing lean that greets each horizon before the particulars arrive.
It sets the ground the looming will land on: the same uncertain outcome falls differently on a persona tilted hopeful and one tilted toward dread, met with agency or with helplessness.

## Lever

The standing tilt a persona brings against the futures that will test it.
The carrying force is that no one meets the future neutral -- a persona arrives with a slant, expecting good or ill and feeling able or unable to move it; the resistance is the futures that do not fit the slant, the hopeful horizon that darkens and the dreaded one that comes right, pressing on the orientation without at once changing it.
Slant is prospection at its most dispositional: the colour a persona lays over the future before it knows what the future holds.

## Echo

The horizon met with a slant already in place.
Slant leaves the persona tilted toward what comes -- hopeful or dreading, agent or awaiter -- and hands that standing orientation to the looming that will stir it and the reach that will expose it.
It is prospection at rest: not yet the apprehension of a particular outcome, but the lean a persona always brings, the ground its every facing of the future begins from.
Slant is dated from the orientation, the tilt a persona holds toward any horizon before an outcome is in view.
