# Process: Prospection, Reach

## Taxonomy

[Prospection](process_prospection.md), the shape where hope and dread prove one reach toward the unassured.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: prospection

## Initiated by

The persona reaches toward a future it cannot secure, and the same reaching that hopes is the reaching that dreads.
Reach starts wherever a persona's pull toward what may come is seen whole -- the [hope](@chbrain/khai-engine-hope/position_hope.md) for a good that is not guaranteed and the [anxiety](@chbrain/khai-engine-anxiety/process_anxiety.md) of a harm that is not ruled out, both drawing on the one [uncertainty](@chbrain/khai-engine-anxiety/position_uncertainty.md) that keeps the outcome open.
It is prospection's twist: not two feelings but one faculty, the reach toward an unassured future that cannot hope without exposing itself to dread.

## Direction

Toward the future reached for, and the exposure the reaching cannot avoid.
Reach reads the persona leaning into what it cannot hold -- and finding that the lean is single: to want a good that might not come is already to stand where its not-coming can be felt, so the hope and the dread are not opposed but the two faces of one caring about an outcome that is not settled.
It moves the persona to the knife-edge the whole composite turns on: the more it reaches for the wanted future, the more exposed it is to the feared one, because both live in the same uncertainty.

## Lever

The reach toward a wanted future against the exposure that reaching for the unassured requires.
The carrying force is that a persona can only hope for what is not guaranteed -- a certain good is not hoped for but simply had, and hope lives only where the outcome is open; the resistance is that the same openness is where the dread lives, so there is no hoping without the exposure the anxiety feels, and the persona that could close the future to certainty to escape the dread would lose the hope in the same act.
Reach rides the composite's keystone knife-edge: the exposure that makes a persona anxious is the openness that lets it hope, the loss and the value one faculty -- to reach toward an unassured future at all is to hope and to dread with a single motion.

## Echo

The persona reaching, hoping and dreading with the one motion.
It shows a persona leaning into what it cannot secure -- wanting the good, exposed to the ill -- and carries beneath it the composite's keystone: that hope and anxiety are not two emotions to be sorted but one reach toward a future that is not assured, and the caring that opens a persona to the one opens it to the other.
Reach is the note the whole composite closes on: prospection is not a hope to be protected from a dread but a single exposure to the unknown future, the price and the gift of caring about what has not yet come.
Reach is dated from the reaching, the moment the persona leaned toward a future it could neither secure nor stop wanting.
