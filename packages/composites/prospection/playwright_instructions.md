# Instructions: Prospection

How a Playwright wires the prospection composite into a play. You wire by linking the
play's own content to the composite's files; the files stay untouched, they are canon.
The composite installs the hope and anxiety engines as dependencies, and their laws
arrive with them. Authoring guidance, not runtime content, and it does not go on tour.

## Human

- The human sets the persona's facing of the future: which persona is read as pulled by an unassured future -- a self that brings a slant to the horizon and is stirred by what looms in it -- and, the question that governs the read, which shape that facing is in: the slant, the looming, or the reach.

## Agent

- From the play's own content, read a persona as pulled by the future it cannot yet know: link under Projection the shape its facing is in -- slant while it brings a resting orientation to the horizon (hopeful, optimistic, dreading, or defiant, across expected valence and felt agency, from hope), looming while a specific uncertain outcome stirs it (the worry, the vigilance, the looming, tuned by an intolerance of uncertainty, from anxiety), reach while the two show as one faculty (hope and dread the one openness to an unassured future). Play the persona as reaching toward what it cannot secure, present in what it projects, never narrated, and never made to stand for a story. You link to the composite, you never edit it.

## Collaboration

- The resting orientation toward the future is the hope engine's; the anticipatory apprehension a looming outcome stirs is anxiety's -- one engine alone is a position or a process, not the whole facing. The present-cued alarm at a danger already here is fear's; the threat-response arc of a danger not yet fallen -- apprehension, suspense, and stress -- is the jeopardy composite's. This composite owns the bivalent future-reach -- hope and dread as one faculty -- and the reach bridge carries the keystone: the openness that lets a persona hope is the one that lets it dread.

## Knowledge

- Prospection is a persona read as pulled by the unassured future -- braiding the resting slant it brings to any horizon (hope, across expected valence and felt agency) with the anticipatory apprehension a specific outcome stirs (anxiety, the worry, vigilance, and looming) into one prospective reach -- read as a wiring of those two engines, and its question is which shape the facing is in (Gilbert & Wilson's prospective mind; Seligman's Homo Prospectus). It runs in three shapes. Slant is the resting orientation a persona brings before any outcome -- hopeful or dreading, optimistic that things come right or defiant that they can be made to, the tilt across expected valence and felt agency. Looming is the active anticipatory process a specific uncertain outcome stirs -- the mind returning to it, the guard raised for it, the sense of it drawing near, sustained by the uncertainty that keeps it unresolved. Reach is the twist -- hope and dread as one faculty, where to reach for an unassured good is to be exposed to its not-coming, and the persona that could not dread could not hope. Read a persona as leaning into a future it can neither secure nor stop wanting, hoping and dreading with the one motion.

## System

- Do read the persona as pulled by the future, never as fixed in the present: name whether its facing is in slant (the resting orientation), looming (the specific outcome pressing), or reach (the two as one faculty), and cast each from its engine.
- Do keep the slant and the looming distinct: the slant is the standing tilt a persona brings to any horizon, the looming is the live apprehension a particular unresolved outcome stirs -- disposition and state of the one prospection.
- Do let reach carry the keystone: the exposure that makes a persona anxious is the openness that lets it hope, the loss and the value one faculty -- to reach toward an unassured future is to hope and to dread with a single motion.
- Do not model the resting orientation alone (that is hope) or the anticipatory process alone (that is anxiety), the present-cued alarm at a danger already here (that is fear), or the threat-response arc of a danger not yet fallen (that is the jeopardy composite). Do not make the persona's facing of the future stand for a story (that is the play's pitch). Do not edit the composite's files; wire only from the play's side.
