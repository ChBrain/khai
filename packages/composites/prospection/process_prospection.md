# Process: Prospection

## Taxonomy

A persona facing the future it cannot yet know.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: prospection

## Initiated by

A persona is read not as living only in the present but as pulled by a future that has not yet arrived and is not assured.
Prospection starts wherever a persona faces what is coming, bringing the resting slant it holds toward any horizon ([hope](@chbrain/khai-engine-hope/position_hope.md), across expected valence and felt agency) and meeting the anticipatory apprehension a specific outcome stirs ([anxiety](@chbrain/khai-engine-anxiety/process_anxiety.md), the worry, vigilance, and looming of an uncertain threat).
This composite adds no new emotion; it reads what the two make together: the prospective reach, hope and dread as one openness to a future that is not guaranteed.

## Direction

Toward the whole facing of the unassured future, read through its shapes.
The composite wires the two engines and reads their working in three shapes: [Slant](process_prospection_slant.md), the orientation a persona brings to any horizon; [Looming](process_prospection_looming.md), the apprehension a specific outcome stirs; and [Reach](process_prospection_reach.md), the twist -- the prospective pull itself, hope and dread as one faculty.
A persona does not meet the future blank: it brings a slant to it, it is stirred by what looms in it, and it reaches into it -- and the reaching that hopes for a good it cannot secure is the same reaching that dreads a harm it cannot rule out.

## Lever

The persona's pull toward an unassured future against the wish to be indifferent to what it cannot control.
The carrying force is that a persona cares about a future it does not hold -- it slants toward it, is stirred by it, and reaches into it, unable to stop mattering to what has not yet come; the resistance is the wish to be free of it, to not hope so as not to dread, to close the horizon to certainty and feel nothing about what waits.
Prospection is read wherever a persona is granted its reach toward the future: not a self fixed in the present but one leaning into what may come, exposed by exactly the caring that lets it hope.

## Echo

The persona revealed as a self that leans into a future it cannot secure.
Prospection does not close; it is the persona going on facing what has not yet come, gathering the two engines into one reading -- a self that slants, is stirred, and reaches toward the unassured.
It hands off what it is not: the present-cued alarm at a danger already here is the fear engine's, not this; the threat-response arc of a danger not yet fallen -- the baseline apprehension, the suspense, the stress -- is the jeopardy composite's; and the future made to stand for a story is authored key, the play's pitch, not the force.
The persona is dated from its own reaching, and read as a self that hopes and dreads with the one faculty, pulled by a future it cannot know.
