# Piece: Artifice, Discord

## Taxonomy

[Artifice](piece_artifice.md), the bridge where the five forces war with each other. Discord is the object whose forces do not align at all -- usable but unfit, honest but hooking, the [ergonomics](@chbrain/khai-engine-ergonomics/piece_ergonomics.md) fighting the [usability](@chbrain/khai-engine-usability/piece_usability.md), the [captology](@chbrain/khai-engine-captology/piece_captology.md) undercutting the [agency](@chbrain/khai-engine-agency/piece_agency.md) it should have served, the [guile](@chbrain/khai-engine-guile/piece_guile.md) tripping the very use it was meant to bend -- so no single reading wins and the object misfits itself. Alexander's misfit: the form that fails to resolve its forces, where the mismatch between the made thing and what it must do shows through as friction, and the object serves no end cleanly, not even the maker's.

## Place

At the object at odds with itself -- the powerful tool no one can hold, the honest interface that also nags until the honesty is drowned, the beautiful machine that hurts to use, wherever two forces were each pursued and never reconciled.

## Load Bearing

What discord holds up is failure that belongs to no single force. Each atom, audited alone, may pass -- the usability is fine, the ergonomics is fine -- and yet the object is bad, because the forces were not composed and they cancel. Remove the conflict -- reconcile the forces, or subordinate them to one end -- and the object resolves toward concord or extraction; the discord rests on the forces having been struck without a settlement, each optimized against the others. It is the most common bridge and the least dramatic to any one specialist, because the fault is not in any part but in the whole that was never made whole -- the reason a thing built by many hands, each doing its part well, can still be unusable.

## Apparent

What shows is friction with no obvious villain -- an object that is somehow wrong while every component is somehow right. What is built, or rather what was not built, is the reconciliation: the settlement was skipped, and the forces left to fight show up to the user as an object that resists them from several directions at once. Discord is the bridge that hides in plain sight, because it looks like nothing more than a thing that is a little bad in every direction, and the composite is what names the badness as the missing settlement rather than a set of separate flaws.

## Yearbook

The history of a discord is the record of an object that fought itself into disuse -- the tool abandoned though nothing in it was quite broken, the redesign that fixed each part and not the whole, the friction endured until the user found something that cohered. It is dated from the misfit it never resolved: not the drama of an extraction unmasked, but the quiet attrition of a made thing that asked its user to hold its contradictions and was, in the end, set down.
