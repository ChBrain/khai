# Piece: Artifice, Extraction

## Taxonomy

[Artifice](piece_artifice.md), the bridge where the five forces align for the maker against the user. Extraction is the object whose forces pull the same way and that way is the maker's take: the hook that will not let go ([captology](@chbrain/khai-engine-captology/piece_captology.md)), the trick that misleads ([guile](@chbrain/khai-engine-guile/piece_guile.md)), the enlistment the user cannot refuse ([agency](@chbrain/khai-engine-agency/piece_agency.md)), all worn under the surface of serving them ([usability](@chbrain/khai-engine-usability/piece_usability.md) as costume). It is concord's mirror -- the forces just as aligned, but aimed at the user rather than for them. The attention-economy object, the machine built to extract: designed so well that the taking feels like a gift until the bill comes.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: artifice

## Place

At the object built to take -- the feed that harvests the hours, the checkout that hides the cost, the free service whose product is the user, every made thing whose polish is the instrument of the extraction and not a kindness.

## Load Bearing

What extraction holds up is a taking that outlives the user's noticing. The forces are turned together on the user -- hooked to keep them, tricked to move them, conscripted so they cannot leave -- and the usability is real, which is the point: the object must serve enough to keep the user inside the machine that takes from them. Remove the alignment-against and the object falls back toward concord or discord; the extraction rests on all the forces genuinely pointed at the user's expense. Its deepest form is the one the user defends, mistaking the mechanism for a benefit, because the surface never stopped saying service.

## Apparent

What shows is a generous, polished, frictionless thing -- often more polished than the concordant object, because the polish is load-bearing for the take. What is built is the settlement turned against the user: every force that looks like care is care aimed back at the maker's ledger. Extraction is the most seductive of the three bridges, because it must be: it cannot afford to look like what it is, and its whole craft is the gap between the service apparent and the taking borne.

## Yearbook

The history of an extraction is the record of a taking and its reckoning -- the hours it harvested, the money it drew, the trust it spent, and the day the mechanism was seen for what it was. It is dated from the unmasking that concord never meets: the users who woke to the machine, the regulation that named the pattern, the reputation that curdled when the gift was revealed as a bill. The object is judged from what it took and from how long the surface held before the take showed through.
