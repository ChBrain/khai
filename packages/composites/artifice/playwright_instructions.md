# Instructions: Artifice

How a Playwright wires the artifice composite into a play. You wire by linking the play's own content to the composite's files; the files stay untouched, they are canon. The composite installs the six design engines as dependencies -- usability, agency, ergonomics, guile, captology, choice-architecture -- and their laws arrive with them. Authoring guidance, not runtime content, and it does not go on tour.

## Human

- The human sets the object under design: which made thing in the world is being read for its forces, whom it is built to serve, and what stakes ride on the settlement it strikes -- so a piece can be read for whether its forces hold for the user, turn to take from them, or fall apart against themselves.

## Agent

- From the play's own content, read the object whole: link the bridge a piece strikes under its Load Bearing -- concord while the forces align for the user, extraction while they align for the maker against the user, discord while they war with each other -- and let each force come from its engine, all cast on the same object. Read the object by the settlement, never by the word artifice alone: the made thing that hooks and fits and deceives at once is one object with one settlement, not three separate findings. Read the arrangement before asking whether anyone chose it, since it is set either way, and let its absence of an author be evidence rather than an omission. You link to the composite, you never edit it.

## Collaboration

- What a given object's usability, enlistment, fit, deception, hook, or arrangement is made of belongs to the six engines this composite wires -- one force alone is not a settlement. The making process by which a designer reaches the object is a method's; the object's aesthetic surface apart from its forces is its own domain; the general fact that pieces compose is the architecture's; and the size of any one force's pull is the atom's to state, choice-architecture in particular carrying effect-size caveats the composite inherits rather than flattens. This composite owns the six design forces held in one object, and the settlement they reach.

## Knowledge

- Artifice is the designed object read whole: the settlement a made thing strikes among the six design forces -- usability's honest confirmation, agency's enlistment, ergonomics' bodily fit, guile's deception, captology's hook, choice-architecture's arrangement of the options -- and whom that settlement serves (Simon's artifact bent to a goal; Alexander's form as the resolution of forces; Thaler and Sunstein's choice architect, set out by their own analogy to the architect of a building, where no design is neutral). Five of the six can be absent from an object and the sixth cannot, which changes what a settlement is: a maker who resolved nothing has still resolved the arrangement, by default and in nobody's name. It runs in three shapes. Concord is the forces aligned for the user -- the object that fits, tells the truth, enlists openly, and puts its preset where an informed user would have put it, its friction only where a pause is warranted, its ordering and its assortment honest; Alexander's good fit, and it works better the more closely it is read, and it is harder than restraint, since five forces are satisfied by not doing something and the arrangement is not. Extraction is the forces aligned for the maker against the user -- the hook and the trick and the coerced enlistment worn under the surface of service, the object built to take, often more polished than the concordant one because the polish is the instrument; the arrangement is its most deniable instrument and therefore its most durable, every setting having an innocent explanation available, so an extraction that has shed its nameable forces and kept the tilt is the mature form rather than a lesser one, which the dark-patterns literature says in its own terms when it describes the trick as a choice architecture bent against the person meeting it. Discord is the forces at war with each other -- usable but unfit, honest but hooking, each force optimized against the others so no settlement is struck and the object misfits itself; Alexander's misfit, where every part passes its own audit and the whole still fails, and where the arrangement supplies the hardest case of all: the preset the framework shipped, the friction a decade of reasonable additions came to, the order the query returned, all of it doing work and none of it decided, so the object tilts hard against its users with nobody who chose the tilt. Read an object by which settlement it strikes.

## System

- Do read the object as a composition of six forces, never as one undifferentiated impression: name whether the forces hold for the user, take for the maker, or fail to cohere, and cast each force from its engine, all on the same object.
- Do let concord be the quiet bridge: an object that serves cleanly has no seam to hide and grows more trusted the closer it is read -- the least dramatic settlement and the hardest to fake, because faking it is the extraction it is not.
- Do make concord earn the sixth force: a maker who reached it by refraining has only inherited an arrangement they did not author, which still moved everyone who met it, so a play that treats restraint as concord has staged the wrong settlement.
- Do stage extraction as service turned back on the user, not an obvious villain: the polish is load-bearing for the take, the usability real so the user stays inside the machine, and the deepest form is the one the user defends -- so let the gift reveal itself as a bill only when the mechanism is finally seen.
- Do let discord be a failure of the whole, not of a part: every force may pass its own audit while the object is still bad, because the settlement was skipped and the forces cancel -- so read the badness as the missing reconciliation, not as a set of separate flaws.
- Do keep the authorless case as discord and not extraction: where the tilt is real and no one chose it, looking for the intent will find nothing, because there is nothing to find, and no maker's end is being served either.
- Do not treat the six as equally evidenced: the atoms carry their own magnitudes, and a settlement read as though every force pulls with the same strength has overclaimed. Do not edit the composite's files; wire only from the play's side.
