# Undertow: Reference

## Line of Work

Undertow as **composite**: the ground a persona feels from, read as a fact about what they are
looking at. It is not a fourth mechanism but a wiring of three: the **body** engine supplies the
set-point drifted out of range and pulling, the **temperament** engine supplies the resting
disposition arrived with and returned to, and the **mood** engine supplies the affective colour
laid on every read before any single event arrives. This composite adds no fourth engine; it reads
the seam the three make -- a continuous supply of feeling that arrives without a note saying where
it came from -- and stages that seam into three bridges, closing on the discovery that a state
with nothing to attach it to gets attached to whatever is in view.

The domain does not model any single ground -- hunger, fatigue, pain, arousal, and sickness are
the body engine's; the five axes and their poles are temperament's; the two-axis circumplex and
its quadrants are mood's; and one alone is a process or a position, not the shape. Nor is it the
sharp feeling that fires at an event and passes, which is the emotion engine's; nor the organism
under demand building toward discharge, which is the stress engine's; nor the requirement a
persona must have met, which is the appetite composite's. It owns the standing state a persona
judges from, and what happens to it when a judgement is asked for.

The spine is Schwarz and Clore's feelings-as-information account, which is where the joining is
argued rather than asserted here. Its claim is that a persona confronted with an evaluative
question consults their current affective state and reads it as a response to the target -- "how
do I feel about it?" -- so a feeling produced by something else is spent on whatever is being
judged, and, decisively, the effect disappears when the actual source is made salient. Their 1983
demonstration used the weather: respondents reported greater satisfaction with their lives on
sunny days than on rainy ones, but not when the interviewer first drew attention to the weather.
Russell's core-affect account supplies the substrate the theory needs: a continuous
neurophysiological state, read on valence and activation, that is always present, always
available as information, and not intrinsically about anything -- which is precisely what makes
it misattributable. Between them, the bodily condition, the resting baseline, and the affective
background stop being three subjects and become one ground with one problem: it is informative and
unaddressed. What this composite adds on top is the ordering by how locatable the source is --
body naming itself, temperament never varying, mood varying and unlabelled -- and the reading of
the first two through the misattribution logic established for the third.

**A caveat this composite carries rather than buries.** The specific weather-and-life-satisfaction
result has a contested replication record: Lucas and Lawless, working with a much larger sample,
found the association between daily weather and life satisfaction very small, and the exchange
that followed is a live methodological dispute about design rather than a settled overturning.
The load-bearing claim here is not the size of a weather effect but the moderation -- that an
unattributed feeling is used and an attributed one is not -- which rests on a far wider base of
misattribution paradigms than the single study that made the finding famous.

over the body, temperament, and mood engines. The first two rows are the **joining** -- the
published work that sets these as one informative ground -- and the rest anchor the individual
bridges.

## Restrictions

What the composite refuses to model, and to whom it delegates.

- **The single grounds (the atoms)**: The composite wires the three but owns none. Hunger through
  sickness are the body engine's; the five axes are temperament's; the circumplex quadrants are
  mood's. Undertow reads only the shape the three share; one ground alone is a process or a
  position, not the whole of what a persona judges from.
- **The feeling that fires and passes (emotion)**: The sharp, object-directed response to an event
  is the emotion engine's. Undertow is what was already running when it fired, and what remains
  when it has gone.
- **The requirement, not the state (appetite)**: What a persona must have and where the must came
  from is the appetite composite's, whose given bridge owns the requirement itself. Undertow owns
  the physical state that makes a deficit felt, which is the body engine's side of the same event.
- **The organism under demand (stress, resilience)**: The load building toward discharge is the
  stress engine's, and the coping that meets it is the resilience composite's. Undertow reads the
  ground under an ordinary judgement, not a system under strain.
- **The return to a level (lasting)**: The erosion of a good and the baseline that reclaims it are
  the lasting composite's. Undertow reads what the level is doing to a judgement now; lasting
  reads the returning over time.
- **The catalogued shortcut (estimation, bias)**: Reading a likelihood off an anchor, off ease of
  recall, or off a resemblance is the estimation composite's, and the standing tilts held as
  positions are the bias engine's. Undertow owns the affective and bodily ground being spent as
  information, which is a different source of error from a named heuristic.
- **The surround acting inward (sensorium)**: What a place gives the body through smell, sound,
  and touch is the sensorium composite's. Undertow is the organism acting outward: the same body,
  supplying rather than receiving.
- **The warping of felt time (absorption)**: Attention, flow, and time-perception are the
  absorption composite's. Undertow reads what colours a judgement, not what happens to the clock.
- **The state as authored symbol (the play's pitch)**: A mood made to **stand for** a story -- the
  grey morning that mirrors a theme of exhaustion -- is refused: that is authored key, the play's
  pitch, not the force on the persona. Undertow sets the ground; the meaning read into it is the
  play's.

## Encoding

Source to constraint. A composite root over three engine atoms it declares as dependencies, with
three bridges, ordered by how easily the persona can locate the source.

- **[undertow](process_undertow.md)** (the root): the ground a persona feels from -- the
  information a feeling genuinely carries, against the question of what it is information about.
  Anchored by Schwarz & Clore and Russell.
- **[the demand](process_undertow_demand.md)** (bridge): a set-point out of range, pulling the
  persona toward the deficit and narrowing them around it -- the one ground that names itself, and
  still is not corrected for. Anchored by Cannon (1932), Craig (2002), and Melzack & Wall (1965).
- **[the set](process_undertow_set.md)** (bridge): the resting tendencies that tilt every act and
  decide none, invisible from inside because a ground constant across a life supplies no contrast.
  Anchored by Costa & McCrae (1992) and Eysenck (1967).
- **[the tint](process_undertow_tint.md)** (bridge): the two-axis background carried across a
  stretch and spent on the nearest subject, since it varies and still arrives unaddressed.
  Anchored by Russell (1980), Thayer (1989), and Schwarz & Clore (1983) with the Lucas & Lawless
  caveat; the composite's twist and keystone, that a state with nothing to attach it to gets
  attached to whatever is in view -- and that making the real source salient is what removes it.
