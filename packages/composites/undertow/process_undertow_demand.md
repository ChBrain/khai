# Process: Undertow, the Demand

## Taxonomy

[Undertow](process_undertow.md), the ground that names itself.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: undertow

## Initiated by

A set-point has drifted out of range and the body has registered the gap.
The Demand starts wherever the [body](@chbrain/khai-engine-body/process_body.md) is pulling -- the [hunger](@chbrain/khai-engine-body/process_hunger.md) of fuel run down, the [fatigue](@chbrain/khai-engine-body/process_fatigue.md) of rest owed, the [pain](@chbrain/khai-engine-body/process_pain.md) of integrity breached, the [arousal](@chbrain/khai-engine-body/process_arousal.md) of charge risen, and the [sickness](@chbrain/khai-engine-body/process_sickness.md) of defence engaged.
It is the one ground of the three that arrives with its own name on it.

## Direction

Toward the deficit, and away from everything else.
The Demand reads the narrowing -- the meeting that gets shorter as lunch recedes, the argument that sharpens at the end of a long day, the patience that returns with a sandwich and was never a matter of character -- each a persona pulled toward a set-point and thinned around it, with everything that would serve the gap coming forward and everything else receding.
It moves the persona from having a body to being run by one: the pull growing as the drift grows, and the world's demands answered with _not yet, attend to this first_.

## Lever

The legibility of a demand that says what it is against the reach it takes while it says it.
The carrying force is that this ground is the honest one -- hunger presents as hunger and pain as pain, so a persona can in principle name it, and a body that could not make its deficits felt would not close them; the resistance is that naming it does not switch it off: the narrowing runs while the persona knows exactly what is narrowing them, so the reach thins and the judgements made in the thinned reach are made anyway, and the persona attributes the sharpness to the meeting rather than to the hour.
The Demand is undertow at its most locatable and least deniable, and still not corrected for.

## Echo

The deficit felt, named, and acting anyway.
The Demand leaves the persona narrowed around a set-point and judging from inside the narrowing -- and hands the pattern on to the grounds that do not announce themselves at all.
It sets the shape the other two will repeat with the label removed: a real state, a real effect on what the persona concludes, and no correction applied.
The Demand is dated from the drift, the moment a range was left.
