# Process: Undertow, the Tint

## Taxonomy

[Undertow](process_undertow.md), the ground that varies and still arrives unlabelled, where the three are seen for one thing.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: undertow

## Initiated by

A persona is carrying a tone across a stretch of hours, and it is about to be spent on the first thing that asks for a verdict.
The Tint starts wherever [mood](@chbrain/khai-engine-mood/position_mood.md) is doing its work -- a background on two axes, pleasant or unpleasant and activated or quiet, running as [buoyant](@chbrain/khai-engine-mood/position_buoyant.md), [serene](@chbrain/khai-engine-mood/position_serene.md), [irritable](@chbrain/khai-engine-mood/position_irritable.md), or [flat](@chbrain/khai-engine-mood/position_flat.md), tilting which feelings fire and how they land.
It is the colour laid on every read before any single event arrives.

## Direction

Toward a verdict about something else, carrying the tone inside it.
The Tint reads the spending -- the plan that looks unworkable on a bad morning and obvious on a good one, the friend judged cooler than usual by a persona who slept badly and has not connected the two, the whole life rated better under a clear sky -- each a real feeling attached to the nearest available subject because it came with no subject of its own.
It moves the persona from having a mood to having an opinion, and nothing in the opinion records where the weight came from.

## Lever

The genuine information in an affective background against the absence of an address on it.
The carrying force is that a mood is not a distortion but a summary -- a persona for whom things have been going badly is carrying a reading of that, and reading it is useful; the resistance is that the summary is undifferentiated: it says _things are not good_ without saying _about what_, and a mind asked to evaluate something while holding an unattributed feeling will use the feeling, which is why drawing attention to the actual source removes the effect -- a persona reminded what the weather is stops rating their life by it.
The Tint rides the composite's keystone knife-edge: a state with nothing to attach it to gets attached to whatever is in view, so the persona's verdict on the thing is partly a reading of themselves, delivered in the grammar of a fact about the thing.

## Echo

The feeling spent on the nearest subject and filed as a judgement.
It shows the three grounds complete -- one that names itself and narrows anyway, one that never varies and so is taken for the terrain, and one that varies without a name and lands on whatever is in front of it -- and carries beneath it the composite's keystone: the persona is always judging from a state as well as about a thing, and the state does not label itself.
The correction exists and is small: the effect goes when the source is made salient, which is why _ask what they have eaten and slept_ is a real instrument and _tell them they are in a mood_ is not.
The Tint is the note the whole composite closes on: what a persona concludes carries a component they would sincerely deny is in it, and they are not being dishonest, only unable to see the ground from on it.
The Tint is dated from the stretch, not from the moment the verdict was given.
