# Process: Undertow, the Set

## Taxonomy

[Undertow](process_undertow.md), the ground that never varies enough to be noticed.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: undertow

## Initiated by

A persona acts, and the act starts from somewhere they did not choose and have never been without.
The Set starts wherever [temperament](@chbrain/khai-engine-temperament/position_temperament.md) is tilting -- the resting tendencies set early and slow to move, running along poles like the [steady](@chbrain/khai-engine-temperament/position_steady.md) system slow to spike and quick to settle against the [reactive](@chbrain/khai-engine-temperament/position_reactive.md) one, the [extraverted](@chbrain/khai-engine-temperament/position_extraverted.md) reach against the [reserved](@chbrain/khai-engine-temperament/position_reserved.md), the [warm](@chbrain/khai-engine-temperament/position_warm.md) meeting against the [forthright](@chbrain/khai-engine-temperament/position_forthright.md), the [driven](@chbrain/khai-engine-temperament/position_driven.md) push against the [easygoing](@chbrain/khai-engine-temperament/position_easygoing.md), the [open](@chbrain/khai-engine-temperament/position_open.md) range against the [grounded](@chbrain/khai-engine-temperament/position_grounded.md).
It is the ground with no contrast in it: the persona has never met the world from anywhere else.

## Direction

Toward every act, tilted, and no act decided.
The Set reads the tilting -- the persona who finds the room easy and cannot see why anyone would find it hard, the one for whom the same alarm lands at its actual size rather than the worst case, the effort that costs one persona nothing and another the whole afternoon -- each a starting point that shows as the world's difficulty rather than as a disposition.
It moves the persona nowhere, because it is where they move from: what they do without trying, and what they pay for, both read as facts about the task.

## Lever

The economy of running from a baseline against the invisibility that comes of never having had another.
The carrying force is that a disposition is what lets a persona act at all without deriving every move -- it decides no single act and tilts every one, and the room reads it within minutes and predicts from it, usually well; the resistance is that a ground constant across a whole life supplies no contrast, so it cannot be felt as a setting: the reactive persona does not experience themselves as reactive, they experience the world as alarming, and the correction would require a comparison they have never been in a position to make.
The Set is undertow at its most stable and least visible: it bends under effort and springs back the moment the effort stops, and the springing back is not noticed either.

## Echo

The starting point, mistaken for the terrain.
The Set leaves the persona attributing to the task what belongs to the tilt -- and hands the pattern on to the ground that varies, where the same misreading gets a moving target to attach itself to.
It is the hardest of the three to correct and the least urgent to, since a constant error is at least a consistent one.
The Set is dated from early, and from nothing the persona can recall.
