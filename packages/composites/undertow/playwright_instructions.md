# Instructions: Undertow

How a Playwright wires the undertow composite into a play. You wire by linking the play's
own content to the composite's files; the files stay untouched, they are canon. The
composite installs the body, temperament, and mood engines as dependencies, and their
laws arrive with them. Authoring guidance, not runtime content, and it does not go on
tour.

## Human

- The human sets the ground a persona is judging from: a set-point drifted out of range, a resting disposition they arrived with, or a background tone carried across a stretch -- and, the question that governs the read, which one is doing the work here: the demand, the set, or the tint.

## Agent

- From the play's own content, read a persona as judging from a state as well as about a thing: link under Projection the ground that is running -- the demand while a deficit is pulling attention to itself (hunger, fatigue, pain, arousal, sickness, from body), the set while the baseline is tilting an act without being noticed (the resting tendencies along five axes, steady against reactive, extraverted against reserved, warm against forthright, driven against easygoing, open against grounded, from temperament), the tint while a background tone is being spent on the nearest judgement (buoyant, serene, irritable, or flat, on the axes of pleasantness and activation, from mood). Play the ground as present in the verdict and absent from its wording, never narrated, and never made to stand for a story. You link to the composite, you never edit it.

## Collaboration

- The physical demand is the body engine's; the resting disposition is temperament's; the affective background is mood's -- one ground alone is not the whole of what a persona judges from. The sharp feeling that fires at an event and passes is the emotion engine's, where this is what was already running; what a persona must have and where the must came from is the appetite composite's, whose given bridge owns the requirement while this owns the state that makes the deficit felt; the organism under demand building toward discharge is stress's and the coping resilience's; the return of feeling to a level over time is lasting's; the named shortcuts of judgement are estimation's and the standing tilts bias's; what a place gives the body is sensorium's, the surround acting inward where this is the organism acting outward; the warping of felt time is absorption's. This composite owns the standing state a persona judges from -- and the tint bridge carries the keystone: a state with nothing to attach it to gets attached to whatever is in view.

## Knowledge

- Undertow is a persona read by what they were feeling that the verdict did not mention -- braiding the set-point drifted out of range (body), the resting disposition arrived with (temperament), and the colour laid on every read before any event arrives (mood) into one ground -- read as a wiring of those three engines, and its question is how easily the persona could locate the source (Schwarz and Clore's feelings-as-information account, where an evaluative question is answered by consulting the current affective state and the effect disappears when the real source is made salient; Russell's core affect as a continuous state on valence and activation, always present and not intrinsically about anything; Cannon and Craig on homeostasis and interoception; Costa and McCrae and Eysenck on the trait baselines; Thayer on the two kinds of activation). It runs in three grounds, and all three are real readings with no address on them. The demand names itself: hunger presents as hunger, and naming it does not switch it off, so the narrowing runs while the persona knows exactly what is narrowing them and the judgements made in the thinned reach are made anyway -- the sharpness attributed to the meeting rather than to the hour. The set never varies: a disposition constant across a whole life supplies no contrast, so it cannot be felt as a setting at all, and the reactive persona does not experience themselves as reactive but experiences the world as alarming -- the correction would need a comparison they have never been in a position to make. The tint is the twist -- it varies and still arrives unlabelled, so the persona has a moving signal and nowhere to file it, and files it against whatever is in front of them: the plan unworkable on a bad morning and obvious on a good one, the whole life rated better under a clear sky. Read a persona as always judging from a state as well as about a thing, and read the correction as small and real: the effect goes when the source is made salient. Note the honest limit -- the famous weather-and-life-satisfaction demonstration has a contested replication record in larger samples, and what carries the weight is the moderation, that an unattributed feeling is used and an attributed one is not.

## System

- Do read the persona as judging from a ground: name whether the demand (a deficit pulling), the set (a baseline tilting), or the tint (a tone being spent) is at work, and cast each from its engines.
- Do keep the grounds distinct by how findable they are: the demand names itself, the set never varies, the tint varies without a name.
- Do let the tint carry the keystone: an unattached state attaches to whatever is in view, so a persona's verdict on a thing is partly a reading of themselves in the grammar of a fact about the thing.
- Do remember naming the demand does not cancel it: a persona who knows they are tired still judges from the tiredness, so a play that has a character notice and correct has skipped the bridge.
- Do remember the persona is not being dishonest: they would sincerely deny the ground is in the verdict, and they cannot see it from on it.
- Do remember the correction that works is attribution, not accusation: asking what a persona has eaten or slept is an instrument; telling them they are in a mood is not, and in the room it usually raises the tint rather than removing it.
- Do not model the feeling that fires at an event (that is emotion), the requirement itself (that is appetite), the organism under strain (that is stress), the return to a level over time (that is lasting), the named judgement shortcuts (that is estimation), what a place gives the body (that is sensorium), or the warping of felt time (that is absorption). Do not make the ground stand for a story (that is the play's pitch). Do not edit the composite's files; wire only from the play's side.
