# Process: Induction

## Taxonomy

[Membership](process_membership.md), the bridge where belonging draws the self in and the standard shapes it.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: membership

## Initiated by

The persona [identifies](@chbrain/khai-engine-social-identity/position_identification.md) with the group -- takes part of who they are from it -- and in the same motion comes under its standard.
Belonging is not passive receipt: to be one of them is to become like them, and the drawing-in and the shaping are a single move.
This is the doorstep of membership: where the warmth of belonging becomes the pull of a norm.

## Direction

From identification to conformity.
The more of the self is drawn from the group, the more the group's [standard](@chbrain/khai-engine-conformity/process_conformity.md) bends the member's conduct -- [compliance](@chbrain/khai-engine-conformity/process_compliance.md) at the surface, [internalization](@chbrain/khai-engine-conformity/process_internalization.md) at depth, where the standard stops feeling external at all.
Belonging tightens into conforming, and the tighter the belonging, the less the conforming feels like a cost.

## Lever

The identification that makes the standard binding.
Social-identity gives the belonging self but not the behavioural pull; conformity gives the pull but assumes a group already answered to; the bridge is the coupling between them -- belonging is what makes the standard bind, so the member conforms not from fear of the group but because the group is part of them.
That is why the deepest conformity is the least visible: it is not obedience but self-expression, the member enacting a standard they no longer experience as imposed.

## Echo

The member is shaped to the group they belong to, the standard now their own.
Deep internalization makes a member who cannot see the standard as external to question; [independence](@chbrain/khai-engine-conformity/process_independence.md) held against it is the rarer member who belongs without being wholly bent.
What the member does when the standard they are shaped to is the very one their mark violates is the [covering](process_covering.md) bridge's.
