# Process: Membership

## Taxonomy

The standing bind of belonging to a group -- a composite over social-identity, conformity, stigma, and loyalty.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: membership

## Initiated by

A persona belongs to a group, and belonging is never only warmth -- it is a bind.
From the group they draw part of who they are; to it they bend their conduct; in it they may carry a mark that would discredit them; and out of it they cannot easily go, for the allegiance that holds them raises the cost of leaving.
This is membership: the standing web of pulls a group has on one of its own. The composite adds no fifth engine; it wires the seams the four will not cross alone -- identity draws, conformity bends, stigma marks, loyalty holds.

## Direction

Toward staying and fitting -- or toward the strain when belonging and its price pull apart.
The member is drawn in and shaped, holds or hides what would not fit, and is kept from leaving; where those pulls agree, membership is ease, and the person disappears into the group without friction.
Where they contradict -- the member whose mark the group's own standard forbids, held in by a loyalty they cannot spend -- membership becomes the vise the drama lives in.

## Lever

The four pulls, running on one member at once, reinforcing or contradicting.
Identity draws the self from the group; conformity bends the conduct to its standard; stigma marks what that standard would reject; loyalty raises the cost of the exit. Strong identification makes conformity effortless -- until the standard is the one the member's mark violates, and then loyalty is what keeps them from leaving the trap.
No single atom stages this: the bind lives in the seams, where the four pulls agree into ease or cross into a belonging that costs.

## Echo

The member fits and stays, or is held in a belonging that costs them the truth of who they are.
The bind runs as [induction](process_induction.md), [covering](process_covering.md), or [tether](process_tether.md) -- the pull-in and shaping, the mark managed to stay, the hold against leaving.
The felt ache of not belonging, of exclusion, is the loneliness engine's; the personal self apart from any group -- the "I" to this "we" -- is the identity engine's; the group as an entity in its own right is the group engine's. This composite owns the member's bind: belonging and its price, borne by one person.
