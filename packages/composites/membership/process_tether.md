# Process: Tether

## Taxonomy

[Membership](process_membership.md), the bridge where loyalty holds the member in against the cost of leaving.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: membership

## Initiated by

The belonging turns costly -- the standard chafes, the mark strains against its concealment, the group errs or wounds -- and the member would go, but [loyalty](@chbrain/khai-engine-loyalty/position_loyalty.md) raises the price of the [exit](@chbrain/khai-engine-loyalty/position_exit.md) past what they are willing to pay.
They are held: not caged, but tethered, free to strain against the line and unable to cross it.
This is the seam that makes membership a bind rather than a choice remade each day.

## Direction

Toward staying -- and toward [voice](@chbrain/khai-engine-loyalty/position_voice.md) as the one alternative to a leaving loyalty forbids.
Held against exit, the member who cannot leave and will not simply comply is pushed toward voice -- trying to change the group from within -- or, failing that, toward enduring in silence.
The direction is inward pressure: the exit sealed, the energy that would have left turns back on the group as protest or back on the self as endurance.

## Lever

The exit-cost loyalty imposes, set against the pains the other pulls generate.
Loyalty owns the exit, the voice, and the allegiance that raises the cost; but it does not stage what the member is being held into -- a conformity that chafes, a mark that must stay hidden, a self drawn from a group that would not have all of it. The bridge is that coupling: loyalty is the tether that turns a painful belonging into a bind, converting what would be exit into voice or a swallowed endurance.
The stronger the loyalty, the higher the pain it will hold a member through -- which is its worth, and its danger.

## Echo

The member stays -- voicing, enduring, or slowly worn -- held in a belonging whose price they keep paying because leaving would cost more.
When the loyalty finally gives and the exit-cost is paid at last, the leaving itself is the loyalty engine's exit; the ache that follows, of a belonging lost, is the loneliness engine's.
This bridge owns the hold: the reason a member does not simply go, and the strain of a person kept where belonging has turned to bind.
