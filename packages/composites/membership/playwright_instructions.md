# Instructions: Membership

How a Playwright wires the membership composite into a play. You wire by linking the play's own content to the composite's files; the composite and its atoms stay untouched, they are canon. Installing the composite brings its four atoms -- social-identity, conformity, stigma, loyalty -- as dependencies, and the Roadie plumbs the composite's law into Instructions Knowledge on deploy. Authoring guidance, not runtime content, and it does not go on tour.

## Human

- The human defines the membership terrain: the group and the persona's place in it, the standard it holds, whatever mark the persona carries that the group would discredit, and what leaving would cost -- so the four pulls have real content and somewhere to agree or contradict.

## Agent

- From the play's own content, a plot links the membership bridge it stages (induction, covering, or tether) under its Tension. Cast the member from the atoms -- their belonging from social-identity, their conduct from conformity, their mark from stigma, their allegiance from loyalty -- and let the bridge show the bind: the pull-in and shaping, the mark managed to stay, the tether that holds against leaving. You link to the composite and its atoms; you never edit them.

## Collaboration

- The felt ache of not belonging, or of a belonging lost, is the loneliness engine's; the personal "I" apart from any group is the identity engine's; the group as an entity in its own right is the group engine's. The belonging self is social-identity's, the standard's pull is conformity's, the mark and its passing are stigma's, the exit and voice are loyalty's. This composite supplies only the seams -- the member's bind the four atoms make together but none makes alone.

## Knowledge

- Membership is one persona's standing bind to a group: the self they draw from it, the standard it bends them to, a mark it would discredit, and the allegiance that raises the cost of leaving. Induction is the pull-in -- identification drawing the self from the group and bringing the member under its standard, belonging tightening into conforming. Covering is the mark managed -- a stigma the standard would discredit, kept hidden by passing and covering so belonging can hold. Tether is the hold -- loyalty raising the exit cost so a costly belonging cannot simply be left, pushing the member to voice or endurance.

## System

- Do let the pulls agree or contradict: where identity, conformity, mark, and loyalty line up, the member fits without friction; the drama is where they cross -- the marked member the standard forbids, held in by loyalty.
- Do let deep belonging make conformity invisible: the most conformed member does not feel the standard as external at all, so read it as self-expression, not obedience.
- Do let covering cost: the labour of not being wholly seen wears on a member over time, and the closer the group, the higher the stakes of the mark.
- Do delegate the ache of exclusion to the loneliness engine and the personal self to identity. Do not edit the composite's or the atoms' files; wire only from the play's side.
