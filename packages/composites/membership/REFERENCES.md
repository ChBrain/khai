# Membership: Reference

## Line of Work

Membership as **composite**: one persona's standing bind to a group -- the self they draw from it, the standard it bends them to, a mark it would discredit, and the allegiance that raises the cost of leaving. It is not a new engine but a wiring of four: **social-identity** supplies the belonging self, the "we" a persona takes into who they are; **conformity** supplies the group's standard and its pull on conduct; **stigma** supplies a mark the group would discredit; and **loyalty** supplies the allegiance that raises the exit cost. The composite adds no fifth phenomenon; it stages the seams the four will not cross apart -- belonging tightening into conforming, a mark managed to keep belonging, and loyalty holding the member in when belonging turns costly.

The domain does not model the felt ache of not belonging, of exclusion, which is the loneliness engine's; nor the personal self apart from any group, the "I" to this "we," which is the identity engine's; nor the group as an entity in its own right, which is the group engine's. It owns the member's side of the tie: belonging and its price, borne by one person.

The spine is the psychology of belonging as a bind. Belonging is a fundamental human need, and a group answers it -- but never for free: to belong is to draw the self from the group, to be bent to its standard, to manage whatever in oneself the standard would reject, and to be held against leaving by an allegiance that raises the exit cost. Where those pulls agree, the member disappears into the group without friction; where they contradict -- a mark the group's own standard forbids, a loyalty that will not let the member go -- membership becomes a vise. The three bridges are the bind's course: induction, covering, tether.

## Origin

The social psychology of belonging, group influence, spoiled identity, and the costs of exit, wired as a composite over four engines.

## Restrictions

What the composite refuses to model, and to whom it delegates.

- **Loneliness**: The composite models the bind of belonging, not the ache of its absence. The felt lack a persona carries when they do not belong, or when a belonging is lost, is owned by the loneliness engine; membership owns the pulls of being held in a group, and hands the pain of exclusion, before or after, to loneliness.
- **Identity**: The composite models the self drawn from the group, not the self apart from it. The personal "I" a persona is beneath any membership -- their self-concept as their own -- is owned by the identity engine; this composite governs the group-belonging "we" and its binds, not the individual identity beneath the memberships.
- **Group**: The composite models the member's bind, not the group as an entity. The group as a thing in its own right -- its structure, its boundary, its life -- is owned by the group engine; membership reads one persona's tie to it, the pulls on the member, not the collective they are a member of.
- **The atoms**: The composite adds no content its atoms already own. The belonging self is the social-identity engine's; the standard's pull on conduct is the conformity engine's; the discrediting mark and its passing are the stigma engine's; the exit, voice, and exit-cost are the loyalty engine's. Membership owns only the seams -- the bind the four make together that none makes alone.

## Encoding

Source to constraint, per file.

- **[membership](process_membership.md)** (the root): One persona's standing bind to a group, four pulls -- identity, conformity, stigma, loyalty -- running on one member at once, agreeing into ease or crossing into a belonging that costs. Anchored by Baumeister & Leary (the need to belong) and Tajfel & Turner (the belonging self).
- **[induction](process_induction.md)** (bridge): Identification draws the self from the group and brings the member under its standard -- belonging tightening into conforming, deepest where the standard stops feeling external. Wires social-identity to conformity. Anchored by Tajfel & Turner (identification) and the psychology of group influence.
- **[covering](process_covering.md)** (bridge): The member carries a mark the standard they conform to would discredit, so belonging is held by passing and covering -- a self kept partly unseen. Wires stigma to conformity. Anchored by Goffman (stigma and passing) and Yoshino (covering).
- **[tether](process_tether.md)** (bridge): When belonging turns costly, loyalty raises the exit price past what the member will pay, converting a leaving into voice or a swallowed endurance. Wires loyalty to the pains the other pulls generate. Anchored by Hirschman (exit, voice, and loyalty).
