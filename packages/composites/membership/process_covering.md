# Process: Covering

## Taxonomy

[Membership](process_membership.md), the bridge where a mark is managed to keep belonging.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: membership

## Initiated by

The member carries a [stigma](@chbrain/khai-engine-stigma/piece_stigma.md) -- an attribute that would discredit them in the very group whose standard they conform to -- and belonging now demands its management.
The standard the member has been shaped to is the one their mark violates, so fitting in and being fully seen have come apart.
This is the vise particular to a marked member: the tighter they belong, the more there is to hide.

## Direction

From the mark toward its concealment, for the sake of the belonging.
To keep the belonging, the member [passes](@chbrain/khai-engine-stigma/process_passing.md) where the mark can be hidden and covers where it cannot -- downplaying a known mark to keep it from filling the room -- so that membership is held by the labour of not being wholly seen.
The direction is inward and effortful: belonging maintained by a steady management of the self's discreditable part.

## Lever

The standard the mark fails, and the belonging that makes the hiding worth it.
Stigma gives the mark and the passing; conformity gives the standard; but neither stages the vise -- that the member conforms to a group by a standard which is exactly what would mark them -- so the closer the belonging, the heavier the concealment. The [bearer](@chbrain/khai-engine-stigma/position_bearer.md)'s stance decides whether the mark is hidden, softened, or worn openly.
The bridge is that coupling: belonging turning a mark into a burden of concealment, and concealment into the price of belonging.

## Echo

The member belongs, at the cost of a self kept partly unseen -- or discloses, and risks the [discrediting](@chbrain/khai-engine-stigma/process_discrediting.md) that could end the belonging outright.
The closer the group, the higher the stakes of the mark, and the more a disclosure would cost; the labour of covering is itself a strain that wears on the member over time.
What holds a marked member inside a group that would reject them, when leaving might spare them the whole labour, is the [tether](process_tether.md) bridge's.
