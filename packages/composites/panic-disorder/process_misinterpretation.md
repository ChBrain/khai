# Process: Catastrophic Misinterpretation

## Taxonomy

Movement 2 of [Panic Disorder](process_panic_disorder.md). The precise sizing-up that turns a normal somatic sensation into a perceived existential emergency.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: panic_disorder

## Initiated by

The detection of a somatic sensation (a skipped heartbeat, breathlessness, or dizziness) passed up from the somatic vigilance movement.

## Direction

Upward and absolute. The persona performs a rapid [appraisal](@chbrain/khai-engine-coping/process_appraisal.md) of the sensation, concluding instantly that it signifies immediate disaster (e.g., a heart attack, suffocation, or losing control).

## Lever

The leap to catastrophe. This is the engine of Clark's cognitive model: the sensation is not just uncomfortable, it is lethal. Because the appraisal happens so fast, it bypasses logical review. A skipped beat is not from too much coffee; it is cardiac arrest. This catastrophic read dumps adrenaline into the system, producing the exact terrifying physical symptoms the persona was afraid of, closing the loop into a full acute attack.

## Echo

The triggering of the acute panic attack, accompanied by an overwhelming urge to seek immediate salvation.
