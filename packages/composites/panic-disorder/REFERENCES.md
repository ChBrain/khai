# Panic Disorder: Reference

## Line of Work

Panic disorder as **composite**: the syndrome read as a compound of three atoms this package declares as dependencies, none of which carries it alone. The composite adds only the read of the compound; every mechanism it names belongs to an atom.

**The organising claim is that panic disorder is not frequent terror, but a catastrophic cognitive loop.** Clark's cognitive model is what the composite is built on. An ordinary physiological sensation -- a skipped heartbeat, a wave of dizziness, or breathlessness -- is misinterpreted as an existential threat. This catastrophic read dumps adrenaline into the system, producing the exact terrifying physical symptoms the persona feared, closing the loop into an acute attack.

**Four movements, and they run as a self-reinforcing cycle.** _Somatic vigilance_ takes the anticipatory dread of the next attack and focuses the persona's attention inward, lowering the perceptual threshold for normal bodily fluctuations. _Catastrophic misinterpretation_ is the appraisal that turns the detected sensation into an emergency. _Safety behaviour_ is the rapid bailout -- fleeing the room or holding a wall -- which successfully reduces the immediate terror but prevents the persona from learning the sensation was harmless, cementing the false belief. _Restriction_ is the behavioural aftermath, where the persona narrows their life to avoid triggering the somatic trap.

**The twist is that the reaction to a sensation manufactures the next sensation.** Every attempt to check whether the persona is safe produces the evidence that they are not. Scanning the chest for a palpitation raises the heart rate. Focusing on breathing makes it feel shallow. The engine's trap is that the body itself has become the feared object.

**On the boundary with agoraphobia (row 44), and why it matters here.** The composite strictly defines restriction as withdrawal driven by an approaching internal threat (the next attack), rather than an inescapable physical location. A persona avoids a crowded theatre not because the exit is blocked, but because the stress might raise their heart rate and trigger the internal somatic loop. By explicitly not routing through the `space` engine, the boundary between panic disorder and agoraphobia is kept structurally distinct.

## Restrictions

What the composite refuses to model, and to whom it delegates.

- **The acute attack itself (panic)**: The raw terror and sympathetic nervous system override belong to the `fear` engine. This composite reads only the architecture that triggers and maintains it.
- **The general anticipation of threat (vigilance/worry)**: Scanning and rumination belong to the `anxiety` engine. This composite reads only the case where the vigilance is pointed directly at the persona's own internal physiology.
- **The sizing up of a stressor (appraisal)**: Evaluation of threats belongs to the `coping` engine. This composite reads only the specific catastrophic read that jumps instantly to lethality.
- **The spatial trap (agoraphobia)**: Avoidance of specific locations where escape is constrained belongs to a separate agoraphobia composite (row 44) reading over the `space` engine. This composite's restriction is purely a retreat from internal somatic triggers.
- **Diagnosis**: The composite is authored persona architecture for a play. It is not a diagnostic instrument, carries no criteria or thresholds, and makes no claim about any real person.

## Encoding

Source to constraint. Clark's cognitive model becomes the root's Lever and the misinterpretation movement, staging the precise leap from sensation to catastrophe. Klein's anticipatory dread becomes the somatic vigilance movement, shifting the focus inward. Salkovskis's work on safety behaviours becomes the third movement, whose Lever explains why the successful escape guarantees the next attack. The boundary work explicitly defining restriction against agoraphobia keeps the composite's footprint exact.
