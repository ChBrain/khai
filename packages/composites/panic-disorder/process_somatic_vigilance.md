# Process: Somatic Vigilance

## Taxonomy

Movement 1 of [Panic Disorder](process_panic_disorder.md). The active hyper-scanning of the body for feared sensations, driven by the dread of the next attack.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: panic_disorder

## Initiated by

The anticipatory dread of the next panic attack, compelling the persona to monitor their own physiology for early warning signs.

## Direction

Inward and amplifying. The persona applies [vigilance](@chbrain/khai-engine-anxiety/process_vigilance.md) to their own body, searching for the physiological shifts they [worry](@chbrain/khai-engine-anxiety/process_worry.md) will escalate into terror.

## Lever

The hyper-focus. By constantly monitoring their heart rate, breathing, or dizziness, the persona lowers their perceptual threshold, making them acutely aware of normal somatic fluctuations that would usually go unnoticed. The act of watching for the symptom becomes the mechanism that amplifies the symptom, setting the stage for the catastrophic misinterpretation.

## Echo

A stream of false positives. The persona detects minor, normal physiological shifts and passes them to the next movement for appraisal, convinced that the body is about to fail.
