# Process: Safety Behaviour

## Taxonomy

Movement 3 of [Panic Disorder](process_panic_disorder.md). The mal-adaptive reliance on crutches and rapid bailouts when an attack is perceived.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: panic_disorder

## Initiated by

The onset of the acute panic attack, driving an overwhelming need to prevent the catastrophized outcome.

## Direction

Outward and urgent. The persona executes a rapid [escape](@chbrain/khai-engine-coping/process_escape.md) or deploys a specific crutch (e.g., sitting down, fleeing the room, holding onto a wall, or seeking reassurance).

## Lever

The false rescue. The safety behaviour successfully reduces the immediate terror, but at a profound long-term cost. Because the persona did not die, they attribute their survival entirely to the safety behaviour ("I didn't have a heart attack because I sat down"). This prevents them from learning that the somatic sensation was harmless to begin with, firmly entrenching the catastrophic misinterpretation for the next cycle.

## Echo

A reinforced dependency. The persona survives the attack, but their belief in the lethality of the symptoms and the necessity of the bailout is cemented, paving the way for long-term restriction.
