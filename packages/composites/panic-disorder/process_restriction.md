# Process: Restriction

## Taxonomy

Movement 4 of [Panic Disorder](process_panic_disorder.md). The behavioural consequence and general withdrawal from activities driven by the approaching internal threat.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: panic_disorder

## Initiated by

The accumulated dread of the recursive panic cycle, forcing the persona to restructure their life to avoid triggering the internal somatic trap.

## Direction

Inward and contracting. The persona withdraws from activities (e.g., exercise, caffeine, or solitary driving) due to the [looming](@chbrain/khai-engine-anxiety/process_looming.md) threat of the next attack, narrowing their world.

## Lever

The avoidance of the body, not the map. This restriction is structurally defined as withdrawal driven by an approaching internal threat, rather than an inescapable physical location. The persona is not avoiding a grocery store because the exit is blocked; they are avoiding the grocery store because the stress of the checkout line might raise their heart rate and trigger the somatic feedback loop. The restriction is a desperate attempt to keep the body's physiology completely flat and un-triggered.

## Echo

A diminished life and a heightened baseline of vigilance. The persona's map shrinks, but because the feared object is their own physiology, the restriction can never fully succeed, leaving the cycle primed to begin again.
