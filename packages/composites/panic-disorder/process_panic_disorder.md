# Process: Panic Disorder

## Taxonomy

Panic Disorder. The recursive somatic feedback loop that turns a singular terrifying event into a self-reinforcing trap. It is not just frequent terror, but an architecture where the body itself becomes the feared object.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: panic_disorder

## Initiated by

The initial acute panic attack, which leaves behind a heightened sensitivity to internal sensations and a profound dread of its return.

## Direction

Inward and spiralling. The cycle accelerates: the hyper-attention of [`anxiety`'s vigilance](@chbrain/khai-engine-anxiety/process_vigilance.md) detects a normal sensation, which [`coping`'s appraisal](@chbrain/khai-engine-coping/process_appraisal.md) sizes up as an emergency, triggering the acute [`fear`'s panic](@chbrain/khai-engine-fear/process_panic.md), which forces the rapid bailout of [`coping`'s escape](@chbrain/khai-engine-coping/process_escape.md), driving the long-term restriction of [`anxiety`'s looming](@chbrain/khai-engine-anxiety/process_looming.md).

## Lever

The trap. The engine's twist is that the reaction to a sensation manufactures the next sensation, so every attempt to check whether you are safe produces the evidence that you are not. When the persona scans their chest for palpitations, the anxiety of the scan itself raises the heart rate, which the persona reads as proof of an impending heart attack. The bailout -- sitting down, fleeing the room, clutching a wall -- removes the immediate terror but reinforces the belief that the sensation was truly dangerous, ensuring the cycle will repeat. The disorder is not the panic attack itself, but the architecture built to avoid the next one, which gradually shrinks the persona's world until the fear of the fear is entirely decoupled from any external threat.

## Echo

An exhausted vigilance. The persona is left with a body that cannot be trusted, and a life organised around not provoking it.
