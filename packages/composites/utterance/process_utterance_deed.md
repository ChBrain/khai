# Process: Utterance, the Deed

## Taxonomy

[Utterance](process_utterance.md), the freight where the saying is itself an act.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: utterance

## Initiated by

The persona says something and, in saying it, does something that was not done before.
The Deed starts wherever a [speech act](@chbrain/khai-engine-speech-act/process_speech_act.md) carries its force -- the [assertive](@chbrain/khai-engine-speech-act/process_assertive.md) that commits the speaker to how things are, the [directive](@chbrain/khai-engine-speech-act/process_directive.md) that puts something on the hearer to do, the [commissive](@chbrain/khai-engine-speech-act/process_commissive.md) that binds the speaker to a future, the [expressive](@chbrain/khai-engine-speech-act/process_expressive.md) that discharges a feeling into the room, and the [declaration](@chbrain/khai-engine-speech-act/process_declaration.md) that alters the world simply by being competently said.
It is the saying as doing: not a description of a state but a move that changes one.

## Direction

Toward the world altered by the saying of it.
The Deed reads the force an utterance carries -- the promise that now exists and can be broken, the order that now stands and can be defied, the naming that from this moment is the name, the apology that has been made and cannot be unmade -- each leaving the situation different from before the words.
It moves the persona from speaking to having acted: a commitment now on the record, a claim on someone's conduct, or a fact of the social world created by nothing but the utterance.

## Lever

The force that makes a saying binding against the conditions that must hold for it to bind.
The carrying force is that certain sayings simply are their acts -- to say the words is to have promised, ordered, named, or apologized, with no further step required; the resistance is that the force depends on standing: the wrong speaker, the wrong setting, or the wrong sincerity leaves the words said and the act unperformed, so a persona can utter a promise and be found not to have made one.
The Deed is the freight at its most accountable: the one part of the cargo that can be pointed to and held.

## Echo

The world changed by a saying, and the speaker now standing behind it.
The Deed leaves the persona committed, obliging, or having altered a fact -- bound by what the saying did rather than by what it described -- and hands that act to the implication that rides beside it and the manner that pitched it.
It is the freight that can be named: what was done in saying it, and what the doer is now answerable for.
The Deed is dated from the saying, the moment the words performed the act rather than reporting it.
