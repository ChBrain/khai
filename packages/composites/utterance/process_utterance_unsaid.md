# Process: Utterance, the Unsaid

## Taxonomy

[Utterance](process_utterance.md), the freight the hearer works out that was never stated.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: utterance

## Initiated by

The persona says one thing and the hearer takes another, correctly, from what was left out.
The Unsaid starts wherever [implicature](@chbrain/khai-engine-implicature/process_implicature.md) does its work -- the [maxims](@chbrain/khai-engine-implicature/process_maxims.md) of cooperative talk that let an answer beside the point still count as an answer, the [flouting](@chbrain/khai-engine-implicature/process_flouting.md) that means precisely by breaking them in the open, and the [scalar](@chbrain/khai-engine-implicature/process_scalar.md) reading by which saying some conveys not all and saying adequate conveys no better than that.
It is meaning without wording: the hearer reasoning from the assumption that the speaker was cooperating, and arriving where the speaker meant them to.

## Direction

Toward a meaning delivered whole and never once put into words.
The Unsaid reads the hearer's inference doing the speaker's work -- the reference that praises only punctuality and so damns the rest, the reply that changes the subject and so refuses, the "he had some of the money" that says he did not have all of it -- each landing exactly, and none of it said.
It moves the persona from what it stated to what it conveyed: a message complete in the hearer, assembled out of the gap between what was said and what cooperation required.

## Lever

The cooperation that makes the inference reliable against the deniability it leaves.
The carrying force is that hearers assume cooperative talk and reason accordingly, which makes implication as dependable as statement -- speakers rely on it constantly, and it usually works; the resistance is that nothing was asserted, so nothing can be pinned: the persona who implied it can say it never said it, and be literally right while both parties know what passed.
The Unsaid is the freight at its most efficient and least accountable: full delivery, no record.

## Echo

The meaning received in full, with nothing on the record to receive it from.
The Unsaid leaves the persona having conveyed what it did not state -- the damning praise, the refusal dressed as a change of subject, the limit set by a small word -- and hands that unquotable load to the manner that pitched it and lets it be disowned.
It is the freight without words: meaning made by what was left out, and answerable to no one because nothing was said.
The Unsaid is dated from the inference, the moment the hearer worked out what the speaker meant and never claimed.
