# Process: Utterance

## Taxonomy

What a saying carries beyond its words.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: utterance

## Initiated by

A persona speaks, and what passes is never only what the words contain.
Utterance starts wherever a saying carries more than it states -- the [speech act](@chbrain/khai-engine-speech-act/process_speech_act.md) it performs by being said, the [implicature](@chbrain/khai-engine-implicature/process_implicature.md) a hearer works out that was never stated, and the [register](@chbrain/khai-engine-register/position_register.md) and [tone](@chbrain/khai-engine-tone/process_tone.md) it arrives in.
This composite adds no fifth engine; it reads what the four make together: an utterance as freight rather than content, a saying weighed by everything it carries past its words.

## Direction

Toward the whole cargo of a saying, read through its parts.
The composite wires the four engines and reads their working in three shapes: [the Deed](process_utterance_deed.md), what the saying does; [the Unsaid](process_utterance_unsaid.md), what it conveys without stating; and [the Manner](process_utterance_manner.md), the twist -- the variety and key it comes in, and the load that leaves nothing to quote.
A persona never merely says a thing: the saying acts, it implies, and it is pitched -- and a hearer receives all three at once, weighted in an order the speaker does not control.

## Lever

Everything an utterance carries against the words that are all it can be held to.
The carrying force is that saying is doing and meaning outruns wording -- an utterance binds, obliges, admits, excludes, and colours, all before its content is weighed; the resistance is the record, which keeps only the words: what was performed, implied, and pitched leaves no quotable trace, so the freight arrives in full and answers for nothing.
Utterance is read wherever a persona is granted the whole of what it sent: not a content delivered but a cargo, most of which was never in the sentence.

## Echo

The saying revealed as freight, only some of it in the words.
Utterance does not close; it is the persona going on carrying more than it says, gathering the four engines into one reading -- a saying that acts, implies, and is pitched.
It hands off what it is not: the moving of meaning across the gap between speaking and hearing, and the mother tongue it moves in, is the language engine's; two personas grounding against different frames until they talk past each other is the crosstalk composite's; the deliberate creation of a false belief is the deception engine's, read in the opacity composite; the labour of performing a self before an audience is the presentation composite's; and the saying made to stand for a story is authored key, the play's pitch, not the force.
The utterance is dated from the saying, and read for the whole of what it carried, not the part of it that can be repeated.
