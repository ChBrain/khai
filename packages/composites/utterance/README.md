# Utterance

Utterance as composite: what a saying carries beyond its words -- the act it performs rather than describes (speech-act), the meaning a hearer works out that was never stated (implicature), and the variety and affective key it is said in (register, tone) -- across the deed, the unsaid, and the manner, and the drama is that what a persona cannot be quoted as saying is what it most surely communicated.
