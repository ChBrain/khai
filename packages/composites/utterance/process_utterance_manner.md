# Process: Utterance, the Manner

## Taxonomy

[Utterance](process_utterance.md), the freight carried by how the saying arrives, and the load that leaves nothing to quote.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: utterance

## Initiated by

The persona chooses a variety to speak in and a key to speak in, and both are read before the content is weighed.
The Manner starts wherever the pitch of a saying carries -- the [register](@chbrain/khai-engine-register/position_register.md) a persona commands, working as a [badge](@chbrain/khai-engine-register/position_badge.md) of who belongs, a [shorthand](@chbrain/khai-engine-register/position_shorthand.md) among those who share it, a [closure](@chbrain/khai-engine-register/position_closure.md) against those who do not, and a thing needing [translation](@chbrain/khai-engine-register/position_translation.md) at the border; and the [tone](@chbrain/khai-engine-tone/process_tone.md) it is tuned to, its [cue](@chbrain/khai-engine-tone/process_cue.md), its [key](@chbrain/khai-engine-tone/process_key.md), and the [attitude](@chbrain/khai-engine-tone/process_attitude.md) it takes toward what it tells.
It is the pitch of the freight: not what was done nor what was implied, but the variety and key the whole arrives in.

## Direction

Toward a hearing settled before the content is reached.
The Manner reads the pitch doing its work -- the register admitting one hearer as an insider and shutting another out in the same breath, the key setting whether a thing is grave or a joke, the attitude deciding whether the teller is with the hearer or above them -- so the same words in another variety or another key are a different message entirely.
It moves the persona from having content to having a hearing: the words unchanged, the reception already decided by how they came.

## Lever

The manner that settles the meaning against the fact that it cannot be quoted.
The carrying force is that pitch outranks content -- a hearer sorts a saying by its register and key before weighing what it says, and a correct sentence in the wrong variety or the wrong tone communicates its opposite; the resistance is that none of it is in the words: the register can be called mere shop-talk, the tone denied outright, and a persona can say with perfect truth that it never said any such thing while the room knows exactly what it said.
The Manner rides the composite's keystone knife-edge: what a persona cannot be quoted as saying is what it most surely communicated -- the deed can be named and the words can be read back, but the implication and the pitch carry the heaviest load and leave no trace to answer for.

## Echo

The message settled by its pitch, and nothing on the record that settled it.
It shows a persona's saying arriving in a variety and a key that decide its reception before its content is weighed -- admitting, excluding, elevating, or mocking -- and carries beneath it the composite's keystone: that the parts of an utterance which land hardest are exactly the parts with no words to point at, so the most reliably delivered message is the most deniable one.
The Manner is the note the whole composite closes on: an utterance is cargo, and a persona is answerable only for the manifest, never for the freight.
The Manner is dated from the pitching, the moment the saying arrived in a variety and a key that had already decided how it would be taken.
