# Instructions: Utterance

How a Playwright wires the utterance composite into a play. You wire by linking the play's
own content to the composite's files; the files stay untouched, they are canon. The
composite installs the speech-act, implicature, register, and tone engines as dependencies,
and their laws arrive with them. Authoring guidance, not runtime content, and it does not go
on tour.

## Human

- The human sets what a persona's saying carries: which utterance is read as freight rather than content -- an act performed, a meaning implied, a variety and key it arrives in -- and, the question that governs the read, which part of the cargo is in play: the deed, the unsaid, or the manner.

## Agent

- From the play's own content, read a persona's saying for everything it carries past its words: link under Projection the part of the freight in play -- the deed while the saying performs (the assertive committing the speaker, the directive obliging the hearer, the commissive binding a future, the expressive discharging a feeling, the declaration altering the world by being competently said, from speech-act), the unsaid while meaning is conveyed without being stated (the maxims of cooperative talk, the flouting that means by breaking them, the scalar reading by which some conveys not all, from implicature), the manner while the pitch settles the hearing (the badge, shorthand, closure, and translation of a variety, and the cue, key, and attitude of a telling, from register and tone). Play the saying as cargo, present in what the persona projects, never narrated, and never made to stand for a story. You link to the composite, you never edit it.

## Collaboration

- The illocutionary force is the speech-act engine's; the inference from cooperative talk is implicature's; the specialized variety is register's; the affective key is tone's -- one engine alone is not the whole cargo. The moving of meaning across the gap between speaking and hearing is the language engine's; two personas talking past each other is the crosstalk composite's; the built false belief is deception's, in the opacity composite; the labour of performing a self is presentation's. This composite owns the freight of a saying -- an utterance read as cargo, most of it never in the sentence -- and the manner bridge carries the keystone: what a persona cannot be quoted as saying is what it most surely communicated.

## Knowledge

- Utterance is a persona's saying read for what it carries beyond its words -- braiding the act it performs by being said (speech-act), the meaning a hearer works out that was never stated (implicature), and the variety and affective key it arrives in (register, tone) into one cargo -- read as a wiring of those four engines, and its question is which part of the freight is in play (Austin's saying as doing; Searle's kinds of force; Grice's cooperative principle; Halliday's register). It runs in three parts. The deed is the saying as act: the promise that now exists and can be broken, the order that now stands and can be defied, the naming that from this moment is the name -- each leaving the situation different, and each depending on standing, since the wrong speaker or setting leaves the words said and the act unperformed. The unsaid is meaning without wording: the hearer assuming cooperation and reasoning from it, so the reference that praises only punctuality damns the rest, the reply that changes the subject refuses, and saying some conveys not all -- delivered in full, with nothing on the record. The manner is the twist -- the register that admits one hearer as an insider and shuts another out in the same breath, and the tone whose key decides whether a thing is grave or a joke and whose attitude decides whether the teller stands with the hearer or above them -- where the freight is seen for what it is: the deed can be named and the words quoted, but the implication and the pitch carry the heaviest load and leave no trace, so a persona can say with perfect truth that it never said any such thing while the room knows exactly what it said. Read a saying as cargo, and read the most surely delivered part as the most deniable.

## System

- Do read a saying as freight, never as content alone: name whether the deed (what it does), the unsaid (what it conveys unstated), or the manner (the variety and key it arrives in) is in play, and cast each from its engine.
- Do keep the parts distinct: the deed changes the situation and can be pointed to, the unsaid arrives complete with nothing on the record, the manner settles the hearing before the content is weighed.
- Do let the manner carry the keystone: what lands hardest is what has no words to point at -- a persona is answerable for the manifest and never for the freight.
- Do not model the moving of meaning across the gap or the tongue it moves in (that is language), two personas talking past each other (that is crosstalk), the built false belief (that is deception, in opacity), or the labour of performing a self (that is presentation). Do not make the saying stand for a story (that is the play's pitch). Do not edit the composite's files; wire only from the play's side.
