# Jeopardy: Reference

## Line of Work

Jeopardy as **composite**: a persona under a threat that has not yet fallen, meeting it over time with three responses at once -- the baseline nervous system they bring to it, the anticipatory hold on the outcome not yet landed, and the routing of a demand that exceeds their resources. It is not a new engine but a wiring of three: **apprehension** supplies the standing filter that sets how sharply the threat registers, **suspense** supplies the anticipation that holds the outcome present while it is only possible, and **stress** supplies the response that fires when the load outstrips the reserve. The composite adds no fourth phenomenon; it stages the seams the three will not cross apart -- the disposition tuning the anticipation, the anticipation tipping into the response, and the reprieve that discharges both or the chronicity when none comes.

The domain does not model the felt fear once the threat is present, nor the flood of relief once it lifts, which are the emotion engine's; nor the loss, if the threatened harm actually arrives, which is the grief engine's. It owns the state before the outcome lands: the being-under-threat itself, held across disposition, dread, and response.

The spine is the appraisal-and-response account of threat: a potential harm is met not passively but by a whole system that appraises it against the resources to cope, holds it in anticipation while its outcome is uncertain, and mobilizes a defensive response -- with a standing disposition setting the sensitivity of the whole. Where the threat is brief, the system spends and recovers; where it is prolonged or never resolves, the mobilization built for a burst is made to run long, and the cost turns chronic. The three bridges are the state's course: onset, mounting, release.

## Origin

The psychology and neuroscience of threat, anticipation, and stress, wired as a composite over three engines.

## Restrictions

What the composite refuses to model, and to whom it delegates.

- **Emotion**: The composite models the being-under-threat before the outcome, not the emotion the outcome brings. The felt fear once the threat is present, and the flood of relief once it lifts, are owned by the emotion engine; jeopardy holds the state while the outcome is still pending, and hands the feeling of its arrival or passing to emotion.
- **Grief**: The composite models the threat while it is still a threat, not the loss if it lands. When the harm actually arrives, the suspense ends into harm, and what that loss costs -- the mourning of what is gone -- is owned by the grief engine; jeopardy owns the jeopardy, not the aftermath of its worst case.
- **The atoms**: The composite adds no content its atoms already own. The baseline nervous-system filter is the apprehension engine's; the anticipatory hold on a possible negative outcome is the suspense engine's; the routing of a demand that exceeds resources into fight, flight, freeze, or fawn is the stress engine's. Jeopardy owns only the seams -- the state the three make together that none makes alone.

## Encoding

Source to constraint, per file.

- **[jeopardy](process_jeopardy.md)** (the root): A persona under a threat not yet fallen, met at once by disposition, anticipation, and response, the three in and out of step. Anchored by Lazarus & Folkman (threat appraisal and the response that follows).
- **[onset](process_onset.md)** (bridge): The threat lands on the baseline and opens the anticipation at a gain the disposition sets -- the same cue gripping a hyperreactive system and passing a hyporeactive one. Wires apprehension to suspense. Anchored by Gray (the threat-sensitive system and its standing gain).
- **[mounting](process_mounting.md)** (bridge): As the interval closes and the demand outruns resources, the anticipation tips into the stress response, and the two compound -- dread draining the reserve, depletion sharpening the dread. Wires suspense to stress. Anchored by LeDoux (defensive mobilization) and Lazarus & Folkman (demand exceeding resources).
- **[release](process_release.md)** (bridge): A reprieve discharges anticipation and response together, or none comes and the mobilization built for a burst grinds into chronic exhaustion. Wires suspense's reprieve to stress's stand-down. Anchored by Selye (adaptation to exhaustion when the stressor persists).
