# Process: Jeopardy

## Taxonomy

The experience of being under threat -- a composite over apprehension, suspense, and stress.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: jeopardy

## Initiated by

A persona faces a harm that has not yet fallen -- a danger closing in, a verdict pending, an ordeal ahead -- and the whole of them turns to meet it.
This is jeopardy: being under threat over time, before the outcome lands.
It is never one response but three at once -- the baseline the persona brings to it, the anticipatory hold on what might happen, and the routing of a demand that outstrips resources -- and the composite adds no fourth engine. It wires the seams the three will not cross alone: apprehension sets the gain, suspense holds the outcome, stress carries the load.

## Direction

Toward the outcome, held under load the whole way.
The persona is carried toward a resolution they cannot yet see, and the interval between now and then is not empty waiting but active meeting -- anticipating and responding at once, the body already spending against a blow that has not come.
The direction is fixed by the threat; what varies, and where the drama lives, is how the three atoms carry the persona there.

## Lever

The three running together over one threat, in and out of step.
The baseline tunes how sharply the threat registers; the anticipation holds it present while it is still only possible; the response fires when the demand exceeds what the persona has -- and each feeds the others. A hair-trigger baseline turns a small threat into acute dread; dread sustained too long drains the resources the response needs; a response that fires and finds nothing to fight leaves the dread with nowhere to go.
No single atom stages this: the state lives in the seams, where disposition, anticipation, and response amplify or undercut one another.

## Echo

The threat lands or lifts, and the persona is discharged or left running.
A reprieve releases the hold and the load together; harm arriving ends the suspense and hands what follows onward; and a threat that neither lands nor lifts holds the persona in a spend that has no end.
The state runs as [onset](process_onset.md), [mounting](process_mounting.md), or [release](process_release.md) -- the bridges by which the threat registers, the anticipation tips into response, and the whole discharges or grinds chronic. The felt fear once the threat is present, and the relief once it lifts, are the emotion engine's; the loss, if the harm actually arrives, is the grief engine's. This composite owns the state before then: the being-under-threat itself.
