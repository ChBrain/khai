# Process: Mounting

## Taxonomy

[Jeopardy](process_jeopardy.md), the bridge where the anticipation tips into the response and the two compound.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: jeopardy

## Initiated by

The interval [closes](@chbrain/khai-engine-suspense/process_closing.md): the outcome draws nearer, the hold tightens, and the demand of meeting the threat begins to outrun what the persona has to meet it with.
This is where the anticipatory hold stops being only a hold and becomes a call on the body -- where suspense hands off to the [stress](@chbrain/khai-engine-stress/process_stress.md) response.
The waiting is no longer passive; it costs, and the cost is now being paid.

## Direction

Toward the response engaging while the anticipation still runs -- the two compounding, not succeeding each other.
As the demand exceeds resources, the routing fires -- [fight](@chbrain/khai-engine-stress/process_fight.md), [flight](@chbrain/khai-engine-stress/process_flight.md), [freeze](@chbrain/khai-engine-stress/process_freeze.md), or [fawn](@chbrain/khai-engine-stress/process_fawn.md) -- but the threat has not landed, so the response fires into a future, not a present, and the dread goes on beneath it.
The direction is a tightening spiral: dread drains the resources the response draws on, and the depletion sharpens the dread.

## Lever

The feedback the atoms will not close.
Suspense stages the closing interval but not the body's giving-way under it; stress stages the demand-exceeds-resources routing but not the anticipation that is draining the resources before the demand even arrives.
Mounting is the coupling between them -- anticipation spending down the reserve, the thinned reserve tipping the routing early, the early routing feeding back as more to dread -- a compounding neither engine owns alone, and the reason a long threat exhausts a persona before it ever strikes.

## Echo

The persona is now both dreading and responding, each worsening the other, carried tight toward the outcome.
Which response the routing chose colors the whole state -- the freeze that cannot act on the fear, the fight that has no target yet, the flight with nowhere to run, the fawn that appeases a threat not yet present.
Whether the spiral is broken by the threat lifting or by the persona giving out is the [release](process_release.md) bridge's.
