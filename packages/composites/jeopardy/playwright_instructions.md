# Instructions: Jeopardy

How a Playwright wires the jeopardy composite into a play. You wire by linking the play's own content to the composite's files; the composite and its atoms stay untouched, they are canon. Installing the composite brings its three atoms -- apprehension, suspense, stress -- as dependencies, and the Roadie plumbs the composite's law into Instructions Knowledge on deploy. Authoring guidance, not runtime content, and it does not go on tour.

## Human

- The human defines the jeopardy terrain: the threat the persona faces, how far off and how uncertain its outcome is, and what the persona brings to it -- so the danger is real, the interval has length, and the disposition has a gain to set.

## Agent

- From the play's own content, a plot links the jeopardy bridge it stages (onset, mounting, or release) under its Tension. Cast the persona from the atoms -- their baseline from apprehension, their anticipation from suspense, their response from stress -- and let the bridge show the state: the threat landing sharply or dully by disposition, the dread tipping into the fight-flight-freeze-fawn response as the interval closes, the reprieve that discharges it or the grind when none comes. You link to the composite and its atoms; you never edit them.

## Collaboration

- The felt fear once the threat is present, and the relief once it lifts, are the emotion engine's; the loss, if the harm actually arrives, is the grief engine's. The baseline nervous-system filter is apprehension's, the anticipatory hold is suspense's, the demand-exceeds-resources routing is stress's. This composite supplies only the seams -- the being-under-threat the three atoms make together but none makes alone.

## Knowledge

- Jeopardy is a persona under a threat not yet fallen, met at once by three things: the baseline they bring (how sharply their nervous system answers a threat), the anticipation that holds the outcome present while it is only possible, and the response that fires when the demand exceeds their resources. Onset is the threat landing on the disposition and opening the dread at a gain the baseline sets. Mounting is the dread tipping into the stress response as the interval closes, the two compounding. Release is the reprieve that discharges both -- or its absence, which grinds the response chronic.

## System

- Do let the disposition set the gain: the same threat should grip a hair-trigger persona and pass a blunted one, so two characters in the same danger are in visibly different states.
- Do let the anticipation and the response compound, not alternate: a long threat drains the persona before it strikes, the dread spending the reserve the response will need.
- Do let a threat that never resolves turn chronic -- the mobilization built for a burst worn into a permanent weather -- and distinguish that grind from a threat survived and discharged.
- Do delegate the felt fear and relief to the emotion engine and the loss, if the harm lands, to grief. Do not edit the composite's or the atoms' files; wire only from the play's side.
