# Process: Onset

## Taxonomy

[Jeopardy](process_jeopardy.md), the bridge where the threat registers on the disposition and the anticipation opens.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: jeopardy

## Initiated by

A threat appears -- a cue of harm to come -- and lands on the baseline the persona brings to it.
This is the doorstep of jeopardy: the point where a signal in the world becomes a hold in the mind, and the [suspense](@chbrain/khai-engine-suspense/process_suspense.md) opens.
What opens, and how sharply, is not set by the threat alone but by the nervous system it lands on.

## Direction

From a cue to an anticipatory hold, at a gain the disposition sets.
The same signal opens acute dread in a [hyperreactive](@chbrain/khai-engine-apprehension/position_hyperreactive.md) system and barely registers in a [hyporeactive](@chbrain/khai-engine-apprehension/position_hyporeactive.md) one -- so onset is where two personas facing the identical threat begin already in different places, one gripped, one blithe.
The bridge is that setting-of-the-gain: the threat is the same, the opening is not.

## Lever

The baseline reactivity, tuning the anticipation it feeds.
Apprehension gives the standing filter -- how strongly this nervous system answers a threat cue -- and suspense gives the hold that filter opens, but neither stages the coupling: apprehension is a disposition, not an event, and suspense assumes a hold already open without owning where its intensity was set.
Onset is the coupling -- the disposition converted into a live anticipation pitched to its gain -- and it decides whether the persona has over-read a shadow or under-read a real blade.

## Echo

The anticipation is open, and pitched high or low by the baseline that received the threat.
A well-tuned onset reads the danger for what it is; a hair-trigger one is already spending against a threat that may be nothing; a blunted one leaves the persona walking calmly toward real harm.
From here the hold either resolves quickly or, as the interval closes, tips toward the response -- which is the [mounting](process_mounting.md) bridge's.
