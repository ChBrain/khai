# Process: Release

## Taxonomy

[Jeopardy](process_jeopardy.md), the bridge where the threat lifts and the state discharges, or does not and turns chronic.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: jeopardy

## Initiated by

The threat resolves, or refuses to.
A [reprieve](@chbrain/khai-engine-suspense/process_reprieve.md) arrives -- the danger passes, the verdict comes back clear, the interval closes on nothing -- and the hold can let go; or no reprieve comes, and the threat neither strikes nor departs, holding the persona in a spend with no end.
This is the exit of jeopardy, and it has two doors.

## Direction

Toward discharge, or toward chronicity.
Discharged: the reprieve releases the anticipation and the stress response together -- the held breath goes out, the mobilized body stands down, and the persona returns, spent but restored, to the baseline they came in on.
Chronic: with no release, the [mounting](process_mounting.md) does not end -- the stress response, built for a burst, is made to run long, and what was mobilization becomes grinding depletion, the dread worn smooth into a permanent weather.

## Lever

The reprieve that discharges both -- or its absence, which strands them.
Suspense owns the reprieve that ends the hold; stress owns the response that must eventually stand down or exhaust; but neither owns the joint discharge, nor the failure of it. A reprieve that releases the suspense but comes after the stress response has already run to exhaustion does not restore the persona; a stress response that stands down while the suspense still holds leaves the dread without a body behind it.
Release is the coupling of the two exits -- and the case the atoms cannot stage, where one lets go and the other cannot.

## Echo

The persona is discharged and restored, or held in a threat that never ends and pays for it in the long grind.
When the threat does land rather than lift, the suspense ends but into harm, and what the loss then costs is the grief engine's; when it lifts, the flood of relief that follows is the emotion engine's. This bridge owns the letting-go itself, or the failure of it -- the difference between a threat survived and a threat that becomes the persona's climate.
It closes the state jeopardy holds: the being-under-threat is over, one way or the other, or it has become the standing condition of a life.
