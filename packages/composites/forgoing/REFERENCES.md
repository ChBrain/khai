# Forgoing: Reference

## Line of Work

Forgoing as **composite**: what it costs to ask for help that only somebody marked would need. It is
not a third mechanism but a wiring of two: the **stigma** engine supplies the discrediting attribute
and the discounting it draws, and the **asking** engine supplies the request for discretionary help,
its reluctance, and the misestimate that runs one way. This composite adds no third engine. It reads
the one thing the two make together, which is a request whose price is paid in identity rather than in
imposition.

The warrant is accordingly integrative rather than primary: the sources below are the literature of
help forgone because seeking it would classify the seeker, where each atom's own REFERENCES carries the
primary science of its side. **Corrigan is the joining.** His argument is not that stigma is
unpleasant and separately that people do not seek care; it is that the second follows from the first
by a specific route he named **label avoidance** -- people forgo services in order not to acquire the
category the services are addressed to. That is a claim about the two engines together, and it is why
this composite exists.

This is the third composite to wire the stigma engine. The **membership** composite reads the mark
from the group's side; the **carrying** composite reads what concealing it costs, wiring stigma with
secret and shame; and this one reads what it does at the single moment a request would disclose it. An
atom carries a phenomenon, and a composite carries a question asked of it. The **asking** engine is
wired here for the first time, and the pairing is not a convenience: asking's largest term is the
request never made, which closes with no result and leaves no record, and that absence is precisely
the population the mark is then described without.

**The Price** is where the two atoms are actually in contact. Asking prices a request on the
imposition -- the interruption, the hour, the thing somebody would have to move -- and the asking
engine never has to price anything else. Here the request is also the disclosure, because the service
is defined by who it is for: joining the queue at the clinic, the debt line, the food bank, or the
programme says the thing before anybody has spoken. So the persona is comparing two quantities of
different kinds, a service that may or may not work against a fact about them that does not expire.

**The Avoidance** is Corrigan's own term and the composite's practical core. The persona is not
declining the help; they are declining the classification, and every sentence available to them --
it is not for me, my case is not that bad, I will manage -- is defensible and sometimes true. Because
the refusal is about the label, nothing done to the service reaches it. Shorter waits, better
provision, and a friendlier front desk change the offer and leave the price where it was, which is why
services are so regularly surprised by their own take-up.

**The Undercount** is the loop, and it is where the composite is seen for one thing. The unmade
requests leave no record, and the records that remain are of the cases that could not be concealed --
the crisis, the late presentation, the version severe enough that passing stopped being available. The
category is therefore described by its worst cases, that description is what the next persona weighs,
and the estimate is confirmed to each of them separately by evidence the others generated by staying
away.

Hence the **twist**, at the root. **The forgoing is what keeps the mark worth avoiding.** Every
persona's decision is individually sound, none of them is coordinating with any other, and together
they maintain the reputation each was responding to. Nobody is misinformed on purpose, and the
correction is not available, because the evidence that would supply it consists entirely of people who
did not come forward.

The composite is built so that nobody has to be behaving badly for it to run, and a play should keep
it that way. The help is good. The staff are decent and are not gatekeeping anybody. The persona has a
sound reason and keeps it. The gap stays open.

**On the evidence, stated plainly.** **Corrigan's chain is a model, and a well-argued one, supported
mostly by cross-sectional data.** Public stigma to self-stigma to label avoidance to non-treatment is
a route rather than a measurement, and the 2014 review he co-authored is candid that the causal links
are inferred. **Clement's systematic review is the best single evidence leg and it is a modest one**:
144 studies, a median association between stigma and help-seeking that is small to moderate, with
disclosure concerns and internalised stigma the components most consistently related -- which is what
the Price bridge reads -- and a literature that is very nearly all cross-sectional, so the direction is
not established. **Stigma is not the largest barrier and the composite does not claim it is.**
Structural barriers -- cost, availability, waiting, time off work, transport -- are large, are measured
better, and in several samples outrank stigma; a play that reads every unmade request as a stigma
effect will be wrong most of the time. **Schomerus and Angermeyer's finding is the uncomfortable one
and it is kept**: two decades in which public beliefs about causes improved substantially while desired
social distance did not, which means the mechanism is not simply what people believe, and it is a
direct problem for any account -- including the cheerful reading of this composite -- in which better
information dissolves the price. **The Undercount bridge is an inference, and it is the composite's
least evidenced and most important claim.** It follows from the two atoms plus the treatment-gap
literature; nobody has measured the loop, and it should be read as a structural argument of the same
kind as its atoms' twists rather than as a finding. **The evidence base is overwhelmingly mental
health.** The extension to debt advice, literacy, addiction, domestic abuse, HIV, and benefit
entitlement is plausible, is what makes this a khai engine rather than a clinical note, and is much
thinner than the mental-health case. What should not be taken from this composite is that people who
forgo help are behaving irrationally, that services are at fault, or that anti-stigma campaigns do not
work -- the evidence on campaigns is mixed and the composite takes no position beyond Schomerus's
specific result. The claim is narrow: the request is the disclosure, the classification is what gets
declined, and the declining is what supplies the reputation that priced it.

## Restrictions

What the composite refuses to model, and to whom it delegates.

- **The mark itself (stigma)**: The discrediting attribute, the discounting it draws, the bearer's
  position, and the work of passing are the stigma engine's, whole. This composite reads none of them
  again; it declares the engine and links its members.
- **The request itself (asking)**: The reluctance, the imposition, the misestimate that runs one way,
  the act and its form, and what granting does to the helper are the asking engine's, whole. The
  composite adds only what happens when the request is also a disclosure.
- **Holding a discrediting attribute (carrying)**: What it costs to keep an attribute unseen, the tell,
  and the keeping are the carrying composite's, which wires stigma with secret and shame. That
  composite reads the concealment; this one reads a single moment in which concealment and a need
  collide.
- **Belonging to a people (membership, social-identity, intergroup)**: Who counts as one of us, and
  the relations between groups, are those packages'. The persona here is not seeking membership; they
  are avoiding an assignment.
- **The condition the help is for (illness, addiction, debt, loneliness)**: What the persona actually
  has, and what living with it is like, are those engines'. The composite is indifferent to the
  content of the difficulty and reads only the price of naming it.
- **What the record becomes (measure, legibility, document)**: Scoring, the state's readable
  categories, and the file as an object are those engines'. The Undercount reads the absence of
  records, not their use.
- **Being properly heard by an institution (heeding, recognition)**: What a fair procedure buys, and
  what being seen as a person by an institution does, are those packages'. Those begin once the
  persona has presented; this composite ends where they do.
- **The provision itself (the world)**: Funding, waiting lists, eligibility rules, staffing, and
  outreach design are matters of policy and practice. They set the offer, and this composite reads the
  price, which is why improving the first does not move the second.
- **The refusal as authored symbol (the play's pitch)**: Help declined in order to **stand for** a
  theme -- the outstretched hand refused that mirrors a motif of pride -- is refused: that is authored
  key, the play's pitch. Forgoing sets what the asking would have established; the meaning read into it
  is the play's.

## Encoding

Source to constraint. Two atoms, one root, three bridges.

- **[forgoing](process_forgoing.md)** (the root): help that exists, is available, and is not asked
  for, read on what the request would establish, carrying the twist -- the forgoing is what keeps the
  mark worth avoiding. Anchored by Corrigan (2004) over
  [stigma](@chbrain/khai-engine-stigma/piece_stigma.md) and
  [asking](@chbrain/khai-engine-asking/process_asking.md).
- **[the Price](process_forgoing_price.md)**: the request that is itself the disclosure, where a
  chance is weighed against a permanence. Anchored by Clement et al. (2015) and Link & Phelan (2001).
- **[the Avoidance](process_forgoing_avoidance.md)**: the classification declined rather than the
  help, which is why improving the service does not reach the decision. Anchored by Corrigan (2004,
  2014).
- **[the Undercount](process_forgoing_undercount.md)**: the loop, where the composite is seen for one
  thing -- the visible population assembled by exclusion, and the estimate it confirms. Anchored by
  Thornicroft (2007) and Schomerus & Angermeyer (2012).
