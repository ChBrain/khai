# Instructions: Forgoing

How a Playwright wires the forgoing composite into a play. You wire by linking the play's own content to the composite's files; the files stay untouched, they are canon. The composite declares two atoms as dependencies and links their members by name -- you never copy an atom's reading into the play, you point at it. The Roadie plumbs the composite's law into Instructions Knowledge on deploy. Authoring guidance, not runtime content, and it does not go on tour.

## Human

- The human decides what the persona needs and what taking it would name them, and keeps the second decision off the page, because the composite is read on what the request would establish and nobody in the scene will say it. Make the help good and the staff decent: the composite does not need a gatekeeper and stops working if it gets one. And give the persona a sound reason for declining, then let them keep it -- they are not being obtuse.

## Agent

- From the play's own content, a persona links under Projection which bridge is running: whether they are pricing a request that would disclose, declining the category rather than the service, or reasoning from a picture the absent assembled. The link is audited rather than failed, because a persona can be in either atom without being in this arrangement. Read the composite on what the request would establish, never on what it would obtain. Keep it the joining: the mark and its discounting are stigma's, the request and its reluctance are asking's, the cost of concealment is the carrying composite's. You link, you never edit.

## Collaboration

- The discrediting attribute, the discounting it draws, the bearer, and passing are the stigma engine's, whole; the request, the reluctance, the misestimate, and what granting does are the asking engine's, whole; what concealing an attribute costs is the carrying composite's; who counts as one of us is membership's and social-identity's; the condition itself is illness's, addiction's, debt's, or loneliness's; what a record becomes is measure's and legibility's; what a fair hearing buys once the persona has presented is heeding's and recognition's. Funding, waiting lists, eligibility, and outreach design are policy and set the offer, not the price. Whether a production's refusal was about the label or about the need, and whether a take-up problem is a provision problem or a price problem, are the team's review.

## Knowledge

- Forgoing is what it costs to ask for help that only somebody marked would need. It is not a third mechanism but a wiring of two: the stigma engine supplies the discrediting attribute and the discounting it draws, and the asking engine supplies the request for discretionary help, its reluctance, and the misestimate that runs one way. It adds no third engine and reads the one thing the two make together -- a request whose price is paid in identity rather than in imposition. It wires on a persona at Projection and runs in three bridges. The Price is where the two atoms touch: asking prices a request on the imposition and never has to price anything else, but here the service is defined by who it is for, so joining the queue is the disclosure before anybody has spoken -- and the persona is weighing a service that may or may not work against a fact about them that does not expire. The Avoidance is the practical core: the persona declines the classification rather than the help, every sentence available to them is defensible and sometimes true, and because the refusal is about the label nothing done to the service reaches it, which is why shorter waits and a friendlier front desk change the offer and leave the price where it was. The Undercount is the loop: the unmade requests leave no record, the records that remain are the cases that could not be concealed, and the category is therefore described by its worst instances -- which is what the next persona weighs. The twist is at the root: the forgoing is what keeps the mark worth avoiding, since every persona's decision is individually sound, none of them is coordinating, and together they maintain the reputation each was responding to.

## System

- Do decide what taking the help would name the persona, and keep it unspoken. Nobody in the scene will say it, and it prices everything.
- Do make the help good. It works, it is available, and the people staffing it are decent -- staging a gatekeeper replaces the mechanism with an obstacle.
- Do give the persona a defensible reason and let it stand. It is not for me, my case is not that bad, I will manage: all available, all sometimes true, and none of them the reason.
- Do put the disclosure in the queue rather than in a conversation. Being seen waiting is the whole of it, and it happens before anybody speaks.
- Do let somebody improve the service and watch it fail. A shorter wait and a warmer front desk change the offer and leave the price untouched, which is the Avoidance working exactly as written.
- Do stage the trade honestly. A chance at help against a permanent fact, and the persona is right that those are not the same kind of thing.
- Do close on the undercount rather than the refusal. What the persona sees of the category is the cases that could not be hidden, and they are reasoning correctly from it.
- Do not model the mark itself (stigma), the request itself (asking), the cost of concealment (carrying), belonging (membership, social-identity), the condition (illness, addiction, debt, loneliness), the record (measure, legibility), or the hearing once they have presented (heeding, recognition). Do not make the refusal stand for a theme; that is authored key, the play's pitch. Do not edit the composite's or the atoms' files; wire only from the play's side.
