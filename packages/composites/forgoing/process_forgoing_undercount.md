# Process: Forgoing, the Undercount

## Taxonomy

[Forgoing](process_forgoing.md), what the forgoing does to the mark, and where the composite is seen for one thing.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: forgoing

## Initiated by

The people who avoided the category are not in it, and the category gets described without them.
The Undercount starts once asking's [unmade requests](@chbrain/khai-engine-asking/process_the_ask_unmade.md) accumulate: each closes with no result and leaves no record, and here the missing records are of a particular kind -- the mild, the early, the managed, and the recovered.

## Direction

Toward a category composed of its worst cases.
What stays visible is what could not be concealed: the crisis, the late presentation, the version severe enough that [passing](@chbrain/khai-engine-stigma/process_passing.md) stopped being available. That is what the public sees, what gets reported, and what the next persona weighs when pricing their own request.
So the reputation is accurate about the visible population and wrong about the actual one, and the error is nobody's doing.

## Lever

The truth of every individual case against the falsity of the picture they make together.
The carrying force is that nothing here is fabricated and nobody is being deceived: the severe cases are real, the accounts of them are honest, the services describing them are describing what walks through the door, and a persona reasoning from what they can observe is reasoning correctly from the evidence available; the resistance is that the observable set was assembled by exclusion, so the evidence that would correct the estimate consists entirely of people who declined to produce any, and there is no vantage -- not the persona's, not the service's, not the public's -- from which the shape of what is missing can be seen.
The Undercount is a self-confirming figure that nobody computed.

## Echo

A category that stays exactly as bad as it is said to be.
It is where the composite is seen for one thing, and it is the loop the two engines make: asking's unmade requests supply stigma with its evidence, and stigma prices asking's next request off it.
The twist is here: **the forgoing is what keeps the mark worth avoiding.** Each persona's decision is individually sound, none of them is coordinating with any other, and together they maintain the reputation each of them was responding to -- which is why a campaign arguing that the category is not so bad is arguing against evidence its own audience is generating.
The Undercount is dated continuously and is never taken.
