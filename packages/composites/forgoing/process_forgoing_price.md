# Process: Forgoing, the Price

## Taxonomy

[Forgoing](process_forgoing.md), what a request costs when making it names the mark.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: forgoing

## Initiated by

The persona works out what asking would involve, and the sum is not the ordinary one.
The Price starts where asking's [imposition](@chbrain/khai-engine-asking/process_the_imposition.md) -- the interruption, the hour, the thing somebody would have to move -- is joined by something the asking engine never has to price: that the request is itself a [discrediting](@chbrain/khai-engine-stigma/process_discrediting.md) disclosure.
There is no version of the request that leaves it out. The service is defined by who it is for.

## Direction

Toward a request that cannot be made without saying what it is about.
Most help can be asked for without disclosing anything: the lift to the airport says nothing about the persona. This help cannot. The clinic, the debt line, the food bank, the literacy class, the programme -- each is addressed to a category, so joining the queue is the disclosure, before anybody has spoken.
And it discloses to a particular set of people: a receptionist who lives locally, a manager who signs the form, a system that keeps a record.

## Lever

The size of what is on offer against the durability of what it costs.
The carrying force is that the trade is often plainly worth making and the persona knows it: the help is substantial, the disclosure runs to a small number of people who are bound to discretion, and a persona who refuses every service that requires naming a difficulty will be worse off across a life for it; the resistance is that the two sides of the trade are not the same kind of thing -- the help is a service that may or may not work, the disclosure is a fact about them that does not expire and cannot be recalled -- so what looks like a cost-benefit comparison is a chance being weighed against a permanence.
The Price is paid in identity and quoted in convenience.

## Echo

A request that has been read and not made.
It is the bridge where the two engines are actually in contact: what asking prices as a cost to the helper, stigma converts into a cost to the asker.
What it hands upward is not a decision but a preference -- the persona will now favour any route that does not require the naming, including routes that do not work.
The Price is dated from the moment the persona learned what the service is called.
