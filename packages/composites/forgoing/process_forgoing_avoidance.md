# Process: Forgoing, the Avoidance

## Taxonomy

[Forgoing](process_forgoing.md), the persona declining the category rather than the help.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: forgoing

## Initiated by

The persona does not decide against the help. They decide against being one of those.
The Avoidance starts where the [bearer](@chbrain/khai-engine-stigma/position_bearer.md) position is available and is refused: the attribute may well be present, and taking the name for it is a separate act with consequences of its own.

## Direction

Toward a refusal that is about the label and reads as being about the service.
The persona will say the help is not for them, that their case is not that bad, that they will manage, that these things are overdiagnosed -- and every one of those sentences is available, defensible, and sometimes true.
What is actually being declined is the classification, which is why nothing about improving the service reaches the decision. Better provision, shorter waits, and a friendlier front desk change the offer and leave the price exactly where it was.

## Lever

The reasonableness of not wanting the name against what the name was attached to.
The carrying force is that declining a category is a legitimate act and frequently the right one: categories are crude, they travel much further than the situation warrants, they attach permanently, and a persona who accepts every label offered to them has given away something real and hard to get back; the resistance is that the category and the help arrive bolted together, so there is no way to take the second without the first, and the refusal is recorded by everybody -- the persona included -- as a judgement about whether they needed it.
The Avoidance is a decision about a word, filed as a decision about a need.

## Echo

A need unmet by somebody who has considered the matter carefully.
It shows why provision alone does not close the gap it was built to close, and why services are so regularly surprised by their own take-up.
What it hands upward is an absence: the persona is not a non-attender in anybody's records, because they never appeared in them.
The Avoidance is dated from the first time the persona heard what people say about the category.
