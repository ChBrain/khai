# Remainder

Remainder as composite: what a persona's sense of the time they have left does to what they did with the time they had -- an open-ended future that tilts toward knowledge and expansion or a foreshortened one that tilts toward meaning and the present (the horizon), the measuring of a choice against the better outcome imaginable, in the forms of commission, inaction, and anticipation (the tally), and the closing that makes the tally bearable by making it unactionable, since a regret hurts in proportion to the opportunity still open to it (the closing) -- wired over the time-horizon and regret engines.

This engine is defined by its [manifest](package.json), which the canon renders as the WIRES card. The manifest is the single source of truth; this README is generated, not edited by hand.
