# Process: Remainder, the Closing

## Taxonomy

[Remainder](process_remainder.md), the door shutting, and where the two are seen for one thing.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: remainder

## Initiated by

The thing the persona regrets not doing has stopped being available.
The Closing starts wherever a [limited](@chbrain/khai-engine-time-horizon/position_limited.md) horizon has taken the opportunity out from under an [inaction](@chbrain/khai-engine-regret/process_inaction.md) regret -- the course not begun, the reconciliation not sought, the work not attempted -- and the entry stops being a demand and becomes a fact.
It is the face of the composite where something eases and nothing improved.

## Direction

Toward peace, arrived at by subtraction.
The Closing reads what has actually changed, which is not the persona and not the past: the tally is the same list it always was, every entry still accurate, and the only difference is that none of it can now be answered -- so the ache that was a call to act, going unanswered, has nothing left to call for.
It moves the persona out of a demand and into an account.

## Lever

The relief of a settled reckoning against what settled it.
The carrying force is that the relief is real and the persona is not deceiving themselves: a foreshortened horizon genuinely tilts them toward what already matters, toward the people they have rather than the ones they might meet, and a life read at that horizon is not a diminished reading but a differently weighted one; the resistance is that the same shortening is what closed the doors, so the peace and the loss are not two events but one -- and a persona given their future back, by a reprieve or a remission, gets the regrets back with it, at full weight, which is the test that shows what was doing the work.
The Closing rides the composite's keystone knife-edge: what makes a regret bearable is the impossibility of acting on it, so the same life costs more to a persona who still could.

## Echo

An account closed, by the closing of everything that could have amended it.
It shows the two engines complete -- a horizon that sets the price and a tally that is priced -- and carries beneath it the composite's keystone: the peace of a short horizon is bought with the possibility that was doing the hurting, so it cannot be had earlier and cannot be recommended, and a persona who arrived at it has not learned anything they could pass on.
This is why the Closing is the hardest of the three to stage and the only one worth closing on: the tally must not change, the persona must not become wiser, the relief must be genuine, and the audience must be able to see that what moved was the door -- and a play that stages it as acceptance earned has replaced the mechanism with a virtue.
The Closing is the note the whole composite closes on: what a persona could still have done is legible in what it costs them to remember.
The Closing is dated from the horizon and not from the life, and it runs backwards over all of it.
