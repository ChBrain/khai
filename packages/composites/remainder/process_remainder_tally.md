# Process: Remainder, the Tally

## Taxonomy

[Remainder](process_remainder.md), the roads not taken, counted.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: remainder

## Initiated by

A persona sets what happened beside what could have happened.
The Tally starts wherever [regret](@chbrain/khai-engine-regret/process_regret.md) is running -- over an act in its [commission](@chbrain/khai-engine-regret/process_commission.md) form, sharp and specific and about a thing done; over a road not taken in its [inaction](@chbrain/khai-engine-regret/process_inaction.md) form, milder and vaguer and about a life; and forward, in [anticipation](@chbrain/khai-engine-regret/process_anticipation.md), shaping a choice before it is made.
It is the face of the composite where the accounting is accurate.

## Direction

Toward a list, and a claim that it should have gone otherwise.
The Tally reads where the entries cluster, which is not where the losses were largest: regret concentrates where the opportunity was greatest and the door has not obviously shut -- education, work, the people one might have known -- so a persona's account of their own life is a map of what was available to them rather than of what went wrong.
It moves nothing, and asks to be acted on.

## Lever

The accuracy of the counting against what the counting is for.
The carrying force is that regret is not a malfunction but a signal with a use: it marks where a different choice would have paid, which is information worth having so long as another choice is coming -- and the anticipation form shows the system working forward, steering a persona away from what they would later mind; the resistance is that the signal has no way to switch itself off when the choices stop coming, so it keeps reporting on doors that closed decades ago at the same volume it reports on ones still open, and the persona has no dial for it.
The Tally spends the persona's peace to keep a record nobody asked for.

## Echo

A list of what might have been, all of it correct.
It is the bridge that supplies what the horizon acts on, and it supplies it honestly: nothing here is self-pity or distortion, and a play does not need the persona to be wrong.
What it hands upward is a set of entries whose weight is not fixed by their content -- and the fact that what settles the weight is not in this bridge.
The Tally is dated from every fork the persona still remembers, which is not the same as every fork there was.
