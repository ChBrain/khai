# Process: Remainder, the Horizon

## Taxonomy

[Remainder](process_remainder.md), how much time the persona takes themselves to have.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: remainder

## Initiated by

A persona has a sense of how much is left, and it is doing work they have not noticed.
The Horizon starts wherever [time horizon](@chbrain/khai-engine-time-horizon/position_time-horizon.md) is set -- [expansive](@chbrain/khai-engine-time-horizon/position_expansive.md), an open-ended future that tilts the persona toward knowledge, novelty, and expansion, or [limited](@chbrain/khai-engine-time-horizon/position_limited.md), a foreshortened one that tilts them toward emotional meaning, depth, and the present.
It is the face of the composite that is not about the past at all.

## Direction

Toward a reweighting of everything the persona wants.
The Horizon reads it as a perception rather than a fact, which is what makes it stageable: it tracks time-left as the persona reads it and not age, so a diagnosis, an ending, a leaving, or a reprieve moves it in the young exactly as in the old -- and a persona given back their future goes back to wanting what an open horizon wants.
It moves nothing in the world, and re-sorts everything the persona is aiming at.

## Lever

The usefulness of knowing how much time there is against the fact that nobody does.
The carrying force is that the reweighting is sound: a persona with a long future is right to invest in knowledge and new ties, and one with a short future is right to spend it on what already matters -- neither is a distortion, and the shift is a good allocation rule doing its job; the resistance is that the input is an estimate made from cues the persona does not audit -- a birthday, an illness, a leaving date, the age a parent reached -- so a rule that would be correct given the number is running on a number nobody has.
The Horizon spends nothing, and quietly sets the exchange rate for everything else.

## Echo

A future assumed, and every goal already sorted by it.
It is the bridge that installs the variable, before there is anything for it to act on.
What it hands upward is not a mood or a stage of life but a term -- one the next two bridges are read against, and one that can be moved inside a scene.
The Horizon is dated from whatever last told the persona how much time there was, which is rarely anything reliable.
