# Process: Remainder

## Taxonomy

What a persona's sense of the time they have left does to what they did with the time they had.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: remainder

## Initiated by

Somebody is going back over their life, and how much of it is left is doing the scoring.
Remainder starts wherever both are in play: a resting sense of how much time there is, reweighting every goal held, through [time horizon](@chbrain/khai-engine-time-horizon/position_time-horizon.md); and a choice measured against the better outcome that can be imagined, through [regret](@chbrain/khai-engine-regret/process_regret.md).
This composite adds no third engine. It reads the one thing the two make together, which is that the past is not a fixed quantity but one scored against a future.

## Direction

Toward a settled account, reached by losing the means of unsettling it.
The composite wires the two engines and reads their working in three shapes: [the Horizon](process_remainder_horizon.md), how much time the persona takes themselves to have; [the Tally](process_remainder_tally.md), the roads not taken; and [the Closing](process_remainder_closing.md), the twist -- the opportunity shutting, and the tally going quiet with it.
The three are not three ages of a life. They are one reckoning, and a variable that decides what the reckoning costs.

## Lever

What a persona did against how much they could still do about it.
The carrying force is that the tally is honest: the roads not taken were real roads, the persona is not exaggerating, and the account they give of their own life would survive being checked -- nothing in this composite requires anybody to be wrong about anything; the resistance is that the same tally is not the same weight twice, since a regret is a demand where something can still be done and a fact where nothing can, so the horizon is not a background condition on the reckoning but the term that sets its price.
Remainder is read on the horizon and never on the life, because the life is not what changed.

## Echo

The same history, costing a different amount.
Remainder does not close; it is a past being scored against a future, gathered into one reading -- a horizon, a tally, a closing.
It hands off what it is not: the missing of what is gone -- regret alongside nostalgia and loneliness -- is longing's, which wires this same regret engine as one of the ways a persona reaches backward where this runs it against the time left; a threat to existence and what a persona builds over it is post-traumatic-growth's, and mortality there is a confrontation rather than a clock; the savouring and the fading of what is good is lasting's; what a persona might yet become is selfhood's; the confidence manufactured by a hard choice is picking's; what a persona holds another to have coming is deserving's, and nothing here is owed to anybody; and a shortening life made to **stand for** a theme is authored key, the play's pitch, not the force.
The persona is dated from what they think they have left, and their history is re-priced every time that moves.
