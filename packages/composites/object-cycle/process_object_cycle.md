# Process: Object-Cycle

## Taxonomy

A composite reading over three engine atoms (no canon file of its own): the [wear](@chbrain/khai-engine-wear/process_wear.md), [decay](@chbrain/khai-engine-decay/process_decay.md), and [restoration](@chbrain/khai-engine-restoration/process_restoration.md) engines this package declares as dependencies.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: object-cycle

## Initiated by

An object lives a whole material life, and no single engine sees the whole of it.
[Wear](@chbrain/khai-engine-wear/process_wear.md) sees what use spends, [decay](@chbrain/khai-engine-decay/process_decay.md) what time takes, [restoration](@chbrain/khai-engine-restoration/process_restoration.md) what a hand mends; each answers for its own phase, and none sees the arc the three make across a thing's lifespan.
The composite starts where an object is read over time -- new, then used, then aged, then mended or not -- and the whole life becomes legible: not a phase, but the biography of a thing (Kopytoff's cultural biography, Appadurai's social life of things), read as a material arc rather than a career of status.

## Direction

Across the object's life, phase handing to phase.
The composite follows the wear of use and the decay of time as they draw the object down, and the mending that answers them or does not; it does not stop at the fresh object or the spent one.
Its direction is the whole span -- and at its turning point stands the question no single phase asks: as the thing wears and ages, what does its keeper's care make of it?

## Lever

What the object-cycle spends -- the assumption that an object's decline is simply its end.
Wear and decay are plainly loss; but the composite bites at the response, because a worn and aged thing can be mended and kept, let go and replaced, or prized for the very marks its life left on it -- and the word old hides which.
The composite holds the three phases together so that the object's fate cannot be read from its condition alone: the same worn thing is a candidate for the bin, the workbench, or the vitrine, and which it becomes is a matter of care, not of wear.

## Echo

The object carried through its whole life, and its fate read by what care made of it.
The atoms each run their own phase and cannot see the family: the [upkeep](process_object_cycle_upkeep.md) where care meets the degradation and keeps the thing in service, the [discard](process_object_cycle_discard.md) where care withdraws and the thing is used up and let go, and the [treasure](process_object_cycle_treasure.md) where the wear and decay themselves become the object's worth.
This composite owns the material arc of a thing and the fate its care decides; the object's standing worth to a persona is the heirloom and extended-self engines', its career of status is biography's, and the keeper's feeling for it is the persona's own.
