# Comic

The comic as composite: the mirth comedy is built to produce -- a violation of expectation (surprise) held in a play frame that keeps it benign (humor), the amusement that compound answers with (emotion), and the crossing it makes into laughter when the frame holds and the violation lands -- and the drama lives at the two edges where the crossing fails, taken too hard as offense and too soft as flatness.
