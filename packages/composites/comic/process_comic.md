# Process: Comic

## Taxonomy

A composite reading over three atom engines (no canon file of its own): the surprise, humor, and emotion engines this package declares as dependencies.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: comic

## Initiated by

A persona meeting a violation it is safe to enjoy.
The comic is not any one atom's state but their compound: the [surprise](@chbrain/khai-engine-surprise/process_surprise.md) engine holds the violated expectation -- the incongruity, the thing the persona's model said would not happen -- the [humor](@chbrain/khai-engine-humor/position_humor.md) engine holds the play stance through which the persona takes that violation as comedy rather than threat, and the [emotion](@chbrain/khai-engine-emotion/process_appraisal_joy.md) engine holds the felt charge the compound discharges as mirth. McGraw and Warren named the condition that answers this meeting: benign violation, a thing that is at once a wrong and okay -- a norm, an expectation, or a dignity transgressed, and simultaneously held safe enough to laugh at. The composite begins where a violation is met inside a frame that keeps it benign.

## Direction

From a violation met toward laughter, across the play frame.
The wrong is the [jolt](@chbrain/khai-engine-surprise/process_jolt.md) of an expectation broken; the benignness is the humor engine's stance that holds the break as play, in practical disengagement, not a real threat to be answered. Held with no frame, the violation is only offense. But held inside a frame that keeps it safe -- the fiction a fiction, the target un-harmed, the stakes suspended -- the incongruity resolves not into alarm but into mirth, and the persona laughs. The direction is one way and doubly gated: the frame must hold and the violation must land, or the crossing does not complete.

## Lever

Whether a production reads the comic in three, and holds both the frame and the transgression. A safe nothing is humor's frame over no violation, tame and unfunny; a real wound is the violation with no frame to make it benign. The comic is the violation (surprise) held in play (humor) and discharged as mirth (emotion), met from a safety that lets the wrong be enjoyed. The lever is to stage a genuine violation and a frame that genuinely keeps it benign, because that simultaneity is the whole condition of the comic pleasure and the thing a production most easily loses -- to one side or the other.

## Echo

The crossing completes, or it fails at one of its two joints. When the frame holds and the violation lands, the incongruity discharges as mirth and laughter, the comic release that carries -- in Frye's reading -- toward renewal and a restored society, and the atoms resume. When the frame collapses and the violation is taken as a real wrong, the persona is stung into [offense](position_comic_offense.md), a transgression with no remove to make it benign; when the violation never lands and the moment is wholly safe, the persona is left in [flatness](position_comic_flatness.md), a frame held over nothing incongruous. A production that stages only the completed laugh has cast an audience that is always amused and never offended or bored; the comic this composite is for is the one that can fail at either edge.
