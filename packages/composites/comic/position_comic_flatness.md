# Position: Comic, Flatness

## Taxonomy

[Comic](process_comic.md), the state where the violation never lands and the frame holds over nothing.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: comic

## Has

A play frame held over a moment with nothing incongruous in it. The [humor](@chbrain/khai-engine-humor/position_humor.md) engine's stance is set to take the thing as comedy, and the persona is ready and willing to laugh, but the [surprise](@chbrain/khai-engine-surprise/process_surprise.md) engine's expectation-break has not fired: nothing is actually transgressed, the setup pays off exactly as the model predicted, the edge is sanded to safety. This is the benignness holding with the violation failed, the too-soft edge of the comic where the frame waits over an absence, so the [emotion](@chbrain/khai-engine-emotion/process_emotion.md) that should have discharged as mirth never gathers, and the moment lands inert.

## Orders

Supply the missing violation, or let the moment pass. Flatness demands the one thing the safe frame has left out, a real incongruity: the persona must find the wrong the joke needs -- sharpen the edge, break a genuine expectation, risk a real transgression -- or accept that there is nothing here to laugh at and move on. The order is to answer an emptiness, so the persona strains for the funny that is not there or lets the tame moment die, and either way the crossing to laughter does not run.

## Loses

The mirth the comic alone can give, and the life of the moment with it. Where the completed crossing discharges a violation as laughter, flatness has no violation to discharge; the persona forfeits the benign-violation pleasure not to injury but to inertia, and is left with a frame and nothing to spend it on. It loses, too, the occasion itself: a joke so safe it transgresses nothing is not offensive but forgettable, and the room that might have laughed merely waits, the comic promised and never delivered.

## Drives

Toward a violation supplied, or the bit abandoned. The persona finds or sharpens the incongruity the frame was waiting for -- a real expectation broken -- and the comic reopens with something to convert; or no violation can be found and the bit is dropped as tame, the safe setup retired for want of a wrong to make it funny. A production stages the flatness as a persona willing to laugh at a moment that gives it nothing to laugh at, and reads the drama in whether the violation arrives or the comic falls flat. This is the twin of [offense](position_comic_offense.md): there the violation lands with no frame, here the frame holds over nothing.
