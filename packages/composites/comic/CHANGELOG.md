# @chbrain/khai-composite-comic

## 0.1.1

### Patch Changes

- 954802a: Add the comic composite (process: root + two bridge states) -- the deliberate twin of the `tragic` composite. Where the tragic reads a fall witnessed from a distance that holds and crossing into catharsis, the comic reads a violation held in a frame that keeps it benign and crossing into laughter. The gap analysis (Tier-3 #26) flagged that `tragic` existed but comedy had no counterpart.

  - **Root (process):** `comic` -- reads the mirth comedy is built to produce as a compound over three atoms it declares as dependencies: the `surprise` engine's violated expectation (the incongruity), the `humor` engine's play stance (the frame that keeps the violation benign), and the `emotion` engine's felt charge (the mirth discharged). The composite adds no fourth atom, only McGraw & Warren's **benign violation** -- a wrong that is simultaneously okay -- and its crossing into laughter.
  - **Bridge states (position, the two edges the crossing fails at):**
    - `comic, offense` -- the too-hard edge: the play frame collapses and the violation is taken as real, so it stings instead of amusing (violation firing, benignness failed).
    - `comic, flatness` -- the too-soft edge: the violation never lands and the frame holds over nothing incongruous, so it bores instead of amusing (benignness holding, violation failed).

  This is the first composite to structurally mirror an existing one edge-for-edge: the two bridge states are the benign-violation analogues of `tragic`'s distress (distance collapses) and spectacle (engagement never lands).

  **Scope note.** Each part of the compound stays with its atom (`surprise` the violated expectation, `humor` the play stance and its styles, `emotion` the felt charge); the humor _style_ a persona jokes in stays with `humor`; the dramaturgy that stages the comedy stays with the arc engines. The composite reads only the compound and its crossing, restating no atom -- every hard link resolves through a declared dependency.

  Warranted (LORE, integrative) on McGraw & Warren (2010, "Benign Violations" -- the comic condition and, by its two failures, the two edges), Morreall (_Taking Laughter Seriously_; _Comic Relief_ -- the incongruity met in a play frame), and Frye (_Anatomy of Criticism_ -- comedy as the movement toward renewal). Each atom's own REFERENCES carries the primary science of its part. Set at patch as the free level. No whitelist required -- the `comic` / `comic_offense` / `comic_flatness` stems are unique.
