# Position: Comic, Offense

## Taxonomy

[Comic](process_comic.md), the state where the play frame collapses and the violation is taken as real.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: comic

## Has

A violation at full pressure and no frame to keep it benign. The [surprise](@chbrain/khai-engine-surprise/process_surprise.md) engine's expectation-break has landed and registered as a genuine wrong -- a norm, a dignity, a person really transgressed -- but the [humor](@chbrain/khai-engine-humor/position_humor.md) engine's play stance has failed to hold it as play: the target is felt to be really harmed, the stakes really live, the fiction taken for fact. This is the violation firing with the benignness failed, the too-hard edge of the comic where the wrong arrives without the safety that would make it laughable, so the [emotion](@chbrain/khai-engine-emotion/process_appraisal_anger.md) that answers is not mirth but the sting of an affront.

## Orders

Answer the wrong, or refuse it. Offense demands the one thing the comic is meant to suspend, that a violation be taken seriously: the persona must treat the transgression as real -- take offense, defend the target, withdraw the goodwill the joke presumed -- because the frame that would have let it pass as play is gone. The order is to meet a wrong that has escaped its frame, so the persona bristles, objects, or goes cold, and either way the crossing to laughter does not run.

## Loses

The mirth the comic alone can give, and the goodwill with it. Where the completed crossing discharges the violation as laughter, offense only banks it as injury; the persona forfeits the benign-violation pleasure and is left with the wrong unconverted. It loses, too, the bond the shared laugh would have built: because the violation was taken as real, the one who offered it as a joke is read as having meant the harm, and the comic occasion that could have drawn the room together instead divides it.

## Drives

Toward a frame regained, or the joke disowned. The persona recovers the play stance -- is reassured the target is unharmed and the stakes suspended, and the violation reopens as benign -- or the frame cannot be regained and the joke is repudiated, called cruel or beyond the line, the goodwill withdrawn to keep the wrong from standing. A production stages the offense as a persona genuinely unable to hold the violation as play, and reads the drama in whether the frame returns or the comic is refused before it can complete. This is the twin of [flatness](position_comic_flatness.md): there the frame holds over nothing, here the violation lands with no frame.
