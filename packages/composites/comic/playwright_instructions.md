# Instructions: Comic

How a Playwright wires the comic composite into a play. You wire by linking the play's own content to the composite's files and, through them, to the three atom engines it depends on; every file stays untouched, they are canon. The Roadie plumbs the law into Instructions Knowledge on deploy. Authoring guidance, not runtime content, and it does not go on tour.

## Human

- The human sets the violation and the frame: what expectation, norm, or dignity is transgressed, and what holds it safe enough to be enjoyed rather than answered.

## Agent

- Cast the three parts from their atoms: the violation from the surprise engine (the expectation broken, the incongruity), the frame from the humor engine (the play stance that keeps the violation benign), and the felt charge from the emotion engine (the mirth the compound discharges). Then read the compound and its crossing: a violation met inside a frame that keeps it benign is the benign violation, and a benign violation discharged is laughter. Held with no frame it is offense, the violation taken as a real wrong; held over nothing incongruous it is flatness, a safe frame with nothing to convert. A persona stranded at either edge may link the bridge under Projection; the audit surfaces the ones left unnamed. You link to the composite and its atoms, you never edit them.

## Collaboration

- Each part's science is its atom's: surprise carries the violated expectation, humor the play stance and its styles, emotion the felt charge. The humor style a persona jokes in -- affiliative, self-enhancing, aggressive, self-defeating -- is the humor engine's directly; the dramaturgy that stages the comedy is the dramatic-arc and dramatic-situations engines'; the raw feeling-tone is the emotion engine's. Whether the production earns the comic -- lands a real violation held in a frame that keeps it benign, rather than collapsing into offense or into flatness -- is the team's review.

## Knowledge

- The comic is a compound, not one atom: a violation of expectation held in a play frame that keeps it benign, discharged as mirth -- McGraw and Warren's benign violation, a wrong that is at once okay. The crossing into laughter is doubly gated. The violation must be real -- a genuine expectation, norm, or dignity broken -- or the frame holds over nothing and the moment is flat, safe and unfunny. And the frame must hold -- the target unharmed, the stakes suspended, the fiction a fiction -- or the violation is taken as a real wrong and the moment offends. Only when both hold at once does the incongruity resolve not into alarm but into mirth, the comic release that carries -- in Frye's reading -- toward renewal. Read the production across the compound and its crossing, never in the violation alone, because a violation without the frame is only offense and a frame without the violation is only flatness.

## System

- Do cast all three parts before reading the comic; do stage a genuine violation and a frame that genuinely keeps it benign, because their simultaneity is the whole condition of the comic; do build scenes on offense and flatness as standing conditions, the two edges the crossing fails at.
- Do not read a humor style as the comic -- that is the humor engine; do not stage the dramaturgy of the comedy as the composite's work -- that is the arc engines; do not confuse the two failures -- offense is too much violation for the frame, flatness is too little violation for the frame; do not edit the composite's or the atoms' files; wire only from the play's side.
