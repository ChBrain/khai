# Comic: Reference

## Line of Work

The comic as **composite**: the mirth comedy is built to produce, read whole across the atoms it spans. McGraw and Warren named the condition comedy is built to satisfy, the benign violation, and this composite reads the three its atoms hold: the surprise engine's violated expectation (the incongruity, the thing the model said would not happen), the humor engine's play stance (the frame through which the violation is taken as comedy rather than threat), and the emotion engine's felt charge (the mirth the compound discharges). This package authors none of those readings again. It declares the three engines as dependencies, wires their members by hard package links, and adds only what no atom holds: the compound the meeting produces, a wrong that is at once okay, and the crossing that compound makes across the play frame into laughter, or fails to make at either edge.

The warrant is accordingly integrative, not primary. The comic emotion is not a fourth atom beside surprise, humor, and emotion; it is what those three compose when a violation is met inside a frame that keeps it benign, and the mirth that compound yields is the response the benign-violation account explains and the play theories of Morreall and the renewal reading of Frye set in their fuller frame. The sources below are the literature of that compound and its failure; each atom's own REFERENCES carries the primary science of its part. It is the deliberate twin of the tragic composite: where the tragic reads a fall witnessed from a distance that holds and crosses into catharsis, the comic reads a violation held in a frame that keeps it benign and crosses into laughter, each failing at the two edges where its gating condition breaks.

## Restrictions

What the composite refuses to model, and to whom it delegates.

- **Each part of the compound**: The violated expectation and its aftermath -- the alarm, the reorientation, the revision -- is the surprise engine's; the habitual stance toward comedy, the humor style through which the persona frames the joke, is the humor engine's; the felt affect the mirth belongs to is the emotion engine's. The composite restates none of them; a bridge links, it never re-authors.
- **The humor style as such**: Which relational posture a persona takes toward comedy -- affiliative, self-enhancing, aggressive, or self-defeating -- is the humor engine's directly; the composite reads the comic emotion those postures aim at producing, not the postures themselves. The one is how a persona jokes; the other is the mirth a joke, well-made, discharges.
- **The plot that stages the comedy**: The shape of the comic arc from complication to resolution is the dramatic-arc engine's, the bounded set of comic collisions the dramatic-situations engine's. The composite reads the emotion the staged comedy produces, not the dramaturgy that stages it.
- **The felt charge along the way**: The raw affective register beneath the mirth -- and beneath the offense and the boredom of the two edges -- is the emotion engine's; the composite reads the comic compound and its crossing, not the moment-to-moment feeling-tone it rides on.

## Encoding

Source to constraint. A root (the comic crossing) with two bridge states, each an edge where the crossing fails, wired by hard package links into the atoms it spans.

- **[comic](process_comic.md)** (the root): a violation met (surprise) and held in play (humor) and discharged as mirth (emotion), the benign violation that compound answers with, and the crossing that compound makes into laughter when the play frame holds and the violation lands. Anchored by McGraw & Warren (the benign-violation condition) and Frye (the comic movement toward renewal).
- **[comic, offense](position_comic_offense.md)** (bridge state, the too-hard edge): the play frame collapses and the violation is taken as real, so the wrong arrives without the safety that would make it benign and the crossing does not run. Anchored by McGraw & Warren (the violation firing with the benignness failed) and Morreall (the play frame that fails to hold) over the surprise, humor, and emotion members it links.
- **[comic, flatness](position_comic_flatness.md)** (bridge state, the too-soft edge): the violation never lands and the frame holds over nothing incongruous, so no mirth gathers. Anchored by McGraw & Warren (the benignness holding with the violation failed) over the humor, surprise, and emotion members it links.
