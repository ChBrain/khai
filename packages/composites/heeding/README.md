# Heeding

Heeding as composite: what a persona gives a rule they did not agree with, and what buys it -- the encounter with the machinery that applies the rule, read for whether they got voice, a neutral decider, ordinary respect, and evident good faith, and returning a settled sense of where they stand (the hearing); the compliance, acceptance, and cooperation that reading purchases, extended past the case and running on how they were treated rather than on what they were given (the deference); and the question held as a moral mandate, on which the persona judges the procedure by its answer instead of the answer by its procedure, so fairness stops buying anything and a scrupulous process reaching the wrong result is worse than a rigged one (the mandate) -- wired over the law, recognition, and moral-conviction engines.
