# Instructions: Heeding

How a Playwright wires the heeding composite into a play. You wire by linking the play's own content to the composite's files and, through them, to its atoms; the engine files stay untouched, they are canon. The Roadie plumbs the composite's law into Instructions Knowledge on deploy. Authoring guidance, not runtime content, and it does not go on tour.

## Human

- The human sets what the persona was given and, separately, how they were treated -- and keeps the two apart, because the composite only works if they can come apart. Decide before the scene whether the question at issue is a preference for this persona or a mandate, since that decides whether any of the rest of the machinery can operate on them at all.

## Agent

- From the play's own content, a persona links under Projection the hearing they were given and whether the question is a mandate for them. Read the hearing on four things and nothing else: whether they got to say their piece to somebody who might be moved, whether the decider had no stake, whether they were treated with ordinary courtesy, and whether the decider looked to be trying to do right by them. Then read what it bought. Keep it the purchase: what makes a rule a rule is law's, the claim and encounter sequence is recognition's, the mandate stamp is moral-conviction's. You link to the composite and its atoms, you never edit them.

## Collaboration

- What makes a rule bind, the eight failings, and the gap between declared and applied are the law engine's; the claim, encounter, and standing are recognition's; the mandate stamp and its two faces are moral-conviction's. Obeying a command against one's conscience is obedience's, and this persona is not being commanded. The person made by a lifetime of institutional power is subjection's. What somebody has coming is deserving's, condemnation's, and moral-account's. Whether a production's procedure is genuinely fair, and whether a persona genuinely holds the question as a mandate rather than as a strong preference, are the team's review.

## Knowledge

- Heeding is what a persona gives a rule they did not agree with, and what buys it. It wires on a persona at Projection over three engines: law supplies what is reaching them, met at the gap rather than as a text; recognition supplies the encounter that answers their claim and returns a standing; moral-conviction supplies whether the question is a preference or a mandate. Three bridges carry it. The hearing is the encounter read for voice, a neutral decider, ordinary respect, and evident good faith -- none of them about the outcome, all of them about the persona -- and what it returns is a settled sense of where they stand, since how an institution's officials handle somebody when something is at stake is the best evidence available about what they are worth to it. The deference is what that reading buys: obedience unwatched, acceptance of a decision the persona still thinks wrong, cooperation the next time -- tracking the hearing far more than the ruling and far more still than the severity of what was at stake, which makes it the cheapest legitimacy an institution has and a balance it can spend without replenishing. The mandate is the twist: where the persona holds the question as objective and owing nothing to any authority, the mechanism reverses and they judge the procedure by the answer rather than the answer by the procedure -- so fairness buys nothing, and it runs the other way too, since a vindicated mandate will accept the result from a process the persona would otherwise call rigged. The keystone is that a fair hearing buys compliance only on the questions a persona does not already consider settled, and on the ones they do the fairness makes the loss worse, because a scrupulous process reaching the wrong answer removes the last available explanation for it.

## System

- Do stage the treatment rather than the ruling, and let them come apart: the composite reads nothing at all if the persona who was heard is also the persona who won.
- Do read the hearing on the four elements and resist adding a fifth; a persona who was given voice by somebody with no stake, treated decently, and evidently dealt with in good faith has been paid, and that is the whole of the currency.
- Do let the deference extend past the case -- unwatched compliance, cooperation next time -- and let it decay when hearings stop being given, since that is where an institution's balance actually runs down.
- Do keep the fairness real rather than performed where a refusal has to mean something: a procedure staged as fair and secretly rigged is a different play, and the mandate bridge needs the fairness genuine to land.
- Do not stage a mandate refusal as stubbornness or as a fault. The persona is refusing the only instrument a plural society has for deciding between people who are each certain, and refusing it in the name of the certainty that made the instrument necessary -- that is a position, and playing it as a flaw replaces the mechanism with a character note.
- Do not model what makes a rule a rule (law), the acknowledgment sequence itself (recognition), the mandate stamp (moral-conviction), obedience to a command (obedience), the person made by institutional power (subjection), or what somebody has coming (deserving). Do not make the refusal stand for integrity or the compliance for docility; that is authored key, the play's pitch. Do not edit the engine files; wire only from the play's side.
