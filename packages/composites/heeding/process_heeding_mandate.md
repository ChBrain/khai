# Process: Heeding, the Mandate

## Taxonomy

[Heeding](process_heeding.md), the question fairness cannot buy, and where the three are seen for one thing.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: heeding

## Initiated by

The thing being decided is one the persona already knows the answer to.
The Mandate starts wherever the issue carries [moral conviction](@chbrain/khai-engine-moral-conviction/position_moral_conviction.md) -- held as an objective fact rather than a preference, and as owing nothing to any rule or authority -- so that the [intolerance](@chbrain/khai-engine-moral-conviction/position_intolerance.md) face is already turned toward the procedure before the procedure has done anything.
It is the face of the composite where the mechanism that has been working stops, and does not degrade gracefully.

## Direction

Toward a persona who cannot be reconciled by anything the process can offer.
The Mandate reads the reversal: where the question is a mandate, the persona judges the procedure by the answer it reached rather than the answer by the procedure that reached it, so voice, neutrality, courtesy, and evident good faith stop predicting acceptance and the outcome takes over entirely.
And it runs the other way too -- a persona who gets the answer their mandate requires will accept it from a process they would otherwise call rigged, which is the same finding wearing its other face.

## Lever

The fairness of a hearing against the certainty of an answer.
The carrying force is that the persona is not being unreasonable by their own lights: a mandate is held as a fact about the world, and no procedure, however scrupulous, has standing to determine a fact -- a fair trial that acquits someone the persona knows to be guilty has not settled the question, it has failed at it; the resistance is that this is precisely what a procedure is for, so the persona is refusing the only instrument a plural society has for deciding between people who are each certain, and refusing it in the name of the certainty that made the instrument necessary.
The Mandate rides the composite's keystone knife-edge: a fair hearing buys compliance only on the questions a persona does not already consider settled, and on the ones they do, the fairness makes the loss worse rather than better.

## Echo

A verdict that cannot be appealed and cannot be accepted.
It shows the three engines complete -- a rule applied, an encounter that returned a standing, and a mandate that will not take standing as payment -- and carries beneath it the composite's keystone: fairness is not a universal solvent but a currency with a boundary, and the boundary is drawn by the persona rather than by the institution.
This is why the Mandate is the hardest of the three to stage and the only one worth closing on: the procedure must be genuinely, visibly fair, the persona must be genuinely sincere, neither must be a hypocrite, and the audience must be able to see that the fairness is what closed the last door -- because a scrupulous process that reaches the wrong answer has removed the persona's last available explanation for the loss, and left them with nothing to conclude except that the institution is not merely mistaken but complicit.
A play that stages the refusal as stubbornness has replaced the mechanism with a fault.
The Mandate is dated from the conviction and not from the hearing, and it was in place before anybody sat down.
