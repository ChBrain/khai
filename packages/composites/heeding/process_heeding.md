# Process: Heeding

## Taxonomy

What a persona gives a rule they did not agree with, and what buys it.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: heeding

## Initiated by

Somebody has been ruled against, and is deciding what to do about it.
Heeding starts wherever all three are in play: a rule reaching a persona through the machinery that actually applies it, through [law](@chbrain/khai-engine-law/position_law.md); a bid for acknowledgment answered by somebody with the standing to answer it, through [recognition](@chbrain/khai-engine-recognition/process_recognition.md); and whether the question at issue is held as a preference or as a mandate, through [moral conviction](@chbrain/khai-engine-moral-conviction/position_moral_conviction.md).
This composite adds no fourth engine. It reads the one thing the three make together, which is that the treatment is doing more work than the ruling.

## Direction

Toward compliance a persona was not compelled into, or the exact point where nothing will produce it.
The composite wires the three and reads their working in three shapes: [the Hearing](process_heeding_hearing.md), the encounter and what it returns about where the persona stands; [the Deference](process_heeding_deference.md), what the hearing buys and how little the outcome has to do with it; and [the Mandate](process_heeding_mandate.md), the twist -- the questions on which fairness stops purchasing anything.
The three are not stages of a dispute. They are one transaction, and a variable that decides whether it can be made at all.

## Lever

How a persona was treated against what they were given.
The carrying force is that fair treatment is genuinely doing the work and not standing in for a better outcome: across obedience to the law, acceptance of an adverse decision, and willingness to cooperate afterward, what a persona was handed matters far less than whether they were heard by somebody neutral who treated them as though they counted -- and the effect holds when they lost; the resistance is that this is exactly what makes it purchasable, since a procedure can be made to feel fair without being made to decide differently, and a persona who leaves satisfied has been paid in standing rather than in the thing they came for.
Heeding is read on the encounter and never on the ruling, because the ruling is not what moved them.

## Echo

A rule obeyed by somebody who thinks it is wrong, and a rule not obeyed by somebody who has run out of reasons to.
Heeding does not close; it is a compliance being purchased or found to be unpurchasable, gathered into one reading -- a hearing, a deference, a mandate.
It hands off what it is not: what makes a rule a rule, and the gap between the rule declared and the rule applied, is law's; the acknowledgment of who a persona is or what they have done, in its claim, encounter, and standing phases, is recognition's; the stamp that turns a position from a preference into an imperative is moral-conviction's; obeying the command of an authority against one's own conscience is obedience's, and this persona is not being commanded; the person made by institutional power is subjection's; the costly sanctioning of a defector without any office is altruistic-punishment's; what a persona holds another to have coming is deserving's; and a trial made to **stand for** a theme is authored key, the play's pitch, not the force.
The persona is dated from the encounter, and everything they do afterward is priced by how it went.
