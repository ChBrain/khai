# Process: Heeding, the Deference

## Taxonomy

[Heeding](process_heeding.md), what the hearing buys.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: heeding

## Initiated by

The persona has been answered, and is doing something about it or not.
The Deference starts wherever the [standing](@chbrain/khai-engine-recognition/process_standing.md) returned by the encounter has settled, and shows in which stance toward legality the persona takes up next: [with the law](@chbrain/khai-engine-law/position_with_the_law.md), engaging the machinery as a thing that can be worked; or [against the law](@chbrain/khai-engine-law/position_against_the_law.md), routing around it and dealing with officials as weather.
It is the face of the composite where the purchase is made or refused.

## Direction

Toward compliance nobody had to compel, extended past the case that produced it.
The Deference reads a persona doing what the ruling requires without being watched, accepting a decision they still think wrong, and cooperating the next time the institution needs something from them -- and it reads all of that as running on the hearing rather than on the ruling.
The strangeness is the size of the gap: whether the persona won matters much less than whether they were heard, and the severity of what they face matters less still, so the lever an institution reaches for first is close to the weakest one it has.

## Lever

Compliance freely given against compliance that has to be produced.
The carrying force is that this is the cheapest legitimacy there is and it is not a trick: an institution that gives voice, decides neutrally, treats people with respect, and is visibly trying to do right by them earns deference it does not have to enforce, and the alternative -- enforcing everything against everybody -- is not available at any price; the resistance is that deference bought this way is deference to the institution rather than agreement with the decision, so it can be spent, and an institution that keeps spending it while deciding against the same personas is drawing on a balance it is not replenishing.
The Deference is what makes a legal order possible and what makes it exploitable, and there is no version that is only one.

## Echo

A rule kept by somebody who thinks it is wrong.
It shows the two engines complete -- an encounter that returned a standing, and a stance toward legality taken up from it -- and it is the composite's ordinary case, the one that holds almost everywhere almost all of the time.
What it cannot do is survive contact with a question the persona has already settled, which is what the last bridge is for.
The Deference is dated from the hearing and not from the ruling, and it decays at the rate the hearings do.
