# Process: Heeding, the Hearing

## Taxonomy

[Heeding](process_heeding.md), the encounter with the machinery, and what it returns.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: heeding

## Initiated by

A persona has come in front of somebody who can decide about them.
The Hearing starts wherever law stops being a text and becomes an encounter -- at [the gap](@chbrain/khai-engine-law/position_gap_law.md), where the rule as declared meets the rule as applied, usually through the [discretion](@chbrain/khai-engine-law/position_discretion.md) of whoever is at the counter -- and where the persona's [claim](@chbrain/khai-engine-recognition/process_claim.md) meets an [encounter](@chbrain/khai-engine-recognition/process_encounter.md) with somebody holding the standing to answer it.
It is the face of the composite that has not reached the decision yet.

## Direction

Toward a reading of where the persona stands, taken from how the process handled them.
The Hearing reads four things and nothing else: whether the persona got to say their piece to somebody who might be moved by it, whether the decider seemed to have no dog in the fight, whether they were treated with ordinary courtesy and dignity, and whether the decider appeared to be trying to do right by them.
None of the four is about the outcome, all four are about the persona, and it is the second reading -- what this encounter says about their [standing](@chbrain/khai-engine-recognition/process_standing.md) -- that the rest of the composite runs on.

## Lever

The information a procedure carries against the information it was built to deliver.
The carrying force is that the reading is sound: a persona has no independent way to learn what they are worth to an institution, and how its officials handle them when something is at stake is the best evidence available -- so treating the encounter as a message about standing is not a confusion, it is the correct use of the only data there is; the resistance is that the message and the decision come apart cleanly, since an institution can supply voice, neutrality, respect, and evident good faith at low cost while conceding nothing, and a persona reading the message accurately will still misread what it predicts.
The Hearing spends the institution very little, and it is the whole of what the persona will remember.

## Echo

A persona who has been placed, before they have been answered.
It is the bridge that installs the variable, before there is anything for it to buy.
What it hands upward is not a verdict and not a mood but a settled sense of standing -- and the deference the next bridge reads is bought with that, not with the ruling.
The Hearing is dated from the encounter itself, and a persona remembers how they were treated long after they have stopped being able to state what was decided.
