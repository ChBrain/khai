# Process: Appetite

## Taxonomy

What a persona must have, and how the must was made.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: appetite

## Initiated by

A persona cannot do without something, and the question is not what it is but where the cannot came from.
Appetite starts wherever a requirement is pressing -- the plural lacks a persona arrives carrying, ordered by prepotency, through [needs](@chbrain/khai-engine-needs/process_needs.md); the demand taken in from outside at whatever depth it reached, through [motivation](@chbrain/khai-engine-motivation/process_motivation.md); and the currency the organism came to count on by being paid it, through [addiction](@chbrain/khai-engine-addiction/process_addiction.md).
This composite adds no fourth engine; it reads what the three make together: one kind of pressure with three provenances, and a persona who cannot feel which is which.

## Direction

Toward the whole of what a persona cannot do without, read by where each must came from.
The composite wires the three engines and reads their working in three shapes: [the Given](process_appetite_given.md), the requirement the organism arrived with; [the Taken](process_appetite_taken.md), the demand internalised from outside; and [the Made](process_appetite_made.md), the twist -- the requirement manufactured by the pursuing.
The three are not degrees of legitimacy. They are three accounts of how a pressure got there, and the persona under it has the same experience in all three.

## Lever

The reliability of a requirement against the fact that it can be installed.
The carrying force is that musts are not a defect in a person -- a self that wanted everything equally would move toward nothing, so the ordering of requirements is what makes conduct possible, and a demand taken in deeply enough becomes a genuine reason rather than a borrowed one; the resistance is that the same machinery accepts a requirement that was never anybody's: the pursuit installs its own necessity, the baseline resets under it, and what the persona reports is not a preference they are indulging but a thing they need.
Appetite is read wherever a persona is granted what they cannot do without: not a self choosing among wants but one already answering to requirements, most of which they did not decide to have.

## Echo

The persona revealed by what they cannot go without.
Appetite does not close; it is the requiring running, gathering the three engines into one reading -- a lack arrived with, a demand taken in, a need manufactured.
It hands off what it is not: the installing of a response until it runs without consulting why is the ingraining composite's, and the discount by which an outcome is weighed against its distance is the reward engine's inside it; the standing character a persona brings to any pursuit is the striving composite's; the crossing from an intention to an act begun is the volition composite's; the momentary override of an impulse is the self-control engine's; the aversive state of an unengaged mind seeking a target the situation will not supply is the boredom engine's; the felt lack of a resource that captures the mind and taxes it is the scarcity engine's; the prolonging of a good already arrived is the lasting composite's; and the must made to stand for a story is authored key, the play's pitch, not the force.
The persona is dated from the pressing, and read as a self whose requirements have histories they cannot recover from inside.
