# Appetite: Reference

## Line of Work

Appetite as **composite**: what a persona must have, and how the must was made. It is not a
fourth mechanism but a wiring of three: the **needs** engine supplies the requirement the organism
arrives with, ordered by prepotency and split between lacks that quiet when filled and growths
that do not; the **motivation** engine supplies the demand taken in from outside, at a depth that
decides what kind of effort it yields; and the **addiction** engine supplies the requirement
manufactured by the pursuing itself. This composite adds no fourth engine; it reads the seam the
three make -- three provenances for one kind of pressure -- and stages that seam into three
bridges, closing on the discovery that the provenance is exactly what the persona cannot feel.

The domain does not model any single provenance -- prepotency, deficiency, and actualization are
the needs engine's; intrinsic, identified, introjected, external, and amotivated are motivation's;
craving, relief, acclaim, and closeness are addiction's; and one alone is a process, not the
shape. Nor is it the installing of a response until it runs without consulting why, which is the
ingraining composite's; nor the standing character a persona brings to any pursuit, which is the
striving composite's; nor the crossing from an intention to an act begun, which is the volition
composite's. It owns the provenance: not what a persona wants but what they cannot do without,
and where the cannot came from.

The spine is that three literatures each describe a requirement and disagree only about where it
originates. Maslow supplied the given: needs ordered by prepotency, the more basic pressing harder
when unmet, and -- the distinction the composite leans on hardest -- deficiency needs driven by
lack and stilled by satisfaction set against being-needs that grow with feeding and do not
satiate; Tay and Diener supplied the empirical correction that keeps the ordering soft rather than
a staircase, the needs universal and predictive of well-being but operating somewhat
independently. Deci and Ryan supplied the taken: organismic integration, the continuum along which
an externally regulated demand becomes introjected, then identified, then integrated, with the
introjected stop the diagnostic one -- a should fully inside the persona and not yet theirs,
yielding effort that is real, brittle, and contingent on the pressure. Robinson and Berridge
supplied the made, and with it the keystone: incentive sensitization, in which the dopaminergic
"wanting" system sensitizes with repetition while "liking" does not, so the pull grows as the
pleasure fades and the persona is drawn hardest to what satisfies least; Koob and Volkow supplied
the allostatic half, the reward baseline resetting downward so the behaviour comes to be sought to
escape a deficit that the behaviour itself created. Read together they say something none says
alone: a requirement built by its own answering is a real state of the organism, reported
accurately by the persona under it, and indistinguishable from within from one they were born
with. The three bridges are those three provenances: the given, the taken, and the made.

wired as a composite over the needs, motivation, and addiction engines.

## Restrictions

What the composite refuses to model, and to whom it delegates.

- **The single provenances (the atoms)**: The composite wires the three but owns none. Prepotency,
  deficiency, and actualization are the needs engine's; intrinsic, identified, introjected,
  external, and amotivated are motivation's; craving, relief, acclaim, and closeness are
  addiction's. Appetite reads only the shape the three make; one provenance alone is a process,
  not the whole of what a persona must have.
- **The installing of a response (ingraining)**: How a cue comes to predict, how an outcome is
  weighed by its distance, and how repetition grooves an act into its context are the ingraining
  composite's. Appetite reads where a requirement came from; ingraining reads how a response to it
  gets laid down until it no longer consults why.
- **The character of the pursuit (striving)**: The achievement motive, the regulatory focus, and
  the grit a persona brings to any goal are the striving composite's. Appetite is what must be
  had, not what the persona is like in going after it.
- **The crossing into action (volition)**: The passage from an intention to an act begun is the
  volition composite's, and the momentary override of an impulse is the self-control engine's.
  Appetite supplies the pressure those answer to.
- **The unengaged mind (boredom)**: The aversive state of a mind whose mobilized attention seeks a
  target the situation will not supply is the boredom engine's -- a deficit of stimulation rather
  than a requirement with a provenance.
- **The mind under shortage (scarcity)**: Tunnelling, bandwidth, and the trap -- what a felt lack
  of a resource does to the capacity to think and plan -- are the scarcity engine's. Appetite
  reads the requirement; scarcity reads the cognitive cost of carrying it unmet.
- **What becomes of a good already arrived (lasting)**: The attention that enlarges a pleasure,
  the reception the telling meets, and the baseline that reclaims it are the lasting composite's.
  Appetite stops at the pressure toward the thing.
- **The must as authored symbol (the play's pitch)**: A requirement made to **stand for** a story
  -- the hunger that mirrors a theme of emptiness, the addiction that signals a fall -- is refused:
  that is authored key, the play's pitch, not the force on the persona. Appetite sets the
  provenance; the meaning read into it is the play's.

## Encoding

Source to constraint. A composite root over three engine atoms it declares as dependencies, with
three bridges, the provenances of a must.

- **[appetite](process_appetite.md)** (the root): what a persona must have and how the must was
  made -- one kind of pressure with three histories, and a persona who cannot feel which is which.
  Anchored by Maslow, Deci & Ryan, and Robinson & Berridge read together.
- **[the given](process_appetite_given.md)** (bridge): the requirements the organism arrives
  carrying, claimed by prepotency and released as deficits close -- and split between the lack
  spent by its satisfaction and the growth deepened by it. Anchored by Maslow (1943, 1962) and Tay
  & Diener (2011) on the soft ordering.
- **[the taken](process_appetite_taken.md)** (bridge): the demand from outside let in to a depth
  that decides the quality of the effort, with the introjected stop the diagnostic case -- fully
  inside, not yet the persona's, and brittle for it. Anchored by Deci & Ryan (1985; 2000) and Deci
  (1971) on overjustification.
- **[the made](process_appetite_made.md)** (bridge): the requirement manufactured by the pursuing,
  the baseline reset so the bonus becomes the level, the wanting sensitizing as the liking
  flattens. Anchored by Robinson & Berridge (1993) and Koob & Volkow (2016); the composite's twist
  and keystone, that from inside a made must presses exactly like a given one -- with the honest
  qualification the bridge carries, that satisfaction separates a given deficiency from a made
  compulsion and leaves growth on the same side as capture, the two told apart only by which
  direction they open.
