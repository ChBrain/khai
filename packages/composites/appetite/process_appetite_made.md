# Process: Appetite, the Made

## Taxonomy

[Appetite](process_appetite.md), the provenance at which the pursuing built the requirement, and the three are seen for what they are.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: appetite

## Initiated by

A persona needs something the organism did not need before it started getting it.
The Made starts wherever [addiction](@chbrain/khai-engine-addiction/process_addiction.md) has done its work -- the [craving](@chbrain/khai-engine-addiction/process_craving.md) in which a cue fires the pursuit before anything has arrived and the charge lives in the closing rather than the having, the [relief](@chbrain/khai-engine-addiction/process_relief.md) in which the baseline sinks until the release is needed simply to return to level, and the [acclaim](@chbrain/khai-engine-addiction/process_acclaim.md) and [closeness](@chbrain/khai-engine-addiction/process_closeness.md) that make standing and belonging into currencies the organism comes to count on.
It is the provenance nobody supplied: the requirement was manufactured by the answering of it.

## Direction

Toward the source again, at a cost the persona would not have paid at the start.
The Made reads the recalibration -- the payout that once arrived as a bonus and is now the level that must be reached to feel normal, the pull that no longer tracks how good the reward is but how bad its absence has become, the persona who can name the cost while paying it.
It moves the persona from moving toward a pleasure to moving away from a lack, and the lack is one the pursuing put there.

## Lever

The reality of the requirement against the fact that the pursuit is what made it real.
The carrying force is that this is not pretence and not weakness: the baseline genuinely resets, going without genuinely feels worse than it did before the persona ever started, and a made must is a real state of the organism in exactly the way a given one is -- which is why the persona reporting a need is reporting accurately; the resistance is that the requirement has no reason outside itself, so it does not answer to being met -- the wanting sensitizes as the liking flattens, and the persona is pulled hardest toward what pleases them least.
The Made rides the composite's keystone knife-edge: from inside, a must a persona made presses exactly like one they were born with -- and satisfaction is the only thing that tells them apart, since a given lack quiets when it is filled and a made one does not.

## Echo

The manufactured requirement, indistinguishable from the inside and unanswerable from within.
It shows the three provenances complete -- a lack arrived with, a demand let in, a need built by the pursuing -- and carries beneath it the composite's keystone: the organism has no test for where a must came from, which is why an addiction is honestly reported as a need and why an instruction to simply stop is addressed to the wrong category.
The test that does exist cuts awkwardly and is worth keeping honest: what deepens as it is fed is not only capture but growth, since an actualization need also widens with feeding, so satisfaction separates the given deficiency from the made compulsion and leaves becoming and capture on the same side, told apart only by which direction they open -- one widening the persona and one narrowing them.
The Made is the note the whole composite closes on: a persona can be given a need by nothing but their own answering of it, and will be right to call it one.
The Made is dated from the recalibration, the moment the bonus became the baseline.
