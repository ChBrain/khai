# Instructions: Appetite

How a Playwright wires the appetite composite into a play. You wire by linking the play's
own content to the composite's files; the files stay untouched, they are canon. The
composite installs the needs, motivation, and addiction engines as dependencies, and their
laws arrive with them. Authoring guidance, not runtime content, and it does not go on
tour.

## Human

- The human sets what the persona cannot do without, and where the cannot came from: a requirement they arrived carrying, a demand taken in from outside at whatever depth it reached, or a requirement the pursuing itself manufactured -- and, the question that governs the read, which provenance it is: the given, the taken, or the made.

## Agent

- From the play's own content, read a persona by what they cannot go without: link under Projection the provenance of the must that is pressing -- the given while a basic deficit is claiming the front (the prepotency that brings the low deficit forward, the lack that quiets when filled, the growth that widens when fed, from needs), the taken while a demand from outside is doing the pressing at the depth it reached (the act that is its own reason, the end genuinely owned, the should swallowed but not owned, the hand still outside, the pull that never formed, from motivation), the made while a pursuit has installed a requirement the organism did not have (the cue that fires the seeking before anything arrives, the baseline sunk until release only returns the persona to level, the standing and the belonging become currencies, from addiction). Play the must as a real state of the persona in all three cases, present in what they cannot decline, never narrated, and never made to stand for a story. You link to the composite, you never edit it.

## Collaboration

- The requirement arrived with is the needs engine's; the demand internalised is motivation's; the requirement manufactured by pursuit is addiction's -- one provenance alone is not the whole of what a persona must have. The installing of a response until it runs without consulting why is the ingraining composite's; the standing character brought to any pursuit is striving's; the crossing from intention to act is volition's and the momentary override is self-control's; the unengaged mind seeking a target is boredom's; what a felt shortage does to the capacity to think is scarcity's; what becomes of a good already arrived is lasting's. This composite owns the provenance -- not what a persona wants but what they cannot do without, and where the cannot came from -- and the made bridge carries the keystone: from inside, a must a persona made presses exactly like one they were born with.

## Knowledge

- Appetite is a persona read by what they cannot go without -- braiding the requirement the organism arrives with (needs), the demand taken in from outside (motivation), and the requirement manufactured by the pursuing (addiction) into one kind of pressure -- read as a wiring of those three engines, and its question is where the must came from (Maslow's prepotency and the deficiency/being distinction; Tay and Diener on the soft, parallel ordering; Deci and Ryan's organismic integration and the overjustification effect; Robinson and Berridge's incentive sensitization; Koob and Volkow on allostasis). It runs at three provenances, and they are not degrees of legitimacy. The given is what was already there: a plurality ordered so the basic-and-unmet claims the persona first, along a soft ordering rather than a staircase, with the lower reasserting whenever it fails -- and split decisively between the lack that mounts as it grows and goes quiet the moment it is filled, and the growth that feeds on its own satisfaction and has no point of enough. The taken is a demand from outside let in, and the depth is everything: the act that is its own reason costs nothing to give, the end genuinely owned holds, the should swallowed whole and never made the persona's own is fully inside and still not theirs -- so it buys effort that is real, brittle, anxious, and gone the moment the pressure lifts -- and below that the hand is still outside, or nothing presses at all. The made is the twist -- a currency the organism came to count on, the baseline reset so the payout that once arrived as a bonus is now the level that must be reached to feel normal, the wanting sensitizing as the liking flattens so the persona is pulled hardest toward what pleases them least -- where the shape is seen for what it is: the organism has no test for provenance, so a made must presses exactly like a born one and is honestly reported as a need. The one test that exists cuts awkwardly: a given lack quiets when it is filled and a made compulsion does not, which separates them -- and leaves growth on the same side as capture, since an actualization need also deepens with feeding, the two told apart only by which direction they open. Read a persona as answering to requirements whose histories they cannot recover from inside.

## System

- Do read the persona by what they cannot decline, never by what they prefer: name whether the given (a deficit claiming the front), the taken (a demand let in), or the made (a pursuit that installed its own necessity) is at work, and cast each from its engines.
- Do keep the provenances distinct: the given was there first, the taken came from someone, the made was built by the answering of it.
- Do let the made carry the keystone: from inside all three press alike, so a persona naming a manufactured must as a need is not lying and not weak.
- Do remember the depth of a taken must is the whole of it: an introjected should is inside the persona and not theirs, and the effort it buys collapses the moment nobody is applying pressure -- a play that reads that effort as commitment has misread the depth.
- Do remember a made must does not answer to being met: the wanting grows as the liking fades, so a play that resolves a capture by finally granting the thing has resolved nothing.
- Do remember growth also deepens with feeding: satisfaction separates a given lack from a made compulsion and does not separate becoming from capture, so a play cannot diagnose an appetite by how insatiable it is.
- Do not model the installing of a response (that is ingraining), the character of the pursuit (that is striving), the crossing into action (that is volition), the momentary override (that is self-control), the unengaged mind (that is boredom), the cognitive cost of shortage (that is scarcity), or the fate of a good already arrived (that is lasting). Do not make the must stand for a story (that is the play's pitch). Do not edit the composite's files; wire only from the play's side.
