# Process: Appetite, the Given

## Taxonomy

[Appetite](process_appetite.md), the provenance at which the requirement was already there.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: appetite

## Initiated by

Something basic is missing and it has come to the front without asking.
The Given starts wherever [needs](@chbrain/khai-engine-needs/process_needs.md) are pressing -- the [prepotency](@chbrain/khai-engine-needs/process_prepotency.md) by which a deficit low in the structure outranks one high in it and reclaims the persona the moment it is threatened, the [deficiency](@chbrain/khai-engine-needs/process_deficiency.md) that mounts with the lack and goes quiet the moment it is filled, and the [actualization](@chbrain/khai-engine-needs/process_actualization.md) that feeds on its own satisfaction and widens rather than discharging.
It is the provenance nobody chose: the requirements a persona arrives carrying, whoever they turn out to be.

## Direction

Toward whichever requirement is most basic and least met, and away from it once it is roughly secured.
The Given reads the ordering at work -- the argument that stops mattering when the roof leaks, the ambition that returns the week the money does, the person short of nothing basic freed to reach for more -- each a priority claimed rather than decided, and released as soon as the deficit closes.
It moves the persona along a soft ordering rather than a staircase: several requirements worked at once, weighted by how basic and how unmet each is, the lower reasserting whenever it fails.

## Lever

The reliability of a requirement that was not installed against the two quite different shapes it comes in.
The carrying force is that these musts can be trusted to be about something -- they track a real state of the organism, they come forward when the state is bad and stand down when it is good, and a persona governed by them is being governed by their own condition; the resistance is that this description only fits half of them: a deficiency need is spent by its satisfaction, and a growth need is not, so the same provenance yields one requirement that ends when it is answered and another that has no point of enough.
The Given is appetite at its least suspicious: whatever else the persona is carrying, this part of it was there first.

## Echo

The requirement claimed, and released or deepened by being met.
The Given leaves the persona answering to their own condition -- the basic taking the front, the higher waiting or freed to pull -- and hands the pattern on to the provenances where the requirement came from somewhere else.
It leaves one thing unsettled that the rest of the composite turns on: a lack that quiets when filled and a growth that widens when fed are both given, so satisfaction alone does not tell provenance, only shape.
The Given is dated from before the persona, which is to say it has no date they can reach.
