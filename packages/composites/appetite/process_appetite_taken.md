# Process: Appetite, the Taken

## Taxonomy

[Appetite](process_appetite.md), the provenance at which the requirement came from outside and was let in.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: appetite

## Initiated by

Something the persona did not originally require has become something they act on, and how deeply it was let in decides what kind of effort it yields.
The Taken starts wherever [motivation](@chbrain/khai-engine-motivation/process_motivation.md) has a source -- the [intrinsic](@chbrain/khai-engine-motivation/process_intrinsic.md) act that is its own reason and costs nothing to give, the [identified](@chbrain/khai-engine-motivation/process_identified.md) end genuinely owned, the [introjected](@chbrain/khai-engine-motivation/process_introjected.md) should swallowed whole and never made the persona's own, the [external](@chbrain/khai-engine-motivation/process_external.md) hand still outside, and the [amotivated](@chbrain/khai-engine-motivation/process_amotivated.md) pull that never formed at all.
It is the provenance with a depth to it: the same demand can sit anywhere along the way in.

## Direction

Toward an act, at a quality of effort set by how far the demand got.
The Taken reads the depth showing in the working -- the practice kept up because the practising is the point, the standard held because the persona means it, the task done to keep the guilt from landing, the task done because someone is watching, the task not done at all -- each the same outward move and none of them the same thing.
It moves the persona from being pressed to pressing themselves, which is not the same as owning it: an introjected should is fully inside and still not theirs, so the effort it buys is brittle, anxious, and drops the moment the pressure lifts.

## Lever

The genuine reason a deeply taken demand becomes against the borrowed pressure a half-taken one stays.
The carrying force is that internalisation is how a person comes to require things worth requiring -- almost nothing a persona values was theirs at the start, and a demand taken all the way in is not a lesser reason for having arrived from outside; the resistance is that the process stalls at every depth, and the shallow stops are the ones that look like commitment from outside: the persona acting to avoid their own guilt is visibly trying, and what they are serving is the pressure rather than the act.
The Taken is appetite at its most consequential and least visible: the provenance is the same and the depth is everything.

## Echo

The demand inside, at whatever depth it reached.
The Taken leaves the persona requiring something they were given -- freely and self-sustainingly if it went all the way, brittly and anxiously if it stopped short -- and hands the pattern on to the provenance where nobody gave it and it arrived anyway.
It also leaves the warning the last bridge collects: a requirement can be installed in a persona from outside without their consent or their notice, and it will still be theirs to answer.
The Taken is dated from the swallowing, the moment a demand stopped needing anyone present to enforce it.
