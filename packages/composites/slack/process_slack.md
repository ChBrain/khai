# Process: Slack

## Taxonomy

The room a persona has before a shortfall starts doing their thinking.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: slack

## Initiated by

A persona is short of something -- time, money, company, capacity -- and the shortfall has begun to be felt rather than merely to be true.
Slack starts wherever that felt lack is working: capturing attention and taxing what is left, through [scarcity](@chbrain/khai-engine-scarcity/process_scarcity.md); showing up in what then gets chosen, through [decision](@chbrain/khai-engine-decision/process_decision.md); and falling last on the live override that must now do everything, through [self-control](@chbrain/khai-engine-self-control/process_self_control.md).
This composite adds no fourth engine. It reads where the tax lands, which is the part neither atom can say alone: on exactly the two faculties a persona would need to get out.

## Direction

Toward a persona who is behaving like somebody else and will be described as though they always were.
The composite wires the three engines and reads their working in three shapes: [the Tunnel](process_slack_tunnel.md), attention captured; [the Tax](process_slack_tax.md), capacity taken; and [the Grip](process_slack_grip.md), the twist -- the arrangement removed, so that everything must now be held by hand.
The three are not three symptoms. They are one condition, taking three things in order, each of which makes the next harder to survive.

## Lever

The reality of what the shortfall does against the near-invisibility of it as a cause.
The carrying force is that the mechanism is not weakness and does not feel like one: a lack that matters draws attention properly, sharpens the persona on the pressing thing, and spends what it must to do so, which is why the same person under room and under shortfall tests as two different people and neither of them is pretending; the resistance is that only the result shows -- the missed appointment, the poor choice, the lapse -- while the room that was or was not there is not on the surface at all, so the condition is read off as disposition by everyone including the persona.
Slack is read on a persona as a condition, never as a trait; the trait reading is the mistake the composite exists to name.

## Echo

A persona short of room, and about to be scored on what the shortage produced.
Slack does not close; it is the shortfall working, gathered into one reading -- a tunnel, a tax, a grip.
It hands off what it is not: the machinery of pursuing a goal -- the executive function, the goal itself, the if-then plan laid down in advance -- is the volition composite's, which owns the arrangement where this owns whether there was room to make one; the drive that sets how hard a persona goes at things is the striving composite's, and what they believe about their own capacity is capability's; the wanting itself, its needs and its captures, is the appetite composite's, where this reads only what the wanting costs when there is no margin; attention as engagement rather than as capture by a lack is absorption's; the systematic errors of judgement in their own right are the estimation composite's; money as a relation between people rather than as a quantity somebody is short of is owing's; and a hardship made to **stand for** a story is authored key, the play's pitch, not the force on the persona.
The persona is dated from the room they had, and read as somebody whose character was being measured on an instrument that was really reading their circumstances.
