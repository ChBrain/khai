# Process: Slack, the Grip

## Taxonomy

[Slack](process_slack.md), the arrangement a shortfall removes, and where the three are seen for one thing.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: slack

## Initiated by

There is no way to set things up so the problem does not arise, so the problem arises, and must be held.
The Grip starts wherever [self-control](@chbrain/khai-engine-self-control/process_self_control.md) is having to work live -- the [restraint](@chbrain/khai-engine-self-control/process_restraint.md) exercised in the moment, the [delay](@chbrain/khai-engine-self-control/process_delay.md) held against a want that is present, the [lapse](@chbrain/khai-engine-self-control/process_lapse.md) when it is not held -- and where a standing [disciplined](@chbrain/khai-engine-self-control/position_disciplined.md) reading is about to be taken off the result.
It is the face of the composite that is scored, and the only one that is.

## Direction

Toward a persona holding by hand what other people have arranged not to have to hold.
The Grip reads the difference between two ways of not doing a thing: the persona with room who does not keep it in the house, does not pass that way, has the payment leave the account before it can be spent -- and the persona without room, who meets the same thing every day and must decline it every time, awake, in the moment, on purpose.
It moves nobody anywhere. It is what running in place costs.

## Lever

The exactness of live resistance as a measure of effort against its exactness as a measure of nothing else.
The carrying force is that this is really being done -- the declining is real, repeated, and expensive, and the persona doing it is exerting far more control than the one who never faces the question; the resistance is that only failures are events. The arrangement that prevents a hundred confrontations produces no record, and the confrontation lost once produces one, so the person with the most room shows the fewest lapses and is credited with the most discipline, while the person with none shows the most and is credited with the least.
The Grip rides the composite's keystone knife-edge: what reads as discipline is mostly room, so the trait is substantially a report on the arrangement a persona could afford, and the one gripping hardest scores worst.

## Echo

Held, mostly, and marked down for the times it was not.
It shows the three faces complete -- attention taken, capacity taken, arrangement taken -- and carries beneath it the composite's keystone: the shortfall lands last on the faculty that would have to do the escaping, and then that faculty's output is read as the persona's character, which closes the account in the wrong name.
This is why it is the hardest of the three to stage and the only one worth closing on: the play must show the arrangement that was never made, and an arrangement that was never made has no scene in it, no line, and nothing anybody in the room can point to.
The Grip is the note the whole composite closes on: what a persona has been holding is legible in what they could not arrange, and nowhere else without a discount.
The Grip is dated from every ordinary day it did not fail, none of which was recorded.
