# Process: Slack, the Tunnel

## Taxonomy

[Slack](process_slack.md), the attention a shortfall takes, and the sharpness that is the same act as the blindness.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: slack

## Initiated by

Something is short, and the mind has already gone to it without being sent.
The Tunnel starts wherever [tunneling](@chbrain/khai-engine-scarcity/process_tunneling.md) is running -- the lack that captures attention and holds it, narrowing the field to the pressing thing -- and it is the first thing [scarcity](@chbrain/khai-engine-scarcity/process_scarcity.md) does, before any cost has been paid anywhere else.
It is the face of the composite that looks, from close up, like focus.

## Direction

Toward one thing done well and everything else out of frame.
The Tunnel reads the narrowing as a single act with two faces -- the deadline that makes the last afternoon the most productive of the month, the shortage of money that makes a persona better than any economist at what a given sum will buy -- and reads what falls outside the frame at the same moment: the letter unopened, the appointment missed, the cheaper option available next month that is not thought of because next month is not in the tunnel.
It moves the persona to competence on the urgent thing and out of contact with the rest.

## Lever

The genuine gain inside the frame against everything the frame excludes.
The carrying force is that the capture is appropriate and mostly correct: a lack that matters should draw attention, and the persona who attends to it is right to, which is why this is not an error to be corrected and cannot be talked out of anybody; the resistance is that the frame is not chosen and not noticed -- what is outside it does not feel neglected, it feels absent, so the persona does not experience themselves as trading anything away and cannot report the trade afterwards.
The Tunnel spends everything not pressing, at no felt cost.

## Echo

A persona sharp where it counts and gone everywhere else.
It is the bridge that establishes the composite's shape by refusing the easy version: the mechanism is not a deficit, it is an allocation, and it produces the competence and the neglect with the same movement, so a play cannot show one without the other.
What it hands upward is the reason the next two bridges are not simply about being tired -- the persona is not diminished here, they are aimed, and the cost is in the aiming.
The Tunnel is dated from the moment the lack became pressing, which is not the moment it became true.
