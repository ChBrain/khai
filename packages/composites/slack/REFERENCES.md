# Slack: Reference

## Line of Work

Slack as **composite**: the room a persona has before a shortfall starts doing their thinking. It
is not a fourth mechanism but a wiring of three: the **scarcity** engine supplies what a felt lack
does to attention and capacity, the **decision** engine supplies what then gets chosen, and the
**self-control** engine supplies the live override that is what remains when there is no room to
arrange things otherwise. This composite adds no fourth engine; it reads the seam the three make
-- that the tax a shortfall levies falls on precisely the two faculties a persona would need to
get out of it -- and stages that seam into three bridges, closing on the discovery that what reads
as discipline is mostly room.

The domain does not model any single mechanism -- tunneling, bandwidth, and the trap are the
scarcity engine's; the intuitive, deliberate, heuristic, satisficing, and paralysed modes are
decision's; restraint, delay, lapse, and the disciplined trait are self-control's; and one alone is
a process, not the condition. Nor is it the machinery of pursuing a goal, which is the volition
composite's; nor the drive that sets how hard a persona goes at things, which is striving's; nor
what they believe about their own capacity, which is capability's; nor the wanting itself, which is
appetite's. It owns the room: how much a persona has, what its absence takes in what order, and
what the taking is then mistaken for.

The spine is Mullainathan and Shafir, whose account is the join. Scarcity in their sense is not
having little but the felt experience of having less than one needs, and it does three things in
sequence: it captures attention on the lack, producing a real focus dividend inside the frame and
neglect of everything outside it; it occupies a portion of mental capacity that cannot be put
down, so that fewer resources are available for anything else; and, through both, it degrades the
quality of what gets decided. Slack is their own term for what abundance provides and scarcity
removes -- the margin that lets a person absorb a shock without a trade-off, forget a thing without
a consequence, and arrange their affairs rather than manage them. The composite takes that account
and follows it one step further than either atom does alone, to where the tax finally lands.

That last step comes from the correction the self-control literature has made to itself, and the
self-control engine already carries it. The strength model -- willpower as a depleting resource --
did not survive multi-lab replication, and the composite deliberately rests on none of it. What
did survive is a different and more useful finding: people high in trait self-control are not
winning more contests against temptation, they are having fewer contests. Experience sampling finds
they report fewer desires and less resistance, and the trait's predictive power runs through
habits and situation selection rather than through effortful override. That reframes the trait
entirely. Not keeping it in the house, not passing that way, having the payment leave the account
before it can be spent -- these are arrangements, and arrangements cost room to make. Which is what
scarcity removes. So the persona without slack must do by live resistance, every day, what the
persona with slack does by never meeting the question; and because arrangements produce no record
while lapses produce events, the one exerting more control is scored as having less.

**On what holds and what does not, stated plainly.** The three faces are not equally evidenced.
The claim that a felt shortfall captures attention and occupies capacity has good support, and the
strongest of it is field and longitudinal rather than laboratory: the lab priming studies in this
literature have had a mixed replication record, and the headline before-and-after harvest result
has been re-analysed and argued over, so a specific effect size should not be quoted from this
composite. The reframing of trait self-control through habit and situation selection is well
supported and is what the keystone rests on; the depletion model is not, and is excluded. The
marshmallow paradigm's long-run predictive power is substantially attenuated once family
background is controlled, which the composite treats as evidence for its own reading rather than
against it, since the attenuation is precisely the trait turning out to have been reporting the
child's room. And the paralysed mode's usual anchor, choice overload, is contested with a
meta-analytic mean near zero -- the same bound khai's choice-architecture engine states -- so the
composite claims the substitution toward cheaper modes and not that abundance of options is itself
disabling. The three bridges are the tunnel, the tax, and the grip.

## Origin

The scarcity literature, judgement and decision research, and the self-control literature after its
own correction, wired as a composite over the scarcity, decision, and self-control engines. The
first two rows are the **joining** -- the published work that sets the lack, the deciding, and the
holding as one condition -- and the rest anchor the individual bridges and their bounds.

## Restrictions

What the composite refuses to model, and to whom it delegates.

- **The three mechanisms (the atoms)**: The composite wires the three but owns none. Tunneling,
  bandwidth, and the trap are the scarcity engine's; the five decision modes are decision's;
  restraint, delay, lapse, and the disciplined trait are self-control's. Slack reads only where the
  tax lands and what it is mistaken for, and one mechanism alone is a process rather than the
  condition.
- **The machinery of pursuit (volition)**: Executive function, the goal, and the if-then plan laid
  down in advance are the volition composite's -- and this is the sharpest boundary, because an
  implementation intention **is** an arrangement. Volition owns how an arrangement works; slack owns
  whether there was room to make one. A persona with a plan and no slack is running volition's
  machinery on this composite's terms, and the two should be linked together rather than confused.
- **The drive and the self-belief (striving, capability)**: How hard a persona goes at things --
  the achievement motive, the regulatory focus, the grit -- is the striving composite's; what they
  believe about their own capacity and where they locate control is capability's. Slack is a
  condition rather than a disposition or a belief, and it is precisely the thing those two are
  liable to be credited for.
- **The wanting (appetite)**: Needs, motivation, and the captured wanting of addiction are the
  appetite composite's. Slack does not model desire; it reads what desire costs when there is no
  margin to arrange around it, so a persona can be read by both at once and the readings answer
  different questions.
- **Attention as engagement (absorption)**: Attention, flow, and time-perception are the absorption
  composite's, which owns attention given. Slack's tunnel is attention **taken** -- unbidden,
  unchosen, and about a lack -- and the difference between being absorbed and being captured is
  the difference between the two composites.
- **The biases in their own right (estimation)**: Anchoring, availability, and representativeness
  are the estimation composite's. Slack reads only the substitution toward cheaper modes under
  load; which specific error a cheap mode then makes is estimation's to name.
- **Money as a relation (owing)**: The gift, the debt, and what settling does to a bond are the
  owing composite's. Slack reads a quantity somebody is short of, and is indifferent to whom they
  owe it or what the owing means between them.
- **The hardship as authored symbol (the play's pitch)**: A shortfall made to **stand for** a theme
  -- the poverty that mirrors a motif of diminishment -- is refused: that is authored key, the
  play's pitch, not the force on the persona. Slack sets what was taken; the meaning read into it
  is the play's.

## Encoding

Source to constraint. A composite root over three engine atoms it declares as dependencies, with
three bridges, ordered by what the shortfall takes.

- **[slack](process_slack.md)** (the root): the room a persona has before a shortfall starts doing
  their thinking, read as a condition and never as a trait. Anchored by Mullainathan & Shafir
  (2013), whose term this is.
- **[the tunnel](process_slack_tunnel.md)** (bridge): attention captured by the lack, sharpening
  the persona inside the frame and blinding them outside it with one movement. Anchored by
  Mullainathan & Shafir on the focus dividend and tunneling.
- **[the tax](process_slack_tax.md)** (bridge): capacity taken, so the deciding slides toward the
  heuristic, the satisficing, and the deferred -- each economy reasonable, the aggregate not.
  Anchored by Shah, Mullainathan & Shafir (2012) and by Simon, Tversky & Kahneman on the cheaper
  modes themselves.
- **[the grip](process_slack_grip.md)** (bridge): the arrangement removed, so that what others do
  by never meeting the question must be done by live resistance every day. Anchored by Galla &
  Duckworth (2015), Hofmann et al. (2012), and de Ridder et al. (2012) on situation selection, and
  bounded by the ego-depletion replications; the composite's twist and keystone, that arrangements
  leave no record while lapses are events, so the persona exerting the most control is credited
  with the least.
