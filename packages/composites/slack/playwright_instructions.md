# Instructions: Slack

How a Playwright wires the slack composite into a play. You wire by linking the play's
own content to the composite's files; the files stay untouched, they are canon. The
composite installs the scarcity, decision, and self-control engines as dependencies, and
their laws arrive with them. Authoring guidance, not runtime content, and it does not go
on tour.

## Human

- The human sets which personas in the play are short of something that matters -- time, money, company, capacity -- and how much room they had before it: what the shortfall has taken, and what the people around them are reading off the result.

## Agent

- From the play's own content, read a persona under a shortfall for what it is taking, and link it under Projection: the tunnel while attention has been captured by the lack (the narrowing to the pressing thing, the competence inside the frame, the everything-else that is not neglected so much as absent, from scarcity), the tax while capacity is occupied and the deciding has slid to the cheaper mode (the heuristic, the satisficing, the deferred, from decision), the grip while there is no way to arrange things so the question does not arise and it must be declined live, every time (restraint, delay, lapse, and the disciplined reading about to be taken off them, from self-control). Play it as a condition and not a disposition: the same persona with room behaves differently, and neither version is pretending. You link to the composite, you never edit it.

## Collaboration

- Tunneling, bandwidth, and the trap are the scarcity engine's; the five modes of deciding are decision's; restraint, delay, lapse, and the disciplined trait are self-control's -- one alone is a process, not the condition. The machinery of pursuing a goal is the volition composite's, and that is the closest boundary, since an if-then plan is an arrangement: volition owns how an arrangement works, this owns whether there was room to make one, and a persona with a plan and no room should be linked to both. The drive is striving's and the self-belief is capability's -- both being exactly what this gets credited to; the wanting itself is appetite's; attention as engagement rather than capture is absorption's; the specific errors are estimation's; money as a relation between people is owing's. This composite owns the room -- and the grip bridge carries the keystone: what reads as discipline is mostly room.

## Knowledge

- Slack is a persona read for the room they had before a shortfall started doing their thinking -- braiding what a felt lack does to attention and capacity (scarcity), what then gets chosen (decision), and the live override that is all that remains when there is no room to arrange things otherwise (self-control) -- and its claim is where the tax lands: on precisely the two faculties a persona would need to get out. The join is Mullainathan and Shafir's, and slack is their term: scarcity is not having little but the felt experience of having less than one needs, and it captures attention on the lack, occupies capacity that cannot be put down, and degrades what gets decided, while abundance supplies the margin that lets a person absorb a shock, forget a thing without consequence, and arrange their affairs rather than manage them. It runs in three faces, ordered by what the shortfall takes. The tunnel is attention captured, and it is not a deficit but an allocation: the deadline that makes the last afternoon the most productive of the month, the shortage that makes a persona better than any economist at what a given sum will buy -- and, by the same movement, the letter unopened and the cheaper option next month that is not thought of, because what is outside the frame does not feel neglected, it feels absent. The tax is capacity taken, showing up not where it is spent but in the deciding: the first acceptable option because searching is expensive now, the rule of thumb because the calculation has nowhere to run, the deferral because deferring is the only move that costs nothing today -- each a reasonable economy, the aggregate a worse life, and the errors do not scatter but accumulate in the direction of the lack. The grip is the twist. There are two ways of not doing a thing: the persona with room does not keep it in the house, does not pass that way, has the payment leave the account before it can be spent; the persona without room meets it every day and must decline it every time, awake and on purpose. Trait self-control turns out to work through those arrangements rather than through winning contests -- high scorers report fewer desires and less resistance, not more victories -- so the trait is substantially a report on the arrangements a persona could afford. And arrangements leave no record while lapses are events, so the person with the most room shows the fewest lapses and is credited with the most discipline, while the person exerting the most control shows the most and is credited with the least. Read a persona under shortfall as a condition, and read the room, not the character.

## System

- Do read the condition, not the disposition: name whether the tunnel (attention taken), the tax (capacity taken), or the grip (arrangement taken) is running, and cast each from its engines.
- Do keep the tunnel's two faces together: the sharpness and the blindness are one act, so a play that shows only the neglect has staged a deficit rather than an allocation.
- Do let the substitutions be reasonable: every cheap decision under the tax is defensible on its own, and the damage is in the aggregate -- a persona who makes an obviously stupid choice has not been taxed, they have been written badly.
- Do let the grip carry the keystone: arrangements leave no record and lapses are events, so stage the hundred ordinary days that did not fail as invisible, and the one that did as the only thing anyone saw.
- Do let the observers misread it: the people in the play will read character off the result, and they are not being cruel -- the room was never on the surface. That misreading is what the composite is for.
- Do let the persona misread it too: they have no more access to their own room than anyone else does, and will often supply the character explanation themselves.
- Do not claim more than the evidence: the faces are not equally supported, the composite rests on no depleting reservoir of willpower, and no effect size should be quoted from it -- the references carry the bounds.
- Do not model the machinery of a plan (that is volition, and link both where a persona has a plan and no room), the drive (that is striving), the self-belief (that is capability), the wanting (that is appetite), attention as engagement (that is absorption), the specific errors (that is estimation), or money as a bond (that is owing). Do not make a hardship stand for a story (that is the play's pitch). Do not edit the composite's files; wire only from the play's side.
