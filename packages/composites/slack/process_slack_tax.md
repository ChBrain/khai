# Process: Slack, the Tax

## Taxonomy

[Slack](process_slack.md), the capacity a shortfall takes, and what then gets decided with what is left.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: slack

## Initiated by

The lack is being carried, and the carrying is using something.
The Tax starts wherever [bandwidth](@chbrain/khai-engine-scarcity/process_bandwidth.md) is going -- the part of a persona that is permanently occupied by a shortfall it cannot put down -- and it shows up not there but in [decision](@chbrain/khai-engine-decision/process_decision.md), where the [deliberate](@chbrain/khai-engine-decision/process_deliberate.md) gives way to the [heuristic](@chbrain/khai-engine-decision/process_heuristic.md), the [satisficing](@chbrain/khai-engine-decision/process_satisficing.md), and the [paralysed](@chbrain/khai-engine-decision/process_paralysed.md).
It is the face of the composite where a persona is doing worse work with the same mind.

## Direction

Toward choices made in a cheaper mode than the persona would otherwise use.
The Tax reads the substitution rather than the failure -- the first acceptable option taken because the search is expensive now, the rule of thumb used because the calculation has nowhere to run, the decision deferred entirely because deferring is the only move that costs nothing today -- and reads that each of these is a reasonable economy that produces, in aggregate, a worse life.
It moves the persona through their days on settings they never selected.

## Lever

The efficiency of the cheaper mode against the compounding of what it gets wrong.
The carrying force is that the substitutions work: a heuristic is fast and usually adequate, satisficing is the correct response to a costly search, and the persona under tax is often making the best available choice given what is available to them -- there is nothing to correct in any single one; the resistance is that the mode is doing worse on exactly the decisions where the shortfall bites hardest, so the errors do not scatter, they accumulate in the direction of the lack, and the persona has no way to notice that their method has changed because the method is not what they are attending to.
The Tax spends the capacity that would have caught it.

## Echo

A persona deciding at a discount, and unaware there is a discount.
It is the bridge where the condition first becomes legible to an outsider and is legible in the wrong terms: what shows is a run of poor choices, and poor choices are what a play, and everyone in it, will read as a person.
What it hands upward is the hardest part of the composite -- that the errors point the same way as the explanation, so the evidence that would convict the persona of causing their shortfall is exactly the evidence the shortfall produces.
The Tax is dated from the first choice that was made the cheap way, which will not be remembered as a choice about how to choose.
