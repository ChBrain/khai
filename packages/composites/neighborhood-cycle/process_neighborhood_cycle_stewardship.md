# Process: Neighborhood-Cycle, Stewardship

## Taxonomy

[Neighborhood-Cycle](process_neighborhood_cycle.md), the bridge where the place returns for the people who held it.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: neighborhood-cycle

## Initiated by

The place falls, and is taken back without being taken over.
Here the [reclamation](@chbrain/khai-engine-reclamation/process_reclamation.md) comes for the community that survived the [dereliction](@chbrain/khai-engine-dereliction/process_dereliction.md) -- the conservation led by the residents, the public reinvestment tied to the sitting tenants, the trust or the covenant that keeps the recovered value in the hands that held the loss -- and the [gentrification](@chbrain/khai-engine-gentrification/process_gentrification.md) is refused, mitigated, or turned to the occupants' account.
Stewardship starts wherever the return is bound to serve the ones already there.

## Direction

Toward a recovered place that keeps its people.
The fabric is repaired and the rent held, the character kept by the community that made it rather than sold back to it, the rent-gap closed to the residents' benefit rather than a developer's.
Stewardship moves the place up without moving its people out -- the recovery and the tenure defended as one thing.

## Lever

What stewardship spends -- the ease of letting the return follow the capital.
The default of a widened rent-gap is turnover; stewardship is the harder path that holds the recovered value for the community, and it costs the vigilance, the organization, and often the public claim that turnover never has to muster.
A production holds this lever by making the keeping deliberate and effortful -- the land trust, the tenant union, the protected tenancy -- never a thing that simply happens when a place improves.

## Echo

The place come back for the ones who stayed.
It is the rarer turn and the one the composite marks as the alternative to displacement: proof that a place's recovery and its community's survival are not opposites, only usually made so.
Stewardship is dated from the return that kept its people, and it stands against turnover as the answer to the cycle's one question -- for whom -- resolved, this once, in the community's favor.
