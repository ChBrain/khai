# Process: Neighborhood-Cycle, Attrition

## Taxonomy

[Neighborhood-Cycle](process_neighborhood_cycle.md), the bridge where no human return comes at all.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: neighborhood-cycle

## Initiated by

The place falls, and the return never arrives -- or arrives only by moving the loss elsewhere.
Two shapes share the bridge: the place written off for good, where the [dereliction](@chbrain/khai-engine-dereliction/process_dereliction.md) runs to permanent ruin because the rent-gap never widens enough to call [gentrification](@chbrain/khai-engine-gentrification/process_gentrification.md) back and no [reclamation](@chbrain/khai-engine-reclamation/process_reclamation.md) is ever funded; and the serial displacement, where the community is cleared from one disinvested place only to be pushed from the next, the return following them nowhere.
Attrition starts wherever the cycle takes and does not give back.

## Direction

Toward a place, or a people, worn away by a turn that never completes for them.
The rust-belt town capital left and never wanted; the tenants displaced from block to block ahead of each wave, always in the disinvested place and never in the recovered one.
Attrition moves toward exhaustion -- the fabric to a ruin no one reclaims, the community to a dispersal no return ever gathers.

## Lever

What attrition spends -- the assumption that decline is always followed by return.
The rent-gap logic promises re-entry, but only where re-entry pays; where it does not, the place simply falls and stays fallen, and the composite refuses to pretend a recovery is coming.
A production holds this lever by letting some places, and some people, get no third act -- the loss without the revival, which turnover at least disguises and stewardship at least earns.

## Echo

The place or the people left in loss, with no return to read.
Where no human return comes to the emptied place, the return that does arrive is not this cycle's: the wild reclaiming the abandoned ground -- the weeds in the lot, the trees in the floor -- is succession's, the non-human answer to a human withdrawal, and the composite hands that arrival across rather than claiming it.
Attrition is dated from the return that never came, and it is the bridge that keeps the cycle honest: not every fall is a prelude to a rise, and some places and people simply bear the loss.
