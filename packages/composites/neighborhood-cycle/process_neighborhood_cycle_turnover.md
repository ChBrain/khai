# Process: Neighborhood-Cycle, Turnover

## Taxonomy

[Neighborhood-Cycle](process_neighborhood_cycle.md), the bridge where the place returns for capital and the community is displaced.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: neighborhood-cycle

## Initiated by

The full extractive turn runs to its end: a place falls, and its recovery is a taking-over.
[Dereliction](@chbrain/khai-engine-dereliction/process_dereliction.md)'s disinvestment digs the rent-gap; [gentrification](@chbrain/khai-engine-gentrification/process_gentrification.md) re-enters to close it; and the [reclamation](@chbrain/khai-engine-reclamation/process_reclamation.md) that follows -- often an adaptive reuse -- keeps the fabric and clears the people.
Turnover starts wherever the return is real and the returnees are new: the place comes back, and its prior community does not.

## Direction

Toward a reoccupied place and a displaced population.
The fabric is saved, upgraded, sometimes beautifully restored; the loft keeps the mill and prices out the neighborhood, the character is sold back as the very authenticity the displacement erased.
Turnover moves the place up and its people out in one motion -- the down and the up of Smith's see-saw completed, the community turned over with the buildings.

## Lever

What turnover spends -- the belief that a place saved is a good served.
The improvement is genuine, which is what makes the turn hard to refuse; but the composite reads past the fabric to the tenancy, and finds the return served capital, not community.
A production holds this lever by letting the reclamation be real and the displacement be its cost in the same breath -- the revival and the eviction as one event, not two.

## Echo

The place restored and its community gone.
This is the dominant real-world turn, and the one the composite exists to name: not decline, which is plainly loss, but return that reads as triumph and is, for the displaced, the completion of the loss.
Turnover is dated from the wave that took the place back -- for whoever could pay to hold it -- and it stands as the cycle's default unless something makes the return serve the ones who bore the fall.
