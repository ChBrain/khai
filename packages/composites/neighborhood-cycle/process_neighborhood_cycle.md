# Process: Neighborhood-Cycle

## Taxonomy

A composite reading over three engine atoms (no canon file of its own): the [dereliction](@chbrain/khai-engine-dereliction/process_dereliction.md), [gentrification](@chbrain/khai-engine-gentrification/process_gentrification.md), and [reclamation](@chbrain/khai-engine-reclamation/process_reclamation.md) engines this package declares as dependencies, over the decay they all act upon.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: neighborhood-cycle

## Initiated by

A built place turns through more than one phase across its life, and no single engine sees the whole turn.
[Dereliction](@chbrain/khai-engine-dereliction/process_dereliction.md) sees the decline, [gentrification](@chbrain/khai-engine-gentrification/process_gentrification.md) the capital re-entry, [reclamation](@chbrain/khai-engine-reclamation/process_reclamation.md) the taking-back; each answers as if its own phase ran alone, and none sees the arc the three make when they run in sequence over one place.
The composite starts where a place is read across time -- fell, and then what, and for whom -- and the whole turn becomes legible: not a phase, but the life of a place.
This is the produced cycle, not the fated one: decline is done and return is chosen (Smith's see-saw of capital, Marcuse's paired abandonment and gentrification), against the older determinism that read the lifecycle as a place's natural fate and used that reading to justify the redlining that dug the decline.

## Direction

Across the place's life, phase handing to phase.
The composite does not stop at the fall or the return; it follows the disinvestment that opens the rent-gap into the re-entry that closes it, and the re-entry into the reclamation that keeps, sells, or fakes what remains.
Its direction is the whole arc -- and at its turning point stands the question no single phase asks: when the place comes back, for whom does it come back?

## Lever

What the cycle spends -- the assumption that a place's return is a simple good.
Decline is plainly loss; but return is where the composite bites, because a place can come back for its people or come back over them, and the word revival hides which.
The composite holds the phases together precisely so that the return cannot be read as unmixed: every taking-back is also a question of who is kept and who is cleared, and the cycle's meaning rests on that answer, not on the fact of return.

## Echo

The place carried through its whole turn, and the return read by whom it served.
The atoms each run their own phase and cannot see the family: the [turnover](process_neighborhood_cycle_turnover.md) where the place returns for capital and the community is displaced, the [stewardship](process_neighborhood_cycle_stewardship.md) where it returns for the people who held it, and the [attrition](process_neighborhood_cycle_attrition.md) where no human return comes at all -- and where none comes, the biological reclaiming that fills the void is succession's, not this cycle's.
This composite owns the arc of a built place and the question its return forces; the feeling of the people carried through it is the persona's own engines', and the natural fate the old lifecycle imagined it is not.
