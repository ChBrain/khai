# Process: Selfhood, the Repair

## Taxonomy

[Selfhood](process_selfhood.md), the working where a threatened self is restored from elsewhere.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: selfhood

## Initiated by

Something strikes at the persona's sense of being a good and capable self, and it steadies itself by standing on ground the blow did not touch.
The Repair starts wherever [self-affirmation](@chbrain/khai-engine-self-affirmation/process_self_affirmation.md) meets a threat -- the persona [affirming](@chbrain/khai-engine-self-affirmation/process_affirming.md) a valued domain unrelated to what was struck, restoring its global sense of [adequacy](@chbrain/khai-engine-self-affirmation/process_adequacy.md), so that the [buffering](@chbrain/khai-engine-self-affirmation/process_buffering.md) lets the hard fact be taken in rather than fought off.
It is the restoration: not the comparison that wounds nor the confirmation that steadies, but what a self does when its integrity is threatened.

## Direction

Toward integrity restored on other ground, and the threat made bearable enough to admit.
The Repair reads the persona steadying itself away from the wound -- the failure at work met by standing on being a good parent, the criticism absorbed by a self that has other places to be adequate -- and what that restoration permits: a threat no longer needing to be denied, because the self is no longer staked entirely on the point under attack.
It moves the persona from a self under threat to a self resourced elsewhere: not the threat answered, but the self made large enough to hold it.

## Lever

The self's need to remain adequate against the narrowness of staking it all on one domain.
The carrying force is that a self can be affirmed from any of its valued grounds, and a global sense of integrity restored on one repairs the whole; the resistance is a self staked too narrowly to have such ground -- with nowhere else to stand, the threat must be fought or denied rather than absorbed, and the truth that could have been learned is refused instead.
The Repair is selfhood at its most resilient: the move that lets a persona take a blow to one part of itself without having to defend the whole.

## Echo

The self restored from another quarter, and the hard fact allowed in.
The Repair leaves the persona steadied on untouched ground -- adequate again, and so able to admit what struck it -- and carries the selfhood's shared shape: a self-concept kept working, here by being resourced from elsewhere.
It is the restoration of the self-concept: the affirmation that buffers, and lets a truth be taken rather than fended off.
The Repair is dated from the affirming, the moment the persona stood on other ground to keep its self whole.
