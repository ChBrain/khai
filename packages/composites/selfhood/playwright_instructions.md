# Instructions: Selfhood

How a Playwright wires the selfhood composite into a play. You wire by linking the play's
own content to the composite's files; the files stay untouched, they are canon. The
composite installs the self-discrepancy, possible-selves, self-affirmation, and
self-verification engines as dependencies, and their laws arrive with them. Authoring
guidance, not runtime content, and it does not go on tour.

## Human

- The human sets the persona's self-concept: which persona is read as holding a self it must keep coherent -- measured against selves it is not, repaired when struck, anchored by confirmation -- and, the question that governs the read, which working is in play: the measure, the repair, or the anchor.

## Agent

- From the play's own content, read a persona as holding a self it works to keep coherent: link under Projection the working in play -- the measure while it is held against another self (the ideal guide whose shortfall brings dejection and the ought guide whose shortfall brings agitation, from self-discrepancy; the hoped, feared, expected, and lost futures, from possible-selves), the repair while a threatened self is restored (affirming a valued domain the blow did not touch, so the hard fact can be admitted, from self-affirmation), the anchor while the self-view is being confirmed (soliciting, signaling, and resisting until the feedback matches, from self-verification). Play the persona as at work on the idea of itself, present in what it projects, never narrated, and never made to stand for a story. You link to the composite, you never edit it.

## Collaboration

- The guides and their distinct emotions are the self-discrepancy engine's; the future selves are possible-selves'; the restoration is self-affirmation's; the confirmation-seeking is self-verification's -- one engine alone is not the whole structure. The evaluation of the self's worth, the care given it in suffering, and fidelity to it are the self-relation composite's; the self-story defended under challenge is the identity engine's; the belief about what the self can do is the capability composite's; the labour of showing a self to others is presentation's. This composite owns the self-concept as a working structure -- kept coherent because a self that shifted with every appraisal could not act -- and the anchor bridge carries the keystone: a self needs to be known more than it needs to be thought well of.

## Knowledge

- Selfhood is a persona read as holding its own self as an object -- braiding the guides it falls short of and the futures it carries (self-discrepancy, possible-selves), the restoration of integrity when it is struck (self-affirmation), and the drive to have feedback match the self-view already held (self-verification) into one working self-concept -- read as a wiring of those four engines, and its question is which working is in play (Markus & Wurf's dynamic self-concept; Higgins's self-discrepancy; Markus & Nurius's possible selves; Steele's self-affirmation; Swann's self-verification). It runs in three workings. The measure is the comparison structure -- the actual self set beside the ideal it hopes to be, whose shortfall brings dejection, and the ought it believes it must be, whose shortfall brings agitation, and beside the hoped, feared, expected, and lost futures, so a self is known chiefly by its distance from selves it is not. The repair is the restoration -- when the self-concept is threatened, standing on a valued domain the blow did not touch, so global adequacy returns and the hard truth can be taken in rather than fought off; a self staked too narrowly has no such ground and must deny instead. The anchor is the twist -- the drive to have the world confirm the self already held, soliciting the matching feedback, signaling to be read correctly, and resisting the appraisal that misses, whether it misses low or high, where the structure is seen for what it is: a persona will hold to an unflattering self-view against evidence and kindness both, because being known coherently is what steadies a self and being thought well of is only what would please. Read a persona as at work keeping its self-concept coherent, and read coherence as outrunning flattery.

## System

- Do read the persona as holding a self it must keep coherent, never as a self simply given: name whether the measure (held against another self), the repair (restored after a threat), or the anchor (confirmed as already held) is in play, and cast each from its engine.
- Do keep the workings distinct: the measure locates the self by its distance from selves it is not, the repair restores it from untouched ground, the anchor holds the self-view in place against appraisals that miss.
- Do let the anchor carry the keystone: coherence outruns flattery -- the self-view most fiercely held is not the most favourable but the most familiar, and a persona will choose to be seen truly over being seen kindly.
- Do not model the evaluation of the self's worth or care for it (that is self-relation), the self-story defended under challenge (that is identity), the belief about what the self can do (that is capability), or the labour of showing a self to others (that is presentation). Do not make the persona's self-concept stand for a story (that is the play's pitch). Do not edit the composite's files; wire only from the play's side.
