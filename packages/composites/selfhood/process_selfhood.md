# Process: Selfhood

## Taxonomy

The self as an object to itself.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: selfhood

## Initiated by

A persona is read not only as a self that acts but as a self it holds, looks at, compares, and works to keep coherent.
Selfhood starts wherever a persona takes its own self as an object -- measuring it against the [self-guides](@chbrain/khai-engine-self-discrepancy/process_self_discrepancy.md) it falls short of and the [possible selves](@chbrain/khai-engine-possible-selves/position_possible_selves.md) it might become, restoring it by [self-affirmation](@chbrain/khai-engine-self-affirmation/process_self_affirmation.md) when it is threatened, and holding it steady by the [self-verification](@chbrain/khai-engine-self-verification/process_self_verification.md) that seeks matching feedback.
This composite adds no fifth engine; it reads what the four make together: a self-concept that compares, defends, and holds itself in place.

## Direction

Toward the whole work of holding a self-concept, read through its workings.
The composite wires the four engines and reads their working in three shapes: [the Measure](process_selfhood_measure.md), the other selves a persona is held against; [the Repair](process_selfhood_repair.md), what it does when the self-concept is threatened; and [the Anchor](process_selfhood_anchor.md), the twist -- the confirmation it seeks to keep the self-view in place.
A persona does not simply have a self: it holds one against selves it is not, patches it when it is struck, and works on the world until the world reflects it back the way it already reads itself.

## Lever

The self's need to hold together against everything that would unsettle it.
The carrying force is that a self-concept must stay coherent to work at all -- it organizes what a persona expects, attempts, and takes personally, and a self that shifted with every piece of feedback could not act; the resistance is the constant supply of unsettling material, the shortfall from the ideal, the failure that threatens integrity, the flattering or damning view another holds.
Selfhood is read wherever a persona is granted its self-concept as a thing to be maintained: not a self simply given but one held against comparison, defended under threat, and confirmed until it is stable.

## Echo

The persona revealed as a self at work on the idea of itself.
Selfhood does not close; it is the persona going on holding its self-concept, gathering the four engines into one reading -- a self measured, repaired, and anchored.
It hands off what it is not: the evaluation of the self's worth, the care it gives itself in suffering, and its fidelity to itself are the self-relation composite's; the self-story a persona tells about who they are and defends under challenge is the identity engine's; the belief about what the self can do is the capability composite's; the labour of showing a self to others is the presentation composite's; and the self-concept made to stand for a story is authored key, the play's pitch, not the force.
The persona is dated from its own self-holding, and read as a self that must keep itself coherent to remain a self at all.
