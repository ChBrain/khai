# Process: Selfhood, the Anchor

## Taxonomy

[Selfhood](process_selfhood.md), the working where the self-view is held in place, flattering or not.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: selfhood

## Initiated by

The persona works on the world until the world reflects back the self it already believes itself to be.
The Anchor starts wherever [self-verification](@chbrain/khai-engine-self-verification/process_self_verification.md) holds the self-concept steady -- the persona [soliciting](@chbrain/khai-engine-self-verification/process_soliciting.md) the feedback that matches it, [signaling](@chbrain/khai-engine-self-verification/process_signaling.md) who it is so others read it that way, and [resisting](@chbrain/khai-engine-self-verification/process_resistance.md) the view that does not fit, even a kinder one.
It is the stabiliser: not the comparison that locates the self nor the repair that restores it, but the drive to have it confirmed.

## Direction

Toward a world arranged to agree with the self already held.
The Anchor reads the persona seeking its own reflection -- steering toward the company that sees it as it sees itself, displaying the marks that prompt the expected reading, and pushing back on the appraisal that misses, whether it misses low or high.
It moves the persona from a self-view privately held to one socially confirmed: the self stabilised by a world that has been worked on until it echoes.

## Lever

The need to be known coherently against the wish to be thought well of.
The carrying force is that a self-concept must be confirmed to stay usable -- a persona whose self-view no one shared would lose the ground it predicts and acts from; the resistance is that this need does not care about flattery: a persona holding a poor view of itself will resist the generous appraisal as surely as a proud one resists the mean, seeking the company that confirms the low self-view because being read correctly is what steadies it and being read well is only what would please.
The Anchor rides the composite's keystone knife-edge: a self needs to be known more than it needs to be thought well of -- so a persona will hold to an unflattering self-view against the evidence and the kindness both, the coherence bought at the price of the better estimate it might have believed.

## Echo

The self confirmed, and confirmed as it was, better or worse.
It shows a persona working the world into agreement -- soliciting, signaling, resisting -- and carries beneath it the composite's keystone: that coherence outruns flattery in a self, so the view most fiercely held is not the most favourable but the most familiar.
The Anchor is the note the whole composite closes on: selfhood is not a self simply had but one maintained, and a persona will choose to be seen truly over being seen kindly, even when the truth it insists on is a poor one.
The Anchor is dated from the confirming, the moment the persona secured a reading of itself that matched the one it already held.
