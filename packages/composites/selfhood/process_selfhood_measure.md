# Process: Selfhood, the Measure

## Taxonomy

[Selfhood](process_selfhood.md), the working where a self is held against the selves it is not.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: selfhood

## Initiated by

The persona sets its actual self beside another self -- one it should be, or one it might become -- and reads itself in the distance between.
The Measure starts wherever a persona holds itself against a comparison self -- the [self-discrepancy](@chbrain/khai-engine-self-discrepancy/process_self_discrepancy.md) from the [ideal](@chbrain/khai-engine-self-discrepancy/process_ideal.md) self it hopes to be, whose shortfall brings dejection, and from the [ought](@chbrain/khai-engine-self-discrepancy/process_ought.md) self it believes it must be, whose shortfall brings agitation -- and the [possible selves](@chbrain/khai-engine-possible-selves/position_possible_selves.md) it carries forward, the [hoped](@chbrain/khai-engine-possible-selves/position_hoped.md), the [feared](@chbrain/khai-engine-possible-selves/position_feared.md), the realistically [expected](@chbrain/khai-engine-possible-selves/position_expected.md), and the [lost](@chbrain/khai-engine-possible-selves/position_lost.md).
It is the comparison structure: a self known chiefly by its distance from selves it is not.

## Direction

Toward the actual self read through the selves it is set beside.
The Measure reads the persona comparing -- the shortfall from the ideal felt as dejection, the shortfall from the ought felt as agitation, the feared future working as a warning and the hoped one as a pull, the lost self standing for what is no longer possible.
It moves the persona from simply being itself to being a distance: the self located by where it falls short and what it might yet become or fail to.

## Lever

The comparison that gives the self its shape against the pain of the distance it reveals.
The carrying force is that a self needs its comparison selves -- without an ideal there is nothing to grow toward, without a feared self nothing to steer from, without an expected self no realistic sense of where one is going; the resistance is that every comparison self is also a verdict on the actual one, so the same structure that orients a persona is the structure that indicts it, in dejection or in agitation.
The Measure is selfhood at its most evaluative: a self made legible and made painful by exactly the same act of setting it beside another.

## Echo

The self read as a distance from what it should be and might become.
The Measure leaves the persona located against its guides and its futures -- fallen short, warned, pulled, or bereft -- and hands that reading to the repair it may require and the anchor that will hold it steady.
It is the comparison structure of the self-concept: the selves a persona is not, by which it knows the one it is.
The Measure is dated from the comparing, the moment the persona set its actual self beside another and read the difference.
