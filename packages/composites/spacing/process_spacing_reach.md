# Process: Spacing, the Reach

## Taxonomy

[Spacing](process_spacing.md), the bubble pressing against what the room allows.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: spacing

## Initiated by

A persona takes a place in the room, and finds the distance is not theirs to set.
The Reach starts wherever [proxemics](@chbrain/khai-engine-proxemics/process_proxemics.md) is running -- the [zone](@chbrain/khai-engine-proxemics/process_zone.md) appropriate to the tie, the [intrusion](@chbrain/khai-engine-proxemics/process_intrusion.md) that fires when a body is closer than the tie warrants, and the [equilibrium](@chbrain/khai-engine-proxemics/process_equilibrium.md) that holds total closeness at a tolerable level by trading one channel against another.
It is the face of the composite where the persona adjusts and does not know they are adjusting.

## Direction

Toward a level of closeness that will be reached one way or another.
The Reach reads the compensation rather than the discomfort: a persona held closer than the tie warrants does not simply endure it, they withdraw on some other channel -- the eyes go elsewhere, the body turns, the talk stays shallow, the visit is short -- and a persona held further away than the tie warrants leans in, holds a gaze longer, or raises their voice to close the gap.
It moves whatever is still free to move, since the one thing that is not free is where the furniture is.

## Lever

The requirement that closeness balance against a room that has fixed one of its terms.
The carrying force is that the balancing is automatic and reliable: it is not a preference the persona is indulging but a regulation running underneath the conversation, and it will find some channel, which is why it never simply fails; the resistance is that the channels are not interchangeable in what they cost -- distance is cheap to adjust and invisible when it works, while the substitutes are not, since a withdrawn gaze reads as coldness, a shallow topic reads as having nothing to say, and an early exit reads as a judgement about the company.
The Reach spends the persona's warmth to pay a bill the room presented.

## Echo

A body at the wrong distance, and the payment made somewhere else.
It is the bridge that explains why an arrangement shows up as a mood: the adjustment is real, it is visible, and it looks exactly like a disposition.
What it hands upward is a room full of people behaving in ways that have an obvious explanation and a true one, and only the obvious one is in the room.
The Reach is dated from where the persona had to sit.
