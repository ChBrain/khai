# Instructions: Spacing

How a Playwright wires the spacing composite into a play. You wire by linking the play's own
content to the composite's files; the files stay untouched, they are canon. The composite
installs the space and proxemics engines as dependencies, and their laws arrive with them.
Authoring guidance, not runtime content, and it does not go on tour.

## Human

- The human sets how the rooms in the play are arranged, and which gatherings will be judged by what happened in them.

## Agent

- From the play's own content, read a gathering and link under Projection what is running: the fixture while the arrangement stands as it was left by somebody absent, affording what it affords and inducing what it induces (from space), the reach while a persona's portable bubble is pressing against a distance the room has fixed and the equilibrium is paying for it on another channel (from proxemics), the verdict while the outcome is being explained by the people who were there. Read the room and never the temperament, since the same people in a different arrangement are different people. You link to the composite, you never edit it.

## Collaboration

- Affordance, territory, and atmosphere are the space engine's; the zone, the intrusion, and the equilibrium are proxemics' -- one alone is a place or a process, not the conversion. What a place comes to mean to somebody who lives in it is dwelling's, and this composite needs no history with the room; how a settlement is made readable and how its street runs is peopling's, which works at the scale of a district where this works at the scale of a room; a crowd's own effects are midst's, which turn on how many others are present where this turns on where they can be, so three people in a badly arranged room run this with no crowd at all; the arrangement of a **made thing** so a choice goes one way is artifice's, where a maker chose it for that and here nobody did; what a group has established about what may be said is airing's, and a play may run both and find they disagree; a distance chosen and defended against a rival is vigil's, and these distances are imposed. This composite owns the conversion -- and the verdict bridge carries the keystone: the arrangement sets the ceiling on contact and takes none of the credit.

## Knowledge

- Spacing is a gathering read while the room's arrangement decides what distances are available in it -- braiding the setting (space: the physical place as a party to what happens in it, acting on whoever enters through what it affords, what can be claimed in it, and the mood it induces) with the requirement (proxemics: the portable bubble whose size encodes the relationship, and the equilibrium that holds total closeness at a tolerable level across channels). The join is Osmond's and Sommer's. Osmond, designing psychiatric wards, named the distinction: an arrangement is sociofugal when it holds people apart -- chairs in a row against a wall, everybody shoulder to shoulder and nobody face to face -- and sociopetal when it draws them together. Sommer and Ross tested it on a geriatric day room that was newly furnished, pleasant, and quiet; they moved the chairs off the walls into small groups around tables, and conversation roughly doubled. Nothing about the patients had changed, and nothing about the patients was what had been wrong. It runs in three faces. The fixture is the arrangement, and it is not a trick: rooms are laid out for cleaning, for capacity, for sightlines, for the fire regulations, by somebody who was solving a different problem and is not present -- so there is no designer to blame and often no design at all. But a background does not get looked at, which is what makes it a background, so the one feature of the situation that is fixed and consequential is also the one nobody will name. The reach is the bubble meeting it: a persona held closer than the tie warrants does not endure it, they withdraw on another channel -- eyes elsewhere, body turned, talk kept shallow, visit cut short -- and one held further away leans in or raises their voice. The regulation never fails; it only picks a channel. And the channels are not interchangeable in what they cost, since distance is cheap and invisible when it works while a withdrawn gaze reads as coldness and an early exit reads as a judgement about the company. The verdict is the twist: the people are visible, mobile, and each did something specific, while the arrangement is fixed, silent, and was there first, so the causal search stops at the only moving parts. Here the two are seen for one thing: the arrangement sets the ceiling on contact and takes none of the credit, so a room is praised or written off through the people in it -- and the test that would settle it is moving the furniture, which is cheap and almost never tried, because nobody proposes rearranging a room to check a claim about somebody's temperament.

## System

- Do read the room and never the temperament: name whether the fixture (an arrangement standing), the reach (a bubble pressing), or the verdict (an outcome being explained) is running, and cast each from its engines.
- Do fix the arrangement before anybody speaks and show it to the audience first, so the cause is in view before the effect.
- Do never let a character remark on it: a persona who named the seating would sound as though they were changing the subject, and a play that lets somebody say it was the chairs has replaced the mechanism with a diagnosis.
- Do let the compensation show on a channel other than distance -- gaze, orientation, the depth of the topic, the length of the visit -- because that is where it goes and that is what looks like character.
- Do let the verdict be sincere, specific, and about somebody: the persona reaching for it is doing ordinary causal work and would be right most of the time.
- Do keep the arrangement innocent: nobody set it out against anybody, and giving it an author turns this into a different composite.
- Do not stage this as temperament being an illusion or rooms determining behaviour: people do differ, and most of what happens in a room is about them.
- Do not model what a place comes to mean over time (that is dwelling), a district and its street (that is peopling), the effects of numbers (that is midst), an arrangement chosen to move a decision (that is artifice), what a group has established about speech (that is airing), or a distance chosen and defended (that is vigil). Do not make a room stand for a theme (that is the play's pitch). Do not edit the composite's files; wire only from the play's side.
