# Process: Spacing, the Fixture

## Taxonomy

[Spacing](process_spacing.md), the arrangement as it stands, set by somebody absent.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: spacing

## Initiated by

The room is already laid out when anybody arrives.
The Fixture starts wherever [space](@chbrain/khai-engine-space/place_space.md) is doing its work -- what the setting [affords](@chbrain/khai-engine-space/place_affordance.md) by its form, what can be claimed and held in it as [territory](@chbrain/khai-engine-space/place_territory.md), and the [atmosphere](@chbrain/khai-engine-space/place_atmosphere.md) it induces before a word is said.
It is the face of the composite where nothing is happening and everything is decided.

## Direction

Toward a set of distances that are available and a set that are not.
The Fixture reads the arrangement as an argument rather than as scenery: chairs in a row along a wall put everybody shoulder to shoulder and nobody face to face, a table of a certain width makes one distance compulsory, a corridor with nowhere to stand makes a conversation a decision -- and each of these was settled by somebody who is not in the room and had other reasons.
It moves nobody, and narrows what anybody can do next.

## Lever

The ordinariness of an arrangement against how much of the evening it has already settled.
The carrying force is that the arrangement is not a trick and usually was not aimed at anybody: rooms are laid out for cleaning, for capacity, for sightlines, for the fire regulations, and the person who set them out was solving a different problem -- so there is no designer to blame and often no design at all; the resistance is that a background does not get looked at, which is what makes it a background, so the one feature of the situation that is fixed and consequential is also the one nobody will name -- and a persona who did name it would sound as though they were changing the subject.
The Fixture spends nothing, and quietly sets the range.

## Echo

A room, arranged, with its argument already made.
It is the bridge that puts the cause in view before the effect, so that the audience has seen the chairs before anybody gets blamed.
What it hands upward is a range of permitted distances, and the fact that nothing in the room will remind anybody it was a range.
The Fixture is dated from whenever it was last moved, which is usually long ago and for another reason.
