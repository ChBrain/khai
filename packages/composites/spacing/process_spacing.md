# Process: Spacing

## Taxonomy

What a room's arrangement does to the distances the people in it can hold.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: spacing

## Initiated by

People are in a room somebody else laid out, and they have distances they need to keep.
Spacing starts wherever those two meet: a physical setting acting on whoever enters and claimed by them in return, through [space](@chbrain/khai-engine-space/place_space.md); and the portable bubble whose size encodes the relationship, through [proxemics](@chbrain/khai-engine-proxemics/process_proxemics.md).
This composite adds no third engine. It reads the one thing the two make together, which is that a room can make a distance unavailable and cannot make anybody stop needing it.

## Direction

Toward less contact, or more, and an explanation that names the wrong cause.
The composite wires the two engines and reads their working in three shapes: [the Fixture](process_spacing_fixture.md), the arrangement as it stands; [the Reach](process_spacing_reach.md), the bubble pressing against it; and [the Verdict](process_spacing_verdict.md), the twist -- what everybody concludes, which is about the people.
The three are not three descriptions of a room. They are a constraint, a requirement, and the misattribution that follows from their meeting.

## Lever

What the arrangement permits against what the bodies in it require.
The carrying force is that both are real and neither is negotiable in the moment: chairs are where they are, a table is as wide as it is, and a persona's tolerable distance is not something they can decide to change on being asked -- so the two meet without either giving, in a room where nobody has done anything; the resistance is that the arrangement is invisible in the way a background is invisible, having been set by somebody who is not present and never mentioned since, so the only things left in view when an explanation is wanted are the people.
Spacing is read on the room and never on the temperament, because the same people in a different arrangement are different people.

## Echo

A gathering that went a certain way, and a reason given that will not survive moving the chairs.
Spacing does not close; it is an arrangement meeting a set of bodies, gathered into one reading -- a fixture, a reach, a verdict.
It hands off what it is not: a place's meaning to somebody who lives in it -- attachment, restoration, the third place -- is dwelling's; how a settlement is made readable and how its street life runs is peopling's; a crowd's own effects on a persona is midst's; the arrangement of a **made thing** so a choice goes one way is artifice's, where the object is designed to move a decision rather than a body; how a room comes to be safe to speak in is airing's; what a persona does to hold a distance from a rival is vigil's; and a room made to **stand for** a theme is authored key, the play's pitch, not the force.
The gathering is dated from whoever set the chairs out, and they are not in the scene.
