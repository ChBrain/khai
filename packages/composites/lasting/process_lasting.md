# Process: Lasting

## Taxonomy

Whether anything of a good thing survives its arrival.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: lasting

## Initiated by

Something good has happened to a persona, and the question is no longer whether it happened but what will be left of it.
Lasting starts wherever a good is being made more or less of -- the attention that enlarges a pleasure or kills it, through [savoring](@chbrain/khai-engine-savoring/process_savoring.md); the reception the telling meets, through [capitalization](@chbrain/khai-engine-capitalization/position_capitalization.md); and the return to the level held before, through [hedonic adaptation](@chbrain/khai-engine-hedonic-adaptation/process_hedonic_adaptation.md).
This composite adds no fourth engine; it reads what the three make together: a good thing does not keep itself.

## Direction

Toward the whole fate of a good, read through what becomes of it.
The composite wires the three engines and reads their working in three shapes: [the Attending](process_lasting_attending.md), the good made fuller or thinner by the notice paid it; [the Telling](process_lasting_telling.md), the good carried outward and waiting on a reply; and [the Fading](process_lasting_fading.md), the twist -- the good absorbed as the feeling comes home to its level.
The first two are things a persona and a partner can do; the third happens whether or not anybody does anything, and it is the one that finishes.

## Lever

The work that makes a good last against the reversion that undoes it anyway.
The carrying force is that attention and a witness genuinely change the size of a good -- a moment dwelt in is larger than the same moment rushed past, and a delight told to someone who turns toward it pays back more than it cost; the resistance is that none of it is permanent, because a feeling is a reaction to change, and a change that becomes the new constant stops being a change, so the response to it decays by the weakening of the reaction, by the rising of the standard, and by the pull of a dispositional baseline.
Lasting is read wherever a persona is granted a good and then asked what is left of it: not a self whose fortunes accumulate but one who returns, repeatedly, to their own level.

## Echo

The persona revealed by what survives of what went right.
Lasting does not close; it is the arc of a good running, gathering the three engines into one reading -- a pleasure attended, a delight told, a change absorbed.
It hands off what it is not: the present feeling itself, the mood a persona carries beneath the moment, is the mood engine's; the resting temperament that sets the height of the baseline is the temperament engine's; the softening of what hurts rather than the heightening of what pleases is the coping side, held by the resilience composite; the valuation of an outcome by its distance is the reward engine's; the tolerance that drives a dependency to need ever more is the addiction engine's; the measuring of one's lot against other people's rather than one's own past is the bias engine's; and the windfall made to stand for a story is authored key, the play's pitch, not the force.
The persona is dated from the arrival, and read as a self whose gains are borrowed against a level that will ask for them back.
