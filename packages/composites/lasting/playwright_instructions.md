# Instructions: Lasting

How a Playwright wires the lasting composite into a play. You wire by linking the play's
own content to the composite's files; the files stay untouched, they are canon. The
composite installs the savoring, capitalization, and hedonic-adaptation engines as
dependencies, and their laws arrive with them. Authoring guidance, not runtime content,
and it does not go on tour.

## Human

- The human sets the fate of a persona's good: which good thing is read as being made more or less of -- attended to or rushed past, told and answered or told and let fall, or simply absorbed as the feeling comes home to its level -- and, the question that governs the read, which fate it is meeting: the attending, the telling, or the fading.

## Agent

- From the play's own content, read a persona through what is left of what went right: link under Projection the fate the good is meeting -- the attending while the notice paid is deciding its size (the narrowing that lets it fill the field, the banking of it as a memory sharpened by its own brevity, the kill-joy turn that hunts the flaw and thins it, from savoring), the telling while it is out of the persona's hands and waiting on a reply (the sharing that amplifies, and the responder's four receptions, only one of which builds the bond, from capitalization), the fading while the change is being absorbed (the response worn down, the standard risen to swallow the gain, the level reclaiming the persona, from hedonic-adaptation). Play the persona as holding a good that will not keep itself, present in what they do with it and what is done to it, never narrated, and never made to stand for a story. You link to the composite, you never edit it.

## Collaboration

- The attention that enlarges or kills a pleasure is the savoring engine's; the reception a telling meets is capitalization's; the return to baseline is hedonic-adaptation's -- one fate alone is not the fate of a good. The persisting affective background is the mood engine's; the resting disposition that sets the height of the baseline is temperament's; the softening of what hurts is the coping side, held by the resilience composite; the weighing of an outcome by its distance is the reward engine's; tolerance and the needing of ever more is addiction's; measuring one's lot against other people's rather than one's own past is the bias engine's. This composite owns the arc between a good's arrival and the persona's return to their own level -- and the fading bridge carries the keystone: what returns a persona from the worst is what takes the best away.

## Knowledge

- Lasting is a good thing read for what survives of it -- braiding the attention that makes a pleasure fuller or kills it (savoring), the reception the telling meets (capitalization), and the return to the level held before (hedonic-adaptation) into one arc -- read as a wiring of those three engines, and its question is which fate the good is meeting (Bryant on savoring as the counterpart to coping; Bryant and Veroff's ways and kill-joy thinking; Langston's capitalization; Gable's responder typology; Brickman and Campbell's treadmill; the lottery-winners-and-accident-victims symmetry; Diener, Lucas and Scollon on the setpoint's limits). It runs in three fates. The attending is the notice paid before anyone else is involved -- a good fully met is larger than the same good half-noticed, a moment banked and felt as fleeting is deepened rather than soured -- and it is one lever, so the faculty that enlarges a pleasure is the faculty that can kill it, and some personas cannot receive a good without undercutting it as it arrives. The telling is the good carried outward, the strongest single amplifier there is, and then out of the persona's control: only the reply that turns toward the news and draws it out builds the bond, while the steady nod makes nothing of it, the deflation dressed as realism is felt more sharply than absence, and the turning-away teaches the sharer not to bring the next one -- and there is no neutral answer, since the withheld reply is itself one. The fading is the twist -- the response worn down by repetition, the standard climbing exactly as fast as the persona advances, the dispositional level reclaiming them -- where the arc is seen for what it is: a feeling is a reaction to change, and a change that becomes the constant stops being one, so the baseline is not a flaw but the machinery working; it simply does not know which direction anybody wanted, and takes the windfall back with the same patience it uses on the wound. Read a persona as returning, repeatedly, to their own level, and read the fading as mercy and theft at once.

## System

- Do read the persona through what is left of a good, never through the good alone: name whether the attending (the notice paid), the telling (the answer drawn), or the fading (the level reclaiming) is at work, and cast each from its engines.
- Do keep the fates distinct: the attending is private and the persona's own, the telling is out of their hands, the fading happens whether or not anyone acts.
- Do let the fading carry the keystone: the return that erases the best of a life is the return that carries a persona back from the worst.
- Do remember there is no neutral reception: a persona that guards its enthusiasm has answered, and the sharer read the answer.
- Do remember savoring cannot manufacture a good: it can only make more of one that is there, or less -- a play that has a persona savour their way out of a bad situation has broken the fate.
- Do remember the setpoint is real but not immovable: sustained change and deliberate practice can move it slowly; what cannot be done is to make a circumstance go on feeling like a change once it has become the constant.
- Do not model the feeling itself (that is mood), the height of the baseline (that is temperament), the softening of what hurts (that is resilience), the weighing of an outcome by its distance (that is reward), the needing of ever more (that is addiction), or the comparison against other people (that is bias). Do not make the good stand for a story (that is the play's pitch). Do not edit the composite's files; wire only from the play's side.
