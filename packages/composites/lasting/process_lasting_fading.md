# Process: Lasting, the Fading

## Taxonomy

[Lasting](process_lasting.md), the fate a good meets whether or not anybody does anything, where the arc is seen for what it is.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: lasting

## Initiated by

The good has stopped being news, and the feeling it brought is on its way back to the level that was there before it.
The Fading starts wherever [hedonic adaptation](@chbrain/khai-engine-hedonic-adaptation/process_hedonic_adaptation.md) is running -- the [habituation](@chbrain/khai-engine-hedonic-adaptation/process_habituation.md) that wears the response down by repetition until the luxury stops feeling like luxury, the [aspiration](@chbrain/khai-engine-hedonic-adaptation/process_aspiration.md) that raises the standard exactly as fast as the persona advances so each gain is swallowed by the bar it sets, and the [setpoint](@chbrain/khai-engine-hedonic-adaptation/process_setpoint.md) that reclaims the persona to their own characteristic altitude.
It is the fate no attending and no witness can call off.

## Direction

Toward a persona back at their own level with their circumstances unchanged.
The Fading reads the reversion at work -- the raise that thrilled for a month, the house that was a marvel for a season and is now the place the keys are kept, the achievement that has become the merely owed -- each a gain kept in full while the satisfaction it was got for has quietly gone.
It moves the persona from changed to unchanged: the situation permanently better and the feeling exactly where it started, which is why chasing circumstances buys less lasting well-being than it promises.

## Lever

The mercy of the return against the theft of it.
The carrying force is that a feeling is a reaction to change, and a system that went on signalling a constant would be signalling nothing -- so the baseline is not a flaw in the machinery but the machinery working, reserving affect for what moves and freeing the persona from what does not; the resistance is only that the machinery does not know which direction anybody wanted, and it takes the windfall back with the same patience it uses on the wound.
The Fading rides the composite's keystone knife-edge: the return to baseline that erases the best of a life is the same return that carries a persona back from the worst -- the level reclaims from fortune and disaster alike, which is why lasting happiness is so stubborn to raise by circumstance and, mercifully, so resilient to lower.

## Echo

The change absorbed, and the persona home at their own altitude.
It shows the good finally reclaimed -- the response worn down, the standard risen, the level reasserted -- and carries beneath it the composite's keystone: what a persona would have to give up to keep a joy permanent is the same thing that will one day return them from a grief, so the fading is not a defect to be beaten but the price of being survivable.
The baseline is not wholly fixed, and sustained change or deliberate practice can slowly move it; what cannot be done is to make a circumstance go on feeling like a change once it has become the constant.
The Fading is the note the whole composite closes on: what could not be kept was never kept by anyone, and the persona is not failing at it.
The Fading is dated from the levelling, the moment a good stopped registering and the situation stayed exactly as good as it was.
