# Process: Lasting, the Telling

## Taxonomy

[Lasting](process_lasting.md), the fate a good meets in the answer it draws.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: lasting

## Initiated by

The good has been carried out of the single mind and is now waiting on somebody else.
The Telling starts where [savoring by expression](@chbrain/khai-engine-savoring/process_sharing.md) hands the good over -- the strongest single amplifier there is, the pleasure grown in the telling -- and where [capitalization](@chbrain/khai-engine-capitalization/position_capitalization.md) decides what becomes of it: the [active-constructive](@chbrain/khai-engine-capitalization/position_active_constructive.md) reply that turns fully toward the news and draws it out, alone of the four in building the bond; the [passive-constructive](@chbrain/khai-engine-capitalization/position_passive_constructive.md) nod that holds steady without making anything of it; the [active-destructive](@chbrain/khai-engine-capitalization/position_active_destructive.md) deflation that looks like attention and works against the news; and the [passive-destructive](@chbrain/khai-engine-capitalization/position_passive_destructive.md) turning-away that leaves the sharer talking into quiet.
It is the one fate a good meets that the persona who owns it does not control.

## Direction

Toward a good doubled or a good returned smaller than it went out.
The Telling reads the reply landing -- the friend who stops everything and asks for the whole story, the partner who says that's nice and returns to their day, the one who wonders aloud whether the promotion means more travel -- each an answer given in a small moment that most personas underrate, and each read exactly by the one who brought the news.
It moves the good from private to jointly held: enlarged and the tie warmer for it, or shrunk and the sharer taught not to bring the next one.

## Lever

The gain that only a witness can supply against the fact that there is no neutral reply.
The carrying force is that a good expressed and met pays back more than it cost -- the telling makes the persona relive it and the witness's gladness adds to it, which is why the response to good news predicts a bond as strongly as the response to bad; the resistance is that the responder cannot decline the position: there is no answer that is not one, the withheld or lukewarm reply is itself an answer, and engaged deflation is felt more sharply than absence.
The Telling is lasting at its most exposed: a persona's good placed in another's hands with no way to take it back.

## Echo

The good enlarged by a witness, or spent on one.
The Telling leaves the good doubled and the tie stronger, or leaves the sharer more alone with it than before they spoke -- and either way hands what remains to the fading, which will work on it regardless of how it was received.
The persona who guards its enthusiasm has not stayed neutral; it has answered.
The Telling is dated from the reply, the moment a good passed into somebody else's keeping and came back a different size.
