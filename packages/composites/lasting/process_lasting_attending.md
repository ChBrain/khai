# Process: Lasting, the Attending

## Taxonomy

[Lasting](process_lasting.md), the fate a good meets in the notice it is paid.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: lasting

## Initiated by

A good thing is happening, and the persona either turns toward it or does not.
The Attending starts wherever [savoring](@chbrain/khai-engine-savoring/process_savoring.md) is at work or refused -- the [sharpening](@chbrain/khai-engine-savoring/process_sharpening.md) that narrows the whole field of notice onto a single pleasure so it is fully met rather than half-felt, the [keeping](@chbrain/khai-engine-savoring/process_keeping.md) that banks the moment as a memory and lets its own brevity concentrate it, and the [dampening](@chbrain/khai-engine-savoring/process_dampening.md) that throws the same lever the other way, hunting the flaw, deflecting the praise, and thinning the good on purpose.
It is the fate a good meets before anyone else has heard of it.

## Direction

Toward a good enlarged, or a good cut short, by the same faculty.
The Attending reads the notice being paid -- the meal actually tasted, the walk taken slowly because it is nearly over, the compliment argued down, the holiday spent dreading its end -- each a good of the same size met by an attention that made more of it or less.
It moves the persona from having a good to having what they made of it: not the event, which is fixed, but the amount of it that was let in.

## Lever

The attention that amplifies a pleasure against the same attention aimed at its flaw.
The carrying force is that a good left unattended fades at its own rate and a good attended to does not -- focus intensifies it, memory extends it, and the knowing that it will end, held rightly, sharpens rather than sours it; the resistance is that the lever is one lever, so the faculty that enlarges a pleasure is the faculty that can kill it, and some personas cannot receive a good moment without undercutting it as it arrives.
The Attending is lasting at its most private: what a persona does with a good before anybody else is involved.

## Echo

The good sized by the notice it was paid.
The Attending leaves the persona with more or less of a pleasure than the event alone would have given -- fuller for having been fully met, or thin and quick for having been rushed past or refused -- and hands what remains to the telling and the fading that will work on it next.
It cannot manufacture a good; it can only make more of a good that is there, or less.
The Attending is dated from the turning, the moment a persona leaned into a pleasure or away from it.
