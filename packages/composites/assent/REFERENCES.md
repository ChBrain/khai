# Assent: Reference

## Line of Work

Assent as **composite**: how a persona holds something true, and what evidence can reach. It is
not a fourth mechanism but a wiring of three: the **belief** engine supplies the conviction that
disconfirming evidence bears on and forces to a settlement, the **superstition** engine supplies
the link between things that share no mechanism and cannot be disproved from within, and the
**faith** engine supplies the standing orientation held prior to and apart from any single claim
proved or disproved. This composite adds no fourth engine; it reads the seam the three make --
three distances between a holding and the evidence that could touch it -- and stages that seam
into three bridges, closing on the discovery that the last two are unarguable for opposite
reasons.

The domain does not model any single distance -- update, rationalize, deny, convert, and suspend
are the belief engine's; contagion, similarity, control, omen, and luck are superstition's; trust
and surrender are faith's; and one alone is a process or a position, not the shape. Nor is it the
repair of a persona's account of their own conduct, which is the squaring composite's; nor the
judging of a likelihood under uncertainty, which is the estimation composite's; nor the felt
weather of meeting the unknown, which is the knowing composite's; nor a persona's monitoring of
its own knowing, which is the metacognition engine's. It owns the relation: not what a persona
holds, but what would have to happen for them to stop.

The spine is the demarcation problem, taken as a description of persons rather than of theories.
Popper set the terms: what marks a claim as empirical is that some observation could count
against it, and the immunising stratagem -- the auxiliary account that absorbs every failure --
is how a claim keeps the appearance of saying something while giving up the risk. Evans-Pritchard
had already supplied the ethnographic demonstration in Zande witchcraft belief, where a failed
oracle is met by a secondary elaboration ready to hand (the taboo was broken, the medicine was
weak) so that no single failure ever reaches the system, and the believer is not being stupid: each
individual explanation is locally reasonable, and the closure is a property of the web rather than
of any one move. That is the sealed bridge exactly, and the superstition engine states the same
finding in its own terms -- the read changes when the persona stops granting the link its power,
not when an outcome contradicts it. Against that, Wittgenstein's _On Certainty_ supplies the
distinction the composite turns on: some propositions stand fast not because they have been
defended but because they are what defending is done from -- hinges on which the door of doubt
turns, exempt from doubt as a condition of doubting anything. James's account of religious life,
the sense of an unseen order and of one's supreme good as lying in harmonious relation to it, is
that structure lived rather than analysed, and Pargament shows it functioning as an orienting
stance drawn on hardest under strain. The finding the composite exists to state is that the sealed
and the prior share the property Popper's test detects and are not the same kind of thing: one is
a claim that has withdrawn from the risk it appeared to take, the other never took it. The three
bridges are those three distances: the exposed, the sealed, and the prior.

## Origin

The psychology of belief change, the anthropology of magical thinking, and the epistemology of
what stands fast, wired as a composite over the belief, superstition, and faith engines.

## Restrictions

What the composite refuses to model, and to whom it delegates.

- **The single distances (the atoms)**: The composite wires the three but owns none. Update,
  rationalize, deny, convert, and suspend are the belief engine's; contagion, similarity, control,
  omen, and luck are superstition's; trust and surrender are faith's. Assent reads only the
  relation the three make; one distance alone is a process or a position, not the shape.
- **The account of one's own conduct (squaring)**: Where what is being defended is the persona's
  picture of themselves -- a cause placed to spare the self, a belief bent to cover a deed, a
  standard suspended for one act -- that is the squaring composite's. Assent owns the holding of a
  claim about the world and what evidence can do to it; when the object of the defence is the
  persona rather than the claim, it is squaring's.
- **Judging under uncertainty (estimation)**: Reading a likelihood off an anchor, off ease of
  recall, or off a resemblance is the estimation composite's -- the forming of a judgment. Assent
  is the maintaining of a holding once formed.
- **The feeling of meeting the unknown (knowing)**: Surprise, interest, confusion, and awe are the
  knowing composite's. Assent reads what the holding does about the evidence, not what the persona
  feels about the gap.
- **Knowing about one's own knowing (metacognition)**: The meta-level monitoring of whether one
  knows, and the control steered back on it, is the metacognition engine's. Assent reads the
  distance from evidence, which is mostly not available to that monitoring.
- **The standing tilt (bias)**: The catalogued slants held as positions are the bias engine's.
  Assent reads a particular holding's relation to a particular reading.
- **The bet on a person (trust)**: The persona's wager on another's reliability, extended, tested,
  withheld, betrayed, or repaired, is the trust engine's. The trust inside this composite is
  faith's -- reliance on an unseen order, not on somebody.
- **Particular contents held (belief-in-a-just-world, moral-conviction)**: The standing conviction
  that the world is fair is the belief-in-a-just-world engine's; attitudes held as moral absolutes
  are the moral-conviction engine's, inside the conscience composite. Assent is indifferent to
  what is held and reads only how.
- **The holding as authored symbol (the play's pitch)**: A conviction made to **stand for** a story
  -- the superstition that mirrors a theme of helplessness, the faith that signals a redemption --
  is refused: that is authored key, the play's pitch, not the force on the persona. Assent sets
  the distance; the meaning read into it is the play's.

## Encoding

Source to constraint. A composite root over three engine atoms it declares as dependencies, with
three bridges, the distances between a holding and the evidence that bears on it.

- **[assent](process_assent.md)** (the root): not what a persona holds but at what distance the
  evidence stands from it -- the correction a reachable holding permits against the footing an
  unreachable one supplies. Anchored by Popper's demarcation, read as a description of persons.
- **[the exposed](process_assent_exposed.md)** (bridge): the conviction a reading can press on,
  settled by update, rationalization, denial, conversion, or suspension -- the only distance where
  a settlement is possible, and the one where update is merely one exit of five. Anchored by
  Festinger (1957; 1956) and Lord, Ross & Lepper (1979).
- **[the sealed](process_assent_sealed.md)** (bridge): the link that makes predictions none of
  which can fail, every null result routed into an account of why it does not count. Anchored by
  Evans-Pritchard (1937) on secondary elaboration, Popper on immunising stratagems, and Rozin &
  Nemeroff and Langer on the content the seal is built around.
- **[the prior](process_assent_prior.md)** (bridge): the orientation held before any tenet is
  stated, shown in the leaning and never in the case made, whose absence of evidence is not a
  dodge. Anchored by Wittgenstein (1969) on hinge propositions and James (1902); the composite's
  twist and keystone, that being out of reach of evidence is not one condition but two, and only
  one of them is an evasion.
