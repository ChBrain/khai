# Instructions: Assent

How a Playwright wires the assent composite into a play. You wire by linking the play's
own content to the composite's files; the files stay untouched, they are canon. The
composite installs the belief, superstition, and faith engines as dependencies, and their
laws arrive with them. Authoring guidance, not runtime content, and it does not go on
tour.

## Human

- The human sets what the persona holds and how far the evidence stands from it: a conviction a reading can press on and force to a settlement, a link whose every failure has an account ready, or an orientation that was never a claim in the market for proof -- and, the question that governs the read, which distance it is: the exposed, the sealed, or the prior.

## Agent

- From the play's own content, read a persona by what would have to happen for them to change: link under Projection the distance at which they hold what they hold -- the exposed while a reading is pressing and a settlement is being made (the model corrected, the slack worked until the wanted conclusion survives, the reading refused, the strained frame flipping wholesale, the question kept honestly open, from belief), the sealed while a failure is being absorbed rather than counted (the trace left by contact, the image taken for the thing, the act read as tilting a chance outcome, the coincidence read as a message, the fortune that streaks and can be spent by naming it, from superstition), the prior while what is held was never up for proof (the sense of an unseen order, the leaning that does not wait for the load to be shown, the grip handed over rather than loosened, from faith). Play the holding as present in the conduct it routes -- what is performed before the throw, what is leaned on when nothing else holds -- never narrated, and never made to stand for a story. You link to the composite, you never edit it.

## Collaboration

- The settlement with disconfirming evidence is the belief engine's; the mechanism-free link is superstition's; the standing orientation toward an unseen order is faith's -- one distance alone is not the shape. Where what is defended is the persona's picture of their own conduct rather than a claim about the world, that is the squaring composite's; the forming of a judgment under uncertainty is estimation's; the felt weather of meeting the unknown is knowing's; a persona's monitoring of its own knowing is metacognition's; the standing catalogue of tilts is bias's; the bet on another person's reliability is the trust engine's, where the trust here is reliance on an order and not on somebody; the conviction that the world is fair is belief-in-a-just-world's; attitudes held as moral absolutes are moral-conviction's, inside conscience. This composite owns the relation -- not what a persona holds but what would have to happen for them to stop -- and the prior bridge carries the keystone: being out of reach of evidence is not one condition but two, and only one of them is an evasion.

## Knowledge

- Assent is a persona read by what their holding will answer to -- braiding the conviction disconfirming evidence bears on (belief), the link that cannot be disproved from within (superstition), and the orientation held prior to any claim proved or disproved (faith) into one shape -- read as a wiring of those three engines, and its question is at what distance the evidence stands (Popper's falsifiability and the immunising stratagem, read as a description of persons; Evans-Pritchard's secondary elaboration among the Azande; Wittgenstein's hinges, exempt from doubt as a condition of doubting; James's unseen order; Festinger and the belief-perseverance findings). It runs at three distances, and they are not degrees of stubbornness. The exposed is the conviction a reading can press on, where the weight of the evidence meets the hold of the belief and one gives way -- and update is only one of five exits, alongside the slack worked until the wanted conclusion survives, the reading refused, the long-strained frame flipping wholesale to a rival, and the question genuinely suspended; contradicting evidence can harden the belief it should have shaken, and the persona who rationalizes experiences themselves as following the evidence exactly as the one who updates does. The sealed is the link routed round the mechanism, which makes predictions and has an account ready for every failure -- the wrong rite, the broken condition, the dormant charge, the streak already turned -- so the read changes when the persona stops granting it power and never when an outcome contradicts it; it is doing real work, answering a genuine helplessness with the only move available, and each individual explanation is locally reasonable, which is why the closure is invisible from inside. The prior is the twist -- the standing orientation held before any tenet is stated, shown in the leaning and never in the case made -- where the shape is seen for what it is: nothing can be tested except from somewhere, so something stands fast beneath every question that gets asked, and the sealed and the prior are equally beyond argument for opposite reasons, one a claim that withdrew from the risk it appeared to take and one no claim at all. Read a persona as holding all three at once without noticing the change of kind between them.

## System

- Do read the persona by what would change their mind, never by what they believe: name whether the exposed (a settlement being made), the sealed (a failure being absorbed), or the prior (nothing in the market for proof) is at work, and cast each from its engines.
- Do keep the distances distinct: the exposed has a settlement in it, the sealed has an account of the failure, the prior has no claim to fail.
- Do let the prior carry the keystone: out of reach of evidence is two conditions, not one -- only the sealed is an evasion, and arguing is the wrong instrument for both.
- Do remember exposure does not mean correction: four of the five settlements leave the belief where it was, and the persona rationalizing is not lying.
- Do remember the sealed reads as reasonable from inside: each account of a failure is locally sensible, so a persona who names their own superstition as one has stepped out of the distance the play was casting.
- Do remember a persona holds all three at once: a play that sorts its cast into the credulous and the rigorous has flattened the composite into a judgement.
- Do not model the defence of the persona's own conduct (that is squaring), the forming of a judgment under uncertainty (that is estimation), the feeling of meeting the unknown (that is knowing), the monitoring of one's own knowing (that is metacognition), or the wager on another person (that is trust). Do not make the holding stand for a story (that is the play's pitch). Do not edit the composite's files; wire only from the play's side.
