# Process: Assent, the Prior

## Taxonomy

[Assent](process_assent.md), the distance at which there was never a claim for evidence to reach, and the three are seen for what they are.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: assent

## Initiated by

The persona is standing on something that was not arrived at and is not defended, and it is holding.
The Prior starts wherever [faith](@chbrain/khai-engine-faith/position_faith.md) is in force -- the sense that an unseen order is there and that the persona's own good rests in a harmonious relation to it, held before any tenet is stated or tested; the [trust](@chbrain/khai-engine-faith/position_trust.md) that leans its weight on that order without demanding it first prove the load; and the [surrender](@chbrain/khai-engine-faith/position_surrender.md) in which the claim to control is handed over rather than merely loosened.
It is the distance where the question of evidence does not arise, and its absence is not a dodge.

## Direction

Toward a persona whose footing includes a relation they cannot verify, and who cannot get back to ground that is all visible.
The Prior reads the standing beneath the plans -- what is leaned on when nothing else holds, what the persona does not argue for because they were never arguing, what the room reads under the reasons rather than in them.
It moves the persona nowhere, because it is what they were moving from: not a conclusion in the mind but the floor the mind is standing on, which is why it shows in the leaning and never in the case made.

## Lever

The ground an unprovable footing supplies against the fact that it is unprovable.
The carrying force is that nothing can be tested except from somewhere -- a persona with no holding exempt from doubt has nothing to doubt with, so something stands fast beneath every question that gets asked, and this is a condition of having questions rather than a failure to have answered one; the resistance is that the persona pays for it in kind: the footing includes a relation that cannot be shown, there is no way back to a stance answering only to the demonstrable, and from outside it is indistinguishable from a claim that has simply been rigged.
The Prior rides the composite's keystone knife-edge: being out of reach of evidence is not one condition but two -- a claim built so that nothing counts against it, and no claim at all -- and only the first is an evasion.

## Echo

The unreachable holding, and the two quite different reasons a holding is unreachable.
It shows the ladder complete -- a conviction the world can press on, a link that absorbs every failure, an orientation that was never in the market -- and carries beneath it the composite's keystone: the sealed and the prior look identical by the only test anyone thinks to apply, which is whether the persona would give it up, and they are not the same thing at all; one has taken the risks of a claim and quietly withdrawn from them, and one never made a claim to withdraw from.
The practical consequence is that arguing is the wrong instrument for both, for different reasons, and that a persona can hold all three at once without noticing the change of kind between them.
The Prior is the note the whole composite closes on: what can be corrected can be lost, so what a persona finally stands on is whatever they were never going to lose -- and that is the ground and the delusion both, told apart by which one ever pretended to be evidence.
The Prior is dated from the standing, which is to say from before the persona could date anything.
