# Process: Assent, the Exposed

## Taxonomy

[Assent](process_assent.md), the distance at which evidence reaches the holding and something has to give.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: assent

## Initiated by

A reading has arrived that bears on what the persona holds, and the two cannot both stand as they are.
The Exposed starts wherever [belief](@chbrain/khai-engine-belief/process_belief.md) is being settled -- the [update](@chbrain/khai-engine-belief/process_update.md) in which the model yields exactly as far as the evidence pushes, the [rationalize](@chbrain/khai-engine-belief/process_rationalize.md) that works the slack until the wanted conclusion survives, the [deny](@chbrain/khai-engine-belief/process_deny.md) that refuses the reading outright, the [convert](@chbrain/khai-engine-belief/process_convert.md) in which a long-strained frame does not bend but flips wholesale to a rival that takes up the anomalies at once, and the [suspend](@chbrain/khai-engine-belief/process_suspend.md) that keeps both pressing rather than relieving the tension early.
It is the only distance at which a settlement is possible, because it is the only one where the evidence is admitted to bear.

## Direction

Toward a holding that has been made to answer, one way or another.
The Exposed reads the settling -- the estimate corrected by a figure, the study explained away because of who funded it, the report refused as fabricated, the decades-long position abandoned in an afternoon, the question genuinely left open -- each a different way the weight of a reading met the hold of a conviction.
It moves the persona from holding a claim to having answered for it, and the answer is rarely the one they would have named in advance.

## Lever

The correction the exposure permits against the four other exits it also permits.
The carrying force is that this is what having a belief about the world means -- a conviction the world can press on can also be improved by it, and update, and suspension, are only available here; the resistance is that exposure does not select for update: the settlement turns on which of the two the persona cannot let go, so contradicting evidence can harden the belief it should have shaken, and the persona who rationalizes and the persona who updates both experience themselves as following the evidence.
The Exposed is assent at its most correctable and least reliable: everything can be fixed here, and mostly is not.

## Echo

The holding made to answer, and the answer usually not an update.
The Exposed leaves the persona with a conviction that has met the world once and will have to meet it again at the next reading that bears -- and hands the pattern on to the distances where no reading bears at all.
That is its limit and its whole value: a belief that can be corrected is a belief that can be lost, and only the exposed one is either.
The Exposed is dated from the pressing, the moment a reading arrived that the holding could not simply pass.
