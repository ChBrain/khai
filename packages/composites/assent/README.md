# Assent

Assent as composite: how a persona holds something true, and what evidence can reach -- the belief that disconfirming evidence bears on, and settles by updating, rationalizing, denying, converting, or suspending (belief); the link between things that share no mechanism, which does not yield because it cannot be disproved from within (superstition); and the standing orientation toward an unseen order, held prior to and apart from any single claim proved or disproved (faith) -- across the exposed, the sealed, and the prior, and the drama is that being out of reach of evidence is not one condition but two, and only one of them is an evasion.
