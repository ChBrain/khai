# Process: Assent

## Taxonomy

How a persona holds something true, and what evidence can reach.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: assent

## Initiated by

A persona is holding something, and the question is not whether it is true but what could change it.
Assent starts wherever that relation is live -- the conviction disconfirming evidence bears on and forces to a settlement, through [belief](@chbrain/khai-engine-belief/process_belief.md); the link between things that share no mechanism, which does not yield because it cannot be disproved from within, through [superstition](@chbrain/khai-engine-superstition/position_superstition.md); and the standing orientation toward an unseen order, held prior to and apart from any single claim proved or disproved, through [faith](@chbrain/khai-engine-faith/position_faith.md).
This composite adds no fourth engine; it reads what the three make together: not what a persona holds, but at what distance the evidence stands from it.

## Direction

Toward the whole of what a holding will and will not answer to, read through that distance.
The composite wires the three engines and reads their working in three shapes: [the Exposed](process_assent_exposed.md), the conviction the world can press on; [the Sealed](process_assent_sealed.md), the link whose failures are absorbed rather than counted; and [the Prior](process_assent_prior.md), the twist -- the orientation that was never in the market for proof.
The three are not degrees of stubbornness. They are three different arrangements between a holding and the world, and only the first has a settlement in it.

## Lever

The correction a reachable holding permits against the footing an unreachable one supplies.
The carrying force is that a persona who could be argued out of everything would have nothing to argue from -- something has to stand fast for anything else to be tested against it, and the holdings that no evidence will move are exactly the ones a persona leans on when the visible ground gives way; the resistance is that this is the same property a rigged claim has, so the reliable footing and the sealed delusion are indistinguishable by the one test anyone thinks to apply, which is whether the persona would give it up.
Assent is read wherever a persona is granted the ground they stand on: not a mind weighing propositions but one already at some distance from the evidence, and mostly not choosing the distance.

## Echo

The persona revealed by what would have to happen for them to change.
Assent does not close; it is the holding running, gathering the three engines into one reading -- a conviction pressed, a link sealed, an orientation standing beneath.
It hands off what it is not: the repair of a persona's account of their own conduct, so the self-picture survives what they did, is the squaring composite's, and where the thing being defended is the persona rather than the claim, it is theirs; the standing catalogue of tilts held as positions is the bias engine's; the judging of a likelihood under uncertainty, by anchor, by ease of recall, by resemblance, is the estimation composite's; the felt weather of meeting the unknown, its surprise, interest, confusion, and awe, is the knowing composite's; a persona's knowing about its own knowing, its monitoring and its calibration, is the metacognition engine's; the bet on another's reliability is the trust engine's; the standing conviction that the world is fair is the belief-in-a-just-world engine's; attitudes held as moral absolutes are the moral-conviction engine's, inside the conscience composite; and the holding made to stand for a story is authored key, the play's pitch, not the force.
The persona is dated from the distance, and read as a self whose ground is not all of one kind.
