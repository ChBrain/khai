# Process: Assent, the Sealed

## Taxonomy

[Assent](process_assent.md), the distance at which the holding makes predictions and no failure counts against it.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: assent

## Initiated by

The persona holds a link between things that share no mechanism, and the link has just failed, and nothing has happened to it.
The Sealed starts wherever [superstition](@chbrain/khai-engine-superstition/position_superstition.md) is doing its work -- the [contagion](@chbrain/khai-engine-superstition/position_contagion.md) by which contact leaves a trace that does not wash out, the [similarity](@chbrain/khai-engine-superstition/position_similarity.md) by which the image is the thing, the [control](@chbrain/khai-engine-superstition/position_control.md) that reads one's own act as tilting an outcome chance rules, the [omen](@chbrain/khai-engine-superstition/position_omen.md) that reads a coincidence as a message, and the [luck](@chbrain/khai-engine-superstition/position_luck.md) that treats fortune as a substance which adheres, streaks, and can be spent by naming it.
It is the distance that looks exactly like the exposed one and behaves nothing like it.

## Direction

Toward a persona acting on the world they read rather than the world that answers.
The Sealed reads the absorption at work -- the rite performed wrong, the condition broken, the charm gone dormant, the streak that had already turned -- each a null result routed into an account of why the null result does not count, offered in good faith and usually before anyone has asked.
It moves the persona nowhere: the read changes only when they stop granting the link its power, never when an outcome contradicts it, because there is no outcome that would.

## Lever

The predictions the link makes against the fact that none of them can fail.
The carrying force is that the link is doing real work -- it makes an uncontrollable outcome feel routable, it supplies an act where there was nothing to do, and the persona who performs the rite before the throw is answering a genuine helplessness with the only move available; the resistance is that every failure has a secondary account ready, so the effort and worry spend on links that do nothing and the cost is paid in full whether or not the link was ever there.
The Sealed is assent at its most deceptive: it wears the shape of a claim about the world, it takes the risks a claim takes, and it has quietly given up the one thing a claim is for.

## Echo

The link intact, the outcome absorbed, and the persona still ahead of the evidence.
The Sealed leaves the holding exactly where it was after a test that should have moved it -- which is what distinguishes it from the exposed, where a settlement of some kind is forced -- and hands the question on: whether every holding evidence cannot reach is this.
It is not. That is what the prior is for.
The Sealed is dated from the first absorbed failure, the moment a null result was explained rather than counted.
