# Wildland: Reference

## Line of Work

Wildland as **composite**: the setting read as a living, self-shaping agent -- the land
that makes and breaks its own ground, wears it, floods and parches it, burns it, and
reclaims itself, on its own vast time and indifferent to us. It is not a new force but a
wiring of five: the **geomorphology** engine supplies the ground's sudden violence, the
**erosion** engine the slow surface wearing, the **hydrology** engine the water regime,
the **fire** engine the burn, and the **succession** engine the biological reclaiming.
The **climate** engine is the referenced governing frame, not a wired atom -- it sets the
odds the five play out within (whether the place tends wet or dry, its fire season, its
freeze-thaw) rather than acting on the ground itself. This composite adds no new force; it
reads what the five make together as the land's own life, and stages that into its shapes:
the sudden making and breaking, the slow shaping, and the wild's reclaiming.

The domain does not model any single force -- each is an engine's, and one alone is a
process, not the life; nor the climate that governs the odds, which is the frame the life
runs within; nor the built fabric raised on the ground, which is the space engine's; nor
the persona's feeling for the land, which is the persona's own engines'. It owns the
land's own life: the setting granted agency, read not as the fixed stage the human drama
plays upon but as a thing that made itself before us, shapes itself around us, and will
reclaim the ground after us.

The spine is the shift from reading the land as backdrop to reading it as agent -- and,
at its far edge, the recognition that the land's life is indifferent to the human and
outlasts them. The physical-geography tradition long read the land as a body in motion:
Lyell's earth in process, Hutton's deep cycle, Marsh's early reckoning of how land and
life shape each other. The systems view made the land one working whole, the forces
coupled: Lovelock and Margulis read the living earth as a self-regulating system, the life
and the land shaping one another. And Weisman carried the reading to its sharpest point --
the world without us, the land reclaiming the human's own works the moment the hand
withdraws, the reclaiming that is the human's loss and the land's return at once. The
three bridges are those readings staged: upheaval, wearing, and reclaiming.

## Origin

The physical geography of the land as a living, self-shaping system, wired as a composite
over the five land-forces engines under the climatic frame.

## Restrictions

What the composite refuses to model, and to whom it delegates.

- **The single forces (the atoms)**: The composite wires the five engines but owns none.
  What a given making-and-breaking is -- the uplift, quake, eruption, subsidence -- is
  geomorphology's; a given wearing -- scour, deflation, frost, deposition -- is erosion's;
  a given water -- flood, drought, groundwater, wellspring -- is hydrology's; a given burn
  -- ignition, conflagration, exclusion, renewal -- is fire's; a given reclaiming --
  pioneer, progression, maturity, rewilding -- is succession's. Wildland reads only what
  the five make as a life; a single force alone is a process, not the whole.
- **The climate that governs the odds (climate)**: The climate engine is the frame, not an
  actor. It sets whether a place tends wet or dry, its fire season, its freeze-thaw pace --
  the regime the five forces play out within -- but it does not itself raise the ground or
  burn it. Wildland references climate as the governing frame and reads the five that act
  within it; climate is the odds, wildland the life those odds shape.
- **The built fabric on the ground (space)**: The layout, the walls, the affordances, the
  standing induced mood of a made setting are the space engine's; wildland is the land's
  own life beneath and around what is built on it. Space builds on the ground; wildland is
  the ground living its own life, and reclaiming what is built when the building stops.
- **The persona's feeling, and the land as authored symbol**: How the land's life makes a
  persona feel -- the awe of the deep time, the dread of the reclaiming city, the peace of
  the overgrown ruin -- is the persona's own engines'; wildland delivers the land's own
  working, not the feeling. And the land's life made to **stand for** a human story -- the
  reclaiming forest that mirrors a fall, the upheaval that mirrors a breaking life -- is
  refused: that is authored key, the play's pitch, not the force on the place.

## Encoding

Source to constraint. A composite root over five engine atoms it declares as
dependencies, under the referenced climatic frame, with three bridges.

- **[wildland](process_wildland.md)** (the root): the setting read as a living, self-shaping
  agent -- the land that makes, wears, burns, and reclaims itself, on its own time,
  indifferent to us. Anchored by Marsh and Lovelock & Margulis (the land as an acting,
  living whole).
- **[upheaval](process_wildland_upheaval.md)** (bridge): the land's sudden making and
  breaking -- the ground raised, quaked, and poured new, the conflagration, the flood, the
  place remade in a stroke. Anchored by McPhee.
- **[wearing](process_wildland_wearing.md)** (bridge): the land's slow shaping -- the
  surface worn grain by grain, the long drought, the mountain worn to a plain at the pace
  the climate sets. Anchored by McPhee.
- **[reclaiming](process_wildland_reclaiming.md)** (bridge): the wild taking itself back --
  the succession greening the ground, the rewilding of what was human, the fire's renewal,
  the deposition building new land. Anchored by Weisman; the composite's twist and the
  family's keystone, the world without us.
