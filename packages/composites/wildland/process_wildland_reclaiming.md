# Process: Wildland, Reclaiming

## Taxonomy

[Wildland](process_wildland.md), the bridge where the land takes itself back.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: wildland

## Initiated by

The forces that made and wore the land give way to the life that returns to it, and the land reclaims itself.
Reclaiming starts wherever the living moves back onto the ground the other forces cleared -- the [succession](@chbrain/khai-engine-succession/process_succession.md) greening the raw earth, the [rewilding](@chbrain/khai-engine-succession/process_rewilding.md) that takes back what was human, the fire's [renewal](@chbrain/khai-engine-fire/process_renewal.md), the worn material laid down as new ground by erosion's [deposition](@chbrain/khai-engine-erosion/process_deposition.md).
It is the land's return: the ground the earth broke, the fire cleared, the water drowned, and the wearing lowered, taken back by the living.

## Direction

Toward the land made whole again by its own returning life.
Reclaiming reads the land healing and taking over -- the burn greening, the delta building, the field going to forest, the abandoned city closing over with wood -- the setting recovering from every violence and patience the other bridges dealt it.
It moves the place from cleared to reclaimed, and, on the human's ground, from ours to the wild's: the road cracked by the root, the wall pulled down by the vine, the works given back to the living the moment the hand that held them falls.

## Lever

The living's patient return against the human wish to hold the land apart from it.
The carrying force is that the wild is always waiting at the edge, and every ground the other forces open is ground the living will take -- the reclaiming as sure as the ruin; the resistance is only the human hand that keeps a place its own, the maintenance that holds the wild off, failing the moment it stops.
Reclaiming rides the family's keystone knife-edge: the wild retaking the human place is the human's loss and the land's return at once, the ruin of the one the reclaiming of the other.

## Echo

The land taken back by its own life, and the world going on without us.
It shows a place reclaimed -- the green through the ruin, the forest in the street, the delta risen, the burn come back -- and carries beneath it the land's-forces family's keystone: that the land, left alone, takes itself back, and its reclaiming is indifferent to whether we were ever here.
Reclaiming is the note the whole composite closes on: the setting is not our stage but a living thing that made itself before us, shapes itself around us, and will reclaim the ground we stand on after us.
Reclaiming is dated from the return, the moment the living began to take the land home.
