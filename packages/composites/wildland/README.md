# Wildland

Wildland as composite: the setting read as a living, self-shaping agent -- the land that makes and breaks (geomorphology), wears (erosion), floods and parches (hydrology), burns (fire), and reclaims itself (succession), all under the climatic regime -- and the drama is the land's own life, staged in three shapes: upheaval (the sudden making and breaking), wearing (the slow shaping), and reclaiming (the wild taking itself back, the world without us).
