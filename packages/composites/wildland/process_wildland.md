# Process: Wildland

## Taxonomy

The living land.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: wildland

## Initiated by

A place is read not as a backdrop but as an agent, a land with a life of its own.
Wildland starts wherever a setting is understood to make and unmake itself -- the ground it raises and breaks ([geomorphology](@chbrain/khai-engine-geomorphology/process_geomorphology.md)), the surface it wears ([erosion](@chbrain/khai-engine-erosion/process_erosion.md)), the water it floods and parches with ([hydrology](@chbrain/khai-engine-hydrology/process_hydrology.md)), the fire it carries ([fire](@chbrain/khai-engine-fire/process_fire.md)), and the life that reclaims it ([succession](@chbrain/khai-engine-succession/process_succession.md)) -- all within the regime the climate sets.
This composite adds no new force; it reads what the five make together: the land living its own life, on its own vast time, indifferent to us.

## Direction

Toward the whole life of the land, read through its shapes.
The composite wires the five land-forces and reads their working in three shapes: [Upheaval](process_wildland_upheaval.md), the land's sudden making and breaking; [Wearing](process_wildland_wearing.md), its slow shaping; and [Reclaiming](process_wildland_reclaiming.md), the twist -- the wild taking itself back.
The land does not live at one pace: it is remade in a quake's instant and lowered over a million years, drowned in a season and reclaimed over a century, and the climate's regime sets the odds of which force, where, and how hard.

## Lever

The land's own agency against the human habit of reading it as scenery.
The carrying force is that the setting is never inert -- it builds, wears, floods, burns, and greens whether or not anyone watches, on timescales that dwarf the human; the resistance is the reflex to see the land as a fixed stage for the human drama, the backdrop that only the plot moves.
Wildland is read wherever the place is granted its own life: not the setting the characters act upon but the agent that acts, on its own terms, in its own time.

## Echo

The setting revealed as a living, self-shaping thing.
Wildland does not close; it is the land going on with its own life, gathering the five forces into one reading -- a place that makes and wears and remakes itself.
It hands off what it is not: the climate that governs the odds is the frame, not the act; the built fabric on the ground is space's; the persona's feeling for the land is the persona's own; and the land made to stand for a human story is authored key, the play's pitch, not the force.
The place is dated from the land's own working, and read as an agent with a life longer and larger than any who cross it.
