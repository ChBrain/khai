# Process: Wildland, Upheaval

## Taxonomy

[Wildland](process_wildland.md), the bridge where the land makes and breaks itself in a stroke.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: wildland

## Initiated by

The land's forces strike suddenly, and the place is remade in an instant or a violent season.
Upheaval starts wherever the land does its sudden work -- the ground [uplifted](@chbrain/khai-engine-geomorphology/process_uplift.md), [quaked](@chbrain/khai-engine-geomorphology/process_quake.md), or [erupted](@chbrain/khai-engine-geomorphology/process_eruption.md) new; the [conflagration](@chbrain/khai-engine-fire/process_conflagration.md) run free; the [flood](@chbrain/khai-engine-hydrology/process_flood.md) risen over the land.
It is the land's violence: not the slow shaping but the catastrophe, the place changed faster than anything on it can follow.

## Direction

Toward the place transfigured in the stroke.
Upheaval reads the land at its most sudden -- the coast raised in a jolt, the ground poured over with lava, the valley drowned in a night, the forest crowned out in an afternoon -- the setting made new or unmade in a span the human feels as disaster.
It moves the place from what it was to what the violence leaves: the risen fault scarp, the ash field, the flooded plain, the black burn -- the land's own hand, fallen hard and fast.

## Lever

The stored force of the land against the illusion of the settled ground.
The carrying force is the energy the land holds and releases -- the strain in the fault, the magma's pressure, the fuel and wind of the fire, the water gathered upstream; the resistance is the human assumption that the ground is fixed, the place stable, the setting safe -- the assumption the upheaval breaks.
The land is quiet until it is not, and the stroke is the measure of how much it had stored: the longer the calm, the harder the making and breaking when it comes.

## Echo

The place remade by the land's sudden violence.
Upheaval leaves the setting transfigured in a stroke -- raised, cracked, poured, drowned, or burned -- and hands the changed ground to the slow forces that will shape it and the life that will reclaim it.
It is the land's life at its most dramatic and its most indifferent: the violence that levels a city is the violence that built the mountains, the land making and breaking on a scale that has nothing to do with the human it happens to.
Upheaval is dated from the stroke, the instant or the season the land remade the place under those who lived on it.
