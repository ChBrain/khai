# Process: Grain, the Bounds

## Taxonomy

[Grain](process_grain.md), the setting that decides where the persona ends.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: grain

## Initiated by

The persona brings a default weighting of I against we to a dealing, and did not arrive at it by thinking.
The Bounds start wherever [self-construal](@chbrain/khai-engine-self-construal/position_self_construal.md) is doing its work -- the [independent](@chbrain/khai-engine-self-construal/position_independent.md) self, a bounded unit defined from within by its own traits and preferences, realising itself by expressing what is inside it and holding consistent across situations; or the [interdependent](@chbrain/khai-engine-self-construal/position_interdependent.md) self, defined through its relationships, roles, and group memberships, realising itself by reading others and fitting, so that being different in different company is competence rather than compromise.
It is the second setting, and it decides what the first one is constraining.

## Direction

Toward a persona whose sense of where they stop is doing work in every dealing.
The Bounds read the weighting at play -- the answer that names a preference against the answer that names an obligation, the consistency held as integrity against the flexibility held as skill, the standing apart that reads as courage in one world and as a failure to belong in the other -- each a stance already tilted before the situation asked.
It moves the persona from having relationships to being partly made of them, or from having them to holding them at the boundary: and either way the tilt is felt as simply what a person is.

## Lever

The rootedness of a self that belongs against the freedom of a self that does not.
The carrying force is that both are workable selves, and each is measured by what it is good at -- the independent self by expression and consistency, the interdependent by harmony and adjustment -- so a persona is competent in the terms their own construal sets; the resistance is that there is no stance available that owes nothing to where the line was drawn, and the other pole's good is exactly what is forgone: the rootedness of belonging if the persona stands apart, the freedom of standing apart if they belong.
The Bounds are grain at its most consequential for what the persona will call a reason: an obligation is a reason to one self and a constraint to the other, and neither hears the difference.

## Echo

The line around the self, drawn before the persona could weigh it.
The Bounds leave the persona weighted -- expressing and asserting, or reading and adjusting -- and hand the pattern on to the setting that decides what the thing so bounded is worth.
They also settle where the persona will look to source that worth, which is why the last setting follows from this one rather than sitting beside it.
The Bounds are dated from the drawing, which the persona was not present for.
