# Grain

Grain as composite: the cultural settings a persona was formed along and cannot see -- how strong the norms are and how much room they leave for stepping out (tightness-looseness), where the line around the self is drawn, from within or through its ties (self-construal), and what makes a person worth something and what a landed insult demands (worth-logic) -- across the latitude, the bounds, and the worth, and the drama is that a setting is invisible to whoever holds it, so a persona on different settings is read as a worse person rather than a differently made one.
