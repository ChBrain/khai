# Process: Grain

## Taxonomy

The cultural settings a persona was formed along, and cannot see.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: grain

## Initiated by

A persona is about to act, and three things are already decided that they did not decide.
Grain starts wherever those settings are in force -- the strength of the norm field and the room it leaves for stepping out, through [tightness-looseness](@chbrain/khai-engine-tightness-looseness/position_tightness_looseness.md); where the line around the self falls, through [self-construal](@chbrain/khai-engine-self-construal/position_self_construal.md); and the rule by which worth is assigned and defended, through [worth-logic](@chbrain/khai-engine-worth-logic/position_worth_logic.md).
This composite adds no fourth engine; it reads what the three make together: a persona already set before the situation arrives, along settings that are not opinions and were never chosen.

## Direction

Toward the whole of what a persona brought and did not decide, read setting by setting.
The composite wires the three engines and reads their working in three shapes: [the Latitude](process_grain_latitude.md), the room the norms leave; [the Bounds](process_grain_bounds.md), where the self is drawn; and [the Worth](process_grain_worth.md), the twist -- what makes a person count and what a landed insult demands.
The three are not independent: a tight field, a self defined through its ties, and a worth that must be granted tend to arrive together, as do their opposites, so a persona is usually cut along all three at once.

## Lever

The coherence a set of settings gives a life against the fact that none of them can be seen from inside it.
The carrying force is that these are solutions, not defects -- every world has to answer how much deviance it can carry, where one person ends and the next begins, and what makes a person worth something, and a persona supplied with answers can act without deriving them; the resistance is that an answer supplied that early does not present itself as an answer: it presents as how things are, so the persona has no vantage from which to notice they are running settings at all, and meets a persona running different ones with no category for the difference except character.
Grain is read wherever a persona is granted their formation: not a self with views about norms, selves, and worth, but one already cut along a particular set of them.

## Echo

The persona revealed by what they never had to decide.
Grain does not close; it is the settings holding, gathering the three engines into one reading -- a latitude, a boundary, a rule of worth.
It hands off what it is not: the formation absorbed in a particular family, its habitus, its cells, and the capital transmitted through it, is the heritage engine's, which is the same move at the size of a household where this is at the size of a world; the persona's yielding to a norm is the conformity engine's and to an authority the obedience engine's, where this is the field they yield within; identification with a particular group is the social-identity engine's, inside the membership and intergroup composites; the line a persona takes in an encounter and the work of saving it is the face engine's, inside the presentation composite, where this owns the standing cultural logic and not the interactional move; the resting sense of one's own worth is the self-esteem engine's, inside the self-relation composite, and the felt pride and shame of worth won and lost are the pride and shame engines', inside the self-conscious composite; honour as a personal virtue of integrity is the virtue engine's; the abstraction of a representation by psychological distance is the construal engine's, a different sense of the word; the rank a persona holds and the capital they trade on is the standing composite's; and the setting made to stand for a story is authored key, the play's pitch, not the force.
The persona is dated from before they could have objected, and read as a self that will mistake its own grain for the shape of the world.
