# Instructions: Grain

How a Playwright wires the grain composite into a play. You wire by linking the play's
own content to the composite's files; the files stay untouched, they are canon. The
composite installs the tightness-looseness, self-construal, and worth-logic engines as
dependencies, and their laws arrive with them. Authoring guidance, not runtime content,
and it does not go on tour.

## Human

- The human sets the persona's settings: the norm field they were formed under and the room it leaves, where the line around their self falls, and the rule by which their worth is assigned and an affront to it priced -- and, the question that governs the read, which setting is doing the work in this scene: the latitude, the bounds, or the worth.

## Agent

- From the play's own content, read a persona as already set before the situation arrives: link under Projection the setting doing the work -- the latitude while the room a world leaves is deciding what an act costs (the tight regime of clear rules strongly enforced with little tolerance, the loose one where much passes and each act meets room to improvise, from tightness-looseness), the bounds while the weighting of I against we is deciding what counts as a reason (the self defined from within by its own traits and held consistent, the self defined through its ties and rightly different in different company, from self-construal), the worth while an affront is being priced (worth inherent and inalienable so an insult can be deflected, worth claimed and precarious so a challenge cannot be let pass, worth granted by position so the order must not be breached, from worth-logic). Play the setting as the persona's sense of how things are, present in what they do not think to question, never narrated, and never made to stand for a story. You link to the composite, you never edit it.

## Collaboration

- The strength of the norm field is the tightness-looseness engine's; the self-boundary is self-construal's; the rule of worth is worth-logic's -- one setting alone is not the grain. The formation absorbed in a particular household is the heritage engine's, the same move at the size of a family; the persona's yielding to a norm or an authority is conformity's and obedience's, where this owns the field they yield within; identification with a particular group is social-identity's, inside membership and intergroup; the line taken in an encounter and the work of saving it is the face engine's, inside presentation, where this owns the standing logic rather than the interactional move; the resting sense of one's own worth is self-esteem's, inside self-relation, and the felt pride and shame are their own engines', inside self-conscious; honour as a personal virtue is virtue's; construal as psychological distance is the construal engine's, a different word; rank and capital are standing's. This composite owns what was answered before the persona arrived -- and the worth bridge carries the keystone: a setting is invisible to whoever holds it, so a persona on different settings is read as a worse person rather than a differently made one.

## Knowledge

- Grain is a persona read by what they never had to decide -- braiding the strength of the norm field and the latitude it grants (tightness-looseness), where the line around the self is drawn (self-construal), and the rule by which worth is assigned and defended (worth-logic) into one set of settings -- read as a wiring of those three engines, and its question is which setting is doing the work (Triandis on cultural syndromes, where collectivism and tightness bear on which aspects of the self get sampled; Leung and Cohen on cultural logics as alternative solutions to two universal problems, a person's worth and social order; Gelfand on norm strength and its ecological-threat roots; Markus and Kitayama on the two construals; Singelis on their separability; Nisbett and Cohen on honour; Ho on face). It runs in three settings, and they co-vary rather than sitting independently: a tight field, a self defined through its ties, and a worth that must be granted tend to arrive together, as do their opposites. The latitude is the room the world leaves, a property of the regime and not of anyone's yielding -- tight buys coordination, predictability, and safety through constraint, loose buys openness and improvisation, and no regime gets both, so whichever a persona was raised inside they were raised without the other pole's good and are unlikely to have missed it. The bounds are the default weighting of I against we, which decides what will count as a reason: an obligation is a reason to one self and a constraint to the other, the independent self measured by expression and consistency and the interdependent by harmony and adjustment, each competent in the terms its own construal sets, and each forgoing the other's good -- the rootedness of belonging, or the freedom of standing apart. The worth is the twist -- not the feeling of worth but the logic beneath it, setting the exchange rate between an insult and the self, so the same remark is nothing to a dignity-holder, a wound to an honour-holder, and a disgrace to a face-holder -- where the settings are seen for what they are: three answers to a question every world must answer, supplied so early that they arrive as the shape of the world rather than as one available shape. Read a persona as carrying settings rather than holding opinions, and read the friction between two personas cut differently as evidence about neither of them.

## System

- Do read the persona as already set before the scene: name whether the latitude (the room the world leaves), the bounds (the weighting of I against we), or the worth (what an affront costs) is doing the work, and cast each from its engines.
- Do keep the settings distinct: the latitude is a property of the field, the bounds are where the persona ends, the worth is what the thing so bounded is owed.
- Do let the worth carry the keystone: a setting is invisible to whoever holds it, so the misreading runs in both directions at once and neither party can supply the correction from inside.
- Do remember these are answers, not defects: each pole is a workable solution and the persona holding one is competent in its terms -- a play that ranks the settings has stopped reading them.
- Do remember the settings co-vary but are not one thing: a persona can be tight and independent, or loose and interdependent, and the mismatch is where the most interesting friction lives.
- Do remember the bounds are a weighting, not a type: a persona tilts toward one construal and can hold and draw on both, and a situation can prime the other at a cost.
- Do not model the formation of one household (that is heritage), a persona's yielding to a norm or an authority (that is conformity and obedience), identification with a group (that is social-identity), the line taken in an encounter (that is face, inside presentation), the felt worth and its emotions (that is self-esteem, pride, and shame), honour as a personal virtue (that is virtue), or construal as psychological distance (that is the construal engine). Do not make a setting stand for a story (that is the play's pitch). Do not edit the composite's files; wire only from the play's side.
