# Process: Grain, the Worth

## Taxonomy

[Grain](process_grain.md), the setting that prices an affront, and where the settings are seen for what they are.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: grain

## Initiated by

An insult has landed, or has not, and which it was depended on a rule the persona never chose.
The Worth starts wherever [worth-logic](@chbrain/khai-engine-worth-logic/position_worth_logic.md) is running -- [dignity](@chbrain/khai-engine-worth-logic/position_dignity.md), where worth is inherent and inalienable so an insult can be deflected and need cost nothing; [honor](@chbrain/khai-engine-worth-logic/position_honor.md), where worth is claimed and conferred and therefore precarious, so a challenge cannot be let pass; or [face](@chbrain/khai-engine-worth-logic/position_face_worth.md), where worth is granted by position and held by propriety, so the order must not be breached to assert oneself.
It is not the feeling of worth but the logic beneath it, setting the exchange rate between an insult and the self.

## Direction

Toward a persona for whom the same words are nothing, a wound, or a disgrace.
The Worth reads the pricing -- the remark let go by one persona and answered by the next and quietly absorbed by a third, each reading the other two as strange: the dignity-holder unable to see why an insult should cost anything, the honor-holder unable to let a challenge stand, the face-holder unable to breach the order to assert itself.
It moves the persona nowhere at all: it was already the case, and it decides what the persona's pride and shame are about before either is felt.

## Lever

The answer a logic supplies against the blindness that comes with having one.
The carrying force is that every world has to answer where a person's worth comes from and how an affront to it is met, the three logics are three workable answers to that one question, and a persona supplied with one does not have to derive it under pressure -- which is exactly when it is needed; the resistance is that holding one forgoes the others: the dignity-holder loses the honor-holder's vigilance and the face-holder's attunement to place, the honor-holder loses the dignity-holder's serene indifference, the face-holder loses the inner anchor and the readiness to fight -- and none of the three experiences its own logic as a logic.
The Worth rides the composite's keystone knife-edge: a setting is invisible to whoever holds it, so a persona on different settings is not read as differently made but as worse made -- and the sentences that do it are about character every time, that he has no dignity, that she has no shame, that they have no honour.

## Echo

The affront priced, and the pricer mistaken for a person of bad character.
It shows the three settings complete -- a latitude, a boundary, a rule of worth -- and carries beneath it the composite's keystone: each is an answer to a question every world must answer, supplied so early that it arrives as the shape of the world rather than as one available shape, so the friction between two personas cut differently presents to both of them as evidence about the other, and to neither as evidence about themselves.
This is why the misreading runs in both directions at once and why neither party can supply the correction from inside.
The Worth is the note the whole composite closes on: a persona will mistake their own grain for the way things are, and will be most certain of it exactly where a different world answered the same question differently.
The Worth is dated from the answer, which was given before the persona could have asked.
