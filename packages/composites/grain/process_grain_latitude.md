# Process: Grain, the Latitude

## Taxonomy

[Grain](process_grain.md), the setting that decides how much room the persona has before it acts.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: grain

## Initiated by

The persona meets an act already constrained, or already free, and the constraint was there before them.
The Latitude starts wherever [tightness-looseness](@chbrain/khai-engine-tightness-looseness/position_tightness_looseness.md) is in force -- the [tight](@chbrain/khai-engine-tightness-looseness/position_tight.md) regime of clear rules strongly enforced, pervasive monitoring and swift sanction, where the range of the allowable is narrow and known and a departure is noticed and answered; or the [loose](@chbrain/khai-engine-tightness-looseness/position_loose.md) regime where much passes, the rules are weaker and weakly enforced, and each act meets room to improvise.
It is a property of the field, not of anyone's yielding to it: set before a persona conforms or refuses.

## Direction

Toward a persona whose range was decided somewhere above them.
The Latitude reads the field doing its work -- the pause before an unusual remark in a room that watches, the shrug that meets the same remark in a room that does not, the reliability that reads as virtue in one world and rigidity in the other -- each a departure priced by a regime rather than by the person departing.
It moves the persona from choosing to choosing-within: the same act costing what the field charges for it, and the field never itemising the bill.

## Lever

The coordination a narrow field buys against the variety a wide one keeps.
The carrying force is that a world must answer how much deviance it can carry, and both answers are real answers -- a tight field buys predictability, safety, and the ability to act in concert, and tightens for reasons, under threats that make the constraint worth its cost; a loose field buys openness, improvisation, and room for what has not been tried; the resistance is that no regime gets both, so whichever a persona was raised inside, they were raised without the other pole's good and are unlikely to have missed it.
The Latitude is grain at its most external and least noticed: the one setting that is visibly a property of the world, and still not experienced as one.

## Echo

The room a persona had, decided before they entered it.
The Latitude leaves the persona already priced -- constrained and reliable, or free and improvisational -- and hands the pattern on to the settings that decide who is being constrained and what they are worth.
It leaves the shared shape the rest will repeat: a setting is a trade, and the pole a persona was not raised on is the one they cannot feel the absence of.
The Latitude is dated from the field, which is to say from before the persona was in it.
