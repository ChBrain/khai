# Grain: Reference

## Line of Work

Grain as **composite**: the cultural settings a persona was formed along, and cannot see. It is
not a fourth mechanism but a wiring of three: the **tightness-looseness** engine supplies the
strength of the norm field and the room it leaves for stepping out of it, the **self-construal**
engine supplies where the line around the self falls, and the **worth-logic** engine supplies the
rule by which worth is assigned and an affront to it priced. This composite adds no fourth engine;
it reads the seam the three make -- three settings that co-vary, each trading one pole's good for
the other's -- and stages that seam into three bridges, closing on the discovery that none of them
is visible to the persona holding it.

The domain does not model any single setting -- the tight and loose regimes are the
tightness-looseness engine's; the independent and interdependent construals are self-construal's;
the dignity, honor, and face logics are worth-logic's; and one alone is a position, not the grain.
Nor is it the formation absorbed in a particular family, which is the heritage engine's; nor a
persona's yielding to a norm or an authority, which are the conformity and obedience engines';
nor identification with a particular group, which is the social-identity engine's inside the
membership and intergroup composites. It owns the settings: what was answered before the persona
arrived, and is carried as though it were the shape of the world.

The spine is the concept of a **cultural syndrome**, and both flanks of this composite are joined
in the literature rather than here. Triandis established the term: a syndrome is a shared pattern
of beliefs, attitudes, norms, roles and values organised around a theme, with within-culture
variance small relative to between-culture variance -- and he set collectivism, tightness-looseness
and cultural complexity as dimensions of cultural variation bearing directly on which aspects of
the self get sampled, finding interdependent self-concepts more likely in tight and collective
worlds and independent ones in loose and individualist worlds. That is the latitude and the bounds
argued as one thing. Leung and Cohen then supplied the same move on the far flank, using an almost
identical definition -- a cultural logic as a constellation of shared beliefs, values, behaviours
and practices organised around a central theme -- building explicitly on earlier classifications
that had run on values, beliefs and self-construals, and framing dignity, honor and face as
alternative pathways for solving two problems every society must answer: **the problem of a
person's worth, and the problem of cooperation, or social order.** Those two problems are this
composite's three bridges, since the latitude is how a world secures order, the worth is how it
answers worth, and the bounds are where it draws the person whose worth is at stake and who is
held in that order. What this composite adds on top is the consequence for a persona rather than
for a culture: that an answer supplied that early is not experienced as an answer, so a difference
of setting is read as a defect of person. The three bridges are those three settings: the
latitude, the bounds, and the worth.

## Origin

The cross-cultural psychology of norms, the self, and self-worth, wired as a composite over the
tightness-looseness, self-construal, and worth-logic engines. The first two rows are the
**joining** -- the published work that argues these settings are one pattern -- and the rest anchor
the individual bridges.

## Restrictions

What the composite refuses to model, and to whom it delegates.

- **The single settings (the atoms)**: The composite wires the three but owns none. The tight and
  loose regimes are the tightness-looseness engine's; the independent and interdependent construals
  are self-construal's; dignity, honor, and face are worth-logic's. Grain reads only the pattern
  the three make; one setting alone is a position, not the grain.
- **The formation of one household (heritage)**: The habitus absorbed before any choice was
  possible, the family cells it formed in, the capital transmitted, and the hysteresis when it
  meets a different field are the heritage engine's. Grain is the same move at the size of a
  world where heritage is at the size of a family, and the two should be cast together rather
  than folded into one another.
- **The yielding, not the field (conformity, obedience)**: A persona's going along with a norm is
  the conformity engine's and with an authority the obedience engine's. Grain owns the field they
  yield within, which is set before anyone conforms or refuses.
- **Identification with a group (social-identity, membership, intergroup)**: Taking a particular
  group as part of who one is, and what follows between groups, are the social-identity engine's
  and the membership and intergroup composites'. Grain is carried whether or not any group is
  salient.
- **The interactional move, not the logic (face, presentation)**: The line a persona takes in an
  encounter, the threat to it, and the work of saving it are the face engine's, inside the
  presentation composite. Grain owns the standing cultural logic in which face is where worth
  lives -- a setting a persona brings to every encounter, not a move made in one.
- **The felt worth and its emotions (self-esteem, self-relation, pride, shame, self-conscious)**:
  The resting sense of one's own worth is the self-esteem engine's inside the self-relation
  composite, and the pride and shame of worth won and lost are their own engines' inside the
  self-conscious composite. Grain sets what those feelings are about.
- **Honour as a personal virtue (virtue)**: Integrity held as a character strength is the virtue
  engine's. The worth bridge reads honour as a cultural logic of worth, which a persona holds
  whether or not they are honourable in that other sense.
- **Construal as psychological distance (construal)**: The abstraction of a representation by
  distance is the construal engine's -- a different sense of the word, and not this.
- **Rank and what is traded on (standing)**: Status, hierarchy, power, and capital are the
  standing composite's. Grain reads the rule by which worth is assigned, not the position a
  persona currently occupies under it.
- **The setting as authored symbol (the play's pitch)**: A cultural setting made to **stand for** a
  story -- the honour code that mirrors a theme of futility -- is refused: that is authored key,
  the play's pitch, not the force on the persona. Grain sets the settings; the meaning read into
  them is the play's.

## Encoding

Source to constraint. A composite root over three engine atoms it declares as dependencies, with
three bridges, the settings a persona is cut along.

- **[grain](process_grain.md)** (the root): the cultural settings a persona was formed along and
  cannot see -- the coherence a set of them gives a life, against the fact that none is visible
  from inside it. Anchored by Triandis on cultural syndromes and Leung & Cohen on cultural logics.
- **[the latitude](process_grain_latitude.md)** (bridge): the strength of the norm field and the
  room it leaves, a property of the regime rather than of anyone's yielding, with no regime buying
  both order and openness. Anchored by Gelfand et al. (2011) and Gelfand (2018).
- **[the bounds](process_grain_bounds.md)** (bridge): where the line around the self falls, and
  the default weighting of I against we brought to any dealing -- read as a weighting rather than
  a type, per Singelis. Anchored by Markus & Kitayama (1991), Triandis, and Singelis (1994).
- **[the worth](process_grain_worth.md)** (bridge): the rule beneath the feeling, setting the
  exchange rate between an insult and the self -- dignity deflecting, honor answering, face
  preserving the order. Anchored by Leung & Cohen (2011), Nisbett & Cohen (1996), and Ho (1976);
  the composite's twist and keystone, that a setting is invisible to whoever holds it, so a persona
  on different settings is read as a worse person rather than a differently made one.
