# Instructions: Glide

How a Playwright wires the glide composite into a play. You wire by linking the play's own
content to the composite's files; the files stay untouched, they are canon. The composite
installs the metacognition, memory, and confusion engines as dependencies, and their laws
arrive with them. Authoring guidance, not runtime content, and it does not go on tour.

## Human

- The human sets which personas are learning something under their own supervision, and when they find out whether it worked.

## Agent

- From the play's own content, read a persona who is learning and judging their own learning, and link under Projection what is running: the monitor while a judgment of learning is being formed from whatever cues the moment offers (from metacognition), the trace while the material is actually being laid down on conditions the persona cannot observe (from memory), the ease while the next hour is being allocated on the strength of the reading and confusion is being cleared out of the way (from metacognition's steering and from confusion). Read the whole loop and never one reading, since a single misjudgement is a small thing and this is not one. You link to the composite, you never edit it.

## Collaboration

- Monitoring, steering, calibration, and the metacognitive trait are the metacognition engine's; the five kinds of remembering are memory's; impasse, resolve, stall, and the ambiguity trait are confusion's -- one alone is a process, not the loop. The tilt itself -- a persona who trusts what processes smoothly, the fluency heuristic, the misinterpreted-effort case -- is the bias engine's, catalogued there as a standing position, and this composite does not restate it: it begins where that leaves off, since the misreading is a control signal and selects what happens next. The family of judgemental errors is estimation's; a mind that has left the task is unmoored's, and this persona is on the task throughout; absorbed effortless engagement is absorption's, and flow is a real performance rather than an illusion about one; what a persona believes about their own capability is capability's; a conviction manufactured by having had to commit is picking's; the account given after the failure is squaring's. This composite owns the loop -- and the ease bridge carries the keystone: effort is the multiplier.

## Knowledge

- Glide is a persona read while they learn something and judge, at the same time, how it is going -- braiding the loop (metacognition: a meta-level monitoring the object-level in judgments of learning and feelings of knowing, and steering it by allocating study, with calibration deciding whether any of it is accurate) with what is being built (memory: a trace laid down and later rebuilt rather than replayed) and with the felt state of the conditions that build it (confusion: the disequilibrium of what will not yet fit, forking into worked resolution or into a stall). The join is Bjork's: retrieval strength, how readily something comes to hand now, and storage strength, how durably it is held, are two quantities with one signal between them -- and the conditions that raise the second lower the first in the moment, which is why spacing, interleaving, and retrieval practice all make the session go worse and the outcome go better. Karpicke and Roediger measured what that does to a learner left to their own judgement: students who repeatedly retrieved material retained far more a week later than students who repeatedly restudied it, and predicted the opposite, because restudy felt better at the time. The forecast was not merely inaccurate, it was inverted. It runs in three faces. The monitor is the reading, and nothing about it is laziness: the persona has to decide what is finished, there is only so much time, and a judgment of learning is the steering rather than an indulgence -- but the meta-level has no window onto storage and never has had, so it infers from cues, and the loudest cue is how easily the material is coming, which belongs to the session and not to the trace. The trace is what is actually happening, on conditions nobody in the room can see, and the two quantities can move in opposite directions with no error message when they do. The ease is the twist: material that has gone smooth is marked finished and dropped, material that fights is returned to -- a rule that is correct wherever fluency tracks durability and precisely backwards where the smoothness was made by the session -- and the same rule clears confusion on sight, which is the felt marker of the one condition that was working. Here the three are seen for one thing: the error does not average out over a long effort, it compounds, because the reading governs what gets read next -- so the persona working the most hours is the one it costs the most, and the whole of the correction is a single closed-book question asked cold, which is almost never available because it costs an hour and feels like a test rather than like study.

## System

- Do read the whole loop and never one reading: name whether the monitor (a judgment being formed), the trace (material being laid down), or the ease (an hour being allocated) is running, and cast each from its engines.
- Do make the persona diligent rather than lazy, since the composite runs hardest on somebody working properly, and a play that has them cutting corners has staged an excuse instead of this.
- Do give the failure a delay: nothing goes wrong at the time, which is the whole point, and the persona must be genuinely surprised when it arrives.
- Do withhold the closed-book question: the persona must be given no way to find out, since one cold retrieval would have told them and the play needs to show why it was never taken.
- Do let every individual decision be defensible, because no single application of the rule is wrong and the persona could account for all of them.
- Do not stage this as difficulty being good in itself: the difficulties that help are specific and named, plenty of difficulty is simply difficulty, and a persona who finds something easy is not thereby failing to learn it.
- Do not model the tilt itself (that is bias, which already catalogues the smooth-is-mastery reading as a standing position), the errors as a family (that is estimation), a mind off the task (that is unmoored), absorbed engagement (that is absorption, and flow is a real performance), a standing belief about one's own ability (that is capability), a conviction made by a hard choice (that is picking), or the account given after the failure (that is squaring). Do not make a misplaced confidence stand for a theme (that is the play's pitch). Do not edit the composite's files; wire only from the play's side.
