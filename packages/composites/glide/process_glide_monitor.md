# Process: Glide, the Monitor

## Taxonomy

[Glide](process_glide.md), a reading with no line to the thing it reads.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: glide

## Initiated by

A persona asks themselves whether they have got it yet.
The Monitor starts wherever [metacognition](@chbrain/khai-engine-metacognition/process_metacognition.md) is running its meta-level over the object-level -- [monitoring](@chbrain/khai-engine-metacognition/process_monitoring.md) in judgments of learning and feelings of knowing, [steering](@chbrain/khai-engine-metacognition/process_steering.md) study time and search effort on what the monitoring reports, with [calibration](@chbrain/khai-engine-metacognition/process_calibration.md) deciding whether any of it is accurate and a standing [metacognitive](@chbrain/khai-engine-metacognition/position_metacognitive.md) skill setting how well the persona knows their own edges.
It is the face of the composite where the persona is doing exactly the right thing.

## Direction

Toward a number the persona will act on, arrived at without access to the quantity it names.
The Monitor reads the construction rather than the verdict: a judgment of learning is not a measurement taken from storage, because there is no instrument for that -- it is an inference from whatever the moment offers, and what the moment offers most loudly is how easily the material is coming.
It moves the persona from working to knowing how the work is going, which is the only way the work gets governed at all.

## Lever

The indispensability of self-assessment against the poverty of what it has to go on.
The carrying force is that monitoring is a genuine faculty and often good: a persona can tell a word is on the tip of their tongue, can feel a proof come apart, can sense the difference between having read something and having understood it -- and none of this composite works unless the monitoring is taken seriously, since a persona who never assessed themselves would simply stop at random; the resistance is that the cue set is thin and the loudest cue in it is fluency, which is generated by conditions rather than by learning, so the same reading that catches a genuine gap will also report mastery where there is only familiarity, and it reports both in the same voice.
The Monitor spends nothing, and produces the only signal the rest of the loop has.

## Echo

A confident reading, honestly taken, from an instrument pointed at the wrong thing.
It is the bridge that establishes the composite's honesty: nothing here is laziness or self-flattery, and the persona is doing the thing they should be doing.
What it hands upward is a reading and a fact about it -- that its principal cue is smoothness, and smoothness belongs to the session rather than to the storage.
The Monitor is dated from the moment the persona asked, and it answers immediately.
