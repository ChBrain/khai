# Glide: Reference

## Line of Work

Glide as **composite**: a persona learning something while, at the same time, deciding how the
learning is going. It is not a fourth mechanism but a wiring of three: the **metacognition** engine
supplies the loop -- a meta-level monitoring the object-level and steering it -- the **memory** engine
supplies what is actually being laid down, and the **confusion** engine supplies the felt state of the
conditions that lay it down best.

What the composite is **not** about needs saying first, because khai already owns it. The
misjudgement itself -- a persona trusting what processes smoothly, the fluency heuristic, the
misinterpreted-effort case where a passage reread until the eye stops snagging is logged as mastery --
is the bias engine's, held there as a standing tilt among two hundred others. This composite starts
after that and asks a different question: what the misreading then **governs**. Because the reading is
not idle. It is the steering signal, and it decides where the next hour goes.

The joining is Bjork's. The new theory of disuse separates two quantities that a persona has only one
signal for: retrieval strength, how readily something comes to hand right now, and storage strength,
how durably it is held. They are not the same and can move in opposite directions -- and the
conditions that raise the second tend to lower the first in the moment. Hence the desirable
difficulties: spacing, interleaving, and retrieval practice all make the session go worse and the
outcome go better. Karpicke and Roediger supplied the sharpest demonstration of what that does to a
learner left to their own judgement. Students who repeatedly retrieved material retained far more a
week later than students who repeatedly restudied it -- and predicted the opposite, because restudy
felt better at the time. The learner's own forecast was not merely inaccurate; it was inverted.

Koriat's accessibility model, which the metacognition atom already carries, explains why. The
meta-level has no window onto storage and never has had, so a judgment of learning is an inference
from cues rather than a readout, and the loudest cue available is how easily the material is coming.
Fluency is real, immediate, and belongs to the session rather than to the trace.

D'Mello and Graesser complete the third bridge from the other side. Confusion -- the disequilibrium
of what will not yet fit -- is not a failure state: held and worked through to a resolution it produces
deeper learning than fluent processing does, and its unproductive fork is not the confusion but the
stall. So the felt marker of the productive condition is the one a persona steering by comfort is most
likely to eliminate on sight.

That is the keystone, and it is what the composite adds to the tilt the bias engine catalogues. A
single misreading is a small thing that would average out. This one does not average out, because the
reading governs what is read next: material that has gone smooth is dropped and material that fights
is returned to, so the policy systematically removes the material that still needed the time. And it
compounds fastest in the persona putting in the most hours, since effort is the multiplier rather
than the corrective.

It is the second composite to wire the memory engine and the second to wire confusion. Elsewhere runs
memory as one of the ways a mind leaves the room, alongside imagination; here it is run as the thing
being built while somebody watches the wrong dial. Knowing runs confusion as one of the epistemic
emotions met with interest, awe, and surprise; here it is run as the felt cost of the study regime
that works. Same engines, different questions, and no composite owns them.

**On the evidence, stated plainly.** This composite rests on an unusually well-replicated body of
work, which is worth saying because the others in this canon often cannot. The testing effect,
spacing, and the dissociation between judgments of learning and actual retention have all been
replicated many times, across materials, ages, and laboratories, and the practical recommendations
have survived meta-analysis. What is less settled is the theory underneath: the storage/retrieval
distinction is a well-supported framework rather than a measured pair of quantities, and competing
accounts of why the testing effect works are live. The confusion result is smaller and more
conditional than the others -- it holds where the confusion is resolved and where the learner has the
resources to resolve it, and an unresolved impasse is straightforwardly bad, which is why the atom
carries the stall as its own fork. What should not be taken from this composite is that difficulty is
good in itself, or that a persona who finds study easy is failing to learn. The difficulties that help
are specific and named, and plenty of difficulty is simply difficulty. The claim is conditional: where
a persona is judging their own learning with no external check, expect the judgement to track
smoothness, expect the steering to follow the judgement, and expect the error to accumulate rather
than cancel.

## Origin

Metamemory research, the psychology of learning and retention, and the study of the knowledge
emotions, wired as a composite over the metacognition, memory, and confusion engines. The first two
rows are the **joining** -- the published work that separates felt learning from actual learning and
then measures what a learner left to their own judgement does about it -- and the rest anchor the
individual bridges and their bounds.

## Restrictions

What the composite refuses to model, and to whom it delegates.

- **The tilt itself (bias)**: The persona who trusts what processes smoothly, the fluency heuristic,
  the illusory-truth effect, and the misinterpreted-effort case are all the bias engine's, catalogued
  there as standing positions under ease. This composite does not restate them and does not own them.
  It begins where they leave off: not that the persona misreads smoothness as mastery, but that the
  misreading is a control signal, so it selects what happens next rather than merely mis-describing
  what happened.
- **The errors as a family (estimation)**: Anchoring, availability, and representativeness are the
  estimation composite's. Glide reads one loop in one domain and does not model judgemental error in
  general.
- **The three sides (the atoms)**: The composite wires the three but owns none. Monitoring, steering,
  calibration, and the metacognitive trait are the metacognition engine's; the five kinds of
  remembering are memory's; impasse, resolve, stall, and the ambiguity trait are confusion's.
- **A mind off the task altogether (unmoored)**: Mind-wandering, boredom, and rumination are the
  unmoored composite's. Glide's persona is on the task throughout and working properly; that is the
  point of it, and a play that has them drifting has staged something else.
- **Absorbed effortless engagement (absorption)**: Flow, attention, and time perception are the
  absorption composite's, and flow is a genuine performance state rather than an illusion about one.
  The two should not be run as one: a persona in flow is performing, a persona gliding is estimating.
- **What a persona believes about their own capability (capability)**: Self-efficacy, mindset, locus
  of control, and self-handicapping are the capability composite's. Glide is not about a standing
  belief in one's own ability; it is about a specific reading taken during a specific hour.
- **The confidence that follows a hard choice (picking)**: A conviction manufactured by having had to
  commit is the picking composite's. Glide's confidence is manufactured by smoothness and is not the
  residue of a decision.
- **The account after the failure (squaring)**: When the persona is finally tested and fails, what
  they say about it -- the reassigned cause, the loosened standard -- is the squaring composite's.
  Glide stops at the allocation.
- **The misjudgement as authored symbol (the play's pitch)**: A misplaced confidence made to **stand
  for** a theme -- the student who mirrors a motif of self-deception -- is refused: that is authored
  key, the play's pitch, not the force. Glide sets what the persona studied next; the meaning read
  into it is the play's.

## Encoding

Source to constraint. A composite root over three engine atoms it declares as dependencies, with
three bridges.

- **[glide](process_glide.md)** (the root): a persona learning while judging how the learning is
  going, read across the whole loop and never at one reading. Anchored by Bjork & Bjork (1992).
- **[the monitor](process_glide_monitor.md)** (bridge): the meta-level with no line to its own
  storage, inferring from cues of which smoothness is the loudest. Anchored by Koriat (1993) and
  Nelson & Narens (1990).
- **[the trace](process_glide_trace.md)** (bridge): what is actually being laid down, on conditions
  the persona cannot observe, with present accessibility and future availability as two quantities
  that need not move together. Anchored by Bjork & Bjork (1992), Tulving, and Bartlett.
- **[the ease](process_glide_ease.md)** (bridge): the reading governing the steering, so smooth
  material is dropped and confusion is eliminated on sight. Anchored by Karpicke & Roediger (2008)
  and D'Mello et al. (2014); the composite's twist and keystone, that the error compounds rather than
  averages because the reading selects what is read next, and compounds fastest in the persona
  working hardest.
