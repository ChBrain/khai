# Process: Glide

## Taxonomy

A persona learning something, while deciding how the learning is going.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: glide

## Initiated by

Somebody is working at something, alone, with nobody to tell them how it is going.
Glide starts wherever that is running: a meta-level reading the object-level and steering it, through [metacognition](@chbrain/khai-engine-metacognition/process_metacognition.md); a trace being laid down and later rebuilt rather than replayed, through [memory](@chbrain/khai-engine-memory/process_memory.md); and the disequilibrium of what will not yet fit, through [confusion](@chbrain/khai-engine-confusion/process_confusion.md).
This composite adds no fourth engine. It is also not about the misjudgement itself, which the bias engine already holds as a standing tilt; it is about what the misjudgement then governs.

## Direction

Toward a confident persona who will fail later, and a policy that made sure of it.
The composite wires the three engines and reads their working in three shapes: [the Monitor](process_glide_monitor.md), a reading with no direct line to what it is reading; [the Trace](process_glide_trace.md), what is actually being built; and [the Ease](process_glide_ease.md), the twist -- the reading deciding where the next hour goes.
The three are not three stages of study. They are a control loop whose feedback signal is produced by the thing that spoils the result.

## Lever

The necessity of judging one's own learning against the absence of anything to judge it with.
The carrying force is that the persona has to make the call and cannot decline it: there is only so much time, it has to go somewhere, and something must decide what is finished -- so a judgment of learning is not an indulgence, it is the steering; the resistance is that the meta-level has no window onto storage and never has had, so it infers from whatever is available, and what is most available is how smoothly the material is going down -- a cue that is real, immediate, and pointing the wrong way.
Glide is read across the whole loop and never at one reading, because a single misjudgement is a small thing and this is not one.

## Echo

An hour well spent, on the wrong material, by somebody being careful.
Glide does not close; it is one turn of a loop that will turn again, gathered into one reading -- a monitor, a trace, an ease.
It hands off what it is not: the tilt itself -- the persona who trusts what processes smoothly, the misinterpreted-effort case, the fluency heuristic -- is the bias engine's, catalogued there as a standing position, and the family of judgemental errors is estimation's; a mind that has left the task altogether is unmoored's; a state of absorbed effortless engagement is absorption's, which is a real performance and not this; what a persona believes about their own capability is capability's; the confidence that follows a hard choice is picking's; the account given after the failure will be squaring's; and a misplaced confidence made to **stand for** a theme is authored key, the play's pitch, not the force.
The persona is dated from the hour they stopped, and from why they thought they could.
