# Process: Glide, the Ease

## Taxonomy

[Glide](process_glide.md), the reading deciding what happens next, and where the three are seen for one thing.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: glide

## Initiated by

The persona decides what to do with the next hour.
The Ease starts wherever [steering](@chbrain/khai-engine-metacognition/process_steering.md) acts on what the monitoring reported -- and it starts hardest where [confusion](@chbrain/khai-engine-confusion/process_confusion.md) is present, since an [impasse](@chbrain/khai-engine-confusion/process_confusion_impasse.md) held and worked to a [resolve](@chbrain/khai-engine-confusion/process_confusion_resolve.md) is the productive fork and a [stall](@chbrain/khai-engine-confusion/process_confusion_stall.md) is the one that abandons it, with a standing tolerance of [ambiguity](@chbrain/khai-engine-confusion/position_ambiguity.md) deciding which the persona can bear.
It is the face of the composite where a misreading stops being a misreading and becomes a rule.

## Direction

Toward an allocation that removes exactly what still needed the time.
The Ease reads the policy rather than the error: material that has gone smooth is marked finished and dropped, material that fights is marked unlearned and returned to -- which sounds correct and is correct in the cases where fluency tracks durability, and is precisely backwards in the cases where the smoothness was made by the session; and the same rule eliminates confusion on sight, which is the felt marker of the one condition that was working.
It moves the persona's effort off the material that needed it, again, every hour, in the same direction.

## Lever

The reasonableness of studying what feels unlearned against what that rule does over a long effort.
The carrying force is that no single application of the rule is wrong and the persona could defend every one of them: they are not avoiding difficulty out of cowardice, they are allocating scarce time by the only report they have, and a persona who ignored their own sense of what was unlearned would be steering by nothing at all; the resistance is a single closed-book question, asked cold, which is the whole of the correction and is almost never available -- because it costs an hour, it feels like a test rather than like study, and it is unpleasant precisely in proportion to how much it would tell them.
The Ease rides the composite's keystone knife-edge: the error does not average out over a long effort, it compounds, because the reading governs what gets read next -- and it compounds fastest in the persona who is working hardest.

## Echo

Diligence, running a policy that could not have been designed better to fail.
It shows the three engines complete -- a monitor with one loud cue, a trace built by conditions that quiet it, and a control loop closing between them -- and carries beneath it the composite's keystone: effort is the multiplier, so the persona who works the most hours is the one the mechanism costs the most, and there is nothing they could have noticed at the time.
This is why the Ease is the hardest of the three to stage and the only one worth closing on: the persona must be diligent rather than lazy, must have done the reasonable thing at every point, must be given no test until it is too late, and must be genuinely surprised -- and a play that has them cutting corners has staged an excuse rather than this.
The Ease is the note the whole composite closes on: what a persona never had to answer for cold is legible in what they stopped studying first.
The Ease is dated from the last easy pass, and it will not be audited for weeks.
