# Process: Glide, the Trace

## Taxonomy

[Glide](process_glide.md), what is actually being built while the reading is being taken.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: glide

## Initiated by

Something is going in, and something else is being made of it.
The Trace starts wherever [memory](@chbrain/khai-engine-memory/process_memory.md) is doing its work -- a cue meeting a trace and the persona rebuilding rather than replaying -- across the [semantic](@chbrain/khai-engine-memory/process_semantic.md) fact stripped of its scene, the [episodic](@chbrain/khai-engine-memory/process_episodic.md) scene re-entered, the [procedural](@chbrain/khai-engine-memory/process_procedural.md) skill run without words, and the [prospective](@chbrain/khai-engine-memory/process_prospective.md) intention held for later.
It is the face of the composite that nobody can see, including the persona it is happening in.

## Direction

Toward a durability that will not be tested for some time.
The Trace reads the quantity the monitor is trying to estimate, and reads that it is two quantities: how readily something comes to hand right now, and whether it will come to hand later from a cold start -- and these are not the same and do not have to move together, which is the whole difficulty.
It moves at its own rate, on conditions the persona is not consulted about.

## Lever

The work the conditions are doing against the persona's inability to observe them.
The carrying force is that the conditions genuinely decide the outcome: material met again after a gap, pulled up from nothing rather than reread, mixed with other material rather than blocked with its own kind, is built more durably -- these are real effects and they are large; the resistance is that every one of them makes the session feel worse, since a gap costs recall, a cold retrieval sometimes fails, and mixed material never settles into a rhythm -- so the regime that builds the trace and the regime that produces a smooth session are close to opposites, and the persona has no way to notice this from inside a single hour.
The Trace spends the persona's comfort in exact proportion to how much it is giving them.

## Echo

A durable thing being made unpleasantly, or a smooth hour making nothing much.
It is the bridge that supplies the mismatch the last one exploits, and it locates it precisely: the trouble is not that the monitor is crude but that its best cue is anti-correlated with the outcome over exactly the range that matters.
What it hands upward is two divergent quantities and one signal, and no error message when they come apart.
The Trace is dated from the delay before anybody finds out, which is the only clock it runs on.
