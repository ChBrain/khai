# Position: Information Processing

## Taxonomy

Movement of the hoarding composite. It routes into the `categorization` engine for the deficit in forming broad categories, and into the `decision` engine for the resulting paralysis.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: hoarding

## Has

A deficit in information processing where every item is perceived as unique and un-fileable. This relies on the categorization mechanisms in [categorization](@chbrain/khai-engine-categorization/process_categorization.md) and its related processes.

## Orders

The persona to avoid making choices about what to discard, leading to a state of decision paralysis, routing through [decision](@chbrain/khai-engine-decision/process_decision.md) and [decision](@chbrain/khai-engine-decision/process_paralysed.md).

## Loses

The ability to efficiently process and organize possessions.

## Drives

The accumulation of clutter as objects are saved because they cannot be categorized or decided upon.
