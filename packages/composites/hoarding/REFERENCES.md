# Hoarding: Reference

## Line of Work

Hoarding as **composite**: the syndrome read as a compound of six atoms this package declares as dependencies, none of which carries it alone. The composite adds only the read of the compound; every mechanism it names belongs to an atom.

**The organising claim is that the pile is not a collection of things kept, but a collection of decisions not made.** Frost & Hartl's model is what the composite is built on, and reading it that way is what keeps the engine from becoming a description of a room. Ordinary disposal is cheap because it is delegated: an object joins a kind, the kind carries a rule, the rule decides. Where categories will not generalise, each object arrives as its own exemplar, so a judgement that is normally free costs a full deliberation -- and nobody can afford several hundred deliberations a day. What follows is not untidiness but deferral, and the clutter is its residue.

**Four components, and they run as a cycle rather than a list.** _Information processing_ takes the categorisation and memory-confidence deficits: kinds that will not generalise, so there is no rule to stand on, and a memory that will not vouch for what it cannot see, so filing something away destroys it. _Emotional attachment_ is two beliefs rather than one, which is why it is so hard to argue with -- the object is an extension of the self (Belk's sense, carried by `extended-self`) and it is insurance against a future lack (carried by `scarcity`) -- and the two defeat different arguments, so _you'll never use it_ and _you can buy another_ each answer only half. _Behavioural avoidance_ is where the cycle closes: the escape from the distress of deciding is **keeping**, which removes the distress instantly at no visible cost and is therefore reinforced on every use. _Excessive acquisition_, the specifier carried by the large majority of cases, is the same failure at the other door.

**The twist is that the value is manufactured at the moment of discard.** While an object sits unregarded the persona will call it worthless and mean it. Open the bag and it becomes unbearable to lose. So the attachment that defeats a clear-out is created _by_ the clear-out: every attempt to solve the problem generates the resistance that beats it, the persona is sincere in both statements an hour apart, and help delivered as force -- clearing the house while they are out -- reliably produces both a bereavement and a refilled house.

**On the boundary with OCD, and why it matters here.** Hoarding was a criterion of obsessive-compulsive personality disorder and a symptom dimension of OCD before **DSM-5** made it a diagnosis in its own right, and **Mataix-Cols et al.**'s review is the argument that carried: saving is frequently ego-syntonic rather than an ego-dystonic obsession, the course is chronic and progressive with age rather than waxing and waning, response to standard OCD protocols is poor, and only a minority of hoarding cases carry OCD. The composite takes that split seriously and routes nothing through an obsessional mechanism. But it is worth being exact about what the split licenses: it establishes that hoarding is not OCD, not that hoarding is simple. Every mechanism above belongs to an existing atom and only the syndrome is new, which is why this is a composite rather than an engine.

**On the evidence, stated plainly.** Frost & Hartl's model is the field's organising framework and is a clinical-cognitive formulation rather than a measured causal chain; its components are supported unevenly. The categorisation and decision-making deficits are the best evidenced of the four, though effect sizes are modest and overlap heavily with unaffected populations. The memory-confidence account is well described and less settled. Excessive acquisition is a specifier rather than a requirement, so a persona without it is still within the syndrome. And the composite deliberately does not model insight, which varies widely and is often poor -- a persona may hold the room to be normal, which changes how the twist plays but is a variable the author sets rather than something the engine fixes. What should not be taken from it: that any of this is a diagnosis, that a cluttered room implies it, or that the persona is choosing.

## Restrictions

What the composite refuses to model, and to whom it delegates.

- **The forming of kinds (categorization)**: Graded membership, prototypes, exemplars, and theory-based concepts belong to the `categorization` engine. This composite reads only the case where kinds will not generalise, so disposal has no rule to stand on.
- **The resolving between options (decision)**: Intuitive, deliberate, heuristic, and satisficing routes belong to the `decision` engine. This composite reads only the paralysis specific to discarding, where the case is unique and no threshold permits satisficing.
- **The possession that carries identity (extended-self)**: Appropriation, the possessor, and dispossession belong to the `extended-self` engine. This composite reads only the case where the claim attaches to ordinary objects in quantity.
- **The felt lack (scarcity)**: Tunnelling, bandwidth, and the trap belong to the `scarcity` engine. This composite reads only the object held as insurance against a future want.
- **The handling of a stressor (coping)**: Appraisal, problem-focused, and escape routes belong to the `coping` engine. This composite reads only the case where the escape _is_ retention, and is therefore reinforced on every use.
- **The bounded holding of objects (collection)**: The collector, the hunt, completion, and dispersal belong to the `collection` engine. That engine is the ordinary case this composite is defined against: a series with edges and a state called complete, neither of which is available here.
- **Obsession and compulsion**: The composite routes nothing through an obsessional mechanism, following the DSM-5 split. A persona whose saving is driven by ego-dystonic intrusions and neutralising rituals is not this.
- **Insight**: How much the persona holds the situation to be a problem varies widely and is often poor. The composite leaves it to the author rather than fixing it.
- **Diagnosis**: The composite is authored persona architecture for a play. It is not a diagnostic instrument, carries no criteria or thresholds, and makes no claim about any real person.

## Encoding

Source to constraint. Frost & Hartl's information-processing component becomes the first movement's Has, and their model's cyclic structure becomes the root's Direction -- deficit to stall to escape to attachment, and round. Their avoidance component becomes the third movement, whose Has states the thing the model is easiest to misread on: the escape is keeping, not discarding badly, which is why it is reinforced. Frost & Steketee's case material becomes the root's Lever and its twist, including why a forced clearance produces a bereavement and a refilled house. Mataix-Cols et al.'s review becomes the Restriction that routes nothing through obsession, and the Line of Work's care about what the split does and does not license. Pertusa and Saxena's boundary work becomes the evidence limits, stated rather than smoothed.
