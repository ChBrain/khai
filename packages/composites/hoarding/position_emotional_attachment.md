# Position: Emotional Attachment

## Taxonomy

Movement of the hoarding composite. It routes into the `extended-self` engine for the emotional bond to possessions, and the `scarcity` engine for the perceived future need of the objects.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: hoarding

## Has

A profound emotional attachment to possessions, viewing them as extensions of identity. This relies on the mechanisms described in [extended-self](@chbrain/khai-engine-extended-self/piece_extended_self.md) and [extended-self](@chbrain/khai-engine-extended-self/process_appropriation.md).

## Orders

The persona to preserve items to prevent the loss of identity or memories, and to guard against future deprivation, routing through [scarcity](@chbrain/khai-engine-scarcity/process_scarcity.md) and [scarcity](@chbrain/khai-engine-scarcity/process_trap.md).

## Loses

The ability to separate personal identity from inanimate objects.

## Drives

The refusal to discard items, even those with no objective value, due to the distress of losing a part of the self or facing imagined future scarcity.
