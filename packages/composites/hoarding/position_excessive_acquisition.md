# Position: Excessive Acquisition

## Taxonomy

Movement of the hoarding composite. It routes into the `collection` engine to model the excessive acquisition that is the primary specifier for most hoarding cases.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: hoarding

## Has

An excessive drive to acquire items that are not needed and for which there is no available space. This relies on the mechanisms described in [collection](@chbrain/khai-engine-collection/piece_collection.md) and [collection](@chbrain/khai-engine-collection/process_hunting.md), but lacks the order and boundaries of a typical collection.

## Orders

The persona to actively gather objects (through buying, collecting free items, or even stealing) providing temporary pleasure or relief from negative affect.

## Loses

Control over the intake of possessions, leading to an environment overwhelmed by clutter.

## Drives

The continued expansion of the hoard, exacerbating the distress and dysfunction caused by the information processing deficits and emotional attachments.
