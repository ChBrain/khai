# Position: Hoarding, Excessive Acquisition

## Taxonomy

[Hoarding](process_hoarding.md), the same mechanism running at the door rather than at the bin.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: hoarding

## Has

The fourth component, carried by the large majority of cases as a specifier rather than by all of them, and it is the same failure encountered from the other side. An object met in the world is also singular and unrepeatable, so passing it up is also a decision without a rule, and _acquiring_ is the move that costs nothing now -- the mirror of the retention that ends the stall at home. Where a [collector](@chbrain/khai-engine-collection/position_collector.md) is [hunting](@chbrain/khai-engine-collection/process_hunting.md) a defined series toward [completion](@chbrain/khai-engine-collection/process_completing.md), this persona is not: there is no set, no boundary, and no state in which the holding is finished, which is exactly the line between the ordinary case and this one. Acquisition is free at the point of use -- skips, sales, giveaways, things simply found -- so the constraint that ordinarily limits accumulation, cost, is not operating either.

## Orders

Take it now, because the reason not to is a decision and the reason to is a feeling.
The excessive-acquisition movement orders the persona to gather -- the free newspaper, the third kettle, the box someone left out -- and to experience the taking as opportunity rather than as risk, since the future in which it becomes another undischarged judgement is not present at the moment of taking. The room sees someone bringing more in while unable to let anything out and reads it as the contradiction that proves the whole thing is a choice. It stages the two halves as one mechanism: the persona is not inconsistent, they are applying the same broken rule at both doors.

## Loses

The boundary that would make holding things finishable. Carrying this movement, the persona forgoes the collector's great advantage -- a set with edges, a state called complete, a reason to stop -- and pays in an estate that grows faster than any clearing can reduce it. It loses the defence of thrift, since the acquisitions are genuinely cheap or free and therefore look harmless one at a time. And it loses the last of the room's patience: whatever sympathy the difficulty of discarding earns is spent the moment more arrives at the door, because to an observer that is the point at which the problem stops looking involuntary. What it trades is a bounded holding for an intake with no closing condition.

## Drives

On that open intake:
the object in the world is as unfileable as the object at home, and taking it is the move that costs nothing, so the estate grows from both ends. Excessive acquisition is one of four movements, the specifier most cases carry, set beside the [information processing](position_information_processing.md) whose failure it shares, the [emotional attachment](position_emotional_attachment.md) that will price the new object once it is held, and the [behavioral avoidance](position_behavioral_avoidance.md) that is its exact counterpart at the bin. That both doors run one mechanism is the [root's](process_hoarding.md); the ordinary bounded holding of objects -- the collector, the hunt, the completion -- is the collection engine's, where this movement owns the specifically hoarding case in which there is no series, no boundary, and no condition under which the gathering is done.
