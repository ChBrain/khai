# Position: Behavioral Avoidance

## Taxonomy

Movement of the hoarding composite. It routes into the `coping` engine to represent the use of saving and accumulation to avoid the distress of discarding.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: hoarding

## Has

A tendency to experience intense negative affect when faced with the prospect of discarding possessions.

## Orders

The persona to avoid the distress by postponing or abandoning the decision to discard, functioning as an avoidant coping mechanism. This routes through [coping](@chbrain/khai-engine-coping/process_coping.md) and [coping](@chbrain/khai-engine-coping/process_escape.md).

## Loses

The opportunity to process the negative affect, reinforcing the belief that discarding is intolerable.

## Drives

The chronic maintenance of the hoarding behavior, as the temporary relief of avoiding the decision negatively reinforces the saving behavior.
