# Position: Hoarding, Behavioral Avoidance

## Taxonomy

[Hoarding](process_hoarding.md), the saving chosen because it is the move that costs nothing now.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: hoarding

## Has

The third component, and the one that makes the syndrome self-sustaining rather than merely painful. Given an object that will not file and an attachment that prices its loss highly, the persona faces a stressor, and [coping](@chbrain/khai-engine-coping/process_coping.md) supplies the available responses. The [appraisal](@chbrain/khai-engine-coping/process_appraisal.md) is accurate: discarding _will_ hurt, and the hurt is immediate and certain. So the [escape](@chbrain/khai-engine-coping/process_escape.md) is taken -- and the crucial point is what the escape actually is. It is not throwing the object away badly. It is **keeping**, which removes the distress completely, instantly, and at no visible cost, and is therefore reinforced every single time it is used. [Problem-focused](@chbrain/khai-engine-coping/process_problem.md) coping is available in principle and is never selected, because it requires paying now for a benefit that is diffuse and later. The clutter is the accumulated residue of a strategy that works.

## Orders

Take the option that ends the discomfort, which is always the same option.
The behavioral-avoidance movement orders the persona to postpone -- to close the bag, to move the pile to another room, to schedule the sorting for a weekend that arrives and passes -- and to feel relief each time, because relief is exactly what the move delivers. The room sees procrastination and reads it as a failure of will, applying the remedy for laziness to a behaviour that is not lazy but successful. It stages a persona who is not avoiding effort but avoiding _loss_, and who has found a reliable way to avoid it, which is why exhortation and deadlines change nothing.

## Loses

The future, in exchange for the hour. Carrying this movement, the persona forgoes the small distributed cost of deciding as it goes, and pays a compounding one: every deferral makes the next pass larger, the room less usable, the task more shameful and therefore more avoided. It loses the ability to invite anyone in, which removes the outside pressure that might have interrupted the cycle and the help that might have made it bearable. And it loses the standing to be believed about its own intentions -- having said many times that it will deal with it, and meant it each time, it is now heard as someone who says that. What it trades is a life-sized problem later for no discomfort at all today, which is a bargain the nervous system will take every time it is offered.

## Drives

On that reinforced escape:
keeping ends the distress immediately and costs nothing visible, so it is chosen again, and the residue accumulates. Behavioral avoidance is one of four movements, the engine of the cycle, set beside the [information processing](position_information_processing.md) that creates the stall and the [emotional attachment](position_emotional_attachment.md) that makes the stall painful, with the [excessive acquisition](position_excessive_acquisition.md) filling from the other end. That the pile is the residue of a strategy that works rather than a failure of effort is the [root's](process_hoarding.md); the effortful, appraisal-driven handling of a stressor is the coping engine's, where this movement owns the specifically hoarding case in which the escape from distress is _retention_, and is therefore rewarded on every use.
