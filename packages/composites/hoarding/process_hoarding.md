# Process: Hoarding

## Taxonomy

The composite. The root reads the multi-component syndrome of hoarding as formulated by Frost & Hartl, reading over information processing deficits (categorization, decision), emotional attachment to possessions (extended-self), and the avoidance of distress (coping), often accompanied by excessive acquisition (collection). It is decoupled from OCD.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: hoarding

## Initiated by

The presentation of a possession, a need to discard an item, or an opportunity to acquire a new one.

## Direction

The syndrome operates as a self-reinforcing cycle. The categorization deficit makes every item seem unique, leading to decision paralysis. To avoid the distress of deciding and discarding, the persona saves the item. The emotional attachment to the item deepens over time, increasing the perceived cost of discarding. The resulting clutter impairs function, but the underlying mechanisms protect the hoard.

The persona's living space becomes unmanageable, relationships may be strained due to the clutter, and the distress associated with discarding prevents resolution.
