# Instructions: Hoarding

- Sets the requirement for the composite.
- Decides whether the disorder is a background trait or the central plot driver.

## Agent

- Speaks as the Playwright.
- Writes the persona using the hoarding composite and its atoms.
- Ensures the mechanics of the composite are accurately represented.

## Collaboration

- The Playwright consults the Director for staging requirements.
- The Playwright refines the implementation based on the Human's feedback.

- Understands that hoarding is a distinct syndrome, not a form of OCD.
- Knows the six atoms of the composite: `categorization`, `extended-self`, `decision`, `scarcity`, `coping`, and `collection`.
- Avoids treating the disorder as a simple symptom list.

## System
