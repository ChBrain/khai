# Process: Spectating, the Access

## Taxonomy

[Spectating](process_spectating.md), what the audience is let to know and whose knowledge they hold.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: spectating

## Initiated by

The audience has been told something a figure in the story has not.
The Access starts wherever [dramatic irony](@chbrain/khai-engine-dramatic-irony/process_dramatic_irony.md) is running -- the [foreknowledge](@chbrain/khai-engine-dramatic-irony/process_foreknowledge.md) that puts the audience ahead of somebody, and the [double meaning](@chbrain/khai-engine-dramatic-irony/process_double_meaning.md) by which every line then arrives twice, as the figure means it and as the audience hears it.
It is the face of the composite the teller sets first and the audience never sets at all.

## Direction

Toward a seat with a particular view, occupied without being chosen.
The Access reads the distribution rather than the information -- who was told, who was not, in what order, and how long the gap is held open -- and reads what a gap does once it exists: the audience cannot un-know, so they read every subsequent line against what the figure cannot see, and the figure's ordinary sentence becomes unbearable, or funny, or damning, entirely by virtue of the seat.
It moves the audience nowhere and decides most of what follows.

## Lever

The richness a gap buys against the fact that nobody in the audience is choosing the seat.
The carrying force is that discrepant awareness is the most productive instrument a teller has: it makes an ordinary exchange carry weight, converts a plain line into two, and generates the ache of watching somebody walk into a thing -- all without any character behaving differently; the resistance is that it is calibrated entirely from outside the audience, and the audience has no way to feel the calibration, since what one has been told does not arrive labelled as a choice somebody made.
The Access spends nothing of the audience's and sets the terms of everything they will do next.

## Echo

An audience seated, informed, and unaware of having been placed.
It is the bridge that establishes the composite's order: this is the instrument, and the other two are what it produces, so a production that wants a verdict works here rather than on the verdict.
What it hands upward is the thing the next bridge will not be able to see -- that the moral apparatus about to run has already had its inputs chosen.
The Access is dated from what the audience was told, and from the moment they could no longer not know it.
