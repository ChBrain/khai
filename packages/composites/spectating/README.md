# Spectating

Spectating as composite: what a story does to the one receiving it -- the knowledge they are given and the head they are given it from (the access), the moral side they take and the enjoyment that rides on it (the siding), and the carrying-in that stills the counterarguing (the carry) -- wired over the dramatic-irony, allegiance, and transportation engines, and closing on the discovery that the state in which a story works best is the state in which its audience can least tell what it is doing to them.
