# Process: Spectating

## Taxonomy

What a story does to the one receiving it.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: spectating

## Initiated by

A story is being told and somebody is taking it in.
Spectating starts wherever that is running: as the gap of discrepant awareness in which they know what a figure does not, through [dramatic irony](@chbrain/khai-engine-dramatic-irony/process_dramatic_irony.md); as the moral bond they form to the figures, through [allegiance](@chbrain/khai-engine-allegiance/process_allegiance.md); and as the carrying-in that takes them out of the room, through [transportation](@chbrain/khai-engine-transportation/process_transportation.md).
This composite adds no fourth engine. It stages the dependency the allegiance atom already declares when it anchors Smith -- that a spectator's siding rests on their alignment -- and follows it to what that leaves the receiving persona holding.

## Direction

Toward a verdict the audience will experience as theirs, reached from a position they did not set.
The composite wires the three engines and reads their working in three shapes: [the Access](process_spectating_access.md), what they are let to know and from whom; [the Siding](process_spectating_siding.md), the moral bond that follows; and [the Carry](process_spectating_carry.md), the twist -- the absorption that stills the checking.
The three are not three pleasures. They are one arrangement, set in that order by whoever is telling, and received in reverse.

## Lever

The audience's real judgement against the fact that its inputs are somebody's to choose.
The carrying force is that nothing here is a trick and the faculties are working properly: an audience genuinely monitors the figures, genuinely sorts them, genuinely feels the fortunes land, and the monitoring is the same moral apparatus they use on people -- which is why stories reach it at all; the resistance is that the apparatus runs on what it is given, and what it is given is authored, so the same conduct produces opposite verdicts depending on whose interiority the audience was let into and in what order they learned things.
Spectating is read on the persona receiving the story, never on the story: this composite models the audience, and what is done to them is the play's.

## Echo

A persona holding a conviction about people who do not exist, arrived at honestly.
Spectating does not close; it is the receiving running, gathered into one reading -- an access, a siding, a carry.
It hands off what it is not: the affective shapes an audience is taken through -- the pity and grief, the amusement, the wonder and confusion, the dread of the not-yet -- are the tragic, comic, knowing, and jeopardy composites', which own what is felt where this owns the position it is felt from; a mind going elsewhere of its own accord, imagined or remembered, is the elsewhere composite's, which wires this same transportation engine as one of the ways a mind leaves the room, where this reads what a made story does once the audience is inside; attention held as such is the absorption composite's; one persona working on another's position by argument is the swaying composite's, and the boundary is the useful one, since a story is not received as an attempt on anybody and therefore never raises what an attempt raises; the authored key a production sets over its own events is the play's pitch, which is the instrument this composite reads the effect of and never the effect itself.
The persona is dated from what they were told, and by whom, and in what order.
