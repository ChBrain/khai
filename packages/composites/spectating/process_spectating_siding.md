# Process: Spectating, the Siding

## Taxonomy

[Spectating](process_spectating.md), the moral bond the audience forms, and the one they will call their own.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: spectating

## Initiated by

The audience has begun sorting the figures into the ones they want to do well and the ones they do not.
The Siding starts wherever [allegiance](@chbrain/khai-engine-allegiance/process_allegiance.md) is running -- the untiring [verdict](@chbrain/khai-engine-allegiance/process_verdict.md) that monitors and sorts, the [rooting](@chbrain/khai-engine-allegiance/process_rooting.md) that follows it, and the [payoff](@chbrain/khai-engine-allegiance/process_payoff.md) by which enjoyment rides on whether fortunes match the disposition.
It is the face of the composite the audience is most certain about.

## Direction

Toward wanting particular outcomes for particular people, and enjoying the story to the degree it supplies them.
The Siding reads the monitoring as the ordinary moral apparatus running on supplied inputs -- the same sorting an audience does on the people around them, applied to figures who do not exist -- and reads what it produces: a stake. Once the sorting has happened the audience is no longer watching events, they are waiting for outcomes, and the pleasure of the thing has become contingent on the outcomes matching.
It moves the audience from observing a story into having something at risk in it.

## Lever

The genuineness of the verdict against the authorship of what it was reached on.
The carrying force is that the judging is real and not naive: an audience will revise, will hold a figure to account for what they do rather than what they are called, and will refuse a verdict a story has not earned -- which is why disposition cannot simply be assigned by a teller and has to be produced; the resistance is that the apparatus is fed, and the same conduct yields opposite verdicts depending on whose interiority the audience was let into -- give them the reasons and the sorting moves, with nothing in the conduct changed at all.
The Siding spends the audience's neutrality and returns a stake, on inputs the previous bridge chose.

## Echo

A conviction about people who do not exist, arrived at honestly and not independently.
It is the bridge where the composite's claim comes due: the audience experiences the verdict as a judgement about conduct, and it is substantially a report on position -- so the certainty is real, the reasoning is sound, and the inputs were somebody's to pick.
What it hands upward is the question the last bridge answers badly: whether an audience, in the state a good story puts them in, retains the capacity to notice any of this.
The Siding is dated from the first outcome the audience wanted, which they will remember as their own reading.
