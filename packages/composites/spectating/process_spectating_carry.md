# Process: Spectating, the Carry

## Taxonomy

[Spectating](process_spectating.md), the audience carried in, and where the three are seen for one thing.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: spectating

## Initiated by

The room has gone, and the audience is in the story instead.
The Carry starts wherever [transportation](@chbrain/khai-engine-transportation/process_transportation.md) is running -- the [entry](@chbrain/khai-engine-transportation/process_entry.md) that takes them out of the here-and-now, the [imagery](@chbrain/khai-engine-transportation/process_imagery.md) that furnishes what they are in, and the [resonance](@chbrain/khai-engine-transportation/process_resonance.md) that keeps them there -- and it goes further or less far by a standing [transportability](@chbrain/khai-engine-transportation/position_transportability.md) that varies by person.
It is the face of the composite everyone wants, including the audience.

## Direction

Toward a state in which the story is the world and nothing is being weighed against it.
The Carry reads what the absorption costs rather than what it gives -- and what it costs is the counterarguing: a persona reading a claim in an essay meets it with objections, and the same persona inside a narrative world does not, because objecting requires standing outside a thing and they are not outside it.
It moves the audience all the way in, and takes their footing with them.

## Lever

The depth of the immersion against the faculty the immersion suspends.
The carrying force is that this is the state everybody is after: the story lands, the figures matter, the hours pass, and an audience that is not carried has had a worse evening and taken less from it -- so nobody involved, on either side, has any interest in less of it; the resistance is that the suspended faculty is precisely the one that would notice the seat they were put in and the inputs their verdict ran on, and it is suspended by the very thing that makes the story good, so the check is unavailable in exact proportion to how well the thing is working.
The Carry rides the composite's keystone knife-edge: the state in which a story works best is the state in which its audience can least tell what it is doing to them.

## Echo

All the way in, and holding a verdict they will keep.
It shows the three complete -- an access somebody set, a siding it produced, and a carry that removes the standing from which either could be examined -- and carries beneath it the composite's keystone: the audience's certainty about the people in a story is the least independent thing about their experience and feels like the most, and the better the telling, the more that is true.
This is why it is worth closing on and hardest to stage without cheapening: nothing here is deception, nobody is being fooled, and the faculties are all working correctly. A story is simply not received as an attempt on anybody, so it never raises what an attempt raises, and the audience walks out with a conviction they built themselves out of materials they did not choose.
The Carry is the note the whole composite closes on: what a story did to an audience is legible in what they were told and when, and not at all in what they conclude they think.
The Carry is dated from the moment the room went away.
