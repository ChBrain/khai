# Instructions: Spectating

How a Playwright wires the spectating composite into a play. You wire by linking the
play's own content to the composite's files; the files stay untouched, they are canon.
The composite installs the dramatic-irony, allegiance, and transportation engines as
dependencies, and their laws arrive with them. Authoring guidance, not runtime content,
and it does not go on tour.

## Human

- The human sets what the audience is to be told and when, and whose inside they are to be given -- which is the same as setting what they will conclude, and should be decided on purpose.

## Agent

- From the play's own content, read the persona receiving the story and link under Projection what is running: the access while knowledge is being distributed (the foreknowledge that puts them ahead of a figure, the double meaning by which every line then arrives twice, from dramatic-irony), the siding while a verdict is forming (the untiring monitoring that sorts the figures, the rooting that follows, the enjoyment riding on fortunes matching it, from allegiance), the carry while they have gone in (the entry out of the here-and-now, the imagery that furnishes it, the resonance that keeps them, tuned by a standing transportability, from transportation). Set the access first, since it is the instrument and the other two follow from it. You link to the composite, you never edit it.

## Collaboration

- Foreknowledge and double meaning are the dramatic-irony engine's; the verdict, the rooting, and the payoff are allegiance's; entry, imagery, resonance, and transportability are transportation's -- one alone is a process, not the arrangement. What is felt while watching is the tragic, comic, knowing, and jeopardy composites', which own the affective shape where this owns the position it is felt from; a mind leaving the room of its own accord is elsewhere's, which wires this same transportation engine and asks what a persona does when they go, where this asks what a made story does once they are inside; attention held as such is absorption's; working on a position by argument is swaying's, and the contrast is the point rather than an overlap; the authored key a production sets over its own events is the play's pitch, which is the instrument this reads the effect of and never the effect. This composite owns the position -- and the carry bridge carries the keystone: the state in which a story works best is the state in which its audience can least tell what it is doing to them.

## Knowledge

- Spectating is the one persona a play does not cast: whoever is taking it in, read as a persona like any other, since an audience monitors and sorts and judges with the same apparatus it uses on people. It braids the gap of discrepant awareness in which they hold knowledge a figure lacks (dramatic-irony), the moral bond they form to the figures (allegiance), and the carrying-in that takes them out of the room (transportation). The join is Smith's and the allegiance engine already carries it: the structure of sympathy runs in three levels -- recognition of a figure as an agent, alignment with them through access to what they know and feel, and allegiance, the moral evaluation, which rests on the alignment beneath it. That dependency is the claim. Allegiance cannot simply be assigned by a teller: an audience holds a figure to what they do rather than to what they are called, revises, and refuses a verdict a story has not earned. But the apparatus is fed, and what it is fed is who the audience was let into and in what order -- so the same conduct produces opposite verdicts depending on the seat, with nothing in the conduct changed. It runs in three faces, in the order the teller sets them. The access is what the audience is told and whose knowledge they hold: a gap cannot be un-known, so every subsequent line arrives twice, as the figure means it and as the audience hears it, and an ordinary sentence becomes unbearable or funny or damning entirely by virtue of the seat -- calibrated wholly from outside the audience, who have no way to feel the calibration, since what one has been told does not arrive labelled as somebody's choice (Pfister, Sternberg, Evans). The siding is the moral monitoring that sorts the figures and the rooting that follows, and it produces a stake: once the sorting has happened the audience is no longer watching events but waiting for outcomes, and the pleasure has become contingent on the outcomes matching (Zillmann and Cantor; Raney). It is genuine, revisable, and reached on inputs somebody chose. The carry is the twist: absorption into the story world stills the counterarguing, because objecting requires standing outside a thing and the audience is not outside it, so belief shifts in the story's direction and shifts further the further they were carried (Green and Brock). Here the three are seen for one thing: the state that makes a story work suspends the faculty that would notice the seat and the inputs, so the check is unavailable in exact proportion to how well the telling is going. Nothing here is deception and nobody is fooled -- the faculties are all working correctly, and a story is simply not received as an attempt on anybody, so it never raises what an attempt raises. Read an audience by what they were told and when, and never by what they conclude they think.

## System

- Do read the audience, not the story: name whether the access (knowledge being distributed), the siding (a verdict forming), or the carry (they have gone in) is running, and cast each from its engines.
- Do set the access on purpose and first: it is the instrument, so a production that wants a verdict works on who is let in and when, not on the verdict.
- Do let the siding be earned: an audience refuses a disposition a story has not produced, so a figure declared sympathetic and shown otherwise will be sorted on the showing.
- Do let the verdict feel like the audience's own, because it is -- reached honestly, by a sound apparatus, on materials they did not choose.
- Do let the carry carry the keystone: the better the telling, the less available the check, so the most effective evening is the one after which the audience is least able to say what happened to them.
- Do not stage this as manipulation: nobody is deceived, the faculties work, and a play that makes its audience dupes has misread a structural fact as a trick.
- Do not swap it with argument: a story reaches a position by going around the defences rather than through them, so swaying's instruments and this composite's are not interchangeable, and a scene that argues has left this composite.
- Do not model what is felt while watching (that is tragic, comic, knowing, jeopardy), a mind leaving of its own accord (that is elsewhere, wiring the same engine to a different question), attention held as such (that is absorption), or the authored key itself (that is the play's pitch, which this reads the effect of). Do not edit the composite's files; wire only from the play's side.
