# Process: Pilgrimage, the Arrival

## Taxonomy

[Pilgrimage](process_pilgrimage.md), the visitors and what they can and cannot do.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: pilgrimage

## Initiated by

Somebody comes who did not know them.
The Arrival starts when the plot acquires [a marker](@chbrain/khai-engine-tourism/process_the_marker.md): a mention in a guide, a line in a book, a documentary, a route drawn on a map by somebody who never met the dead.

## Direction

Toward a grave with two kinds of visitor standing in the same square metre.
Nothing is intrinsically worth seeing, so what makes this plot a destination is that it has been said to be one, and the people who come arrive holding [a prior image](@chbrain/khai-engine-tourism/process_the_prior_image.md) of it. They come for real reasons: a death that stands for something they care about, a life that mattered to them at a distance, a history they want to be near, and sometimes a grief of their own with nowhere else to put it.
What they do when they get there is an hour: read the stone, stand, photograph, leave something, go. What [the visiting](@chbrain/khai-engine-grave/process_the_visiting.md) requires is secateurs, water, a cloth, and a return in six weeks.

## Lever

The sincerity of the people who come against the one thing none of them can do.
The carrying force is that the visitors are not intruders and the attention is not a desecration: they are moved, they behave well, they are often the only people who still say the name aloud, and a family that has watched everybody else forget can find the strangers a comfort rather than an affront; the resistance is that the object issues chores and none of them is a chore anybody will drive three hours to perform, so the number of people at the plot rises and the number of hands on it does not -- and the tributes they leave are themselves things that will have to be cleared away by whoever is doing the clearing.
The Arrival puts more people at the grave and no more hands on it.

## Echo

A visited plot with the same one relative weeding it.
It is where the two atoms first touch, and the touch is physical: a family's maintenance schedule meeting a stream of hours that leave objects behind.
The Arrival is dated from the first mention in print and does not stop once it starts.
