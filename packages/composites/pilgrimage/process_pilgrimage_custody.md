# Process: Pilgrimage, the Custody

## Taxonomy

[Pilgrimage](process_pilgrimage.md), who maintains it and to whose standard.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: pilgrimage

## Initiated by

The upkeep outgrows the family.
The Custody starts when a plot that one relative could keep decent needs a gravel path, a bin, a sign, a repair to a stone that is being touched by two hundred people a month, and somebody to answer the letters.

## Direction

Toward maintenance paid for by strangers and therefore specified by them.
A trust, a council, a society, or a foundation takes it on, and with the money comes a standard: presentable rather than decent, legible to somebody who has just arrived, safe for numbers, consistent with the other sites in the portfolio. Opening hours appear. So does a rope, a plaque explaining who is under the stone, and a rule about what may be left.
The small absolute powers that [the next of kin](@chbrain/khai-engine-grave/position_next_of_kin.md) held -- the wording, who else may go in, whether to renew -- become a consultation, and consultation is a different thing from standing.

## Lever

A grave that will now never lapse against a family that no longer decides what it looks like.
The carrying force is that this is a rescue and everybody involved knows it: [the lapsing](@chbrain/khai-engine-grave/process_the_lapsing.md) is the ordinary fate of every plot and it has been averted here, the term will be renewed forever, the lettering will be recut before it fades, and the relatives are relieved of a duty that was outlasting their capacity to perform it; the resistance is that whoever pays sets the standard, and the standard is written for people arriving with no context -- so the plot becomes clear, safe, explained, and slightly unlike anything the family would have chosen.
The Custody buys the object a future and prices it in decisions.

## Echo

A perfectly kept grave under somebody else's rules.
It is the mechanism by which the transfer happens, and it happens through generosity rather than through any dispute.
The Custody is dated from the first grant and is renewed with every budget.
