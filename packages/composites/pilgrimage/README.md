# Pilgrimage

Pilgrimage as composite: what happens to a death when strangers start coming to the place of it. It is not a third mechanism but a wiring of two -- the tourism engine supplies the marking that makes somewhere worth going and the account that travels home, and the grave engine supplies the leased plot, the burial right, and a relationship that consists entirely of maintenance. This composite adds no third engine; it reads the one thing the two make together, which is a death looked after by people it did not happen to. Three bridges: the Arrival, where sincere visitors come in numbers and none of them can perform the chores the object issues, so the plot gains people and no hands; the Custody, where the upkeep outgrows the family and a trust takes it on, buying the grave a future it would never otherwise have had and pricing it in decisions, since whoever pays sets a standard written for strangers; and the Claim, the twist -- the better a death is looked after, the less of it belongs to the people it happened to.

This engine is defined by its [manifest](package.json), which the canon renders as the WIRES card. The manifest is the single source of truth; this README is generated, not edited by hand.
