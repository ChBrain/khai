# Process: Time

## Taxonomy

Temporality, the whole.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: time

## Initiated by

Everything that exists exists in time, and time bears on it two ways at once.
Time starts wherever a thing or an unfolding is placed in it: the measured passage it accrues -- [chronos](@chbrain/khai-engine-chronos/process_chronos.md), the clock running and the years gathering -- and the opportune moments that punctuate it -- [kairos](@chbrain/khai-engine-kairos/process_kairos.md), the fitting instant to act.
This composite adds no new force; it reads what the two make together -- the flow and the moment, and how they meet.

## Direction

Toward the whole of a thing's or an unfolding's life in time, read through its two faces.
The composite wires the two time-engines and reads their phase relation in three shapes: [timeliness](process_time_timeliness.md), the moment that lands right on the passage, the two clocks agreeing; [untimeliness](process_time_untimeliness.md), the moment out of time -- too early, too late, or missed -- the two clocks at odds; and [fullness](process_time_fullness.md), the twist, where the long passage come to maturation meets the moment come to ripeness, the right thing at the right time.
On an object the measured face is cast from chronos under its Yearbook; on an unfolding -- a plot, a plan, a process -- the moment face is cast from kairos under its own chapter; the composite reads what the two make.

## Lever

The agreement of the two clocks against their falling out of step.
The carrying force is that passage and moment can be brought into tune -- the ripe act done at the right point in the flow, the season met; the resistance is that they drift apart -- the moment comes before the thing is ready, or the thing is ready but the moment has passed.
Time whole is read where chronos and kairos are read together: not the measure alone, nor the moment alone, but whether the two fall into step or out of it.

## Echo

The thing or unfolding read in the whole of its time -- in season, out of season, or come to its fullness.
Time does not close; it is the medium all else runs in, and this composite gathers its two faces into one reading.
It hands off what it is not: the material the years work is decay's and wear's, the object's status-career biography's, a persona's felt time time-perception's; time keeps the measure and the moment, and how they meet.
What follows is dated from the time it was read in -- whether the moment came in tune with the passage, or out of it.
