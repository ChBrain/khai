# Process: Time, Untimeliness

## Taxonomy

[Time](process_time.md), the bridge where the moment falls out of the passage.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: time

## Initiated by

The moment and the passage are at odds -- the act comes too early or too late, the window missed, or the thing itself out of its time.
Here [kairos](@chbrain/khai-engine-kairos/process_kairos.md)'s [window](@chbrain/khai-engine-kairos/process_window.md) has shut while [chronos](@chbrain/khai-engine-chronos/process_chronos.md) ran on, or a thing's [age](@chbrain/khai-engine-chronos/process_age.md) leaves it out of its era -- the anachronism, the relic among the new.
Untimeliness starts wherever the two clocks disagree: the ripe act with no moment left, the moment with the thing not yet ready, the thing whose time has passed.

## Direction

Toward the thing or act out of time.
The premature comes before the passage has readied it; the belated comes after the moment has shut; the outdated is a thing whose era the flow has left behind.
It moves the thing or unfolding out of step -- the plan sprung too soon, the chance taken too late, the object legible only against a time it no longer belongs to.

## Lever

The cost of the mismatch against the wish that the two had met.
The carrying force is that passage and moment can fall out of step and usually do -- too early spends the moment before the thing is ready, too late lets it pass, and the out-of-era thing reads as strange against the present; the resistance is whatever would have brought the two into tune, the read of the moment or the patience for the passage that was not there.
Untimeliness is read where chronos and kairos are read together and found at odds.

## Echo

The moment missed or the thing outdated, the two clocks disagreeing.
Untimeliness leaves an act that failed though the thing was ready, or a thing stranded out of its era though it endures -- the premature, the belated, the anachronistic.
It is dated from the mismatch, and it is the bridge that keeps time honest: not every act meets its moment, and not every thing belongs to the time it finds itself in.
