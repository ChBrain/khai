# Process: Time, Timeliness

## Taxonomy

[Time](process_time.md), the bridge where the moment lands right on the passage.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: time

## Initiated by

The opportune moment comes at the right point in the measured flow, and the two clocks agree.
Here [chronos](@chbrain/khai-engine-chronos/process_chronos.md)'s passage and [kairos](@chbrain/khai-engine-kairos/process_kairos.md)'s moment fall into step: the act done in season, the [ripe](@chbrain/khai-engine-kairos/process_ripeness.md) moment met when the thing is [matured](@chbrain/khai-engine-chronos/process_maturation.md) enough to meet it.
Timeliness starts wherever the seized moment also sits right in the sequence -- the harvest at the ripe point of the season, the intervention at the juncture the dynamic has grown to.

## Direction

Toward the act in tune with time.
The moment is taken where the passage has readied it: the [window](@chbrain/khai-engine-kairos/process_window.md) opens just as the thing comes due, the decision made at the point both the flow and the fitting instant call for it.
It moves toward the act that is right in both clocks at once -- neither a good moment on an unready thing nor a ready thing with no moment, but the two coincident.

## Lever

The tune of the two clocks against the ease of their drifting.
The carrying force is that when passage and moment agree the act lands doubly right -- the season met, the moment seized, nothing forced and nothing wasted; the resistance is how rarely, and how briefly, the two hold in tune.
Timeliness is read where chronos and kairos are read together and found in step: the measured readiness and the opportune instant meeting in the same act.

## Echo

The act done in season, well-placed in both clocks.
Timeliness leaves the thing or unfolding advanced by an act that met its time -- the ripe deed at the right moment, the sense that this was done neither early nor late but exactly when it should be.
It is dated from the meeting of the two, and it is the bridge where a play's time reads as right: the passage and the moment agreeing.
