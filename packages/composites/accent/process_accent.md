# Process: Accent

## Taxonomy

What it costs a persona to be understood in a room, and who the room charges for it.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: accent

## Initiated by

Somebody is saying something true in a room that will make them work for it.
Accent starts wherever both gates are in place: a persona moving meaning across the gap on channels each held at its own width, through [language](@chbrain/khai-engine-language/process_using_language.md); and a specialist code layered on the base tongue that binds insiders and shuts others out, through [register](@chbrain/khai-engine-register/position_register.md).
This composite adds no third engine. It reads the one thing the two make together, which is that difficulty felt by a listener does not stay with the listener.

## Direction

Toward a persona taken less seriously than what they said deserves.
The composite wires the two engines and reads their working in three shapes: [the Width](process_accent_width.md), how tightly each channel grips the tongue; [the Code](process_accent_code.md), the register on top of it; and [the Discount](process_accent_discount.md), the twist -- the room charging its own effort to the speaker.
The three are not degrees of fluency. They are two independent gates and a bill that arrives at the wrong address.

## Lever

What a persona has to say against what it costs the room to receive it.
The carrying force is that both parties are doing their jobs: the persona has something worth hearing and is working to get it out, the listener is attending and is not withholding anything on purpose, and neither of them is in a position to see the difference between a hard idea and a hard delivery; the resistance is that effort is felt without a label -- a listener experiences their own strain as a property of what they are hearing, so the strain arrives already attributed, and no amount of goodwill separates the two because the separation is not available from inside.
Accent is read on the room's effort and never on the persona's competence, since the competence is not what is being measured.

## Echo

A good point, made twice as hard, and marked down for the difficulty.
Accent does not close; it is meaning crossing a gap and being priced on arrival, gathered into one reading -- a width, a code, a discount.
It hands off what it is not: what a persona means past what they said, and how they pitch it, is the utterance composite's, which wires this same register engine among the ways a persona talks where this runs it as a gate; the tilt that trusts what processes smoothly is the bias engine's, catalogued there as a standing position; a persona steering their own study by that same smoothness is glide's; a rejection standing in for a failed sorting is anomaly's; a role and a read that disagree is unbecoming's, and its scissors close on conduct where this closes on delivery; managing a face in front of others is presentation's; the act that becomes a record is writ's; and a foreignness made to **stand for** a theme is authored key, the play's pitch, not the force.
The persona is dated from the widths they arrived holding, and the room from what it charged them.
