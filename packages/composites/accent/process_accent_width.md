# Process: Accent, the Width

## Taxonomy

[Accent](process_accent.md), how tightly each channel grips the tongue.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: accent

## Initiated by

A persona has to get something out, in a language they hold at some particular distance.
The Width starts wherever [language](@chbrain/khai-engine-language/process_using_language.md) is running a channel at a width -- [speaking](@chbrain/khai-engine-language/process_speaking.md) with the tongue [borrowed](@chbrain/khai-engine-language/process_speaking_borrowed.md), [carried](@chbrain/khai-engine-language/process_speaking_carried.md), [worn](@chbrain/khai-engine-language/process_speaking_worn.md), or held as a [mother tongue](@chbrain/khai-engine-language/process_speaking_mother_tongue.md), while [hearing](@chbrain/khai-engine-language/process_hearing.md) may be [decoded](@chbrain/khai-engine-language/process_hearing_decoded.md), [followed](@chbrain/khai-engine-language/process_hearing_followed.md), [caught](@chbrain/khai-engine-language/process_hearing_caught.md), or native, and [thinking](@chbrain/khai-engine-language/process_thinking.md) runs underneath at its own depth.
It is the face of the composite where the work is being done and nobody can see it.

## Direction

Toward a sentence that arrives, having been assembled.
The Width reads per channel and not per persona, which is the thing rooms get wrong: the widths do not move together, so a persona may read a language easily and speak it haltingly, may catch every joke and be unable to make one, may think in one tongue and be answering in another -- and a room takes the narrowest channel it can see as the measure of the whole.
It moves meaning outward at a cost that is paid before anything is heard.

## Lever

What the persona knows against how much of them is spent getting it out.
The carrying force is that the assembling is real work and it is invisible: a borrowed tongue is held as an object rather than inhabited, so each sentence is built rather than produced, and the capacity that goes into building it is capacity not going into anything else -- timing, wit, the second thought, the graceful qualification; the resistance is that the room only ever meets the output, so the persona is heard at exactly the level the assembling left them, and the effort that produced it is the one part of the exchange nobody has access to.
The Width spends the persona's fluency to buy their meaning, and the room sees only what was bought.

## Echo

A thing said correctly, at a cost that does not appear in it.
It is the bridge that establishes what is actually happening: nothing is missing from the persona, and something has been subtracted from the delivery.
What it hands upward is a gap between what a persona has and what a room can hear -- and the fact that this gap exists before any specialist code is layered on top of it.
The Width is dated from wherever the persona learned each channel, which is four different dates.
