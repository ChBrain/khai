# Process: Accent, the Discount

## Taxonomy

[Accent](process_accent.md), the room charging its own effort to the speaker, and where the two are seen for one thing.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: accent

## Initiated by

A listener finds this harder to take in than the last thing they heard.
The Discount starts wherever that difficulty is being read as a fact about the speaker rather than about the crossing -- whichever gate produced it, the [width](@chbrain/khai-engine-language/process_speaking_borrowed.md) at which the tongue is being held or the [closure](@chbrain/khai-engine-register/position_closure.md) of a code the persona does not command.
It is the face of the composite where nobody does anything wrong and somebody pays.

## Direction

Toward a verdict on the speaker, assembled out of the listener's own experience.
The Discount reads the misattribution precisely: a listener does not feel effort as effort, they feel the material as less clear, and from less clear they get to less considered, less certain, and less likely to be right -- so the deduction is made in the currency of credibility and is never registered as a deduction at all.
It moves the persona down in the room's estimate by an amount the room could not name.

## Lever

The listener's need to judge what they heard against having only their experience of hearing it.
The carrying force is that the listener is not being unfair and would be appalled to be told they were: they are attending, they want the point, they would repeat the sentence back if asked -- and using how easily something goes down as a cue to how sound it is works most of the time, because most of the time the difficulty is in the thing; the resistance is the single move that undoes it, which is knowing that the difficulty has another possible source and discounting the discount -- available, effective, and requiring the listener to notice a feeling they experience as a perception.
The Discount rides the composite's keystone knife-edge: the harder a persona works to be understood, the less they are believed, because the work shows up in the room as difficulty and the difficulty is billed to them.

## Echo

Somebody marked down for the distance they came.
It shows the two engines complete -- a channel held at a width and a code layered above it -- and carries beneath it the composite's keystone: the two gates compound, so a persona speaking at a borrowed width inside a closed register is discounted twice for one crossing, and each deduction is invisible to the person making it.
This is why the Discount is the hardest of the three to stage and the only one worth closing on: the persona must be right about the thing, the listener must be decent and attentive, no slight may be intended or perceived, and the audience must be able to hold the content next to the reception -- and a play that supplies somebody sneering has staged a snub rather than this.
The Discount is the note the whole composite closes on: what a crossing cost is legible only in how much the crosser was believed.
The Discount is dated from the moment the listener found it hard, which they will not remember.
