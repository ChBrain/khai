# Process: Hate

## Taxonomy

The composition of a hate -- a composite over disgust, anger, and contempt.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: hate

## Initiated by

One persona hates another, and a hate is never one thing.
Sternberg drew it as the mirror of his triangle of love: three components, each the dark twin of a corner of love -- the negation of intimacy where love has intimacy, the passion of hostility where love has the passion of attraction, and the commitment to devaluation where love has the commitment to stay.
This composite reads a hate by which of the three it is made of -- disgust gives the negation of intimacy, anger gives the passion, contempt gives the commitment. It adds no fourth engine; it wires the corners into the shapes a hate can take.

## Direction

Toward one of the kinds a hate can be, set by which corners are present -- and toward the hardening as they gather and settle.
All three at once is the consummate hate, the full dark triangle, the burning need to annihilate a target repellent, hated, and fixed as bad; two corners give the recognizable partial hates; one corner alone is not yet hate but its ingredient -- disgust alone is revulsion, anger alone a passing fury, contempt alone a cold dismissal.
The direction is not fixed: a hate hardens as a hot loathing settles into a cold verdict, or a cold contempt catches fire.

## Lever

The three corners, present or absent, and gathering at different rates.
Disgust recoils fast and can fade; anger flares hot and passes; contempt is decided and then holds cold and long -- so the corners are never in step, and a hate's shape shifts and hardens as the heat cools into a fixed devaluation or a settled contempt takes fire. That love and hate move on the same substrate is not only Sternberg's parallel but the brain's: Zeki and Romaya find the two share a common subcortical circuit.
The kinds are the combinations, and the danger is the settling: a hot hate that will pass, or a cold one fixed as essence and beyond appeal.

## Echo

The hate is whole, partial, or a single corner mistaken for the whole.
It runs as [boiling](process_boiling.md) (disgust and anger), [seething](process_seething.md) (disgust and contempt), or [burning](process_burning.md) (anger and contempt) -- the three two-cornered hates -- with the consummate hate the having of all three at once.
The incident anger at a provocation is the anger engine's; the visceral revulsion reflex is the disgust engine's; the cold dismissal is the contempt engine's -- and the behavioral infliction of harm the hate motivates is the aggression engine's, its moral licensing the moral-disengagement engine's. This composite owns the composition itself: what a given hate is made of.
