# Instructions: Hate

How a Playwright wires the hate composite into a play. You wire by linking the play's own content to the composite's files; the files stay untouched, they are canon. The composite installs its three atoms (disgust, anger, contempt) as dependencies, and their laws arrive with them. Authoring guidance, not runtime content, and it does not go on tour.

## Human

- The human sets the hate terrain: who hates whom, and what feeds it -- so a moment can be read for which of the three corners hold it, and how they are hardening.

## Agent

- From the play's own content, place the hate: link the bridge a plot stages under its Tension -- boiling while the hate is hot revulsion and fury, seething while it is cold settled revilement, burning while it is the devaluing drive to annihilate -- and let the corners come from the atoms: the negation of intimacy from disgust, the passion from anger, the commitment from contempt. Read the hate by which corners are present and how they settle over time, never in the word hate alone. You link to the composite, you never edit it.

## Collaboration

- The incident anger at a provocation is the anger engine's; the visceral revulsion reflex is the disgust engine's; the cold dismissal is the contempt engine's -- these are the atoms the composite wires, and a single corner alone is not hate. The behavioral infliction of harm the hate motivates is the aggression engine's; the moral licensing of that harm is the moral-disengagement engine's. This composite owns the composition -- what a given hate is made of.

## Knowledge

- Hate is not one feeling but a triangle of three components, the deliberate mirror of Sternberg's triangle of love: the negation of intimacy (distancing, revulsion -- from disgust), the passion (hot hostility, the wish to harm -- from anger), and the commitment (cold devaluation, the target fixed as bad by nature -- from contempt). Love and hate share a common neural circuit (Zeki & Romaya), so they are built the same way. The kinds of hate are the combinations of which corners are present: boiling (negation of intimacy and passion), seething (negation of intimacy and commitment), burning (passion and commitment), and the consummate hate (all three -- the burning need for annihilation of a target repellent, hated, and fixed as bad). A single corner alone is not yet hate: disgust alone is revulsion, anger alone a passing fury, contempt alone a cold dismissal. Read a hate by which corners hold it and how they harden.

## System

- Do read a hate as a composition, never as one lump: name which corners are present -- the recoil, the heat, the fixed verdict -- and cast them from the atoms (disgust, anger, contempt), not from a bare word.
- Do stage the bridges and their hardening: boiling as the raw, hot loathing of a fresh enemy; seething as the cold, durable revilement of a long grudge; burning as the devaluing fury that leans in toward annihilation -- and let a hate move between them as heat cools into a fixed verdict or a cold contempt catches fire.
- Do let the consummate hate be the full triangle -- repellent, hated, and fixed as bad -- the most dangerous because it both recoils and drives, and because its essentializing lets it pass from a person to a whole kind.
- Do keep the composite the composition, not the deed: delegate the behavioral harm to the aggression engine and the moral licensing that frees the persona to inflict it to the moral-disengagement engine. Do not edit the composite's files; wire only from the play's side.
