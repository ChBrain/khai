# Process: Seething

## Taxonomy

[Hate](process_hate.md), the bridge where the negation of intimacy and commitment meet without passion.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: hate

## Initiated by

One persona holds another at a cold distance and fixed beneath regard -- [revulsion](@chbrain/khai-engine-disgust/process_sociomoral.md) refusing it closeness, and [contempt](@chbrain/khai-engine-contempt/process_contempt.md) fixing it as bad and beneath notice -- without the heat of active fury.
This is seething hate: cold, settled revilement, the recoil gone quiet and permanent.
It is the hate of the long grudge and the despised outsider, all distance and low verdict, the fire long since banked.

## Direction

Toward a cold, durable enmity.
Negation of intimacy and commitment together make a hate that lasts -- the target kept away and fixed as worthless -- and needs no heat to sustain it; what holds it is the standing refusal and the settled verdict, not any passion.
The direction is cold and durable: the hate seethes low and long, hardest to move because nothing in it burns to burn out.

## Lever

The negation of intimacy and commitment, without the third corner.
Disgust gives the refusal of closeness, contempt the fixed devaluation; the bridge is their meeting -- the hate that is all distanced-and-despised without heat. Where passion is added and it catches fire, it becomes the consummate hate; without it, it stays the quiet, corrosive revilement that can hold for a lifetime.
The lever is the missing corner: what seething hate lacks is the heat, which is why it endures where hotter hates burn out.

## Echo

The hate seethes, cold and fixed.
It is the most durable of the partial hates -- the target held at a distance and beneath regard as a settled matter, requiring no fresh provocation to renew -- and it can pass quietly from a person to a whole kind the person is taken to stand for. If passion returns to it, it flares toward [burning](process_burning.md) or the consummate hate; the cold verdict beneath it is contempt's, the refusal of closeness disgust's, and the essentializing of the target as bad by nature is what makes it last.
