# Hate: Reference

## Line of Work

Hate as **composite**: the composition of a hate -- which of Sternberg's three components it is made of, and how they harden over time. It is not a new engine but a wiring of three, the deliberate mirror of the love composite: **disgust** supplies the negation of intimacy (the distancing and revulsion that push the target away), **anger** supplies the passion (the hot hostility, the wish to see it harmed), and **contempt** supplies the commitment (the cold devaluation that fixes the target as beneath regard and bad by nature). The composite adds no fourth phenomenon; it stages the corners into the shapes a hate can take -- the two-cornered partial hates, the three-cornered consummate hate, and the single corners that are not yet hate but its ingredients.

The domain does not model the incident anger at a provocation, which is the anger engine's; nor the visceral revulsion reflex, which is the disgust engine's; nor the cold dismissal that looks down and turns away, which is the contempt engine's -- these are the atoms it wires, and a single corner alone is not hate. Nor does it model the behavioral infliction of harm the hate may motivate, which is the aggression engine's; nor the moral licensing of that harm, which is the moral-disengagement engine's. It owns the composition itself: what a given hate is made of, and how its corners gather and settle.

The spine is the structural account of hate, built as love's twin. Sternberg's duplex theory of hate casts it, in explicit parallel with his triangular theory of love, as a triangle of three components -- the negation of intimacy, passion, and the decision/commitment to devalue -- whose combinations yield the recognizable kinds of hate. The parallel is not only conceptual: Zeki and Romaya's imaging of the "hate circuit" finds that romantic love and hate share a common subcortical substrate (the putamen and insula), diverging elsewhere -- so love and hate run on the same machinery, and building hate as love's structural mirror follows the brain. Fischer and colleagues supply what the composition alone cannot see: hate as a durable, eliminatory sentiment grounded in a dispositional, essentializing attribution -- the emergent whole the three corners make when they fire together and settle. The three bridges are the two-cornered hates: boiling, seething, burning.

## Restrictions

What the composite refuses to model, and to whom it delegates.

- **Anger**: The composite wires anger for the passion corner but does not own it. The incident anger at a specific provocation -- hot, time-limited, aimed at redress -- is the anger engine's; hate's passion is that heat recruited into a standing sentiment against a target. A single corner of passion alone is not hate but a passing fury.
- **Disgust**: The composite wires disgust for the negation-of-intimacy corner but does not own it. The visceral revulsion reflex, the recoil from the contaminating, is the disgust engine's; hate's negation of intimacy is that revulsion turned into the deliberate refusal of closeness to a person. Disgust alone is revulsion, not yet hate.
- **Contempt**: The composite wires contempt for the commitment corner but does not own it. The cold looking-down that judges another beneath regard and turns away is the contempt engine's; hate's commitment is that devaluation fixed as a standing verdict on the target's nature. Contempt alone is a cold dismissal, not yet hate.
- **Aggression**: The composite models the sentiment's composition, not the harmful act. The behavioral infliction of harm the hate may motivate -- and the pleasure at an enemy's misfortune -- is the aggression and schadenfreude engines'; hate is the standing sentiment that may drive harm but is not the harming.
- **Moral disengagement**: The composite models the hate, not the licensing of harm done in its name. The dehumanizing, self-exculpating moves that switch off self-sanction so harm can be inflicted without guilt are the moral-disengagement engine's; the composite's essentializing verdict can feed them but is the sentiment's own appraisal, not the mechanism that frees the persona to act.

## Encoding

Source to constraint. A composite root over three atoms it declares as dependencies, with three two-cornered bridges.

- **[hate](process_hate.md)** (the root): The composition of a hate -- which of the three components it is made of, and how they harden. Anchored by Sternberg (the duplex theory) and Zeki & Romaya (the shared circuit).
- **[boiling](process_boiling.md)** (bridge): Negation of intimacy and passion without commitment -- hot revulsion, the loathing that recoils and rages at once, not yet fixed. Wires disgust and anger.
- **[seething](process_seething.md)** (bridge): Negation of intimacy and commitment without passion -- cold, settled revilement, the target kept at a distance and fixed as beneath regard, durable and essentializing. Wires disgust and contempt.
- **[burning](process_burning.md)** (bridge): Passion and commitment without the distancing -- the devaluing fury that leans in, the drive to annihilate a target held worthless. Wires anger and contempt.
