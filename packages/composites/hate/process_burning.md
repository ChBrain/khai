# Process: Burning

## Taxonomy

[Hate](process_hate.md), the bridge where passion and commitment meet without the negation of intimacy.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: hate

## Initiated by

One persona burns against another it has judged worthless, and leans in rather than away -- [hostility](@chbrain/khai-engine-anger/position_hostility.md) driving at the target, and [contempt](@chbrain/khai-engine-contempt/process_contempt.md) fixing it as beneath regard and bad -- without the recoil that would keep it at a distance.
This is burning hate: the devaluing fury, the drive to annihilate a target held worthless.
It is the hate that closes in rather than pushing away -- the persecutor's hate, engaged and bent on the enemy's undoing.

## Direction

Toward the enemy's annihilation.
Passion and commitment together make a hate that acts -- the fury given a fixed, devalued target and no revulsion to hold it back -- so it drives toward the target's destruction rather than merely its distance.
The direction is inward at the enemy, not away from it: the hate burns toward contact and harm, the most dangerous of the partial hates because it does not recoil.

## Lever

Passion and commitment, without the third corner.
Anger gives the drive, contempt the fixed low verdict that licenses it; the bridge is their meeting -- the hate that is all fury-and-devaluation, leaning in. Where the negation of intimacy is added and the target becomes repellent as well as worthless, it becomes the consummate hate, complete; without that recoil, the persona stays engaged with the enemy it means to destroy.
The lever is the missing corner: what burning hate lacks is the distance, which is why it closes in where colder hates hold off.

## Echo

The hate burns, engaged and bent on harm.
Of the partial hates it is the one nearest to action -- a devalued target and an active fury, wanting the enemy undone; where revulsion joins it, it completes into the consummate hate, the full need for annihilation of a target repellent, hated, and fixed as bad. The behavioral infliction of the harm it drives toward is the aggression engine's, and the moral licensing that frees the persona to inflict it is the moral-disengagement engine's; this bridge owns the composition -- the fury and the devaluation meeting without the recoil of [seething](process_seething.md) or the rawness of [boiling](process_boiling.md).
