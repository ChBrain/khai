# Process: Boiling

## Taxonomy

[Hate](process_hate.md), the bridge where the negation of intimacy and passion meet without commitment.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: hate

## Initiated by

One persona recoils from another and burns against it at once -- [revulsion](@chbrain/khai-engine-disgust/process_sociomoral.md) pushing the target away, and [hostility](@chbrain/khai-engine-anger/position_hostility.md) wanting it harmed -- without yet a settled verdict that the target is bad to the core.
This is boiling hate: hot loathing, the recoil and the fury together.
It is the hate of the fresh betrayal and the raw enemy, all heat and distance, its judgment not yet fixed.

## Direction

Toward the height of loathing, and its instability.
Negation of intimacy and passion together make a hate that feels total -- repelling and enraging at once -- but nothing yet holds it save the two heats that brought it, and heat is the fastest to cool.
The direction is hot and unsettled: the hate boils on feeling, with no fixed devaluation beneath it to keep it once the heat goes.

## Lever

The negation of intimacy and passion, without the third corner.
Disgust gives the recoil, anger the drive to harm; the bridge is their meeting -- the hate that is all repelled-and-enraged but not yet a verdict. Where the heat cools and no commitment to devaluation was formed, it thins toward indifference; where contempt is added and fixes the target as bad, it becomes the consummate hate.
The lever is the missing corner: what boiling hate has is hot, and what it lacks is what would make it last.

## Echo

The hate boils, hot and unfixed.
Left as it is, it either hardens into a settled devaluation or burns out; the classic turn is the heat cooling while the disgust remains, which settles boiling hate toward [seething](process_seething.md).
If the recoil too was shallower than it felt, what remains when the fury goes is little, and the enmity fades. The behavioral harm this hate may drive is the aggression engine's to take up.
