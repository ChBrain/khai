# Capability

Capability as composite: a persona's belief about its own power -- the theory it holds of where ability comes from and where control lies (mindset, locus-of-control), the task-specific confidence that theory yields (self-efficacy), and the handicap it erects to keep the belief from being tested (self-handicapping) -- across the theory, the reach, and the guard, and the drama is that a self can protect its belief in its own ability only by never letting the ability be cleanly tested.
