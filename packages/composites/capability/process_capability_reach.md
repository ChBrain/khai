# Process: Capability, the Reach

## Taxonomy

[Capability](process_capability.md), the part where a persona judges what it can do here.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: capability

## Initiated by

The persona faces a particular task and judges whether it can organize and execute what the task requires.
The Reach starts wherever [self-efficacy](@chbrain/khai-engine-self-efficacy/position_self_efficacy.md) settles on a task -- a belief built and eroded by [mastery](@chbrain/khai-engine-self-efficacy/position_mastery.md) in having done it before, by the [vicarious](@chbrain/khai-engine-self-efficacy/position_vicarious.md) sight of someone like oneself doing it, and by the [persuasion](@chbrain/khai-engine-self-efficacy/position_persuasion.md) of what one is told one can do.
It is the working confidence: not the standing theory of ability nor the excuse held in reserve, but the judgment of this attempt.

## Direction

Toward the attempt made or not made, and the effort sustained or withdrawn.
The Reach reads the persona's task-specific belief doing its work -- deciding what is attempted at all, how much effort is committed, and how long it survives difficulty -- so that the same task is entered by one persona as workable and by another as beyond it, on belief rather than on ability.
It moves the persona from a task in front of it to a task taken up or declined: the reach set by what it believes it can execute, and revised by what the doing then shows.

## Lever

The belief that makes the attempt possible against the difficulty that tests it.
The carrying force is a confidence specific enough to act on -- built most durably by having actually done it, and by seeing one's like succeed; the resistance is the difficulty that arrives, which a strong reach reads as a call for more effort and a weak one reads as confirmation that the attempt was beyond it, so the belief tends to make itself true either way.
The Reach is capability at its most operative: the belief that determines whether the ability ever gets a chance to be used.

## Echo

The task taken up or set down on the strength of a belief.
The Reach leaves the persona committed or withdrawn -- the attempt made and effort sustained, or the task declined before ability was consulted -- and carries the capability's shared shape: a self acting on what it holds about its own power.
It is the working edge of the belief: what a persona thinks it can do here, and therefore what it will find out.
The Reach is dated from the judging, the moment the persona settled what it believed it could execute.
