# Process: Capability, the Theory

## Taxonomy

[Capability](process_capability.md), the part where a persona holds a standing view of where ability and control come from.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: capability

## Initiated by

The persona meets a challenge already holding a theory of what ability is and who decides outcomes.
The Theory starts wherever those standing frames are in place -- the [mindset](@chbrain/khai-engine-mindset/position_mindset.md) that holds ability as a [fixed](@chbrain/khai-engine-mindset/position_fixed.md) amount set early or a capacity [grown](@chbrain/khai-engine-mindset/position_growth.md) through effort, and the [locus of control](@chbrain/khai-engine-locus-of-control/position_locus_of_control.md) that expects outcomes to follow from a persona's own action ([internal](@chbrain/khai-engine-locus-of-control/position_internal.md)) or from forces outside it ([external](@chbrain/khai-engine-locus-of-control/position_external.md)).
It is the frame before the task: not the confidence about this attempt nor the excuse for its failure, but the theory that decides what any attempt could mean.

## Direction

Toward the challenge already coloured by what the persona holds about ability and control.
The Theory reads the persona's standing frame doing its work -- effort read as the path to growth or as evidence of insufficient talent, a setback read as information or as a verdict, an outcome credited to its own action or to luck, fate, and the powerful.
It moves the persona from a neutral challenge to an interpreted one: the same difficulty meaning opportunity to one theory and exposure to another, before any effort is spent.

## Lever

The frame that makes effort meaningful against the frame that makes it incriminating.
The carrying force is a theory that lets effort count -- ability grown by work, outcomes answering to one's own action, difficulty a place to develop; the resistance is the frame that turns the same facts against the self -- if ability is fixed, needing to try is proof of not having it, and if control lies outside, effort is beside the point either way.
The Theory is capability at its most foundational: the standing view that decides whether trying is a route to power or a confession of its lack.

## Echo

The challenge met through a theory already in place.
The Theory leaves the persona framed -- ability held as fixed or growable, control as its own or the world's -- and hands that frame to the reach it will shape and the guard it may require.
It is the lens of the whole belief: what a persona holds about where ability comes from, which decides what every later attempt can prove.
The Theory is dated from the framing, the standing view a persona brought before the challenge arrived.
