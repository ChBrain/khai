# Process: Capability, the Guard

## Taxonomy

[Capability](process_capability.md), the part where the belief is protected from the test that would settle it.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: capability

## Initiated by

The persona faces an outcome that could refute its belief about its own ability, and arranges in advance that the outcome will not be able to.
The Guard starts wherever [self-handicapping](@chbrain/khai-engine-self-handicapping/process_self_handicapping.md) goes up before the verdict -- an obstacle [acquired](@chbrain/khai-engine-self-handicapping/process_acquired.md) in fact, the drink taken or the preparation skipped, or merely [claimed](@chbrain/khai-engine-self-handicapping/process_claimed.md) in advance, the illness or the impossible schedule announced, by a persona practised enough at it to be a [handicapper](@chbrain/khai-engine-self-handicapping/position_handicapper.md).
It is the defence of the belief: not the theory nor the reach, but the arrangement that keeps either from being disproved.

## Direction

Toward an outcome that can no longer say anything definite about ability.
The Guard reads the persona building its alibi in advance -- the handicap set so that failure belongs to the obstacle and not to the self, and success, arriving despite it, counts double -- so that whichever way the outcome falls, the belief about ability survives it.
It moves the persona from a test that could settle the question to one that cannot: the verdict pre-emptively spoiled, the self-theory made safe.

## Lever

The self-belief protected against the attainment that protecting it costs.
The carrying force is that a belief about one's own ability is worth defending, and the handicap defends it perfectly -- an unfalsifiable self-theory, immune to the outcome; the resistance is that the defence is paid for in the real world: the drink is really drunk, the preparation really skipped, the performance really worse, so the persona buys its self-image with the very attainment the image was about.
The Guard rides the composite's keystone knife-edge: a self can protect its belief in its ability only by never letting the ability be cleanly tested -- the protection and the sabotage are one act, and the belief kept safe is kept safe from ever being true.

## Echo

The verdict spoiled in advance, and the belief left standing on nothing.
It shows a persona arranging its own obstacle before an outcome -- failure pre-assigned to the handicap, success made to count double -- and carries beneath it the composite's keystone: that a self-belief defended this way is bought at the price of the achievement that would have justified it.
The Guard is the note the whole composite closes on: capability is not only what a persona believes it can do but what it will do to keep that belief unexamined, and the belief most fiercely protected is the one never allowed to be earned.
The Guard is dated from the erecting, the moment the persona put an obstacle between its ability and the verdict.
