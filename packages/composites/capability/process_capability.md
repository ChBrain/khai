# Process: Capability

## Taxonomy

A persona's belief about its own power.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: capability

## Initiated by

A persona is read not only by what it can do but by what it believes it can do -- and that belief acts before any attempt is made.
Capability starts wherever a self holds a view of its own power -- the [mindset](@chbrain/khai-engine-mindset/position_mindset.md) of where ability comes from and the [locus of control](@chbrain/khai-engine-locus-of-control/position_locus_of_control.md) of where outcomes are decided, the [self-efficacy](@chbrain/khai-engine-self-efficacy/position_self_efficacy.md) that belief yields for a task at hand, and the [self-handicapping](@chbrain/khai-engine-self-handicapping/process_self_handicapping.md) that keeps the belief from a clean verdict.
This composite adds no fifth engine; it reads what the four make together: a self-belief that guides action and defends itself.

## Direction

Toward the whole belief a self holds about its own power, read through its parts.
The composite wires the four engines and reads their working in three shapes: [the Theory](process_capability_theory.md), where a persona holds ability and control to come from; [the Reach](process_capability_reach.md), what it believes it can do here; and [the Guard](process_capability_guard.md), the twist -- the obstacle that keeps the belief from being tested.
A persona does not meet a task with bare ability: it brings a theory of where ability comes from, a confidence about this particular attempt, and, when the verdict threatens, a way of arranging that the verdict never quite comes.

## Lever

The belief a self holds about its power against the tests that would settle it.
The carrying force is that this belief acts on its own -- it decides what is attempted, how long effort lasts, what a setback is taken to mean, before ability itself has said anything; the resistance is the world's verdict, the outcome that would confirm or refute the belief, and a self that cannot bear the refutation will bend the test rather than revise the belief.
Capability is read wherever a persona is granted its self-belief: not a measure of what it can do but the working theory it holds about that, guiding it and guarding itself.

## Echo

The persona revealed as a self acting on what it believes about its own power.
Capability does not close; it is the persona going on with its theory of itself, gathering the four engines into one reading -- a self that frames, reaches, and guards.
It hands off what it is not: the standing character of pursuit -- the motive, the orientation, the stamina -- is the striving composite's; the crossing from wanting to doing is the volition composite's; the persona's regard for its own worth is the self-esteem engine's, read in the self-relation composite; the actual competence a task demands is not modelled at all, only the belief about it; and the self-belief made to stand for a story is authored key, the play's pitch, not the force.
The persona is dated from its own believing, and read as a self that acts on its theory of its power and protects that theory from the proof.
