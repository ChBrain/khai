# Instructions: Borderline

How a Playwright wires the borderline composite into a play. You wire by linking the play's own content to the composite's files and, through them, to the five atom engines it depends on; every file stays untouched, they are canon. The Roadie plumbs the law into Instructions Knowledge on deploy. Authoring guidance, not runtime content, and it does not go on tour. This composite authors a character whose instability is organised by the relationship in front of them; it is not a diagnosis of any real person.

## Human

- The human sets the transaction, both halves of it: a persona with an unusually reactive emotional system, and an environment that answered its reports of that system with dismissal, punishment, or an unpredictable mixture. It sets, too, which movement is most in play, and who in the play is currently load-bearing for the persona's sense of itself.

## Agent

- Cast the compound along one of four movements: emotional vulnerability, affect that arrives fast and large and leaves slowly, with no modulating middle (the regulation engine); interpersonal hypersensitivity, availability read moment to moment and a small distance acted on as an ending (the attachment engine); identity diffusion, a self that does not consolidate and splits rather than integrates (the identity and self-esteem engines); dyscontrol, the impulsivity or self-harm that interrupts a state which cannot be regulated (the self-control engine). A persona running a movement may link it under Projection; the audit surfaces the ones left unnamed. You link to the composite and its atoms, you never edit them.

## Collaboration

- The emotion-regulation repertoire is the regulation engine's. The strategies for closeness under threat are the attachment engine's. The achieved, foreclosed, moratorium and diffuse positions are the identity engine's, and the resting self-attitude the self-esteem engine's. The override of an impulse, and the lapse, are the self-control engine's. An intense relationship, a bad week for someone's self-image, or a single impulsive act is not this syndrome. And where a persona's disturbance is a stable, pervasive negative self-concept and a withdrawal from relationships following prolonged inescapable trauma, that is the cptsd composite and not this one -- the line is whether the instability is triggered and reorganised by the relationship in front of them. This composite supplies the read of the compound, as a character's architecture.

## Knowledge

- Borderline organisation is a transaction, not a defect (Linehan): a biological emotional vulnerability -- high sensitivity, high reactivity, slow return to baseline -- meeting an environment that invalidated the persona's reports of its own experience. Where the moderate signal was dismissed and the extreme one answered, the persona learned to oscillate between inhibition and extremity and never practised the middle. Hold four movements. Emotional vulnerability: feeling arrives before the account of it and is still present when the next cue lands, so the persona is rarely met at rest. Interpersonal hypersensitivity (Gunderson's organising phenotype): availability is measured continuously on an instrument calibrated too fine, and the readings are acted on with the insecure strategies -- close the distance and demand proof, leave first, or under enough threat both at once. Identity diffusion (Kernberg): integration fails, so the good and bad versions of a person are never assembled and the persona switches between them; splitting is that structure operating, not a tactic, and it applies to the self as much as to others. Dyscontrol: the impulsivity and self-harm are the affect problem solved by other means -- a state that cannot be brought down can be interrupted, and the interruption works. The twist binds them: the effort to prevent an ending is what produces the ending, each departure is real, and insight does not dissolve it.

## System

- Do stage both halves of the transaction. A persona written with the vulnerability and no invalidating environment is a temperament, not this; one written with the environment and no vulnerability is a different injury.
- Do make the invalidation ordinary rather than cruel -- you're not really upset, it's just drama, she's manipulating you -- because that is how it arrives, and because a persona disbelieved when calm has learned something true about what works.
- Do let the twist land as a trap and not a mistake. The persona's fear produces the evidence for itself, the departures are real, and understanding the mechanism does not stop it.
- Do write the dyscontrol as instrumental. The act ends a state; it is not addressed to whoever finds it, and the room's reading of it as a demand is the misreading the composite exists to expose.
- Do not write the persona as fixed. Zanarini's longitudinal work has symptomatic remission as the norm over ten to sixteen years, outstripping functional recovery -- a borderline persona changes, and often the feeling settles before the life does.
- Do not stage the idealisation and devaluation as insincerity. Both versions are meant; the structure that would hold them together is what is missing.
- Do not use this as a diagnosis, a checklist, or a claim about a real person, and do not reproduce the clinical reattribution of these behaviours as manipulation. It is authored architecture for a play.
