# Process: Borderline

## Taxonomy

A composite reading over five atom engines (no canon file of its own): the regulation, attachment, identity, self-esteem, and self-control engines this package declares as dependencies.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: borderline

## Initiated by

A persona with an unusually reactive emotional system grows up somewhere that treats its reports of that system as wrong.
The compound begins in a transaction, not in either party to it. Linehan's biosocial theory is the entry condition and the composite takes it seriously as a transaction: a biological emotional vulnerability -- high sensitivity to cues, high reactivity, and a slow return to baseline -- meeting an **invalidating environment**, one that punishes, trivialises, or responds erratically to the communication of private experience. Neither alone does it. The vulnerable persona in a validating environment learns to name and modulate what it feels; the ordinary persona in an invalidating one learns to distrust an instrument that was working. What the transaction teaches is the thing that will later be read as character: since moderate expression is dismissed and extreme expression is answered, the persona learns to oscillate between inhibiting what it feels and displaying it at a volume that cannot be ignored, and never learns the middle register, because the middle register was the one that got nothing.

## Direction

From a vulnerability nobody helped it name, through a self assembled out of other people, toward a persona whose every register is set by whoever is in the room.
Four movements carry it, and they are the four domains the diagnostic criteria cluster into rather than a list of symptoms. The [regulation](@chbrain/khai-engine-regulation/process_regulation.md) engine's levers were never built out, so affect arrives fast, large, and slow to leave. [Attachment](@chbrain/khai-engine-attachment/process_attachment.md) becomes an instrument of continuous measurement -- Gunderson's interpersonal hypersensitivity -- in which the other's availability is read moment to moment and a small unavailability registers as the beginning of an ending. The self does not consolidate: Kernberg's identity diffusion, the [diffuse](@chbrain/khai-engine-identity/position_diffuse.md) position held not as an adolescent stage but as a standing condition, with [self-esteem](@chbrain/khai-engine-self-esteem/position_contingent.md) contingent on a regard that changes hourly. And the [self-control](@chbrain/khai-engine-self-control/process_self_control.md) failures -- the impulsivity, the self-harm -- are not separate recklessness but the affect problem solved by other means, because an unbearable state that will not be regulated can still be interrupted.

## Lever

Whether the environment answers the persona's report of its own experience. That is the transaction's live end, and it is the one thing in the compound that other characters control. An environment that responds to the moderate signal makes the extreme one unnecessary; an environment that responds only to the extreme one manufactures it and then reads it as the persona's nature. Stage the invalidation as ordinary rather than cruel -- the parent who says _you're not really upset_, the partner who calls it drama, the clinician who calls it manipulation -- because that is how it usually arrives, and because a persona who was disbelieved when calm has learned something true about what works.

**The twist is that the fear is self-confirming, and the persona can see it happening.** The interpersonal hypersensitivity produces the frantic effort to prevent an ending -- the testing, the demand for proof, the pre-emptive rejection that gets there first, the rage at a small unavailability -- and those efforts are what actually exhaust the other person and produce the ending. The prediction manufactures its own evidence. What makes it a trap rather than a mistake is that each confirmation is real: the persona is not imagining that people leave. They do leave, and they leave _because of_ the behaviour the fear of their leaving caused, so every departure arrives as proof that the fear was justified and the vigilance insufficient. Insight does not dissolve it. A persona who understands the mechanism perfectly still cannot stop reading the room, and now watches themselves do the thing that will cost them the person.

## Echo

The syndrome surfaces through four movements: [emotional vulnerability](position_emotional_vulnerability.md), the affect that arrives fast, large, and slow to leave, with the modulating middle never built; [interpersonal hypersensitivity](position_interpersonal_hypersensitivity.md), the continuous reading of another's availability in which a small distance registers as an ending; [identity diffusion](position_identity_diffusion.md), the self that does not consolidate and reorganises around whoever is present, splitting the same person into all-good and all-bad because the two were never integrated; and [dyscontrol](position_dyscontrol.md), the impulsivity and self-harm that interrupt a state that cannot be regulated. The emotion-regulation machinery is the regulation engine's; the strategies for closeness under threat are the attachment engine's; the achieved, foreclosed, moratorium and diffuse positions are the identity engine's and the resting self-attitude the self-esteem engine's; the effortful override of an impulse is the self-control engine's. Borderline organisation is what those five look like in a persona who was never helped to name what it felt -- read across the compound, never in a single episode, and authored as a character's architecture, never diagnosed onto a real person.
