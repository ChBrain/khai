# Process: Owing

## Taxonomy

How an obligation between two persons is made, carried, and discharged.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: owing

## Initiated by

Something has passed from one persona to another, and the passing left a claim behind.
Owing starts wherever that claim is being carried -- the transfer that binds precisely because no price was named, through the [gift](@chbrain/khai-engine-gift/process_gift.md); the same bond written as a figure one party may call in, through [debt](@chbrain/khai-engine-debt/piece_debt.md); and the general equivalent that can discharge any claim at all, through [money](@chbrain/khai-engine-money/piece_money.md).
This composite adds no fourth engine; it reads what the three make together: one obligation, at three degrees of exactness.

## Direction

Toward the whole life of an obligation, read through the degrees to which it has been made exact.
The composite wires the three engines and reads their working in three shapes: [the Giving](process_owing_giving.md), the transfer whose obligation must not be named; [the Owed](process_owing_owed.md), the bond written as a claim; and [the Settling](process_owing_settling.md), the twist -- the account that can be closed exactly.
Each stage makes the claim more nameable: the gift cannot say what is owed, the debt says how much, and money says it in a currency anyone will take from anyone.

## Lever

The exactness that makes obligation portable against the vagueness that made it a bond.
The carrying force is that a world cannot run on unmeasured obligation -- a claim must be sayable to be enforced, transferable to reach a stranger, and dischargeable if anyone is ever to be free of it, and every step toward the figure is a step toward being able to leave; the resistance is that the tie was the unmeasured part, so what is bought with exactness is paid for in binding, and the persona who can settle in full is the persona with nothing outstanding to hold them.
Owing is read wherever a persona is granted what they are still carrying: not a self that trades and walks away but one held by what has not yet been squared.

## Echo

The persona revealed as bound by what remains outstanding.
Owing does not close; it is the obligation running, gathering the three engines into one reading -- a transfer that binds, a claim that presses, an account that could be cleared.
It hands off what it is not: the wrong done and the reckoning after it -- the felt debt of guilt, the spoken account of repair, the grievance of the wronged -- is the moral-account composite's; the standing a persona holds and the capital they trade on are the standing composite's; the loyalty and disclosure that make an intimacy are the love composite's; a shared resource governed by its users is the commons engine's; the felt lack that captures the mind is the scarcity engine's; where personal worth comes from and how it is defended is the worth-logic engine's; and the debt made to stand for a story is authored key, the play's pitch, not the force.
The persona is dated from the transfer, and read as a self whose ties are measured by what has not been paid.
