# Process: Owing, the Settling

## Taxonomy

[Owing](process_owing.md), the stage where the account can be closed exactly, and the closing is what the tie does not survive.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: owing

## Initiated by

The obligation has reached a currency anyone will take from anyone, and it can now be paid in full.
The Settling starts wherever [money](@chbrain/khai-engine-money/piece_money.md) does its work -- the [conversion](@chbrain/khai-engine-money/process_conversion.md) that sets an hour, a skill, a harm, or a kindness equal to a sum and so makes it exchangeable for anything a sum can buy, the standing of the [holder](@chbrain/khai-engine-money/position_holder.md) who can wait where others must act and convert a want into a purchase without asking anyone's leave, and the [earmarking](@chbrain/khai-engine-money/process_earmarking.md) by which people refuse money's own logic and dress a fungible sum back in meaning, because they feel what the abstraction costs.
It is the last degree of exactness: the claim is dischargeable, transferable, and owed to no one in particular.

## Direction

Toward a persona free of the obligation, and of what it held.
The Settling reads the closing at work -- the loan repaid to the day and the friendship afterwards oddly formal, the favour answered with a cheque, the return that matched the gift so exactly it read as a refusal -- each an account squared and a relation with nothing left outstanding in it.
It moves the persona from bound to clear: able to leave, owing nothing, met by everyone as an equal, and no longer held to anybody by anything.

## Lever

The freedom of a claim that can be discharged against the bond that lived on its not being.
The carrying force is that settlement is not a betrayal of obligation but its completion -- a claim that can never be cleared is a claim without an exit, and the ability to pay is the ability to be released from a creditor, a patron, or a family, which is most of what the general equivalent is for; the resistance is that the tie was never the sum, and once priced a thing does not easily return to being priceless, so the number sticks and the quality it stood for is now known to have a figure.
The Settling rides the composite's keystone knife-edge: what settles the account ends the relation -- a return identical to the gift signals equivalence and closes it, so an obligation is binding exactly in proportion to how little it can be paid off, and liquidity spends the option of not counting.

## Echo

The account closed, and the relation closed with it.
It shows the obligation at its most exact -- priced, portable, and dischargeable -- and carries beneath it the composite's keystone: a bond between two people lives on what remains outstanding, so the very exactness that lets a persona finally be free of a claim is the exactness that leaves nothing holding them, and this is why people earmark, refuse to be paid, and take offence at a return that came too quickly and in the right amount.
The Settling is the note the whole composite closes on: what could be settled in full was never the part that bound anyone.
The Settling is dated from the discharge, the moment the sum was cleared and the tie found it had been the sum after all, or had not.
