# Instructions: Owing

How a Playwright wires the owing composite into a play. You wire by linking the play's
own content to the composite's files; the files stay untouched, they are canon. The
composite installs the gift, debt, and money engines as dependencies, and their
laws arrive with them. Authoring guidance, not runtime content, and it does not go on tour.

## Human

- The human sets what is outstanding between two personas: what passed from one to the other, and what it left behind -- a claim that must not be named, a claim written as a figure, or a claim that can be paid off and walked away from -- and, the question that governs the read, which degree of exactness it has reached: the giving, the owed, or the settling.

## Agent

- From the play's own content, read a persona as carrying what has not been squared: link under Projection the degree the obligation has reached -- the giving while the claim must stay unspoken (the transfer that must appear voluntary, the receiving that cannot be refused without refusing the relationship, the interval before the return, from gift), the owed while it stands as a figure one party may call in (the incurring that sets a creditor and a debtor, the servicing that keeps the tie taut, the default that snaps it, from debt), the settling while it can be discharged in a currency anyone will take (the conversion that prices the unlike, the holder's freedom to wait, the earmarking that fights back, from money). Play the persona as bound by what is outstanding, present in what they cannot quite refuse, never narrated, and never made to stand for a story. You link to the composite, you never edit it.

## Collaboration

- The unpriced transfer is the gift engine's; the quantified claim is debt's; the general equivalent is money's -- one degree alone is not the life of an obligation. The wrong done and the reckoning after it is the moral-account composite's; the rank a persona holds and the capital they trade on is standing's; what draws two people together in the first place is love's; a shared resource governed by its users is the commons engine's; the felt lack that captures the mind is scarcity's; where personal worth comes from is worth-logic's. This composite owns the claim -- what one persona is still carrying because of something another handed over -- and the settling bridge carries the keystone: what settles the account ends the relation.

## Knowledge

- Owing is an obligation between two persons read at three degrees of exactness -- braiding the transfer that binds by naming no price (gift), the bond written as a figure one party may call in (debt), and the token that can discharge any claim at all (money) into one claim -- read as a wiring of those three engines, and its question is how exact the claim has become (Mauss's triple obligation to give, receive, and reciprocate; Bourdieu's misrecognition and the interval; Graeber on quantified, enforceable debt; Simmel on money dissolving ties into quantity; Zelizer on earmarking). It runs in three degrees, and each buys portability with binding. The giving is the claim that cannot be stated -- the transfer must appear voluntary or it is not a gift, taking is accepting the obligation and refusing is refusing the relationship, and the whole art is the interval, since an immediate return settles the account and a delayed one sustains the bond -- obligation at its least exact and its most binding, because a claim without a figure cannot be discharged and therefore does not end. The owed is the claim written as a number -- it converts a one-time transfer into a standing tie, sets a creditor who holds a claim on the debtor's future and a debtor who owes not just the amount but the posture of one who owes, and runs from incurring through servicing to discharge or default -- enforceable, plannable, extendable to a stranger, and never neutral, because being owed buys leverage and spends candour. The settling is the twist -- the general equivalent that sets an hour, a harm, or a kindness equal to a sum, that buys its holder the freedom to wait where others must act, and that people earmark back into meaning because they feel what the abstraction costs -- where the shape is seen for what it is: a return identical to the gift signals equivalence and closes it, so an obligation binds in proportion to how little it can be paid off, and once priced a thing does not easily return to being priceless. Read a persona as held by what remains outstanding, and read the clearing of an account as a cost as often as a release.

## System

- Do read the persona through what is outstanding, never as a party to a completed trade: name whether the giving (the claim unnamed), the owed (the claim as a figure), or the settling (the claim dischargeable) is at work, and cast each from its engines.
- Do keep the degrees distinct: the giving cannot say what is owed, the owed says how much, the settling says it in a currency anyone will take from anyone.
- Do let the settling carry the keystone: what settles the account ends the relation -- a bond lives on what has not been squared.
- Do remember the gift's obligation must stay unspoken: a persona who names what is owed has converted the gift into a transaction, and the play has broken the degree.
- Do remember the interval is the mechanism: a return too quick reads as a refusal and one too slow as a default, and both are readable by the other party without a word.
- Do not model the wrong and its reckoning (that is moral-account), the rank a persona holds (that is standing), what draws two people together (that is love), a shared resource and its governance (that is commons), the mind under shortage (that is scarcity), or where personal worth comes from (that is worth-logic). Do not make the obligation stand for a story (that is the play's pitch). Do not edit the composite's files; wire only from the play's side.
