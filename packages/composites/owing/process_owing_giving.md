# Process: Owing, the Giving

## Taxonomy

[Owing](process_owing.md), the stage where the obligation is real and cannot be named.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: owing

## Initiated by

Something is handed over, no price is stated, and both parties know a claim has been made.
The Giving starts wherever the [gift](@chbrain/khai-engine-gift/process_gift.md) does its work -- the [giving](@chbrain/khai-engine-gift/process_giving.md) that must appear voluntary, since a giver who names the expected return has converted the gift into a transaction; the [receiving](@chbrain/khai-engine-gift/process_receiving.md) that is not passive, because taking is accepting the obligation and refusing is refusing the relationship; and the [reciprocating](@chbrain/khai-engine-gift/process_reciprocating.md) whose whole art is the interval, since an immediate return settles the account and a delayed one sustains the bond.
It is obligation at its least exact, and at its most binding.

## Direction

Toward a persona held by something no one will say out loud.
The Giving reads the misrecognition at work -- the favour done without mention, the invitation that will have to be returned, the help offered by someone who will need help -- each a transfer both parties must treat as free while both know it is not, because the moment either says what is owed the thing it created stops working.
It moves the persona from receiving to being held: not a party to a trade but a party to a relation, carrying an amount nobody can state and everybody can feel.

## Lever

The power of an unnamed claim against the impossibility of measuring it.
The carrying force is that vagueness is the mechanism, not a defect of it -- an obligation without a figure cannot be discharged and therefore does not end, which is exactly how a gift builds an ongoing tie rather than a completed exchange; the resistance is that what cannot be measured cannot be checked: the receiver does not know what would be enough, the giver cannot say they were shortchanged, and the whole arrangement runs on a reading of the interval that either party can get wrong.
The Giving is owing at its most relational: a claim strong enough to bind and too unstated to settle.

## Echo

The obligation established and left unspoken.
The Giving leaves the persona owing something they could not put a number on -- an amount set by context and prior history rather than any explicit agreement -- and hands that unnamed claim to the quantifying and settling that will build on it.
It is the first degree of exactness, which is to say almost none: the claim is real, and no one has said what it is.
The Giving is dated from the handing over, the moment a transfer created a debt that must not be called one.
