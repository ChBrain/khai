# Process: Owing, the Owed

## Taxonomy

[Owing](process_owing.md), the stage where the obligation is written as a figure one party may call in.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: owing

## Initiated by

The claim has been given a number, and the number has set the two people at different heights.
The Owed starts wherever the [debt](@chbrain/khai-engine-debt/piece_debt.md) stands -- the [incurring](@chbrain/khai-engine-debt/process_incurring.md) that converts a one-time transfer into a standing tie and makes a [creditor](@chbrain/khai-engine-debt/position_creditor.md) and a [debtor](@chbrain/khai-engine-debt/position_debtor.md) where a moment ago there were two persons, the [servicing](@chbrain/khai-engine-debt/process_servicing.md) that keeps the tie taut by holding off default without ending the obligation, and the [default](@chbrain/khai-engine-debt/process_default.md) in which the claim meets empty hands and the relation snaps or is re-tied on harsher terms.
It is the second degree of exactness: the claim now says how much.

## Direction

Toward a persona whose future is partly already claimed.
The Owed reads the figure doing its work -- the loan that makes a friend into a creditor, the favour large enough that it had to be counted, the sum whose servicing quietly reorganises what the debtor can refuse -- each an obligation clean enough to be stated and therefore heavy enough to be enforced.
It moves the persona from held to accountable: not a party to an unspoken bond but a party to a schedule, deferent where they might have been plain, and calculating the cost of every refusal against the claim they have not yet cleared.

## Lever

The enforceability the figure buys against the equality the figure spends.
The carrying force is that a stated claim can be relied on -- it can be planned around, extended to a stranger, carried across time, and defended when it is not honoured, which is what lets obligation reach past people who already trust each other; the resistance is that the number is never neutral: for as long as it stands the creditor holds a claim on the debtor's future and cannot fully trust that the warmth they receive is freely given, and the debtor owes not just the amount but the posture of one who owes.
The Owed is owing at its most explicit and least deniable: an unequal tie dressed as a temporary one.

## Echo

The bond stated, and the two set at unequal heights by the statement.
The Owed leaves the persona bound to a schedule rather than an understanding -- patience now a thing extended rather than felt, refusal now a cost rather than an option -- and hands that stated claim to the settling that will offer to close it.
It is the second degree of exactness: the claim says how much, and the moral weight of the owing outlives the sum.
The Owed is dated from the incurring, the moment a transfer became a number one person could call in.
