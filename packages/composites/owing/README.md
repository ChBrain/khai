# Owing

Owing as composite: how an obligation between two persons is made, carried, and discharged -- the transfer that binds precisely by naming no price (gift), the obligation quantified into a claim one holds over another (debt), and the general equivalent that can close any account at all (money) -- across the giving, the owed, and the settling, and the drama is that each stage makes the obligation more exact, until it is exact enough to be paid off and the tie it held goes with it.
