# Owing: Reference

## Line of Work

Owing as **composite**: how an obligation between two persons is made, carried, and discharged.
It is not a fourth mechanism but a wiring of three: the **gift** engine supplies the transfer
that binds precisely by naming no price, the **debt** engine supplies the same bond written as a
figure one party may call in, and the **money** engine supplies the general equivalent that can
discharge any claim at all. This composite adds no fourth engine; it reads the seam the three
make when they are set in a row -- one obligation at three degrees of exactness -- and stages
that seam into three bridges, closing on the discovery that the exactness which finally lets an
obligation be paid off is the exactness that leaves nothing holding the parties together.

The domain does not model any single degree -- giving, receiving, and reciprocating are the gift
engine's; the creditor and debtor stances and the arc from incurring through servicing to default
are debt's; conversion, earmarking, and the holder are money's; and one alone is a process, not
the life of an obligation. Nor is it the wrong done and the reckoning after it, which is the
moral-account composite's; nor the rank a persona holds and the capital they trade on, which is
the standing composite's; nor a shared resource governed by its own users, which is the commons
engine's; nor the felt lack that captures the mind, which is the scarcity engine's. It owns the
claim: what one persona is still carrying because of something another handed over.

The spine is Graeber's _Debt_, which is where the three degrees are argued as one history rather
than asserted here. His central distinction is this composite's: unlike other forms of obligation
a debt can be _precisely quantified_, and it is the quantification that lets a debt become simple,
cold, and impersonal, and therefore transferable. Beneath it he sets what he calls human
economies, running on the radical incalculability of a life and on open-ended reciprocity -- bonds
made and remade through gifts and sociability, where nothing is squared because nothing is
counted; above it he sets the coinage of the Axial age, which put a figure on portions of those
economies, and he argues the passage between them is not gradual but forced, the informal
indebtedness of a human economy replaced by mathematically precise and enforceable debt through
violence, usually a state's. That is the ladder: incalculable, quantified, dischargeable, with the
cost of each step named. Gregory's _Gifts and Commodities_ sets the same axis in the ethnographic
register. What this composite adds on top is the reading of the last step as a loss to the parties
rather than only to the society -- that what can be settled in full is what stops holding anyone.
The atoms supply the degrees. Mauss established the triple obligation, to give, to
receive, and to reciprocate, and the structural fact that the gift must appear free; Bourdieu
named the mechanism as misrecognition, and located the bond in the **interval** between gift and
counter-gift, since a return that arrives at once and in equal measure converts a relationship
into a transaction. On the quantified degree, Nietzsche's genealogy supplies the tie between
_Schuld_ as debt and _Schuld_ as guilt, and the weight of owing that outlives the sum. Simmel
completed the arc: money as the pure means become
an end, dissolving personal ties into a universal quantity, buying its holder a freedom whose
price is that qualities become comparable -- against which Zelizer documented the counter-move,
earmarking, in which people dress fungible sums back in meaning precisely because they feel what
the abstraction costs. The three bridges are those three degrees: the giving, the owed, and the
settling.

## Origin

The anthropology of exchange, the morality of debt, and the sociology of money, wired as a
composite over the gift, debt, and money engines. The first two rows are the **joining** -- the
published work that argues these degrees are one progression -- and the rest anchor the individual
bridges.

## Restrictions

What the composite refuses to model, and to whom it delegates.

- **The single degrees (the atoms)**: The composite wires the three but owns none. Giving,
  receiving, and reciprocating are the gift engine's; the creditor and debtor stances and the arc
  from incurring through servicing to default are debt's; conversion, earmarking, and the holder
  are money's. Owing reads only what the three make in a row; one degree alone is a process, not
  the life of an obligation.
- **The wrong and the reckoning after it (moral-account)**: The transgressor's felt debt, the
  dyad's spoken account, and the wronged party's grievance are the moral-account composite's.
  Owing is the claim left by a transfer, not the claim left by a harm; where the two meet, the
  wrong is theirs and the ledger of the transfer is this one's.
- **Rank and what a persona trades on (standing)**: The status, hierarchy, power, and capital a
  persona holds are the standing composite's. Owing reads the unequal stance a live claim
  imposes, not the standing that persists whether or not anything is owed.
- **The bond that is not an obligation (love)**: The desire, loyalty, and disclosure that make an
  intimacy are the love composite's. Owing models what is outstanding between two people, not
  what draws them together in the first place.
- **The shared resource and its governance (commons)**: A common-pool resource governed by its
  own users through crafted institutions is the commons engine's. Owing is dyadic: what one
  persona carries because of what another handed over.
- **The felt lack that captures the mind (scarcity)**: Tunnelling, bandwidth, and the trap are the
  scarcity engine's. Owing models the claim; what shortage does to the mind carrying it is
  scarcity's.
- **Where personal worth comes from (worth-logic)**: The dignity, honour, and face logics by
  which worth is assigned and defended are the worth-logic engine's. Owing reads the obligation,
  not the culture's account of what a person is worth.
- **The debt as authored symbol (the play's pitch)**: An obligation made to **stand for** a story
  -- the loan that mirrors a theme of captivity, the gift that signals a betrayal to come -- is
  refused: that is authored key, the play's pitch, not the force on the persona. Owing sets the
  claim; the meaning read into it is the play's.

## Encoding

Source to constraint. A composite root over three engine atoms it declares as dependencies, with
three bridges, the degrees to which an obligation is made exact.

- **[owing](process_owing.md)** (the root): how an obligation between two persons is made,
  carried, and discharged -- one claim at three degrees of exactness, each step buying
  portability with binding. Anchored by Mauss (1925) and Graeber (2011).
- **[the giving](process_owing_giving.md)** (bridge): the transfer that must appear voluntary,
  whose obligation must not be named, and whose interval before the return is what makes it a
  relationship rather than a transaction. Anchored by Mauss (the triple obligation) and Bourdieu
  (misrecognition, the interval); the first degree, which is almost none.
- **[the owed](process_owing_owed.md)** (bridge): the bond written as a figure -- a claim on the
  debtor's future, two unequal stances, and an arc from incurring through servicing to default.
  Anchored by Graeber (quantified, enforceable obligation) and Nietzsche (the moral weight of
  owing); the second degree, the claim saying how much.
- **[the settling](process_owing_settling.md)** (bridge): the general equivalent that prices the
  unlike and can discharge any claim, the freedom it buys the holder, and the earmarking by which
  people push back against it. Anchored by Simmel (money dissolving qualities into quantity),
  Zelizer (earmarking), and Bourdieu (the equal, immediate return that closes the account); the
  composite's twist and keystone, that what settles the account ends the relation.
