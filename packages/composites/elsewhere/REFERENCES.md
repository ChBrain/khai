# Elsewhere: Reference

## Line of Work

Elsewhere as **composite**: the mind in a place it is not. It is not a fourth mechanism but a
wiring of three: the **imagination** engine supplies the scene built on purpose and held apart
from belief, the **transportation** engine supplies the story world entered until the room
recedes, and the **memory** engine supplies the past assembled from a trace and a cue. This
composite adds no fourth engine; it reads the seam the three make -- one recombining faculty at
three settings of the guard that marks its output as made -- and stages that seam into three
bridges, closing on the discovery that the case presenting as retrieval is the one constructing
unmarked.

The domain does not model any single setting -- conjuring, supposition, and simulation are the
imagination engine's; entry, imagery, resonance, and transportability are transportation's;
episodic, semantic, procedural, emotional, and prospective are memory's; and one alone is a
process, not the shape. Nor is it the spontaneous drift of attention inward, which is the
mind-wandering engine's inside the creativity composite; nor the content-agnostic absorption of
attention, which is the absorption composite's; nor the feeling toward an imagined future, which
is the prospection composite's. It owns the constructing: what a mind assembles when it is not
where it is, and whether anything tells the persona it is assembling.

The spine is the constructive episodic simulation hypothesis, and one of this composite's atoms
already cites it. Schacter and Addis argued that remembering the past and imagining the future
draw on the same core network, and -- the load-bearing half -- that episodic simulation of a
future event _requires_ a memory system permitting the flexible recombination of details from past
events into novel scenarios. Memory is held in pieces precisely so the pieces can be moved. The
convergence was established by three concurrent studies in 2007 and named among that year's
breakthroughs; Hassabis and Maguire supplied the sharpest form of it, patients with hippocampal
damage proving unable to construct novel imagined scenes as well as unable to recollect. It
follows, and Schacter has argued at length, that the failures of memory are not defects bolted
onto an otherwise faithful record but by-products of the recombination the same system is built
to perform -- which Bartlett's reconstructive account, Loftus's misinformation effect, and Nader's
reconsolidation each demonstrate from a different side. Mar and Oatley reach the third bridge from
a separate literature and land in the same place, treating narrative fiction as the abstraction
and simulation of social experience, run on the processes that handle the real thing -- so being
carried into a story is this machinery pointed at a world someone else assembled. What this
composite adds on top is the ordering: that the three differ not in mechanism but in how much
warning the persona gets, imagination holding the quarantine on purpose, transportation spending
it on purpose, and memory never having had one. The three bridges are those three settings: the
conjured, the borrowed, and the rebuilt.

## Origin

The cognitive neuroscience of constructive memory, the philosophy of imagination, and narrative
psychology, wired as a composite over the imagination, transportation, and memory engines. The
first two rows are the **joining** -- the published work that argues these are one machinery -- and
the rest anchor the individual bridges.

## Restrictions

What the composite refuses to model, and to whom it delegates.

- **The single settings (the atoms)**: The composite wires the three but owns none. Conjuring,
  supposition, and simulation are the imagination engine's; entry, imagery, resonance, and
  transportability are transportation's; the five memory kinds are memory's. Elsewhere reads only
  the shape the three share; one setting alone is a process, not the constructing.
- **The drift, not the turn (mind-wandering, creativity)**: The spontaneous, task-unrelated drift
  of attention inward is the mind-wandering engine's, inside the creativity composite, and the
  generating of many novel ideas from a prompt is divergent-thinking's. Elsewhere reads a
  construction that is deliberate or cued, not one that drifts up unbidden.
- **Absorption without a world (absorption)**: The content-agnostic absorption of attention, the
  flow of a matched challenge, and the warping of felt time are the absorption composite's.
  Elsewhere owns being carried into a narrative in particular, which is a different thing from
  being merely absorbed -- transportation's signature is the lowered guard, not the depth of
  attention.
- **The feeling toward the future (prospection)**: The hope and the dread a persona holds toward
  what they picture are the prospection composite's. Elsewhere is the picturing; prospection is
  what it is like to hold it.
- **The future self, the counterfactual, the sweetened return (possible-selves, regret,
  nostalgia)**: The self a persona hopes or dreads to become is possible-selves', inside selfhood;
  the undoing of an actual past is regret's and the sweetened return to a real one nostalgia's,
  both inside longing. Elsewhere owns the construction, not the particular use it is put to.
- **The pretend stance (play-mode)**: The as-if attitude taken toward a real activity is
  play-mode's, inside exploration. Elsewhere reads a made representation, not a stance toward
  something present.
- **Influence in general (persuasion, allegiance)**: The general mechanism of influence is the
  persuasion engine's, of which narrative transport is one route, and the audience's moral bond to
  particular characters is the allegiance engine's. Elsewhere owns the transport itself.
- **What evidence can reach (assent)**: How a persona holds a claim and what would change it is
  the assent composite's. Elsewhere reads the construction that arrives; whether the persona then
  treats it as true, and what could move them off it, is assent's.
- **The construction as authored symbol (the play's pitch)**: A recollection made to **stand for** a
  story -- the false memory that mirrors a theme of self-deception -- is refused: that is authored
  key, the play's pitch, not the force on the persona. Elsewhere sets what is constructed; the
  meaning read into it is the play's.

## Encoding

Source to constraint. A composite root over three engine atoms it declares as dependencies, with
three bridges, ordered by how much the persona knows they are constructing.

- **[elsewhere](process_elsewhere.md)** (the root): the mind in a place it is not -- the
  flexibility that lets it assemble the absent, against the same flexibility running where nothing
  marks it. Anchored by Schacter & Addis (2007).
- **[the conjured](process_elsewhere_conjured.md)** (bridge): the deliberate construction held
  inside an offline quarantine, which is exactly what lets it be worked. Anchored by Currie &
  Ravenscroft (2002), with Kosslyn on imagery and Nichols & Stich on the supposition workspace via
  the atom.
- **[the borrowed](process_elsewhere_borrowed.md)** (bridge): the story world entered, the
  quarantine spent by the engagement itself, so the tale's claims pass a guard a bald assertion
  would meet. Anchored by Gerrig (1993) and Green & Brock (2000).
- **[the rebuilt](process_elsewhere_rebuilt.md)** (bridge): the past assembled from trace and cue,
  gaps filled with what fits, returned changed and filed back changed. Anchored by Bartlett
  (1932), Loftus (1974), and Nader (2000); the composite's twist and keystone, that the faculty a
  persona trusts most is the one running with no quarantine at all -- memory's unreliability being
  the same recombination that makes imagining possible, in the one place nothing warns of it.
