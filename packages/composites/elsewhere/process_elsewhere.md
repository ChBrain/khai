# Process: Elsewhere

## Taxonomy

The mind in a place it is not.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: elsewhere

## Initiated by

A persona is holding something before the mind that is not in front of them, and the holding is being done by the same machine every time.
Elsewhere starts wherever that construction is running -- the scene built on purpose and marked off from belief, through [imagination](@chbrain/khai-engine-imagination/process_imagination.md); the story world entered until the room recedes, through [transportation](@chbrain/khai-engine-transportation/process_transportation.md); and the past assembled from a trace and a cue, through [memory](@chbrain/khai-engine-memory/process_memory.md).
This composite adds no fourth engine; it reads what the three make together: one recombining faculty at three settings of the guard that marks its output as made.

## Direction

Toward the whole of what a mind does when it is not where it is, ordered by how much the persona knows they are constructing.
The composite wires the three engines and reads their working in three shapes: [the Conjured](process_elsewhere_conjured.md), the quarantine held on purpose; [the Borrowed](process_elsewhere_borrowed.md), the quarantine spent on purpose; and [the Rebuilt](process_elsewhere_rebuilt.md), the twist -- the quarantine that was never there.
The machinery does not change across the three; only the label on its output does, and the label is what the persona has to go on.

## Lever

The flexibility that lets a mind assemble what is not present against the same flexibility running where nothing marks it.
The carrying force is that a persona who could only report what is in front of them could not plan, rehearse, warn themselves, or be moved by anything they had not lived -- and the mechanism that makes all of that possible is recombination, the taking of parts from what was and putting them where they were not; the resistance is that recombination has no native sense of its own reach, so the same operation that builds a useful scenario out of remembered parts will quietly fill a gap in a recollection with what fits, and the persona receives both as what their mind gave them.
Elsewhere is read wherever a persona is granted what they are picturing: not a self reporting on the absent but one building it, at a level of self-warning that varies and is not chosen.

## Echo

The persona revealed by what they are holding that is not there.
Elsewhere does not close; it is the constructing running, gathering the three engines into one reading -- a scene made, a world entered, a past assembled.
It hands off what it is not: the spontaneous, task-unrelated drift of attention inward is the mind-wandering engine's, inside the creativity composite, where this is deliberate or cued rather than drifting; the content-agnostic absorption of attention and the flow of a matched challenge are the absorption composite's, where this owns being carried into a narrative in particular; the generating of many novel ideas from a prompt is the divergent-thinking engine's, inside creativity; the feeling toward a future imagined -- its hope and its dread -- is the prospection composite's, where this is the imagining and not the emotion; the future self a persona hopes or dreads to become is possible-selves', inside selfhood; the counterfactual undoing of an actual past is regret's, inside longing, and the sweetened return to a real one is nostalgia's; the pretend stance taken toward a real activity is play-mode's, inside exploration; the general mechanism of influence is the persuasion engine's, of which narrative transport is one route; the moral bond to particular characters is the allegiance engine's; and the recollection made to stand for a story is authored key, the play's pitch, not the force.
The persona is dated from the construction, and read as a self that cannot tell, from inside, which of its pictures was marked.
