# Process: Elsewhere, the Borrowed

## Taxonomy

[Elsewhere](process_elsewhere.md), the setting at which the persona enters a world someone else built and the guard goes down.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: elsewhere

## Initiated by

A persona reads, watches, or hears a story and is carried into it.
The Borrowed starts wherever [transportation](@chbrain/khai-engine-transportation/process_transportation.md) is running -- the [entry](@chbrain/khai-engine-transportation/process_entry.md) across the threshold into the tale, the [imagery](@chbrain/khai-engine-transportation/process_imagery.md) that makes its events pictured rather than merely followed, and the [resonance](@chbrain/khai-engine-transportation/process_resonance.md) that makes them felt, tuned by a standing [transportability](@chbrain/khai-engine-transportation/position_transportability.md) the persona brings to every tale.
It is the setting where the construction is someone else's and the persona knows it is invented, and neither fact protects them.

## Direction

Toward a world that brightens as the surround dims.
The Borrowed reads the crossing -- the room, the hour, and the persona's own concerns receding while the story's events become, for a while, their present; the reader who looks up and has to place themselves again; the traveller who journeys into the tale and comes back with the tale still on them.
It moves the persona out of their own construction and into a made one they did not make, attended and pictured and felt as if lived.

## Lever

The felt reality of the invented against the guard that reality is bought with.
The carrying force is that a persona will go somewhere a story takes them that no argument could take them, and go willingly, because the going is the pleasure -- and having gone, they return not quite the same, carrying the tale's images, feelings, and beliefs; the resistance is what pays for it: the transported persona is less able to counterargue, its critical defences lowered by the very engagement, so the story's claims and values slip past a guard that a bald assertion would have met head-on.
The Borrowed is elsewhere at its most persuasive and least defended: what has no claim on being true is what gets past what guards the true.

## Echo

The world entered, and the traveller returned carrying it.
The Borrowed leaves the persona changed by something they knew was invented -- which is why narrative, and not argument, is the route by which stories move what an audience thinks and feels -- and hands the pattern on to the setting where nothing at all announces itself as construction.
The knowing does not help: a persona fully aware they are reading fiction is the persona whose defences the fiction has lowered.
The Borrowed is dated from the threshold, the moment the room stopped being where the persona was.
