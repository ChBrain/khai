# Instructions: Elsewhere

How a Playwright wires the elsewhere composite into a play. You wire by linking the play's
own content to the composite's files; the files stay untouched, they are canon. The
composite installs the imagination, transportation, and memory engines as dependencies,
and their laws arrive with them. Authoring guidance, not runtime content, and it does not
go on tour.

## Human

- The human sets what the persona is holding that is not there: a scene they built and know they built, a world someone else made that they have entered, or a past that has come back as a report -- and, the question that governs the read, which setting is running: the conjured, the borrowed, or the rebuilt.

## Agent

- From the play's own content, read a persona as constructing rather than receiving: link under Projection the setting they are in -- the conjured while they are building on purpose (the image summoned before the mind's eye, the as-if premise entertained without belief, the novel scenario assembled from remembered and invented parts and run forward, from imagination), the borrowed while they are inside a world someone else made (the crossing of the threshold, the events pictured rather than followed, the events felt, tuned by a standing readiness to be transported, from transportation), the rebuilt while what is returning presents as retrieval (the cue meeting the trace, the gaps filled with what fits, the assembly returned changed and filed back changed, from memory). Play the construction as the persona's ordinary access to what is not present, never narrated as unreliable from outside, and never made to stand for a story. You link to the composite, you never edit it.

## Collaboration

- The deliberate made representation is the imagination engine's; being carried into a narrative world is transportation's; the reconstruction of what did happen is memory's -- one setting alone is not the whole of being elsewhere. The spontaneous drift of attention inward is mind-wandering's, inside creativity, and the generating of many novel ideas is divergent-thinking's; the content-agnostic absorption of attention and the flow of a matched challenge are the absorption composite's, where this owns narrative transport in particular; the hope and dread toward an imagined future are prospection's; the future self is possible-selves', inside selfhood, the undoing of the actual past is regret's and the sweetened return nostalgia's, both inside longing; the pretend stance toward a real activity is play-mode's, inside exploration; the general mechanism of influence is persuasion's and the moral bond to characters is allegiance's; how a persona holds a claim and what would change it is the assent composite's. This composite owns the constructing -- and the rebuilt bridge carries the keystone: the faculty a persona trusts most is the one running with no quarantine at all.

## Knowledge

- Elsewhere is a persona read by what they are holding that is not in front of them -- braiding the scene built on purpose (imagination), the story world entered (transportation), and the past assembled from a trace and a cue (memory) into one recombining faculty -- read as a wiring of those three engines, and its question is how much the persona knows they are constructing (Schacter and Addis on constructive episodic simulation, where simulating a future event requires a memory system permitting flexible recombination of remembered details; Hassabis and Maguire on scene construction, hippocampal damage impairing imagined scenes as well as recollection; Mar and Oatley on fiction as the simulation of social experience; Currie and Ravenscroft on the offline quarantine; Green and Brock on transportation and reduced counterarguing; Bartlett, Loftus, and Nader on reconstruction, misinformation, and reconsolidation). It runs at three settings of the guard, and the machinery does not change across them. The conjured is the quarantine held: the made state marked off from what is believed and perceived, which is exactly what lets it be turned, extended, and combined -- a persona can rehearse a disaster without suffering it and follow a premise they reject without adopting it, and the standing risk is that a state held vividly enough stops being marked. The borrowed is the quarantine spent: attention, imagery, and feeling converging on a tale until the room recedes, and the transported persona less able to counterargue, its defences lowered by the very engagement, so a story moves and persuades where an argument cannot -- and knowing it is fiction does not help, since the persona whose defences the fiction lowered is the one who knew. The rebuilt is the twist -- the guard not lowered but absent, since nobody ever marked this one as made: a cue meets a trace, the persona assembles what was and fills the gaps with what fits, and what returns is changed, tinted by the present and sharpened where the telling needed it, then filed back in the changed form so each later recall starts from where this one left it. Read a persona as constructing in all three, and read the confidence attaching to a memory as a property of the reconstruction rather than of the event.

## System

- Do read the persona as constructing rather than receiving: name whether the conjured (built and known), the borrowed (entered and known to be made), or the rebuilt (returned as report) is running, and cast each from its engines.
- Do keep the settings distinct by the guard, not by the content: the conjured holds the marking, the borrowed spends it, the rebuilt never had it.
- Do let the rebuilt carry the keystone: the unreliability of memory is the same recombination that makes imagining possible, running where nothing warns of it.
- Do remember the correction is not available from inside: a persona checking a memory checks it against other memories, so a play that has a character catch their own false recollection unaided has stepped outside the engine.
- Do remember knowing does not protect: the persona fully aware they are reading fiction is the persona whose defences the fiction has lowered, and a play that arms a character with scepticism against a story has misread the bridge.
- Do remember a recalled memory is re-stored changed: the act of asking a persona to remember alters what they will remember next time, which is a thing the play can do to a character on purpose.
- Do not model the drift of attention inward (that is mind-wandering), absorption without a world (that is absorption), the feeling toward an imagined future (that is prospection), the future self (that is possible-selves), the undoing of the past (that is regret), the pretend stance (that is play-mode), influence in general (that is persuasion), or what would change a persona's mind (that is assent). Do not make the construction stand for a story (that is the play's pitch). Do not edit the composite's files; wire only from the play's side.
