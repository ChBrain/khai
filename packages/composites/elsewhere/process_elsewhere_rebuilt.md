# Process: Elsewhere, the Rebuilt

## Taxonomy

[Elsewhere](process_elsewhere.md), the setting at which nothing marks the construction, and the three are seen for one thing.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: elsewhere

## Initiated by

A cue lands, meets a trace, and what comes back arrives with the authority of a report.
The Rebuilt starts wherever [memory](@chbrain/khai-engine-memory/process_memory.md) is doing its work -- the assembling of what was from the trace and the cue together, the gaps filled with what fits, and the assembled version returned and filed back in its changed form, whether as [episodic](@chbrain/khai-engine-memory/process_episodic.md), [semantic](@chbrain/khai-engine-memory/process_semantic.md), [procedural](@chbrain/khai-engine-memory/process_procedural.md), [emotional](@chbrain/khai-engine-memory/process_emotional.md), or [prospective](@chbrain/khai-engine-memory/process_prospective.md).
It is the setting where the guard is not lowered but absent: nobody ever marked this one as made.

## Direction

Toward a past the persona carries forward as though it had been kept.
The Rebuilt reads the assembling -- the detail that was never there and is now certain, the argument remembered in the shape the retelling gave it, the childhood scene recalled from outside the body it was lived in, the account that firms up a little every time it is given -- each a reconstruction offered to the persona in the one register that does not announce itself.
It moves the persona nowhere they can notice: what returns is changed, tinted by the present and sharpened where the telling needed it, and it is filed back changed, so each later recall starts from where this one left it.

## Lever

The recombination that makes every other elsewhere possible against the same recombination running unlabelled here.
The carrying force is that this is not a weakness bolted onto memory but the reason memory is built the way it is: a system that stored episodes whole and returned them intact could not take a part from one and a part from another and assemble a scenario that has never happened, and that assembly is what lets a persona plan, rehearse, and be warned -- the past is held in pieces precisely so the pieces can be moved; the resistance is that a system built to recombine will recombine when nobody asked it to, filling a gap with what fits as readily as it fills a scenario, and it has no way to flag which it just did.
The Rebuilt rides the composite's keystone knife-edge: the faculty a persona trusts most is the one running with no quarantine at all -- imagination holds the marking on purpose, transportation spends it on purpose, and memory never had one to spend.

## Echo

The construction that arrived labelled as retrieval.
It shows the three settings complete -- a scene marked as made, a world known to be invented and entering anyway, a past that presents as kept -- and carries beneath it the composite's keystone: the unreliability of memory is not a separate fact about memory but the same flexible recombination that makes imagining possible, running in the one place where nothing warns the persona it is running.
So the correction is never available from inside: a persona checking a memory checks it against other memories, and the confidence they feel is a property of the reconstruction rather than of the event.
The Rebuilt is the note the whole composite closes on: what a persona takes to be the past is the last thing they made of it.
The Rebuilt is dated from the most recent recall, which is the only version there now is.
