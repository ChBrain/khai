# Process: Elsewhere, the Conjured

## Taxonomy

[Elsewhere](process_elsewhere.md), the setting at which the persona builds and knows they are building.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: elsewhere

## Initiated by

A persona turns from what is present to hold before the mind something that is not, on purpose.
The Conjured starts wherever [imagination](@chbrain/khai-engine-imagination/process_imagination.md) is at work -- the [conjuring](@chbrain/khai-engine-imagination/process_conjuring.md) that recreates a quasi-perceptual image before the mind's eye, the [supposition](@chbrain/khai-engine-imagination/process_supposition.md) that entertains an as-if premise without believing it, and the [simulation](@chbrain/khai-engine-imagination/process_simulation.md) that assembles a novel scenario from remembered and invented parts and runs it forward to see how it would go.
It is the setting where the guard is highest: the made state marked off from what the persona believes and perceives.

## Direction

Toward a representation held and worked rather than received.
The Conjured reads the constructing -- the room measured before the furniture is bought, the argument rehearsed against an opponent not present, the consequence of a premise the persona rejects followed out anyway, the route walked in the mind before it is walked -- each a thing summoned deliberately and then turned, extended, and combined.
It moves the persona from perceiving to composing: the image rotated, the supposition drawn out, the scenario run forward, all of it available to be worked precisely because none of it is being taken as real.

## Lever

The quarantine that makes the made state useful against the pull of the actual that keeps collapsing it.
The carrying force is the decoupling itself: a state held as merely entertained can be worked without cost, so the persona can rehearse a disaster without suffering it and follow a premise they reject without adopting it -- the offline running of the same capacities perception and belief use online, kept offline on purpose; the resistance is the tether to the real, the difficulty of picturing against a crowded percept and of supposing against a fixed conviction, and the standing risk at the other end, that a state held long enough or vividly enough stops being marked and becomes a belief or a percept.
The Conjured is elsewhere at its most deliberate and most honest: everything about it is construction and the persona knows it.

## Echo

The absent held before the mind, and labelled as made.
The Conjured leaves the persona with something they built and can still see the seams of -- and hands the pattern on to the settings where the marking weakens and then disappears.
The quarantine is the whole of what distinguishes it: hold the made state apart and it can be worked, let it collapse into belief and it is no longer imagined.
The Conjured is dated from the turning, the moment a persona chose to look at what was not there.
