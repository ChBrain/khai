# Inconsistency: Reference

## Line of Work

Inconsistency as **process**: an account that must be believed and cannot be delivered by the person giving it.

This composite adds no third engine. It wires two that khai already carries, and reads only the thing they make
together.

**[Interpreting](@chbrain/khai-engine-interpreting/process_interpreting.md)** supplies a conversation conducted
through a third person: a rendering neither party can check, a compression that is systematic rather than
random, and a role defined as an absence. **[Credibility](@chbrain/khai-engine-credibility/process_credibility.md)**
supplies the credit extended to a first-person account, the discount applied before listening, and what a
shortfall does to the persona giving it. Neither engine, on its own, describes what happens when the two
arrangements are the same event.

**Maryns is the joining.** Working through the Belgian asylum procedure, she follows what happens to a
narrative between the mouth that produces it and the file that decides on it: the account is interpreted,
transcribed, summarised, and filed, and each of those steps is a transformation. What the assessor then has is
a textual artifact, and what the assessor does with it is compare it against a later one. Variation produced by
the procedure's own machinery arrives at the decision as variation in the speaker. Blommaert makes the
adjacent argument from the other side: narrative resources are unequally distributed, so what a procedure
reads as evasion, vagueness, or incoherence is frequently a person telling their story with the resources they
have, in a genre the procedure does not recognise. Between them, the joining is argued rather than asserted --
the channel is doing work, the work shows up as a property of the person, and nobody involved intends it.

An engine can be in 0..n composites: an atom carries a phenomenon, and a composite carries a question asked
of it. **Interpreting is wired here for the first time. Credibility is wired for the second** -- the raising
composite reads the standing of somebody who says a harm is coming, where the account is doubted for what it
claims; here the account is doubted for having changed, and the change was made in transit. The pairing is
structural rather than convenient, because what one engine says the channel drops is exactly what the other
says the hearer reads.

**The transit** removes the manner and delivers the content. What compression drops first is not arbitrary:
hedges, self-corrections, repetitions, false starts, and emotional colouring. What a hearer weighs when
deciding whether somebody is being straight with them is hedges, self-corrections, repetitions, false starts,
and emotional colouring. The channel strips the exact evidence the assessment runs on, and delivers what
remains in an even professional voice belonging to somebody else.

**The record** makes the rendering permanent and the original unavailable. The file becomes the account. The
persona's own words are unrecorded, in a language nobody in the procedure reads, and the only other person who
heard them was the interpreter -- whose version is the document. So the report has no second source, and cannot
acquire one, because the check and the thing being checked are the same object.

**The verdict** is a fair test applied to a contaminated sample. A date has moved, a sequence has swapped, a
detail is present in one telling and absent from the other. The assessor discounts, which is a sound
inference; the discount generalises from the detail to the person, and every other claim in the file is
re-read through it.

Hence the **twist**, in the root's Echo, completed by the verdict. **The channel makes the discrepancy and the
teller is charged with it, because the only witness to the difference is the one nobody thinks was in the
room.** The interpreter heard both tellings and knows why they diverged. The role is defined as an absence,
and an absence cannot testify to what it did -- not because anybody excluded them, but because the arrangement
has no place from which they could speak.

The composite is built so that nobody has to be acting badly for it to run, and a play should keep it that
way. The assessor is reasonable and is using the only instrument they have. The interpreter is competent. The
persona is telling the truth, or is not, and the composite never says which.

**On the evidence, stated plainly.** **The joining is argued from one country's procedure.** Maryns's work is
close discourse analysis of a bounded set of Belgian asylum cases, which is the right method for showing how a
narrative is transformed in transit and is not a survey of hearings in general; a reader should take the
mechanism as demonstrated there and plausible elsewhere, not as measured everywhere. **Consistency is a good
test and the composite does not say otherwise.** An account that changes between tellings genuinely is weaker
evidence, an assessor who ignored that would be failing the people with true accounts, and nothing here
recommends abandoning the criterion. The claim is about what the criterion is being applied to. **The
composite cannot tell a manufactured discrepancy from a real one.** Neither can the assessor, the persona, or
the interpreter, and this is the composite's weakest joint stated as plainly as it can be: there is no test
offered here, and anybody who reads this as a method for identifying wrongly-decided cases has read it wrong.
The reading is that the gap is not clean evidence, not that it is never evidence. **The two atoms are wired
across a gap in the literature.** Fricker's credibility deficit and Maryns's channel effects were developed
independently, on different questions, and the empirical link between them -- that channel-made discrepancies
feed the identity-prejudicial discount specifically -- is this composite's inference, drawn because both
mechanisms plainly operate in the same room, and not something either author establishes. What should not be
taken from this composite is that interpreted assessments are worthless, that assessors are prejudiced, or
that a persona whose account changed was telling the truth. The claim is narrow: the difference between two
tellings is partly the instrument's, and the instrument does not appear in the file.

## Origin

The linguistic study of what happens to a narrative inside a procedure, joined to the philosophy of testimonial
credit. The twist follows from Maryns's account of the transformations between the telling and the file.

## Restrictions

What the composite refuses to model, and to whom it delegates.

- **The mediated conversation itself (interpreting)**: The summoning, the relaying, the coordinating, and the
  invisibility are the atom's. This composite reads only what the arrangement does to an account being
  assessed.
- **Credit and its shortfall (credibility)**: The report with no second source, the discount applied before
  listening, the deficit that attaches to a knower, and the wrong that has no name are the atom's. This
  composite reads only the discrepancy that feeds them.
- **Two frames misgrounding (crosstalk, grounding, repair)**: Two personas each grounding against their own
  frame, and the widening gap that follows, are those packages'. Inconsistency has one account, one channel,
  and one assessor.
- **The doubted warner (raising, warning)**: The standing of somebody who says a harm is coming, and the
  scrutiny that scales with the implication, are those packages'. There the account is doubted for what it
  claims; here it is doubted for having changed.
- **The institution's record (document, register, writ, legibility)**: The certificate, the entry, the written
  instrument, and the state's readable index are those engines'. This composite reads the file only as the
  thing that replaced the event.
- **The law and the procedure (the world, org, total-institution)**: Asylum law, evidential standards, appeal
  rights, and the funding of language services are matters of practice and statute. They set the format; this
  composite reads what the arrangement does to the three people inside it.
- **A contradiction as authored symbol (the play's pitch)**: A discrepancy staged in order to **stand for** a
  theme -- the slip that mirrors a motif of fracture -- is refused: that is authored key, the play's pitch.
  Inconsistency sets what differed and what the difference was taken to mean; the meaning is the play's.

## Encoding

Source to constraint. A composite: one root and three bridges, each hard-linking the atoms it joins.

- **[inconsistency](process_inconsistency.md)** (the root): an account that must be believed and cannot be
  delivered by the person giving it, carrying the twist -- the channel makes the discrepancy and the teller is
  charged with it. Anchored by Maryns (2006).
- **[the transit](process_inconsistency_transit.md)**: what the rendering strips, which is what the assessment
  reads. Joins interpreting's compression and voicing to credibility's manner.
- **[the record](process_inconsistency_record.md)**: the document that replaces the event and cannot be
  corroborated. Joins interpreting's relaying to credibility's corroboration.
- **[the verdict](process_inconsistency_verdict.md)**: the gap read as a fact about the teller, where the twist
  completes. Joins credibility's discount and deficit to interpreting's finding.
