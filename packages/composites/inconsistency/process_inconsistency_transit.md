# Process: Inconsistency, the Transit

## Taxonomy

[Inconsistency](process_inconsistency.md), what the account loses on the way.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: inconsistency

## Initiated by

The account is told, and something else arrives.
The Transit is two things arriving together: [the compression](@chbrain/khai-engine-interpreting/process_the_compression.md), which keeps what is load bearing and drops the rest, and [the manner](@chbrain/khai-engine-credibility/process_the_manner.md), which is what a hearer actually reads a speaker's honesty from.

## Direction

Toward a version stripped of exactly the features the assessment runs on.
What compression drops first is systematic rather than random: hedges, self-corrections, repetitions, false starts, and emotional colouring. What a hearer weighs, when deciding whether somebody is being straight with them, is hedges, self-corrections, repetitions, false starts, and emotional colouring.
So the channel removes the evidence and the assessment proceeds on what is left, which is content -- flattened, tidied, and delivered in an even professional voice by [somebody speaking in the first person](@chbrain/khai-engine-interpreting/process_the_voicing.md) who is not the person whose account it is.

## Lever

The necessity of the compression against the specificity of what it takes.
The carrying force is that the compression is not a failure and cannot be dispensed with: a rendering that reproduced every hesitation would be unusable, would double the length of an already long procedure, and would not obviously help anybody; the resistance is that the losses land precisely on the channel that carries sincerity, so a frightened person and a composed liar arrive at the assessor's ear in the same register, and the difference between them was left in the other language.
The Transit removes the manner and delivers the content.

## Echo

An account with its substance intact and its texture gone.
It is where the two atoms first touch: what one engine says is dropped is what the other says is read.
The Transit is dated to the telling and happens again at the next one.
