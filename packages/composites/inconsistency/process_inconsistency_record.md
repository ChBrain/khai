# Process: Inconsistency, the Record

## Taxonomy

[Inconsistency](process_inconsistency.md), the version that becomes the account.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: inconsistency

## Initiated by

Somebody writes down what arrived.
The Record is two things arriving together: [the relaying](@chbrain/khai-engine-interpreting/process_the_relaying.md), which produces an utterance nobody in the room can check, and [the corroboration](@chbrain/khai-engine-credibility/process_the_corroboration.md), the report that has no second source.

## Direction

Toward a document that replaces the event it describes.
From the moment it is written, the file is the account. The persona's own words are gone -- unrecorded, in a language nobody in the procedure reads -- and what remains is a rendering of them, transcribed by a third hand, summarised by a fourth, and filed.
Every later telling is compared against that document rather than against what was said, and the persona has no access to it, cannot check it, and in most procedures will never see the version they are being measured against.

## Lever

The necessity of a record against the disappearance of the thing recorded.
The carrying force is that a procedure without a record would be worse in every direction -- no continuity, no review, no appeal, and no protection against an assessor's memory -- and writing it down is the single most important safeguard the persona has; the resistance is that the account has exactly one source and no way to be corroborated, because the only other person who heard the original was the interpreter, and the interpreter's version is the document, so the check and the thing being checked are the same object.
The Record makes the rendering permanent and the original unavailable.

## Echo

A file that is now the only version of a life.
It shows why the persona cannot correct the discrepancy: they are not being compared to themselves.
The Record is dated to the transcription and does not change after it.
