# Process: Inconsistency, the Verdict

## Taxonomy

[Inconsistency](process_inconsistency.md), the gap read as a fact about the teller.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: inconsistency

## Initiated by

The second telling does not match the first.
A date has moved, a sequence has swapped, a name is rendered differently, a detail present in one account is absent from the other.

## Direction

Toward a discount applied to everything the persona says, on the strength of a difference the channel made.
The assessor now has [a reason to discount](@chbrain/khai-engine-credibility/process_discount_credibility.md) and applies it. From there it generalises: the inconsistency is not read as a fault in one detail but as information about the speaker, so [the deficit](@chbrain/khai-engine-credibility/process_the_deficit.md) attaches to the person and every other claim in the file is re-read through it.
And when the case is later reviewed, [the finding](@chbrain/khai-engine-interpreting/process_the_finding.md) lands where it can: on the interpretation, if anybody thinks to look, and on the applicant if nobody does.

## Lever

The soundness of the inference against the origin of the evidence.
The carrying force is that this is not prejudice and should not be staged as prejudice: contradiction really is evidence, assessors really do have to decide, and a procedure that ignored inconsistency would be no procedure at all -- an assessor who waved through every changing account would be failing the people with true ones; the resistance is that nobody in the room can distinguish a discrepancy the channel made from a discrepancy the persona made, the assessor least of all, so a sound inference is drawn from an unsound observation and lands with the full weight of a reasonable decision.
The Verdict is a fair test applied to a contaminated sample.

## Echo

A decision that everybody involved could defend, about a difference nobody made on purpose.
It is where the composite's twist completes: the discrepancy is the channel's and the charge is the persona's, and the one participant who heard both tellings has no standing to say which it was.
The Verdict is dated to the decision and is very hard to reopen.
