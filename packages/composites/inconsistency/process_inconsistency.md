# Process: Inconsistency

## Taxonomy

An account that must be believed, delivered through somebody else (no khai file of its own yet).

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: inconsistency

## Initiated by

A persona has to be believed, and cannot deliver their own account.
They have no shared language with the person assessing them, so every word arrives through a third party, and the assessment is made on what arrives.

## Direction

Toward a decision about a person's truthfulness, taken on a version of their words that nobody in the room produced alone.
This is not a third mechanism. It is a wiring of two. [Interpreting](@chbrain/khai-engine-interpreting/process_interpreting.md) supplies a conversation conducted through a third person, in which the rendering cannot be checked by either party. [Credibility](@chbrain/khai-engine-credibility/process_credibility.md) supplies the credit extended to a first-person account and what a shortfall does to the persona giving it.
The composite reads the one thing the two make together: an account told twice, through a channel that alters it both times, assessed by somebody who can only compare the two versions.

## Lever

The reasonableness of the test against the fact that the channel is what it is testing.
The carrying force is that consistency is a sound heuristic and the assessor is not being unfair in using it: an account that changes between tellings is, in ordinary life, weaker evidence than one that does not, and an assessor with no other instrument and a real decision to make is doing the only sensible thing available; the resistance is that the account was compressed differently on each occasion, first-personed by a different mouth, and rendered by somebody who chose between a word and its effect twice, so the two versions were never going to match, and the mismatch is the instrument's own output being read as the specimen's property.
Inconsistency is a test that measures the channel and reports on the person.

## Echo

A file with two versions in it, and a decision resting on the gap.
Inconsistency does not close; it is an account being assessed through somebody, gathered into one reading -- a transit, a record, a verdict.
The composite's twist is here. **The channel makes the discrepancy and the teller is charged with it, because the only witness to the difference is the one nobody thinks was in the room.** The interpreter heard both tellings, knows why they diverged, and has no standing to say so: the role is defined as an absence, and an absence cannot testify to what it did. So the one person who could resolve the question is structurally unavailable to it, and not because anybody excluded them.
It hands off what it is not: the mediated conversation itself is interpreting's; the credit extended to a first-person account and the deficit applied before listening are credibility's; two personas each grounding against their own frame is the crosstalk composite's; the standing of somebody who says a harm is coming is the raising composite's; and a contradiction staged to **stand for** a theme is authored key, the play's pitch.
The persona is dated from the first telling, which is the one they can no longer amend.
