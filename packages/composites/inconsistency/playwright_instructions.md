# Instructions: Inconsistency

How a Playwright wires the inconsistency composite into a play. You wire by linking the play's own content to the composite's files; the files stay untouched, they are canon. The composite declares two atoms as dependencies and links their members by name -- you never copy an atom's reading into the play, you point at it. The Roadie plumbs the composite's law into Instructions Knowledge on deploy. Authoring guidance, not runtime content, and it does not go on tour.

## Human

- The human keeps the assessor reasonable and the interpreter competent, because the composite needs no villain and collapses into a simpler and less true story the moment it gets one. Stage both tellings in full and let the audience hear what the room does not. Then decide privately whether the persona is telling the truth, and never resolve it on stage: the whole composite is the fact that nobody in the room can, and a production that answers the question has replaced the mechanism with a plot.

## Knowledge

- Inconsistency is an account that must be believed and cannot be delivered by the person giving it. It is not a third mechanism but a wiring of two: the interpreting engine supplies a conversation conducted through a third person, in which the rendering cannot be checked by either party, and the credibility engine supplies the credit extended to a first-person account and what a shortfall does to the persona giving it. It adds no third engine and wires on a persona at Projection, and it reads the one thing the two make together, which is an account told twice through a channel that alters it both times, assessed by somebody who can only compare the versions. Three bridges. The transit removes the manner and delivers the content: what compression drops first -- hedges, self-corrections, repetitions, false starts, emotional colouring -- is exactly what a hearer weighs when deciding whether somebody is being straight with them, so a frightened person and a composed liar arrive in the same even professional register, spoken in the first person by somebody else. The record makes the rendering permanent and the original unavailable: the file becomes the account, the persona's own words go unrecorded in a language nobody in the procedure reads, and the only other witness to the original was the interpreter, whose version is the document, so the check and the thing checked are one object. The verdict is a fair test applied to a contaminated sample: contradiction really is evidence, the assessor really must decide, the discount generalises from the detail to the person, and nobody in the room can tell a discrepancy the channel made from one the persona made. The twist: the channel makes the discrepancy and the teller is charged with it, because the only witness to the difference is the one nobody thinks was in the room -- the interpreter heard both tellings, knows why they diverged, and has no standing to say so, since the role is defined as an absence and an absence cannot testify to what it did.

## System

- Do stage both tellings. Months apart, the same events, and the differences small enough that an audience has to be paying attention.
- Do make the interpreter good on both occasions. Different interpreters, or the same one on a different day, and neither of them careless.
- Do let the compression be reasonable. Every cut is one the audience would have made, and the sum of them removes the persona's voice.
- Do keep the assessor sympathetic. They are not hostile, they have a decision to make, they have a file, and consistency is the only instrument in it.
- Do show the file being read. The persona is being compared to a document they have never seen, in a language they do not read.
- Do let the interpreter notice. They know exactly why the two versions differ, they are in the room, and there is no moment in the procedure where that could be said.
- Do close without resolving it. Nobody learns whether the account was true, and the decision stands either way.
- Do not model the mediated conversation itself (interpreting), credit and its shortfall (credibility), two frames misgrounding (crosstalk, grounding, repair), the doubted warner (raising, warning), or the institution's record (document, register, writ, legibility). Do not make a contradiction stand for a theme; that is authored key, the play's pitch. Do not edit the composite's files or the atoms'; wire only from the play's side.
