# Process: Uptake

## Taxonomy

How far a thing travels through a population, and what stops it.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: uptake

## Initiated by

A thing is spreading, and every persona who takes it up is also saying something.
Uptake starts wherever those two run together: an idea moving through a social system by adopters and the places it passes, through [adoption](@chbrain/khai-engine-adoption/process_adopt_idea.md); and the part of a self a persona draws from the groups they belong to, through [social identity](@chbrain/khai-engine-social-identity/position_social_identity.md).
This composite adds no third engine. It reads the one thing the two make together, which is that the population is not a medium the thing moves through but an audience reading everybody in it.

## Direction

Toward a ceiling nobody set and the merits did not explain.
The composite wires the two engines and reads their working in three shapes: [the Curve](process_uptake_curve.md), the sequence and its places; [the Signal](process_uptake_signal.md), what having it says about whoever has it; and [the Turn](process_uptake_turn.md), the twist -- the point where the saying changes because the sayers did.
The three are not three phases of a launch. They are one thing travelling, and one question about it getting a different answer as it goes.

## Lever

What a thing is good for against what having it says about you.
The carrying force is that both are real and the first is usually why anybody started: the thing works, it is better than what it replaces, and each persona who takes it up has an ordinary reason -- so a play does not need anybody to be posturing for this to run; the resistance is that a population reads its own members, so the set of people who have a thing becomes a fact about the thing, and a persona deciding whether to take it up is deciding, whether or not they notice, which set to be counted in.
Uptake is read on the population and never on the thing, because the thing has not changed at any point in the story.

## Echo

A thing abandoned by the people who made it worth having.
Uptake does not close; it is one pass of a thing through a population, gathered into one reading -- a curve, a signal, a turn.
It hands off what it is not: a persona's standing bind to one group -- the self drawn from it, the standard it bends them to, the cost of leaving -- is the membership composite's, which wires this same social-identity engine on the bond where this runs it on a thing; the us/them line and what it switches on is intergroup's; an attempt to persuade and what it raises is swaying's; talk moving through a population without a thing attached is grapevine's; a level crossing a threshold and not coming back is margin's, which is the shape without the audience; wanting what somebody else has is outdone's; and a fashion made to **stand for** a theme is authored key, the play's pitch, not the force.
The thing is dated from who had it first, and from the moment that stopped being an advantage.
