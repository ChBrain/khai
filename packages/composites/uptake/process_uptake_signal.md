# Process: Uptake, the Signal

## Taxonomy

[Uptake](process_uptake.md), what having it says about whoever has it.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: uptake

## Initiated by

A persona takes a thing up, and is read for having taken it up.
The Signal starts wherever [social identity](@chbrain/khai-engine-social-identity/position_social_identity.md) is in play -- the part of the self drawn from the groups a persona belongs to -- and it runs most plainly where [identification](@chbrain/khai-engine-social-identity/position_identification.md) is secure, since a persona merged with a group takes up what the group has and is read as one of them for it.
It is the face of the composite where the thing stops being only a thing.

## Direction

Toward an adoption that is also a claim.
The Signal reads what the taking up says rather than what it does: the object is worn as much as used, and the population reads its wearers, so a persona choosing it is choosing to be counted among the people who have it -- usually without formulating that, and usually while giving a reason about the thing.
It moves nothing about the object, and attaches to it everybody who currently holds it.

## Lever

The usefulness of a thing against the company it puts a persona in.
The carrying force is that the two normally point the same way and nobody has to choose: the thing is good, the people who have it are people the persona would not mind being counted with, and the reason given is the real reason -- so the signal does its work silently and the persona would deny it was operating; the resistance is that the signal is not under anybody's control, since it is composed of everybody who has the thing, and a persona can neither audit that set before adopting nor keep it as it was afterwards.
The Signal spends nothing while the company holds, and cannot be renegotiated when it does not.

## Echo

A thing being used, and worn, and read, all at once.
It is the bridge that makes the population the subject: the object's meaning is assembled out of its holders, so it is revised by every arrival and by nobody in particular.
What it hands upward is a meaning with a moving author -- and the fact that the personas who care most about what it says are exactly the ones who got there first.
The Signal is dated from whoever is holding the thing today, and it will be re-dated tomorrow.
