# Instructions: Uptake

How a Playwright wires the uptake composite into a play. You wire by linking the play's own
content to the composite's files; the files stay untouched, they are canon. The composite
installs the adoption and social-identity engines as dependencies, and their laws arrive
with them. Authoring guidance, not runtime content, and it does not go on tour.

## Human

- The human sets which things in the play are spreading, who picked them up first, and who arrives later to change what they mean.

## Agent

- From the play's own content, read a thing travelling and link under Projection what is running: the curve while it moves through adopters and the places it passes, from the outside across the chasm to the old ground (from adoption), the signal while holding it is saying something about whoever holds it (from social-identity), the turn while the saying has changed and the early holders are taking the exit or keeping the thing and moving the terms (from social-identity's mobility and creativity). Read the population and never the thing, since the thing has not changed at any point. You link to the composite, you never edit it.

## Collaboration

- The five adopter positions and the four places are the adoption engine's; identification, mobility, creativity, and competition are social-identity's -- one alone is a process or a position, not the ceiling. A persona's standing bind to a group is the membership composite's, which wires this same social-identity engine on a bond where this runs it on a thing; the us/them line is intergroup's, and uptake needs no hostility and no contest since the newcomers are doing nothing wrong; an attempt to persuade and what it raises is swaying's, and nobody in uptake is being persuaded; talk moving with no object attached is grapevine's; a level crossing a threshold for physical rather than social reasons is margin's, which is this shape without the audience; wanting what somebody else has is outdone's, and this persona is not coveting but noticing. This composite owns the ceiling -- and the turn bridge carries the keystone: the evidence of success is the mechanism of decline.

## Knowledge

- Uptake is a thing read while it travels through a population -- braiding the movement (adoption: innovators, early adopters, the early and late majority, laggards, passing the outside, the chasm, the tipping point, and the old ground, with a persona's position relative to the thing rather than fixed) with what the movement means (social identity: the part of a self drawn from the groups a persona belongs to). The join is Berger and Heath's, and it is in neither atom: diffusion research had explained the spread of a thing by the thing -- relative advantage, compatibility, complexity, trialability, observability -- and they showed that in domains where a possession or practice signals identity, and only in those domains, people abandon what they hold when the wrong others take it up. The population is not a medium the thing moves through, it is an audience reading everybody in it. It runs in three faces. The curve is the sequence, and the merits do real work in it -- something better or cheaper genuinely travels further, and every adopter has an ordinary reason -- but the merits are constant while the curve is not, so they cannot explain why a thing sits still for years and then moves, why some never cross the chasm, or why anything goes backwards. The signal is what holding it says: the object is worn as much as used, so a persona choosing it is choosing to be counted among the people who have it, usually without formulating that and usually while giving a reason about the thing. The meaning is assembled out of the current holders, so it is revised by every arrival and by nobody in particular, and it cannot be audited in advance or kept as it was. The turn is the twist: past some point the holders change and the reading changes with them, and the early ones reach for one of two moves the atom already carries -- mobility, the exit taken as an individual, or creativity, keeping the thing and changing the terms so the distinction is recovered without surrendering the object. Here the two are seen for one thing: a thing travels until it starts saying the wrong thing about the people who hold it, so the ceiling is set by who else arrived rather than by the merits, and the same fact drives both halves, since being taken up by others is what spreads a thing and being taken up by the wrong others is what stops it.

## System

- Do read the population and never the thing: name whether the curve (a thing moving), the signal (holding it saying something), or the turn (the saying having changed) is running, and cast each from its engines.
- Do make the thing identity-bearing rather than merely useful, since a thing nobody is read by does not run this at all and most adoptions carry no signal.
- Do keep the object unchanged across the whole arc, so the audience can put the leaver's reason next to the thing and see what the persona cannot say.
- Do let the leaver give a reason about the object -- that it has got worse, been diluted, lost the point -- and let them half believe it, because nobody says out loud that the wrong people have it.
- Do make the newcomers sympathetic and their reasons ordinary: nobody in this has behaved badly, and a play that makes them contemptible has staged snobbery rather than the mechanism.
- Do name who arrived to trigger the turn, even if no character does, so the play knows what changed.
- Do not stage this as popularity destroying value in general: most things that spread keep spreading, and the mechanism runs only where the thing says something about its holder.
- Do not model a persona's bind to a group (that is membership, wiring the same engine on a bond), the us/them line (that is intergroup), an attempt to persuade (that is swaying), talk with no object (that is grapevine), a physical threshold (that is margin), or coveting what another has (that is outdone). Do not make a fashion stand for a theme (that is the play's pitch). Do not edit the composite's files; wire only from the play's side.
