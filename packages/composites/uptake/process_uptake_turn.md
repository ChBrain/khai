# Process: Uptake, the Turn

## Taxonomy

[Uptake](process_uptake.md), the signal inverting, and where the two are seen for one thing.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: uptake

## Initiated by

The wrong people have it now.
The Turn starts wherever the arrival of the [late majority](@chbrain/khai-engine-adoption/position_late_majority.md) past [the tipping point](@chbrain/khai-engine-adoption/place_the_tipping_point.md) has changed what the thing says, and the personas who were [early](@chbrain/khai-engine-adoption/position_early_adopter.md) reach for one of the two moves available: [mobility](@chbrain/khai-engine-social-identity/position_mobility.md), the exit taken as an individual where the boundary reads as permeable, or [creativity](@chbrain/khai-engine-social-identity/position_creativity.md), keeping the thing and changing the terms of comparison to recover the distinction.
It is the face of the composite where success does the damage.

## Direction

Toward abandonment, or toward a redrawn line inside the thing.
The Turn reads which move is taken and what each costs: the exit is clean and gives up the thing entirely, while the reframe keeps it and manufactures an inside -- the earlier version, the harder practice, the part the newcomers do not do -- so the distinction is restored without the object being surrendered.
It moves the early holders off, or moves the boundary in, and either way the thing is no longer what it was.

## Lever

What the thing is still worth using against what it now says about the user.
The carrying force is that nobody in this has behaved badly and nothing was faked: the newcomers took up a good thing for good reasons, the early holders liked it before it meant anything, and the meaning changed under all of them without any decision -- which is why the leavers reach for a reason about the object, that it has got worse, been diluted, lost the point, and often half believe it; the resistance is that the reason is checkable and the object usually has not changed, so a play can put the two side by side and let the audience see what the persona cannot say.
The Turn rides the composite's keystone knife-edge: a thing travels until it starts saying the wrong thing about the people who hold it, so the ceiling is set by who else arrived and not by the merits, and the evidence of success is the mechanism of decline.

## Echo

A thing at its most widespread, and worth least to the people who carried it.
It shows the two engines complete -- a curve, and a population reading everybody on it -- and carries beneath it the composite's keystone: the same fact drives both halves, since being taken up by others is what spreads a thing and being taken up by the wrong others is what stops it, and there is no version of the first that does not eventually produce the second.
This is why the Turn is the hardest of the three to stage and the only one worth closing on: the object must not change, the newcomers must be sympathetic, the leaver must give a reason about the thing and mean it, and nobody must say out loud who arrived -- and a play that lets a character name the snobbery has replaced the mechanism with a confession.
The Turn is the note the whole composite closes on: what a thing was worth to whoever had it early is legible in when they put it down.
The Turn is dated from an arrival nobody registered as an event.
