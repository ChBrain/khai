# Process: Uptake, the Curve

## Taxonomy

[Uptake](process_uptake.md), the sequence a thing moves along and the places it passes.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: uptake

## Initiated by

Somebody has it and almost nobody else does.
The Curve starts wherever [an idea is being adopted](@chbrain/khai-engine-adoption/process_adopt_idea.md) -- taken up first by [innovators](@chbrain/khai-engine-adoption/position_innovator.md) out on [the outside](@chbrain/khai-engine-adoption/place_the_outside.md), then by [early adopters](@chbrain/khai-engine-adoption/position_early_adopter.md) who must carry it across [the chasm](@chbrain/khai-engine-adoption/place_chasm.md), then by the [early](@chbrain/khai-engine-adoption/position_early_majority.md) and [late majority](@chbrain/khai-engine-adoption/position_late_majority.md) past [the tipping point](@chbrain/khai-engine-adoption/place_the_tipping_point.md), and last by [laggards](@chbrain/khai-engine-adoption/position_laggard.md) holding [the old ground](@chbrain/khai-engine-adoption/place_the_old_ground.md).
It is the face of the composite that is simply a thing getting more common.

## Direction

Toward more people having it than had it before.
The Curve reads position as relative rather than fixed: a persona is an innovator for one thing and a laggard for another, so nobody in the play is an early-adopter type -- they are early to **this**, which is a fact about the pairing and not about them.
It moves the thing outward through the population, and the population is never the same shape twice.

## Lever

The thing's own merits against how much of the travelling they can account for.
The carrying force is that the merits do real work: something better, cheaper, or easier is genuinely more likely to travel, each adopter has an ordinary reason, and most of the curve can be told as a story about the thing improving its case; the resistance is that the merits are constant while the curve is not, so they cannot explain why the same thing sits still for years and then moves, why some things never cross the chasm, or why anything ever goes backwards -- and whatever supplies those has to be looked for in the population rather than in the thing.
The Curve spends nothing, and leaves the interesting variance unexplained.

## Echo

A thing further along than it was, and a shape the merits did not draw.
It is the bridge that lays out the track and states plainly what running along it does not account for.
What it hands upward is the set of people who currently have it -- which is not a byproduct of the travelling but the composite's next subject.
The Curve is dated from the first adopter, and it is read from wherever the thing has got to.
