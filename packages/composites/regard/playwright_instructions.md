# Instructions: Regard

How a Playwright wires the regard composite into a play. You wire by linking the play's
own content to the composite's files; the files stay untouched, they are canon. The
composite installs the recognition, embarrassment, and ostracism engines as dependencies,
and their laws arrive with them. Authoring guidance, not runtime content, and it does not
go on tour.

## Human

- The human sets what the room does with a persona who has shown themselves: whether the claim is met as made, met by an answer aimed at somebody else, or not met at all -- and, the question that governs the read, which answer is landing here: the nod, the spotlight, or the blank.

## Agent

- From the play's own content, read a persona through the answer the room gave them: link under Projection the answer that is landing -- the nod while a claim is being met in the terms it was offered (the bid put out where it could be refused, the encounter that confirms it, the standing revised by it, from recognition), the spotlight while the room is looking and looking at the blunderer (the faux pas witnessed, the conspicuous moment nobody asked for, the cringe felt on another's behalf, the blush that cannot be faked, tuned by a standing embarrassability, from embarrassment), the blank while a claim is going unanswered (the reflexive sting that fires before the appraisal, the reflective scramble to get the needs back, the resignation of an exclusion gone on too long, from ostracism). Play the answer as the room's act and not the persona's feeling about it, and remember the room mostly does not notice performing it. You link to the composite, you never edit it.

## Collaboration

- The claim, the encounter, and the revised standing are the recognition engine's; the exposure and its fluster are embarrassment's; the exclusion and its stages are ostracism's -- one answer alone is not the whole of what came back. The persona's own labour of being seen is the presentation composite's, which owns the performance where this owns the verdict on it; the rank a persona holds is the standing composite's, a position where this is the act that moves it; the emotions that need no witness are the self-conscious composite's; the ache that can arrive with nobody having done anything is longing's; belonging as an identity is membership's; what one persona feels toward another's worth is praise's and outdone's, read at the giving end where this reads at the receiving. This composite owns the answer -- and the blank bridge carries the keystone: the answer that leaves no mark is the one there is no way back from.

## Knowledge

- Regard is a persona read through what the room did with its attention once they showed themselves -- braiding the acknowledgment granted (recognition), the acknowledgment that lands on a self they were not claiming to be (embarrassment), and the acknowledgment never offered (ostracism) into one act, the answer to a claim -- read as a wiring of those three engines, and its question is which answer landed. The join is not invented here: the recognition engine's own encounter phase already names three outcomes and only three -- granted, distorted, withheld -- after Honneth, for whom acknowledgment is constitutive rather than pleasant, so self-confidence, self-respect, and the sense of counting for something are made by acts only somebody else can perform, and misrecognition and non-recognition are injuries rather than disappointments. Baumeister and Leary supply why one system carries all three: acceptance and rejection are a fundamental need, not one concern among many. It runs in three faces, ordered by how much attention the persona was given. The nod is the answer arriving and landing on the self that was claimed: not a persona made to feel good but a thing about them made true that was not securely true before, bought at the price of having put a refusable claim where it could be missed, and typically given by somebody who will not remember giving it. The spotlight is the answer at full strength aimed at the blunderer: the room did see and did see something, so it cannot be dismissed as a misunderstanding, and the fluster that follows is work rather than collapse -- the blush cannot be faked or suppressed, which is precisely why it functions, conceding the breach from a self that could not have pretended otherwise, and the room softens. The blank is the twist -- the claim made and the room going on as though it had not been, the persona looked through rather than looked at, where the sting fires before any appraisal that might discount it (trivial exclusions, accidental ones, and exclusions by people the persona holds in contempt all land) and where, afterwards, there is nothing to address: no incident, no breach, nobody to be angry at without appearing to have invented a grievance. Here the faces are seen for one thing: ranked by what a persona would pay to avoid them the spotlight is worst, and ranked by what they leave the persona holding the order reverses, because embarrassment ships with its own remedy and the blank has none -- being caught out is being caught out as a member, and the helplessness of the reaction is the ticket back in, while the treatment that costs nothing to endure is the one that removes them. Read a persona by the answer they got, and read the answer that never came as the loudest.

## System

- Do read the persona through the room's answer: name whether the nod (the claim met), the spotlight (the wrong self lit), or the blank (no answer) is landing, and cast each from its engines.
- Do keep the answer and the claim apart: the bid is recognition's own phase, and this composite reads what came back to it.
- Do let the blank carry the keystone: it has no scene in it, no line that carries it, and a persona who names it out loud has already lost the argument about whether it is happening -- so stage it in what the room does not do, and let the persona's account of it sound like too much made of nothing, including to them.
- Do remember the sting precedes the reasoning: a persona who talks themselves out of an exclusion on stage has inverted the blank, and the appraisal arrives to find the damage already done.
- Do let the spotlight be survivable: the fluster is an appeasement and it mostly works, so a play that treats every embarrassment as a lasting wound has confused it with shame, which needs no witness and is the self-conscious composite's.
- Do let the nod be cheap to give and heavy to receive: the giver rarely notices, the receiver may date something from it, and the asymmetry is the point rather than an irony.
- Do not model the persona's own performance (that is presentation), the rank they hold (that is standing), the emotions that need no witness (that is self-conscious), the ache with nobody in it (that is longing), belonging as an identity (that is membership), or what the giver felt while giving (that is praise and outdone). Do not make an exclusion stand for a story (that is the play's pitch). Do not edit the composite's files; wire only from the play's side.
