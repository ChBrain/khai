# Process: Regard

## Taxonomy

What the room does with its attention once a persona has shown themselves.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: regard

## Initiated by

A persona has made a claim on somebody else's attention -- by an act, a bid, a bare presence in a room -- and the answer is now the room's to give.
Regard starts wherever that answer is landing: granted as made, through [recognition](@chbrain/khai-engine-recognition/process_recognition.md); granted to a self the persona was not claiming to be, through [embarrassment](@chbrain/khai-engine-embarrassment/process_embarrassment.md); or not given at all, through [ostracism](@chbrain/khai-engine-ostracism/process_ostracism.md).
This composite adds no fourth engine. It takes the three outcomes the [encounter](@chbrain/khai-engine-recognition/process_encounter.md) already names -- confirmed, distorted, withheld -- and reads what each becomes when it stops being a category and happens to somebody.

## Direction

Toward the whole of what came back, ordered by how much attention the persona was given.
The composite wires the three engines and reads their working in three shapes: [the Nod](process_regard_nod.md), attention given and answering; [the Spotlight](process_regard_spotlight.md), attention given at full strength and answering the wrong self; and [the Blank](process_regard_blank.md), the twist -- no attention given, and nothing that can be said to have happened.
The three are not three moods in a room. They are one act, the room's answer to a claim, differing only in where the attention went.

## Lever

The fact that none of it can be issued by the one who needs it.
The carrying force is that the currency is real and it is the only currency there is for this: a persona's confidence in who they are is revised by acts that only somebody else can perform, so the claim has to be put out where it can be refused, and the persona who never claims is never confirmed either; the resistance is that putting it out is the whole of the exposure -- the same gesture that can be met can be missed, and the persona cannot tell in advance which they will get, nor afterwards make the room have meant something else.
Regard is read wherever a persona has been answered by others, and the reading is of the answer rather than of the persona who wanted it.

## Echo

The persona as the room has just left them.
Regard does not close; it is the answer landing, gathering the three engines into one reading -- a nod, a spotlight, a blank.
It hands off what it is not: the persona's own labour of being seen -- the image held up, the front and back regions, the tuning to the room, the feeling managed to sustain it -- is the presentation composite's, which owns the performance where this owns the verdict on it; the rank a persona holds in the order is the standing composite's, a position where this is the act that moves it; the self-evaluative emotions that need no witness are the self-conscious composite's, guilt and shame running with the room empty; the ache of a gap in company, which can arrive with nobody having done anything, is the longing composite's; belonging to a group as an identity is the membership composite's; what one persona feels toward another's worth -- the admiration, the gratitude, the envy -- is praise's and outdone's, read at the giving end where this reads at the receiving; and an exclusion made to **stand for** a story is authored key, the play's pitch, not the force.
The persona is dated from the answer, and read as a self whose standing was just adjusted by somebody who may not remember doing it.
