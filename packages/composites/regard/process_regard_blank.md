# Process: Regard, the Blank

## Taxonomy

[Regard](process_regard.md), the answer that never comes, and where the three are seen for one thing.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: regard

## Initiated by

The claim was made and the room went on as though it had not been.
The Blank starts wherever [ostracism](@chbrain/khai-engine-ostracism/process_ostracism.md) is running -- the [reflexive](@chbrain/khai-engine-ostracism/process_reflexive.md) sting that fires before any judgement about whether it should, the [reflective](@chbrain/khai-engine-ostracism/process_reflective.md) work of trying to get the needs back, and the [resignation](@chbrain/khai-engine-ostracism/process_resignation.md) of an exclusion that went on too long to keep answering.
It is the encounter's withheld answer: the persona looked through rather than looked at.

## Direction

Toward a persona who has been removed without anything having been done to them.
The Blank reads the non-event -- the greeting not returned, the contribution the meeting moved past, the name left off, the conversation that closes as they approach and reopens after -- and reads the peculiar difficulty of it, which is that every instance is deniable and the sum is not.
It moves the persona out of the room while they are still standing in it.

## Lever

The exactness of an unearned wound against the total absence of anything to address.
The carrying force is that the alarm does not wait for the appraisal: the sting fires when the exclusion is trivial, when it is plainly accidental, when the excluders are people the persona holds in contempt -- so the reasoning that would discount it arrives to find the damage already done, which is the clearest evidence in the composite that this currency is not optional and not under the persona's control; the resistance is that nothing happened. Nobody said anything. There is no incident, no breach, nobody to be angry at without seeming to have invented a grievance, and the persona's own account of it sounds, even to them, like too much made of nothing.
The Blank rides the composite's keystone knife-edge: the answer that leaves no mark is the one there is no way back from.

## Echo

Removed, and with no incident to be sorry for.
It shows the three answers complete -- one that confirms the self claimed, one that answers the wrong self and hands back a way in, one that answers nothing -- and carries beneath it the composite's keystone: the [spotlight](process_regard_spotlight.md) is a form of membership and the Blank is the exclusion, so the treatment a persona would trade almost anything to avoid is the one that keeps them, and the treatment that costs them nothing to endure is the one that does not.
This is why it is the hardest of the three to stage and the most worth staging: it has no scene in it, no line of dialogue that carries it, and a persona who names it out loud has already lost the argument about whether it is happening.
The Blank is the note the whole composite closes on: what a room did to a persona is legible in what it did not do, and nowhere else without a discount.
The Blank is dated from nothing -- which is the injury.
