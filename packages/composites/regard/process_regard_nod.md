# Process: Regard, the Nod

## Taxonomy

[Regard](process_regard.md), the answer the persona hoped for, and the one they cannot give themselves.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: regard

## Initiated by

A claim was made and it was met as made.
The Nod starts wherever [recognition](@chbrain/khai-engine-recognition/process_recognition.md) is completing -- the [claim](@chbrain/khai-engine-recognition/process_claim.md) put out where it could have been refused, the [encounter](@chbrain/khai-engine-recognition/process_encounter.md) answering it in the terms it was offered, and the [standing](@chbrain/khai-engine-recognition/process_standing.md) revised by the answer.
It is the face of the composite where the attention arrives and lands on the self that was being claimed.

## Direction

Toward a self-relation that has been altered from outside.
The Nod reads the answer at full strength and correctly aimed -- the credit given by name, the greeting that says you are expected here, the judgement that treats the persona as the sort of person they had been claiming to be -- and reads what it does, which is not to make the persona feel good but to make a thing about them true that was not securely true before.
It moves the persona into a standing they can act from, and could not have talked themselves into.

## Lever

The exactness of a currency that must be minted elsewhere against the cost of asking for it.
The carrying force is that self-confidence, self-respect, and the sense of counting for something are not self-issued: they are constituted by acts of acknowledgment, which is why the nod is worth the exposure of claiming and why the persona who is never answered does not merely feel unappreciated but is actually short of the ground autonomous action is taken from; the resistance is that the claim has to be legible and refusable before it can be met, so the persona must first make themselves available to be missed.
The Nod spends the safety of never having asked.

## Echo

The claim answered, and the persona standing on something the room put under them.
It shows what the other two bridges are deviations from: attention that arrived, and arrived at the right self.
Its quiet condition is that the giver often does not notice giving it -- the nod costs the room nothing and is not remembered by it, while the persona who received it may date something from that afternoon.
The Nod is dated from the moment the answer came back in the terms it was asked in.
