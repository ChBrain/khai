# Process: Regard, the Spotlight

## Taxonomy

[Regard](process_regard.md), the answer that arrives at full strength and lands on the wrong self.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: regard

## Initiated by

The room is looking, and what it is looking at is not the person the persona was claiming to be.
The Spotlight starts wherever [embarrassment](@chbrain/khai-engine-embarrassment/process_embarrassment.md) is running -- the [faux pas](@chbrain/khai-engine-embarrassment/process_faux_pas.md) witnessed, the [conspicuous](@chbrain/khai-engine-embarrassment/process_conspicuous.md) moment of being the centre of an attention nobody asked for, the [cringe](@chbrain/khai-engine-embarrassment/process_cringe.md) felt on somebody else's behalf -- and it is tuned by a standing [embarrassability](@chbrain/khai-engine-embarrassment/position_embarrassability.md) that decides how hard the same light falls.
It is the encounter's distorted answer: acknowledgment given, and given to the blunderer.

## Direction

Toward a self the persona is stuck being in front of everyone who saw.
The Spotlight reads the gap between the claim and the answer -- the name got wrong in front of the person it belonged to, the confident assertion contradicted by someone who would know, the trip on a flat floor -- and reads the fluster that follows as the doing something rather than the falling apart it looks like.
It moves the persona nowhere they chose, and into an urgent, involuntary piece of work.

## Lever

The unwantedness of the exposure against the fact that the exposure is what is being answered with.
The carrying force is that the light is real and correctly pointed -- the room did see, and it did see something -- so the persona cannot dismiss it as a misunderstanding and cannot outrun it while the witnesses are present; the resistance is that the persona is not, in fact, mainly the blunderer, and the whole of the fluster is the attempt to say so with a face rather than an argument.
The Spotlight spends composure and buys back the standing the breach cost.

## Echo

The wrong self shown, and conceded, and largely forgiven.
Its hidden mechanism is the one the persona would never choose: the [blush](@chbrain/khai-engine-embarrassment/process_blush.md) cannot be faked or suppressed, which is exactly why it works -- an appeasement display that proves the breach was registered by somebody who could not have pretended otherwise, and the room softens.
So this bridge carries a remedy inside it. The persona who is caught out is caught out **as a member**: there is an incident, it has a shape, it can be apologised for, laughed at, and outlived, and the very helplessness of the reaction is the ticket back in.
The Spotlight is dated from the moment of being seen, and from the odd fact that the persona was returned to the room by the thing they most wanted to hide.
