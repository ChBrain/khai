# Regard: Reference

## Line of Work

Regard as **composite**: what the room does with its attention once a persona has shown themselves.
It is not a fourth mechanism but a wiring of three: the **recognition** engine supplies the
acknowledgment granted, the **embarrassment** engine supplies the acknowledgment that lands on a
self the persona was not claiming to be, and the **ostracism** engine supplies the acknowledgment
never offered. This composite adds no fourth engine; it reads the seam the three make -- one act,
the room's answer to a claim, differing only in where the attention went -- and stages that seam
into three bridges, closing on the discovery that the answer which leaves no mark is the one there
is no way back from.

The domain does not model any single answer -- the claim, the encounter, and the revised standing
are the recognition engine's; the faux pas, the conspicuous moment, the cringe, and the blush are
embarrassment's; the reflexive, reflective, and resignation stages are ostracism's; and one alone
is a process, not the whole of what came back. Nor is it the persona's own labour of being seen,
which is the presentation composite's; nor the rank a persona holds, which is the standing
composite's; nor the self-evaluative emotions that need no witness, which are the self-conscious
composite's. It owns the answer: what the room did with a claim that was put in front of it, and
the three things that can happen to a persona who made one.

The spine is Honneth, and the join is not asserted here -- it is already declared inside one of the
atoms. The recognition engine's own **encounter** phase names three outcomes and only three: the
acknowledgment is granted, it is withheld, or something other than what was claimed is
acknowledged. That is Honneth's structure, where recognition is constitutive rather than pleasant
-- self-confidence, self-respect, and self-esteem are made by acts only somebody else can perform
-- and where misrecognition and non-recognition are therefore injuries rather than
disappointments, each with its own shape. What this composite does is take those three outcomes,
which sit in the recognition engine as categories of a response, and read what each one becomes
when it stops being a category and happens to a person: confirmed becomes the nod, distorted
becomes the blunder witnessed, withheld becomes being looked through. Honneth wrote the withheld
case out separately in exactly those terms, taking Ellison's invisible man to argue that being
seen and not acknowledged is a distinct injury and not a milder version of being judged badly --
which is this composite's keystone arriving from the philosophical side. Baumeister and Leary
supply the motivational flank: acceptance and rejection are not one concern among many but a
fundamental need, which is why a family of emotions that look unrelated turn out to be one
system's outputs.

What the composite adds on top of that join is the ordering by attention given -- full and
answering, full and misaimed, absent -- and the inversion that follows from it. Ranked by how much
the persona would pay to avoid them, the spotlight is worst and the blank is mildest, since in the
blank nothing at all has happened. Ranked by what they leave the persona holding, the order
reverses: embarrassment ships with its own remedy, because the display cannot be faked or
suppressed and therefore reads as an honest concession, and the room forgives a member who erred.
The blank has no remedy in it, because it has no incident in it. Williams's finding on the
reflexive stage is what makes this more than a figure of speech -- the pain fires before the
appraisal, from despised outgroups and from parties the persona has been told are not people at
all -- so the persona is injured first and left afterwards with nothing that can be addressed,
apologised for, or outlived. The three bridges are those three answers: the nod, the spotlight,
the blank.

## Origin

Recognition theory, the social psychology of self-conscious exposure, and the ostracism
literature, wired as a composite over the recognition, embarrassment, and ostracism engines. The
first two rows are the **joining** -- the published work that sets these as one act with three
outcomes -- and the rest anchor the individual bridges.

## Restrictions

What the composite refuses to model, and to whom it delegates.

- **The single answers (the atoms)**: The composite wires the three but owns none. The claim, the
  encounter, and the revised standing are the recognition engine's; the faux pas, the conspicuous
  moment, the cringe, the blush, and the embarrassability trait are embarrassment's; the reflexive,
  reflective, and resignation stages are ostracism's. Regard reads only the act the three share --
  a room answering a claim -- and one answer alone is a process, not the whole of what came back.
- **The persona's own performance (presentation)**: The image held up in interaction, the front and
  back regions, the tuning to the room, and the feeling managed to sustain it are the presentation
  composite's. That owns what a persona **does to be taken as they mean to be**; this owns **what
  came back**. A persona can perform impeccably and be blanked, and the two composites meet exactly
  there.
- **The rank held (standing)**: Capital, hierarchy, power, status, and the status move are the
  standing composite's -- a position, where this is the act that revises it. Regard is what happens
  in the encounter; standing is where the persona sits once a great many encounters have happened.
- **The emotions that need no witness (self-conscious)**: Guilt, pride, and shame are the
  self-conscious composite's, and they run with the room empty -- shame is available to a persona
  alone in the dark. Embarrassment sits here rather than there because it requires somebody to have
  seen; take the witness away and there is no spotlight, only a private wince.
- **The ache with nobody in it (longing)**: Loneliness, regret, and nostalgia are the longing
  composite's. Loneliness is a felt gap that can arrive with no one having done anything; the blank
  is an act performed by people who are present. The two are frequently confused by the persona
  experiencing them, and the composite does not follow them into that confusion.
- **Belonging as an identity (membership)**: Conformity, loyalty, social identity, and stigma are
  the membership composite's -- being of a group. Regard is a single answer at a single moment,
  which membership is the standing consequence of.
- **The giving end (praise, outdone)**: Admiration, elevation, gratitude, and envy are praise's and
  outdone's -- what one persona feels toward another's worth. Regard reads the receiving end, and
  is indifferent to whether the room felt anything while answering; a nod given absently is still a
  nod.
- **The self's relation to itself (selfhood, self-relation)**: Self-esteem, self-compassion,
  authenticity, and the self's own discrepancies are those composites'. Regard is the outside act
  that revises them, and it insists on the direction: the composite's whole claim is that this
  currency is minted elsewhere.
- **The exclusion as authored symbol (the play's pitch)**: A blanking made to **stand for** a theme
  -- the character written out to mirror a motif of erasure -- is refused: that is authored key, the
  play's pitch, not the force on the persona. Regard sets what the room did; the meaning read into
  it is the play's.

## Encoding

Source to constraint. A composite root over three engine atoms it declares as dependencies, with
three bridges, ordered by how much attention the persona was given.

- **[regard](process_regard.md)** (the root): what the room does with its attention once a persona
  has shown themselves -- the answer, never the claim, and never issuable by the one who needs it.
  Anchored by Honneth (1995) and Baumeister & Leary (1995).
- **[the nod](process_regard_nod.md)** (bridge): the claim answered as made, and a self-relation
  altered by an act only somebody else could perform. Anchored by Honneth (1995) on the constitutive
  spheres and Taylor (1994) on authentic identity acknowledged.
- **[the spotlight](process_regard_spotlight.md)** (bridge): the claim answered at full strength and
  aimed at the blunderer, with the fluster read as work rather than collapse. Anchored by Miller
  (1996) on unwanted exposure and Keltner & Buswell (1997) on the appeasement display -- which is
  why this bridge carries a remedy the next one does not.
- **[the blank](process_regard_blank.md)** (bridge): no answer at all, the persona looked through
  rather than looked at, injured before any appraisal can discount it and left with no incident to
  address. Anchored by Williams (2001) on the reflexive stage and Honneth (2001) with Ellison (1952)
  on invisibility; the composite's twist and keystone, that the answer which leaves no mark is the
  one there is no way back from.
