# Process: Dwelling, the Rooted

## Taxonomy

[Dwelling](process_dwelling.md), the hold where a place is part of who a person is, and cannot be lost without loss.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: dwelling

## Initiated by

The persona is not merely in a place but of it, rooted in ground that has become part of who it is.
The Rooted starts wherever a place enters a self -- the [place-attachment](@chbrain/khai-engine-place-attachment/position_place_attachment.md) of a person [rooted](@chbrain/khai-engine-place-attachment/position_rooted.md) in ground they are from, the [dependence](@chbrain/khai-engine-place-attachment/position_dependence.md) on a place that supports the life they live, and the [homesickness](@chbrain/khai-engine-place-attachment/position_homesick.md) and [placelessness](@chbrain/khai-engine-place-attachment/position_placeless.md) that open when that ground is lost or was never had.
It is the deepest hold: not the place one belongs at nor the one that restores, but the ground that is part of the self, whose loss is a loss of self.

## Direction

Toward the person rooted in ground that has become part of them, and exposed by that rooting.
The Rooted reads the persona bound to a place that has entered its identity -- the ground it is from, the place it depends on, the home it carries the shape of -- and what that binding means when the ground is threatened: the homesickness of being away, the placelessness of having no ground to be from.
It moves the persona to the depth of dwelling: not held by a place it could exchange but rooted in one it is part of, and woundable exactly there.

## Lever

The ground that roots a self against the loss that rooting makes possible.
The carrying force is that a place can become part of who a person is -- the self rooted in it, dependent on it, shaped to it; the resistance is that this very depth is an exposure: the rooted self can be uprooted, and the homesickness and placelessness that follow are the measure of how much the ground had given -- there is no being held so deeply without being woundable by the loss.
The Rooted rides the composite's keystone knife-edge: the place that roots a person is the place whose loss can unmake them, the belonging and the vulnerability one -- a self is held most by the ground it would grieve most to lose.

## Echo

The person rooted in ground it is part of, and exposed by the rooting.
It shows a persona of a place, not merely in it -- rooted, dependent, shaped to its ground -- and carries beneath it the composite's keystone: that the deepest hold a place has on a person is the one whose breaking wounds, so the homesickness of losing it is the very proof of what it gave.
The Rooted is the note the whole composite closes on: dwelling is not a comfort to be had without cost but a being-held that binds a self to its ground, and the ground that makes a person who they are is the ground they cannot lose and stay whole.
The Rooted is dated from the rooting, the moment a place became part of who the persona is.
