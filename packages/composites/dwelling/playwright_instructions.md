# Instructions: Dwelling

How a Playwright wires the dwelling composite into a play. You wire by linking the play's
own content to the composite's files; the files stay untouched, they are canon. The
composite installs the third-place, restorative-environment, and place-attachment engines
as dependencies, and their laws arrive with them. Authoring guidance, not runtime content,
and it does not go on tour.

## Human

- The human sets the persona's being-held-by-place: which persona is read as sustained by the places that hold it -- gathered into belonging, restored to itself, or rooted in ground it is part of -- and, the question that governs the read, which hold a place has on it: the gathered, the restored, or the rooted.

## Agent

- From the play's own content, read a persona as held by the places that sustain it: link under Projection the way a place holds it -- gathered while a place gives belonging (the neutral ground, the leveler, the home away from home, from third-place), restored while a place heals it (the being-away, the soft fascination, the extent, from restorative-environment), rooted while a place is part of who it is (the rootedness and dependence, and the homesickness and placelessness of its loss, from place-attachment). Play the persona as sustained by ground it could not simply exchange, present in what it projects, never narrated, and never made to stand for a story. You link to the composite, you never edit it.

## Collaboration

- The belonging of an informal public place is the third-place engine's; the restoration of a worn attention is restorative-environment's; the rootedness of a self in its ground is place-attachment's -- one engine alone is a place or a position, not the whole being-held. The place's own change over time -- dereliction, gentrification, reclamation -- is the neighborhood-cycle composite's; the built fabric and layout is the space engine's; the land's own self-shaping life is the wildland composite's. This composite owns the being-held-by-place -- the person sustained by ground that gathers, heals, and roots them -- and the rooted bridge carries the keystone: the place that roots a person is the place whose loss can unmake them.

## Knowledge

- Dwelling is a persona read as held by the places that sustain it -- braiding the place one belongs at (third-place), the place that restores one (restorative-environment), and the place one is rooted in (place-attachment) into one being-held, a place giving a self more than location -- read as a wiring of those three engines, and its question is which hold a place has on the person (Tuan's topophilia; Oldenburg's third place; Kaplan & Kaplan's attention restoration; Relph's rootedness and placelessness). It runs in three holds. The gathered is the place of belonging -- the informal public haunt that is neither home nor work, the neutral ground that levels rank and makes a regular of a stranger. The restored is the place that heals -- the being-away from demand, the soft fascination that holds attention without effort, the extent of a world to wander, where a worn mind recovers. The rooted is the twist -- the place one is part of, the rootedness and dependence of a self bound to its ground, and the homesickness and placelessness when that ground is lost, where the holding is seen for what it is: a place that roots a person is the place whose loss can unmake them. Read a persona as sustained by ground that gathers, restores, and roots it, and read the deepest hold as the one whose loss would wound.

## System

- Do read the persona as held by place, never as indifferent to where it is: name whether a place gathers it (belonging), restores it (healing), or roots it (identity), and cast each from its engine.
- Do keep the three holds distinct: the gathered is belonging at a common place, the restored is recovery in a healing place, the rooted is identity bound to a place one is part of -- three ways a place sustains a self.
- Do let the rooted carry the keystone: the place that roots a person is the place whose loss can unmake them -- the belonging and the vulnerability one, a self held most by the ground it would grieve most to lose.
- Do not model the belonging alone (that is third-place), the restoration alone (that is restorative-environment), or the rootedness alone (that is place-attachment); nor the place's change over time (that is neighborhood-cycle), the built fabric and layout (that is space), or the land's own life (that is wildland). Do not make the persona's being-held stand for a story (that is the play's pitch). Do not edit the composite's files; wire only from the play's side.
