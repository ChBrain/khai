# Process: Dwelling, the Restored

## Taxonomy

[Dwelling](process_dwelling.md), the hold where a place heals a worn attention.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: dwelling

## Initiated by

The persona is worn down by demand and comes to a place that restores it without asking anything back.
The Restored starts wherever a place heals a tired mind -- the [restorative environment](@chbrain/khai-engine-restorative-environment/place_restorative_environment.md) that gives a sense of [being away](@chbrain/khai-engine-restorative-environment/place_being_away.md) from the press of tasks, the soft [fascination](@chbrain/khai-engine-restorative-environment/place_fascination.md) that holds attention without effort, and the [extent](@chbrain/khai-engine-restorative-environment/place_extent.md) of a world whole enough to wander in.
It is the place of restoration: not the ground one belongs at nor is rooted in, but the one that lets a spent attention recover.

## Direction

Toward the worn attention healed by a place that asks nothing of it.
The Restored reads the persona set down in a place that restores -- the shore, the wood, the long view -- where directed effort can rest because the place holds the mind softly, of its own accord, and offers extent enough to lose the day's press in.
It moves the persona from depleted to recovered: the fatigue of held attention eased by a ground that gives being-away, fascination, and room, restoring the capacity the demands had spent.

## Lever

The place that restores against the depletion of a life with no away.
The carrying force is that some ground heals directed attention by not demanding it -- the soft fascination that draws the mind without effort, the being-away that lifts the press, the extent that gives room; the resistance is the life with no such place, the attention spent and never restored, the self worn thin for want of a ground to recover in.
The Restored is the hold at its most healing: the place that asks nothing and gives the mind back to itself, the ground where a worn attention recovers.

## Echo

The person restored by a place that heals without asking.
The Restored leaves the persona recovered on ground that gave it being-away, fascination, and room -- the mind returned to itself -- and carries the dwelling's shared shape: a place giving a self more than location, here the restoration of a worn attention.
It is the hold that restores: the environment that heals by demanding nothing, the ground that gives a spent mind back its capacity.
The Restored is dated from the recovery, the moment a place began to heal the attention the persona's demands had worn.
