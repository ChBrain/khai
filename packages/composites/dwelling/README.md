# Dwelling

Dwelling as composite: the person held by place -- the place they belong at (third-place), the place that restores them (restorative-environment), and the place they are rooted in (place-attachment) -- across the gathered, the restored, and the rooted, and the drama is that the place that holds a person is the place whose loss can unmake them.
