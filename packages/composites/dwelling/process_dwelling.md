# Process: Dwelling

## Taxonomy

The person held by place.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: dwelling

## Initiated by

A persona is read not as moving through neutral space but as held by the places that sustain it -- gathered, restored, and rooted by ground that is not interchangeable.
Dwelling starts wherever a place gives a self more than location: the place one belongs at ([third-place](@chbrain/khai-engine-third-place/place_third_place.md)), the place that restores one ([restorative-environment](@chbrain/khai-engine-restorative-environment/place_restorative_environment.md)), and the place one is rooted in ([place-attachment](@chbrain/khai-engine-place-attachment/position_place_attachment.md)).
This composite adds no new force; it reads what the three make together: a person held by place, sustained by ground that gathers, heals, and roots them.

## Direction

Toward the whole of being held by place, read through its holds.
The composite wires the three engines and reads their working in three shapes: [the Gathered](process_dwelling_gathered.md), the place one belongs at; [the Restored](process_dwelling_restored.md), the place that heals; and [the Rooted](process_dwelling_rooted.md), the twist -- the place one is part of, and cannot lose without loss.
A person is not held by place in one way only: some ground gathers them into belonging, some restores their worn attention, and some roots their very sense of who they are -- and the deepest hold is the one whose breaking wounds.

## Lever

The places that sustain a self against the habit of reading all ground as interchangeable.
The carrying force is that places are not neutral to a person -- one gathers them, one restores them, one is part of who they are, and a self deprived of them is diminished; the resistance is the reflex to treat space as fungible, one location as good as another, the ground beneath a life as mere backdrop.
Dwelling is read wherever a persona is granted its bond to sustaining ground: not a self indifferent to where it is but one held, gathered, restored, and rooted by places it could not simply exchange.

## Echo

The persona revealed as a self sustained by the places that hold it.
Dwelling does not close; it is the persona going on being held by place, gathering the three engines into one reading -- a self gathered into belonging, restored to itself, and rooted in ground it is part of.
It hands off what it is not: the place's own change over time -- its dereliction, gentrification, and reclamation -- is the neighborhood-cycle composite's; the built fabric, layout, and affordances of a made setting are the space engine's; the land's own self-shaping life as an agent is the wildland composite's; and the place made to stand for a story is authored key, the play's pitch, not the force.
The persona is dated from its own being-held, and read as a self sustained by ground that gathers, restores, and roots it.
