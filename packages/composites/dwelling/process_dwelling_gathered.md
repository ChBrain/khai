# Process: Dwelling, the Gathered

## Taxonomy

[Dwelling](process_dwelling.md), the hold where a place gives a person belonging.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: dwelling

## Initiated by

The persona is gathered into a place that is neither home nor work, and made a regular where it was a stranger.
The Gathered starts wherever a persona belongs at an informal public place -- the [third place](@chbrain/khai-engine-third-place/place_third_place.md), the [neutral ground](@chbrain/khai-engine-third-place/place_neutral_ground.md) that asks nothing and admits all, the [leveler](@chbrain/khai-engine-third-place/place_leveler.md) that sets rank aside, the [home away from home](@chbrain/khai-engine-third-place/place_home_away.md) where the regulars know the face.
It is the place of belonging: not the ground one is rooted in nor the one that restores, but the third place that gathers a person into easy company.

## Direction

Toward the person made a regular, gathered into the life of a place.
The Gathered reads the persona belonging at a place held in common -- the cafe, the pub, the stoop, the counter -- where presence is enough, rank is left at the door, and the same faces make a stranger, over time, one of the regulars.
It moves the persona from alone to among: gathered by a ground that levels and admits, given the belonging of a place where it is known and expected.

## Lever

The place that gathers against the isolation of ground that belongs to no one.
The carrying force is that an informal common place makes belonging cheap and open -- no invitation needed, rank set aside, the regular welcomed by being present; the resistance is the loss of such places, the ground that gathers no one, the life with a home and a work and no third place between them where a self is simply among others.
The Gathered is the hold at its most convivial: the place that asks nothing and gives belonging, the ground that makes a person a regular.

## Echo

The person gathered into the easy belonging of a common place.
The Gathered leaves the persona among others on neutral ground -- known, expected, one of the regulars -- and carries the dwelling's shared shape: a place giving a self more than location, here the belonging of easy company.
It is the hold that gathers: the third place that levels and admits, the ground where a person belongs by being present.
The Gathered is dated from the belonging, the moment a place gathered the persona into the life it holds in common.
