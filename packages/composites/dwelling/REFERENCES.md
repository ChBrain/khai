# Dwelling: Reference

## Line of Work

Dwelling as **composite**: the person held by place -- gathered, restored, and rooted by
ground that is not interchangeable. It is not a new force but a wiring of three: the
**third-place** engine supplies the **gathered** -- the informal public place that gives
belonging; the **restorative-environment** engine supplies the **restored** -- the place
that heals a worn attention; and the **place-attachment** engine supplies the **rooted** --
the ground that becomes part of who a person is. This composite adds no fourth engine; it
reads what the three share -- a place giving a self more than location -- and stages that
into three ways a place holds a person, closing on the discovery that the deepest hold is
the one whose loss wounds.

The domain does not model any single force -- the belonging is the third-place engine's,
the restoration is restorative-environment's, the rootedness is place-attachment's, and one
alone is a place or a position, not the whole being-held. Nor is it the place's own change
over time -- its dereliction, gentrification, and reclamation -- which is the
neighborhood-cycle composite's; nor the built fabric, layout, and affordances of a made
setting, which is the space engine's; nor the land's own self-shaping life as an agent,
which is the wildland composite's. It owns the being-held-by-place: the person sustained by
ground that gathers, heals, and roots them, read not as a self indifferent to where it is
but as one that could not simply exchange the places that hold it.

The spine is the humanistic geography of place as a resource for the self, ground that is
lived rather than merely occupied. Tuan named the affective bond -- topophilia, the tie
between a person and a place -- and set space against place, the one abstract and
interchangeable, the other charged with meaning and care. Relph read rootedness and its
loss, the authentic sense of place against the placelessness of a world of interchangeable
nowheres. Oldenburg found the third place, the informal public ground -- neither home nor
work -- where a community gathers and a stranger becomes a regular. And Kaplan and Kaplan
read the restorative environment, the place that gives attention back to itself through
being-away, fascination, and extent. The three bridges are those readings staged: the
gathered, the restored, and the rooted.

## Origin

The humanistic geography and environmental psychology of place as a resource for the self,
wired as a composite over the third-place, restorative-environment, and place-attachment
engines.

## Restrictions

What the composite refuses to model, and to whom it delegates.

- **The single engines (the atoms)**: The composite wires the three but owns none. The
  informal public place and its belonging -- neutral ground, leveler, home away from home --
  is the third-place engine's; the restorative place and its components -- being away,
  fascination, extent -- is restorative-environment's; the bond of rootedness and its loss --
  rooted, dependence, homesick, placeless -- is place-attachment's. Dwelling reads only what
  the three make as one being-held; a single engine alone is a place or a position, not the
  whole.
- **The place's change over time (neighborhood-cycle)**: The transformation of a place --
  its dereliction, gentrification, and reclamation -- is the neighborhood-cycle composite's.
  Dwelling is the person's bond to a place, not the place's own turning; the ground may change
  around a self, but dwelling reads how it holds the self, not how it is remade.
- **The built fabric (space)**: The layout, the walls, the affordances, the standing mood of a
  made setting are the space engine's. Dwelling is the felt bond to sustaining ground, not the
  physical arrangement of it; space builds the place, dwelling is the self it holds.
- **The land as agent (wildland)**: The land's own self-shaping life -- making, wearing, water,
  burn, reclaiming -- is the wildland composite's. Dwelling is the person held by place, not
  the place living its own life indifferent to them.
- **The place as authored symbol (the play's pitch)**: A persona's being-held made to **stand
  for** a story -- the home that mirrors a theme of belonging, the placelessness that mirrors a
  lostness -- is refused: that is authored key, the play's pitch, not the force on the persona.
  Dwelling delivers the person's own bond to place; the meaning read into it is the play's.

## Encoding

Source to constraint. A composite root over three engine atoms it declares as dependencies,
with three bridges, the ways a place holds a person.

- **[dwelling](process_dwelling.md)** (the root): the person held by place -- gathered,
  restored, and rooted by ground that is not interchangeable. Anchored by Tuan (topophilia,
  place against space).
- **[the gathered](process_dwelling_gathered.md)** (bridge): the place of belonging -- the
  informal public ground that levels rank and makes a regular of a stranger. Anchored by
  Oldenburg (the third place).
- **[the restored](process_dwelling_restored.md)** (bridge): the place that heals -- being
  away, soft fascination, and extent, where a worn attention recovers. Anchored by Kaplan &
  Kaplan (attention restoration).
- **[the rooted](process_dwelling_rooted.md)** (bridge): the place that is part of who a person
  is -- rootedness and dependence, and the homesickness and placelessness of its loss. Anchored
  by Relph; the composite's twist and keystone, that the place which roots a person is the place
  whose loss can unmake them.
