# Instructions: Blaze

How a Playwright wires the blaze composite into a play. You wire by linking the play's
own content to the composite's files; the files stay untouched, they are canon. The
composite installs the fire and combustion engines as dependencies, and their laws
arrive with them. Authoring guidance, not runtime content, and it does not go on tour.

## Human

- The human sets the fire's own life: which place carries a fire read as a living actor -- a burn that takes, runs, and ends into renewal -- and, the question that governs the read, what stage that life is in: the kindling, the run, or the renewal.

## Agent

- From the play's own content, read a place's fire as a living actor with an arc: link under Shown the stage its fire's life is in -- kindling while the fire takes (the place's ignition becoming a spotting ember, a pyrolysis, a self-heating, a holdover, or a backfire lit on purpose), run while it spreads at full cry (the conflagration or creep becoming a crown, grass, flashover, backdraft, firestorm, jet, or blowup), renewal while its ending begins again (the smoulder and renewal becoming a peat smoulder, a holdover that will wake, or the serotiny that opens the sealed cone). Braid the regime mode (from fire) with the named phenomenon (from combustion) at each stage. Play the fire as an actor present in what the place shows, never narrated, and never made to mirror a persona's own burning. You link to the composite, you never edit it.

## Collaboration

- The whole abstract burn regime is the fire engine's; the flat catalog of every named phenomenon is combustion's; the land's whole self-shaping life, of which fire is one thread, is the wildland composite's. This composite owns the life of a single fire -- the burn read as a living actor across its arc -- and the renewal bridge carries the fire's keystone: the burn that destroys is the burn that renews, the ending that is also a beginning.

## Knowledge

- Blaze is a fire read as a living actor across its whole life -- braiding the place's burn regime (fire, how a place burns and how its modes turn) with the named phenomenon it becomes (combustion, which specific fire it shows) -- read as a wiring of those two engines, and its question is what stage the fire's life is in (Pyne's fire as a living, cyclic process; Christensen's fire-adapted renewal). It runs in three stages. Kindling is how a fire takes -- the regime's ignition becoming a particular birth, the ember that lands and takes, the solid gasified into fuel, the pile that lights itself from within, the fire overwintered and re-emerging, or the fire lit on purpose. Run is the fire at full cry -- the regime's conflagration and creep becoming the named run, the canopy crowning, the front outrunning a person, the room igniting at once, the fire making its own wind, the pressurised torch. Renewal is the twist -- the fire's ending that begins again, the smoulder that persists unseen, the holdover that will wake, and the serotiny that opens the sealed cone onto the cleared and fertilised ground. Read a fire as an actor that is born, runs, and ends into a renewal the living cannot do without.

## System

- Do read the fire as an actor with an arc, never as a flat hazard: name whether its life is in kindling (the taking), run (the full spread), or renewal (the ending that begins again), and braid the regime mode with the named phenomenon at each stage.
- Do keep the two altitudes together: fire gives the regime mode (ignition, conflagration, smoulder, renewal), combustion gives the named face (the spotting, the crown, the peat, the serotiny) -- the composite is the two lived as one fire's life.
- Do let renewal carry the fire's keystone: the burn that destroys is the burn that renews, the same ending a persistence to fear (the holdover that wakes) and a beginning to depend on (the seed that needs the fire).
- Do not model the whole burn regime alone (that is fire), the flat catalog of phenomena (that is combustion), or the land's whole life of which fire is one thread (that is wildland). Do not make the fire's life mirror a persona's own consuming or ruin (that is the play's pitch). Do not edit the composite's files; wire only from the play's side.
