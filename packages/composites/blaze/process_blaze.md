# Process: Blaze

## Taxonomy

The life of a fire.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: blaze

## Initiated by

A fire is read not as a flat event but as an actor with a life of its own -- how it takes, how it runs, and how it ends.
Blaze starts wherever a fire is understood to have an arc, braiding the place's burn regime ([fire](@chbrain/khai-engine-fire/process_fire.md)) with the named phenomenon it becomes ([combustion](@chbrain/khai-engine-combustion/process_combustion.md)) -- the regime that says how a place burns, and the phenomenon that says which fire it shows.
This composite adds no new fire; it reads what the two make together: a single fire lived from spark to aftermath, a regime mode and a named face at every stage.

## Direction

Toward the whole life of a fire, read through its arc.
The composite wires the two fire engines and reads their working in three stages: [Kindling](process_blaze_kindling.md), how a fire takes; [Run](process_blaze_run.md), how it runs at full cry; and [Renewal](process_blaze_renewal.md), the twist -- the ending that begins again.
A fire does not live at one moment: it is born in a spark or an ember or a self-heating core, runs as a crown fire or a flashover or a firestorm, and ends by smouldering out, overwintering, or opening the sealed cone onto cleared ground -- each stage a different regime mode wearing a different named face.

## Lever

The fire's own life against the habit of reading a fire as a single flat thing.
The carrying force is that a fire is never one event -- it takes, it runs, and it ends, and each stage burns by a different logic, on a different fuel, with a different way of being fought; the resistance is the reflex to read a fire as one undifferentiated hazard, the flame that is only ever destruction from the moment it lights to the moment it dies.
Blaze is read wherever a fire is granted its arc: not the event that happens to a place but the actor that is born, lives, and ends, each stage named and behaving in its own way.

## Echo

The fire revealed as a living actor with a life.
Blaze does not close; it is the fire going on through its stages, gathering the regime and the phenomenon into one reading -- a fire that takes, runs, and ends into renewal.
It hands off what it is not: the place's whole abstract burn regime is [fire](@chbrain/khai-engine-fire/process_fire.md)'s; the flat catalog of every named phenomenon is [combustion](@chbrain/khai-engine-combustion/process_combustion.md)'s; the land's whole self-shaping life, of which fire is one thread, is wildland's; and the fire made to mirror a persona's own consuming or ruin is authored key, the play's pitch, not the force.
The fire is dated from its own working, and read as an actor with a life -- a birth, a run, and an end that is also a beginning.
