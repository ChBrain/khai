# Process: Blaze, Renewal

## Taxonomy

[Blaze](process_blaze.md), the bridge where a fire's ending begins again.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: blaze

## Initiated by

The running fire burns down to its tail, and the ending is not only a death but a beginning.
Renewal starts where a place's [smoulder](@chbrain/khai-engine-fire/process_smoulder.md) and [renewal](@chbrain/khai-engine-fire/process_renewal.md) become the fire's aftermath -- the [peat](@chbrain/khai-engine-combustion/process_peat.md) that smoulders on unseen below the surface, the [holdover](@chbrain/khai-engine-combustion/process_holdover.md) that will overwinter and wake again, and the [serotiny](@chbrain/khai-engine-combustion/process_serotiny.md) that opens the fire-sealed cone onto the cleared and fertilised ground.
It is the fire's end that is also its issue: the flame gone but the ground it leaves either still burning below, or opened for the life the fire's heat unlocks.

## Direction

Toward the burn made a beginning by its own ending.
Renewal reads the fire dying into what it leaves -- the smoulder creeping on in the duff, the deep fire holding through the winter, the sealed cone cracked open by the heat, the ash laid down as a seedbed, the fire-adapted seedbank waking on the cleared ground -- the setting recovering, and renewing, from the fire that ran across it.
It moves the place from burned to begun again: the black ground greening, the seed the fire released taking root in the bed the fire cleared.

## Lever

The life that needs the fire against the reading of fire as only destruction.
The carrying force is that the burn is not only loss -- the serotinous cone needs the fire's heat to open, the seedbed needs the fire's clearing, the fire-adapted system is one the fire begets; the resistance is the reflex to read every fire as ruin and every ending as death, the intuition that the burn only takes and never gives, and the exclusion that, withholding the fire, starves the very life the fire renews.
Renewal rides the fire's keystone knife-edge: the burn that destroys is the burn that renews, the same ending a persistence to fear and a beginning to depend on -- the holdover that will wake as a threat and the seed that will wake as a gift, born of one fire.

## Echo

The fire ended into renewal, and the ground begun again.
It shows a place after the fire -- the smoulder still working below, the cone opened, the ash bed green with what the fire released -- and carries beneath it the fire's keystone: that a fire, run to its end, does not simply die but hands the ground on, either still burning or newly cleared for life.
Renewal is the note the whole composite closes on: a fire is not an event that ends but an actor whose ending begins again, its death a smoulder that may wake and a renewal the living cannot do without.
Renewal is dated from the burning-out, the moment the flame died and the ground it left began either to hold the fire or to grow.
