# Process: Blaze, Run

## Taxonomy

[Blaze](process_blaze.md), the bridge where a fire runs at full cry.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: blaze

## Initiated by

The young fire takes its stride, the regime's spread becoming the named run it makes.
Run starts where a place's [conflagration](@chbrain/khai-engine-fire/process_conflagration.md) or [creep](@chbrain/khai-engine-fire/process_creep.md) becomes a specific spreading fire -- the [crown](@chbrain/khai-engine-combustion/process_crown.md) fire in the canopy, the [grass](@chbrain/khai-engine-combustion/process_grass.md) front outrunning a person, the [flashover](@chbrain/khai-engine-combustion/process_flashover.md) that lights a whole room at once, the [backdraft](@chbrain/khai-engine-combustion/process_backdraft.md) that bursts an air-starved one, the [firestorm](@chbrain/khai-engine-combustion/process_firestorm.md) that makes its own wind, the [jet](@chbrain/khai-engine-combustion/process_jet.md) that stabs under pressure, or the [blowup](@chbrain/khai-engine-combustion/process_blowup.md) that erupts up the slope.
It is the fire at full cry: no longer taking but spreading, the regime's mode wearing the named phenomenon's face.

## Direction

Toward the fire at its most itself, spreading by the phenomenon's own logic.
Run reads the fire in its stride -- the canopy running ahead of the surface, the fine-fuel front covering ground faster than a person, the room gone over at the radiant threshold, the fire drawing its own inflowing wind, the pressurised flame pointing hard at what it impinges -- each named phenomenon spreading by its own driver, wind or slope or fuel or ventilation.
It moves the place from a fire that has taken to a fire that runs: the front advancing, the intensity peaking, the phenomenon at the height of what it is.

## Lever

The driver that carries the fire against the suppression that cannot match it.
The carrying force is the fire's driver -- the wind and slope behind the crown fire, the ventilation feeding the flashover, the pressure driving the jet, the coupling that lets a firestorm make its own weather; the resistance is suppression outpaced or outmatched, the front moving faster than crews can cut line, the phenomenon whose own paradox turns the standard method against it.
Run is the fire at the point where the driver, not the fuel, decides whether it can be fought at all: the wind-driven crown and the plume-dominated firestorm run where suppression cannot follow.

## Echo

The fire at full spread, at the height of what it is.
Run leaves the setting in the fire's stride -- the crown running, the room flashed over, the firestorm turning, the front at its peak -- and hands the spent fire to the renewal that follows the burn.
It is the fire at its most dramatic and its most itself: the named phenomenon at full cry, spreading by its own logic until it runs out of fuel, or weather, or the ground to carry it.
Run is dated from the stride, the moment the fire stopped taking and began to spread as the phenomenon it is.
