# Blaze: Reference

## Line of Work

Blaze as **composite**: a fire read as a living actor across its whole life -- how it
takes, how it runs at full cry, and how it ends into persistence or renewal. It is not a
new force but a wiring of two: the **fire** engine supplies the place's burn **regime** --
how a place burns and how its modes turn, ignition to conflagration to smoulder to renewal
-- and the **combustion** engine supplies the named **phenomenon** -- which specific fire
the place shows, the crown fire, the flashover, the peat smoulder, the serotinous
regeneration. This composite adds no new fire; it reads what the two make together as a
single fire's arc, a regime mode and a named face at every stage, and stages that into
three: the taking, the running, and the ending that begins again.

The domain does not model either force alone -- the whole abstract regime is fire's, and
one mode alone is not a life; the flat catalog of every named phenomenon is combustion's,
and one type alone is a process, not the arc. Nor is it the land's whole self-shaping
life, of which fire is only one thread -- that is the wildland composite. It owns the life
of a single fire: the burn granted an arc, read not as a flat event that happens to a
place but as an actor that is born, runs, and ends -- and whose ending is also, on the
fire-adapted ground, a beginning.

The spine is the shift from reading a fire as a flat hazard to reading it as a living,
cyclic process with a life. Fire science long read a fire as a sequence -- ignition,
propagation, extinction -- each stage burning by its own logic and fought its own way; and
Pyne read fire whole, as a recurring, living process in the life of the land, a thing with
a cycle rather than an event with an end. Fire ecology carried the reading to its keystone:
Christensen and the fire-adapted tradition read the burn not as pure destruction but as a
renewer -- the seed that needs the fire, the system the fire begets -- so that a fire's
ending is the ground's beginning. The three bridges are those readings staged: kindling,
run, and the renewal twist.

wired as a composite over the fire and combustion engines.

## Restrictions

What the composite refuses to model, and to whom it delegates.

- **The whole burn regime (fire)**: The place's abstract regime -- its combustibility and
  the full turn of its modes, ignition, creep, conflagration, smoulder, exclusion, renewal
  -- is the fire engine's. Blaze wires fire's modes into an arc but does not own the regime:
  fire reads how a place burns; blaze reads the life of one fire that burns there. One mode
  alone is not a life.
- **The flat catalog of phenomena (combustion)**: The typology of every named fire -- the
  crown, the flashover, the BLEVE, the forge, and the paradox by which each is fought -- is
  the combustion engine's. Blaze draws named phenomena into the stages of an arc but does
  not own the catalog: combustion reads which fire; blaze reads that fire's life. One type
  alone is a process, not the arc.
- **The land's whole life (wildland)**: The setting read as a living, self-shaping agent
  across all its forces -- making, wearing, water, burn, reclaiming -- is the wildland
  composite's, and fire is one thread in it. Blaze is not the land's life but one fire's:
  where wildland reads the burn as one of five forces at the coarse regime level, blaze
  reads a single fire close up, through its own arc.
- **The fire as authored symbol (the play's pitch)**: A fire's life made to **stand for** a
  human story -- the blaze that mirrors a consuming passion, the burning that mirrors a ruin
  or a rebirth -- is refused: that is authored key, the play's pitch, not the fire on the
  place. Blaze delivers the fire's own working; the meaning read into it is the play's.

## Encoding

Source to constraint. A composite root over two engine atoms it declares as dependencies,
with three bridges, the stages of a fire's life.

- **[blaze](process_blaze.md)** (the root): a fire read as a living actor with an arc --
  how it takes, runs, and ends, braiding the place's burn regime (fire) with the named
  phenomenon it becomes (combustion). Anchored by Pyne (fire as a living, cyclic process).
- **[kindling](process_blaze_kindling.md)** (bridge): how a fire takes -- the regime's
  ignition becoming a particular birth, the ember that lands, the solid that gasifies, the
  pile that lights itself, the fire lit on purpose. Anchored by Rothermel (ignition).
- **[run](process_blaze_run.md)** (bridge): the fire at full cry -- the regime's
  conflagration and creep becoming the named run, the crown, the grass front, the
  flashover, the firestorm, the jet. Anchored by Rothermel and Van Wagner (spread).
- **[renewal](process_blaze_renewal.md)** (bridge): the fire's ending that begins again --
  the smoulder that persists, the holdover that will wake, and the serotiny that opens the
  sealed cone onto cleared ground. Anchored by Christensen; the composite's twist and the
  fire's keystone, the burn that destroys and renews at once.
