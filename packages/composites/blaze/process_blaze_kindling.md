# Process: Blaze, Kindling

## Taxonomy

[Blaze](process_blaze.md), the bridge where a fire takes hold.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: blaze

## Initiated by

A fire comes into being, the regime's ignition meeting the particular way this fire is born.
Kindling starts where a place's [ignition](@chbrain/khai-engine-fire/process_ignition.md) becomes a specific birth -- the [spotting](@chbrain/khai-engine-combustion/process_spotting.md) ember that lands and takes, the [pyrolysis](@chbrain/khai-engine-combustion/process_pyrolysis.md) that gasifies the solid into the fuel that burns, the [self-heating](@chbrain/khai-engine-combustion/process_self-heating.md) pile that lights itself from within, the [holdover](@chbrain/khai-engine-combustion/process_holdover.md) that overwintered and re-emerges, or the [backfire](@chbrain/khai-engine-combustion/process_backfire.md) lit on purpose.
It is the fire's birth: not yet a running front but a taking, the moment a source and a fuel find each other and the burn begins to carry.

## Direction

Toward the fire established, from spark to a burn that will hold.
Kindling reads the fire coming into being -- the ember taking in the receptive fuel bed, the solid heating until it gasifies and lights, the deep core reaching its own ignition, the overwintered fire waking, the drip torch laying its line -- the setting's ignition made particular in the way this fire starts.
It moves the place from unburnt to alight: the fire small and young, established enough to run but not yet running.

## Lever

The source and the fuel finding each other against the narrow window in which they take.
The carrying force is that a fire needs its birth -- a source hot enough, a fuel dry and fine enough, the two meeting where the burn can hold; the resistance is everything that denies the taking: the fuel too wet, the ember that lands and dies, the window of moisture and wind that opens for only a moment.
Kindling is the fire won or lost at the start: the same conditions that let a wildfire take let a backfire take, and the fire that fails to kindle never runs.

## Echo

The fire born, small and taking, ready to run.
Kindling leaves the setting alight -- the ember grown to a flame, the smoulder broken into fire, the line laid and carrying -- and hands the young fire to the run that will spread it.
It is the fire at its most fragile and its most decisive: the moment that decides whether there is a fire at all, the birth every running front and every dying ember is dated from.
Kindling is dated from the taking, the moment the fire first held and began to carry.
