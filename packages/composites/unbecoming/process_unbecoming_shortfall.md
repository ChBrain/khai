# Process: Unbecoming, the Shortfall

## Taxonomy

[Unbecoming](process_unbecoming.md), less of what the role wants, found before any conduct.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: unbecoming

## Initiated by

The room compares what it expects of the position with what it expects of this persona, and comes up short.
The Shortfall starts wherever [role](@chbrain/khai-engine-role/position_role.md) is applied to a persona the room reads as [incongruent](@chbrain/khai-engine-role/position_incongruent.md) with it -- the position's picture of its occupant set against the read [gender](@chbrain/khai-engine-gender/position_gender.md) supplies, and the difference charged to the persona.
It is the face of the composite where the judgement is about potential and there is nothing to point at.

## Direction

Toward a lowered estimate that precedes the evidence.
The Shortfall reads what is being compared: not the persona's conduct against the role's requirements, which would be an assessment, but the room's picture of the persona against the room's picture of the role, which is two stereotypes meeting -- so the deficit is found in advance, is about what they are presumed to have less of, and survives an absence of any performance to inspect.
It moves the persona from occupying the position to auditioning for it, permanently.

## Lever

The room's need to predict who will do well against the fact that it is predicting from a read.
The carrying force is that predicting is a real task and cannot be avoided: somebody has to be selected, promoted, or believed before the evidence is in, and the room does it with what it has -- so the shortfall does not feel like prejudice from inside, it feels like judgement under uncertainty, which is what makes it defensible in every individual case; the resistance is that it is not evidence-limited but evidence-resistant, because the same read that produced the estimate also discounts the performance that would correct it, and each new demonstration is received as the exception that the estimate already allowed for.
The Shortfall spends the persona's standing before they have used any, and charges them for a comparison they were not party to.

## Echo

A lower estimate, held sincerely, arrived at before there was anything to estimate.
It is the bridge that gets the persona to the only remaining move, which is to demonstrate the thing they are presumed to lack.
What it hands upward is that demonstration -- and the trap in it, since the qualities the role calls for are exactly the ones the read says this persona should not display.
The Shortfall is dated from the appointment, and never from the work.
