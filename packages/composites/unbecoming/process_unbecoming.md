# Process: Unbecoming

## Taxonomy

What a room does to a persona whose role and whose read do not agree.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: unbecoming

## Initiated by

A persona is standing in a position, and the room has already decided what somebody like them is for.
Unbecoming starts wherever those two are both in force: the sanctioned expectations attached to the position, through [role](@chbrain/khai-engine-role/position_role.md); and the read applied before the persona speaks, through [gender](@chbrain/khai-engine-gender/position_gender.md).
This composite adds no third engine. It stages the incongruity the role atom already names when it carries Eagly and Karau, and follows the penalty to the place nobody looks for it.

## Direction

Toward a judgement the persona cannot answer by behaving differently.
The composite wires the two engines and reads their working in three shapes: [the Read](process_unbecoming_read.md), both expectations standing before anybody acts; [the Shortfall](process_unbecoming_shortfall.md), less of what the role wants, found in advance; and [the Penalty](process_unbecoming_penalty.md), the twist -- the cost that arrives for meeting the role rather than for failing it.
The three are not one prejudice applied three times. They are two demands that cannot both be satisfied, and the persona standing between them.

## Lever

What the position expects of whoever holds it against what the room expects of this persona.
The carrying force is that both expectations are legitimate on their own terms and neither is invented for the occasion: a role really does carry requirements, and a room really does bring a read, and each is applied by people who could give a reasonable account of why -- there is no moment at which anybody decides to be unfair; the resistance is that the two do not agree, and a persona cannot hold a position and not hold it, so every course open to them satisfies one demand by failing the other.
Unbecoming is read on the room and never on the persona, because nothing about the persona is what produces it.

## Echo

A verdict, arrived at reasonably, with no conduct that would have avoided it.
Unbecoming does not close; it is the mismatch running, gathered into one reading -- a read, a shortfall, a penalty.
It hands off what it is not: competing or unclear demands from a role in its own right -- role conflict, overload, ambiguity -- are the role engine's; the read in its own right is the gender engine's; a discrediting attribute borne by the one who has it is carrying's, and this composite's persona is discredited by a position rather than by a mark; the us/them line and the standing catalogue of slants are intergroup's; rank and the moves that contest it are standing's; what a persona holds another to have coming for what they **did** is deserving's, and here nothing has been done; the accounting an observer gives for somebody else's misfortune is befalling's; being held to a face in front of others is presentation's; and a mismatch made to **stand for** a theme is authored key, the play's pitch, not the force.
The persona is dated from the position they took, and from what the room already thought about people who take it.
