# Process: Unbecoming, the Penalty

## Taxonomy

[Unbecoming](process_unbecoming.md), the cost of meeting the role, and where the two are seen for one thing.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: unbecoming

## Initiated by

The persona does the job well, and the room does not like it.
The Penalty starts wherever meeting the [role's](@chbrain/khai-engine-role/position_role.md) requirements is itself the [incongruity](@chbrain/khai-engine-role/position_incongruent.md) -- the conduct the position demands running against what [gender](@chbrain/khai-engine-gender/position_gender.md) prescribes for this persona, so the same act satisfies one standard by breaking the other.
It is the face of the composite where the trigger is success.

## Direction

Toward competence granted and liking withdrawn, in one motion.
The Penalty reads the two verdicts arriving together and reads that they are not in tension for the room: she is clearly very capable, and something about her is hard to warm to -- both sincere, both offered as observations, and the second of them supplied by the first.
It moves the persona out of the shortfall and into a cost they had to succeed to incur.

## Lever

The role's demand for the conduct against the read's prohibition of it.
The carrying force is that this is where the composite's whole difficulty becomes visible: falling short of the role disqualifies the persona and meeting it penalises them, so there is no available conduct that answers both -- and the two judgements never meet, because the first is delivered as an assessment of capability and the second as an impression of character, by people who would each defend their own and have never been asked to hold them together; the resistance is a room that has been made to state both standards in the same sentence, which is rare, unwelcome, and the only thing that dissolves it -- since neither judgement can survive being put next to the other.
The Penalty rides the composite's keystone knife-edge: it is not failure that triggers it but success, so the very evidence the persona produced to answer the shortfall is what supplies the grounds for the penalty.

## Echo

A persona doing the job, and paying for doing it.
It shows the two engines complete -- a position's expectations and a room's prior read -- and carries beneath it the composite's keystone: this is a scissors and not a bias, two blades each defensible alone and lethal together, and its cruelty is that it cannot be answered by conduct, because conduct is the axis it closes on.
This is why the Penalty is the hardest of the three to stage and the only one worth closing on: nobody in the room may be a villain, each verdict must be one the speaker would stand behind, the persona must be genuinely good at the work, and the audience must be able to see both blades at once while no character can.
The Penalty is the note the whole composite closes on: what a room would not permit is legible in what it punished somebody for achieving.
The Penalty is dated from the success, and it does not arrive alone.
