# Instructions: Unbecoming

How a Playwright wires the unbecoming composite into a play. You wire by linking the play's
own content to the composite's files; the files stay untouched, they are canon. The
composite installs the role and gender engines as dependencies, and their laws arrive with
them. Authoring guidance, not runtime content, and it does not go on tour.

## Human

- The human sets which positions in the play are held by personas the room reads differently, and what the room does about it.

## Agent

- From the play's own content, read a persona whose position and whose read pull against each other, and link under Projection what is running: the read while both sets of expectations are standing and neither has been tested (from role and gender), the shortfall while the persona's potential is being discounted in advance of any conduct (from role's incongruence), the penalty while competence is being granted and liking withdrawn on the same behaviour. Read it on the room and never on the persona, since nothing about the persona produces it. You link to the composite, you never edit it.

## Collaboration

- Congruence, incongruence, overload, and ambiguity are the role engine's; the read and its expressions are gender's -- one alone is a position, not a mismatch. A role's own strain, where the competing demands come from the position rather than from a read, is the role engine's and needs no second party; a discrediting attribute borne by the one who has it is carrying's, running on a mark where this runs on a position; the us/them line and the catalogued slants are intergroup's; rank and the moves that contest it are standing's, and this persona already holds the position; what somebody has coming for what they **did** is deserving's, and here nothing has been transgressed except a picture; the accounting an observer gives after a misfortune is befalling's, and this is a standard applied before rather than an explanation offered after; managing a face in front of others is presentation's, which is what a persona under this pressure may attempt and which does not remove the difficulty. This composite owns the mismatch -- and the penalty bridge carries the keystone: the trigger is success, not failure.

## Knowledge

- Unbecoming is a room read while its two expectations of the same person disagree -- braiding the position (role: the sanctioned expectations of whoever occupies it, and whether this persona is congruent or incongruent with them) with the read (gender: what the room applies to a persona before they speak). Neither is unusual, and that is the point: a position with no expectations could not be filled or judged, and a room reads whoever walks into it. The join is Eagly and Karau's, and the role atom already carries it -- prejudice arising not from anything the persona does but from the relation between two pictures the room already holds, in two forms. It runs in three faces. The read is both expectations standing before anybody acts, assembled separately by different histories, with nothing ever having required them to be compatible -- so their disagreement is structural rather than intended and there is no author of it to appeal to. The shortfall is the descriptive form: the room's picture of the persona set against the room's picture of the role, so the deficit is found in advance, is about presumed potential, and survives having no performance to inspect -- and it does not feel like prejudice from inside, it feels like judgement under uncertainty, which is what makes it defensible in every individual case. It is evidence-resistant rather than evidence-limited, because the same read that produced the estimate discounts the performance that would correct it. The penalty is the twist, and the work behind it is Rudman's and Heilman's: women who promoted themselves as the situation rewarded were rated more competent and less likeable on the same behaviour, and women who unambiguously succeeded in male-typed work were not merely doubted but disliked, most strongly where the success was least deniable. The cause is a prescriptive belief about what somebody ought to be like, which evidence cannot correct, rather than a descriptive one about what they are like, which it can. Here the two are seen for one thing: this is a scissors and not a bias -- falling short of the role disqualifies, meeting it penalises, no conduct answers both -- and it stays invisible because the blades are held by different hands and spoken in different registers, an assessment of capability and an impression of character, so neither speaker is dissembling and the two are never asked to hold their verdicts together.

## System

- Do read the room and never the persona: name whether the read (both expectations standing), the shortfall (potential discounted in advance), or the penalty (competence granted and liking withdrawn) is running, and cast each from its engines.
- Do keep every judgement defensible on its own: nobody in the room is a villain, each verdict is one its speaker would stand behind, and a play that supplies a bigot has replaced a scissors with a person and lost what it was for.
- Do put the penalty on the success: the persona must be genuinely good at the work, and the derogation must arrive because of that rather than in spite of it.
- Do keep the two verdicts in different registers and different mouths, since what makes the mechanism survive is that nobody ever has to say both in one sentence -- and if the play wants it dissolved, that is the move, and it is unwelcome.
- Do let the audience see both blades while no character can: the persona least of all, because the difficulty is that there is no conduct that answers both demands and naming it does not supply one.
- Do set the intensity to the setting: the effect is concentrated in male-dominated roles and directive conduct and is much weaker elsewhere, so a room chosen carelessly will read as a claim the composite does not make.
- Do not model a role's own competing demands (that is role, and needs no read), a mark the bearer manages (that is carrying), the us/them line (that is intergroup), a contest over rank (that is standing), a transgression and its sanction (that is deserving), an explanation offered after a misfortune (that is befalling), or the management of a face (that is presentation). Do not make a mismatch stand for a theme (that is the play's pitch). Do not edit the composite's files; wire only from the play's side.
