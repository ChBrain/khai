# Process: Unbecoming, the Read

## Taxonomy

[Unbecoming](process_unbecoming.md), both expectations standing before anybody has acted.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: unbecoming

## Initiated by

Somebody takes a position, and two sets of expectations meet on them.
The Read starts wherever [role](@chbrain/khai-engine-role/position_role.md) is in force -- what the occupant of a position may do and be, whether the persona is [congruent](@chbrain/khai-engine-role/position_congruent.md) with it or [incongruent](@chbrain/khai-engine-role/position_incongruent.md) -- and wherever [gender](@chbrain/khai-engine-gender/position_gender.md) is running as the read the room applies before the persona speaks, in whichever expression it brings, [male](@chbrain/khai-engine-gender/position_male.md) or [female](@chbrain/khai-engine-gender/position_female.md).
It is the face of the composite where nothing has happened and everything is already set.

## Direction

Toward a standard the persona will be measured against and did not write.
The Read reads both expectations as expectations and neither as a fact: the role's requirements are a picture of what somebody in the position is supposed to be like, held by the room and enforced by it, and the read is the same kind of thing arriving earlier -- accountability applied in advance, so the persona is answerable to it whether or not they endorse it.
It moves nobody, and installs the frame everything after this is measured in.

## Lever

The legitimacy of each expectation taken alone against the fact that they were never checked against each other.
The carrying force is that both are ordinary and both are needed: a position without expectations cannot be filled or judged, and a room reads whoever walks into it because that is how rooms work -- so nothing here is exceptional and nobody has yet done anything questionable; the resistance is that the two were assembled separately, by different histories, and nothing ever required them to be compatible, so their disagreement is structural rather than intended and there is no author of it to appeal to.
The Read spends nothing, and hands the persona a standard with two incompatible clauses.

## Echo

Two demands, both in force, neither answerable to the other.
It is the bridge that establishes the composite's honesty: this is not a room deciding to be unfair, it is two ordinary mechanisms overlapping, and every participant could pass a test of good faith.
What it hands upward is the incompatibility itself -- and the fact that it is already complete before the persona has said a word, which is why the next bridge can find a shortfall with nothing to point at.
The Read is dated from the position taken, and from long before the persona was in it.
