# Instructions: Reprisal

How a Playwright wires the reprisal composite into a play. You wire by linking the play's own content to the composite's files and, through them, to its atoms; the engine files stay untouched, they are canon. The Roadie plumbs the composite's law into Instructions Knowledge on deploy. Authoring guidance, not runtime content, and it does not go on tour.

## Human

- The human sets the wrong, the stroke, and the week afterward -- and keeps the last of the three, because that is where the composite lives and it is the part a play most often skips. Decide separately whether an answer ever comes from the other, since that is the only thing that closes the account and the persona does not control it.

## Agent

- From the play's own content, a persona links under Projection the score they hold and whether they have taken the stroke. Read the score on two things: what is genuinely outstanding, and what the persona is telling themselves the settling will be like. Then read the keeping by comparison rather than by feeling -- the one who acted still in it, the one who did not already elsewhere. Keep it the purchase: the stances are forgiveness's, the circling is rumination's, the misprediction is the bias engine's. You link to the composite and its atoms, you never edit them.

## Collaboration

- The releasing or keeping of a grievance and its three stances are the forgiveness engine's; the repetitive dwelling is rumination's; the mispredicted feeling is the bias engine's. Costly third-party sanction with no personal injury is altruistic-punishment's; the spiral of blow and counter-blow is escalation's; one wrong across three ledgers is the moral-account composite's, which wires the same forgiveness engine to a different question; what somebody has coming is deserving's; and where personal worth comes from and how it must be defended is worth-logic's, which matters because in an honour setting retaliation is an obligation rather than an option. Whether a persona is genuinely in the vengeful stance rather than the avoidant one, and whether an answer was genuinely legible as understanding, are the team's review.

## Knowledge

- Reprisal is what a persona expects getting even to do, and what it does. It wires on a persona at Projection over three engines: forgiveness supplies the vengeful stance, the motivation to see the transgressor answer rather than to withdraw or release; bias supplies the forecast through the impact bias, the settling imagined in full colour with the rest of the week left out of the frame; rumination supplies the loop an unfinished episode was already running in. Three bridges carry it. The score is the debt named and the receipt imagined: the wish is well founded, since something was taken and has not been answered for, and a persona who feels nothing when wronged is missing a signal rather than possessing a virtue -- but the estimate is of the wrong quantity, because the persona forecasts the intensity of a moment and buys with it a change in a state. The stroke is the act: it discharges something real and is not hollow, and it changes what the memory is made of, since before it the episode held somebody else's conduct and after it holds the persona's own. The keeping is the twist: personas who retaliate report thinking about the transgressor more, and longer, and feeling worse than personas who did not, while having confidently predicted the opposite -- and so did the non-retaliators, asked to imagine having acted. Nobody misunderstood their feelings; everybody mispredicted a duration. The keystone is that retaliation is the one response which guarantees the matter will not be finished with, because it hands the mind a second thing to work on -- the persona's own conduct, which they can defend, doubt, and rehearse indefinitely, where somebody else's could eventually be put down. There is one exception and the persona does not control it: where the other answers and it is legible that they understood why it was done, the account can genuinely close -- so what a persona wants from a reprisal is a reply, and an act designed to make somebody suffer is poorly designed to produce one.

## System

- Do stage the week after the stroke. A play that ends at the act has staged the forecast rather than the finding, and the composite reads nothing at all without the aftermath.
- Do let the satisfaction at the stroke be real. A play that makes it instantly hollow has replaced the mechanism with a moral, and the audience will read the persona as having been foolish, which they were not.
- Do show the keeping by comparison: put somebody beside the persona who was wronged the same way and did not act, and let the difference be visible in what each of them is still talking about.
- Do keep the persona right about the wrong and right about the moment, and wrong only about how long it would last -- and do not let the room lecture them, since the non-actors in the room would have mispredicted it identically.
- Do decide whether an answer ever comes, and remember that a reprisal built to make somebody suffer is badly built for getting one. A persona who receives a reply that shows the message landed may genuinely put it down; that is the composite's one exit and it should be earned rather than assumed.
- Do not model the stances themselves (forgiveness), the circling (rumination), the misprediction (bias), third-party sanction (altruistic-punishment), the spiral of blow and counter-blow (escalation), the ledgers of one wrong (moral-account), or what somebody has coming (deserving). Do not make the vengeance stand for a theme; that is authored key, the play's pitch. Do not edit the engine files; wire only from the play's side.
