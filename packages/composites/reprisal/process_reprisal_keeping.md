# Process: Reprisal, the Keeping

## Taxonomy

[Reprisal](process_reprisal.md), the matter still open, and where the three are seen for one thing.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: reprisal

## Initiated by

Weeks later, the persona who acted is still turning it over, and the one who did not has stopped.
The Keeping starts wherever [replaying](@chbrain/khai-engine-rumination/process_replaying.md) settles on the episode -- the argument, the message, the moment it was done -- and the loop runs on what the persona themselves did as much as on the wrong that started it.
It is the face of the composite where the mechanism shows, and it shows by comparison rather than by feeling.

## Direction

Toward a matter that will not close, held open by the closing of it.
The Keeping reads the finding straight: personas who retaliate report thinking about the transgressor more, and longer, and feeling worse than personas who did not -- while having confidently predicted the opposite, and while the non-retaliators, asked to imagine having acted, predict the same thing wrongly in the same direction.
Nobody in this has misunderstood their own feelings. They have mispredicted a duration, which is a different failure and a much more common one.

## Lever

The satisfaction of having acted against the mind's appetite for what it has done.
The carrying force is that the persona was right about the moment and right about the wrong -- and if what they wanted was deterrence, standing, or a message delivered, they may well have got it, so the reprisal is not a mistake by every measure; the resistance is that if what they wanted was to be finished with it, they have bought the opposite: an unfinished episode recurs because it is unfinished, and the persona has just added the one element they can never settle, which is whether they should have.
The Keeping rides the composite's keystone knife-edge: retaliation is the one response that guarantees the matter will not be finished with, because it gives the mind something of its own to keep working on.

## Echo

A persona still in it, alone, holding both halves.
It shows the three engines complete -- a stance taken, a forecast spent, and a loop running on the wrong and on the answer to it together.
The one exception belongs here rather than in the earlier bridges, because it is the thing the persona is actually waiting for: where the other answers, and it is legible that they understood why it was done, the account can close and the loop can stop. That is what the persona wanted all along and it is not what they aimed at -- an act designed to make somebody suffer is poorly designed to make them reply, and the reply is the only thing that ends it.
This is why the Keeping is the hardest of the three to stage and the only one worth closing on: the satisfaction must have been real, the persona must not be shown as foolish, the wrong must remain a genuine wrong, and the audience must be able to see that what is running now is the persona's own conduct rather than the injury.
A play that stages the emptiness at the moment of the stroke has replaced the mechanism with a moral.
The Keeping is dated from the stroke and runs for as long as no answer comes.
