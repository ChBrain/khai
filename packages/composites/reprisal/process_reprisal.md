# Process: Reprisal

## Taxonomy

What a persona expects getting even to do, and what it does.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: reprisal

## Initiated by

Somebody has been wronged, and is going to do something about it.
Reprisal starts wherever all three are in play: a grievance held in the [vengeful stance](@chbrain/khai-engine-forgiveness/position_stance_vengeful.md), the wish to see the other answer for it, through [forgiveness](@chbrain/khai-engine-forgiveness/process_forgiveness.md); a confident forecast of how the settling will feel, through the [impact bias](@chbrain/khai-engine-bias/position_impact_bias.md); and a mind already returning to the episode, through [rumination](@chbrain/khai-engine-rumination/process_rumination.md).
This composite adds no fourth engine. It reads the one thing the three make together, which is that the act meant to close the matter is what keeps it open.

## Direction

Toward a settling that does not settle.
The composite wires the three and reads their working in three shapes: [the Score](process_reprisal_score.md), the wish and the forecast attached to it; [the Stroke](process_reprisal_stroke.md), the act taken and what it actually delivers; and [the Keeping](process_reprisal_keeping.md), the twist -- the persona who acted still turning it over long after the one who did not has stopped.
The three are not stages of a feud. They are one transaction, and a prediction about it that is reliably wrong.

## Lever

The relief a persona expects against the relief available.
The carrying force is that the wish is not a defect and the forecast is not stupid: an injury genuinely leaves something outstanding, retaliation genuinely does discharge something in the moment, and a persona who never retaliates at anything is not more evolved, they are more exposed -- the appetite exists because it works, at the level of deterrence and standing; the resistance is that the persona is forecasting the wrong quantity, since what they want is for the matter to be finished and what the act delivers is a satisfaction that lasts about as long as the act, attached to a memory that now has their own conduct in it.
Reprisal is read on what the persona does with the episode afterward, never on what they felt at the moment of the stroke.

## Echo

An injury converted from a thing that happened into a thing the persona did.
Reprisal does not close; it is a settling being attempted, gathered into one reading -- a score, a stroke, a keeping.
The engine's twist is here: retaliation is the one response that guarantees the matter will not be finished with, because it hands the mind a second thing to work on -- its own conduct, which it can defend, doubt, refine, and rehearse -- where before it had only somebody else's, which it could eventually put down.
There is one exception and the persona does not control it: where the other answers, and it is clear they understood why, the account can genuinely close. Which is why what a persona actually wants from a reprisal is a reply, and why an act designed to make somebody suffer so often fails to produce one.
It hands off what it is not: the releasing or keeping of a grievance is forgiveness's; the circling itself is rumination's; the mis-forecast is the bias engine's; costly third-party sanction with nothing coming back is altruistic-punishment's; the spiral between two parties is escalation's; what somebody has coming is deserving's; the ledgers of guilt and repair are moral-account's; and an act of vengeance made to **stand for** a theme is authored key, the play's pitch, not the force.
The persona is dated from the stroke, and everything after it is theirs.
