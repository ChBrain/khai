# Process: Reprisal, the Score

## Taxonomy

[Reprisal](process_reprisal.md), the wish and the forecast attached to it.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: reprisal

## Initiated by

A persona has been wronged and wants the other to feel it.
The Score starts wherever the grievance settles into the [vengeful stance](@chbrain/khai-engine-forgiveness/position_stance_vengeful.md) -- not avoidance, which withdraws, and not benevolence, which releases, but the motivation to see the transgressor answer -- and wherever the persona begins telling themselves what having it answered will be like.
It is the face of the composite that is entirely anticipation.

## Direction

Toward a settled account, imagined in detail and confidently priced.
The Score reads two things at once. The wish: something is outstanding, the persona is the one holding it, and no third party is coming.
And the forecast, which is where the [impact bias](@chbrain/khai-engine-bias/position_impact_bias.md) does its work -- the persona imagines the moment of the settling in full colour, with nothing else in the frame, and reads off how it will feel from how loud that picture is. What is left out of the picture is the week that keeps happening afterward, with the persona in it, remembering.

## Lever

The reality of the debt against the reliability of the estimate.
The carrying force is that the wish is well founded and the persona is not deceiving themselves about the wrong: something was taken, the taker has not answered for it, and holding that is not a failure of character -- societies run on the expectation that wrongs are answered, and a persona who feels nothing when wronged is missing a signal rather than possessing a virtue; the resistance is that the persona is estimating the wrong quantity, since they are forecasting the intensity of a moment and buying with it a change in a state, and those two are not the same purchase.
The Score is where the persona decides what they are owed, and misprices what paying it will do.

## Echo

A debt named, and a receipt imagined that will not be issued.
It is the bridge that installs the expectation, before anything has been done.
What it hands upward is not yet an act but a standard -- the persona now has a picture of how this ends, and everything that follows will be measured against a picture nothing can match.
The Score is dated from the injury, and it grows more detailed the longer it is held.
