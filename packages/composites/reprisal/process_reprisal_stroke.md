# Process: Reprisal, the Stroke

## Taxonomy

[Reprisal](process_reprisal.md), the act, and what it actually delivers.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: reprisal

## Initiated by

The persona has done it.
The Stroke starts at the act: the retaliation taken, the cost imposed, the thing said, the thing withheld, the move made -- and the moment immediately after, which is the only moment the forecast was ever about.

## Direction

Toward a satisfaction that is real and does not last.
The Stroke reads the discharge honestly: there is one, it is not imaginary, and a play that stages retaliation as instantly hollow has lied about the mechanism -- something outstanding has been acted on and the persona feels it.
And it reads what the act has just changed, which is the composition of the memory. Before the stroke the episode contained somebody else's conduct. After it, the episode contains the persona's own.

## Lever

The discharge of the act against what the act has added to the record.
The carrying force is that the stroke does work: standing is restored in the room, the transgressor learns something, deterrence is real, and the persona is not merely indulging themselves -- most of the reason the appetite exists is that acting on it has effects; the resistance is that the persona has now supplied the mind with material it did not have, since their own conduct is a thing they can evaluate, defend, second-guess, and rehearse, and unlike the original injury it is theirs to keep revising.
The Stroke buys a moment and mortgages the aftermath.

## Echo

An account acted on, and enlarged.
It shows what the score could not: that the forecast was about a moment and the cost is about a duration.
What it hands upward is a persona who has done something, which is a different thing to be than a persona to whom something was done -- and the next bridge is about what the mind does with the difference.
The Stroke is dated precisely, and the persona will remember it better than the injury.
