# Reprisal

Reprisal as composite: what a persona expects getting even to do, and what it does -- the grievance held in the vengeful stance together with a confident, detailed forecast of how the settling will feel, imagined in full colour with the rest of the week left out of the frame (the score); the act taken, which genuinely discharges something and is not hollow, and which adds the persona's own conduct to a memory that previously held only somebody else's (the stroke); and the aftermath, where the persona who retaliated thinks about the transgressor more, and longer, and feels worse than the persona who did not, having confidently predicted the opposite (the keeping) -- wired over the forgiveness, bias, and rumination engines, and closing on the one exception the persona does not control: where the other answers and it is legible that they understood, the account can genuinely close.
