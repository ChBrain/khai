# Process: Enmity

## Taxonomy

[Intergroup](process_intergroup.md), the bridge where the tilt hardens into hostility.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: intergroup

## Initiated by

The groups come into [competition](@chbrain/khai-engine-social-identity/position_competition.md) -- over resources, over standing, or over values each feels the other threatens -- and the standing tilt toward the out-group hardens into something aimed.
The out-group is no longer merely a they but a rival, and the prejudice that was a lean becomes a warrant.
This is where the intergroup dynamic turns dangerous: the cognitive tilt acquires a target and a reason.

## Direction

From bias to harm.
Under real or felt threat, favouritism-and-derogation becomes [hostile](@chbrain/khai-engine-aggression/process_hostile.md) or [instrumental](@chbrain/khai-engine-aggression/process_instrumental.md) aggression -- the out-group opposed, excluded, harmed for its own sake or for the in-group's gain -- and where the in-group's own troubles need somewhere to go, blame is [displaced](@chbrain/khai-engine-aggression/process_displaced.md) onto them, the scapegoat made.
The direction is outward and hardening: each hostile act confirms the prejudice that warranted it.

## Lever

The competition that arms the tilt.
Bias gives the prejudgment but not the harm; aggression gives the harm but not its aim at a whole group; social-identity's competition supplies the aim -- the out-group named rival -- and the bridge is where a cognitive lean, given a threat and a target, crosses into a hostile act.
The spiral by which each side's hostility feeds the other's, raising the stakes toward lose-lose, is the escalation engine's; this bridge owns the crossing itself, tilt into harm.

## Echo

The groups are hostile, the line now a front.
Left to run, it hardens further -- the prejudice justifying the aggression, the aggression confirming the prejudice, the out-group's answer read as proof of what it always was.
And yet it is not sealed: what can still reverse it, and it can be reversed, is [contact](process_contact.md).
