# Process: Division

## Taxonomy

[Intergroup](process_intergroup.md), the bridge where the line drawn switches on the tilt.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: intergroup

## Initiated by

A line is drawn -- us and them -- and it need not be a deep one; even an arbitrary sorting will do.
The persona [identifies](@chbrain/khai-engine-social-identity/position_identification.md) with the in-group, and in the same instant the out-group becomes a they.
This is the doorstep of the intergroup dynamic: the boundary itself, before any history or interest has attached to it.

## Direction

From a mere category to a live bias.
Grouping alone -- with no history, no conflict, no stake -- tilts the member toward their own: the in-group favoured ([in-group bias](@chbrain/khai-engine-bias/position_in_group_bias.md)), the out-group prejudged ([prejudice](@chbrain/khai-engine-bias/position_prejudice.md)), flattened into a type ([stereotyping](@chbrain/khai-engine-bias/position_stereotyping.md)) and seen as all alike ([outgroup homogeneity](@chbrain/khai-engine-bias/position_outgroup_homogeneity.md)).
The direction is immediate: to sort is to lean, and the leaning is toward us and against them.

## Lever

The categorization that activates the tilt.
Social-identity gives the belonging but not the prejudgment; bias gives the prejudgment but as a standing tilt, not one a fresh line switches on; the bridge is the coupling between them -- the minimal-group finding, that to sort is to bias, that the boundary alone is enough to lean a member toward their own before a single interaction has occurred.
It is the cheapest and most unsettling of intergroup's mechanisms: no grievance is required, only a line.

## Echo

The tilt is live, seeded by the line alone.
This is the intergroup dynamic's floor, and its most disquieting fact: favouritism and derogation can stand up on nothing but a boundary, which is why they are so easy to provoke and so hard to argue away.
What turns the tilt from a lean into an aimed hostility is the [enmity](process_enmity.md) bridge; what can dissolve it, even so, is [contact](process_contact.md).
