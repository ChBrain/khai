# Instructions: Intergroup

How a Playwright wires the intergroup composite into a play. You wire by linking the play's own content to the composite's files; the composite and its atoms stay untouched, they are canon. Installing the composite brings its three atoms -- social-identity, bias, aggression -- as dependencies, and the Roadie plumbs the composite's law into Instructions Knowledge on deploy. Authoring guidance, not runtime content, and it does not go on tour.

## Human

- The human defines the intergroup terrain: the in-group and the out-group, the line between them, and what -- if anything -- the groups compete over, so the tilt has a boundary to seed on and the enmity has a threat to arm it.

## Agent

- From the play's own content, a plot links the intergroup bridge it stages (division, enmity, or contact) under its Tension. Cast the member from the atoms -- their belonging and contest from social-identity, their tilt toward the out-group from bias, their hostility from aggression -- and let the bridge show the dynamic: the line that seeds the tilt, the competition that arms it, the contact that can dissolve it. You link to the composite and its atoms; you never edit them.

## Collaboration

- The member's bind to their own in-group -- belonging, conformity, the mark, the cost of leaving -- is the membership composite's; the conflict-spiral by which any two parties escalate is the escalation engine's. The in-group belonging and contest are social-identity's, the tilt toward the out-group is bias's, the harm is aggression's. This composite supplies only the seams -- the us/them dynamic the three atoms make together but none makes alone.

## Knowledge

- Intergroup is the us/them relation read from a member's side: belonging to one group shaping the stance toward another. Division is the line -- mere categorization, even arbitrary, switching on in-group favouritism and out-group prejudice before any interaction. Enmity is the hardening -- competition or felt threat turning the tilt into hostile, instrumental, or displaced aggression, the out-group cast rival or scapegoat. Contact is the reduction -- meeting under equal, cooperative terms toward a superordinate goal, which recategorizes them-and-us into a larger we, or, on the wrong terms, confirms the hostility instead.

## System

- Do let the tilt need no grievance: the unsettling floor of the dynamic is that a line alone seeds it, so let arbitrary grouping produce favouritism and derogation before any real conflict.
- Do let competition arm it: the crossing from prejudice to harm wants a threat and a target, so stage the out-group named rival, not merely disliked.
- Do let contact be demanding: mere proximity can worsen the tilt; only equal-status cooperation toward a shared goal reduces it, so let a botched contact harden the front.
- Do delegate the member's bind to their own group to the membership composite and the spiral of harm to escalation. Do not edit the composite's or the atoms' files; wire only from the play's side.
