# Rapport: Reference

## Line of Work

Rapport as **composite**: the felt quality of a connection working, the sense of clicking with another -- built where three things run together and reinforce. It is not a new engine but a wiring of three: **contagion** supplies the unconscious synchrony of two bodies falling into step, the mimicry and affect-convergence that make a pair feel aligned before a word; **grounding** supplies the understanding that keeps landing, each feeling met; and **self-disclosure** supplies the mutual opening that deepens the tie. The composite adds no fourth phenomenon; it stages the seams the three make into a felt whole -- sync easing understanding, understanding making opening safe, opening returned tightening the sync.

The domain does not model the chosen style-convergence a persona makes toward another, which is the accommodation engine's; nor the lasting bond rapport can seed, which is the attachment engine's; nor the felt liking it produces, which is the emotion engine's. It owns the interactional quality itself: the click, the spiral that builds it, and the veneer that counterfeits it.

The spine is the social psychology of rapport: a good interaction is not a single thing but the convergence of coordination (behavioural synchrony), mutual attentiveness (each feeling understood), and positivity (mutual warmth and openness), which together produce the felt sense of rapport and, run in a spiral, build closeness. Because the coordination is its most visible and most forgeable component, rapport's surface can be produced without its substance -- the counterfeit that reads real until the substance is called for. The three bridges are rapport's course: meshing, warming, veneer.

The social psychology of rapport, interactional synchrony, and closeness, wired as a composite over three engines.

## Restrictions

What the composite refuses to model, and to whom it delegates.

- **Accommodation**: The composite models the automatic synchrony of rapport, not the chosen style-convergence toward another. The strategic tuning of one's speech toward or away from an interlocutor is owned by the accommodation engine; a persona may accommodate to build rapport, but rapport's coordination is the unconscious mirroring, and the deliberate convergence is accommodation's.
- **Attachment**: The composite models the interactional quality, not the lasting bond. The enduring tie to a secure base is owned by the attachment engine; rapport is momentary and can arise between strangers, and while a run of it can seed an attachment, the standing bond itself is attachment's.
- **Emotion**: The composite models the connection working, not the feeling it produces. The felt liking, warmth, or delight that rapport generates is owned by the emotion engine; rapport is the interactional condition, and the emotion its condition brings on is emotion's.
- **The atoms**: The composite adds no content its atoms already own. The unconscious mimicry and affect-convergence are the contagion engine's; the understanding built turn by turn is the grounding engine's; the opening of the self and its reciprocity are the self-disclosure engine's. Rapport owns only the seams -- the felt whole the three become that none is alone.

## Encoding

Source to constraint, per file.

- **[rapport](process_rapport.md)** (the root): The felt quality of a connection working, three things -- synchrony, understanding, opening -- running together and reinforcing into the sense of clicking, or worn on the surface without the substance. Anchored by Tickle-Degnen & Rosenthal (the three-component model).
- **[meshing](process_meshing.md)** (bridge): Synchrony and understanding arrive together, so the pair feels aligned beneath the words and met within them -- the click before it deepens. Wires contagion to grounding. Anchored by Chartrand & Bargh (mimicry and liking) and Bernieri & Rosenthal (interactional synchrony).
- **[warming](process_warming.md)** (bridge): The mesh makes opening safe, matched disclosure returns, and rapport climbs by a spiral of opening met by opening -- or the opening outruns the footing and exposes. Wires self-disclosure to the mesh. Anchored by Aron et al. (escalating reciprocal disclosure generating closeness).
- **[veneer](process_veneer.md)** (bridge): The coordination -- rapport's most visible and most forgeable component -- is produced without the understanding or opening beneath it, so the surface parts company with the substance. Wires contagion's mimicry to the absence of the rest. Anchored by Bernieri & Rosenthal (coordination distinct from felt connection).
