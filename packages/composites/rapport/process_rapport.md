# Process: Rapport

## Taxonomy

The felt quality of a connected interaction -- a composite over contagion, grounding, and self-disclosure.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: rapport

## Initiated by

Two personas fall into a good interaction -- the sense of clicking, of being on the same wavelength, of an ease that neither had to manufacture.
This is rapport: the felt quality of a connection working, and it is never one thing but three running together and reinforcing -- the unconscious synchrony of two bodies falling into step, the understanding that keeps landing, and the mutual opening that deepens the tie.
The composite adds no fourth engine. It wires the seams the three make into a felt whole: sync eases understanding, understanding invites opening, opening returned tightens the sync.

## Direction

Toward closeness, built by a positive spiral -- or toward the counterfeit, where the surface of rapport runs without its substance.
Where the three genuinely reinforce, rapport climbs: each round of matched sync, met understanding, and returned opening raises the footing for the next, and the pair closes.
It is the mirror of the crosstalk loop -- the same interactional machinery, running upward into connection instead of downward into talking-past.

## Lever

The three reinforcing, each making the others cheaper.
Synchrony makes the pair feel aligned before a word; grounding makes each feel understood; self-disclosure opens the self and, returned, deepens. Being in sync makes understanding easier, being understood makes opening safe, opening returned tightens the sync -- a loop that gains rather than loses.
No single atom stages this: rapport lives in the seams, the felt whole the three become that none is alone.

## Echo

The pair clicks and closes -- or the rapport is a veneer, coordinated and smooth with nothing underneath.
The connection runs as [meshing](process_meshing.md), [warming](process_warming.md), or [veneer](process_veneer.md) -- the sync-and-understanding that clicks, the opening that deepens it, and the surface worn without the substance.
The chosen style-convergence a persona makes toward another is the accommodation engine's; the lasting bond rapport can seed is the attachment engine's; the felt liking it produces is the emotion engine's. This composite owns the interactional quality itself: the click, and whether anything is under it.
