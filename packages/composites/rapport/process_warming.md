# Process: Warming

## Taxonomy

[Rapport](process_rapport.md), the bridge where the click invites opening and matched disclosure deepens the tie.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: rapport

## Initiated by

The mesh is there -- aligned in body, met in meaning -- and it makes opening safe.
A persona ventures a disclosure, [reciprocity](@chbrain/khai-engine-self-disclosure/process_reciprocity.md) returns it, and the tie warms.
This is where rapport stops being ease and starts becoming closeness: the click, having established that it is safe, begins to be trusted with more of the self.

## Direction

Toward closeness, by a spiral of matched opening.
Each returned disclosure -- [breadth](@chbrain/khai-engine-self-disclosure/process_breadth.md) widening the topics, [deepening](@chbrain/khai-engine-self-disclosure/process_deepening.md) reaching inward toward the core -- raises the safety for the next, so rapport climbs: opening met by opening, warmth by warmth, each step making the next less risky.
The direction is upward and mutual; a disclosure returned in kind is the engine of it, and a disclosure left hanging stalls it.

## Lever

The click that makes disclosure safe, and the disclosure that deepens the click.
Self-disclosure gives the opening and its reciprocity but assumes a footing to open from; the mesh gives that footing; the bridge is the coupling -- being in sync and understood is what makes it safe to open, and opening returned tightens the sync and the understanding both, so the three climb together rather than in turn.
The pace is the whole art: opening matched to the footing deepens, opening that outruns it does not.

## Echo

The pair closes, rapport built and deepening -- or the opening outruns the footing and exposes.
A disclosure ventured before the sync and understanding can hold it does not deepen but alarms, and the [retreat](@chbrain/khai-engine-self-disclosure/process_retreat.md) that follows cools the whole tie, sometimes below where it began.
Warmth built and warmth overreached are a hair apart, paced by reciprocity; the counterfeit of this warmth -- coordination worn without any real opening under it -- is [veneer](process_veneer.md).
