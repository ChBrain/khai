# Process: Veneer

## Taxonomy

[Rapport](process_rapport.md), the bridge where the surface of rapport runs without its substance.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: rapport

## Initiated by

The coordination is present and the rest is not -- [mimicry](@chbrain/khai-engine-contagion/process_mimicry.md) performed, warmth signalled, the motions of rapport made without the understanding or the opening beneath them.
This is rapport's counterfeit: its most visible part produced on its own, standing in for the whole.
It is the salesman's mirror, the practised warmth, the interviewer's easy manner -- the look of connection worn as a technique.

## Direction

Toward a rapport that reads real and is not.
The surface -- sync, smiles, smooth turns -- can be produced deliberately, so a persona can manufacture the appearance of rapport while nothing grounds or opens under it; and in the moment it often passes, because the surface is exactly what the other reads first.
The direction is a hollowing: the form of rapport kept up over an empty room.

## Lever

The separability of the coordination from the rest.
Contagion's [mimicry](@chbrain/khai-engine-contagion/process_mimicry.md) can be faked or performed at will; grounding and self-disclosure cannot be, not really -- a persona cannot counterfeit being understood or having opened, only the signs of them. The bridge is that gap: rapport's most visible component is its most forgeable, so its surface can part company with its substance and still look, briefly, like the thing.
The veneer is coordination standing in for connection -- the one ingredient that is cheap to fake, doing the work of the two that are not.

## Echo

The rapport is hollow -- felt as real in the moment, exposed when the substance is called for and found missing.
When the counterfeit is seen, what looked like closeness curdles faster than no rapport would have, because the other must now revise not just the tie but their own reading of it.
It is the mesh worn without the [warming](process_warming.md) -- the click's surface kept up over nothing; and it is what tells genuine rapport apart, for the real thing is the veneer's opposite: the coordination is the least of it, the ground and the opening the most.
