# Process: Meshing

## Taxonomy

[Rapport](process_rapport.md), the bridge where synchrony and understanding lock into the sense of a shared wavelength.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: rapport

## Initiated by

Two personas fall into sync -- [mimicry](@chbrain/khai-engine-contagion/process_mimicry.md) matching posture, expression, and voice, the [convergence](@chbrain/khai-engine-contagion/process_convergence.md) of affect following -- and at the same time each keeps [understanding](@chbrain/khai-engine-grounding/process_grounding.md) the other, the evidence landing turn on turn.
This is the doorstep of rapport: where being in step and being understood arrive together.
Neither alone is rapport -- sync without understanding is a crowd moving as one, understanding without sync is a transaction -- but together they are the click.

## Direction

From sync-plus-understanding to the felt "we are on the same wavelength."
Being in step and being understood are different things -- one bodily, one of meaning -- and the bridge is where they coincide, so the pair feels aligned beneath the words and met within them at once.
The direction is a settling: two separate rhythms, of body and of meaning, dropping into a shared one.

## Lever

The synchrony that eases the understanding, and the understanding that loosens the synchrony.
Contagion gives the automatic mirroring but not the meaning; grounding gives the understanding but not the bodily sync; the bridge is their coupling -- two in physical step ground each other more easily, the [acknowledgment](@chbrain/khai-engine-grounding/process_acknowledgment.md) reading warmer and the [demonstration](@chbrain/khai-engine-grounding/process_demonstration.md) coming quicker, while being understood loosens the body further into sync.
Each makes the other cheaper, and the two settle together faster than either would alone.

## Echo

The pair is aligned in body and in meaning -- the substrate of rapport, the click before it deepens.
It is stable on its own for a while: two people can mesh warmly and go no further, the acquaintance that is easy but not close.
It is what [warming](process_warming.md) builds on when the opening begins; its surface produced without the meaning under it is [veneer](process_veneer.md).
