# Instructions: Rapport

How a Playwright wires the rapport composite into a play. You wire by linking the play's own content to the composite's files; the composite and its atoms stay untouched, they are canon. Installing the composite brings its three atoms -- contagion, grounding, self-disclosure -- as dependencies, and the Roadie plumbs the composite's law into Instructions Knowledge on deploy. Authoring guidance, not runtime content, and it does not go on tour.

## Human

- The human defines the rapport terrain: the two personas, what draws them into a good interaction, and what is real or merely performed between them -- so the click has somewhere to build, and the veneer has something to counterfeit.

## Agent

- From the play's own content, a plot links the rapport bridge it stages (meshing, warming, or veneer) under its Tension. Cast the pair from the atoms -- their synchrony from contagion, their understanding from grounding, their opening from self-disclosure -- and let the bridge show the connection: the mesh of sync and understanding, the warming spiral of matched disclosure, or the veneer where the surface runs without the substance. You link to the composite and its atoms; you never edit them.

## Collaboration

- The chosen style-convergence a persona makes toward another is the accommodation engine's; the lasting bond rapport can seed is the attachment engine's; the felt liking it produces is the emotion engine's. The unconscious mimicry and affect-convergence are contagion's, the understanding is grounding's, the opening and its reciprocity are self-disclosure's. This composite supplies only the seams -- the felt whole the three atoms become that none is alone.

## Knowledge

- Rapport is the felt quality of a connection working -- the sense of clicking -- built where three things run together and reinforce: the unconscious synchrony of two bodies in step, the understanding that keeps landing, and the mutual opening that deepens. Meshing is the click -- synchrony and understanding arriving together, aligned beneath the words and met within them. Warming is the deepening -- the mesh making opening safe, matched disclosure climbing by a spiral of opening met by opening. Veneer is the counterfeit -- coordination performed without the understanding or opening beneath it, the surface parting from the substance.

## System

- Do let the three reinforce, not just co-occur: sync makes understanding easier, understanding makes opening safe, opening returned tightens the sync -- so the reader feels a spiral building, the mirror of the crosstalk loop.
- Do pace the opening: a disclosure matched to the footing deepens, a disclosure that outruns it exposes and cools; let warmth built and warmth overreached be a hair apart.
- Do let the veneer read real in the moment and curdle when seen: the coordination is the forgeable part, so let a manufactured rapport pass briefly and then collapse harder than none would have.
- Do delegate the chosen convergence to the accommodation engine and the lasting bond to attachment. Do not edit the composite's or the atoms' files; wire only from the play's side.
