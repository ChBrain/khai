# Process: Abiding

## Taxonomy

[Love](process_love.md), the bridge where intimacy and commitment hold without passion -- Sternberg's companionate love.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: love

## Initiated by

The passion has cooled, but the two remain close and committed -- the opened self still there in [reciprocity](@chbrain/khai-engine-self-disclosure/process_reciprocity.md) and [deepening](@chbrain/khai-engine-self-disclosure/process_deepening.md), and [loyalty](@chbrain/khai-engine-loyalty/position_loyalty.md) holding them together.
This is the long, settled love: the decades-married, the deep friendship, the love that outlasts desire.
It is what most long loves become, once the fire that started them has burned down to warmth.

## Direction

Toward endurance.
Intimacy and commitment together make the most durable of the two-cornered loves, because passion's fading -- which ends a merely romantic love -- does not touch it: it was never what held.
The direction is level and long: not the climb of romance but the steady holding of a love that knows itself and has decided to stay.

## Lever

Intimacy and commitment, the passion spent or never central.
Self-disclosure gives the closeness, loyalty the staying; the bridge is their meeting -- a love bound by knowing-and-holding rather than by wanting. The dispositional cousin of this, the persona built to love in this key, is the desire engine's [companionate](@chbrain/khai-engine-desire/position_companionate.md) style; this bridge is the relationship that has come to this shape, whatever style each partner brought to it.
The lever is that the two remaining corners are the slow ones -- intimacy that took years, commitment that was decided -- so what holds this love is exactly what does not easily leave.

## Echo

The love endures, warm and unflaming.
Whether it is felt as enough, or as a passion mourned, is the personas' own -- the same abiding love reads as a deep contentment to one couple and as a settling to another.
A passion rekindled makes it consummate; a commitment that thins without the passion to distract from its thinning can leave it, in time, the empty love of two who stay without the closeness this bridge was named for.
