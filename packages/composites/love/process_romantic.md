# Process: Romantic

## Taxonomy

[Love](process_love.md), the bridge where intimacy and passion meet without commitment.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: love

## Initiated by

Two personas are drawn together -- [passion](@chbrain/khai-engine-desire/position_passionate.md) pulling, and the self opening in [deepening](@chbrain/khai-engine-self-disclosure/process_deepening.md) and [reciprocity](@chbrain/khai-engine-self-disclosure/process_reciprocity.md) -- and they are in love, without yet having decided to stay.
This is the being-in-love: close and aflame, and unpledged.
It is the love of songs and early months, all present tense, its future not yet a question either has answered.

## Direction

Toward the height of romance, and its precariousness.
Intimacy and passion together make a love that feels total -- known and wanted at once -- but nothing yet holds it save the two feelings that brought it, and of the two, passion is the faster to fade.
The direction is upward and unsecured: the love climbs on feeling, with no commitment beneath it to catch a fall.

## Lever

Intimacy and passion, without the third corner.
Self-disclosure gives the opening, desire the drive; the bridge is their meeting -- the love that is all drawn-and-known but not yet promised. Where passion fades and commitment was never built, it thins toward nothing; where commitment is added to it, it becomes consummate.
The lever is the missing corner: what romantic love has is real, and what it lacks is what would make it last.

## Echo

The love burns bright and unpledged.
Left as it is, it either deepens into commitment or burns out; the classic arc is passion cooling while intimacy remains, which turns romantic love toward [abiding](process_abiding.md).
If the intimacy too was thinner than it felt, what remains when the passion goes is little, and the ending is the grief engine's to take up.
