# Instructions: Love

How a Playwright wires the love composite into a play. You wire by linking the play's own content to the composite's files; the composite and its atoms stay untouched, they are canon. Installing the composite brings its three atoms -- self-disclosure, desire, loyalty -- as dependencies, and the Roadie plumbs the composite's law into Instructions Knowledge on deploy. Authoring guidance, not runtime content, and it does not go on tour.

## Human

- The human defines the love terrain: who loves whom, and which of the three corners the love holds -- how much they have opened, how strong the pull, whether they have decided to stay -- so the love has a shape and somewhere for the corners to shift.

## Agent

- From the play's own content, a plot links the love bridge it stages (romantic, abiding, or fatuous) under its Tension. Cast the lovers from the atoms -- their intimacy from self-disclosure, their passion from desire, their commitment from loyalty -- and let the bridge show which corners hold and how they change: the aflame-and-open romance, the settled abiding tie, the headlong fatuous pledge, or the full consummate triangle. You link to the composite and its atoms; you never edit them.

## Collaboration

- The dispositional style of wanting a persona brings -- passionate, companionate, pragmatic, obsessive, selfless -- is the desire engine's (the colours of love); the bond and its security beneath the intimacy is attachment's; the loss when a love ends is grief's. The opening of the self is self-disclosure's, the drive is desire's, the allegiance is loyalty's. This composite supplies only the seams -- the shapes the three corners make together but none makes alone.

## Knowledge

- Love is a triangle of three components: intimacy (two selves opened to each other), passion (the drive that pulls them together), and commitment (the decision to hold the love and stay). The kinds of love are which corners are present. Romantic is intimacy and passion without commitment -- in love, aflame, unpledged. Abiding is intimacy and commitment without passion -- Sternberg's companionate love, the long settled tie that outlasts desire. Fatuous is passion and commitment without intimacy -- the whirlwind, bound fast before the knowing is done. All three at once is consummate; one corner alone is only love's ingredient (liking, infatuation, or empty love).

## System

- Do let the corners change at different rates: passion flares and fades fast, intimacy grows slow and lasts, commitment is decided then maintained -- so a love's shape shifts, romantic cooling toward abiding, fatuous forced to build intimacy late or go empty.
- Do let the drama live in the mismatch: one partner's love strong in a corner the other's is not, or a triangle that was full and has lost a side.
- Do keep the single corners honest: intimacy alone is liking, passion alone infatuation, commitment alone the empty love -- name them as ingredients, not as love whole.
- Do delegate the individual's love-style to the desire engine and the loss of a love to grief. Do not edit the composite's or the atoms' files; wire only from the play's side.
