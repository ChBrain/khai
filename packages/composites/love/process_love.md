# Process: Love

## Taxonomy

The composition of a love -- a composite over self-disclosure, desire, and loyalty.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: love

## Initiated by

Two personas love, and a love is never one thing.
Sternberg drew it as a triangle of three components: intimacy, the closeness of two selves opened to each other; passion, the drive and attraction that pulls them together; and commitment, the decision to hold the love and stay.
This composite reads a love by which of the three it is made of -- self-disclosure gives the intimacy, desire gives the passion, loyalty gives the commitment. It adds no fourth engine; it wires the corners into the shapes a love can take.

## Direction

Toward one of the kinds a love can be, set by which corners are present -- and toward the shifts as they come and go.
All three at once is consummate love, the full triangle, rare and hard to hold; two corners give the recognizable partial loves; one corner alone is not yet love but its ingredient -- intimacy alone is liking, passion alone infatuation, commitment alone the empty love of a bond gone hollow.
The direction is never fixed: a love changes shape as one corner cools or another is built.

## Lever

The three corners, present or absent, and changing at different rates.
Intimacy grows slowly and lasts; passion flares fast and fades; commitment is decided and then maintained -- so the corners are never in step, and a love's shape shifts as passion cools while intimacy holds, or commitment is added to a heat already there.
The kinds are the combinations, and the drama is the mismatch: a love strong in corners the other's is not, or a triangle that was full and has lost a side.

## Echo

The love is whole, partial, or a single corner mistaken for the whole.
It runs as [romantic](process_romantic.md) (intimacy and passion), [abiding](process_abiding.md) (intimacy and commitment), or [fatuous](process_fatuous.md) (passion and commitment) -- the three two-cornered loves -- with consummate love the having of all three at once.
The dispositional style of wanting a persona brings -- passionate, companionate, pragmatic -- is the desire engine's; the bond and its security beneath the intimacy is the attachment engine's; the loss when a love ends is the grief engine's. This composite owns the composition itself: what a given love is made of.
