# Process: Fatuous

## Taxonomy

[Love](process_love.md), the bridge where passion and commitment bind without intimacy.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: love

## Initiated by

The two are swept up -- [passion](@chbrain/khai-engine-desire/position_passionate.md) high and a commitment made fast through [loyalty](@chbrain/khai-engine-loyalty/position_loyalty.md) -- before the slow work of intimacy has been done.
This is the whirlwind: engaged in a month, bound on a feeling, the pledge made on the strength of the drive alone.
It is love committed before it is known.

## Direction

Toward a commitment resting on passion alone.
The two have promised to stay, and mean it, but the knowing that would steady the promise -- the years of opening that make [abiding](process_abiding.md) love durable -- has been skipped, so the whole weight sits on the one corner that fades fastest.
The direction is headlong: fast to bind, and built on the least lasting of the three.

## Lever

Passion and commitment, the intimacy skipped.
Desire gives the drive, loyalty the pledge; the bridge is their meeting without the third corner -- a love committed before it is understood. Because passion fades and no intimacy was built to take its place, the commitment is left holding a love that has lost its reason.
The lever is the missing slow corner: what would have steadied this love is exactly what its speed skipped over.

## Echo

The love is bound fast and built shallow.
When the passion cools, the commitment stands over an intimacy that was never made, and the couple must build it late -- doing belatedly the opening that would have grounded the pledge -- or hold an empty love out of the loyalty that remains.
A fatuous love that does the slow intimate work becomes consummate; one that does not thins toward the empty, a promise kept over nothing.
