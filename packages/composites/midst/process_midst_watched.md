# Process: Midst, the Watched

## Taxonomy

[Midst](process_midst.md), the form where being seen changes what a persona can do.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: midst

## Initiated by

Someone is there to see, and the persona's performance shifts before a word is spoken.
The Watched starts wherever [social facilitation](@chbrain/khai-engine-social-facilitation/process_social_facilitation.md) alters a persona's arousal and effort -- the [enhancement](@chbrain/khai-engine-social-facilitation/process_enhancement.md) that lifts what is easy and well-learned, the [choking](@chbrain/khai-engine-social-facilitation/process_choking.md) that spoils what is hard or newly learned, and the [loafing](@chbrain/khai-engine-social-facilitation/process_loafing.md) that slackens effort once it is pooled and no longer traceable.
It is presence at its plainest: not a command, not a judgement, only eyes -- and the doing already different.

## Direction

Toward the practised act sharpened and the difficult one spoiled, by the same arousal.
The Watched reads the persona under observation -- the runner faster in company, the speaker stumbling over what they knew alone, the rope pulled with less than full strength once several hands are on it -- one force raising the readiest response, which serves when the readiest response is the right one and betrays when it is not.
It moves the persona from doing to performing: the act now taking place in front of someone, and shaped by that before any of its own difficulty is met.

## Lever

The arousal that presence brings against the task that may not want it.
The carrying force is that being seen raises the persona's readiness -- attention narrows, effort rises, the well-drilled comes out better than in private; the resistance is that the same rise wrecks whatever needs a wide, unhurried attention, so the identical audience makes the expert better and the learner worse, and where the effort is pooled and no single hand can be traced, it quietly makes everyone less.
The Watched is presence at its most immediate: one force, doing opposite things according to what the task required.

## Echo

The act lifted, spoiled, or slackened by nothing more than eyes.
The Watched leaves the persona performing rather than simply doing -- better at what it knew, worse at what it was learning, or pulling less than its share -- and hands that altered doing to the spreading of responsibility and the massing that follow.
It is presence at its plainest working: the doing changed before anyone has asked anything of the doer.
The Watched is dated from the being-seen, the moment another's presence began to shape what the persona could do.
