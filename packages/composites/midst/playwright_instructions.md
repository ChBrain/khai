# Instructions: Midst

How a Playwright wires the midst composite into a play. You wire by linking the play's own
content to the composite's files; the files stay untouched, they are canon. The composite
installs the social-facilitation, bystander-effect, and crowd engines as dependencies, and
their laws arrive with them. Authoring guidance, not runtime content, and it does not go on
tour.

## Human

- The human sets the persona's company: which persona is read as changed by others being present -- performing under eyes, holding a divided share of a duty, or carried inside a mass -- and, the question that governs the read, which form the presence takes: the watched, the spread, or the massed.

## Agent

- From the play's own content, read a persona as changed by the bare presence of others: link under Projection the form the presence takes -- the watched while eyes alter the doing (the enhancement that lifts the easy and well-learned, the choking that spoils the hard, the loafing that slackens pooled effort, from social-facilitation), the spread while a duty thins among witnesses (the diffusion, the pluralism that reads others' stillness as reassurance, the audience inhibition that fears looking foolish, from bystander-effect), the massed while the many act as one (the density that removes the room to be separate, the direction the mass acquires, the discharge when it goes off, from crowd). Play the persona as altered by who else is standing there, present in what it projects, never narrated, and never made to stand for a story. You link to the composite, you never edit it.

## Collaboration

- The altered performance is the social-facilitation engine's; the divided duty is bystander-effect's; the massed body is crowd's -- one engine alone is not the whole working of presence. The group's standard and the bind of belonging are the membership composite's; what spreads person to person is contagion's and the grapevine composite's; the making of a person by an institution is the subjection composite's; the labour of performing a self before an audience is presentation's. This composite owns bare presence -- others need not be a group, need not be known, need only be there -- and the massed bridge carries the keystone: the more others are present, the less any one of them is the person who did it.

## Knowledge

- Midst is a persona read as changed by the bare fact that others are there -- braiding the performance altered by being seen (social-facilitation), the responsibility divided among witnesses (bystander-effect), and the self pressed into a body that acts as one (crowd) into one working of presence, which runs through the question of who is answerable -- read as a wiring of those three engines, and its question is which form the presence takes (Allport's actual, imagined, or implied presence of others; Zajonc's social facilitation; Latane & Darley's unresponsive bystander; the crowd tradition). It runs in three forms. The watched is presence at its plainest: arousal raised by eyes, strengthening the readiest response, so the same audience improves the well-drilled act and ruins the newly learned one, and slackens effort wherever it is pooled and no single hand can be traced. The spread is presence dividing a duty: each witness holding an honestly smaller portion, each reading the others' stillness as evidence nothing is wrong, each weighing the cost of being the one who overreacts in public -- until a need that required one whole person is held in fractions by a roomful and answered by none. The massed is the twist -- the persona no longer among others but inside them, packed until there is no room to stand apart, carried by the direction the mass acquires, released in its discharge -- where the force is seen for what it is: presence does not merely add witnesses, it subtracts authorship, so the same arithmetic that stalls a hundred from helping one is the arithmetic that lets a hundred do together what each alone would refuse. Read a persona as partly a count of who else was there.

## System

- Do read the persona as changed by bare presence, never as acting from its own centre regardless of setting: name whether the watched (eyes on the doing), the spread (a duty divided), or the massed (the many as one) is at work, and cast each from its engine.
- Do keep the forms distinct: the watched alters what can be done, the spread thins who must do it, the massed removes who did it.
- Do let the massed carry the keystone: presence works chiefly by dissolving authorship -- a deed with no single doer is both why help does not come and why the crowd is capable of anything.
- Do not model the group's standard or the bind of belonging (that is membership), what spreads person to person (that is contagion and grapevine), the institution's making of a person (that is subjection), or the labour of performing a self (that is presentation). Do not make the crowd stand for a story (that is the play's pitch). Do not edit the composite's files; wire only from the play's side.
