# Midst

Midst as composite: the persona among others -- its performance altered simply by being seen (social-facilitation), its responsibility divided until no one carries it (bystander-effect), and itself massed into a body whose acts have no single author (crowd) -- across the watched, the spread, and the massed, and the drama is that the more others are present, the less any one of them is the person who did it.
