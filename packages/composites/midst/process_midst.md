# Process: Midst

## Taxonomy

The persona among others.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: midst

## Initiated by

A persona is read as changed by the bare fact that others are there -- before anyone commands it, admits it, or ranks it.
Midst starts wherever the presence of others works on a persona -- the [social facilitation](@chbrain/khai-engine-social-facilitation/process_social_facilitation.md) that alters what it can do while it is seen, the [bystander effect](@chbrain/khai-engine-bystander-effect/process_bystander_effect.md) that divides its responsibility among the witnesses, and the [crowd](@chbrain/khai-engine-crowd/place_crowd.md) that presses it into a body acting as one.
This composite adds no fourth engine; it reads what the three make together: presence working on a persona through the question of who is answerable.

## Direction

Toward the whole work of others simply being there, read through its forms.
The composite wires the three engines and reads their working in three shapes: [the Watched](process_midst_watched.md), performance changed by eyes; [the Spread](process_midst_spread.md), responsibility thinned among witnesses; and [the Massed](process_midst_massed.md), the twist -- the many acting as one, and the deed with no author.
Others need do nothing to change a persona: their being there is already enough to lift its easy work and spoil its hard, to stall the help it would otherwise give, and at last to take its acts out of its own hands.

## Lever

The bare presence of others against the assumption that a persona acts as itself wherever it stands.
The carrying force is that presence is never neutral -- it arouses, it divides, it merges, and the same persona alone and among others is not reliably the same actor; the resistance is the belief that character carries across settings, that one who would help alone helps in a crowd, that one who performs alone performs when watched.
Midst is read wherever a persona is granted the company it is in: not a self acting from its own centre regardless, but one whose conduct is partly a fact about how many people are standing there.

## Echo

The persona revealed as a self altered by whoever else is present.
Midst does not close; it is the presence going on working, gathering the three engines into one reading -- a self watched, divided, and massed.
It hands off what it is not: the pressure of a group's standard and the bind of belonging to it are the membership composite's; the affect and talk that spread person to person are the contagion engine's and the grapevine composite's; the shaping of a person by an institution's forming, training, and command is the subjection composite's; the labour of performing a self before an audience is the presentation composite's; and the crowd made to stand for a story is authored key, the play's pitch, not the force.
The persona is dated from the company it stood in, and read as a self whose conduct is partly a count of who else was there.
