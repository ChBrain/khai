# Midst: Reference

## Line of Work

Midst as **composite**: the persona among others -- changed by the bare fact that others are
there, before anyone commands it, admits it, or ranks it. It is not a new force but a wiring
of three: the **social-facilitation** engine supplies the watching, performance altered by
being seen; the **bystander-effect** engine supplies the spreading, responsibility divided
among the witnesses; and the **crowd** engine supplies the massing, the persona pressed into
a body that acts as one. This composite adds no fourth engine; it reads what the three make
together -- presence working on a persona through the question of who is answerable -- and
stages that into three forms, closing on the discovery that the same arithmetic which stalls
a hundred people from helping one is the arithmetic that lets a hundred people do together
what each alone would refuse.

The domain does not model any single force -- the altered performance is the
social-facilitation engine's, the divided duty is bystander-effect's, the massed body is
crowd's, and one alone is a process or a place, not the whole working of presence. Nor is it
the pressure of a group's standard or the bind of belonging to it, which is the membership
composite's; nor the affect and talk that spread from person to person, which are the
contagion engine's and the grapevine composite's; nor the forming, training, and command by
which an institution makes a person, which is the subjection composite's; nor the labour of
performing a self before an audience, which is the presentation composite's. It owns bare
presence: the persona read not as acting from its own centre regardless of setting, but as
one whose conduct is partly a fact about how many people are standing there.

The spine is the founding observation of social psychology itself -- Allport's definition of
the field as the study of how thought, feeling, and behaviour are influenced by the actual,
imagined, or implied presence of others. Zajonc gave the first mechanism: presence raises
arousal, which strengthens the dominant response, so an audience improves the well-learned
and ruins the newly learned -- one force with opposite effects by task. Latané and Darley
gave the second: in an emergency, responsibility diffuses among witnesses, each reading the
others' inaction as evidence that nothing is wrong and each fearing to be the one who
overreacts, so the more people present the less likely any of them helps. And the crowd
tradition -- Le Bon's massed body with a will of its own, corrected by later work reading the
crowd not as a loss of mind but as a shift of the acting unit -- gives the third and the
composite's turn: the deed that belongs to the mass and to no one in it. The three bridges
are those readings staged: the watched, the spread, and the massed.

## Origin

The social psychology of the presence of others, wired as a composite over the
social-facilitation, bystander-effect, and crowd engines.

## Restrictions

What the composite refuses to model, and to whom it delegates.

- **The single engines (the atoms)**: The composite wires the three but owns none. The
  enhancement, choking, and loafing of a watched performance are the social-facilitation
  engine's; the diffusion, pluralism, and audience inhibition of a stalled help are
  bystander-effect's; the density, direction, and discharge of a massed body are crowd's.
  Midst reads only what the three make as one working of presence; a single engine alone is a
  process or a place, not the whole.
- **The group's standard and the bind of belonging (membership)**: The conformity a group
  presses, the identity drawn from it, the stigma it would attach, and the loyalty that raises
  the cost of leaving are the membership composite's. Midst is bare presence -- others need
  not be a group, need not be known, need only be there.
- **What spreads person to person (contagion / grapevine)**: The affect that catches and the
  talk that travels are the contagion engine's and the grapevine composite's. Midst is the
  effect of others being present, not the transmission of something between them.
- **The making of a person by an institution (subjection)**: The forming, training, and command
  by which institutional power produces a person is the subjection composite's. Midst is what
  mere company does, with no authority, no walls, and nothing asked.
- **The labour of being seen (presentation)**: The face claimed, the region held, the display
  tuned, and the feeling managed before an audience are the presentation composite's. Midst is
  what presence does to a persona whether or not it is performing at all.
- **The crowd as authored symbol (the play's pitch)**: A gathering made to **stand for** a
  story -- the mob that mirrors a theme of society's madness, the unhelped victim that mirrors
  a moral -- is refused: that is authored key, the play's pitch, not the force on the persona.
  Midst delivers what presence does; the meaning read into it is the play's.

## Encoding

Source to constraint. A composite root over three engine atoms it declares as dependencies,
with three bridges, the ways others' presence works on a persona.

- **[midst](process_midst.md)** (the root): the persona among others -- watched, divided, and
  massed by the bare fact that others are there. Anchored by Allport (the presence of others
  as the field's own subject).
- **[the watched](process_midst_watched.md)** (bridge): performance altered by eyes -- the easy
  lifted, the hard spoiled, the pooled slackened, all by one arousal. Anchored by Zajonc.
- **[the spread](process_midst_spread.md)** (bridge): responsibility divided among witnesses --
  diffusion, pluralistic ignorance, and audience inhibition, until a need everyone sees is
  answered by no one. Anchored by Latané & Darley.
- **[the massed](process_midst_massed.md)** (bridge): the many acting as one -- density,
  direction, discharge, and a deed with no author. Anchored by Le Bon and the crowd tradition
  after him; the composite's twist and keystone, that the more others are present, the less any
  one of them is the person who did it.
