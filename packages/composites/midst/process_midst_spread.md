# Process: Midst, the Spread

## Taxonomy

[Midst](process_midst.md), the form where responsibility thins among the witnesses.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: midst

## Initiated by

Something needs doing, many could do it, and each one's share of the duty grows smaller as the number grows.
The Spread starts wherever the [bystander effect](@chbrain/khai-engine-bystander-effect/process_bystander_effect.md) inhibits a persona -- the [diffusion](@chbrain/khai-engine-bystander-effect/process_diffusion.md) by which responsibility divides among all who could act, the [pluralism](@chbrain/khai-engine-bystander-effect/process_pluralism.md) by which each reads the others' stillness as evidence that nothing is wrong, and the [audience](@chbrain/khai-engine-bystander-effect/process_audience.md) inhibition that fears acting and looking foolish in front of everyone.
It is presence dividing a duty: the more who could answer, the less any one of them does.

## Direction

Toward a need that everyone sees and no one owns.
The Spread reads the duty thinning -- each witness holding a fraction of the obligation, each reading the others' calm as information, each weighing the cost of being the one who overreacts in public -- until the arithmetic that should make help likelier makes it rarer.
It moves the persona from a witness who would act to one who waits: not callous, but placed among enough others that the call was never quite addressed to it.

## Lever

The many who could act against the fact that a duty divided is a duty no one carries.
The carrying force is that responsibility feels shared when it is shared -- and each person's portion is honestly smaller with every additional witness, so no one is lying when they feel it was not particularly theirs; the resistance is that the need is not divisible at all: it required one whole person, and a roomful of people each holding a fraction produces less than a single passer-by holding all of it.
The Spread is presence at its most corrosive: the crowd that guarantees the need is seen and guarantees it is not met.

## Echo

The need witnessed by everyone and answered by no one.
The Spread leaves the persona still and unblaming of itself -- its portion small, the others' stillness reassuring, the risk of acting alone in public plain -- and hands that thinned answerability to the massing where it disappears entirely.
It is presence dividing what cannot be divided: a duty split until nothing of it lands.
The Spread is dated from the counting, the moment there were enough others present that the duty stopped being any one persona's.
