# Process: Midst, the Massed

## Taxonomy

[Midst](process_midst.md), the form where the many act as one, and the deed has no author.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: midst

## Initiated by

The persona is no longer among others but inside them, pressed into a body that moves and acts on its own account.
The Massed starts wherever the [crowd](@chbrain/khai-engine-crowd/place_crowd.md) takes a persona in -- the [density](@chbrain/khai-engine-crowd/place_density.md) that removes the space to be separate in, the [direction](@chbrain/khai-engine-crowd/place_direction.md) the mass acquires and carries everyone along, and the [discharge](@chbrain/khai-engine-crowd/place_discharge.md) in which what has gathered goes off.
It is presence at its fullest: not eyes on a persona, nor a duty divided among persons, but persons stopping being separate at all.

## Direction

Toward a body that acts, and members who did not each decide it.
The Massed reads the persona taken in -- the packing that leaves no room to step back, the current that makes standing still the difficult act, the moment the gathered pressure releases and the whole moves at once -- so that what happens is done by the crowd and not, in any way a person could point to, by anyone in it.
It moves the persona from an actor among actors to a part of an actor: still moving, still choosing in some fashion, but no longer the unit that the deed can be assigned to.

## Lever

The self given up to the mass against the answerability that goes with it.
The carrying force is that the crowd offers something no single person has -- a size, a certainty, a permission, and the relief of not being the one deciding; the resistance is that this is exactly the same subtraction the spreading began: the crowd does not merely hide the doer, it removes the doer, and so becomes capable of what none of its members would attempt or afterwards recognise as theirs.
The Massed rides the composite's keystone knife-edge: the more others are present, the less any one of them is the person who did it -- so the very presence that stalls a hundred people from helping one is the presence that lets a hundred people do together what each alone would refuse, one arithmetic producing both the paralysis and the violence.

## Echo

The many moving as one, and afterward no one who did it.
It shows a persona carried in a body it is now part of -- pressed, directed, discharged -- and carries beneath it the composite's keystone: that presence works on a persona chiefly by dissolving authorship, and a deed with no single doer is both the reason help does not come and the reason the crowd is capable of anything.
The Massed is the note the whole composite closes on: to be among others is never neutral, and the last thing others' presence takes is the person who could be held to it.
The Massed is dated from the massing, the moment the persona stopped being a separate one and the deed stopped having an author.
