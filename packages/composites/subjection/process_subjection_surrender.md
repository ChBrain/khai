# Process: Subjection, the Surrender

## Taxonomy

[Subjection](process_subjection.md), the working where the self is handed over, and the grip stops being felt.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: subjection

## Initiated by

The persona gives itself up -- to a command it takes as legitimate, or to a place that runs the whole of its life -- and carries out what it would not have chosen.
The Surrender starts wherever the self is handed over: in [obedience](@chbrain/khai-engine-obedience/process_obedience.md), where [legitimacy](@chbrain/khai-engine-obedience/process_legitimacy.md) and the [binding](@chbrain/khai-engine-obedience/process_binding.md) factors of the situation turn a persona into the [instrument](@chbrain/khai-engine-obedience/process_instrument.md) of another's will against its own conscience; and in the [total institution](@chbrain/khai-engine-total-institution/place_total_institution.md), where a [barrier](@chbrain/khai-engine-total-institution/place_barrier.md) cuts off the wider world, life is lived in a [batch](@chbrain/khai-engine-total-institution/place_batch.md) under one authority, and the self admitted is stripped by [mortification](@chbrain/khai-engine-total-institution/place_mortification.md).
It is the last making: the self no longer merely furnished and trained but given up.

## Direction

Toward a persona acting as another's instrument, and a self remade to fit the walls.
The Surrender reads the handover -- the persona who no longer feels itself the author of what it does, only the executor of a legitimate instruction, and the inmate whose name, clothes, hours, and privacy are taken and reissued -- and how each stops feeling like coercion: the command becomes duty, the routine becomes life, and the person accommodates.
It moves the persona from a self that is governed to a self that is administered, and at the far end to one that cannot easily live outside the arrangement that took it.

## Lever

The self given over against the fact that giving it over comes to feel like being oneself.
The carrying force is that surrender is offered as relief and as duty -- the responsibility passes upward, the decisions are made elsewhere, and there is a real ease in being told; the resistance is not the persona's protest but its absence, because the deepest hold is the one no longer experienced as a hold: the obedient persona reports doing its duty, the disciplined one polices itself, and the institutionalized one, released at last, finds it cannot manage the world outside and wants the walls back.
The Surrender rides the composite's keystone knife-edge: the grip is complete only when it stops being felt as a grip -- what begins as being subjected ends as being a self, so the conduct a persona is surest is its own is the best evidence of what made it.

## Echo

The self handed over, and the handing over experienced as ordinary life.
It shows a persona carrying out another's will or living a life the walls have arranged -- dutiful, adjusted, competent -- and carries beneath it the composite's keystone: that power finishes its work by disappearing into the person, so the absence of struggle is not the absence of force but its completion.
The Surrender is the note the whole composite closes on: subjection is not chiefly what is done to a self against its will, but what becomes of a will that has been formed, trained, and finally given up.
The Surrender is dated from the handing over, the moment the persona stopped being the author of its own conduct and did not feel the change.
