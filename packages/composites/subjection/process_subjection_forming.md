# Process: Subjection, the Forming

## Taxonomy

[Subjection](process_subjection.md), the working where a made world is taken as simply how things are.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: subjection

## Initiated by

A world built by people long dead is handed to a newcomer finished, and the newcomer takes it in until it is not a world at all, only reality.
The Forming starts wherever [socialization](@chbrain/khai-engine-socialization/process_socialization.md) does its work -- the [internalization](@chbrain/khai-engine-socialization/process_internalization.md) by which an objective social order becomes a persona's own subjective sense, the [secondary](@chbrain/khai-engine-socialization/process_secondary.md) inductions into each new sector afterward, until the persona stands as a fully [socialized](@chbrain/khai-engine-socialization/position_socialized.md) member.
It is the first making: not conduct trained nor a self surrendered, but a world made to look like the way things simply are.

## Direction

Toward a persona who finds the arrangement natural, and can no longer see it as an arrangement.
The Forming reads the world going in -- the language, the categories, the proprieties, the sense of what a person does and does not do -- taken from those who hold it as unquestionable, so what was one arrangement among many arrives as fact.
It moves the persona from outside to competent: fluent in a world they did not choose, and unable to see the choosing because the alternatives were never presented as alternatives.

## Lever

The world made natural against the arbitrariness it conceals.
The carrying force is that a person must be given a world to have one at all -- no self is assembled from nothing, and the ready-made order is what makes competence, belonging, and sense possible; the resistance is that the same gift hides its own contingency, presenting a particular history as the nature of things, so the persona defends it not as their side but as the truth.
The Forming is subjection at its most invisible: a making that leaves no mark because its product is the very sense of what is obvious.

## Echo

The person made a competent member of a world they take for granted.
The Forming leaves the persona fluent and settled -- knowing what is done, what is said, what is unthinkable -- and hands that formed sense to the training that will shape its conduct and the surrender that may claim it.
It is the first working of subjection: the world handed over and swallowed, and the arrangement now wearing the face of reality.
The Forming is dated from the internalizing, the moment a made world became, for the persona, simply how things are.
