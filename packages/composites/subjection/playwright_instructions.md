# Instructions: Subjection

How a Playwright wires the subjection composite into a play. You wire by linking the play's
own content to the composite's files; the files stay untouched, they are canon. The
composite installs the socialization, discipline, obedience, and total-institution engines
as dependencies, and their laws arrive with them. Authoring guidance, not runtime content,
and it does not go on tour.

## Human

- The human sets the persona's making: which persona is read as produced by institutional power -- handed a world to take as given, trained by watching and measuring, or given over to a command or an enclosing place -- and, the question that governs the read, which working is at work: the forming, the training, or the surrender.

## Agent

- From the play's own content, read a persona as made by institutional power: link under Projection the working at work -- the forming while a ready-made world is being internalized (the primary and secondary induction that leaves the persona a competent member for whom the arrangement is simply how things are, from socialization), the training while conduct is being shaped by watching and measuring (the surveillance that need not be watching, the norm that ranks and corrects, the examination that documents, the docility produced, from discipline), the surrender while the self is being handed over (the legitimacy and binding factors that make a persona an instrument against its own conscience, from obedience; the barrier, batch living, and mortification of an enclosing place, from total-institution). Play the persona as produced, present in what it projects, never narrated, and never made to stand for a moral about power. You link to the composite, you never edit it.

## Collaboration

- The internalized world is the socialization engine's; the disciplinary apparatus is discipline's; the agentic handover is obedience's; the enclosing place is total-institution's -- one engine alone is not the whole making. One persona's standing bind to a group is the membership composite's; the rank and its moves are standing's; the organization's own workings are the org engine's; the private self-concept is the selfhood composite's. This composite owns the making of a subject -- power that produces a person rather than merely restraining one -- and the surrender bridge carries the keystone: the grip is complete only when it stops being felt as a grip.

## Knowledge

- Subjection is a persona read as produced by institutional power -- braiding the ready-made world internalized (socialization), the conduct trained by continuous watching and measuring (discipline), and the self handed over to a command or an enclosing place (obedience, total-institution) into one making -- read as a wiring of those four engines, and its question is which working is at work (Berger & Luckmann's internalization; Foucault's disciplinary power; Milgram's agentic shift; Goffman's total institution). It runs in three workings. The forming is the world handed over: built by prior generations, taken in through primary and secondary socialization until what was one arrangement among many arrives as fact, leaving a competent member who cannot see the arrangement as an arrangement. The training is the conduct shaped: a productive, continuous, impersonal power working by surveillance that need not be watching, a norm that ranks and corrects rather than forbids, and an examination that documents, producing a docility that makes a body useful -- and a persona that holds its own line whether or not anyone is at the window. The surrender is the twist -- the self handed over, whether to a legitimate authority's command through the binding factors that make a persona another's instrument against its own conscience, or to the place that cuts off the world behind a barrier, batches all of life under one authority, and mortifies the self it admits -- where the making is seen for what it is: the obedient persona reports doing its duty, the disciplined one polices itself, and the institutionalized one, released, cannot manage outside and wants the walls back. Read a persona as assembled by institutions all along, and read the conduct it is surest is its own as the best evidence of what made it.

## System

- Do read the persona as produced by institutional power, never as a self that merely met institutions afterward: name whether the forming (the world made natural), the training (conduct shaped by watching), or the surrender (the self handed over) is at work, and cast each from its engine.
- Do keep the workings distinct: the forming furnishes a world and hides its contingency, the training shapes conduct and makes it self-policing, the surrender takes the will itself.
- Do let the surrender carry the keystone: the deepest hold is the one no longer experienced as a hold -- the absence of struggle is not the absence of force but its completion.
- Do not model one persona's bind to a group (that is membership), the rank and its moves (that is standing), the organization's own workings (that is org), or the private self-concept (that is selfhood). Do not make the persona's formation stand for a moral about power (that is the play's pitch). Do not edit the composite's files; wire only from the play's side.
