# Process: Subjection, the Training

## Taxonomy

[Subjection](process_subjection.md), the working where conduct is shaped by being watched and measured.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: subjection

## Initiated by

The persona is placed where it might be seen, ranked against a standard, and written down -- and its conduct begins to shape itself.
The Training starts wherever [discipline](@chbrain/khai-engine-discipline/process_discipline.md) works on a persona -- the [surveillance](@chbrain/khai-engine-discipline/process_surveillance.md) that need not be watching to be effective, the [norm](@chbrain/khai-engine-discipline/process_norm.md) that ranks and corrects rather than forbids, the [examination](@chbrain/khai-engine-discipline/process_examination.md) that measures and documents, producing the [docility](@chbrain/khai-engine-discipline/process_docility.md) that makes a body useful.
It is the second making: not a world internalized nor a self surrendered, but conduct trained by a power that is continuous, productive, and impersonal.

## Direction

Toward a persona who regulates itself because it might be seen.
The Training reads the discipline at work -- the possibility of observation doing what an observer would, the standard turning conduct into a rank and a rank into a correction, the record accumulating into a case -- so the persona comes to hold the line whether or not anyone is at the window.
It moves the persona from watched to self-watching: the guard internalized, the effort now the persona's own, and the power needing to spend less and less to get more.

## Lever

The watching that trains against the freedom it quietly spends.
The carrying force is that this power is productive -- it does not merely forbid, it builds: capacities, punctuality, precision, a body made useful and a self made competent, and much of what a persona is proud of is its work; the resistance is what it costs, since the same training that makes the persona capable makes it governable, and the docility is not a side effect but the point.
The Training is subjection at its most efficient: the moment the persona takes over the watching, the institution no longer has to.

## Echo

The person made capable, and made governable by the same means.
The Training leaves the persona holding its own line -- ranked, recorded, corrected, and competent -- and hands that trained conduct to the surrender that may ask it for everything.
It is the second working of subjection: a power that produces rather than represses, and whose finest achievement is a person who needs no guard.
The Training is dated from the watching, the moment the persona began to conduct itself as though it were seen.
