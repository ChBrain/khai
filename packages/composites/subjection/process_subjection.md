# Process: Subjection

## Taxonomy

The person made by institutional power.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: subjection

## Initiated by

A persona is read not as a self that institutions merely constrain but as a self they produce -- formed, trained, and taken over from outside.
Subjection starts wherever institutional power makes a person -- the ready-made world internalized through [socialization](@chbrain/khai-engine-socialization/process_socialization.md), the conduct trained by the continuous watching and measuring of [discipline](@chbrain/khai-engine-discipline/process_discipline.md), and the self handed over to the command of [obedience](@chbrain/khai-engine-obedience/process_obedience.md) or to the enclosing walls of a [total institution](@chbrain/khai-engine-total-institution/place_total_institution.md).
This composite adds no fifth engine; it reads what the four make together: power that produces a person rather than merely restraining one.

## Direction

Toward the whole making of a subject, read through its workings.
The composite wires the four engines and reads their working in three shapes: [the Forming](process_subjection_forming.md), the world made natural; [the Training](process_subjection_training.md), the conduct shaped by watching; and [the Surrender](process_subjection_surrender.md), the twist -- the self handed over, and the grip that stops being felt.
A person is not made in one stroke: they are handed a world to take as given, trained by a power that watches without needing to look, and at last brought to a place where the self is given up -- each stage leaving less that could have been otherwise.

## Lever

The power that produces a person against the belief that a self is only ever its own.
The carrying force is that institutions do not merely limit a self, they furnish it -- the language it thinks in, the routine it keeps, the standard it measures by, the authority whose word it takes; the resistance is the conviction that one's conduct is one's own, which is exactly the conviction the forming produces, and which makes the power hardest to see where it has worked best.
Subjection is read wherever a persona is granted its makers: not a self that arrived complete and met institutions afterward, but one that institutions have been assembling all along.

## Echo

The persona revealed as a self that institutional power has been making.
Subjection does not close; it is the making going on, gathering the four engines into one reading -- a self formed, trained, and handed over.
It hands off what it is not: one persona's standing bind to a group -- the identity drawn from it, the standard bent to, the mark that discredits, the allegiance that raises the cost of leaving -- is the membership composite's; the rank and its moves are the standing composite's; the organization's own workings are the org engine's; the persona's private self-concept is the selfhood composite's; and the making of a subject turned into a moral about power is authored key, the play's pitch, not the force.
The persona is dated from its own making, and read as a self whose most natural conduct is the deepest mark of what formed it.
