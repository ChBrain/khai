# Position: Complex PTSD, Diminishment

## Taxonomy

[Complex PTSD](process_cptsd.md), the disturbance of the self-concept -- a self collapsed into worthlessness and shame.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: cptsd

## Has

The second disturbance of self-organization: a self-concept collapsed into diminishment. ICD-11 names it precisely -- persistent beliefs about oneself as diminished, defeated, or worthless, accompanied by deep and pervasive feelings of shame, guilt, or failure related to the trauma. Sustained trauma, especially when it began early and came at the hands of caregivers, is internalized: the persona takes the degradation done to it as the truth about itself. It settles into a [low](@chbrain/khai-engine-self-esteem/position_low_esteem.md) resting attitude toward the self -- not a passing bad mood but a standing verdict of worthlessness -- soaked in a [shame](@chbrain/khai-engine-shame/process_shame.md) that is no longer an episode but a trait, the self appraised again and again as exposed, defective, and to blame. The persona believes, at the root, that it is bad.

## Orders

Hold yourself as diminished, defeated, and to blame, as the standing truth.
The diminishment movement orders the persona to carry a settled negative verdict on itself -- to expect its own worthlessness confirmed, to read its suffering as deserved, and to meet the world braced for the exposure of a self it believes is defective. It is not low confidence in a domain but a global condemnation of the self, steeped in shame. The room reads a persona that has taken the trauma's degradation as its identity: apologetic for existing, certain it is the problem, unable to credit its own worth.

## Loses

The baseline sense of its own worth, and the ground to stand on. Holding the diminished self, the persona forgoes the resting assumption that it is worthy of care and capable of good -- the floor most selves stand on without noticing -- and pays in a shame that colours everything: it cannot take praise, expects rejection, and reads every verdict as confirmation of the worst. It loses, too, the ability to protest its own mistreatment, having accepted the premise that it deserves it. What it trades is the intact self-regard the untraumatized keep for a standing conviction of its own worthlessness.

## Drives

On that collapsed self-concept:
holds as a standing verdict of worthlessness, steeped in shame -- the degradation of the trauma taken as the truth about the self. Diminishment is one of three disturbances, the self collapsed, set beside the [dysregulation](position_dysregulation.md) of affect and the [severance](position_severance.md) of the bond. That this collapse is the mark of a self reshaped by sustained trauma is the [root's](process_cptsd.md); the resting global attitude toward the self is the self-esteem engine's, and the self-directed appraisal of exposure is the shame engine's, where this movement owns the specifically trauma-diminished self that has taken its own degradation as identity.
