# Instructions: Complex PTSD

How a Playwright wires the cptsd composite into a play. You wire by linking the play's own content to the composite's files and, through them, to the five atom engines it depends on; every file stays untouched, they are canon. The Roadie plumbs the law into Instructions Knowledge on deploy. Authoring guidance, not runtime content, and it does not go on tour. This composite authors a complexly traumatized character for a play; it is not a diagnosis of any real person.

## Human

- The human sets the sustained, inescapable trauma: not a single incident but a prolonged, repeated one -- captivity, chronic abuse, trafficking, sustained war -- often begun early and run for years, from which the persona could not escape while it formed the self. It sets, too, which disturbance is most in play.

## Agent

- Cast the core PTSD from its atom (the ptsd engine: re-experiencing, evasion, estrangement, hyperarousal), then the three disturbances of self-organization the sustained trauma adds, along one of three movements: dysregulation, affect that will not settle -- flooding reactivity or numbed shutdown (the regulation engine); diminishment, the self collapsed into worthlessness and pervasive shame (the self-esteem and shame engines); severance, the bonds cut or unreachable, trust broken at the root (the attachment engine). A persona disturbed along a movement may link it under Projection; the audit surfaces the ones left unnamed. You link to the composite and its atoms, you never edit them.

## Collaboration

- The core disorder -- the memory that will not become past and its four clusters -- is the ptsd engine's, and complex PTSD carries all of it. The emotion-regulation machinery is the regulation engine's; the resting self-attitude is the self-esteem engine's and the self-directed appraisal of exposure the shame engine's; the strategies for closeness under threat are the attachment engine's. Ordinary low self-worth, a single failed reappraisal, or an insecure attachment on its own is not this syndrome -- the composite reads them only as the trauma-wrought disturbances they become atop full core PTSD. This composite supplies the read of the compound, as a character's architecture.

## Knowledge

- Complex PTSD is the syndrome of prolonged, repeated, inescapable trauma (Herman; ICD-11). It carries the whole of core PTSD and adds a pervasive disturbance in the organization of the self, because a trauma sustained long enough and entrapping enough becomes formative, not merely injurious -- especially when it begins in the years and relationships that build a person. Hold three disturbances, atop the core PTSD: dysregulation, an affect regulation broken down -- swinging between flooding reactivity and numbed, dissociative shutdown, the ordinary levers overrun (regulation); diminishment, a self-concept collapsed -- persistent beliefs of being diminished, defeated, worthless, steeped in deep and pervasive shame, the trauma's degradation taken as identity (self-esteem, shame); severance, relationships broken -- a persistent difficulty in feeling close and sustaining bonds, trust broken at the root by usually-interpersonal harm, attachment hardened toward the avoidant or the disorganized (attachment). Read the persona across the compound and the disturbance in play, and stage the self-disturbance as what survival under inescapable trauma cost -- reshaping, not gratuitous damage.

## System

- Do stage the sustained, inescapable trauma that makes the syndrome complex: duration and entrapment, not a worse single blow -- the self reshaped by what it could not escape.
- Do cast the whole of core PTSD first (the ptsd engine), then layer the three self-organization disturbances on top; complex PTSD is PTSD plus, not instead of.
- Do make the self-disturbance intelligible: the dysregulation, the worthlessness and shame, the severed bonds are what surviving prolonged trauma cost, and repair of them is deeper and slower than for single-incident trauma.
- Do not play ordinary low self-worth, a single regulation failure, or an insecure attachment as the syndrome; those are their own engines. Do not play single-incident PTSD as complex; that is the ptsd engine. Do not treat the composite as a diagnostic test for a real person, and do not diagnose adjacent syndromes; it authors a character. Do not restate the atoms' work; wire only from the play's side, and do not edit the composite's or the atoms' files.
