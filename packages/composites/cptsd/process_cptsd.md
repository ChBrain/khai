# Process: Complex PTSD

## Taxonomy

A composite reading over five atom engines (no canon file of its own): the ptsd, regulation, self-esteem, shame, and attachment engines this package declares as dependencies.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: cptsd

## Initiated by

A persona meets not a single trauma but a prolonged, repeated, inescapable one, and cannot get free of it while it forms.
The compound begins where single-incident trauma leaves off. Complex PTSD follows the traumas that are sustained and from which there is no escape -- captivity, chronic childhood abuse, trafficking, sustained war, prolonged domestic violence (Herman) -- often beginning early and running for years. What makes it complex is not a worse single blow but the duration and the entrapment: a danger that does not end, met by a persona who cannot flee, so that survival itself demands a reshaping of the self. What enters is a trauma long enough and inescapable enough to become the ground the persona grows in.

## Direction

From the whole of core PTSD, through the reshaping that sustained entrapment forces, toward a disturbed organization of the self.
Complex PTSD carries all of [core PTSD](@chbrain/khai-engine-ptsd/process_ptsd.md) -- the [reexperiencing](@chbrain/khai-engine-ptsd/process_reexperiencing.md), the [evasion](@chbrain/khai-engine-ptsd/process_evasion.md), the [estrangement](@chbrain/khai-engine-ptsd/position_estrangement.md), the [hyperarousal](@chbrain/khai-engine-ptsd/process_hyperarousal.md) -- and then adds what only sustained trauma does: it disturbs the organization of the self (ICD-11's disturbances in self-organization). Because the trauma ran through the years and relationships that build a person, it deforms them. Three disturbances result: the persona's affect will no longer [regulate](@chbrain/khai-engine-regulation/process_regulation.md), swinging between flood and numb; its [self-concept](@chbrain/khai-engine-self-esteem/position_low.md) collapses into worthlessness and a pervasive [shame](@chbrain/khai-engine-shame/process_shame.md); and its [relationships](@chbrain/khai-engine-attachment/process_attachment.md) are severed or unreachable, trust broken at the root. The direction is inward and structural -- not a wound the self carries, but a self the wound rebuilt.

## Lever

Whether the trauma was sustained enough to reshape the self, not merely to wound it. The whole distinction between complex and single-incident PTSD turns on this: the ordinary disorder is a self intact but haunted; the complex one is a self reorganized around the trauma, because the trauma lasted long enough, and trapped the persona completely enough, to become formative rather than merely injurious. Stage the entrapment and the duration -- the persona shaped by what it could not escape -- because that is what makes the self-disturbance intelligible rather than gratuitous: the dysregulation, the worthlessness, the severed bonds are what survival under sustained trauma cost. The lever of repair is correspondingly deeper and slower than for single-incident trauma: not only processing the memory but rebuilding the capacities -- affect, self, and bond -- the trauma deformed (Herman's stages; Cloitre's phase-based work).

## Echo

The syndrome surfaces through three movements, ICD-11's disturbances in self-organization, atop the core PTSD the ptsd atom carries: [dysregulation](position_dysregulation.md), the affect that will not settle, flooding or numbing where the regulation engine's levers fail; [diminishment](position_diminishment.md), the self collapsed into worthlessness and shame; and [severance](position_severance.md), the bonds cut or unreachable, trust broken at the root. The core PTSD -- the memory that will not become past and its four clusters -- is the ptsd engine's; the emotion-regulation machinery is the regulation engine's; the resting self-attitude is the self-esteem engine's and the self-directed appraisal of exposure the shame engine's; the strategies for closeness under threat are the attachment engine's. Complex PTSD is a self reorganized by a trauma it could not escape -- read across the compound and the three disturbances, and authored as a character's architecture, never diagnosed onto a real person.
