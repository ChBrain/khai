# Position: Complex PTSD, Severance

## Taxonomy

[Complex PTSD](process_cptsd.md), the disturbance of relationship -- bonds cut or unreachable, trust broken at the root.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: cptsd

## Has

The third disturbance of self-organization: a persistent difficulty in relationships. ICD-11 names it as persistent difficulties in sustaining relationships and in feeling close to others. Because complex trauma is so often interpersonal -- harm done by the very people who should have kept the persona safe -- it breaks trust at its root, and the persona carries that break into every bond after. The [attachment](@chbrain/khai-engine-attachment/process_attachment.md) strategies harden toward the insecure: an [avoidant](@chbrain/khai-engine-attachment/process_avoidant.md) keeping of distance, closeness felt as danger and shut out; or the [disorganized](@chbrain/khai-engine-attachment/process_disorganized.md) collapse of one who needs the other and fears them at once, drawn and terrified in the same breath, because the original source of comfort was also the source of harm. The persona may avoid relationships, sabotage them, or feel a stranger inside them.

## Orders

Hold others at a distance, or approach them in fear, because closeness was where the harm came from.
The severance movement orders the persona to meet connection as threat -- to keep others out, to disbelieve their care, or to want and dread them together -- so that the ordinary reaching for a secure base is jammed. It stages a persona for whom intimacy is not refuge but risk: guarded, hard to reach, or caught in the push-pull of needing what it cannot trust. The room sees relationships that will not hold -- kept shallow, cut off, or churned by a closeness that keeps turning to fear.

## Loses

The refuge of other people, and the trust that makes closeness possible. Carrying the severance, the persona forgoes the secure base -- the relationships that would let it be soothed, seen, and steadied -- precisely because the trauma taught it that the ones who come close are the ones who harm. It pays in isolation: cut off from the very repair that connection could offer, alone with the dysregulation and the shame the other disturbances carry. What it trades is the human refuge the securely attached draw on for a solitude that keeps it safe and keeps it wounded.

## Drives

On that broken bond:
holds others at a distance or approaches them in fear -- trust broken at the root, closeness met as threat -- where a persona with a secure base would reach for refuge. Severance is one of three disturbances, the bond cut, set beside the [dysregulation](position_dysregulation.md) of affect and the [diminishment](position_diminishment.md) of the self. That this rupture is the mark of a self reshaped by sustained, usually interpersonal, trauma is the [root's](process_cptsd.md); the strategies for seeking or giving up on closeness under threat are the attachment engine's, where this movement owns the specifically trauma-severed bond that carries a broken trust into every relationship after.
