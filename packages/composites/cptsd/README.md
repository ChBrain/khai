# Cptsd

Complex PTSD as composite: the syndrome of prolonged, repeated, inescapable trauma, which carries the whole of core PTSD and adds to it a pervasive disturbance in self-organization -- dysregulated affect (regulation), a diminished, worthless self-concept (self-esteem, shame), and severed relationships (attachment) -- across its dysregulation, diminishment, and severance movements (Herman; ICD-11; Cloitre; van der Kolk).
