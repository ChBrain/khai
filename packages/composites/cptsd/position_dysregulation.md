# Position: Complex PTSD, Dysregulation

## Taxonomy

[Complex PTSD](process_cptsd.md), the disturbance of affect -- feeling that will no longer regulate, flooding or numbing.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: cptsd

## Has

The first disturbance of self-organization: an affect regulation that has broken down under a load it was never built to carry. Sustained trauma overwhelms the ordinary capacity to modulate feeling, and the persona swings between two failures. At one pole, heightened emotional reactivity -- flooding, explosive anger, panic that arrives whole and will not be talked down, the [regulation](@chbrain/khai-engine-regulation/process_regulation.md) engine's levers of [reappraisal](@chbrain/khai-engine-regulation/process_reappraisal.md) overrun before they can act. At the other, the opposite failure -- dissociation, shutdown, a numbing that solves the flood by feeling nothing, an over-reliance on [suppression](@chbrain/khai-engine-regulation/process_suppression.md) that empties the persona out. It is not that the persona will not regulate but that, after prolonged trauma, it largely cannot: the machinery is deranged.

## Orders

Meet feeling with too much or too little, because the middle is gone.
The dysregulation movement orders the persona to lose the modulated register -- to flood where a smaller feeling would fit, or to go numb where feeling should be -- so that its affect reads as either overwhelming or absent, rarely proportionate. The room sees a persona swinging: raw and reactive one moment, blank and dissociated the next, with little of the ordinary capacity to bring a feeling up or down to size. It stages affect as a system knocked out of true, not a mood.

## Loses

The proportion and the recoverability of ordinary feeling. Carrying the dysregulation, the persona forgoes the modulated middle -- the capacity to feel something at its right size and to come back down from it -- and pays either way: the flooding costs it control and exhausts it, the numbing costs it presence and the good feelings along with the bad. It loses, too, the trust that its own reactions are safe, which feeds the other disturbances -- the shame of the outburst, the distance the numbing builds. What it trades is the everyday governance of feeling for a system that overshoots or shuts off.

## Drives

On that broken regulation:
swings between flood and numb -- reactivity the levers cannot catch, or a shutdown that feels nothing -- where a persona with intact regulation would modulate. Dysregulation is one of three disturbances, the affect that will not settle, set beside the [diminishment](position_diminishment.md) of the self and the [severance](position_severance.md) of the bond. That this failure is the mark of a self reshaped by sustained trauma is the [root's](process_cptsd.md); the machinery of situation, reappraisal, and suppression it strains is the regulation engine's, where this movement owns the specifically trauma-broken affect that the ordinary levers can no longer hold.
