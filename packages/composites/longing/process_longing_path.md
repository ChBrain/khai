# Process: Longing, the Path

## Taxonomy

[Longing](process_longing.md), the distance where the gap is an outcome not chosen.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: longing

## Initiated by

The persona aches at the distance between the outcome it got and a better one it can imagine having had.
The Path starts wherever a persona measures a choice against its alternative -- the [regret](@chbrain/khai-engine-regret/process_regret.md) of a [commission](@chbrain/khai-engine-regret/process_commission.md) it wishes undone or an [inaction](@chbrain/khai-engine-regret/process_inaction.md) it wishes taken, and the [anticipation](@chbrain/khai-engine-regret/process_anticipation.md) of the regret a choice might bring.
It is longing for the untaken path: not a missing other or a lost time but a counterfactual, the better outcome the persona can picture and did not reach.

## Direction

Toward the outcome that might have been, measured against the one that is.
The Path reads the persona holding the actual against the imagined -- the road taken against the road not, the thing done against the thing left undone -- the sting sharpest where the better outcome is easiest to picture and the choice was most its own.
It moves the persona to the ache of the counterfactual: the outcome it can see so clearly and cannot have, the distance between what it chose and what it might have.

## Lever

The better outcome imagined against the choice that cannot be unmade.
The carrying force is that a persona can always imagine the better path and measure its choice against it; the resistance is that the choice is made and cannot be unmade -- the counterfactual stays vivid and stays out of reach, and the anticipation of it can freeze the next choice as much as the memory of it stings the last.
The Path is longing at its most counterfactual: the ache toward an outcome the persona can picture perfectly and reach never, the distance between the actual and the imagined-better.

## Echo

The persona aching at the path it did not take.
The Path leaves the persona measuring a choice against its alternative -- the commission it wishes undone, the inaction it wishes taken -- and carries the longing's shared shape: an ache that marks the better outcome as one it valued.
It is longing toward the untaken path: the sorrow of an outcome imagined and not had, the distance between the choice made and the one that might have been.
The Path is dated from the measuring, the moment the persona held its choice against the better one it could imagine.
