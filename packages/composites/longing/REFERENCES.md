# Longing: Reference

## Line of Work

Longing as **composite**: the self aching at a gap between what is and what it wants,
remembers, or might have had. It is not a new emotion but a wiring of three: the
**loneliness** engine supplies the absent **other** -- the pain of a needed connection that
is not present; the **regret** engine supplies the untaken **path** -- a choice measured
against the better outcome the persona can imagine; and the **nostalgia** engine supplies
the lost **past** -- the bittersweet longing for one's own history. This composite adds no
fourth engine; it reads what the three share -- a felt distance between the actual and the
desired, remembered, or possible, registered as longing -- and stages that into three
distances a self measures, closing on the discovery that the ache is the measure of what
mattered.

The domain does not model any single force -- the relational pain is the loneliness
engine's, the counterfactual sorrow is regret's, the bittersweet return is nostalgia's, and
one alone is a process, not the shared ache. Nor is it the co-occurrence of joy and sadness
as such -- any mixed feeling -- which is the bittersweet composite's; nor the gap between a
persona's actual and ideal or ought self, which is the self-discrepancy engine's; nor the
grief of a death witnessed as an undeserved fall, which is the tragic composite's. It owns
the longing: the persona reaching across an absence it cannot close, read not as a failure
to be content but as a self still holding what it has lost, and defined by what it aches
toward.

The spine is the recognition that longing is one appraisal wearing three faces -- a
self-relevant discrepancy between the actual and a valued reference, felt as a reach across
a distance. Cacioppo read the relational face: loneliness as social pain, the gap between
the connection a person needs and the one they have, an alarm evolved to drive reconnection.
Roese read the counterfactual face: regret as the comparison of what is against what might
have been, sharpest where the better outcome is most imaginable. And Sedikides and Wildschut
read the temporal face and, with it, the whole composite's turn: nostalgia as a bittersweet
longing that is not merely loss but a restorer of connectedness, continuity, and meaning --
the ache that gives back. The three bridges are those readings staged: the other, the path,
and the past.

## Restrictions

What the composite refuses to model, and to whom it delegates.

- **The single engines (the atoms)**: The composite wires the three but owns none. The pain
  of a missing connection -- the numbness, the withdrawal, the reaching -- is the loneliness
  engine's; the measure of a choice against its better alternative -- commission, inaction,
  anticipated regret -- is regret's; the bittersweet return to one's past and what it restores
  is nostalgia's. Longing reads only what the three share as one ache; a single engine alone
  is a process, not the whole.
- **The mixed feeling as such (bittersweet)**: The co-occurrence of joy and sadness, any two
  opposite valences running at once, is the bittersweet composite's. Nostalgia is bittersweet,
  but longing reads the gap-structure -- the reach across an absence -- not the blend of
  valences; a persona can long without joy, and feel bittersweet without longing.
- **The self against its own standards (self-discrepancy)**: The gap between a persona's actual
  self and its ideal or ought self is the self-discrepancy engine's. Longing is the ache toward
  an absent other, an untaken path, or a lost past -- a distance to what is outside or behind
  the self, not to the self it fails to be.
- **The witnessed fall (grief / tragic)**: The grief of a death or a loss taken in and shaped
  as an undeserved fall is the tragic composite's. Longing is the standing reach across an
  absence, not the acute grief of a bereavement nor the pity and fear of witnessing one.
- **The longing as authored symbol (the play's pitch)**: A persona's longing made to **stand
  for** a story -- the nostalgia that mirrors a theme of lost innocence, the regret that mirrors
  a moral -- is refused: that is authored key, the play's pitch, not the force on the persona.
  Longing delivers the persona's own ache; the meaning read into it is the play's.

## Encoding

Source to constraint. A composite root over three engine atoms it declares as dependencies,
with three bridges, the distances a self measures as one longing.

- **[longing](process_longing.md)** (the root): the self aching at a gap between what is and
  what it wants, remembers, or might have had -- one longing across three distances. Anchored
  by Sedikides & Wildschut (longing as bittersweet, value-bearing).
- **[the other](process_longing_other.md)** (bridge): the relational gap -- the pain that the
  needed connection is not the actual one, numbing, withdrawing, or driving a reach. Anchored
  by Cacioppo (loneliness as social pain).
- **[the path](process_longing_path.md)** (bridge): the counterfactual gap -- a choice measured
  against the better outcome imagined, the sting of the road not taken. Anchored by Roese
  (counterfactual regret).
- **[the past](process_longing_past.md)** (bridge): the temporal gap -- the bittersweet longing
  for one's own past that restores connectedness, continuity, and meaning. Anchored by Sedikides
  & Wildschut; the composite's twist and keystone, that the ache is the measure of what mattered.
