# Process: Longing

## Taxonomy

The self aching at a gap it cannot close.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: longing

## Initiated by

A persona is read not as at rest in what it has but as reaching across a distance to what it does not -- what it wants, remembers, or might have had.
Longing starts wherever a persona aches at a gap between the actual and the desired, the remembered, or the possible -- the absent other it needs ([loneliness](@chbrain/khai-engine-loneliness/process_loneliness.md)), the untaken path it can imagine having chosen ([regret](@chbrain/khai-engine-regret/process_regret.md)), and the lost past it is drawn back to ([nostalgia](@chbrain/khai-engine-nostalgia/process_nostalgia.md)).
This composite adds no new emotion; it reads what the three make together: one longing measured across three distances, the felt gap between what is and what mattered.

## Direction

Toward the whole ache of absence, read through its distances.
The composite wires the three engines and reads their working in three shapes: [the Other](process_longing_other.md), the missing connection; [the Path](process_longing_path.md), the outcome not chosen; and [the Past](process_longing_past.md), the twist -- the lost time, and the ache seen for what it is.
A persona does not long in one direction only: it reaches for the other it is parted from, the path it did not take, and the past it cannot return to -- three distances, one measure, the space between what it has and what it wanted.

## Lever

The persona's reach toward what is absent against the wish to want only what it has.
The carrying force is that a persona cannot help measuring the gap -- between the connection it needs and the one it has, the outcome it got and the one it can imagine, the present and a cherished past; the resistance is the wish to be free of the ache, to close the distance by ceasing to want across it, to hold only what is present and long for nothing.
Longing is read wherever a persona is granted its reach across an absence: not a self content in what it has but one that aches at the distance to what it does not, and is defined by what that distance is drawn toward.

## Echo

The persona revealed as a self that aches at what is not there.
Longing does not close; it is the persona going on reaching across its distances, gathering the three engines into one reading -- a self drawn toward an absent other, an untaken path, a lost past.
It hands off what it is not: the joy and sadness felt together, any mixed feeling, is the bittersweet composite's, not this ache at a gap; the gap between a persona's actual and ideal self is the self-discrepancy engine's; the grief of a death taken in and witnessed as a fall is the tragic composite's; and the longing made to stand for a story is authored key, the play's pitch, not the force.
The persona is dated from its own reaching, and read as a self that aches at a distance, and is measured by what it aches toward.
