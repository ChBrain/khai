# Process: Longing, the Past

## Taxonomy

[Longing](process_longing.md), the distance where the gap is a cherished time now gone, and the ache is seen for what it is.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: longing

## Initiated by

The persona aches at the distance between the present and a cherished past, and the ache turns out to give as much as it costs.
The Past starts wherever a persona is drawn back to its own history -- the bittersweet [nostalgia](@chbrain/khai-engine-nostalgia/process_nostalgia.md) that restores a sense of [connectedness](@chbrain/khai-engine-nostalgia/process_connectedness.md) and [continuity](@chbrain/khai-engine-nostalgia/process_continuity.md), lends [positivity](@chbrain/khai-engine-nostalgia/process_positivity.md), and answers an [existential](@chbrain/khai-engine-nostalgia/process_existential.md) need for meaning.
It is longing for the lost past: a temporal gap that hurts to feel and yet warms in the feeling, the ache that is not only loss.

## Direction

Toward the past the persona cannot return to, and the meaning the return in feeling brings back.
The Past reads the persona drawn to a time now gone -- the sweetness and the sorrow at once -- and what the drawing does: not only the pain of the distance but the connectedness, the continuity of a self across time, the meaning the remembered past restores to the present.
It moves the persona through the ache to what the ache yields: a longing that, in reaching back, carries something of the valued past forward.

## Lever

The ache of the gap against the value the ache turns out to hold.
The carrying force is a self drawn back to what it cannot return to; the resistance is not that the past is unreachable -- it plainly is -- but that the longing refuses to be only loss: the ache is bittersweet, and in the reaching it restores connection, continuity, and meaning, so the very pain of the distance is the sign of what the past was worth.
The Past rides the composite's keystone knife-edge: the ache is the measure of what mattered -- a persona longs, here and in every distance, only for what it valued, so the pain of the gap is the value persisting, the loss and the love the one feeling.

## Echo

The persona aching at a lost time, and warmed by the aching.
It shows a persona drawn back to its own past -- the sweetness and the sorrow together -- and carries beneath it the composite's keystone: that a self aches at a gap only because the thing across it mattered, so the longing is not merely lack but the value enduring, the caring that outlasts the having.
The Past is the note the whole composite closes on: longing is not a wound to be closed but the shape of a self still holding what it has lost, reaching toward the other, the path, and the past because each was worth reaching for.
The Past is dated from the drawing-back, the moment the persona reached toward a cherished time it could not return to.
