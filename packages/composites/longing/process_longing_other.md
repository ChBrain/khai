# Process: Longing, the Other

## Taxonomy

[Longing](process_longing.md), the distance where the gap is a missing connection.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: longing

## Initiated by

The persona aches at the distance between the connection it needs and the one it has.
The Other starts wherever a persona feels the relational gap -- the pain of [loneliness](@chbrain/khai-engine-loneliness/process_loneliness.md), the [numbness](@chbrain/khai-engine-loneliness/process_numbness.md) and [withdrawal](@chbrain/khai-engine-loneliness/process_withdrawal.md) it can bring, and the [reaching](@chbrain/khai-engine-loneliness/process_reaching.md) it can drive toward another.
It is longing for the absent other: not a lost time or an untaken path but a needed connection that is not present, the ache at the space where another should be.

## Direction

Toward the connection that is not there, felt as its absence.
The Other reads the persona measuring the relational distance -- the gap between the closeness it needs and the closeness it has -- and what that gap does to it, numbing and withdrawing it further, or driving it to reach back toward another.
It moves the persona to the ache of separateness: the missing other felt as a pain that is, at root, an alarm -- the signal that connection matters and is not here.

## Lever

The need for the other against the absence that will not fill.
The carrying force is that a persona needs connection and registers its lack as pain; the resistance is the absence itself, the other not present to close the gap, and the risk that the pain turns inward -- to the numbness and withdrawal that widen the distance rather than crossing it.
The Other is longing at its most relational: the ache that the needed other is not here, a pain that is the measure of how much the connection is wanted.

## Echo

The persona aching at the space where another should be.
The Other leaves the persona reaching across a relational gap -- numbed and withdrawn, or driven back toward connection -- and carries the longing's shared shape: an ache that marks what is missing as what is needed.
It is longing toward the absent other: the pain of a connection wanted and not present, the alarm that another matters and is not here.
The Other is dated from the felt gap, the moment the persona registered the distance between the connection it needed and the one it had.
