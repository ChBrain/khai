# Process: Unmoored, the Roam

## Taxonomy

[Unmoored](process_unmoored.md), attention free, and the state the other two are deviations from.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: unmoored

## Initiated by

Attention comes off the task and goes somewhere of its own.
The Roam starts wherever [mind wandering](@chbrain/khai-engine-mind-wandering/process_mind_wandering.md) is running -- the [unbidden](@chbrain/khai-engine-mind-wandering/process_unbidden.md) thought that arrives without being sent for, the [zoning out](@chbrain/khai-engine-mind-wandering/process_zoning_out.md) that takes the persona off without their noticing, and the [tuning out](@chbrain/khai-engine-mind-wandering/process_tuning_out.md) that lets a dull thing run while the mind goes elsewhere.
It is the face of the composite that costs a paragraph and returns a solution.

## Direction

Toward wherever the mind goes when nothing is holding it, and back again with something.
The Roam reads the decoupling as a working faculty -- the walk that untangles the problem, the answer arriving in the shower, the connection made between two things the task had no reason to put together -- and reads the two ways a persona enters it, since letting the mind go and having it leave are different states with different consequences, and only the first is available on purpose.
It moves the persona off the task and, often enough to be worth the loss, somewhere better.

## Lever

The yield of an undirected search against the cost of not being where one is.
The carrying force is that the search is genuinely undirected and that is the point: a mind constrained to the task cannot reach what the task does not suggest, so the wandering is not a lapse in attention but the mode in which the remote thing gets found; the resistance is that the same untargeted quality means the persona cannot say in advance what it will land on -- the roam has no aim, only a range, and it is availability rather than intention that decides what comes into it.
The Roam spends the present moment and is usually worth it.

## Echo

Somewhere else, and back with something.
It is the bridge that establishes the composite's mechanism as a capacity rather than a failure -- which is what makes the other two legible, since they are not different faculties but this one meeting different conditions.
What it hands upward is the variable everything turns on: the roam is what the decoupling does when there is nothing pressing in reach, and the reach is not the persona's to set.
The Roam is dated from the moment attention left, which the persona usually could not name, and from what it happened to find.
