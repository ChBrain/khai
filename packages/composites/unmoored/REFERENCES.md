# Unmoored: Reference

## Line of Work

Unmoored as **composite**: attention that has come off the task. It is not a fourth mechanism but
a wiring of three: the **mind-wandering** engine supplies the decoupling itself, the **boredom**
engine supplies attention mobilised with nothing the situation will supply, and the **rumination**
engine supplies attention locked on something that will not resolve. This composite adds no fourth
engine; it reads the seam the three make -- one decoupling in three states -- and stages it into
three bridges, closing on the discovery that what separates them is not a quality of the mind
doing it.

This is the second composite to wire the mind-wandering engine, and it reads it the other way
round from the first. The creativity composite takes the drift as a stage of making something: the
incubating pass that readies an insight, wired to divergent thinking and paid off by the aha. Here
the same faculty is read as one of the ways attention leaves a task, and the question is not what
it produces but what it lands on. The engine is unchanged and both readings are true of it; an atom
carries a phenomenon, and a composite carries a question asked of it.

The domain does not model any single state -- the unbidden, zoning-out, and tuning-out modes are
the mind-wandering engine's; the searching, reactant, indifferent, and apathetic forms are
boredom's; brooding, replaying, and reflection are rumination's; and one alone is a process, not
the condition attention is in. Nor is it attention **held**, which is the absorption composite's
and this composite's exact complement; nor a deliberate going-elsewhere into a made world, which
is elsewhere's; nor the machinery of holding a task at all, which is volition's. It owns the
off-task state and what decides which one a persona is in.

The join is declared inside two of the three atoms. The boredom engine anchors Eastwood, Frischen,
Fenske and Smilek, whose definition is attentional and is the reason the first seam holds: boredom
is the state of wanting to be engaged and being unable, which places it against successful
engagement rather than against stimulation, and therefore alongside the wandering mind rather than
opposite it. The rumination engine anchors Watkins on constructive and unconstructive repetitive
thought, which supplies the second seam and the composite's sharpest instrument: what makes
repetitive thought arrive somewhere is not its subject and not its quantity but its processing
mode. Held abstractly and evaluatively -- why did this happen, what does it say about me -- the
material does not terminate, because the question has no terminating answer; held concretely and
experientially -- what happened, what now -- the same material runs out. Two passes over identical
content, opposite outcomes, and nothing in the experience marks which is which except that the
first feels deeper.

Smallwood and Schooler supply the shared mechanism the three run on: attention decoupled from the
world and carried inward, a capacity rather than a fault, with costs and benefits that are two
faces of one process. Schooler and colleagues add the meta-awareness finding that explains why a
persona is such a poor witness to their own state -- the decoupling frequently runs without the
persona noticing it has, so the report arrives late and after the fact. And Seli and colleagues
separate intentional from unintentional wandering, which is the difference between letting the mind
go and having it leave: the two have different correlates, and only the first is available on
purpose.

What the composite adds on top of those joins is the claim that follows from putting them
together. If the decoupling is one faculty, and if its landing is set by what is in reach rather
than chosen, and if what it then does with what it found is set by mode rather than content, then
the three states are not three dispositions and not three severities. They are one mechanism under
three conditions: nothing pressing in reach (the roam), nothing that takes (the itch), and
something unresolved in reach held in the mode that does not terminate (the loop). Which is why
the ordinary remedies miss: supplying more to do answers the itch's surface and reproduces its
cause, and telling a persona to think about something else targets content when the variable is
mode.

**On the evidence, stated plainly.** The attentional definition of boredom and the processing-mode
account of repetitive thought are the two the composite rests on, and both are well supported
theoretically and empirically within their literatures. The decoupling account of mind-wandering is
mainstream, though the field has moved on the measurement -- the intentional/unintentional
distinction is a relatively recent refinement and the experience-sampling instruments carry known
limits, since they depend on self-report from a state whose defining feature is that the persona
often does not notice being in it. The much-quoted finding that a wandering mind is an unhappy mind
is carried by the mind-wandering engine and is not load-bearing here: this composite claims that
what attention lands on and how it is held decide the outcome, which is a weaker and better
supported claim than any general relation between wandering and mood. The three bridges are the
roam, the itch, and the loop.

## Origin

The mind-wandering, boredom, and rumination literatures, wired as a composite over those three
engines. The first two rows are the **joining** -- the published work that sets the three as one
subject and supplies what separates them -- and the rest anchor the individual bridges and their
bounds.

## Restrictions

What the composite refuses to model, and to whom it delegates.

- **The three states (the atoms)**: The composite wires the three but owns none. The unbidden,
  zoning-out, and tuning-out modes are the mind-wandering engine's; the searching, reactant,
  indifferent, and apathetic forms are boredom's; brooding, replaying, and reflection are
  rumination's. Unmoored reads only what decides which state a persona is in.
- **The same drift toward a product (creativity)**: The creativity composite wires the
  mind-wandering engine as the incubating pass between divergent thinking and the aha, and reads
  the drift for what it yields. This composite reads the same engine for what it lands on. Both are
  true of it, and a persona can be linked to both at once -- the difference is the question, not
  the phenomenon, and neither composite owns the atom.
- **Attention held (absorption)**: Attention, flow, and time-perception are the absorption
  composite's, which owns the hour that passed unnoticed. That is this composite's exact
  complement, and the pair should not be run as a scale: a persona is not partly absorbed and
  partly unmoored, they are on the task or off it.
- **The deliberate elsewhere (elsewhere)**: Imagination, transportation, and memory are the
  elsewhere composite's -- a going-somewhere that is entered and furnished. The roam is undirected
  and mostly unchosen; where a persona has set out for a made world on purpose, that is elsewhere's
  reading and not this one.
- **Attention captured by a lack (slack)**: The slack composite's tunnel is attention taken by a
  felt shortfall, which is a **target** and a very engaging one. The itch is the opposite condition
  -- mobilised attention with nothing that takes -- and the two are frequently confused because both
  leave a persona unable to attend to what is in front of them.
- **The machinery of holding a task (volition)**: Executive function, the goal, and the if-then
  plan are the volition composite's. Unmoored reads what happens once the holding has failed, and
  is indifferent to how good the machinery was.
- **What the persona finds when they land (self-conscious, longing, prospection)**: The shame, the
  guilt, the regret, the nostalgia, the dread are those composites'. Unmoored reads only that
  attention landed there and did not leave, and it does not model the content it circles.
- **The distraction as authored symbol (the play's pitch)**: An absence made to **stand for** a
  theme -- the character who drifts to mirror a motif of disengagement -- is refused: that is
  authored key, the play's pitch, not the force. Unmoored sets where attention went; the meaning
  read into it is the play's.

## Encoding

Source to constraint. A composite root over three engine atoms it declares as dependencies, with
three bridges, told apart by what is in reach and how it is held.

- **[unmoored](process_unmoored.md)** (the root): attention that has come off the task, read as one
  faculty in three conditions rather than as three dispositions. Anchored by Smallwood & Schooler
  (2015) on decoupling and Schooler et al. (2011) on the meta-awareness lag.
- **[the roam](process_unmoored_roam.md)** (bridge): attention free, undirected, and returning with
  things the task would not have suggested. Anchored by Smallwood & Schooler and by Seli et al.
  (2016) on the intentional/unintentional split.
- **[the itch](process_unmoored_itch.md)** (bridge): attention mobilised and finding no target the
  situation will supply, whose paradigm case is a persona surrounded by options. Anchored by
  Eastwood et al. (2012) and by Elpidorou and Goetz et al. on the signal and its types.
- **[the loop](process_unmoored_loop.md)** (bridge): the same decoupling with something unresolved
  in reach, held in the mode that does not terminate. Anchored by Watkins (2008) on processing mode
  and Nolen-Hoeksema, Wisco & Lyubomirsky (2008); the composite's twist and keystone, that the mode
  which feels most like getting to the bottom of it is the one that never does, so the faculty that
  incubates a solution is the faculty that circles a humiliation.
