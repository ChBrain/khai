# Process: Unmoored

## Taxonomy

Attention that has come off the task.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: unmoored

## Initiated by

A persona is meant to be attending to something and is not.
Unmoored starts wherever that is running: the decoupling itself, through [mind wandering](@chbrain/khai-engine-mind-wandering/process_mind_wandering.md); attention mobilised with nothing the situation will supply, through [boredom](@chbrain/khai-engine-boredom/process_boredom.md); and attention locked on a thing that will not resolve, through [rumination](@chbrain/khai-engine-rumination/process_rumination.md).
This composite adds no fourth engine. It reads that these are one decoupling in three states, which both flanks already declare: the boredom atom defines its subject attentionally, against engagement rather than against stimulation, and the rumination atom carries the processing-mode axis that separates repetitive thought which arrives somewhere from repetitive thought which does not.

## Direction

Toward whatever attention has found instead, and toward whether it can come back.
The composite wires the three engines and reads their working in three shapes: [the Roam](process_unmoored_roam.md), attention free; [the Itch](process_unmoored_itch.md), attention seeking; and [the Loop](process_unmoored_loop.md), the twist -- attention stuck.
The three are not three temperaments and not three degrees of the same malaise. They are one faculty, in three conditions set by what is within reach and how it is being held.

## Lever

The usefulness of a mind that can leave the task against the fact that leaving is not a thing it can be selective about.
The carrying force is that the decoupling is a capacity rather than a fault: a mind that could not come off the task would have no incubation, no rehearsal, no going-over, and the same faculty that loses the paragraph is the one that solves the problem in the shower; the resistance is that it does not choose its landing, so what it finds is whatever is in reach -- and a persona with something unresolved and painful available has the same mechanism running, on that.
Unmoored is read on the persona whose attention has left, and it is indifferent to the quality of the mind: the state is set by the inventory and the mode, not by the person.

## Echo

A persona somewhere other than where they are, and a question about whether they will return with anything.
Unmoored does not close; it is the decoupling running, gathered into one reading -- a roam, an itch, a loop.
It hands off what it is not: attention **held** -- the focus, the flow, the hour that passed unnoticed -- is the absorption composite's, this composite's exact complement; the drift read as a stage of making something, incubating toward an insight, is the creativity composite's, which wires the same engine to a different question and gets a different answer; a deliberate going-elsewhere into a made world, imagined, transported, or remembered, is the elsewhere composite's, where this is undirected and mostly unchosen; attention captured by a felt lack, which is a target rather than the absence of one, is the slack composite's tunnel; the machinery of holding a task in the first place is the volition composite's; what the persona finds when they land -- the shame, the regret, the dread -- belongs to the self-conscious, longing, and prospection composites, and this reads only that they landed there and stayed; and a distraction made to **stand for** a story is authored key, the play's pitch, not the force.
The persona is dated from what was in reach when their attention left.
