# Process: Unmoored, the Loop

## Taxonomy

[Unmoored](process_unmoored.md), attention stuck, and where the three are seen for one thing.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: unmoored

## Initiated by

Attention comes off the task, and what is in reach is a thing that has not been settled.
The Loop starts wherever [rumination](@chbrain/khai-engine-rumination/process_rumination.md) is running -- the [brooding](@chbrain/khai-engine-rumination/process_brooding.md) over why this happened and what it says about the persona, the [replaying](@chbrain/khai-engine-rumination/process_replaying.md) of the scene with the sentence they should have said, and the [reflection](@chbrain/khai-engine-rumination/process_reflection.md) that turns the same material over without arriving.
It is the face of the composite that feels, from inside, like work.

## Direction

Toward the bottom of the thing, and never reaching it.
The Loop reads the circling rather than the sadness -- the same three minutes rehearsed on the drive home, the question asked again in the same words, the conclusion approached and not taken -- and reads what makes it circle: not the amount of thinking and not the subject, but the mode. Held abstractly and evaluatively, as **why did this happen and what does it mean about me**, the material never resolves, because that question has no terminating answer. Held concretely, as **what happened and what next**, the same material runs out.
It moves the persona nowhere, repeatedly, at length.

## Lever

The apparent responsibility of continuing against the fact that continuing is the mechanism.
The carrying force is that stopping feels like avoidance: the thing is genuinely unresolved and genuinely matters, the persona is genuinely trying, and every pass feels like it is closing on something -- which is why the state defends itself, and why advice to think about something else lands as an instruction to be careless; the resistance is only the mode, which nothing in the experience marks, since the abstract-evaluative pass and the concrete one feel the same from inside except that the first feels deeper.
The Loop rides the composite's keystone knife-edge: the mode that feels most like getting to the bottom of it is the one that never does, so the persona is held by the sensation of working.

## Echo

Nowhere, after a long time.
It shows the three states complete -- one decoupling, free where nothing presses, seeking where nothing takes, stuck where something unresolved is in reach -- and carries beneath it the composite's keystone: the difference between them is not a quality of the mind doing it. The faculty that incubates a solution is the faculty that circles a humiliation, and which one a persona gets is set by what was available to land on and how it is held.
This is why the Loop is the hardest of the three to stage and the only one worth closing on: the persona must be visibly getting nowhere and must be right that the thing matters, and any play that makes them merely self-indulgent has removed the reason they cannot stop.
The Loop is the note the whole composite closes on: what a persona's wandering is worth is legible in what was in reach when it left, and nowhere else without a discount.
The Loop is dated from a thing that was never settled, and from every unremarkable moment attention was free to return to it.
