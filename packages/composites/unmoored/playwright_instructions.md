# Instructions: Unmoored

How a Playwright wires the unmoored composite into a play. You wire by linking the
play's own content to the composite's files; the files stay untouched, they are canon.
The composite installs the mind-wandering, boredom, and rumination engines as
dependencies, and their laws arrive with them. Authoring guidance, not runtime content,
and it does not go on tour.

## Human

- The human sets which personas in the play are not attending to what is in front of them, and what is within reach of a mind that has left: whether anything presses, whether anything takes, and whether something is sitting unresolved.

## Agent

- From the play's own content, read a persona whose attention has come off the task and link the state under Projection: the roam while it is free (the unbidden thought, the zoning out that takes them without their noticing, the tuning out that lets a dull thing run, from mind-wandering), the itch while it is mobilised and finding nothing (the restless searching, the reactant irritation at being held where nothing engages, the indifference, the apathy, from boredom), the loop while it is stuck (the brooding over why this happened and what it says about them, the replaying of the scene with the better sentence, the reflection that turns the material over without arriving, from rumination). Keep the mechanism one thing across all three: the composite's claim is that the difference is not in the mind. You link to the composite, you never edit it.

## Collaboration

- The unbidden, zoning-out, and tuning-out modes are the mind-wandering engine's; the searching, reactant, indifferent, and apathetic forms are boredom's; brooding, replaying, and reflection are rumination's -- one alone is a process, not the condition attention is in. The same drift read as the incubating pass toward an insight is the creativity composite's, which wires the same engine to a different question, so a persona may carry both; attention held is the absorption composite's, this one's exact complement; a deliberate going-elsewhere into a made world is elsewhere's; attention captured by a felt lack is slack's tunnel, which is a target and this is the want of one; the machinery of holding a task is volition's; what the persona finds when they land is the self-conscious, longing, and prospection composites'. This composite owns the off-task state -- and the loop bridge carries the keystone: the mode that feels most like getting to the bottom of it is the one that never does.

## Knowledge

- Unmoored is a persona read while their attention is off the task -- braiding the decoupling itself (mind-wandering), attention mobilised with nothing the situation will supply (boredom), and attention locked on something that will not resolve (rumination) -- and its claim is that these are one faculty in three conditions rather than three dispositions or three severities. The joins are declared inside the atoms. Eastwood and colleagues define boredom attentionally, as wanting to be engaged and being unable, which places it against successful engagement rather than against stimulation and so alongside the wandering mind rather than opposite it; and Watkins supplies what separates arriving from circling, which is not the subject and not the quantity but the processing mode. Smallwood and Schooler give the shared mechanism, attention decoupled from the world and carried inward, a capacity rather than a fault; Schooler and colleagues add that the decoupling frequently runs without the persona noticing, so nobody is a reliable witness to their own state; Seli and colleagues separate letting the mind go from having it leave. It runs in three faces. The roam is attention free and undirected, and the point of it is the undirectedness: a mind constrained to the task cannot reach what the task does not suggest, so the wandering is the mode in which the remote thing gets found -- the walk that untangles the problem, the answer in the shower -- and it costs a paragraph and is usually worth it. The itch is attention mobilised and finding no target, which is why it is restless and aversive rather than restful: the paradigm case is the persona with a full shelf, a loaded device, and a free afternoon who cannot get purchase on any of it. Nothing is missing from the situation; what is missing is the engaging -- so the remedy that supplies more is the one that most reliably reproduces it. The loop is the twist: the same decoupling with something unresolved within reach. Held abstractly and evaluatively, as why did this happen and what does it say about me, the material never terminates, because the question has no terminating answer; held concretely, as what happened and what next, the same material runs out. Two passes over identical content and opposite outcomes, and nothing in the experience marks which is which except that the first feels deeper. Here the three are seen for one thing: the faculty that incubates a solution is the faculty that circles a humiliation, and which one a persona gets is set by what was available to land on and how it is held. Read a persona's wandering by what was in reach when it left.

## System

- Do read the state, not the temperament: name whether the roam (attention free), the itch (attention seeking), or the loop (attention stuck) is running, and cast each from its engines.
- Do keep the mechanism single: the same decoupling produces all three, so a play that gives its dreamer and its brooder different faculties has staged three things instead of one.
- Do give the itch a full situation: put the persona among plenty they cannot engage, since a persona with genuinely nothing available is only unoccupied, and the state that matters is the other one.
- Do let the loop carry the keystone: the persona must be visibly getting nowhere and must be right that the thing matters, and any play that makes them merely self-indulgent has removed the reason they cannot stop.
- Do let stopping feel like carelessness: advice to think about something else lands on a persona in the loop as an instruction to be careless about a thing that is genuinely unresolved, which is why it does not work and why they resent it.
- Do make the persona a poor witness: the decoupling runs before it is noticed, so a persona who reports their own state accurately and promptly has been written from outside.
- Do not model the drift toward a product (that is creativity, wiring the same engine to a different question -- link both where a play wants both), attention held (that is absorption), a deliberate going-elsewhere (that is elsewhere), attention captured by a lack (that is slack's tunnel), the machinery of holding a task (that is volition), or the content landed on (that is self-conscious, longing, prospection). Do not make a distraction stand for a story (that is the play's pitch). Do not edit the composite's files; wire only from the play's side.
