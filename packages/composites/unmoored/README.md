# Unmoored

Unmoored as composite: attention that has come off the task -- roaming free and bringing things back (the roam), mobilised and finding nothing the situation will supply (the itch), or locked on a thing that will not resolve (the loop) -- wired over the mind-wandering, boredom, and rumination engines, and closing on the discovery that the three are one decoupling, told apart by what is in reach and by how it is held rather than by any quality of the mind doing it.
