# Process: Unmoored, the Itch

## Taxonomy

[Unmoored](process_unmoored.md), attention mobilised and finding nothing it can take.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: unmoored

## Initiated by

The persona wants to be engaged with something and cannot get hold of anything.
The Itch starts wherever [boredom](@chbrain/khai-engine-boredom/process_boredom.md) is running -- the restless [searching](@chbrain/khai-engine-boredom/process_searching.md) for a target, the [reactant](@chbrain/khai-engine-boredom/process_reactant.md) irritation at being held where nothing engages, the [indifferent](@chbrain/khai-engine-boredom/process_indifferent.md) flatness, and the [apathetic](@chbrain/khai-engine-boredom/process_apathetic.md) state where even the wanting has gone.
It is the face of the composite that everyone diagnoses as a shortage of things to do.

## Direction

Toward a target, and not finding one.
The Itch reads the mobilisation rather than the emptiness -- attention is up, running, and looking, which is why the state is aversive and restless rather than restful -- and reads the case that shows what it actually is: the persona with a full shelf, a loaded device, and an afternoon free, who cannot get purchase on any of it. Nothing is missing from the situation. What is missing is the engaging.
It moves the persona from one thing to the next, none of which takes.

## Lever

The demand of a mobilised attention against a situation that will not meet it.
The carrying force is that the discomfort is doing work: it is a signal that the persona is not spending themselves on anything, and personas who feel it move -- to a new task, a new room, a new project -- which is how the state gets its reputation as a spur; the resistance is that the signal names no remedy, so it is read as a shortage of stimulation and answered with more of it, and more is exactly what the paradigm case already had.
The Itch spends the persona's willingness to sit with anything, and buys motion.

## Echo

A persona in motion, engaged with nothing.
It is the bridge that carries the composite's practical correction: this is not the absence of the roam and it is not cured by supply, because it is a failure of engagement rather than of inventory -- which is why the remedy that adds options is the one that most reliably reproduces it.
What it hands upward is the shape of the third bridge, and the difference between them: here attention is looking for something to take and finding nothing. There it has found something and cannot let go.
The Itch is dated from the moment the persona noticed that nothing was holding them, and from every thing they tried next.
