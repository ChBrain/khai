# Process: Clime

## Taxonomy

A place read as both what its air does and what its air can do.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: clime

## Initiated by

Air is over a place, and there are two true things to say about it that cannot be said at the same time.
Clime starts wherever both are in play: the load falling now, channel by channel and band by band, through [weather](@chbrain/khai-engine-weather/process_weather.md); and the standing regime those loads fall within, through [climate](@chbrain/khai-engine-climate/process_climate.md).
This composite adds no third engine. It reads the relation between the two, which is not a matter of scale: one is a draw and the other is the odds, and no place has ever undergone odds.

## Direction

Toward what a place can be known to be, from the only evidence anybody in it has.
The composite wires the two engines and reads their working in three shapes: [the Day](process_clime_day.md), the load actually falling; [the Terms](process_clime_terms.md), the regime the place is built for; and [the Run](process_clime_run.md), the twist -- the stretch of days that is the only evidence anyone holds and cannot settle anything.
The three are not three timescales. They are one question about a place, asked at the only three places it can be asked.

## Lever

The vividness of what a place undergoes against its near-total silence about what the place is set in.
The carrying force is that the two are genuinely joined -- the regime is nothing but the long run of days, and every day is drawn from the regime, so each is made of the other and neither is invented; the resistance is that the joining runs one way only: the terms govern the day and the day barely reports the terms, and the more extreme and memorable the day, the less it can carry on its own, since an extreme is exactly where one draw says least.
Clime is read on the place and never on the persona standing in it; what the weather does to a mood belongs elsewhere.

## Echo

A place adapted to a thing nobody in it has met, and reasoning from a thing that cannot support the conclusion.
Clime does not close; it is the relation running -- a day, the terms, a run.
It hands off what it is not: the load itself, channel by channel and band by band, is the weather engine's, and the regime with its seasons and extremes is climate's, one alone being a force rather than a reading of the place; the reserve a running process has left before it cannot come back is the margin composite's, which asks how much is there where this asks what can be known -- a regime moving is a drift, and margin reads what the drift is spending while clime reads whether anyone could yet tell; the landscape's own working under the air, its erosion and hydrology and fire and succession, is the wildland composite's; the mood a wet week puts a persona in is the undertow composite's, read inside a person; and a storm made to **stand for** a story is authored key, the play's pitch, not the force.
The place is dated from the run nobody could settle at the time.
