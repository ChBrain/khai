# Instructions: Clime

How a Playwright wires the clime composite into a play. You wire by linking the play's
own content to the composite's files; the files stay untouched, they are canon. The
composite installs the weather and climate engines as dependencies, and their laws arrive
with them. Authoring guidance, not runtime content, and it does not go on tour.

## Human

- The human sets what a place's air is doing and what it is set in: which day is falling, which regime it falls within, and whether a run of days has begun to look like something -- and, the question that governs the read, which face is showing.

## Agent

- From the play's own content, read a place for the face its air is showing and link it under Shown: the day while a load is falling (the wind, water, heat, cold, and sky at whatever band they have reached, from weather), the terms while the regime is governing (the baseline type, the reliable season, the extreme the place is built for, from climate), the run while a stretch of days is being reasoned from (the season out of place, the third failed harvest, the winter that did not arrive). Keep the day and the terms apart -- neither can be observed as the other -- and let personas reason from the run, because that is what people do. Let them be wrong without being foolish. You link to the composite, you never edit it.

## Collaboration

- The wind, water, heat, cold, sky, and season with their bands are the weather engine's; the regime, seasonality, extremity, and shift are climate's -- one alone is a force on a place, not a reading of it. The reserve a running process has left is the margin composite's, and the boundary matters: a regime in shift is a drift, so margin may be run on the same place, but margin asks how much is left where this asks what can yet be known. The landscape's own working under the air is wildland's; what a wet week does to a persona is undertow's, inside a person; who is out in the place is peopling's, on the same chapter. This composite owns the inference -- and the run bridge carries the keystone: the only thing anyone can feel is a sample.

## Knowledge

- Clime is a place read as both what its air does and what its air can do -- braiding the load falling now, channel by channel and band by band (weather), and the standing regime those loads fall within (climate) -- and the relation between the two is not a matter of scale: one is a draw and the other is the odds, and no place has ever undergone odds. The join is published on both sides. Lorenz established what keeps them apart: a system of this kind is deterministic and still unpredictable in its particulars past a horizon of days, because tiny differences grow without bound, while the statistics of the attractor it moves on stay perfectly well defined -- so the terms are knowable exactly where the day is not. Hasselmann established what binds them: weather is fast, essentially random forcing which the slow parts of the system integrate, so the regime is what the weather becomes when it accumulates -- climate made of nothing but days, and no day a report on the climate. It runs in three faces. The day is the load actually falling, on every face at once, undeniable, dated, and where all the damage happens -- and almost silent about the regime, since one draw is compatible with many distributions, and the rarer and more memorable the day the less it can carry alone, so the days that most feel like they must mean something are the ones that individually mean least. The terms are the standing regime the place is built for: the pitch of the roofs, the depth of the foundations, the drain sized for a rain nobody has seen, the crop and the month it goes in -- all fitted to a record rather than a memory, which is why the same rainfall is a wet day in one place and a catastrophe in another, and why the regime is doing more work than the weather in any scene where damage occurs. The run is the twist -- the stretch too long to shrug off and too short to settle, where a moving regime does the only visible showing it will manage while there is still time to act -- and here the two faces are seen for one thing: the day that most feels like proof is the least informative, the record that would be proof is unavailable to anyone's experience, and the run in between is the only evidence a life contains and is exactly the thing that cannot decide. Every single day in a run is individually explicable, so a sequence can be honestly denied one member at a time by people who are not being stupid, each of whose explanations is correct. This is why a shift is argued about precisely while it is happening and obvious only once the argument has stopped mattering, and it is why event attribution answers with a change in the odds rather than a verdict on the case. Read a place by its record, and read its people as holding a sample.

## System

- Do read the place, not the persona in it: name whether the day (a load falling), the terms (the regime governing), or the run (a sequence being reasoned from) is showing, and cast each from its engines.
- Do keep the day and the terms apart: neither can be observed as the other, and a play that treats the regime as merely a lot of weather has collapsed the composite.
- Do let the terms do the damage: the same rainfall is a wet day or a catastrophe depending only on what the place was built for, so put the harm in the mismatch and not in the millimetres.
- Do let the run carry the keystone: it is the only evidence anyone holds, it is where a shift first shows, and it cannot settle -- so stage the argument as unresolvable at the time rather than as one side being obtuse.
- Do let the deniers be right about each day: every member of a run has a correct individual explanation, and a play that makes them stupid has misread the difficulty as a character flaw.
- Do refuse the verdict: never settle, for a single day on stage, whether the regime produced it -- the question has no answer of that shape. Stage the change in the odds, and the position of people who must act without one.
- Do not model the load itself channel by channel (that is weather) or the regime's own modes (that is climate), the reserve a process has left (that is margin), the landscape's working (that is wildland), the mood a wet week produces (that is undertow, inside a person), or who is out in the place (that is peopling). Do not make a storm stand for a story (that is the play's pitch). Do not edit the composite's files; wire only from the play's side.
