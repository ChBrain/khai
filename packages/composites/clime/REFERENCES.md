# Clime: Reference

## Line of Work

Clime as **composite**: a place read as both what its air does and what its air can do. It is not
a third mechanism but a wiring of two: the **weather** engine supplies the load falling now,
channel by channel and band by band, and the **climate** engine supplies the standing regime those
loads fall within. These are the last two engines on the place's Shown chapter left unwired, and
this composite reads them together for the first time. It adds no third engine; it reads the
relation between them -- which is not a matter of scale, since one is a draw and the other is the
odds -- and stages that relation into three bridges, closing on the discovery that the only thing
anyone can feel is a sample.

The domain does not model either force -- the wind, water, heat, cold, sky, and season with their
bands are the weather engine's; the regime, seasonality, extremity, and shift are climate's; and
one alone is a force on a place rather than a reading of it. Nor is it the reserve a running
process has left before it cannot come back, which is the margin composite's; nor the landscape's
own working under the air, which is wildland's; nor what a wet week does to a persona's mood,
which is the undertow composite's, read inside a person. It owns the inference: what a place can
be known to be, from the only evidence anybody standing in it has.

The spine is the pair of results that make the two engines one subject and keep them apart, and
between them they state the composite's whole problem. Lorenz established the first half: a system
of this kind is deterministic and yet unpredictable in its particulars beyond a horizon of days,
because arbitrarily small differences in the starting state grow without bound -- while its
statistics, the shape of the attractor it moves on, remain perfectly well defined. So the terms
can be known when the day cannot, and the two are not the same knowledge differently zoomed.
Hasselmann supplied the second half and the join: weather can be treated as fast, essentially
random forcing which the slow components of the system integrate, so the regime's variability is
not something separate from the weather but is what the weather **becomes** when it is
accumulated. Climate is made of nothing but days, and no day is a report on the climate. Those two
statements together are the seam this composite wires.

The instrument built for exactly this gap is the flank. Detection and attribution science exists
because the question everyone asks after a disaster -- did the regime cause this day? -- is
malformed as posed, and cannot be answered yes or no about any single event. What can be answered
is what the field answers instead: how the odds of an event of that kind differ between a world
with the changed regime and a counterfactual world without it, reported as a change in likelihood
rather than a verdict on the case. Allen set out the framing, Stott, Stone and Allen produced the
first worked attribution of a specific extreme, and the method has since been made routine. The
existence of that machinery is the composite's own claim in institutional form: the naive
inference from the day is not merely weak, it is the wrong shape, and a whole discipline had to be
built to convert what people actually experience into something the question can be asked of.

What the composite adds on top of that join is the third position, which neither engine owns and
which is where everybody actually stands. Between the day, which is felt and proves nothing, and
the record, which proves and cannot be felt, is the run -- the stretch too long to shrug off and
too short to settle. That is the only evidence available from inside a life, it is where a moving
regime does its only visible showing while there is still time to act, and it cannot decide the
question, so every member of it can be honestly explained away one at a time by people who are
not being stupid. This is why a shift is argued about precisely while it is happening and is
obvious only once the argument has stopped mattering. The three bridges are the day, the terms,
and the run.

## Origin

Dynamical meteorology, stochastic climate theory, and event attribution, wired as a composite over
the weather and climate engines. The first two rows are the **joining** -- the published work that
sets the day and the regime as one subject while forbidding either to be read as the other -- and
the rest anchor the individual bridges.

## Restrictions

What the composite refuses to model, and to whom it delegates.

- **The two forces (the atoms)**: The composite wires the two but owns neither. The wind, water,
  heat, cold, sky, and season with their bands are the weather engine's; the regime, seasonality,
  extremity, and shift are climate's. Clime reads only the relation the two stand in -- draw and
  odds -- and one alone is a force on a place rather than a reading of it.
- **The reserve (margin)**: What a running process has left before it cannot come back is the
  margin composite's, and the boundary is sharp because the two look alike from a distance: a
  regime in shift **is** a drift, so margin can be read on it. Margin asks how much is left; clime
  asks what can yet be known. The slide's blindness is that the yardstick moved; the run's
  difficulty is that the sample is too small. A play may run both on the same place, and should
  not confuse a place that cannot tell with a place that has nothing left.
- **The landscape's own working (wildland)**: The geomorphology, erosion, hydrology, fire, and
  succession that run under the air are the wildland composite's. Clime supplies the terms those
  work within and does not model them; the flood is hydrology's and the odds of the flood are here.
- **The mood it produces (undertow, and the persona composites)**: What a wet week does to a
  persona -- the body, the temperament, the mood it settles into -- is the undertow composite's,
  read inside a person. Clime is read on the place, and is indifferent to whether anybody in it
  minded the weather.
- **The place's population (peopling)**: Who is in a place and what their being there is evidence
  of is the peopling composite's, also on the place's Shown chapter. Weather changes who comes out;
  that consequence is peopling's to read, and the load that produced it is here.
- **The verdict on cause in a particular case**: The composite refuses to settle, for any single
  day in a play, whether the regime produced it. That refusal is not caution -- it is the content:
  the question has no answer of that shape, and a production that supplies one has left the
  composite. What can be staged is the change in the odds, and the position of people who must act
  without a verdict.
- **The storm as authored symbol (the play's pitch)**: Weather made to **stand for** a theme -- the
  downpour that arrives to mirror a grief -- is refused: that is authored key, the play's pitch,
  not the force on the place. Clime sets what fell and what governs; the meaning read into it is
  the play's.

## Encoding

Source to constraint. A composite root over two engine atoms it declares as dependencies, with
three bridges.

- **[clime](process_clime.md)** (the root): a place read as both what its air does and what its air
  can do, on the place and never on the persona standing in it. Anchored by Hasselmann (1976) on
  the accumulation that makes one out of the other and Lorenz (1963) on the horizon that keeps them
  apart.
- **[the day](process_clime_day.md)** (bridge): the load actually falling, on every face at once --
  maximum experience, minimum inference, and least informative exactly where it is most memorable.
  Anchored by Lorenz (1963) on the unpredictability of the particular.
- **[the terms](process_clime_terms.md)** (bridge): the standing regime the place is built for and
  adapted to, setting what it can get rather than what it gets, knowable only at second hand.
  Anchored by Köppen on the baseline type and Milanković on the regime as itself a moving quantity.
- **[the run](process_clime_run.md)** (bridge): the stretch too long to dismiss and too short to
  settle, where a moving regime does its only visible showing while there is still time to act.
  Anchored by Allen (2003), Stott, Stone & Allen (2004), and NASEM (2016) on attribution as a
  change in the odds; the composite's twist and keystone, that the only thing anyone can feel is a
  sample, and a sample is not evidence of the odds it came from, though the odds are made of
  nothing else.
