# Clime

Clime as composite: a place read as both what its air does and what its air can do -- the load falling today on every face at once (the day), the standing regime the place was built for and nobody has ever met (the terms), and the run of days that is too long to dismiss and too short to conclude from (the run) -- wired over the weather and climate engines, and closing on the discovery that the only thing anyone can feel is a sample.

This engine is defined by its [manifest](package.json), which the canon renders as the WIRES card. The manifest is the single source of truth; this README is generated, not edited by hand.
