# Process: Clime, the Day

## Taxonomy

[Clime](process_clime.md), the one thing about a place's air that anybody actually undergoes.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: clime

## Initiated by

The air over the place changes, and the place is already inside it.
The Day starts wherever [weather](@chbrain/khai-engine-weather/process_weather.md) is falling -- the [wind](@chbrain/khai-engine-weather/process_wind.md) at whatever band it has reached, the [water](@chbrain/khai-engine-weather/process_water.md), the [heat](@chbrain/khai-engine-weather/process_heat.md) and the [cold](@chbrain/khai-engine-weather/process_cold.md), under the [sky](@chbrain/khai-engine-weather/process_sky.md) the place is lit by -- and it arrives on every face at once, without announcement and without needing anyone to look up.
It is the face of the composite that is fully available to experience, which is what makes it so misleading.

## Direction

Toward a place wholly occupied by conditions that say almost nothing about it.
The Day reads the load as load -- the roof under the [deluge](@chbrain/khai-engine-weather/process_water_deluge.md), the crop under the [scorching](@chbrain/khai-engine-weather/process_heat_scorching.md), the road under the [killing](@chbrain/khai-engine-weather/process_cold_killing.md) cold -- and reads what such a load can and cannot support: it settles entirely what the place is undergoing, and it settles nothing at all about what the place is set in, because one draw from a distribution is compatible with a great many distributions.
It presses the place hard, and leaves the question of the place open.

## Lever

The undeniability of the load against its poverty as evidence.
The carrying force is that nothing else in the composite is this real: the day is felt, dated, photographed, and argued with by everyone in the place at once, and it is where all the damage happens -- the terms never flooded anybody's kitchen; the resistance is that its very extremity is what disqualifies it, since the rarer and more memorable a day, the fewer of it there are and the less one of them can carry, so the days that most feel like they must mean something are precisely the ones that individually mean least.
The Day spends nothing and proves nothing beyond itself.

## Echo

A place fully tested and entirely undiagnosed.
It is the bridge that sets the composite's difficulty by sitting at the vivid end of it: maximum experience, minimum inference, and the reading everyone takes because it is the only one they can take without records.
What it hands upward is the confound the other two exist to escape -- that undergoing a place's air and knowing a place's air are different acts, and only one of them can be done in an afternoon.
The Day is dated from the front that delivered it, and from no one's idea of what it means.
