# Process: Clime, the Terms

## Taxonomy

[Clime](process_clime.md), the standing regime the place is built for and nobody in it has ever met.

## Owner

- Project: khai
- Engine: clime

## Initiated by

The place was built for something, and that something is not any day it has had.
The Terms start wherever [climate](@chbrain/khai-engine-climate/process_climate.md) is governing -- the baseline type the place is set in, through [regime](@chbrain/khai-engine-climate/process_regime.md); the reliable structure its year keeps, through [seasonality](@chbrain/khai-engine-climate/process_seasonality.md); and the worst it is made to survive, through [extremity](@chbrain/khai-engine-climate/process_extremity.md).
It is the face of the composite that is doing all the governing and cannot be looked at.

## Direction

Toward everything in the place that was decided before the day arrived.
The Terms read the adaptation rather than the conditions -- the pitch of the roofs, the depth of the foundations, the crop that is planted and the month it goes in, the storm drain sized for a rain nobody has seen yet, the whole settled life fitted to a regime by people who fitted it to a record and not to a memory -- and read the regime as the thing that sets what the place **can** get, where the day only ever sets what it **does** get.
It moves nothing on any particular afternoon and has already decided how the afternoon will land.

## Lever

The completeness of the regime's authority against its total unavailability to the senses.
The carrying force is that the terms are the operative quantity -- the same rainfall is a wet day in one place and a catastrophe in another, and the difference is entirely in what each was built for, so the regime is doing more work than the weather in every scene where damage occurs; the resistance is that it can only ever be inferred, from records nobody experienced, kept by people who are not here, over a span longer than the working life of anyone who has to act on it.
The Terms spend the possibility of first-hand knowledge.

## Echo

A place correct about a thing none of its inhabitants can verify.
It is the bridge where the composite's asymmetry shows plainly: the day governs nothing and is known completely, the terms govern everything and are known only at second hand.
What it hands upward is the reason the third bridge is hard rather than merely uncertain -- the quantity that matters is not the kind of thing a lifetime can observe, so a persona is always in the position of taking the regime on trust or reasoning to it from a sample far too small.
The Terms are dated from the record, and from the decades before anyone now living was standing here.
