# Expect

- The mode is decided first and said: Mode A, one play file, no plot files,
  no pitch file.
- The output is one play file in the template's shape: frontmatter with
  `khai: play`, a title, a description, a license and a stamp; then the H1
  `Play: <title>`; then exactly six H2s, Estate, Name, Arc, Company,
  Triggers, Stakes, in that order and no other H2.
- The Arc names a bend: it says what makes the plots one telling, and the
  plots it foreshadows could not be reordered without loss. A list of
  scenes is not an Arc.
- The Company is closed: the baker, the apprentice, the miller and the mayor
  are named with what each carries, the oven and the bakery are placed,
  and nothing appears in Triggers or Stakes that the Company does not name.
- Triggers chain: each names what cues the next, and the run has a close.
- The Stakes move: what is lost or won across the run is stated, not left
  where the ask found it.
- The Estate names who answers for the run (the author), and the Name is
  the title the run is billed under, as a work.
- No em-dash or en-dash anywhere in the output; colons, ellipses (...) or
  line breaks instead.
- Nothing beyond the ask: no fifth character, no plot files, no pitch, no
  second location the ask did not name.
- Where the ask is silent, the playwright asks the author, in at most two
  questions after the file, rather than inventing.
