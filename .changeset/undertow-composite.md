Add the undertow composite, the ground a persona feels from, read as a fact about what they are looking at. Wires three atoms -- the body engine (the set-point drifted out of range and pulling), the temperament engine (the resting disposition arrived with and returned to), and the mood engine (the affective colour laid on every read before any single event arrives) -- and reads the seam the three make: a continuous supply of feeling that arrives without a note saying where it came from. Staged as three bridges, ordered by how easily the persona can locate the source: the demand (the one ground that names itself, and still narrows the reach while the persona knows exactly what is narrowing them, so the sharpness is attributed to the meeting rather than to the hour), the set (a disposition constant across a whole life supplies no contrast, so it cannot be felt as a setting at all -- the reactive persona does not experience themselves as reactive but experiences the world as alarming), and the tint (the twist -- it varies and still arrives unlabelled, so the persona has a moving signal and nowhere to file it, and files it against whatever is in front of them). Carries the keystone: a state with nothing to attach it to gets attached to whatever is in view, so a persona verdict on a thing is partly a reading of themselves delivered in the grammar of a fact about the thing -- and the correction is attribution rather than accusation, since the effect goes when the real source is made salient. Wires at Persona/Projection (audit). The joining is published: Schwarz and Clore feelings-as-information account, where an evaluative question is answered by consulting the current affective state and a feeling produced elsewhere is spent on what is being judged, with Russell core affect supplying the substrate, a continuous state read on valence and activation that is always present and not intrinsically about anything, which is what makes it misattributable. What the composite adds is the ordering by locatability and the reading of the first two grounds through the misattribution logic established for the third. Carries an honest caveat rather than burying it: the famous weather-and-life-satisfaction demonstration has a contested replication record, Lucas and Lawless finding the association very small in a much larger sample, and what carries the weight is the moderation rather than the effect size. Bridge anchors: Cannon and Craig on homeostasis and interoception, Melzack and Wall on gate control, Costa and McCrae and Eysenck on the trait baselines, Thayer on the two kinds of activation. Distinct from emotion (the feeling that fires at an event), appetite (the requirement, where this is the state that makes the deficit felt), stress and resilience, lasting (the return to a level over time), estimation and bias (the named shortcuts and standing tilts), sensorium (the surround acting inward, where this is the organism acting outward), and absorption.
