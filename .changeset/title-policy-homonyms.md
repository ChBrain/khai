Let a house declare a display title two kinds legitimately share, in
`titlePolicy.homonyms`. `titleCollisions` had one hardcoded escape, the
Playwright wiring guide, and no way for a root to say anything: the only lever
was renaming content. That is the right answer when a title is careless and the
wrong one when a culture's persona and the plot about them are the same
historical figure, because the H1 is pinned to `declared` and `declared` is the
culture's own word for its own thing.

The declaration is the same shape as `memberPolicy.homonyms` and
`scholarPolicy.homonyms` -- where nothing in the data distinguishes two cases,
the maintainer declares it and the build computes the rest -- and it names the
bearers rather than muting the title, which is what separates it from `exempt`:
exempting a file removes it from the check, so the next kind to take that title
passes unnoticed, while a declared pair still fails the moment a third kind
joins it.

Wired on the collection path only, deliberately: an engine monorepo has never
needed it (the wall went in green across every engine, composite and fixture),
and that is also where the root, and so the config, is in hand. Additive and
back-compat: with nothing declared the behaviour is identical, which the tests
assert directly.
