Add the elsewhere composite, the mind in a place it is not. Wires three atoms -- the imagination engine (the scene built on purpose and held apart from belief), the transportation engine (the story world entered until the room recedes), and the memory engine (the past assembled from a trace and a cue) -- and reads the seam the three make: one recombining faculty at three settings of the guard that marks its output as made. Staged as three bridges, ordered by how much the persona knows they are constructing: the conjured (the quarantine held, the made state marked off from what is believed and perceived, which is exactly what lets it be turned and extended and combined, so a persona can rehearse a disaster without suffering it), the borrowed (the quarantine spent, since the transported persona is less able to counterargue with its defences lowered by the very engagement, so a story moves and persuades where an argument cannot, and knowing it is fiction does not help), and the rebuilt (the twist -- the guard not lowered but absent, a cue meeting a trace and the gaps filled with what fits, what returns changed and filed back changed so each later recall starts from where this one left it). Carries the keystone: the faculty a persona trusts most is the one running with no quarantine at all -- the unreliability of memory is not a defect bolted onto it but the same flexible recombination that makes imagining possible, running in the one place where nothing warns the persona it is running. Wires at Persona/Projection (audit). The joining is published and one atom already cites it: Schacter and Addis on constructive episodic simulation, where remembering the past and imagining the future draw on one core network and simulating a future event requires a memory system permitting flexible recombination of remembered details, with Hassabis and Maguire supplying the sharpest corroboration in patients unable to construct novel imagined scenes as well as unable to recollect; and Mar and Oatley reaching the third bridge from narrative psychology, fiction as the abstraction and simulation of social experience. What the composite adds is the ordering by guard. Bridge anchors: Currie and Ravenscroft on the offline quarantine, Gerrig and Green and Brock on transportation and reduced counterarguing, Bartlett and Loftus and Nader on reconstruction, misinformation, and reconsolidation. Distinct from mind-wandering and divergent-thinking inside creativity (the drift and the generating), absorption (attention without a world), prospection (the feeling toward an imagined future), possible-selves and regret and nostalgia (particular uses of the construction), play-mode (the pretend stance toward something present), persuasion and allegiance, and assent (what would change a persona mind).
