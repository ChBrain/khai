Add the selfhood composite, the self as an object to itself. Wires four atoms -- the self-discrepancy engine (the ideal and ought guides a persona falls short of), the possible-selves engine (the hoped, feared, expected, and lost futures it carries), the self-affirmation engine (integrity restored from an untouched valued domain when the self is struck), and the self-verification engine (the drive to have feedback match the self-view already held) -- and reads them as one working self-concept that compares, defends, and holds itself in place. Staged as three bridges: the measure (the self located by its distance from selves it is not -- the ideal shortfall bringing dejection, the ought shortfall agitation, the futures pulling and warning), the repair (standing on ground the blow did not touch, so a hard truth can be admitted rather than denied), and the anchor (the twist -- soliciting, signaling, and resisting until the world confirms the self already held). Carries the keystone: a self needs to be known more than it needs to be thought well of -- a persona will hold to an unflattering self-view against evidence and kindness both, because the view most fiercely held is not the most favourable but the most familiar. Wires at Persona/Projection (audit). Anchored in Markus & Wurf's dynamic self-concept, Higgins's self-discrepancy, Markus & Nurius's possible selves, Steele's self-affirmation, and Swann's self-verification. Distinct from self-relation (the evaluative stance toward the self), identity (the self-story defended), capability (the belief about what the self can do), and presentation (the labour of being seen).
