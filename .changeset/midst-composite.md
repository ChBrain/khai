Add the midst composite, the persona among others -- changed by the bare fact that others are there, before anyone commands it, admits it, or ranks it. Wires three atoms -- the social-facilitation engine (performance altered by being seen), the bystander-effect engine (responsibility divided among witnesses), and the crowd engine (the massed body that acts as one) -- and reads them as one working of presence, running through the question of who is answerable. Staged as three bridges: the watched (one arousal that lifts the well-learned, spoils the newly learned, and slackens the pooled), the spread (a duty thinned by diffusion, pluralistic ignorance, and audience inhibition, until a need requiring one whole person is held in fractions by a roomful and answered by none), and the massed (the twist -- density, direction, and discharge, the many acting as one). Carries the keystone: the more others are present, the less any one of them is the person who did it -- presence does not merely add witnesses, it subtracts authorship, so the same arithmetic that stalls a hundred people from helping one is the arithmetic that lets a hundred do together what each alone would refuse. Wires at Persona/Projection (audit). Anchored in Allport's definition of the field, Zajonc's social facilitation, Latane & Darley's unresponsive bystander, and the crowd tradition after Le Bon. Distinct from membership (the group's standard and the bind of belonging), contagion/grapevine (what spreads person to person), subjection (the institution's making of a person), and presentation (the labour of performing a self).
