Add the metacognition engine (multi-type: process, position) -- a persona's knowing about its own knowing, the meta-level that watches the object-level of cognition and steers it. The gap analysis found this homeless: the codebase modeled the object-level (memory, reasoning) and the watching of self-presentation (self-monitoring), but not the second-order layer where a persona reads the state of its own knowing and acts on that read. Nelson & Narens's two-level model names it -- an object-level and a meta-level, joined by two flows.

- **Root (process):** `metacognition` -- cognition turned upon itself, the meta-level watching and steering the object-level on the two flows.
- **Forms (process):** `monitoring` (the upward flow -- the meta-level reading the object-level: judgments of learning, feelings of knowing, tip-of-the-tongue, confidence; inferential and cue-based per Koriat), `steering` (the downward flow -- Nelson & Narens's control: allocating study time, terminating search, choosing strategy, all governed by the monitoring), `calibration` (the accuracy of the monitoring -- whether confidence matches performance, and how over- or under-confident it runs).
- **Trait (position):** `metacognitive` -- a standing skill at knowing one's own mind, from the finely calibrated to the systematically blind (Fleming & Lau's metacognitive sensitivity; Schraw & Dennison's metacognitive awareness).

Warranted (LORE) on Flavell (the founding), Nelson & Narens (the two-level model, monitoring and control), Koriat (the cue-based, inferential account of metacognitive judgments), Dunlosky & Metcalfe (the judgment taxonomy and study allocation), Fleming & Lau (metacognitive sensitivity), and Schraw & Dennison (the MAI). Bounded against the wider `cognition` (the object-level itself vs the watching of it), `self-monitoring` (watching one's self-presentation to others vs watching one's own mind -- same word, different object), `bias` (which owns the named, systematic failures of calibration -- overconfidence, Dunning-Kruger, the curse of knowledge, the illusion of explanatory depth -- as members), the knowledge emotions (`curiosity`/`interest`/`confusion`, which own the feelings a monitored gap provokes), and `executive-function` (the general control machinery vs the effort a self-read specifically directs). Set at patch as the free level. No whitelist required -- the `metacognition`/`monitoring`/`calibration`/`metacognitive` stems are unique, and `steering` was chosen over `control` precisely because `control` is superstition's.
