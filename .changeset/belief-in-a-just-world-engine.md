Add the belief-in-a-just-world engine (position: anchor + expressions) -- a persona's standing conviction that the world is fair, that people get what they deserve and deserve what they get. The gap analysis (Tier-3 #21) found the `bias` engine already owns the just-world _hypothesis_ (the victim-derogation attribution), but not the standing _worldview_ itself or its two validated faces and its self-buffering function.

- **Anchor (position):** `belief-in-a-just-world` -- the standing worldview that the world is fair, beneath a persona's readings of fortune and misfortune.
- **Faces (position):**
  - `personal` -- the world assumed fair _to me_: the adaptive, self-directed belief that buffers wellbeing and sustains trust, deferral, and long effort (Dalbert's personal BJW -- the stronger resource).
  - `general` -- the world assumed fair _to all_: the harsher, other-directed belief that justifies the status quo, hardens toward the disadvantaged, and defends itself by concluding an innocent victim must have deserved it.

**Scope note -- victim-derogation attribution stays with `bias`.** The `bias` engine's `position_just_world.md` ("Just-World Hypothesis," in its Coherence-Patterning family) already owns the specific cognitive move -- the innocent sufferer searched for the flaw that explains their fate. This engine takes the standing worldview and Dalbert's personal/general structure (genuinely absent from bias, especially the personal self-buffer), and delegates the pure attribution bias to that member -- one phenomenon, one engine, the same split used for `moral-licensing`↔`bias`.

Warranted (LORE) on Lerner (1980, the founding -- the need to believe the world just, and victim derogation as its defense), Dalbert (personal vs general just-world beliefs; the personal belief as a wellbeing resource), Hafer & Begue (2005, the consolidated evidence), and Rubin & Peplau (the belief's social-attitudinal correlates). Bounded against the `bias` just-world-hypothesis member (the attribution vs the conviction beneath it), `schadenfreude` (the pleasure at a deserved fall vs the belief in desert), `comparison` (the neutral ranking vs the moralized one), and `moral-judgment` (the fairness verdict on a particular act vs the global worldview). Set at patch as the free level. No whitelist required -- the `just_world_belief`/`personal`/`general` stems are unique (distinct from bias's `just_world`).
