Add the opacity composite, a persona read as a self that controls what others may know of it. Wires three atoms -- the secret engine (the self withheld), the deception engine (the self shown false), and the self-disclosure engine (the self opened true) -- into one axis, the truth-status of the projected self, staged as three bridges: withheld (the self kept back -- the keeper, the concealment, the retreat, the exposure), feigned (the self shown false -- the false belief constructed, delivered, and maintained), and revealed (the twist -- the self opened true, breadth and depth surrendered, where being known costs the safety of not being seen). Wires at Persona/Projection (audit). Anchored in Goffman's managed self, Altman & Taylor's social penetration and privacy dialectic, Bok's ethics of concealment, and Jourard's transparent self. Guile (the deceptive object) is deliberately excluded -- it wires the piece/Apparent surface and lives in the artifice composite.
