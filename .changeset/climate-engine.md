Add the climate engine: the standing, long-run atmospheric regime a place is set in, wired on: place/Shown. The fifth engine of the land's-forces family (F-place) and weather's long-run sibling -- what a place expects, against weather's what it gets on the day. A depth-1 process engine -- a regime root over four modes: regime (the standing type, arid/humid/tropical/polar), seasonality (the reliable seasonal structure the year runs), extremity (the extreme the regime imposes and the place is built for), and shift (the regime itself moving over the decades, stranding everything set to the old normal). It governs the odds every other place-force plays out within. First release, so an empty changeset (publishes at 0.1.0).
