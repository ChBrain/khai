Add the erosion engine: the slow wearing of a place's surface by agents from outside, wired on: place/Shown. The second engine of the land's-forces family (F-place) and geomorphology's complement. A depth-1 process engine -- a wearing root over four modes: scour (water's cut), deflation (wind's strip), frost (ice's shatter), and the deposition twist (the worn material laid down elsewhere as new ground -- the loss become a gift downstream). The exogenic, gradual counterpart to geomorphology's sudden endogenic violence: geomorphology raises the range, erosion wears it to stumps. First release, so an empty changeset (publishes at 0.1.0).
