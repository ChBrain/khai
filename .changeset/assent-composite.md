Add the assent composite, how a persona holds something true and what evidence can reach. Wires three atoms -- the belief engine (the conviction disconfirming evidence bears on and forces to a settlement), the superstition engine (the link between things that share no mechanism, which cannot be disproved from within), and the faith engine (the standing orientation held prior to and apart from any single claim proved or disproved) -- and reads the seam the three make: three distances between a holding and the evidence that could touch it. Staged as three bridges: the exposed (the only distance where a settlement is possible, and where update is one exit of five, alongside the slack worked until the wanted conclusion survives, the reading refused outright, the long-strained frame flipping wholesale to a rival, and the question honestly suspended -- so exposure does not select for correction, and the persona who rationalizes experiences themselves as following the evidence exactly as the one who updates does), the sealed (the link that makes predictions none of which can fail, every null result routed into an account of why it does not count -- the wrong rite, the broken condition, the dormant charge -- so the read changes when the persona stops granting it power and never when an outcome contradicts it), and the prior (the twist -- the orientation held before any tenet is stated, shown in the leaning and never in the case made, whose absence of evidence is not a dodge). Carries the keystone: being out of reach of evidence is not one condition but two, and only one of them is an evasion -- the sealed is a claim that withdrew from the risk it appeared to take, the prior never made a claim to withdraw from, and they are indistinguishable by the only test anyone thinks to apply, which is whether the persona would give it up. Wires at Persona/Projection (audit). Anchored in Popper's falsifiability and immunising stratagems read as a description of persons, Evans-Pritchard on secondary elaboration among the Azande, Wittgenstein's hinge propositions in On Certainty, James's unseen order, Festinger's dissonance and When Prophecy Fails, Lord, Ross and Lepper on belief perseverance, and Rozin and Nemeroff and Langer on the content the seal is built around. Distinct from squaring (where what is defended is the persona's picture of their own conduct rather than a claim about the world), estimation (the forming of a judgment under uncertainty rather than the maintaining of a holding), knowing (the felt weather of meeting the unknown), metacognition (a persona's monitoring of its own knowing), bias (the standing catalogue of tilts), trust (the wager on another person, where the trust here is reliance on an order), belief-in-a-just-world, and moral-conviction.
