Add the longing composite, a persona aching at a gap it cannot close. Wires three atoms -- the loneliness engine (the absent other), the regret engine (the untaken path), and the nostalgia engine (the lost past) -- and reads them as one longing: a felt distance between the actual and the desired, remembered, or possible. Staged as three bridges: the other (the relational gap -- the pain of a needed connection not present, its numbness, withdrawal, and reaching), the path (the counterfactual gap -- a choice measured against the better outcome imagined, commission and inaction), and the past (the twist -- the temporal gap, the bittersweet longing for one's own past that restores connectedness, continuity, and meaning). Carries the keystone: the ache is the measure of what mattered -- a self longs only for what it valued. Wires at Persona/Projection (audit). Anchored in Cacioppo's loneliness-as-social-pain, Roese's counterfactual regret, and Sedikides & Wildschut's bittersweet nostalgia. Distinct from bittersweet (the joy/sadness blend as such), self-discrepancy (the actual/ideal-self gap), and tragic (the witnessed fall).
