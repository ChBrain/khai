Add the dereliction engine: the decline phase of the neighborhood-cycle family -- the decline a place undergoes when an authority stops maintaining it, the withdrawal of care that hands the place to decay. Four forms over a withdrawal root: disrepair (care reduced, still held), abandonment (care ended, the place left), disinvestment (care pulled from a whole area, the rent-gap opened -- the seam to gentrification), and ruin (the place given fully over, Edensor's excess -- dereliction's own age-value). Wires on: place at Offers. Lineage: Edensor, Ginsberg, Ruskin, Beauregard.
