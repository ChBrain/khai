Add the transference engine (process: root + forms) -- the carrying of a past relationship onto a present figure. Second engine of the Freud effort, and the one with the strongest _modern_ empirical spine: not Freud alone, but the social-cognitive science built on him.

- **Root (process):** `transference` -- a stored mental representation of a significant other (a parent, an early love, a formative figure) is triggered when a new person resembles them, and applied to that new person, so the feelings, expectations, and relational patterns owed the old figure are lived out toward the new; the resemblance shifts perception, expectation, affect, and even the self the persona becomes (Freud; Andersen & Chen's relational self).
- **Forms (process)** -- the three configurations:
  - `positive` -- the warmth of the old bond (affection, idealization, trust) met on a figure who did not earn it.
  - `negative` -- the injury of the old bond (hostility, mistrust, fear) met on a figure who did not provoke it, cast as the one who once hurt.
  - `countertransference` -- the reciprocal: the responder's own significant-other representation triggered in turn, so both relate through overlaid pasts.

The defining mark is the misfit -- the present person is not the past one, yet is treated as if they were; the transference can even make itself come true.

**Warrant is a lineage, not solo work.** Freud (1912 "The Dynamics of Transference"; 1915 "Observations on Transference-Love") is the origin, but the concept's modern warrant is **experimental**: Klein (1952, object relations -- transference as projected internal object relations), and above all Andersen & Chen (2002, "The Relational Self," _Psych Review_) with the Andersen/Glassman transference paradigm, which showed resemblance to one's own significant other reliably producing transference in affect, expectancy, and self-definition -- moving the concept from clinical inference to demonstrated everyday process.

**Scope note.** The standing bond and its working model (secure/anxious/avoidant/disorganized) stay with `attachment` -- transference is that stored relationship _activated_ onto a present figure; the projecting of a single disowned trait stays with `bias`'s projection member (transference carries a whole relationship, not one attribute); the defensive _use_ transference can be put to stays with `defense`, and the intrapsychic conflict it plays out with `structural-model`. Set at patch. No whitelist required -- the `transference` / `transference_positive` / `transference_negative` / `countertransference` stems are unique.
