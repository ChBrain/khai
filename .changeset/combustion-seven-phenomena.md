Add seven named phenomena the catalog lacked, each with its own science, sensory
signature, and extinguishment paradox, raising the catalog to 58 members. Wildland
gains the coal-seam fire (geological fuel alight underground for decades to millennia,
extinguishment priced past the land above it -- Stracher & Taylor) and the WUI fire
(ember attack making the houses the fuel, loss mapped to Cohen's home ignition zone,
not the front). The compartment domain gains the cladding fire (the combustible
envelope carrying flame past every compartment line, where stay-put turns lethal --
the Grenfell Tower Inquiry) and the tunnel fire (backlayering below critical velocity,
the ventilation that allocates the smoke -- Ingason, Li & Lönnermark). The industrial
domain gains the hydrogen fire (4-75 percent flammable, ignited by almost nothing,
nearly invisible -- isolate before extinguishing, or the flame becomes the cloud --
NFPA 2) and the runaway reaction (exponential heat against linear cooling, lost while
the gauges read calm -- the CSB's T2 investigation, DIERS). The made-fire domain gains
the pyrotechnics (self-oxidised display compositions whose tested hazard class belongs
to the packaging, transitioning in bulk to mass explosion -- the Oosting Commission's
Enschede report). Each arrives with an Origin-table row, an Encoding entry, and its
clauses in the card and playwright instructions.
