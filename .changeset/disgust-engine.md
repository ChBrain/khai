Add the disgust engine -- a multi-type engine spanning process and position. Disgust is the rejection-and-contamination response: a persona meets something it appraises as offensive to take in, touch, or be near -- a potential contaminant -- and recoils, with revulsion, the impulse to expel or withdraw, and the sense of a taint that spreads and clings. Oral in origin (the guardian of the mouth), it is unique among the emotions in its contamination logic.

- **Domains (process):** `pathogen` (disease avoidance -- the behavioral immune system, biased toward false alarms), `sexual` (mate choice -- recoil from reproductively costly partners and acts, kin-tuned and sex-differentiated), `sociomoral` (the moral order -- violations met as impurity, condemnation that dehumanizes and forecloses repair) -- Tybur, Lieberman & Griskevicius's three functional domains.
- **Mechanism (process):** `contamination` -- the laws of sympathetic magic (contagion and similarity, negativity dominance, indelibility) that make disgust spread, cling, and resist reason (Rozin & Nemeroff).
- **Trait (position):** `sensitivity` -- disgust sensitivity, the standing, partly heritable threshold and reach that tilts moral judgment, disease behavior, contamination anxiety, and purity attitudes (the Disgust Scale; the Three-Domain Disgust Scale).

Fills the missing basic emotion: of the five the `emotion` root names (joy, sadness, anger, fear, disgust), disgust had no engine. Promotes it from the single `emotion/process_disgust.md` node to a standalone engine (via the `disgust` homonym whitelist), keeping `emotion` a balanced appraisal taxonomy while disgust gets its own depth. A process root fans into process and position members; each validates against its own khai chapters. Bounded against `emotion` (disgust as one basic affect vs its internal structure), `contempt` (the cold downward look, disgust's sibling in the CAD triad), `stigma` (the social mark and exclusion vs the visceral rejection that feeds it), `moral-judgment` (the reasoned appraisal vs the moral recoil), and `mortality` (death-management vs contamination-avoidance). Warranted (LORE) on Rozin, Haidt & McCauley (core disgust and the oral origin), Tybur, Lieberman & Griskevicius (the three functional domains, 2009), Schaller & Park (the behavioral immune system), Rozin, Lowery, Imada & Haidt (the CAD triad, 1999), Rozin & Nemeroff (sympathetic magic), and Haidt, McCauley & Rozin (the Disgust Scale). Set at patch as the free level; a new engine may warrant a minor at the maintainer's `bump:minor` label.
