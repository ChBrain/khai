Add the ingraining composite, how a response gets installed in a persona until it runs without consulting why. Wires three atoms -- the conditioning engine (the neutral cue that comes to predict), the reward engine (the outcome weighed by how near it stands), and the habit engine (the repetition that grooves an act into its context) -- and reads the seam the three make when they run in series: one loop from situation to act that shortens at every stage. Staged as three bridges: the pairing (a cue that reliably precedes something significant comes to evoke the response in advance, spreading by generalization and fading only under repeated disconfirmation -- the first shortening, since the outcome no longer has to arrive to be answered), the discount (the near looming at near-full weight, the far thinned until it must compete against present things carrying their whole value, the wait itself an active cost the observer does not see -- the second shortening, since the far consequence barely enters the sum), and the groove (the twist -- the response repeated in a stable context until it comes without being summoned, chunked and retrieved by the setting faster than deliberation can intervene, revealed only when friction removes the cue). Carries the keystone: what is finally installed no longer consults the reason it was installed for -- a cue can fire when the response is no longer wanted, because the strength of the link lies in the pairing and not in what the act is now worth. Wires at Persona/Projection (audit). Anchored in Rescorla's contingency-not-contiguity reading of Pavlovian conditioning and the Rescorla-Wagner error-driven model, Ainslie's hyperbolic discounting and preference reversal, Adams and Dickinson's 1981 outcome-devaluation result (the overtrained response survives the devaluing of its own outcome), Wood and Neal's habit-goal interface, and Graybiel on chunking. Distinct from addiction (pursuit past its cost in a needed reward currency), striving (the standing character a persona brings to any pursuit), volition (the crossing from wanting to doing), self-control (the momentary override), and motivation and needs (where the drive springs from, and what is required).
