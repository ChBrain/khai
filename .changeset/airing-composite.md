Add the airing composite, what a working collective can hear about itself. Wires three atoms -- the group engine (the stage its shared pattern has reached), the org engine (the stance it is organised to value and what holding it costs), and the psychological-safety engine (the interpersonal climate that prices what a persona can risk saying) -- and reads the seam the three make: each grain deciding some part of whether what needs saying gets said. Staged as three bridges: the stage (the pattern still being set, where storming begins the first time someone says what they actually think and the room flinches or does not, so the stage that looks most like dysfunction is the one finding the edges, and by performing the price list is invisible), the stance (what the collective is for, which decides what counts as a contribution and what as obstruction -- control runs on the record, so what was done is documented, what was documented is auditable, and what is auditable can be enforced, but the record is not collected, it is handed over, and a stance strong enough to enforce a standard is usually strong enough to make handing it over costly), and the silence (the twist -- the climate held as a never-stated history of who spoke up and what followed, running high, low, fragile where candour is priced above zero, or performative where a declared openness sits over an enforcement history that contradicts it). Carries the keystone, which is a measured result rather than a reading: teams rated better on independent grounds reported higher error rates, not lower, the difference being willingness to speak rather than frequency of error -- so a reporting rate reads the climate before it reads the trouble, and the collective reporting more trouble is usually the healthier one, quiet reading as well-run and generally being its opposite. Wires at Persona/Projection (audit); the first composite to wire a place-typed atom alongside process-typed ones. The joining is published: Edmondson and Lei trace the construct from the 1960s organizational scholars through its renaissance and set out contemporary research explicitly at the individual, group, and organizational levels of analysis, so the climate, the group, and the organisation are one literature with one construct running across them. What the composite adds is the extension of the inversion down the other two grains, and the consequence for a persona rather than a team, that the price is never itemised. Bridge anchors: Edmondson (1999, 2018) and Frazier and colleagues meta-analysis of 136 studies; Tuckman and Tuckman and Jensen on the developmental sequence; Quinn and Rohrbaugh and Cameron and Quinn on competing values. Distinct from midst (the mere presence of others), membership and intergroup (belonging, and between groups), subjection (the institution forming the person), crosstalk and rapport (the mechanics of talk and the tie between two), trust (the bet on one person, where this is the room), opacity (what a persona withholds for their own reasons, where this is what the room withholds from them), tightness-looseness inside grain (a culture wide field rather than a local climate), and escalation.
