Add the surprise engine: the response to a violated expectation -- the mind's alarm that its model of the world has just been wrong, and the interrupt-and-reorient it fires to deal with the mismatch. The root (`process_surprise.md`) declares surprise as pre-valence and corrective: triggered not by what a persona cares about but by a discrepancy between what happened and what their schema predicted (a prediction error), functioning to interrupt ongoing activity, seize attention, analyze the anomaly, and update the schema. Four forms: `startle` (the reflex -- a sudden intense stimulus firing a defensive jump below appraisal, faster and lower than the emotions and hard to suppress), `jolt` (the appraised interrupt -- an expectation broken, ongoing action halted, attention pinned to the anomaly; needs no loud stimulus, only a wrong model), `sensemaking` (the resolution -- turning to the anomaly, finding what was wrong, and repairing the schema so the model tracks the world better), and `dismissal` (the failure -- the anomaly explained away and the model kept unrevised, so the persona is set up to be surprised the same way again).

Found via a clean gap-audit pass: surprise is a basic emotion conspicuously absent from the `emotion` engine's five modes (anger/disgust/fear/joy/sadness), and it stands as its own engine rather than a sixth mode because it is a distinct mechanism -- an expectation-violation interrupt, not a caring-appraisal. Bounded against `emotion` (the valenced feeling surprise resolves into -- delight, dismay), `attention` (the spotlight it seizes -- attention owns capture and orienting), `belief` (the schema-revision it drives), `awe` (the vastness-sized surprise that overflows into accommodation), and `curiosity` (the standing pull a surprise can awaken). Mode names avoid collisions: `reckoning` (betrayal), `shock` (grief), and `freeze` (stress) are taken, so the resolution form is `sensemaking`. Warranted (LORE) on Meyer, Reisenzein & Schützwohl (the cognitive-evolutionary process model), Reisenzein (surprise as metacognitive), Ekman, Friesen & Simons (the startle reflex distinguished from surprise), and Darwin (the expression of surprise). Set at patch as the free level; a new engine may warrant a minor at the maintainer's `bump:minor` label.
