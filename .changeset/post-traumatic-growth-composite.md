Add the post-traumatic-growth composite (process: root + two movements) -- positive transformation beyond the prior baseline, wrought through the struggle with trauma. The gap analysis (Tier-3 #34) specified a composite over `meaning` + `narrative` + `mortality` + `identity`.

- **Root (process):** `post-traumatic-growth` -- reads Tedeschi & Calhoun's model over the four atoms it declares as dependencies. A seismic event shatters the persona's **assumptive world** (Janoff-Bulman -- the beliefs that the world is benevolent and meaningful and the self worthy), and the rebuilding runs through the `meaning` engine's reconstruction of meaning, the `narrative` engine's revision of the life story, the `mortality` engine's existential confrontation, and the `identity` engine's remaking of the self. Growth comes **through** the struggle, not despite it -- and beyond the old baseline, not back to it.
- **Movements (position, grouping the five PTGI domains):**
  - `strength` -- the **self** transformed: personal strength ("I survived that, I can bear what comes") and new possibilities; the remade self and its agentic redemptive story (leans `identity` + `narrative`).
  - `wisdom` -- the **philosophy of life** transformed: deepened appreciation of life, existential/spiritual change, and a richer valuing of others, born of facing mortality and rebuilding meaning (leans `mortality` + `meaning`).

This is the first **four-atom** composite; hard links resolve across all four declared dependencies.

**Scope note.** Bounded against the `resilience` composite (the _maintenance_ of baseline -- coming through unbroken rather than remade; this delegates the mere-maintenance case there), against each atom (which owns its part of the rebuilding), against `stress`/the chronic-dysfunction trajectory (**growth can coexist with ongoing post-traumatic distress -- a separate axis from the disorder, not its opposite**), and against `hedonic-adaptation` (the mere return of pleasure to a setpoint vs a remaking of values and self).

Warranted (LORE, integrative) on Tedeschi & Calhoun (1996 PTGI; 2004 conceptual foundations -- growth through struggle, the five domains, the shattered/rebuilt assumptive world), Janoff-Bulman (1992, _Shattered Assumptions_), Calhoun & Tedeschi (2006, the handbook -- intrusive vs deliberate rumination), and Park (2010, the meaning-making model). Each atom's own REFERENCES carries the primary science of its part. Set at patch as the free level. No whitelist required -- the `post_traumatic_growth` / `growth_strength` / `growth_wisdom` stems are unique.
