Add the dwelling composite, a person held by the places that sustain them. Wires three atoms -- the third-place engine (the place one belongs at), the restorative-environment engine (the place that heals a worn attention), and the place-attachment engine (the ground one is rooted in) -- and reads them as one being-held-by-place: a place giving a self more than location. Staged as three bridges: the gathered (belonging at an informal public place -- the neutral ground, the leveler, the home away from home), the restored (recovery in a healing place -- being away, soft fascination, extent), and the rooted (the twist -- the place that is part of who one is, the rootedness and dependence, and the homesickness and placelessness of its loss). Carries the keystone: the place that roots a person is the place whose loss can unmake them. Wires at Persona/Projection (audit). Anchored in Tuan's topophilia, Oldenburg's third place, Kaplan & Kaplan's attention restoration, and Relph's rootedness and placelessness. Distinct from neighborhood-cycle (the place's change over time), space (the built fabric), and wildland (the land as agent).
