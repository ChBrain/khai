Add two behaviour modes to the fire engine, giving a place's burn regime the combustion-mode read the four-mode arc lacked: creep (the low, cool, frequent, self-limiting surface fire that maintains a place below the threshold of running) and smoulder (the flameless, hidden, months-long glowing burn of peat, duff, and root, water-shedding, overwintering, and re-emergent). Anchors smoulder in Rein's smouldering-combustion work; leaves renewal the sole twist.
