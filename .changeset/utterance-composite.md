Add the utterance composite, what a saying carries beyond its words. Wires four atoms -- the speech-act engine (the force by which an utterance performs rather than describes), the implicature engine (the meaning a hearer works out that was never stated), and the register and tone engines (the specialized variety and affective key it arrives in) -- and reads them as one cargo: an utterance as freight rather than content. Staged as three bridges: the deed (the promise that now exists and can be broken, the order that now stands, the naming that from this moment is the name -- each depending on standing, so the wrong speaker leaves the words said and the act unperformed), the unsaid (the hearer assuming cooperation and reasoning from it, so the reference praising only punctuality damns the rest and saying some conveys not all -- delivered in full with nothing on the record), and the manner (the twist -- the register that admits one hearer and shuts out another in the same breath, the key that decides whether a thing is grave or a joke). Carries the keystone: what a persona cannot be quoted as saying is what it most surely communicated -- the deed can be named and the words quoted, but the implication and the pitch carry the heaviest load and leave no trace, so a persona is answerable for the manifest and never for the freight. Wires at Persona/Projection (audit). Anchored in Austin's saying as doing, Searle's kinds of force, Grice's cooperative principle, and Halliday's register. Distinct from language (the channel meaning crosses), crosstalk (two personas talking past each other), deception/opacity (the built false belief), and presentation (the labour of performing a self).
