Add the ostracism engine (process) -- being ignored and excluded, and the staged response it triggers. Fills a gap the `loneliness` engine explicitly delegates away (it models the chronic subjective gap, not specific rejections): ostracism is the acute event of being cut off by others, and its threat to four fundamental needs -- belonging, control, self-esteem, and meaningful existence -- runs a characteristic temporal arc (Williams' need-threat model).

- **Root (process):** `ostracism` -- being shut out, and the four-need threat, detected by a fast system tuned to miss no real exclusion (and so firing at trivial ones).
- **Stages (process), Williams' temporal model:** `reflexive` (the immediate, source-independent social pain -- hurting even when the exclusion is trivial, expected, accidental, or by a despised group, on the pain circuitry Eisenberger et al. tie to physical pain), `reflective` (the coping -- fortifying the threatened needs prosocially when belonging seems recoverable, or antisocially when it does not), `resignation` (the chronic pole -- prolonged, inescapable exclusion depleting coping into alienation, helplessness, and depression).

Warranted (LORE) on Williams (the temporal need-threat model; Cyberball), Eisenberger/Lieberman/Williams (social pain overlapping physical-pain circuitry), Baumeister & DeWall (the antisocial fork), Twenge (the prosocial fork), and Williams & Nida (consequences and coping). Bounded against `loneliness` (the chronic subjective gap -- resignation hands off to it), `stigma` (the mark that invites exclusion vs the exclusion itself), `betrayal` (a trusted bond violated vs source-independent exclusion), and `sadness` (the depressive affect resignation feeds). Set at patch as the free level. No whitelist required -- the `ostracism` root and `reflexive`/`reflective`/`resignation` stage stems are unique.
