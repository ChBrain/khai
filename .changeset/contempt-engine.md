Add the contempt engine: the downward look through which a persona reckons with another judged beneath them -- regard withdrawn, worth lowered, another held below the level at which they merit full consideration. The root (`process_contempt.md`) declares contempt as a sentiment that says, where anger says _you wronged me_ and disgust says _you revolt me_, _you are beneath me_ -- colder and more lasting than either, and the strongest sign a bond is failing. It is the mirror of `envy`: envy looks up at a superior with pain and wants the gap gone; contempt looks down at an inferior with dismissal and wants the gap kept. Three forms carry the downward look: `disdain` (the cold form -- regard quietly withheld, the other dropped beneath notice and dismissed by omission rather than attack), `scorn` (the hot form -- the sneer, mockery, and open derision that lower the other aloud and before an audience), and `condescension` (the veiled form -- the patronizing talk-down that treats the other as lesser under the cover of benevolence, so the lowering is deniable).

Found via the clean audit pass; carved against the anger+disgust blend by its coldness and endurance (contempt recruits both but functions as a lasting devaluation, not a hot objection or a visceral rejection). Bounded against `emotion` (the hot anger and the contamination-disgust it recruits), `envy` (the upward reckoning it mirrors), `status`/`hierarchy` (the rank itself vs the affective devaluation), `moral-judgment` (the lens that lit the judgment), and `self-esteem` (the resting self-worth that self-contempt lowers). The patronizing speech that carries condescension is `accommodation`'s over-accommodation; this owns the contempt beneath the manner. Warranted (LORE) on Gervais & Fessler (contempt as devaluing sentiment), Rozin et al. (the CAD triad), Ekman & Friesen (the universal contempt expression), Gottman (contempt as the strongest predictor of relationship dissolution), and Fiske et al. (the stereotype content model). Set at patch as the free level; a new engine may warrant a minor at the maintainer's `bump:minor` label.
