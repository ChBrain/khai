Add the outdone composite -- the responses to upward comparison read as one family. One reckoning with another's superior standing, split by how the persona meets it -- warmly, with the pull to rise, or with the wish to pull down -- into admiration, benign envy, and malicious envy (van de Ven, Zeelenberg & Pieters). The composite authors no emotion of its own: it declares the `admiration` and `envy` engines as dependencies, wires their members by hard package links, and adds only the meeting axis the three share and the three motions across it.

- **Root:** `outdone` -- the family as one reckoning with one dial (how another's advantage is met), landing in admiration (rise toward), benign envy (move up), or malicious envy (pull down).
- **Bridges (the drama the atoms cannot stage alone):** `outdone, sort` (one comparison met three ways, forked by the appraisals of deservingness and attainability -- van de Ven's deservingness-and-control account), `outdone, turn` (the responses tipping into one another and darkening -- admiration souring to envy, benign envy hardening to malicious as the path up closes, the warm-to-hostile counterpart of condemnation's anger-to-contempt), `outdone, blend` (admiring and envying the same rival at once -- the respected rival, the frenemy, the sustained ambivalence).

Newly wireable now that `admiration` is a standalone engine and `envy` already splits into benign and malicious forms. Warranted (LORE, integrative) on van de Ven, Zeelenberg & Pieters (benign vs malicious envy, 2009; the deservingness-and-control sort, 2011), Festinger and Smith (social comparison), the evolutionary status-gap accounts (Hill & Buss), and the ambivalent-relationship literature. Bounded against `comparison` (the comparing mechanism vs the emotional response to the upward case), `schadenfreude` (the joy when the envied falls vs the response up to the fall), `status`/`hierarchy` (the rank vs the response to it), and `praise` (admiration as freely-given regard vs admiration as the benign pole of an upward comparison that could also have stung). Set at patch as the free level. No whitelist required -- the `outdone` root and `outdone_*` bridge stems are unique.
