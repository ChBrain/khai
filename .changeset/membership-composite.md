Add the membership composite: one persona's standing bind to a group -- the self they draw from it, the standard it bends them to, a mark it would discredit, and the allegiance that raises the cost of leaving. It is not a new engine but a wiring of four: `social-identity` supplies the belonging self, `conformity` supplies the group's standard and its pull on conduct, `stigma` supplies a mark the group would discredit, and `loyalty` supplies the allegiance that raises the exit cost. The root (`process_membership.md`) reads the four pulls running on one member at once, agreeing into ease or crossing into a belonging that costs; three bridges are its course: `induction` (identification draws the self from the group and brings the member under its standard -- belonging tightening into conforming, deepest where the standard stops feeling external), `covering` (the member carries a mark the standard they conform to would discredit, so belonging is held by passing and covering -- a self kept partly unseen), and `tether` (when belonging turns costly, loyalty raises the exit price past what the member will pay, converting a leaving into voice or a swallowed endurance).

Named `membership` rather than `belonging` because the stem `belonging` already belongs to the `bias` engine (a belonging-motive), and `membership` avoids overlapping the `loyalty` atom that `allegiance` would; the atoms are individual-in-group (belonging, the group's pull, a hidden mark, allegiance), not between-groups relations. khai's third composite over the group/social engines, wiring `social-identity`, `conformity`, `stigma`, and `loyalty` through hard links resolved by declared dependencies. Bounded against `loneliness` (the ache of not belonging or of a belonging lost vs the bind of being held in), `identity` (the personal "I" apart from any group vs the group-belonging "we"), and `group` (the group as an entity vs the member's tie to it); adds no content its atoms already own. Warranted (LORE) on Baumeister & Leary (the need to belong), Tajfel & Turner (social identity), Goffman and Yoshino (stigma, passing, and covering), and Hirschman (exit, voice, and loyalty). Set at patch as the free level; a new composite may warrant a minor at the maintainer's `bump:minor` label.
