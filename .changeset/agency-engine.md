Add the agency engine (packages/engines/agency): the political pole of the design domain, sibling to usability (the cognitive pole), and the second engine to declare `on: piece`. Agency as piece, the object as social delegate -- how a designed thing prescribes and forbids conduct as a political actor, enforcing a program on whom. A depth-1 engine: the delegate root over four facets -- script (Akrich, the scenario inscribed in the form), prescription (Latour/Winner/Verbeek, the deontic must and cannot), delegation (Latour's missing masses, the absent maker's will handed to the object), enlistment (Callon, the users conscripted into a program). Wires the default law at instructions/Knowledge and one specific link at piece/Load Bearing -- the sharp break from usability's Apparent: agency is what the object structurally enforces (invisible until removed), not what it shows about use. Bounds against usability (the Verbeek split), a persona's own agency (self-efficacy, on a position), space (a portable delegate vs an ambient field -- the bearer test), the history family, and the norm/compliance engines (delegation is the norm-to-artifact handoff). Sets up the artifice composite over usability + agency (now unblocked). First release; source only, tests to follow as a separate dormant PR.
