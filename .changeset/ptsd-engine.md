Add the ptsd engine (process: root + four clusters) -- post-traumatic stress disorder, a trauma that will not become past: a memory stored raw that fires as though the danger were present, driving re-experiencing and hyperarousal, clamped down by an avoidance that only keeps it alive. This is the first engine of a new **clinical tier** for khai, and the first built to foreground **mechanics** -- a disorder is a loop, not a mood, and the engine stages the loop.

- **Root (process):** `ptsd` -- the disorder as a self-maintaining circuit. A cue fires re-experiencing, arousal spikes, avoidance clamps down, and because the avoidance brings relief before the memory can be processed, it preserves the raw trace and forecloses the updating that would end it -- so the loop turns again and the estrangement deepens. The Direction and Lever chapters carry the mechanic.
- **Clusters (DSM-5 criteria B-E):**
  - `reexperiencing` (process, B) -- the past forced into the present: intrusion, nightmare, flashback, bodily distress at cues.
  - `evasion` (process, C) -- the avoidance of trauma reminders, inner and outer; the relief-reinforced manoeuvre that _maintains_ the disorder (the pivot of the mechanic).
  - `estrangement` (position, D) -- the standing negative alterations in cognition and mood: corroded belief, distorted blame, numbing, detachment.
  - `hyperarousal` (process, E) -- the body kept on a war footing: hypervigilance, startle, irritability, broken sleep.

**Warrant is a lineage, not solo work.** From the "war neurosis" first observed (Kardiner) and Freud's traumatic neurosis, through diagnostic codification (DSM-III 1980; DSM-5 2013), to the cognitive and neurobiological models of the maintaining loop -- Foa (the fear structure and the extinction avoidance blocks), Ehlers & Clark (maintaining processes), Brewin (dual representation: why the memory returns as present), van der Kolk (the body's stuck alarm).

**Scope note.** Bounded against `stress` (the acute response PTSD can follow), `grief`/`sadness` (loss and low mood), `anxiety`/`fear` (future dread and present threat -- PTSD's fear is a past event re-firing), and `memory`/`body` (the general faculties it distorts). Stems `ptsd`/`reexperiencing`/`evasion`/`estrangement`/`hyperarousal` are unique -- distinct from the homonym senses in `goal` (avoidance), `body` (arousal), `sadness` (dysphoria), `proxemics` (intrusion), `surprise` (startle) -- so no whitelist is needed.

**This is authored persona-architecture -- the structure of a traumatized character, for a play -- never a diagnostic instrument for a real person**, and the content says so throughout. Complex PTSD (the disturbances in self-organization after prolonged trauma; Herman, Cloitre, ICD-11) is planned as a composite over this engine and others, not folded in here. Set at patch.
