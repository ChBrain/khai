Add the rapport composite: the felt quality of a connection working -- the sense of clicking with another -- built where three things run together and reinforce. It is not a new engine but a wiring of three: `contagion` supplies the unconscious synchrony of two bodies falling into step, `grounding` supplies the understanding that keeps landing, and `self-disclosure` supplies the mutual opening that deepens the tie. The root (`process_rapport.md`) reads the three reinforcing into felt rapport -- sync easing understanding, understanding making opening safe, opening returned tightening the sync, the mirror of the crosstalk loop run upward. Three bridges are its course: `meshing` (synchrony and understanding arrive together, so the pair feels aligned beneath the words and met within them -- the click before it deepens), `warming` (the mesh makes opening safe, matched disclosure returns, and rapport climbs by a spiral of opening met by opening -- or the opening outruns the footing and exposes), and `veneer` (the coordination, rapport's most visible and most forgeable component, is produced without the understanding or opening beneath it, so the surface parts company with the substance -- the counterfeit that reads real until called on).

The positive counterpart to `crosstalk`, over the same communicative machinery run the other way; shares only `grounding` with it, and uses grounding's success moves where crosstalk uses misgrounding. Bounded against `accommodation` (the chosen style-convergence vs rapport's unconscious synchrony), `attachment` (the lasting bond rapport can seed vs the momentary interactional quality), and `emotion` (the felt liking it produces vs the connection working); adds no content its atoms already own. Warranted (LORE) on Tickle-Degnen & Rosenthal (the three-component model of rapport), Chartrand & Bargh (the chameleon effect -- mimicry and liking), Aron et al. (escalating reciprocal disclosure generating closeness), and Bernieri & Rosenthal (interactional synchrony, and its gap from felt connection). Set at patch as the free level; a new composite may warrant a minor at the maintainer's `bump:minor` label.
