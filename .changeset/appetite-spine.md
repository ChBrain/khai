Name the connective source for the appetite composite. A composite's atoms carry their own anchors, but those justify the parts and never the joining; the Line of Work previously asserted that "three literatures each describe a requirement and disagree only about where it originates" with no author behind the claim that they are describing one thing. Replaces that with Berridge's survey of motivation concepts in behavioral neuroscience (Physiology & Behavior, 2004), which takes the historically influential concepts in turn -- the homeostatic drive a deficit generates, the incentive salience a stimulus acquires, and the learning that binds them -- and argues them as components of one motivational system rather than rival accounts of one thing, which is this composite's claim in the register the neuroscience uses. Deci and Ryan supply the joining on the other flank, since self-determination theory is built on basic psychological needs and derives its motivations from them, so the given and the taken are one account there too. Berridge leads the Origin table marked as the joining, distinguishing it from the rows that anchor individual bridges, and the Line of Work now states what the composite adds on top of the published joining: the reading of the three as provenances, that the question worth asking of a must is where it came from, and the keystone that follows, that the organism has no test for it.
