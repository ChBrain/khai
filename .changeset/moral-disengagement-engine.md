Add the moral-disengagement engine: the cognitive moves by which a persona selectively switches off its own moral self-sanctions to do harm without self-censure -- and still regard itself as decent. The root (`process_moral_disengagement.md`) declares Bandura's account of moral agency: conduct is ordinarily governed by anticipatory self-condemnation, and disengagement deactivates that deterrent for a specific act, the standards intact for others and suspended just for this -- how ordinary, otherwise-decent people do cruel things and live with themselves. Four forms map to Bandura's four loci in the self-regulatory process: `justification` (reconstruing the conduct -- moral justification, euphemistic labeling, advantageous comparison), `displacement` (obscuring the agency -- displacement and diffusion of responsibility), `minimizing` (distorting the consequences -- disregarding, minimizing, or misconstruing the harm), and `blaming` (devaluing the recipient -- dehumanization and attribution of blame).

A foundational moral-psychology gap, and the off-switch that pairs tightly with `guilt`. Bounded against `moral-judgment` (the lens for judging others vs disabling one's own conscience), `guilt` (the self-sanction that fires vs its forestalling), `aggression` (the harmful act vs what licenses it), `deception` (misleading another vs exonerating oneself -- it misleads the self), and `contempt`/`intergroup` (the standing devaluation and the group relation vs the purposive victim-devaluing that permits a harm). Four is the faithful count: Bandura's eight mechanisms cluster at these four loci. No homonym gate: `moral-disengagement` and the form stems `justification`/`displacement`/`minimizing`/`blaming` are all free. Warranted (LORE) on Bandura (_Moral Disengagement in the Perpetration of Inhumanities_, 1999; _Selective Moral Disengagement_, 2002; the mechanisms study with Barbaranelli, Caprara & Pastorelli, 1996; the 2016 book; and _Social Foundations of Thought and Action_, 1986). Set at patch as the free level; a new engine may warrant a minor at the maintainer's `bump:minor` label.
