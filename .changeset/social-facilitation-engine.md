Add the social-facilitation engine: how the presence of others changes a persona's own arousal, effort, and performance -- before anyone has said a word. The root (`process_social_facilitation.md`) declares Zajonc's drive theory: the mere presence of others (an observing audience or a co-actor) raises arousal, which strengthens the dominant response -- lifting a simple or well-learned task and spoiling a complex or novel one -- while pooling the persona's contribution unaccountably in a group slackens effort instead. Three forms carry it: `enhancement` (the lift -- presence sharpening a dominant, well-learned, or simple task, the expert crisper before a crowd), `choking` (the spoiling -- presence and felt evaluation impairing a non-dominant, complex, or novel task, the pressured shot missed), and `loafing` (the reversal -- effort dropping when the contribution is pooled and unaccountable, each hand pulling less on the rope).

A foundational, recognizable social-psychology gap (Triplett's racing cyclists, Zajonc's audience effect, choking under pressure, the free rider). Bounded against `crowd` (the mass of bodies as an environment vs the presence effect on the individual -- social facilitation works from as little as one observer), `conformity` (matching others' positions vs the arousal/effort effect on performance), `group` (the group's development over time vs one member's effort-drop), and `body`/`apprehension` (the raw arousal and the evaluation anxiety it recruits vs the performance consequence). No homonym gate: `social-facilitation` and the form stems `enhancement`/`choking`/`loafing` are all free (`inhibition` avoided -- executive-function owns it). Warranted (LORE) on Triplett (the founding co-action study, 1898), Zajonc (social facilitation / drive theory, 1965), Cottrell (evaluation apprehension, 1972), Latané, Williams & Harkins (social loafing, 1979), and Baumeister (choking under pressure, 1984). Set at patch as the free level; a new engine may warrant a minor at the maintainer's `bump:minor` label.
