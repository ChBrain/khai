Add the embodied-metaphor engine (position: anchor + expressions) -- how a persona's abstract concepts are scaffolded on recurring, near-universal embodied source domains, so bodily and sensorimotor experience gives the abstract its shape and leaks into abstract judgment (in both directions). The gap analysis (Tier-3 #36) flagged this as impeccably warranted but poor engine-shape (diffuse mechanism), maintainer judgment; built as a **scoped position engine** over the primary-metaphor families rather than the whole diffuse principle.

- **Anchor (position):** `embodied-metaphor` -- the structuring principle beneath a persona's abstract thought: it thinks affection, importance, morality, power, and valence through the body, and the scaffolding leaks both ways (an incidental sensation tilts an abstract judgment; an abstract state colours bodily experience) -- Lakoff & Johnson's conceptual metaphor, Williams & Bargh's experimental scaffolding.
- **Faces (position)** -- five well-evidenced primary metaphors, each a bodily _source_ scaffolding an abstract _target_:
  - `temperature` -- warm/cold ↔ affection/hostility (Williams & Bargh: warm cup → warmer person).
  - `verticality` -- up/down ↔ good, power, happy, more (Meier & Robinson; Schubert).
  - `weight` -- heavy/light ↔ importance, seriousness (Jostmann, Lakens & Schubert).
  - `cleanliness` -- clean/dirty ↔ moral purity/pollution (Zhong & Liljenquist's Macbeth effect).
  - `brightness` -- light/dark ↔ good/evil, positive/negative (Meier, Robinson & Clore).

**Scope note -- the scaffolding, not the mechanism or the targets.** The general capacity to map any source onto any target stays with `analogy` (embodied metaphor is the entrenched, near-universal _bodily_ subset); the rhetorical use of a metaphor to persuade stays with `framing`; the setting of representational level by psychological distance stays with `construal` (a different concrete↔abstract route); and the **target concepts themselves** stay with their own engines (affection → love/attachment, power → hierarchy, moral judgment → moral-judgment, disgust → disgust, valence → emotion). This engine owns only the specific bodily mappings a persona lives by without choosing them.

Warranted (LORE) on Lakoff & Johnson (1980, _Metaphors We Live By_; 1999, _Philosophy in the Flesh_ -- conceptual metaphor grounded in the body), Williams & Bargh (2008, physical/interpersonal warmth), Meier & Robinson (2004, good-is-up; brightness-valence with Clore), Jostmann, Lakens & Schubert (2009, weight-importance), and Zhong & Liljenquist (2006, "Washing Away Your Sins"). Set at patch as the free level. No whitelist required -- the `embodied_metaphor` / `temperature` / `verticality` / `weight` / `cleanliness` / `brightness` stems are unique (`warmth`/`embodiment` avoided as taken).
