Add the weather engine (packages/engines/weather): the first engine to declare `on: place`, the opening proof of the cross-type frontier. Weather as process, the atmospheric force a place undergoes, moving what the place shows before anyone speaks. Templated on the language engine: a channels-by-bands tree of 28 members. Five transient channels (wind by Beaufort, water by precipitation-rate, heat and cold by the Fanger/wet-bulb/wind-chill thresholds, sky by the daylighting/bioclimatic tradition) each climb one four-band intensity ladder (still/stirring/besieging/routing; sky three, capped at shut); a sixth standing channel, season, never climbs but sets the floor the others rise from, the way language keeps thinking underneath. Wires the default law at instructions/Knowledge and one specific link at place/Shown. Bounds against space (the setting-as-such), climate (the abstract regime), the mood engines and pathetic fallacy (refused: a weather made to mirror feeling is the play's pitch), and the downstream events (hydrology, fire, erosion, soundscape). First release; source only, tests to follow as a separate dormant PR.
