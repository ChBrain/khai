Add the striving composite, the standing character a persona brings to any pursuit. Wires three dispositional atoms -- the achievement-motive engine (why one strives: hope of success or fear of failure), the regulatory-focus engine (how one strives: promotion's eagerness or prevention's vigilance), and the grit engine (how long one stays: passion and perseverance) -- and reads them as one striving character, prior to any particular goal. Staged as three bridges: the pull (the motive that decides whether the attempt is made at all, and bends goal choice toward the too-easy or the too-hard), the stance (the orientation that shapes the chase, fitting the task or fighting it), and the stay (the twist -- the stamina that holds a distant aim through setback and plateau). Carries the keystone: the stamina that carries a persona through setback is the stamina that holds it to a path it should have left -- a self that cannot be discouraged cannot be informed. Wires at Persona/Projection (audit). Anchored in Atkinson & McClelland's achievement motive, Higgins's regulatory focus, Duckworth's grit, and the costly-perseverance finding from Lucas & Gratch. Distinct from volition (the crossing from wanting to doing), self-control (the momentary override), mindset (beliefs about ability), and goal (the particular aim).
