Add the combustion engine: a flat catalog of the named phenomena of fire, each fixed by its fuel and chemistry, physical dynamics, sensory signature, and extinguishment paradox. Where the fire engine reads a place's abstract burn regime, combustion reads the specific phenomenon. Opens with the wildland domain -- crown, grass, chaparral, peat, holdover, spotting, slash, and serotiny -- and grows domain by domain (compartment, industrial, made fire to follow). Wires on place/Shown.
