Add the sensorium composite, the place as the body meets it apart from the eye. Wires three atoms -- the smellscape engine (the olfactory field), the soundscape engine (the acoustic field), and the touch engine (the haptic field) -- and reads the shape all three independently take: a ground that works below notice, a figure that rises from it, and an absence read as a presence. Staged as three bridges: the ground (the base smell, the keynote, the texture and thermal -- unremarked precisely because it never changes, so the body acclimatises and can no longer name the layer that colours everything else), the figure (the plume, signal, and press that rise, and the redolence and soundmark that bind a place to memory and name it -- owing their force to the ground they contrast with), and the absence (the twist -- the scentless room, the silence, the surface withheld from the hand). Carries the keystone: a place is felt hardest where a sense has been taken away -- the senses are built for a world that always supplies something, so an absence is a violated expectation the body reads as information, and the withheld field registers more strongly than any field supplied. Wires at Place/Shown (audit), the first composite on the place surface since blaze. Anchored in Pallasmaa's body-met place against the eye-addressed one, Schafer's keynote/signal/soundmark and silence-as-element, Porteous's smellscape, and the sensory-anthropology reading of the visual bias in what gets recorded. Distinct from space (the setting as a party to events), street-life (the place as a social stage), proxemics (the distance between persons), body (the body's own demands), and dwelling (how a place sustains a person).
