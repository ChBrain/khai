Add the appetite composite, what a persona must have and how the must was made. Wires three atoms -- the needs engine (the requirement the organism arrives with, ordered by prepotency and split between lacks that quiet when filled and growths that do not), the motivation engine (the demand taken in from outside, at a depth that decides what kind of effort it yields), and the addiction engine (the requirement manufactured by the pursuing itself) -- and reads the seam the three make: three provenances for one kind of pressure. Staged as three bridges: the given (the plural requirements a persona arrives carrying, the basic-and-unmet claiming them first along a soft parallel ordering rather than a staircase, with the lower reasserting whenever it fails), the taken (a demand from outside let in to a depth that is everything -- the act that is its own reason costs nothing to give, while the should swallowed whole and never made the persona's own is fully inside and still not theirs, buying effort that is real, brittle, and gone the moment the pressure lifts), and the made (the twist -- a currency the organism came to count on, the baseline reset so the payout that once arrived as a bonus is now the level that must be reached to feel normal, the wanting sensitizing as the liking flattens so the persona is pulled hardest toward what pleases them least). Carries the keystone: from inside, a must a persona made presses exactly like one they were born with -- the organism has no test for provenance, which is why an addiction is honestly reported as a need and why an instruction to simply stop is addressed to the wrong category. The bridge carries the honest qualification too: satisfaction separates a given lack from a made compulsion and leaves growth on the same side as capture, since an actualization need also deepens with feeding, the two told apart only by which direction they open. Wires at Persona/Projection (audit). Anchored in Maslow's prepotency and the deficiency/being distinction, Tay and Diener on the soft parallel ordering across 123 countries, Deci and Ryan's organismic integration and the overjustification effect, Robinson and Berridge's incentive sensitization, and Koob and Volkow on allostasis. Distinct from ingraining (the installing of a response until it runs without consulting why), striving (the character brought to any pursuit), volition and self-control (the crossing into action and the momentary override), boredom (the unengaged mind seeking a target), scarcity (what a felt shortage does to the capacity to think), and lasting (what becomes of a good already arrived).
