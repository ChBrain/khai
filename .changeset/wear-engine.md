Add the wear engine: the second element of the object-lifecycle family -- the degradation an object undergoes through use rather than time, decay's complement (the unused thing rots where it sits, the used thing wears in the hand). Four modes over a use root, three of loss and one twist: abrasion (surface rubbed away by friction), flexure (part weakened and cracked by repeated stress -- named flexure since the body engine owns fatigue), depletion (the consumable spent to nothing), and seasoning (the twist -- use that improves and individualizes, worn in not out, wear's answer to decay's patina). Wires on: piece at Load Bearing. Lineage: Pye, Bowden & Tabor (tribology), Wohler (fatigue), Semenov (use-wear), Csikszentmihalyi & Rochberg-Halton.
