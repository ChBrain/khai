Add the presentation composite, the self performed before an audience, read as labour rather than display. Wires four atoms -- the face engine (the image claimed in interaction and the redress that protects it), the backstage engine (the frontstage where the claim is held and the backstage where it is dropped), the self-monitoring engine (the watching and tuning that fits the display to the room), and the emotional-labor engine (the feeling managed to sustain it). Staged as three bridges: the front (the image claimed and defended, held where the audience can see), the tuning (the high self-monitor becoming what each room rewards against the low one letting the same self show regardless), and the cost (the twist -- the feeling masked on the surface or induced by method until real, and the backstage that would give the self back). Carries the keystone: the mask worn deeply enough stops being a mask -- what makes the display convincing is what makes it no longer a display. Wires at Persona/Projection (audit). Anchored in Goffman's presentation of self, Brown & Levinson's face, Snyder's self-monitoring, and Hochschild's managed heart. Distinct from opacity (the truth-status of what is let through), authenticity/self-relation (whether the self is true to itself), standing (the rank competed for), and rapport (the connection's felt quality).
