Add the knowing composite -- the knowledge emotions read as one family. Surprise, interest, and confusion are the feelings a mind runs as it meets the novel and works to comprehend it, and they share one appraisal space: how far a thing violates expectation, how novel and complex it is, and whether it can be grasped. The composite authors no emotion of its own: it declares the `surprise`, `interest`, and `confusion` engines as dependencies, wires their members by hard package links, and adds only the shared axis and the three motions across it.

- **Root:** `knowing` -- the family as one reckoning with the unexpected, sorted by appraisal into surprise (violation → jolt), interest (novel + graspable → engage), or confusion (novel + ungraspable → block).
- **Bridges (the drama the atoms cannot stage alone):** `knowing, sort` (one encounter forked three ways by expectation-violation and the novelty/coping appraisals -- Silvia's appraisal structure with Meyer et al. on surprise), `knowing, turn` (the three tipping into one another as the appraisals shift -- surprise settling, interest curdling to confusion, confusion opening to interest, both ways -- D'Mello & Graesser's transitions), `knowing, arc` (the trajectory of a whole knowledge episode from the opening jolt through engagement and impasse to comprehension or disengagement -- the affect dynamics of learning, and why the productive arc runs _through_ confusion, not around it).

Newly wireable now that `surprise`, `interest`, and `confusion` all stand as engines -- the capstone over Silvia's knowledge-emotions quartet (with `awe` the fourth, handed its vastness case). Warranted (LORE, integrative) on Silvia (the knowledge emotions and their appraisal structure), D'Mello & Graesser (the affect dynamics of learning), Meyer/Reisenzein/Schützwohl (surprise's expectation-violation), Pekrun (epistemic emotions), and D'Mello et al. 2014 (confusion beneficial for learning). Bounded against `awe` (the vast-yet-unassimilable), `curiosity` (the resting disposition beneath the episodic emotions), `flow` (total absorption of a matched challenge), and `boredom` (the flat state the failed arc decays into). Set at patch as the free level. No whitelist required -- the `knowing` root and `knowing_*` bridge stems are unique.
