Add the hydrology engine: the water regime of a place, wired on: place/Shown. The third engine of the land's-forces family (F-place). A depth-1 process engine -- a water root over four modes: flood (water in destructive excess), drought (water in destructive deficit), groundwater (the hidden subsurface store), and the wellspring twist (the same water read as the place's own life -- the perennial river, the oasis, the reason a place is settled). It is the water itself: it supplies the agent erosion works and inherits the input weather delivers, but reads the balance over the season and year, not the rain of the hour.
