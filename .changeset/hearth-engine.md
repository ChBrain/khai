Add the hearth engine: the kept flame as an institution of a place -- fire that must
not die, and what its keeping means. A root over five modes: tending (the daily
feeding and watching, the flame as a clock of care), banking (the fire put to sleep
in its own ash to survive the night, revived from the seed coal), rekindling (the
relight from one's own coal, a carried ember, or a neighbour's hearth -- renewal and
debt at once), going-cold (the hearth extinguished as the social event: departure,
eviction, death, sanction), and perpetual (the eternal flame -- Vesta's fire, the
Atash Bahram fed five times daily, the sanctuary lamp -- and the crisis when it goes
out). Anchored by Fustel de Coulanges (the sacred hearth as the household's
institution), Goudsblom (fire-keeping preceding fire-making by a million years),
Bachelard, Boyce, and Beard. Delegates the flame's mechanics to combustion, the burn
regime and the escaped fire to fire, the rite to ritual, and the flame-as-mirror to
the play's pitch. Fills the "kept flame" row of ENGINE-GAPS' fire-adjacent cluster.
