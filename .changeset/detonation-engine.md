Add the detonation engine: the blast as its own force, read at doctrine, history, and
investigation altitude -- never formulation, hardware, or engineering, which the engine
refuses outright rather than delegates. A root over five modes: initiation (the
controlled shot, the whole craft spent before the button), sympathetic (the neighbour
that fires because the first did, quantity-distance siting as the whole defence),
misfire (the shot that did not go -- the silence that is the hazard, the imposed wait),
uxo (the unexploded that waits decades -- Flanders' iron harvest, the German city
evacuations, mine action as a generational task), and breach (the wall opened on
purpose, the fall designed as carefully as the standing). Anchored by Cooper's
Explosives Engineering, NFPA 495 and the UN quantity-distance tradition, the
GICHD/IMAS mine-action standards, MSHA/OSHA misfire rules, and Loizeaux's demolition
account. Combustion keeps every explosion that grows out of burning; the fire a blast
starts is fire's; the incendiary stays a future engine's. Fills the "detonation" row
of ENGINE-GAPS' fire-adjacent cluster. First release: empty changeset. Tests follow
in their own PR.
