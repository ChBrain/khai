Add the crosstalk composite: two personas talking past each other in a self-sustaining loop, where each grounds what they hear against their own frame of the exchange, so every attempt to understand misfires into a fresh misgrounding and the gap widens turn by turn instead of closing. It is not a new engine but a wiring of three: `framing` supplies the mismatched frames each grounds against, `grounding` supplies the misgrounding that results, and `accommodation` supplies the style divergence that widens the rift. The root (`process_crosstalk.md`) reads the loop the three atoms will not stage apart; three bridges are its course: `mismatch` (two frames fail to meet, so honest evidence of understanding is false to the other -- misgrounding by structure, invisible from inside each frame), `loop` (each misgrounded turn feeds the next, style diverging as frustration mounts, the gap compounding while each punctuates the sequence as the other's fault), and `impasse` (a clarification that reaches the frame catches it and re-grounds the two, or the loop hardens into estrangement -- the catch having to repair a whole frame, not a word, which is why crosstalk persists where plain misunderstanding resolves).

khai's first composite over the communication-science engines, wiring `grounding`, `framing`, and `accommodation` through hard links resolved by declared dependencies. Bounded against `escalation` (the conflict-spiral a crosstalk can ignite -- retaliation toward lose-lose -- vs the understanding-spiral it is) and `repair` (the mending of the relationship a hardened crosstalk damages vs the misunderstanding that estranged it); adds no content its atoms already own. Warranted (LORE) on Gumperz (contextualization cues and the source of the term), Watzlawick, Beavin & Jackson (the punctuation of the interaction sequence), Tannen (conversational style and compounding mutual misunderstanding), and Laing (the interpersonal knot). Set at patch as the free level; a new composite may warrant a minor at the maintainer's `bump:minor` label.
