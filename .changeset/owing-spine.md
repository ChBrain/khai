Name the connective source for the owing composite. A composite's atoms carry their own anchors, but those justify the parts and never the joining; the Line of Work previously asserted "a single line of argument running from anthropology through sociology to the history of money" with no author behind the line. Replaces that with Graeber's Debt: The First 5,000 Years, which argues the three degrees as one history -- human economies running on the radical incalculability of a life and on open-ended reciprocity, the quantification that alone lets an obligation become simple, cold, impersonal and therefore transferable, and the Axial-age coinage that put a figure on portions of those economies, with the passage between them forced rather than gradual. Gregory's Gifts and Commodities is added as the same axis in the ethnographic register. Both lead the Origin table marked as the joining, distinguishing them from the rows that anchor individual bridges, and the Line of Work now states what the composite adds on top of the published joining: the reading of the last step as a loss to the parties rather than only to the society, that what can be settled in full is what stops holding anyone. Graeber's row is re-scoped from an owed-bridge anchor to the spine, and the duplicate summary of his argument is trimmed from the atom run.
