Add the usability engine (packages/engines/usability): the third proof of the cross-type frontier and the first engine to declare `on: piece`. Usability as piece, the design of an object acting on its user -- how the form communicates and governs its use, the design of everyday things read on the object. A depth-1 engine: the conceptual-model root over four facets -- grip (Gibson affordance, the action the form offers), signifier (Norman, the perceivable cue), feedback (Wiener/Norman, the confirming response), constraint (Norman/Verbeek, the form that forbids the wrong use). Wires the default law at instructions/Knowledge and one specific link at piece/Apparent. Resolves the naming: the object-design engine is usability, not affordance (the affordance stem is space's, same science different scale); Gibson's affordance is carried as grip. Bounds against space (affordance of the setting), the history family (biography/patina), agency (the object as social delegate -- constraint stays cognitive, social program handed to agency), and a persona's skill (persona-side). First release; source only, tests to follow as a separate dormant PR.
