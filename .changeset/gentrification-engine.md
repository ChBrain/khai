Add the gentrification engine: the re-entry phase of the neighborhood-cycle family -- the capital re-entry a place undergoes when the rent-gap disinvestment opened grows wide enough to profit, converting what the place offered into what it withholds behind a price and displacing its prior occupants. Four facets over a re-entry root: rent-gap (the mechanism, receiving dereliction's handoff), upgrading (the fabric improved, the seam nearest restoration), eviction (the whole of displacement, the Withheld made human), and commodification (the place's character sold back as authenticity). Wires on: place at Withheld. Lineage: Glass, Smith, Zukin, Marcuse.
