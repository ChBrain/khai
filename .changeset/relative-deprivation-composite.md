Add the relative-deprivation composite (process: root + two forms) -- the grievance that arises when an unfavorable comparison is appraised as unjust and ignites resentment. The gap analysis (Tier-3 #32) noted `comparison` is a neutral evaluation; this is the affective-injustice layer, and leans composite.

- **Root (process):** `relative-deprivation` -- reads the compound over two atoms it declares as dependencies: the `comparison` engine's **upward comparison** (the persona or its group against a better-off referent) and the `anger` engine's **grievance and indignation** (the resentment the gap ignites). The composite adds no third atom, only the **injustice appraisal** -- the judgment that the disadvantage is undeserved and wrongful, which turns a neutral difference into a felt deprivation (Runciman; Smith & Pettigrew's three requirements: comparison + disadvantage + felt injustice).
- **Forms (position, the two levels the grievance is pitched at):**
  - `relative deprivation, egoistic` -- the persona deprived as an **individual** against other individuals; personal resentment, stress, self-focused response (Runciman; Crosby).
  - `relative deprivation, fraternal` -- the persona's **group** deprived against other groups; the form that drives **collective action**, protest, and intergroup hostility (Runciman; Smith & Pettigrew's meta-analytic finding that group-based, not individual, RD predicts collective action).

**Scope note.** The neutral evaluation of standing stays with `comparison`; the resentment itself stays with `anger`; the pain at another's specific advantage (absent the injustice-and-group core) stays with `envy`; the identification with the group whose deprivation is felt stays with `social-identity`. The composite reads only the compound and its injustice layer, restating no atom -- every hard link resolves through a declared dependency.

Warranted (LORE, integrative) on Runciman (1966, _Relative Deprivation and Social Justice_ -- the egoistic/fraternal distinction), Smith & Pettigrew (2012, the theoretical and meta-analytic model), Crosby (1976, the preconditions of egoistic RD), and Walker & Smith (2002, the consolidation).
