Stamp any of the three house kinds. `--kind <stage|work|canon>` sets the house's identity and structure: a `work` or `canon` house is named `khai-<source>`, indexes a collection named for itself (or `--collection <name>`), declares it in `khai.collection`, and gets that directory and registry key instead of `plays`. `stage` is the default and is unchanged.
