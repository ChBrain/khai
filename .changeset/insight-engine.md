Add the insight engine (process: root + forms) -- the knowledge emotion of the sudden aha, a problem at impasse whose representation restructures all at once and the solution arrives whole, with high confidence and no gradual feeling-of-warmth ramp. The gap analysis (Tier-3 #27) placed this in the knowledge-emotion family alongside `confusion`, `curiosity`, `interest`, `surprise`, and `awe`, distinct from `confusion`'s effortful resolve.

- **Root (process):** `insight` -- the aha: a persona stuck at an impasse, its analytic effort spent, when the frame gives way and the solution appears whole. Marked by Metcalfe & Wiebe's signature: feeling-of-warmth stays flat through the impasse, then leaps at solution (where step-by-step solving warms steadily). The impasse breaks because the representation restructures (Ohlsson).
- **Forms (process)** -- Ohlsson/Knoblich's three mechanisms of representational change:
  - `re-encoding` -- a misleading element reinterpreted or overlooked information added; a part read anew.
  - `constraint-relaxation` -- a self-imposed constraint the problem never required, dropped, opening forbidden moves.
  - `chunk-decomposition` -- a tight perceptual chunk broken into parts that can be regrouped.

**Scope note -- bounded within the knowledge-emotion family.** The effortful, warmth-ramped sensemaking of the not-yet-comprehended stays with `confusion` (insight is the sudden break that can _end_ that struggle, not the struggle itself); the appetite that drives the search stays with `curiosity`; the alarm at a violated expectation stays with `surprise`. Also contrasted against incremental/analytic problem solving (the non-insight route that warms steadily) -- this engine owns only the sudden-restructuring case.

Warranted (LORE) on Metcalfe & Wiebe (1987, "Intuition in Insight and Noninsight Problem Solving" -- the flat-then-jump warmth signature), Ohlsson (1992, representational change theory -- impasse as representation, aha as restructuring), Knoblich, Ohlsson, Haider & Rhenius (1999 -- constraint relaxation and chunk decomposition operationalized on matchstick arithmetic), and Mednick (1962, the associative basis of the leap). Set at patch as the free level. No whitelist required -- the `insight` / `re_encoding` / `constraint_relaxation` / `chunk_decomposition` stems are unique (the `insight` root is distinct from `bias`'s `asymmetric_insight`).
