Add the jeopardy composite: a persona under a threat that has not yet fallen, meeting it over time with three responses at once -- the baseline nervous system they bring, the anticipatory hold on the outcome not yet landed, and the routing of a demand that exceeds their resources. It is not a new engine but a wiring of three: `apprehension` supplies the standing filter that sets how sharply the threat registers, `suspense` supplies the anticipation that holds the outcome present while it is only possible, and `stress` supplies the response that fires when the load outstrips the reserve. The root (`process_jeopardy.md`) reads the state the three make together; three bridges are its course: `onset` (the threat lands on the disposition and opens the anticipation at a gain the baseline sets -- the same cue gripping a hyperreactive system and passing a hyporeactive one), `mounting` (as the interval closes and the demand outruns resources, the anticipation tips into the fight/flight/freeze/fawn response, and the two compound -- dread draining the reserve, depletion sharpening the dread), and `release` (a reprieve discharges both at once, or none comes and the mobilization built for a burst grinds into chronic exhaustion).

Named `jeopardy` rather than `threat` because the stem `threat` is already a member of the `awe` engine (threat-based awe); this composite is the experience of being under threat over time. khai's second composite over the emotion/stress engines, wiring `apprehension`, `suspense`, and `stress` through hard links resolved by declared dependencies. Bounded against `emotion` (the felt fear once the threat is present and the relief once it lifts vs the state before the outcome) and `grief` (the loss if the harm actually arrives vs the threat while it is still a threat); adds no content its atoms already own. Warranted (LORE) on Lazarus & Folkman (threat appraisal and the transactional model), Gray (the threat-sensitive system and its standing gain), LeDoux (defensive survival circuits), and Selye (the general adaptation syndrome and exhaustion when the stressor persists). Set at patch as the free level; a new composite may warrant a minor at the maintainer's `bump:minor` label.
