Name the connective source for the ingraining composite. A composite's atoms carry their own anchors, but those justify the parts and never the joining; the Line of Work previously asserted "the convergence of three independently established literatures" with no author behind the convergence. Replaces that with Balleine and Dickinson's two-system account of instrumental action (Neuropharmacology, 1998), where the valuation and the groove are argued as one architecture -- a goal-directed process built from response-outcome contingency and acquired incentive value, and a stimulus-response habit mechanism that takes over with repetition -- and Holmes, Marchand and Coutureau's review of Pavlovian-to-instrumental transfer (2010), which closes the prediction into the same system, a cue that merely predicts a reward eliciting or amplifying the instrumental response aimed at it. Both are added to the Origin table marked as the joining, distinguishing them from the rows that anchor individual bridges, and the Line of Work now states plainly what the composite adds on top of the published joining: the direction of travel, that each stage shortens the loop between situation and act.
