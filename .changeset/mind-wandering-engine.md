Add the mind-wandering engine (process: root + forms) -- self-generated, task-unrelated thought that decouples attention from the world and carries it inward, the drift away from the task. The gap analysis (Tier-3 #28) found `attention` owns the selecting of the spotlight, but nothing owned the self-generated drift away from it.

- **Root (process):** `mind-wandering` -- attention leaves the task and the outer world, decouples from perception (the signature mechanism), and turns inward to a train of thought the persona did not read off the environment, running to the self and, with a marked prospective bias, to the future (Smallwood & Schooler). Double-edged: the cost of the missed exit and the wandering-mind unhappiness (Killingsworth & Gilbert), against the benefit of plans made and problems incubated.
- **Forms (process)** -- the modes by intention and meta-awareness:
  - `unbidden` -- the spontaneous drift, begun without intent, against the will to stay on task (Seli).
  - `zoning-out` -- the drift run _without_ meta-awareness, the persona unaware it has even left (Schooler; the deepest decoupling -- the page read without reading).
  - `tuning-out` -- the noticed and often chosen drift, aware disengagement that can plan and be steered (Schooler; deliberate mind-wandering). The modes are not exclusive: a drift can begin unbidden, sink into zoning out, or be caught and owned as tuning out.

**Scope note -- bounded against the neighbours.** The allocation of the attentional spotlight stays with `attention` (this is the drift _away_, a lapse of the sustained attention it holds); the repetitive, negative, non-resolving circling stays with `rumination` (mind-wandering ranges freely, not defined by distress); the aversive unengaged-and-seeking signal stays with `boredom` (mind-wandering is where that mind goes); the total task absorption stays with `flow` (the near-opposite -- full coupling vs decoupling).

Warranted (LORE) on Smallwood & Schooler (2015, "The Science of Mind Wandering" -- the definition and perceptual decoupling), Schooler et al. (2011 -- the tuning-out/zoning-out meta-awareness distinction), Seli, Risko, Smilek & Schacter (2016 -- intentionality as a central dimension), and Killingsworth & Gilbert (2010, "A Wandering Mind Is an Unhappy Mind" -- prevalence and affective cost). Set at patch as the free level. No whitelist required -- the `mind_wandering` / `unbidden` / `zoning_out` / `tuning_out` stems are unique.
