not combustion, a sibling place-force to weather. A root over six modes: sun (the
place's governor -- the day's arc, the seasons' hinges, the solstice-aligned monument),

the magnetosphere -- the Carrington Event carrying it to Havana), and starfield (the
fixed ceiling and oldest navigation, now invisible to a third of humanity -- Falchi et
al. 2016). Anchored by Krupp and Aveni's cultural astronomy and the historical record.
