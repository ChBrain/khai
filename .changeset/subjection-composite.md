Add the subjection composite, the person made by institutional power. Wires four atoms -- the socialization engine (the ready-made world internalized until it is simply how things are), the discipline engine (Foucault's surveillance, norm, examination, and the docility they produce), the obedience engine (the agentic handover to a legitimate command), and the total-institution engine (the barrier, batch living, and mortification of an enclosing place) -- and reads them as one making: power that produces a person rather than merely restraining one. Staged as three bridges: the forming (a made world swallowed until the arrangement wears the face of reality, leaving a competent member who cannot see it as an arrangement), the training (conduct shaped by a watching that need not watch, until the persona holds its own line and the institution need spend nothing), and the surrender (the twist -- the self handed to a command against its own conscience, or to a place that runs all of life and strips the self it admits). Carries the keystone: the grip is complete only when it stops being felt as a grip -- the obedient persona reports doing its duty, the disciplined one polices itself, the institutionalized one wants the walls back, and the conduct a persona is surest is its own is the best evidence of what made it. Wires at Persona/Projection (audit). Anchored in Berger & Luckmann's internalization, Foucault's disciplinary power, Milgram's agentic shift, and Goffman's total institution. Distinct from membership (a persona's bind to a group), standing (rank and its moves), org (the organization's own workings), and selfhood (the private self-concept).
