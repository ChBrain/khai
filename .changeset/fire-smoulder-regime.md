Re-ground the fire engine's smoulder mode at regime altitude. The mode had been
anchored in Rein's smouldering-combustion work and carried the phenomenon's own
mechanics (retained heat, oxygen-starved creep, the lancing paradox, spread rates,
overwintering) -- content the combustion engine now owns as peat, duff, and holdover.
Smoulder is rewritten as the regime read: the place whose ground is itself the fuel,
whose fire is governed by the water table, woken by drainage and drought, burning
beneath a spared-looking surface on a clock of seasons at the cost of its stored
carbon -- anchored by Turetsky et al. (Global vulnerability of peatlands to fire and
carbon loss, 2015) with Pyne. Adds the reciprocal delegation fire was missing (the
named phenomenon and its mechanism are combustion's; fire reads the regime, combustion
reads the type) across the card, REFERENCES restrictions, and playwright instructions,
and fixes the stale four-mode law in index.mjs to name all six modes.
