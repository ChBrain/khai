Add the reclamation engine: the reclamation phase of the neighborhood-cycle family -- the deliberate intervention (restoration and conservation) that arrests a place's decay and takes the fallen place back, shown in what it makes of the age it inherits. Named reclamation rather than restoration to avoid the grief engine's restoration stem, and because Viollet-le-Duc's strict "restoration" is only one stance here. Four stances over an intervention root: conservation (Ruskin -- keep the age as found), reconstruction (Viollet-le-Duc -- rebuild to an ideal whole), reuse (adaptive reuse, the seam nearest gentrification), and facsimile (Lowenthal -- rescue as falsification, the family's ambivalence closed). Wires on: place at Shown. Lineage: Ruskin, Viollet-le-Duc, Riegl, Lowenthal.
