Add the prospection composite, a persona pulled by the unassured future. Wires two atoms -- the hope engine (the resting slant a persona brings to any horizon, across expected valence and felt agency) and the anxiety engine (the anticipatory apprehension a specific looming outcome stirs) -- and reads them as one prospective faculty, staged as three bridges: slant (the standing orientation before any outcome -- hopeful, optimistic, dreading, defiant), looming (the active anticipation a specific uncertain outcome stirs -- worry, vigilance, looming, intolerance of uncertainty), and reach (the twist -- hope and dread as one faculty, where to reach for an unassured good is to be exposed to its not-coming). Wires at Persona/Projection (audit). Anchored in Gilbert & Wilson's prospection, Seligman's Homo Prospectus, Snyder's hope theory, and Barlow's anxious apprehension. Distinct from the jeopardy composite (the mono-valent threat-response arc); prospection is the bivalent future-reach.
