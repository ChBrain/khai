Add the self-verification engine (process) -- a persona's drive to confirm its existing self-concept, seeking, eliciting, and holding onto feedback that matches how it already sees itself, even when that self-view is negative. The gap analysis (Tier-2 #16) found this homeless: the codebase held the self-enhancement motive (the `bias` engine's self-serving member) but not its counterweight -- Swann's drive to confirm firmly held self-views, which diverges from enhancement whenever a self-view is negative.

- **Root (process):** `self-verification` -- the drive to confirm the self-concept, running on the self-view's _content_ rather than its favorability.
- **Routes (process):**
  - `soliciting` -- seeking self-confirming feedback and building a self-confirming world (selecting partners, roles, and settings that verify the self-view).
  - `signaling` -- eliciting self-confirming reactions through identity cues and self-presentation (identity negotiation).
  - `resistance` -- rejecting self-discrepant feedback even when favorable (discounting, avoiding, and withdrawing from appraisals that do not fit).

Warranted (LORE) on Swann (1983, the founding; 2012, the mature statement and marriage/roommate studies), Swann/Rentfrow/Guinn (2003, the routes), and Swann & Pelham (2002, leaving over-favorable relationships -- the signature resistance). Bounded against `self-esteem` (the _level_ of self-worth vs the drive to confirm whatever the self-view is), `identity` (the self-story's _content_ vs the motive to ratify it), the `bias` engine's self-serving member (the opposing _enhancement_ motive -- the two align when the self-view is positive and pull apart when negative), the `bias` engine's cognitive self-confirming/coherence family (the _passive processing_ slant, which this engine delegates while owning the _active interpersonal_ routes -- one phenomenon, one engine), and `dissonance` (the general inconsistency drive vs the specifically self-confirming one). Set at patch as the free level. No whitelist required -- the `self_verification`/`soliciting`/`signaling`/`resistance` stems are unique (`seeking` was avoided as a compound head owned by bias/reward; `confirmation`/`self_consistency` are bias's).
