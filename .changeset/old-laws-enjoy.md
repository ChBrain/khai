Record the canon decision that opens the **production layer**: a house may
publish more than one package, and the third package layer beside the engine and
the composite is one khai play published on its own.

The order sets the discriminator on the canon's own class vocabulary
(`khai.class: "house"`, no new word), fixes what a production's manifest carries
and what its validator deliberately does not (no WIRES card, no generated README,
no LORE warrant), and records why khai-pack is not the alternative: it ships
bytes, and what the split needs is a reference npm can check. The house registry
card is unchanged, with the reasoning for why a `packages[]` array would be a
hand-kept list of things khai cannot see. Documentation only; the contract ships
with khai-tests.
