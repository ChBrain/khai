Add the conditioning engine: how a neutral cue that reliably predicts a significant outcome comes, by that prediction, to evoke an anticipatory response of its own -- Pavlovian (classical) conditioning, the learning of what predicts what. The root (`process_conditioning.md`) declares the modern, prediction-based account: a cue is learned as a signal not by mere pairing but by contingency and information -- it is conditioned insofar as it improves the prediction of the outcome. Three forms carry it: `acquisition` (the learning route -- the predictive link built by surprise, learning proportional to prediction error, a redundant cue blocked), `extinction` (the waning route -- the cue presented without the outcome and the response fading, as new learning laid over the old rather than erasure, proven by spontaneous recovery, renewal, and reinstatement), and `generalization` (the spreading route -- the response transferred to resembling cues along a similarity gradient, then sharpened to the true predictor by discrimination).

A foundational behavioral-science gap. Bounded against `habit` (the response repeated into intentionless automaticity -- the instrumental side -- vs the classical learning of a cue's prediction), `reward` (the discount rate on the outcome's value vs the learning that a cue predicts the outcome at all), `addiction` (the wanting-turned-needing that draws on cue-evoked craving and relapse vs the predictive learning itself), and `representativeness` (likelihood judged by resemblance to a type vs the transfer of a conditioned response along a stimulus-similarity gradient). No homonym gate: `conditioning` and the form stems `acquisition`/`extinction`/`generalization` are all free. Warranted (LORE) on Pavlov (_Conditioned Reflexes_, 1927), Rescorla (contingency not contiguity, 1968; "it's not what you think it is", 1988), Rescorla & Wagner (the error-driven model, 1972), Kamin (blocking, 1969), and Bouton (extinction as new learning, renewal and reinstatement, 2002). Set at patch as the free level; a new engine may warrant a minor at the maintainer's `bump:minor` label.
