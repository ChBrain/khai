Add the geomorphology engine: the ground's own violence, the landform made and broken by forces from within and below, wired on: place/Shown. The first engine of the land's-forces family (F-place). A depth-1 process engine -- a ground root over four modes: uplift (the ground raised), quake (the fault ruptured), eruption (new ground poured from below, and what it buries), and subsidence (the ground given way). It is the sudden, endogenic counterpart to erosion's slow exogenic wearing, and reads the making and the breaking of the land as one force. First release, so an empty changeset (publishes at 0.1.0).
