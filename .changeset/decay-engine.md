Add the decay engine: the material substrate of the neighborhood-cycle family -- the material entropy a place undergoes, matter losing the order imposed on it, shown in the place's own fabric. Four modes over an entropy root: rust (metal to oxide), rot (organic to soil), crumble (mineral to grit), and patina (the same process prized as the value of age, the seam handed to restoration). Wires on: place at Shown. Lineage: Simmel, Riegl, Trigg, DeSilvey.
